## IntegralLattices: 2026-08-14 to 2026-09-01 (`7759fe7` to `8745177`)

The roadmap's summit is in: the **spinor glue lattice `D₈⁺` is isometric to the `E₈` root lattice** ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₈IsometryD8Plus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/D8Plus/Isometry.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₈IsometryD8Plus)), by an explicit rational map carrying Bourbaki's simple roots to glue roots whose Gram matrix is the `E₈` Cartan matrix, and its discriminant form agrees with the general `H⊥/H` comparison ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.d8PlusDiscriminantQuadraticIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/D8Plus/Isometry.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.d8PlusDiscriminantQuadraticIsometry)). Behind it stand the two general theorems of Layer 4: **Nikulin's even-overlattice correspondence** ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.evenIntermediateCarrierOrderIsoIsotropicSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Overlattice/Isotropic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.evenIntermediateCarrierOrderIsoIsotropicSubgroup)), an order isomorphism between even overlattices `L ≤ M ≤ L^∨` and quadratic-isotropic subgroups of `A_L`, with its integral/bilinear-isotropic sibling; and **the discriminant form of an even overlattice is `H⊥/H`** ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IntermediateCarrier.discriminantOrthogonalQuotientIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Overlattice/OrthogonalQuotient.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IntermediateCarrier.discriminantOrthogonalQuotientIsometry)), natural in lattice isometries. The index and discriminant laws `[L_H:L]=|H|`, `disc(L_H)·|H|²=disc(L)`, and the criterion that `L_H` is unimodular exactly when `H` is Lagrangian ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.dual_intermediateCarrierOfDiscriminantSubgroup_eq_self_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Overlattice/Dual.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.dual_intermediateCarrierOfDiscriminantSubgroup_eq_self_iff)) round out the layer.

Every row of the Layer 5 table is verified as a finite quadratic module, not a group order: `⟨2m⟩` for all nonzero `m` ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.rankOneDiscriminantEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RankOne.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.rankOneDiscriminantEquiv)), `Aₙ` ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeADiscriminantQuadraticIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeA.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeADiscriminantQuadraticIsometry)), the checkerboard `Dₙ` in Conway–Sloane coordinates with its simple-root basis, for even `n` ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.checkerboardDiscriminantQuadraticIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeD/Basic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.checkerboardDiscriminantQuadraticIsometry)) and odd `n` ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.checkerboardCyclicQuadraticIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeD/Basic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.checkerboardCyclicQuadraticIsometry)), and `E₆`, `E₇`, `E₈` ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₆DiscriminantQuadraticIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeE.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₆DiscriminantQuadraticIsometry), [`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₇DiscriminantQuadraticIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeE.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₇DiscriminantQuadraticIsometry), [`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isUnimodular_typeE₈RootLattice`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeE.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isUnimodular_typeE₈RootLattice)).

Underneath is a large foundation: the bundled `IntegralLattice` with signatures, isometries, Gram invariants, scaling, orthogonal sums and the radical quotient (TauCeti#3190 onward); duality, where `L^∨` is a lattice and `A_L` finite exactly when `L` is nondegenerate ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.fg_dualCarrier_iff_nondegenerate`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Dual/Finiteness.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.fg_dualCarrier_iff_nondegenerate)); `#A_L = disc L` ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.natCard_discriminantGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Discriminant/Cardinality.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.natCard_discriminantGroup)) with a Smith decomposition of `A_L`; and finite bilinear and quadratic modules with primary decomposition and `H⊥/H` quotients. Not there: the bilinear comparison `A_{L_H} ≅ H⊥/H` for integral but odd overlattices, and the invariant-factor divisibility chain is recorded only for positive-determinant Gram matrices (TauCeti#5305). The ADE lattices are built on coordinate spaces from their Cartan matrices; no general bridge from Tau Ceti's root data landed.

## IntegralLattices: 2026-09-01 to 2026-10-01 (`8745177` to `06fa4da`)

The gap this window closes in the roadmap proper is in Layer 2. The discriminant group's invariant factors are now defined for every nondegenerate lattice, indefinite and negative definite ones included, and proved to be a Smith normal form of the Gram matrix ([`exists_gramSmithInvariantFactors_smith_normal_form`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Discriminant/Smith.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.exists_gramSmithInvariantFactors_smith_normal_form)). Underneath is Smith normal form for every nonsingular integer matrix ([`Matrix.exists_smith_normal_form_of_det_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/SmithNormalForm.html#Matrix.exists_smith_normal_form_of_det_ne_zero)), and on top of it `A_L` splits into cyclic groups whose orders form a divisibility chain and multiply to the discriminant ([`discriminantGroupInvariantFactorsEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Discriminant/Smith.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.discriminantGroupInvariantFactorsEquiv)). An even lattice also now carries its integer half-norm [quadratic form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Quadratic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.quadraticForm), the unique one whose polar form is the lattice form.

Most of the remaining work goes beyond this roadmap's stated scope, toward the larger quadratic-forms programme. A positive definite lattice has finite shells, a minimum, a [kissing number](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Minimum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.kissingNumber), and only finitely many isometries ([`IsPosDef.finite_isometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Finite.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPosDef.finite_isometry)). In a unimodular lattice the characteristic vectors exist and all have the same norm mod 8 ([`characteristicVector_integralNorm_modEq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Characteristic/Unimodular.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsUnimodular.characteristicVector_integralNorm_modEq)), which sets up van der Blij's congruence but does not prove it. On the local side, symmetric forms diagonalize over `ℤ_p` for odd `p` ([`exists_orthogonal_basis_of_isUnit_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/BilinearForm/ValuationRing.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.IsSymm.exists_orthogonal_basis_of_isUnit_two)), and `ℤ_p ⊗ L` sits inside `ℚ_p ⊗ V` as a full lattice ([`isBaseChange_localizationToCompletion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Localization.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isBaseChange_localizationToCompletion)).

Two things are still not established by this window's declarations: the bilinear comparison `A_{L_H} ≅ H⊥/H` for integral but odd overlattices, and its componentwise form over orthogonal sums. Those are the two clauses that keep Layer 4 partial. The ADE Cartan matrices are now proved positive definite ([`posDef_map_intCast_cartanMatrix_of_isSimplyLaced`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/FiniteType/SimplyLaced.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.posDef_map_intCast_cartanMatrix_of_isSimplyLaced)), but there is still no general conversion of Tau Ceti's root data into integral lattices.

## IntegralLattices: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-18 (`06fa4da` to `02c89d0`)

The main new mathematics this window goes beyond Layer 3's requirements: finite quadratic modules now have Gauss sums and the invariant they define. For a nondegenerate module the Gauss sum is `√#A · e^{2πi k/8}` ([`IsNondegenerate.gaussSum_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/GaussSum.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.IsNondegenerate.gaussSum_eq)). The class `k ∈ ℤ/8` it determines ([`gaussSign`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/GaussSum.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.gaussSign)) is an isometry invariant, additive over orthogonal sums and zero on metabolic modules. It is also unchanged by isotropic reduction, because `G(A) = |H| · G(H⊥/H)` ([`gaussSum_eq_card_mul_gaussSum_orthogonalQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/Orthogonal/GaussSum.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.gaussSum_eq_card_mul_gaussSum_orthogonalQuotient)). So an even overlattice has the same invariant as the lattice it enlarges. Milgram's formula, which identifies the invariant of `A_L` with the signature `n₊ − n₋` mod 8, has not landed.

The same modules acquired the first of Nikulin's normal forms. **Every nondegenerate quadratic module of odd order is an orthogonal sum of cyclic ones** ([`exists_isometry_pi_oddCyclic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/OddCyclic/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.exists_isometry_pi_oddCyclic)). Cyclic modules of odd or 2-power order are classified up to isometry by their coefficient modulo unit squares ([`nonempty_isometry_dyadicCyclic_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/Dyadic/Classification.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.nonempty_isometry_dyadicCyclic_iff)). The dyadic rank-two blocks `u` and `v` are now defined and can be recognised from a suitable pair of generators, but the full 2-primary decomposition has not been assembled.

The rest is lattice infrastructure, mostly beyond the roadmap's stated scope. For positive definite lattices this includes Hermite's inequality `min L ≤ (4/3)^{(n−1)/2} (det L)^{1/n}` ([`minimum_le_rpow_mul_determinant_rpow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Hermite.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPosDef.minimum_le_rpow_mul_determinant_rpow)), with equality for `A₂`, and successive minima. The identity `covol² = det` ([`covolume_range_sq_eq_determinant`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Covolume.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.covolume_range_sq_eq_determinant)) supplies the README's optional `IsZLattice` comparison. Also new are the level and the scale and norm ideals, restriction to sublattices with orthogonal splitting, and a zero-sum coordinate model of `Aₙ`. Layer 4 still lacks the bilinear `H⊥/H` comparison for odd overlattices and its orthogonal-sum form.
