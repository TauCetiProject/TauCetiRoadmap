## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The roadmap's completion criterion is met. The general APIs of Layers 1 to 3, every row of the Layer 5 table and the `D₈⁺ ≅ E₈` isometry are formalized. Layer 4 is done apart from two secondary clauses: the bilinear `H⊥/H` comparison for odd overlattices, and its form over orthogonal sums. Recent work has gone past the roadmap's scope, into Gauss sums, Nikulin's normal forms for finite quadratic modules, and invariants of positive definite lattices.

### Named results

- **The `D₈⁺ ≅ E₈` isometry**: the spinor glue enlargement of the checkerboard lattice `D₈` is isometric to the `E₈` root lattice. The isometry is an explicit rational map, and its glue roots have the `E₈` Cartan matrix as their Gram matrix ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₈IsometryD8Plus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/D8Plus/Isometry.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₈IsometryD8Plus)).
- **Nikulin's even-overlattice correspondence**: for an even nondegenerate lattice, the even overlattices `L ≤ M ≤ L^∨` are order-isomorphic to the quadratic-isotropic subgroups of `A_L`. Their discriminant forms are `H⊥/H`, and `L_H` is unimodular exactly when `H` is Lagrangian ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.evenIntermediateCarrierOrderIsoIsotropicSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Overlattice/Isotropic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.evenIntermediateCarrierOrderIsoIsotropicSubgroup)).
- **The ADE discriminant forms**: for `Aₙ`, for `Dₙ` of each parity, and for `E₆`, `E₇` and `E₈`, the discriminant quadratic module is identified up to isometry with an explicit cyclic or Klein-four model. The quadratic values are checked, not only the group orders ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.checkerboardDiscriminantQuadraticIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeD/Basic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.checkerboardDiscriminantQuadraticIsometry)).
- **The cyclic decomposition of the discriminant group**: for every nondegenerate lattice, `A_L` is a product of cyclic groups. Their orders are the Smith invariant factors of the Gram matrix and multiply to `|det L|` ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.discriminantGroupInvariantFactorsEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Discriminant/Smith.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.discriminantGroupInvariantFactorsEquiv)).
- **The Gauss-sum invariant**: a nondegenerate finite quadratic module has Gauss sum `√#A · e^{2πi k/8}`, and the resulting class in `ℤ/8` is additive and survives isotropic reduction to `H⊥/H`. As a result, it is preserved on passing to an even overlattice ([`TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.IsNondegenerate.gaussSum_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/GaussSum.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.IsNondegenerate.gaussSum_eq), [`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IntermediateCarrier.gaussSign_discriminantQuadraticModule`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Overlattice/GaussSum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IntermediateCarrier.gaussSign_discriminantQuadraticModule)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`TauCeti.IntegralLattice`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Basic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice) is the single carrier for the whole roadmap: a full `ℤ`-submodule of a rational space with an integral symmetric form, where nondegeneracy is a mixin. Indefinite and degenerate lattices are objects of the same type. Restriction to an arbitrary submodule now produces a lattice of the same type in its own rational span ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.restrict`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Restriction.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.restrict)).
- [`TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/Quadratic.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule) is a finite abelian group with a `ℚ/ℤ`-valued quadratic form. It comes with orthogonal complements, primary decomposition, `H⊥/H` quotients, and splitting along a nondegenerate subgroup. The gluing theorem, the ADE models and the odd-order normal form ([`TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.exists_isometry_pi_oddCyclic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/OddCyclic/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.exists_isometry_pi_oddCyclic)) are all stated in this setting.
- The [level](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Level.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.level) of a lattice is the least `N` for which `N · B(x,x)` is even on the dual. For an even lattice it is proved to be the annihilator of the discriminant form, which links the lattice side to the finite-module side.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 1, 2, 3 and 5 are done. In Layer 5, type `D` and `E₈` use simple-root coordinates, while `Aₙ`, `E₆` and `E₇` are built from their Cartan matrices, as the README allows. `Aₙ` is now also identified with its zero-sum coordinate model. Layer 4 is done apart from two clauses: the bilinear comparison `A_{L_H} ≅ H⊥/H` for integral but odd overlattices, and its componentwise form over orthogonal sums. The most recent work, on Gauss sums, Nikulin's 2-adic and odd normal forms, Hermite's inequality, successive minima, scale and norm ideals, and the rational form class, lies outside the roadmap's layers and does not close either clause.

## The frontier

- **Bilinear `H⊥/H` for odd overlattices**: the isometry `A_{L_H} ≅ H⊥/H` of discriminant bilinear modules for an integral overlattice that need not be even. The evidence does not show that this has landed.
- **Orthogonal-sum form of the comparison**: the README's componentwise statement for `A_{L_H} ≅ H⊥/H`. The equivalence `A_{L⊥M} ≃ A_L × A_M` and the finite-module splitting along a nondegenerate subgroup are both available.
- **Lattices from Tau Ceti's root data**: a construction that turns a pinned root datum into an `IntegralLattice` whose Gram matrix is its Cartan matrix. Positive definiteness of the simply-laced Cartan matrices is already proved ([`TauCeti.DynkinType.posDef_map_intCast_cartanMatrix_of_isSimplyLaced`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/FiniteType/SimplyLaced.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.posDef_map_intCast_cartanMatrix_of_isSimplyLaced)).
- **`IsZLattice` comparison**: optional according to the README. The covolume identity `covol² = det` ([`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.covolume_range_sq_eq_determinant`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Covolume.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.covolume_range_sq_eq_determinant)) connects positive definite lattices to Mathlib's real lattices, but no general comparison has been stated.
