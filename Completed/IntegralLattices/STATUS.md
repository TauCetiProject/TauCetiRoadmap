## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All five layers are done: the lattice and finite-form APIs carry the overlattice correspondence, the ADE discriminant tables and the `D₈⁺ ≅ E₈` glue isometry. Work beyond the charter has reached the unimodular classification up to rank nine, a table of the 24 Niemeier lattices, and masses and class numbers of explicit genera. A general dyadic normal form, Nikulin's existence theorems and the mass formula itself are still absent.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [Integral lattices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Basic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice) share one carrier for definite, indefinite and degenerate forms; the radical quotient and restriction constructions let the general API reach each case.
- The [discriminant quadratic module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Discriminant/Quadratic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.discriminantQuadraticModule) connects an even lattice to its finite form. It underlies the gluing theorem, the ADE calculations, Milgram's theorem, and the statement that the genus determines the discriminant form.
- The [mass](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Mass.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.mass) of a genus, with class numbers and proper class numbers, is the quantity a mass formula would compute. A [mass check](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Mass.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.sum_inv_natCard_isometry_eq_mass_iff) certifies complete sets of class representatives.

### Roadmap coverage

All five layers are done. Layers 1 to 3 supply the lattice, duality, discriminant-group and finite-form APIs, with Smith invariant factors for every nondegenerate lattice. Layer 4 includes the quadratic and bilinear `H⊥/H` comparisons and their orthogonal-sum versions. Layer 5 verifies every rank-one and [ADE table row](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeD/Basic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.checkerboardDiscriminantQuadraticIsometry) as a finite quadratic module, and the `D₈⁺ ≅ E₈` isometry. Outside the charter, Tau Ceti now has Nikulin's odd-order decomposition and the classification of 2-elementary and cyclic dyadic finite quadratic modules; van der Blij's congruence; the genus of a unimodular lattice; local densities at every prime with closed forms at odd primes; the volume of `O(n)`; exact masses, including [`1/696729600` for `E₈`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/E8Genus.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.mass_typeE₈RootLattice) and [`3/4` for the two-class genus of determinant 23](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Binary/Determinant23/Genus.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.mass_binaryDeterminant23); invariance of mass and class number under scaling; Eichler's unique decomposition into indecomposables; and binary forms through the ring class field and Gauss reduction.

## The frontier

- **General dyadic normal form** — [sign walking](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/BilinearForm/SignWalking.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.IsJordanSplitting.exists_signWalk_add_two_of_odd_of_odd) and [oddity fusion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/BilinearForm/Oddity/Fusion.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.IsJordanSplitting.exists_oddityFusion_succ) are proved for Jordan splittings over `ℤ₂`, and lattices whose constituents are all of type II are classified. What remains is to assemble these moves into a canonical 2-adic symbol and isometry criterion with type I constituents, and the matching classification of arbitrary 2-primary finite quadratic modules across several exponents.
- **The dyadic local density and the mass formula** — densities have closed forms at odd primes, but at 2 only rank-one and hyperbolic-plane values are computed. A Smith-Minkowski-Siegel formula also needs the adelic volume comparison for `SO(V)`, which the active `IntegralLattices` roadmap assigns to its successor `OrthogonalTamagawaAndLatticeMass`.
- **Nikulin's existence theorems and primitive embeddings** — [primitive embeddings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PrimitiveEmbedding.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPrimitiveEmbedding) are defined, with only the rank-one-into-hyperbolic-plane example. Existence of an even lattice with given signature and discriminant form, the existence half of Theorem 3.6.2, and the converse of Corollary 1.9.4 remain open. The uniqueness theorems pass through Eichler's theorem, which the active roadmap places outside its scope.
- **Completeness of the Niemeier table** — the 24 lattices are distinct, but no proof that every even unimodular lattice of rank 24 is among them has landed, nor any classification in rank ten to twenty-three, including the even rank-sixteen pair.
- **Pinned root-data bridge** — for `E₈` the lattice is identified with the weight lattice of the pinned root datum; the other ADE types are still built from coordinates.
