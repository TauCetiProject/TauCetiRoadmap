## ContourIntegration: 2026-07-04 to 2026-08-01 (`5d29c9d` to `6919462`)

The summit landed. `hungerbuhlerWasem_residueTheorem` (TauCeti#966) is HW Thm 3.3: for `f` holomorphic on `U ∖ S` and meromorphic at each point of a finite `S`, and a null-homologous closed piecewise-`C¹` immersion that may pass *through* the singularities, the Cauchy principal value of `∮ f` is `2πi · Σ_s n_s(γ) · Res_s f`, weighted by the generalized non-integer winding numbers <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/HungerbuhlerWasem.html#TauCeti.Contour.hungerbuhlerWasem_residueTheorem>. Conditions (A′) and (B) enter as hypotheses; at simple poles both are automatic (TauCeti#972, TauCeti#1205), so that regime is unconditional, and its one-point winding-`½` case is the half-residue `πi · Res_s f`.

The classical backbone arrived in the same window: the homology Cauchy theorem, by Dixon's argument (TauCeti#911, TauCeti#919) <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/HomologyCauchy.html#TauCeti.Contour.homologyCauchyTheorem>, its integral formula for `f` and for every iterated derivative (TauCeti#1464), the classical residue theorem for a null-homologous cycle avoiding its poles (TauCeti#1439), and the argument principle counting zeros and poles by winding weight (TauCeti#1463).

Underneath, the winding number acquired its geometry — the model sector's `α/2π`, hence `½` at a smooth crossing and `1/6` at a `π/3` corner (TauCeti#1385, TauCeti#705), and the circle's `1` inside, `0` outside (TauCeti#967, TauCeti#1203). The worked examples close the loop, notably the principal value of `e^{iaz}/z` along `[−R, R]` tending to `πi` (TauCeti#1271) <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/WorkedExamples/HalfDisc/Dirichlet.html#TauCeti.Contour.tendsto_cauchyPV_realSegment_dirichlet>. Two gaps deserve naming: HW Prop 2.3's real bounded-integrand formula is proved only off the curve (TauCeti#962), with the crossing value `(ẋÿ − ẏẍ)/(2(ẋ² + ẏ²))` separate and under a `C²` hypothesis (TauCeti#1488); and homotopy invariance is stated for `C²` homotopies (TauCeti#1364). About a fifth of the pull requests here added no declarations at all — module splits and proof consolidation.

## ContourIntegration: 2026-08-01 to 2026-09-04 (`6919462` to `0672a6c`)

Hungerbühler–Wasem Proposition 2.2 is now present as an actual crossing decomposition: a closed piecewise-`C¹` immersion is replaced by a point-avoiding curve and finitely many model-sector contributions, giving its winding number as an integer plus the sum of crossing angles divided by `2π` ([`exists_crossingDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Crossing/ImmersionDecomposition.html#TauCeti.Contour.IsPwC1ImmersionOn.exists_crossingDecomposition)). Proposition 2.3 likewise now covers on-curve interior crossings: under one-sided `C^{1,1}` control, the real winding integrand is bounded and integrable and computes the generalized winding number ([`windingNumber_eq_real_integral_of_closed_interior_crossings`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Winding/RealIntegral/OnCurve.html#TauCeti.Contour.windingNumber_eq_real_integral_of_closed_interior_crossings)).

The theory was also lifted from one closed curve to finite integral contour cycles. Principal values, winding numbers and integrals extend additively over generators, and the cycle-level [Hungerbühler–Wasem generalized residue theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Cycle/HungerbuhlerWasem.html#TauCeti.Contour.Cycle.hungerbuhlerWasem_residueTheorem) now includes the unconditional simple-pole regime; the classical residue theorem and Cauchy integral formulas have corresponding null-homologous cycle forms.

Two earlier restrictions disappeared. Winding number is invariant under an arbitrary continuous path homotopy through the punctured plane, rather than only a `C²` homotopy ([`windingNumber_eq_of_pathHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Winding/Number/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.Contour.windingNumber_eq_of_pathHomotopy)), and the worked half-disc argument now yields the real [Dirichlet integral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/WorkedExamples/HalfDisc/SineIntegral.html#TauCeti.Contour.tendsto_integral_sin_div_atTop), `∫₀ᴿ sin x / x dx → π/2`.
