## Where this roadmap stands

**Layer 0 (curves, cycles, the generalized winding number)** — essentially complete. `IsPiecewiseC1On` and `IsPwC1ImmersionOn` carry the curve regularity <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/PwC1ImmersionOn.html#TauCeti.Contour.IsPwC1ImmersionOn>, with interval-integrability of `γ' • (f ∘ γ)` for `f` continuous on the image and reparametrization invariance of the contour integral. `HasCauchyPV` and `HasCauchyPVAt` are kept strictly apart from ordinary integrability, and `windingNumber` is defined as the normalized principal value for *every* `z₀` <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Winding/Number/Basic.html#TauCeti.Contour.windingNumber>, together with `IsNullHomologous` and its lattice lemmas. Off the curve it is locally constant, constant on each component of the complement, and zero on unbounded components. The one Layer-0 item with no supporting declaration is **integrality**: nothing here states `n_{z₀}(C) ∈ ℤ` for `z₀` off the curve. Homotopy invariance is present but narrower than stated — it requires a path homotopy whose extension to the square is `C²`.

**Layer 1 (the geometry of the winding number, HW §2)** — partly done, and the parts that the summit consumes are done. The model sector has winding `α/2π` <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/ModelSector/Closed.html#TauCeti.Contour.windingNumber_closedModelSector>, giving `½` at a smooth crossing and `1/6` at a `π/3` corner, and the valence-formula values at `i` and at `ρ` are stated in their own right (`windingNumber_at_i`, `windingNumber_at_rho`). The two-ray corner contributes nothing, so all of the sector's index comes from the arc. A piecewise-`C¹` immersion meets a point only finitely often, with the crossing finset characterized. What is **not** finished: HW Prop 2.2's decomposition `n_{z₀}(Λ) = n_{z₀}(Λ̃) + Σ_ℓ α_ℓ/2π` does not appear as a statement, and HW Prop 2.3's real bounded-integrand formula is proved only in the **off-curve** case <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Winding/RealIntegral.html#TauCeti.Contour.windingNumber_eq_real_integral_of_closed>. The crossing value `½·k·|Λ̇|` exists separately, in chord-expansion form and under a `C²` hypothesis at the crossing, with the circle as a check.

**Layer 2 (residues, the Cauchy primitive, the classical residue theorem)** — done, and further than planned. The FTC along an arc is available in both `deriv` and explicit-velocity forms; `residue` is the order-`(−1)` Laurent coefficient against `meromorphicOrderAt`, with additivity, scaling, the simple-pole limit `lim (z − z₀) f(z)`, quotient formulas (`g/h` at a simple zero of `h`, `(zⁿ − 1)⁻¹` at a root of unity), and the simple-pole decomposition `f = analytic + Σ Res_s f/(z − s)`. Both scopes of the classical residue theorem landed: the circle case `classicalResidueTheorem_circle`, and the general arbitrary-cycle case the roadmap deferred to Layer 3+, `classicalResidueTheorem_nullHomologous` <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Residue/Cycle.html#TauCeti.Contour.classicalResidueTheorem_nullHomologous>. The argument principle is there in the null-homologous-cycle form <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Argument/Cycle.html#TauCeti.Contour.argumentPrinciple_nullHomologous>, rather than the circle form pinned in `Suggested.lean`, along with a local single-zero-or-pole version and a winding-weighted zero count for analytic `f`.

**Layer 3 (the global Cauchy theorem)** — done. `homologyCauchyTheorem` <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/HomologyCauchy.html#TauCeti.Contour.homologyCauchyTheorem> is proved by Dixon's argument, with the auxiliary function and its two halves split across their own modules, plus a form taking a supplied off-curve point. The homology Cauchy integral formula follows for `f` and for every iterated derivative, and null-homotopic implies null-homologous (for `C²` homotopies).

**Layer 4 (Hungerbühler–Wasem, HW Thm 3.3)** — done in the pinned form. `hungerbuhlerWasem_residueTheorem` <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/HungerbuhlerWasem.html#TauCeti.Contour.hungerbuhlerWasem_residueTheorem> holds under conditions (A′) and (B), which are structures with flatness (`FlatOfOrder`), crossing and basepoint angles, and sector compatibility; a discharge layer turns them into the hypotheses of the polar-part principal-value theorem. At worst-simple poles both conditions are automatic, giving an unconditional statement and the half-residue `πi · Res_s f`. Higher-order poles go through the sector-cancellation identity, the per-window principal value, and the antiderivative-difference estimate. The pinned narrowings stand: a single closed curve rather than a cycle, a finite `S`, `MeromorphicAt` at each singularity, basepoint off the poles.

**Worked examples** — all of the roadmap's acceptance criteria are realised: the model sector's `α/2π`; `n_c(circle) = 1` inside and `0` outside; two simple poles inside a circle returning `2πi(Res₁ + Res₂)`; the half-disc boundary with winding `½` about the origin and its diameter carrying the half-residue; and the improper integral, `PV ∫ e^{iaz}/z` along `[−R, R]` tending to `πi` <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/WorkedExamples/HalfDisc/Dirichlet.html#TauCeti.Contour.tendsto_cauchyPV_realSegment_dirichlet>.

## The frontier

The named milestones are all claimed, so the remaining work is filling in the theory around them.

- **Integrality off the curve.** `n_{z₀}(C) ∈ ℤ` for `z₀ ∉ C`, via the primitive-of-`exp` argument. Nothing in the current declarations supplies it, and it is the missing half of the Layer-0 classical picture (vanishing on the unbounded component and local constancy are both in place).
- **HW Prop 2.2 as a statement.** The finiteness of crossings is available; the decomposition of `n_{z₀}(Λ)` into an avoiding curve plus a sum of model sectors is not.
- **HW Prop 2.3 on the curve.** The real bounded-integrand formula currently assumes `z₀` off the curve. The on-curve `C^{1,1}` version — the form that makes the integrand bounded where the complex integral needs a principal value — is the natural next target, and would connect the existing crossing-value limit to the integral it is supposed to serve.
- **Cycles.** Everything is stated for a single closed curve on `[a, b]`. The roadmap's `ℤ`-combinations of closed curves are not yet a type, and both the residue theorems and HW Thm 3.3 would want them.
- **Weakening the homotopy hypothesis.** Homotopy invariance and the null-homotopic case of Cauchy's theorem both require a `C²` path homotopy; the continuous case is the standard statement.
- **The valence formula.** The two values it consumes, `½` at `i` and `1/6` per `ρ`-corner, are proved and unblocked; what remains is on the Modular Forms side.
- **Module layout.** The tree has been reorganised into `Winding/`, `Residue/`, `PolarPart/`, `Cauchy/PrincipalValue/` and `Crossing/`, which is the shape the roadmap asks for: statements in Mathlib's vocabulary, with the fundamental-domain-specific scaffolding kept out.
