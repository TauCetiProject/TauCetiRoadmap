## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All four layers are done: the orthogonal group and its arithmetic inputs, the Lipschitz group and spinor norm, the local topology with the local spinor norms, and the adelic points with their class sets. The latest work has been consolidation. The spinor norm now lands in the literal quotient `Kˣ ⧸ (Kˣ)²` that the roadmap pins, and the dimension-four worked example now includes the centres of the even Clifford algebras. What remains of the subject belongs to the named successors.

### Named results

- **The image of Spin is the spinor kernel** — for a nondegenerate form over a field of characteristic not two, the image of `Spin(Q) → SO(Q)` is exactly the kernel of the spinor norm ([`range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm)).
- **Spin is the even unitary group up to dimension five, and not beyond** — for `1 ≤ dim V ≤ 5`, Spin(Q) = U(C₀, σ) ([`evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Five.html#CliffordAlgebra.evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup)). From dimension six the inclusion is strict: for three hyperbolic planes Spin is SL₄ ([`hyperbolicSixSpinEquivSpecialLinear`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Six/SpecialLinear.html#TauCeti.hyperbolicSixSpinEquivSpecialLinear)) inside an even unitary group that is GL₄.
- **Spin₅ is a form of Sp₄** — in dimension five, reversal is a symplectic involution of the even Clifford algebra. Spin(Q) is Sp₄(K) when that algebra splits ([`exists_spinGroupEquivSymplecticGroup_of_finrank_eq_five`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Symplectic.html#CliffordAlgebra.exists_spinGroupEquivSymplecticGroup_of_finrank_eq_five)). Otherwise it is a twisted form of Sp₄ that splits over a separably closed extension.
- **The compactness criterion** — over a locally compact field such as ℝ or `ℚ_p`, O(Q) is compact exactly when Q is anisotropic ([`isCompact_orthogonalGroup_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Compact/Basic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.isCompact_orthogonalGroup_iff)). The same holds for SO and, because `Spin → SO` is proper, for Spin.
- **Rational points are discrete in the full adeles, not in the finite adeles** — the rational points of O, SO and Spin are discrete in `G(𝔸)` for every compatible compact-open tuple ([`discreteTopology_range_fullAdelicSpinDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Diagonal/Discrete.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.discreteTopology_range_fullAdelicSpinDiagonal)). For an isotropic space of dimension at least three they accumulate at the identity in `G(𝔸_f)` ([`not_discreteTopology_range_finiteAdelicSpecialOrthogonalDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Diagonal/NonDiscrete/Basic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.not_discreteTopology_range_finiteAdelicSpecialOrthogonalDiagonal)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The spinor norm on `O(Q)` ([`orthogonalSpinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.orthogonalSpinorNorm)), now valued in `Kˣ ⧸ (Kˣ)²`. A canonical equivalence with the additive square-class group ([`multiplicativeSquareClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/SquareClassGroup/Multiplicative.html#TauCeti.multiplicativeSquareClassEquiv)) lets results move between the two conventions, and the local index and cardinality formulas are stated on the multiplicative side.
- The adelic point groups for each of O, SO and Spin: finite, full ([`fullAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/FullAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.fullAdelicSpin)) and away from S ([`awayAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/AwayAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.awayAdelicSpin)). They come with rational diagonals, the componentwise maps Spin → SO → O, and, when S is empty, an identification of the away points with the finite-adelic points. Together with the continuous adelic spinor norm, these are the carriers the successor's approximation statements will use.
- The class sets `G(ℚ) \ G(𝔸_f) / U` ([`finiteAdelicSpinDoubleCoset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/DoubleCoset/Basic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.finiteAdelicSpinDoubleCoset)), with the three comparisons the roadmap names: a surjection for an inclusion of compact opens, a bijection for conjugate ones, and transport along equivalences of tuples. Genus and spinor-genus counting will be stated on these.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layers 0 to 3 are all done.** Every case of 1F from dimension three to six is done. Dimension four now also has the centre computations: the even Clifford algebras of `H ⟂ H` and of the real sum of four squares both have split centre, but the real one is `ℍ × ℍ` and not two copies of `M₂(ℝ)` ([`isEmpty_realCliffordFourZeroEven_algEquiv_matrix_prod`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/RealForm/Four.html#TauCeti.isEmpty_realCliffordFourZeroEven_algEquiv_matrix_prod)). In Layer 3 the finite-adelic counterexample covers every isotropic space of dimension at least three, which is more than the split case the README asks for. Finiteness of the class sets is out of scope by design.

## The frontier

- **`OrthogonalTamagawaAndLatticeMass`** — strong approximation for Spin (the closure of the rational spin image in adelic SO is the adelic spinor kernel), Tamagawa numbers and the mass formula. All of these consume this roadmap's exports. They wait on an algebraic-group point functor and on generic strong-approximation and Tamagawa inputs, none of which this roadmap imports.
- **`AlgebrasWithInvolution`** — `Spin ≅ SU(C₀, σ)` in dimension six and an intrinsic `Sp(C₀, σ)` in dimension five. Both need the reduced norm of a central simple algebra, which nothing here supplies.
- **Finiteness of the class sets** — `G(ℚ) \ G(𝔸_f) / U` is defined with its comparisons, but finiteness needs reduction theory, which belongs downstream.
