## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Every part of the roadmap is done. The Hermite functions and the Chebyshev polynomials are complete orthonormal bases of their `L²` spaces. Both come from one family-agnostic layer, which also supplies the weighted-measure and multidimensional bases. Each basis has an explicit expansion and Parseval API. The Hermite basis also has its two structural consequences: it diagonalizes the `L²` Fourier transform, and on Schwartz space it carries the ladder operators. What is left lies outside the roadmap's own targets.

### Named results

- **The Hermite basis of `L²(ℝ)`** — [`hermiteHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.hermiteHilbertBasis) is a Hilbert basis over every `RCLike` scalar field, and its vectors are provably the Hermite functions `ψₙ`. Parseval's identity ([`tsum_norm_sq_inner_hermiteFunctionLp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Parseval.html#TauCeti.tsum_norm_sq_inner_hermiteFunctionLp)) and the expansion of an arbitrary `L²` function are proved on top of it.
- **The Chebyshev basis** — [`chebyshevTHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.chebyshevTHilbertBasis) makes the normalized `Tₙ` a Hilbert basis of `L²(measureT)`, and every function in that space equals its Chebyshev series ([`hasSum_chebyshevT_expansion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/Parseval.html#TauCeti.hasSum_chebyshevT_expansion)).
- **Moment determinacy** — a finite measure on `ℝ` with a single finite exponential moment is determined by its polynomial moments ([`Measure.ext_of_forall_integral_pow_eq_of_exists_integrable_exp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/Determinacy.html#TauCeti.Measure.ext_of_forall_integral_pow_eq_of_exists_integrable_exp)). Every basis here gets its completeness from this one result. The proof goes through characteristic functions and analyticity on a strip, not through the entire Fourier integral the roadmap proposed.
- **Hermite diagonalization of the Fourier transform** — the rescaled Hermite functions ([`twoPiHermiteHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Fourier/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.twoPiHermiteHilbertBasis)) are eigenvectors of the unitary Fourier transform of `L²(ℝ; ℂ)` with eigenvalue `(-i)ⁿ` ([`fourier_twoPiHermiteFunctionLp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Fourier/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.fourier_twoPiHermiteFunctionLp)). The transform of any `f` is therefore its Hermite series with the coefficients rotated.
- **The canonical commutation relation** — the creation and annihilation operators are continuous linear maps on `𝒮(ℝ, ℝ)` satisfying `[a, a†] = 1` ([`hermiteAnnihilationCLM_comp_hermiteCreationCLM_sub_hermiteCreationCLM_comp_hermiteAnnihilationCLM`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Operator.html#TauCeti.hermiteAnnihilationCLM_comp_hermiteCreationCLM_sub_hermiteCreationCLM_comp_hermiteAnnihilationCLM)), and the Hermite functions are eigenvectors of the oscillator with eigenvalue `n + 1/2`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`weightL2Isometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/WeightL2Isometry.html#TauCeti.weightL2Isometry) is multiplication by `√w`, as an isometry `L²(w·μ) ≃ₗᵢ L²(μ)`. Together with transport of Hilbert bases along isometries, it gives every family in both normalizations. For Chebyshev it produces the envelope basis of `L²((-1,1]; dx)` ([`chebyshevTEnvelopeHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/Envelope.html#TauCeti.chebyshevTEnvelopeHilbertBasis)).
- [`hilbertBasisOfWeightedMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/WeightedOrthogonalBasis.html#TauCeti.hilbertBasisOfWeightedMeasure) turns an orthogonality relation plus completeness into a Hilbert basis. Since moment determinacy supplies completeness, a new polynomial family needs only its orthogonality relation and one exponential moment.
- [`HilbertBasis.prod`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Product.html#HilbertBasis.prod) and [`piHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Pi.html#TauCeti.piHilbertBasis) build bases of binary and finite product measures from bases of the factors. In both, a Dynkin argument proves that the elementary tensors are complete. The binary tensor is also a bounded bilinear map `L²(μ) × L²(ν) → L²(μ ⊗ ν)` of norm at most one, with no finiteness assumption on the measures ([`Lp.prodMulL`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/Product.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.prodMulL)).

### Roadmap coverage

All four parts are done. **Part A** is fully proved, with expansion API: the Hermite polynomial calculus and orthogonality (A1), the pointwise Hermite-function API with parity, ladder identities and the oscillator eigen-equation (A2), the basis with Parseval and the Fourier eigenrelation (A3), and the Gaussian-measure basis (A3′). **Part B** (B1 through B3) is complete in both normalizations. **Part C** has the Chebyshev basis, its envelope form, and the cosine transfer under `x = cos θ` that its acceptance criterion names. **Part D** has the bases of `L²(ℝ^ι)` and of the Gaussian product measure for finite `ι`, each with multi-index Parseval.

## The frontier

- **Multidimensional Fourier diagonalization**: the one-dimensional eigenrelation and the product basis are both in place. Nobody has yet stated their combination on `L²(ℝ^ι)`.
- **Ladder operators on `L²`**: `a` and `a†` exist only on Schwartz space. On `L²` they are unbounded, so extending them needs closed-operator packaging rather than continuous linear maps. The README treats this as a downstream target, not a milestone.
- **Laguerre and Jacobi**: out of scope by design. The roadmap leaves them to a separate future roadmap, and the families would have to be defined before any `L²` basis statement about them.
