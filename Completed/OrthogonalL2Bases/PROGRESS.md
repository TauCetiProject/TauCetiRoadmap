## OrthogonalL2Bases: 2026-06-28 to 2026-08-03 (`4893c9f` to `11ef09d`)

Two summits landed. The Hermite functions are now a Hilbert basis of `L²(ℝ)` over any `RCLike` scalar field ([`hermiteHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.hermiteHilbertBasis)), and the Chebyshev polynomials `Tₙ` are a Hilbert basis of `L²` for Mathlib's Chebyshev measure ([`chebyshevTHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.chebyshevTHilbertBasis)). Each ships the element-level lemma identifying its vectors with the normalized family, so neither is a bare separability statement, and both complete through one mechanism: a finite measure on `ℝ` carrying a single finite exponential moment is determined by its polynomial moments ([`Measure.ext_of_forall_integral_pow_eq_of_exists_integrable_exp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/Determinacy.html#TauCeti.Measure.ext_of_forall_integral_pow_eq_of_exists_integrable_exp), TauCeti#1028). That proof runs through characteristic functions and analyticity of the complex moment generating function on a strip, not the roadmap's proposed entire Fourier integral.

The family-agnostic layer beneath both is in place, in both normalizations: `√w`-envelope functions on `L²(μ)` and bare polynomials on the weighted measure, exchanged by multiplication by `√w` ([`weightL2Isometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/WeightL2Isometry.html#TauCeti.weightL2Isometry)) and transport of a Hilbert basis along a linear isometric equivalence ([`HilbertBasis.mapₗᵢ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HilbertBasisMap.html#HilbertBasis.mapₗᵢ)). Product bases followed for two factors and for finite products ([`prodHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Product.html#TauCeti.prodHilbertBasis), [`piHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Pi.html#TauCeti.piHilbertBasis)); the work there is completeness, by a Dynkin argument carrying vanishing integrals on rectangles to vanishing integrals on every finite-measure set. Composed with Part A this gives the multidimensional bases [`hermiteFunctionPiBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/PiBasis.html#TauCeti.hermiteFunctionPiBasis) and [`gaussianHermitePiBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Hermite/PiBasis.html#TauCeti.gaussianHermitePiBasis).

Around the summits: Parseval and the Hermite expansion of an arbitrary `L²` function ([`hasSum_hermiteFunctionLp_expansion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Parseval.html#TauCeti.hasSum_hermiteFunctionLp_expansion)); the eigenrelation `𝓕 Φₙ = (-i)ⁿ Φₙ`, stated for the `√(2π)`-rescaled family that Mathlib's Fourier convention needs ([`fourier_twoPiHermiteFunction`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Fourier.html#TauCeti.fourier_twoPiHermiteFunction)); the cosine basis of `L²((0, π])` pulled back along `x = cos θ` ([`chebyshevCosineHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/Cosine/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.chebyshevCosineHilbertBasis)); and the Hermite generating function and Schwartz-space ladder relations.

## OrthogonalL2Bases: 2026-08-03 to 2026-09-30 (`11ef09d` to `e440f4e`)

The Fourier transform of `L²(ℝ)` is now diagonalized by the Hermite functions as an operator, not just integral by integral. The rescaled Hermite functions that fit Mathlib's `e^{-2πixξ}` convention form their own Hilbert basis of `L²(ℝ)` ([`twoPiHermiteHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Fourier/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.twoPiHermiteHilbertBasis)). Each is an eigenvector of the unitary Fourier transform with eigenvalue `(-i)ⁿ` ([`fourier_twoPiHermiteFunctionLp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Fourier/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.fourier_twoPiHermiteFunctionLp)), so the transform of any `f ∈ L²` is its Hermite series with the coefficients rotated by `(-i)ⁿ` ([`hasSum_fourier_twoPiHermiteFunctionLp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Fourier/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.hasSum_fourier_twoPiHermiteFunctionLp)). As a corollary, the fourth power of the transform is the identity ([`fourier_fourier_fourier_fourier`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Fourier/HilbertBasis.html#TauCeti.fourier_fourier_fourier_fourier)).

The ladder operators arrived as continuous linear operators on the real Schwartz space, along with the number and oscillator operators, their actions on the Hermite functions, and the [canonical commutation relation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Function/Operator.html#TauCeti.hermiteAnnihilationCLM_comp_hermiteCreationCLM_sub_hermiteCreationCLM_comp_hermiteAnnihilationCLM) `[a, a†] = 1` (TauCeti#3120). They live on `𝒮(ℝ)` and not on `L²`, where they would be unbounded.

The other bases got their expansion API. The Chebyshev, cosine, Gaussian Hermite and both multidimensional Hermite bases now each have explicit integral coefficients, Parseval's identity and an expansion theorem, for instance [`hasSum_chebyshevT_expansion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/Parseval.html#TauCeti.hasSum_chebyshevT_expansion) and [`hasSum_gaussianHermitePi_expansion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Hermite/Pi/Parseval.html#TauCeti.hasSum_gaussianHermitePi_expansion). On product functions, the multi-index coefficients factor. Chebyshev also gained its second normalization. The envelope functions `Tₙ(x)(1-x²)^{-1/4}` form a basis of `L²((-1,1]; dx)` ([`chebyshevTEnvelopeHilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Chebyshev/Envelope.html#TauCeti.chebyshevTEnvelopeHilbertBasis)), and the weight-change isometry exchanges this basis with the polynomial one. The rest is supporting measure theory, such as products of weighted measures.

## OrthogonalL2Bases: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (`e440f4e` to `8a32441`)

A quiet window, confined to the finite product basis of Part B. The tensor inner-product identity, that the inner product of two pointwise products `x ↦ ∏ᵢ fᵢ(xᵢ)` in `L²(Measure.pi μ)` is the product of the coordinatewise inner products, is now proved directly for any `Fintype` index ([`inner_L2piMul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Pi.html#TauCeti.inner_L2piMul)), with two consequences stated alongside it: the tensor construction is norm-multiplicative ([`norm_L2piMul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Pi.html#TauCeti.norm_L2piMul)), and coordinatewise orthonormal families multiply to an orthonormal family indexed by dependent functions ([`orthonormal_L2piMul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/L2/Pi.html#TauCeti.orthonormal_L2piMul)). The rest of the change reworks the existing lemmas around them, including the completeness argument that carries orthogonality to basis tensors over to every elementary tensor. No new basis or roadmap target came with it; the roadmap remains complete.
