## InfinitaryLogic: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-19 (`1f193ee` to `9fc5d5a`)

The roadmap's first window brought it to within one packaged statement of its summit. Karp's theorem landed at a common carrier ([`karp_theorem_at`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Karp.html#TauCeti.karp_theorem_at)): relational structures coded into one carrier are potentially isomorphic exactly when they agree on its infinitary sentences. It is also packaged as full L∞ω-equivalence ([`karp_theorem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Karp.html#TauCeti.karp_theorem), TauCeti#18226), and for countable structures as isomorphism exactly when they agree on Lω₁ω sentences ([`countable_infEquivAt_nat_iff_iso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Karp.html#TauCeti.countable_infEquivAt_nat_iff_iso)). On the Scott side, every countable structure satisfies its [canonical Scott sentence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Sentence.html#TauCeti.realize_scottSentence_self), every model of that sentence, of any size, is [potentially isomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/PotentialIso.html#TauCeti.potentialIso_of_realize_scottSentence) to it, and the [internal Scott rank is countable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Rank.html#TauCeti.internalScottRank_lt_omega_one). What is missing is Scott's isomorphism theorem stated as one theorem for countable models. It now follows in two steps from what is here, but it is stated only for [finite structures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/CharacteristicSentence.html#TauCeti.realize_scottSentence_iff_of_finite).

Underneath sits a full development: the fixed-carrier syntax [`BoundedFormulaInf`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/Syntax.html#FirstOrder.Language.BoundedFormulaInf) with carrier codings, transport, quantifier rank, and substitution and relabelling with their general realization laws. Back-and-forth equivalence comes with finite and coherent infinite Ehrenfeucht-Fraïssé games, and [potential isomorphism is exactly a winning coherent strategy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Game/Infinite.html#TauCeti.potentialIso_iff_nonempty_coherentEFStrategy). There is also a proof that the back-and-forth refinement of a countable structure [stabilizes below ω₁](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Stabilization.html#TauCeti.exists_complete_self_stabilization).

The examples check the theory against real structures. Pure sets have Scott rank one. K₂ ⊔ K₃ has [rank three](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/CompleteGraphSum.html#TauCeti.internalScottRank_completeGraph_two_sum_three), and so does a [rigid six-vertex graph](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/RigidGraph.html#TauCeti.internalScottRank_rigidSixGraph). The Scott sentence of a countable dense linear order holds [exactly in nonempty dense linear orders](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/DenseLinearOrder.html#TauCeti.realize_scottSentence_iff_of_dlo) of any cardinality. With countably many unary predicates, a [one-point structure has no first-order Scott sentence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/NatPredicate.html#TauCeti.not_exists_sentence_iff_nonempty_equiv_natPredicateStructure). Still absent: the free-variable support and transitivity of potential isomorphism.

## InfinitaryLogic: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25 (`9fc5d5a` to `e8ea0d1`)

The summit is reached. [Scott's isomorphism theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.scott_isomorphism) now holds as the roadmap states it: in a countable relational language, every countable structure has an Lω₁ω sentence that holds in a countable structure exactly when that structure is isomorphic to it (TauCeti#18977). There is no counting hypothesis. The proof is short because the earlier windows did the work: models of the Scott sentence are potentially isomorphic to the structure, and countable potentially isomorphic structures are isomorphic. The same statement holds at [any countable stabilizing level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.realize_scottSentenceAt_iff). The two API gaps flagged last time are also closed: [potential isomorphism is transitive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/PotentialIso.html#TauCeti.PotentialIso.trans), by composing partial equivalences, and infinitary formulas have a [free-variable support](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/FreeVarSupport.html#FirstOrder.Language.BoundedFormulaInf.freeVarSupport). It is countable at carrier ℕ, and [realization depends only on it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/FreeVarSupport.html#FirstOrder.Language.BoundedFormulaInf.realize_congr_freeVarSupport).

The rest of the window filled in worked examples. A countable graph is [isomorphic to the Rado graph](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/RandomGraph.html#TauCeti.nonempty_iso_radoGraph_iff) exactly when it has the extension property, and such graphs of any size have Scott rank one. Two equivalence relations of any cardinality are [potentially isomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/EquivalenceRelation.html#Setoid.potentialIso_fromRel_iff) exactly when they have equally many classes of each size. ℤ and ℤ + ℤ are [elementarily equivalent but separated by an Lω₁ω sentence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/DiscreteOrder.html#TauCeti.not_infEquivAt_nat_int_sumLex_int). That separation goes through a new bound: back-and-forth equivalence at level α gives [agreement up to quantifier rank α](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Karp.html#TauCeti.BFEquiv.realize_iff_of_qrank_le). Above ℵ₀, there are [two dense linear orders of size ℵ₁](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/DenseLinearOrder.html#TauCeti.exists_dlo_aleph_one_potentialIso_isEmpty_equiv) that are potentially isomorphic but not isomorphic. Only one of the README's worked examples is still missing: the Lω₁ω sentence for a fixed well-order type.
