## EffectiveBounds: 2026-06-12 to 2026-09-01 (`ecbb688` to `8745177`)

The summit of this roadmap landed: the **effective Hermite–Minkowski count** ([`NumberField.ncard_setOf_finiteDimensional_abs_discr_le_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/EffectiveBounds/HermiteCount/Basic.html#NumberField.ncard_setOf_finiteDimensional_abs_discr_le_le)), an explicit upper bound `(2C+1)^(D+1)·D` on the number of number fields inside a fixed extension `A/ℚ` with `|d_K| ≤ N`, where `D` is Mathlib's degree bound `rankOfDiscrBdd N` and `C` is an explicit coefficient height ([`NumberField.coeffBoundOfDiscrBdd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/EffectiveBounds/HermiteCount/Basic.html#NumberField.coeffBoundOfDiscrBdd)). The proof has three counting steps: every such field is generated by a root of an integer polynomial of bounded degree and height ([`NumberField.exists_mem_rootSet_eq_adjoin_of_abs_discr_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/EffectiveBounds/HermiteCount/Basic.html#NumberField.exists_mem_rootSet_eq_adjoin_of_abs_discr_le)), those polynomials and their roots are counted, and a finite set of generators produces at most that many simple fields ([`TauCeti.IntermediateField.ncard_setOf_exists_mem_eq_adjoin_simple_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/IntermediateField/Card.html#TauCeti.IntermediateField.ncard_setOf_exists_mem_eq_adjoin_simple_le)). Monotone, threshold and natural-discriminant forms are packaged around [`NumberField.hermiteCountBound`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/EffectiveBounds/HermiteCount/Basic.html#NumberField.hermiteCountBound).

In Layer 1 the **explicit ideal count** ([`NumberField.card_ideal_absNorm_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/EffectiveBounds/IdealCount/Basic.html#NumberField.card_ideal_absNorm_le)), `#{I ≠ ⊥ : N(I) ≤ X} ≤ X²·2ⁿ` for `X ≥ 1`, is here, with the abstract group lemma behind the unit-square index ([`TauCeti.index_square_le_of_closure_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/PowMonoidHom.html#TauCeti.index_square_le_of_closure_eq_top)) and the trace-form diagonalisation that evaluates a square-root basis discriminant as `4a` ([`TauCeti.Algebra.discr_one_elem_eq_of_sq_algebraMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Trace.html#TauCeti.Algebra.discr_one_elem_eq_of_sq_algebraMap)). The discriminant bound `|d_F| ≤ |disc b|`, the class-number bound `h_F ≤ |d_F|·4ⁿ` and the unit-square index `[O_F^× : (O_F^×)²] ≤ 2ⁿ` were migrated in TauCeti#141, TauCeti#144 and TauCeti#151, followed by quadratic specialisations, degree-monotone and product corollaries, the equality companion for an integral basis, and the bounded-polynomial counts feeding Layer 2; none of these appears as a declaration here, because later file consolidation re-attributed them out of this window. The declaration list understates what landed.

Layer 0 gives the measure-free engine: polydiscs, a packing bound ([`TauCeti.GeometryOfNumbers.finite_and_ncard_le_of_subset_box_of_separated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GeometryOfNumbers/Doubling.html#TauCeti.GeometryOfNumbers.finite_and_ncard_le_of_subset_box_of_separated)) and a doubling bound with factor `49^#ι` ([`TauCeti.GeometryOfNumbers.ncard_inter_box_two_le_pow_mul_ncard_inter_box_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GeometryOfNumbers/Doubling.html#TauCeti.GeometryOfNumbers.ncard_inter_box_two_le_pow_mul_ncard_inter_box_one)), stated for an additive subgroup of `ι → ℂ` and taking finiteness of `Λ ∩ box r 2` as a hypothesis. All three worked examples close: `|d_{ℚ(i)}| = 4` ([`NumberField.WorkedExamples.abs_discr_cyclotomicField_four`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/EffectiveBounds/WorkedExamples.html#NumberField.WorkedExamples.abs_discr_cyclotomicField_four)), `h_{ℚ(√−5)} ≤ 320` ([`NumberField.WorkedExamples.classNumber_adjoinRoot_sqrt_neg_five_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/EffectiveBounds/WorkedExamples.html#NumberField.WorkedExamples.classNumber_adjoinRoot_sqrt_neg_five_le)), and the Gaussian lattice doubling by `49` ([`TauCeti.GeometryOfNumbers.gaussianLattice_ncard_inter_box_two_le_fortyNine_mul_ncard_inter_box_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GeometryOfNumbers/RankTwoDoubling.html#TauCeti.GeometryOfNumbers.gaussianLattice_ncard_inter_box_two_le_fortyNine_mul_ncard_inter_box_one)). Layer 3 has only its base case, the regulator of a rank-zero field being `1` ([`NumberField.Units.regulator_eq_one_of_rank_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/EffectiveBounds/Regulator.html#NumberField.Units.regulator_eq_one_of_rank_eq_zero)).
