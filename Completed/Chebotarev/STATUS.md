## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The summit has been reached. The library proves the Chebotarev density theorem over an arbitrary number field in three forms: Dirichlet density, natural density and prime counting. The splitting-type, non-Galois and arithmetic-progression corollaries are proved, now in natural density as well, and so are the roadmap's acceptance tests. Every milestone of the fourteen layers is proved.

### Named results

- **The Chebotarev density theorem** — for a finite Galois extension `L / K` of number fields and a conjugacy class `C` of `Gal(L/K)`, the primes of `𝓞 K` with Frobenius class `C` have Dirichlet density `#C / #Gal(L/K)` ([`hasDirichletDensity_frobeniusPrimeSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Density/Chebotarev.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasDirichletDensity_frobeniusPrimeSet)).
- **Natural-density Chebotarev** — the same primes have natural density `#C / #Gal(L/K)`, measured against a prime ideal theorem for `K` that the development derives itself rather than imports ([`hasNaturalDensity_frobeniusPrimeSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/PrimeCounting/FrobeniusPrimeCount.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasNaturalDensity_frobeniusPrimeSet)).
- **The Chebotarev prime number theorem** — `π_C(x)` is asymptotic to `(#C / #Gal(L/K)) Li(x)`, reached through `ψ_C(x) / x → #C / #Gal(L/K)` and `ϑ_C`, starting from the abelian case ([`frobeniusPsi_asymptotic_of_mul_comm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/PrimeCounting/Chebotarev.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.frobeniusPsi_asymptotic_of_mul_comm)).
- **Chebotarev for splitting types** — for `E = L^H`, the primes of `K` whose residue degrees in `E` form the multiset `μ` have density equal to the proportion of `Gal(L/K)` acting on the cosets of `H` with cycle type `μ`. This holds both in Dirichlet density ([`hasDirichletDensity_setOf_map_inertiaDeg_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Density/SplittingType.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasDirichletDensity_setOf_map_inertiaDeg_eq)) and in natural density ([`hasNaturalDensity_setOf_map_inertiaDeg_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/PrimeCounting/SplittingType.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasNaturalDensity_setOf_map_inertiaDeg_eq)). It is the classical non-Galois form and contains the completely split case.
- **Dirichlet's theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions** — for `m ≠ 0` and `a` a unit mod `m`, the primes `p ≡ a (mod m)` have Dirichlet density `1 / φ(m)` ([`hasDirichletDensity_primesCongruent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Density/PrimesCongruent.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasDirichletDensity_primesCongruent)). The counting side specialises the same way, to natural density and to the prime number theorem for arithmetic progressions.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Frobenius prime set** ([`frobeniusPrimeSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/FrobeniusPrimeSet.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.frobeniusPrimeSet)) — the common carrier for every density and counting statement. It assigns no class at a ramified prime, so any splitting condition becomes a finite disjoint union of its fibres, outside a finite set. This is how the splitting-type corollaries pass to natural density.
- **The cyclic fixed-field fibre count** ([`fixedField_frobenius_fiber_card`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/FixedField/FiberCount.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.fixedField_frobenius_fiber_card)) — over an unramified prime with Artin class `C` represented by `σ`, the primes of `L^⟨σ⟩` with relative Frobenius `σ` number exactly `#G / (#C · orderOf σ)`. This count drives both the density contraction and the weighted contraction for `ϑ`.
- **The crossing constant** ([`crossingConstant`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Crossing/CrossingConstant.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Chebotarev.crossingConstant)) — the proportion of auxiliary-group elements whose order is divisible by `f`. It approaches `1 / #G` as the congruence level grows, which is what carries the crossing from the abelian case to the general one.
