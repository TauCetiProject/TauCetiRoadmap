## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Every layer is done. A finite regular fan has a Hausdorff complex manifold with a holomorphic torus action, the orbit–cone correspondence, a normal-crossings boundary, holomorphic toric maps with a properness criterion, and a torus-equivariant biholomorphism with the complex points of its fan scheme. The README's standard examples are worked out. What remains is refinement, not milestones.

### Named results

- **Orbits are quotient tori** — the torus orbit of a cone σ is a complex manifold biholomorphic to the torus of N ⧸ N_σ ([`Fan.analyticConeOrbitTorusDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Orbit/Complex.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticConeOrbitTorusDiffeomorph)). Its closure, with the subspace topology, is an embedded complex submanifold of dimension rank N − dim σ ([`Fan.exists_isManifold_closure_analyticConeOrbit_isSmoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Boundary/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.exists_isManifold_closure_analyticConeOrbit_isSmoothEmbedding)).
- **The normal-crossings form of the toric boundary** — near every point there is a holomorphic chart in which each ray-indexed boundary component through the point is a coordinate hyperplane, and each component has multiplicity one ([`Fan.exists_partialDiffeomorph_analyticBoundaryComponent_normalForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Boundary/NormalForm.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.exists_partialDiffeomorph_analyticBoundaryComponent_normalForm)).
- **The cone-by-cone properness criterion** — for regular fans with nonempty source, a toric map is proper exactly when the real preimage of every target cone is the union of the source cones mapped into it ([`FanHom.isProperMap_analyticMap_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Map/Proper.html#TauCeti.Toric.FanHom.isProperMap_analyticMap_iff)). Star subdivisions are proper blow-downs as a consequence ([`Fan.isProperMap_analyticMap_starSubdivisionAlong`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Map/Subdivision.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.isProperMap_analyticMap_starSubdivisionAlong)).
- **Compactness is completeness** — a nonempty regular fan has compact realization exactly when it is complete ([`Fan.compactSpace_analyticRealization_iff_isComplete`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Compact.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.compactSpace_analyticRealization_iff_isComplete)), and the same holds for the algebraic complex points.
- **The algebraic–analytic comparison** — the complex points of the fan scheme over Spec ℂ are biholomorphic to the realization, by the identity on every affine chart, torus-equivariantly ([`Fan.algebraicAnalyticEquiv_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Comparison/TorusAction.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicAnalyticEquiv_smul)). It is natural for toric maps, characters and products, and restricts to biholomorphisms of orbits and boundary components, so algebraic orbits are quotient tori too ([`Fan.algebraicConeOrbitTorusHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Comparison/Orbit.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicConeOrbitTorusHomeomorph)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The complex manifold of a regular fan**: affine charts glued along face localizations into a Hausdorff, second-countable complex manifold modelled on ℂ^n (`Fan.isManifold_analyticRealization`). Every analytic statement above lives on it, and the dense torus sits in it as an open biholomorphic copy ([`Fan.analyticTorusPartialDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/ZeroCone.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticTorusPartialDiffeomorph)).
- **The torus action on the fan scheme**: the affine coactions glue to an internal action of the dense torus on the fan scheme over Spec ℂ ([`Fan.algebraicRealizationModObj`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/TorusAction/Action.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicRealizationModObj)). On complex points it is the character action ([`specMap_smul_eq_lift_comp_affineToricSchemeAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/TorusAction/Coaction.html#TauCeti.Toric.specMap_smul_eq_lift_comp_affineToricSchemeAction)), which is what lets the comparison be stated equivariantly.
- **The fan of projective space**: built from an integral basis ([`Fan.projectiveSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/ProjectiveSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.projectiveSpace)). Its scheme is `Proj ℂ[X_j]` and its realization is ℂℙⁿ with the homogeneous-ratio atlas ([`projectiveSpaceDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/ProjectiveSpace/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.projectiveSpaceDiffeomorph)), the test case for the comparison and properness theorems.
