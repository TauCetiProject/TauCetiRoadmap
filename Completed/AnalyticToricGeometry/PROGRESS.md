## AnalyticToricGeometry: 2026-09-06 to 2026-09-26 (`fe1fb42` to `759eb3e`)

The toric scheme of a finite regular fan now exists: [`Fan.algebraicRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/Scheme.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicRealization) glues the affine toric schemes of its cones along the open immersions of their faces, each chart is an open subscheme, and fan morphisms induce morphisms of toric schemes compatibly with composition ([`FanHom.algebraicMap_comp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/Scheme.html#TauCeti.Toric.FanHom.algebraicMap_comp)). On the analytic side, the complex points of the affine chart of a regular cone form a complex manifold whose structure does not depend on the extending basis ([`contMDiff_id_coneChartedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.contMDiff_id_coneChartedSpace)), face localization is a biholomorphism onto an open subset ([`IsRegularCone.faceAffinePointPartialDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/FaceLocalization.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsRegularCone.faceAffinePointPartialDiffeomorph)), and the affine orbit-cone correspondence ([`faceEquivOrbitRelQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/Orbit.html#TauCeti.Toric.faceEquivOrbitRelQuotient)) matches the faces of a regular cone with the torus orbits in its chart, reversing the closure order.

Underneath is an algebraic supplier built on Mathlib's pointed cones: integral lattices defined by scalar extension, toric cones as a predicate, the unique primitive generator of each ray ([`IsToricCone.existsUnique_primitiveGenerator`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Ray/Primitive.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsToricCone.existsUnique_primitiveGenerator)), regular cones and fans, products and subdivisions of fans (TauCeti#6789, TauCeti#6938), and the identification of the dual semigroup of a regular cone with ℕ^k × ℤ^(n−k). The character and mixed-monomial calculus is complete, including a composition law for mixed monomial maps stated only where the torus coordinates are invertible ([`mixedMonomialMap_comp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/MixedMonomial.html#TauCeti.Toric.mixedMonomialMap_comp)). Gluing over a fan has begun: the open overlaps of the analytic charts and their transition maps are constructed, and the transitions satisfy the cocycle law on triple overlaps ([`Fan.analyticOverlapHomeomorph_cocycle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Cocycle.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticOverlapHomeomorph_cocycle)).

Not there yet: the transitions have not been assembled into a `TopCat.GlueData`, so there is no global complex manifold, and nothing is stated about torus actions over a fan, boundary divisors, analytic toric maps, properness, or the comparison with algebraic complex points. The torus acts on affine complex points but not yet on the schemes, finite generation of the dual semigroup is proved only for regular cones, and the face strata carry complex coordinates but no dimension formula.

## AnalyticToricGeometry: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `0d3161a`)

A regular fan now has a complex manifold. The affine analytic charts of its cones are assembled into a `TopCat.GlueData` along their overlap transitions ([`Fan.analyticGlueData`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/GlueData.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticGlueData)), and the glued space is Hausdorff ([`Fan.t2Space_analyticRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Topology.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.t2Space_analyticRealization)), second countable, locally compact, and a complex manifold modelled on ℂ^n ([`Fan.isManifold_analyticRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.isManifold_analyticRealization)). Each affine chart is included as a biholomorphism onto an open subset, for the complex structure of any extending basis ([`Fan.analyticAffineChartPartialDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticAffineChartPartialDiffeomorph)).

Hausdorffness is proved the way the roadmap asks, by showing the gluing relation between two charts is closed rather than storing separation in the fan. The key input is the toric separation lemma ([`exists_mem_dualSemigroup_neg_mem_dualSemigroup_inf_ker_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/DualSemigroup/Separation.html#TauCeti.Toric.exists_mem_dualSemigroup_neg_mem_dualSemigroup_inf_ker_eq)): when two cones meet in a common face, one character separates them, so the dual semigroup of the intersection is generated by the two dual semigroups. It rests on Gordan's and Tucker's theorems of the alternative ([`exists_forall_dual_pos_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/Cone/Alternative.html#TauCeti.exists_forall_dual_pos_iff)). Generic tools were built along the way: a criterion that a glued space is Hausdorff exactly when each chart relation is closed ([`TopCat.GlueData.t2Space_iff_isClosed_chartRel`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Category/TopCat/GlueData.html#TopCat.GlueData.t2Space_iff_isClosed_chartRel)), and a manifold structure on any space covered by open embeddings of manifolds with smooth overlaps ([`isManifold_chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Gluing.html#TauCeti.isManifold_chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding)).

Two smaller pieces landed. Affine torus orbits have their dimension: the rank of the lattice minus the dimension of the face ([`finrank_affineConeOrbit_model`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/Orbit/Dimension.html#TauCeti.Toric.finrank_affineConeOrbit_model)). Equivalences of fans ([`FanEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/Equiv.html#TauCeti.Toric.FanEquiv)) exist, and regularity and completeness are invariant under them. Still missing: invariance of the realization under these equivalences, functoriality for open subfans, and any torus action on the glued manifold.

## AnalyticToricGeometry: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (`0d3161a` to `8a32441`)

The orbit–cone correspondence now holds on the glued manifold of a regular fan: the cones of the fan correspond bijectively to the torus orbits of its analytic realization, each cone to the orbit of its distinguished point ([`Fan.coneEquivOrbitRelQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Orbit.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.coneEquivOrbitRelQuotient)). The orbits partition the realization, the closure of the orbit of a cone τ is the union of the orbits of the cones containing τ ([`Fan.closure_analyticConeOrbit`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Orbit.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.closure_analyticConeOrbit)), so inclusion of cones reverses the closure order, and the stabilizer of the distinguished point of σ is the subtorus of torus points trivial on the characters vanishing on σ ([`Fan.mem_stabilizer_analyticDistinguishedPoint_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Orbit.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.mem_stabilizer_analyticDistinguishedPoint_iff)). The proof carries the single-chart correspondence across the charts, after showing that the stratum containing a point does not depend on which chart it is seen in.

This rests on the other new piece, the torus action on the realization. The coordinate-free complex torus acts on each affine chart by rescaling monomial values; these actions commute with face localization and glue to a group action on the realization for which every chart inclusion is equivariant ([`Fan.smul_analyticAffineChartι`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/TorusAction/Basic.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.smul_analyticAffineChartι)). The action is jointly continuous, and holomorphic both on a single regular affine chart and on the whole realization ([`Fan.contMDiffSMul_complexTorus_analyticRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/TorusAction/Holomorphic.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.contMDiffSMul_complexTorus_analyticRealization)).

Not there yet: orbits are not identified with quotient tori, and nothing constructs the ray-indexed boundary hypersurfaces or their normal-crossings form.

## AnalyticToricGeometry: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05 (`8a32441` to `1d09589`)

The toric boundary of a regular fan's manifold is now in place. Each ray gives a closed, torus-invariant component, the closure of its orbit ([`Fan.analyticBoundaryComponent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Boundary/Basic.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticBoundaryComponent)). For a nonempty fan these components cover exactly the complement of the dense torus ([`Fan.iUnion_analyticBoundaryComponent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Boundary/Basic.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.iUnion_analyticBoundaryComponent)). Near every point there is a holomorphic chart in which they are coordinate hyperplanes, which is the simple-normal-crossings normal form ([`Fan.exists_partialDiffeomorph_analyticBoundaryComponent_normalForm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Boundary/NormalForm.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.exists_partialDiffeomorph_analyticBoundaryComponent_normalForm)). The boundary components meet exactly when their rays lie in a common cone, and their intersection is then that cone's orbit closure ([`Fan.iInter_analyticBoundaryComponent_eq_closure_analyticConeOrbit`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Boundary/Intersection.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.iInter_analyticBoundaryComponent_eq_closure_analyticConeOrbit)). They are natural under fan equivalences and open subfans.

Analytic toric maps also arrived. A fan morphism glues to a map of realizations ([`FanHom.analyticMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Map/Basic.html#TauCeti.Toric.FanHom.analyticMap)) that is holomorphic, functorial and equivariant. It is the unique continuous map extending the induced map of dense tori ([`FanHom.eq_analyticMap_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Map/Torus.html#TauCeti.Toric.FanHom.eq_analyticMap_iff)), and it pulls orbits back cone by cone. A fan equivalence gives a biholomorphism ([`FanEquiv.analyticDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Map/Equiv.html#TauCeti.Toric.FanEquiv.analyticDiffeomorph)). Open subfans embed as open local biholomorphisms, and orbit charts agree on overlaps, which completes the gluing layer. Properness has not been started.

On the algebraic side, the window proved Gordan's lemma for every lattice-rational cone ([`IsLatticeRational.fg_dualSemigroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/DualSemigroup/Finiteness.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsLatticeRational.fg_dualSemigroup)), closure of toric cones under intersection, and the separation lemma for faces. Together these extend the fan scheme to every finite fan, now as a scheme over Spec ℂ. Its complex points, taken as morphisms over Spec ℂ, are homeomorphic to the analytic realization of a regular fan by the identity on each chart ([`Fan.algebraicAnalyticHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Comparison.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicAnalyticHomeomorph), TauCeti#11776). Not yet proved: that this homeomorphism is holomorphic or natural, that the torus coaction on coordinate rings glues to the fan scheme, or that the product-fan scheme is the fibre product. So far there is only a comparison morphism.

## AnalyticToricGeometry: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07 (`1d09589` to `8334df2`)

Two Layer 4 and 5 milestones landed. First, **orbits are quotient tori**: the orbit of a cone σ in the realization of a regular fan is homeomorphic to the torus of the quotient lattice N ⧸ N_σ, equivariantly, with the distinguished point going to the identity ([`Fan.analyticConeOrbitTorusHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Orbit/QuotientTorus.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticConeOrbitTorusHomeomorph)), and the stabilizer of that point is the kernel of the induced torus map ([`Fan.stabilizer_analyticDistinguishedPoint`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Orbit/QuotientTorus.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.stabilizer_analyticDistinguishedPoint)). The sublattice N_σ of lattice vectors in the span of σ ([`coneSublattice`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Cone/Sublattice.html#TauCeti.Toric.coneSublattice)) is shown to be saturated, so the quotient is free. Second, the **cone-by-cone properness criterion**: for a morphism of regular fans with nonempty source, the analytic map is proper exactly when the real preimage of every target cone is the union of the source cones mapped into it ([`FanHom.isProperMap_analyticMap_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Map/Proper.html#TauCeti.Toric.FanHom.isProperMap_analyticMap_iff)). Its inputs are compactness of the parts of a chart where finitely many generating monomials are bounded, and Hoffman's error bound for a polyhedral cone ([`exists_forall_abs_apply_sub_le_of_forall_neg_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/Cone/ErrorBound.html#TauCeti.exists_forall_abs_apply_sub_le_of_forall_neg_le)).

Products also arrived on the analytic side. The realization of a product of regular fans is the product of the realizations, even when a factor is empty ([`Fan.analyticProdHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Product.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticProdHomeomorph)), and products of fan morphisms act as products of analytic maps under it. This is a homeomorphism only: nothing yet says it is a biholomorphism. On the algebraic side, the affine torus coaction is now an internal action of the zero-cone monoid object in the opposite category of complex algebras ([`affineCoordinateRingCoactionModObj`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/TorusAction/Internal.html#TauCeti.Toric.affineCoordinateRingCoactionModObj)), and restriction to a face respects it. Transporting it to the affine schemes and gluing it over the fan remain to be done.

## AnalyticToricGeometry: 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-24 (`8334df2` to `271e1af`)

This window closes the roadmap's remaining gaps. Star subdivisions now give proper maps: the star subdivision of a regular fan along a nonzero cone ([`Fan.starSubdivisionAlong`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/StarSubdivision.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.starSubdivisionAlong)) is regular with the same support, so its blow-down is proper ([`Fan.isProperMap_analyticMap_starSubdivisionAlong`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Map/Subdivision.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.isProperMap_analyticMap_starSubdivisionAlong)). The blow-down is a biholomorphism away from the exceptional divisor ([`Fan.starSubdivisionAlongPartialDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Map/StarSubdivision.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.starSubdivisionAlongPartialDiffeomorph)), and that divisor is identified as the preimage of the cone's orbit closure. On the algebraic side, the affine torus actions glue to an action of the dense torus on the fan scheme over Spec ℂ ([`Fan.algebraicRealizationModObj`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/TorusAction/Action.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicRealizationModObj)), and toric maps and products respect it. The algebraic–analytic comparison is now torus-equivariant ([`Fan.algebraicAnalyticEquiv_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Comparison/TorusAction.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicAnalyticEquiv_smul)) and matches characters, orbits and boundary components on the two sides. As a consequence, the algebraic complex points are compact exactly when the fan is complete (TauCeti#13483).

The acceptance examples also arrived. The toric scheme of the standard projective-space fan is `Proj ℂ[X_j]` ([`Fan.isIso_projectiveSpaceToProj`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/ProjectiveSpace/Proj.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.isIso_projectiveSpaceToProj)), its realization is biholomorphic to ℂℙⁿ with its homogeneous-ratio atlas ([`projectiveSpaceDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/ProjectiveSpace/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.projectiveSpaceDiffeomorph)), and the comparison respects homogeneous coordinates. Affine space, the zero-cone torus and the projective line, shown to be the Riemann sphere, get the same treatment. The blow-up of the plane at the origin is proper and embeds as the classical incidence locus in ℂ² × ℙ¹ ([`isClosedEmbedding_affinePlaneBlowUpIncidenceMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Examples/AffinePlane/BlowUp/Incidence.html#TauCeti.Toric.isClosedEmbedding_affinePlaneBlowUpIncidenceMap)).

Smaller pieces finish Layers 4 and 5. Global character functions now transform by their character under the torus ([`Fan.analyticCharacter_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Character.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticCharacter_smul)), boundary components have multiplicity one and meet transversally, and orbits and boundary components of product fans are computed. The product realization is a biholomorphism ([`Fan.analyticProdDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Product/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticProdDiffeomorph)), and products of proper toric maps are proper. One caveat remains from before: the complex structure on algebraic complex points is pulled back from the analytic side.
