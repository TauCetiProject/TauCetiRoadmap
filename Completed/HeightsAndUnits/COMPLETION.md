## Pending implementation endpoints

- **Whole integral-kernel basis, #20658.** Candidate [`845df50aaf6bf535acdbbd11145d7feec4e63d1a`](https://github.com/sqrt-of-2/TauCeti/blob/845df50aaf6bf535acdbbd11145d7feec4e63d1a/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Arakelov/BombieriVaaler.lean). `Matrix.exists_basis_ker_mulVecLin_prod_norm_le` constructs a `Module.Basis` of `LinearMap.ker A.mulVecLin`, indexed by `Fin (#columns−#rows)`, with the exact factorial loss `k!/2^(k−1)` times `sqrt(det(A*Aᵀ))/minorGcd A`, including k=0. It is a basis of the entire integral kernel, not merely an independent family. The matrix statement requires only a finite coordinate type; a finite order needed for the existing subspace-height calculation is installed in its proof.
- **Full Bombieri–Vaaler Theorem1, #19468.** Candidate [`d39d38c3ebb56851d007cd0e93402b52953465e8`](https://github.com/roed-math/TauCeti-w2-fresh/blob/d39d38c3ebb56851d007cd0e93402b52953465e8/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Arakelov/SiegelsLemma.lean), `Height/Arakelov/SiegelsLemma.lean`, proves `Matrix.exists_ne_zero_mulVec_eq_zero_norm_le`: for independent rows and p<n, a nonzero integer solution has norm at most `(sqrt(det(A*Aᵀ))/minorGcd A)^(1/(n−p))`. The proof selects the smallest member of the existing independent integral solution family. It is the full matrix bound, not only a scalar-height statement.
- **Finite matrix entry height and acceptance test, #20707.** Candidate [`d28289888c10627d82ae084513dbdd158a3ebb5f`](https://github.com/tauceti-ai-for-science/TauCeti/blob/d28289888c10627d82ae084513dbdd158a3ebb5f/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Matrix.lean), `Height/Matrix.lean`, defines entry heights only for finite row/column types and proves their tuple comparison, product bound with its exact cardinality constant, logarithmic/scaling/reindexing/extension API, and the specified rational2×2 attainment example. This is distinct from row-space Plücker height and from the constant-one Arakelov matrix bound.

## Canonical names and scope

The current entry-height names are `Matrix.mulHeight`, `Matrix.logHeight` and `Matrix.mulHeight_mul_le`, rather than the prototype's `matrixMulHeight` spelling. The minor gcd is `Matrix.minorGcd`. The S-unit rank statement is `TauCeti.finrank_sUnits_numberField`, and the canonical quotient/lattice bridge is `TauCeti.NumberField.SUnit.logEmbeddingEquiv`; these realize the corresponding requested forms without introducing a second S-unit carrier. Subspace heights retain their existing canonical coordinate-order parameter; the integer-matrix kernel basis contract itself does not expose one.

Layers 0–1 use arbitrary admissible absolute-value fields where the statements allow it. The number-field absolute/relative distinction and degree factors are retained. The gauge basis theorem does not assume a Haar measure or compact body; those hypotheses enter the successive-minima and covolume consequences. The regulator upper bound is universal over fundamental systems, while the reduced lower-product bound is existential with the exact Bugeaud–Győry constant. S consists only of finite places; its infinite places remain implicit, and the torsion-free quotient is identified with the full logarithmic lattice before computing the S-unit rank. Effective height lower bounds, S-unit equations, class-number calculations and DAT's general number-field Siegel theorem remain outside this roadmap's ownership.
