## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The roadmap is complete. The peripheral product identity and the peripheral-power theorem hold for every `p`-adic unit. The group of peripheral automorphisms has its exponent character, closedness, explicit elements of exponent `-1`, permutation symmetries and a section over the principal units. ### Named results

- **The peripheral product identity** — for every unit `u` there are conjugators, the first one trivial, such that the conjugated `u`-th powers of the basis and of the cusp multiply to `1` in order ([`exists_defect_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Identity.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_defect_eq_one)). It is proved without any Galois group: [solutions exist at every level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Identity.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_mem_level) of the closed lower central series, and [compactness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Identity.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_defect_eq_one_of_forall_exists_mem_level) passes to the limit. The unit hypothesis is necessary: at exponent `2` in rank two over `p = 2`, [the identity fails even modulo `γ_2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Nonunit.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.not_exists_peripheral_identity_two).
- **The peripheral-power theorem** — for every unit `u`, some continuous automorphism of a free pro-`p` group of finite rank sends each basis element and the cusp to a conjugate of its `u`-th power, and fixes the first basis element's power exactly ([`exists_peripheralAut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_peripheralAut)). The [form for every conjugate of the cusp](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_peripheralAut_conj_cusp) gives the conjugator explicitly.
- **The permutation-and-exponent homomorphism** — in rank at least two, an automorphism that permutes the peripheral classes up to a common power [determines the permutation and the exponent](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Permutation.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.IsPeripheralPermAut.unique), which gives a homomorphism [`permData`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Permutation.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.permData) to `Perm (Fin (r + 1)) × ℤ_pˣ`. In rank one [this fails](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Permutation.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_isPeripheralPermAut_swap_rank_one).
- **The section over the principal units** — in positive rank, the exponent character has a continuous homomorphic section over `1 + p^k ℤ_p` for `k ≥ 1`, or `k ≥ 2` when `p = 2` ([`exists_section_principalUnits`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Section.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_section_principalUnits)). No section over all of `ℤ_pˣ` is claimed.
- **Closedness of the peripheral automorphisms** — they form a closed subgroup of the continuous automorphisms in the congruence topology ([`isClosed_peripheralAut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Topology.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.isClosed_peripheralAut)), and the exponent is [continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Topology.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.continuous_exponent) on it.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The peripheral automorphism group and its exponent** ([`peripheralAut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Group.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.peripheralAut), [`exponent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Group.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exponent)) — the automorphisms that are peripheral of some unit exponent. The exponent is a surjective homomorphism to `ℤ_pˣ` whose kernel contains the inner automorphisms.
- **The peripheral levels** ([`level`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Lifting.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.level)) — closed sets of conjugators whose defect lies in the `n`-th term of the closed lower central series. A pair [lies in every level exactly when its defect is trivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Lifting.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.forall_mem_level_iff), and that is what lets compactness finish the identity.
- **The conjugation-transfer lemma** ([`map_conj_eq_conj_of_map_eq_conj`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/ConjugationTransfer.html#TauCeti.map_conj_eq_conj_of_map_eq_conj)) — moves a "conjugate of a power" statement from one element to any conjugate of it, with an explicit conjugator. This lets the cusp-conjugate and opposite-convention dyadic forms follow in one line.
