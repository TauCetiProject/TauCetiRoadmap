## RealAlgebraicGeometry: 2026-09-27 to 2026-09-30 (`4a0e5e3` to `e440f4e`)

The roadmap's first stretch landed. Every ordered field now has an ordered algebraic real closure in its own universe ([`exists_realClosure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/RealClosure/Basic.html#TauCeti.RealClosure.exists_realClosure)), built by Zorn over ordered intermediate fields of the algebraic closure, with orders extended through odd-degree and square-root extensions (TauCeti#9411). Over an arbitrary real closed field, the complexification is [algebraically closed](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/RealClosure/AlgebraicClosed.html#TauCeti.RealClosure.isAlgClosed_quadraticAlgebra), irreducible polynomials have [degree at most two](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/RealClosure/AlgebraicClosed.html#Polynomial.natDegree_le_two_of_irreducible), and from this come [polynomial IVT](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/RealClosure/IVT.html#Polynomial.exists_root_Ioo) and [polynomial Rolle](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/RealClosure/AbstractRolle.html#TauCeti.RealClosure.polynomialRolle_of_isRealClosed), all without completeness. The reals also got their [`IsRealClosed` instance](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/IsRealClosed/Real.html#Real.instIsRealClosed).

[Sturm–Tarski](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Sturm/Tarski.html#TauCeti.Sturm.sum_sign) followed, and with it [Sturm's root count](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Sturm/Tarski.html#TauCeti.Sturm.card_roots), on bounded, unbounded and [whole-line](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Sturm/Infinity.html#TauCeti.Sturm.sum_sign_univ) intervals. There is a catch: each version assumes the roots of `p` in the interval are simple, which the roadmap says must not be needed, and there is no Cauchy index or closed-endpoint form yet. Thom encodings are done: a root is [determined by its derivative signs](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Thom.html#Polynomial.thomEncoding_injOn), each sign condition is [order-convex](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Thom.html#Polynomial.ordConnected_preimage_thomEncoding), and the [ordering rule](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Thom.html#Polynomial.lt_iff_thomEncoding) is proved as specified.

Two later layers have begun. For sign determination, [Tarski queries](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/SignDetermination/Defs.html#Polynomial.tarskiQuery) satisfy the full BKR matrix identity, and [inverting the tensor matrix](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/SignDetermination/Roots.html#Polynomial.fullInverse_mulVec_tarskiQuery) recovers every sign-condition count. The recursive reduction is not there yet. Subresultants now have their [principal coefficients](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Subresultant/Basic.html#Polynomial.psc) and [polynomials](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Subresultant/Polynomial.html#Polynomial.subresultant) as Sylvester minors, with degree bounds, symmetry, scaling and coefficient-map laws.

## RealAlgebraicGeometry: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (`e440f4e` to `8a32441`)

Sturm–Tarski no longer needs simple roots: [`Sturm.sum_sign`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Sturm/Tarski.html#TauCeti.Sturm.sum_sign) now holds for any nonempty signed remainder chain whose head may have repeated roots, and the same goes for the half-line and [whole-line](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Sturm/Infinity.html#TauCeti.Sturm.sum_sign_univ) forms (TauCeti#10411). The subresultant gcd criterion also landed. [`natDegree_gcd_eq_iff_psc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Subresultant/GCD.html#Polynomial.natDegree_gcd_eq_iff_psc) says that, at the actual degrees, the degree of `gcd(p,q)` is the first index with a nonzero principal subresultant coefficient. Its proof reads relations `A*q + B*p = 0` as kernel vectors of the Sylvester-type matrix, so it does not wait on the subresultant Bézout identities. From it come the [distinct-root count by principal coefficients](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Subresultant/RootCount.html#Polynomial.card_roots_toFinset_eq_iff_psc), the formula [`deg p − deg gcd(p,p')`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Roots.html#Polynomial.natDegree_sub_natDegree_gcd_derivative_eq_card_roots_toFinset) for the number of distinct roots, and the fact that [root multiplicities in a gcd are minima](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Roots.html#Polynomial.rootMultiplicity_gcd).

Layer 4 has begun with continuity of roots counted with multiplicity. Over `ℂ`, or any proper algebraically closed normed field, the roots of a nearby monic polynomial of the same degree can be [matched bijectively](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/ContinuityOfRoots.html#Polynomial.Monic.exists_prod_X_sub_C_norm_sub_lt) with those of `f`, with every matched pair close. There is an equivalent [disc form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/ContinuityOfRoots.html#Polynomial.Monic.exists_countP_roots_mem_ball_eq_rootMultiplicity) and a version for [continuous families](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/ContinuityOfRoots.html#Polynomial.eventually_exists_C_mul_prod_X_sub_C_norm_sub_lt) whose leading coefficient does not vanish. Real roots, connected bases and simultaneous matching of several polynomials are not there yet.

[Semialgebraic sets](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsSemialgebraic) are now defined as the algebra of sets generated by polynomial zero and positivity sets. They come with the Boolean, polynomial-preimage, coordinate-change, section and product closure the roadmap asks for. [Reducta](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Reductum.html#Polynomial.reducta) arrived with the fact that [every specialization is the image of one of them](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Reductum.html#Polynomial.exists_mem_reducta_map_eq), the input the Collins projection needs. The rest of the window was smaller work: mean-value and monotonicity consequences of polynomial Rolle, sign-variation lemmas, and a cleanup of the order-extension cones.

## RealAlgebraicGeometry: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07 (`047d72b` to `87cb0c1`)

Puiseux with parameters now holds in its monic form. If a monic family has analytic coefficients in `(x,y)` and discriminant `y^a·u` with `u` nowhere zero, then substituting `y = t^{d!}` [splits it into analytic linear factors on a full disc](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Puiseux/Monic.html#TauCeti.Polynomial.exists_analyticOnNhd_prod_X_sub_C_of_discr_eq_pow_mul), including `t = 0` where roots collide, and every difference of two roots is a power of `t` times a unit (TauCeti#12479). The extension across `t = 0` [carries its conjugation permutation along](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Puiseux/Conjugation.html#TauCeti.Polynomial.exists_analyticOnNhd_monicOfCoeff_eq_prod_X_sub_C_conj). On the real side, such a prepared splitting [restricts to an analytic real delineation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Stack/Puiseux.html#TauCeti.exists_delineation_on_hyperplane) on a ball in the hyperplane `y = 0`. The nonmonic version and the local discriminant theorem are still missing.

Lazard's side gained its target notion. A [Lazard delineation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Stack/Lazard.html#TauCeti.LazardDelineation) is a common stack of roots of Lazard evaluations, so members may be nullified along the base. On every section and sector each member is [Lazard valuation-invariant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Stack/Lazard.html#TauCeti.LazardDelineation.lazardValuation_snoc_of_mem_sectionSet), hence sign-invariant over a preconnected base, and over an analytic submanifold the [root functions are analytic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Stack/Lazard.html#TauCeti.LazardDelineation.analyticOnSubmanifold_root). One [evaluator serves a whole finite family](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/MvPolynomial/Lazard.html#TauCeti.exists_isLazardEvaluator_analytic_units) along analytic centers, with jointly analytic unit forms. The lifting theorem, which says such delineations exist, has not landed.

The rest prepares the local discriminant theorem. A polynomial of constant ambient order along an analytic parametrization is a power times a unit along one fixed direction, with a [complexified form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/MvPolynomial/Complexification.html#MvPolynomial.exists_complexification_eval_add_smul_eq_pow_mul). A continuous root [is analytic wherever its multiplicity is locally constant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/MultipleRoots/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polynomial.analyticAt_of_eventually_rootMultiplicity_eq), so the sections of a delineation with analytic coefficients over an analytic submanifold are again analytic submanifolds. Products of analytic submanifolds, complexification of charts, and [delineability from invariant fibre data](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Stack/Delineation.html#TauCeti.nonempty_delineation) with local-to-global gluing round out the window.

## RealAlgebraicGeometry: 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-08 (`87cb0c1` to `b8db047`)

Every finite family of real polynomials now has an adapted cylindrical algebraic decomposition ([`exists_isCAD_signInvariant`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isCAD_signInvariant)), proved by induction on dimension through Collins delineability, with nullified members, repeated roots and rootless cylinders included (TauCeti#11891). From it come projection closure, the Tarski–Seidenberg theorem over `ℝ` ([`IsSemialgebraic.image_tail`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/Existence.html#TauCeti.IsSemialgebraic.image_tail)), and finitely many [sample points](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_finite_image_sign_eval_eq) realizing every sign vector of a family. The last missing input was that a delineation over a semialgebraic base [is a semialgebraic stack](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Stack/Semialgebraic.html#TauCeti.Delineation.isSemialgebraicStack): its sections and sectors are cut out by membership in the shared root set and the number of shared roots below the height, with no derivatives added to the family. Quantifier elimination and the image and composition APIs are still stated with projection closure as a hypothesis; that hypothesis is now a theorem over `ℝ`, but the unconditional statements have not been written down.

On the analytic side, nonmonic Puiseux with parameters landed ([`exists_analyticOnNhd_nonmonic_laurent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Puiseux/Laurent/Factorization.html#TauCeti.Polynomial.exists_analyticOnNhd_nonmonic_laurent)). If the discriminant is nonzero off `y = 0` and the leading and constant coefficients are powers of `y` times analytic units, then after `y = t^{d!}` the family splits completely, with every root of the form `t^e·w` near the hyperplane, and degree drops there allowed. It rests on a [pole-removing branch construction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Puiseux/Nonmonic.html#TauCeti.Polynomial.exists_analyticOnNhd_nonmonic_powerSubstitution) and on splittings in reciprocal coordinates.

The rest of the window prepared the local discriminant theorem. In a ramified splitting with power-times-unit discriminant, the ambient order is [constant along every root section](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Puiseux/SectionOrder.html#TauCeti.orderAt_root_eq_of_puiseux).
