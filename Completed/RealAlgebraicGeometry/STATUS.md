## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All eight layers are done. Over `ℝ` the roadmap has the full Collins route to CAD and unconditional quantifier elimination. It also has McCallum's order-invariant projection under well-orientedness, and Lazard's valuation-invariant projection, which needs no such hypothesis. Recent work has strengthened the univariate and subresultant layers rather than opening new ground.

### Named results

- **Validity of Lazard's projection** — every finite family of real polynomials in `n` variables has a CAD of `ℝⁿ` into analytic submanifolds on whose cells each member has constant Lazard valuation and constant sign. This holds without well-orientedness and with nullification allowed: [`exists_isCAD_lazardValuationReindex_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/Lazard/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isCAD_lazardValuationReindex_eq). The valuation is read with the coordinates in reverse.
- **Correctness of McCallum's projection** — a well-oriented family has a CAD into analytic submanifolds on which every member has constant order of vanishing: [`exists_isCAD_orderAt_eq_of_isMcCallumWellOriented`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/McCallum/Basic.html#TauCeti.exists_isCAD_orderAt_eq_of_isMcCallumWellOriented). The [sign-invariant variant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/McCallum/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isCAD_signInvariant_of_isMcCallumWellOriented_projection) needs well-orientedness only below the top level.
- **Existence of adapted CADs** — every finite family of real polynomials has a sign-invariant cylindrical algebraic decomposition, proved through Collins delineability: [`exists_isCAD_signInvariant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isCAD_signInvariant).
- **The Tarski–Seidenberg theorem** — projections of semialgebraic sets are semialgebraic. This is an [instance over `ℝ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/Existence.html#TauCeti.instHasSemialgebraicProjectionsReal), so [quantifier elimination](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/QuantifierElimination.html#TauCeti.exists_isQF_realize_iff) holds unconditionally there. Semialgebraic sets are also exactly the sets [definable in the field language](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/RingLanguage.html#TauCeti.isSemialgebraic_iff_definable_ring_real), with no order symbol.
- **Thom's lemma** — if a sign is prescribed for every derivative of a polynomial, including the polynomial itself, the set of points realising those signs has at most one point or is an open interval: [`preimage_derivativeSign_subsingleton_or_eq_Ioo`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Thom/Interval.html#Polynomial.preimage_derivativeSign_subsingleton_or_eq_Ioo).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The local discriminant theorem.** A polynomial of constant degree over a connected analytic submanifold, nowhere nullified there and with discriminant of constant order, is [analytically delineable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Stack/Discriminant/Global.html#TauCeti.exists_analytic_delineation_of_orderAt_discr_eq). This is the engine behind the McCallum results.
- **Squarefree bases.** [`IsSquarefreeBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/SquarefreeBasis.html#Finset.IsSquarefreeBasis) gives primitive, squarefree, pairwise coprime factors that reconstruct each input up to a constant. McCallum's projection, lifting and well-orientedness are stated for such a basis, as the README specifies. Two counterexamples show the hypotheses are needed: lifting fails [over positive-dimensional nullifying cells](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/McCallum/Basic.html#TauCeti.exists_nullified_mcCallumProjection_orderAt_ne), and it fails when the projection is only [sign-invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Projection/McCallum/SignInvariant.html#TauCeti.exists_signInvariant_mcCallumProjection_stackCells_not_signInvariant).
- **Lazard lifting.** A family has a Lazard delineation over a connected analytic submanifold on which its Lazard projection is [valuation-invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Projection/Lazard/Lifting.html#TauCeti.nonempty_lazardDelineation_of_lazardProjection). Its [reordered-base CAD form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD/Lazard/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsCAD.exists_isCAD_lazardValuation_eq_of_lazardDelineation_reindex) is the inductive step of the Lazard recursion.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 1–8 are all done. Layer 1 has real closures, together with the characterisation that a field is [real closed exactly when a quadratic extension is algebraically closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/RealClosure/QuadraticExtension.html#TauCeti.RealClosure.isRealClosed_iff_isAlgClosed_of_isQuadraticExtension). The remaining layers are BKR sign determination (Layer 3), root continuity and common matching (Layer 4), Collins delineability (Layer 5), CAD and quantifier elimination over `ℝ` (Layer 6), McCallum's projection for a squarefree basis (Layer 7), and Lazard evaluation, projection and CAD existence (Layer 8).

## The frontier

- **No README milestone remains open.** Every layer's targets are proved in the library. By the README's own choice of scope, the topological and CAD results are over `ℝ` rather than an arbitrary real closed field.
- **Lazard's theorem in the native coordinate order** is optional. The validity theorem still reads the Lazard valuation with the coordinates reversed, which matches how Lazard evaluation treats them during the recursion.
