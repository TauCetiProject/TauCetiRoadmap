## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Every layer's stated milestones are proved, from the graphon carrier through both representation summits and sampling convergence, and the representation and limit-existence theorems hold on any atomless standard Borel carrier. The graph-specific exchangeable laws now also adapt to a generic relational-law API, as the README requires. What remains is housekeeping.

### Named results

- **The Lovász–Szegedy characterization** — a graph parameter is the homomorphism density of a graphon on an atomless standard Borel carrier exactly when it is isomorphism invariant, multiplicative, normalized, and reflection positive ([`lovasz_szegedy_representability`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Representability/Representation.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.lovasz_szegedy_representability)).
- **The Diaconis–Janson graphon-mixture representation** — every exchangeable law on infinite graphs is a mixture of graphon sampling laws, for exactly one mixing measure on graphon space ([`existsUnique_bind_infiniteSampleLawOnSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/ExchangeableGraphLaw/Infinite/Mixture.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.InfiniteExchangeableGraphLaw.existsUnique_bind_infiniteSampleLawOnSpace)). Its extreme points are now identified as well: [dissociated relational laws of graphs are exactly graphon sampling laws](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/ExchangeableGraphLaw/Relational/Dissociated.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.isDissociated_iff_exists_toRelationalLaw_sampleExchangeableLaw).
- **The convergence equivalence** — for graphons on arbitrary, possibly different, probability carriers, convergence in cut distance is the same as convergence of every homomorphism density ([`tendsto_cutDist_iff_forall_homDensity_tendsto`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.tendsto_cutDist_iff_forall_homDensity_tendsto)). As a corollary, every convergent sequence of finite graphs [has a limit graphon](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.exists_graphon_tendsto_cutDist_finiteGraphGraphon_iff_forall_homDensityFin_cauchySeq).
- **Lovász–Szegedy compactness** — the cut-distance quotient of graphons on any atomless standard Borel carrier is a compact metric space ([`GraphonSpace.instCompactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Compact.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.GraphonSpace.instCompactSpace)).
- **Frieze–Kannan weak regularity** — every graphon is within ε in cut norm of a block-average step graphon with at most 4^⌈1/ε²⌉ parts ([`weak_regularity_frieze_kannan`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/StepGraphon/Regularity.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.weak_regularity_frieze_kannan)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The coupling cut distance** — [`cutDist`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Distance.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist) satisfies an [arbitrary-carrier triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Triangle.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist_triangle), so graphon space is a genuine metric quotient over every carrier.
- **The unit-interval representative** — [`unitIntervalRepr`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/UnitIntervalEmbedding.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.Graphon.unitIntervalRepr) isometrically embeds every graphon space in the unit-interval one. Total boundedness, compactness, convergence and sampling convergence all reach other carriers through it.
- **The relational-law adapter** — a [generic exchangeable relational law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/RelationalLaw/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.ExchangeableRelationalLaw) lives in the general probability library, and [exchangeable laws on infinite graphs are exactly those relational laws of the graph language concentrated on simple graphs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/ExchangeableGraphLaw/Relational/Basic.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.InfiniteExchangeableGraphLaw.relationalLawEquiv). The adapter carries dissociation, ergodicity and extremality across, so the graph structures are specializations of the generic API rather than a parallel theory.
