## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Every layer's stated milestones are proved, from the graphon carrier through both representation summits and sampling convergence. Recent work has removed unit-interval restrictions: the representation and limit-existence theorems now hold on any atomless standard Borel carrier. It has also proved the compatibility the README requires between the graph-level mixture representation and Aldous–Hoover codings.

### Named results

- **The Lovász–Szegedy characterization** — a graph parameter is the homomorphism density of a graphon on an atomless standard Borel carrier exactly when it is isomorphism invariant, multiplicative, normalized, and reflection positive ([`lovasz_szegedy_representability`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Representability/Representation.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.lovasz_szegedy_representability)).
- **The Diaconis–Janson graphon-mixture representation** — every exchangeable law on infinite graphs is a mixture of graphon sampling laws for exactly one mixing measure on graphon space ([`existsUnique_bind_infiniteSampleLawOnSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/ExchangeableGraphLaw/Infinite/Mixture.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.InfiniteExchangeableGraphLaw.existsUnique_bind_infiniteSampleLawOnSpace)). The bundled form is the equivalence `graphonMixtureLawEquiv`.
- **The convergence equivalence** — for graphons on arbitrary, possibly different, probability carriers, convergence in cut distance is the same as convergence of every homomorphism density ([`tendsto_cutDist_iff_forall_homDensity_tendsto`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.tendsto_cutDist_iff_forall_homDensity_tendsto)). As a corollary, every convergent sequence of finite graphs has a limit graphon on any atomless standard Borel carrier ([`exists_graphon_tendsto_cutDist_finiteGraphGraphon_iff_forall_homDensityFin_cauchySeq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.exists_graphon_tendsto_cutDist_finiteGraphGraphon_iff_forall_homDensityFin_cauchySeq)).
- **Lovász–Szegedy compactness** — the cut-distance quotient of graphons on any atomless standard Borel carrier is a compact metric space ([`GraphonSpace.instCompactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Compact.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.GraphonSpace.instCompactSpace)).
- **Frieze–Kannan weak regularity** — every graphon is within ε in cut norm of a block-average step graphon with at most 4^⌈1/ε²⌉ parts ([`weak_regularity_frieze_kannan`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/StepGraphon/Regularity.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.weak_regularity_frieze_kannan)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The coupling cut distance** — [`cutDist`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Distance.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist) satisfies an [arbitrary-carrier triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Triangle.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist_triangle), so graphon space is a genuine metric quotient over every carrier.
- **The unit-interval representative** — [`unitIntervalRepr`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/UnitIntervalEmbedding.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.Graphon.unitIntervalRepr) gives an isometric embedding of every graphon space into the unit-interval one. This is how total boundedness, compactness, convergence and sampling convergence transfer off the unit interval.
- **Density coordinates** — [`homDensityCoords`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Coordinates.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.homDensityCoords) is a [closed embedding](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Coordinates.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.isClosedEmbedding_homDensityCoords) of unit-interval graphon space into a product of real lines. It makes measurability on graphon space checkable density by density, and the mixture representation rests on it.
