- **The cancellation library over `ℤ_p[G]`**: this consists of Krull–Schmidt, [Swan's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/GroupAlgebra/Swan.html#TauCeti.nonempty_linearEquiv_of_projective_of_tensorRat), the torsion criterion and the stable-to-isomorphism step. Together these turn rational identities into integral generator counts.

## The frontier

- **Explicit generators under finite extensions**: the map from the marked presentation of `G_L(p)` into `G_K(p)` is in general not injective, and the images of the marked generators need not generate. Nothing yet writes those images as words in the marked generators of `G_K(p)`. The roadmap does not ask for this, but it is the natural next refinement.
- **Retiring the local cyclotomic character**: the README expects this roadmap's `localCyclotomicCharacter` to be replaced by Tau Ceti's own once the pin moves. That is maintenance, not mathematics.
