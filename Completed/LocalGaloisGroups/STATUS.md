## The frontier

- **Explicit generators under finite extensions**: the finite-extension compatibility places `G_L(p)` inside `G_K(p)` abstractly. It does not say how the marked generators of `G_L(p)` are written in those of `G_K(p)`. The roadmap does not ask for this, but it is the natural next refinement.
- **Retiring the local cyclotomic character**: the README expects this roadmap's `localCyclotomicCharacter` to be replaced by Tau Ceti's own once the pin moves. That is maintenance, not mathematics.
