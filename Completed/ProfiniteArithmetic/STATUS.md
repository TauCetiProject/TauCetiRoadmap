## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All four layers are done: the ring `ẑ` with its decomposition, idempotents and units, the profinite and `ℓ`-adic powers, continuous automorphisms with the congruence topology and the outer action of an extension, and the graded `ℤ_p`-Lie algebra of the closed lower central series with free pro-`p` bases in degrees zero and one. The README's worked examples are all in, including the non-integral idempotent with components `1, 0, 1, 0, …`.

### Named results

- **The pro-`p` automorphism theorem** — for a pro-`p` group `G`, the continuous automorphisms acting trivially on the Frattini quotient [form a pro-`p` group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/ProP.html#TauCeti.IsProP.isProP_ker_mapQuotient_proPFrattini), which is [open](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/ProP.html#TauCeti.ContinuousAut.isOpen_ker_mapQuotient_proPFrattini) when `G` is topologically finitely generated, so `ContinuousAut G` is virtually pro-`p`.
- **`gr_1` of a free pro-`p` group is the exterior square of `gr_0`** — for the free pro-`p` group of finite rank `r`, the bracket induces [`⋀²gr_0 ≃ gr_1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/LowerCentralSeries.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exteriorSquareEquivLcsGradedPieceOne) over `ℤ_p`, so `gr_1` is free of [rank `r choose 2`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/LowerCentralSeries.html#TauCeti.freeProP.finrank_lcsGradedPiece_one) on the brackets `[x̄_i, x̄_j]`, `i < j`.
- **The outer action of an extension** — for a normal subgroup `N` of a topological group `E`, conjugation descends to [`E ⧸ N →* ContinuousOut N`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/OuterAction.html#TauCeti.outerAction), with kernel the image of `N ⊔ C_E(N)`, and it is [continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/OuterAction.html#TauCeti.continuous_outerAction) whenever `N` is compact.
- **The product decomposition of `ẑ`** — the `ℓ`-adic components give an isomorphism of topological rings [`ẑ ≃ ∏_ℓ ℤ_ℓ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.zHat.ringEquivPiPadicInt), and on units [`ẑˣ ≃ ∏_ℓ ℤ_ℓˣ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Units.html#TauCeti.zHat.unitsEquivPiPadicInt).
- **The idempotents of `ẑ`** — every idempotent is [`ω_S` for exactly one set `S` of primes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Idempotent.html#TauCeti.zHat.isIdempotentElem_iff_exists_idemOfSet), the element with component `1` on `S` and `0` off it, and `ω_S` is [an integer only when it is `0` or `1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Idempotent.html#TauCeti.zHat.idemOfSet_mem_range_intCast_iff).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The profinite power** — [`zpowHat`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Pow.html#TauCeti.zpowHat), `x ^ᶻ a`, is defined for a profinite group in any universe and [agrees with the `ℓ`-adic power](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Pow.html#TauCeti.zpowHat_eq_padicPow_component) on a pro-`ℓ` group, which is what downstream Tate-twist and peripheral-power statements need.
- **The graded Lie algebra** — the direct sum of the graded pieces is a Lie ring with a [functorial Lie homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Graded/LieRing.html#TauCeti.gradedLieHom) for every continuous homomorphism, and for a pro-`ℓ` group it is a [Lie algebra over `ℤ_ℓ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Graded/LieRing.html#TauCeti.IsProP.gradedLieAlgebra). This is the object PeripheralActions consumes.
- **The congruence topology on `ContinuousAut`** — for a topologically finitely generated profinite group it is [compact](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Profinite.html#TauCeti.ContinuousAut.compactSpace), Hausdorff and totally disconnected, and conditions such as [carrying `x` into the conjugacy class of `y`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Congruence.html#TauCeti.ContinuousAut.isClosed_isConj) cut out closed sets, the form the closed-graph arguments of PeripheralActions use.

### Roadmap coverage

**All four layers are done**, as previously reported, and this window only added to them. Layer 0 has the ring `ẑ` with its limit description, components, product decomposition, units and character assembly, and now the full classification of its idempotents. Layer 1 has the profinite power with its calculus, closed procyclic subgroups and `ℓ`-parts, and the unit-exponent behaviour of the `ℓ`-adic power. Layer 2 has `ContinuousAut` and `ContinuousOut` with the congruence topology, the closedness of conjugacy conditions, the pro-`p` theorem, the lift of `GL_n(𝔽_p)` to a free pro-`p` group and the outer action, the last proved for compact `N`, which includes the roadmap's case. Layer 3 has the closed lower central series as a `ℤ_p`-Lie algebra, the spanning theorem, finite generation of the pieces, and free pro-`p` bases in degrees zero and one.
