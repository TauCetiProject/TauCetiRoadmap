## McKaySkewGroup: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`579c47e` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap's first concrete model of a finite subgroup of SU(2) has landed: the [binary dihedral subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryDihedralSubgroup) of order 4n. It is generated by r = diag(exp(πi/n), exp(−πi/n)) and s = !![0, 1; −1, 0] and is built as the image of a [faithful](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryDihedralHom_injective) representation of Mathlib's dicyclic group, so it comes with an [isomorphism to `QuaternionGroup n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryDihedralEquiv) (TauCeti#33621). Every element has the [normal form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.mem_binaryDihedralSubgroup_iff) r^j or s·r^j with j < 2n, the two forms never coincide, and the group has [exactly 4n elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.natCard_binaryDihedralSubgroup). The relations r^(2n) = 1, s² = r^n = −1 and s r s⁻¹ = r⁻¹ hold for the actual matrices, and the generators are matched with the quaternions cos(π/n) + sin(π/n)i and j. For n = 1 the group is shown to be cyclic of order four, which is why the family proper starts at Q₈. A few supporting facts about the quarter turn in SU(2) and the circle character of ZMod (2n) came with it.

This is one of the five concrete models in Layer 0. The cyclic, binary tetrahedral, octahedral and icosahedral models are still missing, and so are Klein's classification and conjugacy theorem and everything from the McKay matrix onward.
