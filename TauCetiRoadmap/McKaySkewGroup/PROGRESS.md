## McKaySkewGroup: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`579c47e` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap's first concrete model of a finite subgroup of SU(2) has landed: the [binary dihedral subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryDihedralSubgroup) of order 4n. It is generated by r = diag(exp(πi/n), exp(−πi/n)) and s = !![0, 1; −1, 0] and is built as the image of a [faithful](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryDihedralHom_injective) representation of Mathlib's dicyclic group, so it comes with an [isomorphism to `QuaternionGroup n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryDihedralEquiv) (TauCeti#33621). Every element has the [normal form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.mem_binaryDihedralSubgroup_iff) r^j or s·r^j with j < 2n, the two forms never coincide, and the group has [exactly 4n elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryDihedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.natCard_binaryDihedralSubgroup). The relations r^(2n) = 1, s² = r^n = −1 and s r s⁻¹ = r⁻¹ hold for the actual matrices, and the generators are matched with the quaternions cos(π/n) + sin(π/n)i and j. For n = 1 the group is shown to be cyclic of order four, which is why the family proper starts at Q₈. A few supporting facts about the quarter turn in SU(2) and the circle character of ZMod (2n) came with it.

This is one of the five concrete models in Layer 0. The cyclic, binary tetrahedral, octahedral and icosahedral models are still missing, and so are Klein's classification and conjugacy theorem and everything from the McKay matrix onward.

## McKaySkewGroup: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

Three more of Layer 0's five concrete finite subgroups of SU(2) are now built: the cyclic, binary tetrahedral and binary octahedral ones. The [cyclic model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/Cyclic.html#TauCeti.SU2.cyclicSubgroup) is generated by diag(exp(2πi/n), exp(−2πi/n)), has [exactly n elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/Cyclic.html#TauCeti.SU2.natCard_cyclicSubgroup), and is [identified with `Multiplicative (ZMod n)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/Cyclic.html#TauCeti.SU2.cyclicEquiv). It also comes with the uniqueness fact that the odd case of the classification will need: [every finite subgroup of the diagonal torus is the cyclic model of its order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/Cyclic.html#Subgroup.eq_SU2_cyclicSubgroup_of_le_torus) (TauCeti#33139).

The two exceptional groups are built in unit-quaternion coordinates. The [binary tetrahedral subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryTetrahedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryTetrahedralSubgroup) is the set of unit quaternions with [half-integer coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryTetrahedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.mem_binaryTetrahedralSubgroup_iff_exists_int), the units of the Hurwitz order. It has [24 elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryTetrahedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.natCard_binaryTetrahedralSubgroup) and is [generated by i, j and ω = (−1 + i + j + k)/2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryTetrahedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryTetrahedralSubgroup_eq_closure). The [binary octahedral subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryOctahedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.binaryOctahedralSubgroup) adjoins the 24 points with two nonzero coordinates ±1/√2. It has [48 elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryOctahedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.natCard_binaryOctahedralSubgroup), [contains the tetrahedral group with index two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SU2/BinaryOctahedral.html#TauCeti.SU2.relIndex_binaryTetrahedralSubgroup_binaryOctahedralSubgroup), and is generated by the binary dihedral generators of order sixteen together with ω. Each model contains the expected binary dihedral subgroup, so its coordinate and presentation generators are related. Both groups are pinned down by integer-point counts on small spheres (sums of four squares equal to 4 or 2), which are checked by `decide`.

What is still missing in Layer 0 is the binary icosahedral model, the SL₂(ℂ) copies and determinant-one alternating forms that the roadmap asks of every model, and all of Klein's classification and conjugacy. Nothing has landed beyond Layer 0.
