## EllipticIntegrals: 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-08 (`898397d` to `d2083f0`)

This first report covers most of the roadmap's classical core. [Legendre's relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Relation.html#TauCeti.Real.legendre_relation) `E K′ + E′ K − K K′ = π/2` is proved by differentiating in the modulus. The AGM side is complete too. The integral representation [`agmIntegral a b = π/2 / M(a, b)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/AGM/Basic.html#TauCeti.agmIntegral_eq_pi_div_two_div_agm) is built to the shape of mathlib4#34397, and it gives [Gauss's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Gauss.html#TauCeti.Real.legendreK_eq_pi_div_two_mul_agm) `K(k) = π / (2 M(1, k′))`. From there come Landen's transformation, the [AGM series for `E`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/AGM/SecondKind.html#TauCeti.Real.legendreEc_eq_agm_tsum) and the [Gauss–Brent–Salamin formula for π](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/BrentSalamin.html#TauCeti.Real.pi_eq_four_mul_agm_sq_div_one_sub_tsum). The special values followed: [`K(1/√2) = Γ(1/4)²/(4√π)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Lemniscate.html#TauCeti.Real.legendreK_one_div_sqrt_two), and Gauss's identity [`ϖ = π / M(1, √2)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Lemniscate.html#TauCeti.Real.lemniscateConstant_eq_pi_div_agm) for the lemniscate constant.

Underneath this sits the Legendre integrals' full basic API, ending in the [order isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Legendre.html#TauCeti.Real.legendreFOrderIso) `φ ↦ F(φ, k)`. On top of that come the derivatives in `k`, [Legendre's differential equations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/ModulusDerivative.html#TauCeti.Real.legendreK_differential_equation), the [hypergeometric series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Hypergeometric.html#TauCeti.Real.legendreK_eq_ordinaryHypergeometric) and the logarithmic singularity at `k = 1`. Inverting `F` gives the [amplitude](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/JacobiElliptic/Basic.html#TauCeti.Real.jacobiAm) and the real Jacobi functions, which [degenerate to `tanh`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/JacobiElliptic/Degeneration.html#TauCeti.Real.tendsto_jacobiSn_nhdsLT_one) and `sech` as `k → 1⁻`. On the geometric side, the window proves the ellipse perimeter `4aE(e)` and the [real period of a curve with one real root](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/RealPeriod.html#WeierstrassCurve.realPeriod_eq_two_mul_pi_div_agm_of_one_real_root) as an AGM.

[Carlson's integrals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Carlson/Basic.html#Complex.carlsonRF) `R_F, R_J, R_D, R_C, R_G` are now defined on slit-plane nodes. They come with symmetry, homogeneity, holomorphy in each variable, the elementary `R_C`, and the [dictionary with Legendre's integrals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Carlson/Legendre.html#TauCeti.Real.ofReal_legendreF_eq_carlsonRF). One gap remains: the duplication theorem is proved only at nonnegative real arguments. The window adds the complex [duplication node](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/EllipticIntegral/Carlson/Duplication/Domain.html#Complex.carlsonDuplicationLambda) and its slit-plane lemmas, but not the theorem on the whole domain.
