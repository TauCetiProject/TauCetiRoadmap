## ScatteringDiagrams: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`349d0ea` to `d9fbe7b`)

The first named result of this roadmap is the [Minkowski–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/Cone/MinkowskiWeyl.html#PointedCone.fg_iff_dualFG): a pointed cone in a finite-dimensional space over an ordered field is finitely generated exactly when it is cut out by finitely many halfspaces, lineality allowed. It is one of the headlines stated in `Suggested.lean`. The refinement with rational generators and functionals for lattice-rational cones has not been stated yet. Alongside it, every [face of a finitely generated cone is exposed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/Cone/Face/Exposed.html#PointedCone.IsFaceOf.isExposed_of_fg), rational affine polyhedra arrived with transport along integral-affine equivalences, and countably many finite families of proper affine subspaces were shown to have a [dense common generic locus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Affine/Generic.html#TauCeti.ExceptionalArrangement.dense_iInter_isGeneric), which is what generic paths will need.

Most of the window laid the Layer 0 foundations. These are [positive degree data](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/PositiveDegrees/Basic.html#TauCeti.PositiveDegrees) and their maps with cutoff moduli, with [every nonzero degree on a unique supported ray](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/PositiveDegrees/Ray/Basic.html#TauCeti.PositiveDegrees.existsUnique_supportedRay_multiplier). Completions of filtered algebras are shown to be [inverse limits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FilteredAlgebra/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AlgFiltration.completionAlgebraConeIsLimit), and completed groups of towers to [agree with Mathlib's limit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/CompletedGroup/Basic.html#TauCeti.CompletedGroup.limitConeIso). Ingredients for the BCH group also appeared: the free Lie algebra's word-length grading has [the lower central series as its degree filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Free/LowerCentral.html#TauCeti.FreeLieAlgebra.lowerCentralSeries_toSubmodule_eq_iSup), PBW symmetrization became a [coalgebra map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/PBW/Symmetrization/Coalgebra.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.pbwSymmetrizationCoalgHom), and over a rational algebra the [primitive elements of a symmetric algebra are its generators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/Primitive.html#SymmetricAlgebra.isPrimitiveElem_iff_mem_range_ι). The BCH element and group, and the completed exponential, are not there yet.

On the categorical side, [limits over a Grothendieck construction can be computed fiberwise](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Grothendieck.html#CategoryTheory.Limits.limitFiberwiseLimitIso). Graded coefficient correspondences arrived with their transfers, degree-history expansions and nilpotent recursions. The first scattering-specific object is [directed wall spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/ScatteringDiagram/WallSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.DirectedWallSpaces), where walls with proportional normals commute.
