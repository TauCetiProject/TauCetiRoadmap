### Named results

- **The PBW symmetrization theorem** — over a commutative ℚ-algebra, symmetrization from the symmetric algebra of any Lie algebra to its enveloping algebra is a filtered coalgebra isomorphism, with no freeness or flatness assumption ([`exists_pbwSymmetrizationEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/PBW/Symmetrization/Equiv.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.exists_pbwSymmetrizationEquiv)). This is a `Suggested.lean` headline, following Petracci.
- **The Minkowski–Weyl theorem, with its rational form** — a pointed cone over an ordered field is finitely generated exactly when it is cut out by finitely many halfspaces ([`PointedCone.fg_iff_dualFG`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/Cone/MinkowskiWeyl.html#PointedCone.fg_iff_dualFG)), and for a lattice-rational cone both the generators and the functionals can be taken integral ([`isLatticeRational_iff_exists_dual_image`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Cone/RationalDuality.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsIntegralLattice.isLatticeRational_iff_exists_dual_image)).
- **Gordan's lemma** — the lattice points of a lattice-rational cone form a finitely generated monoid, and with a strictly positive integral functional such cones become positive degree data ([`fg_comap_toAddSubmonoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Cone/Finiteness.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsLatticeRational.fg_comap_toAddSubmonoid)).
- **Primitive elements of an enveloping algebra** — over a commutative rational algebra they are exactly the image of the Lie algebra, the uncompleted form of the step that will identify completed primitives with the completed Lie algebra ([`primitiveEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/Primitive.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.primitiveEquiv)).
- **Finiteness of faces** — a convex polyhedron has only finitely many faces, and every convex extreme subset of one is exposed ([`finite_setOf_isExposed`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Polyhedron/Face.html#TauCeti.IsConvexPolyhedron.finite_setOf_isExposed)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Degree truncations of positively graded Lie algebras** ([`truncatedLie`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Graded/Truncation.html#TauCeti.PositiveDegrees.truncatedLie)) are nilpotent, their successive transition kernels are central, and graded Lie maps induce maps between them along cutoff moduli. These are the levels at which the BCH groups and wall groups will be built. The completed Lie algebra is identified with the [product of the graded pieces under a convolution bracket](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Graded/Completion.html#TauCeti.PositiveDegrees.completionLieEquivPi).
- **Finite polyhedral complexes** ([`FinitePolyhedralComplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/PolyhedralComplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.FinitePolyhedralComplex)) of rational-normal cells, unbounded cells and lineality allowed, come with walls, joints, subcomplexes, subdivision and integral-affine transport. Layer 2's labelled complexes will sit on these.
- **Polyhedral cochains** ([`PolyhedralCochain`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/PolyhedralCochain/Basic.html#TauCeti.PolyhedralCochain)) are oriented cells modulo reversal and subdivision, with a universal property and an [intersection pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/PolyhedralCochain/Pairing.html#TauCeti.OrientedCell.intersectionPairing) against transverse affine simplices that respects the relations.
