### Named results

- **The PBW symmetrization theorem** — over a commutative ℚ-algebra, symmetrization from the symmetric algebra of any Lie algebra to its enveloping algebra is a filtered coalgebra isomorphism, with no freeness or flatness assumption ([`exists_pbwSymmetrizationEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/PBW/Symmetrization/Equiv.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.exists_pbwSymmetrizationEquiv)). This is a `Suggested.lean` headline, following Petracci.
- **The Minkowski–Weyl theorem, with its rational form** — a pointed cone over an ordered field is finitely generated exactly when it is cut out by finitely many halfspaces ([`PointedCone.fg_iff_dualFG`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/Cone/MinkowskiWeyl.html#PointedCone.fg_iff_dualFG)), and for a lattice-rational cone both the generators and the functionals can be taken integral ([`isLatticeRational_iff_exists_dual_image`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Cone/RationalDuality.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsIntegralLattice.isLatticeRational_iff_exists_dual_image)).
- **Gordan's lemma** — the lattice points of a lattice-rational cone form a finitely generated monoid, so such cones with a strictly positive integral functional give positive degree data ([`fg_comap_toAddSubmonoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Cone/Finiteness.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsLatticeRational.fg_comap_toAddSubmonoid)).
- **Primitive elements of an enveloping algebra** — over a commutative rational algebra they are exactly the image of the Lie algebra. This is the uncompleted form of the step that will identify completed primitives with the completed Lie algebra ([`primitiveEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/Primitive.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.primitiveEquiv)).
- **Finiteness of faces** — a convex polyhedron has only finitely many faces, and every convex extreme subset of one is exposed ([`finite_setOf_isExposed`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Polyhedron/Face.html#TauCeti.IsConvexPolyhedron.finite_setOf_isExposed)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Degree truncations of positively graded Lie algebras** ([`truncatedLie`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Graded/Truncation.html#TauCeti.PositiveDegrees.truncatedLie)) are nilpotent, and their successive transition kernels are central. These are the levels at which the BCH groups and wall groups will be built. A Lie action by derivations on a filtered algebra already [exponentiates at each finite order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Filtered/Exponential.html#TauCeti.AlgFiltration.truncatedExpLieAction).
- **Finite polyhedral complexes** ([`FinitePolyhedralComplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/PolyhedralComplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.FinitePolyhedralComplex)) have rational-normal cells, and unbounded cells and lineality are allowed. They come with subdivision, integral-affine transport, a [common refinement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/PolyhedralComplex/CommonRefinement.html#TauCeti.FinitePolyhedralComplex.commonRefinement) and an exact [comparison with toric fans](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/PolyhedralComplex/Fan.html#TauCeti.FinitePolyhedralComplex.toFan). Layer 2's labelled complexes will sit on these.
- **Polygonal path products** ([`wallPathProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/ScatteringDiagram/Path/Product.html#Quiver.Path.wallPathProduct)) multiply wall labels along admissible paths, grouping simultaneous coplanar crossings into one event. They give the transport that §2.1's path products and consistency will be stated with.
