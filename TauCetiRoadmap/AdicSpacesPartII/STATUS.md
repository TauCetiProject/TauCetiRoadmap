### Named results

- **Fibre products of affinoid adic spaces** (Huber's Proposition 3.7, Wedhorn's Theorem 8.56) — for adic maps of sheafy complete Huber pairs, `Spa` of a sheafy completed tensor product is the fibre product, and sheafiness is automatic when one factor is stably sheafy and the other map is topologically of finite type ([`affinoid_isPullback`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Pullback/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.affinoid_isPullback), [`affinoid_isPullback_of_isStablySheafyRing_left`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Pullback/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.affinoid_isPullback_of_isStablySheafyRing_left)).
- **Fibre products are local on the source** — if each member of an open cover of `X` has a fibre product with `Y` over `S`, these glue to `X ×_S Y` ([`hasPullback_of_cover`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Pullback/Cover.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.hasPullback_of_cover)).
- **Huber's bijection for affinoid adic spaces** — morphisms `Spa(B, B⁺) → Spa(A, A⁺)` of adic spaces between complete sheafy pairs are exactly the morphisms of Huber pairs `(A, A⁺) → (B, B⁺)` ([`ofPairHomEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.ofPairHomEquiv)).
- **Huber's Lemma 2.3** — over a Huber ring with a noetherian ring of definition, linear maps of finite modules are strict, and completing a finite module is completed scalar extension ([`isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Strict.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing), [`completionTensorHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/CompletedScalars.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.completionTensorHomeomorph)).
- **Analytic locally noetherian spaces are locally Huber-standard** — on an analytic adic space the two chart conditions coincide, so the étale theory's hypothesis costs nothing there ([`isLocallyHuberStandard_iff_isLocallyNoetherian_of_analyticPoints_eq_univ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PreAdicSpace/LocallyHuberStandard.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.isLocallyHuberStandard_iff_isLocallyNoetherian_of_analyticPoints_eq_univ)).

## The frontier

- **Huber's sheafiness theorem, noetherian ring of definition (R0.2)**: prove the filtered Čech differentials strict by Artin–Rees on the blow-up charts, deduce exactness of the completed complex, then refine by standard rational coverings to get sheafiness and stable sheafiness for noetherian type.
- **Morphisms locally of finite presentation (R0.6)**: the witness-chart class is now available, so this definition, its comparison with local finite type over analytic or noetherian bases, and composition and restriction are next. It gates unramified, smooth and étale morphisms.
- **General fibre products (R0.7)**: the gluing lemma reduces existence to pieces of an open cover of the source; what remains is the affinoid-over-non-affinoid case, the non-adic base change by an ascending union of affinoids, and base-change stability. The Huber-pair form of R0.3 is a prerequisite.
- **Closed subspaces and separatedness (R0.8, R0.10)**: closed adic subspaces from ideal sheaves and the locally closed diagonal, then the separated and proper classes; the diagonal needs general fibre products.
- **Analytification (R1)**: Huber's fibre product of a scheme with an adic space and the analytification functor over `K` have not begun.
