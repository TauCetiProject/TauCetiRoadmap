### Named results

- **Fibre products of affinoid adic spaces** (Huber's Proposition 3.7, Wedhorn's Theorem 8.56) — for adic maps of sheafy complete Huber pairs, `Spa` of a sheafy completed tensor product is the fibre product, and sheafiness is automatic when one factor is stably sheafy and the other map is topologically of finite type ([`affinoid_isPullback`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Pullback/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.affinoid_isPullback), [`affinoid_isPullback_of_isStablySheafyRing_left`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Pullback/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.affinoid_isPullback_of_isStablySheafyRing_left)).
- **Huber's bijection for affinoid adic spaces** — morphisms `Spa(B, B⁺) → Spa(A, A⁺)` of adic spaces between complete sheafy pairs are exactly the morphisms of Huber pairs `(A, A⁺) → (B, B⁺)` ([`ofPairHomEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.ofPairHomEquiv)).
- **Huber's Lemma 2.3** — over a Huber ring with a noetherian ring of definition, linear maps of finite modules are strict, and completing a finite module is completed scalar extension as a topological module over the completed ring ([`isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Strict.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing), [`completionTensorHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/CompletedScalars.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.completionTensorHomeomorph)).
- **Adic morphisms are those that preserve analytic points** — Huber's Proposition 3.2(ii), the pointwise test for adicness ([`isAdic_iff_mapsTo_analyticPoints`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Morphisms/Adic.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.isAdic_iff_mapsTo_analyticPoints)).
- **The adic spectrum of a directed colimit** — under a condition on the colimit's ideal of definition that filtered colimits of Tate pairs satisfy, `Spa(L, L⁺)` is the inverse limit of the `Spa(Aᵢ, Aᵢ⁺)` and its rational subsets come from single stages ([`spaDirectLimitHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/DirectLimit.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spaDirectLimitHomeomorph), [`exists_spaComap_preimage_rationalSubset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/DirectLimit.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.exists_spaComap_preimage_rationalSubset)).

## The frontier

- **Strictness of the algebraic Čech complex**: use the blow-up charts and Artin–Rees to prove the filtered differentials strict; completing then gives exactness of the completed complex.
- **Huber's sheafiness theorem, noetherian ring of definition**: combine that exactness with refinement by standard rational coverings, then deduce stable sheafiness for rings of noetherian type. This closes R0.2.
- **Fibre products beyond affinoids (R0.7)**: glue the affinoid case along morphisms locally of finite type, including the ascending union of affinoids when the base change is not adic. The Huber-pair form of the rational-localisation comparison (R0.3) is a prerequisite.
- **Finite presentation and locally Huber-standard spaces (R0.6)**: these gate unramified, smooth and étale morphisms, which also need continuous differentials beyond their definition.
- **Closed subspaces and separatedness (R0.8, R0.10)**: closed adic subspaces from ideal sheaves and the locally closed diagonal, then the separated and proper classes. The diagonal needs fibre products beyond affinoids.
