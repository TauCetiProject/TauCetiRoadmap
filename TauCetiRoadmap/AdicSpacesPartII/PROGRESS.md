## AdicSpacesPartII: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`4e9057a` to `c1c6773`)

The first work on this roadmap lays the ring-level ground for Huber's sheafiness theorem in the case of a noetherian ring of definition, without yet proving it. Three of the inputs Huber's proof calls for are now in place. The [Čech complex of localizations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Cech.html#TauCeti.cechComplexLocalization_exact) of a module is exact when the elements generate the unit ideal. [Completion preserves strict exact sequences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Nonarchimedean/Completion/Strict.html#AddMonoidHom.exact_completion) of nonarchimedean groups. And Huber's Lemma 2.3 holds over a Huber ring with a noetherian ring of definition: [linear maps of finite modules are strict](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Strict.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing), and over a complete base [submodules are closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Complete.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isClosed_of_isNoetherianRing). An Artin–Rees statement for finitely generated submodules and Huber's lemma on the spectrum of an open subring (TauCeti#19147) fill out the toolkit.

The classes the rest of the roadmap is stated in also arrived: [Huber rings of noetherian type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType), [Huber's standing class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/HuberStandard.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsHuberStandard), [adic homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/AdicHom/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsAdicHom) and [adic rings of finite ideal type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicRing/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAdicRing). Noetherian type is preserved by [completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Completion/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType.completion) and by quotients, and in a complete ring of this type [every ideal is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/Quotient.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType.isClosed_ideal). The underlying algebra is the EGA statement that [a complete ring is noetherian when its quotient by a finitely generated ideal of definition is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Noetherian.html#Ideal.isNoetherianRing_of_isAdicComplete). On the topological side, [locally spectral spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/LocallySpectral.html#TauCeti.IsLocallySpectralSpace) and locally spectral maps now have their basic theory.

Nothing geometric has landed yet. There are no adic morphism classes, no completed tensor products and no fibre products, and the analytification layer has not been started.

## AdicSpacesPartII: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`c1c6773` to `e8ea0d1`)

This window brought the sheafiness proof for rings with a noetherian ring of definition most of the way to its last step. The third part of Huber's Lemma 2.3 is now proved: over a Huber ring with a noetherian ring of definition, [completing a finite module is the same as base change to the completed ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.ofTensorProduct_bijective), and the comparison map [is a homeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isHomeomorph_ofTensorProduct) (TauCeti#19533). The blow-up step that the last report listed as missing now has its geometry. The [blow-up of a submodule](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Submodule.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.submoduleBlowup), `Proj(⊕ Jⁿ Tⁿ)`, is [proper](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Submodule.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.submoduleBlowup.isProper_toSpec) and noetherian over the base, and its standard opens are [the spectra of the chart rings `C[F/S]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Submodule.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.submoduleBlowup.basicOpenIsoSpec).

The Čech complex of rational localizations also has its topology now. [Huber's filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianCech/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.NoetherianCech.hasBasis_nhds_zero_filtration), read off the chart rings, is a basis of neighbourhoods of zero on each term, and the differentials are continuous. The [completed complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianCech/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.NoetherianCech.completedTerm) is defined, and its differentials [compose to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianCech/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.NoetherianCech.completedDifferential_comp_differential). Two things are still missing: the Artin–Rees argument that the algebraic differentials are strict, and with it exactness of the completed complex, which is the sheafiness theorem itself.

Work on completed tensor products has started. The [tensor topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.tensorTopology) on `B ⊗_A C`, for adic structure maps, [makes it a Huber ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.isHuberRing_tensorTopology) with adic coprojections, and it [does not depend on the choice of pairs of definition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.TensorDatum.topology_eq). Behind this is the fact that [an adic homomorphism is adic for every pair of definition of its source](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/AdicHom/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsAdicHom.exists_pairOfDefinition_map_eq). The window also added completions of products and a [dense-range form of noetherianity for complete rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Noetherian.html#Ideal.isNoetherianRing_of_isAdicComplete_of_denseRange).

## AdicSpacesPartII: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-08 (`e8ea0d1` to `d2083f0`)

The main result of this window is the [completed tensor product of Huber pairs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Pair.html#TauCeti.Huber.Pair.completedTensor) along adic maps. It comes with its plus ring and [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Pair.html#TauCeti.Huber.Pair.completedTensor.existsUnique_lift), functoriality, and [cancellation of base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.Huber.CompletedTensor.cancelBaseChange) `(B ⊗̂_A C) ⊗̂_C D ≃ B ⊗̂_A D`. For adic rings it [agrees with the adic completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Adic.html#TauCeti.Huber.CompletedTensor.equivAdicCompletion). Base change of a restricted power series presentation [has the expected kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Presentation.html#TauCeti.Huber.CompletedTensor.ker_baseChangePresentation). Around it, the uniform theory arrived in full: the [spectral seminorm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TateGauge/Spectral/Seminorm.html#TauCeti.Huber.spectralSeminorm) of a Tate ring, its [spectral topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TateGauge/Spectral/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.SpectralTop), which [has the same adic spectrum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/SpectralTop.html#Subring.comap_toSpectralTop_preimage_spa), the [uniformization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TateGauge/Spectral/Uniformization.html#TauCeti.Huber.Pair.uniformization) and the [uniform completed tensor product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Uniform.html#TauCeti.Huber.Pair.uniformCompletedTensor). Separately, Banach modules got a [completed tensor product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/BanachModule/CompletedTensor.html#TauCeti.Huber.BanachModule.CompletedTensor) with its universal property.

The first morphism classes of adic spaces landed. A morphism is [adic exactly when it preserves analytic points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Morphisms/Adic.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.isAdic_iff_mapsTo_analyticPoints) (Huber's Proposition 3.2), and [quasi-compact exactly when its underlying map is spectral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Morphisms/QuasiCompact.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.quasiCompact_iff_isSpectralMap). There are also [morphisms of weakly finite type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Morphisms/WeaklyFiniteType.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.IsWeaklyFiniteType) and [locally noetherian adic spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PreAdicSpace/LocallyNoetherian.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.IsLocallyNoetherian). At ring level, [noetherian type ascends along maps topologically of finite type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/TopologicallyFiniteType.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType.of_isTopologicallyFiniteType) (Huber's Corollary 3.4). The [module of continuous differentials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Kaehler/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.ContinuousKaehlerDifferential) is defined. Smaller lines of work began on [Pro-categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Pro/Basic.html#TauCeti.Pro), [limits of spectral spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/Limits/Basic.html#TopCat.spectralSpace_limitCone) and [quasicompact objects of a topos](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Topos/Quasicompact.html#TauCeti.Topos.IsQuasicompact).

Huber's sheafiness theorem for a noetherian ring of definition is still not proved. The Čech differentials now respect Huber's filtration, but the Artin–Rees strictness argument has not landed. Nothing has started on analytification.

## AdicSpacesPartII: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-11 (`d2083f0` to `1c6a1a4`)

Fibre products of affinoid adic spaces are now in the library. For adic maps of sheafy complete Huber pairs, [`Spa` of the completed tensor product is the fibre product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Pullback/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.affinoid_isPullback) in adic spaces whenever that tensor product is sheafy (Huber's Proposition 3.7, Wedhorn's Theorem 8.56), and [sheafiness is automatic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Pullback/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.affinoid_isPullback_of_isStablySheafyRing_left) when one factor is stably sheafy and the other structure map is topologically of finite type. Underneath it is [Huber's bijection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.ofPairHomEquiv) between morphisms of complete affinoid adic spaces and morphisms of Huber pairs, which supplies the morphism of adic spaces induced by a map of pairs. This work (TauCeti#27665) landed before the window and is recorded here late, together with [morphisms locally of +weakly finite type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Morphisms/PlusWeaklyFiniteType.html#TauCeti.AdicSpace.LocallyOfPlusWeaklyFiniteType), the fact that [completed localisations commute with completed tensor products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Rational.html#TauCeti.Huber.CompletedTensor.completionLocEquiv) as topological rings, and [projectivity of the blow-up of a finitely generated submodule](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Submodule/Projective.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.submoduleBlowup.isProjectiveOver).

Within the window, [the adic spectrum of a directed colimit of Huber pairs is the inverse limit of the adic spectra of the stages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/DirectLimit.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spaDirectLimitHomeomorph), and [each of its rational subsets comes from a single stage](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/DirectLimit.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.exists_spaComap_preimage_rationalSubset). The hypotheses are those of a filtered colimit of Tate pairs with compatible rings of definition, but that colimit pair is not constructed and the completed form is not stated. The third part of Huber's Lemma 2.3 reached its final form: [completing a finite module is completed scalar extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/CompletedScalars.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.completionTensorHomeomorph) as a topological module over the completed ring. The rest was supporting algebra for the amalgamation of valuation rings and revisions to spectral-space lemmas.

Huber's sheafiness theorem for a noetherian ring of definition is still not proved; the Artin–Rees strictness step is the gap. Fibre products exist only for affinoids, and the analytification layer has no functor yet.
