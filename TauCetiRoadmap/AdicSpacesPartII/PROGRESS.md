## AdicSpacesPartII: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-19 (`4e9057a` to `9fc5d5a`)

The noetherian half of Huber's sheafiness argument got its ring-level inputs. A ring complete for a finitely generated ideal `I` is noetherian once `R ⧸ I` is ([`Ideal.isNoetherianRing_of_isAdicComplete`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Noetherian.html#Ideal.isNoetherianRing_of_isAdicComplete), EGA 0 7.2.6), the completion of a noetherian ring for an adic topology is noetherian ([`IsAdic.isNoetherianRing_completion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Completion/Noetherian.html#IsAdic.isNoetherianRing_completion), EGA 0 7.3.1), and so the completion of a Huber ring of noetherian type is again of noetherian type ([`IsNoetherianType.completion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Completion/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType.completion)). Most of Huber's Lemma 2.3 followed. Over a Huber ring with a noetherian ring of definition, every linear map of finite modules is strict ([`isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Strict.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing), via an Artin-Rees statement for finitely generated submodules). When the ring is also complete, finite modules are Hausdorff and their submodules are closed ([`isClosed_of_isNoetherianRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Complete.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isClosed_of_isNoetherianRing)). Completeness of the modules themselves is not among the new results. Separately, completion was shown to preserve strict exact sequences of nonarchimedean groups ([`AddMonoidHom.exact_completion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Nonarchimedean/Completion/Strict.html#AddMonoidHom.exact_completion)), which is the Bourbaki lemma the Čech argument needs.

The vocabulary of the roadmap also arrived. There are Huber rings of noetherian type, [Huber's standing class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/HuberStandard.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsHuberStandard), and [adic homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/AdicHom/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsAdicHom). The adic homomorphisms come with the expected closure properties, the Tate and discrete cases, and adicness of topologically finite type maps (TauCeti#17275). Adic rings of finite ideal type ([`IsAdicRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicRing/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAdicRing)) came with the EGA facts on ideals of definition and their radicals. Locally spectral spaces and maps ([`IsLocallySpectralSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/LocallySpectral.html#TauCeti.IsLocallySpectralSpace)) came with their comparison to spectral spaces and closure under composition.
