## AdicSpacesPartII: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`4e9057a` to `c1c6773`)

The first work on this roadmap lays the ring-level ground for Huber's sheafiness theorem in the case of a noetherian ring of definition, without yet proving it. Three of the inputs Huber's proof calls for are now in place. The [Čech complex of localizations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Cech.html#TauCeti.cechComplexLocalization_exact) of a module is exact when the elements generate the unit ideal. [Completion preserves strict exact sequences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Nonarchimedean/Completion/Strict.html#AddMonoidHom.exact_completion) of nonarchimedean groups. And Huber's Lemma 2.3 holds over a Huber ring with a noetherian ring of definition: [linear maps of finite modules are strict](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Strict.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing), and over a complete base [submodules are closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Complete.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isClosed_of_isNoetherianRing). An Artin–Rees statement for finitely generated submodules and Huber's lemma on the spectrum of an open subring (TauCeti#19147) fill out the toolkit.

The classes the rest of the roadmap is stated in also arrived: [Huber rings of noetherian type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType), [Huber's standing class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/HuberStandard.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsHuberStandard), [adic homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/AdicHom/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsAdicHom) and [adic rings of finite ideal type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicRing/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAdicRing). Noetherian type is preserved by [completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Completion/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType.completion) and by quotients, and in a complete ring of this type [every ideal is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/Quotient.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType.isClosed_ideal). The underlying algebra is the EGA statement that [a complete ring is noetherian when its quotient by a finitely generated ideal of definition is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Noetherian.html#Ideal.isNoetherianRing_of_isAdicComplete). On the topological side, [locally spectral spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/LocallySpectral.html#TauCeti.IsLocallySpectralSpace) and locally spectral maps now have their basic theory.

Nothing geometric has landed yet. There are no adic morphism classes, no completed tensor products and no fibre products, and the analytification layer has not been started.

## AdicSpacesPartII: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`c1c6773` to `e8ea0d1`)

This window brought the sheafiness proof for rings with a noetherian ring of definition most of the way to its last step. The third part of Huber's Lemma 2.3 is now proved: over a Huber ring with a noetherian ring of definition, [completing a finite module is the same as base change to the completed ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.ofTensorProduct_bijective), and the comparison map [is a homeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isHomeomorph_ofTensorProduct) (TauCeti#19533). The blow-up step that the last report listed as missing now has its geometry. The [blow-up of a submodule](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Submodule.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.submoduleBlowup), `Proj(⊕ Jⁿ Tⁿ)`, is [proper](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Submodule.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.submoduleBlowup.isProper_toSpec) and noetherian over the base, and its standard opens are [the spectra of the chart rings `C[F/S]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Submodule.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.submoduleBlowup.basicOpenIsoSpec).

The Čech complex of rational localizations also has its topology now. [Huber's filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianCech/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.NoetherianCech.hasBasis_nhds_zero_filtration), read off the chart rings, is a basis of neighbourhoods of zero on each term, and the differentials are continuous. The [completed complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianCech/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.NoetherianCech.completedTerm) is defined, and its differentials [compose to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianCech/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.NoetherianCech.completedDifferential_comp_differential). Two things are still missing: the Artin–Rees argument that the algebraic differentials are strict, and with it exactness of the completed complex, which is the sheafiness theorem itself.

Work on completed tensor products has started. The [tensor topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.tensorTopology) on `B ⊗_A C`, for adic structure maps, [makes it a Huber ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.isHuberRing_tensorTopology) with adic coprojections, and it [does not depend on the choice of pairs of definition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/Topology.html#TauCeti.Huber.TensorDatum.topology_eq). Behind this is the fact that [an adic homomorphism is adic for every pair of definition of its source](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/AdicHom/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsAdicHom.exists_pairOfDefinition_map_eq). The window also added completions of products and a [dense-range form of noetherianity for complete rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Noetherian.html#Ideal.isNoetherianRing_of_isAdicComplete_of_denseRange).
