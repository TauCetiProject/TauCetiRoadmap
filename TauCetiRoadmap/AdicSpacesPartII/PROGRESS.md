## AdicSpacesPartII: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`4e9057a` to `c1c6773`)

The first work on this roadmap lays the ring-level ground for Huber's sheafiness theorem in the case of a noetherian ring of definition, without yet proving it. Three of the inputs Huber's proof calls for are now in place. The [Čech complex of localizations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Cech.html#TauCeti.cechComplexLocalization_exact) of a module is exact when the elements generate the unit ideal. [Completion preserves strict exact sequences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Nonarchimedean/Completion/Strict.html#AddMonoidHom.exact_completion) of nonarchimedean groups. And Huber's Lemma 2.3 holds over a Huber ring with a noetherian ring of definition: [linear maps of finite modules are strict](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Strict.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isStrictMap_of_isNoetherianRing), and over a complete base [submodules are closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Complete.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isClosed_of_isNoetherianRing). An Artin–Rees statement for finitely generated submodules and Huber's lemma on the spectrum of an open subring (TauCeti#19147) fill out the toolkit.

The classes the rest of the roadmap is stated in also arrived: [Huber rings of noetherian type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType), [Huber's standing class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/HuberStandard.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsHuberStandard), [adic homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/AdicHom/Basic.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsAdicHom) and [adic rings of finite ideal type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicRing/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAdicRing). Noetherian type is preserved by [completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Completion/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType.completion) and by quotients, and in a complete ring of this type [every ideal is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/NoetherianType/Quotient.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsNoetherianType.isClosed_ideal). The underlying algebra is the EGA statement that [a complete ring is noetherian when its quotient by a finitely generated ideal of definition is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Noetherian.html#Ideal.isNoetherianRing_of_isAdicComplete). On the topological side, [locally spectral spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/LocallySpectral.html#TauCeti.IsLocallySpectralSpace) and locally spectral maps now have their basic theory.

Nothing geometric has landed yet. There are no adic morphism classes, no completed tensor products and no fibre products, and the analytification layer has not been started.
