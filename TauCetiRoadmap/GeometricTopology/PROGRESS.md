## GeometricTopology: 2026-06-20 to 2026-09-01 (`7285899` to `8745177`)

Almost all of it is substrate. The **boundary of a manifold with boundary is a manifold** one dimension down ([`TauCeti.isManifold_boundary`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Charts.html#TauCeti.isManifold_boundary)), for the Euclidean half-space model, and every boundary point has a smooth **local collar** ([`TauCeti.IsProductCollarChart.diffeomorphProd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Collar/Diffeomorph.html#TauCeti.IsProductCollarChart.diffeomorphProd)); the global collar, gluing and connected sum have not begun. The **PL groupoid** ([`TauCeti.PLGroupoid`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PLGroupoid.html#TauCeti.PLGroupoid)) exists, with its inclusion into the continuous groupoid. **Smooth embeddings are locally flat** ([`TauCeti.IsLocallyFlat.of_isSmoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/Smooth.html#TauCeti.IsLocallyFlat.of_isSmoothEmbedding)), boundaryless case only, and in codimension one **local flatness is local bicollaring** ([`TauCeti.isLocallyFlat_iff_isEmbedding_and_isLocallyBicollared`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/Bicollar.html#TauCeti.isLocallyFlat_iff_isEmbedding_and_isLocallyBicollared)), the local half of Brown's theorem. Isotopy and ambient isotopy were defined once in general, and self-diffeomorphisms form a group ([`TauCeti.Diff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Group.html#TauCeti.Diff)) with the reference inclusion **O(n+1) → Diff(Sⁿ)** ([`TauCeti.orthogonalToDiffSphere`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Sphere.html#TauCeti.orthogonalToDiffSphere)); with no topology on the group, nothing homotopy-theoretic is stateable yet.

Knot theory began with algebra: the braid group as the type-A Artin–Tits group ([`TauCeti.BraidGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/Braid.html#TauCeti.BraidGroup)), its **Burau representation** ([`TauCeti.KnotTheory.burau`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Burau/Basic.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.burau)) unreduced and reduced, the **Jones representation** into the Temperley–Lieb algebra ([`TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.jones`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/TemperleyLieb.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.jones)), and Markov equivalence with the component count as its first invariant. The **Alexander polynomial of a Seifert matrix** ([`TauCeti.KnotTheory.alexander`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Alexander.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.alexander)) is symmetric, invariant under congruence and enlargement, and gives the trefoil and figure-eight their expected values. A smooth circle presentation exists ([`TauCeti.SmoothCircleEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/SmoothCircle.html#TauCeti.SmoothCircleEmbedding)), but there are no diagrams, no Seifert surface of a knot, no Reidemeister or Markov theorem, no slice-ness; Dehn surgery has only its slope bookkeeping ([`TauCeti.FramedBoundaryTorus.slopeEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/DehnSurgery/Slope.html#TauCeti.FramedBoundaryTorus.slopeEquiv)).

The simplicial layer is the most developed. Abstract simplicial complexes have a geometric realization, the **boundary of the standard simplex realizes to a sphere** ([`AbstractSimplicialComplex.realizationStandardSuccSimplexBoundaryHomeomorphSphere`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/Simplex/BoundarySphere.html#AbstractSimplicialComplex.realizationStandardSuccSimplexBoundaryHomeomorphSphere)), settling that acceptance check, and **barycentric subdivision preserves the realization** ([`AbstractSimplicialComplex.barycentricSubdivisionRealizationHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/Subdivision/Homeomorph.html#AbstractSimplicialComplex.barycentricSubdivisionRealizationHomeomorph)). Links, stellar subdivision, **combinatorial manifolds** ([`PreAbstractSimplicialComplex.IsCombinatorialManifold`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/CombinatorialManifold.html#PreAbstractSimplicialComplex.IsCombinatorialManifold)), triangulability and collapse (a finite cone collapses to its apex, [`PreAbstractSimplicialComplex.IsCone.collapsesTo_point`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/Collapse/Cone.html#PreAbstractSimplicialComplex.IsCone.collapsesTo_point)) are in place. The reconciliation with the chart-based PL groupoid and every Kirby-list statement remain ahead; a premature quotient of ambient-isotopy classes was removed again in TauCeti#810.

## GeometricTopology: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-19 (`8745177` to `d834065`)

Knot theory got its diagrams. Gauss codes and PD codes are now first-class types, based and unbased, oriented, unoriented and framed, with the conversion from one to the other and a count of components (TauCeti#5849, TauCeti#5945), and on top of them the **Kauffman bracket**, the state sum over the `2ⁿ` smoothings of a diagram ([`TauCeti.PDCode.kauffmanBracket`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Kauffman.html#TauCeti.PDCode.kauffmanBracket)). It behaves as it should: mirroring inverts the variable, a kink multiplies the bracket by `-a³` ([`kauffmanBracket_kink`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Kauffman.html#TauCeti.PDCode.kauffmanBracket_kink)), and after the writhe correction the **first Reidemeister move leaves it alone** ([`normalizedKauffmanBracket_reidemeisterOne`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/OrientedReidemeisterOne.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.normalizedKauffmanBracket_reidemeisterOne)). Only that move exists so far; without Reidemeister II and III the bracket is not yet a link invariant, and there is no Jones polynomial.

The first theorem separating two knots also landed. The Alexander polynomial read off the unreduced Burau representation changes only by a unit under a Markov move ([`associated_burauAlexander`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Burau/Alexander.html#TauCeti.MarkovEquiv.associated_burauAlexander)), and that suffices to show the trefoil braid is not Markov equivalent to the trivial one-strand braid ([`not_markovEquiv_sigma_pow_three_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Burau/Alexander.html#TauCeti.not_markovEquiv_sigma_pow_three_one), TauCeti#6033). Signatures came with it: the signature of a Seifert matrix survives enlargement and is `-2` for the trefoil and `0` for the figure eight, and the **Tristram-Levine signature** ([`tristramLevineSignature`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/TristramLevine.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.tristramLevineSignature)) is congruence-invariant, additive under connected sum, negated by mirroring, and equal to the classical signature at `ω = -1`.

The manifold side was mostly statements and closure properties. Global collars have a definition and an API ([`TauCeti.IsCollar`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Collar/Global.html#TauCeti.IsCollar)) but no existence theorem, so gluing stays blocked; the **annulus conjecture** ([`TauCeti.AnnulusConjecture`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/Annulus.html#TauCeti.AnnulusConjecture)) and **Zeeman's conjecture** ([`TauCeti.ZeemanConjecture`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/Zeeman.html#TauCeti.ZeemanConjecture)) are stated, not proved. The weak Whitney topology arrived for maps from a manifold into a normed space ([`chartWeakWhitneyTopology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Jet.html#ContMDiffMap.chartWeakWhitneyTopology)), which does not yet reach the diffeomorphism group.

## GeometricTopology: 2026-09-19 to 2026-09-26 (`d834065` to `759eb3e`)

**Riemannian volume is now an isometry invariant.** The chart densities `√det g`, which change by the Jacobian of the coordinate change, glue into one measure ([`TauCeti.riemannianVolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Volume.html#TauCeti.riemannianVolume)); a differentiable homeomorphism whose tangent maps preserve the metric carries one such measure onto the other, with no orientation hypothesis ([`Homeomorph.measurePreserving_riemannianVolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Isometry.html#Homeomorph.measurePreserving_riemannianVolume)), and so preserves the total volume of a compact manifold ([`TauCeti.riemannianTotalVolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Total.html#TauCeti.riemannianTotalVolume)). Curvature became a smooth tensor field satisfying the **first Bianchi identity** for torsion-free connections ([`CovariantDerivative.curvatureTensor_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Bianchi.html#CovariantDerivative.curvatureTensor_cyclic_eq_zero)), skew-adjoint for metric connections, and contracting to Ricci and scalar curvature, the latter `n(n-1)κ` on the constant-curvature model. Sectional curvature, and with it hyperbolic metrics, has not started.

The diffeomorphism group got its topology. Maps into a manifold carry the weak Whitney topology of compact chart-derivative tests ([`ContMDiffMap.manifoldWeakWhitneyTopology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Topology.html#ContMDiffMap.manifoldWeakWhitneyTopology)), diffeomorphisms of a compact manifold inherit it, and a jointly smooth family of diffeomorphisms becomes a continuous map into them ([`Diffeomorph.ofSmoothFamily`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Topology.html#Diffeomorph.ofSmoothFamily)). So a diffeotopy is a path from the identity, and the **inclusion O(n+1) → Diff(Sⁿ) is continuous** ([`TauCeti.continuous_orthogonalToDiffSphere`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Sphere.html#TauCeti.continuous_orthogonalToDiffSphere)); composition and inversion are not yet shown continuous. **Smooth concordance** of embeddings arrived in collared form and is an equivalence relation for a finite-dimensional ambient model ([`Concordant.equivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothEmbedding/Concordance.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.Concordant.equivalence)), with ambient isotopic embeddings concordant; there is no concordance group yet.

Smaller pieces: the Tristram-Levine signature is unchanged by row and column enlargement ([`tristramLevineSignature_enlargeColumn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/TristramLevine/Enlargement.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.tristramLevineSignature_enlargeColumn)), so with congruence it survives every move generating S-equivalence; adding a crossing-free circle to a PD code multiplies the Kauffman bracket by the loop value; and the Jones units satisfy the braid relations behind the third Reidemeister move, which is not yet a move on diagrams. The **triangulation conjecture** is stated and proved in dimension zero ([`TauCeti.TriangulationConjecture`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/TriangulationConjecture.html#TauCeti.TriangulationConjecture)), so Manolescu's theorem can now be written as its failure from dimension five on. The remaining pull requests tidied the immersion, Temperley-Lieb and Kauffman-bracket files.

## GeometricTopology: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

Two Kirby-list theorems can now be stated. [Smale's conjecture](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Smale.html#TauCeti.SmaleConjecture) is written in its sharp form: the specific inclusion `O(4) → Diff(S³)`, not just some map, is a homotopy equivalence for the weak Whitney topology. The [virtual fibering conjecture](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/VirtualFibering.html#TauCeti.VirtualFiberingConjecture), Agol's theorem, says every closed hyperbolic 3-manifold has a finite cover that fibers over the circle. Neither is proved. Around Smale, the diffeomorphisms fixing any set pointwise form a [closed subgroup](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/FixingSubgroup/Topology.html#Diffeomorph.isClosed_fixingSubgroup), which supplies the relative group `Diff(M, ∂M)`, and the action of `Diff(M)` on `M` is [jointly continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Evaluation.html#Diffeomorph.applyContinuousSMul). Composition and inversion are still not shown continuous.

Agol's statement pulled most of the rest of the Riemannian layer after it. [Sectional curvature](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Sectional.html#CovariantDerivative.sectionalCurvature) arrived along with constant sectional curvature. A bundled [hyperbolic metric](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.HyperbolicMetric) is a complete metric of constant curvature `-1`, and it is [proved to have sectional curvature `-1`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.HyperbolicMetric.hasConstantSectionalCurvature). Its total volume is [`hypVolumeOfMetric`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/Volume.html#TauCeti.hypVolumeOfMetric). Mostow invariance, which would make that volume independent of the metric, is not there, and neither is the Weeks manifold. On the topological side there are [mapping tori](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MappingTorus.html#TauCeti.MappingTorus), finite covers, incompressible surfaces and a possibly nonorientable [Haken predicate](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Haken.html#TauCeti.IsPossiblyNonorientableHakenThreeManifold).

Smaller results. The [Tristram-Levine signature is invariant under S-equivalence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/TristramLevine/SEquivalence.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.IntegralSquareMatrix.SEquivalent.tristramLevineSignature_eq), which is now an equivalence relation. Layer 10 has its first object, a [foliation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Foliation.html#TauCeti.Foliation) given as an involutive distribution, and the standard contact distribution is [shown not to be involutive](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution.html#TauCeti.not_isInvolutiveDistribution_standardContactDistribution). Layer 1 gained the [normal space](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothEmbedding/NormalSpace.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.NormalSpace) of a smooth embedding and a [tubular-neighbourhood](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/TubularNeighborhood.html#TauCeti.IsTubularNeighborhood) interface, but no existence theorem yet. It also has an explicit collar, though only for the boundary of the half-space. The rest extended the PD-code API, adding unlinks, a positive kink, and the fact that mirroring, reversal and relabelling commute.

## GeometricTopology: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (`e440f4e` to `8a32441`)

Knot diagrams can now tell whether they are drawn in the plane. A PD code has faces, traced by turning counterclockwise at each crossing and running along the arc, and a code is [planar](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Planar.html#TauCeti.PDCode.IsPlanar) when each component of its graph embeds in the sphere. This comes with Euler's formula for the underlying graph ([`eulerChar_toPermutationTriple`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Planar.html#TauCeti.PDCode.eulerChar_toPermutationTriple)) and an example showing that [not every PD code is planar](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Planar.html#TauCeti.PDCode.exists_not_isPlanar). With faces available, the second Reidemeister move exists as [clasp insertion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ClaspInsertion.html#TauCeti.PDCode.insertClasp). Inserting the clasp inside a face [preserves planarity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ClaspInsertion.html#TauCeti.PDCode.isPlanar_insertClasp_iff), and inserting it across two faces destroys it. The Kauffman bracket has not yet been shown invariant under this move, and the third move does not exist. Braid words now [close up to oriented PD codes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/BraidWord/PDCode.html#TauCeti.BraidWord.closure), with each crossing taking the sign of its letter. This is the first map from braids to diagrams, though nothing yet compares Markov moves with diagram moves. The right-handed trefoil's [normalized bracket](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Trefoil.html#TauCeti.normalizedKauffmanBracket_rightHandedTrefoilPDCode) is computed as `A⁻⁴ + A⁻¹² − A⁻¹⁶`.

On the manifold side, [composition of diffeomorphisms is jointly continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Composition.html#Diffeomorph.continuousMul) for the weak Whitney topology on a compact manifold. The proof goes through the continuity of [Taylor composition](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/ContDiff/FaaDiBruno.html#Filter.Tendsto.taylorComp) in the Faà di Bruno formula. Inversion is still open, so `Diff(M)` is not yet a topological group. [Topological concordance](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/Concordance/Basic.html#TauCeti.TopologicalConcordance) of locally flat embeddings arrived in the same collared form as the smooth version. It is [an equivalence relation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/Concordance/Basic.html#TauCeti.TopologicallyConcordant.equivalence), and [smoothly concordant embeddings are topologically concordant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/Concordance/Smooth.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.Concordant.exists_topologicallyConcordant), though only when the manifolds have no boundary. The closed unit ball is now a manifold with boundary, and [its boundary is the sphere](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/ClosedBall.html#TauCeti.boundary_closedBall).

## GeometricTopology: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-04 (`8a32441` to `41e5e49`)

The [Jones polynomial](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Jones.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.jonesPolynomial) of an oriented diagram is now defined. It is the writhe-normalized Kauffman bracket rewritten in `t^(1/2) = A⁻²`, and it is [unchanged by the third Reidemeister move](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Jones.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.jonesPolynomial_reidemeisterThree) for every height order of the three strands, as well as by the first move and by clasp insertion, which within a face is the second. The right-handed trefoil gets [`t + t³ − t⁴`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Jones.html#TauCeti.jonesPolynomial_rightHandedTrefoilPDCode). On the braid side, a Markov trace on Temperley-Lieb gives a [Markov-invariant Jones trace](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/TemperleyLieb.html#TauCeti.MarkovEquiv.jonesTrace_eq) that agrees with the diagram computation on the trefoil. Diagrams also have an Alexander module and [elementary ideals](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Alexander/Basic.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.elementaryIdeal), but these are shown invariant only under the first move. Reidemeister's theorem is still missing, so none of this is yet an invariant of knots.

`Diff(M)` of a compact manifold [is a topological group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Inversion.html#Diffeomorph.isTopologicalGroup), now that inversion is continuous, and [Watanabe's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Disc.html#TauCeti.WatanabeTheorem) is stated. Brown's [collaring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Collar/Brown.html#TauCeti.IsLocallyCollared.isCollared) and [bicollaring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/TwoSided.html#TauCeti.IsLocallyBicollaredWithSides.exists_isBicollar) theorems are proved for compact domains. Smoothly slice knots are [topologically slice](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Slice/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsSmoothlySlice.isTopologicallySlice), and slice genus is defined.

Layer 8 began. The [eight Thurston geometries](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/ModelGeometry/Basic.html#TauCeti.ModelGeometry) form an enumeration, with explicit metrics on Nil, Sol and the universal cover of `SL₂ℝ`. A [geometric structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/GeometricStructure/Basic.html#TauCeti.HasGeometricStructure) is a quotient `X/Γ`. Lens spaces have [fundamental group `ℤ/m`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/UniversalCover/LensSpace/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.LensSpace.fundamentalGroupMulEquiv) and are spherical. Seifert fibrations are defined. The [geometrization conjecture](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Geometrization.html#TauCeti.GeometrizationConjecture) is stated as the existence of a torus decomposition, without the canonical JSJ decomposition. Mostow rigidity and the virtual Haken conjecture are also stated. Foliations gained leaves and tautness, and polygonal knots gained Δ-moves and local flatness.
