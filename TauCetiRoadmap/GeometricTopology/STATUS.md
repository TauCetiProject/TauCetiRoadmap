## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 3 is done: `Diff(M)` is a topological group, and the Smale and Watanabe statements are written. Every other layer except 9 is partial. Knot theory and locally flat embeddings are furthest along: both the Jones polynomial and the Alexander module of a diagram are now invariant under Reidemeister moves. Dehn filling, gluing and the concordance group have not begun, and neither has Layer 9.

### Named results

- **The Jordan–Brouwer separation theorem**: the complement of an embedded `n`-sphere in `Sⁿ⁺¹` has exactly two path components. It is proved with singular homology ([`natCard_zerothHomotopy_sphere_compl_range_eq_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/JordanBrouwer.html#TauCeti.natCard_zerothHomotopy_sphere_compl_range_eq_two)).
- **Brown's bicollaring theorem for spheres**: a locally flat `n`-sphere in `Sⁿ⁺¹` whose complement is disconnected is globally bicollared ([`IsLocallyFlat.isBicollared_of_not_isPreconnected_compl_range`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/Sphere.html#TauCeti.IsLocallyFlat.isBicollared_of_not_isPreconnected_compl_range)). Jordan–Brouwer discharges the disconnectedness hypothesis.
- **Reidemeister invariance of the Jones polynomial**: the Jones polynomial of an oriented PD code is unchanged by all three Reidemeister moves ([`OrientedPDCode.jonesPolynomial_reidemeisterThree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Jones.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.jonesPolynomial_reidemeisterThree)).
- **Reidemeister invariance of the Alexander module**: Reidemeister-equivalent diagrams have isomorphic Alexander modules over `ℤ[T, T⁻¹]` ([`ReidemeisterEquiv.nonempty_alexanderModule_linearEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Alexander/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.ReidemeisterEquiv.nonempty_alexanderModule_linearEquiv)). The same holds for every [elementary ideal](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Alexander/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.ReidemeisterEquiv.elementaryIdeal_eq).
- **`Diff(M)` is a topological group**: for a compact manifold, composition and inversion are continuous in the weak Whitney topology ([`Diffeomorph.isTopologicalGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Inversion.html#Diffeomorph.isTopologicalGroup)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Tubular neighbourhoods in Euclidean space** ([`exists_isOpenEmbedding_normalTubeOfRadius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/TubularNeighborhood/Noncompact.html#TauCeti.exists_isOpenEmbedding_normalTubeOfRadius)): every `C²` embedded submanifold, compact or not, has an open tube of varying radius, and its normal bundle is a [vector bundle](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/TubularNeighborhood/VectorBundle.html#TauCeti.normalContMDiffVectorBundle). This is what puts surgery within reach. Submanifolds of a general manifold are not yet covered.
- **Solid torus neighbourhoods and knot exteriors** ([`exists_isSolidTorusNeighborhood_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/SolidTorusNeighborhood/Sphere.html#TauCeti.exists_isSolidTorusNeighborhood_sphere), [`knotExterior`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/SolidTorusNeighborhood/Exterior.html#TauCeti.knotExterior)): a `C²` knot in `S³` has a compact exterior whose frontier is the boundary torus. Dehn filling will glue along that torus.
- **The eight Thurston geometries** ([`ModelGeometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/ModelGeometry/Basic.html#TauCeti.ModelGeometry)): together with geometric structures as quotients `X/Γ`, they make [geometrization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Geometrization.html#TauCeti.GeometrizationConjecture) and the [JSJ theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/JSJDecomposition.html#TauCeti.JSJTheorem) stateable.

### Roadmap coverage

- **Layer 3** is done.
- **Layer 2** is done except for local flatness of general PL embeddings.
- **Layer 4** has the Jones polynomial and the Alexander module invariant under all diagram moves. Braid closures respect every Markov move except free cancellation. It lacks Reidemeister's and Markov's theorems, the projection of polygonal knots to diagrams, and HOMFLY.
- **Layer 1** has local and abstract collars and Euclidean tubular neighbourhoods. It has no boundary collars for general manifolds, no tubular neighbourhoods in a general ambient manifold, and no gluing, handles or connected sum.

- **Layer 10** has foliations, leaves and tautness, but no Euler class.

- **Layer 9** is untouched.

## The frontier

- **Free cancellation on braid closures**: what remains is the Reidemeister equivalence of the closures. The crossing order and the successor structure after inserting an inverse pair are already computed. Once this lands, Markov-equivalent braids will have equivalent closures.
- **The knot exterior as a manifold**: it is compact and its frontier is the boundary torus. It still has to become a compact manifold with torus boundary. Dehn filling then also needs Layer 1's gluing.
- **HOMFLY**: the Jimbo weighted trace satisfies the skein relation and is conjugation-invariant. The crossing half of Markov stabilization is still missing.
- **Combinatorial manifolds are manifolds**: the local tools are in place, including the punctured-star product, radial extension of link homeomorphisms, and joins of spheres. What remains is to show that every vertex star of a combinatorial manifold is a ball, and then to prove the reconciliation with the PL groupoid.
- **PL embeddings are locally flat**: affine graphs are [locally flat](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/PL.html#ContinuousAffineMap.isLocallyFlat_affineGraph). What remains is the general statement for PL embeddings, stated against Layer 1's PL groupoid.
