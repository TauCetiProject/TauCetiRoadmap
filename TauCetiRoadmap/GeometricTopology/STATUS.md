## The frontier

- **Global collars and boundary-piece gluing** — Prove collar existence for general manifold boundaries and construct smooth gluing, including corners along partial seams; handle attachment and Dehn filling need this interface.
- **Dehn filling and the unknot identities** — Glue the framed exterior to a solid torus along a chosen slope, then identify the zero, infinite and rational unknot fillings.
- **Thom classes of oriented plane bundles** — Assemble the local product-bundle classes into global classes for nontrivial bundles, making foliation Euler classes available beyond the trivial case.
- **Heegaard splitting existence** — Establish a splitting for every closed orientable 3-manifold, using a triangulation or Morse-function construction, so genus is defined without an extra existence hypothesis.
- **Whitehead's converse** — Construct a combinatorial triangulation from a PL atlas to complete the equivalence between the chart-based and simplicial notions.
