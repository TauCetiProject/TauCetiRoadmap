## The frontier

- **General boundary gluing** — prove global collar existence and construct gluing along boundary pieces, including corners; handles, Dehn filling and cobordism composition need this operation.
- **Thom classes for oriented plane bundles** — extend existence beyond trivial bundles so foliation Euler classes are available without an extra Thom-class hypothesis.
- **Geometric knots and diagrams** — construct generic projections and prove the correspondence between ambient isotopy and Reidemeister moves; the converse Markov correspondence also remains.
- **Heegaard splitting existence** — prove every closed orientable 3-manifold admits a splitting, using triangulation or Morse theory, to remove the existence hypothesis from its genus.
- **Whitehead's converse** — construct a combinatorial triangulation of every chart-based PL manifold, completing the comparison with the link condition.
