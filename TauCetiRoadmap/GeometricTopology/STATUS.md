## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Two layers are complete: the topology of compact-manifold diffeomorphism groups (Layer 3) and the statements of the Thurston geometries and decompositions (Layer 8). The other nine are partial. Smooth connected sum is fully in place. General boundary gluing, Dehn filling, the concordance group and smooth triangulation existence are not.

### Named results

- **The sphere identity for connected sum** — For connected, Hausdorff, oriented smooth n-manifolds without boundary and n ≥ 1, connected sum with Sⁿ is diffeomorphic to the original manifold ([`TauCeti.OrientedConnectedSum.nonempty_diffeomorph_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ConnectedSum/Oriented.html#TauCeti.OrientedConnectedSum.nonempty_diffeomorph_sphere)).
- **The Schoenflies theorem** — Every planar Jordan curve is the image of the unit circle under a homeomorphism that also carries the disc onto its inside ([`TauCeti.IsJordanCurve.exists_homeomorph_image_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Jordan/Schoenflies.html#TauCeti.IsJordanCurve.exists_homeomorph_image_sphere)).
- **The degree inequality for simplicial volume** — A map carrying one real fundamental class to d times another satisfies |d|‖N‖ ≤ ‖M‖ ([`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.abs_mul_simplicialVolume_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/LocalHomology/SimplicialVolume.html#TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.abs_mul_simplicialVolume_le)).
- **Combinatorial manifolds are PL manifolds** — The realization of a positive-dimensional combinatorial manifold has a half-space atlas with PL transition maps ([`exists_chartedSpace_hasGroupoid_PLGroupoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/CombinatorialManifold/PL.html#PreAbstractSimplicialComplex.IsCombinatorialManifold.exists_chartedSpace_hasGroupoid_PLGroupoid)).
- **Markov invariance of the HOMFLY polynomial** — The polynomial does not change under Markov moves on braids. Transporting it to diagrams is unfinished ([`MarkovEquiv.homfly_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Homfly/Existence.html#TauCeti.MarkovEquiv.homfly_eq)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Well-defined oriented connected sum** — The result does not depend on the choice of orientation-compatible coordinate balls, for connected, Hausdorff, σ-compact smooth manifolds without boundary. Constructions can therefore be compared up to diffeomorphism ([`TauCeti.ConnectedSum.nonempty_diffeomorph_of_isOrientationReversingAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ConnectedSum/Independence.html#TauCeti.ConnectedSum.nonempty_diffeomorph_of_isOrientationReversingAt)).
- **Secant approximation of star immersions** — On every fine enough edgewise subdivision, the secant map of an injective C¹ star immersion of a finite complex is a closed embedding. This is the approximation step the Cairns–Whitehead theorem needs ([`TauCeti.IsStarImmersion.eventually_isClosedEmbedding_secantMap_edgewiseSubdivision`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothTriangulation/StarImmersion.html#TauCeti.IsStarImmersion.eventually_isClosedEmbedding_secantMap_edgewiseSubdivision)).
- **Cohomology of product Thom pairs** — Top-degree relative cohomology is identified with maps out of the base's zeroth homology. This gives the local data for building global Thom classes ([`TauCeti.prodThomPairSingularCohomologyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/ProdThomPair.html#TauCeti.prodThomPairSingularCohomologyEquiv)).

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 3 and 8 are done. In Layer 8 that means the model geometries and decomposition statements are available, not that the classification theorems are proved. Layers 1 and 5 are partial: connected sum is established, but general collars, gluing along boundary pieces, handles and Dehn filling are missing. Layer 2 reaches [local flatness of PL curves](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/PL.html#TauCeti.IsLocallyFlat.of_isPLMap); higher-dimensional PL embeddings are open. Layers 4 and 6 have their invariants and concordance foundations. Layer 4 still needs the passage from geometric knots to diagrams and the converse Markov theorem; Layer 6 needs the concordance group and the cobordism category. Layer 7 lacks a proof that hyperbolic volume is independent of the metric, and a concrete Weeks manifold. Layer 9 lacks the existence of splittings in general. Layer 10 needs Thom classes for nontrivial oriented plane bundles. Layer 11 has collapse, the forward PL comparison, subdivision machinery, secant approximation and a PL statement of [Zeeman's conjecture](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/Zeeman.html#TauCeti.ZeemanConjecture). It lacks the Cairns–Whitehead theorem, Whitehead's converse, the dimension-two and dimension-three triangulation theorems, and the dunce hat.

## The frontier

- **The Cairns–Whitehead theorem** — Build a smooth triangulation of every smooth manifold from the secant approximations now available. It would also give one route to Heegaard splittings of every closed orientable 3-manifold.
- **Global collars and gluing along boundary pieces** — Prove that collars exist for general manifold boundaries, and construct smooth gluing, including corners along partial seams. Handle attachment depends on this interface.
- **Dehn filling and the unknot identities** — Glue the framed exterior to a solid torus along a chosen slope, then identify the zero, infinite and rational fillings of the unknot. This needs the gluing above.
- **Thom classes of oriented plane bundles** — Assemble the local product-bundle classes into global classes for nontrivial bundles, so that the Euler classes of foliations exist beyond the trivial case.
- **Whitehead's converse and the dunce hat** — Construct a combinatorial triangulation from a PL atlas. Separately, prove that the dunce hat is contractible but not collapsible, and that its cylinder satisfies Zeeman's conjecture.
