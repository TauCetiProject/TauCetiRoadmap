## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Two layers are complete: the topology of diffeomorphism groups (Layer 3), and the Thurston geometries with the decomposition statements (Layer 8). The other nine are partial. Brown's generalized Schoenflies theorem, smooth connected sum and Zeeman's dunce-hat case are in place. General boundary gluing, the concordance group, Reidemeister's theorem and smooth triangulations of every manifold are not.

### Named results

- **The generalized Schoenflies theorem**: for m ≥ 2, one homeomorphism of Sᵐ carries a bicollared (m−1)-sphere onto the equator and the closures of its two complementary domains onto the closed hemispheres ([`TauCeti.IsBicollar.exists_homeomorph_eq_equatorInclusion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Schoenflies/Standard.html#TauCeti.IsBicollar.exists_homeomorph_eq_equatorInclusion)). In the plane no bicollar is needed: every Jordan curve is the image of the unit circle under a homeomorphism that also carries the disc onto its inside ([`TauCeti.IsJordanCurve.exists_homeomorph_image_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Conformal/Jordan/Schoenflies.html#TauCeti.IsJordanCurve.exists_homeomorph_image_sphere)).
- **The sphere identity for connected sum**: for connected, Hausdorff, oriented smooth n-manifolds without boundary and n ≥ 1, the connected sum with Sⁿ is diffeomorphic to the original manifold ([`TauCeti.OrientedConnectedSum.nonempty_diffeomorph_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ConnectedSum/Oriented.html#TauCeti.OrientedConnectedSum.nonempty_diffeomorph_sphere)).
- **The real projective plane embeds in ℝ⁴**: the Veronese map into symmetric trace-free 3×3 matrices, followed by stereographic projection from a pole off its image, is a smooth embedding ([`Projectivization.isSmoothEmbedding_projectivePlaneEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Projectivization/ProjectivePlane.html#Projectivization.isSmoothEmbedding_projectivePlaneEmbedding)).
- **Zeeman's theorem for the dunce hat**: the dunce hat is contractible but not collapsible, while its ordered cylinder is collapsible, the first nontrivial instance of Zeeman's conjecture ([`TauCeti.collapsible_orderedCylinder_dunceHat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/DunceHat.html#TauCeti.collapsible_orderedCylinder_dunceHat)).
- **Combinatorial manifolds are PL manifolds**: the realization of a positive-dimensional combinatorial manifold has a half-space atlas with PL transition maps ([`exists_chartedSpace_hasGroupoid_PLGroupoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialComplex/CombinatorialManifold/PL.html#PreAbstractSimplicialComplex.IsCombinatorialManifold.exists_chartedSpace_hasGroupoid_PLGroupoid)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Approximation of star immersions**: on every fine enough edgewise subdivision, the secant map of an injective C¹ star immersion is a closed embedding ([`eventually_isClosedEmbedding_secantMap_edgewiseSubdivision`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothTriangulation/StarImmersion.html#TauCeti.IsStarImmersion.eventually_isClosedEmbedding_secantMap_edgewiseSubdivision)), and the embedding can be made rectilinear wherever a cut-off function is one ([`eventually_isStarImmersion_and_injective_lineMap_secantMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothTriangulation/Perturbation.html#TauCeti.IsStarImmersion.eventually_isStarImmersion_and_injective_lineMap_secantMap)). These are the approximation steps the Cairns–Whitehead theorem needs.
- **Property P and Property R**: both are stated for knots ([`TauCeti.HasPropertyP`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/DehnSurgery/NontrivialKnot.html#TauCeti.HasPropertyP)). With the Kronheimer–Mrowka and Gabai theorems taken as hypotheses, simply connected surgeries and S¹ × S² zero-surgeries detect the unknot.
- **Cohomology of product Thom pairs**: top-degree relative cohomology is identified with maps out of the base's zeroth homology, the local data for global Thom classes ([`TauCeti.prodThomPairSingularCohomologyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/ProdThomPair.html#TauCeti.prodThomPairSingularCohomologyEquiv)).

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 3 and 8 are done; in Layer 8 that means the model geometries and decomposition statements, not the classification theorems. Layer 1 has connected sum (well defined and associative up to diffeomorphism), tubular neighbourhoods, disc bundles and the embedding of ℝP² in ℝ⁴; it lacks general collars, gluing along boundary pieces, handles, and embeddings of all compact surfaces in ℝ⁴. Layer 2 now has the generalized Schoenflies theorem for m ≥ 2 and local flatness of PL curves; it lacks higher-dimensional PL embeddings and the low-dimensional annulus theorem. Layer 4 has polynomial invariants of closed braids, including Markov invariance of HOMFLY, but not Reidemeister's theorem, the converse Markov theorem or HOMFLY on diagrams. Layer 5 has Dehn fillings and the Property P and R statements, but not Lickorish–Wallace or the cyclic surgery theorem. Layer 6 has sliceness, caps and signatures invariant under algebraic concordance, but not the concordance group. Layer 7 has the degree inequality for simplicial volume but not metric-independence of hyperbolic volume or a concrete Weeks manifold. Layer 9 lacks a Heegaard splitting of every closed orientable 3-manifold, Layer 10 lacks Thom classes of nontrivial plane bundles, and Layer 11 lacks Cairns–Whitehead, the Radó and Moise theorems, Bing's house and the step from Zeeman to Poincaré.

## The frontier

- **The Cairns–Whitehead theorem**: assemble a smooth triangulation of every smooth manifold from the secant approximation, perturbation and rectilinearization lemmas now available. It would also open a route to Heegaard splittings of every closed orientable 3-manifold.
- **Reidemeister's theorem**: connect polygonal knots to diagrams. Inscribed polygons, Δ-move invariance of Gauss codes and kinks as first Reidemeister moves are in place; the theorem itself is not.
- **Global collars and gluing along boundary pieces**: prove collars exist for general boundaries and construct smooth gluing along partial seams. Handle attachment, and the connected sums of surface embeddings in ℝ⁴, depend on this.
- **The annulus theorem in low dimensions**: the roadmap proves the cases n + 1 ≤ 3 from Schoenflies. Dimension three also needs Moise's triangulation theorem from Layer 11.
- **Thom classes of oriented plane bundles**: assemble the local product classes into global classes for nontrivial bundles, so that Euler classes of foliations exist beyond the trivial case.
