## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 3 is done: `Diff(M)` of a compact manifold is a topological group, and the Smale and Watanabe statements are written, though neither is proved. Every other layer except 9 is partial. Knot theory (layer 4) is the deepest of them, and layer 8 has opened with the eight geometries and a statement of geometrization. Layer 9 has not begun.

### Named results

- **Reidemeister invariance of the Jones polynomial**: the Jones polynomial of an oriented PD code is unchanged by the third Reidemeister move, for every height order of the three strands ([`OrientedPDCode.jonesPolynomial_reidemeisterThree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Jones.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.jonesPolynomial_reidemeisterThree)). It is also unchanged by the first move and by clasp insertion, which within a face is the second.
- **`Diff(M)` is a topological group**: for a compact Hausdorff manifold with a locally compact model, composition and inversion are continuous in the weak Whitney topology ([`Diffeomorph.isTopologicalGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Inversion.html#Diffeomorph.isTopologicalGroup)).
- **Brown's bicollaring theorem**: an injective map from a compact space that is locally bicollared, with two disjoint open sides, has a global bicollar ([`IsLocallyBicollaredWithSides.exists_isBicollar`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LocallyFlat/TwoSided.html#TauCeti.IsLocallyBicollaredWithSides.exists_isBicollar)). The one-sided version is [Brown's collaring theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Collar/Brown.html#TauCeti.IsLocallyCollared.isCollared).
- **The fundamental group of a lens space**: `L(m; ℓ₀, …, ℓₖ)` with `k ≥ 1` has fundamental group `ℤ/m` ([`LensSpace.fundamentalGroupMulEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/UniversalCover/LensSpace/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.LensSpace.fundamentalGroupMulEquiv)). So the order `m` is a topological invariant, and three-dimensional lens spaces are [spherical manifolds](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/GeometricStructure/Basic.html#TauCeti.LensSpace.hasGeometricStructure_spherical).
- **Isometry invariance of Riemannian volume**: a metric-preserving diffeomorphism carries one Riemannian volume onto the other ([`Homeomorph.measurePreserving_riemannianVolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Isometry.html#Homeomorph.measurePreserving_riemannianVolume)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The weak Whitney topology on diffeomorphisms** ([`Diffeomorph.weakWhitneyTopology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Topology.html#Diffeomorph.weakWhitneyTopology)) is the space where the Smale and [Watanabe](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Disc.html#TauCeti.WatanabeTheorem) statements live.
- **The eight Thurston geometries** ([`ModelGeometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/ModelGeometry/Basic.html#TauCeti.ModelGeometry)) form a closed enumeration of concrete simply connected Riemannian models. With [geometric structures](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/GeometricStructure/Basic.html#TauCeti.HasGeometricStructure) as quotients `X/Γ`, they make Thurston's geometrization stateable.
- **The Alexander module of a diagram** ([`OrientedPDCode.AlexanderModule`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/Alexander/Basic.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.AlexanderModule)) has Fitting ideals as its elementary ideals. For the trefoil, the first of them is generated by `t − 1 + t⁻¹`. This is the diagram route to the Alexander polynomial.

### Roadmap coverage

## The frontier

- **Alexander invariance under the second and third moves**: the Alexander module is invariant under the first Reidemeister move and under relabelling. The other two moves would give the diagram Alexander polynomial, to compare with Burau and Seifert.
- **Reidemeister's theorem**: polygonal knots now have Δ-moves and [combinatorial equivalence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Polygon/DeltaMove.html#TauCeti.SimplePolygon.CombinatoriallyEquivalent), but no projection to PD codes. Until there is one, the Jones polynomial is an invariant of diagrams up to moves, not yet of knots.
- **Brown's theorem for spheres**: a locally flat `(n−1)`-sphere in `Sⁿ` should be bicollared. The bicollaring theorem needs the two complementary sides from Jordan–Brouwer separation. The first homological step toward that is the [acyclicity of the complement of a cube in a sphere](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/CubeComplement.html#TauCeti.isZero_reducedSingularHomologyFunctor_sphere_compl_range_cube).
- **Collars of manifold boundaries, and gluing**: Brown's collaring theorem still has to be applied to the boundary of a compact manifold. Gluing is needed for handles, connected sum, Dehn filling, the concordance group and Heegaard splittings.
- **Metric-independent hyperbolic volume**: [Mostow rigidity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/Mostow.html#TauCeti.MostowRigidity) is stated but not proved. The volume of a manifold, as opposed to that of a metric, still has to be derived from it, and the Weeks manifold still has to be constructed.
