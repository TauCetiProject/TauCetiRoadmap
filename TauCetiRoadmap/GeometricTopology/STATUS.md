### Named results

- **Isometry invariance of Riemannian volume** — a differentiable homeomorphism whose tangent maps preserve the metric carries one Riemannian volume onto the other, with no orientation hypothesis ([`Homeomorph.measurePreserving_riemannianVolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Isometry.html#Homeomorph.measurePreserving_riemannianVolume)). So total volume is an isometry invariant of compact Riemannian manifolds ([`TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.riemannianTotalVolume_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Total.html#TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.riemannianTotalVolume_eq)).
- **The Burau-Alexander polynomial is a Markov invariant** — it changes only by a unit under either Markov move ([`TauCeti.MarkovEquiv.associated_burauAlexander`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Burau/Alexander.html#TauCeti.MarkovEquiv.associated_burauAlexander)). This shows that the trefoil braid `σ₀³` is not Markov equivalent to the trivial braid ([`TauCeti.not_markovEquiv_sigma_pow_three_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Burau/Alexander.html#TauCeti.not_markovEquiv_sigma_pow_three_one)).
- **S-equivalence invariance of the Tristram-Levine signature** — the signature of a Seifert matrix is unchanged under S-equivalence, now an equivalence relation of its own ([`TauCeti.KnotTheory.IntegralSquareMatrix.SEquivalent.tristramLevineSignature_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/TristramLevine/SEquivalence.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.IntegralSquareMatrix.SEquivalent.tristramLevineSignature_eq)). At `ω = -1` it gives `-2` for the trefoil and `0` for the figure eight ([`TauCeti.KnotTheory.signature_trefoilSeifertMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Signature.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.signature_trefoilSeifertMatrix)).
- **Continuity of O(n+1) → Diff(Sⁿ)** — the reference inclusion is continuous for the weak Whitney topology ([`TauCeti.continuous_orthogonalToDiffSphere`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Sphere.html#TauCeti.continuous_orthogonalToDiffSphere)), which is what the statement of [Smale's conjecture](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Smale.html#TauCeti.SmaleConjecture) needs.
- **Smooth concordance is an equivalence relation** — concordance of smooth embeddings is stored in collared form so that concordances stack, and it is an equivalence relation for a finite-dimensional ambient model ([`TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.Concordant.equivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothEmbedding/Concordance.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.Concordant.equivalence)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The weak Whitney topology on diffeomorphisms** ([`Diffeomorph.weakWhitneyTopology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Topology.html#Diffeomorph.weakWhitneyTopology)) is the space layer 3's homotopy statements live in. It has a jointly continuous action and closed pointwise-fixing subgroups ([`Diffeomorph.closedFixingSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/FixingSubgroup/Topology.html#Diffeomorph.closedFixingSubgroup)), which give the relative group `Diff(M, ∂M)`.
- **Hyperbolic metrics** ([`TauCeti.HyperbolicMetric`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.HyperbolicMetric)) are bundled complete metrics of constant curvature `-1`. Bundling keeps the metric as data, so it can be integrated to a volume ([`TauCeti.hypVolumeOfMetric`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/Volume.html#TauCeti.hypVolumeOfMetric)), which is the quantity Mostow invariance and the Weeks target speak about.
- **Virtually fibered spaces** ([`TauCeti.IsVirtuallyFibered`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VirtuallyFibered.html#TauCeti.IsVirtuallyFibered)) are built from mapping tori and finite covers, and they make Agol's theorem stateable as the [virtual fibering conjecture](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/VirtualFibering.html#TauCeti.VirtualFiberingConjecture).

## The frontier

- **`Diff(M)` as a topological group** — composition and inversion must be shown continuous, the latter needing an inverse-function estimate. Watanabe's statement also needs homotopy groups of `Diff(D⁴, ∂)`.
- **Mostow invariance of hyperbolic volume** — `hypVolumeOfMetric` exists, but a metric-independent `hypVolume` needs Mostow's theorem in dimension at least 3. The Weeks target further needs the Weeks manifold constructed, by Dehn filling (blocked on layer 5) or by face-pairings.
- **Reidemeister II and III on PD codes** — PD codes carry no planar or face data, so these moves cannot yet be stated. They would make the normalised bracket a link invariant and give the Jones polynomial.
- **The collar and tubular-neighbourhood theorems** — `IsCollar` and `IsTubularNeighborhood` have no producer beyond the half-space and zero-section models. These theorems are the missing input for gluing, and through gluing for handles, connected sum, Dehn filling and Heegaard splittings.
- **Seifert matrices of knots** — the signatures are S-equivalence invariants of matrices, but no knot yet produces a Seifert matrix. That construction is what would connect them to concordance.
