### Named results

- **Isometry invariance of Riemannian volume** — a differentiable homeomorphism whose tangent maps preserve the metric carries one Riemannian volume onto the other, with no orientation hypothesis ([`Homeomorph.measurePreserving_riemannianVolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Isometry.html#Homeomorph.measurePreserving_riemannianVolume)), so the total volume of a compact Riemannian manifold ([`TauCeti.riemannianTotalVolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Total.html#TauCeti.riemannianTotalVolume)) is an isometry invariant.
- **Continuity of O(n+1) → Diff(Sⁿ)** — the reference inclusion is continuous for the weak Whitney topology ([`TauCeti.continuous_orthogonalToDiffSphere`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Sphere.html#TauCeti.continuous_orthogonalToDiffSphere)), and the smooth-families map makes any jointly smooth family of diffeomorphisms a continuous map into the space of diffeomorphisms ([`Diffeomorph.ofSmoothFamily`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Topology.html#Diffeomorph.ofSmoothFamily)).
- **Smooth concordance is an equivalence relation** — concordance of smooth embeddings, stored in collared form so that concordances stack, is an equivalence relation for a finite-dimensional ambient model ([`TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.Concordant.equivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothEmbedding/Concordance.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.Concordant.equivalence)); knot concordance is the case of circle embeddings.
- **The Burau-Alexander polynomial is a Markov invariant** — it changes only by a unit under either Markov move ([`TauCeti.MarkovEquiv.associated_burauAlexander`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Burau/Alexander.html#TauCeti.MarkovEquiv.associated_burauAlexander)), which shows that the trefoil braid `σ₀³` is not Markov equivalent to the trivial braid ([`TauCeti.not_markovEquiv_sigma_pow_three_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Burau/Alexander.html#TauCeti.not_markovEquiv_sigma_pow_three_one)).
- **The Tristram-Levine signature of a Seifert matrix** — unchanged by congruence and by row and column enlargement, the moves that generate S-equivalence ([`TauCeti.KnotTheory.tristramLevineSignature_enlargeColumn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/TristramLevine/Enlargement.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.tristramLevineSignature_enlargeColumn)); at `ω = -1` it is the classical signature, `-2` for the trefoil and `0` for the figure eight ([`TauCeti.KnotTheory.signature_trefoilSeifertMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Signature.html#TauCeti.KnotTheory.signature_trefoilSeifertMatrix)).

## The frontier

- **Stating the Smale conjecture** — the inclusion `O(4) → Diff(S³)` is now continuous, so its being a homotopy equivalence can be written down. Making `Diff(M)` a topological group also needs continuous composition and inversion, the latter needing an inverse-function estimate.
- **Reidemeister II and III on PD codes** — the bracket already survives algebraic clasp insertion, but PD codes carry no planar face data, so the moves cannot yet be stated; they would make the normalised bracket a link invariant and give the Jones polynomial.
- **Sectional curvature and hyperbolic metrics** — sectional curvature and constant curvature `-1` are what a hyperbolic metric and its volume need next. Mostow invariance is a separate theorem, and the Weeks target also needs the Weeks manifold constructed.
- **The collar theorem** — `IsCollar` still has no producer. Existence of a global collar is the missing input for gluing, and through it for handles, connected sum, Dehn filling and cobordism composition.
- **Seifert matrices of knots** — the Alexander polynomial and the signatures are invariants of Seifert matrices and braids, while concordance lives on smooth embeddings; a Seifert matrix built from a knot would connect them, as a concordance-invariant signature requires.
