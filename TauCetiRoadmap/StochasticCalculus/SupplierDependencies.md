# Shared stochastic and point-process contracts

The [PointProcesses proposal](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/pull/417)
owns the generic marked point-process theory. Its contracts consumed here include
the following extensions. These are theorem and construction targets at that
owner, using the native measure and filtration APIs.

| Owner and contract | Inputs and output used by StochasticCalculus |
|---|---|
| Layer 0, point-measure measurability | The carrier is locally finite integer-valued measures; its measurable structure is generated by measurable count evaluations. Prove the measurable-map criterion used to turn an everywhere-defined càdlàg counting realization into a point-measure-valued random variable. |
| Layer 4, `isPoissonPointProcess_of_Ioc_counts` | A measurable `ξ : Ω → PointMeasure ℝ≥0`, a.s. zero mass at the origin, and independent Poisson counts with the specified homogeneous intensity on every finite disjoint family of bounded `(s,t]` intervals imply `IsPoissonPointProcess` with that intensity. Prove the generating-ring extension and limiting arguments. Include zero intensity; finite-arrival and exponential-gap conclusions require positive intensity. |
| Layers 4 and 10, ground measure and event indices | Construct `eraseMarks` and `groundTemporal`, their measurable/count identities and adaptedness of restrictions. Prove `eventTime N n = nthPoint (eraseMarks N) (n+1)`; the Layer 4 index zero is an origin sentinel. |
| Layer 10, history and compensator | Construct the raw count-generated internal history and prove adaptedness of the restricted measure, then its ambient-null augmentation using SC S0.3. `IsCompensator` includes adaptedness, measurable random marked measure, predictable cumulative coordinates and the nonnegative predictable-integral identity. Prove uniqueness and the finite-horizon-integrable compensated-integral martingale theorem. |
| Layer 11, forward adapted-Poisson compensator | Given measurable `N : Ω → TemporalPointMeasure E`, its underlying measure-valued `IsAdaptedPoisson P F N μ`, and `h_local : ∀ K, IsCompact K → μ (K ×ˢ univ) < ∞`, prove `IsCompensator P F N (fun _ => ⟨μ, h_local⟩)`. SC S0.6 supplies these exact inputs in the homogeneous Unit-mark case. |

The forward compensator theorem is distinct from reverse Watanabe. Sigma-finite
master Poisson measures have their measure-valued predictable Campbell theorem;
finite-ground carrier lifting occurs after restriction to finite mark bands.
The explicit hazard construction uses initial data in the stopped-history code,
an infinite terminal wait/cemetery mark, and an ambient-null-augmented history.
Pointwise raw stopped-field identities require everywhere time simplicity;
completed conditional-integral identities use their stated a.s. qualifications.
These hazard and band results remain PointProcesses contracts, rather than extra
hypotheses of the homogeneous specialization.

The construction order separates definitions from existence theorems:

1. SC S0.3 constructs generic ambient-null and usual augmentation from native
   filtration theory. `RawLocalization.lean` imports native modules only.
2. PointProcesses constructs ground maps, histories and the compensator predicate.
   Its explicit hazard and forward Poisson routes use these foundations and do
   not assume scalar Doob–Meyer or marked-batch existence.
3. SC S0.6 supplies homogeneous Unit-mark and filtration adapters. SC S2.5
   constructs scalar Doob–Meyer and the counting-process/Stieltjes dictionary,
   consuming the earlier ground map and compensator predicate.
4. PointProcesses' finite-horizon-integrable marked-batch existence consumes that
   scalar theorem. It retains the complete ambient measure, right-continuous
   filtration, null-at-zero, adaptedness and expected finite count hypotheses.

## Financial consumers

FinancialMathematics M0.1 consumes SC S9.4's generic progressive-coefficient linear
SDE; M0.1–M0.4 own trading, admissibility, completeness, replication and pricing.
MarketImpact consumes those financial contracts for its endogenous market models.
The generic SDE is not supplied by a financial model.

FinancialMathematics M0.4 consumes S6.2's full Gaussian exponential expectation
and its truncated-exponential identity
`E_x[exp(αB_t−α²t/2)1_{B_t<a}]=exp(αx)Φ((a−x−αt)/√t)`
for `t>0` and real `α`, with `Φ` the CDF of `gaussianReal 0 1`.
Reflection and complements supply the upper-tail call event. Its call/put theorem
uses `σ>0`, positive spot, strike and remaining maturity; the zero-volatility
asset remains covered by M0.1 and the generic SDE theorem. StandardDistributions
owns the Gaussian CDF identities. These interfaces identify the financial
consumers; asset modelling, strategies and pricing remain their contracts.

## Lean normalization adapter

`PointProcessesAdapter.lean` follows SC Suggested and proves its half-line measure,
finite-horizon restriction and predictable-measure trim equal to the common
`Measure.comap ((↑) : ℝ≥0 → ℝ) volume` formula. PointProcesses uses this same
transparent convention; equality with its named definitions follows by unfolding
that convention. This supplies the normalization used by the Poisson
stochastic-integral specialization. The shared point-process construction and
compensator contracts above remain owned by PointProcesses.
