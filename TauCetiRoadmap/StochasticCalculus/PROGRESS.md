## StochasticCalculus: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`156cbc0` to `aa6e43e`)

The roadmap's jump example now has its filtration-relative form: a [Poisson process of rate α relative to a filtration](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Filtered.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPoissonProcess) starts at zero, is adapted, and has Poisson increments independent of the past (TauCeti#38584). Its first martingales follow: the [compensated process N_t − αt](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Martingale.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPoissonProcess.martingale_compensated) and its [compensated square](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Martingale.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPoissonProcess.martingale_compensated_sq_sub) (N_t − αt)² − αt are martingales. Around the definition sit the moments, the passage to smaller filtrations and null augmentations, the fact that the [future increment process](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Filtered.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPoissonProcess.shift) is again Poisson, and a [converse](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Filtered.html#ProbabilityTheory.HasIndepIncrements.isFilteredPoissonProcess_natural) from independent Poisson increments to the natural filtration. Existence, path regularization and the jump-time and waiting-time laws have not landed.

For the local Itô integral, the [energy localizer](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/EnergyLocalizer.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.energyLocalizer) stops an integrand when a repaired, continuous square-energy clock reaches a level. On the raw filtration, with no usual conditions, these times form a [localizing sequence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Integral/Energy/Localization.html#TauCeti.LocallyBrownianIntegrable.isLocalizingSequence_energyLocalizer) for any locally Brownian-integrable integrand, and each stopped cutoff is [square-integrable against Brownian motion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Integral/Energy/Localization.html#MeasureTheory.IsStronglyPredictable.brownianIntegrable_ite_le_energyLocalizer) (TauCeti#36063). The integral process itself is still to be built.

[Pointwise predictable times](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Predictable/StoppingTime.html#TauCeti.IsPredictableTime), defined by everywhere-finite announcing sequences, got their elementary closure properties, including [countable suprema](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Predictable/StoppingTime.html#TauCeti.IsPredictableTime.iSup), and the predictable stochastic intervals between them. This is input to the predictable section theorem, which has not landed. Smaller additions: Brownian motion's [conditional second moment](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Martingale.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.condExp_sq), and [nested partitions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/EMetricSpace/PartitionVariation.html#TauCeti.exists_nested_partitions_tendsto_eVariationOn) whose variations increase to the total variation.

## StochasticCalculus: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`aa6e43e` to `b235000`)

Two Layer 6 headlines landed. The strong Markov property says that at an almost surely finite stopping time, the restarted increment path of a filtered Brownian motion with continuous paths [is again a Brownian motion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/StrongMarkov.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.isBrownianReal_stoppedValue_shift) and [is independent of the stopped σ-algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/StrongMarkov.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.indep_stoppedValue_shift). The roadmap's `brownian_strongMarkov` target asks for exactly this, and there are versions for the natural filtration and its right-continuous hull. The Paley–Wiener–Zygmund theorem says that on one full-measure event the path is [differentiable at no time t ≥ 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/NowhereDifferentiable.html#ProbabilityTheory.IsPreBrownianReal.ae_not_differentiableAt), with no right derivative at 0. It needs only the Brownian finite-dimensional laws, not path continuity. The same work adds the fixed-time Dini oscillation, nowhere-monotone paths, and Gaussian small-ball bounds.

Layer 0's measurability theory grew. The [debut theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Debut.html#MeasurableSet.isStoppingTime_debut) says the debut of a progressive set is a stopping time when the filtration is right-continuous and complete. The [optional σ-algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Filtration/Optional.html#MeasureTheory.Filtration.optional) arrived, sitting between the predictable and progressive σ-algebras. The [Souslin operation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/MeasurableSpace/Souslin.html#TauCeti.souslinOperation) is the first piece of the engine behind the predictable section theorem; the capacity argument and the section itself have not landed. Random variables modulo null sets now have a [wrapper type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/L0/Basic.html#TauCeti.L0) whose metric gives convergence in measure and is [complete](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/L0/Complete.html#TauCeti.L0.instCompleteSpace).

Other work prepares two missing summits. For Wiener measure, Brownian finite-dimensional laws are [realised on every countable time set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Countable.html#TauCeti.exists_measurePreserving_brownianRealProjectiveFamily_isKolmogorovProcess_of_countable) with Kolmogorov moment bounds, and an almost surely continuous process has [exactly one law on continuous paths](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/PathLaw/Continuous.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_continuousMap_measure_forall_finset_map_restrict_eq) with its finite-dimensional laws. The Kolmogorov–Chentsov step joining them is still open. For Doob–Meyer, processes of [class DL](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Martingale/ClassD.html#TauCeti.IsClassDL) are defined, and discrete compensators of a class-DL submartingale are [uniformly integrable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Martingale/DoobMeyer/UniformIntegrable.html#TauCeti.IsClassDL.uniformIntegrable_predictablePart). The decomposition itself has not landed.
