## StochasticCalculus: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`156cbc0` to `aa6e43e`)

The roadmap's jump example now has its filtration-relative form: a [Poisson process of rate α relative to a filtration](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Filtered.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPoissonProcess) starts at zero, is adapted, and has Poisson increments independent of the past (TauCeti#38584). Its first martingales follow: the [compensated process N_t − αt](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Martingale.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPoissonProcess.martingale_compensated) and its [compensated square](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Martingale.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPoissonProcess.martingale_compensated_sq_sub) (N_t − αt)² − αt are martingales. Around the definition sit the moments, the passage to smaller filtrations and null augmentations, the fact that the [future increment process](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Filtered.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPoissonProcess.shift) is again Poisson, and a [converse](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Filtered.html#ProbabilityTheory.HasIndepIncrements.isFilteredPoissonProcess_natural) from independent Poisson increments to the natural filtration. Existence, path regularization and the jump-time and waiting-time laws have not landed.

For the local Itô integral, the [energy localizer](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/EnergyLocalizer.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.energyLocalizer) stops an integrand when a repaired, continuous square-energy clock reaches a level. On the raw filtration, with no usual conditions, these times form a [localizing sequence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Integral/Energy/Localization.html#TauCeti.LocallyBrownianIntegrable.isLocalizingSequence_energyLocalizer) for any locally Brownian-integrable integrand, and each stopped cutoff is [square-integrable against Brownian motion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Integral/Energy/Localization.html#MeasureTheory.IsStronglyPredictable.brownianIntegrable_ite_le_energyLocalizer) (TauCeti#36063). The integral process itself is still to be built.

[Pointwise predictable times](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Predictable/StoppingTime.html#TauCeti.IsPredictableTime), defined by everywhere-finite announcing sequences, got their elementary closure properties, including [countable suprema](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Predictable/StoppingTime.html#TauCeti.IsPredictableTime.iSup), and the predictable stochastic intervals between them. This is input to the predictable section theorem, which has not landed. Smaller additions: Brownian motion's [conditional second moment](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Martingale.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.condExp_sq), and [nested partitions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/EMetricSpace/PartitionVariation.html#TauCeti.exists_nested_partitions_tendsto_eVariationOn) whose variations increase to the total variation.
