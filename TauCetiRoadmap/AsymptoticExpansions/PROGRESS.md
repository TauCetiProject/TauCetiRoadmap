## AsymptoticExpansions: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`59880b1` to `aa6e43e`)

This first window brought the Euler–Maclaurin formula ([`TauCeti.sum_Icc_eq_eulerMaclaurin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Asymptotics/Sum/EulerMaclaurin.html#TauCeti.sum_Icc_eq_eulerMaclaurin)), DLMF 2.10.1: for `f` of class `C^{2m}` on `[a, n]` it writes `∑_{j=a}^n f(j)` as `∫_a^n f`, endpoint terms and Bernoulli-weighted odd derivatives, plus an exact remainder against the periodic Bernoulli function. It rests on a version of every order `k ≥ 1` ([`TauCeti.sum_Icc_eq_eulerMaclaurin_of_hasDerivAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Asymptotics/Sum/EulerMaclaurin.html#TauCeti.sum_Icc_eq_eulerMaclaurin_of_hasDerivAt)), and `f` may take values in any real Banach space. Alongside it came the discrete Volterra theorem that Layer 12 rests on ([`TauCeti.existsUnique_discrete_volterra_solution`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Asymptotics/DiscreteVolterra.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_discrete_volterra_solution)): a summation equation `h(n) = ∑_{m>n} K(n,m)(J(m) + ψ(m)h(m))` with `|K(n,m)| ≤ P(n)Q(m)` has exactly one solution that is `O(P)`, and that solution satisfies `|h(n)| ≤ P(n)Φ(n)e^{Ψ(n)}`. Some tail-sum estimates for nonnegative series were added to support it.

Four other layers got their first results. The Parseval formula for Mellin transforms ([`TauCeti.integral_mul_comp_mul_eq_mellinInv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/MellinTransform/Convolution.html#TauCeti.integral_mul_comp_mul_eq_mellinInv), DLMF 2.5.5) recovers `∫₀^∞ f(t)h(xt) dt` as the inverse Mellin transform of `𝓜f(1−z)𝓜h(z)` wherever that integral is continuous in `x`. The Gamma integral with a complex rate, `∫₀^∞ t^{s−1}e^{−zt} dt = Γ(s)z^{−s}` for `Re z > 0` ([`Complex.integral_cpow_mul_exp_neg_mul_Ioi_of_re_pos`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/IntegralComplexRate.html#Complex.integral_cpow_mul_exp_neg_mul_Ioi_of_re_pos)), starts Layer 4. If `f(x) ∼ x` is continuous and increasing, then `f(x) = y` has a unique large root, and that root is `∼ y` ([`MonotoneOn.root_isEquivalent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Asymptotics/TranscendentalEquation.html#MonotoneOn.root_isEquivalent)). The Liouville transformation ([`TauCeti.deriv_deriv_liouvilleTransform_of_normalized`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/LiouvilleTransformation.html#TauCeti.deriv_deriv_liouvilleTransform_of_normalized)) turns `w″ = (u²f + g)w` into `W″ = (u² + ψ)W`.

The foundation is still missing. Layer 1's notions of asymptotic scale and asymptotic expansion do not exist yet, so none of these results is stated as an expansion. Watson's lemma, Laplace's method and the continuous Volterra theorem have not started.
