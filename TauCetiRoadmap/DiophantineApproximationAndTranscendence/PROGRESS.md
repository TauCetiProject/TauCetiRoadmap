## DiophantineApproximationAndTranscendence: 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-19 (`9a8010f` to `a5cde7b`)

Roth's theorem landed: for a real algebraic irrational α and any κ > 2, only finitely many rationals ξ satisfy |ξ − α| ≤ H(ξ)^(−κ) ([`IsAlgebraic.finite_setOfPred_abs_sub_le_mulHeight₁_rpow_neg`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/Theorem.html#IsAlgebraic.finite_setOfPred_abs_sub_le_mulHeight₁_rpow_neg)), so every such α has [irrationality exponent exactly two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/Theorem.html#IsAlgebraic.irrationalityExponent_eq_two) (TauCeti#18176). It also comes in a version for complex algebraic α, and as the [Thue–Roth inequality for binary forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/BinaryForm.html#MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.exists_pos_mul_max_abs_rpow_le_abs_eval), |F(x, y)| ≫ max(|x|, |y|)^(d−κ) (TauCeti#18272). The proof follows Pottmeyer's notes: multivariate Hasse derivatives and a weighted index, generalized Wronskians, [Roth's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/IndexEstimate.html#TauCeti.weightedIndex_le_of_logHeight_le) bounding the index of a polynomial at a point of rapidly growing heights, and the [auxiliary polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/Auxiliary.html#TauCeti.exists_roth_auxiliary_polynomial) built from Siegel's lemma. Thue's older argument was also completed independently, ending in [Thue's approximation theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Thue/GapPrinciple.html#TauCeti.thue_approximation_real) for exponents above d/2 + 1. The corollary that Thue equations F(x, y) = m have finitely many solutions is not yet stated.

The foundations went in beneath this. The roadmap's own heights now exist: the primitive minimal polynomial, naive height, house and Mahler measure, with Northcott finiteness for each, [Kronecker's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/MahlerMeasure.html#TauCeti.mahlerMeasure_eq_one_iff) that Mahler measure one means a root of unity, and [Siegel's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SiegelsLemma.html#NumberField.exists_ne_zero_int_vec_abs_le_of_house_le) over a number field. On the approximation side the window brought Dirichlet's theorem for systems of linear forms, [Kronecker's approximation theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Kronecker.html#TauCeti.kronecker_approximation), the irrationality, linear-form, simultaneous and algebraic approximation exponents, the fact that [almost every real number has exponent two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Exponent.html#TauCeti.ae_irrationalityExponent_eq_two), and badly approximable numbers, with Littlewood's conjecture kept as a stated hypothesis.

Transcendence has started. Hermite's integral identity and estimate, formal exponential sums with Galois conjugation, and Hermite's auxiliary polynomial are in place for Lindemann–Weierstrass, but the theorem itself has not landed.

## DiophantineApproximationAndTranscendence: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25 (`a5cde7b` to `d96e9ce`)

The Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem landed. In [Baker's form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Lindemann/Theorem.html#TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp) it says that the exponentials of distinct algebraic numbers are linearly independent over the algebraic numbers. In the [algebraic independence form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Lindemann/Theorem.html#TauCeti.algebraicIndependent_exp) it says that the exponentials of ℚ-linearly independent algebraic numbers are algebraically independent (TauCeti#19623). Both are proved without extra hypotheses, by reduction to [injectivity of exponential evaluation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Lindemann/Galois.html#TauCeti.LindemannWeierstrass.expEval_injective) over a Galois number field. That step completes Hermite's argument: the auxiliary values are nonzero algebraic integers for large primes p, yet eventually smaller than 1. The ℚ-space of [algebraic logarithms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/AlgebraicLogs.html#TauCeti.algebraicLogs), with all branches, also arrived.

Work toward Gelfond–Schneider began. It has [Schneider's auxiliary function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/SchneiderAuxiliary.html#RingHom.exists_schneider_auxiliary), a [zero estimate for real exponential polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Exp/ExponentialPolynomial.html#TauCeti.card_lt_of_sum_eval_mul_exp_eq_zero) via an iterated Rolle theorem, and [Schwarz's lemma with prescribed zeros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Schwarz.html#TauCeti.norm_le_mul_prod_div_pow). The extrapolation estimates and the theorem itself are not yet in place.

Layer 2 gained its first real objects. These are the [twisted height](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Twisted.html#TauCeti.twistedHeight) over the places of a number field, [nondegenerate solutions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/UnitEquation/Nondegenerate.html#TauCeti.IsNondegenerateSolution) of unit-type equations, [block homogenization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/BlockHomogenize.html#MvPolynomial.blockHomogenize), and coprimality of Plücker coordinates of saturated lattices. There is also Gaussian-measure comparison on symmetric convex sets from Bombieri–Gubler's appendix. Elsewhere, [M(x⁻¹) = M(x)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Absolute.html#TauCeti.mahlerMeasure_inv) closes one Layer 0 loose end. The block-homogeneous jet lemmas finish §1.7, and the [square-free divisor lemma for binary forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/BinaryForm.html#MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.exists_dvd_isHomogeneous_not_sq_dvd) prepares the Thue-equation corollary, which is still unstated.

## DiophantineApproximationAndTranscendence: 2026-10-25 to 2026-10-26 (`d96e9ce` to `938acbb`)

Layer 4 has begun, with the structure of the rational S-units. For a finite set S of primes, a rational number is an S-unit exactly when it is [a signed product of powers of primes in S](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SUnits/Rat/Structure.html#TauCeti.mem_sUnits_rat_iff_eq_sign_mul_prod), and recording the sign and the p-adic valuations gives [an isomorphism with ℤˣ × ℤ^S](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SUnits/Rat/Structure.html#TauCeti.sUnitsRatMulEquiv). It follows that the group is [finitely generated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SUnits/Rat/Structure.html#TauCeti.sUnits_rat_fg) of [rank |S|](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SUnits/Rat/Structure.html#TauCeti.finrank_sUnits_rat), and the [height of an S-unit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SUnits/Rat/Structure.html#TauCeti.mulHeight₁_sUnits_rat) is the larger of its numerator and denominator, each written as a product over S (TauCeti#21337). Everything sits on Mathlib's S-unit subgroup of 𝓞 ℚ rather than a new carrier. The balanced divisors in the rest of §4.1 are not started.

On the p-adic side of Layer 3, the [one-factor estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/UnitFiltration/Pow/SubOne.html#TauCeti.exists_log_normalizedValuationWithZero_zpow_sub_one_le) landed: in a finite extension of ℚ_p, the valuation of α^b − 1 is at most C(1 + log max(1, |b|)) whenever α^b ≠ 1 (TauCeti#21326). This is the n = 1 case of Yu's bound in §3.7, proved directly by separating nonunits, roots of unity and principal units. The key input is that a power of a deep principal unit [shifts its filtration step by exactly v(n)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/UnitFiltration/Pow/Basic.html#TauCeti.pow_mem_unitFiltration_add_natCastValuation_iff). The several-factor Yu construction has not started.

## DiophantineApproximationAndTranscendence: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-06 (`938acbb` to `385ec2d`)

The Gelfond–Schneider theorem landed in its real form: for real algebraic α > 0 with α ≠ 1 and real algebraic irrational β, the number α^β is transcendental ([`TauCeti.transcendental_rpow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Theorem.html#TauCeti.transcendental_rpow)) ([TauCeti#24280](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/24280)). It follows Schneider's route, whose missing extrapolation, house and denominator bounds of Evertse's Lemma 4.27 arrived first. A second route through Gelfond's auxiliary function has its construction and a [Liouville lower bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Gelfond/Value.html#TauCeti.exists_one_le_pow_mul_norm_gelfond_value), but not the matching upper bound. [Thue's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/BinaryForm.html#MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.finite_setOf_eval_eq_of_three_le_card_roots) is now stated as well: an integer binary form with at least three distinct roots takes each nonzero value at only finitely many integer points. Two applications came with it, [binomial equations ax^n + cy^n = m](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/BinaryForm.html#TauCeti.finite_setOf_mul_pow_add_mul_pow_eq) and the worked example [y³ = 2x(x − 3)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/SuperellipticEquation/WorkedExamples.html#TauCeti.finite_setOf_pow_three_eq_two_mul_mul_sub_three). [Evertse's lower bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Denominator.html#TauCeti.one_le_natDenominator_pow_mul_house_pow_mul_norm) den(z)^d house(z)^(d−1)|z| ≥ 1 and the [continued-fraction formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/ContinuedFractions/PartialQuotients.html#Irrational.irrationalityExponent_eq_two_add_limsup) μ(ξ) = 2 + limsup log aₖ₊₁/log qₖ ([TauCeti#26180](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26180)) close Layer 0. Layer 1 is closed by the [transcendence of Σ b^(−3ᵏ)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/Lacunary.html#TauCeti.transcendental_tsum_inv_pow_pow) for b ≥ 2, proved from Roth's theorem for Σ b^(−cᵏ) with any c ≥ 3 ([TauCeti#26075](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26075)).

Layer 4 moved furthest. [Balanced divisors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Units/Balanced.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Units.boundedDivisors) give a finite set representing every divisor modulo units, and on them Thue equations reduce to unit equations with an explicit [size bound for the solutions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/UnitEquation/Thue.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Units.max_abs_le_of_eval_homogenize_eq). The exponent bounds for [unit equations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/UnitEquation/ExponentBound.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Units.max_abs_le_of_mul_add_mul_eq_one), for [Győry's finitely generated groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/UnitEquation/FinitelyGenerated.html#TauCeti.NumberField.SUnit.max_abs_le_of_mul_add_mul_eq_one), for the [rational S-unit equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/UnitEquation/RatSUnit.html#TauCeti.abs_padicValRat_le_of_add_eq_one) and for [a^m − b^n = k](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ExponentialEquation/Gap.html#TauCeti.finite_setOf_pow_sub_pow_eq) are all proved, but each takes its lower bound for linear forms in logarithms as a hypothesis. Those lower bounds are not yet proved here, so none of these exponent bounds is unconditionally effective. The Thue reduction to unit equations needs no such hypothesis.

Groundwork for them began: [mixed partial derivatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Baker/MDeriv.html#TauCeti.Baker.mDeriv) of entire functions, Baker's integer-valued Δ polynomials, Waldschmidt's [interpolation matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Waldschmidt/Interpolation/Matrix.html#TauCeti.waldschmidtInterpolationMatrix) with an upper rank bound and [denominator bounds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Waldschmidt/Interpolation/Denominator.html#Subring.mul_waldschmidtInterpolationMatrix_mem) ([TauCeti#27120](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27120)), p-adic Schwarz lemmas on integer grids, and [Taylor translation of restricted power series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Restricted/Taylor.html#PowerSeries.IsRestricted.taylor) for Yu's local estimates ([TauCeti#27164](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27164)). Layer 5 opened with [D-finite power series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/DFinite.html#PowerSeries.IsDFinite), closed under sums, products and derivatives. Layer 2 gained the [closed form of linear recurrences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/LinearRecurrence/ClosedForm.html#LinearRecurrence.isSolution_iff_exists_eq_sum_eval_mul_pow), successive infima of twisted heights, [local weights of subspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Twisted/Weight.html#TauCeti.localWeight) ([TauCeti#25481](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25481)), and the archimedean ([TauCeti#25095](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25095)) and [nonarchimedean](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ExteriorPower/Overlap.html#AbsoluteValue.iSup_plucker_overlap_le) ([TauCeti#26271](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26271)) overlap inequalities that submodularity of subspace heights will need.
