### Named results

- **Roth's theorem** — for a real algebraic irrational α and κ > 2, only finitely many rationals ξ satisfy |ξ − α| ≤ H(ξ)^(−κ) ([`IsAlgebraic.finite_setOfPred_abs_sub_le_mulHeight₁_rpow_neg`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/Theorem.html#IsAlgebraic.finite_setOfPred_abs_sub_le_mulHeight₁_rpow_neg)). The constant is not computable.
- **The Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem** — exponentials of distinct algebraic numbers are linearly independent over the algebraic numbers ([`TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Lindemann/Theorem.html#TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp)).
- **The Gelfond–Schneider theorem** — if λ ≠ 0 has e^λ algebraic and β is algebraic and irrational, then e^(βλ) is transcendental, for every branch ([`TauCeti.transcendental_exp_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Gelfond/Theorem.html#TauCeti.transcendental_exp_mul)). It is proved by Gelfond's method, and Schneider's route separately gives the [real form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Theorem.html#TauCeti.transcendental_rpow).
- **Thue's theorem** — an integer binary form whose dehomogenization has at least three distinct roots takes each nonzero value at only finitely many integer points ([`MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.finite_setOf_eval_eq_of_three_le_card_roots`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/BinaryForm.html#MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.finite_setOf_eval_eq_of_three_le_card_roots)). It is deduced from Roth and is therefore ineffective.
- **Struppeck–Vaaler submodularity** — Euclidean subspace heights satisfy H(U ∩ W) H(U + W) ≤ H(U) H(W) with no extra hypotheses ([`Submodule.subspaceHeight_inf_mul_sup_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Arakelov/Subspace/Submodularity.html#Submodule.subspaceHeight_inf_mul_sup_le)). This is the height geometry the subspace theorem is built on.

It is the starting point for the explicit lower bound that Layer 4 needs.

## The frontier

- **Corollaries of Gelfond–Schneider (§3.3).** The transcendence of e^(πα), including e^π, and the ℚ̄-independence of two ℚ-independent logarithms are now short applications of [`TauCeti.transcendental_exp_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Gelfond/Theorem.html#TauCeti.transcendental_exp_mul).
- **Wronskian degree and multiplicity bounds (§1.4).** These are deg W + ℓ(ℓ−1)/2 ≤ Σ deg gⱼ and the summed root-multiplicity bound; with the explicit gap-principle constants they would close Layer 1 again.
- **Waldschmidt's lower bound (§3.6).** The matrix, its field, its delta-polynomial basis and its denominators are in place. What remains is the multiplicity argument giving full rank, and then the measure itself. That measure would make the §§4.2–4.3 bounds unconditional.
- **Unit equations and Thue–Mahler (§2.3).** Thue–Mahler finiteness now reduces to finiteness of unit equations in finitely generated groups. Over ℚ that needs the p-adic approximation theorem of the same section.
- **Yu's several-factor bound (§3.7).** The p-adic Schwarz lemmas and the finite-difference extrapolation are Yu's first local estimates. The effective S-unit and Thue–Mahler bounds of §4.5 wait on this bound.
