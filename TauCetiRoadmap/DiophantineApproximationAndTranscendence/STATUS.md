### Named results

- **Roth's theorem** — for a real algebraic irrational α and κ > 2, only finitely many rationals ξ satisfy |ξ − α| ≤ H(ξ)^(−κ) ([`IsAlgebraic.finite_setOfPred_abs_sub_le_mulHeight₁_rpow_neg`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/Theorem.html#IsAlgebraic.finite_setOfPred_abs_sub_le_mulHeight₁_rpow_neg)), so α has [irrationality exponent two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/Theorem.html#IsAlgebraic.irrationalityExponent_eq_two). The constant is not computable.
- **The Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem** — exponentials of distinct algebraic numbers are linearly independent over the algebraic numbers ([`TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Lindemann/Theorem.html#TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp)).
- **The Gelfond–Schneider theorem** — for real algebraic α > 0 with α ≠ 1 and real algebraic irrational β, α^β is transcendental ([`TauCeti.transcendental_rpow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Theorem.html#TauCeti.transcendental_rpow)), by Schneider's auxiliary function. The complex and arbitrary-logarithm forms are left to the LinearFormsInLogarithms supplier.
- **Thue's theorem** — an integer binary form whose dehomogenization has at least three distinct roots takes each nonzero value at only finitely many integer points ([`MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.finite_setOf_eval_eq_of_three_le_card_roots`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Roth/BinaryForm.html#MvPolynomial.IsHomogeneous.finite_setOf_eval_eq_of_three_le_card_roots)), deduced from Roth and therefore ineffective.
- **The p-adic one-factor estimate** — in a finite extension of ℚ_p, v(α^b − 1) ≤ C(1 + log max(1, |b|)) whenever α^b ≠ 1 ([`TauCeti.exists_log_normalizedValuationWithZero_zpow_sub_one_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/UnitFiltration/Pow/SubOne.html#TauCeti.exists_log_normalizedValuationWithZero_zpow_sub_one_le)), the n = 1 case of Yu's bound.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Mahler measure of an algebraic number** ([`TauCeti.mahlerMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/MahlerMeasure.html#TauCeti.mahlerMeasure)), with the naive height, the house and Northcott finiteness, is where every height estimate in the roadmap starts.
- **Balanced divisors** ([`TauCeti.NumberField.Units.boundedDivisors`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Units/Balanced.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Units.boundedDivisors)) give a finite set of divisors of α that represents every divisor modulo units. This is what turns a Thue equation into finitely many unit equations.
- **Waldschmidt's interpolation matrix** ([`TauCeti.waldschmidtInterpolationMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Waldschmidt/Interpolation/Matrix.html#TauCeti.waldschmidtInterpolationMatrix)) packages the derivatives of the auxiliary exponential polynomials at grid points. It is the starting point for the explicit lower bound for linear forms in logarithms that Layer 4 is waiting for.

## The frontier

- **Waldschmidt's lower bound (§3.6).** The interpolation matrix exists with an upper rank bound. Next come the multiplicity argument giving full rank on the residual subspace, and then the measure. That measure would discharge the hypotheses in §§4.2–4.3 and make those bounds unconditional.
- **Transcendence of Σ b^(−3ᵏ) (§1.6).** This is the last Layer 1 item, and it is a direct corollary of Roth's theorem.
- **Corollaries of Gelfond–Schneider (§3.3).** The transcendence of e^(πα), including e^π, and the ℚ̄-independence of two ℚ-independent logarithms. Gelfond's second route still needs its upper bound for the derivative value.
- **Unit equations over ℚ (§2.3).** Nondegenerate solutions and the rational S-units are in place. Finiteness needs the p-adic approximation theorem of the same section.
- **Yu's several-factor bound (§3.7).** The p-adic grid Schwarz lemmas are the first of Yu's local estimates. Rational S-unit and Thue–Mahler finiteness in §4.5 wait on this bound.
