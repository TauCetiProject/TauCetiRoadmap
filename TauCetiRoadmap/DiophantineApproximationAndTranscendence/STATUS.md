### Named results

- **The Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem** — exponentials of distinct algebraic numbers are linearly independent over the algebraic numbers ([`TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Lindemann/Theorem.html#TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp)).
- **The Gelfond–Schneider theorem** — if λ ≠ 0 has e^λ algebraic and β is algebraic and irrational, then e^(βλ) is transcendental, for every branch ([`TauCeti.transcendental_exp_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Gelfond/Theorem.html#TauCeti.transcendental_exp_mul)). Its two-logarithm form, that ℚ-independent logarithms of algebraic numbers are [independent over the algebraic numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Corollaries.html#TauCeti.mul_add_mul_ne_zero_of_linearIndependent), is also proved.
- **Transcendence of Gelfond's constant** — e^π is transcendental ([`TauCeti.transcendental_exp_pi`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Corollaries.html#TauCeti.transcendental_exp_pi)), and so is [e^(πα)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Corollaries.html#TauCeti.transcendental_exp_pi_mul) for every algebraic α that is not a rational multiple of i.
- **Struppeck–Vaaler submodularity** — Euclidean subspace heights satisfy H(U ∩ W) H(U + W) ≤ H(U) H(W) with no extra hypotheses ([`Submodule.subspaceHeight_inf_mul_sup_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Arakelov/Subspace/Submodularity.html#Submodule.subspaceHeight_inf_mul_sup_le)). This is the height geometry the subspace theorem is built on.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Integral-point lattices of subspaces**, with Schmidt's covolume identity ([`Submodule.covolume_integerLattice`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Arakelov/Subspace/Covolume.html#Submodule.covolume_integerLattice)). This turns subspace heights into lattice volumes, which is what Minkowski's theorem needs for the fixed-field Siegel lemma.
- **Waldschmidt's interpolation matrix** ([`TauCeti.waldschmidtInterpolationMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Waldschmidt/Interpolation/Matrix.html#TauCeti.waldschmidtInterpolationMatrix)). ## The frontier

- **The fixed-field Siegel lemma (§2.8).** The covolume identity was its named lattice input. What remains is Minkowski's first theorem on the integral points of the kernel, which gives a nonzero solution of small height.
- **Explicit gap-principle constants (§1.1).** Restating the gap principle with the roadmap's explicit ε, C and λ would close Layer 1.
- **Waldschmidt's lower bound (§3.6).** The matrix, its field, its delta rows and columns and their denominators are in place. What remains is the multiplicity argument giving full rank, and then the measure itself. That measure would make the §§4.2–4.3 bounds unconditional.
- **Yu's several-factor bound (§3.7).** The p-adic Schwarz, extrapolation and interpolation lemmas are Yu's local inputs. The effective S-unit and Thue–Mahler bounds of §4.5 wait on this bound.
- **Unit equations and Thue–Mahler (§2.3).** Thue–Mahler finiteness reduces to finiteness of unit equations in finitely generated groups. Over ℚ, that finiteness needs the p-adic approximation theorem from the same section.
