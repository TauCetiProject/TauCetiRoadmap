- **The Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem** — exponentials of distinct algebraic numbers are linearly independent over the algebraic numbers ([`TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/Lindemann/Theorem.html#TauCeti.linearIndependent_exp)).
- **The Gelfond–Schneider theorem** — if λ ≠ 0 has e^λ algebraic and β is algebraic and irrational, then e^(βλ) is transcendental, for every branch ([`TauCeti.transcendental_exp_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Gelfond/Theorem.html#TauCeti.transcendental_exp_mul)). Its two-logarithm form, that ℚ-independent logarithms of algebraic numbers are [independent over the algebraic numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Corollaries.html#TauCeti.mul_add_mul_ne_zero_of_linearIndependent), is also proved.
- **Transcendence of Gelfond's constant** — e^π is transcendental ([`TauCeti.transcendental_exp_pi`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Corollaries.html#TauCeti.transcendental_exp_pi)), and so is [e^(πα)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Transcendental/GelfondSchneider/Corollaries.html#TauCeti.transcendental_exp_pi_mul) for every algebraic α that is not a rational multiple of i.
- **Struppeck–Vaaler submodularity** — Euclidean subspace heights satisfy H(U ∩ W) H(U + W) ≤ H(U) H(W) with no extra hypotheses ([`Submodule.subspaceHeight_inf_mul_sup_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Arakelov/Subspace/Submodularity.html#Submodule.subspaceHeight_inf_mul_sup_le)). ## The frontier

- **The fixed-field Siegel lemma (§2.8).** The covolume identity was its lattice input. What remains is Minkowski's first theorem on the integral points of the kernel, giving a nonzero solution of small height. The small-height hyperplane bound in §2.4 also consumes it.
- **Explicit gap-principle constants (§1.1).** Restating the gap principle with the roadmap's explicit ε, C and λ would close Layer 1.
- **Rank packing for unit equations (§2.4).** With the exponent seminorm in place, the next step is the bound (1 + 2C/δ₀)^r on δ₀-separated points of norm at most C. Schmidt's small-point bound and the large-solution assembly follow, and the n = 2 case would discharge the finiteness hypothesis on which Thue–Mahler finiteness now rests.
- **Waldschmidt's lower bound (§3.6).** The matrix, its field, delta rows and columns and their denominators are in place. What remains is the multiplicity argument giving full rank, and then the measure itself, which would make the §§4.2–4.3 bounds unconditional.
- **Yu's several-factor bound (§3.7).** The p-adic Schwarz, extrapolation and interpolation lemmas are its local inputs. The effective S-unit and Thue–Mahler bounds of §4.5 wait on it.
