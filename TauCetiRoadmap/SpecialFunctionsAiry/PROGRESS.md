## SpecialFunctionsAiry: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`f7a30b5` to `aa6e43e`)

The Airy roadmap has started. The Airy functions [`airyAi`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Basic.html#TauCeti.airyAi) and [`airyBi`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Basic.html#TauCeti.airyBi) are now defined through the regularized hypergeometric function, normalized as in DLMF and proved entire, and the values of each function and its derivative at the origin (DLMF 9.2.3–9.2.6) are checked against those normalizations (TauCeti#35471). Each has an everywhere convergent Maclaurin series, but [in its Gamma-denominator form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Basic.html#TauCeti.hasSum_airyAi_gamma). The Pochhammer forms of DLMF 9.4.1–9.4.4 are not proved yet, and neither is Airy's equation itself.

Ahead of Layer 7, the roadmap's auxiliary Gauss triplication formula, [Γ(z) Γ(z + ⅓) Γ(z + ⅔) = 2π 3^{½ − 3z} Γ(3z)](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/Multiplication.html#Complex.Gamma_mul_Gamma_add_third), landed for every complex `z`. It comes as the case m = 3 of the full [Gauss multiplication formula](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/Multiplication.html#Complex.prod_Gamma_add_div), which was proved over the reals by Bohr–Mollerup and extended to ℂ (TauCeti#38171). On the way came the [product of Γ(k/m)](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/Multiplication.html#Complex.prod_Gamma_div) (DLMF 5.5.7), the classical [product of sin(πk/m)](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/FiniteProduct.html#Real.prod_sin_pi_mul_div), and log-convexity of shifted, rescaled Γ.

## SpecialFunctionsAiry: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-24 (`aa6e43e` to `d25d526`)

Airy's equation is now proved. [`Ai`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Equation.html#TauCeti.airyAi_ode) and `Bi` satisfy `w″ = z w`, and their [Wronskian is `1/π`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Equation.html#TauCeti.wronskian_airyAi_airyBi). [Every entire solution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Equation.html#TauCeti.eq_airy_combination) is an explicit combination of the two, fixed by its value and derivative at the origin (TauCeti#38189). The Riccati form came with it. Factorial triplication `(3k)! = 27^k k! (⅓)_k (⅔)_k` gives the [Pochhammer Maclaurin series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Maclaurin.html#TauCeti.hasSum_airyAi) of DLMF 9.4.1 and 9.4.3, but not yet the derivative series 9.4.2 and 9.4.4. [Conjugation symmetry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Real.html#Complex.airyAi_conj) makes both functions real on the real line, and `Bi` is positive there and [tends to infinity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Real.html#Real.tendsto_airyBi_atTop). The connection formulas and the Bessel and Kummer relations are still open.

Several later layers began from this base. The Scorer function [`Hi`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Scorer.html#TauCeti.scorerHi) is defined by its Laplace integral, proved entire, and shown to [solve `Hi″ = z Hi + 1/π`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Scorer.html#TauCeti.scorerHi_ode). Products of solutions [satisfy the third-order equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/ProductEquation.html#TauCeti.productEquation_mul_of_airy_ode) of DLMF 9.11.1, with uniqueness from initial data, and they have the antiderivatives 9.11.5–9.11.11. DLMF 9.11.3, the `J₀` integral for `Ai²`, is proved only [with a Gaussian factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/SquareBessel.html#TauCeti.integral_exp_cube_add_cube_mul_exp_neg_eq_integral_besselJ) inserted; the limit as the factor tends to 1 is still missing. The asymptotic coefficients `u_k`, `v_k` and the multiplier [`χ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/Coefficients.html#Real.airyChi) landed, with 9.7.4 obtained from Wendel's inequalities for Gamma ratios.

On the zeros, the work so far is formal. Rational power series built with formal binomial powers and reversion give the coefficients of the modulus and phase expansions (DLMF 9.8.20–9.8.23) and of the [zero expansions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/ZeroSeries.html#TauCeti.airyZeroSeriesT_reversion) (9.9.18–9.9.21), and they match DLMF's printed values. The modulus, the phase and the zeros themselves are not yet defined, apart from an [enumeration of sector zeros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Airy/SectorZero.html#TauCeti.airySectorZero) that is conditional on there being infinitely many.
