### Named results

- **The away-`S` decomposition** — for a finite set `S` of indices, a restricted product is the plain product of the factors at `S` times the restricted product away from `S`, continuous for every reference family and a homeomorphism once the reference subgroups away from `S` are open ([`awayDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Away/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.awayDecomposition), [`continuous_awayDecomposition_symm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Away/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.continuous_awayDecomposition_symm)).
- **Non-open reference subgroups can break the group topology** — the restricted product of copies of `ℚ` relative to the trivial subgroups (finitely supported rational sequences, with the final topology over their finite-dimensional stages) is not a topological group ([`not_isTopologicalGroup_restrictedProduct_rat_bot`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/NotContinuousMul.html#TauCeti.not_isTopologicalGroup_restrictedProduct_rat_bot)), so for this family the inverses of the sum splitting and of the away decomposition are discontinuous ([`not_continuous_restrictedProductSum_symm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/NotContinuousMul.html#TauCeti.not_continuous_restrictedProductSum_symm), [`not_continuous_awayDecomposition_symm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/NotContinuousMul.html#TauCeti.not_continuous_awayDecomposition_symm)).
- **The continuity criterion for diagonals** — the diagonal of continuous coordinate homomorphisms is continuous when one cofinite set of indices is integral for every element ([`continuous_rationalDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Diagonal.html#TauCeti.continuous_rationalDiagonal)); pointwise eventual integrality is not enough, since for discrete factors with trivial reference subgroups, infinitely many of them nontrivial, the restricted-product topology is strictly finer than the induced one ([`continuous_eval_and_not_continuous_rationalDiagonal_range_coeMonoidHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Diagonal.html#TauCeti.continuous_eval_and_not_continuous_rationalDiagonal_range_coeMonoidHom)).
- **The surjectivity criterion for componentwise maps** — a componentwise map is surjective exactly when every coordinate map is and all but finitely many carry the source reference subgroup onto the target one ([`restrictedProductMap_surjective_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Map.html#TauCeti.restrictedProductMap_surjective_iff)), so for coordinatewise isomorphisms eventual bijectivity on reference subgroups is necessary, not just sufficient, for an equivalence ([`restrictedProductMap_surjective_iff_eventually_bijOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Congr/Right.html#TauCeti.restrictedProductMap_surjective_iff_eventually_bijOn)).
- **Compactness of integral subgroups** — the subgroup cut out by compact subgroups eventually inside the reference subgroups is compact with no openness hypothesis ([`isCompact_forall_mem_of_eventually_subset`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Basic.html#TauCeti.isCompact_forall_mem_of_eventually_subset)), although FLT's version assumes the reference subgroups open; the everywhere-integral case ([`isCompact_integralSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Basic.html#TauCeti.isCompact_integralSubgroup)) follows from it.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Componentwise maps from eventual preservation** — [`restrictedProductMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Map.html#TauCeti.restrictedProductMap) accepts coordinate maps that preserve the reference subgroups only outside a finite set, as arithmetic supplies them; only the everywhere-preserving form carries integral subgroups into integral subgroups ([`not_forall_mapsTo_integralSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Map.html#TauCeti.not_forall_mapsTo_integralSubgroup)).
- **Change of reference family and double cosets** — [`restrictedProductCongr`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Congr/Basic.html#TauCeti.restrictedProductCongr) identifies the restricted products of two families agreeing off a finite set, coordinatewise the identity and continuous both ways; double-coset spaces transport along it only to the transported subgroups ([`doubleCosetCongr`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Congr/DoubleCoset.html#TauCeti.doubleCosetCongr)), since the new family's integral subgroup is in general the wrong target ([`exists_map_integralSubgroup_ne`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Congr/Basic.html#TauCeti.exists_map_integralSubgroup_ne)).
- **Additive forms** — the maps, equivalences and diagonal also exist for additive groups, for instance [`addAwayDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Away/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.addAwayDecomposition) and [`addRationalDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Diagonal.html#TauCeti.addRationalDiagonal), the forms an adelic consumer needs to split the adeles of a global field along finitely many places and to embed the field diagonally.
