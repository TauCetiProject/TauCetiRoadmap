## RestrictedProducts: 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-26 (`223d161` to `759eb3e`)

The roadmap is complete. Its centrepiece is the away-`S` decomposition: for a finite set `S` of indices, a restricted product is the plain product of the factors at `S` times the restricted product away from `S` ([`awayDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Away/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.awayDecomposition)), continuous for every reference family and with continuous inverse once the reference subgroups away from `S` are open ([`continuous_awayDecomposition_symm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Away/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.continuous_awayDecomposition_symm)). As the roadmap prescribes, it composes three equivalences, each with coordinate formulas both ways: reindexing along an equivalence of index types ([`restrictedProductReindex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Congr/Left.html#TauCeti.restrictedProductReindex)), splitting over a sum of index types ([`restrictedProductSum`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Sum.html#TauCeti.restrictedProductSum)), and collapsing over a finite index type ([`restrictedProductOfFinite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Finite.html#TauCeti.restrictedProductOfFinite)).

That openness hypothesis is necessary, and this is proved: the restricted product of copies of `ℚ` relative to the trivial subgroups, that is, finitely supported rational sequences with the final topology over their finite-dimensional stages, is not a topological group ([`not_isTopologicalGroup_restrictedProduct_rat_bot`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/NotContinuousMul.html#TauCeti.not_isTopologicalGroup_restrictedProduct_rat_bot)), so for this family the inverses of the sum splitting and of the away decomposition are discontinuous bijections ([`not_continuous_restrictedProductSum_symm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/NotContinuousMul.html#TauCeti.not_continuous_restrictedProductSum_symm), [`not_continuous_awayDecomposition_symm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/NotContinuousMul.html#TauCeti.not_continuous_awayDecomposition_symm)). Likewise the diagonal of continuous coordinate homomorphisms is continuous when one cofinite set of indices is integral for every element ([`continuous_rationalDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Diagonal.html#TauCeti.continuous_rationalDiagonal)), and pointwise eventual integrality does not suffice: for discrete factors with trivial reference subgroups, infinitely many of them nontrivial, the restricted-product topology is strictly finer than the one induced from the full product ([`not_isInducing_coe_bot`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/TopologicalSpace.html#TauCeti.not_isInducing_coe_bot)).

Underneath sit integral subgroups, whose compactness here needs no openness hypothesis ([`isCompact_forall_mem_of_eventually_subset`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Basic.html#TauCeti.isCompact_forall_mem_of_eventually_subset)); componentwise maps from eventual preservation of the reference subgroups, with a surjectivity criterion showing that coordinatewise isomorphisms induce an equivalence exactly when they are eventually bijective on reference subgroups ([`restrictedProductMap_surjective_iff_eventually_bijOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Congr/Right.html#TauCeti.restrictedProductMap_surjective_iff_eventually_bijOn)); and the change of reference family, along which double-coset spaces transport only to the transported subgroups ([`doubleCosetCongr`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/RestrictedProduct/Congr/DoubleCoset.html#TauCeti.doubleCosetCongr)). The maps, equivalences and diagonal also exist for additive groups (TauCeti#8830, TauCeti#9050). The sum splitting is stated for the cofinite filter only, and its documentation says so.
