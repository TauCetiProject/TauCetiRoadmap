## Where this roadmap stands

Layers 3 to 6 and Layer 8 are partial, most of them with groundwork only. The Surfaces sub-roadmap has groundwork for its first three layers but none of their theorems, and nothing for its last two.

### Named results

- **The Lichnerowicz estimate** — on a closed manifold of dimension n ≥ 2 with `Ric ≥ (n−1)kg`, every positive Laplace eigenvalue is at least `nk` ([`finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Lichnerowicz/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul)). It comes from the scalar Bochner formula ([`laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Bochner/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient)).
- **Einstein's constancy theorem** — on a connected manifold of dimension other than two, `Ric = a g` forces `a` to be constant ([`eq_of_ricciTensor_eq_smul_inner`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Einstein.html#CovariantDerivative.eq_of_ricciTensor_eq_smul_inner)).
- **Realization of Jacobi fields by geodesic variations** — every Jacobi field along a maximal geodesic is the variation field of a smooth family of geodesics around any compact set, with no completeness assumption ([`exists_geodesicVariation_of_isCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Realization.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.exists_geodesicVariation_of_isCompact)).
- **The first variation of length** — for a `C²` family whose central curve has constant speed `C > 0`, the derivative of length is `(⟨V,γ′⟩|ₐᵇ − ∫⟨V, D_tγ′⟩)/C`, endpoints free ([`hasDerivAt_toReal_pathELength`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/FirstVariation/Length.html#TauCeti.Manifold.hasDerivAt_toReal_pathELength)).
- **The quadratic metric expansion in normal coordinates** — `⟨d exp_p(v)w, d exp_p(v)z⟩ = ⟨w,z⟩ − ⅓⟨R(w,v)v,z⟩` with error at most `C|v|³|w||z|` on one tangent ball ([`exists_abs_inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_sub_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Normal/Expansion.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_abs_inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_sub_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Jacobi fields** ([`IsJacobiFieldOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn)): along a maximal geodesic they exist for all initial data, form a space of dimension 2n and compute `d exp`. They feed every comparison theorem of Layer 4 and the index theory of Layer 3.
- **The model sine and its Riccati quotient** (`curvatureSine`, [`curvatureCotangent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Curvature/Cotangent.html#TauCeti.curvatureCotangent)): the solutions of `y″ + κy = 0` for every sign of κ, and `s_κ′/s_κ` with its Riccati equation. These are the comparison functions for Rauch and Hessian comparison.
- **The polar Jacobian** ([`polarJacobian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Polar/Jacobian.html#TauCeti.Manifold.polarJacobian)): `|d(exp_p)_(rv)(rw)|`, positive and smooth on a normal domain. Together with a [change of variables](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/ChangeOfVariables.html#PartialDiffeomorph.setLIntegral_riemannianVolume_eq) for Riemannian volume, it is what the polar length and area formulas of the Surfaces sub-roadmap need.

## The frontier

- **The polar Jacobian equation (Surfaces 1.1)**: prove `j_rr + Kj = 0` with `j_r(0) = 1`. The Jacobian, the polar Gauss lemma and Jacobi fields computing `d exp` are all in place, and 1.4's formulas `L′ = 2π − ∫K`, `V′ = L` follow from it by Fubini.
- **Rauch comparison**: prove `K ≤ κ ⇒ |J(t)| ≥ s_κ(t)|D_tJ(0)|` before the first model zero, and its reverse. Nothing upstream blocks it.
- **Second variation and the index form (Layer 3)**: build the index form on piecewise `C¹` fields vanishing at the endpoints, starting from the first variation formulas, piecewise paths and the commutation of mixed derivatives.
- **The cubic normal-coordinate jet**: add `−⅙⟨(∇_vR)(e_i,v)v,e_j⟩` with an `O(|v|⁴)` bound uniform over unit vectors. The Euclidean limits of rescaled metrics (2.3) come after it.
- **Conformal invariance of the surface Dirichlet energy (Surfaces Layer 5)**: independent of the other Surfaces layers, and still not begun.
