## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Every layer of the main roadmap is partial, and none is finished. Comparison geometry now has Cartan–Hadamard and Günther's volume bound, and the index-form theory reaches nullity but not the Morse index theorem. Spherical harmonics are identified with the full eigenspaces of the round sphere. The Surfaces sub-roadmap has groundwork in five of its six layers but none of its integral formulas. MinimalSurfaces has the Euclidean minimal graph equation and Hersch's balancing lemma. Most of its layers have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Cartan–Hadamard theorem**: on a complete manifold of nonpositive sectional curvature, `exp_p` is a covering map from the tangent space ([`cartanHadamard_isCoveringMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Covering/CartanHadamard.html#TauCeti.Manifold.cartanHadamard_isCoveringMap)). It is a diffeomorphism in the simply connected case, and every such manifold is aspherical.
- **Günther's volume comparison**: under `K ≤ κ`, a ball of radius `r` on which `exp_p` is injective has volume at least that of the radius-`r` ball in the model space of curvature `κ` ([`guenther`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Guenther.html#TauCeti.guenther)).
- **Index-form nullity equals conjugate multiplicity**: on endpoint-zero piecewise `C¹` fields along a geodesic, the kernel of the index form is exactly the Jacobi fields ([`finrank_ker_endpointZeroIndexForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/IndexForm/Kernel.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_ker_endpointZeroIndexForm)). The [second variation of energy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/SecondVariation/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.hasDerivAt_deriv_energy) identifies the index form as the Hessian at a geodesic with fixed endpoints.
- **Eigenfunctions of the round sphere**: for `m ≥ 1`, every nonzero `C²` solution of `Δf = −λf` on `Sᵐ` has `λ = d(d+m−1)` and is the restriction of a harmonic homogeneous polynomial of degree `d` ([`eq_harmonicSphereRestriction_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Sphere/Harmonic.html#TauCeti.eq_harmonicSphereRestriction_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul)).
- **Rauch comparison**: under `K ≤ κ`, a normal Jacobi field with `J(0) = 0` satisfies `|J(t)| ≥ s_κ(t)|D_tJ(0)|` up to the first zero of the model sine ([`rauch_norm_ge_of_sectional_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Comparison.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.rauch_norm_ge_of_sectional_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The index form on piecewise fields** ([`piecewiseIndexForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/IndexForm/Piecewise.html#TauCeti.Manifold.piecewiseIndexForm)): a symmetric bilinear form on continuous piecewise `C¹` sections along a curve, with integration by parts through the corner jumps. This is the space on which the Morse index theorem is to be stated.
- **The universal covering manifold** ([`exists_universalCover`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/UniversalCover.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_universalCover)): a Hausdorff, second-countable manifold carrying the pullback metric, which is complete when the base is and has the same curvature. Cartan–Hadamard uses it now, and Bonnet–Myers and Frankel's theorem will need it.
- **The Euclidean minimal graph operator** ([`fluxDivergence_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Euclidean/MinimalGraph/Equation/Operator.html#TauCeti.MinimalGraph.fluxDivergence_eq_zero_iff)): its divergence and nondivergence forms agree, and its principal form gives ellipticity under a gradient bound. It is the starting point for MinimalSurfaces Layer 2.

### Roadmap coverage

In the main roadmap, **Layers 1 and 2** are done except for the converse parallel-curvature criterion, horizontal geodesics and the projective-space quotients in Layer 1, and the Euclidean limits of rescaled metrics (2.3) in Layer 2. **Layer 3** has first and second variation of energy and the piecewise index form with its kernel. It lacks the second variation of length, periodic variations, the Morse index theorem, `H¹` paths and the path topology of R3.1–R3.4. **Layer 4** has Rauch under `K ≤ κ`, the covering criteria, Cartan–Hadamard and Günther. It lacks reverse Rauch, Hessian comparison, Bishop–Gromov, Bonnet–Myers and the later items through R4.9. **Layers 5 to 7** remain groundwork: the normal bundle, the distance function on normal balls, and R7.0 with the Lichnerowicz estimate. **Layer 8** has R8.1 and the smooth form of R8.2. It lacks the `L²` form, irreducibility (R8.3), Takahashi (R8.4) and the minimality of the Veronese example (R8.5). In **Surfaces**, Layers 1–3 and 5 are partial, Layer 6 has only the one-variable cutoff profile, and Layer 4 is untouched. In **MinimalSurfaces**, Layer 2 has the Euclidean graph equation and part of 2.6. Layer 10 has Hersch's lemma, and Layer 11 has only the genus formula for finite covers. Layers 3–9 and 12 are untouched.

## The frontier

- **The Morse index theorem (R3)**: the piecewise index form, its kernel and nullity are proved. What remains is the finite reduction through broken Jacobi fields and the count of conjugate points strictly inside the interval.
- **Second variation of length (R3)**: derive it from the energy formula with free endpoints. Bonnet–Myers, Synge and MinimalSurfaces 11.1 all consume it.
- **Ricci comparison (R4)**: reverse Rauch, the Riccati Hessian comparison and then Bishop–Gromov. Günther's proof supplies the radial Jacobian factorization these need.
- **The Veronese example and Takahashi (R8.4–R8.5)**: the induced metric `3g` and the antipodal fibres are proved. Minimality in `S⁴(1/√3)` and the embedding of `ℝP²` remain.
- **Conformal invariance of the surface Dirichlet energy (Surfaces 5.1)**: the gradient and volume rescaling laws are in place, but their combination in dimension two is still missing.
