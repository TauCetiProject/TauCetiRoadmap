### Named results

- **Einstein's constancy theorem**: on a connected manifold of dimension other than two, a torsion-free metric connection with `Ric = a g` has `a` constant ([`eq_of_ricciTensor_eq_smul_inner`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Einstein.html#CovariantDerivative.eq_of_ricciTensor_eq_smul_inner)). It consumes the second Bianchi identity through its contracted form.
- **Jacobi fields in constant curvature**: along a geodesic through points of curvature κ, every Jacobi field is an affine tangential part plus parallel normal fields weighted by the model cosine and sine ([`exists_eq_affine_smul_add_curvatureCosine_smul_add_curvatureSine_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Classification.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.exists_eq_affine_smul_add_curvatureCosine_smul_add_curvatureSine_smul)). For κ > 0 and speed c, a normal field vanishing at `a` vanishes again at `a + π/(√κ c)` and not before ([`apply_add_pi_div_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/ConstantCurvature.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.apply_add_pi_div_eq_zero)).
- **The Bochner formula**: with `Δ = div grad`, `Δ|grad f|²` equals twice the squared Hessian, plus twice `d(Δf)(grad f)`, plus twice `Ric(grad f, grad f)` ([`laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Bochner/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient)). Its integrated form `∫(|Hess f|² + Ric(grad f, grad f)) = ∫(Δf)²` holds for compactly supported f ([`integral_sum_hessian_sq_add_ricci`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Bochner/Integral.html#TauCeti.Manifold.integral_sum_hessian_sq_add_ricci)).
- **The Lichnerowicz estimate**: on a closed manifold of dimension n ≥ 2 with `Ric ≥ (n−1)kg`, every positive Laplace eigenvalue with a nonzero smooth eigenfunction is at least `nk` ([`finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Lichnerowicz/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul)). In the equality case `Hess f = −kfg` ([`hessian_eq_neg_mul_inner_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Lichnerowicz/Equality.html#TauCeti.Manifold.hessian_eq_neg_mul_inner_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul)).
- **Curvature of bi-invariant metrics**: for the metric of an Ad-invariant inner product, `∇_X Y = ½[X,Y]` and `R(X,Y)Z = −¼[[X,Y],Z]` ([`curvatureTensor_leftInvariantMetric_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/LieGroup/AdjointInvariant.html#ContinuousLinearMap.curvatureTensor_leftInvariantMetric_one)), so sectional curvature is nonnegative ([`sectionalCurvature_nonneg`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/LieGroup/BiInvariant.html#CovariantDerivative.IsLeviCivitaConnection.sectionalCurvature_nonneg)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Jacobi fields** ([`IsJacobiFieldOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn)): `C²` solutions of `D_t²J + R(J,γ′)γ′ = 0`. They come with uniqueness from initial data, the Wronskian, smoothness, reparametrization, isometry transport, and the fact that variation fields of geodesic families are Jacobi fields. Every comparison theorem in R4 and the index theory of R3 start here.
- **The curvature operator on bivectors** ([`curvatureEndomorphism`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/ExteriorSquare.html#CovariantDerivative.curvatureEndomorphism)): the self-adjoint `Rop` on `Λ²TM`, also available on two-forms. With it, scalar curvature is twice its trace, eigenvalue bounds give sharp Ricci bounds, and constant curvature κ is `Rop = κ Id`.
- **The model sine and cosine** ([`curvatureSine`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Curvature.html#TauCeti.curvatureSine)): the solutions `s_κ` and `c_κ` of `y″ + κy = 0` for all signs of κ at once, which serve as the comparison functions of R4.

### Roadmap coverage

R1 is largely done in items 1–3: symmetries, both Bianchi identities, the Ricci identity, frame and moving-frame formulas, isometry naturality, polarization, Schur's theorem ([`eq_of_forall_hasSectionalCurvatureAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Schur.html#CovariantDerivative.eq_of_forall_hasSectionalCurvatureAt)), Einstein constancy, parallel curvature and locally symmetric surfaces. I could not find flat-implies-locally-Euclidean, or the converse half of the parallel-transport characterization of ∇R = 0. Item 4 has Christoffel jets and the Gauss lemma but not the metric expansion in normal coordinates. The transformation laws are present for scaling and conformal change, and the connection is present for products and warped products. Bi-invariant metrics are done, including completeness of left-invariant metrics. Riemannian submersions, homogeneous quotients and the projective examples are untouched. R2 is partial: everything except global existence, the 2n-dimensional solution space, realizing initial data by geodesic variations, and the identification of `d exp` with Jacobi fields. R3 has only the piecewise first variation with corner terms and the commutation of mixed covariant derivatives. R4 has its models (spheres of every radius, great circles as geodesics, upper half-space, early hyperboloid work), the nonpositive-curvature exponential bound, and compactness of universal covers with finite fundamental group, but none of its comparison theorems. R7.0 is substantially done. R8.1 lacks only the identification of restricted harmonic polynomials as round-sphere eigenfunctions. R5a and R6 keep their earlier flat-ambient groundwork.

## The frontier

- **Existence of Jacobi fields**: solve the Jacobi equation on compact intervals from arbitrary initial data. That gives the full 2n-dimensional space and then `d exp_(tv)(tw) = J(t)`. Normal-coordinate jets and Rauch comparison both wait on this.
- **Rauch comparison**: prove `K ≤ κ ⇒ |J(t)| ≥ s_κ(t)|D_tJ(0)|` before the first model zero, and its reverse. The nonpositive-curvature exponential bound and the model functions are in place.
- **Second variation and the index form (R3)**: the piecewise first variation and the curvature commutation of mixed derivatives are the ingredients still to be combined.
- **Spherical eigenvalues of harmonic polynomials**: show that a harmonic homogeneous polynomial of degree d restricts to an eigenfunction of the round Laplacian with eigenvalue `d(d+m−1)`. This finishes R8.1.
- **Riemannian submersions and flat metrics**: the O'Neill tensors, and flat-implies-locally-Euclidean, are R1's remaining structural pieces. Neither depends on R2.
