## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Jacobi fields are essentially done, and Layer 1's curvature calculus is done apart from the parallel-transport criterion for `∇R = 0` and the O'Neill curvature formulas. Comparison geometry has started, with Rauch comparison under an upper curvature bound, and the scalar Bochner and Lichnerowicz theory of Layer 7 is in place. Every other layer of the main roadmap is partial, most of them groundwork only. The Surfaces sub-roadmap has its polar Jacobian equation but none of its integral formulas. The MinimalSurfaces sub-roadmap has not begun.

### Named results

- **Rauch comparison** — under `K ≤ κ`, a normal Jacobi field with `J(0) = 0` along a unit-speed geodesic satisfies `|J(t)| ≥ s_κ(t)|D_tJ(0)|` up to the first zero of the model sine ([`rauch_norm_ge_of_sectional_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Comparison.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.rauch_norm_ge_of_sectional_le)).
- **Flat means locally Euclidean** — the curvature of a smooth metric vanishes on an open set exactly when each point of it has a neighbourhood isometric to a Euclidean ball ([`forall_curvatureTensor_leviCivitaConnection_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Flat.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.forall_curvatureTensor_leviCivitaConnection_eq_zero_iff)).
- **The Lichnerowicz estimate** — on a closed manifold of dimension n ≥ 2 with `Ric ≥ (n−1)kg`, every positive Laplace eigenvalue is at least `nk` ([`finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Lichnerowicz/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul)).
- **Realization of Jacobi fields by geodesic variations** — every Jacobi field along a maximal geodesic is the variation field of a smooth family of geodesics around any compact set, with no completeness assumption ([`exists_geodesicVariation_of_isCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Realization.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.exists_geodesicVariation_of_isCompact)).
- **The polar Jacobian equation** — on a surface, the radial Jacobian of geodesic polar coordinates satisfies `j_rr + Kj = 0` wherever `j ≠ 0` ([`deriv_deriv_polarJacobian_add_sectionalCurvature_mul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Polar/JacobiEquation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.deriv_deriv_polarJacobian_add_sectionalCurvature_mul)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Jacobi fields** ([`IsJacobiFieldOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn)): along a maximal geodesic they exist for all initial data and compute `d exp`. In constant curvature their conjugate times are the model zeros, each with multiplicity `dim M − 1`. They carry Rauch comparison now, and the index theory of Layer 3 later.
- **The polar Jacobian and geodesic circles** ([`polarJacobian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Polar/Jacobian.html#TauCeti.Manifold.polarJacobian), [`geodesicCircle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Polar/Circle.html#TauCeti.Manifold.geodesicCircle)): a full geodesic circle has length `∫ j dθ`. With the Jacobi equation this is what the polar length and area formulas of the Surfaces sub-roadmap need.
- **Discrete energy of broken paths** ([`discreteEnergyStrictSublevel`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MetricSpace/DiscreteEnergy/Basic.html#TauCeti.discreteEnergyStrictSublevel)): fixed-endpoint vertex tuples below an energy level, with continuous maps that forget vertices and insert new ones without changing energy. This is the finite-dimensional model for the path topology of R3.2.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 1** is done except the parallel-transport characterization of `∇R = 0` and the O'Neill curvature formulas and the examples that follow from them. **Layer 2** has all of R2 and the quadratic normal-coordinate jet. The cubic `∇R` term is missing, although its input `D⁴J` at a zero is now proved, and so are the Euclidean limits of 2.3. **Layer 3** has first variation of length and energy, piecewise fields and discrete energy, but no second variation, index form, H¹ paths or path CW models. **Layer 4** has Rauch comparison under `K ≤ κ`, the model functions, rays and the nonpositive-curvature exponential results. The reverse Rauch bound, Hessian comparison and the volume comparisons are missing, and R4.9 is not established. **Layers 5 and 6** have groundwork only. **Layer 7** has R7.0 but nothing from R7.1 onwards, and **Layer 8** has R8.1 except the spherical eigenvalue identification. In **Surfaces**, Layer 1 has the polar Jacobian equation and circle lengths but no normal strips, distance to an arc, or polar length and area formulas. Layers 2 and 3 have groundwork: cut times of geodesics, geodesic curvature, and the turning integral as a degree. Layers 4 to 6 are untouched. In **MinimalSurfaces**, Layers 2 to 12 are all untouched.

## The frontier

- **Polar length and area (Surfaces 1.4)**: prove `L′ = 2π − ∫_{B_r} K`, `V′ = L` by integrating `j_rr = −Kj` against the circle-length formula. Surfaces Layer 6's logarithmic cutoff then becomes reachable.
- **Reverse Rauch and Hessian comparison**: prove the `K ≥ κ` bound before conjugacy, then compare the Hessian of distance with `s_κ′/s_κ`. The Riccati identity for eikonal functions and a matrix Riccati determinant bound are already proved.
- **Second variation and the index form (Layer 3)**: build the index form on the space of piecewise-smooth fields with fixed endpoints, which is now available.
- **The cubic normal-coordinate jet**: add `−⅙⟨(∇_vR)(e_i,v)v,e_j⟩` with a uniform `O(|v|⁴)` bound, from the fourth covariant derivative of Jacobi fields at a zero.
- **Conformal invariance of the surface Dirichlet energy (Surfaces 5)**: both lemmas it is to be assembled from, the gradient and volume rescaling laws, are already in the library, and nothing else blocks it.
