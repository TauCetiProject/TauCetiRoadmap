## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Jacobi fields and normal-coordinate jets are done except for the Euclidean limits of rescaled metrics. Layer 1's curvature calculus now includes O'Neill's curvature formula, and comparison geometry has Rauch under an upper curvature bound and the covering criteria that Cartan–Hadamard needs. Every other layer of the main roadmap is partial, several of them groundwork only. The Surfaces sub-roadmap has its polar Jacobian equation but none of its integral formulas. MinimalSurfaces has only multi-valued graph bookkeeping.

### Named results

- **Rauch comparison**: under `K ≤ κ`, a normal Jacobi field with `J(0) = 0` along a unit-speed geodesic satisfies `|J(t)| ≥ s_κ(t)|D_tJ(0)|` up to the first zero of the model sine ([`rauch_norm_ge_of_sectional_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Comparison.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.rauch_norm_ge_of_sectional_le)).
- **The cubic normal-coordinate expansion**: `⟨d exp_p(v)w, d exp_p(v)z⟩ = ⟨w,z⟩ − ⅓⟨R(w,v)v,z⟩ − ⅙⟨(∇_vR)(w,v)v,z⟩ + O(|v|⁴|w||z|)`, uniformly on one tangent ball ([`exists_abs_inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_sub_add_derivCurvatureTensor_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Normal/Expansion.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_abs_inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_sub_add_derivCurvatureTensor_le)).
- **O'Neill's curvature formula**: under a Riemannian submersion, a horizontal plane gains `3|A_u w|²/|u ∧ w|²` in sectional curvature when pushed down to the base ([`sectionalCurvature_mfderiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Submersion/Curvature.html#TauCeti.IsRiemannianSubmersion.sectionalCurvature_mfderiv)).
- **Local isometries from complete manifolds are coverings**: a local Riemannian isometry out of a geodesically complete manifold is a covering map, and the same holds for length-nondecreasing local diffeomorphisms ([`isCoveringMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Covering/Criterion.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRiemannianIsometry.isCoveringMap)).
- **The Lichnerowicz estimate**: on a closed manifold of dimension n ≥ 2 with `Ric ≥ (n−1)kg`, every positive Laplace eigenvalue is at least `nk` ([`finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Lichnerowicz/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Jacobi fields** ([`IsJacobiFieldOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn)): along a maximal geodesic they exist for all initial data, compute `d exp`, and are realized by geodesic variations. They are the input to Rauch comparison, to the normal-coordinate jets and to the index form.
- **The index form** ([`indexForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/IndexForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.indexForm)): `∫⟨D_tV, D_tW⟩ − ⟨R(V,γ′)γ′, W⟩` for `C¹` fields along a curve. It is symmetric and additive over subdivisions, and integration by parts holds. On a Jacobi field it reduces to boundary terms, which is the step that conjugate-point arguments use.
- **The polar Jacobian and geodesic circles** ([`polarJacobian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Polar/Jacobian.html#TauCeti.Manifold.polarJacobian)): on a surface it satisfies `j_rr + Kj = 0`, and a full geodesic circle has length `∫ j dθ`. These are the inputs for the polar length and area formulas.

Layer 5 has only the Dirichlet energy and the conformal volume law, and Layers 4 and 6 are untouched. In **MinimalSurfaces**, Layer 2 has the deck-overlap and injectivity part of 2.6, and Layers 3 to 12 are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Cartan–Hadamard (R4.3, item 4)**: the norm-nondecreasing differential of `exp_p` under `K ≤ 0` and the covering criterion for length-nondecreasing local diffeomorphisms are both proved. What remains is to combine them and add the simply connected corollaries.
- **Conformal invariance of the surface Dirichlet energy (Surfaces 5.1)**: the energy, the gradient rescaling law and the volume rescaling law are all in place. Their combination in dimension two is missing.
- **Second variation (Layer 3)**: differentiate energy twice along a variation with corner and endpoint terms, and identify the fixed-endpoint Hessian with the index form.
- **Euclidean limits of rescaled metrics (Layer 2.3)**: the cubic jet is now available. What remains is `C^m` convergence of `λ²F*g` to the Euclidean metric, uniform over centres in a compact set and over frames.
- **Polar length and area (Surfaces 1.4)**: integrate `j_rr = −Kj` against the circle-length formula to get `L′ = 2π − ∫_{B_r} K`.
