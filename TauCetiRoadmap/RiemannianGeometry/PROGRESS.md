## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-24 (`0420037` to `fe8ca8b`)

The second Bianchi identity landed in full generality: for a smooth connection on any vector bundle, paired with a smooth torsion-free connection on the tangent bundle, the cyclic sum of covariant derivatives of the curvature vanishes ([`covariantDerivative_curvatureOperator_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/SecondBianchi.html#CovariantDerivative.covariantDerivative_curvatureOperator_cyclic_eq_zero)). The proof needed the Jacobi identity for the manifold Lie bracket ([`jacobi_identity_mlieBracket_apply`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/LieBracket.html#VectorField.jacobi_identity_mlieBracket_apply)) and smoothness of brackets and of the curvature operator.

Sectional curvature now determines the curvature tensor. An explicit polarization identity ([`six_mul_apply_eq_of_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Multilinear/SectionalPolarization.html#LinearMap.six_mul_apply_eq_of_cyclic_eq_zero)) shows that the Riemann tensor is the only form with the curvature symmetries that has the given sectional numerators ([`riemannCurvatureTensor_eq_of_forall_linearIndependent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Riemann.html#CovariantDerivative.IsMetricCompatible.riemannCurvatureTensor_eq_of_forall_linearIndependent)). It follows that constant sectional curvature κ is the same as `R(w,u)v = κ(⟨u,v⟩w − ⟨w,v⟩u)` ([`hasConstantSectionalCurvature_iff_isConstantCurvatureTensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.hasConstantSectionalCurvature_iff_isConstantCurvatureTensor)), and so Ric = (n−1)κg and the scalar curvature is n(n−1)κ ([`ricciTensor_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.HasSectionalCurvatureAt.ricciTensor_eq), [`scalarCurvature_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.HasSectionalCurvatureAt.scalarCurvature_eq)). Low dimensions are handled honestly. In dimension one or less there are no two-planes, so every κ qualifies ([`hasSectionalCurvatureAt_of_finrank_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Sectional.html#CovariantDerivative.hasSectionalCurvatureAt_of_finrank_le_one)), and from dimension two up κ is unique.

The third strand computes the curvature of a connection shifted by an endomorphism-valued one-form ([`curvatureOperator_addOneForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/AddOneForm.html#CovariantDerivative.curvatureOperator_addOneForm)). Applied to the Levi-Civita connection, it gives the curvature of a conformally rescaled metric ([`curvatureOperator_leviCivitaConnection_rescale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Conformal.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.curvatureOperator_leviCivitaConnection_rescale)), already for a `C¹` metric.

## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-06 (`fe8ca8b` to `a67a650`)

Jacobi fields arrived, and R2 went from nothing to most of the way there. A [Jacobi field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn) along a geodesic is determined by its initial value and derivative, and the variation field of any family of geodesics is one ([`isJacobiFieldOn_variationField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Variation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isJacobiFieldOn_variationField)). Every Jacobi field splits into a tangential part `(a + bt)γ′` and a normal part. In constant curvature κ it is written out explicitly with the model sine and cosine ([`exists_eq_affine_smul_add_curvatureCosine_smul_add_curvatureSine_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Classification.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.exists_eq_affine_smul_add_curvatureCosine_smul_add_curvatureSine_smul)), which gives the conjugate point at time `π/(√κ c)` for a geodesic of speed c. In nonpositive curvature the norms of Jacobi fields are convex and the exponential differential never shrinks a vector ([`norm_le_norm_mfderiv_riemannianExp_of_sectionalCurvature_nonpos`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Comparison.html#TauCeti.Manifold.norm_le_norm_mfderiv_riemannianExp_of_sectionalCurvature_nonpos)). Killing fields restrict to Jacobi fields (TauCeti#23717). Existence is still missing, so the solution space is only known to have dimension at most 2n.

On the curvature side, [Einstein's constancy theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Einstein.html#CovariantDerivative.eq_of_ricciTensor_eq_smul_inner) now says that `Ric = a g` forces `a` to be constant away from dimension two, and [locally symmetric surfaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/LocallySymmetric.html#CovariantDerivative.hasParallelCurvature_iff_isLocallyConstant_scalarCurvature_of_finrank_eq_two) are exactly those of locally constant curvature. For an Ad-invariant metric on a Lie group, `R(X,Y)Z = −¼[[X,Y],Z]` ([`curvatureTensor_leftInvariantMetric_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/LieGroup/AdjointInvariant.html#ContinuousLinearMap.curvatureTensor_leftInvariantMetric_one)), so sectional curvature is nonnegative. Supporting work added the Ricci identity, the curvature operator on bivectors, frame formulas, and the connections of scaled, product and warped-product metrics.

The scalar Bochner formula landed ([`laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Bochner/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient)) together with its integrated form, and with it the [Lichnerowicz estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Lichnerowicz/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul): on a closed manifold with `Ric ≥ (n−1)kg`, every positive eigenvalue is at least `nk`. In the equality case the eigenfunction satisfies `Hess f = −kfg`. Harmonic polynomials also arrived, with the [Fischer decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Harmonic.html#MvPolynomial.harmonicSubmodule_sup_map_mulLeft_normSq) and the dimension formula.

## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`a67a650` to `7ca783d`)

Jacobi fields can now be realized by geodesics: every Jacobi field along a maximal geodesic is the variation field of a smooth family of geodesics sharing one time domain around any compact parameter set ([`exists_geodesicVariation_of_isCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Realization.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.exists_geodesicVariation_of_isCompact)). Completeness is not needed. This rests on smoothness of the [geodesic spray](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Smoothness.html#TauCeti.Manifold.contMDiff_geodesicSpray) and of the geodesic flow in its initial data (TauCeti#31912). The Jacobi equation also produced the quadratic jet of the metric in normal coordinates: the second radial derivative of `⟨d exp_p(tv) w, d exp_p(tv) z⟩` at zero is `−⅔⟨R(w,v)v, z⟩` ([`deriv_deriv_inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Normal/Jet.html#TauCeti.Manifold.deriv_deriv_inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_zero)). The cubic `∇R` term and the uniform remainder bound are still to come.

The curvature formalism gained its exterior-calculus form. The second Bianchi identity now reads [`d^∇F = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Exterior/Two.html#CovariantDerivative.exteriorCovariantDerivativeTwo_curvatureForm) for the induced endomorphism connection, with no metric or torsion hypothesis, and has a [version with torsion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Derivative/Torsion.html#CovariantDerivative.derivCurvatureTensor_cyclic_eq_neg_torsion). [Cartan's second structure equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/ConnectionForm.html#IsLocalFrameOn.coeff_curvatureOperator_eq_mextDeriv_add_wedge) `F = dA + A ∧ A` holds in any `C²` local frame, and the factor in `½[ω∧ω] = ω∧ω` is pinned down ([`half_bracketWedge_commutator_self`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Alternating/Wedge/Commutator.html#ContinuousAlternatingMap.half_bracketWedge_commutator_self)). Supporting work covered curvature of Hom connections and the trivial connection.

Warped products `g_M + f² g_N` now have their [Ricci tensor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/WarpedProd/Ricci.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsWarpedProduct.ricciTensor_eq) and [scalar curvature](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/WarpedProd/Scalar.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsWarpedProduct.scalarCurvature). For Riemannian submersions, the O'Neill tensors are [skew-adjoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Submersion/ONeill.html#TauCeti.IsRiemannianSubmersion.inner_oneillA), the horizontal and vertical projections are smooth, and horizontal lifts commute with isometries. The O'Neill curvature formulas are not yet there. A vector field whose flow preserves norms [is Killing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Killing/Converse.html#TauCeti.Manifold.inner_leviCivitaConnection_self_eq_zero_of_eventually_norm_mfderiv_flowOf), and [piecewise `Cⁿ` paths](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PiecewisePath.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsPiecewiseContMDiffOn) are ready for R3. Nothing landed for the Surfaces sub-roadmap.

## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`7ca783d` to `d9fbe7b`)

The first variation of length landed for smooth families: if the central curve of a `C²` family has constant speed `C > 0`, the derivative of length at zero is `(⟨V,γ′⟩|ₐᵇ − ∫ₐᵇ ⟨V, D_tγ′⟩)/C`, with the endpoints free to move ([`hasDerivAt_toReal_pathELength`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/FirstVariation/Length.html#TauCeti.Manifold.hasDerivAt_toReal_pathELength)). Alongside it came the inequality `L² ≤ 2(b−a)E` between length and energy, [variations with a prescribed moving endpoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/Chart/EndpointVariation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_contMDiffAt_endpointVariation), [extension of a geodesic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Extension.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsGeodesicCurveOn.exists_isGeodesicCurveOn_Ioo_of_continuousOn_Icc) past the ends of a closed interval on which it stays continuous, and gluing lemmas for piecewise paths. The second variation and the index form have not started.

In normal coordinates the metric now has an honest quadratic expansion, `⟨d exp_p(v)w, d exp_p(v)z⟩ = ⟨w,z⟩ − ⅓⟨R(w,v)v,z⟩` up to an error `C|v|³|w||z|` on one tangent ball ([`exists_abs_inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_sub_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Normal/Expansion.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_abs_inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_sub_le)), so `|d exp(tT)(tY)|² = t² − ⅓K t⁴ + O(t⁵)` uniformly. Radial parallel transport in a normal domain was built as a [linear isometry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Normal/Parallel.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsNormalDomain.radialParallelIsometry), and the connection coefficients of the normal frame [vanish at the centre](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Normal/Frame/Connection.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsNormalDomain.normalFrameConnectionCoefficient_self). The cubic `∇R` term is still missing. Curvature also gained pullbacks along local diffeomorphisms, and the model Riccati function `s_κ′/s_κ` ([`curvatureCotangent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Trigonometric/Curvature/Cotangent.html#TauCeti.curvatureCotangent)) for later comparison arguments.

The Surfaces sub-roadmap got its first pieces. The [polar Jacobian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Polar/Jacobian.html#TauCeti.Manifold.polarJacobian) is positive and smooth on a normal domain and has the right one-sided behaviour at the centre, but its equation `j_rr + Kj = 0` is not proved yet. Riemannian volume has a [change of variables](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/ChangeOfVariables.html#PartialDiffeomorph.setLIntegral_riemannianVolume_eq) along partial diffeomorphisms. The [cut time of a ray](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MetricSpace/RayCutTime.html#TauCeti.rayCutTime) from a set is defined in metric spaces and is measurable in the parameter. The [oriented unit normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Curve/OrientedNormal.html#TauCeti.Manifold.orientedUnitNormal) of a curve prepares geodesic curvature for Gauss–Bonnet.
