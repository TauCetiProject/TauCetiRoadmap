## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-24 (`0420037` to `fe8ca8b`)

The second Bianchi identity landed in full generality: for a smooth connection on any vector bundle, paired with a smooth torsion-free connection on the tangent bundle, the cyclic sum of covariant derivatives of the curvature vanishes ([`covariantDerivative_curvatureOperator_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/SecondBianchi.html#CovariantDerivative.covariantDerivative_curvatureOperator_cyclic_eq_zero)). The proof needed the Jacobi identity for the manifold Lie bracket ([`jacobi_identity_mlieBracket_apply`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/LieBracket.html#VectorField.jacobi_identity_mlieBracket_apply)) and smoothness of brackets and of the curvature operator.

Sectional curvature now determines the curvature tensor. An explicit polarization identity ([`six_mul_apply_eq_of_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Multilinear/SectionalPolarization.html#LinearMap.six_mul_apply_eq_of_cyclic_eq_zero)) shows that the Riemann tensor is the only form with the curvature symmetries that has the given sectional numerators ([`riemannCurvatureTensor_eq_of_forall_linearIndependent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Riemann.html#CovariantDerivative.IsMetricCompatible.riemannCurvatureTensor_eq_of_forall_linearIndependent)). It follows that constant sectional curvature κ is the same as `R(w,u)v = κ(⟨u,v⟩w − ⟨w,v⟩u)` ([`hasConstantSectionalCurvature_iff_isConstantCurvatureTensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.hasConstantSectionalCurvature_iff_isConstantCurvatureTensor)), and so Ric = (n−1)κg and the scalar curvature is n(n−1)κ ([`ricciTensor_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.HasSectionalCurvatureAt.ricciTensor_eq), [`scalarCurvature_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.HasSectionalCurvatureAt.scalarCurvature_eq)). Low dimensions are handled honestly. In dimension one or less there are no two-planes, so every κ qualifies ([`hasSectionalCurvatureAt_of_finrank_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Sectional.html#CovariantDerivative.hasSectionalCurvatureAt_of_finrank_le_one)), and from dimension two up κ is unique.

The third strand computes the curvature of a connection shifted by an endomorphism-valued one-form ([`curvatureOperator_addOneForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/AddOneForm.html#CovariantDerivative.curvatureOperator_addOneForm)). Applied to the Levi-Civita connection, it gives the curvature of a conformally rescaled metric ([`curvatureOperator_leviCivitaConnection_rescale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Conformal.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.curvatureOperator_leviCivitaConnection_rescale)), already for a `C¹` metric.
