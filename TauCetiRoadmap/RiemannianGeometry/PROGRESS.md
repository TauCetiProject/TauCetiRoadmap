## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-24 (`0420037` to `fe8ca8b`)

The second Bianchi identity landed in full generality: for a smooth connection on any vector bundle, paired with a smooth torsion-free connection on the tangent bundle, the cyclic sum of covariant derivatives of the curvature vanishes ([`covariantDerivative_curvatureOperator_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/SecondBianchi.html#CovariantDerivative.covariantDerivative_curvatureOperator_cyclic_eq_zero)). The proof needed the Jacobi identity for the manifold Lie bracket ([`jacobi_identity_mlieBracket_apply`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/LieBracket.html#VectorField.jacobi_identity_mlieBracket_apply)) and smoothness of brackets and of the curvature operator.

Sectional curvature now determines the curvature tensor. An explicit polarization identity ([`six_mul_apply_eq_of_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Multilinear/SectionalPolarization.html#LinearMap.six_mul_apply_eq_of_cyclic_eq_zero)) shows that the Riemann tensor is the only form with the curvature symmetries that has the given sectional numerators ([`riemannCurvatureTensor_eq_of_forall_linearIndependent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Riemann.html#CovariantDerivative.IsMetricCompatible.riemannCurvatureTensor_eq_of_forall_linearIndependent)). It follows that constant sectional curvature κ is the same as `R(w,u)v = κ(⟨u,v⟩w − ⟨w,v⟩u)` ([`hasConstantSectionalCurvature_iff_isConstantCurvatureTensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.hasConstantSectionalCurvature_iff_isConstantCurvatureTensor)), and so Ric = (n−1)κg and the scalar curvature is n(n−1)κ ([`ricciTensor_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.HasSectionalCurvatureAt.ricciTensor_eq), [`scalarCurvature_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.HasSectionalCurvatureAt.scalarCurvature_eq)). Low dimensions are handled honestly. In dimension one or less there are no two-planes, so every κ qualifies ([`hasSectionalCurvatureAt_of_finrank_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Sectional.html#CovariantDerivative.hasSectionalCurvatureAt_of_finrank_le_one)), and from dimension two up κ is unique.

The third strand computes the curvature of a connection shifted by an endomorphism-valued one-form ([`curvatureOperator_addOneForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/AddOneForm.html#CovariantDerivative.curvatureOperator_addOneForm)). Applied to the Levi-Civita connection, it gives the curvature of a conformally rescaled metric ([`curvatureOperator_leviCivitaConnection_rescale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Conformal.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.curvatureOperator_leviCivitaConnection_rescale)), already for a `C¹` metric.

## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-06 (`fe8ca8b` to `385ec2d`)

[Einstein's constancy theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Einstein.html#CovariantDerivative.eq_of_ricciTensor_eq_smul_inner) landed: if Ric = a·g for a function a on a connected manifold of dimension other than two, then a is constant. The [Bochner formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Bochner/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient) for Δ|grad f|², once [integrated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Bochner/Integral.html#TauCeti.Manifold.integral_sum_hessian_sq_add_ricci), gives the [Lichnerowicz estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Lichnerowicz.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul): on a closed n-manifold, n ≥ 2, with Ric ≥ (n−1)k·g, every positive Laplace eigenvalue is at least nk. The equality case is not there.

Jacobi fields went from nothing to most of R2. Geodesics are now [smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Smoothness.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsGeodesicCurveOn.contMDiffOn_infty), and a [Jacobi field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn) along one has a conserved Wronskian, is fixed by its initial data, and splits into tangential and normal parts; variations through geodesics [produce Jacobi fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Variation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isJacobiFieldOn_variationField), and so do Killing fields. In constant curvature every Jacobi field is [an explicit sine–cosine combination](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Classification.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.exists_eq_affine_smul_add_curvatureCosine_smul_add_curvatureSine_smul), and in curvature κ > 0 a normal field vanishing at a [vanishes again](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/ConstantCurvature.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.apply_add_pi_div_eq_zero) at a + π/(c√κ). Jacobi fields also compute the exponential differential: [d(exp_p)_(tv)(tw) = J(t)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Exponential/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.eq_mfderiv_riemannianExp) when J(0) = 0 and D_tJ(0) = w ([TauCeti#25449](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25449)). In nonpositive curvature this differential [does not shrink norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Comparison.html#TauCeti.Manifold.norm_le_norm_mfderiv_riemannianExp_of_sectionalCurvature_nonpos), and exp is a [local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Nonsingular.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isLocalDiffeomorphOn_riemannianExp_of_sectionalCurvature_nonpos) on its domain ([TauCeti#26730](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26730)). Missing: existence for arbitrary initial data (the solution space is only shown to have [dimension at most 2n](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Space.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_jacobiFieldSpace_le)) and realization by variations.

Index theory and comparison geometry have started. [Conjugate points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Conjugate/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsConjugateOn) have multiplicity at most n − 1 ([TauCeti#26388](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26388)), equal to the [nullity of d exp](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/ExpKernel.html#TauCeti.Manifold.conjugateMultiplicity_eq_finrank_ker_mfderiv_riemannianExp) ([TauCeti#27565](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27565)). R3's module of piecewise C¹ fields ([TauCeti#27335](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27335)) and discrete path energy ([TauCeti#27260](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27260)) are in place. A complete noncompact manifold has a [ray](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Ray.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_isGeodesicCurveOn_isometry_domRestrict_Ici) from every point ([TauCeti#25826](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25826)).

Curvature calculus also grew. [Parallel curvature](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/LocallySymmetric.html#CovariantDerivative.HasParallelCurvature) follows from constant curvature, and on surfaces it is [equivalent to locally constant scalar curvature](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/LocallySymmetric.html#CovariantDerivative.hasParallelCurvature_iff_isLocallyConstant_scalarCurvature_of_finrank_eq_two). Bi-invariant Lie group metrics have curvature [−¼[[a,b],c]](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/LieGroup/BiInvariant.html#CovariantDerivative.IsLeviCivitaConnection.curvatureTensor_one) and hence nonnegative sectional curvature. Left-invariant metrics are complete. Warped products have O'Neill's horizontal, vertical and [mixed curvature formulas](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/WarpedProd.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsWarpedProduct.sectionalCurvature_horizontal_vertical) where the warping function is nonzero ([TauCeti#26685](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26685), [TauCeti#26699](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26699), [TauCeti#26701](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26701), [TauCeti#26705](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26705)), and Riemannian submersions have [horizontal lifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Submersion/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsRiemannianSubmersion.horizontalLift) ([TauCeti#26707](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26707)). Toward the Weitzenböck identities, the [number-operator identities](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/ExteriorPower/NumberOperator.html#Module.Basis.sum_wedgeLeft_comp_koszulDifferential_coord) on exterior powers ([TauCeti#27593](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27593)) and Lipschitz-preserving smoothing ([TauCeti#27206](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27206)) are in place. On the spherical-harmonics side, the Fischer decomposition and the [dimension of the harmonic polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Harmonic.html#MvPolynomial.finrank_harmonicSubmodule_add_two) landed, along with [injective restriction to the sphere](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/MvPolynomial/Sphere.html#MvPolynomial.harmonicSphereRestriction_injective). The link to the round sphere's Laplacian is still to come.
