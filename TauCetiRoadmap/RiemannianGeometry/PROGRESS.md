## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-24 (`0420037` to `fe8ca8b`)

The second Bianchi identity landed in full generality: for a smooth connection on any vector bundle, paired with a smooth torsion-free connection on the tangent bundle, the cyclic sum of covariant derivatives of the curvature vanishes ([`covariantDerivative_curvatureOperator_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/SecondBianchi.html#CovariantDerivative.covariantDerivative_curvatureOperator_cyclic_eq_zero)). The proof needed the Jacobi identity for the manifold Lie bracket ([`jacobi_identity_mlieBracket_apply`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/LieBracket.html#VectorField.jacobi_identity_mlieBracket_apply)) and smoothness of brackets and of the curvature operator.

Sectional curvature now determines the curvature tensor. An explicit polarization identity ([`six_mul_apply_eq_of_cyclic_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Multilinear/SectionalPolarization.html#LinearMap.six_mul_apply_eq_of_cyclic_eq_zero)) shows that the Riemann tensor is the only form with the curvature symmetries that has the given sectional numerators ([`riemannCurvatureTensor_eq_of_forall_linearIndependent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Riemann.html#CovariantDerivative.IsMetricCompatible.riemannCurvatureTensor_eq_of_forall_linearIndependent)). It follows that constant sectional curvature κ is the same as `R(w,u)v = κ(⟨u,v⟩w − ⟨w,v⟩u)` ([`hasConstantSectionalCurvature_iff_isConstantCurvatureTensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.hasConstantSectionalCurvature_iff_isConstantCurvatureTensor)), and so Ric = (n−1)κg and the scalar curvature is n(n−1)κ ([`ricciTensor_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.HasSectionalCurvatureAt.ricciTensor_eq), [`scalarCurvature_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Polarization.html#CovariantDerivative.HasSectionalCurvatureAt.scalarCurvature_eq)). Low dimensions are handled honestly. In dimension one or less there are no two-planes, so every κ qualifies ([`hasSectionalCurvatureAt_of_finrank_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Sectional.html#CovariantDerivative.hasSectionalCurvatureAt_of_finrank_le_one)), and from dimension two up κ is unique.

The third strand computes the curvature of a connection shifted by an endomorphism-valued one-form ([`curvatureOperator_addOneForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/AddOneForm.html#CovariantDerivative.curvatureOperator_addOneForm)). Applied to the Levi-Civita connection, it gives the curvature of a conformally rescaled metric ([`curvatureOperator_leviCivitaConnection_rescale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Conformal.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.curvatureOperator_leviCivitaConnection_rescale)), already for a `C¹` metric.

## RiemannianGeometry: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-06 (`fe8ca8b` to `a67a650`)

Jacobi fields arrived, and R2 went from nothing to most of the way there. A [Jacobi field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn) along a geodesic is determined by its initial value and derivative, and the variation field of any family of geodesics is one ([`isJacobiFieldOn_variationField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Variation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isJacobiFieldOn_variationField)). Every Jacobi field splits into a tangential part `(a + bt)γ′` and a normal part. In constant curvature κ it is written out explicitly with the model sine and cosine ([`exists_eq_affine_smul_add_curvatureCosine_smul_add_curvatureSine_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Classification.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsJacobiFieldOn.exists_eq_affine_smul_add_curvatureCosine_smul_add_curvatureSine_smul)), which gives the conjugate point at time `π/(√κ c)` for a geodesic of speed c. In nonpositive curvature the norms of Jacobi fields are convex and the exponential differential never shrinks a vector ([`norm_le_norm_mfderiv_riemannianExp_of_sectionalCurvature_nonpos`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Jacobi/Comparison.html#TauCeti.Manifold.norm_le_norm_mfderiv_riemannianExp_of_sectionalCurvature_nonpos)). Killing fields restrict to Jacobi fields (TauCeti#23717). Existence is still missing, so the solution space is only known to have dimension at most 2n.

On the curvature side, [Einstein's constancy theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Einstein.html#CovariantDerivative.eq_of_ricciTensor_eq_smul_inner) now says that `Ric = a g` forces `a` to be constant away from dimension two, and [locally symmetric surfaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/LocallySymmetric.html#CovariantDerivative.hasParallelCurvature_iff_isLocallyConstant_scalarCurvature_of_finrank_eq_two) are exactly those of locally constant curvature. For an Ad-invariant metric on a Lie group, `R(X,Y)Z = −¼[[X,Y],Z]` ([`curvatureTensor_leftInvariantMetric_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/LieGroup/AdjointInvariant.html#ContinuousLinearMap.curvatureTensor_leftInvariantMetric_one)), so sectional curvature is nonnegative. Supporting work added the Ricci identity, the curvature operator on bivectors, frame formulas, and the connections of scaled, product and warped-product metrics.

The scalar Bochner formula landed ([`laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Bochner/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.laplaceBeltrami_norm_sq_mgradient)) together with its integrated form, and with it the [Lichnerowicz estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Lichnerowicz/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.finrank_mul_le_of_laplaceBeltrami_eq_neg_mul): on a closed manifold with `Ric ≥ (n−1)kg`, every positive eigenvalue is at least `nk`. In the equality case the eigenfunction satisfies `Hess f = −kfg`. Harmonic polynomials also arrived, with the [Fischer decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Harmonic.html#MvPolynomial.harmonicSubmodule_sup_map_mulLeft_normSq) and the dimension formula.
