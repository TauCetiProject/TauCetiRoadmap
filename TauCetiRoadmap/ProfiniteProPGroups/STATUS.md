## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 6 (cohomological dimension) and Layer 10 (free groups on profinite spaces, of any rank) are complete. The Demushkin classification is done for `q ≠ 2`, where existence, normal form and uniqueness are all proved. At `q = 2` only existence and the span statements are proved. Demushkin duality has groundwork but none of its theorems.

### Named results

- **Serre's theorem at arbitrary rank** — a pro-`p` group has `cd_p ≤ 1` exactly when it is free pro-`p` on a pointed profinite space whose generators converge to the identity ([`TauCeti.IsProP.cohomologicalDimensionAt_le_one_iff_exists_convergesToOne_continuousMulEquiv_presentation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Pointed/Serre.html#TauCeti.IsProP.cohomologicalDimensionAt_le_one_iff_exists_convergesToOne_continuousMulEquiv_presentation)).
- **The pro-`p` Nielsen–Schreier theorem** — every closed subgroup of a free pro-`p` group, of any rank, is free pro-`p` ([`TauCeti.freeProP.exists_convergesToOne_continuousMulEquiv_of_isClosed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/ClosedSubgroup.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exists_convergesToOne_continuousMulEquiv_of_isClosed)). An open subgroup of index `m` in a free group of rank `n` has rank `1 + m(n - 1)`.
- **Labute's Theorem 4** — a Demushkin group has exactly one continuous character to `ℤ_pˣ` with the prescription property (`TauCeti.IsDemushkin.existsUnique_hasPrescriptionProperty`). When `q ≠ 2` its image is [`1 + qℤ_p`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/Character/Image.html#TauCeti.range_demushkinCharacter_eq_unitsPrincipal).
- **The classification of Demushkin groups with `q ≠ 2`** — at every prime, such a group has [Labute's normal form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/NeTwo.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.exists_continuousMulEquiv_presentedProP_demushkinWordNeTwo_of_demushkinQ_ne_two) `x₁^q (x₁, x₂) ⋯ (x_{n-1}, x_n)`. It is therefore [determined by its rank and `q`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_of_demushkinQ_eq). At `q = 2` those two invariants [do not suffice](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Character.html#TauCeti.exists_isDemushkin_demushkinQ_eq_two_isEmpty_continuousMulEquiv).
- **The existence theorem for Demushkin groups** — explicit one-relator groups realise every admissible pair of rank and orientation image, in [even rank](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isDemushkin_range_eq_of_even_of_lt), [odd rank](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isDemushkin_range_eq_unitsPlusMinus_of_odd) and [rank one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isDemushkin_one_range_eq_zpowers_neg_one).
