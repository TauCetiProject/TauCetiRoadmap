### Named results

- **The classification of Demushkin groups** — two Demushkin groups with the same rank and the same image of the canonical character [are isomorphic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_of_range_demushkinCharacter_eq). This covers Labute's Theorems 2, 3, 5 and 6, including the branch with image `{±1} × U^(f)` ([Theorem 6](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Two/Even/UnitsPlusMinus.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_of_even_demushkinRank_of_range_eq_unitsPlusMinus)). Every case also has a marked normal form, such as [the even-rank dyadic one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Marked.html#TauCeti.isDemushkin_marked_of_q_two_even).
- **Tate's theorem** — an infinite Demushkin group has [`cd_p G = 2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/CohomologicalDimension.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_two). Its cup pairings into `H²(G, I(χ)/pⁱ) ≅ ℤ/pⁱ` are [perfect on every finite module killed by `pⁱ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/Duality/Twisted.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.dualityMap1_zModTwist_bijective).
- **The open-subgroup theorem** — an [open subgroup `U` of an infinite Demushkin group `G` is Demushkin](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/OpenSubgroup.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.openSubgroup). It satisfies [`n(U) − 2 = [G : U](n(G) − 2)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/OpenSubgroup.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.demushkinRank_openSubgroup_sub_two), and its canonical character is [the restriction of that of `G`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/OpenSubgroup.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.demushkinCharacter_openSubgroup), so the classification identifies `U`. Conversely, the [recognition criteria](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/Recognition.html#TauCeti.isDemushkin_iff_finrank_cohomFp_two_openSubgroup) characterize Demushkin groups through their open normal subgroups.
- **Serre's theorem at arbitrary rank** — a pro-`p` group has `cd_p ≤ 1` exactly when it is [free pro-`p` on a pointed profinite space](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Pointed/Serre.html#TauCeti.IsProP.cohomologicalDimensionAt_le_one_iff_exists_convergesToOne_continuousMulEquiv_presentation). This gives the pro-`p` [Nielsen–Schreier theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/ClosedSubgroup.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exists_convergesToOne_continuousMulEquiv_of_isClosed).
- **The Golod–Shafarevich inequality** — a nontrivial finite `p`-group [satisfies `d(G)² < 4 r(G)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/GolodShafarevich.html#IsPGroup.sq_topologicalGeneratorRankNat_lt_four_mul_finrank_cohomFp_two), so it needs many relations relative to its generators.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Demushkin predicate** — [`TauCeti.IsDemushkin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin) agrees with Labute's cup-form definition. Its `q`-invariant and its canonical character, which is unique by Labute's Theorem 4, sort the groups into the normal-form cases.
- **Successive approximation** — [a relator that agrees with a model modulo the lower `p`-series can be moved onto it by an automorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/SuccessiveApproximation/Basic.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exists_continuousMulEquiv_apply_eq_of_range_basisModificationDelta_eq_top). The hypotheses are graded span conditions. At `p = 2` the degree-zero step needs the [polarization identity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/BasisModification/LevelZero.html#TauCeti.freeProP.basisModificationDeltaZero_add_of_two) of the basis-modification map. The dyadic cases of the classification were proved by approximation constrained to the kernel of the orientation (TauCeti#10458).
- **Lifting along an extension class** — when the pushforward of an extension along a coefficient map has the class of a second extension, the map [lifts to a continuous homomorphism of the extensions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/GroupExtension/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.ProfiniteGroupExtension.exists_continuous_monoidHom_of_contCohomologyClass_map_eq). This holds for compact kernels, which is the case `LocalGaloisGroups` uses.
