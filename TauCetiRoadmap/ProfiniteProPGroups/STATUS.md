## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The infinite-rank theory of Layer 10 is complete, and Serre's characterisation of free pro-`p` groups by `cd_p ≤ 1` holds at every rank. The Demushkin classification is half-built: the invariants, the canonical character and the existence theorem are proved, while uniqueness and the marked normal forms are not. Demushkin duality has not begun.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

Its first theorems are the abelianization structure theorem `G^{ab} ≅ ℤ_p^{n-1} × ℤ_p/q` and a well-defined [`q`-invariant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/QInvariant.html#TauCeti.demushkinQ), which together sort Demushkin groups into the cases the normal forms treat.
- **The successive-approximation theorem** — [a relator agreeing with a model modulo the lower `p`-series can be moved onto it by an automorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/SuccessiveApproximation.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exists_continuousMulEquiv_apply_eq_of_range_basisModificationDelta_eq_top) once the graded span conditions hold. This is the engine behind Labute's normal forms.
- **Labute's relation module** — [`TauCeti.labuteE`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/Labute/RelationModule.html#TauCeti.labuteE), the abelianized kernel of the orientation with its completed-group-algebra action. Together with the earlier power-series and dyadic coordinates it sets up the `q = 2` even-rank case.

## The frontier

- **Exact normal forms in the remaining cases** — extend successive approximation beyond odd `p` with `q = p`, to `q = p^f`, `q = 0` and the dyadic odd-rank case, where the span statement is already proved.
- **Labute's module criteria** — the generator expression, the principal annihilator and the membership criterion for the relator class. These unlock the `q = 2` even-rank arguments of Labute's Theorems 5 and 6.
- **Uniqueness and the marked classification** — Labute's Theorem 2 and the four marked statements. They need the complete set of normal forms above.
- **Demushkin duality** — `cd_p = 2` for infinite Demushkin groups, the perfect pairings, the open-subgroup theorem and the recognition criteria. They need the open-subgroup equality for `cd_p` from Layer 6.
- **The Sylow equality `cd_p G = cd_p G_p`** — now reachable, since the Layer 2 existence and conjugacy theorems are present.
