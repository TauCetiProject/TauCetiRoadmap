### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Demushkin predicate** — [`TauCeti.IsDemushkin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin) agrees with Labute's cup-form definition. Its [`q`-invariant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/QInvariant.html#TauCeti.demushkinQ) and canonical character sort the groups into the cases that the normal forms treat.
- **Successive approximation** — [a relator that agrees with a model modulo the lower `p`-series can be moved onto it by an automorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/SuccessiveApproximation/Basic.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exists_continuousMulEquiv_apply_eq_of_range_basisModificationDelta_eq_top) once the graded span conditions hold. A constrained version inside the kernel of the orientation is what proves Theorem 5.
- **Tate's duality maps** — `H^i(G, M) → Hom(H^{2-i}(G, Hom(M, N)), H²(G, N))` in degrees 0, 1 and 2, with projection formulas for restriction and corestriction. For a Demushkin group they are bijective at `𝔽_p`, for example [in degree 1](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/Duality.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.dualityMap1_bijective), and the cup square is [a perfect pairing](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/CupForm.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.cupFp_bijective).
