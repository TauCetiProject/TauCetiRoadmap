## ProfiniteProPGroups: 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-01 (`cf980b4` to `8745177`)

The first material on this roadmap is foundational: most of Layer 0 and the opening definition of Layer 3. The **limit description of a profinite group** now exists in the unbundled form the later layers consume ([`TauCeti.existsUnique_forall_mk_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Limit.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_forall_mk_eq)): a compatible family of elements of the finite quotients comes from a unique element. Beside it sits the **correspondence theorem for open normal subgroups** ([`TauCeti.QuotientGroup.comapMk'OpenNormalOrderIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Quotient.html#TauCeti.QuotientGroup.comapMk'OpenNormalOrderIso)), a lattice isomorphism between the open normal subgroups of `G ⧸ N` and those of `G` above `N`; the quotient of a profinite group by a closed normal subgroup is totally disconnected ([`TauCeti.QuotientGroup.instTotallyDisconnectedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Basic.html#TauCeti.QuotientGroup.instTotallyDisconnectedSpace)), which the README names as the one missing ingredient for `G ⧸ N` to be profinite; and the open normal subgroups of a profinite group intersect trivially ([`Subgroup.iInf_openNormalSubgroup_eq_bot`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Basic.html#Subgroup.iInf_openNormalSubgroup_eq_bot)) and are cofinal among the open subgroups above any closed subgroup ([`Subgroup.eq_iInf_sup_openNormalSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Basic.html#Subgroup.eq_iInf_sup_openNormalSubgroup)).

The **universal property of the profinite completion** is exposed without the category ([`TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.continuousMonoidHomEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Completion.html#TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.continuousMonoidHomEquiv)): continuous homomorphisms from the completion to a profinite group correspond to abstract homomorphisms from the original group, with extensionality along the dense image ([`TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.continuousMonoidHom_ext`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Completion.html#TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.continuousMonoidHom_ext)) and bijectivity of the unit on a finite group ([`TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.etaFn_bijective_of_finite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Completion.html#TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.etaFn_bijective_of_finite)). From Layer 3, the frozen predicate `IsProP` ([`TauCeti.IsProP`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#TauCeti.IsProP)), every quotient by an open normal subgroup being a `p`-group, landed with its covariant API: an abstract `p`-group is pro-`p` in any topology ([`IsPGroup.isProP`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#IsPGroup.isProP)), the property passes to continuous surjective images ([`TauCeti.IsProP.of_surjective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#TauCeti.IsProP.of_surjective)) and to quotients by arbitrary normal subgroups ([`TauCeti.IsProP.quotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#TauCeti.IsProP.quotient)), and on a discrete group it is just being a `p`-group ([`TauCeti.isProP_iff_isPGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#TauCeti.isProP_iff_isPGroup)) (TauCeti#5264).

What is not there: Layer 0's instance chain, the theorem that open means closed of finite index, and the compactness lemma for directed families of closed sets; for `IsProP`, closed subgroups, finite products, inverse limits, and the equivalence with limits of finite `p`-groups. Layers 1, 2 and 4 through 10 are untouched.

## ProfiniteProPGroups: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-26 (`8745177` to `759eb3e`)

Serre’s theorem now reaches arbitrary rank: a pro-`p` group of cohomological dimension at most one is [free on a pointed profinite space](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Pointed/Serre.html#TauCeti.IsProP.exists_convergesToOne_continuousMulEquiv_presentation_of_cohomologicalDimensionAt_le_one), with generators converging to the identity. The [pointed free pro-`C` construction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Pointed/Basic.html#TauCeti.freeProCPointed.existsUnique_lift) supplies the universal property behind this statement. This is the implication from `cd_p ≤ 1`; the broader arbitrary-rank theory still lacks the comparison of the two free constructions on an infinite set and the closed-subgroup theorem.

The finite-rank theory gained [Burnside’s basis theorem in cardinal form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/DualRank.html#TauCeti.IsProP.topologicalGeneratorRank_eq_rank_continuousZModDual), identifying generator rank with the dimension of the continuous `𝔽_p`-dual, and [finite-quotient determinacy](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/FiniteQuotients.html#TauCeti.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_of_forall_isFiniteContinuousQuotient_iff): the same finite continuous quotients determine a profinite group up to isomorphism when one group is topologically finitely generated. Presentations, relation-rank calculations, and the lower `p`-series comparison machinery developed alongside these results.

For the eventual Demushkin classification, the completed group algebra has [power-series coordinates in the procyclic case](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/CompletedGroupAlgebra/PowerSeries.html#TauCeti.completedGroupAlgebra.powerSeriesCoordinate) and a [dyadic coordinate over `ℤ₂[C₂]`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/CompletedGroupAlgebra/DyadicCoordinate.html#TauCeti.completedGroupAlgebra.dyadicCoordinate). The latter is a group ring, with [no integral splitting into its two eigenspaces](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/GroupAlgebraCyclicTwo.html#TauCeti.monoidAlgebraPadicIntCyclicTwo_isIdempotentElem_iff). The Demushkin predicate, Labute’s relation module, and the classification itself have not landed.

## ProfiniteProPGroups: 2026-09-30 to 2026-09-30 (`dec7a58` to `0d3161a`)

Demushkin groups with `q ≠ 2` are now classified. For every prime, such a group has [Labute's normal form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/NeTwo.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.exists_continuousMulEquiv_presentedProP_demushkinWordNeTwo_of_demushkinQ_ne_two) `x₁^q (x₁, x₂) ⋯ (x_{n-1}, x_n)`. That extends the earlier odd-`p`, `q = p` case to every `q ≠ 2`. It also gives [uniqueness](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_of_demushkinQ_eq): two Demushkin groups with the same rank and the same `q ≠ 2` are isomorphic. The inputs are Labute's constrained span statement for the kernel of an exponent sum and a graded functional attached to crossed homomorphisms of the orientation (TauCeti#10164, TauCeti#10193). The corollary to Labute's Theorem 4 also landed: the canonical character has image [`1 + qℤ_p` when `q ≠ 2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/Character/Image.html#TauCeti.range_demushkinCharacter_eq_unitsPrincipal). At `q = 2` the orientation images of all the dyadic normal forms are computed, and they show that [rank and `q` do not determine the group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/NormalForm/Character.html#TauCeti.exists_isDemushkin_demushkinQ_eq_two_isEmpty_continuousMulEquiv).

Layer 6 now has its last two milestones. The first is Serre's theorem that [`cd_p U = cd_p G` for open `U`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/OpenSubgroup.html#TauCeti.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_isOpen_of_ne_top) when `cd_p G` is finite. The second is the [Sylow equality `cd_p G = cd_p P`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Sylow/CohomologicalDimension.html#TauCeti.IsProPSylow.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq), which rests on injectivity of restriction to subgroups of index prime to `p`.

The `q = 2` case is well prepared but not finished. The [dyadic span statement](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/BasisModification.html#TauCeti.freeProP.range_basisModificationDelta_sup_gradedPowIterSpan_compl_eq_top_two) of Labute's Proposition 5 is proved, including at the even-rank normal form, and Labute's module has explicit generators in one even-rank branch. The exact `q = 2` normal forms, the module's annihilator and membership criteria, and the marked classification are still missing. Groundwork for duality also appeared: the dual of a short exact sequence, and the adjointness of cup products with connecting maps. There are no perfect pairings yet.
