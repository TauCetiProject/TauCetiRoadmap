## ProfiniteProPGroups: 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-01 (`cf980b4` to `8745177`)

The first material on this roadmap is foundational: most of Layer 0 and the opening definition of Layer 3. The **limit description of a profinite group** now exists in the unbundled form the later layers consume ([`TauCeti.existsUnique_forall_mk_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Limit.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_forall_mk_eq)): a compatible family of elements of the finite quotients comes from a unique element. Beside it sits the **correspondence theorem for open normal subgroups** ([`TauCeti.QuotientGroup.comapMk'OpenNormalOrderIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Quotient.html#TauCeti.QuotientGroup.comapMk'OpenNormalOrderIso)), a lattice isomorphism between the open normal subgroups of `G ⧸ N` and those of `G` above `N`; the quotient of a profinite group by a closed normal subgroup is totally disconnected ([`TauCeti.QuotientGroup.instTotallyDisconnectedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Basic.html#TauCeti.QuotientGroup.instTotallyDisconnectedSpace)), which the README names as the one missing ingredient for `G ⧸ N` to be profinite; and the open normal subgroups of a profinite group intersect trivially ([`Subgroup.iInf_openNormalSubgroup_eq_bot`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Basic.html#Subgroup.iInf_openNormalSubgroup_eq_bot)) and are cofinal among the open subgroups above any closed subgroup ([`Subgroup.eq_iInf_sup_openNormalSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Basic.html#Subgroup.eq_iInf_sup_openNormalSubgroup)).

The **universal property of the profinite completion** is exposed without the category ([`TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.continuousMonoidHomEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Completion.html#TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.continuousMonoidHomEquiv)): continuous homomorphisms from the completion to a profinite group correspond to abstract homomorphisms from the original group, with extensionality along the dense image ([`TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.continuousMonoidHom_ext`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Completion.html#TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.continuousMonoidHom_ext)) and bijectivity of the unit on a finite group ([`TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.etaFn_bijective_of_finite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Completion.html#TauCeti.ProfiniteCompletion.etaFn_bijective_of_finite)). From Layer 3, the frozen predicate `IsProP` ([`TauCeti.IsProP`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#TauCeti.IsProP)), every quotient by an open normal subgroup being a `p`-group, landed with its covariant API: an abstract `p`-group is pro-`p` in any topology ([`IsPGroup.isProP`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#IsPGroup.isProP)), the property passes to continuous surjective images ([`TauCeti.IsProP.of_surjective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#TauCeti.IsProP.of_surjective)) and to quotients by arbitrary normal subgroups ([`TauCeti.IsProP.quotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#TauCeti.IsProP.quotient)), and on a discrete group it is just being a `p`-group ([`TauCeti.isProP_iff_isPGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP.html#TauCeti.isProP_iff_isPGroup)) (TauCeti#5264).

What is not there: Layer 0's instance chain, the theorem that open means closed of finite index, and the compactness lemma for directed families of closed sets; for `IsProP`, closed subgroups, finite products, inverse limits, and the equivalence with limits of finite `p`-groups. Layers 1, 2 and 4 through 10 are untouched.

## ProfiniteProPGroups: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-26 (`8745177` to `de52a34`)

Serre’s theorem now identifies a topologically finitely generated pro-`p` group of cohomological dimension at most one with a finite-rank free pro-`p` group ([`TauCeti.IsProP.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_freeProP_of_cohomologicalDimensionAt_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Serre.html#TauCeti.IsProP.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_freeProP_of_cohomologicalDimensionAt_le_one)). Profinite Sylow subgroups now [exist](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Sylow/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isProPSylow) and are [conjugate](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Sylow/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.IsProPSylow.exists_map_conj_eq); [finite-quotient determinacy](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/FiniteQuotients.html#TauCeti.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_of_forall_isFiniteContinuousQuotient_iff) says that a topologically finitely generated profinite group is determined up to topological isomorphism by its continuous finite quotients.

The groundwork is broad: [Burnside’s basis theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/DualRank.html#TauCeti.IsProP.topologicalGeneratorRank_eq_rank_continuousZModDual) identifies generator rank with the dimension of the continuous `𝔽_p`-dual, and the [structure theorem for finitely generated abelian pro-`p` groups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/StructureTheorem.html#TauCeti.IsProP.exists_continuousMulEquiv_pi_padicInt_prod_pi_zmod) gives a product of a finite `p`-group with a finite power of `ℤ_p`. The [completed group algebra of an infinite procyclic pro-`p` group has power-series coordinates](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/CompletedGroupAlgebra/PowerSeries.html#TauCeti.completedGroupAlgebra.powerSeriesCoordinate). The free-group theorem has a finite-generation hypothesis; the Demushkin classification and the arbitrary-rank free theory are not established here.
