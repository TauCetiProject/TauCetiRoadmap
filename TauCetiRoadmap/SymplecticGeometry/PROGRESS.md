## SymplecticGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-23 (`8387174` to `ddc3f97`)

The cotangent bundle of a smooth manifold is now a symplectic manifold in Tau Ceti. The Liouville form [`liouvilleForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.liouvilleForm), λ(v) = p(dπ v), is built globally on [`CotangentBundle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Cotangent.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle), and is tautological: [pulling it back along a one-form returns that form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.mpullback_liouvilleForm). Its negated derivative ω = −dλ is [closed and nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isSymplectic_symplecticForm), and [in every bundle chart it is the existing linear form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.inChartAt_symplecticForm_apply), which confirms that no sign convention moved (TauCeti#19402). Diffeomorphisms have [cotangent lifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Diffeomorph/Cotangent.html#Diffeomorph.cotangent) that [preserve ω](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Lift.html#Diffeomorph.mpullback_cotangent_symplecticForm) and λ. Translating fibres by a one-form α shifts λ by π*α, and [preserves ω exactly when α is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Fiberwise.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.mpullback_vectorBundleTranslateDiffeomorph_symplecticForm_eq_self_iff); dilating fibres rescales both forms. Underneath this sit chart transition formulas, the fact that bundle projections are submersions, and an identification of one-forms with sections of the cotangent bundle.

On the linear side, Lagrangian subspaces are now shown to be [exactly the maximal isotropic ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isLagrangian_iff_maximal_isIsotropic), in any dimension. Coisotropic subspaces [have at least half the dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsCoisotropic.finrank_le_two_mul_finrank). [Symplectic subspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsSymplectic) get their own predicate, [split the space with their complement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isSymplectic_iff_isCompl), and behave as expected under products, transport and rescaling. Separately, `AddCircle p` became [a smooth manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/AddCircle.html#AddCircle.instIsManifold) whose projection from ℝ is [a local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/AddCircle.html#AddCircle.isLocalDiffeomorph_coe). This is the start of the positive-period circle foundation that the integrable-systems and suspension work will build on.

Still missing from the cotangent layer: cotangent bundles of products and of open subsets, equivariance of lifts under group actions, and pullback along maps that are not diffeomorphisms. The Lagrangian work is still entirely linear, with no Lagrangian embeddings yet.

## SymplecticGeometry: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-07 (`ddc3f97` to `e583e15`)

Contact geometry went from nothing to a working theory. A one-form is a [contact form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Basic.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.IsContactForm) exactly when [α ∧ (dα)ⁿ vanishes nowhere](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Volume.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.isContactForm_iff_wedge_wedgePow_apply_ne_zero), and exactly when [the Levi form of its kernel is nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Levi.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.isContactForm_iff_forall_bijective_distributionLeviForm). Built on top of this are the [Reeb field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Reeb.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.reebVectorField), [contact Hamiltonian vector fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Hamiltonian.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.contactHamiltonianVectorField) and their flows, and a [Jacobi bracket that satisfies the Jacobi identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Jacobi.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.IsContactForm.jacobiBracket_jacobi). The form dz − θ [is contact exactly when −dθ is nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Contactization.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.isContactForm_contactization_iff), so T*M × ℝ is a contact manifold in which [one-jets of functions are Legendrian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/OneJet.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isLegendrianEmbedding_contactization_liouvilleForm). The big theorems are still missing: contact Darboux, Gray stability and the Legendrian neighbourhood theorem. Only their ingredients are in place, namely a [contact-Moser vector field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Moser.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.contactMoserVectorField) and a pointwise linear normal form.

On the matrix side, [polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Polar.html#Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.existsUnique_posDef_mul_unitary) is proved with uniqueness. The [real powers of a positive symplectic matrix stay symplectic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Polar/Symplectic.html#SymplecticGroup.rpow_mem), and from this [Sp(2n, ℝ) is path connected](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SymplecticGroup/Connected.html#Matrix.isPathConnected_symplecticGroup), as are U(n) and GL(n, ℂ). The [Schur–Horn theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/UnitaryGroup/SchurHorn.html#Matrix.range_re_diag_unitary_mul_diagonal_mul_star) landed in both directions: the diagonals of the unitary conjugates of diag(w) form exactly the permutahedron of w. The [Kodaira–Thurston manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/KodairaThurston.html#TauCeti.KodairaThurston) now exists, with [fundamental group H(ℤ) × ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/UniversalCover/KodairaThurston.html#TauCeti.KodairaThurston.fundamentalGroupMulEquiv) and [first Betti number three](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/KodairaThurston.html#TauCeti.KodairaThurston.finrank_deRhamCohomology_one). Its symplectic form and the non-Kähler argument have not landed.

Much of the remaining work laid foundations. The [smooth mapping torus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/MappingTorus.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus) has its suspension flow, time and fibre forms, orientation and [closed orbits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/MappingTorus/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus.closedOrbit), but no symplectic form Ω yet. Positive-period circles gained a [time form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/AddCircle.html#AddCircle.timeForm), orientations, tori and families. Coadjoint stabilizers became Lie subgroups, the KKS form exists algebraically, and fundamental vector fields of actions were set up. The extraction for this window was truncated, so this is not a complete survey.
