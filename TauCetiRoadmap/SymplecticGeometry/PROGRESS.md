## SymplecticGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-23 (`8387174` to `ddc3f97`)

The cotangent bundle of a smooth manifold is now a symplectic manifold in Tau Ceti. The Liouville form [`liouvilleForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.liouvilleForm), λ(v) = p(dπ v), is built globally on [`CotangentBundle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Cotangent.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle), and is tautological: [pulling it back along a one-form returns that form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.mpullback_liouvilleForm). Its negated derivative ω = −dλ is [closed and nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isSymplectic_symplecticForm), and [in every bundle chart it is the existing linear form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.inChartAt_symplecticForm_apply), which confirms that no sign convention moved (TauCeti#19402). Diffeomorphisms have [cotangent lifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Diffeomorph/Cotangent.html#Diffeomorph.cotangent) that [preserve ω](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Lift.html#Diffeomorph.mpullback_cotangent_symplecticForm) and λ. Translating fibres by a one-form α shifts λ by π*α, and [preserves ω exactly when α is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Fiberwise.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.mpullback_vectorBundleTranslateDiffeomorph_symplecticForm_eq_self_iff); dilating fibres rescales both forms. Underneath this sit chart transition formulas, the fact that bundle projections are submersions, and an identification of one-forms with sections of the cotangent bundle.

On the linear side, Lagrangian subspaces are now shown to be [exactly the maximal isotropic ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isLagrangian_iff_maximal_isIsotropic), in any dimension. Coisotropic subspaces [have at least half the dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsCoisotropic.finrank_le_two_mul_finrank). [Symplectic subspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsSymplectic) get their own predicate, [split the space with their complement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isSymplectic_iff_isCompl), and behave as expected under products, transport and rescaling. Separately, `AddCircle p` became [a smooth manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/AddCircle.html#AddCircle.instIsManifold) whose projection from ℝ is [a local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/AddCircle.html#AddCircle.isLocalDiffeomorph_coe). This is the start of the positive-period circle foundation that the integrable-systems and suspension work will build on.

Still missing from the cotangent layer: cotangent bundles of products and of open subsets, equivariance of lifts under group actions, and pullback along maps that are not diffeomorphisms. The Lagrangian work is still entirely linear, with no Lagrangian embeddings yet.

## SymplecticGeometry: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-07 (`ddc3f97` to `08a51bc`)

Contact geometry arrived, from definitions to a working calculus. A one-form is contact exactly when the Levi form of its kernel is nondegenerate, or equivalently, by [the contact volume criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Volume.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.isContactForm_iff_wedge_wedgePow_apply_ne_zero), when α ∧ (dα)ⁿ vanishes nowhere. Contact forms carry [Reeb fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Reeb.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.reebVectorField), each function has a [unique contact Hamiltonian field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Hamiltonian.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.IsContactForm.isContactHamiltonianVectorField_iff_eq), and the resulting [Jacobi bracket satisfies the Jacobi identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Jacobi.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.IsContactForm.jacobiBracket_jacobi). Contactization links this to symplectic geometry: [the one-jet space T*M × ℝ is contact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Contactization.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isContactForm_contactization_liouvilleForm), and [exact Lagrangian maps are exactly the projections of horizontal lifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Contactization.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.isExactLagrangianMap_iff_isHorizontalMap_contactization). Contact Darboux, Gray stability, symplectization and the Legendrian neighbourhood theorems have not landed; toward them there is only the linear normal form at a point and the contact-Moser equation.

On the matrix side, the Schur–Horn theorem is proved in both directions: the diagonals of [a symmetric operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SchurHorn.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.range_re_inner_apply_self_eq_convexHull_eigenvalues_comp_perm), or of [the unitary orbit of a diagonal matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/UnitaryGroup/SchurHorn.html#Matrix.range_re_diag_unitary_mul_diagonal_mul_star), fill exactly the convex hull of the permuted eigenvalues. [Polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Polar.html#Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.existsUnique_posDef_mul_unitary) and a smooth matrix logarithm give [path-connectedness of the real symplectic group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SymplecticGroup/Connected.html#Matrix.isPathConnected_symplecticGroup), U(n) and GL(n,ℂ). For group actions, [coadjoint stabilizers are embedded Lie subgroups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Adjoint/Representation/Coadjoint/Stabilizer.html#TauCeti.Lie.isEmbeddedLieSubgroup_stabilizer_coadjointRepresentation), and the [KKS form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Coadjoint.html#Module.Dual.kksForm) is computed for so(3) and the Heisenberg algebra, though only on the algebraic tangent model, not on orbit manifolds.

The circle became [a Lie group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/AddCircle.html#AddCircle.instLieAddGroup) with time form and orientation, and on it sits the [smooth mapping torus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/MappingTorus.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus) with its forms, suspension flow and [embedded closed orbits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/MappingTorus/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus.isSmoothEmbedding_closedOrbit_iff). The [Kodaira–Thurston manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/KodairaThurston.html#TauCeti.KodairaThurston) has [fundamental group H(ℤ) × ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/UniversalCover/KodairaThurston.html#TauCeti.KodairaThurston.fundamentalGroupMulEquiv) and [first Betti number three](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/KodairaThurston.html#TauCeti.KodairaThurston.finrank_deRhamCohomology_one). Part of this window's work lies in modules this report did not survey, so whether the symplectic forms on the suspension and on Kodaira–Thurston landed is not established here.
