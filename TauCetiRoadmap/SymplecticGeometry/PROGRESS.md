## SymplecticGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-23 (`8387174` to `ddc3f97`)

The cotangent bundle of a smooth manifold is now a symplectic manifold in Tau Ceti. The Liouville form [`liouvilleForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.liouvilleForm), λ(v) = p(dπ v), is built globally on [`CotangentBundle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Cotangent.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle), and is tautological: [pulling it back along a one-form returns that form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.mpullback_liouvilleForm). Its negated derivative ω = −dλ is [closed and nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isSymplectic_symplecticForm), and [in every bundle chart it is the existing linear form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Liouville.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.inChartAt_symplecticForm_apply), which confirms that no sign convention moved (TauCeti#19402). Diffeomorphisms have [cotangent lifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Diffeomorph/Cotangent.html#Diffeomorph.cotangent) that [preserve ω](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Lift.html#Diffeomorph.mpullback_cotangent_symplecticForm) and λ. Translating fibres by a one-form α shifts λ by π*α, and [preserves ω exactly when α is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Fiberwise.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.mpullback_vectorBundleTranslateDiffeomorph_symplecticForm_eq_self_iff); dilating fibres rescales both forms. Underneath this sit chart transition formulas, the fact that bundle projections are submersions, and an identification of one-forms with sections of the cotangent bundle.

On the linear side, Lagrangian subspaces are now shown to be [exactly the maximal isotropic ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isLagrangian_iff_maximal_isIsotropic), in any dimension. Coisotropic subspaces [have at least half the dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsCoisotropic.finrank_le_two_mul_finrank). [Symplectic subspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsSymplectic) get their own predicate, [split the space with their complement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/Basic.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isSymplectic_iff_isCompl), and behave as expected under products, transport and rescaling. Separately, `AddCircle p` became [a smooth manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/AddCircle.html#AddCircle.instIsManifold) whose projection from ℝ is [a local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/AddCircle.html#AddCircle.isLocalDiffeomorph_coe). This is the start of the positive-period circle foundation that the integrable-systems and suspension work will build on.

Still missing from the cotangent layer: cotangent bundles of products and of open subsets, equivariance of lifts under group actions, and pullback along maps that are not diffeomorphisms. The Lagrangian work is still entirely linear, with no Lagrangian embeddings yet.

## SymplecticGeometry: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-05 (`ddc3f97` to `6b391d7`)

The headline result is the classification of cotangent lifts: a diffeomorphism T*M → T*N is [the lift of a base diffeomorphism exactly when it preserves the Liouville form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Classification.html#Diffeomorph.exists_cotangent_eq_iff_mpullback_liouvilleForm). The cotangent layer also gained open restrictions, products, the cotangent relation along a general smooth map, and equivariant lifts. On the matrix side, [polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Polar.html#Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.existsUnique_posDef_mul_unitary) landed with uniqueness, smooth dependence and a contraction to the unitary factor that [stays inside the symplectic group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Polar/Symplectic.html#SymplecticGroup.joinedIn_polarUnitary). So [Sp(2n,ℝ) is path connected](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SymplecticGroup/Connected.html#Matrix.isPathConnected_symplecticGroup), as are U(n) and GL(n,ℂ). The [Schur–Horn theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/UnitaryGroup/SchurHorn.html#Matrix.range_re_diag_unitary_mul_diagonal_mul_star) also landed: the diagonals of a Hermitian matrix's unitary orbit fill exactly the convex hull of its permuted eigenvalues.

Contact geometry went from nothing to a working floor. A one-form is [contact exactly when the Levi form of its kernel is nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Levi.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.isContactForm_iff_forall_bijective_distributionLeviForm). Reeb fields, contact Hamiltonian fields and their flows have their basic theory, and the Jacobi bracket [satisfies the Jacobi identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Jacobi.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.IsContactForm.jacobiBracket_jacobi). The contactization dz − θ is [contact exactly when −dθ is nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Contactization.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.isContactForm_contactization_iff), and one-jets of functions are [Legendrian embeddings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Legendrian.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isLegendrianEmbedding_oneJet). The contact-Moser equation is solved pointwise, but contact Darboux and Gray stability are not here yet.

The suspension layer now has the [smooth mapping torus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/MappingTorus.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus), with its time form, fibre form and suspension flow, and closed orbits that are [embedded exactly at the least period](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/MappingTorus/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus.isSmoothEmbedding_closedOrbit_iff). The symplectic form ds∧dt + ω_v and the block almost-complex structure are still missing. Smaller threads: the circle became a Lie group with time form and orientation, the positive-period circle family was built, a nondegenerate algebraic KKS form arrived, and the Kodaira–Thurston manifold exists as a compact quotient, though its symplectic form is not visible in this window's evidence.
