- **Neighbourhood classification.** Extend the normal-bundle construction to the isotropic contact theorem and the twisted one-jet germ; the symplectic side still needs the Lagrangian and coisotropic embedding theorems.
- **General moment-map convexity.** Construct the proper-orbit symplectic slice and convexity charts at nontrivial isotropy, then the nonabelian cross-section argument.
- **Delzant uniqueness.** Build and glue equivariant local models over faces to classify toric manifolds with the same moment polytope.
- **Duistermaat–Heckman density.** Establish variation of reduced classes and polynomial density, retaining finite isotropy in locally free levels.
- **The strict annulus theorem.** Complete the isotopy, twist factorization, injectivity and flow cup-length arguments leading to two distinct fixed points.
