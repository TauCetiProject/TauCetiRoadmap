## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Done: the intrinsic cotangent phase space, correspondences and generating functions, Lagrangian mechanics, and contact Darboux and Gray stability. Every other layer is partial, though most now have their central construction: the reduced space, the KKS form, the Delzant quotient and global action–angle coordinates. Not yet proved: Delzant classification, convexity for general tori and Kirwan convexity, Duistermaat–Heckman densities, Weinstein's Lagrangian neighbourhood theorem and the annulus fixed-point theorem.

### Named results

- **The Marsden–Weinstein–Meyer theorem** — for a proper Hamiltonian action, the reduced space μ⁻¹(c)/G_c is a smooth manifold and [carries a unique symplectic form pulling back to the restricted one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Hamiltonian/Reduction/ReducedSpace.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsEquivariantMomentMap.existsUnique_isSymplectic_mpullback_quotientMk_eq_mpullback). [Cotangent reduction at zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Hamiltonian/Reduction/Cotangent/ReducedSpace.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.zeroReducedSpaceDiffeomorph) identifies μ⁻¹(0)/G with T*(Q/G).
- **The Delzant construction** — every Delzant polytope gives a [symplectic manifold with a Hamiltonian torus action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Delzant/Quotient/Hamiltonian.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsDelzant.hamiltonianTorusData_delzantQuotientSymplecticForm) whose fixed points are exactly the vertices.
- **Global action–angle coordinates** — a regular Lagrangian torus fibration with a Lagrangian section and exact periods [is symplectomorphic to its model T*B/Λ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Hamiltonian/Liouville/Period/Model.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.IsLagrangianTorusFibration.exists_actionAngle_of_lagrangian_section). Conversely, global coordinates force a section.
- **Gray's stability theorem** — on a compact manifold, a smooth family of contact structures [is carried into itself by an isotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Contact/Gray/Relative.html#TauCeti.IsContactDistributionFamily.exists_diffeomorph_map_mfderiv_eq). No chosen contact forms or coorientation are needed, and there is a relative version near a set where the structures do not move.
- **Perfect Morse heights on coadjoint orbits** — on a coadjoint orbit of a compact Lie group, [every Morse height is perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Coadjoint/Height/Perfect.html#Module.Dual.isPerfectMorse_orbitMap_coadjointRepresentation_apply). Odd Betti numbers vanish and the orbits are simply connected.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Kirillov–Kostant–Souriau form** on the orbit manifold G/G_ν. It is [symplectic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Coadjoint/Orbit.html#Module.Dual.isSymplectic_coadjointOrbitForm) and makes the orbit map an equivariant moment map.
- **Quotients of Lie groups.** The [quotient manifold theorem for closed subgroups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Quotient.html#Subgroup.isManifold_orbitRelQuotientOfIsClosed) is in, along with Lie group structure on G/N and balanced products G ×_K Z.
- **The local-to-global principle for convexity.** A proper map with convexity charts everywhere [has convex image](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/LocalToGlobal.html#IsProperMap.convex_range_of_isConvexChart) and connected fibres. Charts now exist at locally free points, which gives Atiyah's theorem for circle actions.

## The frontier

- **Torus and Kirwan convexity (T3).** The moment image lies in the hull of the fixed-point images. Equality holds when isotropy is trivial off the fixed points. What remains are convexity charts at points with nontrivial isotropy, built from the slice model, and then Kirwan's nonabelian version and Kostant's theorem for general compact groups.
- **Delzant classification (T5).** What remains is uniqueness up to equivariant symplectomorphism, and the fibre and face-isotropy theorems on general toric manifolds.
- **Lagrangian and Legendrian neighbourhoods (C4.7, CG5).** The symplectic neighbourhood theorem and Legendrian normal-bundle identification are in. Weinstein's Lagrangian neighbourhood theorem, the coisotropic embedding theorem and the strict Legendrian neighbourhood theorem are not.
- **The strict annulus fixed-point theorem (I5).** The reduction to the standard area form, the cyclic action and the exit-time deformation are in. Still missing are the isotopy to the identity, the twist factorization, the cup-length bound and the final two-fixed-point statement.
- **Duistermaat–Heckman (T4).** What remains is absolute continuity and polynomial density of the pushforward measure on chambers, together with the level orbibundles and the variation of reduced classes.
