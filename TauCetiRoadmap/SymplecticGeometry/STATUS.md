## The frontier

- **The reduced space (R3)** — combine the null directions of a regular level's form with the descent criterion to build the reduced symplectic manifold.
- **Local action-angle coordinates (I2)** — construct actions and angles with `ω = Σ dθᵢ ∧ dIᵢ` near a compact regular fiber from the smooth period functions.
- **The homological step of S2** — show that a class in the image of `[S¹_s] × H₁(Y_φ; ℤ)` pairs to zero with `ω_v`, so the zero-vertical-area classification applies.
- **Relative Gray stability (CG3)** — Turn the invariant horizontal Moser equation into a neighborhood-germ isotopy fixing the prescribed closed subset, retaining the flow-domain argument without compactness.
- **Smooth coadjoint-orbit forms (R2)** — Assemble the algebraic form on the actual homogeneous quotient and prove smoothness, closedness and the orbit moment-map identity.
