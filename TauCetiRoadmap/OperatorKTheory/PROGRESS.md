## OperatorKTheory: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`1be2b5c` to `522d48f`)

The first K-group has arrived. The [projection monoid V(R)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/ProjectionMonoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.ProjectionMonoid) collects matrix projections of every size up to Murray–von Neumann equivalence, and the [projection K₀-group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/ProjectionMonoid/KZero.html#TauCeti.ProjectionKZero) is its Grothendieck group. It is functorial for star homomorphisms, unital or not, every class is a [difference [p] − [q]](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/ProjectionMonoid/KZero.html#TauCeti.ProjectionKZero.exists_eq_sub), and the [equality criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/ProjectionMonoid/KZero.html#TauCeti.ProjectionKZero.of_mk_sub_of_mk_eq_of_mk_sub_of_mk_iff) says [p] − [q] = [p′] − [q′] exactly when p ⊕ q′ ⊕ r and p′ ⊕ q ⊕ r are equivalent for some projection r (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/3182). This is the roadmap's K₀ of a unital algebra. For a nonunital algebra the group is the naive one, not yet the K₀ defined through the unitization.

Underneath it sits [rectangular Murray–von Neumann equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/MurrayVonNeumann.html#Matrix.IsMurrayVonNeumannEquivalent) of projections of different sizes. It is an equivalence relation, compatible with block sums, reindexing and entrywise star homomorphisms, implemented by partial isometries, and the same as [unitary conjugacy after stabilization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/MurrayVonNeumann.html#Matrix.isMurrayVonNeumannEquivalent_iff_exists_mem_unitary_mul_fromBlocks_mul_star) (TauCeti#42073). A parallel version was written for C*-matrices. Every idempotent of a C*-algebra now has a [unique projection with the same right ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Idempotent.html#IsIdempotentElem.existsUnique_isStarProjection_mul_eq_right), given by a norm-continuous formula and similar to the idempotent through an explicit unit. The comparison of K₀ with projective modules will need this step.

Finally, [positive tracial functionals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/TracialFunctional.html#TauCeti.PositiveTracialFunctional) are defined without a norm-one requirement, so that they pull back along any star homomorphism. An example shows why: pulling back a tracial state along a nonunital map can give the [zero functional](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/TracialFunctional.html#TauCeti.not_isTracialState_sndTracialFunctional_comp_inl). The pairing of these functionals with K₀ has not landed yet.
