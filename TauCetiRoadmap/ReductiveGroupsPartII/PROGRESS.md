## ReductiveGroupsPartII: 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-09 (`1e7f75b` to `d92acc5`)

The main result this window is the congruence filtration of an integral group model. For an affine group over a complete discrete valuation ring that is formally smooth and of finite type, the successive quotients of the [congruence subgroups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/CongruenceSubgroup/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.congruenceSubgroup) have [order `q^d`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/CongruenceSubgroup/Quotient.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.natCard_quotient_congruenceSubgroup_pow_succ), where `d` is the rank of the cotangent module at the identity. Alongside this, the congruence subgroups [form a neighbourhood basis of the identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/CongruenceSubgroup/Topology.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.hasBasis_nhds_one_congruenceSubgroup), the first one [is pro-`p`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/CongruenceSubgroup/ProP.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.isProP_congruenceSubgroup), and for `G_m` they [recover the unit filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/MultiplicativeGroup/CongruenceSubgroup.html#TauCeti.MultiplicativeGroup.map_map_congruenceSubgroup_eq_unitFiltration).

All of this sits on the point topology, which also landed this window. Points of an affine group form a [topological group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/Group.html#TauCeti.PointTopology.isTopologicalGroup). Over a local field this group is Hausdorff, totally disconnected, locally compact and second countable, and its integral points form a [compact open subset](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/IntegralPoints.html#TauCeti.PointTopology.isOpenEmbedding_integralPoints). The topology is compatible with tensor products, base change, fibre products and localizations. On `GL_n` it is the [topology of invertible matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Topology.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.pointsContinuousMulEquiv). The completed maximal unramified extension [`Ĕ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Completion.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.Breve) was also built, with its continuous arithmetic [Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Completion.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.frobenius). Its valuation is discrete, and a uniformizer of `E` [stays a uniformizer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/DiscreteValuation.html#Irreducible.algebraMap_integer_breve) (TauCeti#25572, TauCeti#27960). Two things are still missing: the Frobenius fixed points have not been identified with `E`, and the residue field of `Ĕ` has not been shown to be algebraically closed.

Weil restriction along a finite projective algebra now [preserves smoothness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction/Smooth.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.instSmooth) and is [functorial in group homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/WeilRestriction.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.mapBialgHom). It is compatible with [towers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction/Composition.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.compAlgEquiv) and, on points, with the [point topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/WeilRestriction.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.pointsHomeomorph). The points of `Res_{B/A} GL_n` are identified with [`GL_n(B ⊗ T)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/WeilRestriction.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.generalLinearPoints).

## ReductiveGroupsPartII: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`d92acc5` to `b235000`)

The completed maximal unramified extension `Ĕ` now has the properties the rest of the roadmap relies on. The fixed field of its arithmetic Frobenius is the base local field `E` ([`fixedPoints_frobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.fixedPoints_frobenius)), and on integers the fixed ring is `𝒪[E]`. Frobenius is [congruent to the `q`-th power map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.frobenius_congr), the residue field is [algebraically closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/ResidueField.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.residueField_isAlgClosed), and `E` is a [closed subfield](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/DiscreteValuation.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.isClosedEmbedding_algebraMap). `Ĕ` is [not algebraic over `E`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.not_isAlgebraic) in any characteristic, and has [infinite transcendence degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.transcendenceDegree_infinite) in characteristic zero (TauCeti#28011). These rest on general lemmas about valued extensions and Frobenius-type automorphisms.

The Deligne torus [`S`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/Basic.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.S) `= Res_{ℂ/ℝ} G_m` arrived with the README's conventions. Its real points are [homeomorphic to `ℂˣ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/Basic.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.isHomeomorph_realPointsMulEquiv), it [splits over `ℂ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/Splitting.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.complexSplitting), and conjugation [exchanges the characters `(p, q)` and `(q, p)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/Splitting.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.characterGroup_conj). Weil restriction gained [semilinear functoriality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction/Semilinear.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.semilinearMap), and with it the [induced Galois action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction/Semilinear.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.inducedAction) that integral restriction along Galois extensions needs. The point topology on schemes became functorial: morphisms are [continuous on points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/Scheme/Functoriality.html#TauCeti.SchemePointTopology.continuous_map), closed immersions give [closed embeddings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/Scheme/ClosedImmersion.html#TauCeti.SchemePointTopology.isClosedEmbedding_of_isClosedImmersion), and separated schemes have [Hausdorff](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/Scheme/Separation.html#TauCeti.SchemePointTopology.t2Space_of_isSeparated) points.

Bruhat–Tits theory took its first valued step. [Valuations of a root datum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/BruhatTits/Valuation.html#TauCeti.BruhatTits.Valuation) arrived with their filtration subgroups, the commutator axiom, equipollence under shifts, and compatibility with the torus. There is not yet an action of `N` on valuations, nor a notion of discreteness. On the dual side, the [automorphism groups of a root pairing and of its flip](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Flip.html#RootPairing.Equiv.autFlip) are identified by inverse transpose. That identification was among six pull requests that landed earlier and are recorded here late; the others gave the root datum in a group, norm-one tori and the separable splitting of a Weil restriction.
