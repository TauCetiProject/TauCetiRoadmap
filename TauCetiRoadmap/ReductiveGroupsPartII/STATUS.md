## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The two foundational layers are nearly complete. RG2.0 lacks only the smooth-group analytic theory. RG2.0a has the Deligne torus and Edixhoven's tame fixed-point theorem, but not integral Weil restriction or the descent of reductivity. Bruhat–Tits theory proper has its abstract root datum and valuations, but no apartment yet. The dual side has only the automorphism identification for the flipped datum. Buildings, parahorics and the decompositions have not begun.

### Named results

- **Frobenius fixed points of `Ĕ`** — the arithmetic Frobenius of the completed maximal unramified extension fixes exactly the base local field `E`, and on integers exactly `𝒪[E]`; the residue field of `Ĕ` is algebraically closed ([`fixedPoints_frobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.fixedPoints_frobenius)).
- **Rational points as Frobenius fixed points** — for an affine group over `E`, `G(E)` is isomorphic as a topological group to the Frobenius-fixed subgroup of `G(Ĕ)`, and likewise for integral points ([`rationalPointsContinuousMulEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Points.html#TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.rationalPointsContinuousMulEquiv)).
- **Edixhoven's tame fixed-point theorem** — for a finite projective `B/A` with an action of a group of order invertible in `A`, the fixed-point scheme of the Weil restriction of a smooth `B`-scheme with semilinear action is smooth over `A` ([`smooth_quotient_fixedPointIdealOfInduced`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction/FixedPoints.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.smooth_quotient_fixedPointIdealOfInduced)).
- **Indices of the congruence filtration** — for a formally smooth affine group of finite type over a complete discrete valuation ring with residue field of order `q`, each successive congruence quotient has `q^d` elements, `d` the rank of the cotangent module at the identity ([`natCard_quotient_congruenceSubgroup_pow_succ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/CongruenceSubgroup/Quotient.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.natCard_quotient_congruenceSubgroup_pow_succ)).
- **Integral points are compact open** — for a finite-type algebra over the valuation ring of a local field, the integral points form a compact open subspace of the `E`-points ([`isOpenEmbedding_integralPoints`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/IntegralPoints.html#TauCeti.PointTopology.isOpenEmbedding_integralPoints)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The point topology on schemes** — glued from affine charts, it is now functorial. Morphisms are [continuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/Scheme/Functoriality.html#TauCeti.SchemePointTopology.continuous_map), immersions give embeddings, separated schemes have Hausdorff points, and schemes locally of finite type have [locally compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/Scheme/LocallyCompact.html#TauCeti.SchemePointTopology.locallyCompactSpace_of_locallyOfFiniteType) points. Non-affine models and flag varieties can therefore be treated topologically.
- **The Deligne torus** — [`S`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/Basic.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.S) `= Res_{ℂ/ℝ} G_m` with its splitting over `ℂ`, its weight, its norm, its cocharacter `μ` and its characters, all with the sign conventions the Hodge-theoretic consumers expect.
- **Valuations of a root datum** — [`BruhatTits.Valuation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/BruhatTits/Valuation.html#TauCeti.BruhatTits.Valuation) on top of the (DR1)–(DR6) [root datum in a group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/BruhatTits/RootDatum.html#TauCeti.BruhatTits.RootDatum). It comes with filtration subgroups, value sets, equipollence under shifts and torus compatibility, which are the data from which the apartment is built.

## The frontier

- **The apartment** — define the `N`-action on valuations and `Valuation.IsDiscrete`, the torus valuation map `v : Z(K) → V`, and the apartment as an affine space under `V`. All of these build directly on the existing valuations.
- **Steinberg's theorem and the rational torus over `Ĕ`** — now that `Ĕ` is a complete discrete valuation field with algebraically closed residue field, RG2.1.1's inputs on the field side are in place. The group-theoretic side needs ReductiveGroups layers 6–7.
- **Remaining Weil restriction targets** — `integralPointsEquiv` and the generic-fibre comparison for integral models, `reductive_res_iff`, and the induced character lattices of restricted tori.
- **The dual Galois action** — `dualGaloisAction = autFlip ∘ μ_G` with preservation of the dual base and finite image. After that, `AbsoluteRootData` and the dual group over `ℤ`, which wait on ReductiveGroups layer 9's Chevalley–Demazure groups.
- **Smooth morphisms are open, and analytic charts** — these feed RG2.4's unimodularity and adjoint-modulus results. The positive-characteristic case additionally waits on MassFormula Layer 2.
