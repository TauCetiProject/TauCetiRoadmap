## NoncommutativeGeometry: 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-20 (`8de27a9` to `68a457e`)

The algebraic cyclic theory of N1 is now in place for unital algebras. Connes' [cyclic cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology) is built as the cohomology of the cyclic cochain complex, and Connes' operator B is proved to satisfy [B² = 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/ConnesOperator.html#TauCeti.connesB_connesB) and to [anticommute with the Hochschild coboundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/ConnesOperator.html#TauCeti.hochschildCoboundary_connesB_add_connesB_hochschildCoboundary), alongside the cyclic-bicomplex relations for the cyclic norm and the bar contraction. The ground ring is computed: its cyclic cohomology is [the coefficient module in even degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/GroundRing.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.selfEquiv) (when 2 is not a zero divisor there) and [vanishes in odd degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/GroundRing.html#TauCeti.isZero_cyclicCohomology_self_of_odd). [Reduced](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Reduced.html#TauCeti.reducedCyclicCohomology) and [relative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Relative.html#TauCeti.relativeCyclicCohomology) cyclic cohomology follow the README's conventions. The relative theory handles nonunital algebras through their forced unitization, and the augmentation splits the scalar restriction.

The first piece of N2 has also landed. Cochains extend to matrices by the generalized trace φ#Tr, and this extension descends to [cyclic cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Matrix.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.matrixExtension). Connes' [even pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/EvenPairing.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.evenPairing) of an idempotent with a degree-2m class is well defined, and [for idempotent matrices it is given by Connes' formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/EvenPairing.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.evenPairing_matrixExtension_mk). It pairs with individual idempotents only, though: there is no algebraic K₀ yet, no stabilization or conjugation invariance, and no odd pairing.

Supporting work started the analytic and differential sides of N1. It adds [universal one-forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NoncommutativeGeometry/UniversalDifferential/OneForms.html#TauCeti.UniversalOneForms) over the forced unitization, which represent bimodule derivations, together with their tensor coordinates. It also adds the projective tensor product of locally convex spaces with its [continuous universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/PiTensorProduct/UniversalProperty.html#TauCeti.ProjectiveTensorProduct.liftEquiv) and its [completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/PiTensorProduct/Projective/Completion.html#TauCeti.CompletedProjectiveTensorProduct). Finally, it proves that [currying the first argument](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/HochschildCohomology/Dual.html#TauCeti.multilinearCurryFirstEquiv_hochschildCoboundary) matches the scalar Hochschild coboundary with the one valued in the dual bimodule.

## NoncommutativeGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `69cd1ab`)

The cyclic bicomplex comparison landed: over a ring containing ℚ, the inclusion of Connes' cyclic cochain complex into the [total complex of the cyclic bicomplex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Bicomplex.html#TauCeti.cyclicTotalComplex) is [a quasi-isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Bicomplex.html#TauCeti.quasiIso_cyclicCochainComplexToTotal), so the two definitions of cyclic cohomology agree. It rests on [exactness of the bicomplex rows](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/RowExactness.html#TauCeti.range_one_sub_cyclicOperator_eq_ker_cyclicNorm), proved with a cyclic-averaging homotopy. [Hochschild cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/HochschildCohomology/Complex.html#TauCeti.hochschildCohomology) now exists as the cohomology of a cochain complex, together with two of the three SBI maps, [I](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Hochschild.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.toHochschild) and [B](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Hochschild.html#TauCeti.hochschildCohomology.connesB), and the identity [B ∘ I = 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Hochschild.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.toHochschild_comp_connesB). The periodicity map S and exactness of the sequence are still missing.

On the pairing side, [Connes' odd pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/OddPairing.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.oddPairing) of an invertible with an odd cyclic class is defined with the README's (1+i)⁻¹ 2⁻ⁿ Γ(n/2+1)⁻¹ constant. It is [unchanged by stabilization u ↦ u ⊕ 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/OddPairing.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.oddPairing_matrixExtension_of_val_eq_fromBlocks_one) and additive on direct sums. The even pairing became [conjugation invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/EvenPairing/Invariance.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.evenPairing_units_conj), [invariant under polynomial homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/EvenPairing/Invariance.html#TauCeti.cyclicCohomology.evenPairing_eval_algebraMap_eq) and additive on direct sums. Neither pairing descends to K-theory yet, because algebraic K₀ and K₁ have not been built.

Cycles are in now. A closed graded trace on a differential graded algebra, with a map ρ that need not preserve the unit, has a [character that is a cyclic cocycle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Cycle.html#TauCeti.cycleCocycle). The converse, realizing every cyclic cocycle by universal forms, is still open. Traces and derivations give the cocycles τ(a₀δa₁) and the [two-derivation 2-cocycle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Derivation.html#TauCeti.traceDerivationPairCocycle) that has the shape of the future torus cocycle. On the analytic side, b, B, the cyclic operator and the cyclic norm act on jointly continuous cochains. These satisfy [bB + Bb = 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Continuous.html#TauCeti.continuousHochschildCoboundary_connesB_add_connesB_hochschildCoboundary) and are continuous for bounded convergence, but continuous cyclic cohomology itself is not yet defined.
