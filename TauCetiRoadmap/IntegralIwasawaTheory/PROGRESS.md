## IntegralIwasawaTheory: 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-19 (`aeb8fe6` to `b235000`)

The first milestone of this roadmap has landed: the [Hasse unit index of every prime-power cyclotomic field is one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Units/HasseUnitIndex.html#TauCeti.IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.indexRealUnits_eq_one_of_prime_pow). In other words, the units of ℚ(ζ_{p^a}) are [generated by the real units and the roots of unity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Units/HasseUnitIndex.html#TauCeti.IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.realUnits_sup_torsion_eq_top_of_prime_pow), for every prime p, including 2, and every exponent a. This is the roadmap's §1.5 target `primePowerHasseUnitIndex_one`, in the form `Suggested.lean` proposes (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/188).

The proof goes through a [norm criterion valid in any CM field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Units/HasseUnitIndex.html#TauCeti.NumberField.IsCMField.indexRealUnits_eq_one_of_not_isSquare_abs_norm): if |N(ζ − 1)| is not a rational square for some root of unity ζ, the index is one. For a primitive p^a-th root of unity with p^a > 2 that norm is p, which is not a square. The conductors p^a ≤ 2, where the field is ℚ and not CM, are handled separately. The rest of §1.5 has not started: the circular units, the regulator determinant and the index formula h(F⁺). Nothing has landed yet for the cyclotomic towers of Layer 0 or for any later layer.
