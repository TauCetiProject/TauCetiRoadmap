## Varifolds: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`b40fe6c` to `58174b5`)

The Euclidean theory of varifolds now reaches the first variation and stationarity. [`Euclidean.firstVariation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/FirstVariation.html#Euclidean.firstVariation) defines δV(X) as the integral of the tangential divergence tr(P ∘ DX) against V. It is a continuous linear functional on C¹ test fields, bounded by k‖V‖(K) sup|DX| and [continuous under varifold convergence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/FirstVariation.html#Euclidean.tendsto_firstVariation). On top of it come stationarity, locally bounded first variation and the total first variation ‖δV‖. Simon's [lower semicontinuity of ‖δV‖](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/Stationary.html#Euclidean.firstVariationMass_le_liminf) gives that [limits of stationary varifolds are stationary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/Stationary.html#Euclidean.IsStationary.of_varifoldTendsto). The roadmap's test cases are proved too. Lebesgue measure carrying a constant plane is [stationary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/StationaryExamples.html#Euclidean.firstVariation_map_prodMk_right_eq_zero), and the vertical lift of a line [does not have locally bounded first variation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/LineVarifold.html#Euclidean.not_exists_abs_firstVariation_verticalLift_le).

Most of Layer 1 landed underneath this. The Grassmannian G(E, k) is now a compact space of orthogonal projections. The Grassmann bundle of a vector bundle with inner-product fibres is locally trivial and has a [proper projection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/GrassmannBundle.html#TauCeti.grassmannBundle.isProperMap_proj). Euclidean varifolds have a weight and can be restricted to open sets. Varifold convergence comes with its semicontinuity laws, locality and a [compactness theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/Convergence.html#TauCeti.exists_subseq_varifoldTendsto). It is drawn from the vague compactness of Radon measures (TauCeti#45097).

Other foundations also arrived. Approximate tangent spaces of measures are now defined through blow-ups, with [uniqueness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/ApproximateTangentSpace.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.HasApproxTangentSpace.unique) and multiplicity equal to density. There are k-dimensional densities, with Simon's [upper density bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Density.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.measure_le_mul_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_of_upperDensity_lt) against Hausdorff measure. There is also the [Riesz representation for vector-valued functionals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Mass/Polar/Vector.html#Distribution.apply_eq_integral_inner_polarVector) of locally finite mass, with a unit polar field. All of this is Euclidean. Nothing yet defines varifolds, weights or first variations on a Riemannian manifold. Rectifiable varifolds, push-forward, the Riesz decomposition and monotonicity are still to come.
