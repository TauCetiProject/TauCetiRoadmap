## PerfectoidSpaces: 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-13 (`8615789` to `aa6e43e`)

The roadmap's opening stretch laid the ring-theoretic foundations of Layers P0 and P1, and its headline is Fontaine's θ. For any ring of integral elements `R⁺` of a perfectoid Tate ring, with no base field assumed, θ : W(R♭⁺) → R⁺ is [surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Pair.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.surjective_fontaineTheta_of_isPerfectoidTateRing) and its [kernel is principal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Pair.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.ker_fontaineTheta), generated by a distinguished nonzerodivisor of the form `p + [ϖ]α`. The same work shows that every such `R⁺` is [integral perfectoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Pair.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsRingOfIntegralElements.isIntegralPerfectoid) in the sense of Bhatt-Morrow-Scholze (TauCeti#27981). Around θ sit the [tilt](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Basic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.uniformTilt) of a complete uniform Tate ring, built directly with its limit addition and with a [θ that agrees with Mathlib's `fontaineTheta`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Theta.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.uniformTilt.theta_eq_fontaineTheta), and the basic classification results: [in characteristic p, perfectoid means perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PerfectRing.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidTateRing_iff_perfectRing), and [perfectoid fields are characterised](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Field.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidField_iff) as the non-discretely valued complete fields with ‖p‖ < 1 and Frobenius surjective on `K°/p`. The examples are there too: ℂ_p is perfectoid, ℚ_p is not, and a product is perfectoid exactly when both factors are.

On the almost side, P0 now has its categorical core. [Almost modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Module.html#TauCeti.Almost.Module), the Serre quotient by almost zero modules, form an abelian, complete and cocomplete [closed symmetric monoidal category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Closed.html#TauCeti.Almost.Module.monoidalClosed), with the adjoint triple `M_! ⊣ M^a ⊣ M_*`; for almost algebras, [almostification is left adjoint to almost elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Algebra.html#TauCeti.Almost.Algebra.adj). Almost finite generation and presentation acquired Gabber-Ramero's extension and Schanuel-type lemmas, and almost finite projective modules were defined. The [almost setup (R°, R°°)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/AlmostSetup.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.almostSetup) of a perfectoid Tate ring now exists, so this is where the two layers meet.

Still to come: the untilt along a primitive ideal and the classification of untilts, the tilting equivalence of rings, almost flatness and the étale notions of P0, and everything from rational localisation and the sheaf theorem onward.

## PerfectoidSpaces: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-25 (`aa6e43e` to `522d48f`)

The untilt has arrived. For a perfect complete Tate ring `R` of characteristic `p` with ring of integral elements `R⁺` and a primitive `ξ ∈ W(R⁺)`, the [untilt](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PrimitiveUntilt.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.untilt) `(W(R⁺)/(ξ))[1/[ϖ]]` is now a complete Hausdorff Tate ring, and it is [a perfectoid Tate ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PrimitiveUntilt.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.untilt.isPerfectoidTateRing), following Scholze's Theorem 3.17 in *Étale cohomology of diamonds* (TauCeti#39177). Alongside it come the multiplicative, continuous [sharp map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PrimitiveUntilt.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.untilt.sharp) `R → A` and a version of θ out of `W(R⁺)[1/[ϖ]]` whose [kernel is generated by the ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PrimitiveUntilt.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.untilt.ker_theta). The library also has `A⁺ = W(R⁺)/(ξ)`, `A⁺/(ϖ♯) ≅ R⁺/(ϖ)`, and the fact that `J = (p)` gives back `R⁺[1/ϖ]`. Not yet proved: that the tilt of the untilt is `(R, R⁺)` with `ker θ = J`, that `A⁺` is a ring of integral elements, functoriality, and so the classification of untilts. The route passed through [Bhatt-Morrow-Scholze's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.IsPerfectoidTateRing.of_bijective_frobeniusModPow), which produces a perfectoid Tate ring from a ring of definition on which Frobenius is bijective.

On the field side, the [tilt of a perfectoid field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Field.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.tiltField.instIsPerfectoidField) is a perfectoid field of characteristic `p` with norm `‖x‖ = ‖x♯‖`. Its valuation ring, value group and residue field, and its comparison with Mathlib's `Tilt`, are still to come. Kedlaya's [metric criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PseudoUniformizerRoots/MetricCriterion.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.PseudoUniformizerRoots.isPerfectoidTateRing_iff_exists_gauge_sub_pow_le_mul) also landed: a uniform Tate ring is perfectoid exactly when `p`-th powers approximate every element up to a fixed relative error in the spectral gauge. The prismatic input needed later in P4 has started too: [distinguished elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DeltaRing/Distinguished.html#TauCeti.DeltaRing.IsDistinguished) of δ-rings, [prisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Prismatic/Prism.html#TauCeti.Prism), and [rigidity of maps of prisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Prismatic/Rigidity.html#TauCeti.Prism.map_ideal_eq).

In almost mathematics, Gabber-Ramero's [unitisation `B_!!`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/ShriekShriek.html#TauCeti.Almost.Algebra.shriekShriek) is built, with [`B ≅ (B_!!)^a`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/ShriekShriek.html#TauCeti.Almost.Algebra.shriekShriekUnitIso), though not yet its adjunction. [Inverting an element of `m`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Invert.html#TauCeti.Almost.invert) is now an exact, strong monoidal functor to honest modules, and it makes [almost exact complexes exact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Invert.html#TauCeti.Almost.exact_localizedModuleFunctor_of_isAlmostZero_homology).
