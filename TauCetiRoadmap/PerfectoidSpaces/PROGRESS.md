## PerfectoidSpaces: 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-13 (`8615789` to `aa6e43e`)

The roadmap's opening stretch laid the ring-theoretic foundations of Layers P0 and P1, and its headline is Fontaine's θ. For any ring of integral elements `R⁺` of a perfectoid Tate ring, with no base field assumed, θ : W(R♭⁺) → R⁺ is [surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Pair.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.surjective_fontaineTheta_of_isPerfectoidTateRing) and its [kernel is principal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Pair.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.ker_fontaineTheta), generated by a distinguished nonzerodivisor of the form `p + [ϖ]α`. The same work shows that every such `R⁺` is [integral perfectoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Pair.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsRingOfIntegralElements.isIntegralPerfectoid) in the sense of Bhatt-Morrow-Scholze (TauCeti#27981). Around θ sit the [tilt](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Basic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.uniformTilt) of a complete uniform Tate ring, built directly with its limit addition and with a [θ that agrees with Mathlib's `fontaineTheta`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Theta.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.uniformTilt.theta_eq_fontaineTheta), and the basic classification results: [in characteristic p, perfectoid means perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PerfectRing.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidTateRing_iff_perfectRing), and [perfectoid fields are characterised](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Field.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidField_iff) as the non-discretely valued complete fields with ‖p‖ < 1 and Frobenius surjective on `K°/p`. The examples are there too: ℂ_p is perfectoid, ℚ_p is not, and a product is perfectoid exactly when both factors are.

On the almost side, P0 now has its categorical core. [Almost modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Module.html#TauCeti.Almost.Module), the Serre quotient by almost zero modules, form an abelian, complete and cocomplete [closed symmetric monoidal category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Closed.html#TauCeti.Almost.Module.monoidalClosed), with the adjoint triple `M_! ⊣ M^a ⊣ M_*`; for almost algebras, [almostification is left adjoint to almost elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Algebra.html#TauCeti.Almost.Algebra.adj). Almost finite generation and presentation acquired Gabber-Ramero's extension and Schanuel-type lemmas, and almost finite projective modules were defined. The [almost setup (R°, R°°)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/AlmostSetup.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.almostSetup) of a perfectoid Tate ring now exists, so this is where the two layers meet.

Still to come: the untilt along a primitive ideal and the classification of untilts, the tilting equivalence of rings, almost flatness and the étale notions of P0, and everything from rational localisation and the sheaf theorem onward.
