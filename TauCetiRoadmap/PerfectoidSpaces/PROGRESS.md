## PerfectoidSpaces: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-24 (`8615789` to `fe8ca8b`)

The roadmap's first theorems are about perfectoid Tate rings themselves. The definition, [`IsPerfectoidTateRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.IsPerfectoidTateRing), follows Scholze's: a complete uniform Tate ring with a pseudouniformiser `ϖ` such that `ϖ^p` divides `p` in `R°` and Frobenius `R°/ϖ → R°/ϖ^p` is bijective. Alongside it come Scholze's remark that [surjectivity of Frobenius suffices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.IsPerfectoidTateRing.of_surjective), and the theorem that [in characteristic `p`, perfectoid means perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PerfectRing.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidTateRing_iff_perfectRing). That proof goes through the open mapping theorem, which shows a [perfect complete Tate ring is uniform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Perfect.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsUniform.of_perfectRing) (TauCeti#20283).

The standard examples are in. [Complete algebraically closed fields with `‖p‖ < 1` are perfectoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/IsAlgClosed.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidTateRing_of_isAlgClosed), and in particular so is `ℂ_p`. [`ℚ_p` is not](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Padic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.not_isPerfectoidTateRing_padic), even though Frobenius on `𝔽_p` is bijective. Both factors of a [product are perfectoid exactly when the product is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidTateRing_prod_iff). That rests on new Huber-ring facts: products of Tate rings are Tate, and `(A × B)° = A° × B°`. Separately, the valuation-ring inputs to the almost-mathematics setup `(K°, K°°)` landed, including the fact that the [maximal ideal of `𝒪_{ℂ_p}` is idempotent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Valuation/IdempotentMaximalIdeal.html#Valuation.isIdempotentElem_maximalIdeal_of_isAlgClosed) and [not finitely generated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Complex/MaximalIdeal.html#TauCeti.PadicComplexInt.not_fg_maximalIdeal).

Still missing from the first two subsections: the Frobenius-modulo-`p` criterion, the `ℂ_p[ε]/(ε²)` non-example, and the adic completeness of rings of integral elements. Nothing yet touches tilts, the basic-setup structure, or anything geometric.
