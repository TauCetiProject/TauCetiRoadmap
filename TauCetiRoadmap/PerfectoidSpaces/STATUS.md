## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The roadmap is still in its foundational ring theory. P1 has perfectoid Tate rings, tilts, the principal kernel of θ, and now the untilt along a primitive element, which is perfectoid; the identification of its tilt and the classification of untilts are not done. P0 has the categorical core of almost mathematics, `B_!!` and inversion of `ϖ`, but not flatness or étale morphisms. P4 has its first prismatic steps. P2, P3 and P5–P7 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Fontaine's θ has principal kernel**: for any ring of integral elements `R⁺` of a perfectoid Tate ring, θ : W(R♭⁺) → R⁺ is surjective and its kernel is generated by a distinguished nonzerodivisor `p + [ϖ]α` ([`ker_fontaineTheta`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Pair.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.ker_fontaineTheta)).
- **The untilt along a primitive element is perfectoid**: for `R` perfect, complete and of characteristic `p`, and `ξ ∈ W(R⁺)` primitive, `(W(R⁺)/(ξ))[1/[ϖ]]` is a perfectoid Tate ring ([`untilt.isPerfectoidTateRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PrimitiveUntilt.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.untilt.isPerfectoidTateRing)).
- **The tilt of a perfectoid field is a perfectoid field**: it has characteristic `p` and norm `‖x‖ = ‖x♯‖` ([`tiltField.instIsPerfectoidField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Field.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.tiltField.instIsPerfectoidField)).
- **The characterisation of perfectoid fields**: a complete ultrametric field is perfectoid exactly when it is not discretely valued, has ‖p‖ < 1, and has Frobenius surjective on `K°/p` ([`isPerfectoidField_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Field.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidField_iff)).
- **Kedlaya's metric criterion**: a uniform Tate ring with compatible roots of a pseudouniformiser is perfectoid exactly when `p`-th powers approximate every element up to one fixed relative error in the spectral gauge ([`isPerfectoidTateRing_iff_exists_gauge_sub_pow_le_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PseudoUniformizerRoots/MetricCriterion.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.PseudoUniformizerRoots.isPerfectoidTateRing_iff_exists_gauge_sub_pow_le_mul)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The tilt of a complete uniform Tate ring** ([`uniformTilt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Basic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.uniformTilt)) needs no perfectoid hypothesis. It is identified with Mathlib's `PreTilt` on integral elements, and its θ is [Mathlib's `fontaineTheta`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Theta.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.uniformTilt.theta_eq_fontaineTheta), so Mathlib's Witt vector results apply directly.
- **Almost modules** ([`Almost.Module`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Module.html#TauCeti.Almost.Module)) form an abelian, closed symmetric monoidal category with `M_! ⊣ M^a ⊣ M_*`. [Inverting an element of `m`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Invert.html#TauCeti.Almost.invert) is an exact, monoidal functor to `V[1/ϖ]`-modules, which turns almost vanishing into vanishing.
- **Prisms** ([`Prism`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Prismatic/Prism.html#TauCeti.Prism)) come with distinguished elements of δ-rings and the rigidity of maps of prisms. These are the first pieces of the perfect-prism dictionary that P4.15 needs.

## The frontier

- **The tilt of the untilt (P1.20).** Show that `A⁺ = W(R⁺)/(ξ)` is a ring of integral elements and that the untilt's tilt is `(R, R⁺)` with `ker θ = J`. Then build `untilt.map`. This unlocks the classification of untilts by primitive ideals (P1.21) and the tilting equivalence (P1.27).
- **The rest of P1.15.** For the tilt of a perfectoid field, prove `K♭° ≅ lim K°/ϖ`, `|K♭^×| = |K^×|`, the residue-field isomorphism, and the comparison with Mathlib's `Tilt` and `PreTilt.val`.
- **The rest of P1.18.** Extend θ to `W(R♭⁺)[1/[ϖ]] → R` with kernel `(ξ)`, and prove the bijection between rings of integral elements of `R` and of `R♭`.
- **Almost algebra beyond the core (P0.9, P0.11).** Prove the adjunction `B ↦ B_!!` and develop almost flatness and projectivity. Almost finite étale morphisms (P0.12–P0.15), and eventually almost purity, depend on these.
- **Perfectoid Tate rings that are fields (P1.12–P1.14).** These need the Gelfand spectrum of a Banach ring first.
