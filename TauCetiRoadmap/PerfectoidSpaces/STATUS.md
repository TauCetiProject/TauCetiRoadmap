## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The roadmap is in its foundational layers. P1 has perfectoid Tate rings, the tilt, the comparison with Mathlib's `PreTilt` and the principal kernel of Fontaine's θ, but no untilts yet. P0 has the categorical core of almost mathematics but stops before flatness and étale morphisms. P2 through P7 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Fontaine's θ has principal kernel** — for any ring of integral elements `R⁺` of a perfectoid Tate ring, θ : W(R♭⁺) → R⁺ is surjective and its kernel is generated by a distinguished nonzerodivisor `p + [ϖ]α` ([`ker_fontaineTheta`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Pair.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.ker_fontaineTheta)); along the way every such `R⁺` is shown to be integral perfectoid.
- **Perfectoid means perfect in characteristic p** — a complete Hausdorff Tate ring of characteristic `p` is perfectoid exactly when it is perfect ([`isPerfectoidTateRing_iff_perfectRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PerfectRing.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidTateRing_iff_perfectRing)).
- **The characterisation of perfectoid fields** — a complete ultrametric field is perfectoid exactly when it is not discretely valued, has ‖p‖ < 1, and has Frobenius surjective on `K°/p`, which makes ℂ_p an example ([`isPerfectoidField_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/Field.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.isPerfectoidField_iff)).
- **Compatible p-power roots of a pseudouniformiser** — every perfectoid Tate ring has a pseudouniformiser `ϖ` with a compatible system of roots `ϖ^(1/pⁿ)` ([`PseudoUniformizerRoots.nonempty`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/PseudoUniformizerRoots/Existence.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.PseudoUniformizerRoots.nonempty)).
- **The tilt of a perfectoid ring is perfectoid** — more generally, a complete uniform Tate ring that receives a continuous map from a perfectoid Tate ring has a perfectoid tilt ([`RingHom.isPerfectoidTateRing_uniformTilt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/OfHom.html#RingHom.isPerfectoidTateRing_uniformTilt)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The tilt of a complete uniform Tate ring** ([`uniformTilt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Basic.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.uniformTilt)) is built with no perfectoid hypothesis. It is a complete perfect topological ring of characteristic `p`, functorial in the ring and in Huber pairs. On integral elements it is identified with Mathlib's `PreTilt`, with its θ equal to Mathlib's `fontaineTheta` ([`theta_eq_fontaineTheta`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/UniformTilt/Theta.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.uniformTilt.theta_eq_fontaineTheta)), so Mathlib's Witt vector results apply directly.
- **Almost modules** ([`Almost.Module`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Module.html#TauCeti.Almost.Module)) over an arbitrary basic setup are the Serre quotient by almost zero modules. They form an abelian, complete and cocomplete [closed symmetric monoidal category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Almost/Closed.html#TauCeti.Almost.Module.monoidalClosed) with the adjoint triple `M_! ⊣ M^a ⊣ M_*`, and almost algebras sit over them with almostification left adjoint to almost elements.
- **The almost setup (R°, R°°)** ([`almostSetup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfectoid/AlmostSetup.html#TauCeti.Perfectoid.almostSetup)) of any Huber ring with compatible roots of a pseudouniformiser is the meeting point of P0 and P1, where almost mathematics becomes available over perfectoid rings.

## The frontier

- **Untilts along primitive ideals (P1.19–P1.21).** Form `W(R♭⁺)/(ξ)[1/[ϖ]]`, prove it is a perfectoid Tate pair whose tilt is `(R, R⁺)`, and classify untilts by primitive ideals. Completeness of quotients by distinguished elements, and the reduction of Witt quotients modulo `p`, are already proved.
- **The rest of P1.18.** Extend θ to `W(R♭⁺)[1/[ϖ]] → R` with kernel generated by `ξ`, and prove the inclusion-preserving bijection between rings of integral elements of `R` and of `R♭`.
- **Tilts of perfectoid fields (P1.12–P1.15).** Show that a perfectoid Tate ring which is a field is a perfectoid field, and that the tilt of a perfectoid field is one. This needs the Gelfand spectrum of a Banach ring first.
- **Almost algebra beyond the core (P0.8, P0.9, P0.11).** Modules over an almost algebra, the left adjoint `B_!!`, and flatness and almost projectivity over almost algebras. These are what almost finite étale morphisms (P0.12–P0.15) and, eventually, almost purity require.
- **The tilting equivalence of rings (P1.27–P1.28).** This waits on the untilt, and P2's tilting homeomorphism in turn waits on it.
