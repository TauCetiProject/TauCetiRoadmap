## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** A regular fan has a Hausdorff, second-countable complex manifold glued from its affine charts, the complex torus acts on it holomorphically, and the orbit–cone correspondence holds on it. Layers 1, 2 and the gluing theorem are done; the gluing layer still lacks open subfans and the orbit layer lacks the toric boundary. Toric maps, properness and the algebraic–analytic comparison have not begun.

### Named results

- **The orbit–cone correspondence for a regular fan** — the cones of the fan correspond bijectively to the torus orbits of its realization, inclusion of cones reversing the closure order, and the closure of an orbit is the union of the orbits of the larger cones ([`Fan.coneEquivOrbitRelQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Orbit.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.coneEquivOrbitRelQuotient), [`Fan.closure_analyticConeOrbit`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Orbit.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.closure_analyticConeOrbit)).
- **The complex manifold of a regular fan** — gluing the affine analytic charts along face localizations gives a Hausdorff, second-countable complex manifold modelled on ℂ^n, in which each affine chart is a biholomorphism onto an open subset ([`Fan.isManifold_analyticRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.isManifold_analyticRealization), [`Fan.t2Space_analyticRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Topology.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.t2Space_analyticRealization)).
- **The holomorphic torus action** — the coordinate-free complex torus acts holomorphically on the realization, and every chart inclusion is equivariant ([`Fan.contMDiffSMul_complexTorus_analyticRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/TorusAction/Holomorphic.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.contMDiffSMul_complexTorus_analyticRealization)).
- **The separation lemma** — two lattice-rational cones meeting in a common face are separated by a single character, so the dual semigroup of the intersection is generated by the two dual semigroups; this is what makes the realization Hausdorff ([`exists_mem_dualSemigroup_neg_mem_dualSemigroup_inf_ker_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/DualSemigroup/Separation.html#TauCeti.Toric.exists_mem_dualSemigroup_neg_mem_dualSemigroup_inf_ker_eq)).
- **The complex structure on a regular affine chart** — the complex points of a regular cone's affine toric scheme form a complex manifold of shape ℂ^k × (ℂ^*)^(n−k), independent of the extending basis ([`isManifold_coneChartedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.isManifold_coneChartedSpace)).

## The frontier

- **The toric boundary** — for each ray, make the closure of its orbit an invariant closed embedded hypersurface; for a nonempty fan, show these cover the complement of the dense torus with multiplicity one, then prove the holomorphic coordinate-hyperplane normal form in regular charts. The orbit closure formula it needs is now in place.
- **Open subfans** — glue the identity-on-points chart comparisons into a map from the realization of an open subfan to the ambient one, prove it an open embedding onto the union of the subfan's charts, functorial for nested subfans, and then holomorphic. Layer 5's restriction results use it.
- **Toric maps** — glue the maps of affine complex points induced by dual semigroups into the chart of the least target cone, prove holomorphy and functoriality, then the cone-by-cone properness criterion and, for nonempty fans, completeness exactly when the realization is compact.
- **Orbits as quotient tori** — identify each orbit with the torus modulo its stabilizer subtorus, and show the cone-orbit charts agree on overlaps; this closes Layer 3, item 2 and Layer 4, item 2.
- **The fan scheme over Spec ℂ** — descend the structure morphisms of the affine charts and glue the affine torus actions. Layer 6's complex points are morphisms over Spec ℂ, so it needs this first.
