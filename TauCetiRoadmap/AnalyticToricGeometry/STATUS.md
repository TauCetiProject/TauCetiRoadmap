## Where this roadmap stands

### Named results

- **Unique primitive generators** — every ray of a toric cone in an integral lattice has exactly one primitive lattice generator, and these generators span the cone ([`IsToricCone.existsUnique_primitiveGenerator`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Ray/Primitive.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsToricCone.existsUnique_primitiveGenerator), [`IsToricCone.hull_primitiveGenerator`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Ray/Generation.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsToricCone.hull_primitiveGenerator)).
- **Regular coordinates on the dual semigroup** — the dual semigroup of a regular cone with k rays in a rank-n lattice is isomorphic to ℕ^k × ℤ^(n−k), which gives the affine chart its shape ℂ^k × (ℂ^*)^(n−k) ([`IsRegularCone.nonempty_dualSemigroup_addEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/DualSemigroup/Regular.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsRegularCone.nonempty_dualSemigroup_addEquiv)).
- **The complex structure on a regular affine chart** — the complex points of a regular cone's affine toric scheme form a complex manifold whose structure does not depend on the extending basis or the generating family ([`isManifold_coneChartedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.isManifold_coneChartedSpace), [`contMDiff_id_coneChartedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.contMDiff_id_coneChartedSpace)).
- **Face localization is an open embedding** — for a face τ of a regular cone σ, the chart of τ is biholomorphic to an open subset of the chart of σ, and the algebraic face morphism is an open immersion ([`IsRegularCone.faceAffinePointPartialDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/FaceLocalization.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsRegularCone.faceAffinePointPartialDiffeomorph), [`IsRegularCone.isOpenImmersion_faceAffineToricSchemeMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/FaceLocalization.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsRegularCone.isOpenImmersion_faceAffineToricSchemeMap)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The toric scheme of a regular fan** — the colimit of the affine toric schemes of the cones along their face open immersions, covered by the charts as open subschemes, with fan morphisms inducing morphisms of toric schemes functorially; it is the algebraic side of the Layer 6 comparison ([`Fan.algebraicRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/Scheme.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicRealization), [`FanHom.algebraicMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/Scheme.html#TauCeti.Toric.FanHom.algebraicMap)).
- **The monomial-embedding topology** — complex points are topologized through any finite generating family of the semigroup, and the choice does not matter, so all charts and face maps live on one basis-free topology ([`affinePointTopology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/AffinePoint.html#TauCeti.Toric.affinePointTopology), [`affinePointTopology_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/AffinePoint.html#TauCeti.Toric.affinePointTopology_eq)).
- **Typed mixed exponents** — exponent data with no block from boundary to torus coordinates, so the pattern the roadmap forbids cannot be written down; basis changes in the charts are instances, and gluing toric maps in Layer 5 will reuse it ([`MixedExponent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/MixedMonomial.html#TauCeti.Toric.MixedExponent)).

## The frontier

- **Assemble the analytic gluing datum** — the overlap transitions satisfy the identity, symmetry and cocycle laws; what remains is to package them with the chart diagram as a `TopCat.GlueData` ([`Fan.analyticOverlapHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Overlap.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticOverlapHomeomorph), [`Fan.analyticOverlapTransition_trans_symm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Transition.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticOverlapTransition_trans_symm), [`Fan.analyticOverlapHomeomorph_cocycle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Cocycle.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticOverlapHomeomorph_cocycle)).
- **The glued complex manifold** — transport the affine charts to the glued space, show the chart inclusions are open holomorphic embeddings, and prove Hausdorffness and second countability; the README makes this depend on a generic complex-manifold gluing theorem.
- **Face strata as submanifolds** — show each face stratum is holomorphically embedded in its affine chart, of dimension the lattice rank minus the dimension of the face; this closes Layer 2.
- **Torus action on the schemes** — construct the torus action on the affine and fan schemes, connect the affine chart to the Toric project's `ToricVariety` instance, and identify the scheme of a product fan with the product.
- **General cones** — Gordan's lemma and open immersions of faces for non-regular cones, and closure of toric cones under intersection, are off the critical path: later layers use regular fans, where intersections are faces.
