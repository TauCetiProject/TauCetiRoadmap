## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The character calculus (Layer 1) is done, and a regular fan now has a Hausdorff, second-countable complex manifold glued from its affine charts. This settles the core of Layer 3, which lacks only its functoriality items. Layers 0 and 2 are done apart from a few pieces: the torus action on the schemes, and embedding the orbit strata as submanifolds. The theory on the glued manifold (Layers 4 to 6) has not begun.

### Named results

- **The complex manifold of a regular fan** — gluing the affine analytic charts along face localizations gives a complex manifold modelled on ℂ^n, which is Hausdorff and second countable. Each affine chart sits inside it as a biholomorphism onto an open subset ([`Fan.isManifold_analyticRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.isManifold_analyticRealization), [`Fan.t2Space_analyticRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Topology.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.t2Space_analyticRealization), [`Fan.analyticAffineChartPartialDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticAffineChartPartialDiffeomorph)).
- **The separation lemma** — if two lattice-rational cones meet in a common face, a single character separates them, so the dual semigroup of the intersection is generated by the two dual semigroups. This is the input for Hausdorffness ([`exists_mem_dualSemigroup_neg_mem_dualSemigroup_inf_ker_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/DualSemigroup/Separation.html#TauCeti.Toric.exists_mem_dualSemigroup_neg_mem_dualSemigroup_inf_ker_eq), [`dualSemigroup_inf_eq_sup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/DualSemigroup/Separation.html#TauCeti.Toric.dualSemigroup_inf_eq_sup)).
- **The complex structure on a regular affine chart** — the complex points of a regular cone's affine toric scheme form a complex manifold, and the structure does not depend on the extending basis ([`isManifold_coneChartedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/Manifold.html#TauCeti.Toric.isManifold_coneChartedSpace)).
- **The affine orbit-cone correspondence** — the faces of a regular cone correspond bijectively to the torus orbits in its chart, reversing the closure order. Each orbit has complex dimension equal to the rank minus the dimension of its face (`faceEquivOrbitRelQuotient`, [`finrank_affineConeOrbit_model`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Cone/Orbit/Dimension.html#TauCeti.Toric.finrank_affineConeOrbit_model)).
- **Regular coordinates on the dual semigroup** — the dual semigroup of a regular cone with k rays in a rank-n lattice is ℕ^k × ℤ^(n−k), which gives the chart its shape ℂ^k × (ℂ^*)^(n−k) ([`IsRegularCone.nonempty_dualSemigroup_addEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/DualSemigroup/Regular.html#TauCeti.Toric.IsRegularCone.nonempty_dualSemigroup_addEquiv)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The analytic realization as a glued space** — the charts, overlaps and transitions of a regular fan packaged as a `TopCat.GlueData`, as the roadmap requires. Every later layer states its results on this space ([`Fan.analyticGlueData`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Analytic/Fan/GlueData.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.analyticGlueData)).
- **The toric scheme of a regular fan** — the affine toric schemes glued along their face open immersions, functorial in fan morphisms. It is the algebraic side of the Layer 6 comparison ([`Fan.algebraicRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/Scheme.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.algebraicRealization)).
- **Manifolds from open covers** — a space covered by open embeddings of manifolds with smooth overlaps gets a manifold structure. This is the generic gluing theorem the README called for, and it is reusable beyond toric geometry ([`isManifold_chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Gluing.html#TauCeti.isManifold_chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding)).

### Roadmap coverage

Layer 1 is done. Layer 0 is done except for four gaps: the torus action on the affine and fan schemes, the link to the Toric project's `ToricVariety` class, compatibility of fan schemes with products, and closure of toric cones under intersection. For non-regular cones, Gordan's lemma and face open immersions are also missing. Fan equivalences now exist, and regularity and completeness are invariant under them. Layer 2 lacks one piece: face orbits carry complex coordinates and have their dimension computed, but are not yet shown to be holomorphically embedded submanifolds of the chart. Layer 3 is done except item 5, invariance of the realization under fan equivalence and functoriality for open subfans and subdivisions. The source also shows no statement that cone-orbit charts agree on overlaps. Layers 4, 5 and 6 are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Glue the torus action** — extend the affine torus actions to the analytic realization and prove the chart inclusions equivariant. This opens Layer 4 and needs only what is now in place.
- **Fan-orbit correspondence and boundary** — carry the affine orbit-cone correspondence over to the glued manifold, then build the ray-indexed boundary hypersurfaces with their holomorphic normal-crossings normal form.
- **Functoriality of the realization** — show a fan equivalence induces a biholomorphism, open subfans give open submanifolds, and a subdivision gives a holomorphic map. Layer 5's gluing of mixed monomial maps along fan morphisms extends this.
- **Orbit strata as submanifolds** — show each affine orbit is holomorphically embedded in its chart. This closes Layer 2.
- **Torus action on the schemes** — construct it on the affine and fan schemes, and connect the affine chart to the Toric project's `ToricVariety` instance. Layer 6's equivariant comparison needs this.
