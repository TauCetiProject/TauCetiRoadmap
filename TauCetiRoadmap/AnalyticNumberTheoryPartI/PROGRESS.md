## AnalyticNumberTheoryPartI: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`51ff4a4` to `aa6e43e`)

The first step towards the uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros has landed: every primitive quadratic Dirichlet character is a Kronecker character, namely the character of a fundamental discriminant `D` with `|D|` equal to its level ([`exists_isFundamentalDiscr_apply_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/Quadratic/FundamentalDiscriminant.html#DirichletCharacter.IsPrimitive.exists_isFundamentalDiscr_apply_eq)). In characteristic other than two the discriminant is pinned down as `D = χ(−1)N` ([`apply_eq_fundamentalDiscriminantCharCast_ite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/Quadratic/FundamentalDiscriminant.html#DirichletCharacter.IsPrimitive.apply_eq_fundamentalDiscriminantCharCast_ite)). The proof goes through local factors, with the prime-power case worked out separately: the Legendre character at an odd prime, and `χ₄`, `χ₈` or `χ₈'` at powers of two ([`exists_isPrimeDiscriminant_apply_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/Quadratic/FundamentalDiscriminant.html#DirichletCharacter.IsPrimitive.exists_isPrimeDiscriminant_apply_eq)). The Kronecker-symbol corollary `χ(p) = (d/p)` that the roadmap also asks for is not yet stated.

The rest of the window lays the groundwork for Mertens's estimate. Chebyshev's identity `log ⌊x⌋! = Σ_{n≤x} Λ(n)⌊x/n⌋` arrived in [von Mangoldt](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebyshev/LogFactorial.html#Chebyshev.log_floor_factorial_eq_sum_vonMangoldt) and [`ψ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebyshev/LogFactorial.html#Chebyshev.log_floor_factorial_eq_sum_psi) forms, together with the two-sided bound `|log ⌊x⌋! − (x log x − x)| ≤ (1/2) log x + 1` ([`abs_log_floor_factorial_sub_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Log/Factorial.html#Real.abs_log_floor_factorial_sub_le)). The uniform Euler logarithmic derivative also landed: `L′/L(s, χ) + Σ_p χ(p) log p / p^s` is bounded on `re s > 1` by one constant, whatever the modulus and the character ([`norm_logDeriv_LFunction_add_tsum_prime_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/LFunction/LogDeriv.html#DirichletCharacter.norm_logDeriv_LFunction_add_tsum_prime_le)). Alongside it comes `−ζ′/ζ(1 + η) = η⁻¹ + O(1)` for `0 < η ≤ 1` ([`exists_norm_neg_logDeriv_riemannZeta_one_add_sub_inv_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta/LogDerivNearOne.html#TauCeti.exists_norm_neg_logDeriv_riemannZeta_one_add_sub_inv_le)). Mertens's first theorem itself has not landed.

The supporting work covers the prime tail `Σ_{p≥y} log p / p² ≤ 2 log 4 / y`, a normalized Mellin weight integral with Jensen's bound for `log log`, and the weighted-prime Mellin identity ([`hasSum_weightedPrimeSeries`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebyshev/WeightedPrimeMellin.html#TauCeti.hasSum_weightedPrimeSeries)). That identity rests on the elementary bound `(1 + log t)²` for the partial sums, not on the Mertens bound the roadmap describes.
