## TaubesGromov: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`fe84824` to `273ea28`)

The first work on this roadmap is algebra: the parts of Taubes's curve count that can be stated without any analysis. The four-twist weights of TG2 now exist. Each embedded square-zero torus, labelled by a determinant sign and a count of negative twists, gets a local multiplicity series [`torusWeightSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/TorusWeight.html#TauCeti.torusWeightSeries) in ℤ⟦t⟧. The two signs give mutually inverse series ([`torusWeightSeries_mul_not`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/TorusWeight.html#TauCeti.torusWeightSeries_mul_not)), and the coefficient patterns are computed explicitly. On a homology ray the weight series of a family of tori is the product of the local factors at t^m. Its coefficients come from the [independent-component expansion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/TorusWeight.html#TauCeti.coeff_rayTorusWeightSeries), and with finitely many tori in each class the [infinite product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/TorusWeight.html#TauCeti.coeff_tprod_expand_torusWeightSeries) is computed by a finite cutoff (TauCeti#19004). The labels are inputs here. The flat sign lines and determinant signs that should produce them, and the disjointness theorem that makes the product the actual count, are not yet built.

The [good-curve component inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/GoodCycle.html#TauCeti.sum_mul_apply_self_sub_apply_canonical_le) also landed, together with its consequences for a [good cycle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/GoodCycle.html#TauCeti.IsGoodCycle) through generic points: the images are embedded and disjoint, exceptional spheres occur once, and only square-zero tori orthogonal to κ repeat. All of this is proved for an abstract integral intersection form, with the geometric inputs (positivity, singular adjunction, the generic-point bounds) taken as hypotheses.

For the mapping-torus normalization tests of TG11.2, a symplectic matrix's polynomial p(t) = det(1 − tP) satisfies [p′(1) = g·p(1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Charpoly/Symplectic.html#SymplecticGroup.derivative_eval_one_charpolyRev), which gives the coarse coefficients det(1 − P)(a + 1 − g) of p(t)/(1 − t)². For [Arnold's cat map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/CatMap.html#TauCeti.catMap) the series is 1 − t − 2t² − 3t³ − ⋯, and every iterate is nondegenerate.

## TaubesGromov: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

The analysis of TG4 has started, with the vacuum normalization for plane vortices. The linearized gauge-fixed operator L₀ at the vacuum satisfies the [Weitzenböck identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/VacuumOperator/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.Vortex.temperedVacuumAdjoint_temperedVacuumOperator) L₀*L₀ = −Δ + 1/4 and the [energy identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/VacuumOperator/Basic.html#TauCeti.Vortex.integral_norm_sq_vacuumOperator) ‖L₀x‖² = ‖∇x‖² + ‖x‖²/4. It is [invertible on tempered fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/VacuumOperator/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.Vortex.temperedVacuumOperatorEquiv) with inverse L₀*(−Δ + 1/4)⁻¹, it [restricts to a bijection H^s → H^(s−1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/VacuumOperator/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.Vortex.memSobolev_temperedVacuumOperator_iff), and its inverse has the [gap bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/VacuumOperator/Basic.html#TauCeti.Vortex.integral_norm_sq_vacuumOperatorEquiv_symm_le) ‖L₀⁻¹y‖ ≤ 2‖y‖ (TauCeti#20441). Underneath is general machinery: Green's identity on Schwartz space, the resolvent of the Laplacian as a Fourier multiplier, and [elliptic regularity for −Δ + c](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TemperedDistribution/LaplacianResolvent.html#TemperedDistribution.memSobolev_add_two_iff_neg_laplacian_add_smul) on tempered distributions. This covers only the vacuum. Degree-m vortices, their existence and moduli, and the Fredholm theory at an actual vortex are still to come.

On the mapping-torus side, the [integral Wang torsion order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/MappingTorus/Torsion.html#TauCeti.MappingTorus.card_torsion_homology_one_eq_natAbs_det) is now proved. If H₁ of the fibre is free and det(1 − φ_*) ≠ 0, the torsion of H₁(T_φ) has order |det(1 − φ_*)|. It rests on a Wang cokernel isomorphism in singular homology. The orbit-torus weights of TG11.1 were matched with local zeta factors: for a nondegenerate 2 × 2 return map P of determinant one, the weight series picked out by the signs of det(1 ∓ P) has logarithmic derivative Σ sgn det(1 − Pᵏ) tᵏ ([`X_mul_derivative_torusWeightSeries_of_det_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/OrbitTorusWeight.html#TauCeti.X_mul_derivative_torusWeightSeries_of_det_eq_one)). This is pure matrix algebra. The determinant comparison with the actual normal operators is not there yet.

For TG10.2, the [pointwise energy identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HodgeStar.html#Orientation.inner_ιMulti_ιInv_contractLeft_add_hodgeStar) for a two-form split along a unit vector in an oriented four-space landed, with exterior-algebra lemmas behind it. The integrated identity and the Dirac side are still missing.

## TaubesGromov: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-09 (`93d1fe5` to `7ca783d`)

The plane vortices of TG4 got past the vacuum and onto actual degree-m vortices, short of existence. Two smooth vortices with the same zeros and the same end framing differ by a [unique framed gauge transformation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Vortex.existsUnique_framedGaugeGroup_smul_eq), which is the uniqueness half of Taubes's classification. A vortex framed in degree m has [flux m](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/Flux.html#TauCeti.Vortex.integral_curvature_eq_of_tendsto_sectionValue_mul_inv_pow), and its [energy density decays](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/DerivativeDecay.html#TauCeti.Vortex.exists_energyDensity_le_mul_exp) like exp(−c|z|) for every c < 1/2. The ansatz α = eᵘf [reduces the equation to a scalar one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/Background.html#TauCeti.Vortex.laplacian_backgroundAmplitude_add_eq_iff), but that scalar equation has not been solved. On the linear side, covariant translations lie in the [kernel of the gauge-fixed operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/TranslationKernel.html#TauCeti.Vortex.gaugeFixedOperator_covariantTranslation_of_equation_eq_zero), L_x L_x* has a [spectral gap](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/SpectralGap.html#TauCeti.Vortex.exists_pos_mul_integral_norm_sq_le_integral_norm_sq_gaugeFixedAdjoint), and L_x is [coercive outside a large disc](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/ExteriorEstimate.html#TauCeti.Vortex.exists_exterior_integral_norm_sq_gaugeFixedOperator_ge). Fredholmness and the index 2m are not yet proved. The [affine field space A_m^s](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/FieldSpace.html#TauCeti.Vortex.framedFieldSpace) and the [Sobolev gauge group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/FramedGauge/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.Vortex.sobolevGaugeGroup) are defined, but the Banach charts are not.

On the curve side, the [weighted configuration count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/ConfigurationCount.html#TauCeti.configurationCount) now exists, tori on a square-zero ray are [disjoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/ConfigurationCount.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.pairwise_of_apply_self_eq_zero), and the ray count [equals the ray series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/ConfigurationCount.html#TauCeti.configurationCount_eq_coeff_rayTorusWeightSeries). All of this is still stated for abstract candidates with free labels. For mapping tori, the product of the orbit-torus weights [is the orbit zeta function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/OrbitTorus/Zeta.html#TauCeti.orbitZeta_eq_mapRingHom_tprod_ofPowerSeries_torusWeightSeries). The model orbit-torus normal operator [has kernel exactly when det(1 − P) = 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/OrbitTorus/NormalOperator.html#TauCeti.exists_ne_zero_orbitTorusOperator_eq_zero_iff_det_eq_zero), a fact that rests on a Fredholm alternative for twisted periodic ODEs.

Smaller pieces fill in the rest. They include Gromov's finiteness of classes under a comass bound, Taubes's splitting of the quadratic map, zero localization, the ellipticity of δ₀, and the Spinᶜ lift of plane rotations.

## TaubesGromov: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`7ca783d` to `1b19ebb`)

Every step of Taubes's variational existence proof for plane vortices is now proved. For a polynomial P of degree at least one, Taubes's functional [attains its minimum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/Minimizer.html#TauCeti.Vortex.exists_forall_sobolevReducedFunctional_le) on H¹(ℂ), and a minimizer is a [weak solution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/EulerLagrange.html#TauCeti.Vortex.integral_inner_gradient_add_integral_mul_eq_zero) of the reduced equation. Weak solutions are [classical C² vortices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/Regularity.html#TauCeti.Vortex.exists_contDiff_two_ae_eq_and_equation_amplitudeField_eq_zero), and square-integrable ones [carry the degree-m end framing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/Asymptotics.html#TauCeti.Vortex.equation_amplitudeField_eq_zero_and_tendsto_sectionValue_of_memLp). No theorem yet chains these into existence for each divisor. On the uniqueness side, the zero divisor is now a [complete invariant of the framed gauge orbit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Vortex/Divisor.html#TauCeti.Vortex.exists_framedGaugeGroup_smul_eq_iff). The Sobolev gauge group acts freely on the field space and is exponential near the identity, and the equation and gauge-fixed operator act on the Sobolev scale. Fredholmness and the index 2m are still open.

The large-parameter analysis moved from pointwise algebra to integrated estimates. These are proved in a flat chart with Taubes's coarse bounds taken as hypotheses. Under them the curvature current has [r-independent mass](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/CurrentMass.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.exists_mass_integral_wedge_le_of_add_hodgeStar_eq), the cutoff current lies [within O(r^(−1/2)) of the positive (1,1)-cone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/ConeDefect.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.integral_infDist_positiveCone_le), and integer disc charges [stabilize along a sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/DiscCharge.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.exists_int_eventually_integral_cutoffCurrent_comp_closedBall_eq). Lower density and covering bounds for the zero set also landed. Nothing yet derives the coarse bounds from actual solutions.

Mapping tori got their first theorem about actual curves. A nonconstant pseudoholomorphic horizontal curve in S¹ × Y_φ lies over a closed orbit and [covers its orbit torus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Suspension/Leaf.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus.exists_surjective_orbitTorus_comp_eq_of_isPseudoholomorphicAt). The zeta assembly now runs over the [closed orbits of an actual diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/OrbitTorus/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus.orbitZeta_closedOrbitHomologyClass_eq_mapRingHom_tprod), but its labels are still free. Twisted periodic ODEs got the [orientation sign of their determinant line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/Periodic.html#TauCeti.sign_detLineEndEquiv_detLineTwistedLinearOperatorEquiv). Smaller pieces round out the window: a weighted count through points, period-doubling and birth-death identities for the ray series, and Künneth-type results for products with spheres.
