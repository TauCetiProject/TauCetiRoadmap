## TaubesGromov: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`fe84824` to `273ea28`)

The first work on this roadmap is algebra: the parts of Taubes's curve count that can be stated without any analysis. The four-twist weights of TG2 now exist. Each embedded square-zero torus, labelled by a determinant sign and a count of negative twists, gets a local multiplicity series [`torusWeightSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/TorusWeight.html#TauCeti.torusWeightSeries) in ℤ⟦t⟧. The two signs give mutually inverse series ([`torusWeightSeries_mul_not`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/TorusWeight.html#TauCeti.torusWeightSeries_mul_not)), and the coefficient patterns are computed explicitly. On a homology ray the weight series of a family of tori is the product of the local factors at t^m. Its coefficients come from the [independent-component expansion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/TorusWeight.html#TauCeti.coeff_rayTorusWeightSeries), and with finitely many tori in each class the [infinite product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/TorusWeight.html#TauCeti.coeff_tprod_expand_torusWeightSeries) is computed by a finite cutoff (TauCeti#19004). The labels are inputs here. The flat sign lines and determinant signs that should produce them, and the disjointness theorem that makes the product the actual count, are not yet built.

The [good-curve component inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/GoodCycle.html#TauCeti.sum_mul_apply_self_sub_apply_canonical_le) also landed, together with its consequences for a [good cycle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/GoodCycle.html#TauCeti.IsGoodCycle) through generic points: the images are embedded and disjoint, exceptional spheres occur once, and only square-zero tori orthogonal to κ repeat. All of this is proved for an abstract integral intersection form, with the geometric inputs (positivity, singular adjunction, the generic-point bounds) taken as hypotheses.

For the mapping-torus normalization tests of TG11.2, a symplectic matrix's polynomial p(t) = det(1 − tP) satisfies [p′(1) = g·p(1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Charpoly/Symplectic.html#SymplecticGroup.derivative_eval_one_charpolyRev), which gives the coarse coefficients det(1 − P)(a + 1 − g) of p(t)/(1 − t)². For [Arnold's cat map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/CatMap.html#TauCeti.catMap) the series is 1 − t − 2t² − 3t³ − ⋯, and every iterate is nondegenerate.
