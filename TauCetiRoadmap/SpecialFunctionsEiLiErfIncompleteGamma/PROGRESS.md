## SpecialFunctionsEiLiErfIncompleteGamma: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`8063bdc` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap's first piece landed in one pull request (TauCeti#34719): Tricomi's entire incomplete Gamma function [`γ*(a, z)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.tricomiIncompleteGamma), defined as `e^{−z}` times Mathlib's regularized Kummer function `𝐌(1, a + 1, z)`, with everything subsection 1.1 asks of it. It is [entire in the argument](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.differentiable_tricomiIncompleteGamma) and [in the parameter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.differentiable_tricomiIncompleteGamma_left); it has both power series of DLMF 8.7.1, the [series in `z`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.hasSum_tricomiIncompleteGamma) for all `a` and the [series in `−z`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.hasSum_tricomiIncompleteGamma_of_ne_neg_natCast) for `a ∉ −ℕ`; [Tricomi's integral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.tricomiIncompleteGamma_eq_integral) `γ*(a, z) = Γ(a)⁻¹ ∫₀¹ t^{a−1} e^{−zt} dt` for `Re a > 0`; and the [second-order differential equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.tricomiIncompleteGamma_ode) 8.2.14. The special values `γ*(a, 0) = 1/Γ(a + 1)`, [`γ*(−n, z) = zⁿ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.tricomiIncompleteGamma_neg_natCast) and `γ*(1, z) = (1 − e^{−z})/z` are there too, along with the [recurrence in the parameter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteGamma/Tricomi.html#Complex.tricomiIncompleteGamma_eq_mul_tricomiIncompleteGamma_add_one) and the derivative `∂γ*/∂z = −a γ*(a + 1, z)`.

The supporting work is about regularized hypergeometric series and reciprocal Gamma values: the series of `𝐌` with at most as many upper as lower parameters converges absolutely everywhere and can be differentiated term by term, `1/Γ(s + k)` decays like `1/k!` when `Re s ≥ 1`, and a finite alternating sum of reciprocal Gamma values links the two series.

The rest of Layer 1 has not started. The regularized function `P`, the lower and upper functions `γ(a, z)` and `Γ(a, z)`, the bridge to Tau Ceti's real incomplete Gamma, and the continuation to the logarithmic plane are all still to come. Nothing has landed yet for the later layers.
