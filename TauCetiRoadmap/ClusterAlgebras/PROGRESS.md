## ClusterAlgebras: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`90a709e` to `1c6a1a4`)

The cluster algebras roadmap has started at its two foundations: the coefficients and the exchange matrices. Fomin and Zelevinsky's [coefficient semifields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/CoefficientSemifield/Basic.html#TauCeti.CoefficientSemifield), commutative groups with an auxiliary addition that multiplication distributes over, now exist with their homomorphisms, products, the one-element object and the units of suitable semifields such as the nonnegative rationals. Each one is [torsion-free](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/CoefficientSemifield/Basic.html#TauCeti.CoefficientSemifield.instHasUniqueRoots), and a new general instance, that [unique roots give two unique products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/UniqueProds/TorsionFree.html#HasUniqueRoots.to_twoUniqueProds), makes the group ring ℤ[P] a domain (TauCeti#35513). The tropical and universal subtraction-free semifields are not there yet.

On the matrix side, [exchange matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExchangeMatrix) are extended integer matrices that carry a chosen skew-symmetrizer, and [matrix mutation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Mutation.html#TauCeti.matrixMutation) is defined on them. It is [an involution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Mutation.html#TauCeti.matrixMutation_involutive) and [preserves every skew-symmetrizer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Mutation.html#Matrix.IsSkewSymmetrizer.matrixMutation). It also commutes with passing to the principal block, with reindexing, with restriction and with block-diagonal sums (TauCeti#35579). The ice-quiver dictionary, the factorization of mutation through two involutive matrices, and rank invariance are still to come.

## ClusterAlgebras: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`1c6a1a4` to `aa6e43e`)

The exchange-matrix layer now has its main structural theorem: the factorization of mutation as `μ_k(B̃) = E_ε B̃ F_ε` through two [involutive integer matrices](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Factorization.html#TauCeti.matrixMutationLeft), proved as [the signed factorization of extended mutation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Factorization.html#TauCeti.matrixMutation_eq_mul). From it follow [invariance of rank](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Factorization.html#TauCeti.rank_matrixMutation) and of [column independence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Factorization.html#TauCeti.linearIndependent_col_matrixMutation_iff) under mutation, over any commutative ring. Mutation also preserves [the common divisors of each column](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Divisibility.html#TauCeti.dvd_matrixMutation_iff) and which columns are zero. [Column compatibility](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Compatibility.html#Matrix.IsColumnCompatible) of an extended matrix with an alternating form is defined, and nonzero weights [force independent columns](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Compatibility.html#Matrix.IsColumnCompatible.linearIndependent). The [principal extension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Compatibility.html#TauCeti.ExchangeMatrix.principalExtension) of any exchange matrix [has independent columns](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/ExchangeMatrix/Compatibility.html#TauCeti.ExchangeMatrix.linearIndependent_principalExtension) and comes with a compatible form. Still missing here are the ice-quiver dictionary and mutation of the form itself.

The first seeds have arrived. [Y-seeds](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/YSeed/Basic.html#TauCeti.YSeed) over a coefficient semifield mutate by the normalized Y-formulas, and this mutation is [an involution](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/YSeed/Basic.html#TauCeti.YSeed.mutation_involutive) that [commutes with coefficient specialization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/YSeed/Basic.html#TauCeti.YSeed.map_mutation) and relabelling. Beneath them, the [Laurent ring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Laurent/Basic.html#TauCeti.LaurentRing.aevalEquiv) is shown to be the free commutative algebra on invertible generators and [the localization of the polynomial ring at its variables](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Laurent/Polynomial.html#TauCeti.LaurentRing.isLocalization) (TauCeti#35548). The [tropical semifield](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/CoefficientSemifield/Tropical.html#TauCeti.Trop) `Trop(J)` also landed, with generators, evaluations and reindexing, though only in the integer-exponent case and without the max version or adjoined zero.

On the Laurent-phenomenon side, the order-theoretic half of the upper-bound argument is in place. If [upper bounds](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/UpperBound/Lattice.html#TauCeti.Mutation.upperBound) satisfy local three-chart inclusions, they [equal the intersection of all reachable charts](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/UpperBound/Lattice.html#TauCeti.Mutation.upperBound_eq_iInf_foldl_of_local), and from this follows [membership of every reachable generator in every chart](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cluster/UpperBound.html#TauCeti.Mutation.mem_chart_foldl_of_upperBound_invariant). This is stated for abstract involutions, so no seed yet supplies those local inclusions.
