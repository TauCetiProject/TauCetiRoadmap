## NoncongruenceModularForms: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`50ef3ee` to `aa6e43e`)

The roadmap's foundation is in place: [the presentation of the modular group as a triangle group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/TriangleGroup.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.modularEquiv), `PSL₂(ℤ) ≅ Δ(3, 2, ∞)`, now holds with the cusp generator sent to `T` itself and the order-three generator to `U = S T⁻¹` (TauCeti#37064). Injectivity does not come from the ping-pong route the roadmap sketches. It comes from the existing geometric presentation, read off the fundamental triangle by Poincaré's polygon theorem, composed with a new [mirror automorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Basic.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.mirror) of any triangle group. Underneath it sit the pinned integral generators [R = ST](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Modular/Generators.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.R) and [U](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Modular/Generators.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.U), with every sign recorded (`R³ = −I` but `U³ = I`), their projective orders, and the [surjective homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Modular/Generators.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.modularHom) from the triangle group. The free-product corollaries (torsion classes, freeness of torsion-free subgroups, the abelianization `ℤ/6`) are not there yet.

On the level side, the [strict level](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/CongruenceSubgroups/StrictLevel.html#Subgroup.strictLevel) of a subgroup of `SL₂(ℤ)` landed (TauCeti#36404). It is the least `N` with every conjugate of `Tᴺ` in the group, with no sign allowed. It is [the least common multiple of the strict cusp widths](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/CongruenceSubgroups/StrictLevel.html#Subgroup.strictLevel_eq_lcm_cuspTranslationOrbitWidth), it [turns intersections into least common multiples](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/CongruenceSubgroups/StrictLevel.html#Subgroup.strictLevel_inf), cusp by cusp as well as globally, and it is [unchanged by passing to the normal core](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/CongruenceSubgroups/StrictLevel.html#Subgroup.strictLevel_normalCore). `Γ(N)`, `Γ₀(N)` and `Γ₁(N)` have strict level `N`. This is the modulus the strict Wohlfahrt theorem needs, but that theorem itself, and the permutation-side general level, have not yet been proved.
