## CombinatorialHeegaardFloer: 2026-06-19 to 2026-08-03 (`06f94e6` to `11ef09d`)

Grid diagrams acquired their move calculus, and with it links. Commutation of non-interleaving adjacent rows or columns, stabilization splitting an `O`- or `X`-marking, and their inverses are now a single reversible relation [`IsMove`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsMove); a [grid link](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move.html#TauCeti.GridLink) is the quotient of grid diagrams of every size by the equivalence it generates, and [two diagrams present the same link exactly when moves connect them](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.toGridLink_eq_iff_movesTo), with [`GridLink.lift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move.html#TauCeti.GridLink.lift) turning move-invariant functions on diagrams into functions on links (TauCeti#1589). The roadmap's convention that a link *is* a grid diagram modulo grid moves is now a definition, so nothing downstream waits on Cromwell's theorem.

Two standard families arrived: the [unknot grid](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Unknot.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unknot) of every size, and [`torusLink`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.torusLink) for the `(p+1, q+1)` torus link, both with the gradings of their marked states. A torus grid has [`gcd (p+1) (q+1)` components](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.componentCount_torusLink), hence [represents a knot exactly when the winding numbers are coprime](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.isKnot_torusLink_iff), with the trefoil and the Hopf link as instances; the count rests on a general cycle type for [a power of a cycle](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/CyclePower.html#TauCeti.cycleType_pow_of_isCycle). Grid homology itself is computed only where the differential vanishes for size reasons: on grids of size at most two it is [all of the chain module](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.finrank_fullyBlockedHomology_of_le_two), of dimension `n!`, so a `2 × 2` unknot grid has [the two-dimensional homology](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.finrank_fullyBlockedHomology_of_two) that the `W = 𝔽 ⊕ 𝔽` stabilization factor predicts.

Lattice homology reached a chain complex: the weighted differential on plumbing-lattice cubes over `𝔽₂[U]` [squares to zero](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/Differential.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.latticeDifferential_comp_self), cubical dimension grades it [into a complex](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/ChainComplex.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.latticeChainComplex) whose homology [vanishes above the number of vertices](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/Bounded.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.isZero_latticeChainHomology_of_card_lt). Around it: intersection forms and characteristic covectors, [blow-up splitting off an orthogonal `⟨-1⟩` summand](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/BlowUp.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.intersectionForm_blowUpVertexEquiv), negative-definiteness [from strict diagonal dominance](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/DiagonalDominance.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.isNegativeDefinite_of_degree_lt_neg_weight), and [`E₈` negative definite](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/E8.html#TauCeti.e8Plumbing_isNegativeDefinite) by an explicit completion of squares. All of this is characteristic two: Némethi's `ℤ[U]` theory and Neumann-move invariance are untouched.

## CombinatorialHeegaardFloer: 2026-08-03 to 2026-09-08 (`11ef09d` to `b53ed55`)

Grid homology reached its first Euler-characteristic milestone. The grid determinant formula says that the alternating Alexander state sum of a grid diagram is a sign times a monomial times [the determinant of its weight matrix](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Determinant.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stateSum_eq_smul_T_mul_det_weightMatrix), whose entries are the monomials of the local Alexander weights of the grid points (TauCeti#4336); and for a diagram presenting an odd number of components that expression is exactly [the graded Euler characteristic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.OddComponentGridDiagram.gradedEulerChar_eq_smul_T_mul_det_weightMatrix) of the bigraded grid chain module (TauCeti#4479), checked against `1 - T⁻²` on the standard `2 × 2` unknot grid. This is a chain-level statement: `∂² = 0` is not yet proved in general, so there is no homology to take an Euler characteristic of, and matching the determinant to the Alexander polynomial belongs to the reconciliation lane. Along the way the Alexander grading was shown to be [integral exactly for diagrams with an odd number of components](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Grading/Parity.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.alexander_exists_int_iff), so knot grids qualify.

The unblocked complex arrived: [`GC⁻`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Unblocked.html#TauCeti.GridChainMinus) over `𝔽₂[V₀, …, V_{n-1}]` with its rectangle-counting differential, the simply blocked theory as its specialization at `V_i = 0`, and the bigrading in which [`∂⁻` has bidegree `(-1, 0)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Grading/UnblockedChain.html#TauCeti.OddComponentGridDiagram.unblockedDifferential_mem_bigradedChainMinusPiece) and each variable has bidegree `(-2, -1)`. The proof that `∂⁻` squares to zero is being run through the book's trichotomy on juxtaposed empty rectangles: the annular terms [vanish over any coefficient ring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Differential/Square/Annulus.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedDifferential_sq_single_apply_self), the disjoint ones cancel in characteristic two, and of the four overlapping configurations one now has [its recut](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Differential/Square/Recut.html#TauCeti.GridRectangleDecomposition.exists_isRepartition_of_mem_unblockedRectangles_of_left_eq_left).

Lattice homology got its first computation: a one-vertex negative-definite plumbing has [a single `U`-tower](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/OneVertex.html#TauCeti.coefficientEquivOneVertexPlumbingLatticeHomology) in each spin^c structure. More generally a minimal-weight lattice point gives an augmentation-one cycle spanning a free `𝔽₂[U]`, so the homology of `E₈` is at least [nonzero](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/E8.html#TauCeti.e8Plumbing_not_isZero_latticeHomology) throughout. Edge blow-up, the characteristic-weight filtration, and a `⊗W`-stabilization quotient for bigraded Poincaré series round out the window.
