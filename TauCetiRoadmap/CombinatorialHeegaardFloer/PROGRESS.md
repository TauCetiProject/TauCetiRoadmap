## CombinatorialHeegaardFloer: 2026-06-19 to 2026-08-03 (`06f94e6` to `11ef09d`)

Grid diagrams acquired their move calculus, and with it links. Commutation of non-interleaving adjacent rows or columns, stabilization splitting an `O`- or `X`-marking, and their inverses are now a single reversible relation [`IsMove`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsMove); a [grid link](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move.html#TauCeti.GridLink) is the quotient of grid diagrams of every size by the equivalence it generates, and [two diagrams present the same link exactly when moves connect them](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.toGridLink_eq_iff_movesTo), with [`GridLink.lift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move.html#TauCeti.GridLink.lift) turning move-invariant functions on diagrams into functions on links (TauCeti#1589). The roadmap's convention that a link *is* a grid diagram modulo grid moves is now a definition, so nothing downstream waits on Cromwell's theorem.

Two standard families arrived: the [unknot grid](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Unknot.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unknot) of every size, and [`torusLink`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.torusLink) for the `(p+1, q+1)` torus link, both with the gradings of their marked states. A torus grid has [`gcd (p+1) (q+1)` components](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.componentCount_torusLink), hence [represents a knot exactly when the winding numbers are coprime](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.isKnot_torusLink_iff), with the trefoil and the Hopf link as instances; the count rests on a general cycle type for [a power of a cycle](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/CyclePower.html#TauCeti.cycleType_pow_of_isCycle). Grid homology itself is computed only where the differential vanishes for size reasons: on grids of size at most two it is [all of the chain module](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.finrank_fullyBlockedHomology_of_le_two), of dimension `n!`, so a `2 × 2` unknot grid has [the two-dimensional homology](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.finrank_fullyBlockedHomology_of_two) that the `W = 𝔽 ⊕ 𝔽` stabilization factor predicts.

Lattice homology reached a chain complex: the weighted differential on plumbing-lattice cubes over `𝔽₂[U]` [squares to zero](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/Differential.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.latticeDifferential_comp_self), cubical dimension grades it [into a complex](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/ChainComplex.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.latticeChainComplex) whose homology [vanishes above the number of vertices](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/Bounded.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.isZero_latticeChainHomology_of_card_lt). Around it: intersection forms and characteristic covectors, [blow-up splitting off an orthogonal `⟨-1⟩` summand](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/BlowUp.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.intersectionForm_blowUpVertexEquiv), negative-definiteness [from strict diagonal dominance](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/DiagonalDominance.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.isNegativeDefinite_of_degree_lt_neg_weight), and [`E₈` negative definite](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/E8.html#TauCeti.e8Plumbing_isNegativeDefinite) by an explicit completion of squares. All of this is characteristic two: Némethi's `ℤ[U]` theory and Neumann-move invariance are untouched.

## CombinatorialHeegaardFloer: 2026-08-03 to 2026-09-08 (`11ef09d` to `b53ed55`)

Grid homology reached its first Euler-characteristic milestone. The grid determinant formula says that the alternating Alexander state sum of a grid diagram is a sign times a monomial times [the determinant of its weight matrix](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Determinant.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stateSum_eq_smul_T_mul_det_weightMatrix), whose entries are the monomials of the local Alexander weights of the grid points (TauCeti#4336); and for a diagram presenting an odd number of components that expression is exactly [the graded Euler characteristic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.OddComponentGridDiagram.gradedEulerChar_eq_smul_T_mul_det_weightMatrix) of the bigraded grid chain module (TauCeti#4479), checked against `1 - T⁻²` on the standard `2 × 2` unknot grid. This is a chain-level statement: `∂² = 0` is not yet proved in general, so there is no homology to take an Euler characteristic of, and matching the determinant to the Alexander polynomial belongs to the reconciliation lane. Along the way the Alexander grading was shown to be [integral exactly for diagrams with an odd number of components](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Grading/Parity.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.alexander_exists_int_iff), so knot grids qualify.

The unblocked complex arrived: [`GC⁻`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Unblocked.html#TauCeti.GridChainMinus) over `𝔽₂[V₀, …, V_{n-1}]` with its rectangle-counting differential, the simply blocked theory as its specialization at `V_i = 0`, and the bigrading in which [`∂⁻` has bidegree `(-1, 0)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Grading/UnblockedChain.html#TauCeti.OddComponentGridDiagram.unblockedDifferential_mem_bigradedChainMinusPiece) and each variable has bidegree `(-2, -1)`. The proof that `∂⁻` squares to zero is being run through the book's trichotomy on juxtaposed empty rectangles: the annular terms [vanish over any coefficient ring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Differential/Square/Annulus.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedDifferential_sq_single_apply_self), the disjoint ones cancel in characteristic two, and of the four overlapping configurations one now has [its recut](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Differential/Square/Recut.html#TauCeti.GridRectangleDecomposition.exists_isRepartition_of_mem_unblockedRectangles_of_left_eq_left).

Lattice homology got its first computation: a one-vertex negative-definite plumbing has [a single `U`-tower](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/OneVertex.html#TauCeti.coefficientEquivOneVertexPlumbingLatticeHomology) in each spin^c structure. More generally a minimal-weight lattice point gives an augmentation-one cycle spanning a free `𝔽₂[U]`, so the homology of `E₈` is at least [nonzero](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/E8.html#TauCeti.e8Plumbing_not_isZero_latticeHomology) throughout. Edge blow-up, the characteristic-weight filtration, and a `⊗W`-stabilization quotient for bigraded Poincaré series round out the window.

Grid homology now exists in general. The unblocked grid differential [squares to zero](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Differential/Square/Zero.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedDifferential_comp_self_eq_zero) in characteristic two, and so do the fully blocked and simply blocked ones (TauCeti#6211). That completes milestone G.3 and makes [`GH⁻`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/Unblocked.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedHomology) a genuine homology module. `X`-marking homotopies show that [all variables of a knot grid act identically on it](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/XHomotopy/Complex.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsKnot.homologyMap_X_smul_eq), which makes it [an `𝔽[U]`-module](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/Unblocked.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsKnot.unblockedHomologyModule) with an Alexander grading. Specializing one variable to zero gives a long exact sequence, and [a class that dies in `GĤ` is a multiple of `U`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/ExactSequence.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsKnot.exact_X_smul_simplyBlockedHomologyMap). The grid determinant is now [the graded Euler characteristic of fully blocked homology](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.OddComponentGridDiagram.gradedHomologyEulerChar_eq_smul_T_mul_det_weightMatrix), not just of the chain module.

Work on invariance began from both sides, and neither side is finished. For stabilization, the complex of an `X`-stabilization [is a mapping cone](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Cone.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedComplexStabilizeXIsoHomotopyCofiber), and the resulting [stabilization map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Map.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stabilizeXMap) is [a quasi-isomorphism provided one component of an `X`-homotopy is](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Map.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.quasiIso_stabilizeXMap). That hypothesis is still open. A graded Nakayama lemma, saying that [quasi-isomorphisms of free complexes over a polynomial ring can be detected modulo the variables](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReductionModVariables.html#LinearMap.homologyMap_bijective_of_mapRange_constantCoeff), is ready to help. For commutation, the [pentagon map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Commutation/Pentagon.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.pentagonMap) is defined, and its chain-map identity has been [reduced to the overlapping domains](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Commutation/Disjoint/Reduction.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.pentagonMap_unblockedDifferential_single_eq_iff_overlap), which are being handled by recuts.

Lane H has its first code: [generators](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/Generator.html#TauCeti.HeegaardIntersectionSystem.Generator) of finite Heegaard intersection data. Lattice homology gained [spin^c structures as characteristic orbits](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Plumbing/SpinC.html#TauCeti.PlumbingGraph.characteristicOrbits) and the canonical weight of all-`-2` plumbings. On the algebra side there is the [top non-torsion degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/GradedModule/NonTorsionDegree.html#TauCeti.InternalGrading.supNonTorsionDegree) of a graded `k[X]`-module, which is the shape τ will take. There is no structure theorem yet.

## CombinatorialHeegaardFloer: 2026-09-30 to 2026-09-30 (`dec7a58` to `0d3161a`)

Grid homology met its first torus knots. On the standard grid of a torus knot, the `X`-marking state is a cycle that no power of `U` kills, so [`GH⁻` is not a torsion module](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.not_isTorsion_unblockedHomology_torusLink), and its [top non-torsion Alexander degree is `p q / 2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.two_mul_supNonTorsionDegree_torusLink), the Seifert genus of the `(p+1, q+1)` torus knot (TauCeti#10002). The instances are degree `1` on the `5 × 5` trefoil grid and [degree `3` on the `7 × 7` grid of `T(3,4)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.supNonTorsionDegree_torusLink_two_three). This is the computation behind `τ(T_{3,4}) = 3`, but only for the grid. `τ` is not defined, and without invariance nothing is yet claimed about the knot. The proof is a parity argument: every state has an even number of counted rectangles into the `X`-marking state, so setting all variables to `1` kills every boundary coefficient there.

Stabilization invariance moved closer. [`H_I^N` is now a quasi-isomorphism whenever its reduction modulo the variables induces a bijection on homology](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Reduction.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.quasiIso_stabilizeXOffCenterToCenterHom_of_bijective), and that reduction is a comparison of [fully blocked complexes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Reduction.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.constantCoeffReduction_stabilizeXCenterDifferential_eq_fullyBlockedDifferential). What remains is a statement about finite-dimensional complexes over `𝔽₂`, but it is still unproved. The `X`-marking homotopies were packaged as [chain homotopies](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/XHomotopy/Complex.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedComplexXHomotopy). Through a [component permutation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Diagram/Components.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.componentPerm), they also relate the variables of consecutive markings on one link component.

For commutation, the overlapping rectangle–pentagon cases gained two more recuts, and there is supporting work on toroidal rectangles, reflections, and row non-interleaving. The pentagon map is still not shown to be a chain map, and no hexagon homotopy exists yet.

## CombinatorialHeegaardFloer: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (`0d3161a` to `22df7c0`)

The `X`-stabilization step of grid invariance now has every ingredient of the book's argument, but they have not yet been put together. Its one open hypothesis is that a reduced comparison of fully blocked complexes over `𝔽₂` is bijective on homology. On the algebra side, a square-zero map is exact once its generators are [paired by a weight-preserving matching](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/SquareZero/Contraction.html#LinearMap.ker_le_range_of_matching) over a well-founded weight (TauCeti#10298). That rests on a contracting-homotopy criterion: [exactness when `d h + h d - 1` strictly lowers the weight](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/SquareZero/Contraction.html#LinearMap.ker_le_range_of_forall_mem_support_lt). The mapping cone was also [rewritten on the disjoint union of the two bases](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/SquareZero.html#LinearMap.sumMappingCone) so that the criterion applies to it. On the grid side, the cone's generators are [the grid states of the stabilized diagram](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Cone.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stabilizeXConeStateEquiv). [Swapping the two new rows](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Matching.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stabilizeXMatching) gives a fixed-point-free matching on them (TauCeti#10395). A [level](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Level.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stabilizeXLevel) built from a balanced weight is constant on matched pairs, and it [strictly drops across any rectangle covering an outer square](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Level.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stabilizeXLevel_lt_or_disjoint). Exactness of the reduced cone has not been stated, so `X`-stabilization is not yet known to be a quasi-isomorphism.

The rest of the window is support. There is a general formula for how pairing a state with a row- and column-balanced weight [changes across a rectangle](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Grading/MarkingCount.html#TauCeti.GridRectangleBetween.JWeight_sub_JWeight_eq_sum). There are size bounds on the fully blocked differential, which [reaches at most `n choose 2` states from each generator](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Differential/Support/Cardinality.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.fullyBlockedDifferentialOnGenerator_support_card_le_choose). Column-swap lemmas tidy the `∂² = 0` argument. Commutation invariance did not move.

## CombinatorialHeegaardFloer: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05 (`22df7c0` to `1d09589`)

The concordance invariant τ is now defined on knot grids, as [minus the top non-torsion Alexander degree of `GH⁻`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/Tau.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsKnot.tau), and computed on the standard torus knot grids, where [`2τ = −pq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink/Tau.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.two_mul_tau_torusLink) for the `(p+1, q+1)` torus knot (TauCeti#10453). On the `7 × 7` grid of `T(3,4)` this gives [`τ = −3`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink/Tau.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.tau_torusLink_two_three). That is the roadmap's `τ(T_{3,4}) = 3` up to sign, and the orientation convention behind the sign has not been reconciled. τ is still a function of a diagram, not yet of a grid link.

Most of invariance landed. The `X`-stabilization map [is bijective on `GH⁻`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stabilizeXHomologyMap_bijective), so [τ is unchanged by `X`-stabilization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsKnot.tau_stabilizeX). This closes the reduced cone comparison left open last time. A [half-turn symmetry of `GH⁻`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/HalfTurn.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedHomologyRotateEquiv) carries this to [the other corner](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/NorthEast.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stabilizeXSuccHomologyMap_bijective), and [every stabilization is connected to one of these two corners](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move/Stabilization.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsStabilization.exists_commutationMovesTo) by cyclic permutations and commutations. Several more overlapping rectangle–pentagon configurations were paired off (TauCeti#11440), but the pentagon map is still not a chain map, and there is no hexagon homotopy.

Grid links can now be told apart by their number of components, so [the Hopf link is not the unknot](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Move/Components.html#TauCeti.GridLink.toGridLink_torusLink_one_one_ne_toGridLink_unknot). Lanes L, H and K did not move.
