## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Lane G has grid homology in all three flavours over `𝔽₂`. Every grid move now has its chain-level invariance argument: stabilization and cyclic permutation act by isomorphisms on `GH⁻`, and commutation has both its chain map and its homotopy equation. These are not yet assembled into a link invariant, so τ is still defined on diagrams, not on grid links. The Milnor conjecture has started, through crossing changes and unknotting numbers. Lanes ALG, L and H are partial, and Lane K has not begun.

### Named results

- **Commutation invariance at chain level** — the pentagon-counting map [is a chain map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Commutation/InitialPentagon/Overlap/Residual/Assembly.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.commutationMap_comp_unblockedDifferential), and the hexagon-counting map satisfies [`∂H + H∂ = 1 + Ψ∘Φ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Commutation/Homotopy/Equation.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedDifferential_commutationHomotopy_add_eq), over any semiring of characteristic two.
- **Stabilization invariance of `GH⁻`** — the `X`-stabilization map [is bijective on unblocked grid homology](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/Homology.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.stabilizeXHomologyMap_bijective). On fully blocked homology, stabilization [multiplies the bigraded Poincaré series by `W`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Stabilization/FullyBlocked/Homology.html#TauCeti.OddComponentGridDiagram.bigradedFullyBlockedHomologyPoincareSeries_stabilizeX), the predicted grid-size dependence.
- **τ of torus knot grids** — on the standard grid of the `(p+1, q+1)` torus knot, [`2τ = −pq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/TorusLink/Tau.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.two_mul_tau_torusLink). This gives `τ(T_{3,4}) = 3` up to an orientation sign that has not been reconciled, and it is still a statement about a single grid.
- **The structure theorem for graded `k[X]`-modules** — a finitely generated graded `k[X]`-module over a field, with `X` of nonzero degree, [is an internal direct sum of homogeneous free towers and cyclic torsion summands](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/GradedModule/CyclicDecomposition.html#TauCeti.InternalGrading.exists_isInternal_span_singleton). This is the algebraic home of τ.
- **The Euler characteristic of grid homology** — the graded Euler characteristic of fully blocked homology [is the normalized grid determinant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.OddComponentGridDiagram.gradedHomologyEulerChar_eq_smul_T_mul_det_weightMatrix).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [The unknotting number of a grid link](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/CrossingChange/UnknottingNumber.html#TauCeti.GridLink.unknottingNumber) is its distance from the unknot when crossing changes are presented as cross-commutations. It is at most one for the trefoil and three for `T(3,4)`. Across a crossing change the commutation map either preserves the grading or [has bidegree `(-2, -1)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/CrossingChange/Grading.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.commutationBidegree_eq_of_notMem_columnArc), which is exactly the input that the existing comparison lemma for τ expects.
- [τ of a knot grid](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/Tau.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.IsKnot.tau) is minus the top non-torsion Alexander degree of `GH⁻`. It is already invariant under stabilization, cyclic permutation, the half-turn and the diagonal reflection, and the commutation map [preserves the `X`-marking class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Grid/Homology/Commutation/XMarking.html#TauCeti.GridDiagram.unblockedHomologyCommutationMap_XMarkingClass) that detects it on torus knots.
- The [hat differential](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/HatDifferential.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.hatDifferential) counts nonnegative Maslov-index-one domains on a pointed Heegaard diagram. On [nice diagrams](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/Nice.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.IsNice) it only counts bigon-like and square-like moves.

## The frontier

- **τ as a grid-link invariant.** Turn the commutation chain map and homotopy into a bijection on `GH⁻` that preserves τ, lift τ through the grid-link quotient, and settle the sign against `τ(T_{3,4}) = 3`.
- **`|τ(K)| ≤ u(K)`.** Show that the two crossing-change maps compose to multiplication by `U` in each direction, and then feed them to the comparison lemma. This needs τ on grid links first.
- **The Milnor conjecture for `T_{2,3}` and `T_{3,4}`.** Once the bound holds, the upper bounds on unknotting numbers and the torus-knot τ values close these cases. The general `(p, q)` statement also needs unknotting upper bounds for every torus knot.
- **Lattice homology of `E₈`, and Neumann invariance.** Nonvanishing is known but the rank is not. Invariance needs the blow-up comparisons carried up to homology.
- **The hat complex.** Prove `∂² = 0` for the hat differential on nice, weakly admissible diagrams. This opens `HF̂_st` and the lens-space and `S¹ × S²` acceptance checks.
