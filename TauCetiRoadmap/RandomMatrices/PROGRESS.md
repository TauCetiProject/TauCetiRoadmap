## RandomMatrices: 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-08 (`f39dcd8` to `a4fcb57`)

The two limiting laws of the roadmap now exist with their full toolkit. The [Marchenko–Pastur law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.marchenkoPastur) is built as an atom at zero plus a reweighted semicircle, with its exact support, its [moments as sums over noncrossing partitions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Moments.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_pow_marchenkoPastur) (equivalently, Narayana polynomials), its resolvent transform [characterised as the upper-half-plane root](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/ResolventTransform.html#TauCeti.Probability.eq_resolventTransform_marchenkoPastur_iff) of `yzm² + (z+y-1)m + 1 = 0`, and [weak continuity in the aspect ratio](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Continuity.html#TauCeti.Probability.continuous_marchenkoPastur) down to `δ₁` at `y = 0` (TauCeti#27664). The centre/variance [semicircle family](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Semicircle/LocationScale.html#TauCeti.Probability.semicircleReal) got its Catalan moments and the same kind of transform characterisation. Underneath both sits [Stieltjes inversion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ResolventTransform/Inversion.html#MeasureTheory.tendsto_intervalIntegral_im_resolventTransform), with the half-atom endpoint terms, together with uniqueness and moment expansions of the resolvent transform.

The deterministic spectral layer filled out. It now has the [Hoffman–Wielandt inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HoffmanWielandt.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.sum_sq_eigenvalues₀_sub_le_frobenius_norm_sub_sq) and its [singular-value analogue](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HoffmanWielandt.html#Matrix.sum_sq_singularValues_toEuclideanLin_sub_le_frobenius_norm_sub_sq), Weyl's additive inequalities, the [rank perturbation bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SpectralMeasure/RankPerturbation.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.abs_spectralMeasure_Iic_sub_le_rank_div_card) for spectral distribution functions, and the resolvent identities a Wigner proof uses: the [Ward identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Resolvent/Basic.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.sum_norm_inv_sub_smul_one_apply_sq), [leave-one-out](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Resolvent/LeaveOneOut.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.inv_sub_smul_one_apply_self_eq) and Sherman–Morrison. On the probabilistic side came the [Azuma–Hoeffding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/Azuma.html#TauCeti.Probability.measureReal_sum_range_ge_le_of_bounded_martingale_differences), [Bernstein](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/Bernstein.html#ProbabilityTheory.iIndepFun.measureReal_sum_ge_le_of_ae_le) and [Efron–Stein](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/EfronStein.html#TauCeti.Probability.efronStein) inequalities, the [symmetrization inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Independence/Symmetrization.html#ConvexOn.symmetrization_inequality), Gaussian integration by parts, and tensorization of Poincaré inequalities.

The [Wigner matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/Wigner.html#TauCeti.wignerMatrix) has a constructor with its second-moment normalisation and minor/column independence, and large deviations started with the [contraction principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/LargeDeviations/Contraction.html#TauCeti.Probability.HasLDP.map). No global law has landed yet: neither the Wigner semicircle law nor Marchenko–Pastur convergence is proved, and the circular law is not yet defined.

## RandomMatrices: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-11 (`a4fcb57` to `a6027b1`)

The last two named laws of Layer 3 arrived. The [circular law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/CircularLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.circularLaw) is the uniform law on the unit disc, with [rotation invariance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/CircularLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_rotation_circularLaw) and its [mixed moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/CircularLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_pow_mul_conj_pow_circularLaw). The [quarter-circle law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/QuarterCircle.html#TauCeti.Probability.quarterCircle) has [Catalan even moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/QuarterCircle.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_pow_two_mul_quarterCircle), and [its square has the Marchenko–Pastur law of ratio one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/QuarterCircle.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_sq_quarterCircle). On the concentration side, the [Gaussian Poincaré inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Gaussian/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.poincareInequality_stdGaussian_iff) now holds for the standard Gaussian on any finite-dimensional space, with constant exactly 1. It comes with an [optimal Poincaré constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Constant.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.poincareConstant) that is the supremum of the factors' constants on a product, and with the [Mehler semigroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Mehler.html#TauCeti.Probability.mehler). The Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality itself is still missing: so far it is only shown that [no constant below 1 can work](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Gaussian/LogSobolevSharpness.html#TauCeti.Probability.not_logSobolevInequality_gaussianReal).

The Wigner moment method moved forward. Closed walks that traverse each edge of a tree twice are [counted by Catalan numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/TreeWalk/Basic.html#TauCeti.TreeWalk.card_closedTreeWalk) through Dyck words. A [first-visit relabelling](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/TreeWalk/Leading.html#TauCeti.TreeWalk.isTreeWalk_canonicalWalk_cyclic) turns leading trace words into such walks, and a word with no singleton edge [visits at most `k/2 + 1` vertices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/TraceWord/Counting.html#TauCeti.card_image_le_div_two_add_one_of_no_singleton_edge). The first and second empirical spectral moments of a Wigner matrix [concentrate in probability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/SpectralMoment/Second.html#TauCeti.Probability.tendsto_second_spectralMoment_sub_mean_probability), with `O(n⁻²)` variance. Higher moments, and the semicircle law itself, are not done yet.

Elsewhere, the sample covariance got its [expected normalized trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/SampleCovarianceMoment.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_one_div_card_mul_re_trace_sampleCovariance_eq_one). The resolvent became [jointly stable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SpectralMeasure/ResolventStability.html#Matrix.norm_resolventTransform_spectralMeasure_sub_le) in the matrix and the spectral parameter. Layer 7 also began, with the finite-dimensional [spectral oracle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/Oracle.html#Matrix.norm_sub_unitary_diagonal_sq_le) for squared Frobenius loss (TauCeti#30283).

## RandomMatrices: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`a6027b1` to `b235000`)

The [Hanson–Wright inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/HansonWright.html#TauCeti.hanson_wright) is proved in the roadmap's form: for independent `K`-sub-Gaussian coordinates and a real matrix `A`, `XᵀAX` strays from its mean by more than `t` with probability at most `2exp(-c·min(t²/(K²‖A‖_F²), t/(K‖A‖)))`, with `c = 1/3200` (TauCeti#27774). It comes with a [complex version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/HansonWright.html#ProbabilityTheory.iIndepFun.measureReal_norm_star_dotProduct_mulVec_sub_integral_gt_le), [convex decoupling](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Independence/Decoupling.html#ProbabilityTheory.iIndepFun.integral_convex_offDiag_le), conditional forms for a matrix independent of the vector, and the Rademacher example showing [no constant above `2 log 2` works](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/HansonWright.html#TauCeti.le_two_mul_log_two_of_hanson_wright). The [complex Wick formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/ProperComplex/MixedMoments.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_prod_mul_prod_conj_sub_properComplexGaussianMeasure) also landed, with equal-degree moments written [as permanents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/ProperComplex/MixedMoments.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_prod_mul_prod_conj_sub_properComplexGaussianMeasure_eq_permanent).

Marchenko–Pastur convergence is now largely reduced to its pieces, though not yet stated. The pieces are [stability of the self-consistent equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Stability.html#TauCeti.Probability.norm_sub_resolventTransform_marchenkoPastur_le), [convergence as vanishing quadratic residuals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Stability.html#TauCeti.Probability.tendsto_marchenkoPastur_iff_quadratic_tendsto_zero), almost-sure [leave-one-column-out concentration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/QuadraticForm.html#TauCeti.Probability.ae_tendsto_norm_star_dotProduct_sampleCovariance_resolvent_mulVec_sub_trace), and proofs that centring, rescaling and entry truncation leave the limit unchanged. For Wigner matrices, [odd expected moments vanish](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/MomentBounds.html#TauCeti.tendsto_inv_mul_integral_trace_pow_two_mul_add_one_wignerMatrix) in the limit and even ones reduce to leading words.

The [complex Ginibre](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/Ginibre/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.ginibreComplex) and [Gaussian Gram](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/Ginibre/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.gaussianGramComplex) laws exist, with their [explicit density](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/Ginibre/Density.html#TauCeti.Probability.ginibreComplex_eq_withDensity), unitary invariance and almost-sure full rank. Layer 10 began, with the [negative second-moment identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/NegativeSecondMoment.html#Matrix.sum_inv_sq_singularValues_eq_sum_inv_infDist_sq) and [invertibility via distance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/InvertibilityViaDistance.html#TauCeti.measure_exists_isIncompressibleVector_norm_sum_lt_le) over incompressible vectors. Some work landed earlier and is recorded here late, notably the [resolvent-transform criterion for weak convergence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ResolventTransform/WeakConvergence.html#MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure.tendsto_iff_tendsto_resolventTransform) and the [singular-value measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SpectralMeasure/SingularValues.html#Matrix.singularValueMeasure).

## RandomMatrices: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`b235000` to `afdb531`)

Both global laws are now proved. [Wigner's semicircle law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/Wigner/SemicircleLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.wigner_semicircle_ae) holds almost surely in the roadmap's form: the matrices are corners of one array, the upper entries are i.i.d. with finite second moment and centred variance one, and the diagonal may have any common law. A [triangular-array version in probability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/Wigner/Lindeberg/Semicircle.html#TauCeti.Probability.wigner_semicircle_in_probability_of_lindeberg) holds under the Lindeberg condition. The [Marchenko–Pastur law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/SampleCovariance/MarchenkoPastur.html#TauCeti.Probability.marchenkoPastur_ae) holds almost surely for i.i.d. complex arrays with `p/n → y > 0`. Under a fourth moment, the [extreme Wigner eigenvalues](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/Wigner/Edge/Limit.html#ProbabilityTheory.iIndepFun.ae_tendsto_eigenvalues₀_zero_and_card_sub_one_wignerMatrix) tend to `±2`. The sample-covariance edge is proved only conditionally on a growing-moment trace bound. The facts extract was truncated, so these results were read from the documentation.

Several tools arrived alongside. The [method of moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/WeakConvergence.html#MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure.tendsto_of_tendsto_integral_pow_of_isMomentDeterminate) now works for any moment-determinate limit, with an exponential-moment criterion for determinacy. [Herbst's argument](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Herbst.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.LogSobolevInequality.hasSubgaussianMGF_of_lipschitzWith) is proved, but the Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality it needs is still missing. Also new are [Esseen's concentration inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/CharacteristicFunction/Esseen.html#MeasureTheory.measureReal_closedBall_le_integral_norm_charFun) and a [least-singular-value tail](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/LeastSingularValue.html#TauCeti.Probability.exists_measureReal_singularValues_card_sub_one_lt_le) reduced to column distances. Layer 8 began with [Andréief's identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/Determinant/Andreief.html#MeasureTheory.integral_det_mul_det) and finite determinantal ensembles, whose [gap probability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Determinantal/Count.html#TauCeti.measureReal_determinantalEnsemble_particleCount_eq_zero) is `det(I - G_B)` (TauCeti#42616). The [spectral change of variables](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/SelfAdjointMatrix/ChangeOfVariables.html#TauCeti.lintegral_image_spectralMatrix) on self-adjoint matrices now has its Vandermonde Jacobian.

For large deviations there are now weak principles, exponential tightness, [exponential equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/LargeDeviations/ExponentialEquivalence.html#TauCeti.Probability.HasLDP.of_exponentiallyEquivalent) and [Cramér's theorem for Gaussians](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/LargeDeviations/Gaussian.html#ProbabilityTheory.iIndepFun.hasLDP_map_sum_range_div_of_hasLaw_gaussianReal). The logarithmic energy is lower semicontinuous and [attains its minimum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/BetaGas/LogarithmicEnergy.html#TauCeti.Probability.exists_isMinOn_logarithmicEnergy). Toward the circular law, logarithmic potentials landed with `ΔU = 2πL_A` and the [potential continuity theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/LogarithmicPotential/Convergence.html#MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure.tendsto_of_ae_tendsto_logarithmicPotential). Girko's log-determinant identity has not landed yet.
