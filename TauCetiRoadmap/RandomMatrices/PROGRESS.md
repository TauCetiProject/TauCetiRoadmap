## RandomMatrices: 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-08 (`f39dcd8` to `a4fcb57`)

The two limiting laws of the roadmap now exist with their full toolkit. The [Marchenko–Pastur law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.marchenkoPastur) is built as an atom at zero plus a reweighted semicircle, with its exact support, its [moments as sums over noncrossing partitions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Moments.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_pow_marchenkoPastur) (equivalently, Narayana polynomials), its resolvent transform [characterised as the upper-half-plane root](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/ResolventTransform.html#TauCeti.Probability.eq_resolventTransform_marchenkoPastur_iff) of `yzm² + (z+y-1)m + 1 = 0`, and [weak continuity in the aspect ratio](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Continuity.html#TauCeti.Probability.continuous_marchenkoPastur) down to `δ₁` at `y = 0` (TauCeti#27664). The centre/variance [semicircle family](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Semicircle/LocationScale.html#TauCeti.Probability.semicircleReal) got its Catalan moments and the same kind of transform characterisation. Underneath both sits [Stieltjes inversion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ResolventTransform/Inversion.html#MeasureTheory.tendsto_intervalIntegral_im_resolventTransform), with the half-atom endpoint terms, together with uniqueness and moment expansions of the resolvent transform.

The deterministic spectral layer filled out. It now has the [Hoffman–Wielandt inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HoffmanWielandt.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.sum_sq_eigenvalues₀_sub_le_frobenius_norm_sub_sq) and its [singular-value analogue](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HoffmanWielandt.html#Matrix.sum_sq_singularValues_toEuclideanLin_sub_le_frobenius_norm_sub_sq), Weyl's additive inequalities, the [rank perturbation bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SpectralMeasure/RankPerturbation.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.abs_spectralMeasure_Iic_sub_le_rank_div_card) for spectral distribution functions, and the resolvent identities a Wigner proof uses: the [Ward identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Resolvent/Basic.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.sum_norm_inv_sub_smul_one_apply_sq), [leave-one-out](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Resolvent/LeaveOneOut.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.inv_sub_smul_one_apply_self_eq) and Sherman–Morrison. On the probabilistic side came the [Azuma–Hoeffding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/Azuma.html#TauCeti.Probability.measureReal_sum_range_ge_le_of_bounded_martingale_differences), [Bernstein](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/Bernstein.html#ProbabilityTheory.iIndepFun.measureReal_sum_ge_le_of_ae_le) and [Efron–Stein](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/EfronStein.html#TauCeti.Probability.efronStein) inequalities, the [symmetrization inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Independence/Symmetrization.html#ConvexOn.symmetrization_inequality), Gaussian integration by parts, and tensorization of Poincaré inequalities.

The [Wigner matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/Wigner.html#TauCeti.wignerMatrix) has a constructor with its second-moment normalisation and minor/column independence, and large deviations started with the [contraction principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/LargeDeviations/Contraction.html#TauCeti.Probability.HasLDP.map). No global law has landed yet: neither the Wigner semicircle law nor Marchenko–Pastur convergence is proved, and the circular law is not yet defined.

## RandomMatrices: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-11 (`a4fcb57` to `a6027b1`)

The last two named laws of Layer 3 arrived. The [circular law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/CircularLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.circularLaw) is the uniform law on the unit disc, with [rotation invariance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/CircularLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_rotation_circularLaw) and its [mixed moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/CircularLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_pow_mul_conj_pow_circularLaw). The [quarter-circle law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/QuarterCircle.html#TauCeti.Probability.quarterCircle) has [Catalan even moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/QuarterCircle.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_pow_two_mul_quarterCircle), and [its square has the Marchenko–Pastur law of ratio one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/QuarterCircle.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_sq_quarterCircle). On the concentration side, the [Gaussian Poincaré inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Gaussian/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.poincareInequality_stdGaussian_iff) now holds for the standard Gaussian on any finite-dimensional space, with constant exactly 1. It comes with an [optimal Poincaré constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Constant.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.poincareConstant) that is the supremum of the factors' constants on a product, and with the [Mehler semigroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Mehler.html#TauCeti.Probability.mehler). The Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality itself is still missing: so far it is only shown that [no constant below 1 can work](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Gaussian/LogSobolevSharpness.html#TauCeti.Probability.not_logSobolevInequality_gaussianReal).

The Wigner moment method moved forward. Closed walks that traverse each edge of a tree twice are [counted by Catalan numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/TreeWalk/Basic.html#TauCeti.TreeWalk.card_closedTreeWalk) through Dyck words. A [first-visit relabelling](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/TreeWalk/Leading.html#TauCeti.TreeWalk.isTreeWalk_canonicalWalk_cyclic) turns leading trace words into such walks, and a word with no singleton edge [visits at most `k/2 + 1` vertices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/TraceWord/Counting.html#TauCeti.card_image_le_div_two_add_one_of_no_singleton_edge). The first and second empirical spectral moments of a Wigner matrix [concentrate in probability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/SpectralMoment/Second.html#TauCeti.Probability.tendsto_second_spectralMoment_sub_mean_probability), with `O(n⁻²)` variance. Higher moments, and the semicircle law itself, are not done yet.

Elsewhere, the sample covariance got its [expected normalized trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/SampleCovarianceMoment.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_one_div_card_mul_re_trace_sampleCovariance_eq_one). The resolvent became [jointly stable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SpectralMeasure/ResolventStability.html#Matrix.norm_resolventTransform_spectralMeasure_sub_le) in the matrix and the spectral parameter. Layer 7 also began, with the finite-dimensional [spectral oracle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/Oracle.html#Matrix.norm_sub_unitary_diagonal_sq_le) for squared Frobenius loss (TauCeti#30283).
