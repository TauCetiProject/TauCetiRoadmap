## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The deterministic and transform foundations are essentially complete, and the concentration layer now has Hanson–Wright. Both global laws are close: expected Wigner moments have their Catalan limits, and Marchenko–Pastur convergence has been reduced to a self-consistent equation and quadratic-form concentration. Neither limit theorem is stated yet. Layer 10 has begun, and Layer 8 has not.

### Named results

- **The Hanson–Wright inequality** — [`hanson_wright`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/HansonWright.html#TauCeti.hanson_wright): a quadratic form in independent sub-Gaussian coordinates has the mixed Gaussian/exponential tail `2exp(-c·min(t²/(K²‖A‖_F²), t/(K‖A‖)))`. It comes with complex, decoupled and conditional versions, and with the `2 log 2` sharpness check.
- **Catalan limits of Wigner moments** — the normalized expected trace of `Hₙ^(2m)` tends to `s^m` times the `m`th Catalan number, under independent centred entries with bounded moments and no identical-distribution hypothesis. The odd moments [tend to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/MomentBounds.html#TauCeti.tendsto_inv_mul_integral_trace_pow_two_mul_add_one_wignerMatrix).
- **The Marchenko–Pastur law** — [`marchenkoPastur`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.marchenkoPastur) has its exact support, noncrossing-partition moments, characterised resolvent transform and a [quantitative stability estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Stability.html#TauCeti.Probability.norm_sub_resolventTransform_marchenkoPastur_le) for its self-consistent equation.
- **Stieltjes inversion and the resolvent criterion** — [interval masses are recovered from the transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ResolventTransform/Inversion.html#MeasureTheory.tendsto_intervalIntegral_im_resolventTransform). Weak convergence, and [weak convergence in probability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ResolventTransform/InProbability.html#MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure.tendsto_measure_integral_iff_resolventTransform), are each equivalent to convergence of resolvent transforms on the upper half-plane.
- **The Gaussian Poincaré inequality** — [the standard Gaussian on a finite-dimensional space has Poincaré constant exactly 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Gaussian/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.poincareInequality_stdGaussian_iff). The logarithmic Sobolev analogue is still only known to be [sharp if true](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Gaussian/LogSobolevSharpness.html#TauCeti.Probability.not_logSobolevInequality_gaussianReal).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Empirical spectral and singular-value measures**, built on [`empiricalFiniteMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/EmpiricalMeasure.html#TauCeti.Probability.empiricalFiniteMeasure) and [`singularValueMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SpectralMeasure/SingularValues.html#Matrix.singularValueMeasure). They come with rank, Wasserstein, Hoffman–Wielandt and resolvent-stability estimates, which let a global law be proved after centring, rescaling, truncation or a low-rank change.
- **The Wigner constructor** [`wignerMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/Wigner.html#TauCeti.wignerMatrix), with its finite-coordinate law and the trace-word, tree-walk and relabelling combinatorics behind the moment method.
- **Ginibre and Gaussian Gram laws** [`ginibreComplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/Ginibre/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.ginibreComplex) and [`gaussianGramComplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/Ginibre/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.gaussianGramComplex), with real counterparts, an explicit density, unitary invariance and almost-sure full rank. They are the Gaussian identification cases for Marchenko–Pastur and the circular law.

## The frontier

- **Marchenko–Pastur convergence (§4.4).** The stability estimate, the residual criterion, leave-one-column-out concentration and the truncation reductions are in place. What remains is to show that the sample-covariance resolvent nearly solves the quadratic equation, and to assemble the in-probability and almost-sure theorems for i.i.d. arrays.
- **The Wigner semicircle law (§§4.2–4.3).** The Catalan limits of expected moments are proved. What remains is the `O(n⁻²)` variance bound beyond the second moment, the passage from moments to weak convergence (blocked on the moment-determinacy form of §3.2, or done through the resolvent route instead), truncation down to finite variance, and the almost-sure statement.
- **Least singular values (§10.1).** Compressible and incompressible vectors and invertibility via distance exist. Small-ball and Littlewood–Offord estimates and the Rudelson–Vershynin bound remain, followed by Girko hermitization and the circular law.
- **The Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality (§2.2).** The Mehler semigroup and the sharpness bound are in place. The inequality, Herbst's argument and Lipschitz concentration remain, and the spectral concentration of §2.3 waits on them.
- **Matrix Bernstein and Chernoff (§2.6).** Not started. They need Golden–Thompson and Lieb concavity first.
