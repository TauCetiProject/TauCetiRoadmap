## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The foundations are largely built: spectral measures, deterministic eigenvalue and resolvent estimates, the resolvent-transform toolkit, the semicircle and Marchenko–Pastur laws, and an algebraic free-probability layer through the free central limit theorem. None of the roadmap's random-matrix limit theorems is proved yet. Layers 7, 8, 10 and 11 have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Marchenko–Pastur law** — [`marchenkoPastur`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.marchenkoPastur) is a probability measure with exact support, the atom `(1-1/y)₊` at zero, moments [summed over noncrossing partitions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Moments.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_pow_marchenkoPastur), a resolvent transform [characterised as the upper-half-plane root](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/ResolventTransform.html#TauCeti.Probability.eq_resolventTransform_marchenkoPastur_iff) of its quadratic, and weak continuity in `y ≥ 0`.
- **Stieltjes inversion** — [`tendsto_intervalIntegral_im_resolventTransform`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ResolventTransform/Inversion.html#MeasureTheory.tendsto_intervalIntegral_im_resolventTransform) recovers `μ(a,b)` plus half the endpoint atoms from the imaginary part of the transform, so a finite measure is [determined by its transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ResolventTransform/Inversion.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.ext_of_resolventTransform).
- **The Stieltjes continuity theorem** — `tendsto_iff_tendsto_resolventTransform`: probability measures on `ℝ` converge weakly exactly when their transforms converge on the upper half-plane, with countable-determining-set and almost-sure versions. The compact-support method of moments, `tendsto_of_tendsto_integral_pow`, sits alongside it.
- **The Hoffman–Wielandt inequality** — [the sorted spectra of Hermitian `A`, `B` are within `‖A-B‖_F`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HoffmanWielandt.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.sum_sq_eigenvalues₀_sub_le_frobenius_norm_sub_sq) in `ℓ²`, with the [singular-value analogue](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HoffmanWielandt.html#Matrix.sum_sq_singularValues_toEuclideanLin_sub_le_frobenius_norm_sub_sq) for rectangular matrices.
- **The free central limit theorem** — normalised sums of free centred self-adjoint elements in a C*-probability space converge weakly to the centred semicircle law, under uniform moment bounds rather than identical distribution.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Empirical spectral measures.** `Matrix.spectralMeasure` for Hermitian matrices, the characteristic-root measure `eigenvalueMeasure` and a singular-value measure, all built on [`empiricalFiniteMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/EmpiricalMeasure.html#TauCeti.Probability.empiricalFiniteMeasure). They come with continuity, the spectral Wasserstein coupling, tightness from trace powers, and the rule that a [vanishing rank fraction preserves weak spectral limits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SpectralMeasure/WeakConvergence.html#Matrix.tendsto_spectralMeasure_iff_of_rank_sub_div_card_tendsto_zero).
- **The semicircle family** [`semicircleReal a v`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Semicircle/LocationScale.html#TauCeti.Probability.semicircleReal), parametrised by variance, with Catalan moments and its transform. Its link `m_{SC(1+y,y)} = 1 + z m_{MP_y}` is what defines Marchenko–Pastur.
- **The Wigner constructor** [`wignerMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/Wigner.html#TauCeti.wignerMatrix) on the corners of one infinite array. It comes with its second-moment normalisation, independence of a minor from the removed column, and the first trace-word reduction (walks that read some edge exactly once contribute nothing). The GOE/GUE `gaussianEnsemble` is built on top of it.
