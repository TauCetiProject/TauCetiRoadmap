## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The deterministic and transform foundations are essentially complete. Spectral measures, eigenvalue perturbation, resolvent estimates, Stieltjes inversion and continuity, and all four named limiting laws (semicircle, Marchenko–Pastur, circular, quarter-circle) are in place, along with algebraic free probability through the free central limit theorem. None of the roadmap's random-matrix limit theorems is proved yet. Layers 8 and 10 have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Marchenko–Pastur law** — [`marchenkoPastur`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MarchenkoPastur/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.marchenkoPastur) has its exact support, noncrossing-partition moments and a characterised resolvent transform. At ratio one it is [the law of the square of a quarter-circle variable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/QuarterCircle.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_sq_quarterCircle).
- **Stieltjes inversion** — [`tendsto_intervalIntegral_im_resolventTransform`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ResolventTransform/Inversion.html#MeasureTheory.tendsto_intervalIntegral_im_resolventTransform) recovers interval masses, with half the endpoint atoms, from the transform. With the Stieltjes continuity theorem, it turns weak convergence into convergence of transforms on the upper half-plane.
- **The Hoffman–Wielandt inequality** — [sorted Hermitian spectra are within the Frobenius distance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HoffmanWielandt.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.sum_sq_eigenvalues₀_sub_le_frobenius_norm_sub_sq) in `ℓ²`, and the same holds for singular values.
- **The Gaussian Poincaré inequality** — [the standard Gaussian on a finite-dimensional space has Poincaré constant exactly 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Gaussian/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.poincareInequality_stdGaussian_iff). The matching lower bound for the logarithmic Sobolev constant is [also proved](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Concentration/Poincare/Gaussian/LogSobolevSharpness.html#TauCeti.Probability.not_logSobolevInequality_gaussianReal), but not the inequality itself.
- **The free central limit theorem** — normalised sums of free centred self-adjoint elements converge weakly to the semicircle law, under uniform moment bounds rather than identical distribution.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Empirical spectral measures** `Matrix.spectralMeasure` and `eigenvalueMeasure`, built on [`empiricalFiniteMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/EmpiricalMeasure.html#TauCeti.Probability.empiricalFiniteMeasure). They come with the rank, Wasserstein and resolvent-stability estimates that every global law reduces to.
- **The Wigner constructor** [`wignerMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrix/Wigner.html#TauCeti.wignerMatrix) and its trace-word combinatorics. Singleton-edge words vanish, and leading words relabel to tree walks [counted by Catalan numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/TreeWalk/Basic.html#TauCeti.TreeWalk.card_closedTreeWalk). Together these are most of the moment method for the semicircle law.
- **The circular law** [`circularLaw`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/CircularLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.circularLaw), the uniform disc law with rotation invariance and mixed moments. It is the target measure for Layer 10.

## The frontier

- **The Wigner semicircle law (§§4.2–4.3).** What remains is to sum the tree-walk leading terms into the Catalan limit of expected moments of every order, and to extend the `O(n⁻²)` variance bound beyond the second moment. Then come the in-probability and almost-sure statements. Two `wignerMatrix` constructors, in `RandomMatrix` and `RandomMatrices`, still coexist and should be merged.
- **Marchenko–Pastur convergence (§4.4).** `sampleCovariance` and its expected trace exist. The rectangular trace-word count and the limit theorem are not started.
- **The Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality (§2.2).** The sharp constant is known to be the only candidate, and the Mehler semigroup is in place. The inequality itself, Herbst's argument and Lipschitz concentration remain.
- **Hanson–Wright and matrix Bernstein (§§2.3, 2.6).** Neither has started. Matrix Bernstein needs Golden–Thompson and Lieb concavity first.
- **Free convolution and asymptotic freeness (§§6.3, 6.5).** The analytic R-transform and `⊞` remain. Asymptotic freeness of Wigner matrices waits on the Layer 4 moment method.
