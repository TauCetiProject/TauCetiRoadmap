## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory (Layers 1–4) is done. So is the local class formation, with finite local reciprocity and the cohomological dimension of `G_K` (Layer 6). Tate local duality holds for finite modules of order prime to the characteristic, and the local Weil group has everything but its reciprocity isomorphism. The global theory has its formations and several inputs to the fundamental inequalities. Still missing: local existence, the Brauer sequence, the global invariant, and everything from global reciprocity on.

### Named results

- **Tate's theorem** — for a class formation, [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso) `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` is cup product with the fundamental class, in every integer degree.
- **Finite local reciprocity** — for a finite Galois extension `L/K` of nonarchimedean local fields, [`localArtinEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinEquiv) identifies `Kˣ/N_{L/K}(Lˣ)` with `Gal(L/K)^ab`, and it [sends a uniformizer to arithmetic Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinMap_uniformizer).
- **The absolute local Artin map** — [`artinMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap) `Kˣ → G_K^ab` is continuous with dense image. Its kernel is the intersection of the norm groups, and it satisfies [norm functoriality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap_norm). It is not yet known to be injective.
- **Tate local duality** — for `n` invertible in `K` and a finite Galois module `A` killed by `n`, cup product gives bijections `Hⁱ(G_K, A) ≃ Hom(H²⁻ⁱ(G_K, Hom(A, μₙ)), H²(G_K, μₙ))` in degrees [0](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap0_kummerCoeff_bijective), [1](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap1_kummerCoeff_bijective) and [2](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap2_kummerCoeff_bijective). These are stated on the explicit cochain model, not as the README's named pairing.
- **The cohomological dimension of a local Galois group** — `cd_ℓ G_K = 2`, and [`scd_ℓ G_K = 2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/CohomologicalDimension/Strict.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.strictCohomologicalDimensionAt_absoluteGaloisGroup_eq_two), for every prime `ℓ` invertible in `K`, hence for every prime when `K/ℚ_p` is finite.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The local invariant and the local class formation** — [`invMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap) identifies `Br K` with `ℚ/ℤ`, normalized by arithmetic Frobenius. [`localClassFormation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localClassFormation) uses it as its invariant, so the Brauer side and reciprocity share one normalization.
- **The idele-class formation and the sum of local invariants** — [`globalFormation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/Formation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalFormation) is the discrete `G_K`-module of idele classes. On idele layers, [`ideleSumLocalInv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/LayerInvariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ideleSumLocalInv) adds up the local invariants, and `globalInv` will descend this sum to idele classes.
- **The local Weil group** — [the Weil topology](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/WeilGroup/Topology.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.weilTopology_unique) is the unique group topology in which inertia, with its profinite topology, is open. The [Weil transfer](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/WeilGroup/Transfer.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.weilTransfer) `W_L → W_K` has open image of index `[L:K]`, and that image is the kernel of `W_K → Gal(L/K)`.

## The frontier

- **The fundamental inequalities for idele classes** — [Tate's lattice lemma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Lattice.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.herbrandQuotient_eq_of_isBaseChange) is in, along with the permutation and semi-local computations. Next are the Herbrand quotient of the `S`-units and `S`-ideles through the logarithmic embedding, then `degree_le_card_normQuotient_of_isCyclic`, `card_H2_globalFormation_dvd_degree` and `subsingleton_h1_globalFormation`.
- **The archimedean package** — the class formation at a real place, `infiniteArtinAt` with its character formula, and archimedean norm functoriality. `sumLocalInv_eq_zero` needs all three.
- **The Brauer–Hasse–Noether sequence, then `globalInv`** — exactness of `0 → Br K → ⨁_v Br K_v → ℚ/ℤ → 0` in invariant coordinates, then the cyclotomic refinement, the killing of the obstruction, and independence of the lift.
- **The Tate–Nakayama theorem** — `Ĥ^r(Γ, M) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(Γ, M ⊗ C)` when `Tor₁(M, C) = 0`. The Nakayama–Rim resolutions it consumes are now available.
- **Local abelian existence** — every open finite-index subgroup of `Kˣ` is a norm group. This gives injectivity of `artinMap`, which `localWeilArtinEquiv` waits on.
