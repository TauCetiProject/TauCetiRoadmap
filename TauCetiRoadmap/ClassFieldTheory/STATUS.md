## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory (Layers 0–4) and the whole local theory (Layers 5–9) are done. Globally, the fundamental inequalities, `H¹` vanishing and the Brauer–Hasse–Noether sequence are in, and Hilbert and quadratic reciprocity follow from them. The global invariant has not landed, so global reciprocity, global existence and the class fields are still ahead.

### Named results

- **The fundamental inequalities for idele classes** — for cyclic `L/K` the [first inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/IdeleClass/FirstInequality.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.finrank_le_index_range_ideleClassNormMap) gives `[C_K : N_{L/K}(C_L)] ≥ [L : K]`, and on every layer of the idele-class formation [`#H²` divides the degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/IdeleClass/SecondInequality.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.natCard_H2_globalFormation_dvd_degree) and [`H¹` vanishes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/IdeleClass/SecondInequality.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.subsingleton_h1_globalFormation).
- **The sum formula** — the local invariants of every Brauer class of a number field [sum to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/Brauer/SumFormula.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.sumLocalInv_brLocalization_eq_zero). With the Hasse principle `brLocalization_injective` and the surjectivity of the sum, this makes `0 → Br K → ⨁_v Br K_v → ℚ/ℤ → 0` exact.
- **Hilbert reciprocity** — [`hilbertProductFormula`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/Hilbert/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.hilbertProductFormula) says the local Hilbert invariants of `a, b ∈ Kˣ` sum to zero over all places. Over `ℚ` it [gives quadratic reciprocity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/QuadraticReciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.sum_finiteHilbertInvariantAt_add_sum_infiniteHilbertInvariantAt_eq_zero_iff) and both supplementary laws.
- **Local reciprocity for the Weil group** — for a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero, [`localWeilArtinEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/WeilGroup/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localWeilArtinEquiv) is an isomorphism of topological groups from `Kˣ` onto the topological abelianization of `W_K`.
- **Tate's local Euler characteristic formula** — for `F/ℚ_p` finite and a finite smooth discrete Galois module `A`, [`#H¹ = #H⁰ · #H² · p^{[F:ℚ_p] v_p(#A)}`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/EulerCharacteristic/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.eulerCharacteristic_mixed).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The archimedean Artin maps** — [`infiniteArtinAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Archimedean/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.infiniteArtinAt) is the Artin map of the class formation at a real or complex place, trivial at a complex place and with kernel `ℝ_{>0}` at a real one. Its character formula is what turns the cyclotomic product formula into the sum formula.
- **Prescribed invariants on prime-degree layers** — every prime-degree layer of the idele formation [carries a class of invariant `1/p`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/PrescribedInvariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.exists_ideleSumLocalInv_eq_one_div_degree_of_prime). It is the hypothesis of the existing obstruction-killing step, and so the last arithmetic input `globalInv` needs.
- **Global norm subgroups and conductors** — [`globalNormSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/NormSubgroup.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalNormSubgroup) reads the norm subgroup of a layer on `C_K`, open and of finite index, and [`abelianConductor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/AdmissibleModulus.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.abelianConductor) is the least admissible modulus of `L/K`. They are the carriers on which Layer 12 states the correspondence and ray-class reciprocity.

## The frontier

- **The cyclic Hasse norm theorem** — [the conditional form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/HasseNorm.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.mem_normGroup_iff_unitEmbedding_mem_range_ideleNormMap) assumes `H¹(Gal(L/K), C_L) = 0`, which the layer form of `H¹` vanishing now supplies. What remains is that transport, the named `cyclicHasseNorm`, and the placewise local-norm predicates with `isGlobalNorm_iff_isLocalNormEverywhere`.
- **The global invariant `globalInv`** — feed the prime-degree classes into the obstruction-killing step to make it unconditional, descend `ideleSumLocalInv` along it, then prove injectivity and range: `H²(Gal(L/K), C_L)` is cyclic of order `[L : K]`.
- **The global class formation and global reciprocity** — package `globalInv` as `globalClassFormation`, transport the abstract Artin map to `C_K / N_{L/K}(C_L) ≃ Gal(L/K)^ab`, and prove local compatibility, triviality on principal ideles and the Frobenius normalization. Blocked on `globalInv`.
- **Global existence** — the Kummer case `exists_abelianLayer_globalNormSubgroup_eq_of_pow_mem`, then the index induction of Milne VII 9.5, which already exists for an abstract class formation. Needs global reciprocity.
- **Class fields and Kronecker–Weber** — ray, Hilbert, narrow Hilbert and quadratic ring class fields as values of `globalClassField`, then `kroneckerWeber`. All wait on the global correspondence.
