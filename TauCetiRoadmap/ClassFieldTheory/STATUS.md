## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Every layer of the build plan has its milestones proved: the abstract theory, the local theory up to the local class-field correspondence and the Weil group, and the global theory through existence, the correspondence, the named class fields, Kronecker–Weber and Hilbert reciprocity. What is left is a few of the worked examples the roadmap asks for, not new theory.

### Named results

- **Tate's theorem** — for a class formation, [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso) `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` is cup product with the fundamental class, in every integer degree. Both the local and the global Artin maps come from it.
- **Local reciprocity for the Weil group** — for a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero, the local Artin map is an isomorphism of topological groups from `Kˣ` onto the topological abelianization of `W_K` ([`localWeilArtinEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/WeilGroup/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localWeilArtinEquiv)).
- **The global class-field correspondence** — open finite-index subgroups of `C_K` correspond, reversing order, to finite abelian extensions of `K` ([`globalClassFieldCorrespondence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/Correspondence.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalClassFieldCorrespondence)). It rests on [global existence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/Existence.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalAbelianExistence), proved by the Artin–Tate induction (TauCeti#20781).
- **The Kronecker–Weber theorem** — every finite abelian extension of `ℚ` embeds in a cyclotomic field ([`kroneckerWeber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/KroneckerWeber.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.kroneckerWeber)). The proof derives it from global existence and identifies the least cyclotomic level with the conductor.
- **Hilbert reciprocity** — the Hilbert invariants of `a, b ∈ Kˣ` sum to zero over all places of a number field ([`hilbertProductFormula`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/Hilbert/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.hilbertProductFormula)). Over `ℚ`, quadratic reciprocity is [equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/QuadraticReciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.sum_finiteHilbertInvariantAt_add_sum_infiniteHilbertInvariantAt_eq_zero_iff) to the vanishing of the Hilbert sum.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The global invariant** — [`globalInv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalInv) sums the local invariants of an idele lift after refinement, which avoids the `H³` obstruction to lifting idele classes. It makes the idele classes a [global class formation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalClassFormation), so global reciprocity is an instance of the abstract theory.
- **The local invariant** — [`invMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap) identifies `Br K` with `ℚ/ℤ` and is the invariant of the [local class formation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localClassFormation). The Brauer side, local reciprocity and the sum formula therefore share one normalization.
- **Class fields as values of the correspondence** — the [ray class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/RayClassField.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.rayClassField), the Hilbert and narrow Hilbert class fields, and the [ring class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/RingClass/Field.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ringClassField) of a quadratic order are each the class field of a named norm subgroup. Their Galois groups are the ray class group, the class group and `Pic O`, obtained by transport rather than computed separately.
