## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory, Layers 1–4, is done. Local reciprocity is proved, both for finite extensions and for the absolute Artin map, but local existence, local duality and the cohomological dimension of `G_K` have not landed. The Weil group and everything global have not begun.

### Named results

- **Finite local reciprocity** — for a finite Galois extension `L/K` of nonarchimedean local fields, [`localArtinEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinEquiv) identifies `Kˣ/N_{L/K}(Lˣ)` with `Gal(L/K)^ab`. It does not depend on the embedding of `L`, and it [sends a uniformizer to arithmetic Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinMap_uniformizer).
- **The absolute local Artin map** — [`artinMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap) `Kˣ → G_K^ab` restricts to the finite maps. It is [continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.continuous_artinMap) with dense image, and its [kernel is the intersection of the norm groups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ker_artinMap_eq_iInf). On the unramified quotient it is the [normalized valuation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.unramifiedCoordinate_artinMap).
- **The local invariant** — [`invMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap) identifies `Br K` with `ℚ/ℤ`, normalized by arithmetic Frobenius. Restriction [multiplies it by the degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap_brRes), and corestriction [preserves it](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap_brCor).
- **Tate's theorem** — for a class formation, [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso) `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` is cup product with the fundamental class. It is compatible with restriction, corestriction, towers and conjugation, and it obeys the [scaled inflation formula](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Inflation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso_infl).
- **The character formula** — the abstract Artin map satisfies `χ(artinMap a) = inv(a ∪ δχ)` ([`character_artinMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.character_artinMap)), and [any map satisfying it is the Artin map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.eq_artinMap_of_character). This pins the sign convention once and for all.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The local class formation** — [`localClassFormation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localClassFormation) takes `invMap`, carried to the finite layers, as its invariant, so the Brauer side and reciprocity share a single normalization. Every abstract theorem about class formations now applies to local fields.
- **The absolute Artin map of a class formation** — [`absoluteArtinMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/AbsoluteArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.absoluteArtinMap) is the inverse limit of the finite maps, and it is [characterized by them](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/AbsoluteArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.eq_absoluteArtinMap_of_forall_abelianizationRestrict). The global formation will inherit it unchanged.
- **Local norm subgroups** — [`localNormSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/LocalExistence/NormSubgroup.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localNormSubgroup) gives the norm group of the class field of an open normal subgroup. It is open, of finite index and monotone, and it handles composita and intersections. For abelian layers its [index is the degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/LocalExistence/AbelianLayer.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.index_localNormSubgroup). These are the carriers that local existence will be stated on.

## The frontier

- **Local abelian existence** — show that every open finite-index subgroup of `Kˣ` is the norm group of a finite abelian extension: for `K/ℚ_p` via Kummer theory, and in general for prime-to-`p` index. Uniqueness, `localClassField`, both correspondences and injectivity of `artinMap` then follow. The README lists local duality as a prerequisite.
- **Perfectness of local Tate duality** — prove `tateDualityPairing_perfect_mixed` and the Euler characteristic. The [dévissage through coinduced modules](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Cup/Duality/OpenSubgroup.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dualityMap2_bijective_discreteCoind_of_bijective) is in place, and the `(1,1)` pairing on `μₙ` is already identified with the local symbol.
- **Norm functoriality of the absolute map** — prove `artinMap_norm`, `Art_K(N_{L/K} x)` as the image of `Art_L(x)`. The [layer transport from `E` to `K`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Map.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localFormationLayerEquiv) and `invMap_brRes` are the inputs. Naturality in `K`, `geometricArtinMap` and conductors are also needed to close Layer 7.
- **Cohomological dimension of `G_K`** — prove `H³(G_K, (Kˢ)ˣ) = 0` from `tateIso`, then `cd_ℓ = scd_ℓ = 2` for `K/ℚ_p`. The `scd` bound also needs perfect duality. The quadratic test `localArtinMap_quadratic_eq_hilbertSymbol` is the other open item in Layer 6.
- **Tate–Nakayama** — the tensor-product generalization of the cup-product criterion, the last item in Layer 0.
