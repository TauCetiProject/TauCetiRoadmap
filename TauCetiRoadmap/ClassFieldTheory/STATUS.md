## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory has reached its first summit. Tate's theorem is proved for class formations, and the abstract Artin map exists with its kernel, surjectivity and norm limitation. The Artin–Tate diagrams, the character formula and uniqueness are still missing. On the local side, the Brauer group, Kummer theory, the unramified invariant and the local symbol are in place, but the local class formation is not. The absolute local theory, existence, the Weil group and everything global have not begun.

### Named results

- **Tate's theorem** — for a finite group, cup product with a suitable degree-two class is an isomorphism in every Tate degree ([`cup_bijective_of_forall_isPGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Cup/TateTheorem.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.cup_bijective_of_forall_isPGroup)). For a class formation it gives [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso), `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)`, which is cup product with the fundamental class.
- **Artin reciprocity for a finite normal layer** — [`artinEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.artinEquiv) identifies `A^U / N(A^V)` with `(U/V)^ab`. The [Artin map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.artinMap) is surjective, and its [kernel is exactly the norm subgroup](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.ker_artinMap).
- **Norm limitation** — a layer and its maximal abelian sublayer have the same norm subgroup ([`normSubgroup_maximalAbelianLayer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/NormLimitation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.normSubgroup_maximalAbelianLayer)). It is deduced from reciprocity, not from existence, which is what both existence layers need.
- **The unramified local invariant** — on an unramified layer, [`unramifiedInv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.unramifiedInv) is normalized by arithmetic Frobenius, so that [the class of `a` has invariant `v_K(a)/[L:K]`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.unramifiedInv_unramifiedClass).
- **The Kummer isomorphism and the Steinberg relation** — [`kummerEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/MuNRep.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.kummerEquiv) identifies `Fˣ/(Fˣ)ⁿ` with `H¹(G_F, μₙ)`. The bilinear [local symbol](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/LocalSymbol.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localSymbol) built from it [vanishes on `(a, 1 − a)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/LocalSymbol.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localSymbol_kummerClass_eq_zero_of_add_eq_one).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

Because Tate's theorem, reciprocity and norm limitation are all stated over it, the local and global formations inherit them as soon as they exist.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 1 and 2 are done. The other assessed layers are partial:

- **Layer 0** is partial. The Tate cup product is [associative in all tridegrees](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Cup/Associativity.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.cup_assoc), natural and unital. It commutes with restriction in nonnegative bidegrees and in bidegree `(r, 0)`. Graded commutativity, restriction compatibility in the remaining bidegrees, the projection formula and the Tate–Nakayama generalization remain.
- **Layer 3** has `tateIso` but not its compatibilities with restriction, corestriction, towers, conjugation and scaled inflation.
- **Layer 4** has reciprocity, the kernel, surjectivity and norm limitation. The four Artin–Tate diagrams, the character formula and uniqueness remain.
- **Layer 5** has the Galois dictionary, Kummer theory, `Br F`, the unramified invariant and the local symbol with bilinearity and Steinberg. The symbol takes its identification `H²(μₙ) ≃ ℤ/n` as a parameter. Still missing are `invMap` on the full Brauer group, the `brRes`/`brCor` squares, `H²(μₙ) ≃ ℤ/n` itself, and local duality.
- **Layers 6 and 7** are partial only in their ingredients. Layer 6 has the units formation, Hilbert 90, solvability, the solvable `H²` bound and the field-theoretic norm quotient, but not the formation itself. For Layer 7, only the [unramified coordinate](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Coordinate.html#TauCeti.unramifiedCoordinate) `G_K^ab → ℤ̂` exists.
- **Layers 8–14** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Artin–Tate diagrams** — prove `artinMap_groundInclusion`, `artinMap_groundNorm`, `artinMap_conj` and `artinMap_quotient`. The [ground-level norm](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/GroundNorm.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.LayerRestriction.groundNorm) exists, and degree −2 restriction and corestriction are identified with transfer and inclusion. What is missing is the matching `tateIso` compatibility from Layer 3.
- **The character formula and uniqueness** — prove `χ(artinMap a) = inv(class(a) ∪ δχ)`. The cyclic computation of `a ∪ δχ` as two-periodicity is in place.
- **The local invariant on the full Brauer group** — extend `unramifiedInv` to `invMap` on `Br K`, with the restriction and corestriction squares. That would also let the local symbol use the invariant rather than a supplied identification.
- **The local class formation** — assemble the units formation, Hilbert 90, the solvable `H²` bound and `invMap` into a `ClassFormation`. This is blocked on the full `invMap` and the cyclic Herbrand-quotient computation. `localArtinEquiv` would then follow from the units dictionary already in place.
- **Remaining cup-product laws** — graded commutativity, the projection formula and the Tate–Nakayama tensor-product generalization.
