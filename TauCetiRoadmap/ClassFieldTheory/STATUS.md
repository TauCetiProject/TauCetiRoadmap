## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory is done, and so are local class field theory through the correspondence and the Weil group and global class field theory through existence, the correspondence, the named class fields, Kronecker–Weber and Hilbert reciprocity. The rewritten roadmap adds open targets to Layers 7 and 13, so those two layers are partial. One half of the conductor–discriminant formula is already proved. The principal ideal theorem, the lcm half of that formula and the three local unit-filtration and normalization statements are not.

### Named results

- **Tate's theorem** — for a class formation, [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso) `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` is cup product with the fundamental class, in every integer degree. Both the local and the global Artin maps come from it.
- **Local reciprocity for the Weil group** — for a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero, the local Artin map is an isomorphism of topological groups from `Kˣ` onto the topological abelianization of `W_K` ([`localWeilArtinEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/WeilGroup/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localWeilArtinEquiv)).
- **The global class-field correspondence** — open finite-index subgroups of `C_K` correspond, reversing order, to finite abelian extensions of `K` ([`globalClassFieldCorrespondence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/Correspondence.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalClassFieldCorrespondence)). It rests on [global existence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/Existence.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalAbelianExistence), which is proved by the Artin–Tate induction.
- **The Kronecker–Weber theorem** — every finite abelian extension of `ℚ` embeds in a cyclotomic field ([`kroneckerWeber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/KroneckerWeber.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.kroneckerWeber)). The least cyclotomic level is the norm of the conductor's finite part.
- **Hilbert reciprocity** — the Hilbert invariants of `a, b ∈ Kˣ` sum to zero over all places of a number field ([`hilbertProductFormula`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/Hilbert/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.hilbertProductFormula)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The global invariant** — [`globalInv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalInv) sums the local invariants of an idele lift after refinement, which avoids the `H³` obstruction to lifting idele classes. It makes the idele classes a [global class formation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalClassFormation), so global reciprocity is an instance of the abstract theory.
- **The local invariant** — [`invMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap) identifies `Br K` with `ℚ/ℤ` and is the invariant of the [local class formation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localClassFormation). The Brauer side, local reciprocity and the sum formula therefore share one normalization.
- **Class fields as values of the correspondence** — the [ray class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/RayClassField.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.rayClassField), the Hilbert and narrow Hilbert class fields, and the [ring class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/RingClass/Field.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ringClassField) of an order are each the class field of a named norm subgroup. The ring class field is defined for every order of every number field. Their Galois groups are the ray class group, the class group and `Pic O`, obtained by transport.

## The frontier

- **The principal ideal theorem** — two steps are proved. Extended to the Hilbert class field `H`, every idele class of `K` is a norm from any unramified Galois extension containing `H`, and an idelic criterion detects when `Cl_K → Cl_H` is trivial. What remains is to join them in `isPrincipal_map_hilbertClassField` by identifying the relevant norm group with the trivial-modulus ray subgroup.
- **The lcm half of the conductor–discriminant formula** — `abelianConductor_eq_lcm`: at each finite place the conductor exponent is the maximum of the character conductor exponents, and a real place divides the conductor exactly when it divides one of them.
- **The upper-numbering theorem** — `normResidue_map_unitFiltration`: reciprocity carries `U(K,n)` onto the image of `G(L/K)^n`, in every characteristic. The inputs the README names, including the norm-group criterion for `G^v = 1` and compatibility of upper numbering with quotients, are in the source.
- **Absolute unit-filtration reciprocity** — `map_artinMap_unitFiltration_eq_upper` for every `n`. Depth zero is done; the general case follows from the finite theorem by the profinite characterization of closed subgroups, which needs ProfiniteProPGroups Layer 0.
- **The cyclotomic normalization without the valuation hypothesis** — `cyclotomicCharacter_artinMap`: the README sketches an elementary argument, via Hensel's lemma, for removing the compatibility assumption from the existing unit theorem.
