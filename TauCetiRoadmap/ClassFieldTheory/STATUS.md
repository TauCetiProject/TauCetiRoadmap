## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory, Layers 1–4, is done. Local reciprocity is proved for finite extensions and for the absolute Artin map, and Tate local duality holds for finite modules of order prime to the characteristic. The global theory has begun with the idele and idele-class formations and local invariants of Brauer classes. Local existence, the cohomological dimension of `G_K`, the Weil group and global reciprocity have not landed.

### Named results

- **Finite local reciprocity** — for a finite Galois extension `L/K` of nonarchimedean local fields, [`localArtinEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinEquiv) identifies `Kˣ/N_{L/K}(Lˣ)` with `Gal(L/K)^ab`, and it [sends a uniformizer to arithmetic Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinMap_uniformizer).
- **The absolute local Artin map** — [`artinMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap) `Kˣ → G_K^ab` is continuous with dense image, and its kernel is the intersection of the norm groups. It satisfies [norm functoriality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap_norm). For `ℓ` prime to `q = #k_K`, the [`ℓ`-adic cyclotomic character of `Art_K(x)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Cyclotomic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.abelianizedLocalCyclotomicCharacter_artinMap) is `q^{v(x)}`.
- **Tate local duality** — for `n` invertible in `K` and every finite Galois module `A` killed by `n`, cup product gives bijections `Hⁱ(G_K, A) ≃ Hom(H²⁻ⁱ(G_K, Hom(A, μₙ)), H²(G_K, μₙ))` in degrees [0](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap0_kummerCoeff_bijective), [1](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap1_kummerCoeff_bijective) and [2](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap2_kummerCoeff_bijective). These statements use the explicit cochain model, not the README's `tateDualityPairing_perfect_mixed`.
- **The local invariant** — [`invMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap) identifies `Br K` with `ℚ/ℤ`, normalized by arithmetic Frobenius. Restriction [multiplies it by the degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap_brRes).
- **Tate's theorem** — for a class formation, [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso) `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` is cup product with the fundamental class. In degree one it shows that [the third cohomology of a class formation vanishes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/DegreeThree.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.subsingleton_continuousCohomology_three), and in particular [`H³(G_K, (Kˢ)ˣ) = 0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/CohomologicalDimension.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.subsingleton_h3_unitsRep).

Local existence will be stated on these carriers.

## The frontier

- **Cohomological dimension of `G_K`** — `H³` vanishing is done. What remains is `cd_ℓ G_K = 2` for `K/ℚ_p` through the Sylow reduction and the Kummer sequence, and then `scd_ℓ G_K = 2` from the `(2,0)` case of duality at `ℤ/ℓᵐ`. This closes Layer 6.
- **The fundamental inequalities for idele classes** — prove `degree_le_card_normQuotient_of_isCyclic` and `card_H2_globalFormation_dvd_degree`, then `subsingleton_h1_globalFormation`. These are the arithmetic heart of Layer 10. The Herbrand quotient of permutation modules is now available for the `S`-unit computation.
- **The Brauer–Hasse–Noether sequence, then `globalInv`** — prove that `0 → Br K → ⨁_v Br K_v → ℚ/ℤ → 0` is exact in invariant coordinates. Then build `globalInv` by refinement: the cyclotomic input, killing the lifting obstruction, and independence of the lift.
- **Local abelian existence** — show that every open finite-index subgroup of `Kˣ` is the norm group of a finite abelian extension. Uniqueness, `localClassField`, both correspondences and injectivity of `artinMap` follow.
- **Duality in the README's form, then the Euler characteristic** — restate the bijections as `tateDualityPairing_perfect_mixed`, then prove `eulerCharacteristic_mixed` and `eulerCharacteristic_finrank_fp`. The new exactness of the Tate dual is the dimension-shifting input.
