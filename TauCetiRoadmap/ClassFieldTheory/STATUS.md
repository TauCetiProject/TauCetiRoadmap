## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory, Layers 1–4, is done. Local reciprocity is proved for finite extensions and for the absolute Artin map, which is now functorial for norms and isomorphisms. Tate local duality holds for finite modules of order prime to the characteristic. Local existence, the Euler characteristic, the cohomological dimension of `G_K`, the Weil group and everything global have not landed.

### Named results

- **Finite local reciprocity** — for a finite Galois extension `L/K` of nonarchimedean local fields, [`localArtinEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinEquiv) identifies `Kˣ/N_{L/K}(Lˣ)` with `Gal(L/K)^ab`. It [sends a uniformizer to arithmetic Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinMap_uniformizer), and on quadratic extensions it [agrees with the Hilbert symbol](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Quadratic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinMap_quadratic_eq_hilbertSymbol) when `2` is invertible.
- **The absolute local Artin map** — [`artinMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap) `Kˣ → G_K^ab` is continuous with dense image, and its kernel is the intersection of the norm groups. It satisfies [norm functoriality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap_norm), `Art_L(x) ↦ Art_K(N_{L/K} x)`, and it is [natural in the field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap_congr).
- **Tate local duality** — for `n` invertible in `K` and every finite Galois module `A` killed by `n`, cup product gives bijections `Hⁱ(G_K, A) ≃ Hom(H²⁻ⁱ(G_K, Hom(A, μₙ)), H²(G_K, μₙ))` in degrees [0](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap0_kummerCoeff_bijective), [1](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap1_kummerCoeff_bijective) and [2](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/FiniteModule.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.dualityMap2_kummerCoeff_bijective). - **The local invariant** — [`invMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap) identifies `Br K` with `ℚ/ℤ`, normalized by arithmetic Frobenius. - **Tate's theorem and the character formula** — for a class formation, [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso) `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` is cup product with the fundamental class. ### Notable definitions and infrastructure

Local existence will be stated on these carriers.
