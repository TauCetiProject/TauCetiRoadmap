## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory is done. Local class field theory is done through the correspondence and the Weil group, and global class field theory through existence, the correspondence, the named class fields with their splitting laws, Kronecker–Weber and Hilbert reciprocity. Two theorems remain open in the whole roadmap: the principal ideal theorem in Layer 13, and the unit cyclotomic normalization without its valuation-compatibility hypothesis in Layer 7.

### Named results

- **Tate's theorem** — for a class formation, [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso) `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` is cup product with the fundamental class, in every integer degree. Both the local and the global Artin maps come from it.
- **The global class-field correspondence** — open finite-index subgroups of `C_K` correspond, reversing order, to finite abelian extensions of `K` ([`globalClassFieldCorrespondence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/Correspondence.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalClassFieldCorrespondence)). It rests on [global existence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/Existence.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalAbelianExistence), proved by the Artin–Tate induction.
- **The Kronecker–Weber theorem** — every finite abelian extension of `ℚ` embeds in a cyclotomic field ([`kroneckerWeber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/KroneckerWeber.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.kroneckerWeber)), and the least cyclotomic level is the norm of the conductor's finite part.
- **The conductor–discriminant formula** — for finite abelian `L/K` of number fields, the relative discriminant is the product of the finite parts of the conductors of the characters of `Gal(L/K)` ([`relDiscr_eq_finprod_finitePart_abelianConductor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/AdmissibleModulus/ConductorDiscriminant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.relDiscr_eq_finprod_finitePart_abelianConductor)), and the conductor of `L/K` is their [maximum place by place](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/AdmissibleModulus/CharacterConductors.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.exponent_abelianConductor_eq_iSup_fixedField_ker).
- **Hilbert reciprocity** — the Hilbert invariants of `a, b ∈ Kˣ` sum to zero over all places of a number field ([`hilbertProductFormula`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/Hilbert/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.hilbertProductFormula)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The global invariant** — [`globalInv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalInv) sums the local invariants of an idele lift after refinement, which avoids the `H³` obstruction to lifting idele classes. It makes the idele classes a class formation, so global reciprocity is an instance of the abstract theory and [agrees with its abstract Artin map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalArtinEquiv_mk).
- **The absolute upper ramification filtration** — [`localAbsoluteUpperRamificationGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand/AbsoluteFiltration.html#TauCeti.localAbsoluteUpperRamificationGroup) is defined through every finite Galois level. It is closed, equals inertia at `0` and surjects onto each finite level, which is what lets finite unit-filtration reciprocity pass to the limit.
- **Class fields as values of the correspondence** — the [ray class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/RayClassField.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.rayClassField), the Hilbert and narrow Hilbert class fields, and the [ring class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/RingClass/Field.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ringClassField) of any order are each the class field of a named norm subgroup. Their Galois groups come by transport, and their splitting laws follow: a prime [splits completely in the Hilbert class field exactly when it is principal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/Splitting.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ncard_primesOver_hilbertClassField_eq_finrank_iff).

## The frontier

- **The principal ideal theorem**: `isPrincipal_map_hilbertClassField`. The [class-formation form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/PrincipalIdeal.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.groundInclusion_mem_normSubgroup_of_range_eq_commutator) is proved, and so are the norm statement for unramified Galois extensions containing the Hilbert class field and an idelic triviality criterion for `Cl_K → Cl_H`. What remains is to identify the relevant norm group with the trivial-modulus ray subgroup and read the result on ideals.
- **The cyclotomic normalization without the valuation hypothesis**: `cyclotomicCharacter_artinMap`. The README sketches an elementary argument that the compatibility assumption of the existing unit theorem always holds, since a discrete valuation must vanish on `ℤ_pˣ`.
- **Aligning names with the README**: the conductor–discriminant formula is proved as `multiplicity_relDiscr_eq_finsum_exponent_abelianConductor` and the character-maximum and real-place theorems, not as `count_relDiscr_eq_finsum_abelianConductor_exponent` and `abelianConductor_eq_lcm`. Either the README should name the existing statements or the packaged forms should be added.
