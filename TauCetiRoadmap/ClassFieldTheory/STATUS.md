## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The abstract theory is done. Local class field theory is done through the correspondence and the Weil group, and global class field theory through existence, the correspondence, the named class fields, Kronecker–Weber and Hilbert reciprocity. Two targets remain open in the whole roadmap: the principal ideal theorem in Layer 13, and the unit cyclotomic normalization without its valuation-compatibility hypothesis in Layer 7.

### Named results

- **Tate's theorem** — for a class formation, [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso) `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` is cup product with the fundamental class, in every integer degree. Both the local and the global Artin maps come from it.
- **The upper-numbering theorem** — for a finite Galois extension of nonarchimedean local fields, reciprocity carries `U(K,n)` onto the image of `Gal(L/K)^n` in `Gal(L/K)^ab` ([`normResidue_map_unitFiltration`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/UnitFiltration.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.normResidue_map_unitFiltration)). Its [absolute form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/UnitFiltration.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.map_artinMap_unitFiltration_eq_upper) holds for the absolute Artin map in either characteristic.
- **The global class-field correspondence** — open finite-index subgroups of `C_K` correspond, reversing order, to finite abelian extensions of `K` ([`globalClassFieldCorrespondence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/Correspondence.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalClassFieldCorrespondence)). It rests on [global existence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/GlobalExistence/Existence.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalAbelianExistence), proved by the Artin–Tate induction.
- **The Kronecker–Weber theorem** — every finite abelian extension of `ℚ` embeds in a cyclotomic field ([`kroneckerWeber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/KroneckerWeber.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.kroneckerWeber)), and the least cyclotomic level is the norm of the conductor's finite part.
- **Hilbert reciprocity** — the Hilbert invariants of `a, b ∈ Kˣ` sum to zero over all places of a number field ([`hilbertProductFormula`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/Hilbert/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.hilbertProductFormula)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The global invariant** — [`globalInv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Global/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.globalInv) sums the local invariants of an idele lift after refinement, which avoids the `H³` obstruction to lifting idele classes. It makes the idele classes a class formation, so global reciprocity is an instance of the abstract theory and shares the [local invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Invariant.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.invMap)'s normalization.
- **The absolute upper ramification filtration** — [`localAbsoluteUpperRamificationGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand/AbsoluteFiltration.html#TauCeti.localAbsoluteUpperRamificationGroup) is defined through every finite Galois level, with no subgroup parameter. It is closed, equals inertia at `0`, has wild inertia as the closure of its positive depths, and surjects onto each finite level, which is what lets finite unit-filtration reciprocity pass to the limit.
- **Class fields as values of the correspondence** — the [ray class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/RayClassField.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.rayClassField), the Hilbert and narrow Hilbert class fields, and the [ring class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassFields/RingClass/Field.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ringClassField) of any order are each the class field of a named norm subgroup. Their Galois groups, the ray class group, the class group and `Pic O`, come by transport.
