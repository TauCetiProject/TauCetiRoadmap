## ClassFieldTheory: 2026-09-04 to 2026-09-21 (`13bcb97` to `93386bc`)

The roadmap's central object now exists. An abstract [class formation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation) is defined in the finite-layer form of Artin–Tate: `H¹` vanishes on every finite normal layer, and `H²` carries an injective invariant map onto the order-`[K : F]` subgroup of `ℚ/ℤ`, compatible with restriction, inflation and conjugation. From these axioms, `H²` of each layer is [cyclic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.isAddCyclic_H2) of order exactly `[K : F]`, and the [fundamental class](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.fundamentalClass) is derived as the unique class of invariant `1/[K : F]`, not chosen. It [restricts](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.fundamentalClass_restrict) to the fundamental class, and it [corestricts](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.fundamentalClass_cor) and [inflates](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.fundamentalClass_infl) to the correctly scaled multiples. The three hypotheses of Tate's theorem are also proved separately, for the layer of every subgroup (TauCeti#7407).

Underneath this sits the layer machinery: [formations](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.Formation), finite normal layers, and the finite quotient system. Restriction, corestriction, inflation and conjugation are defined on ordinary and Tate cohomology with their tower laws, and [corestriction after restriction is the relative degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Restriction.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.LayerRestriction.tateCor_tateRes) in every Tate degree. Abelian layers, the maximal abelian sublayer and the [class field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassField.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.classField) of an open normal subgroup round out the Galois side. The class field is [abelian exactly when its layer is](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassField.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.isAbelianGalois_classField_iff).

Much of the window filled gaps in the generic cohomology supplier. The work covered corestriction and Shapiro compatibilities, Tate restriction and corestriction in negative degrees, dimension shifting, [two-periodicity for cyclic groups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Periodic.html#Rep.FiniteCyclicGroup.periodicIso) and the [Sylow reduction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/Sylow.html#TauCeti.groupCohomology.isZero_of_isZero_sylow) of Tate's triviality criterion. The Herbrand quotient is now [multiplicative in short exact sequences](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/HerbrandQuotient.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.herbrandQuotient_eq_mul_of_shortExact) and [unchanged by maps with finite kernel and cokernel](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/HerbrandQuotient.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.herbrandQuotient_eq_of_finite_kernel_of_finite_cokernel). The cup product has not landed, so Tate's theorem itself, and the Artin map it would produce, are still ahead.

## ClassFieldTheory: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`de52a34` to `e440f4e`)

Tate's theorem and the abstract Artin map both landed in this window. [Tate's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Cup/TateTheorem.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.cup_bijective_of_forall_isPGroup) is proved for a general finite group, deduced from Tate's [isomorphism criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/IsoCriterion.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.map_bijective_of_forall_isPGroup) for maps of representations. Its layer form [`tateTheorem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.tateTheorem) keeps the three hypotheses as separate arguments. Applied to a class formation it gives [`tateIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Theorem.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.tateIso), `Ĥ^r(U/V, ℤ) ≃ Ĥ^{r+2}(U/V, A^V)` in every integer degree, and its underlying map is provably cup product with the fundamental class. In degree −2 this is the [Nakayama map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.nakayamaNegTwo), whose inverse is [Artin reciprocity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.artinEquiv) `A^U / N(A^V) ≃ (U/V)^ab`. The [Artin map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.artinMap) is [surjective](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.surjective_artinMap), and its [kernel is the norm subgroup](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.ker_artinMap). Still missing are the compatibilities with restriction, corestriction and inflation, the Artin–Tate diagrams, the character formula and norm limitation.

Beneath this, the Tate cup product gained a unit, naturality in both coefficients, and associativity and compatibility with restriction in low bidegrees. Restriction and corestriction are now [uniform in every Tate degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Restriction/AllDegrees.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.res_comp_cor), with composite equal to multiplication by the index.

The local side began in earnest. The [Brauer group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.Br) is defined on continuous cohomology, and [every Brauer class is inflated from a finite level](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.exists_brLevelInfl_eq). The [Kummer isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/MuNRep.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.kummerEquiv) holds for `n` invertible in the field, and the image of `H²(μₙ)` in `Br F` is [the `n`-torsion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Torsion.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.h2MuToBr_range). The [local invariant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.unramifiedInv) exists only on unramified layers so far, normalized by arithmetic Frobenius. Ingredients for the local class formation arrived too: the [formation of units](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Units.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.unitsFormation) with Hilbert 90 on its finite layers, and [solvability of local Galois groups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Solvable.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.isSolvable_algEquiv). The formation itself has not landed.
