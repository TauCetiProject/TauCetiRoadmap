## ClassFieldTheory: 2026-09-04 to 2026-09-21 (`13bcb97` to `93386bc`)

The roadmap's central object now exists. An abstract [class formation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation) is defined in the finite-layer form of Artin–Tate: `H¹` vanishes on every finite normal layer, and `H²` carries an injective invariant map onto the order-`[K : F]` subgroup of `ℚ/ℤ`, compatible with restriction, inflation and conjugation. From these axioms, `H²` of each layer is [cyclic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.isAddCyclic_H2) of order exactly `[K : F]`, and the [fundamental class](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.fundamentalClass) is derived as the unique class of invariant `1/[K : F]`, not chosen. It [restricts](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.fundamentalClass_restrict) to the fundamental class, and it [corestricts](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.fundamentalClass_cor) and [inflates](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.fundamentalClass_infl) to the correctly scaled multiples. The three hypotheses of Tate's theorem are also proved separately, for the layer of every subgroup (TauCeti#7407).

Underneath this sits the layer machinery: [formations](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.Formation), finite normal layers, and the finite quotient system. Restriction, corestriction, inflation and conjugation are defined on ordinary and Tate cohomology with their tower laws, and [corestriction after restriction is the relative degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/Restriction.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.LayerRestriction.tateCor_tateRes) in every Tate degree. Abelian layers, the maximal abelian sublayer and the [class field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassField.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.classField) of an open normal subgroup round out the Galois side. The class field is [abelian exactly when its layer is](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/ClassField.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.isAbelianGalois_classField_iff).

Much of the window filled gaps in the generic cohomology supplier. The work covered corestriction and Shapiro compatibilities, Tate restriction and corestriction in negative degrees, dimension shifting, [two-periodicity for cyclic groups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Periodic.html#Rep.FiniteCyclicGroup.periodicIso) and the [Sylow reduction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/Sylow.html#TauCeti.groupCohomology.isZero_of_isZero_sylow) of Tate's triviality criterion. The Herbrand quotient is now [multiplicative in short exact sequences](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/HerbrandQuotient.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.herbrandQuotient_eq_mul_of_shortExact) and [unchanged by maps with finite kernel and cokernel](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/HerbrandQuotient.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.herbrandQuotient_eq_of_finite_kernel_of_finite_cokernel). The cup product has not landed, so Tate's theorem itself, and the Artin map it would produce, are still ahead.

## ClassFieldTheory: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (`e440f4e` to `22df7c0`)

The norm limitation theorem landed. In any class formation, a layer and its maximal abelian sublayer have the same norm subgroup ([`normSubgroup_maximalAbelianLayer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/NormLimitation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.normSubgroup_maximalAbelianLayer)). As the roadmap prescribes, it comes from reciprocity by counting: a refinement keeps the norm subgroup [exactly when it keeps the order of the abelianized Galois group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/NormLimitation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ClassFormation.map_normSubgroup_eq_iff). The Artin–Tate diagrams are not yet proved, but their ingredients are in place. These are the [norm between ground levels](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/GroundNorm.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.LayerRestriction.groundNorm), plus the facts that in Tate degree −2 restriction [is the group-theoretic transfer](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/ArtinLowDegree.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.LayerRestriction.tateHMinusTwoEquivAbelianization_trivialTateRes) and corestriction [is the map induced by inclusion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Formation/Tate/ArtinLowDegree.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.LayerRestriction.tateHMinusTwoEquivAbelianization_trivialTateCor).

The Tate cup product is now [associative in all integer tridegrees](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Cup/Associativity.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.cup_assoc). It [commutes with restriction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Cup/Restriction/Nonnegative.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.cup_res_of_nonneg) in nonnegative bidegrees and in bidegree `(r, 0)`, and it obeys the sign rule `x ∪ δy = (-1)^p δ(x ∪ y)` [when the first factor is flat](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Cup/Delta.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.cup_δ_of_flat). For a cyclic group, [cup product with `δχ` is two-periodicity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/TateCohomology/Cup/Cyclic.html#TauCeti.TateCohomology.cup_characterConnectingClass_eq_groupCohomologyπEven), which the character formula will need.

On the local side, the [local symbol](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/LocalSymbol.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localSymbol) is defined as the Kummer cup product followed by an identification `H²(μₙ) ≃ ℤ/n`. ## ClassFieldTheory: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-04 (`22df7c0` to `41e5e49`)

Local class field theory arrived. The units of a separable closure of a nonarchimedean local field now form a [class formation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ClassFormation.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localClassFormation) (TauCeti#11528), and so the abstract theory yields [finite local reciprocity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinEquiv), `Kˣ/N_{L/K}(Lˣ) ≃ Gal(L/K)^ab`. This isomorphism does not depend on how `L` is embedded, and it [sends a uniformizer to arithmetic Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinMap_uniformizer) on unramified layers. Passing to the limit gives the [absolute local Artin map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.artinMap) `Kˣ → G_K^ab`. It is [continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.continuous_artinMap), has dense image, and its [kernel is the intersection of all norm groups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.ker_artinMap_eq_iInf). For an abelian extension the [norm index equals the degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/LocalExistence/AbelianLayer.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.index_localNormSubgroup). A concrete check confirms the sign convention: over `ℚ₂`, the symbol of `2` [squares a fifth root of unity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Cyclotomic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localArtinMap_Q2_zeta5).
