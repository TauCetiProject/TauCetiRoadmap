## The frontier

- **Upper bounds for the other P1 presentations**: `M₁₂`, `M₂₂`, `M₂₃`, `M₂₄` and Higman–Sims have simple models and surjections from their presentations. Each needs a checked coset-enumeration or normal-form bound on the exact presented group, after which simplicity transports as it did for `M₁₁`.
- **`²A_r(q)` simplicity**: the fixed points are `SU_{r+1}(q)`, which acts primitively on isotropic points, and transvection, Siegel and rank-two root groups exist. What remains is generation by the elementary unitary group, perfectness with the small cases, and the bridge to `d.Group`.
- **The orthogonal families `B_r` (r ≥ 3), `D_r` and `²D_r`**: these need orthogonal carriers identified with their fixed points, plus perfectness and an Iwasawa structure, with `𝔽₂` and `𝔽₃` handled at arbitrary rank.
- **Exceptional and twisted families (`G₂`, `F₄`, `E₆`–`E₈`, `³D₄`, `²E₆`, Ree, Tits)**: these wait on T1's fixed-point root calculus and BN-pairs. The `.tits` case needs its separate ATLAS-model route.
- **Assembly A0**: this is blocked on T2, T3, P1 and P2. The elementary and finiteness branches are ready to be consumed.
