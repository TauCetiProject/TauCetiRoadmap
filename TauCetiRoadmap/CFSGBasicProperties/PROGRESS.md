## CFSGBasicProperties: 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-08 (`8115f49` to `a4fcb57`)

The first concrete simplicity theorems for Lie-type entries on the classification list have landed. [Every `A_r(q)` is simple](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/BasicProperties/TypeA.html#TauCeti.ValidLieTypeIndex.isSimpleGroup_A): its candidate group is identified with `PSL_{r+1}(𝔽_q)`, and that group is simple by Iwasawa's criterion, with [`PSL_n(F)` simple for `n ≥ 3` over every field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/PSL/Simple.html#Matrix.ProjectiveSpecialLinearGroup.isSimpleGroup_of_three_le_card). The same route gives [every `B₂(q)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/BasicProperties/TypeB/Two.html#TauCeti.ValidLieTypeIndex.isSimpleGroup_B_two) as `PSp₄(𝔽_q)`. The [Suzuki groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/BasicProperties/Suzuki.html#TauCeti.ValidLieTypeIndex.isSimpleGroup_suzuki) instead go through a rank-one Bruhat decomposition and the BN-pair it defines. The cyclic and alternating entries are settled ([alternating](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/BasicProperties/Elementary.html#TauCeti.CFSGIndex.isSimpleGroup_alternating)), and [every Lie-type group on the list is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/BasicProperties/LieTypeFinite.html#TauCeti.ValidLieTypeIndex.finite): every coordinate of a fixed point is a root of `X^m − X`.

The general machinery behind these results is in place too. [Tits's simplicity theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TitsSystem/NormalSubgroup.html#TauCeti.TitsSystem.isSimpleGroup_quotient_normalCore) and its [split form for the derived central quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TitsSystem/NormalClosure.html#TauCeti.TitsSystem.isSimpleGroup_derivedCentralQuotient) are proved, along with [an Iwasawa criterion with the same target](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupAction/Iwasawa.html#MulAction.IwasawaStructure.isSimpleGroup_derivedCentralQuotient). There is also groundwork for the families that are not yet done. The `²A_r(q)` fixed points are [identified with the special unitary group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/BasicProperties/TwistedA.html#TauCeti.TypeALieIndex.specialUnitaryEquivFixedPoints), and unitary transvection groups are built for it. Other pieces cover the centre of the `E₇` fixed points and fixed-point Bruhat cells.

On the sporadic side, the transcribed presentation of `M₁₁` is [shown to have 7920 elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/BasicProperties/Sporadic/Mathieu/Eleven.html#TauCeti.SporadicName.natCard_group_M11) and is identified with the permutation model (TauCeti#28427). Simplicity of that exact carrier is not yet proved. The presentation toolkit grew alongside it, with coset tables, normal-closure programs, double-coset covers and Reidemeister–Schreier subgroup presentations. Separately, the [kissing number 196560 of the Leech lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech/MinimalVectors.html#TauCeti.kissingNumber_leechLattice) is proved, as input for the Conway groups.
