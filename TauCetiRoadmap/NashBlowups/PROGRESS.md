## NashBlowups: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`d274015` to `0253c4f`)

This first window of the Nash blowups roadmap laid groundwork; the Nash blowup itself is not yet constructed. Two criteria from the roadmap's preliminaries landed. [Smoothness over a perfect field is tested at closed points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Locus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.smooth_iff_isRegularLocalRing_closedPoints): a scheme locally of finite type over a perfect field is smooth exactly when its local rings at closed points are regular. And a reduced scheme over a field [has dense smooth locus if and only if it is geometrically reduced](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Smooth/Locus/Dense.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.dense_smoothLocus_iff_geometricallyReduced). With the [smooth locus of dimension d](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Smooth/Locus/OfDim.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.smoothLocusOfDim), and the fact that it is the whole smooth locus of a scheme pure of dimension d, this settles the roadmap's standing hypotheses.

The predicate behind the triviality and smoothness criteria, that the differentials [admit a locally free quotient of rank d](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Differentials/LocallyFreeQuotient.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.HasLocallyFreeQuotientOfDifferentials), is now defined. It comes with restriction to opens, the identity quotient on smooth schemes, the forward half of the stalk criterion, and a general [uniqueness of locally free quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Reduced.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.existsUnique_iso_comp_eq) on reduced schemes. Two steps of the characteristic-zero criterion are in. The first gives [coordinates from a free quotient of the differentials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Kaehler/Coordinates.html#KaehlerDifferential.exists_coordinates_of_kaehler_surjection): elements x_j with dual derivations, splitting off the span of the dx_j from the kernel. The second says that [minimal primes over a derivation-stable ideal are stable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Derivation/MinimalPrime.html#Derivation.apply_mem_of_mem_minimalPrimes) in residual characteristic zero, with no Noetherian hypothesis. The Lie derivative that joins the two steps, and the criterion itself, are still open.

Smaller supporting results landed too. The [cotangent map of a closed immersion is surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Differentials/ClosedImmersion.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.epi_cotangentMap_of_isClosedImmersion), and [normality survives base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Normal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isNormalScheme_pullback_of_isSeparablyGenerated), but only along finitely generated, separably generated extensions. Several of the README's worked checks are formalised as well, including the base-change maps over k(s) and over the field with two elements (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/3841).
