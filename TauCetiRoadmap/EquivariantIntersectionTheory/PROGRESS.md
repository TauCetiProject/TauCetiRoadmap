## EquivariantIntersectionTheory: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`bfc502b` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap opened with its first milestone, the shift classification for graded rings (TauCeti#34721). The shifts `A(d)` and `A(e)` of a graded ring are graded-isomorphic exactly when `d − e` is the degree of a homogeneous unit ([`sub_mem_homogeneousUnitDegrees_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Units.html#TauCeti.sub_mem_homogeneousUnitDegrees_iff)), so the [shift class group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Units.html#TauCeti.ShiftClassGroup), the degree group modulo those degrees, classifies them. The same work proves the module form the roadmap asks for: a compatible grading of `A` with a homogeneous unit `u` of degree `c` is `u` times the shift `A(−c)` ([`eq_map_mulLeft_of_isUnit_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Units.html#TauCeti.eq_map_mulLeft_of_isUnit_mem)), and such a grading is a shift exactly when it has a homogeneous generator ([`exists_linearEquiv_iff_exists_isUnit_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Units.html#TauCeti.exists_linearEquiv_iff_exists_isUnit_mem)).

The first computations came with it. For a group algebra graded through a homomorphism `f`, the homogeneous unit degrees are the range of `f` ([`homogeneousUnitDegrees_gradeBy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/Grading.html#TauCeti.homogeneousUnitDegrees_gradeBy)), and so for the Laurent ring with `T` in degree `w` the shift class group is [`ZMod |w|`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Laurent/Units.html#TauCeti.shiftClassGroupLaurentPolynomialEquiv): the graded ring of `𝔾_m` acting on itself by `s ↦ s^w`. What this does not yet give is the connected-spectrum statement of 0.2, under which every grading of `A` has a homogeneous generator, so the shift classes are all the gradings; the localisation grading, weighted polynomial rings and the generic discriminant are also still to come.
