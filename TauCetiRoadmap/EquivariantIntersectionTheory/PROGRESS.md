## EquivariantIntersectionTheory: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`bfc502b` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap opened with its first milestone, the shift classification for graded rings (TauCeti#34721). The shifts `A(d)` and `A(e)` of a graded ring are graded-isomorphic exactly when `d − e` is the degree of a homogeneous unit ([`sub_mem_homogeneousUnitDegrees_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Units.html#TauCeti.sub_mem_homogeneousUnitDegrees_iff)), so the [shift class group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Units.html#TauCeti.ShiftClassGroup), the degree group modulo those degrees, classifies them. The same work proves the module form the roadmap asks for: a compatible grading of `A` with a homogeneous unit `u` of degree `c` is `u` times the shift `A(−c)` ([`eq_map_mulLeft_of_isUnit_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Units.html#TauCeti.eq_map_mulLeft_of_isUnit_mem)), and such a grading is a shift exactly when it has a homogeneous generator ([`exists_linearEquiv_iff_exists_isUnit_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Units.html#TauCeti.exists_linearEquiv_iff_exists_isUnit_mem)).

The first computations came with it. For a group algebra graded through a homomorphism `f`, the homogeneous unit degrees are the range of `f` ([`homogeneousUnitDegrees_gradeBy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/Grading.html#TauCeti.homogeneousUnitDegrees_gradeBy)), and so for the Laurent ring with `T` in degree `w` the shift class group is [`ZMod |w|`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Laurent/Units.html#TauCeti.shiftClassGroupLaurentPolynomialEquiv): the graded ring of `𝔾_m` acting on itself by `s ↦ s^w`. What this does not yet give is the connected-spectrum statement of 0.2, under which every grading of `A` has a homogeneous generator, so the shift classes are all the gradings; the localisation grading, weighted polynomial rings and the generic discriminant are also still to come.

## EquivariantIntersectionTheory: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

Milestone 0.2 is proved: over a ring with connected prime spectrum, every grading of the free module of rank one compatible with the ring's grading has a homogeneous generator ([`exists_isUnit_mem_of_connected`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Generator.html#TauCeti.GradedAlgebra.exists_isUnit_mem_of_connected)), so with the shift classification already in place the shift class group now classifies all such gradings, not just the shifts (TauCeti#35622). The proof records the homogeneous components of `1` as a [Laurent polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/DirectSum/Laurent.html#TauCeti.decomposeLaurent), which [is always a unit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Generator.html#TauCeti.GradedAlgebra.isUnit_decomposeLaurent_one), and then shows that on a connected spectrum a Laurent unit has a [unit coefficient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Laurent/Units/Connected.html#LaurentPolynomial.exists_isUnit_coeff_of_isUnit), the others being nilpotent. This replaces the README's route through the domain case with a statement about Laurent units over any connected base.

Milestone 0.3 is also largely in: for `f` homogeneous of degree `e`, the localisation `A_f` carries the [grading](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Localization/Away.html#TauCeti.GradedAlgebra.awayGrading) in which `a / fⁿ` has degree `deg a − n e`, negative degrees included, with its graded-algebra structure, the localisation map as a [graded homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Localization/Away.html#TauCeti.GradedAlgebra.awayGradedAlgHom), and its degree-zero part [identified](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Localization/Away.html#TauCeti.GradedAlgebra.awayGradingZeroEquiv) with Mathlib's homogeneous localisation (TauCeti#35941). The comparison with the nonnegatively graded interface of AlgebraicVectorBundles, which the README also asks for, is not there yet.
