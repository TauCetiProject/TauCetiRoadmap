### Named results

- **Collins delineability** — if the Collins projection of a finite family is sign-invariant on a preconnected `S ⊆ ℝⁿ`, the fibres have a common stack of continuous ordered roots over `S`, with constant multiplicities and signs. No well-orientedness assumption is needed: [`nonempty_delineation_of_signInvariant_collinsProjection`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Projection/Delineability.html#TauCeti.nonempty_delineation_of_signInvariant_collinsProjection).
- **The Sturm–Tarski theorem** — over any real closed field, the variation drop of a signed remainder chain is the sum of `sign q` over the distinct roots of `p`. It holds without squarefreeness, on every kind of interval: [`Sturm.sum_sign_Ioo`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Sturm/OneSided.html#TauCeti.Sturm.sum_sign_Ioo).
- **The Cauchy index by signed subresultants** — the whole-line Cauchy index of `q/p` is the permanences minus variations of the signed principal subresultant coefficients: [`cauchyIndex_univ_eq_permanencesMinusVariations_signedPsc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Subresultant/CauchyIndex.html#Polynomial.cauchyIndex_univ_eq_permanencesMinusVariations_signedPsc).
- **Splitting by power substitution** — a monic family with analytic coefficients and simple roots over a simply connected domain times a punctured disc splits into distinct analytic linear factors after `t ↦ t^n`, for `d! ∣ n`. This is the punctured case of Puiseux with parameters: [`exists_analyticOnNhd_prod_X_sub_C_powerSubstitution`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Puiseux/PowerSubstitution.html#TauCeti.Polynomial.exists_analyticOnNhd_prod_X_sub_C_powerSubstitution).
- **Quantifier elimination, given projection closure** — projection closure of semialgebraic sets is [equivalent](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/QuantifierElimination.html#TauCeti.forall_isSemialgebraic_image_tail_iff) to every ordered-ring formula with parameters having a quantifier-free equivalent: [`exists_isQF_realize_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/QuantifierElimination.html#TauCeti.exists_isQF_realize_iff).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Cylindrical algebraic decompositions.** [`IsCAD`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/CAD.html#TauCeti.IsCAD) is the recursive definition by semialgebraic stacks over cells, with finiteness, partition, connected cells and cylindricity proved. Once an adapted CAD exists, its API already yields projection closure and with it the conditional results above.
- **Uniform root loci.** The [graph of the `i`th root](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/RootCount.html#TauCeti.isSemialgebraic_setOf_isRoot_card_roots_lt) of a polynomial family, and the [regions between roots](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/RootCount.html#TauCeti.isSemialgebraic_setOf_not_isRoot_card_roots_lt), are semialgebraic. This is the bridge for proving that delineated stacks over semialgebraic bases are semialgebraic.
- **The Lazard valuation.** [`lazardValuation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/LazardValuation.html#MvPolynomial.lazardValuation) is the lexicographically least Taylor exponent at a point. Via [evaluators](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/LazardValuation.html#TauCeti.exists_isLazardEvaluator) it becomes an order along monomial curves, which is the input that connects Layer 8 to Layer 7's constant-order lemma.

## The frontier

- **Semialgebraic stacks** — apply the root-locus descriptions to the product of the active subfamily, so that delineated stacks over semialgebraic bases are semialgebraic.
- **CAD existence** — induct on dimension through Collins delineability and semialgebraic stacks. This gives projection closure and so makes quantifier elimination and the image APIs unconditional.
- **Puiseux with parameters** — extend the punctured splitting across `y = 0` using the Riemann extension theorem and root bounds, with discriminant `y^a·u`, and then the nonmonic version.
- **McCallum's theorem** — the local discriminant theorem on analytic submanifolds, then recursive order-invariance under well-orientedness using the delineating set.
- **Lazard lifting** — the uniform parameterized identity along monomial curves, analytic Lazard delineability, and CAD existence without well-orientedness.
