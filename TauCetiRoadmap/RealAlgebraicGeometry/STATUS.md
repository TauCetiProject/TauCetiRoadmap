### Named results

- **Collins delineability** — if the Collins projection of a finite family is sign-invariant on a preconnected `S ⊆ ℝⁿ`, the fibres have a common stack of continuous ordered roots over `S`, with constant multiplicities and signs, and no well-orientedness assumption: [`nonempty_delineation_of_signInvariant_collinsProjection`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Projection/Delineability.html#TauCeti.nonempty_delineation_of_signInvariant_collinsProjection).
- **The Sturm–Tarski theorem** — over any real closed field, the variation drop of a signed remainder chain is the sum of `sign q` over the distinct roots of `p`, without squarefreeness and on every kind of interval: [`Sturm.sum_sign_Ioo`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Sturm/OneSided.html#TauCeti.Sturm.sum_sign_Ioo).
- **The Cauchy index by signed subresultants** — the whole-line Cauchy index of `q/p` is the permanences minus variations of the signed principal subresultant coefficients: [`cauchyIndex_univ_eq_permanencesMinusVariations_signedPsc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Subresultant/CauchyIndex.html#Polynomial.cauchyIndex_univ_eq_permanencesMinusVariations_signedPsc).
- **Monic Puiseux with parameters** — a monic family with analytic coefficients and discriminant `y^a·u`, `u` nowhere zero, splits into analytic linear factors on a full disc after `y = t^{d!}`, with every root difference a power of `t` times a unit: [`exists_analyticOnNhd_prod_X_sub_C_of_discr_eq_pow_mul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Puiseux/Monic.html#TauCeti.Polynomial.exists_analyticOnNhd_prod_X_sub_C_of_discr_eq_pow_mul).
- **Quantifier elimination, given projection closure** — projection closure of semialgebraic sets is [equivalent](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/QuantifierElimination.html#TauCeti.forall_isSemialgebraic_image_tail_iff) to every ordered-ring formula with parameters having a quantifier-free equivalent: [`exists_isQF_realize_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/QuantifierElimination.html#TauCeti.exists_isQF_realize_iff).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Cylindrical algebraic decompositions.** `IsCAD` is the recursive definition by semialgebraic stacks over cells, with finiteness, partition, connected cells and cylindricity proved. Once an adapted CAD exists, this API yields projection closure and makes the conditional results above unconditional.
- **Uniform root loci.** The [graph of the `i`th root](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/RootCount.html#TauCeti.isSemialgebraic_setOf_isRoot_card_roots_lt) of a polynomial family, and the regions between roots, are semialgebraic. This is the bridge for proving that delineated stacks over semialgebraic bases are semialgebraic.
- **Lazard delineations.** A [`LazardDelineation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Stack/Lazard.html#TauCeti.LazardDelineation) is a common stack of roots of Lazard evaluations, built on the Lazard valuation. Valuation- and sign-invariance on its sections and sectors, and analyticity of its roots over analytic submanifolds, are already proved. That leaves Lazard's lifting theorem with a single job: producing one.

## The frontier

- **Semialgebraic stacks** — apply the root-locus descriptions to the product of the active subfamily, so that delineated stacks over semialgebraic bases are semialgebraic.
- **CAD existence** — induct on dimension through Collins delineability and semialgebraic stacks. This gives projection closure, which makes quantifier elimination and the image APIs unconditional.
- **The local discriminant theorem** — link constant order of the discriminant on an analytic submanifold to the `y^a·u` hypothesis of monic Puiseux, using the new directional-order factorizations, then restrict to a real analytic delineation with constant order on sections.
- **Nonmonic Puiseux** — Laurent forms of nonmonic roots are extracted from a given splitting of the integral normalization, but that splitting still has to be constructed by power substitution and extension.
- **Lazard lifting** — prove that a polynomial with constant Lazard valuations of its discriminant, leading and trailing coefficients on a connected analytic submanifold has a Lazard delineation, then build CAD by Lazard projection without well-orientedness.
