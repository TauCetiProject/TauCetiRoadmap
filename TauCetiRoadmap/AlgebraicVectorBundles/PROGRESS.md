## AlgebraicVectorBundles: 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-26 (`334b19e` to `759eb3e`)

Sheaves of modules over a sheaf of commutative rings on a small site now form a closed symmetric monoidal category ([`TauCeti.SheafOfModules.monoidalClosed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/Closed.html#TauCeti.SheafOfModules.monoidalClosed)), which specialises to `X.Modules` on a scheme and is L0A's milestone (TauCeti#7335, TauCeti#7391). The tensor product is the sheafified sectionwise tensor product and the internal Hom is the sheafified presheaf internal Hom. Restriction to an open was then made strong symmetric monoidal ([`SheafOfModules.overFunctorMonoidal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/Restriction/Monoidal.html#SheafOfModules.overFunctorMonoidal)), with a comparison map for internal Homs.

Most of the remaining work built the categories of L0B. Quasi-coherence, finite presentation and local freeness are each preserved by tensor products ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isQuasicoherent_tensorObj`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/TensorProduct.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isQuasicoherent_tensorObj)), by direct sums and by pullback along any morphism of schemes ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocallyFree_pullback`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocallyFree_pullback)), and local freeness and finite presentation can be checked on a cover ([`SheafOfModules.IsLocallyFree.of_coversTop`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/LocallyFree.html#SheafOfModules.IsLocallyFree.of_coversTop)). This gives `QCoh(X)` as a symmetric monoidal category and the category of finite locally free sheaves ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/FiniteLocallyFree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf)), which is additive, symmetric monoidal, stable under pullback, and contains the invertible sheaves.

Toward the L0B milestone, that a quasi-coherent sheaf is finite locally free exactly when it is dualizable, finite free sheaves are shown to be self-dual ([`TauCeti.SheafOfModules.exactPairingFree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/Dual.html#TauCeti.SheafOfModules.exactPairingFree)), and restriction inverts the internal-Hom comparison out of them. The milestone itself, rank, the internal-Hom stalk and base-change comparisons, and monoidal pullback of modules are not there yet, and L0C, L1B and L2 have not started. L1A has one input lemma: sections of a quasi-coherent module over a basic open are the localization of its sections over the affine open, as in Hartshorne II.5.3 ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocalizedModule_basicOpenRestrict`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Localization.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocalizedModule_basicOpenRestrict)).

## AlgebraicVectorBundles: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `0d3161a`)

This window assembled most of the pieces for L0B's duality milestone without yet gluing them. On the algebra side, an `R`-module is dualizable exactly when it is finite projective ([`ModuleCat.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_finite_projective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/Dualizable.html#ModuleCat.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_finite_projective)), the affine model for the statement to come. In any closed symmetric monoidal category, an object is dualizable exactly when the dual-tensor comparison `Hom(Y, 𝟙) ⊗ Y → Hom(Y, Y)` is invertible ([`TauCeti.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isIso_dualTensorIhom_app`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Rigid/OfClosed.html#TauCeti.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isIso_dualTensorIhom_app)), and the canonical double-dual map of a dualizable object is an isomorphism ([`TauCeti.isIso_doubleDualMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Rigid/DoubleDual.html#TauCeti.isIso_doubleDualMap)). For sheaves, a finite locally free sheaf is shown to have a cover by finite free charts, each carrying a local exact pairing and an invertible dual-tensor comparison ([`SheafOfModules.IsLocallyFree.exists_isLocallyFreeData_isFiniteType_isIso_dualTensorIhom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/LocalDuality.html#SheafOfModules.IsLocallyFree.exists_isLocallyFreeData_isFiniteType_isIso_dualTensorIhom)). What is still missing is the step from these local pairings to a global dual in `QCoh(X)`, and the converse.

Finite locally free sheaves now have a rank ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.rank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Rank.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.rank)), a locally constant function computed by the size of any local basis. Its rank loci are clopen, it is compatible with pullback, and a finite locally free sheaf is invertible exactly when its rank is one everywhere ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.isInvertible_iff_forall_rank_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Rank.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.isInvertible_iff_forall_rank_eq_one)). Pullback of these sheaves also gained its identity and composition isomorphisms.

The rest is groundwork for L0A's monoidal pullback. Pushforward and restriction of scalars of presheaves of modules are lax braided monoidal (TauCeti#10140). Sections of a presheaf internal Hom over `U` are morphisms of the restrictions to `U`, and restriction to a slice commutes with internal Hom (TauCeti#9844, TauCeti#10195). Strong monoidal pullback and the stalk comparison are not there yet.

## AlgebraicVectorBundles: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-05 (`0d3161a` to `1d09589`)

Finite locally free sheaves now form a rigid category ([`FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.rigidCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.rigidCategory)), with both duals of `E` given by `Hom(E, 𝒪_X)` and a natural double-dual isomorphism ([`evalNatIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Bidual.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.evalNatIso)). This gives half of L0B's milestone on every scheme: a finite locally free quasi-coherent sheaf is dualizable in `QCoh(X)` ([`nonempty_hasLeftDual_of_isFiniteLocallyFree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.QuasicoherentSheaf.nonempty_hasLeftDual_of_isFiniteLocallyFree)). The converse is proved only on affine schemes ([`nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Tilde/Dual.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.QuasicoherentSheaf.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree)). Underneath it sits the affine comparison: finitely generated projective `R`-modules are equivalent, monoidally, to finite locally free sheaves on `Spec R` ([`finiteProjectiveEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Affine/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.finiteProjectiveEquiv)). Fixed-rank subcategories also arrived, and rank one is now equivalent to `InvertibleSheaf X` as a category ([`fixedRankOneEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/FixedRank.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.InvertibleSheaf.fixedRankOneEquiv)).

For L0A, the internal-Hom stalk comparison is done: when the source is finitely presented, the stalk of `Hom(M, N)` is the module of linear maps between stalks ([`ihomStalkEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/InternalHom/FinitePresentation.html#SheafOfModules.ihomStalkEquiv)). Monoidal pullback has advanced but is not finished. Pullback preserves tensor products with globally presented factors, and pullback of quasi-coherent sheaves from an affine base is symmetric monoidal ([`pullbackFromAffineBraided`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback/Affine.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.pullbackFromAffineBraided)). Pullback along a general morphism is not yet strong monoidal.

L1A has started. The relative spectrum of a quasi-coherent commutative algebra is glued from the spectra of its sections over affine opens ([`relativeSpec`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.CommMon.relativeSpec)), with an affine structure morphism and the expected charts. It is packaged as a contravariant functor to affine `X`-schemes ([`relativeSpec`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Functor.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpec)). Its universal property and base change are still to come.

## AlgebraicVectorBundles: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07 (`1d09589` to `8334df2`)

L0C has begun. Sheaves of modules now have symmetric powers ([`SheafOfModules.symmetricPower`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/SymmetricPower.html#SheafOfModules.symmetricPower)) and exterior powers ([`SheafOfModules.exteriorPower`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/ExteriorPower/Basic.html#SheafOfModules.exteriorPower)), each the sheafification of the sectionwise construction, with degrees zero and one identified with `𝒪_X` and the sheaf itself. Exterior powers go further. The `n`-th exterior power of the free sheaf on a finite ordered set is free on its `n`-element subsets ([`exteriorPowerFreeIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/ExteriorPower/Free.html#SheafOfModules.exteriorPowerFreeIso)). As a result, exterior powers of a finite locally free sheaf are finite locally free ([`isFiniteLocallyFree_exteriorPower`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/ExteriorPower/LocallyFree.html#SheafOfModules.isFiniteLocallyFree_exteriorPower)), with rank `(rank E x).choose n` ([`rank_exteriorPower_apply`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/ExteriorPower.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.rank_exteriorPower_apply)). Symmetric powers have no such result yet, and the determinant and the pullback comparisons that make up the L0C milestone have not started.

On the relative-Spec side, the second functor of the L1B anti-equivalence now exists: [`affineFunctions`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/AffineFunctions.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.affineFunctions) sends an affine `p : V → X` to the quasi-coherent algebra `p_* 𝒪_V` and a morphism over `X` to pullback of regular functions, as the README asks. Over an affine base, relative Spec is naturally the spectrum of global sections ([`relativeSpecAffineIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Affine.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecAffineIso)). The functor-of-points property is still missing, and so are the recovery of `A` from `Spec_X A` and base change.

Smaller additions: kernels of morphisms of quasi-coherent sheaves are quasi-coherent on any scheme ([`isQuasicoherent_kernel`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Quasicoherent/Kernels.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isQuasicoherent_kernel)), and there is a comparison map from the stalk of a tensor product to the tensor product of stalks, though no proof yet that it is an isomorphism.

## AlgebraicVectorBundles: 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-19 (`8334df2` to `9fc5d5a`)

Two milestones closed. Relative Spec is now an anti-equivalence between quasi-coherent commutative algebras on `X` and affine `X`-schemes ([`relativeSpecEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecEquiv)), with the actual `p_* 𝒪_V` as inverse and both triangle identities proved. It rests on the functor-of-points property, `Hom_X(T, Spec_X A) ≃ Hom(A, q_* 𝒪_T)` natural in `T` and `A` ([`relativeSpecHomIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/UniversalProperty.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecHomIso)), on recovering `A` as `p_* 𝒪`, and on recovering an affine scheme from its function algebra. And L0B's milestone now holds on every scheme: a quasi-coherent sheaf is dualizable in `QCoh(X)` exactly when it is finite locally free ([`nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Dualizable.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.QuasicoherentSheaf.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree)). The same pull request made pullback of quasi-coherent sheaves strong symmetric monoidal along every morphism ([`pullbackBraided`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback/Monoidal/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.QuasicoherentSheaf.pullbackBraided)), and later work made its identity and composition isomorphisms monoidal.

On the polynomial side, symmetric powers of a vector bundle are finite locally free ([`isFiniteLocallyFree_symmetricPower`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/SymmetricPower/LocallyFree.html#SheafOfModules.isFiniteLocallyFree_symmetricPower)), with rank the number of degree-`n` monomials, and the determinant is a functor from rank-`r` bundles to invertible sheaves ([`determinant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Determinant.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.determinant)). Internal Hom out of a finitely presented sheaf is quasi-coherent, and it commutes with arbitrary pullback when the source is finite locally free. Much of the remaining work built pullback comparisons for symmetric and exterior powers. These are proved to be isomorphisms only between affine spectra and for free sheaves, so L0C's milestone is still open.

L2A has begun. Graded quasi-coherent algebras exist ([`GradedQuasicoherentAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Quasicoherent/GradedAlgebra/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.GradedQuasicoherentAlgebra)), along with their affine graded rings of sections, which localize on basic opens. Relative Spec that keeps the grading is again an anti-equivalence ([`gradedRelativeSpecEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Graded.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.gradedRelativeSpecEquiv)). The graded symmetric algebra, `linearSpec` and the `QCoh(X)ᵒᵖ ≃ LinearSch(X)` milestone have not appeared yet.

## AlgebraicVectorBundles: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25 (`9fc5d5a` to `69cd1ab`)

Relative Spec now commutes with base change: for any `f : Y → X`, the relative spectrum of a pulled-back algebra is the fibre product `Y ×_X Spec_X A`, naturally in the algebra ([`relativeSpecBaseChangeIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecBaseChangeIso)), and compatibly with identities and composites ([`relativeSpecPullbackIso_comp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecPullbackIso_comp)). With the symmetric algebra of a quasi-coherent sheaf now built, `Spec_X (Sym F)` is shown to represent linear functionals, `Hom_X(T, Spec_X Sym F) ≃ Hom(q^* F, 𝒪_T)` ([`relativeSpecSymmetricAlgebraPullbackHomEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/SymmetricAlgebra.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecSymmetricAlgebraPullbackHomEquiv)). That is the universal property `V_lin(F)` is meant to have, though `linearSpec` itself is not defined yet.

The first half of the L2A factorization is in place. The graded symmetric algebra is left adjoint to taking the degree-one piece ([`gradedSymmetricAlgebraAdjunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Quasicoherent/GradedAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/Adjunction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.gradedSymmetricAlgebraAdjunction)). It gives an equivalence between quasi-coherent sheaves and graded algebras freely generated in degree one ([`gradedSymmetricAlgebraEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Quasicoherent/GradedAlgebra/Free.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.gradedSymmetricAlgebraEquiv)), whose inverse is the degree-one functor. Composing with the graded relative-Spec anti-equivalence is the remaining step to `QCoh(X)ᵒᵖ ≃ LinearSch(X)`.

L0C's pullback comparisons are now isomorphisms on every scheme. Symmetric and exterior powers of a quasi-coherent sheaf commute with pullback along any morphism ([`isIso_pullbackSymmetricPowerComparison_app`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/SymmetricPower/Pullback/Quasicoherent.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isIso_pullbackSymmetricPowerComparison_app), [`isIso_pullbackExteriorPowerComparison_app`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/ExteriorPower/Pullback/Quasicoherent.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isIso_pullbackExteriorPowerComparison_app)), which is proved by reducing to affine charts. The fixed-rank determinant gets a pullback isomorphism compatible with identities and composites ([`pullbackDeterminantIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Determinant/Pullback.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.pullbackDeterminantIso)), and the dual's comparison gets the same coherence. Flat base change of internal Hom out of a finitely presented sheaf is proved over an affine base or for a globally presented source ([`isIso_pullbackIhomComparison_app_of_isAffine`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback/InternalHom/Flat.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isIso_pullbackIhomComparison_app_of_isAffine)), but not yet on a general base.
