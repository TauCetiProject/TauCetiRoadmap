## AlgebraicVectorBundles: 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-26 (`334b19e` to `759eb3e`)

Sheaves of modules over a sheaf of commutative rings on a small site now form a closed symmetric monoidal category ([`TauCeti.SheafOfModules.monoidalClosed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/Closed.html#TauCeti.SheafOfModules.monoidalClosed)), which specialises to `X.Modules` on a scheme and is L0A's milestone (TauCeti#7335, TauCeti#7391). The tensor product is the sheafified sectionwise tensor product and the internal Hom is the sheafified presheaf internal Hom. Restriction to an open was then made strong symmetric monoidal ([`SheafOfModules.overFunctorMonoidal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/Restriction/Monoidal.html#SheafOfModules.overFunctorMonoidal)), with a comparison map for internal Homs.

Most of the remaining work built the categories of L0B. Quasi-coherence, finite presentation and local freeness are each preserved by tensor products ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isQuasicoherent_tensorObj`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/TensorProduct.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isQuasicoherent_tensorObj)), by direct sums and by pullback along any morphism of schemes ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocallyFree_pullback`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocallyFree_pullback)), and local freeness and finite presentation can be checked on a cover ([`SheafOfModules.IsLocallyFree.of_coversTop`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/LocallyFree.html#SheafOfModules.IsLocallyFree.of_coversTop)). This gives `QCoh(X)` as a symmetric monoidal category and the category of finite locally free sheaves ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/FiniteLocallyFree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf)), which is additive, symmetric monoidal, stable under pullback, and contains the invertible sheaves.

Toward the L0B milestone, that a quasi-coherent sheaf is finite locally free exactly when it is dualizable, finite free sheaves are shown to be self-dual ([`TauCeti.SheafOfModules.exactPairingFree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/Dual.html#TauCeti.SheafOfModules.exactPairingFree)), and restriction inverts the internal-Hom comparison out of them. The milestone itself, rank, the internal-Hom stalk and base-change comparisons, and monoidal pullback of modules are not there yet, and L0C, L1B and L2 have not started. L1A has one input lemma: sections of a quasi-coherent module over a basic open are the localization of its sections over the affine open, as in Hartshorne II.5.3 ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocalizedModule_basicOpenRestrict`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Localization.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocalizedModule_basicOpenRestrict)).
