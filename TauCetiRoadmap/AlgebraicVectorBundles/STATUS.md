### Named results

- **The closed symmetric monoidal category of sheaves of modules** — over a sheaf of commutative rings on a small site, and so on `X.Modules` for a scheme `X`, with tensor product and internal Hom the sheafifications of their presheaf counterparts ([`TauCeti.SheafOfModules.monoidalClosed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/Closed.html#TauCeti.SheafOfModules.monoidalClosed)).
- **Quasi-coherence is a monoidal property** — tensor products of quasi-coherent `𝒪_X`-modules are quasi-coherent, and likewise for local freeness and finite presentation ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isQuasicoherent_tensorObj`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/TensorProduct.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isQuasicoherent_tensorObj)).
- **Local freeness and finite presentation are local** — a sheaf of modules that is locally free, or finitely presented, on each member of a cover is so globally ([`SheafOfModules.IsLocallyFree.of_coversTop`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/LocallyFree.html#SheafOfModules.IsLocallyFree.of_coversTop), [`SheafOfModules.IsFinitePresentation.of_coversTop`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/Quasicoherent/FinitePresentationDescent.html#SheafOfModules.IsFinitePresentation.of_coversTop)).
- **Pullback preserves quasi-coherent, finitely presented and locally free modules** — along any morphism of schemes, giving pullback functors on quasi-coherent and on finite locally free sheaves ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocallyFree_pullback`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocallyFree_pullback)).
- **Sections over a basic open are a localization** — for quasi-coherent `M`, affine open `U` and `f ∈ Γ(X, U)`, restriction `Γ(M, U) → Γ(M, D(f))` is localization at the powers of `f` (Hartshorne II.5.3) ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocalizedModule_basicOpenRestrict`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Localization.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isLocalizedModule_basicOpenRestrict)).

## The frontier

- **Monoidal pullback of modules (L0A)** — pullback as a strong symmetric monoidal functor with coherent identity and composition comparisons, and the internal-Hom stalk comparison. The pullback comparisons of L0B and L0C depend on it, as does pulling back quasi-coherent algebras in L1A's universal property.
- **Internal Hom in `QCoh(X)` (L0B)** — quasi-coherence of `Hom(E, F)` for finitely presented `E`, the comparison `f^* Hom(E, F) → Hom(f^* E, f^* F)` with its two isomorphism criteria (finitely presented source and flat `f`, or finite locally free source and any `f`), and the comparison with finite projective modules over affines through `tildeEquiv`.
- **Relative Spec (L1A)** — quasi-coherent commutative algebras as commutative monoids in `QCoh(X)`, then `Spec_X` glued from affine-local spectra, with its functor-of-points property. The basic-open localization lemma supplies the compatibility on basic opens that this gluing needs.
