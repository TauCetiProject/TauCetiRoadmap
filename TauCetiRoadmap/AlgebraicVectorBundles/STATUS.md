### Named results

- **Linear schemes are dual to quasi-coherent sheaves** — `linearSpec F = Spec_X Sym F` and the degree-one functor are quasi-inverse between `QCoh(X)ᵒᵖ` and affine `X`-schemes with a grading freely generated in degree one ([`linearSpecEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/LinearScheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.linearSpecEquiv)).
- **Points of a vector bundle are sections** — morphisms `T → Spec_X Sym(E^∨)` over `X` are sections of `q^* E`, naturally in `E` and `T` ([`totalSpaceHomIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/TotalSpace/FunctorOfPoints.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.totalSpaceHomIso)). The same dictionary covers direct sums, tensor products, internal Hom, duals, powers and the determinant.
- **The relative-Spec anti-equivalence** — relative Spec and `p_* 𝒪_V` are mutually inverse between quasi-coherent commutative algebras on `X` and affine `X`-schemes ([`relativeSpecEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecEquiv)), compatibly with base change ([`relativeSpecBaseChangeIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecBaseChangeIso)).
- **Dualizable means finite locally free** — on any scheme, a quasi-coherent sheaf has a dual in `QCoh(X)` exactly when it is finite locally free ([`nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Dualizable.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.QuasicoherentSheaf.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree)).
- **Polynomial operations commute with pullback** — on schemes, symmetric and exterior powers of quasi-coherent sheaves commute with pullback along every morphism ([`isIso_pullbackSymmetricPowerComparison_app`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/SymmetricPower/Pullback/Quasicoherent.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isIso_pullbackSymmetricPowerComparison_app)). So does the determinant ([`pullbackDetIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Determinant/Pullback.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.pullbackDetIso)).

## The frontier

- **Stalks of a pulled-back module (Layer 1)** — `(f^* G)_y ≅ 𝒪_{Y,y} ⊗ G_{f(y)}` for a morphism of ringed spaces. Layer 1's monoidal statement, Layer 2's exactness and flat-Hom statements, and Layer 3's power comparisons all route through it.
- **Strong monoidal pullback on all module sheaves (Layer 1)** — invertibility of the oplax comparison `δ` with no quasi-coherence hypothesis, checked on stalks, with identity and composition coherences.
- **Flat pullback into arbitrary targets (Layer 2)** — exactness on all module sheaves, finite presentation of pullback for ringed spaces, and flat base change of internal Hom into a non-quasi-coherent target. The scheme route via local presentations needs only the exactness statement.
- **Exterior multiplication and the determinant of an extension (Layer 3)** — sheafify the wedge product, then build `det E′ ⊗ det E″ ≅ det E` with the subsheaf first, the split case with its Koszul sign `(−1)^(rank E · rank F)`, and the Picard-group identity. The graded-line comparison of §3.3 additionally waits on PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras §5.1.
- **Weight grading and recognition of linear schemes (Layer 4)** — grade the functions of an affine scheme with an `𝔸¹_X`-action by weight, and show that module schemes whose weight-graded algebra is free in degree one are exactly the linear schemes.
