### Named results

- **The relative-Spec anti-equivalence** — relative Spec and `p_* 𝒪_V` are mutually inverse between quasi-coherent commutative algebras on `X` and affine `X`-schemes, with both triangle identities ([`relativeSpecEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecEquiv)).
- **Base change of relative Spec** — for any `f : Y → X`, `Spec_Y(f^* A) ≅ Y ×_X Spec_X A` naturally in `A` ([`relativeSpecBaseChangeIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecBaseChangeIso)), compatibly with identities and composites ([`relativeSpecPullbackIso_comp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecPullbackIso_comp)).
- **`Spec_X (Sym F)` represents linear functionals** — `Hom_X(T, Spec_X Sym F) ≃ Hom(q^* F, 𝒪_T)` for every scheme `q : T → X` ([`relativeSpecSymmetricAlgebraPullbackHomEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/SymmetricAlgebra.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecSymmetricAlgebraPullbackHomEquiv)). This is the defining property of `V_lin(F)`, proved before `linearSpec` exists as a named functor.
- **Dualizable means finite locally free** — on any scheme, a quasi-coherent sheaf has a dual in `QCoh(X)` exactly when it is finite locally free ([`nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Dualizable.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.QuasicoherentSheaf.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree)), with the double-dual map an isomorphism ([`evalNatIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Bidual.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.evalNatIso)).
- **Polynomial operations commute with pullback** — symmetric and exterior powers of quasi-coherent sheaves commute with pullback along every morphism ([`isIso_pullbackSymmetricPowerComparison_app`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/SymmetricPower/Pullback/Quasicoherent.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isIso_pullbackSymmetricPowerComparison_app)), and so does the fixed-rank determinant ([`pullbackDeterminantIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Determinant/Pullback.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.pullbackDeterminantIso)), coherently with identities and composites.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The graded symmetric algebra and free graded algebras** — `Sym F = ⨁ Symⁿ F` as a graded quasi-coherent algebra, left adjoint to the degree-one functor ([`gradedSymmetricAlgebraAdjunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Quasicoherent/GradedAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/Adjunction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.gradedSymmetricAlgebraAdjunction)). It is an equivalence onto the algebras freely generated in degree one ([`gradedSymmetricAlgebraEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Quasicoherent/GradedAlgebra/Free.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.gradedSymmetricAlgebraEquiv)). This is the first factor of `QCoh(X)ᵒᵖ ≃ LinearSch(X)`.
- **Graded relative Spec** — relative Spec that keeps the grading is an anti-equivalence on graded quasi-coherent algebras ([`gradedRelativeSpecEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Graded.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.gradedRelativeSpecEquiv)). Its graded rings of sections localize on basic opens and keep finite generation and generation in degree one when passing to smaller affine opens. This is the second factor.
- **Finite locally free sheaves with rank** — an additive, rigid, pullback-stable category with locally constant rank and fixed-rank subcategories ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/FiniteLocallyFree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf)), on which L2B will be built.

## The frontier

- **`linearSpec` and `QCoh(X)ᵒᵖ ≃ LinearSch(X)` (L2A)** — compose `gradedSymmetricAlgebraEquiv` with graded relative Spec, name the functors `linearSpec` and `degreeOne`, and identify the essential image with intrinsic linear schemes. Both factors are now in place.
- **Module-scheme structure on `V_lin(F)` (L2A)** — zero, addition and scalar multiplication, their affine formulas, and the recognition theorem matching graded and module-structure-preserving morphisms.
- **Flat base change of internal Hom (L0B)** — glue the affine-base case to `f^* Hom(E, F) ≅ Hom(f^* E, f^* F)` for finitely presented `E`, flat `f` and any base. The chart-to-global criterion used for powers ([`isIso_pullbackComparison_app_of_isQuasicoherent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isIso_pullbackComparison_app_of_isQuasicoherent)) looks like the natural tool.
- **Determinant in arbitrary rank (L0C)** — a determinant line for locally constant rank, functorial on the core of all finite locally free sheaves.
- **Geometric vector bundles (L2B)** — `totalSpace` as `linearSpec(E^∨)`, `FinLocFree(X) ≃ GeomVB(X)` and the sheaf–geometry dictionary. This waits on the L2A milestone.
