- **Exterior powers of vector bundles** — the `n`-th exterior power of a finite locally free sheaf of rank `r` at `x` is finite locally free of rank `r choose n` there ([`rank_exteriorPower_apply`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/ExteriorPower.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.rank_exteriorPower_apply)). ### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Finite locally free sheaves with rank** — an additive, symmetric monoidal, pullback-stable category whose rank is locally constant with clopen loci ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/FiniteLocallyFree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf)). Its fixed-rank subcategories ([`FixedRank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/FixedRank.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.FixedRank)) are where the determinant functor will live, and in rank one they recover `InvertibleSheaf X`.
- **Relative Spec and its coordinate algebra** — `Spec_X(A)` is glued from affine charts and packaged as a contravariant functor to affine `X`-schemes ([`relativeSpec`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Functor.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpec)). In the other direction, [`affineFunctions`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/AffineFunctions.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.affineFunctions) sends `p : V → X` to the actual `p_* 𝒪_V`. Over an affine base the first is naturally `Spec` of global sections. Together these are the two named functors that L1B needs.
- **Pullback from an affine base is symmetric monoidal** — on quasi-coherent sheaves ([`pullbackFromAffineBraided`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback/Affine.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.pullbackFromAffineBraided)). This is the affine-local case of L0A's monoidal pullback.

### Roadmap coverage

L0A is partial. The closed symmetric monoidal structure, the restriction formulas and the internal-Hom stalk isomorphism for finitely presented sources are proved. Module pullback is strong symmetric monoidal only from an affine base. L0B is partial. Rank, fixed rank, rigidity, the double dual and the affine comparison are proved, and quasi-coherent sheaves are now known to be closed under kernels. Missing are the converse of the milestone on non-affine schemes, quasi-coherence of internal Hom, and its base-change comparison. L0C is partial: symmetric and exterior powers of sheaves exist, and exterior powers of finite locally free sheaves are finite locally free with the binomial rank. Missing are the analogous result for symmetric powers, the determinant, and every pullback comparison. L1A is partial: both functors and affine normalization exist, but the functor-of-points property, recovery of `A` and base change do not. L1B, L2A and L2B are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Universal property of relative Spec (L1A)** — `Hom_X(T, Spec_X A) ≃ Hom(A, f_* 𝒪_T)`, natural in `T` and `A`, and the isomorphism `affineFunctions(Spec_X A) ≅ A`. With both functors already in place, these lead directly to L1B's unit, counit and anti-equivalence.
- **Dualizable implies finite locally free (L0B)** — extend the affine theorem to every scheme by restricting a dual to affine opens. This needs restriction to an open to preserve dualizability.
- **Strong symmetric monoidal pullback (L0A)** — glue the affine-base statement into one for a general morphism, with identity and composition coherence. L0C's pullback comparisons and L2B's dictionary depend on it.
- **Determinant and symmetric powers of vector bundles (L0C)** — finite local freeness and the rank formula for symmetric powers, then the top exterior power as a functor from `FixedRank r` to `InvertibleSheaf X`.
- **Internal Hom in `QCoh(X)` (L0B)** — quasi-coherence of `Hom(E, F)` for finitely presented `E`, and `f^* Hom(E, F) → Hom(f^* E, f^* F)` with its flat and finite-locally-free isomorphism criteria.
