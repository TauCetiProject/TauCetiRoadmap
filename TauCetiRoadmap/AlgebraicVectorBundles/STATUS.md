## The frontier

- **Dualizable implies finite locally free (L0B)** — extend the affine theorem to every scheme by restricting a dual to affine opens. This needs dualizability to be preserved by restriction to an open, which the affine pullback result covers only when the target is affine.
- **Universal property of relative Spec (L1A)** — `Hom_X(T, Spec_X A) ≃ Hom(A, f_* 𝒪_T)` naturally in `T` and `A`, then `affineFunctions` and recovery of `A` as the pushforward of the structure sheaf. After that, base change, and then L1B's anti-equivalence.
- **Strong symmetric monoidal pullback (L0A)** — glue the affine-base statement to a general morphism, with identity and composition coherence. L0C's pullback comparisons and L2B's dictionary depend on it.
- **Internal Hom in `QCoh(X)` (L0B)** — quasi-coherence of `Hom(E, F)` for finitely presented `E`, and `f^* Hom(E, F) → Hom(f^* E, f^* F)` with its flat and finite-locally-free isomorphism criteria.
- **Symmetric and exterior powers, determinant (L0C)** — not begun. The fixed-rank subcategories and the rank-one equivalence are ready to receive the determinant functor.
