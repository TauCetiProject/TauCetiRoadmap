## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The sheaf-theoretic foundations are nearly complete. L0A is done, L0B's duality milestone holds on every scheme, and the relative-Spec anti-equivalence of L1B is proved. Pullback comparisons for polynomial operations on general schemes and pseudofunctoriality of relative Spec remain open. ### Named results

- **The relative-Spec anti-equivalence** — relative Spec and `p_* 𝒪_V` are mutually inverse between quasi-coherent commutative algebras on `X` and affine `X`-schemes, with both triangle identities ([`relativeSpecEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecEquiv)).
- **The universal property of relative Spec** — `Hom_X(T, Spec_X A) ≃ Hom(A, q_* 𝒪_T)` for every scheme `T` over `X`, natural in both ([`relativeSpecHomIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/UniversalProperty.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.relativeSpecHomIso)), and equivalently in pullback form `Hom(q^* A, 𝒪_T)`.
- **Dualizable means finite locally free** — on any scheme, a quasi-coherent sheaf has a dual in `QCoh(X)` exactly when it is finite locally free ([`nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Dualizable.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.QuasicoherentSheaf.nonempty_hasLeftDual_iff_isFiniteLocallyFree)). Together with rigidity and the double-dual isomorphism ([`evalNatIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Dual/Bidual.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.evalNatIso)), this is L0B's milestone.
- **The closed symmetric monoidal category of sheaves of modules** — tensor product and internal Hom are the sheafifications of their presheaf counterparts ([`TauCeti.SheafOfModules.monoidalClosed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/Closed.html#TauCeti.SheafOfModules.monoidalClosed)). Pullback of quasi-coherent sheaves is strong symmetric monoidal along every morphism ([`pullbackBraided`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Pullback/Monoidal/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.QuasicoherentSheaf.pullbackBraided)), with monoidal identity and composition isomorphisms.
- **Symmetric and exterior powers of vector bundles** — both are finite locally free, with ranks the number of degree-`n` monomials ([`rank_symmetricPower_apply`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/SymmetricPower/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.rank_symmetricPower_apply)) and `r choose n` respectively.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The determinant functor** — the top exterior power, as a functor from rank-`r` bundles to invertible sheaves ([`determinant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Determinant.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.determinant)). It is computed on free sheaves and in ranks zero and one, but it has no pullback comparison yet.
- **Graded quasi-coherent algebras and graded relative Spec** — nonnegatively graded algebras with quasi-coherent components ([`GradedQuasicoherentAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Quasicoherent/GradedAlgebra/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.GradedQuasicoherentAlgebra)). Their affine graded rings of sections localize on basic opens, and relative Spec that keeps the grading is an anti-equivalence ([`gradedRelativeSpecEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeSpec/Graded.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.gradedRelativeSpecEquiv)). This is the frame into which `linearSpec` will fit.
- **Finite locally free sheaves with rank** — an additive, rigid, pullback-stable category with locally constant rank and fixed-rank subcategories ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/FiniteLocallyFree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf)). Duals now commute with pullback, and so does internal Hom out of a finite locally free sheaf.

## The frontier

- **Pullback of powers on general schemes (L0C)** — glue the affine and free cases into isomorphisms `f^* Symⁿ E ≅ Symⁿ f^* E` and `f^* ⋀ⁿ E ≅ ⋀ⁿ f^* E` for every morphism, then deduce one for the determinant. This closes the L0C milestone.
- **Graded symmetric algebra and `linearSpec` (L2A)** — build `Sym^gr(F)` as a graded quasi-coherent algebra and the subcategory freely generated in degree one. Then `degreeOne` and `linearSpec` give `QCoh(X)ᵒᵖ ≃ LinearSch(X)` through the graded anti-equivalence.
- **Base change of relative Spec (L1A, L1B)** — `Spec_Y(f^* A) ≅ Y ×_X Spec_X A`, then pseudofunctoriality. Pullback of algebras and the pullback form of the universal property are already in place.
- **Flat base change of internal Hom (L0B)** — `f^* Hom(E, F) ≅ Hom(f^* E, f^* F)` for finitely presented `E` and flat `f`, the last missing L0B item.
- **Geometric vector bundles (L2B)** — `totalSpace` as `linearSpec(E^∨)` and `FinLocFree(X) ≃ GeomVB(X)`. This waits on the L2A milestone.
