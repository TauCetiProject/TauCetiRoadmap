## SymplecticFieldTheory: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`80b20e7` to `7ca783d`)

The first piece of this roadmap's Layer 0 algebra has landed: the free supercommutative algebra on an integer-graded module, [`SignedSym`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Basic.html#TauCeti.SignedSym). It is the tensor algebra modulo the Koszul relation xy = (−1)^{pq} yx on homogeneous generators. It will carry the contact differential once orbits and counts exist. Its generators [supercommute with the Koszul sign](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Basic.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.generator_mul_generator), and it has the universal property as an [equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Basic.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.liftEquiv) between sign-compatible linear maps and algebra maps. It also comes with functoriality in degree-preserving maps and an augmentation, which shows the coefficient ring embeds (TauCeti#28580).

The algebra is now [internally ℤ-graded by input degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Grading.html#TauCeti.instGradedAlgebraIntSignedSymSignedSymPiece), with a [graded universal extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Grading.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.gradedLift) that is unique among graded maps (TauCeti#30600). The route is the one the roadmap prescribes. First the tensor algebra gets its own [input-degree grading](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorAlgebra/Grading.html#TauCeti.instGradedAlgebraIntTensorAlgebraTensorAlgebraPiece) induced from a grading of the generators. Then the Koszul relation ideal is shown to be homogeneous, using a general fact that the kernel of a [relation quotient is spanned by the differences of related elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/RingQuot.html#RingQuot.ker_mkRingHom_eq_span).

Still missing from Layer 0: the comparison with Sym(V_even) ⊗ Λ(V_odd), the tensor-product splitting of A(V ⊕ W), the parity reduction, and the odd-derivation theorems. Nothing has landed yet on Reeb orbit multiplicities, orientation lines or good and bad orbits.

## SymplecticFieldTheory: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The algebraic half of Layer 0 is now essentially complete, and the headline is the pair of odd-derivation theorems the roadmap counts among its three Lean-statable results. A Koszul-odd assignment on generators [extends uniquely](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Derivation.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.existsUnique_odd_extension) to a map of the orbit algebra A(V) obeying the signed Leibniz rule, and such a map [squares to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Derivation.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.comp_self_eq_zero_iff) exactly when its square vanishes on generators (TauCeti#33610). For integer gradings this becomes a [criterion for a differential graded algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Derivation.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.isDGAlgebra_liftDerivation_iff). This is the algebraic reduction that Layer 5's d² = 0 will use; the geometric boundary identity itself is still to come.

The structure theory of A(V) filled in around it. When 2 is invertible, the orbit algebra is [Sym(V_even) ⊗ Λ(V_odd)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Parity/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.symTensorExteriorEquiv), and so it is free when both parity parts are. For purely even or purely odd V it is simply the [symmetric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/SymmetricExterior.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.symmetricAlgebraEquiv) or [exterior algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/SymmetricExterior.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.exteriorAlgebraEquiv). The disjoint-orbit splitting identifies A(V × W) with the Koszul-signed graded tensor product A(V) ⊗ A(W): the factor inclusions [supercommute](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Prod/Basic.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.inl_mul_inr), and factor differentials assemble into a [product differential](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/Prod/Differential.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.isDGAlgebra_prodDifferential). Integer gradings acquired a [parity reduction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/GradedModule/Coarsen.html#TauCeti.InternalGrading.parity), which identifies the algebra built from parities alone with the integer-graded one. Supercommutativity and the Leibniz rule pass down to it. Scalar extension [commutes with the construction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.baseChangeEquiv), even in characteristic two, and there is now a [word-length grading](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SignedSymmetricAlgebra/WordLength.html#TauCeti.SignedSym.instGradedAlgebraNatLengthPiece).

Nothing landed on the geometric half of Layer 0. Reeb orbits still lack multiplicities and markers, finiteness below an action bound, and the orientation lines that separate good orbits from bad. Layers 1–9 have not started.
