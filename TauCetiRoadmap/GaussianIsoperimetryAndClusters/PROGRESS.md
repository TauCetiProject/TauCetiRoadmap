## GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`35af95d` to `58174b5`)

The first stretch of this roadmap put in place the one-dimensional Gaussian facts and most of weighted perimeter theory. The Gaussian isoperimetric function [I = φ ∘ Φ⁻¹](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/IsoperimetricProfile.html#TauCeti.Probability.gaussianIsoperimetricProfile) is defined with its full calculus: it is symmetric about 1/2, peaks only there, satisfies [I″ = −1/I](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/IsoperimetricProfile.html#TauCeti.Probability.hasDerivAt_deriv_gaussianIsoperimetricProfile) and is [strictly concave](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/IsoperimetricProfile.html#TauCeti.Probability.strictConcaveOn_gaussianIsoperimetricProfile) (TauCeti#43577). Alongside it come [Mills' ratio bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Cdf.html#TauCeti.Probability.one_sub_cdf_gaussianReal_lt_gaussianPDFReal_div) 1 − Φ(r) < φ(r)/r, the sharpened tail bound 1 − Φ(r) ≤ e^{−r²/2}/2, and the [orthogonal splitting](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/OrthogonalSplitting.html#Submodule.map_add_prod_stdGaussian) of the standard Gaussian as a product over a subspace and its complement.

For a positive C¹ density e^{−W}, [weighted variation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/Basic.html#TauCeti.weightedVariation) is defined by duality with test fields (TauCeti#43588) and identified with the integral of e^{−W} against the Euclidean variation measure. For sets of locally finite perimeter, [weighted perimeter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.weightedPerimeter_eq_lintegral_reducedBoundary) is shown to be the weighted Hausdorff measure of the reduced boundary. Further results are the [weighted Gauss–Green formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/GaussGreen.html#TauCeti.integral_weightedDivergence_eq_integral_reducedBoundary), also for bounded fields, the [weighted coarea formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/Coarea.html#TauCeti.weightedVariation_eq_lintegral_weightedPerimeter), [compactness in L¹(μ)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/Compactness.html#TauCeti.exists_subseq_tendsto_withDensity_symmDiff_of_weightedPerimeter_le) for bounded perimeter, and invariance under linear isometries. A half-space at distance c from the origin is shown to have [Gaussian perimeter φ(c)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/HalfSpace.html#TauCeti.gaussianPerimeter_setOf_inner_lt), and the [Gaussian perimeter of a centred ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/Frontier.html#TauCeti.gaussianPerimeter_ball) is computed. The reduced-boundary and Gauss–Green statements assume dimension at least 2, so the line case is still open. Lower semicontinuity, the perimeter of convex sets, cylinders and the translation derivative have not landed.

[Clusters](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/Cluster.html#TauCeti.Cluster) also arrived, with their total perimeter, equivalence up to null sets, permutation, isometric transport and the fact that merging cells does not increase perimeter. Interfaces, isoperimetric profiles and existence of minimizers are still to come.
