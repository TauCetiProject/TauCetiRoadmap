## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Fixed complex tori are done, and so is the algebra of cyclic affine actions apart from base change in families. Holomorphic families from varying periods are built as manifolds, with covering, submersion, trivialization and marking. Equivariant descent and multiple fibres are partial: the actions, their freeness, the cyclic quotient and its local equation `t = u^m` are in place, but monodromy and the normal and canonical characters are not. Logarithmic transforms have only their first steps, the covering identification and the cyclic normalization.

### Named results

- **Homology of a complex torus** — for a full lattice `L`, the integral homology of `E/L` is the exterior algebra on `L`, naturally in maps induced by lattice-preserving linear maps, and `π₁` and `H₁` are `L` through period displacement ([`orbitRelQuotientHomologyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/ZLattice.html#Submodule.orbitRelQuotientHomologyEquiv)).
- **Kernels of homomorphisms of tori** — the kernel `f⁻¹(L')/L` of an induced homomorphism is an embedded Lie subgroup whose identity component is the torus `ker f/(L ∩ ker f)` ([`isSmoothEmbedding_subtypeVal_ker_orbitRelQuotientMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Algebra/Quotient.html#Submodule.isSmoothEmbedding_subtypeVal_ker_orbitRelQuotientMap)). Its component group is `(L' ∩ range f)/f(L)` ([`orbitRelQuotientComapQuotientEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Submodule/OrbitRelQuotient.html#Submodule.orbitRelQuotientComapQuotientEquiv)).
- **The fixed-point criterion for affine maps** — the `k`-th iterate of `x ↦ A x + t` on a torus has a fixed point exactly when the partial norm `N_{A,k}(t)` lies in the image of `1 - A^k` ([`exists_affineEquiv_iterate_apply_eq_self_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Cyclic/Affine.html#AddEquiv.exists_affineEquiv_iterate_apply_eq_self_iff)). This decides freeness for every nonidentity power, and an [exact-order criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Cyclic/Affine.html#AddEquiv.orderOf_affineEquiv_eq_iff) is proved separately.
- **The integral connecting isomorphism** — for `0 → Λ → E → T → 0`, the connecting map sends the degree-one class of `t` to the class of the norm of a lift, and is an isomorphism `H¹(C_m,T) ≃ H²(C_m,Λ)` ([`cyclicConnectingEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.cyclicConnectingEquiv)).
- **Multiplicity of the central fibre** — for a free cyclic affine action over the rotated disc, the quotient map `t = z^m` reads as `u^m` in every chart, so the central fibre has multiplicity exactly `m` ([`analyticOrderAt_torusFamilyAffineQuotientLift_rootsOfUnityBall`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/RotatedDisc.html#TauCeti.analyticOrderAt_torusFamilyAffineQuotientLift_rootsOfUnityBall)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The manifold structure on a torus family** ([`isManifold_torusFamily`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isManifold_torusFamily)) puts the quotient atlas on `(Y × E)/Λ`. The orbit map is a covering and local diffeomorphism, and the projection is a [submersion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isSubmersionOfComplement_torusFamilyProjection). With holomorphic periods these statements are holomorphic.
- **The affine action on a torus family** ([`torusFamilyAffineMulAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/AffineAction.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyAffineMulAction)) takes a linear cocycle and torus-valued translation sections. Its [freeness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Stabilizer.html#TauCeti.isCancelSMul_torusFamilyAffineMulAction_iff) reduces to the fixed-point criterion on fibres over base stabilizers, so it handles elliptic base points that the linear constructor cannot.
- **The canonical marking** ([`torusFamilyMarking`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/Family.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyMarking)) identifies `H₁` of each fibre with `Λ` by period displacement. It is compatible with the fibre identification and has [trivial monodromy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/Family.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyMarking_loopClass_eq_of_homotopy).

### Roadmap coverage

Milestone 1 is done: the compact connected Lie-group quotient, induced homomorphisms and their kernels, and the covering, `π₁` and homology. Milestone 2 is done except the description of changing an arbitrary marking by `Aut(Λ)` and the regression test that a varying elliptic family is not isotrivial. Milestone 4 is done except naturality of `δ` under base change in a varying family. Milestone 3 is partial: both constructors, proper discontinuity, freeness and submersion charts over a free base are proved, but the monodromy formula, descended trivializations and naturality are not. Milestone 5 has items 1 and 2 and the local equation and multiplicity of item 3. The hypersurface structure of the central fibre and the normal and canonical characters remain. Milestone 6 has the covering identification of item 1 and most of the cyclic normalization of item 2; the logarithmic gauge, regluing, independence and `π₁`/`H₁` computations have not begun.

## The frontier

- **Normal and canonical characters of the multiple fibre.** Show that the central fibre is an embedded hypersurface biholomorphic to `T_0/C_m`, identify the normal character as `ζ`, and prove adjunction. This needs ComplexManifolds Milestone 7.6.
- **Monodromy of the descended family.** Prove that transport on the marking along a lift from `y` to `g • y` is `ρ(g⁻¹)`, then derive the action on homology and the base-change and reversal laws.
- **The logarithmic gauge.** Build the downstairs gauge `G` on a logarithm chart for normalized pure-translation data. Prove independence of root and branch, and identify `λ mod mΛ` with `H¹(C_m,T_0)`.
- **Family base change for the connecting class.** This is the last piece of Milestone 4: fibrewise cohomology comparisons along a pullback of the lattice, vector-space and torus local systems.
- **Regluing and its topology.** Glue `Q` into the ambient manifold through `G⁻¹`, prove independence of the choices, and compute the induced `π₁` and `H₁` maps. This waits on the gauge and on the ComplexManifolds and GeometricTopology gluing APIs.
