## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The cohomological half of the cyclic affine algebra is largely in place, up to the integral connecting isomorphism. The torus family exists as a topological space, with its projection, fibres and a global trivialization. No complex torus has been built as a complex manifold yet, fixed or varying, and the affine maps themselves are still missing. Descent, multiple fibres and logarithmic transforms have not begun.

### Named results

- **The integral connecting class** — for the equivariant sequence `0 → Λ → E → T → 0`, the connecting homomorphism sends the degree-one class of a norm-zero `t` to the class of the norm of any lift of `t`, so that `v/m` goes to `[v]` ([`δ_cyclicRepShortComplex_groupCohomologyπOdd_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.δ_cyclicRepShortComplex_groupCohomologyπOdd_one)).
- **The connecting isomorphism `H¹(C_m,T) ≃ H²(C_m,Λ)`** — when multiplication by `m` is bijective on `E` (as for a real vector space) the connecting map is an isomorphism ([`cyclicConnectingEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.cyclicConnectingEquiv)). Its naturality under morphisms of sequences is also proved ([`map_δ_cyclicRepShortComplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.map_δ_cyclicRepShortComplex)).
- **Degree-one cohomology of a cyclic automorphism** — odd-degree classes are the norm-zero elements modulo the image of `1 - A`, which are the affine twists up to translation conjugacy ([`groupCohomologyπOdd_cyclicRep_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#AddEquiv.groupCohomologyπOdd_cyclicRep_eq_iff)). For the trivial action this group is the `m`-torsion `T[m]` ([`groupCohomologyOddEquivTorsionBy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.groupCohomologyOddEquivTorsionBy)).
- **Uniform period separation on compact sets** — on a compact set of parameters, one positive constant bounds every nonzero period of a continuous family of full lattices from below; in a chosen basis, this bounds the inverse period maps uniformly ([`exists_pos_mul_norm_le_norm_sum_smul_period_of_isCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Basic.html#TauCeti.exists_pos_mul_norm_le_norm_sum_smul_period_of_isCompact)). Hence the period translation action is free and properly discontinuous.
- **The global topological trivialization** — after a lattice basis is chosen, the family quotient `(Y × E)/Λ` is homeomorphic over `Y` to `Y × ℝ^d/ℤ^d` ([`torusFamilyHomeomorphProd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/HomeomorphProd.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyHomeomorphProd)). This trivialization is real-linear on fibres, not holomorphic.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The cyclic representation** ([`cyclicRep`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Cyclic/Automorphism.html#AddEquiv.cyclicRep)) packages an automorphism of order dividing `m` as an object of `Rep ℤ C_m`. Intertwining maps induce morphisms of these objects, which makes the short exact sequence and its naturality expressible.
- **The family projection** ([`torusFamilyProjection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Basic.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyProjection)) descends the first projection to the orbit quotient. It is continuous, open and surjective, and its fibres are [homeomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Fiber.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyFiberHomeomorph) to the pointwise quotients `E/Π(y)`.
- **The period basis** ([`periodBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Basic.html#TauCeti.periodBasis)) turns a lattice basis into a real frame of `E`. Both the frame and its inverse are proved to depend smoothly on the parameter, which is the input to the smooth trivialization.

## The frontier

- **Complex structure on the family quotient.** Apply the complex-manifolds quotient theorem to the period action., making the orbit map a covering and local biholomorphism. The existing trivialization and fibre homeomorphisms then need smooth and biholomorphic upgrades. This depends on the complex-manifolds roadmap's quotient theorem.
- **Affine iterate and order criteria.** Define `x ↦ A x + t` and the partial norms `N_{A,k}`. Then prove the iterate formula, the norm-sum criterion for order dividing `m`, and the separate exact-order criterion. This is purely algebraic and unblocked.
- **Fixed points of every nonidentity power.** Prove that the `k`-th power has a fixed point exactly when `N_{A,k}(t)` lies in the range of `1 - A^k`, and deduce the freeness criterion. Milestones 3 and 5 depend on this.
- **Fixed complex tori.** Milestone 1 needs the Lie-group structure on the quotient, compactness, and induced homomorphisms with their kernels; much could follow from the family construction over a point.
- **Remaining naturality and period-two comparisons.** Milestone 4 still needs naturality for products and for base change in families, and the period-two identities `cyclicNormHom σ 2 = id + σ` and `oneSubPow σ 1 = id − σ` that a sister roadmap consumes.
