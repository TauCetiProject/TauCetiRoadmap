## ComplexTori: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`7bf2270` to `273ea28`)

The first work on complex tori has landed, and it comes from both ends of the roadmap. For families of tori, the key estimate is now proved: **uniform period separation on compact sets**. Take a continuous family of full period lattices. On any compact set of parameters, a single positive constant bounds the norm of every nonzero period from below ([`exists_pos_le_norm_period_of_isCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family.html#TauCeti.exists_pos_le_norm_period_of_isCompact)). Once a lattice basis is chosen, the same estimate becomes a uniform bound for the inverse period maps. It follows that the period translation action of the lattice on the product of base and vector space is free and [properly discontinuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family.html#TauCeti.properlyDiscontinuousVAdd_periodAddAction) (TauCeti#18905). That is the first step of Milestone 2. The orbit quotient still has no complex structure, and there is no family projection or fibre identification yet.

On the algebraic side, an additive automorphism of order dividing `m` now gives an integral representation of the cyclic group of order `m`, the [cyclic representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Cyclic/Automorphism.html#AddEquiv.cyclicRepresentation), whose norm is the ordered sum of powers. Its cohomology is read off concretely in two cases. In odd degree, a [class is zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#AddEquiv.groupCohomologyπOdd_cyclicRep_eq_zero_iff) exactly when it has the form `b - A b`. These classes are the twists of affine maps modulo translation conjugacy. In positive even degree, [two fixed points share a class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#AddEquiv.groupCohomologyπEven_cyclicRep_eq_iff) exactly when they differ by a norm (TauCeti#19061). This settles the cohomological half of Milestone 4's third step. The affine maps themselves are still missing: there is no iterate formula and no fixed-point criterion, and the connecting homomorphism has not been started.

## ComplexTori: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

The headline is Milestone 4's **integral connecting class**. Take the equivariant exact sequence `0 → Λ → E → T → 0` of cyclic representations. For a norm-zero `t` with lift `t̃`, the [connecting homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.δ_cyclicRepShortComplex_groupCohomologyπOdd_one) sends the class of `t` to the class of the norm of `t̃`, and in particular sends `v/m` to `[v]`. When multiplication by `m` is bijective on `E`, as for a real vector space, this map is an [isomorphism `H¹(C_m,T) ≃ H²(C_m,Λ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.cyclicConnectingEquiv) (TauCeti#19414). The proof does not use a periodic complex: it rests on a general vanishing theorem, that a finite group has no [positive-degree cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/Corestriction.html#groupCohomology.subsingleton_of_bijective_nsmul) when multiplication by its order is bijective on the coefficients. Later work proved [naturality of the connecting map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.map_δ_cyclicRepShortComplex) under morphisms of sequences, and change of group for degree-one classes. For trivial actions, it identified odd cohomology with the [`m`-torsion `T[m]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/CyclicAutomorphism.html#TauCeti.groupCohomologyOddEquivTorsionBy) and showed that the snake isomorphism `T[m] ≃ Λ/mΛ` is the connecting isomorphism. The affine maps themselves are still missing.

The torus family gained its topology. Its projection to the base is now defined, and it is continuous, open and surjective. Each literal fibre is [homeomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Fiber.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyFiberHomeomorph) to `E` modulo the periods at that point. Choosing a lattice basis gives a [global trivialization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/HomeomorphProd.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyHomeomorphProd), a homeomorphism over the base onto the product with the fixed torus `ℝ^d/ℤ^d`. The period frame and its inverse depend smoothly on the parameter. All of this is topological so far: the quotient still has no complex or smooth structure.

## ComplexTori: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-08 (`93d1fe5` to `d2083f0`)

The torus family is now a manifold. For a smooth or holomorphic family of full period lattices, the quotient `(Y × E)/Λ` [is a manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isManifold_torusFamily), the orbit map is a [local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isLocalDiffeomorph_torusFamily_mk) and a covering, and the projection to the base is a [submersion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isSubmersionOfComplement_torusFamilyProjection). The global trivialization is now a [diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/DiffeomorphProd.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyDiffeomorphProd), independent of the lattice basis up to the integral change of coordinates. Each fibre has its [canonical marking](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/Family.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyMarking) `H₁(T_y) ≅ Λ`, given by period displacement and with trivial monodromy. For a single torus `E/L` there is now the [homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/ZLattice.html#Submodule.orbitRelQuotientHomologyEquiv) as the exterior algebra on `L`, natural in induced maps. The kernel of an induced homomorphism of tori is an [embedded Lie subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Algebra/Quotient.html#Submodule.isSmoothEmbedding_subtypeVal_ker_orbitRelQuotientMap), with the expected identity component and component group.

The affine side of Milestone 4 arrived as well. There is the iterate formula, the [exact-order criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Cyclic/Affine.html#AddEquiv.orderOf_affineEquiv_eq_iff), and the [fixed-point criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Cyclic/Affine.html#AddEquiv.exists_affineEquiv_iterate_apply_eq_self_iff) for every power. There are also the period-two comparisons and naturality for products and for changes of cyclic group. These feed the linear and [affine actions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/AffineAction.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyAffineMulAction) on torus families. Here freeness is decided [fibrewise at base stabilizers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/Stabilizer.html#TauCeti.isCancelSMul_torusFamilyAffineMulAction_iff), and the descended family is a submersion over a free base.

Multiple fibres have begun. Over the rotated disc, a free cyclic affine action has [exact order `m`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/RotatedDisc.html#TauCeti.orderOf_toPerm_torusFamilyAffineMulAction_rootsOfUnityBall). The quotient's map `t = z^m` is a submersion off the central fibre and has the [local form `t = u^m`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/RotatedDisc.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyAffineQuotientLift_rootsOfUnityBall_symm_eq_pow). Hence the central fibre has [multiplicity `m`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/RotatedDisc.html#TauCeti.analyticOrderAt_torusFamilyAffineQuotientLift_rootsOfUnityBall) (TauCeti#29382). The upstairs family is [recovered as a pullback](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Family/QuotientPullback.html#TauCeti.torusFamilyAffineQuotientPullbackDiffeomorph) of the quotient, and cyclic averaging normalizes a translation cocycle to `Π(z^m)λ/m`. The normal and canonical characters, the logarithmic gauge and the regluing are still missing.
