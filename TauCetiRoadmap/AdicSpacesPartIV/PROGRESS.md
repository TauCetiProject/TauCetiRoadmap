## AdicSpacesPartIV: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`b9268d5` to `b4b3ce5`)

Dagger geometry has begun. The Monsky–Washnitzer algebra [`washnitzerAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WashnitzerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra) of overconvergent power series, the series restricted at some radius strictly above 1, is now defined as a subalgebra of the Tate algebra, together with its basic characterisations: by coefficient bounds, by radii in the divisible hull of the value group, and as a proper dense subring for at least one variable. Its headline is the [unit criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WashnitzerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.isUnit_washnitzerAlgebra_iff): an overconvergent series is invertible exactly when its constant term strictly dominates every other coefficient, and an [overconvergent series invertible in the Tate algebra already has an overconvergent inverse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WashnitzerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.isUnit_toTate_iff) (TauCeti#19077).

In characteristic zero, [termwise primitives of overconvergent series are overconvergent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WashnitzerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.primitive_mem_washnitzerAlgebra). This rests on a Gauss-norm bound showing that [integration costs only an arbitrarily small amount of radius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/Primitive/GaussNorm.html#MvPowerSeries.exists_gaussNorm_primitive_le), which in turn uses the fact that inverses of integers in a characteristic-zero normed field grow at most polynomially. The derivative half of the same statement, with its Cauchy estimate for Hasse derivatives, has not landed.

The rest of the window prepares Weierstrass theory for overconvergent series. [Substitutions of unit-ball polynomials preserve overconvergence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Subst.html#TauCeti.Dagger.subst_mem_washnitzerAlgebra) and give [algebra isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Subst.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.substEquiv). From these comes the [distinguishing automorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Distinguished.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.exists_triangularEquiv_isDistinguished_isUnit): a triangular change of variables makes any nonzero overconvergent series distinguished in the last variable. A [radius-selection theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Distinguished.html#TauCeti.Dagger.exists_isDistinguished_isUnit_of_isDistinguished_one) moves that distinguishedness to unequal radii above 1. Weierstrass division and preparation in the Monsky–Washnitzer algebra itself are the next step and are not yet proved.
