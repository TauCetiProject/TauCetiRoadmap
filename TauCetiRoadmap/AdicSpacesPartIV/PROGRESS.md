## AdicSpacesPartIV: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`b9268d5` to `b4b3ce5`)

Dagger geometry has begun. The Monsky–Washnitzer algebra [`washnitzerAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WashnitzerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra) of overconvergent power series, the series restricted at some radius strictly above 1, is now defined as a subalgebra of the Tate algebra, together with its basic characterisations: by coefficient bounds, by radii in the divisible hull of the value group, and as a proper dense subring for at least one variable. Its headline is the [unit criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WashnitzerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.isUnit_washnitzerAlgebra_iff): an overconvergent series is invertible exactly when its constant term strictly dominates every other coefficient, and an [overconvergent series invertible in the Tate algebra already has an overconvergent inverse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WashnitzerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.isUnit_toTate_iff) (TauCeti#19077).

In characteristic zero, [termwise primitives of overconvergent series are overconvergent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WashnitzerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.primitive_mem_washnitzerAlgebra). This rests on a Gauss-norm bound showing that [integration costs only an arbitrarily small amount of radius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/Primitive/GaussNorm.html#MvPowerSeries.exists_gaussNorm_primitive_le), which in turn uses the fact that inverses of integers in a characteristic-zero normed field grow at most polynomially. The derivative half of the same statement, with its Cauchy estimate for Hasse derivatives, has not landed.

The rest of the window prepares Weierstrass theory for overconvergent series. [Substitutions of unit-ball polynomials preserve overconvergence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Subst.html#TauCeti.Dagger.subst_mem_washnitzerAlgebra) and give [algebra isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Subst.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.substEquiv). From these comes the [distinguishing automorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Distinguished.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.exists_triangularEquiv_isDistinguished_isUnit): a triangular change of variables makes any nonzero overconvergent series distinguished in the last variable. A [radius-selection theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Distinguished.html#TauCeti.Dagger.exists_isDistinguished_isUnit_of_isDistinguished_one) moves that distinguishedness to unequal radii above 1. Weierstrass division and preparation in the Monsky–Washnitzer algebra itself are the next step and are not yet proved.

## AdicSpacesPartIV: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-09 (`b4b3ce5` to `7ca783d`)

The Monsky–Washnitzer algebra `W_n` now has its full Weierstrass theory and the ring theory that follows from it. [Overconvergent Weierstrass division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Weierstrass.html#TauCeti.Dagger.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isDistinguished) and [preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Preparation.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.existsUnique_isUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq) hold for series distinguished at unit radius (TauCeti#20227). From them, `W_n` is [noetherian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.isNoetherianRing), [factorial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.uniqueFactorizationMonoid), Jacobson and [regular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Regular.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.isRegularRing) of [Krull dimension `n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/KrullDimension.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.ringKrullDim_eq_natCard), and the [Tate algebra is faithfully flat over it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/FaithfullyFlat.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.faithfullyFlat_toTate) with the same classical points. Partial and [Hasse derivatives preserve overconvergence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Derivative.html#TauCeti.Dagger.hasseDeriv_mem_washnitzerAlgebra), and in characteristic zero a one-form on `W_n` is [exact exactly when it is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Poincare.html#TauCeti.Dagger.washnitzerAlgebra.exists_pderiv_eq_iff).

[Dagger algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/DaggerAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Dagger.IsDaggerAlgebra), the quotients of `W_n`, came next. They are [noetherian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Dagger.IsDaggerAlgebra.isNoetherianRing) and Jacobson, satisfy Noether normalisation, and are closed under finite extensions. Their map to the affinoid completion is [faithfully flat](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/DaggerAlgebra/FaithfullyFlat.html#TauCeti.Dagger.faithfullyFlat_toCompletion), bijective on maximal ideals and an isomorphism on [completed local rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/DaggerAlgebra/FaithfullyFlat.html#TauCeti.Dagger.adicCompletionAlgEquiv), so regularity transfers both ways and normality descends. [Fringe presentations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/FringePresentation/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.FringePresentation) exist for every dagger algebra. They carry the [fringe topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/FringePresentation/Topology.html#TauCeti.Dagger.FringePresentation.fringeTopology), and finitely presented modules descend to a stage.

On the integral side, the [integral Monsky–Washnitzer algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/IntegralWashnitzer/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.intWashnitzerAlgebra) is [noetherian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/IntegralWashnitzer/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Dagger.intWashnitzerAlgebra.isNoetherianRing) when `K°` is. The [weak completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WeakCompletion/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.weakCompletion) of a finitely generated algebra is functorial and [noetherian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/WeakCompletion/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Dagger.weakCompletion.isNoetherianRing). The [universally finite differentials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Omega/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.Omega) are free on the `dXᵢ` for `W_n` and finite for every dagger algebra. Outside F1, the punctured closed disc is shown [not to be compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Polydisc/PuncturedDisc.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.not_isCompact_puncturedDisc), which an R4 check uses.
