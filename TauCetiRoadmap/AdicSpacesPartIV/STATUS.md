### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [Fringe presentations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/FringePresentation/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.FringePresentation) write a dagger algebra as a colimit of affinoid stages at radii decreasing to 1, and every dagger algebra has one.
