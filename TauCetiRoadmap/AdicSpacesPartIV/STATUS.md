### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [Fringe presentations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/FringePresentation/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.FringePresentation) write a dagger algebra as a colimit of affinoid stages at radii decreasing to 1. They define the fringe topology and let finitely presented modules descend to a stage.
- The [integral Monsky–Washnitzer algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/IntegralWashnitzer/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.intWashnitzerAlgebra) is the ring of power-bounded elements of `W_n`. Over any complete ultrametric field, its `ϖ`-adic completion is [the unit ball of the Tate algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/IntegralWashnitzer/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.intWashnitzerAlgebra.adicCompletionEquiv), with the same reductions modulo `ϖⁿ`, and extending scalars to `K` gives `W_n`.
- [Universally finite differentials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Dagger/Omega/Basic.html#TauCeti.Dagger.Omega) are free on `dX₁, …, dXₙ` for `W_n` and finite for every dagger algebra: the module from which the overconvergent de Rham complex will be built.

## The frontier

- **Automatic continuity (F1.2)**: every `K`-algebra homomorphism of dagger algebras is continuous for the residue norms. With the residue norm now in place, this is the step that makes the norm class independent of the presentation and gives finite modules their canonical topology.

- **The rest of F1.2**: excellence in characteristic zero, Bosch's approximation, and "`A` is a domain iff `Â` is", together with integral closedness of reduced `A` in `Â`.
