## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The dg nerve now exists, with its horn filling, its homotopy category and its mapping spaces, and most of the perfect-ring commutative algebra of E1.6 is proved. No layer is complete. The enhanced derived category of a ringed site has not been assembled, and stability, finality and presentability are still absent. Kan extensions (E3) and the monoidal baseline (E5:abstract) are untouched, and derived completion (E4) has only its point-site predicate.

### Named results

- **The dg nerve is a quasicategory** — every inner horn in the differential graded nerve of a dg category has a filler ([`dgNerve.quasicategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/Quasicategory.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.quasicategory)). Its homotopy category is the degree-zero cohomology category H⁰(C) ([`dgNerve.homotopyCategoryEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/HomotopyCategory.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.homotopyCategoryEquiv)).
- **K-injective complexes model the derived category** — the homotopy category of the dg nerve of K-injective complexes is the derived category, in any abelian category where every complex has a K-injective resolution ([`KInjectiveDGCategory.dgNerveHomotopyCategoryEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/CochainComplex/KInjective.html#TauCeti.KInjectiveDGCategory.dgNerveHomotopyCategoryEquiv)).
- **The Dold–Kan description of mapping spaces** — the right mapping space between two objects of the dg nerve is Dold–Kan applied to the good truncation of their Hom complex, so its homotopy groups are the Hom complex's cohomology in non-positive degrees ([`dgNerve.rightMappingSpaceIsoCochainToSSet`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/RightMappingSpace.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.rightMappingSpaceIsoCochainToSSet)).
- **Kunz's theorem** — Frobenius on a regular ring of characteristic p is flat ([`IsRegularRing.flat_frobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.IsRegularRing.flat_frobenius)).
- **Tor independence of perfect rings** — for perfect algebras B and C over a perfect ring A, `Tor_{n+1}^A(B, C) = 0`, which the roadmap uses for base change on perfect schemes without flatness ([`isZero_Tor_succ_of_perfectRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfect/Tor.html#TauCeti.isZero_Tor_succ_of_perfectRing)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [**The simplicial enrichment of a dg category**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Simplicial.html#TauCeti.DGSimplicialCategory). Its mapping objects are Dold–Kan of good truncations, and its composition uses the Alexander–Whitney lax structure. It is locally Kan. It is the input to the homotopy-coherent nerve comparison.
- [**Locally small simplicial sets**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Quasicategory/LocallySmall.html#SSet.IsLocallySmall), together with [small Kan models](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/SmallModel.html#TauCeti.HasSmallSpaceModel) of homotopy types. They control universes through homotopy type rather than raw simplex sets.
- [**Pro-zero diagrams**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Functor/ProZero.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.IsProZero) and the [product–difference map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/TowerDifference.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.towerDifference) `1 − s` of a tower. The limit of a tower is the kernel of `1 − s`, and `1 − s` is invertible on pro-zero towers. This is the ordinary form of the planned formula `Rlim = fib(1 − s)`.

## The frontier

- **The homotopy-coherent nerve comparison** (`dgNerve_coherentNerveEquivalence`). Both sides exist; what remains is the map θ from the coherent nerve of the enrichment and the proof that it is a categorical equivalence.
- **The enhanced derived category of a ringed site** (`ClassicalDerived.enhancedCategory`, `enhancedDerived`). The K-injective model of the derived category is proved under the hypothesis that K-injective resolutions exist. That hypothesis still has to be proved for module sheaves on a ringed site.
- **Stability and the shift–cone comparison** (`dgNerve_shift_cone_comparison`). This needs E0.4's stable fibre and cofibre calculus, which has not started.
- **The rest of E1.6**. After perfection, the global-dimension bound is proved only for the perfect closure of a regular ring, not for perfectly finitely presented quotients. The almost étale normalization, the `2 dim R` Tor bound with its counterexample, and the converse of Kunz are missing. For the converse, flat Frobenius already gives reducedness and [passes to the completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Flat/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.AdicCompletion.flat_frobenius), whose regularity is that of the ring; the global-dimension bound for perfect quotients is what it still needs.
- **Coherent derived towers** (`derivedTowerLimit`). The coherent version of `1 − s` needs towers in a stable quasicategory, so it waits on stability.
