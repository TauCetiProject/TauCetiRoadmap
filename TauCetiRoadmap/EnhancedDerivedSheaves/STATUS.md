## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The dg nerve exists with horn filling, its homotopy category, its mapping spaces and a criterion for equivalences. Every Grothendieck abelian category now has the injective model structure and K-injective resolutions, so the dg nerve of K-injective complexes models the derived category of module sheaves. Most of the perfect-ring algebra of E1.6 is proved. No layer is complete. The enhanced derived category has not been assembled, and stability, finality and presentability are absent. E3 and E5:abstract are untouched.

### Named results

- **The injective model structure** — for a Grothendieck abelian category, unbounded cochain complexes form a cofibrantly generated model category with quasi-isomorphisms as weak equivalences, monomorphisms as cofibrations and K-injective complexes of injectives as fibrant objects ([`modelCategoryInjective.instModelCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ModelCategory/KInjective/ModelStructure.html#CochainComplex.modelCategoryInjective.instModelCategory)). Its factorizations give [K-injective resolutions in every Grothendieck abelian category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ModelCategory/KInjective/Resolution.html#TauCeti.hasKInjectiveResolutions_of_isGrothendieckAbelian), without boundedness.
- **The dg nerve is a quasicategory** — every inner horn in the differential graded nerve has a filler ([`dgNerve.quasicategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/Quasicategory.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.quasicategory)). Its homotopy category is H⁰(C) ([`dgNerve.homotopyCategoryEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/HomotopyCategory.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.homotopyCategoryEquiv)), and an edge is an equivalence exactly when its H⁰ class is invertible ([`dgNerve.isEquivalence_edgeMk_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.isEquivalence_edgeMk_iff)).
- **K-injective complexes model the derived category** — the homotopy category of the dg nerve of K-injective complexes is the derived category ([`KInjectiveDGCategory.dgNerveHomotopyCategoryEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/CochainComplex/KInjective.html#TauCeti.KInjectiveDGCategory.dgNerveHomotopyCategoryEquiv)). In it, a map of complexes is an equivalence exactly when it is a [quasi-isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/CochainComplex/KInjective.html#TauCeti.KInjectiveDGCategory.isEquivalence_edgeMk_iff_quasiIso). This now holds for every Grothendieck abelian category.
- **The Dold–Kan description of mapping spaces** — the right mapping space of the dg nerve is Dold–Kan applied to the good truncation of the Hom complex ([`dgNerve.rightMappingSpaceIsoCochainToSSet`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/RightMappingSpace.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.rightMappingSpaceIsoCochainToSSet)), so its homotopy groups are the Hom complex's [negative cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/HomotopyGroups.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.rightMappingSpacePiAddEquivHomology).
- **Kunz's theorem** — Frobenius on a regular ring of characteristic p is flat ([`IsRegularRing.flat_frobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.IsRegularRing.flat_frobenius)). Alongside it are [Tor independence of perfect rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfect/Tor.html#TauCeti.isZero_Tor_succ_of_perfectRing) and [descent of regularity along faithfully flat maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Descent.html#TauCeti.IsRegularRing.of_faithfullyFlat).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [**The simplicial enrichment of a dg category**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Simplicial.html#TauCeti.DGSimplicialCategory) is built from the lax monoidal Dold–Kan functor and is locally Kan. It is the input to the homotopy-coherent nerve comparison.
- [**Terminal vertices of the dg nerve**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/TerminalVertex.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.isTerminalVertex_iff) are the objects whose incoming Hom complexes are exact in non-positive degrees. Together with the matching initial-vertex criterion, this makes the zero complex [a zero object](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/Zero.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.isInitialVertex_cochainComplex_of_isZero), the first ingredient of stability.
- [**Weak homotopy equivalences of simplicial sets**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/WeakHomotopyEquivalence.html#SSet.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence) are detected by homotopy classes of maps into Kan complexes, and weak contractibility is the case of the map to a point. This is the notion E0.4's finality is built on.

## The frontier

- **The enhanced derived category** (`enhancedDerived`, `ClassicalDerived.enhancedCategory`). K-injective resolutions of module sheaves are no longer a hypothesis, so the remaining work is to package the dg nerve of K-injective sheaf complexes as the enhancement, with its mapping-space formula and detection of equivalences. Stability is still needed for `enhancedDerived.isStable`.
- **Stability and the shift–cone comparison** (`dgNerve_shift_cone_comparison`). The zero object exists. What remains is fibres and cofibres through mapping cones, the suspension–shift identification with Mathlib's signs, and E0.4's stable fibre calculus.
- **The homotopy-coherent nerve comparison** (`dgNerve_coherentNerveEquivalence`). Both sides exist. What remains is the map θ and the proof that it is a categorical equivalence.
- **Finality** (`IsFinal`). Weak homotopy equivalences and weak contractibility are in place. Final maps, Theorem A and the colimit characterization are not.
- **The converse of Kunz's theorem** (`isRegular_of_frobenius_flat`). Regularity now descends along faithfully flat maps, and flat Frobenius passes to completions. The proof still needs the global-dimension bound for perfectly finitely presented quotients.
