## EnhancedDerivedSheaves: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-23 (`b8a93e2` to `ddc3f97`)

The first work on this roadmap gives the Dold–Kan correspondence its monoidal structure, which the planned simplicial enrichment of a dg category needs. The Alexander–Whitney map is now proved coassociative ([`alexanderWhitney_coassoc`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialObject/AlexanderWhitney.html#CategoryTheory.SimplicialObject.alexanderWhitney_coassoc)), making the alternating face map complex an [oplax monoidal functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialObject/AlexanderWhitney.html#CategoryTheory.SimplicialObject.alternatingFaceMapComplexOplaxMonoidal). It restricts to [normalized chains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DoldKan/Monoidal.html#CategoryTheory.Idempotents.DoldKan.oplaxMonoidalN), and transporting it along the equivalence makes [the inverse Dold–Kan functor Γ lax monoidal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DoldKan/Monoidal.html#CategoryTheory.Idempotents.DoldKan.laxMonoidalΓ) (TauCeti#19606). Separately, good truncation τ≥0 is lax monoidal, for now only for complexes of modules over a ring. The dg nerve and the enrichment itself have not been started.

[Pro-zero diagrams](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Functor/ProZero.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.IsProZero) arrived in an arbitrary abelian category. They are diagrams in which every term is killed by some later transition. They form a [Serre class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Functor/ProZero.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.isSerreClass_isProZero), so the [pro-isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Functor/ProZero.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.proIsomorphisms) satisfy two-out-of-three, and they are unchanged by cofinal reindexing. For towers of modules, pro-zero implies [vanishing lim¹](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/ProZero.html#TauCeti.ModuleTower.subsingleton_limOne_of_isProZero). Multiplication by `a` gives a tower that is [pro-zero exactly when `a` is nilpotent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/ProZero.html#TauCeti.ModuleTower.isProZero_multiplicationTower_iff), which is the roadmap's standard non-example.

Two smaller targets were completed. In a perfect ring the [Frobenius-root ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfect/FrobeniusRootIdeal.html#TauCeti.frobeniusRootIdeal) of `f` is [the radical of `(f)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfect/FrobeniusRootIdeal.html#TauCeti.frobeniusRootIdeal_eq_radical), is idempotent, and is [flat even with zero divisors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfect/FrobeniusRootIdeal.html#TauCeti.flat_frobeniusRootIdeal). For a quasicategory, the [full enhanced subcategory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Quasicategory/FullSubcategory.html#SSet.fullEnhancedSubcategory) on a set of vertices is [again a quasicategory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Quasicategory/FullSubcategory.html#SSet.instQuasicategoryToSSetFullEnhancedSubcategory), and for nerves it agrees with the ordinary full subcategory.

## EnhancedDerivedSheaves: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-06 (`ddc3f97` to `385ec2d`)

The [differential graded nerve](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/Basic.html#TauCeti.dgNerve) has arrived, together with the comparisons that make it usable. [The dg nerve is a quasicategory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/Quasicategory.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.quasicategory), and [its homotopy category is H⁰(C)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/HomotopyCategory.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.homotopyCategoryEquiv). [Its right mapping spaces are Dold–Kan applied to the good truncation of the Hom complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGNerve/RightMappingSpace.html#TauCeti.dgNerve.rightMappingSpaceIsoCochainToSSet), so the homotopy groups of a mapping space are the cohomology of the Hom complex in non-positive degrees. The [simplicial enrichment](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Simplicial.html#TauCeti.DGSimplicialCategory) of a dg category is built from the lax monoidal Dold–Kan functor and is locally Kan. On K-injective complexes, [the homotopy category of the dg nerve is the derived category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/CochainComplex/KInjective.html#TauCeti.KInjectiveDGCategory.dgNerveHomotopyCategoryEquiv), provided K-injective resolutions exist. Two pieces are still missing: the comparison with the homotopy-coherent nerve, and stability of the dg nerve with its shift and cone comparison.

Most of the commutative algebra of E1.6 is now in place. [Kunz's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.IsRegularRing.flat_frobenius) says that Frobenius on a regular ring of characteristic p is flat. [Tor independence of perfect rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Perfect/Tor.html#TauCeti.isZero_Tor_succ_of_perfectRing) says that higher Tor between perfect algebras over a perfect ring vanishes. Supporting them are [the bound on the global dimension of a regular ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/ProjectiveDimension.html#TauCeti.IsRegularRing.hasProjectiveDimensionLE) for all modules, not only finite ones, together with [Serre's converse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/ProjectiveDimension.html#TauCeti.isRegularLocalRing_iff_projectiveDimension_ne_top), [a Cohen structure theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LocalRing/CoefficientField.html#TauCeti.exists_surjective_mvPowerSeries_spanFinrank) for complete local rings that contain a field, and Tor-dimension bounds for perfect presentations. After perfection, the bound on global dimension is proved only for the perfect closure of a regular ring itself. Separately, bounded saturated lattices over a spherically complete field [are finite free](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Field/SaturatedLattice.html#TauCeti.exists_basis_of_isValuationSaturated), as in Bhatt–Scholze.

On the sheaf side, every unbounded complex of module sheaves over a sheaf of commutative rings has [a K-flat resolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/TensorProduct/KFlat.html#CochainComplex.exists_quasiIso_isKFlat_sheafOfModules), and [countable products of abelian sheaves on a replete topos are exact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/Replete.html#TauCeti.countableAB4Star_sheaf). Hypercovers and locally weakly contractible topoi were set up for later layers. Supporting work covered weak contractibility, small mapping-space models, κ-filtered simplicial sets and the product–difference map of a tower.
