## TemperleyLiebOperatorAlgebras: 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-19 (`aeb72b8` to `b235000`)

The first finite realisation of the Jones tower has landed: at loop parameter `δ = √2`, [the even Clifford model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/EvenClifford/Quotient.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.exists_evenCliffordModel) gives a surjective map from the Temperley–Lieb algebra onto [the even complex Clifford algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/EvenClifford/Basic.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.EvenClifford), sending each generator to `√2` times a Clifford projection, carrying the analytic adjoint to the Clifford adjoint, turning the Markov trace into `(√2)^m` times the Clifford scalar trace, and with kernel exactly the trace radical (TauCeti#38465). This is the headline of section 1.7, proved in a slightly stronger form than the roadmap's target, which left the adjoint out. Consequences come with it: [the Jones quotient at `√2` is isomorphic to the even Clifford algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/EvenClifford/Quotient.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.quotientEvenCliffordEquiv), compatibly with the strand inclusions, so it has [dimension `2^(m-1)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/EvenClifford/Quotient.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.finrank_quotient_traceRadical_sqrt_two), and [the Markov trace is positive at `√2`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/EvenClifford/Quotient.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.map_star_mul_self_nonneg_sqrt_two), a direct proof of one discrete-series case of Jones's positivity theorem.

Getting there meant general Clifford-algebra work: [the dimension `2^(n-1)` of an even Clifford algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dimension.html#CliffordAlgebra.finrank_even), [nondegeneracy of the scalar-trace pairing on the even part](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Radical.html#CliffordAlgebra.forall_even_scalarTrace_mul_eq_zero_iff) for a nondegenerate form, and, for the complex sum of squares, Majorana generators, [an analytic adjoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/EvenClifford/Basic.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.cliffordAdjoint) and [faithfulness of the scalar trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/EvenClifford/Basic.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.scalarTrace_cliffordAdjoint_mul_self_eq_zero_iff). The rest of 1.7 is still open: the matrix blocks and Pauli realisation, comparison with the positive path model and C*-tower, and the braid units with their finite braid image.
