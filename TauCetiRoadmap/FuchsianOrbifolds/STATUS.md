### Named results

- **X(1) is the Riemann sphere** — the normalized modular invariant, descended to the compactified level-one quotient, is a biholomorphism onto P¹ sending the elliptic orbits of orders two and three to 1728 and 0, proved through degree one ([`TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Sphere.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph)).
- **Poincaré's polygon theorem, in both directions** — a side-paired polygon satisfying the angle condition, with parabolic transformations at its ideal cycles, generates a [cofinite group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Polygon/Poincare.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.SidePairing.isCofinite_of_isParabolic) that it presents, and every cofinite group [arises this way](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.IsCofinite.exists_sidePairing_closure_range_map_eq) from a Dirichlet polygon.
- **Hyperbolic triangle groups** — every hyperbolic triple of elliptic orders and cusps is realised by a [cofinite Fuchsian group isomorphic to Δ(p, q, r)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/TriangleOrder/Group.html#TauCeti.TriangleOrder.IsHyperbolic.exists_isCofinite_triangleGroup), with the expected elliptic orders and cusp count.
- **The orbifold area formula for cocompact groups** — covolume Γ = 2π(−χ(X(Γ)) + Σₑ(1 − 1/mₑ)) over the elliptic orbits ([`Subgroup.covolume_eq_two_pi_mul_neg_eulerChar_add`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/EulerCharacteristic.html#Subgroup.covolume_eq_two_pi_mul_neg_eulerChar_add)), with the hyperbolicity inequality and the multiplicativity of the orbifold Euler characteristic as corollaries.
- **Riemann–Hurwitz for cocompact Fuchsian groups** — for Δ ≤ Γ of finite index, χ(X(Δ)) = [Γ : Δ] · χ(X(Γ)) − R with no finite-CW hypothesis ([`eulerChar_compactifiedQuotient_eq_index_mul_sub_ramificationDegree_of_compactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.Fuchsian.eulerChar_compactifiedQuotient_eq_index_mul_sub_ramificationDegree_of_compactSpace)). The general cofinite version still assumes finite CW type.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The compactification** — [`Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Basic.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient) adjoins the cusp orbits to Γ\ℍ with q-coordinate charts. For every cofinite group it [is compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.IsCofinite.compactSpace_compactifiedQuotient), and finite-index inclusions induce maps of [degree equal to the index](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Degree.html#Subgroup.degree_compactifiedQuotientMap).
- **Finite-sided Dirichlet polygons** — a discrete group is cofinite [exactly when its Dirichlet domain is a finite-sided polygon](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.isCofinite_iff_exists_convexPolygon_carrier_eq_dirichletDomain), which supplies presentations, compactness and cusp counts for arbitrary cofinite groups.
- **Cell structures from polygons** — a compact Dirichlet polygon makes the quotient of a cocompact group a [finite CW complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/EulerCharacteristic.html#Subgroup.finiteCWType_orbitRel_quotient). This is what removes the finite-CW hypothesis in the cocompact case; no such structure exists yet when there are cusps.

## The frontier

- **Genus** — define `genus` by χ = 2 − 2g. This needs finite CW type for every compact Riemann surface, which waits on AlgebraicTopology Stage 8 item 7 through ComplexManifolds Milestone 1. Once genus exists, Riemann–Hurwitz in 2 − 2g form is a rewrite of the branched-cover formula.
- **Cell count with cusps** — extend the compact-polygon cell structure to X(Γ) with cusps adjoined, giving χ(X(Γ)) = V + c + 1 − (n − k)/2. With genus, this gives `covolume_eq_two_pi_mul_signature` for all cofinite groups.
- **Fuchsian Riemann–Hurwitz for cofinite groups** — the cocompact case is unconditional. With cusps, the finite-CW hypotheses on X(Δ), X(Γ) and the free-locus quotient remain.
- **Minimal covolumes (Layer 4a)** — the bounds π/21 and, with a cusp, π/3, with equality exactly at (0; 2, 3, 7) and (0; 2, 3; 1). Both wait on the signature area formula.
- **L2.1 and GL(2, ℝ) isometries** — independent of the genus chain. Identify the Poincaré Riemannian distance with `dist`, build the half-space comparison isometry, and transfer curvature and completeness.
