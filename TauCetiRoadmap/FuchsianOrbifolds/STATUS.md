### Named results

- **X(1) is the Riemann sphere** — the normalized modular invariant, descended to the compactified level-one quotient, is a biholomorphism onto P¹ sending the elliptic orbits of orders two and three to 1728 and 0 ([`TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Sphere.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph)).
- **Poincaré's polygon theorem, in both directions** — a side-paired polygon satisfying the angle condition, with parabolic transformations at its ideal cycles, generates a [cofinite group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Polygon/Poincare.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.SidePairing.isCofinite_of_isParabolic) that it presents, and every cofinite group [arises this way](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.IsCofinite.exists_sidePairing_closure_range_map_eq) from a Dirichlet polygon.
- **The orbifold area formula for cocompact groups** — covolume Γ = 2π(−χ(X(Γ)) + Σₑ(1 − 1/mₑ)) over the elliptic orbits ([`Subgroup.covolume_eq_two_pi_mul_neg_eulerChar_add`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/EulerCharacteristic.html#Subgroup.covolume_eq_two_pi_mul_neg_eulerChar_add)), with the hyperbolicity inequality and multiplicativity of the orbifold Euler characteristic as corollaries.
- **Riemann–Hurwitz for cocompact Fuchsian groups** — for Δ ≤ Γ of finite index, χ(X(Δ)) = [Γ : Δ] · χ(X(Γ)) − R ([`eulerChar_compactifiedQuotient_eq_index_mul_sub_ramificationDegree_of_compactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.Fuchsian.eulerChar_compactifiedQuotient_eq_index_mul_sub_ramificationDegree_of_compactSpace)). For all cofinite groups, R is [expressed through cusp counts and elliptic orders](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/RamificationDegree.html#TauCeti.Fuchsian.ramificationDegree_eq_index_mul_cusps_add_elliptic_sub) without hypotheses.
- **The Poincaré metric is the hyperbolic distance** — Mathlib's `dist` on ℍ [is the Riemannian distance of the Poincaré metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Riemannian/Distance.html#TauCeti.isRiemannianManifold_upperHalfPlane). Through the [isometry with the half-space model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Riemannian/HalfSpace.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.upperHalfSpaceIsometryEquiv), ℍ has curvature −1 and is complete, and GL(2, ℝ) [acts by Riemannian isometries](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Riemannian/Isometry.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.glToIsom).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The compactification** — [`Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Basic.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient) adjoins cusp orbits to Γ\ℍ with q-coordinate charts. It [is compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.IsCofinite.compactSpace_compactifiedQuotient) for every cofinite group, and it now has finite CW type automatically, because [compact Riemann surfaces do](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/FiniteCWType.html#TauCeti.ComplexManifold.instFiniteCWType).
- **Finite-sided Dirichlet polygons** — a discrete group is cofinite [exactly when its Dirichlet domain is a finite-sided polygon](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.isCofinite_iff_exists_convexPolygon_carrier_eq_dirichletDomain). This supplies presentations, compactness and cusp counts for arbitrary cofinite groups.
- **Genus** — [`genus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/EulerCharacteristic/Genus.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.genus) is (2 − χ)/2 on any space of finite CW type. It makes the 2 − 2g forms of Riemann–Hurwitz and the signature formula statable, and X(1) has genus 0.

## The frontier

- **Euler number of a compact Riemann surface** — prove that b₁ is even, through orientation (ComplexManifolds Milestone 2) and the intersection pairing (AlgebraicTopology Stage 6 items 4–5). Then `eulerChar_eq_two_sub_two_mul_genus` follows, and `riemannHurwitz` becomes a corollary of the existing conditional statement.
- **Cell count with cusps** — extend the compact-polygon cell structure to X(Γ) with cusps, giving χ(X(Γ)) = V + c + 1 − (n − k)/2. With the Euler-number identity, this yields `covolume_eq_two_pi_mul_signature` for all cofinite groups.
- **Minimal covolumes** — `pi_div_twentyOne_le_covolume`, `minimum_area_finite_area_orbifold` and their equality cases. The numerical case analysis is done, so these wait only on the signature area formula.
- **Fuchsian Riemann–Hurwitz with cusps** — X(Δ) and X(Γ) now have finite CW type automatically. The hypothesis on the free-locus quotient remains, and the 2 − 2g form also needs the Euler-number identity.
- **Punctured targets** — the branched-cover formula assumes finite CW type of the target minus its branch values. That is known only for the Riemann sphere minus finitely many points. The general case needs triangulations with marked points (AlgebraicTopology Stage 8 item 7).
