## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0 and 1 are done, and the level-one application has reached X(1) ≃ P¹ through the descended `j` and its degree. For every discrete Γ, the compactified quotient is a Riemann surface with meromorphic descent and extension, but compactness is proved only at level one. Polygon theory now has Gauss–Bonnet with ideal vertices and most of the local input to the Poincaré theorem. The theorem itself is not proved, and genus and Riemann–Hurwitz have not begun.

### Named results

- **X(1) is the Riemann sphere** — the normalized modular invariant, descended to the compactified level-one quotient, is a biholomorphism onto P¹ that sends the elliptic orbits of orders two and three to 1728 and 0 ([`TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Sphere.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph)). The proof goes through degree one.
- **The degree-one biholomorphism theorem** — a finite holomorphic map of degree one between compact connected Riemann surfaces is a biholomorphism with that map as its forward function ([`TauCeti.RiemannSurface.biholomorphOfDegreeEqOne`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Degree.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.biholomorphOfDegreeEqOne)).
- **The degree of a finite-index quotient map is the index** — for discrete Δ ≤ Γ of finite index, every fibre of X(Δ) → X(Γ) has [Γ : Δ] points counted with multiplicity ([`Subgroup.degree_compactifiedQuotientMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Degree.html#Subgroup.degree_compactifiedQuotientMap)).
- **Gauss–Bonnet for convex polygons with ideal vertices** — a convex hyperbolic n-gon with vertices in ℍ ∪ ∂ℍ has area (n − 2)π minus its angle sum, where ideal angles count as zero ([`TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.volume_carrier`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/Convex/GaussBonnet.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.volume_carrier)).
- **Meromorphic descent with the elliptic order formula** — a Γ-invariant meromorphic function on ℍ descends uniquely to Γ\ℍ ([`Subgroup.existsUnique_meromorphicAt_quotientMk`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/MeromorphicDescent.html#Subgroup.existsUnique_meromorphicAt_quotientMk)), and pulling back multiplies orders by the stabilizer order. Under exponential growth bounds such a function [extends across the cusps](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Extension.html#TauCeti.Fuchsian.exists_meromorphicAt_comp_ofQuotient).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The compactification** — [`Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Basic.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient) is the explicit sum of Γ\ℍ and the cusp orbits, with q-coordinate charts from [normalized cusp data](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Cusp/Datum.html#Subgroup.CuspDatum). [`compactSpace_of_compact_truncations`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Compactness.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.compactSpace_of_compact_truncations) reduces compactness to compact truncated fundamental sets. Convex polygons [truncated at their ideal vertices](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/Convex/Truncation.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.isCompact_carrier_diff_iUnion) are already known to be compact.
- **The ramification divisor** — [`ramificationDivisor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Ramification.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.ramificationDivisor) gives each point weight e − 1 and satisfies a chain rule. A map with its branch values removed is a [covering](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/BranchValues.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.FiniteHolomorphicMap.isCoveringMap_restrictPreimage_branchValues_compl). This sets up the excision proof of Riemann–Hurwitz.
- **Side pairings and Dirichlet domains** — [side pairings](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/SidePairing/Basic.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.SidePairing) of convex polygons with ideal vertices come with vertex cycles, angle sums and cycle transformations. Adjacent tiles across a paired side do not overlap and together cover a neighbourhood of the side. Every discrete group has a [Dirichlet fundamental domain](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain.html#Subgroup.exists_isFundamentalDomain_dirichletDomain). It is geodesically convex, and its boundary is [the union of its faces](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/DirichletDomain/Faces.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.frontier_dirichletDomain_eq_iUnion_dirichletFace).

### Roadmap coverage

- **Layer 3** lacks only compactness of the complement of the cusp horodiscs in a finite-area fundamental polygon. Truncated convex polygons are compact, but no such fundamental polygon has yet been built for a general cofinite group.

It lacks the Poincaré polygon theorem, finite-sidedness of Dirichlet polygons for cofinite groups, presentations and triangle groups.

## The frontier

- **The Poincaré polygon theorem** — local tiling along paired sides is done, and discreteness follows from interior no-overlap for compact polygons. What remains is the tiling at vertex cycles, global no-overlap of translates, the fundamental-set theorem and the presentation. The ideal-vertex case also needs parabolic cycle transformations.
- **Finite-sided Dirichlet polygons** — for a cofinite group, show that the Dirichlet domain is a finite-sided convex polygon with paired sides. This gives Layer 2's converse direction and a fundamental polygon to truncate.
- **Compactness for every cofinite group** — feed the compact truncation of such a polygon to `compactSpace_of_compact_truncations`. The same step closes Layer 3.
- **Triangle groups** — after the Poincaré theorem, build the (p, q, r) groups from polygon reflections using the typed finite-or-cusp parameter.
- **Genus and Riemann–Hurwitz** — the README defines genus through the finite-CW Euler characteristic of a compact surface. `compactManifoldFiniteCWType` does not appear in Tau Ceti's source, so this waits on the AlgebraicTopology roadmap. The orbifold signatures, including that of X(1), wait on genus.
