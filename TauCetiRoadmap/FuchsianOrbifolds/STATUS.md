## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0 and 1 are done, and the level-one application has reached X(1) ≃ P¹ through the descended `j` and its degree. For every discrete Γ the compactified quotient is a Riemann surface that carries meromorphic descent and extension. Compactness is still proved only at level one. Polygon theory goes as far as side pairings and Dirichlet domains but not the Poincaré theorem. Genus and Riemann–Hurwitz have not begun.

### Named results

- **X(1) is the Riemann sphere** — the normalized modular invariant, descended to the compactified level-one quotient, is a biholomorphism onto P¹ that sends the elliptic orbits of orders two and three to 1728 and 0 ([`TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Sphere.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph)). The proof goes through degree one.
- **The degree-one biholomorphism theorem** — a finite holomorphic map of degree one between compact connected Riemann surfaces is a biholomorphism with that map as its forward function ([`TauCeti.RiemannSurface.biholomorphOfDegreeEqOne`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Degree.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.biholomorphOfDegreeEqOne)).
- **The degree of a finite-index quotient map is the index** — for discrete Δ ≤ Γ of finite index, every fibre of X(Δ) → X(Γ) has [Γ : Δ] points counted with multiplicity ([`Subgroup.degree_compactifiedQuotientMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Degree.html#Subgroup.degree_compactifiedQuotientMap)).
- **Meromorphic descent with the elliptic order formula** — a Γ-invariant meromorphic function on ℍ descends uniquely to Γ\ℍ ([`Subgroup.existsUnique_meromorphicAt_quotientMk`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/MeromorphicDescent.html#Subgroup.existsUnique_meromorphicAt_quotientMk)), and pulling back multiplies orders by the stabilizer order. Under exponential growth bounds such a function [extends across the cusps](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Extension.html#TauCeti.Fuchsian.exists_meromorphicAt_comp_ofQuotient).
- **Gauss–Bonnet for compact convex polygons** — a convex hyperbolic n-gon with all vertices in ℍ has area (n − 2)π minus its angle sum ([`TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.CompactConvexPolygon.volume_carrier`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/GaussBonnet.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.CompactConvexPolygon.volume_carrier)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The compactification** — [`Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Basic.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient) is the explicit sum of Γ\ℍ and the cusp orbits, with q-coordinate charts from [normalized cusp data](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Cusp/Datum.html#Subgroup.CuspDatum). [`compactSpace_of_compact_truncations`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Compactness.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.compactSpace_of_compact_truncations) reduces compactness to compact truncated fundamental sets, and conjugate groups give [biholomorphic compactifications](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Conj.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.conjBiholomorph).
- **The ramification divisor** — [`ramificationDivisor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Ramification.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.ramificationDivisor) records each point with weight e − 1 and satisfies a chain rule under composition. Together with the fact that a map with its branch values removed is a [covering](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/BranchValues.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.FiniteHolomorphicMap.isCoveringMap_restrictPreimage_branchValues_compl), this sets up the excision proof of Riemann–Hurwitz.
- **Polygons, side pairings and Dirichlet domains** — [convex polygons](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/Convex.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon) may have ideal vertices. Compact ones carry [side pairings](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/SidePairing.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.CompactConvexPolygon.SidePairing) with vertex cycles and cycle transformations. Every discrete group has a [Dirichlet fundamental domain](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain.html#Subgroup.exists_isFundamentalDomain_dirichletDomain) whose faces are [locally finite](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/DirichletDomain/Faces.html#TauCeti.locallyFinite_dirichletFace).

## The frontier

- **The Poincaré polygon theorem** — combine side pairings, the cycle angle conditions and local finiteness of translates into discreteness, the fundamental-set theorem and the presentation. Pairings with ideal vertices and parabolic cycles still have to be added.
- **Finite-sided Dirichlet polygons** — for a cofinite group, show that the Dirichlet domain is a finite-sided convex polygon with locally finite translates. This gives Layer 2's converse direction.
- **Compactness for every cofinite group** — feed compact truncations of such a polygon to `compactSpace_of_compact_truncations`. The same truncation closes Layer 3.
- **Gauss–Bonnet with ideal vertices** — extend the compact-polygon formula to [`ConvexPolygon`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/Convex.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon), where ideal angles are zero. This is needed for the area formula and for triangle groups.
- **Genus and Riemann–Hurwitz** — the README defines genus through the finite-CW Euler characteristic of a compact surface. `compactManifoldFiniteCWType` does not appear in Tau Ceti's source, so this waits on the AlgebraicTopology roadmap. The orbifold signatures, including that of X(1), wait on genus.
