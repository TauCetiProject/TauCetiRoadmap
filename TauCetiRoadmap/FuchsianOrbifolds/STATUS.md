## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done, and for every discrete Γ ≤ PSL(2, ℝ) the coarse quotient and its cusp compactification are proved to be Riemann surfaces; compactness, however, is proved only at level one. Hyperbolic polygons, the compact-surface degree theory and the `j`-function half of Layer 6 have not begun.

### Named results

- **The compactified quotient is a Riemann surface** — for discrete Γ, adjoining one point per cusp orbit to Γ\ℍ, charted there by the q-coordinate `exp(2πiσ(z)/w)`, gives a Hausdorff, second-countable Riemann surface containing Γ\ℍ as an open dense submanifold ([`Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.instIsManifold`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Manifold.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.instIsManifold)).
- **The coarse quotient is a Riemann surface** — Γ\ℍ is one even at elliptic orbits, where the chart for a stabilizer of order m is `u = z^m` in a disc coordinate, and the orbit projection is holomorphic ([`Subgroup.instIsManifoldOrbitRelQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/CoarseQuotient.html#Subgroup.instIsManifoldOrbitRelQuotient)).
- **Shimizu's lemma** — if a discrete Γ contains a translation `z ↦ z + w` with `w ≠ 0`, every element not fixing ∞ has lower-left matrix entry of absolute value at least `1/|w|` ([`Subgroup.inv_le_abs_apply_one_zero_of_upperRightHom_mem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Shimizu.html#Subgroup.inv_le_abs_apply_one_zero_of_upperRightHom_mem)); it makes high horodiscs precisely invariant and separates inequivalent cusps.
- **Finiteness of cusps** — a discrete group has at most as many cusp orbits as its covolume, so a cofinite group has finitely many ([`Subgroup.card_cuspOrbit_le_covolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Cusp/Finite.html#Subgroup.card_cuspOrbit_le_covolume)).
- **Compactness of X(1)** — the effective level-one group is cofinite with a single cusp orbit, and its compactified quotient is compact ([`TauCeti.ModularGroup.compactSpace_compactifiedQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/LevelOne.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.compactSpace_compactifiedQuotient)).

### Roadmap coverage

Layer 0 is done: the topology and faithful holomorphic action of PSL(2, ℝ), proper discontinuity of discrete subgroups, finite cyclic stabilizers with their derivative order, and the free-locus covering and local biholomorphism. Layers 1 to 6 are partial. Layer 3 lacks only compactness of the complement of the cusp horodiscs in a finite-area fundamental polygon. Layer 1 lacks the ramification statement for the orbit projection, independence of the elliptic charts from choices, and meromorphic descent with the order formula. Layer 4 lacks compactness beyond level one, functoriality under conjugation, finite-index maps and the orbifold signature. Layer 6 has the compact X(1) with its one cusp, but not its signature, the descended `j` or `X(1) ≃ P¹`. Layer 2 has only fundamental domains, covolume, cofiniteness and geodesic lines. Layer 5 has only holomorphic descent to Γ\ℍ; its degree API has not begun.

## The frontier

- **Ramification and meromorphic descent on Γ\ℍ** — show the projection has multiplicity m at a point whose stabilizer has order m, so that it ramifies exactly at elliptic points, and descend invariant meromorphic functions with `ord_z f = m · ord_[z] f̄`. The local model through `u ↦ u^m` already scales orders by m.
- **Compactness for every cofinite group** — feed compact truncated fundamental sets to `compactSpace_of_compact_truncations`. The README's route is the finite-sided Dirichlet polygon of Layer 2, which has only geodesic lines so far; the same compact truncation is Layer 3's last missing clause.
- **The compact-surface degree API** — local multiplicity, degree and the degree-one biholomorphism can proceed now. Genus and Riemann-Hurwitz also need the AlgebraicTopology roadmap's finite-CW Euler characteristic, which is not yet in Tau Ceti, and applying the API to a compactified quotient needs its connectedness, not yet proved.
- **Finite-index maps and the orbifold signature** — extend `Γ'\ℍ → Γ\ℍ` over the cusps with `e_cusp = w_Γ'/w_Γ` and elliptic ramification from stabilizer indices, then derive the signature and the orbifold Euler-characteristic and area formula.
- **`X(1) ≃ P¹`** — compute the signature of X(1), then descend and extend the normalized `j`, which waits on the modular-forms roadmap's `JInputs` module (not yet in Tau Ceti); degree one and the biholomorphism then need the degree-one theorem.
