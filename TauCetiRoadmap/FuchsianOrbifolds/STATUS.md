## Where this roadmap stands

The roadmap's remaining gap is genus. Without it there is no Riemann–Hurwitz in 2 − 2g form, no Fuchsian Riemann–Hurwitz and no general orbifold signature.

### Named results

- **X(1) is the Riemann sphere** — the normalized modular invariant, descended to the compactified level-one quotient, is a biholomorphism onto P¹ that sends the elliptic orbits of orders two and three to 1728 and 0. The proof goes through degree one ([`TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Sphere.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph)).
- **Poincaré's polygon theorem** — a side-paired polygon satisfying the angle condition at its finite cycles, with parabolic transformations at its ideal cycles, generates a [cofinite group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Polygon/Poincare.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.SidePairing.isCofinite_of_isParabolic) that it [presents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Polygon/Poincare.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.SidePairing.relatorQuotientTo_bijective_of_isParabolic). Conversely, every cofinite group [arises this way](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.IsCofinite.exists_sidePairing_closure_range_map_eq) from a Dirichlet polygon.
- **Compactness of the compactified quotient** — for every cofinite Γ ≤ PSL(2, ℝ), Γ\ℍ with its finitely many cusp orbits adjoined [is compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.IsCofinite.compactSpace_compactifiedQuotient).
- **Hyperbolic triangle groups** — for each hyperbolic triple of elliptic orders and cusps, some triangle's reflection group is a [cofinite Fuchsian group isomorphic to Δ(p, q, r)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/TriangleOrder/Group.html#TauCeti.TriangleOrder.IsHyperbolic.exists_isCofinite_triangleGroup), with the expected elliptic orders and cusp count. PSL(2, ℤ) is [Δ(3, 2, ∞)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/TriangleGroup.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.triangleGroupEquiv).
- **The branched-cover Euler-characteristic formula** — for a finite holomorphic map, χ(X) = deg f · χ(Y) − R(f) ([`eulerChar_eq_degree_mul_eulerChar_sub_ramificationDegree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Branch/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.FiniteHolomorphicMap.eulerChar_eq_degree_mul_eulerChar_sub_ramificationDegree)). It needs finite CW type, or a triangulation, of the surfaces as a hypothesis. It sits beside the [degree-one biholomorphism theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Degree.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.biholomorphOfDegreeEqOne).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The compactification** — [`Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Basic.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient) is the explicit sum of Γ\ℍ and the cusp orbits, with q-coordinate charts at the cusps. Finite-index inclusions induce maps of [degree equal to the index](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Degree.html#Subgroup.degree_compactifiedQuotientMap).
- **Finite-sided Dirichlet polygons** — a discrete group is cofinite [exactly when its Dirichlet domain is a polygon with finitely many vertices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain/Cofinite.html#Subgroup.isCofinite_iff_exists_convexPolygon_carrier_eq_dirichletDomain), possibly with ideal vertices. This is what supplies presentations, compactness and cusp counts for arbitrary cofinite groups.
- **The quotient as an identification space** — for a fundamental polygon, Γ\ℍ is [homeomorphic to the polygon with its sides glued](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/SidePairing/Identification.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.SidePairing.carrierQuotientHomeomorph). For compact polygons this gives finite CW type and the area formula [2π(−χ(Γ\ℍ) + Σ(1 − 1/mₑ))](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Polygon/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.ConvexPolygon.SidePairing.covolume_eq_two_pi_mul_neg_eulerChar_add).

## The frontier

- **Genus** — define `RiemannSurface.genus` by χ = 2 − 2g. That needs finite CW type for every compact Riemann surface. Tau Ceti's source has a triangulable-to-finite-CW bridge but no `compactManifoldFiniteCWType`, so this still waits on the AlgebraicTopology roadmap or a triangulation theorem.
- **Riemann–Hurwitz** — once genus exists, rewrite the branched-cover formula as 2g_X − 2 = deg f · (2g_Y − 2) + deg R_f.
- **Orbifold area formula with cusps** — extend the compact-polygon formula to cofinite groups, with the term for s cusps, using the compactified quotient in place of Γ\ℍ. Level one already checks it.
- **Standard presentations and signatures** — rewrite the side-pairing presentation of a cofinite group in the form with generators aᵢ, bᵢ, elliptic xⱼ and parabolic pₖ, and derive the signature (g; m₁, …, m_r; s).
- **Fuchsian Riemann–Hurwitz** — apply Riemann–Hurwitz to the compactified maps X(Δ) → X(Γ), whose local multiplicities are already the cusp-width and stabilizer indices.
