## FuchsianOrbifolds: 2026-09-12 to 2026-09-26 (`8619c2a` to `759eb3e`)

For every discrete Γ ≤ PSL(2, ℝ), both quotients the roadmap is built around are now Riemann surfaces. The coarse quotient Γ\ℍ is one even at elliptic orbits, where the chart for a stabilizer of order m is the model `u = z^m` in a disc coordinate, and the orbit projection is holomorphic ([`Subgroup.instIsManifoldOrbitRelQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/CoarseQuotient.html#Subgroup.instIsManifoldOrbitRelQuotient)). Adjoining one point for each cusp orbit, with the q-coordinate `exp(2πiσ(z)/w)` as the chart there, gives a Hausdorff, second-countable Riemann surface in which Γ\ℍ is open and dense ([`Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.instIsManifold`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Manifold.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.instIsManifold)). Compactness is proved only at level one, where the effective modular group is cofinite with a single cusp orbit ([`TauCeti.ModularGroup.compactSpace_compactifiedQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/LevelOne.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.compactSpace_compactifiedQuotient)); for a general cofinite group it is reduced to compact truncated fundamental sets, which the polygon theory has yet to supply.

The cusp side rests on Shimizu's lemma: if a discrete group contains a translation `z ↦ z + w` with `w ≠ 0`, every element not fixing ∞ has lower-left matrix entry of absolute value at least `1/|w|` ([`Subgroup.inv_le_abs_apply_one_zero_of_upperRightHom_mem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Shimizu.html#Subgroup.inv_le_abs_apply_one_zero_of_upperRightHom_mem)). It makes high horodiscs precisely invariant and separates inequivalent cusps; an area count then bounds the number of cusp orbits by the covolume ([`Subgroup.card_cuspOrbit_le_covolume`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Cusp/Finite.html#Subgroup.card_cuspOrbit_le_covolume)), so a cofinite group has finitely many. Invariant holomorphic functions of at most exponential growth at a cusp extend meromorphically across it in the q-coordinate, with order bounded by the growth or decay rate, and have a convergent Laurent q-expansion ([`TauCeti.Subgroup.CuspDatum.hasSum_laurentQExpansion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Cusp/Laurent.html#TauCeti.Subgroup.CuspDatum.hasSum_laurentQExpansion)).

Underneath sit the basic facts about the effective action: proper discontinuity of discrete subgroups, finite cyclic point stabilizers acting by roots of unity in a disc coordinate, and the free-locus quotient as a covering and local biholomorphism (TauCeti#6389, TauCeti#8324). Invariant holomorphic functions descend uniquely to Γ\ℍ ([`Subgroup.existsUnique_mdifferentiable_quotientMk`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Descent.html#Subgroup.existsUnique_mdifferentiable_quotientMk)). Meromorphic descent, fundamental polygons (only covolume and geodesic lines exist) and the compact-surface degree theory are not yet there.

## FuchsianOrbifolds: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

The compact-surface degree theory of Layer 5 has begun. Tau Ceti now defines the [local multiplicity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/LocalMultiplicity.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.localMultiplicity) of a map between Riemann surfaces as a vanishing order in charts, and proves the degree is independent of the fibre: for a finite holomorphic map from a compact connected surface, every fibre has the same number of points counted with multiplicity ([`degree_eq_fiber_sum`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Degree.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.degree_eq_fiber_sum)). Degree is also shown to be positive and multiplicative under composition ([`degree_comp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Degree.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.degree_comp)). The identity and open mapping theorems for Riemann surfaces are proved along the way (TauCeti#9846). The degree-one biholomorphism theorem, divisor pullback, genus and Riemann-Hurwitz are not yet there.

This theory is already in use on quotients. The orbit projection ℍ → Γ\ℍ has local multiplicity equal to the order of the stabilizer ([`localMultiplicity_quotientMk`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Ramification.html#Subgroup.localMultiplicity_quotientMk)), so it ramifies exactly at the elliptic points. Pulling a holomorphic map back through it multiplies local multiplicity by that order; the meromorphic version of this order formula is still missing. The elliptic charts are now shown not to depend on the invariant disc, the linearizing coordinate or the choice of stabilizer generator.

For a finite-index inclusion Δ ≤ Γ, the orbit map extends to a holomorphic [map of compactified quotients](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Map.html#Subgroup.compactifiedQuotientMap). At a cusp its local multiplicity is the integer ratio of cusp widths, and at an interior point it is the index of one stabilizer in the other; both indices multiply in towers. Over every interior point the multiplicities sum to [Γ : Δ] ([`sum_localMultiplicity_compactifiedQuotientMap_fiber_ofQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/InteriorDegree.html#Subgroup.sum_localMultiplicity_compactifiedQuotientMap_fiber_ofQuotient)). The fibres over cusps, and the statement that the degree of this map is the index, are still to come. Separately, half-planes bounded by hyperbolic geodesics give the polygon theory its first building block (TauCeti#8703).

## FuchsianOrbifolds: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (`e440f4e` to `8a32441`)

The Gauss–Bonnet formula for hyperbolic triangles is proved: the invariant area of a nondegenerate triangle in ℍ is its angular defect π − α − β − γ ([`volume_triangle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Triangle.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.volume_triangle)). A separate computation gives the area of a triangle with one vertex at infinity, the region above the unit semicircle between two vertical lines, as a difference of arccosines ([`volume_idealRegion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/IdealRegion.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.volume_idealRegion)). Underneath is the first real polygon geometry on ℍ: geodesic segments, interior and oriented angles that Möbius transformations preserve, the stabilizer of i as the rotation group, and [triangles](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Triangle.html#UpperHalfPlane.triangle) as intersections of three closed half-planes, shown to be convex and compact with angle sum at most π (TauCeti#10582, TauCeti#10610). Polygons with more sides, side pairings and the Poincaré polygon theorem are still to come.

For a finite-index inclusion Δ ≤ Γ of discrete groups, the induced map of compactified quotients now has degree [Γ : Δ] ([`degree_compactifiedQuotientMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Degree.html#Subgroup.degree_compactifiedQuotientMap)). The new ingredient is the fibre over a cusp: there the local multiplicity is [the index of one cusp stabilizer in the other](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Cusp/LocalMultiplicity.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.localMultiplicity_compactifiedQuotientMap_ofCusp_cuspOrbitMk), which is also the ratio of the cusp widths, and these multiplicities sum to [Γ : Δ] as the interior fibres already did. Because every fibre is counted directly, the theorem needs neither cofiniteness nor compactness.

On the generic side, a divisor can now be [pulled back](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Divisor.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.divisorPullback) along a finite holomorphic map of Riemann surfaces, each preimage point weighted by its local multiplicity. Pullback reverses composition and multiplies the degree of a divisor by the degree of the map ([`degree_divisorPullback`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Divisor.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.degree_divisorPullback)). The ramification divisor, the degree-one biholomorphism theorem, genus and Riemann–Hurwitz are not yet there.

## FuchsianOrbifolds: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05 (`8a32441` to `1d09589`)

The level-one endpoint of the roadmap is reached: the normalized modular invariant identifies the compactified quotient X(1) biholomorphically with the Riemann sphere ([`jBiholomorph`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Sphere.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.jBiholomorph)), sending the order-two elliptic point to 1728 and the order-three one to 0. The proof runs in the intended order. `j` descends to the coarse quotient, holomorphic even at the elliptic orbits ([`jQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/ModularInvariant.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.jQuotient)), then extends with a simple pole at the cusp ([`meromorphicOrderAt_jCompactified_ofCusp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Extension.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.meromorphicOrderAt_jCompactified_ofCusp)), so it has [degree one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/LevelOne/Sphere.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.degree_jSphere). The general [degree-one biholomorphism theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Degree.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.biholomorphOfDegreeEqOne) then finishes the argument. Its general inputs also landed in this window. Invariant meromorphic functions [descend uniquely](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/MeromorphicDescent.html#Subgroup.existsUnique_meromorphicAt_quotientMk) to Γ\ℍ, with orders multiplied by the stabilizer order on pullback, and they [extend across cusps](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Extension.html#TauCeti.Fuchsian.exists_meromorphicAt_comp_ofQuotient) under exponential growth bounds. What X(1) still lacks is a stated orbifold signature.

Degree theory gained the [ramification divisor](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/Ramification.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.ramificationDivisor) with its chain rule. It also gained branch values: removing them leaves an [ordinary covering](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RiemannSurface/BranchValues.html#TauCeti.RiemannSurface.FiniteHolomorphicMap.isCoveringMap_restrictPreimage_branchValues_compl). Genus and Riemann–Hurwitz are still missing. Conjugate Fuchsian groups now have [biholomorphic compactified quotients](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Compactification/Conj.html#Subgroup.CompactifiedQuotient.conjBiholomorph).

Polygon theory advanced on several fronts. Gauss–Bonnet now holds for [compact convex polygons](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/GaussBonnet.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.CompactConvexPolygon.volume_carrier): the area is (n − 2)π minus the angle sum. Ideal vertices are not yet covered. Riemannian volume of the Poincaré metric [is Mathlib's invariant measure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/PoincareMetric.html#UpperHalfPlane.riemannianVolume_eq_volume). Every discrete group has a [Dirichlet fundamental domain](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/DirichletDomain.html#Subgroup.exists_isFundamentalDomain_dirichletDomain) with locally finite faces. Compact polygons acquired [side pairings](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Polygon/SidePairing.html#TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.CompactConvexPolygon.SidePairing) whose vertex-cycle transformations are elliptic, and local finiteness of their translates. The Poincaré polygon theorem itself has not been proved.
