## IndexTheory: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`274df60` to `b235000`)

The first report on this roadmap covers two pull requests, both in Layer 1. The headline is the norm-path classification of Fredholm operators: two Fredholm maps between complete complex inner-product spaces lie in the same path component of the Fredholm locus exactly when their indices agree ([`nonempty_path_iff_index_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/PathConnected.html#ContinuousLinearMap.nonempty_path_iff_index_eq), with the explicit path in [`exists_path_of_index_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/PathConnected.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsFredholm.exists_path_of_index_eq)). The two spaces may differ, and no separability is assumed (TauCeti#38894). The ingredients are a compact perturbation that makes an index-zero Fredholm operator invertible ([`exists_isInvertible_sub_isCompactOperator_of_index_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/CompactPerturbation.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsFredholm.exists_isInvertible_sub_isCompactOperator_of_index_eq_zero)) and the path connectedness of the [unitary group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitaryPath.html#Unitary.joined_one) and of the [invertible operators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitaryPath.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsInvertible.exists_path_to_id) of a complex Hilbert space. Unitaries are joined to the identity by a reflection argument rather than by the roadmap's Borel logarithm, so the statement that every unitary is exp(iA) with ‖A‖ ≤ π is still open. So are the image of the index and the criterion for the Fredholm locus to be nonempty.

The second strand is the snake sequence for Fredholm maps (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/195). For a map of short exact sequences of complete spaces, if two of the three vertical maps are Fredholm then so is the third, and the indices add ([`index_eq_add_of_exact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/Snake.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsFredholm.index_eq_add_of_exact)). This rests on an algebraic snake lemma for modules: exact kernel and cokernel rows, the [connecting map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/SnakeLemma.html#Function.Exact.snakeδ) with exactness at both ends, and its identification with Mathlib's.
