## ExponentialCone: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-19 (`9d9d0bb` to `b235000`)

The whole roadmap landed in one pull request (TauCeti#42900). It builds the closed real [exponential cone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.cone) as the closure of the pointed cone generated by its [positive-scale branch](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.positive), the points with `v > 0` and `v·exp(w/v) ≤ u`. Membership is then proved, not assumed. The [carrier theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.mem_cone_iff) says the cone is that branch together with its literal zero face `v = 0, u ≥ 0, w ≤ 0`. The proof goes through the [supporting-halfspace characterization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.mem_cone_iff_forall_exp), built from the tangent bound for `exp`, and an explicit approximation of the zero face showing that [the positive branch is dense in the cone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.closure_positive).

On top of this come the standard conic representations. The exponential and logarithm sections and the [logarithmic perspective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Representations.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.le_mul_log_div_iff_mem_cone) are each a single cone membership. The softplus function is captured by a two-cone [softplus system](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Representations.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.SoftplusSystem): it represents the [softplus perspective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Representations.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.mul_log_one_add_exp_div_le_iff_softplusSystem) at every positive scale and the closed face at scale zero, and the system is empty at negative scale. Logistic costs are the same system with the score negated. A [weighted lifting theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Representations.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.sum_mul_exp_le_iff_exists_mem_cone) turns a finite nonnegative-weight sum of exponentials into one auxiliary cone membership per term. It feeds the [weighted process rows](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/Exponential/Representations.html#TauCeti.ExponentialCone.sum_mul_exp_div_sub_one_le_iff_exists_mem_cone) and the exponential-coefficient rows, which come with their [logarithmic form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Log/ScaledExp.html#Real.mul_exp_le_iff_log_add_le_log).

The affine-score, residual and affine-right-side substitutions are proved as checked examples rather than as named theorems.
