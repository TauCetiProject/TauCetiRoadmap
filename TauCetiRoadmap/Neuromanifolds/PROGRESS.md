## Neuromanifolds: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-08 (`460f771` to `d2083f0`)

This first window built nearly the whole roadmap, and it closes with the example the roadmap was designed around: the real model $g_w(x)=w^2x$ has coefficient image the half-line $[0,\infty)$ but [neurovariety](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Scalar.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.neurovariety_const_sq) the whole line, so [its image is not algebraic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Scalar.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.not_isAlgebraicImage_const_sq), while $f_w(x)=wx$ is closed. Behind it sits the general [neurovariety](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.neurovariety), a new Zariski-closure API over any field that is invariant under [linear changes of coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ZariskiClosure.html#MvPolynomial.zariskiClosure_image_linearEquiv) and under [zero-padding to a larger degree bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.neurovariety_extendByZero_comp). Over the reals, the coefficient image of every jointly polynomial model is [semialgebraic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Semialgebraic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.isSemialgebraic_range_polynomialCoefficientCoordinates). That theorem is stated under projection closure, which the library's Tarski–Seidenberg instance supplies for ℝ.

The bridge between coefficients and functions is complete. Over an infinite domain, two parameters of a polynomial model [realise the same function exactly when their coefficients agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Presentation.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.polynomialCoefficientMap_eq_iff). A supplied coefficient map [round-trips through a joint polynomial tuple](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/FromCoefficients.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.exists_polynomialRealization_of_coefficients), and the comparison [transports from ℝ to ℂ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Map.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.polynomialRealization_map_ofReal_eq_iff). For bias-free monomial MLPs, the output polynomials are [homogeneous of degree the product of the hidden exponents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Homogeneous.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec.isHomogeneous_inputPolynomial_polynomialRealizeWeights_monomial), and permuting or rescaling hidden neurons [preserves the realised function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Symmetry.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec.monomial_hiddenSymmetrySetoid_le_parameterSetoid). That gives Lemma 4 of Finkel–Rodriguez–Wu–Yahl, with identifiability modulo these symmetries defined but not asserted anywhere.

Underneath all of this is the model language. It covers neuromanifolds, fibres and the [quotient equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Basic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.quotientEquiv), composition and finite chains, and dense, activation and valid-convolution layers. It also includes [MLP specifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Basic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec) with concatenation and convolutional chains, both shown jointly polynomial for polynomial activations (TauCeti#24430, TauCeti#25052). With identity activations, linear convolutional networks realise [the products of their convolution matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Convolution/Linear.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.ConvolutionSpec.realizedFunctions_realizeWeights_of_activation_eq_id).
