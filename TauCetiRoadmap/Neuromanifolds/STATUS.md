## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 3 and 4 are done: polynomial models with faithful coefficient coordinates, homogeneity, change of field and symmetries, and neurovarieties with both scalar examples. Layers 1 and 2 are partial. Their substance is proved, but a few named statements are still missing: the degenerate parameter spaces in Layer 1, and the general finite-chain API with chain readings of MLPs in Layer 2.

### Named results

- **The half-line example** — the real model $g_w(x)=w^2x$ has coefficient image $[0,\infty)$ and neurovariety all of ℝ, so [its image is not algebraic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Scalar.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.not_isAlgebraicImage_const_sq). Polynomiality does not force a Zariski-closed image.
- **Coefficients detect functions** — over an infinite domain, two parameters of a jointly polynomial model [realise the same function exactly when their input coefficients agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Presentation.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.polynomialCoefficientMap_eq_iff).
- **Independence of the presentation** — two faithful linear presentations of the same model [have algebraic coefficient images simultaneously](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.isAlgebraicImage_iff_isAlgebraicImage_of_injective). They are related by a linear map that [is a bijection between their neurovarieties](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.exists_linearMap_comp_eq_bijOn_neurovariety), which goes beyond the coordinate changes and degree padding the README requires.
- **Semialgebraicity of polynomial neuromanifolds** — the finite coefficient image of every real jointly polynomial model [is semialgebraic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Semialgebraic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.isSemialgebraic_range_polynomialCoefficientCoordinates). This holds under projection closure, which the library's Tarski–Seidenberg instance supplies over ℝ.
- **Homogeneity of monomial MLPs** — a bias-free MLP with hidden exponents $r_i$ has output polynomials [homogeneous of degree $\prod_i r_i$](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Homogeneous.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec.isHomogeneous_inputPolynomial_polynomialRealizeWeights_monomial), and [any joint polynomial presentation of it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Homogeneous.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec.isHomogeneous_inputPolynomial_of_polynomialRealization_eq) is homogeneous of that degree too.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [neurovariety](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.neurovariety), the Zariski closure of the coefficient image, is kept apart from the closed-image predicate [`IsAlgebraicImage`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.IsAlgebraicImage). A linear map with a linear left inverse on a set [commutes with its Zariski closure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ZariskiClosure.html#MvPolynomial.zariskiClosure_image_linearMap_of_leftInvOn), and the independence results rest on that fact.
- The [polynomial coefficient map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Basic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.polynomialCoefficientMap) is the meeting point of the README's two entry points. Supplied coefficient maps, whether in monomial coordinates or in [any linear presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/LinearPresentation.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.polynomialFromLinearPresentation), round-trip through joint polynomial tuples.
- [MLP specifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Basic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec) and convolutional chains have affine and bias-free parameter spaces, concatenation, transport along ring homomorphisms, and a [first-layer splitting as a composition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Composition.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec.realize_comp_consEquiv). With these, the paper's monomial networks instantiate the general theory layer by layer.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layers 3 and 4 are done.** Every target the README names there is proved, including the two items it listed as still to build: §3.6 homogeneity with its consequences, and §3.7 transport of MLP and [convolutional](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Convolution/Map.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.ConvolutionSpec.map_polynomialRealize) specifications. **Layer 1 is partial.** The neuromanifold, fibres, the quotient equivalence and unique factorisation are proved. So are reparametrisation (the image is [kept exactly when every fibre is met](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Reparametrization.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.realizedFunctions_comp_eq_iff)), restriction, and post- and precomposition, with [equivalence preserved and reflected](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Transport.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.parameterSetoid_postcomp) under injective outputs or surjective inputs. What is missing is §1.1's fibre-nonemptiness criterion, the empty and one-point parameter cases, and the one-way implications that need no injectivity or surjectivity. **Layer 2 is partial.** Composition, the concrete layers, MLPs with concatenation and an explicit parameter-product identification, convolutional chains and finite chains split at either end are all proved. What is missing is the splitting of a chain of length $m+n$, the length-one chain, and statements that MLP and convolutional realisations are chains of their layers. §2.1's one-step formula exists in first-layer form, not in the README's last-layer form.

## The frontier

- **MLPs and convolutional chains as chains (§2.2)** — state that each realisation is the `chain` of its dense or convolution layers and activation layers. Add the length-one chain and the splitting of a chain of length $m+n$ into two sub-chains. Both chains and the existing MLP fold formulas are built from `Fin.dfoldl`, so this should be mostly bookkeeping.
- **The last-layer one-step formula (§2.1)** — read `A.realize`, through `splitParameters`, as the composition of the lower layers, the junction activation and the final dense layer, and do the same for the bias-free realisation. The first-layer analogue already gives the same induction, so this target mainly matches the README's wording.
- **Layer 1 leftovers** — state that the fibre over $g$ is nonempty exactly when $g$ is realised. Also state that an empty parameter space gives an empty neuromanifold, that a one-point space gives a single function, and that postcomposition and precomposition always preserve equivalence. The library currently leaves these to the corresponding Mathlib lemmas without naming them.
- **Beyond the roadmap** — generic identifiability, activation-degree thresholds (Theorem 18 of Finkel–Rodriguez–Wu–Yahl), dimension and degree are explicitly out of scope. The symmetry, identifiability and presentation-independence API is the natural base for a follow-up.
