## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Milestones 2, 3 and 4 have landed: the layer and MLP constructors, polynomial models with faithful coefficient coordinates, and neurovarieties with both acceptance examples. Milestone 1 is done apart from its functoriality items: the general image formulas for restriction and for post- and precomposition are not stated as such.

### Named results

- **The half-line example** — the real model $g_w(x)=w^2x$ has coefficient image $[0,\infty)$ and neurovariety all of ℝ, so [its image is not algebraic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Scalar.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.not_isAlgebraicImage_const_sq). This is the roadmap's witness that polynomiality does not force a Zariski-closed image.
- **Coefficients detect functions** — over an infinite domain, two parameters of a jointly polynomial model [realise the same function exactly when their input coefficients agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Presentation.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.polynomialCoefficientMap_eq_iff), so the neuromanifold is [the evaluation of the coefficient image](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Presentation.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.realizedFunctions_polynomialRealization).
- **Semialgebraicity of polynomial neuromanifolds** — the finite coefficient image of every real jointly polynomial model [is semialgebraic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Semialgebraic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.isSemialgebraic_range_polynomialCoefficientCoordinates). The statement assumes projection closure, which the library's Tarski–Seidenberg instance supplies over ℝ.
- **Homogeneity of monomial MLPs** — a bias-free MLP with hidden exponents $r_i$ has output polynomials [homogeneous of degree $\prod_i r_i$](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Homogeneous.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec.isHomogeneous_inputPolynomial_polynomialRealizeWeights_monomial), which is $r^{L-1}$ for a common exponent.
- **Hidden-neuron symmetries** — permuting and rescaling hidden neurons, with the compensating power on the next layer, [preserves the realised function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Symmetry.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec.monomial_hiddenSymmetrySetoid_le_parameterSetoid) (Lemma 4 of Finkel–Rodriguez–Wu–Yahl). No converse is claimed.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [neurovariety](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.neurovariety) is the Zariski closure of the coefficient image. It is kept separate from the closed-image predicate [`IsAlgebraicImage`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.IsAlgebraicImage). Both are invariant under [linear coordinate changes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ZariskiClosure.html#MvPolynomial.zariskiClosure_image_linearEquiv) and under [zero-padding to a larger degree bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Neurovariety.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.neurovariety_extendByZero_comp), so neither depends on the chosen presentation.
- The [polynomial coefficient map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/Basic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.polynomialCoefficientMap) lands in a product of bounded-degree polynomial spaces, with a uniform input-degree bound. A supplied coefficient map [round-trips through it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Polynomial/FromCoefficients.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.exists_polynomialRealization_of_coefficients), so the two entry points of the README meet.
- [MLP specifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/MLP/Basic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.MLPSpec) and convolutional specifications come with affine and bias-free parameter spaces, flattening to scalar coordinates, concatenation, and proofs that they are jointly polynomial for polynomial activations. This lets the paper's monomial networks instantiate the general theory directly.

### Roadmap coverage

**Milestone 1** has the neuromanifold, fibres, parameter equivalence, the [quotient equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Basic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.quotientEquiv), unique factorisation and transport along parameter equivalences. It is partial: the image formulas for reparametrisation, restriction, postcomposition and precomposition (items 3–4) appear only implicitly, through composition. **Milestone 2** is done as far as the source shows. It covers composition with associativity and units, finite chains, dense, activation (power, ReLU, tanh) and valid-convolution layers, MLPs with concatenation, and convolutional chains. It also covers the linear examples: [two dense layers realise the products $BA$](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Dense.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.realizedFunctions_compose_mulVec_mulVec), and linear convolutional chains realise [products of convolution matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Convolution/Linear.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.ConvolutionSpec.realizedFunctions_realizeWeights_of_activation_eq_id). **Milestone 3** is done, including the round-trip, degree padding, basis change, the homogeneous presentation, ℝ→ℂ transport and [identifiability modulo symmetries](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Neuromanifold/Basic.html#TauCeti.Neuromanifold.forall_identifiableAt_iff_injective). **Milestone 4** is done: the closure API, the two invariance results, semialgebraicity over ℝ and both scalar examples.

## The frontier

- **Functoriality of neuromanifolds (Milestone 1, items 3–4)** — state the image of $f\circ r$ (with equality when $r$ is surjective) and restriction to parameter subsets, including empty ones. Then add postcomposition by $h$, which preserves and reflects equivalence when $h$ is injective, and precomposition by $a$, which reflects equivalence when $a$ is surjective, with the empty- and singleton-parameter cases.
- **An audit of Milestone 2's fine print** — check that permutation compatibility of activation layers and the explicit parameter-product identification under concatenation match the README's wording. The pieces are present, but this report has not checked each statement against the specification.
- **Beyond the roadmap** — generic identifiability, activation-degree thresholds (Theorem 18 of Finkel–Rodriguez–Wu–Yahl), dimension and degree are explicitly out of scope. The symmetry and identifiability API built here is the natural starting point for a follow-up roadmap.
