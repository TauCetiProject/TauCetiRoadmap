## GlobalQuadraticForms: 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-26 (`17200a0` to `759eb3e`)

Hasse–Minkowski over an arbitrary number field now has two of its four cases, each conditional on the ranks below it. [Quaternary descent](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/QuaternaryDescent.html#QuadraticForm.anisotropic_baseChange_iff_quaternary) (O'Meara 58:7) shows that a regular quaternary form whose plain discriminant is the class of a nonsquare `d` is isotropic over `F` exactly when it is isotropic over `F(√d)`. With O'Meara 42:12 on [ternary subspaces](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Quaternary/TernarySubspace.html#QuadraticMap.Nondegenerate.not_anisotropic_restrict_iff_of_finrank_ge_three) and localization along a tower of completions, it yields [the quaternary case](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Quaternary.html#QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_quaternary): a regular quaternary form isotropic at every finite and real place is isotropic, provided the ternary case holds over every number field. The [induction step in rank at least five](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/HigherRank.html#QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_of_five_le_finrank) approximates a vector of a binary summand and only then defines the represented scalar, as the roadmap requires. The binary and ternary cases, which need the global square theorem and the quadratic Hasse norm theorem, are missing.

The finiteness that the rank-five step uses is proved: a form of rank at least three is [isotropic at all but finitely many finite places](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/AnisotropicPlaces.html#QuadraticForm.finite_setOfPred_anisotropic_atFinitePlace), by a residue-field and Hensel argument, while `⟨1, 1⟩` over `ℚ` is [anisotropic at infinitely many finite places](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/BinaryObstruction.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.infinite_anisotropic_sumTwoSquares_atFinitePlace), which is why rank four needs its own proof.

Most of the window built the local layer. Forms are localized at finite, real and complex places as genuine base changes, the four local predicates are defined, and complex places are proved to add nothing (TauCeti#6627). Real places have Sylvester's law, the determinant-sign formula and the archimedean Hasse sign (TauCeti#7994), resting on the [real Hilbert symbol](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/HilbertSymbol/Archimedean.html#TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_real). Vector weak approximation and square-class neighbourhoods at every finite place, dyadic ones included, feed the rank-five step; finite support of localized Hilbert symbols and a global element that is a nonsquare at prescribed places begin Hilbert sign prescription. No finite-place Hasse invariant exists yet: Quadratic Form Invariants has not supplied the `{±1}`-valued local one.

## GlobalQuadraticForms: 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-01 (`759eb3e` to `8a32441`)

The finite-place Hasse invariant now exists. The [Hasse sign at a finite place](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/FiniteHasse.html#QuadraticForm.finiteHasse) `v` is the `{±1}`-valued local Hasse invariant of the localized form. On any global diagonalization `⟨a₁, …, aₙ⟩` it is [the product](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/FiniteHasse.html#QuadraticForm.finiteHasse_eq_prod_hilbertSymbol) `∏_{i<j} (aᵢ, aⱼ)_v`, and it obeys the [orthogonal-sum formula](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/FiniteHasse.html#QuadraticForm.finiteHasse_prod) and the scaling and negation formulas. It is also [trivial at all but finitely many finite places](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/HasseSupport.html#QuadraticForm.hasFiniteMulSupport_finiteHasse). The adapter that restates local classification in global notation has not been written.

This unblocked the invariant carrier. [`GlobalFormInvariants`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Invariants.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants) records a rank, a global discriminant, finite Hasse signs and real positive indices, and [admissibility](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Invariants.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants.IsAdmissible) keeps both small-rank exceptions as separate conditions. Explicit rank-one and rank-two systems show that finite support and product one alone [do not imply admissibility](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Invariants.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants.isAdmissible_rankTwo_iff_eq_empty). Every admissible system is [realized place by place](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/LocalRealization.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants.IsAdmissible.exists_isLocalRealization) by regular forms over each finite completion and over `ℝ`, which is the first step of the existence theorem. Two things are still missing: that the invariants of an actual global form are admissible, which needs the value of the Hilbert product formula, and the construction of a global form from local ones.

Smaller work prepares Hilbert sign prescription. At a real place, the symbol of two global units is `-1` exactly when [both are negative](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/ArchimedeanSymbol.html#TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_unitAtRealPlace_eq_neg_one_iff). There is a [field unit negative at every real place](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Approximation/Weak.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.exists_fieldUnit_negative_at), and an element of odd valuation is a [nonsquare in the completion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/AdicValuation/ValuativeRel.html#IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum.not_isSquare_adicCompletion_of_valuation_eq_exp_of_not_even). Nothing new landed for Hasse–Minkowski itself.

## GlobalQuadraticForms: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05 (`8a32441` to `047d72b`)

Local classification is now stated in global notation, and it closes Layer 2. At a finite place, two localizations are isometric [exactly when](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Classification.html#QuadraticForm.equivalent_atFinitePlace_iff_finrank_eq_and_discr_eq_and_finiteHasse_eq) their rank, localized discriminant and finite Hasse sign agree. With Sylvester's law at the real places, this gives [local equivalence by invariants](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Classification.html#QuadraticForm.locallyEquivalent_iff_finrank_eq_and_discr_eq_and_finiteHasse_eq_and_realPositiveIndex_eq): two regular forms are isometric at every finite and real place exactly when those data and the real positive indices match. A regular form now has its [system of local invariants](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/FormInvariants.html#QuadraticForm.globalInvariants), and that system is [admissible exactly when](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/FormInvariants.html#QuadraticForm.isAdmissible_globalInvariants_iff) the rank is positive and the Hasse product is one. So 3.3 now waits only on the value of the Hilbert product formula.

Hilbert sign prescription got its central object, the [idele Hilbert character](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/IdeleCharacter.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.ideleHilbertCharacter) `φ_b(x) = ∏_v (x_v, b)_v`. Its kernel [has index two](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/IdeleCharacter.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.index_ker_ideleHilbertCharacter) whenever `b` is a nonsquare at some finite or real place. Identifying that kernel with `Kˣ · N(𝕀_E)` still needs the product formula and the norm index, neither of which is in the library. The work behind this gave the Hilbert symbol finite support and multiplicativity across all completions.

Several steps of the existence proof (O'Meara 72:1) are now in place, although the theorem is not. The [correction planes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/CorrectionPlanes.html#QuadraticForm.equivalent_atFinitePlace_binary_one_neg_iff_hilbertSymbol_eq_one) `⟨1, -β⟩` and `⟨α, -αβ⟩` are isometric at `v` exactly when `(α, β)_v = 1`. ## GlobalQuadraticForms: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-13 (`047d72b` to `0a4ee41`)

The first worked example in the roadmap is done: real signatures alone do not classify forms over `ℚ`. The form `⟨1, 1, -3⟩` is [isotropic at the real place](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/TernaryObstruction.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_sumTwoSquaresSubThreeSq_atRealPlace) but [anisotropic at 3](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/TernaryObstruction.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.anisotropic_sumTwoSquaresSubThreeSq_atFinitePlace), and so [anisotropic over `ℚ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/TernaryObstruction.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.anisotropic_sumTwoSquaresSubThreeSq). It is therefore [not isometric](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/TernaryObstruction.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.not_equivalent_sumTwoSquaresSubThreeSq_sumTwoSquaresSubSq) to `⟨1, 1, -1⟩`, even though the two forms agree at every real place. The local input is that [`(-1, p)_p = -1`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/OddResidue/Padic.html#Padic.hilbertSymbol_neg_one_eq_neg_one_of_mod_four_eq_three) when `p ≡ 3 mod 4`. Anisotropy over `ℚ` follows directly from anisotropy at 3, since a rational zero would stay a zero there, so this example does not use Hasse–Minkowski.

Hilbert sign prescription took its next step. [Idele norms lie in the kernel](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/IdeleCharacter.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.range_ideleNormMap_le_ker_ideleHilbertCharacter) of the idele Hilbert character `φ_b`, for any number field `L ⊇ K` in which `b` is a square. This is the norm half of step 3 in the roadmap's pinned proof. It rests on a local lemma: if `b` is a square in a commutative `F`-algebra `M`, then [`(b, N_{M/F}(c)) = 1`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/HilbertSymbol/ExtensionNorm.html#TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_normUnits_eq_one) for every unit `c` of `M`. The other half, `Kˣ ⊆ ker φ_b`, is the product formula, and it is still missing, as is the norm index.

## GlobalQuadraticForms: 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-25 (`0a4ee41` to `69cd1ab`)

Hilbert sign prescription (O'Meara 71:19a) is now proved without extra hypotheses. Given `b` and an even set of finite and real places at which `b` is a nonsquare, [some `a` has](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/SignPrescription.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.exists_hilbertSymbol_eq_ite) `(a, b)_v = -1` exactly at those places. The proof is the roadmap's pinned route. The [kernel of the idele Hilbert character](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/SignPrescription.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.sup_range_ideleNormMap_eq_ker_ideleHilbertCharacter) `φ_b` is identified with `Kˣ · N(𝕀_L)`, using Hilbert reciprocity and Class Field Theory's norm index for `K(√b)/K` (TauCeti#17645). The [form with a free second argument](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/SignPrescription.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.exists_pair_hilbertSymbol_eq_ite) (71:19) follows by weak approximation. The product formula also gives the [Hasse product formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/ProductFormula.html#QuadraticForm.globalInvariants_hasseProduct_eq_one): the finite and real Hasse signs of a regular form multiply to one. So the invariants of every positive-rank form [are admissible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/ProductFormula.html#QuadraticForm.isAdmissible_globalInvariants), which closes Layer 3.

With sign prescription available, the existence theorem (O'Meara 72:1) is [reduced to one global representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/PrescribedLocalForms.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants.IsLocalRealization.exists_locallyRepresents_prod). For an admissible system with local realizations, it builds `W`, `α` and `β` such that `⟨α, -αβ⟩` is represented by `⟨1, -β⟩ ⊥ W` at every place. If that representation also holds over `K`, a global form with the prescribed invariants exists. The step still missing is form representation (6.2), which comes after Hasse–Minkowski. Over `ℚ(√5)`, the explicit form `⟨1, θ, θ⟩` has [two different real signatures and Hasse sign `-1` only at `(2)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/WorkedExamples/Sqrt5.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.Sqrt5.globalInvariants_weightedSumSquares_one_self_self). It was written down by hand, not produced by the existence theorem.

Hasse–Minkowski got a counterexample: for every real place `w₀` there are forms that are [isotropic everywhere except at `w₀`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/DefiniteObstruction.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.exists_anisotropic_and_not_anisotropic_away_from_realPlace) and anisotropic over `K`. Its input is that a regular form is anisotropic at a real place exactly when it is definite there. Over a nonarchimedean local field, regular forms are now [classified by rank, signed discriminant and Clifford invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/Witt.html#TauCeti.RegularFormClass.eq_iff_rank_eq_and_signedDiscr_eq_and_cliffordInvariant_eq). The binary and ternary cases of Hasse–Minkowski are still missing.
