- **Local equivalence by invariants** — two regular forms are isometric at every finite and real place exactly when their ranks, their discriminants at every finite place, their finite Hasse signs and their real positive indices agree ([`QuadraticForm.locallyEquivalent_iff_finrank_eq_and_discr_eq_and_finiteHasse_eq_and_realPositiveIndex_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Classification.html#QuadraticForm.locallyEquivalent_iff_finrank_eq_and_discr_eq_and_finiteHasse_eq_and_realPositiveIndex_eq)).
- **Almost-all triviality of the Hasse sign** — the finite Hasse sign of a regular global form is `1` at all but finitely many finite places (O'Meara 63:14) ([`QuadraticForm.hasFiniteMulSupport_finiteHasse`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/HasseSupport.html#QuadraticForm.hasFiniteMulSupport_finiteHasse)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The finite Hasse sign** — the local Hasse invariant of the localized form, computed from any global diagonalization. Its sum, scaling and negation formulas are what the existence argument's plane swap uses ([`QuadraticForm.finiteHasse`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/FiniteHasse.html#QuadraticForm.finiteHasse)).
- **The invariants of a form** — every regular form has a [system of local invariants](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/FormInvariants.html#QuadraticForm.globalInvariants) in the admissible-invariant carrier [`GlobalFormInvariants`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Invariants.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants). That system is admissible exactly when the rank is positive and the Hasse product is one, the target of the eventual classification.
- **The idele Hilbert character** — `φ_b(x) = ∏_v (x_v, b)_v` on the idele group, with kernel of index two when `b` is a nonsquare somewhere. This is the character through which the roadmap's pinned proof of sign prescription runs ([`ideleHilbertCharacter`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/IdeleCharacter.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.ideleHilbertCharacter)).
