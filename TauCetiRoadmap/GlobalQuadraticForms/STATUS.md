### Named results

- **The quaternary case of Hasse–Minkowski** — a regular quaternary form over a number field that is isotropic at every finite and real place is isotropic, provided the ternary case holds over every number field ([`QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_quaternary`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Quaternary.html#QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_quaternary)).
- **Quaternary descent** — a regular quaternary form whose plain discriminant is the class of a nonsquare `d` is isotropic over `F` exactly when it is isotropic over `F(√d)` (O'Meara 58:7) ([`QuadraticForm.anisotropic_baseChange_iff_quaternary`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/QuaternaryDescent.html#QuadraticForm.anisotropic_baseChange_iff_quaternary)).
- **The Hasse–Minkowski induction step in rank at least five** — a locally isotropic regular form of rank `n ≥ 5` is isotropic once the theorem holds in rank `n − 1` ([`QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_of_five_le_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/HigherRank.html#QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_of_five_le_finrank)).
- **Almost-all triviality of the Hasse sign** — the finite Hasse sign of a regular global form is `1` at all but finitely many finite places (O'Meara 63:14) ([`QuadraticForm.hasFiniteMulSupport_finiteHasse`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/HasseSupport.html#QuadraticForm.hasFiniteMulSupport_finiteHasse)).
- **Local realization of admissible systems** — every admissible system of invariants is realized by regular forms of the prescribed rank over every finite completion and over `ℝ` at every real place ([`IsAdmissible.exists_isLocalRealization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/LocalRealization.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants.IsAdmissible.exists_isLocalRealization)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The finite Hasse sign** — the local Hasse invariant of the localized form, computed from any global diagonalization, with the orthogonal-sum, scaling and negation formulas that the existence argument's plane swap needs ([`QuadraticForm.finiteHasse`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/FiniteHasse.html#QuadraticForm.finiteHasse)).
- **Admissible invariant systems** — a rank, one global discriminant, finite Hasse signs and real positive indices, with [admissibility](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Invariants.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants.IsAdmissible) keeping the rank-one and rank-two exceptions as their own conditions. This is the target type of the eventual classification ([`GlobalFormInvariants`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Invariants.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants)).
- **Canonical localizations** — localizations at finite, real and complex places are genuine base changes. They are compatible with isometries, orthogonal sums, discriminants and towers of completions ([`QuadraticForm.atFinitePlace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Localization.html#QuadraticForm.atFinitePlace)).

## The frontier

- **Binary and ternary Hasse–Minkowski** — the binary case needs the global square theorem (4.2), and the ternary case needs the quadratic Hasse norm adapter (4.3). These rest on Class Field Theory's norm index `card_ideleClassNormQuotient` and Hasse norm theorem `isGlobalNorm_iff_isLocalNormEverywhere`, which are not in the library source. With both cases, 5.5 is an induction over pieces that are already proved.
- **Invariants of a global form are admissible (3.3)** — every condition except the product one is now available. The product condition needs the value of the localized Hilbert product formula, through `hilbertProductFormula` and `hilbertSymbol_eq_cohomological`, neither yet supplied.
- **Local classification in global notation (2.3)** — at a finite place, two localizations are isometric exactly when their rank, localized discriminant and finite Hasse sign agree. This is a transport of the supplier's local classification, and nothing blocks it.
- **Hilbert sign prescription (4.4)** — the inputs for the unprescribed form are in place: a unit negative at every real place, odd valuation forcing a local nonsquare, and the real symbol of global units. The existence half needs the norm index.
- **Existence with prescribed local behavior (7.2)** — O'Meara 72:1. It needs sign prescription and form representation (6.2), so it comes after Hasse–Minkowski.
