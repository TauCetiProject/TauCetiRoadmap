### Named results

- **Hilbert sign prescription** — for `b ∈ Kˣ` and an even set of finite and real places at which `b` is a nonsquare, some `a ∈ Kˣ` has `(a, b)_v = -1` exactly at those places (O'Meara 71:19a), proved from the norm index as the roadmap requires ([`exists_hilbertSymbol_eq_ite`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/SignPrescription.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.exists_hilbertSymbol_eq_ite)).
