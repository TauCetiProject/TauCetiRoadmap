## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The local groundwork is largely built: Layer 1 (archimedean signatures and classification) is done, and Layer 0 lacks only the finite-place Hasse invariant. Hasse–Minkowski isotropy has its quaternary case and its rank-at-least-five induction step, each conditional on lower ranks, but not the binary and ternary cases. The invariant carrier, representation and isometry, existence, and classification (Layers 3 and 6 to 8) have not begun.

### Named results

- **Quaternary descent** — a regular quaternary form whose plain discriminant is the class of a nonsquare `d` is isotropic over `F` exactly when it is isotropic over `F(√d)` (O'Meara 58:7), over any field in which `2` is invertible ([`QuadraticForm.anisotropic_baseChange_iff_quaternary`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/QuaternaryDescent.html#QuadraticForm.anisotropic_baseChange_iff_quaternary)).
- **The quaternary case of Hasse–Minkowski** — a regular quaternary form over a number field that is isotropic at every finite and real place is isotropic, provided the ternary case holds over every number field ([`QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_quaternary`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Quaternary.html#QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_quaternary)).
- **The Hasse–Minkowski induction step in rank at least five** — a locally isotropic regular form of rank `n ≥ 5` is isotropic once the theorem holds in rank `n − 1`, with the scalar defined from an approximated vector as the roadmap pins ([`QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_of_five_le_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/HigherRank.html#QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_of_five_le_finrank)).
- **Almost-all isotropy in rank at least three** — such a form is isotropic at all but finitely many finite places ([`QuadraticForm.finite_setOfPred_anisotropic_atFinitePlace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/AnisotropicPlaces.html#QuadraticForm.finite_setOfPred_anisotropic_atFinitePlace)); the bound is sharp, since `⟨1, 1⟩` over `ℚ` is [anisotropic at infinitely many finite places](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/BinaryObstruction.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.infinite_anisotropic_sumTwoSquares_atFinitePlace).
- **Sylvester's law of inertia at a real place** — regular forms become isometric at a real place exactly when their signatures agree, and every signature `(p, q)` is realized by `p⟨1⟩ ⊥ q⟨−1⟩` ([`QuadraticForm.equivalent_atRealPlace_iff_realSignature_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Signature.html#QuadraticForm.equivalent_atRealPlace_iff_realSignature_eq)).

## The frontier

- **Binary and ternary Hasse–Minkowski** — the binary case needs the global square theorem (4.2), the ternary case the quadratic Hasse norm adapter (4.3). These rest respectively on Class Field Theory's idele-class norm index `card_ideleClassNormQuotient` and Hasse norm theorem `isGlobalNorm_iff_isLocalNormEverywhere`, neither yet in Tau Ceti. With these two cases, 5.5 is an induction over pieces already proved.
- **The finite-place Hasse invariant** — define `finiteHasse` from a `{±1}`-valued local Hasse invariant of the localization, prove it is `1` at almost every place (the good-place Hilbert symbol computation is done), and state local classification in global notation. The supplier's `localHasse` does not exist yet; Tau Ceti has only a Brauer-group-valued Hasse invariant of isometry classes.
- **Hilbert sign prescription (4.4)** — the localized product formula needs its value from Hilbert reciprocity through Quadratic Form Invariants' comparison with the cohomological pairing, and sign prescription needs the norm index. The radicand for the unprescribed form is [already available](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Nonsquare.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.exists_fieldUnit_not_isSquare_at_places).
- **The invariant carrier (Layer 3)** — `GlobalFormInvariants`, admissibility with both small-rank exceptions, and admissibility of the invariants of a global form, once `finiteHasse` and the Hasse product formula exist.
- **Representation, isometry, existence and classification (Layers 6 to 8)** — these follow Hasse–Minkowski. For 6.1, the local step that a locally represented scalar `a` makes `⟨−a⟩ ⊥ Q` locally isotropic is [already proved](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Predicates.html#QuadraticForm.LocallyRepresentsScalar.isLocallyIsotropic_smul_sq_prod).
