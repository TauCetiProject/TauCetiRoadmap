## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Existence is proved: every admissible system of local invariants is realized by a global form, though only for number fields in `Type`. Hasse–Minkowski has its quaternary and rank-at-least-five steps, each conditional on lower ranks, but not the binary and ternary cases or the global square theorem behind them. Representation, isometry, uniqueness and the global classification have not begun.

### Named results

- **Realization of admissible systems (O'Meara 72:1)** — a system of rank, discriminant, finite Hasse signs and real positive indices is [the system of a regular global form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Realization.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants.isAdmissible_iff_exists_globalForm) exactly when it is admissible of positive rank. The form can be chosen [isometric to prescribed local forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Realization.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.GlobalFormInvariants.IsAdmissible.exists_globalForm_equivalent_at_places). The proof is an induction on rank that splits off lines, and it does not use Hasse–Minkowski.
- **Hilbert sign prescription** — for `b ∈ Kˣ` and an even set of places where `b` is a nonsquare, some `a` has `(a, b)_v = -1` exactly there (O'Meara 71:19a). It is proved through the norm index for `K(√b)/K` ([`exists_hilbertSymbol_eq_ite`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/SignPrescription.html#TauCeti.NumberField.QuadraticForm.exists_hilbertSymbol_eq_ite)).
- **The Hasse product formula** — the finite and real Hasse signs of a regular form multiply to one, so the invariants of every positive-rank form are admissible ([`QuadraticForm.globalInvariants_hasseProduct_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/ProductFormula.html#QuadraticForm.globalInvariants_hasseProduct_eq_one)).
- **The quaternary case of Hasse–Minkowski** — a regular quaternary form isotropic at every finite and real place is isotropic, provided the ternary case holds over every number field ([`QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_quaternary`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/Quaternary.html#QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_of_isLocallyIsotropic_quaternary)).
- **The kernel of the global-to-local Witt map** — a Witt class vanishes at every finite and real place exactly when it has even dimension, locally square signed discriminant, locally split Clifford invariant and zero real signatures ([`TauCeti.WittRing.globalToLocal_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/Global/WittLocalization.html#TauCeti.WittRing.globalToLocal_eq_zero_iff)).
