## BanachAlgebras: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (`cc73e43` to `273ea28`)

The first work on this roadmap opens two layers at once. For automatic continuity (A5), the [separating space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SeparatingSpace.html#LinearMap.separatingSpace) of a linear map is now defined as the vertical fibre over zero of the closure of its graph. It is [closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SeparatingSpace.html#LinearMap.isClosed_separatingSpace) and has the expected sequential description. The basic theorem is in: a linear map between Banach spaces is [continuous exactly when its separating space is zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SeparatingSpace.html#LinearMap.continuous_iff_separatingSpace_eq_bot), and it [becomes continuous modulo that space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SeparatingSpace.html#LinearMap.continuous_mkQ_comp). The composition and continuous-perturbation formulas the roadmap asks for also landed. The stability lemma, separating ideals and the automatic-continuity theorems themselves have not started.

On the radical (A1), the Jacobson radical of a nonunital algebra is [defined through its unitization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Jacobson/NonUnital.html#TauCeti.nonUnitalJacobson) as a two-sided ideal. It [agrees with the ordinary radical when a unit exists](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Jacobson/NonUnital.html#TauCeti.nonUnitalJacobson_eq_toTwoSided_jacobson) and is [closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Algebra/Jacobson.html#TauCeti.isClosed_nonUnitalJacobson). The headline is the quasinilpotence characterisation: in a complex Banach algebra, a lies in the radical [exactly when every left multiple ba is quasinilpotent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Algebra/Jacobson.html#TauCeti.Ring.mem_jacobson_iff_forall_gelfandRadius_mul_eq_zero), with a [nonunital counterpart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Algebra/Jacobson.html#TauCeti.mem_nonUnitalJacobson_iff_forall_spectralRadius_mul_eq_zero) (TauCeti#19418). Primitive ideals, Rickart's theorem and semisimplicity of the quotient by the radical are still to come.

## BanachAlgebras: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

The headline of this window is [Cohen–Hewitt factorization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/CohenFactorization.html#ContinuousLinearMap.exists_apply_eq_of_mem_topologicalClosure_span) (TauCeti#20671). If a Banach algebra has a left approximate identity bounded by C, then every vector x in the essential part of a Banach left module factors as x = a·y with ‖a‖ ≤ C and y as close to x as desired. The action only has to be bounded, not contractive. As a corollary, the set of products A·E is [already closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/CohenFactorization.html#ContinuousLinearMap.coe_topologicalClosure_span_range_eq). The groundwork came with it: bounded approximate identities described by nets and by finite sets, and [Dixon's combination](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Ring/ApproximateUnit.html#TauCeti.isApproximateUnit_map_add_sub_mul) of a left and a right one into a two-sided one with bound C + D + CD. Only the left-module version is proved. The right-module and bimodule versions are still to come.

Automatic continuity gained [Sinclair's stability lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SeparatingSpace.html#TauCeti.eventuallyConst_separatingSpace), proved by a gliding hump: the decreasing separating spaces of T R₁⋯Rₙ eventually stop shrinking. Its [intertwining form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SeparatingSpace.html#LinearMap.eventuallyConst_topologicalClosure_map_separatingSpace) says the same for the closed images of the separating space under successive multipliers. Separating ideals are next. On the radical side, every algebraic simple module over a unital Banach algebra now [carries a complete norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Simple/Existence.html#IsSimpleModule.exists_complete_norm) under which the action is contractive. This is the first step toward Rickart's theorem, whose uniqueness of the norm and scalar endomorphisms are not yet proved.

For holomorphic functional calculus, the [homology Cauchy theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Cycle/HomologyCauchy.html#TauCeti.Contour.Cycle.homologyCauchyTheorem) now holds for Banach-valued functions, by scalarising through the dual. The [Cauchy formula for higher derivatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Cycle/Residue.html#TauCeti.Contour.Cycle.cauchyIntegralFormula_iteratedDeriv_smul_nullHomologous) and cycle norm bounds came with it, and so did the fact that [weak holomorphy implies holomorphy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/WeaklyHolomorphic.html#TauCeti.differentiableOn_of_forall_dual). The calculus itself has not started.
