## StableReduction: 2026-09-11 to 2026-09-26 (`f5c0fe1` to `759eb3e`)

The combinatorial core of the Artin–Winters argument is in place. For a numerical type of genus g ≥ 2 and every prime ℓ > 768g − 768, the ℓ-torsion of its weighted Picard group has 𝔽_ℓ-dimension at most g ([`finrank_torsion_le_arithmeticGenus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Picard/Torsion/Genus.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.finrank_torsion_le_arithmeticGenus)), slightly strengthening Stacks Proposition 55.7.4; for minimal types the bound is the first Betti number of the intersection graph. Its inputs are the multiplicity bound mᵢ|aᵢⱼ| ≤ 768g − 768 on minimal types ([`IsMinimal.multiplicity_mul_abs_intersection_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/NumericalType/MultiplicityBound.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.IsMinimal.multiplicity_mul_abs_intersection_le)) and the classification of connected proper sets of (−2)-components as chains, forks or the shapes E₆, E₇, E₈ ([`exists_chain_or_fork_or_exceptional`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/NumericalType/Classification.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.exists_chain_or_fork_or_exceptional)), built configuration by configuration, from negative semidefiniteness of the intersection form to the exclusion of affine E₈ (TauCeti#7596, TauCeti#8207). Contracting (−1)-indices, which preserves the genus and embeds the Picard group, reduces everything to minimal types. The theory is still abstract: no regular model has a numerical type yet.

Most of Layer 0 is in place. Finite separable extensions of a DVR with a chosen place form a category in which any two have a common refinement ([`FiniteDVRExtension.nonempty_binaryCofan`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/DVRExtension/CommonRefinement.html#TauCeti.FiniteDVRExtension.nonempty_binaryCofan)). Models carry an explicit identification of their generic fibre and form a category with base change along such extensions ([`Model.baseChangeFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Model/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.Model.baseChangeFunctor)); generic and special fibres are compared with scheme-theoretic fibres. Relative dimension at most d has its full API, including the bound d + e under composition, and families of curves are defined ([`FamilyOfCurves`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Family.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FamilyOfCurves)). Pure relative dimension still lacks base change, and the module operations needed for differentials and Fitting ideals are missing.

Outside Layers 0 and 6 there is one result: the node model xy = πⁿ over a DVR is regular at its singular point exactly when n = 1 ([`isRegularLocalRing_localization_quotient_X_mul_X_sub_C_pow_iff_of_irreducible`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Node.html#TauCeti.isRegularLocalRing_localization_quotient_X_mul_X_sub_C_pow_iff_of_irreducible)), the regularity half of the test case that opens Layer 4. Nodal families, cohomology in families, blowups, stable curves and stable maps have not started.

## StableReduction: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

Layer 1 has begun, with the local model of a node. For the algebra of xy = a over any ring ([`NodeAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Node/Basic.html#TauCeti.NodeAlgebra)), the window proves that it is flat, finitely presented and of pure relative dimension one ([`NodeAlgebra.pureRelativeDimension_spec`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Node.html#TauCeti.NodeAlgebra.pureRelativeDimension_spec)), smooth wherever either coordinate is invertible, and compatible with base change. Its Kähler differentials are presented by the single relation y dx + x dy = 0, their first Fitting ideal is generated by the two coordinates, and so the locus that ideal cuts out has coordinate ring R/(a) ([`NodeAlgebra.differentialFittingQuotientEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Node/Jacobian/Locus.html#TauCeti.NodeAlgebra.differentialFittingQuotientEquiv)). Underneath is a general theory of Fitting ideals of finite modules, shown independent of the chosen presentation ([`fittingIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FittingIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.fittingIdeal)). All of this concerns the one model: syntomic morphisms, the singular locus of a general morphism and the nodal predicate itself are not there yet.

On the numerical side, minimal types of genus one are now partly classified. With more than one component, a type is minimal of genus one exactly when every component is a (−2)-index ([`isMinimal_and_arithmeticGenus_eq_one_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/NumericalType/GenusOne.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.isMinimal_and_arithmeticGenus_eq_one_iff)). When its intersection graph is not a tree, it is a single cycle with constant multiplicities and weights, the I_n configuration. Tree-shaped graphs are not yet classified. The degree-zero classes of Pic(T) turn out to be exactly its torsion ([`degree_eq_zero_iff_exists_smul_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Picard/DegreeZero.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.degree_eq_zero_iff_exists_smul_eq_zero)).

Layer 0 now has base change for pure relative dimension ([`PureRelativeDimension.of_isPullback`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/PureRelativeDimension.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.PureRelativeDimension.of_isPullback)), proved by showing that pure-dimensionality survives field extension. A first piece of local intersection theory also landed for Layer 4: lengths of R/(f, g) in two-dimensional local rings, additive in g along an irreducible f ([`length_quotient_span_pair_mul_eq_add_of_prime`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Intersection.html#TauCeti.length_quotient_span_pair_mul_eq_add_of_prime)).

## StableReduction: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-05 (`e440f4e` to `1d09589`)

The test case that opens Layer 4 is now worked out in the affine setting. The blowup of the node xy = πⁿ⁺² along (π, x, y) is built as Proj of the Rees algebra and covered by explicit charts: one is the node xy = πⁿ again, and the two others are xt = π. The blowup is flat, locally finitely presented and of pure relative dimension one ([`NodeAlgebra.flat_blowup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Node.html#TauCeti.NodeAlgebra.flat_blowup)). Over a DVR, all of its local rings are regular exactly when n ≤ 1 ([`NodeAlgebra.isRegularLocalRing_stalk_blowup_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Node.html#TauCeti.NodeAlgebra.isRegularLocalRing_stalk_blowup_iff)), so a single blowup resolves xy = π² and xy = π³. Underneath are affine blowup algebras A[I/a], with their universal property, identified with the standard charts of the Rees algebra's Proj ([`reesAlgebra.awayEquivAffineBlowup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/ReesAlgebra/AffineBlowup.html#reesAlgebra.awayEquivAffineBlowup)). There is still no blowup of an ideal sheaf on a scheme, and nothing yet on exceptional divisors, strict transforms or iterating the step.

Layer 1 now has the relative singular locus on schemes. Relative differentials are quasi-coherent and finitely presented, Fitting ideal sheaves exist ([`Scheme.Modules.fittingIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/FittingIdeal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.fittingIdeal)), and Sing is defined as Fitt₁(Ω) for flat, finitely presented curves over an affine base ([`Scheme.singularLocus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/SingularLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.singularLocus)). It is empty for smooth relative curves, and for the node chart it is Spec R/(a). Standard syntomic algebras of relative dimension n also landed, closed under base change and localization ([`IsStandardSyntomicOfRelativeDimension`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Syntomic/StandardSyntomic.html#TauCeti.Algebra.IsStandardSyntomicOfRelativeDimension)). Syntomic morphisms of schemes and the nodal predicate are still missing.

Minimal numerical types of genus zero are classified: there is just one, a single component of multiplicity one, weight one and genus zero ([`isMinimal_and_arithmeticGenus_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/NumericalType/Genus/Zero.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.isMinimal_and_arithmeticGenus_eq_zero_iff)). The special fibre of a model is identified with the zero scheme of π. Its irreducible components are the codimension-one points where π vanishes, which support the divisor of π ([`mem_support_principalDivisor_appTop`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/SpecialFiber/Components.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.mem_support_principalDivisor_appTop)). This is a first step toward the components and multiplicities of Layer 5.
