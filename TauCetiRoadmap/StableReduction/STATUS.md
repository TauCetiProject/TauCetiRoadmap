### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Nodal families**: syntomic of relative dimension one with unramified singular locus, stable under base change, fpqc-local, and split over one separable extension on a proper curve over a field ([`AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Node/Relative.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne)). Prestable curves, pointed curves and prestable maps are built on it.
- **Intersection numbers**: the degree of a line-bundle class pulled back to a curve ([`LineBundleClass.intersectionNumber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.intersectionNumber)). It is additive, symmetric for properly meeting Cartier divisors, and divisible by the weight of the curve: the raw material for the numerical type of a regular model.
- **Dual graphs and stabilization**: abstract dual graphs with clutching and tail-and-bridge contraction. Stabilization terminates in the stable range and its result does not depend on the order of contractions ([`DualGraph.nonempty_iso_of_reflTransGen_of_isStable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/DualGraph/Stabilization.html#TauCeti.DualGraph.nonempty_iso_of_reflTransGen_of_isStable)). Nothing yet attaches a dual graph to an actual curve.

- **Layers 1 and 4 are partial.** Layer 1 has the nodal predicate, its fibrewise criterion and descent, node splitting, and ingredients of the étale local normal form, but not the normal form itself, normalization of nodal curves, or dual graphs of actual curves. It lacks the projection formula, negative semidefiniteness of the vertical intersection matrix, resolution of general models and contraction.
- **Layer 6 is partial**: all combinatorics are proved; the geometric comparisons and the nodal conclusion are missing.

Layer 2 has [flat base change of cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/FlatBaseChange.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.bijective_cohomologyBaseChange) in every degree and, on two-affine covers, [semicontinuity of `H¹`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/Semicontinuity.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.upperSemicontinuous_finrank_cohomology_one_fiber) and base change of `H⁰` when `H¹` vanishes and the sections over the overlap are flat. It lacks the general base-change theorem, Grauert's theorem, the genus in families, the dualizing sheaf and relative ampleness. Layer 3 has prestable and pointed families but no stability. Layer 5 has the special-fibre decomposition but no regular or minimal model. Layers 10 and 11 have prestable maps, contracted components, a degree of a map, evaluation maps and decorated graphs, which restabilize in at most one step after a marking is forgotten, but no `IsStableMap`.
- **Layer 9 has only its first step**, extending rational points of a proper model to sections. **Layer 8 is untouched.**

## The frontier

- **Étale local normal form of a node (Layer 1)**: assemble the Hessian criterion, the étale charts from a split quadratic and the étale-local singular locus into a chart uv = πⁿ over an étale extension of the DVR.
- **Intersection theory on a regular model (Layers 4 to 6)**: negative semidefiniteness of the intersection matrix of special-fibre components, then the numerical type of a regular model.
- **Resolution and regular models (Layers 4 and 5)**: the node `xy = πⁿ` is resolved; a general normal proper model needs normalized blowups at closed points, with those normalizations finite over an arbitrary DVR. Scheme normalization exists, and for an integral scheme it is [finite when the affine rings are Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Finite.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isFinite_fromNormalization_of_isJapanese).
- **Genus-one nodal reduction (Layer 7)**: the minimal types are classified; what is missing is the minimal regular model to apply them to.
- **Stability of curves and maps (Layers 3 and 10)**: `StableFamily` and `IsStableMap` need dual graphs of actual geometric fibres, which wait on the normalization of nodal curves.
