### Named results

- **Nodal reduction in genus zero**: a regular proper curve of genus zero over the fraction field of a DVR that has a divisor of degree one is ℙ¹, and so has smooth reduction ([`HasNodalReduction.of_genus_eq_zero_of_divisor_degree_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/GenusZero.html#TauCeti.HasNodalReduction.of_genus_eq_zero_of_divisor_degree_eq_one)). The roadmap's version replaces the divisor by an extension of degree at most two.
- **Nodes split after one extension**: all nodes of a proper nodal curve over a field become rational over one finite separable extension ([`exists_split_singularLocus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Node/Splitting.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.exists_split_singularLocus)). The singular locus is finite étale.
- **The prime-torsion bound for numerical types**: Stacks Proposition 55.7.4 for every prime ℓ > 768g − 768. A numerical type of genus g ≥ 2 has dim Pic(T)[ℓ] ≤ g ([`NumericalType.finrank_torsion_le_arithmeticGenus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Picard/Torsion/Genus.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.finrank_torsion_le_arithmeticGenus)).
- **The classification of (−2)-configurations**: Stacks Proposition 55.5.17. A connected proper set of components with aᵢᵢ = −2wᵢ is a chain, a fork, or of type E₆, E₇ or E₈ ([`NumericalType.exists_chain_or_fork_or_exceptional`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/NumericalType/Classification.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.exists_chain_or_fork_or_exceptional)).
- **Map-aware stabilization terminates on dual graphs**: in the range 2g − 2 + n + 3d > 0, repeatedly contracting degree-zero rational tails and unmarked rational bridges reaches a stable decorated graph with the same genus and degree ([`DegreeDecoration.exists_reflTransGen_stabilizationStep_isStable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableMaps/DualGraph/Contraction.html#TauCeti.DualGraph.DegreeDecoration.exists_reflTransGen_stabilizationStep_isStable)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Nodal relative curves and prestable families**: the scheme-theoretic criterion, syntomic of relative dimension one with unramified singular subscheme ([`AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Node/Relative.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne)), on which prestable and n-pointed prestable families are built ([`PointedPrestableFamily`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/PointedPrestable/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.PointedPrestableFamily)).
- **Prestable maps**: a pointed prestable family with a map to a target over the base ([`PrestableMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableMaps/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.PrestableMap)), with automorphism groups and contracted components, ready for a stability condition.
- **Relative Proj**: Proj of a graded quasi-coherent algebra, glued from Proj of sections over affine opens ([`relativeProj`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeProj/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.CommMon.relativeProj)). This is the construction blowups, contractions and polarized descent all need.

## The frontier
