### Named results

- **The étale local normal form of a node** — at a non-smooth point over the closed point of a DVR, a nodal relative curve is étale locally uv = 0 or uv = πⁿ over an étale DVR extension, through an actual étale neighbourhood of the point ([`exists_etale_nodeAlgebra_of_isDiscreteValuationRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Node/LocalNormalForm.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.exists_etale_nodeAlgebra_of_isDiscreteValuationRing)).
- **Negative semidefiniteness of the special fibre** — on a regular proper flat model over a DVR, the intersection form of the special-fibre components is negative semidefinite, and for a connected fibre it vanishes only on multiples of the fibre ([`dotProduct_mulVec_specialFiberIntersectionMatrix_nonpos`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/Matrix.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.dotProduct_mulVec_specialFiberIntersectionMatrix_nonpos)).
- **Resolution of the node xy = πⁿ** — repeated blowups give a proper map from a regular, at-worst-nodal scheme that is an isomorphism away from the node ([`NodeAlgebra.exists_resolution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Node/Resolution.html#TauCeti.NodeAlgebra.exists_resolution)).
- **Genus-zero nodal reduction** — a smooth proper geometrically integral genus-zero curve has nodal reduction at every place of a single separable extension of degree at most two, where it becomes ℙ¹ ([`exists_finrank_residueField_le_two_and_forall_of_genus_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/GenusZero.html#TauCeti.HasNodalReduction.exists_finrank_residueField_le_two_and_forall_of_genus_eq_zero)).
- **The prime-torsion bound for numerical types** — Stacks Proposition 55.7.4 for every prime ℓ > 768g − 768, so a numerical type of genus g ≥ 2 has dim Pic(T)[ℓ] ≤ g (`NumericalType.finrank_torsion_le_arithmeticGenus`).

- **Genus of a model's numerical type (Layers 5–6)**: the type is assembled. What remains is to identify its signed genus with the genus of the generic fibre, which needs adjunction.
