### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Numerical types and their weighted Picard group**: the data of Stacks Tag 0C6Z with signed genus in ℤ ([`NumericalType`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/NumericalType/Basic.html#TauCeti.NumericalType)). `Pic(T)` is taken as the cokernel of the weighted relations ([`NumericalType.Pic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Picard/Basic.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.Pic)), and its degree-zero part is exactly its finite torsion subgroup.
- **Models with an identified generic fibre**: flat, finitely presented schemes over the DVR, each with a chosen isomorphism from its generic fibre to the curve ([`Model`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Model/Basic.html#TauCeti.Model)). They form a category with base change along finite DVR extensions, and the uniqueness summit will be stated in it.
- **The local model of a node and Fitting ideals**: the algebra xy = a ([`NodeAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Node/Basic.html#TauCeti.NodeAlgebra)) is flat, finitely presented, of pure relative dimension one and compatible with base change. Fitting ideals of finite modules are defined independently of the presentation ([`fittingIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FittingIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.fittingIdeal)).

## The frontier

- **Nodal families (Layer 1)**: syntomic morphisms, the relative singular locus `Sing(f)` via Fitting ideals of Ω, and `AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne` with its fibrewise criterion and étale local normal form. Normalization and dual graphs follow. Globalising the node computation needs Layer 0's sheaf-level operations.
- **Finishing Layer 0**: the quasi-coherent module operations from which differentials, Fitting ideals and conductors are built on schemes.
- **Genus-one minimal types with tree-shaped graphs**: the rest of the Stacks §55.6 classification. It is purely combinatorial and is the numerical input for genus-one reduction in Layer 7.
- **Blowups and intersection theory (Layer 4)**: blowups as relative Proj of Rees algebras, the step from xy = πⁿ to xy = πⁿ⁻², and intersection multiplicities of vertical divisors built from the new local lengths. Relative Proj is a Layer 2 prerequisite.
- **Full ℓ-torsion after a finite extension (Layer 6)**: a finite separable extension with a rational point and Pic[ℓ] ≅ (ℤ/ℓ)^{2g}, with a degree bound. This consumes the Jacobian roadmap's J-D and J-E contracts.
