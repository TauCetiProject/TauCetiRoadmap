### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Numerical types and their weighted Picard group**: the data of Stacks Tag 0C6Z, with signed genus in ℤ ([`NumericalType`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/NumericalType/Basic.html#TauCeti.NumericalType)), and `Pic(T)` taken as the cokernel of the weighted relations ([`NumericalType.Pic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Picard/Basic.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.Pic)).
- **Models by closure**: the scheme-theoretic closure of a closed subscheme of the generic fibre of P over a DVR is a flat model, proper when P is ([`Model.closure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Model/Closure.html#TauCeti.Model.closure)). Morphisms into a separated model are determined by their generic fibre ([`Model.subsingleton_hom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/Model/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Model.subsingleton_hom)).
- **The relative singular locus**: Sing is the first Fitting ideal sheaf of the relative differentials, for flat, finitely presented curves over an affine base ([`Scheme.singularLocus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/SingularLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.singularLocus)). Fitting ideal sheaves commute with arbitrary pullback, the input for extending it to a general base.

## The frontier

- **Nodal families (Layer 1)**: Sing over a general base and its unramifiedness, then `AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne` with its fibrewise criterion and étale local normal form. Syntomic morphisms and Fitting pullback are now in place.
- **Blowups of schemes (Layer 4)**: the blowup of a quasi-coherent ideal as relative Proj, with its universal property, exceptional divisor and strict transform, so the node computation can be iterated to a regular total space. Relative Proj is a Layer 2 prerequisite.
- **Disjoint sums of sections (Layer 2)**: a sum of pairwise-disjoint sections through the smooth locus is an effective Cartier divisor, compatibly with base change; this is the divisor in ω(Σsᵢ).
- **Multiplicities of the special fibre (Layers 4–5)**: the equality div(π) = Σ mᵢ[Cᵢ] and intersection numbers of vertical divisors.
- **Genus-one minimal types with tree-shaped graphs**: the rest of the Stacks §55.6 classification and the numerical input for genus-one reduction in Layer 7.
