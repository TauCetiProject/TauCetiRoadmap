## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** No layer is complete. The combinatorics of numerical types are finished, genus-zero nodal reduction is proved, and the local theory of nodes is now in place, including the étale local normal form for affine charts. The special fibre of a regular model has its negative semidefinite intersection matrix. There is still no regular or minimal model, no dual graph of an actual curve, no coherent base change, no stability of curves or maps, and no nodal reduction in positive genus. Layer 8 has not begun.

### Named results

- **The étale local normal form of a node**: at a singular point over the closed point of a DVR, a nodal affine chart is étale locally uv = 0 or uv = πⁿ over an étale DVR extension ([`exists_etale_nodeAlgebra_hom_of_isDiscreteValuationRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Node/LocalNormalForm.html#TauCeti.Algebra.IsStandardSyntomicOfRelativeDimension.exists_etale_nodeAlgebra_hom_of_isDiscreteValuationRing)). Not yet stated for schemes.
- **Negative semidefiniteness of the special fibre**: on a regular proper flat model over a DVR, the intersection form of the special-fibre components is negative semidefinite, and for a connected fibre it vanishes only on multiples of the fibre ([`dotProduct_mulVec_specialFiberIntersectionMatrix_nonpos`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/Matrix.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.dotProduct_mulVec_specialFiberIntersectionMatrix_nonpos)).
- **Resolution of the node xy = πⁿ**: blowing up repeatedly gives a proper map from a regular, at-worst-nodal scheme, an isomorphism away from the node ([`NodeAlgebra.exists_resolution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Node/Resolution.html#TauCeti.NodeAlgebra.exists_resolution)).
- **Genus-zero nodal reduction**: a smooth proper geometrically integral genus-zero curve has nodal reduction after a separable extension of degree at most two, where it becomes ℙ¹ ([`HasNodalReduction.exists_finrank_le_two_of_genus_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/GenusZero.html#TauCeti.HasNodalReduction.exists_finrank_le_two_of_genus_eq_zero)).
- **The prime-torsion bound for numerical types**: Stacks Proposition 55.7.4 for every prime ℓ > 768g − 768, so a numerical type of genus g ≥ 2 has dim Pic(T)[ℓ] ≤ g (`NumericalType.finrank_torsion_le_arithmeticGenus`). Minimal types of genus one are also classified ([`IsMinimal.isGenusOneMinimalShape`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/StableReduction/NumericalType/Genus/One/Classification.html#TauCeti.NumericalType.IsMinimal.isGenusOneMinimalShape)).

### Roadmap coverage

- **Layers 1, 4 and 6 are well advanced but partial.** Layer 1 has the nodal predicate, the fibrewise criterion, the affine étale normal form, node splitting, and normalization over a field. It lacks a dual graph of an actual curve with its genus formula, and geometric rational tails and bridges. Layer 4 has blowups with their universal property, the node test case through resolution, and the negative semidefinite vertical matrix. It lacks resolution of normal models and contraction of exceptional curves. Layer 6 has all its combinatorics and a model's numerical data, but not its packaged type, line-bundle specialization, or the ℓ-torsion argument.
- **Layers 0, 2, 3, 5 and 7 are partial.** Layer 0 lacks only the normalization exact sequence. Layer 2 has relative Proj with twisting sheaves and contraction descent, but no cohomology and base change, no dualizing sheaf and no ampleness theory. Layer 3 has prestable and pointed families but no stability. Layer 5 has the special fibre's components, multiplicities, intersection relations and model normalization, but no regular or minimal models. Layer 7 has genus zero only.
- **Layers 9, 10 and 11 have begun.** Layer 9 has only sections extending generic points. Layer 10 has prestable maps, automorphisms and contracted components but no `IsStableMap`. Layer 11 has evaluation maps, polarization degree and decorated dual graphs.
- **Untouched:** Layer 8.

## The frontier

- **Numerical type of a regular model (Layers 5–6)**: every axiom is proved for the intersection matrix. What remains is to assemble the `NumericalType` and identify its genus with that of the generic fibre, which needs adjunction.
- **Regular proper models (Layers 4–5)**: model normalization exists when finite. What remains is finiteness over a non-excellent DVR, the normalized-blowup sequence ending in a regular model, and contraction to a minimal model.
- **Dual graph of a nodal curve (Layer 1)**: normalization and branches over a field are in place. What remains is to build the graph and prove pₐ = Σ g_v + b₁, which needs Layer 2 cohomology. Stability in Layers 3 and 10 waits on it.
- **Cohomology and base change (Layer 2)**: Rⁱf_* and the dualizing sheaf block genus in families, stability, and Layer 8.
- **Nodal reduction in positive genus (Layer 7)**: this waits on the two items above and on the ℓ-torsion of Pic⁰ from the Jacobian roadmap's contracts J-D and J-E.
