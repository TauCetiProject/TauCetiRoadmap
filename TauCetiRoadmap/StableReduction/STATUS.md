### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Nodal families**: syntomic of relative dimension one with unramified singular locus ([`AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Node/Relative.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne)). It is stable under base change, descends along fpqc covers, and underlies prestable curves, prestable maps and nodal reduction.
- **Intersection numbers**: the degree of a line-bundle class on a curve mapping to a scheme, as an Euler characteristic ([`intersectionNumber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.intersectionNumber)). It is additive, compatible with pullback, and symmetric for properly meeting Cartier divisors; the numerical type of a regular model will be computed from it.
- **Dual graphs and stabilization**: abstract dual graphs with clutching and tail-and-bridge contraction. Stabilization terminates, and its result does not depend on the order of contractions ([`nonempty_iso_of_reflTransGen_of_isStable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/DualGraph/Stabilization.html#TauCeti.DualGraph.nonempty_iso_of_reflTransGen_of_isStable)). The same holds for the degree-decorated version. Nothing yet attaches a dual graph to an actual curve.

## The frontier

- **Numerical type of a regular model (Layers 4 to 6)**: the intersection matrix of the special-fibre components, its negative semidefiniteness and Σⱼ aᵢⱼmⱼ = 0, now that intersection numbers exist.
- **Étale local normal form of a node (Layer 1)**: assemble the étale charts and Hessian criteria into étale neighbourhoods mapping to uv = πⁿ over an étale extension of the DVR.
- **Genus-one nodal reduction (Layer 7)**: waits on resolution (Layer 4) and minimal regular models (Layer 5).
- **Stability of curves and maps (Layers 3 and 10)**: `StableFamily` and `IsStableMap` as fibrewise positivity of log-canonical degree. This needs dual graphs of geometric fibres, which need scheme normalization.
- **Resolution of normal models (Layer 4)**: normalized blowups in closed points ending in a regular model, with finite normalization over a non-excellent DVR.
