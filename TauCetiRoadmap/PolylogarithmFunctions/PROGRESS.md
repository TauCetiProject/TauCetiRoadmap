## PolylogarithmFunctions: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`8b70b2e` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap has begun with its foundational definition: the [principal polylogarithm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polylog.polylog) `Liₙ` in every integer weight, given by `z/(1−z)` in weight zero, `−Log(1−z)` in weight one, a logarithmic radial integral in weights two and up, and iterated Euler derivatives `z·d/dz` in negative weight (TauCeti#35267). The basic API around it pins the conventions: every weight [vanishes at zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polylog.polylog_zero), [weight minus one is `z/(1−z)²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polylog.polylog_neg_one), weight one [matches Mathlib's logarithm series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polylog.hasSum_polylog_one) on the disc, and [`Li₁(3) = −log 2 − πi`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polylog.polylog_one_three) fixes the lower boundary value on the cut. Nonpositive weights are shown [analytic on the plane minus 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polylog.analyticOnNhd_polylog_neg_nat).

None of the analysis of weights two and up is there yet: integrability of the radial integrand, the disc series in general weight, holomorphy and the derivative recursion on the cut plane, and the zeta values at 1 all remain open, as does the explicit rational form of the negative weights.

## PolylogarithmFunctions: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

Layer 1 filled in three of its seven pieces. The nonpositive weights are now explicitly rational: [every `Li₋ᵣ(z)` equals `z·A_r(z)/(1−z)^{r+1}`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Nonpositive.html#TauCeti.Polylog.polylog_neg_nat_eq_div), where `A_r` is the [Eulerian polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Eulerian.html#TauCeti.eulerianPolynomial) with `A_r(1) = r!`, and the pole is genuine, since [no `Li₋ᵣ` is continuous at 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Nonpositive.html#TauCeti.Polylog.not_continuousAt_polylog_neg_nat_one) (TauCeti#36800). [Conjugation symmetry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Conj.html#TauCeti.Polylog.polylog_conj) `Liₙ(z̄) = conj Liₙ(z)` holds in every integer weight off the cut, and provably fails on it. The [radial integrand](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/RadialIntegrand.html#TauCeti.Polylog.intervalIntegrable_radialIntegrand) defining weights two and up is integrable on `[0,1]` for every `z`, interior logarithmic singularities included, with a [single integrable majorant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/RadialIntegrand.html#TauCeti.Polylog.exists_norm_radialIntegrand_le) over each compact subset of the cut plane; the uniform integrability over general bounded parameter sets, needed at `z = 1`, is not yet there.

Layer 3 has begun at its rational end. In nonpositive weight, [inversion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Inversion.html#TauCeti.Polylog.polylog_inversion_of_nonpos) gives `Liₙ(z) + (−1)ⁿ Liₙ(1/z) = −1` in weight zero and `0` below, and [distribution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/Distribution.html#TauCeti.Polylog.polylog_distribution_of_nonpos) over the `m`-th roots of unity holds wherever `z^m ≠ 1`, with supporting lemmas on when `1 − z^m` avoids the cut. The positive-weight forms of both, which need holomorphy on the cut plane, remain open.

Layer 4's opening section is complete: Zagier's [coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/BernoulliCoeff.html#TauCeti.Polylog.bernoulliCoeff) `cₖ = 2ᵏBₖ/k!`, their [generating identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/BernoulliCoeff.html#TauCeti.Polylog.mk_bernoulliCoeff_mul_rescale_two_exp_sub_one) `(∑ cₖsᵏ)(e^{2s} − 1) = 2s`, and the [odd vanishing of their even transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/BernoulliCoeff.html#TauCeti.Polylog.sum_bernoulliCoeff_div_factorial_eq_zero_of_odd), proved through midpoint values of Bernoulli polynomials and the Â series (TauCeti#41403). The same work proves the algebraic core of branch independence, that the [parity-projected monodromy sum vanishes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Polylogarithm/BernoulliCoeff.html#TauCeti.Polylog.ite_odd_re_im_sum_bernoulliCoeff_mul_pow_div_factorial_eq_zero) in every weight. The single-valued functions themselves are not yet defined.
