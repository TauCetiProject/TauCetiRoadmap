## HodgeStructures: 2026-08-14 to 2026-09-01 (`0f02274` to `8745177`)

All four milestones of this roadmap have landed. In the pure theory, the **Hodge decomposition** ([`HodgeStructureOn.isInternal_piece`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn.isInternal_piece)) — the pieces H^{p,n−p} of a bounded n-opposed filtration form an internal direct sum — and its converse, **Deligne's equivalence** ([`HodgeStructureOn.decompositionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn.decompositionEquiv)) between weight-n Hodge structures and Hodge decompositions. The L1 summit, **semisimplicity of polarizable rational Hodge structures** ([`exists_isCompl_of_isPolarizable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Orthogonal.html#TauCeti.Hodge.exists_isCompl_of_isPolarizable)): every rational Hodge substructure of a polarizable structure has a complement, over ℚ and over ℂ, orthogonal for some polarizing form. Behind it sit the **Weil operator** ([`HodgeStructureOn.weilOperator`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/WeilOperator.html#TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn.weilOperator)), with C² = (−1)^n, and the **positive-definite Hodge form** ([`Polarization.hodgeForm_self_pos`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/HodgeForm.html#TauCeti.Hodge.Polarization.hodgeForm_self_pos)). In the mixed theory, **Deligne's theorem** that the bigrading I^{p,q} is an internal direct sum recovering both filtrations ([`MixedHodgeStructure.isInternal_deligneSplittingFamily`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Mixed/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.Hodge.MixedHodgeStructure.isInternal_deligneSplittingFamily)), and from it **strictness of morphisms of mixed Hodge structures** for the Hodge filtration ([`MixedHodgeStructure.Hom.range_inf_F_eq_map_F`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Mixed/Strictness.html#TauCeti.Hodge.MixedHodgeStructure.Hom.range_inf_F_eq_map_F)) and the weight filtration ([`MixedHodgeStructure.Hom.range_inf_WQ_eq_map_WQ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Mixed/Strictness.html#TauCeti.Hodge.MixedHodgeStructure.Hom.range_inf_WQ_eq_map_WQ)). The L3 milestone, **the Hodge numbers partition the dimension** ([`HodgeStructureOn.finsum_hodgeNumber_eq_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Dimension.html#TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn.finsum_hodgeNumber_eq_finrank)), came with Hodge types and period-domain points.

The rest is library: the conjugation-parametric `HodgeStructureOn`; lattice conjugation defined and proved unique ([`latticeConj_unique`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Conjugation.html#TauCeti.Hodge.latticeConj_unique)), hence conjugation-equivariance of complexified rational maps ([`rationalMapToComplex_commutes_conj`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.Hodge.rationalMapToComplex_commutes_conj)); morphisms, sub-structures, quotients and strictness for pure structures; duals, tensor products and Tate twists; a preadditive ℚ-linear category of mixed Hodge structures; the isometry group Aut(M,B) of a bilinear form as a subgroup (TauCeti#3148); and the Tate structure ℤ(m) as worked instance at every layer.

Not there: the conjugation relation on Deligne's bigrading holds only modulo W_{p+q−2} ([`MixedHodgeStructure.map_latticeConj_deligneSplitting_sup_WC`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Mixed/Conjugation.html#TauCeti.Hodge.MixedHodgeStructure.map_latticeConj_deligneSplitting_sup_WC)), coarser than the README's ⨆_{r<p,s<q} I^{r,s}; the mixed category has no abelian structure, and there is no bundled category of pure Hodge structures, so semisimplicity exists only as the complement theorem; no effective weight-one instance is built from a lattice with complex structure and Riemann form, only the reverse direction, under effectivity, from Weil-operator eigenspaces to H^{1,0} and H^{0,1} ([`HodgeStructureOn.eigenspace_weilOperator_I`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/WeightOne.html#TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn.eigenspace_weilOperator_I)); and among the L0 companions the internal Hom is missing.
