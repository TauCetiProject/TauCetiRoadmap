## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The first fine modular curve is done: `Y₁(N)` for `N ≥ 4`, smooth and affine where `N` is invertible, together with the Drinfeld–naive comparison of KM §3.7 and the factorisation dual of Layer 2C. Layers 0–7 and 9 are partial, and the main gaps are the quotient step of KM 4.7.0, the pole sheaves of 1C, quotients of elliptic curves and Picard and Cartier–Nishi duality. Layer 8 has only the finiteness of generator schemes over an affine base, and Layer 10 has not begun.

### Named results

- **`Y₁(N)` as a fine moduli scheme** — for `N ≥ 4` invertible on `B`, the naive `[Γ₁(N)]` problem is [represented](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneNaiveRepresentableBy) by an affine scheme [smooth over `B`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllObj.smooth_yOne_structureMap), which also [represents the Drinfeld problem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneRepresentableBy).
- **The naive–Drinfeld comparison (KM §3.7)** — where `N` is invertible, Drinfeld and naive full level structures [coincide](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaIsoGammaNaive) `GL₂(ℤ/Nℤ)`-equivariantly, so `[Γ(N)]` is [étale over `Ell`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.hasProperty_etale_gammaRelRepData) and rigid for `N ≥ 3`.
- **SGA 3, Exposé V, Théorème 4.1, affine case** — an equivalence relation `R ⇉ X` with `X` affine and a finite locally free projection has an affine [quotient in schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/EquivalenceRelation.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isColimitAffineCoequalizerCofork) with `R` as [kernel pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/EquivalenceRelation.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isKernelPair_affineCoequalizerπ), representing the fppf quotient sheaf.
- **The factorisation dual (2C)** — every isogeny `f` of constant degree `n` has a [dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/Isogeny/FactorisationDual.html#TauCeti.GroupScheme.factorisationDual) whose composites with `f` in both orders are `[n]`; it is involutive, reverses composition, commutes with base change, and on elliptic curves is [again of degree `n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.isogenyOfDegree_factorisationDual).
- **Galois theory of finite étale schemes** — over a field `K`, finite étale `K`-schemes are [equivalent to finite continuous `Gal(Kˢ/K)`-sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/Basic.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.galoisEquivalence), compatibly with products, commutative group objects and [alternating pairings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/Basic.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.galoisEquivalenceAlternatingPairingEquiv).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Moduli problems over `Ell/B` and rigidifiers** — relative representability, rigidity and simultaneous problems, the language of every theorem from Layer 4 on. Of KM 4.7.0, the monomorphism and freeness steps are proved and the [scheme of two rigidifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Rigidifier/Double.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.doubleRepresentableBy) is represented and, for rigid `𝒫`, [an equivalence relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Rigidifier/EquivalenceRelation.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.doubleEquivalenceRelation) with finite étale projections, so only the quotient and descent remain.
- **Regularity of a moduli problem (KM 4.12)** — [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.IsRegularOfDim) with the corrected dimension clause and shown [independent of the rigidifier](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.isRegularOfDim_rep_iff_of_representableBy).
- **The Hopf-ideal locus of the Grassmannian** — over an affine base it [classifies finite locally free subgroup schemes of rank `k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/SubgroupScheme.html#Module.Grassmannian.hopfIdealLocusEquivClosedSubgroupScheme), is proper for finite `H`, and carries a finite flat universal family.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 0 is partial.** It now has SGA 3 V 4.1 in the affine case, free finite-group quotients over a general base, fpqc descent of affine, finite and closed-immersion morphisms, descent of `Proj` along flat maps, 0D with its alternating pairings, [the geometric-fibre criterion of 0A](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EffectiveCartierDivisor/Fiber.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.isRelativeEffectiveCartier_iff_forall_isEffectiveCartier_comap_fst_of_isAlgClosed) under weaker hypotheses than 0A.1 asks, and the universal subgroup of 0G over an affine base. Still missing are the limit and descent form of the 0A criterion, descent of polarised curves with spreading out, and 0G over a non-affine base. **Layers 1 and 2 are partial**: 1C and 1E have barely begun; in Layer 2, 2A and 2C are done and 2B, 2D, 2E and 2F are untouched. **Layer 3 is partial**: the full-set locus over an affine base, KM 1.8.2, 1.8.4 and 1.11.3 now join both unbalanced comparisons, but the full-set locus glued over a general base, naive `[Γ₀(N)]` and balanced `[Γ₁(N)]` are missing. **Layer 4 is partial**: no Weierstrass presentation and no quotient step. 4D has the definition of regularity, a strict henselisation `R^{sh}` that is [strictly henselian with residue field `Ω`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StrictHenselization/ResidueField.html#TauCeti.StrictHenselization.residueFieldEquiv), [regularity and dimension under completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/LocalRing.html#TauCeti.AdicCompletion.isRegularLocalRing_iff) and [miracle flatness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/MiracleFlatness.html#TauCeti.Algebra.flat_of_isCohenMacaulay), but not Noetherianity or completion of `R^{sh}`, Stacks 04GW at a prime, or openness of the regular locus. **Layers 5 and 6** are unchanged: 5A is done, 5B lacks representability, 5C is untouched, and Layer 6 has two of three problems. **Layers 7 and 9** have isolated inputs: KM 5.3.4, relative Frobenius, and the Legendre and level-three rings with their anharmonic and tetrahedral invariants. **Layer 8 is partial**: for a finite free group scheme over an affine base the generator scheme `Gˣ` is finite ([TauCeti#27624](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27624)); the rest of 8B, and 8A, 8C and 8D, are missing. **Layer 10 is untouched.**

## The frontier

- **KM 4.7.0 and `Y_full(N)`.** Remaining: take the affine quotient of `𝕄(𝒫, δ)` by the equivalence relation of two rigidifications, and descend the universal curve along it. The descent needs 1E; this would complete the representability half of 5B.
- **Pole sheaves and the Weierstrass presentation (1C, 4B).** Remaining: `𝒪_E(n[0])` with `π_*` locally free of rank `n`, and the presentation theorem. 1E, 2B and 4B all wait on it.
- **Quotients `E/C` (2B) and the dualities (2D–2F).** The factorisation dual no longer needs 2B, but Picard duality, the Weil pairing and endomorphism rigidity still do, and 5B needs that rigidity.
- **`[N-Isog]` and balanced `[Γ₁(N)]` (Layer 6).** Remaining: globalise the Hopf-ideal locus to a non-affine base and specialise it to `E[N]`; the balanced problem needs Cartier duals of cyclic subgroups from Layer 2.
- **Strict henselisation and regularity (4D).** Remaining: Noetherianity and completion of `R^{sh}`, its colimit description at a prime (Stacks 04GW), and openness of the regular locus.
