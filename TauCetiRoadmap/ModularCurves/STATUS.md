## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The first fine modular curve is done: `Y₁(N)` for `N ≥ 4`, smooth and affine where `N` is invertible, together with the Drinfeld–naive comparison of KM §3.7 and the factorisation dual of Layer 2C. Layers 0–7 and 9 are partial, and the main gaps are the quotient step of KM 4.7.0, the pole sheaves of 1C, quotients of elliptic curves and Picard and Cartier duality. Layers 8 and 10 have not begun.

### Named results

- **`Y₁(N)` as a fine moduli scheme** — for `N ≥ 4` invertible on `B`, the naive `[Γ₁(N)]` problem is [represented](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneNaiveRepresentableBy) by an affine scheme [smooth over `B`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllObj.smooth_yOne_structureMap), which also [represents the Drinfeld problem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneRepresentableBy).
- **The naive–Drinfeld comparison (KM §3.7)** — where `N` is invertible, Drinfeld and naive full level structures [coincide](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaIsoGammaNaive) `GL₂(ℤ/Nℤ)`-equivariantly, so `[Γ(N)]` is [étale over `Ell`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.hasProperty_etale_gammaRelRepData) and rigid for `N ≥ 3`.
- **SGA 3, Exposé V, Théorème 4.1, affine case** — an equivalence relation `R ⇉ X` with `X` affine and a finite locally free projection has an affine [quotient in schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/EquivalenceRelation.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isColimitAffineCoequalizerCofork) with `R` as [kernel pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/EquivalenceRelation.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isKernelPair_affineCoequalizerπ), representing the fppf quotient sheaf.
- **The factorisation dual (2C)** — every isogeny `f` of constant degree `n` has a [dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/Isogeny/FactorisationDual.html#TauCeti.GroupScheme.factorisationDual) whose composites with `f` in both orders are `[n]`; it is involutive, reverses composition, commutes with base change, and on elliptic curves is [again of degree `n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.isogenyOfDegree_factorisationDual).
- **Galois theory of finite étale schemes** — over a field `K`, finite étale `K`-schemes are [equivalent to finite continuous `Gal(Kˢ/K)`-sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.galoisEquivalence), compatibly with products and commutative group objects.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Moduli problems over `Ell/B` and rigidifiers** — relative representability, rigidity and simultaneous problems, the language of every theorem from Layer 4 on. Of KM 4.7.0, the monomorphism and freeness steps are proved and the [scheme of two rigidifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Rigidifier/Double.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.doubleRepresentableBy) is represented, so only the quotient and descent remain.
- **Regularity of a moduli problem (KM 4.12)** — [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.IsRegularOfDim) with the corrected dimension clause and shown [independent of the rigidifier](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.isRegularOfDim_rep_iff_of_representableBy).
- **The Hopf-ideal locus of the Grassmannian** — over an affine base it [classifies finite locally free subgroup schemes of rank `k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/SubgroupScheme.html#Module.Grassmannian.hopfIdealLocusEquivClosedSubgroupScheme), is proper for finite `H`, and carries a finite flat universal family.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 0 is partial.** It now has SGA 3 V 4.1 in the affine case, free finite-group quotients over a general base, fpqc descent of affine, finite and closed-immersion morphisms, descent of `Proj` along flat maps, and the universal subgroup of 0G over an affine base. Still missing are transport of alternating pairings through the Galois equivalence, descent of polarised curves with spreading out, and the geometric-fibre form of the 0A criterion. **Layers 1 and 2 are partial**: 1C and 1E have barely begun; in Layer 2, 2A and 2C are done and 2B, 2D, 2E and 2F are untouched. **Layer 3 is partial**: the full-set locus, KM 1.8.2, 1.8.4 and 1.11.3 now join both unbalanced comparisons, but naive `[Γ₀(N)]` and balanced `[Γ₁(N)]` are missing. **Layer 4 is partial**: no Weierstrass presentation, no quotient step, and 4D has the definition of regularity and a henselian strict henselisation but no completion theory. **Layers 5 and 6** are unchanged: 5A is done, 5B lacks representability, 5C is untouched, and Layer 6 has two of three problems. **Layers 7 and 9** have isolated inputs: KM 5.3.4, relative Frobenius, and the Legendre and level-three rings with their anharmonic and tetrahedral invariants. **Layers 8 and 10 are untouched.**

## The frontier

- **KM 4.7.0 and `Y_full(N)`.** Remaining: show the scheme of two rigidifications is a finite locally free equivalence relation on `𝕄(𝒫, δ)`, take its affine quotient, and descend the universal curve along it. The descent needs 1E; this would complete the representability half of 5B.
- **Pole sheaves and the Weierstrass presentation (1C, 4B).** Remaining: `𝒪_E(n[0])` with `π_*` locally free of rank `n`, and the presentation theorem. 1E, 2B and 4B all wait on it.
- **Quotients `E/C` (2B) and the dualities (2D–2F).** The factorisation dual no longer needs 2B, but Picard duality, the Weil pairing and endomorphism rigidity still do, and 5B needs that rigidity.
- **`[N-Isog]` and balanced `[Γ₁(N)]` (Layer 6).** Remaining: globalise the Hopf-ideal locus to a non-affine base and specialise it to `E[N]`; the balanced problem needs Cartier duals of cyclic subgroups from Layer 2.
- **Strict henselisation and regularity (4D).** Remaining: separably closed residue field, Noetherianity of `R^{sh}`, completion, miracle flatness, and openness of the regular locus.
