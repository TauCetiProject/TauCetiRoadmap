## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The first fine modular curve is done: `Y₁(N)` for `N ≥ 4`, which is smooth and affine where `N` is invertible. The Drinfeld–naive comparison of KM §3.7 is done for `[Γ(N)]` and `[Γ₁(N)]`. Layers 0–7 and 9 are partial, and the main gaps are KM 4.7.0, quotients of elliptic curves and all duality theory. Layers 8 and 10 have not begun.

### Named results

- **`Y₁(N)` as a fine moduli scheme** — for `N ≥ 4` invertible on `B`, the naive `[Γ₁(N)]` problem is [represented](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneNaiveRepresentableBy) by an affine scheme cut out of the universal Tate normal form. That scheme is [smooth over `B`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllObj.smooth_yOne_structureMap), and it also [represents the Drinfeld problem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneRepresentableBy).
- **The naive–Drinfeld comparison (KM §3.7)** — where `N` is invertible, Drinfeld and naive full level structures [coincide](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaIsoGammaNaive) `GL₂(ℤ/Nℤ)`-equivariantly. It follows that `[Γ(N)]` is [étale over `Ell`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.hasProperty_etale_gammaRelRepData) (KM 3.7.1) and [rigid for `N ≥ 3`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.isRigid_gamma).
- **Katz–Mazur 3.6.0 for `[Γ(N)]` and `[Γ₁(N)]`** — both Drinfeld problems are relatively representable and finite over `Ell/B`, through the Drinfeld-basis locus ([`gammaRelRepData`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Gamma/Full.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaRelRepData)) and the exact-order scheme ([`gammaOneRelRepData`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Gamma/One.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneRelRepData)).
- **Katz–Mazur 2.3.1** — multiplication by `n ≠ 0` is [an isogeny of degree `n²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.isogenyOfDegree_intCast) over any base, and `E[n]` is [étale wherever `n` is invertible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Torsion/Etale/Locus.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.etale_torsion_hom_of_forall_intCast_ne_zero).
- **Galois theory of finite étale schemes** — over a field `K`, finite étale `K`-schemes are [equivalent to finite continuous `Gal(Kˢ/K)`-sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.galoisEquivalence), and commutative group schemes among them are [finite Galois modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.galoisEquivalenceCommGrp).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Moduli problems over `Ell/B`** — relative representability, rigidity and simultaneous problems. Every theorem from Layer 4 onward is stated in these terms. The freeness step of KM 4.7.0 is proved for them: [`GL₂(ℤ/Nℤ)` acts freely](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Rigidifier/FreeAction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.IsRigid.eq_one_of_comp_simultaneousAction_gammaNaiveAction_base_eq) on `𝕄(𝒫, [Γ(N)])` for rigid `𝒫`.
- **Quotients and descent over a general base** — the [invariant quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/FiniteGroup/Relative.html#CategoryTheory.CommMon.invariantSubalgebraPresheaf) of a scheme affine over `X` by a finite group, and the [descended scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/AffineMorphism.html#TauCeti.DescentDatum.descended) of a descent datum on an affine morphism. These are the gluing inputs to the quotient step of KM 4.7.0.
- **The strict henselisation** — [constructed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StrictHenselization/Algebra.html#TauCeti.StrictHenselization.algebra) as a filtered colimit of pointed étale neighbourhoods and shown to be local. This is the first piece of the local-ring API that Layer 4D owns.

## The frontier

- **KM 4.7.0 and `Y_full(N)`.** Remaining: the finite étale equivalence-relation quotient of `𝕄(𝒫, δ)` and descent of the universal curve along it. This needs SGA 3 V 4.1 from 0C and the descent of 1E, and would complete the representability half of 5B.
