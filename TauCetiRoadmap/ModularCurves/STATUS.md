### Named results

- **`Y₁(N)` as a fine moduli scheme**: for `N ≥ 4` invertible on `B`, the naive `[Γ₁(N)]` problem is [represented](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneNaiveRepresentableBy) by a scheme [smooth over `B`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllObj.smooth_yOne_structureMap), which also [represents the Drinfeld problem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneRepresentableBy).
- **Pointed morphisms are homomorphisms (KM 2.5.1)**: over any base, a morphism of elliptic curves that carries zero to zero [underlies a unique homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Hom/Pointed.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.existsUnique_hom_of_mapsZero). It is proved by a relative rigidity lemma, which also shows that [abelian schemes are commutative](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Rigidity/GroupScheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isCommMonObj_of_isProper_of_flat).
- **The naive–Drinfeld comparison (KM §3.7)**: where `N` is invertible, Drinfeld and naive full level structures [coincide](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaIsoGammaNaive) `GL₂(ℤ/Nℤ)`-equivariantly. Hence `[Γ(N)]` is [étale over `Ell`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.hasProperty_etale_gammaRelRepData).
- **The factorisation dual**: every isogeny `f` of constant degree `n` has a [dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/Isogeny/FactorisationDual.html#TauCeti.GroupScheme.factorisationDual) whose composites with `f` are `[n]`. The construction does not need the quotient `E/ker f`.
- **Quotients by finite groups and finite equivalence relations**: SGA 3 V 4.1 holds in the affine case, which gives an affine [quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/EquivalenceRelation.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isColimitAffineCoequalizerCofork). A free finite group action with an invariant affine cover has a glued [quotient `X/G`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/FiniteGroup/Glued/Basic.html#TauCeti.InvariantQuotient.quotient) that represents the fppf quotient.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The rigidified quotient of KM 4.7.0**: for a rigid problem with affine rigidified moduli scheme, the quotient by the scheme of two rigidifications [is the fppf quotient sheaf](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Rigidifier/Quotient.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.isColimitFppfYonedaDoubleQuotientCofork), with finite flat projection. Only descending the universal curve stands between this and representability.
- **Effective fpqc descent**: schemes [affine over the cover](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Fpqc.html#TauCeti.DescentDatum.exists_isAffineHom_baseChange_hom_isIso_of_quasiCompact) (Stacks 0245), closed subschemes and finite locally free schemes descend along any flat, surjective, quasi-compact morphism over an arbitrary base.
- **The Hopf-ideal locus of the Grassmannian**: over an affine base it [classifies finite locally free subgroup schemes of rank `k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/SubgroupScheme.html#Module.Grassmannian.hopfIdealLocusEquivClosedSubgroupScheme), compatibly with base change. This is the affine core of `[N-Isog]`.
