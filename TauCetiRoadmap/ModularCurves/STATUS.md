## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The first fine modular curve is done: `Y₁(N)` for `N ≥ 4`, smooth and affine where `N` is invertible, together with the Drinfeld–naive comparison of KM §3.7 and the factorisation dual of Layer 2C. Layers 0–9 are partial. The main gaps are the quotient step of KM 4.7.0, the pole sheaves of 1C, quotients of elliptic curves, and Picard and Cartier–Nishi duality. Layer 10 has not begun.

### Named results

- **`Y₁(N)` as a fine moduli scheme**: for `N ≥ 4` invertible on `B`, the naive `[Γ₁(N)]` problem is [represented](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneNaiveRepresentableBy) by a scheme [smooth over `B`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllObj.smooth_yOne_structureMap). It is affine whenever `B` is, and it also [represents the Drinfeld problem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneRepresentableBy).
- **The naive–Drinfeld comparison (KM §3.7)**: where `N` is invertible, Drinfeld and naive full level structures [coincide](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaIsoGammaNaive) `GL₂(ℤ/Nℤ)`-equivariantly. As a result, `[Γ(N)]` is [étale over `Ell`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.hasProperty_etale_gammaRelRepData) and rigid for `N ≥ 3`.
- **Quotients by finite groups and finite equivalence relations (0C)**: for a finite group acting on a scheme with an invariant affine cover, the glued [quotient `X/G`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/FiniteGroup/Glued/Basic.html#TauCeti.InvariantQuotient.quotient) is a categorical quotient in schemes. For a free action it [represents the fppf quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/FiniteGroup/Glued/Fppf.html#TauCeti.InvariantQuotient.isColimitFppfYonedaCofork). SGA 3 V 4.1 holds in the affine case: an equivalence relation with finite locally free projections has an affine [quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/EquivalenceRelation.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isColimitAffineCoequalizerCofork).
- **The factorisation dual (2C)**: every isogeny `f` of constant degree `n` has a [dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/Isogeny/FactorisationDual.html#TauCeti.GroupScheme.factorisationDual) whose composites with `f` are `[n]`. On elliptic curves the dual is [again of degree `n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.isogenyOfDegree_factorisationDual).
- **Galois theory of finite étale schemes**: over a field `K`, finite étale `K`-schemes are [equivalent to finite continuous `Gal(Kˢ/K)`-sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/Basic.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.galoisEquivalence).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Moduli problems over `Ell/B` and rigidifiers**: the language of every theorem from Layer 4 on. Of KM 4.7.0, the monomorphism and freeness steps are proved. The [scheme of two rigidifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Rigidifier/Double.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.doubleRepresentableBy) is [an equivalence relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Rigidifier/EquivalenceRelation.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.doubleEquivalenceRelation) with finite étale projections, so only the quotient and descent remain.
- **The full-set locus (KM 1.9.1)**: this is an [ideal sheaf on an arbitrary base](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FullSetOfSections/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.fullSetLocus) with its universal property and base change. It cuts out the [scheme of full sets of sections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FullSetOfSections/Locus.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.fullSetTupleLocus).
- **The Hopf-ideal locus of the Grassmannian**: over an affine base it [classifies finite locally free subgroup schemes of rank `k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/SubgroupScheme.html#Module.Grassmannian.hopfIdealLocusEquivClosedSubgroupScheme).
