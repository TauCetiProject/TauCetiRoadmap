## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The first fine modular curve is done: `Y₁(N)` for `N ≥ 4` with `N` invertible, together with the naive–Drinfeld comparison and KM 2.5.1. KM 4.7.0 lacks only its final descent step. Layers 0–9 are partial. Quotients of elliptic curves, Picard and Cartier–Nishi duality, the Weil pairing, coarse moduli schemes and almost everything at a prime are still missing. Layer 10 has not begun.

### Named results

- **`Y₁(N)` as a fine moduli scheme** — for `N ≥ 4` invertible on `B`, the naive `[Γ₁(N)]` problem is [represented](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneNaiveRepresentableBy) by a scheme [smooth over `B`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllObj.smooth_yOne_structureMap), which also [represents the Drinfeld problem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneRepresentableBy).
- **Pointed morphisms are homomorphisms (KM 2.5.1)** — over any base, a morphism of elliptic curves that carries zero to zero [underlies a unique homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Hom/Pointed.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.existsUnique_hom_of_mapsZero). The proof uses a relative rigidity lemma rather than Picard duality.
- **The naive–Drinfeld comparison (KM §3.7)** — where `N` is invertible, Drinfeld and naive full level structures [coincide](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaIsoGammaNaive) `GL₂(ℤ/Nℤ)`-equivariantly, so `[Γ(N)]` is [étale over `Ell`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.hasProperty_etale_gammaRelRepData).
- **The factorisation dual** — every isogeny `f` of constant degree `n` has a [dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/Isogeny/FactorisationDual.html#TauCeti.GroupScheme.factorisationDual) whose composites with `f` are `[n]`, constructed without the quotient `E/ker f`.
- **Uniqueness of henselisations (Stacks 08HT)** — two henselisations of a ring at the same field-valued point are identified by a [unique compatible isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/IsHenselization.html#TauCeti.IsHenselization.bijective_of_comp_eq), and the strict henselisation [is one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StrictHenselization/IsHenselization.html#TauCeti.StrictHenselization.isHenselization).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The rigidified quotient of KM 4.7.0** — for a rigid problem with affine rigidified moduli scheme, the quotient by the scheme of two rigidifications [is the fppf quotient sheaf](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Rigidifier/Quotient.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.RelRepData.isColimitFppfYonedaDoubleQuotientCofork), with finite flat projection. Only descending the universal curve stands between this and representability.
- **Effective fpqc descent** — schemes [affine over the cover](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Fpqc.html#TauCeti.DescentDatum.exists_isAffineHom_baseChange_hom_isIso_of_quasiCompact) (Stacks 0245), closed subschemes and finite locally free schemes descend along any flat, surjective, quasi-compact morphism.
- **Quotients by finite groups** — SGA 3 V 4.1 holds in the affine case, and a free finite group action with an invariant affine cover has a glued [quotient `X/G`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/FiniteGroup/Glued/Basic.html#TauCeti.InvariantQuotient.quotient) representing the fppf quotient.

## The frontier

- **KM 4.7.0 and `Y_full(N)`.** Descend the universal elliptic curve along the rigidified quotient and prove the universal property. Gluing over `ℤ[1/6]` also needs the Legendre rigidifier, and the descent needs the polarised descent of 1.5.
- **Polarised descent (0.5, 1.5).** Affine descent is general. What remains is to descend projective curves with a relatively ample line bundle, then the group law, subgroups and level structures.
- **Cartier duality over a general base (0.2).** The [affine identification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/FiniteFlat/Affine.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteFlatCommGroupScheme.affineEquivalence) is in place. Glue the affine dualities along base change, then prove biduality, rank and `(ℤ/Nℤ)ᴰ ≅ μ_N`.
- **Pole sheaves and the Weierstrass presentation (1.3, 3.4).** Construct `𝒪_E(n[0])` with `π_*` locally free of rank `n`, then prove the presentation theorem.
- **Quotients `E/C` and the dualities (2.2, 2.4–2.6).** Picard duality, Cartier–Nishi duality and the Weil pairing are untouched. Layer 5.3 and the determinant refinements need the pairing.
