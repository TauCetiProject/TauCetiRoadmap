## ModularCurves: 2026-10-03 to 2026-10-05 (`e3b226c` to `1d09589`)

The first work on this roadmap fills in the finite group schemes of Layer 0. Over any commutative ring, the constant group on a finite abelian group `G` and the diagonalizable group `D(G)` are now Cartier dual, in both directions and with no hypothesis on the order of `G` ([`ConstantGroup.cartierDualIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/CartierDuality/Constant.html#TauCeti.ConstantGroup.cartierDualIso), [`DiagonalizableGroup.cartierDualIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/CartierDuality/Constant.html#TauCeti.DiagonalizableGroup.cartierDualIso)); taking `G = ℤ/Nℤ` gives the duality between `(ℤ/Nℤ)_S` and `μ_N` that the roadmap asks for. Cartier duality also [preserves the whole locally constant rank function](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/CartierDuality/Rank.html#TauCeti.FiniteLocallyFreeCommAffineGroupSchemeCat.finrank_cartierDual), so the base need not be connected. The kernel of any homomorphism of group schemes, over any base, is now known to be the fibre over the identity and to [commute with arbitrary base change](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/Kernel.html#TauCeti.GroupScheme.kernelBaseChangeIso). As the roadmap requires, nothing claims such a kernel is finite locally free. All of the duality results are over an affine base.

For a finite group acting on `Spec A`, the quotient `Spec A^G` arrived (TauCeti#11266). The projection is integral and surjective, its fibres are [exactly the orbits](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/FiniteGroup/Affine.html#TauCeti.AffineInvariantQuotient.projection_eq_iff_exists_smul), and it is finite when `A` is of finite type over an invariant base. The universal property is proved [for affine targets](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/Affine.html#TauCeti.AffineInvariantQuotient.existsUnique_desc), not for arbitrary schemes.

Layer 1 has begun. The [projective Weierstrass model](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/ProjModel.html#WeierstrassCurve.projModel) is the `Proj` of the graded homogeneous coordinate ring, and it is [proper over its base](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/ProjModel.html#WeierstrassCurve.isProper_projModelOver), with zero section `[0:1:0]`. Smoothness, base change and variable changes are still missing, and so is the definition of an elliptic curve over a scheme.

## ModularCurves: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07 (`1d09589` to `87cb0c1`)

Layer 0 gained two of its named inputs. The first is Deligne's theorem, that a finite locally free commutative group scheme of rank `n` is killed by `n`: for a commutative, cocommutative Hopf algebra that is finite projective of constant rank `n`, every point raised to the `n`-th power is the identity ([`convPow_eq_one_of_rankAtStalk`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/KilledByRank.html#TauCeti.AlgHom.convPow_eq_one_of_rankAtStalk), with the free case [`convPow_finrank_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/KilledByRank.html#TauCeti.AlgHom.convPow_finrank_eq_one) proved by the Tate–Oort norm argument). The second is the [Weil restriction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction) of a finitely presented `B`-algebra along a finite projective `A`-algebra `B`. It is a finitely presented `A`-algebra representing `T ↦ Hom_B(C, B ⊗ T)`, and [its formation commutes with base change](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.baseChangeAlgEquiv). Both are stated for algebras, so over an affine base, and the Hom-scheme special cases that Layer 3 needs have not yet been derived from the Weil restriction.

Layer 1A is now nearly complete. The projective Weierstrass model is [smooth of relative dimension one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Smooth.html#WeierstrassCurve.smoothOfRelativeDimension_one_projModelOver) when the discriminant is a unit, which is checked chart by chart. A change of variables induces [an isomorphism of models](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/ProjModel.html#WeierstrassCurve.projModelVariableChangeIso) over the base that preserves the zero section, and over a domain the model is [integral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Integral.html#WeierstrassCurve.isIntegral_projModel). Elliptic curves over a scheme now have [base change](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/GeomBaseChange.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.baseChange), and the local-Weierstrass condition is stable under it. Its functoriality is not yet proved.

The scheme-theoretic group law has begun (TauCeti#12650). Over any base ring, the Bosma–Lenstra addition laws on a chart cover of `E ×_S E` [agree on overlaps as morphisms of schemes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Addition/Morphism.html#WeierstrassCurve.fst_additionOnPiece_eq_snd_additionOnPiece), not just on points, and they glue to an [addition morphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Addition/Morphism.html#WeierstrassCurve.additionMorphism) `E ×_S E ⟶ E`. This works only over an affine base, and no group axiom has been proved yet.

## ModularCurves: 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-13 (`87cb0c1` to `c7ae9d7`)

The scheme-theoretic addition morphism on the projective Weierstrass model now provably computes the classical group law. On points with values in a field it gives the sum of the two points ([`lift_projModelPoint_additionMorphism_eq_add`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Addition/Points.html#WeierstrassCurve.lift_projModelPoint_additionMorphism_eq_add)). On points with values in any ring, it is given by whichever of the two Bosma–Lenstra laws takes a unit value ([via `addXYZ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Addition/Points.html#WeierstrassCurve.lift_projModelPoint_additionMorphism_of_isUnit_addXYZ), [via `dblAddXYZ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Addition/Points.html#WeierstrassCurve.lift_projModelPoint_additionMorphism_of_isUnit_dblAddXYZ)) (TauCeti#12830). Alongside it came the [negation morphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Neg.html#WeierstrassCurve.projModelNeg) `[X : Y : Z] ↦ [X : -Y - a₁X - a₃Z : Z]`, built from a change of variables. It is an involution that fixes the zero section and commutes with base change and with variable changes.

The model itself gained finishing touches. It is [smooth](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Smooth.html#WeierstrassCurve.smooth_projModelOver) when the discriminant is a unit, and Noetherian over a Noetherian ring. Over any ring it is [geometrically integral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Integral.html#WeierstrassCurve.geometricallyIntegral_projModelOver), which is one of the inputs Layer 2A names for its degree calculation.

There is now a [universal elliptic Weierstrass curve](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Universal.html#WeierstrassCurve.Universal.ellipticCurve) over `ℤ[a₁,…,a₆][Δ⁻¹]`, which is a domain. Every elliptic Weierstrass curve is [its base change along a unique classifying map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Universal.html#WeierstrassCurve.map_specializeElliptic). This is the ring-level form of the Weierstrass scheme in Layer 4B, but the groupoid presentation there still needs the classification of pointed isomorphisms from Layer 1C.

## ModularCurves: 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-20 (`c7ae9d7` to `68a457e`)

The roadmap now has its first modular curve. For `N ≥ 4` invertible on the base, [`Y₁(N)` is a fine moduli scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneNaiveRepresentableBy) for elliptic curves with a point of exact order `N`. It is cut out of the universal Tate normal form, is affine, and is [smooth over the base](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/YOne.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllObj.smooth_yOne_structureMap). Along the way the [Tate normal form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/TateNormalForm.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.existsUnique_tateNormalForm) was proved over an arbitrary scheme, and two gaps in Layer 2A closed. `E[n]` is now [étale over any base on which `n` is invertible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Torsion/Etale/Locus.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.etale_torsion_hom_of_forall_intCast_ne_zero), and multiplication by `n` is [an isogeny of degree `n²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.isogenyOfDegree_intCast), with the degree defined for every finite flat homomorphism of group schemes.

Drinfeld level structures arrived at the same time. Drinfeld bases of `E[N]` and the closed exact-order scheme give two of the three problems in Katz–Mazur 3.6.0: [`[Γ(N)]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Gamma/Full.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaRelRepData) and [`[Γ₁(N)]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Gamma/One.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.gammaOneRelRepData) are relatively representable and finite over `Ell`. [Cyclic subgroups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Geom/CyclicSubgroup.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.IsCyclicSubgroup) and the problem `[Γ₀(N)]` are defined but not yet represented, and the balanced `[Γ₁(N)]` problem has not been started. On the naive side, full level-`N` structures [are rigid for `N ≥ 3`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/NaiveLevel.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.isRigid_gammaNaive) (KM 2.7.2) and carry the right `GL₂(ℤ/Nℤ)`-action.

For the rigidifiers of KM 4.7.0 there are now the Legendre and level-three normal forms with their universal curves, and the [monomorphism step](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Simultaneous.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.ModuliProblem.IsRigid.mono_pullback_lift) of Katz and Mazur's proof. The theorem itself is not yet proved. Layer 0 gained [categorical quotients by Hopf–Galois torsors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/HopfGalois.html#TauCeti.ComoduleAlgebra.existsUnique_SpecMap_comp_eq), effective descent for [affine schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Affine.html#TauCeti.DescentDatum.exists_isAffine_baseChange_hom_isIso) and finite locally free ones, and the [proper relative Grassmannian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/Proper.html#Module.Grassmannian.isProper_toSpec). The first Layer 7 result has also landed: the formal-group lemma KM 5.3.4.
