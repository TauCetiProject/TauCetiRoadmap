## ModularCurves: 2026-10-03 to 2026-10-05 (`e3b226c` to `1d09589`)

The first work on this roadmap fills in the finite group schemes of Layer 0. Over any commutative ring, the constant group on a finite abelian group `G` and the diagonalizable group `D(G)` are now Cartier dual, in both directions and with no hypothesis on the order of `G` ([`ConstantGroup.cartierDualIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/CartierDuality/Constant.html#TauCeti.ConstantGroup.cartierDualIso), [`DiagonalizableGroup.cartierDualIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/CartierDuality/Constant.html#TauCeti.DiagonalizableGroup.cartierDualIso)); taking `G = ℤ/Nℤ` gives the duality between `(ℤ/Nℤ)_S` and `μ_N` that the roadmap asks for. Cartier duality also [preserves the whole locally constant rank function](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/CartierDuality/Rank.html#TauCeti.FiniteLocallyFreeCommAffineGroupSchemeCat.finrank_cartierDual), so the base need not be connected. The kernel of any homomorphism of group schemes, over any base, is now known to be the fibre over the identity and to [commute with arbitrary base change](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/Kernel.html#TauCeti.GroupScheme.kernelBaseChangeIso). As the roadmap requires, nothing claims such a kernel is finite locally free. All of the duality results are over an affine base.

For a finite group acting on `Spec A`, the quotient `Spec A^G` arrived (TauCeti#11266). The projection is integral and surjective, its fibres are [exactly the orbits](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/FiniteGroup/Affine.html#TauCeti.AffineInvariantQuotient.projection_eq_iff_exists_smul), and it is finite when `A` is of finite type over an invariant base. The universal property is proved [for affine targets](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Quotient/Affine.html#TauCeti.AffineInvariantQuotient.existsUnique_desc), not for arbitrary schemes.

Layer 1 has begun. The [projective Weierstrass model](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/ProjModel.html#WeierstrassCurve.projModel) is the `Proj` of the graded homogeneous coordinate ring, and it is [proper over its base](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/ProjModel.html#WeierstrassCurve.isProper_projModelOver), with zero section `[0:1:0]`. Smoothness, base change and variable changes are still missing, and so is the definition of an elliptic curve over a scheme.

## ModularCurves: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07 (`1d09589` to `87cb0c1`)

Layer 0 gained two of its named inputs. The first is Deligne's theorem, that a finite locally free commutative group scheme of rank `n` is killed by `n`: for a commutative, cocommutative Hopf algebra that is finite projective of constant rank `n`, every point raised to the `n`-th power is the identity ([`convPow_eq_one_of_rankAtStalk`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/KilledByRank.html#TauCeti.AlgHom.convPow_eq_one_of_rankAtStalk), with the free case [`convPow_finrank_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/KilledByRank.html#TauCeti.AlgHom.convPow_finrank_eq_one) proved by the Tate–Oort norm argument). The second is the [Weil restriction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction) of a finitely presented `B`-algebra along a finite projective `A`-algebra `B`. It is a finitely presented `A`-algebra representing `T ↦ Hom_B(C, B ⊗ T)`, and [its formation commutes with base change](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction.html#TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction.baseChangeAlgEquiv). Both are stated for algebras, so over an affine base, and the Hom-scheme special cases that Layer 3 needs have not yet been derived from the Weil restriction.

Layer 1A is now nearly complete. The projective Weierstrass model is [smooth of relative dimension one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Smooth.html#WeierstrassCurve.smoothOfRelativeDimension_one_projModelOver) when the discriminant is a unit, which is checked chart by chart. A change of variables induces [an isomorphism of models](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/ProjModel.html#WeierstrassCurve.projModelVariableChangeIso) over the base that preserves the zero section, and over a domain the model is [integral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Integral.html#WeierstrassCurve.isIntegral_projModel). Elliptic curves over a scheme now have [base change](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/GeomBaseChange.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.EllipticCurveGeom.baseChange), and the local-Weierstrass condition is stable under it. Its functoriality is not yet proved.

The scheme-theoretic group law has begun (TauCeti#12650). Over any base ring, the Bosma–Lenstra addition laws on a chart cover of `E ×_S E` [agree on overlaps as morphisms of schemes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Addition/Morphism.html#WeierstrassCurve.fst_additionOnPiece_eq_snd_additionOnPiece), not just on points, and they glue to an [addition morphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Scheme/Addition/Morphism.html#WeierstrassCurve.additionMorphism) `E ×_S E ⟶ E`. This works only over an affine base, and no group axiom has been proved yet.
