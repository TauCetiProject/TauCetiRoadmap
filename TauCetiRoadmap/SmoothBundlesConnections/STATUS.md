### Named results

- **The classification of principal bundles by nonabelian Čech H¹.** Every smooth principal bundle is glued from its transition cocycle ([`exists_equivariant_fiberBundleCoreOfGroupCocycle_coordChange`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/Classification.html#TauCeti.exists_equivariant_fiberBundleCoreOfGroupCocycle_coordChange)). Two bundles trivialized over one cover are equivariantly isomorphic exactly when their cocycles have the same class ([`mk_coordChange_eq_mk_coordChange_iff_exists_equivariant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/Classification.html#TauCeti.NonabelianCechH1.mk_coordChange_eq_mk_coordChange_iff_exists_equivariant)).
- **The regular-fibre theorem.** The fibre of a smooth map over a regular value is a smoothly embedded manifold of dimension dim X − dim N, with tangent space the kernel of the differential, and no Hausdorff hypothesis is needed ([`exists_regularFiber_smoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/ManifoldLevel.html#TauCeti.exists_regularFiber_smoothEmbedding)).
- **The transverse fibre-product theorem.** The fibre product of two transverse smooth maps is a smoothly embedded submanifold of the product, and base change preserves submersions ([`exists_isManifold_transverseFiberProduct_isSmoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberProduct/Manifold.html#TauCeti.exists_isManifold_transverseFiberProduct_isSmoothEmbedding)).
- **The vertical tangent space is the Lie algebra.** Fundamental vector fields give a linear isomorphism from the Lie algebra onto the kernel of the projection's differential ([`verticalLinearEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/FundamentalVectorField.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.verticalLinearEquiv)).
- **Completeness of Lᵖ sections.** For a finite-rank bundle with continuous fibre norms, the Lᵖ space of sections is complete for every Borel measure, so L² is a Hilbert space ([`instCompleteSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/LpSection/Basic.html#TauCeti.LpSection.instCompleteSpace)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Charted total spaces.** [`ManifoldTotalSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Basic.html#TauCeti.ManifoldTotalSpace) gives a bundle with a manifold fibre its own atlas without competing with Mathlib's instances. Smooth descent, smooth division on P ×_M P and the classification above are all stated on it.
- **Tensor-product bundles.** The smooth tensor product of finite-rank vector bundles ([`ContMDiffVectorBundle.tensorProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/TensorProduct.html#ContMDiffVectorBundle.tensorProduct)) comes with a smooth universal property and the identification of E₁* ⊗ E₂ with the Hom bundle ([`bundleDualTensorHomDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/TensorProduct.html#TauCeti.bundleDualTensorHomDiffeomorph)). - **Equivariant exponential charts.** For a group acting by isometries and fixing a point, the exponential map gives an equivariant chart on a small ball ([`exists_isNormalDomain_ball_riemannianExp_mfderiv_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/FixedPoint.html#TauCeti.exists_isNormalDomain_ball_riemannianExp_mfderiv_smul)). It is the local model the slice theorem of B1.4 builds on.

## The frontier

- **The free proper quotient theorem (B1.4).** The equivariant chart at a fixed point exists for isometric actions. What remains is Haar averaging to make an auxiliary metric invariant, the slice model G ×_H B with its tubular-neighbourhood theorem, and the quotient charts that make the orbit map a submersion. The Hopf quotient `S³ → ℂP¹` and Layer 7 wait on this.
- **Ehresmann's fibration theorem (B1.5).** The kernel distribution of a submersion is ready. A horizontal complement from an auxiliary metric, horizontal lifts on maximal intervals and the transport charts remain.
- **Frame bundles and the adjoint bundle (B2.5).** The GL_n frame-bundle equivalence, reductions to closed subgroups and ad(P) with its invariant pairing are needed. These, together with B1.5, are the prerequisites for principal connections in B3.1.
- **The rest of B2.1.** Tensor and Hom bundles still need to be compared with their associated-bundle descriptions. The ℂ-linear Hom bundle over a real base and the complexified dual-tensor comparison also remain.
- **Finishing the L² theory (B2.2–B2.4).** This needs fibre metrics as explicit data, density of compactly supported smooth sections and separability, then the restriction, metric-change and measure-change unitaries and measures from smooth densities.
