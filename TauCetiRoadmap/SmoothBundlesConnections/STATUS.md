## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 1 is through cocycles and now has the quotient manifold theorem for free proper actions, but the global slice theorem and Ehresmann's fibration theorem are still open. Layers 2 and 6 are partial: frame bundles, structure-group extension and ad(P) exist, as do smooth good covers and acyclicity of smooth functions. Principal connections, curvature, holonomy and the acceptance examples (Layers 3, 4, 5 and 7) have not begun.

### Named results

- **The quotient manifold theorem.** When a finite-dimensional Lie group acts smoothly, freely and properly, the orbit space is a manifold of dimension dim M − dim G with the projection a submersion ([`exists_orbitRelQuotient_isSubmersion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/GroupAction/Quotient.html#ProperSMul.exists_orbitRelQuotient_isSubmersion)). The orbits form a smooth principal G-bundle with total space M ([`exists_principalBundle_orbitRelFiber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/Quotient.html#ProperSMul.exists_principalBundle_orbitRelFiber)).
- **The classification of principal bundles by nonabelian Čech H¹.** Two bundles trivialized over one cover are equivariantly isomorphic exactly when their transition cocycles have the same class ([`mk_coordChange_eq_mk_coordChange_iff_exists_equivariant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/Classification.html#TauCeti.NonabelianCechH1.mk_coordChange_eq_mk_coordChange_iff_exists_equivariant)).
- **The Hopf quotient.** The circle quotient of S²ⁿ⁺¹ carries the same smooth structure as ℂPⁿ with its homogeneous-ratio atlas ([`orbitRelQuotientDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/ComplexProjectiveSpace/Hopf.html#TauCeti.ComplexProjectiveSpace.orbitRelQuotientDiffeomorph)), and the Hopf projection is a submersion.
- **The regular-fibre theorem.** The fibre of a smooth map over a regular value is a smoothly embedded manifold of dimension dim X − dim N, with tangent space the kernel of the differential ([`exists_regularFiber_smoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/ManifoldLevel.html#TauCeti.exists_regularFiber_smoothEmbedding)).
- **Acyclicity of smooth functions.** On a Hausdorff second-countable manifold, possibly with boundary, the sheaf of smooth functions has vanishing sheaf cohomology in every positive degree ([`subsingleton_H_smoothSheafAddCommGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Sheaf/Smooth/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_H_smoothSheafAddCommGroup)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The frame bundle.** [`FrameBundle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FrameBundle.html#TauCeti.FrameBundle) is the open set of invertible frames in a Hom bundle. It is a smooth principal GL_n bundle, the starting point for reductions.
- **Extension of structure group.** [`PrincipalBundle.Extend`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/FiberBundle/Principal/Extension.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.Extend) builds P ×_ρ H with a universal property, functoriality and pullback compatibility. Its Čech class is the pushforward of P's, which connects structure-group changes to the H¹ classification.
- **The adjoint bundle.** [`AdjointBundle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Associated/Adjoint.html#TauCeti.AdjointBundle) is ad(P) with its fibrewise bracket and the pairing induced by an Ad-invariant form. Connections and curvature will take values in it.

## The frontier

- **The slice theorem (B1.4).** The slice map G ×_H B → M must be shown injective on a small invariant ball, by properness, so that it is a diffeomorphism onto an invariant neighbourhood of the orbit. Homogeneous spaces G/H and the characterization of principal bundles by their total space build on the quotient theorem.
- **Ehresmann's fibration theorem (B1.5).** Ehresmann connections exist on every submersion from a σ-compact manifold. What remains is horizontal lifts of paths on maximal intervals, transport over coordinate balls, and the bundle charts for a proper surjective submersion over a connected base.
- **Frame bundles and reductions (B2.5).** The frame bundle exists. Still needed are frame evaluation recovering E as an associated bundle, the groupoid equivalence, the orthogonal and unitary reductions through `OrthonormalFrameBundle`, and reductions as sections of the associated G/H-bundle.
- **Principal connections (B3.1).** Nothing has landed; ad(P), the vertical-tangent identification and Ehresmann connections are in place.
- **The rest of B6.1.** Soft sheaves and their acyclicity, the module form, the exponential sequence of circle-valued functions and the Čech comparisons on good covers are the prerequisites for the first Chern class of B6.2.
