### Named results

- **The regular-fibre theorem** — the fibre of a smooth map over a value where the differential is surjective is a manifold of dimension dim X − dim N, smoothly embedded with tangent space the kernel of the differential, without any Hausdorff hypothesis ([`exists_regularFiber_smoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/ManifoldLevel.html#TauCeti.exists_regularFiber_smoothEmbedding)).
- **The transverse fibre-product theorem** — the equal-value subtype of two transverse smooth maps is a smoothly embedded submanifold of the product, and its projections are submersions when the other map is ([`exists_isManifold_transverseFiberProduct_isSmoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberProduct/Manifold.html#TauCeti.exists_isManifold_transverseFiberProduct_isSmoothEmbedding), [`isSubmersion_fst_transverseFiberProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberProduct/Submersion.html#TauCeti.isSubmersion_fst_transverseFiberProduct)).
- **Smooth division on P ×_M P** — on a principal bundle, the element g with p·g = q is a smooth function on the self-fibre product with its transverse atlas, and the action identifies P × G with that fibre product ([`contMDiff_fiberProductDivision`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/FiberProduct.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.contMDiff_fiberProductDivision), [`actionDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/FiberProduct.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.actionDiffeomorph)).
- **The vertical tangent space is the Lie algebra** — fundamental vector fields give a linear isomorphism from the Lie algebra onto the kernel of the projection's differential, and they preserve Lie brackets ([`verticalLinearEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/FundamentalVectorField.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.verticalLinearEquiv), [`mlieBracket_rightFundamentalVectorField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/FundamentalVectorField.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.mlieBracket_rightFundamentalVectorField)).
- **Completeness of Lᵖ sections** — for a finite-rank bundle with continuous fibre norms, the Lᵖ space of sections is complete for every Borel measure, so L² is a Hilbert space ([`instCompleteSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/LpSection/Basic.html#TauCeti.LpSection.instCompleteSpace)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Charted total spaces** — [`ManifoldTotalSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Basic.html#TauCeti.ManifoldTotalSpace) gives a bundle whose fibre is a manifold (such as the structure group) its own atlas on the product model without competing with Mathlib's instances. It is the carrier for smooth descent along the projection and for every principal-bundle statement above.
- **Smooth cocycle gluing** — a smooth group-valued cocycle glues to a smooth fibre bundle, and cohomologous cocycles give equivariantly diffeomorphic bundles ([`contMDiffFiberBundle_fiberBundleCoreOfGroupCocycle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/GroupCocycle.html#TauCeti.contMDiffFiberBundle_fiberBundleCoreOfGroupCocycle), [`diffeomorphOfCohomologous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/GroupCocycle.html#TauCeti.diffeomorphOfCohomologous)). - **Measurable isometric frames** — measurable Gram–Schmidt frames ([`exists_isMeasurableIsometricFrame`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/LpSection/Measurable.html#TauCeti.exists_isMeasurableIsometricFrame)) identify sections with ordinary Lᵖ functions. ## The frontier

- **Nonabelian Čech H¹ (B1.3)** — gluing and the cohomologous-implies-isomorphic direction are done. What remains is the converse (every isomorphism is locally a change of sections), refinement invariance and the pointed set itself.
- **The free proper quotient theorem (B1.4)** — needs Haar averaging of metrics, equivariant exponential charts and slices, then quotient charts making the orbit map a submersion. It also needs HopfRinow's exponential map. The Hopf example `S³ → ℂP¹` and Layer 7 wait on this.
- **Ehresmann's fibration theorem (B1.5)** — the kernel distribution of a submersion is ready. A horizontal complement from an auxiliary metric, horizontal lifts on maximal intervals and the radial-transport charts remain.
- **Frame bundles and the adjoint bundle (B2.5)** — the GL_n frame-bundle groupoid equivalence, reductions to closed subgroups and ad(P) with its invariant pairing. These are the prerequisites, together with B1.5, for principal connections in B3.1.
- **Finishing the L² theory (B2.2–B2.3)** — metrics as explicit data, density of compactly supported smooth sections, separability, then restriction, metric-change and measure-change unitaries and the dual-bundle Riesz map.
