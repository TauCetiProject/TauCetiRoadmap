## SmoothBundlesConnections: 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-09 (`59daf62` to `d92acc5`)

The roadmap opened with the two submanifold theorems its first layer rests on. The regular-fibre theorem ([`exists_regularFiber_smoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/ManifoldLevel.html#TauCeti.exists_regularFiber_smoothEmbedding)) makes the fibre of a smooth map over a regular value a manifold of dimension dim X − dim N, smoothly embedded with tangent space the kernel of the differential; the transverse fibre-product theorem ([`exists_isManifold_transverseFiberProduct_isSmoothEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberProduct/Manifold.html#TauCeti.exists_isManifold_transverseFiberProduct_isSmoothEmbedding)) does the same for two transverse maps, and base change preserves submersions ([`isSubmersion_fst_transverseFiberProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberProduct/Submersion.html#TauCeti.isSubmersion_fst_transverseFiberProduct)). The kernel of a submersion's differential is now a smooth vector bundle ([`contMDiffVectorBundle_ker_mfderiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/Submersion.html#Manifold.IsSubmersion.contMDiffVectorBundle_ker_mfderiv)).

Smooth principal bundles were built on top of this. Invariant maps descend uniquely along the projection ([`existsUnique_contMDiff_comp_proj_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Basic.html#TauCeti.ManifoldTotalSpace.existsUnique_contMDiff_comp_proj_eq)), division is smooth on the genuine fibre product P ×_M P ([`contMDiff_fiberProductDivision`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/FiberProduct.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.contMDiff_fiberProductDivision)), and fundamental vector fields identify the Lie algebra with the vertical tangent space ([`verticalLinearEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/FundamentalVectorField.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.verticalLinearEquiv)) and preserve brackets ([`mlieBracket_rightFundamentalVectorField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/Principal/FundamentalVectorField.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.mlieBracket_rightFundamentalVectorField)). Smooth cocycles glue to smooth bundles, and cohomologous cocycles give diffeomorphic ones ([`diffeomorphOfCohomologous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/FiberBundle/GroupCocycle.html#TauCeti.diffeomorphOfCohomologous)), but the classification by nonabelian Čech H¹ is not yet stated.

In the second layer, the space of Lᵖ sections of a bundle with continuous fibre norms is defined ([`LpSection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/LpSection/Basic.html#TauCeti.LpSection)) and is complete in finite rank for every measure ([`instCompleteSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/LpSection/Basic.html#TauCeti.LpSection.instCompleteSpace)), so L² is a Hilbert space (TauCeti#27539). The metric comes from the fibres' inner products rather than being separate data, as the roadmap asks, and density of smooth sections is still missing. Complexification of real vector bundles also landed ([`complexificationVectorPrebundle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Complexification.html#TauCeti.complexificationVectorPrebundle)). Free proper quotients, Ehresmann's theorem and everything from connections onward have not started.
