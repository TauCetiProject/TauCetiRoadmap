## ShimuraVarieties: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`6b8d433` to `58174b5`)

The Mumford–Tate group arrived. For a homomorphism `h` from the Deligne torus into the real extension of a rational algebraic group, it is built as a Hopf ideal ([`mumfordTateHopfIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/MumfordTate/Basic.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal)) cutting out the smallest rational subgroup through which `h` factors ([minimality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/MumfordTate/Basic.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.le_mumfordTateHopfIdeal_iff)), and it is proved [geometrically connected](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/MumfordTate/Connected.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.geometricallyConnectedCommHopfAlgProperty_quotient_mumfordTateHopfIdeal) with no polarization assumed. Two tools carry this: contracting Hopf ideals along field extensions, and Galois descent of the geometric identity component over a perfect field (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/1645). Alongside it came the dictionary between Hodge structures and the Deligne torus. A real representation [splits into Hodge components](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/Hodge.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.isInternal_hodgeComponent), compatibly with conjugation, [tensor products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/Hodge/TensorProduct.html#TauCeti.DeligneTorus.hodgeComponent_tensor), duals and Tate twists. The Weil operator turns out to be [the action of −i](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/DeligneTorus.html#TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn.deligneAction_neg_I).

On the arithmetic side, [CM types](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/CMType/Basic.html#TauCeti.CMType) arrived with their [reflex fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/CMType/ReflexField.html#TauCeti.CMType.reflexField). An automorphism of ℂ [fixes the reflex field exactly when it fixes the type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/CMType/ReflexField.html#TauCeti.CMType.mem_fixingSubgroup_reflexField_iff), and every type is [induced from a primitive one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/CMType/Primitive.html#TauCeti.CMType.exists_isPrimitive_induced_eq), though uniqueness of that primitive pair is not proved. The Serre torus of a CM field now has its [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SerreTorus/Basic.html#TauCeti.SerreTorus.existsUnique_universalCocharacter_comp_homEquiv), but the transition maps between fields and the limit protorus are still missing. A cocharacter of a group of multiplicative type got a field of definition, [whose degree is the size of its Galois orbit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/MultiplicativeType/FieldOfDefinition.html#TauCeti.MultiplicativeTypeCommHopfAlgCat.finrank_cocharacterFieldOfDefinition).

Finally, [o-minimal structures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/OMinimal/Basic.html#TauCeti.OMinimalStructure) were defined, with the basic closure properties of definable sets and maps. The semialgebraic sets over ℝ, and over any real closed field with Tarski–Seidenberg projections, [form one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/OMinimal.html#TauCeti.OMinimalStructure.semialgebraic). Work on monotonicity and cell decomposition has not started.
