## HilbertModules: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`ec6d3d6` to `1c6a1a4`)

The Hilbert-module roadmap has its first results, both from the opening layer H1. A finite family in a right Hilbert C*-module now has a [Gram matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Gram.html#CStarMatrix.gram) of inner products, and that matrix is [positive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Gram.html#CStarMatrix.gram_nonneg) in the C*-algebra of matrices over the coefficients (TauCeti#33590). The proof goes through the finite-sum quadratic-form criterion, which says that a matrix over a C*-algebra is [positive exactly when all its quadratic forms are](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CStarMatrix.html#CStarMatrix.nonneg_iff_forall_sum_nonneg). Around it, the `CStarMatrix` type gained the real functional-calculus and scalar-tower instances it was missing. Gram positivity is what the roadmap later uses for positivity on interior tensor products and for the comparison between frames and projections.

The second piece is the basic example. A C*-algebra A is now a [right Hilbert module over itself](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Self.html#CStarModule.instMulOpposite), with inner product a*b, and this module is [full](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Self.html#CStarModule.topologicalClosure_span_range_unop_inner_mulOpposite). The construction is separate from Mathlib's left self-module, as the README requires, and a compatibility lemma relates the two through the opposite algebra.

## HilbertModules: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

Adjointable operators, the subject of layer H2, have their foundations. A plain function between C*-modules that has an everywhere-defined adjoint, ⟨Tx, y⟩ = ⟨x, Sy⟩, is automatically complex-linear and commutes with the coefficient action. Between complete modules it is [bounded](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/AdjointPair.html#LinearMap.IsAdjointPair.exists_continuousLinearMaps_cstarModule), by the closed graph theorem applied to its [closed graph](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/AdjointPair.html#LinearMap.IsAdjointPair.isClosed_graph_cstarModule), and its adjoint is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/AdjointPair.html#LinearMap.IsAdjointPair.unique_right_cstarModule) even as a plain function (TauCeti#33076). For bounded adjoint pairs the [C*-identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/AdjointPair.html#LinearMap.IsAdjointPair.norm_adjoint_comp_self_cstarModule) ‖T*T‖ = ‖T‖² holds, and T and T* have the [same norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/AdjointPair.html#LinearMap.IsAdjointPair.norm_eq_cstarModule). The work reuses Mathlib's `IsAdjointPair` on the flipped inner-product forms. The space L_A(E) itself, with composition, completeness and its C*-algebra structure, is not built yet.

Most of H1's elementary module API also landed. Right Hilbert modules now have both forms of [Cauchy–Schwarz](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#CStarModule.star_unop_inner_mul_unop_inner_le), ⟨x,y⟩*⟨x,y⟩ ≤ ‖x‖²⟨y,y⟩ and [its norm version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#CStarModule.norm_unop_inner_le). They also have [norm recovery](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#CStarModule.isGreatest_norm_unop_inner) from inner products against the unit ball, the bound [‖xa‖ ≤ ‖x‖‖a‖](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#CStarModule.norm_op_smul_le), [polarization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#CStarModule.inner_polarization), and [essentiality of the action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#CStarModule.tendsto_op_smul): x e → x along any approximate identity. The proofs work through |x| = √⟨x,x⟩, which needed the square root to commute with passage to the opposite algebra. A separate file derives the additivity, associativity and scalar compatibility of the coefficient action from the inner-product axioms alone, with no unit assumed. The factorizations x = ya and x = y⟨y,y⟩ are still missing from H1, as are completions, direct sums, complements and quotients.
