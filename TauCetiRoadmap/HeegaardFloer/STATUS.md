## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Fredholm theory and a non-sharp Sard–Smale theorem are in place, but the Floer strip operator is not. Layer 1 has a mod-two Morse boundary on compact manifolds whose square vanishes once broken index-two trajectories pair up; that global gluing step is next. Layers 1 to 7 are all partial, no Floer-type homology has been computed in general, and the reconciliations of Layer 8 have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Sard–Smale theorem** ([`isMeagre_image_criticalPoints_of_isFredholm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/SardSmale.html#TauCeti.isMeagre_image_criticalPoints_of_isFredholm)) — a `Cⁿ` Fredholm map on an open subset of a separable Banach space has meagre critical values when `n ≥ (dim ker)² + 1` at every point.

- **The Morse trajectory operator is Fredholm** ([`isFredholm_trajectoryOperator`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/EnergyEstimate/Fredholm.html#TauCeti.WeakDerivStep.isFredholm_trajectoryOperator)) — for a `C¹` path `A` of symmetric operators on a finite-dimensional space with invertible limits and `A′ → 0` at both ends, `u ↦ u' + A u` is Fredholm from `W^{1,2}` to `L²`; along a gradient trajectory of a `C³` function its [index is the difference of the Morse indices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Fredholm.html#IsIntegralCurve.index_trajectoryOperator_hessianOperator).

- **The stable-manifold theorem for a Morse critical point** ([`exists_stableSet_isManifold`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Manifold.html#TauCeti.IsNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_stableSet_isManifold)) — for a globally `C^(n+1)` function with Lipschitz gradient on a finite-dimensional inner product space, the global stable set is a `C^n` manifold modelled on the stable Hessian subspace.

- **The Calderón–Zygmund inequality for `∂̄`** ([`exists_eLpNorm_fderiv_le_eLpNorm_conjWirtingerDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/CalderonZygmund.html#TauCeti.exists_eLpNorm_fderiv_le_eLpNorm_conjWirtingerDeriv)) — for `1 < p < ∞` and a Schwartz map `u` on the plane, `‖Du‖_{Lᵖ} ≤ C ‖∂̄u‖_{Lᵖ}` with a constant `C`. Localized, it gives the [interior estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/InteriorEstimate.html#TauCeti.exists_eLpNorm_fderiv_le_eLpNorm_conjWirtingerDeriv_add_eLpNorm) `‖Du‖_{Lᵖ(K)} ≤ C(‖∂̄u‖_{Lᵖ(U)} + ‖u‖_{Lᵖ(U)})` for smooth `u` and compact `K ⊆ U`.

- **Symmetric powers of Riemann surfaces are complex manifolds** ([`isManifold_symChartedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SymmetricPower/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isManifold_symChartedSpace)) — the elementary-symmetric charts have analytic transition maps, even where points collide.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- For a residual set of parameters, the zeros of a universal Fredholm section of regularity at least `(dim ker)² + 1` [form a manifold of dimension the index](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/Parametric.html#TauCeti.eventually_residual_exists_isManifold_sectionZero). Every moduli space will use this.

- On a compact manifold with a Morse–Smale adapted pseudo-gradient, the [mod-two Morse boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Boundary/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAdaptedPseudoGradient.IsMorseSmale.morseBoundary) counts index-one trajectories, and [its square vanishes exactly when broken index-two trajectories are even in number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Boundary/Square.html#TauCeti.IsAdaptedPseudoGradient.IsMorseSmale.morseBoundary_comp_morseBoundary_eq_zero_iff). This turns `∂² = 0` into a statement about compactified trajectory spaces.

- The [Maslov index of a loop of symplectic matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/SymplecticGroup/Maslov.html#TauCeti.GLSymplectic.maslovIndex), with the homotopy, product, direct-sum and normalization axioms, joins the earlier index for loops of totally real subspaces.

## The frontier

- **`∂² = 0` for the mod-two Morse complex**: glue the local Morse-box models into a compact one-manifold whose boundary is the broken index-two trajectories, then apply the even-endpoint count already available.

- **Morse–Smale genericity**: feed the surjectivity of admissible perturbations into parametric Sard–Smale, so that transversality becomes a theorem rather than a hypothesis.

- **Cauchy–Riemann estimates for maps and bundles**: extend the interior `Lᵖ` estimates from smooth maps to the `W^{1,p}` map and bundle versions of §3.2, and prove the totally real boundary estimates.

- **The Floer strip operator**: prove the Fredholm property of `d/ds + A(s)` when `A(s)` is unbounded on an infinite-dimensional space. This needs Sobolev spaces on strips from §3.1.

- **Spin^c structures and energy in `Sym^g(Σ)`**: identify the group where `ε` lives with `H₁(Y)` in general, define `s_z`, and prove that a holomorphic disk's energy is the area of its domain.
