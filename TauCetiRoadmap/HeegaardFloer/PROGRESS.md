## HeegaardFloer: 2026-06-18 to 2026-08-01 (`e642cc5` to `6919462`)

Lane F0 acquired a working theory of Fredholm operators, which the roadmap lists first among the things nothing existed for. The index is defined and shown additive under composition (TauCeti#1294) and Cartesian products (TauCeti#1113), zero for bijections and for self-adjoint operators (TauCeti#1329), and negated by passing to the adjoint (TauCeti#1381); over Banach spaces the closed-range condition turns out not to be independent, so Fredholmness reduces to finite-dimensional kernel and cokernel (TauCeti#1147). The useful half is stability: the Fredholm operators of a given index form an open set (TauCeti#1349), and the index of a continuous family is locally constant, hence constant along paths and on preconnected parameter sets (TauCeti#1608, <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/ContinuousFamily.html#TauCeti.Continuous.isLocallyConstant_index>).

Lane F2.1's definitions landed in bulk, in linear form: symplectic forms with tameness, invariance and compatibility, the associated metric, the Hermitian inner product of a compatible pair (TauCeti#341), transport and symplectomorphisms (TauCeti#265, TauCeti#613), and the linear cotangent space with its zero section and fibre Lagrangian for the canonical form and maximal totally real for the canonical `J` (TauCeti#1213, TauCeti#1595). Totally real subspaces got their own file, with the half-dimension count and the implication the roadmap actually wants, that a Lagrangian of a taming pair is maximal totally real (TauCeti#286, TauCeti#1046, <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/TotallyReal.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsLagrangian.isMaximalTotallyReal_of_tames>). J-holomorphicity is stated for varying almost complex structures, with the chain rule (TauCeti#1206, <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Varying.html#TauCeti.IsJHolomorphic>).

All of this is pointwise and linear: there are no symplectic manifolds, no energy, and no moduli spaces yet. Sard is still absent, so Sard–Smale and transversality have not begun, and `Sym^g` exists only as the bijection between unordered tuples and monic polynomials (TauCeti#1469).

## HeegaardFloer: 2026-08-01 to 2026-09-04 (`6919462` to `6704398`)

The [Morse–Sard theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Sard/OutermostStratum.html#TauCeti.ContDiff.addHaar_image_criticalPoints_eq_zero) now says that the critical values of a sufficiently smooth map between finite-dimensional real normed spaces have Haar measure zero. Its infinite-dimensional counterpart, [Sard–Smale](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/SardSmale.html#TauCeti.isMeagre_image_criticalPoints_of_isFredholm), gives a meagre critical-value set for sufficiently smooth Fredholm maps on open subsets of separable Banach spaces. Together with the [regular-level-set theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/LevelSet/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isManifold_levelSet), this puts the central abstract transversality package of Lane F0 in place: a regular level set is a smooth manifold whose dimension is the Fredholm index.

Lane M has moved from untouched to a substantial local and dynamical foundation. The [Morse lemma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/NormalForm.html#TauCeti.IsNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_morse_chart) supplies smooth quadratic charts at nondegenerate critical points; the [Morse index](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Index.html#TauCeti.morseIndex), stable and unstable sets, global negative-gradient flows for globally Lipschitz gradients, and forward and backward convergence of trajectories confined to suitable compact sets now accompany it. The stable-manifold theorem, Morse–Smale transversality, trajectory gluing, and the Morse complex itself have not landed.

On the geometric side, smooth almost complex structures and smooth two-forms now live on manifolds, with pseudoholomorphic maps, energy, and the [integrated Wirtinger inequality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Energy.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.Compatible.lintegral_twoForm_le_stdComplexLineEnergy). Elementary-symmetric coordinates also give symmetric powers a [charted-space structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SymmetricPower.html#TauCeti.symChartedSpace), but not yet the smooth or complex manifold structure required for `Sym^g(Σ)`.

## HeegaardFloer: 2026-09-04 to 2026-09-30 (`6704398` to `dec7a58`)

Lane M now has the local stable- and unstable-manifold theorem at a Morse critical point: the points whose negative-gradient trajectories stay nearby form a [closed disk of dimension n minus the Morse index](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/LocalInvariantManifold.html#TauCeti.IsNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_localStableSet_homeomorph_closedBall), and backward trajectories a [disk of dimension the index](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/LocalInvariantManifold.html#TauCeti.IsNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_localUnstableSet_homeomorph_closedBall). Both come from a Lyapunov–Perron construction for hyperbolic equilibria, and they are Lipschitz graphs, not yet shown to be smooth submanifolds. Around this: trajectories converge exponentially, [almost every linear perturbation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Generic.html#TauCeti.ae_hasNondegenerateCriticalPointsOn_sub) of a `C²` function is Morse, the [height function on a sphere](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Sphere/Basic.html#TauCeti.isMorse_sphereHeight) is Morse, and connecting orbits cut each intermediate level exactly once, which is how unparametrized trajectories will be counted.

On the Heegaard side, the [symmetric power of a Riemann surface is a complex analytic manifold](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SymmetricPower/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isManifold_symChartedSpace). In it, a torus built from disjoint curves is [closed and embedded](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sym/Pi.html#TauCeti.Sym.isClosedEmbedding_ofFn_subtypeVal) and [maximal totally real](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SymmetricPower/TotallyReal.html#TauCeti.isMaximalTotallyReal_range_fderiv_symChartAt_ofFn), two such tori meet in as many points as the permanent of their intersection matrix, and holomorphic curves meet the basepoint divisor at isolated points of positive order. Lipshitz's [combinatorial Maslov index](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/MaslovIndex.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.maslovIndex) is additive and computed for a diagram of `S¹ × S²`. The analytic [Maslov index](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TotallyReal/Maslov/Index.html#TauCeti.TotallyRealLoop.maslovIndex) of loops of totally real subspaces is defined as a degree in the circle and satisfies homotopy invariance, normalization and the [direct-sum axiom](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TotallyReal/Maslov/Prod.html#TauCeti.TotallyRealLoop.maslovIndex_prod). It covers loops only; the version for paths is still missing.

Smaller pieces: [supercritical `W^{1,p}` multiplication](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/SupercriticalMultiplication.html#TauCeti.W1p.norm_mul_le) on `ℝⁿ`, the first Cauchy–Riemann-lane estimate; [global parametric transversality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/LevelSet/GlobalParametric.html#TauCeti.isMeagre_setOf_not_isRegularParameter); closed two-forms; zero-energy rigidity for maps into tame targets; and the Gordan and Stiemke alternatives, which prepare admissibility. There is still no Morse complex and no moduli space of strips.

## HeegaardFloer: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02 (`dec7a58` to `d449639`)

The first piece of Gromov compactness has landed: the two-dimensional [mean-value inequality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Laplacian/MeanValueInequality.html#TauCeti.mul_le_eight_mul_setIntegral_ball_of_neg_mul_sq_le_laplacian) of McDuff–Salamon §4.3. It says that a nonnegative function with `Δw ≥ -Aw²` and small integral over a disk is bounded at the centre by eight times its average. It rests on the [sub-mean-value inequality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Harmonic/MeanValue.html#TauCeti.mul_le_setIntegral_ball_of_laplacian_nonneg) and polar coordinates on balls (TauCeti#10454). It has not yet been applied to the energy density of a holomorphic curve, which is where it would bound derivatives.

On the Heegaard side, weak admissibility now has its two classical reformulations: a diagram is weakly admissible exactly when the regions carry [positive areas vanishing on every periodic domain](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/Admissibility.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.weaklyAdmissible_iff_exists_pos_forall_sum_mul_eq_zero), and exactly when it has [finitely many nonnegative periodic domains](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/Admissibility.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.weaklyAdmissible_iff_finite_setOf_mem_periodicDomains_nonneg). The first comes from [Stiemke's lemma for a lattice](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Stiemke.html#TauCeti.exists_pos_forall_sum_mul_intCast_eq_zero_iff). As a consequence, only [finitely many nonnegative domains](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/Admissibility.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.WeaklyAdmissible.finite_setOf_isDomainBetween_basepoint_eq_nonneg) join two generators with given basepoint multiplicities. These are statements about domains only, because energy equals area has not been formalized. In `Sym^g(Σ)`, a curve's [intersection order with the basepoint divisor](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SymmetricPower/BasepointIntersection/Order.html#TauCeti.basepointIntersectionOrder) is now defined. It is independent of the chart and is positive exactly on the divisor, and the window adds an identity principle and finiteness of intersections on compact sets.

Lane F0 now has an [intrinsic linearization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/Linearization.html#TauCeti.sectionLinearization) of a vector-bundle section at a zero, whose Fredholmness, index and surjectivity do not depend on the trivialization. This is the first step toward Fredholm sections. Smaller pieces: Riesz theory for compact perturbations of the identity, derivatives of superposition operators, and the [Maslov index under a loop of automorphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TotallyReal/Maslov/Map.html#TauCeti.TotallyRealLoop.maslovIndex_map).

## HeegaardFloer: 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-05 (`d449639` to `1d09589`)

Lane F0 now has the "moduli space is the zero set of a Fredholm section" package of McDuff–Salamon's Appendix A. The [regular zeros of a Fredholm section](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/Manifold.html#TauCeti.isManifold_sectionZero) of a Banach bundle form a smooth manifold whose dimension is the index, with tangent spaces the kernels of the linearization. Combined with [parametric transversality for bundle sections](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/Parametric.html#TauCeti.isMeagre_setOf_not_isRegularSectionParameter), this gives [generic regular zeros](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/Parametric.html#TauCeti.eventually_residual_exists_isManifold_sectionZero): for a residual set of parameters, the zero set is such a manifold (TauCeti#11852). What F0 still lacks is a Fredholm operator to apply this to, namely the strip operator `d/ds + A(s)`.

For exact Floer theory, the [energy of a holomorphic strip](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Action.html#TauCeti.stdComplexLineEnergy_eq_of_tendstoUniformlyOn) with boundary on two exact graphs is the difference of the [action](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Action.html#TauCeti.cotangentAction) at its ends, and the action decreases along the strip. This is proved only in a linear cotangent space `V × V'`, not yet in `T*M`. On the Heegaard side, the Ozsváth–Szabó [class `ε(x, y)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/CurveHomology.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.epsilon) of two generators is defined in a homology group of `α ∪ β` and [vanishes exactly when a domain joins them](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/CurveHomology.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.epsilon_eq_zero_iff). For a genus-one diagram of `ℝP³` it is [nonzero](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LowDimTopology/Heegaard/RealProjectiveSpace.html#TauCeti.HeegaardRegionSystem.epsilon_realProjectiveSpace_ne_zero) of order two. Spin^c structures themselves are still undefined. A holomorphic curve's [basepoint intersection number](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SymmetricPower/BasepointIntersection/Number.html#TauCeti.basepointIntersectionNumber) is defined, additive, invariant under reparametrization and at least the number of intersection points.

In Lane M, the [Lyapunov–Perron graph maps are `C¹`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/LyapunovPerron/Local.html#ContinuousLinearMap.contDiffAt_localStableGraphMap), so the local stable and unstable sets at a Morse critical point are `C¹` graphs. They are not yet embedded submanifolds and have not been globalized along the flow. Smaller pieces: [density of regular values](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Sard/OutermostStratum.html#ContDiff.dense_compl_image_criticalPoints) and Fredholmness of the parameter projection.
