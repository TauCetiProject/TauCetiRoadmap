## HeegaardFloer: 2026-06-18 to 2026-08-01 (`e642cc5` to `6919462`)

Lane F0 acquired a working theory of Fredholm operators, which the roadmap lists first among the things nothing existed for. The index is defined and shown additive under composition (TauCeti#1294) and Cartesian products (TauCeti#1113), zero for bijections and for self-adjoint operators (TauCeti#1329), and negated by passing to the adjoint (TauCeti#1381); over Banach spaces the closed-range condition turns out not to be independent, so Fredholmness reduces to finite-dimensional kernel and cokernel (TauCeti#1147). The useful half is stability: the Fredholm operators of a given index form an open set (TauCeti#1349), and the index of a continuous family is locally constant, hence constant along paths and on preconnected parameter sets (TauCeti#1608, <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/ContinuousFamily.html#TauCeti.Continuous.isLocallyConstant_index>).

Lane F2.1's definitions landed in bulk, in linear form: symplectic forms with tameness, invariance and compatibility, the associated metric, the Hermitian inner product of a compatible pair (TauCeti#341), transport and symplectomorphisms (TauCeti#265, TauCeti#613), and the linear cotangent space with its zero section and fibre Lagrangian for the canonical form and maximal totally real for the canonical `J` (TauCeti#1213, TauCeti#1595). Totally real subspaces got their own file, with the half-dimension count and the implication the roadmap actually wants, that a Lagrangian of a taming pair is maximal totally real (TauCeti#286, TauCeti#1046, <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Lagrangian/TotallyReal.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.IsLagrangian.isMaximalTotallyReal_of_tames>). J-holomorphicity is stated for varying almost complex structures, with the chain rule (TauCeti#1206, <https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Varying.html#TauCeti.IsJHolomorphic>).

All of this is pointwise and linear: there are no symplectic manifolds, no energy, and no moduli spaces yet. Sard is still absent, so Sard–Smale and transversality have not begun, and `Sym^g` exists only as the bijection between unordered tuples and monic polynomials (TauCeti#1469).
