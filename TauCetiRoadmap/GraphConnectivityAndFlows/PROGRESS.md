## GraphConnectivityAndFlows: 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-20 (`040477b` to `68a457e`)

The first work on this roadmap lands the Ford–Fulkerson augmenting-path optimality criterion: for an `s`–`t` flow, being maximum, having no augmenting walk in the residual network, and saturating some terminal-separating cut are equivalent ([`Flow.tfae_isMaximum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/AugmentingPath.html#TauCeti.Flow.tfae_isMaximum), with a version for flows with signed lower and upper bounds, [`BoundedFlow.tfae_isMaximum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/AugmentingPath.html#TauCeti.BoundedFlow.tfae_isMaximum)). It holds over any linearly ordered additive group of coefficients, so a maximum flow attains a minimum cut ([`Flow.IsMaximum.exists_val_eq_arrowSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/AugmentingPath.html#TauCeti.Flow.IsMaximum.exists_val_eq_arrowSum)). That is not yet max-flow/min-cut itself: nothing so far proves that a maximum flow exists, which the roadmap obtains from terminating shortest-path augmentation, and integrality and flow decomposition have not started. Beneath this sits the network algebra the criterion needs: excess as an additive homomorphism, weak duality, a residual update operation for arbitrary bounds, path augmentation by a walk's bottleneck, arrow reorientation and subnetworks.

On the cut side, minimum cuts of any submodular set function between disjoint terminal sets form a lattice under union and intersection, with a smallest and a largest minimizing side ([`TerminalCut.minimizers`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Basic.html#TauCeti.TerminalCut.minimizers)). Directed cut capacity, the signed upper cut bound and undirected multigraph cut capacity are proved to be instances, the last of them symmetric ([`Graph.isSymmSubmodular_edgeCutCapacity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/CutCapacity.html#Graph.isSymmSubmodular_edgeCutCapacity)), and under symmetry exchanging the terminals swaps the smallest and largest sides, each replaced by its complement.

For undirected multigraphs, edge-labelled walks on Mathlib's `Graph` arrived, with reachability, splitting at a vertex, path extraction, traced subgraphs, compatible unions and incidence counts (TauCeti#18217, TauCeti#18400). The representation bridges to `SimpleGraph` and the component count are still missing.

## GraphConnectivityAndFlows: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `d96e9ce`)

The max-flow/min-cut theorem is now proved. Shortest-path augmentation terminates from any feasible flow over any linearly ordered coefficient group ([`BoundedFlow.wellFounded_shortestAugmentationStep`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/ShortestAugmentation.html#TauCeti.BoundedFlow.wellFounded_shortestAugmentationStep)), so a maximum flow exists and, with last window's optimality criterion, some flow's value equals some cut's capacity ([`Flow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/ShortestAugmentation.html#TauCeti.Flow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum)), also for signed lower and upper bounds and with integrality in any additive subgroup ([`Flow.exists_isMaximum_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/ShortestAugmentation.html#TauCeti.Flow.exists_isMaximum_mem)). For capacities in a cyclic subgroup, such as integers or rationals, any choice of augmenting walks terminates ([`Flow.acc_augmentsTo`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Termination.html#TauCeti.Flow.acc_augmentsTo)). Around it came the path–cycle decomposition of nonnegative assignments ([`exists_pathCycleDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.exists_pathCycleDecomposition)), acyclic flows, capping of large capacities, and max-flow/min-cut between vertex sets ([`SetFlow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/SetFlow.html#TauCeti.SetFlow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum)), built on auxiliary terminals. Vertex splitting, network isomorphisms and change of coefficients also landed, all groundwork for Menger.

Minimum cuts gained their full structure theory. Given a maximum flow, the smallest minimum cut is the set of vertices residually reachable from the source ([`BoundedFlow.IsMaximum.isLeast_filter_positiveReachable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Residual.html#TauCeti.BoundedFlow.IsMaximum.isLeast_filter_positiveReachable)), and the minimum cuts are order-isomorphic to the upper sets of the poset of free residual components ([`BoundedFlow.IsMaximum.minimizersOrderIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Poset.html#TauCeti.BoundedFlow.IsMaximum.minimizersOrderIso)). For symmetric submodular functions, the multi-cut non-crossing lemma ([`TerminalCut.exists_isMinimum_forall_not_crosses`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Noncrossing.html#TauCeti.TerminalCut.exists_isMinimum_forall_not_crosses)) and the ultrametric inequality for minimum cut values supply the interface Gomory–Hu trees need.

On the undirected side, an edge is a bridge exactly when it lies on no cycle ([`Graph.isBridge_iff_forall_cycle_notMem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/Bridge.html#Graph.isBridge_iff_forall_cycle_notMem)), forests are graphs all of whose edges are bridges, and bonds are characterised by connected sides. Connected components, their count and the walk correspondence with `SimpleGraph` arrived, together with native edge connectivity ([`Graph.edgeConnectivity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/EdgeConnectivity.html#Graph.edgeConnectivity)). Cut vertices, blocks and Menger's theorem have not started.

## GraphConnectivityAndFlows: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-06 (`d96e9ce` to `a67a650`)

Menger's theorem is now proved in essentially every form the roadmap asks for: arrow-disjoint and internally vertex-disjoint directed paths ([`exists_paths_arrowSeparator_card_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Menger/Edge.html#TauCeti.exists_paths_arrowSeparator_card_eq)), set-to-set and vertex-capacitated versions, and their transport to simple graphs, multigraphs and digraphs (TauCeti#21442, TauCeti#22086). In its global form, a simple graph is `k`-vertex-connected exactly when every two vertices are joined by `k` internally disjoint paths ([`SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_iff_forall_exists_paths`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/VertexConnectivity.html#SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_iff_forall_exists_paths)). The classical consequences followed: Whitney's inequality for multigraphs ([`Graph.vertexConnectivity_le_edgeConnectivity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/Whitney.html#Graph.vertexConnectivity_le_edgeConnectivity)) and its directed analogue, the fan lemma, Dirac's theorem that any `k` vertices of a `k`-connected graph lie on a common cycle ([`SimpleGraph.IsVertexConnected.exists_isCycle_forall_mem_support`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/PrescribedCycle.html#SimpleGraph.IsVertexConnected.exists_isCycle_forall_mem_support)), and Kőnig's theorem for multiset capacitated matchings ([`Graph.IsBipartition.exists_isCapacitatedMatching_isVertexCover_sum_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Matching/Capacitated.html#Graph.IsBipartition.exists_isCapacitatedMatching_isVertexCover_sum_eq)).

On the flow side, Hoffman's circulation theorem ([`exists_isCirculation_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Hoffman.html#TauCeti.exists_isCirculation_iff)) says a bounded circulation exists exactly when no vertex set must take in more than its upper bounds let out, with integrality and infeasibility certificates, and extends to prescribed and interval excess. Through an edge-to-endpoint network it yields Hakimi's theorem on orientations with prescribed indegrees ([`Graph.exists_orientation_indegree_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Orientation/Hakimi.html#Graph.exists_orientation_indegree_eq_iff)) and its bounded versions. The Gomory–Hu theorem ([`TerminalCut.exists_isCutTree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/GomoryHu.html#TauCeti.TerminalCut.exists_isCutTree)) holds for every symmetric submodular function, with multigraph instances and the query and threshold theorems. Undirected max-flow/min-cut, integer rounding, residual cycle adjustments and nonnegative-capacity interfaces fill in the rest of the flow milestones.

For graph structure, cut vertices are exactly the vertices in two blocks ([`SimpleGraph.isCutVertex_iff_exists_isBlock_ne`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/Block.html#SimpleGraph.isCutVertex_iff_exists_isBlock_ne)), the bridge forest is built, and ear decompositions arrived in undirected and directed form, so far only with the easy direction that a graph with an ear decomposition is 2-edge-connected. The converse ear characterizations, Robbins' theorem, the block–cut forest, capacitated Hall and edge-colouring have not started.

## GraphConnectivityAndFlows: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-11 (`a67a650` to `1b19ebb`)

Robbins' theorem is proved: a multigraph with finitely many edges has a strongly connected orientation exactly when it is 2-edge-connected ([`Graph.exists_stronglyConnected_orientation_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Orientation/StronglyConnected.html#Graph.exists_stronglyConnected_orientation_iff)), with the bridgeless form for connected graphs and a simple-graph version. It rests on the three ear characterizations, all now in. A finite simple graph is 2-vertex-connected exactly when it has an open ear decomposition, which may start from any prescribed cycle ([`SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_two_iff_exists_isOpen_earDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/Ear/Open.html#SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_two_iff_exists_isOpen_earDecomposition)). A multigraph is 2-edge-connected exactly when it grows from one vertex by open and closed ears ([`Graph.isEdgeConnected_two_iff_exists_isVertexStart_earDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/Ear/Characterization.html#Graph.isEdgeConnected_two_iff_exists_isVertexStart_earDecomposition)), and a network is strongly connected exactly when it grows that way by directed ears ([`Network.isStronglyConnected_iff_nonempty_earDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Ear/StrongConnectivity.html#TauCeti.Network.isStronglyConnected_iff_nonempty_earDecomposition)). Open ears also give the Lempel–Even–Cederbaum theorem: `G` has an `st`-numbering exactly when `G` plus the edge `st` is 2-connected ([`SimpleGraph.nonempty_stNumbering_iff_isVertexConnected_two_sup_edge`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/STNumbering.html#SimpleGraph.nonempty_stNumbering_iff_isVertexConnected_two_sup_edge)).

On the matching side, capacitated Hall ([`IsBipartition.exists_isCapacitatedMatching_saturating_left_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Matching/Hall.html#Graph.IsBipartition.exists_isCapacitatedMatching_saturating_left_iff)) and the capacitated Kőnig–Ore formula follow from the capacitated Kőnig theorem, with ordinary Hall and the Hall deficiency of set families. Regular bipartite multigraphs split into perfect matchings, bounded-degree ones embed in regular ones, and together these give Kőnig's edge-colouring theorem: a finite bipartite multigraph is `k`-edge-colourable exactly when no vertex meets more than `k` edges ([`IsBipartition.nonempty_edgeColoring_iff_forall_incidenceCount_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Matching/EdgeColoring.html#Graph.IsBipartition.nonempty_edgeColoring_iff_forall_incidenceCount_le)).

The block–cut graph is a forest, and a tree exactly when a finite graph is connected ([`SimpleGraph.blockCutGraph_isTree_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/BlockCut/Forest.html#SimpleGraph.blockCutGraph_isTree_iff)), with the path correspondence, the component-count formula and multigraph blocks; the common-cycle characterizations of 2-connectivity are complete. Part of this, including the open ears, `st`-numberings and multigraph blocks, landed earlier and is recorded here late (TauCeti#27328, TauCeti#27672). Not yet seen: the simple-graph form of the 2-edge-connected ear characterization and the `Digraph` ear corollary.
