## GraphConnectivityAndFlows: 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-20 (`040477b` to `68a457e`)

The first work on this roadmap lands the Ford–Fulkerson augmenting-path optimality criterion: for an `s`–`t` flow, being maximum, having no augmenting walk in the residual network, and saturating some terminal-separating cut are equivalent ([`Flow.tfae_isMaximum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/AugmentingPath.html#TauCeti.Flow.tfae_isMaximum), with a version for flows with signed lower and upper bounds, [`BoundedFlow.tfae_isMaximum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/AugmentingPath.html#TauCeti.BoundedFlow.tfae_isMaximum)). It holds over any linearly ordered additive group of coefficients, so a maximum flow attains a minimum cut ([`Flow.IsMaximum.exists_val_eq_arrowSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/AugmentingPath.html#TauCeti.Flow.IsMaximum.exists_val_eq_arrowSum)). That is not yet max-flow/min-cut itself: nothing so far proves that a maximum flow exists, which the roadmap obtains from terminating shortest-path augmentation, and integrality and flow decomposition have not started. Beneath this sits the network algebra the criterion needs: excess as an additive homomorphism, weak duality, a residual update operation for arbitrary bounds, path augmentation by a walk's bottleneck, arrow reorientation and subnetworks.

On the cut side, minimum cuts of any submodular set function between disjoint terminal sets form a lattice under union and intersection, with a smallest and a largest minimizing side ([`TerminalCut.minimizers`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Basic.html#TauCeti.TerminalCut.minimizers)). Directed cut capacity, the signed upper cut bound and undirected multigraph cut capacity are proved to be instances, the last of them symmetric ([`Graph.isSymmSubmodular_edgeCutCapacity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/CutCapacity.html#Graph.isSymmSubmodular_edgeCutCapacity)), and under symmetry exchanging the terminals swaps the smallest and largest sides, each replaced by its complement.

For undirected multigraphs, edge-labelled walks on Mathlib's `Graph` arrived, with reachability, splitting at a vertex, path extraction, traced subgraphs, compatible unions and incidence counts (TauCeti#18217, TauCeti#18400). The representation bridges to `SimpleGraph` and the component count are still missing.

## GraphConnectivityAndFlows: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `d96e9ce`)

The max-flow/min-cut theorem is now proved. Shortest-path augmentation terminates from any feasible flow over any linearly ordered coefficient group ([`BoundedFlow.wellFounded_shortestAugmentationStep`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/ShortestAugmentation.html#TauCeti.BoundedFlow.wellFounded_shortestAugmentationStep)), so a maximum flow exists and, with last window's optimality criterion, some flow's value equals some cut's capacity ([`Flow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/ShortestAugmentation.html#TauCeti.Flow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum)), also for signed lower and upper bounds and with integrality in any additive subgroup ([`Flow.exists_isMaximum_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/ShortestAugmentation.html#TauCeti.Flow.exists_isMaximum_mem)). For capacities in a cyclic subgroup, such as integers or rationals, any choice of augmenting walks terminates ([`Flow.acc_augmentsTo`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Termination.html#TauCeti.Flow.acc_augmentsTo)). Around it came the path–cycle decomposition of nonnegative assignments ([`exists_pathCycleDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.exists_pathCycleDecomposition)), acyclic flows, capping of large capacities, and max-flow/min-cut between vertex sets ([`SetFlow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/SetFlow.html#TauCeti.SetFlow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum)), built on auxiliary terminals. Vertex splitting, network isomorphisms and change of coefficients also landed, all groundwork for Menger.

Minimum cuts gained their full structure theory. Given a maximum flow, the smallest minimum cut is the set of vertices residually reachable from the source ([`BoundedFlow.IsMaximum.isLeast_filter_positiveReachable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Residual.html#TauCeti.BoundedFlow.IsMaximum.isLeast_filter_positiveReachable)), and the minimum cuts are order-isomorphic to the upper sets of the poset of free residual components ([`BoundedFlow.IsMaximum.minimizersOrderIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Poset.html#TauCeti.BoundedFlow.IsMaximum.minimizersOrderIso)). For symmetric submodular functions, the multi-cut non-crossing lemma ([`TerminalCut.exists_isMinimum_forall_not_crosses`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Noncrossing.html#TauCeti.TerminalCut.exists_isMinimum_forall_not_crosses)) and the ultrametric inequality for minimum cut values supply the interface Gomory–Hu trees need.

On the undirected side, an edge is a bridge exactly when it lies on no cycle ([`Graph.isBridge_iff_forall_cycle_notMem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/Bridge.html#Graph.isBridge_iff_forall_cycle_notMem)), forests are graphs all of whose edges are bridges, and bonds are characterised by connected sides. Connected components, their count and the walk correspondence with `SimpleGraph` arrived, together with native edge connectivity ([`Graph.edgeConnectivity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/EdgeConnectivity.html#Graph.edgeConnectivity)). Cut vertices, blocks and Menger's theorem have not started.

## GraphConnectivityAndFlows: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-06 (`d96e9ce` to `385ec2d`)

Menger's theorem is proved in every form the roadmap asks for. Between distinct nonadjacent vertices of a finite simple graph, the maximum number of internally disjoint paths equals the minimum size of a separating vertex set ([`SimpleGraph.exists_paths_vertexSeparator_card_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/Menger.html#SimpleGraph.exists_paths_vertexSeparator_card_eq)), with directed, multigraph, edge-disjoint ([`Graph.exists_paths_edgeSeparator_card_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/Menger/Edge.html#Graph.exists_paths_edgeSeparator_card_eq)), set-to-set (also for multigraphs, [TauCeti#25574](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25574)), adjacent-terminal and vertex-capacitated versions, all reduced to max-flow. The global form says a finite graph with more than `k` vertices is `k`-vertex-connected exactly when any two vertices are joined by `k` internally disjoint paths ([`SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_iff_forall_exists_paths`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/VertexConnectivity.html#SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_iff_forall_exists_paths)). The Gomory–Hu theorem landed too: every symmetric submodular set function on a nonempty finite vertex type has a cut tree, whose path minima are the pairwise minimum cut values and whose edges each cut out a minimum cut ([`TerminalCut.exists_isCutTree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/GomoryHu.html#TauCeti.TerminalCut.exists_isCutTree)), with the query theorem, the threshold partition and the multigraph corollaries ([`Graph.exists_isCutTree_edgeCutCapacity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Graph.html#Graph.exists_isCutTree_edgeCutCapacity)).

The consequences of Menger followed: Whitney's inequality, the fan lemma, Dirac's theorem that, for `k ≥ 2`, any `k` vertices of a `k`-connected graph lie on a common cycle ([`IsVertexConnected.exists_isCycle_forall_mem_support`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/PrescribedCycle.html#SimpleGraph.IsVertexConnected.exists_isCycle_forall_mem_support)), the vertex–vertex and vertex–edge common-cycle characterizations of 2-connectivity ([TauCeti#24945](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/24945)), and Kőnig's theorem for capacitated bipartite matchings. The deficiency form of Hall's theorem ([TauCeti#27157](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27157)) and line graphs with proper edge-colourings ([TauCeti#27326](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27326)) opened 6.8 and 6.9. On the circulation side came Hoffman's circulation theorem ([`exists_isCirculation_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Hoffman.html#TauCeti.exists_isCirculation_iff)), interval excess, integer rounding, and Hakimi's theorem on orientations with prescribed indegrees ([`Graph.exists_orientation_indegree_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Orientation/Hakimi.html#Graph.exists_orientation_indegree_eq_iff)); max-flow/min-cut now also holds for weighted multigraphs.

Cut vertices, blocks (a vertex is a cut vertex exactly when it lies in two blocks, [`SimpleGraph.isCutVertex_iff_exists_isBlock_ne`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/Block.html#SimpleGraph.isCutVertex_iff_exists_isBlock_ne)), the block–cut incidence graph ([TauCeti#25768](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25768)), the bridge forest, multigraph isomorphisms and orientations arrived. Ear decompositions exist as data, undirected and directed, and a graph with an ear decomposition is 2-edge-connected ([`Graph.EarDecomposition.isEdgeConnected_two`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/Ear/EdgeConnected.html#Graph.EarDecomposition.isEdgeConnected_two)). Whitney's theorem characterizes 2-vertex-connectivity: a finite simple graph is 2-vertex-connected exactly when it has an open ear decomposition, which can start from any cycle ([`isVertexConnected_two_iff_exists_isOpen_earDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/Ear/Open.html#SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_two_iff_exists_isOpen_earDecomposition), [TauCeti#27328](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27328)). A network is strongly connected exactly when it is weakly connected and every arrow lies on a directed cycle ([TauCeti#25776](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25776)). `st`-numberings arrived with the Lempel–Even–Cederbaum theorem ([`nonempty_stNumbering_iff_isVertexConnected_two`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/STNumbering.html#SimpleGraph.nonempty_stNumbering_iff_isVertexConnected_two), [TauCeti#27672](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27672)). The multigraph and directed ear converses, Robbins' theorem, the forest property of the block–cut graph, capacitated Hall and Kőnig's edge-colouring theorem are not yet proved.
