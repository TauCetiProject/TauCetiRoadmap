## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Most of the roadmap's summits are proved: max-flow/min-cut, the poset of minimum cuts, Menger's theorem in all its forms, Hoffman's circulation theorem, Hakimi's orientation theorem and Gomory–Hu cut trees. What remains sits on the graph-structure side: the ear characterizations and Robbins' theorem, the block–cut forest, `st`-numberings, capacitated Hall and bipartite edge-colouring.

### Named results

- **The max-flow/min-cut theorem** — for nonnegative capacities some flow's value equals some cut's capacity, over any linearly ordered additive group and with integrality in any subgroup containing the capacities ([`TauCeti.Flow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/ShortestAugmentation.html#TauCeti.Flow.exists_val_eq_arrowSum)); the undirected version for weighted multigraphs is also proved ([`Graph.exists_flow_val_eq_edgeCutCapacity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/Flow.html#Graph.exists_flow_val_eq_edgeCutCapacity)).
- **Menger's theorem** — the maximum number of internally disjoint paths between two nonadjacent vertices equals the minimum size of a separating vertex set ([`SimpleGraph.exists_paths_vertexSeparator_card_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/Menger.html#SimpleGraph.exists_paths_vertexSeparator_card_eq)), with arrow-disjoint, edge-disjoint, set-to-set, vertex-capacitated and adjacent-terminal versions for networks, digraphs, simple graphs and multigraphs, and the global form for `k`-connectivity ([`SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_iff_forall_exists_paths`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Connectivity/VertexConnectivity.html#SimpleGraph.isVertexConnected_iff_forall_exists_paths)).
- **The Gomory–Hu theorem** — every symmetric submodular set function on a nonempty finite vertex type has a cut tree, whose path minima give all pairwise minimum cut values and whose edges give actual minimum cuts ([`TauCeti.TerminalCut.exists_isCutTree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/GomoryHu.html#TauCeti.TerminalCut.exists_isCutTree)); weighted multigraphs are an instance ([`Graph.exists_isCutTree_edgeCutCapacity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/MinimumCut/Graph.html#Graph.exists_isCutTree_edgeCutCapacity)).
- **Hoffman's circulation theorem** — a circulation within lower and upper bounds exists exactly when no vertex set has more lower bound entering it than upper bound leaving it, and otherwise a violating set certifies infeasibility ([`TauCeti.exists_isCirculation_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Hoffman.html#TauCeti.exists_isCirculation_iff)).
- **Hakimi's theorem** — a finite multigraph has an orientation with indegrees `d` exactly when `d` sums to the number of edges and no vertex set spans more edges than its total `d` ([`Graph.exists_orientation_indegree_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Orientation/Hakimi.html#Graph.exists_orientation_indegree_eq_iff)), with the bounded-indegree version ([`Graph.exists_orientation_indegree_mem_Icc_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Orientation/Hakimi.html#Graph.exists_orientation_indegree_mem_Icc_iff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Residual updates** ([`TauCeti.residualUpdate`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Residual.html#TauCeti.residualUpdate)): one operation updating any feasible assignment along residual arrows, which augmentation, the minimum-cut theory and the residual cycle adjustments between two feasible assignments all specialise.
- **Multigraph orientations** ([`Graph.Orientation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Orientation/Basic.html#Graph.Orientation)): orientations and the bidirected network of a multigraph carry the directed theory over to undirected graphs; they give the undirected max-flow/min-cut theorem and the indegree theorems, and are what Robbins' theorem will be stated with.
- **Ear decompositions** ([`Graph.EarDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Graph/Connectivity/Ear/Basic.html#Graph.EarDecomposition), [`TauCeti.Network.EarDecomposition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Network/Ear/Basic.html#TauCeti.Network.EarDecomposition)): open and closed ears with prefix and induction API, in multigraphs and directed networks, the data on which the ear characterizations, Robbins' theorem and `st`-numberings are built.

## The frontier

- **Ear characterizations (7.2).** Existence of open ear decompositions for 2-vertex-connected graphs (from a prescribed cycle), of ear decompositions for 2-edge-connected multigraphs, and of directed ear decompositions for strongly connected networks; the 2-edge-connectivity bridge criterion and the easy direction are already in.
- **Robbins' theorem (7.3).** A strongly connected orientation exists exactly when the multigraph is 2-edge-connected; this waits on the directed ear characterization, after which orienting the ears gives it.
- **Capacitated Hall and deficiency (6.8).** The Kőnig–Ore formula, capacitated Hall and ordinary deficiency; the suggested proof normalises a minimum cover and applies the capacitated Kőnig theorem, which is proved.
- **The block–cut forest (2.4).** The block–cut incidence graph, its forest property and the component-count formula, plus the transfer of blocks to multigraphs (2.3); simple-graph blocks and cut vertices are ready.
- **Bipartite edge-colouring and `st`-numberings (6.9, 7.4).** Regular bipartite decomposition and edge-colouring can draw on the flow and Kőnig results; `st`-numberings need open ear decompositions from 7.2 first.
