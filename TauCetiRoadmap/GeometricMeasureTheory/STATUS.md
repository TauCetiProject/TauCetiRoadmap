## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The metric curve theory (G1) is complete, and both the area and coarea formulas of G2 are proved for general Lipschitz maps. The BV layers (G3, G4) are nearly done. The finite-perimeter structure theory (G5) has the blow-up theorem but not De Giorgi's structure theorem, and G6 has Gauss–Green against the perimeter measure and the Jordan index theorem but no traces.

### Named results

- **The coarea formula for Lipschitz maps** — for a Lipschitz map between finite-dimensional inner product spaces and a Lebesgue-measurable set, the integral of the `Hⁿ⁻ᵐ` fibre measures equals the integral of the coarea Jacobian, with almost every fibre countably rectifiable and real-valued forms under integrability ([`LipschitzWith.lintegral_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_eq_lintegral_normDet_adjoint_fderiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Lipschitz.html#LipschitzWith.lintegral_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_eq_lintegral_normDet_adjoint_fderiv)).
- **The area formula with multiplicity** — the weighted `normDet` Jacobian of a Lipschitz map integrates to the Hausdorff integral of the weighted fibre sums, infinite fibres counted as infinity ([`LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Multiplicity.html#LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum)).
- **The blow-up theorem** — in dimension at least two, rescalings of a set at a point of its reduced boundary converge locally in measure to the half-space given by the outer normal ([`TauCeti.tendsto_measure_preimage_add_smul_symmDiff_setOf_inner_lt_inter`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/BlowUp.html#TauCeti.tendsto_measure_preimage_add_smul_symmDiff_setOf_inner_lt_inter)); consequently the perimeter measure [has density one with respect to `Hⁿ⁻¹`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/BlowUp.html#TauCeti.tendsto_variationMeasure_closedBall_div_volume_closedBall) at those points.
- **The Gauss–Green formula** — for every set of locally finite perimeter, `Dχ_s = −ν_s |Dχ_s|` against compactly supported `C¹` vector fields ([`TauCeti.integral_trace_fderiv_mul_indicator_eq_integral_inner_outerNormal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.integral_trace_fderiv_mul_indicator_eq_integral_inner_outerNormal)). The measure is still `|Dχ_s|`, not `Hⁿ⁻¹` on the reduced boundary.
- **The polar decomposition of the BV derivative** — a function of locally finite variation has derivative `Du = σ_u |Du|` with a unit vector field `σ_u` ([`TauCeti.integral_divergenceCLM_mul_eq_neg_integral_inner_polarDensity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/PolarDecomposition.html#TauCeti.integral_divergenceCLM_mul_eq_neg_integral_inner_polarDensity)), and it is weakly differentiable [exactly when `|Du|` is absolutely continuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/WeakDerivative.html#TauCeti.exists_hasWeakFDerivOn_iff).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The variation measure**: [`TauCeti.variationMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/VariationMeasure.html#TauCeti.variationMeasure) is the regular measure `|Du|` on `Ω`, built by compatible restriction with no global finiteness assumption. The perimeter measure and density estimates are stated against it.
- **The Banach space `BV(Ω)`**: [`TauCeti.BV`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Space.html#TauCeti.BV) is the a.e. quotient of integrable functions of finite variation with norm `‖u‖₁ + |Du|(Ω)`, complete and closed under restriction, Lipschitz composition and lattice operations.
- **Measure-theoretic boundaries**: the [essential boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Covering/EssentialBoundary.html#TauCeti.essentialBoundary) and the [reduced boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.reducedBoundary) with its [outer normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.outerNormal), independent of the a.e. representative. The reduced boundary carries the perimeter measure.

### Roadmap coverage

G1 is done. G2 is partial: area formulas, graph and sphere measure, polar integration, and coarea for Lipschitz and locally Lipschitz maps with weighted and real-valued forms are proved. On rectifiable sets with locally finite `μHE[k]`, approximate tangent planes exist a.e., admit a [Borel measurable choice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/MeasurableTangent.html#TauCeti.CountablyRectifiable.exists_measurable_approximate_tangent_projection), and the density theorem holds. Classical tangent planes of differentiable graphs are approximate tangent planes. Their a.e. uniqueness, tangential differentiation and the tangential area formula are missing. G3 is partial but near done: only a dilation statement for `MemBV` and `BV` themselves is missing; the Cantor function is now a [BV function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/OneDimensional/Cantor.html#TauCeti.memBV_cantorFunction) with singular variation measure. G4 is partial, missing only global `L¹` compactness on bounded Lipschitz domains. G5 is partial: perimeter API, density estimates, compactness of blow-ups, the blow-up theorem, density one half and `Hⁿ⁻¹`-density one of the perimeter measure at the reduced boundary are proved. De Giorgi's rectifiability and representation statements, the essential/reduced comparison and the frontier criterion are not. G6 is partial: Gauss–Green against `|Dχ_s|`, the divergence theorem on a half-space, the Jordan index theorem and Green's theorem with its orientation sign are in place. Traces, and finite perimeter with `P(Ω) = H¹(C)` for the inside of a rectifiable Jordan curve, are missing.

## The frontier

- **De Giorgi's structure theorem**: rectifiability of the reduced boundary and `|Dχ_E| = Hⁿ⁻¹|∂*E|`. The blow-up theorem and the density-one comparison with `Hⁿ⁻¹` are proved, so what remains is assembling them through a Hausdorff density argument; this would put Gauss–Green into surface-measure form.
- **Essential versus reduced boundary**: show they differ by an `Hⁿ⁻¹`-null set, then the finite-perimeter criterion and `P(E) ≤ Hⁿ⁻¹(frontier E)`. Needs De Giorgi's theorem.
- **Tangent planes on rectifiable sets**: a.e. uniqueness of the approximate tangent plane, tangential differentiation of Lipschitz maps and the tangential area formula. Existence, measurability and the density theorem are in place.
- **The inside of a rectifiable Jordan curve**: finite perimeter, `P(Ω) = H¹(C)` and the outward normal `−σJτ`. A [pointwise statement that `σ · I v` points inside](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Winding/AbsolutelyContinuous/Jordan.html#AbsolutelyContinuousOnInterval.eventually_mem_filledHull_sdiff_and_notMem_of_hasDerivAt) at regular parameters is available; the measure comparison depends on the G5 structure theory.
- **BV traces on Lipschitz domains**: the trace, its estimate, extension, gluing and integration by parts with the trace term. Nothing has landed.
