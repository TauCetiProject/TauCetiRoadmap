### Named results

- **The area formula with multiplicity** — for a Lipschitz map between finite-dimensional inner product spaces, the weighted `normDet` Jacobian integrates to the Hausdorff integral of the weighted fibre sums, infinite fibres counted as infinity ([`LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Multiplicity.html#LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum)).
- **The coarea formula** — integrating the `Hⁿ⁻ᵐ` measures of the fibres of a nonlinear map gives the integral of its coarea Jacobian, proved for maps with surjective derivative ([`MeasureTheory.lintegral_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_eq_lintegral_normDet_adjoint_fderiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Basic.html#MeasureTheory.lintegral_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_eq_lintegral_normDet_adjoint_fderiv)).
- **The polar decomposition of the BV derivative** — a function of locally finite variation has derivative `Du = σ_u |Du|` with a unit vector field `σ_u` ([`TauCeti.integral_divergenceCLM_mul_eq_neg_integral_inner_polarDensity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/PolarDecomposition.html#TauCeti.integral_divergenceCLM_mul_eq_neg_integral_inner_polarDensity)), and it is weakly differentiable [exactly when `|Du|` is absolutely continuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/WeakDerivative.html#TauCeti.exists_hasWeakFDerivOn_iff).
- **The Gauss–Green formula** — for every set of locally finite perimeter, `Dχ_s = −ν_s |Dχ_s|` against compactly supported `C¹` vector fields ([`TauCeti.integral_trace_fderiv_mul_indicator_eq_integral_inner_outerNormal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.integral_trace_fderiv_mul_indicator_eq_integral_inner_outerNormal)). The measure is still `|Dχ_s|`, not `Hⁿ⁻¹` on the reduced boundary.
- **The relative isoperimetric inequality in a ball** — `min(|E ∩ B_r|, |B_r \ E|)^((n−1)/n) ≤ C_n P(E; B_r)` for `n ≥ 2` ([`TauCeti.min_measure_inter_measure_sdiff_rpow_le_perimeter`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/RelativeIsoperimetric.html#TauCeti.min_measure_inter_measure_sdiff_rpow_le_perimeter)), the analytic input to the density estimates at the reduced boundary.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The variation measure**: [`TauCeti.variationMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/VariationMeasure.html#TauCeti.variationMeasure) is the regular measure `|Du|` on `Ω` agreeing with distributional total variation on open sets, built by compatible restriction and needing no global finiteness. The polar decomposition and the perimeter measure are stated against it.
- **The Banach space `BV(Ω)`**: [`TauCeti.BV`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Space.html#TauCeti.BV) is the a.e. quotient of integrable functions of finite variation with norm `‖u‖₁ + |Du|(Ω)`, [complete](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Space.html#TauCeti.BV.instCompleteSpace) and with restriction to open subsets as a contraction.
- **Measure-theoretic boundaries**: the [essential boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Covering/EssentialBoundary.html#TauCeti.essentialBoundary), defined through Lebesgue density, and the [reduced boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.reducedBoundary) with its [outer normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.outerNormal). Both are independent of the a.e. representative, and the reduced boundary carries the perimeter measure and, in dimension at least two, lies inside the essential boundary.

## The frontier

- **The blow-up theorem**: compactness of perimeter sets, the normal of blow-up limits and the [constant-normal half-space characterisation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/HalfSpace.html#TauCeti.ae_eq_setOf_inner_lt_of_ae_outerNormal_eq) are all proved; what remains is convergence of blow-ups at every reduced-boundary point to the half-space with normal `ν`.
- **De Giorgi's structure theorem**: rectifiability of the reduced boundary and `|Dχ_E| = Hⁿ⁻¹|∂*E|`, which would turn Gauss–Green into its surface-measure form. Needs the blow-up theorem; the density estimates and Hausdorff density comparison are in place.
- **Essential versus reduced boundary**: show they differ by an `Hⁿ⁻¹`-null set, then the finite-perimeter criterion and `P(E) ≤ Hⁿ⁻¹(frontier E)`.
- **Tangent planes on rectifiable sets**: a.e. uniqueness and measurability of the approximate tangent plane, tangential differentiation and the tangential area formula. Existence and the density theorem are in place.
- **Coarea for general Lipschitz maps**: extend the surjective-derivative formula across the critical set, with real integrable versions.
