### Named results

- **The coarea formula for Lipschitz maps** — for a Lipschitz map between finite-dimensional inner product spaces and a Lebesgue-measurable set, integrating the `Hⁿ⁻ᵐ` measures of the fibres gives the integral of the coarea Jacobian, with no surjectivity assumed ([`LipschitzWith.lintegral_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_eq_lintegral_normDet_adjoint_fderiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Lipschitz.html#LipschitzWith.lintegral_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_eq_lintegral_normDet_adjoint_fderiv)). The weighted form is proved only for maps with surjective derivative.
- **The area formula with multiplicity** — the weighted Jacobian of a Lipschitz map integrates to the Hausdorff integral of the weighted fibre sums, infinite fibres counted as infinity ([`LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Multiplicity.html#LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum)).
- **The blow-up theorem** — in dimension at least two, rescalings of a set at a reduced-boundary point converge locally in measure to the half-space of the outer normal ([`TauCeti.tendsto_measure_preimage_add_smul_symmDiff_setOf_inner_lt_inter`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/BlowUp.html#TauCeti.tendsto_measure_preimage_add_smul_symmDiff_setOf_inner_lt_inter)), so the perimeter measure has [density one with respect to `Hⁿ⁻¹`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/BlowUp.html#TauCeti.tendsto_variationMeasure_closedBall_div_volume_closedBall) there.
- **The Gauss–Green formula** — for every set of locally finite perimeter, `∫_s div φ = ∫ ⟪φ, ν_s⟫ d|Dχ_s|` for compactly supported `C¹` fields ([`TauCeti.integral_trace_fderiv_mul_indicator_eq_integral_inner_outerNormal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.integral_trace_fderiv_mul_indicator_eq_integral_inner_outerNormal)); the measure is still `|Dχ_s|`, not `Hⁿ⁻¹` on the reduced boundary.
- **The Jordan index theorem** — a simple closed absolutely continuous plane curve has winding number one sign `σ = ±1` at every point inside ([`AbsolutelyContinuousOnInterval.windingNumber_eq_jordanSign_of_mem_filledHull_sdiff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Winding/AbsolutelyContinuous/Jordan.html#AbsolutelyContinuousOnInterval.windingNumber_eq_jordanSign_of_mem_filledHull_sdiff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The variation measure**: [`TauCeti.variationMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/VariationMeasure.html#TauCeti.variationMeasure) is `|Du|` on `Ω` with no global finiteness assumption, and with the [polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/PolarDecomposition.html#TauCeti.integral_divergenceCLM_mul_eq_neg_integral_inner_polarDensity) `Du = σ_u |Du|` it carries the perimeter measure, density estimates and Gauss–Green.
- **The Banach space `BV(Ω)`**: [`TauCeti.BV`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Space.html#TauCeti.BV), the a.e. quotient with norm `‖u‖₁ + |Du|(Ω)`, complete and closed under restriction, Lipschitz composition and lattice operations; it is the setting the trace operator of Layer 6 is to act on.
- **Strict approximation on open sets**: [smooth functions on an arbitrary open `Ω`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Approximation.html#MeasureTheory.LocallyIntegrableOn.exists_contDiffOn_strict_approximation) converge in `L¹(Ω)` with variations converging to `|Du|(Ω)`, finite or infinite. This already gives the statement of §4.1.

## The frontier

- **Weighted Lipschitz coarea**: the formula with a measurable weight `g`, for every Lipschitz map. The weighted surjective case and the Sard-type exceptional-set lemma are both proved; they need combining.
- **Arclength calculus (§1.1)**: metric speed equals `‖γ'‖` a.e. for BV curves, and `s' = ‖γ'‖`, `γ' = ‖γ'‖ c'(s)` and `γ(t) − γ(a) = ∫ c'` for absolutely continuous curves. The inequality `s' ≥ ‖γ'‖` and the absolutely continuous length formula are in place.
- **De Giorgi's structure theorem**: rectifiability of the reduced boundary and `|Dχ_E| = Hⁿ⁻¹⌊∂*E`, which would turn Gauss–Green into its surface-measure form. Density one is proved, so a covering argument and the rectifiable density theorem remain.
- **Federer's theorem**: essential and reduced boundaries differ by an `Hⁿ⁻¹`-null set, with the finite-perimeter criterion and `P(E) ≤ Hⁿ⁻¹(frontier E)`. Needs De Giorgi and the truncation inequality of §5.1.
- **Classical domains**: perimeter and normal of Lipschitz domains, BV traces with integration by parts, and `P(Ω) = H¹(C)` with normal `−σJτ` inside a rectifiable Jordan curve. All wait on De Giorgi's theorem; nothing has landed.
