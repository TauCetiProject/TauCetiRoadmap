### Named results

- **The area formula with multiplicity** — for a Lipschitz map between finite-dimensional inner product spaces and a Lebesgue-measurable set, the weighted `normDet` Jacobian integrates to the Hausdorff integral of the weighted fibre sums, with infinite fibres counted as infinity ([`LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Multiplicity.html#LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum)).
- **The coarea formula for BV functions** — for `u` locally integrable on an open set, `|Du|(Ω) = ∫ P({u > t}; Ω) dt`, finite or infinite ([`TauCeti.totalVariationOn_eq_lintegral_perimeter`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Coarea.html#TauCeti.totalVariationOn_eq_lintegral_perimeter)).
- **The Banach indicatrix formula** — the length of any continuous curve is the H¹-integral of the number of times it passes through each point, with no rectifiability hypothesis ([`ContinuousOn.eVariationOn_eq_lintegral_encard`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Curve.html#ContinuousOn.eVariationOn_eq_lintegral_encard)).
- **Surface area of the sphere** — `Hⁿ⁻¹(sphere c r) = n ω_n r^(n−1)` ([`TauCeti.euclideanHausdorffMeasure_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Sphere.html#TauCeti.euclideanHausdorffMeasure_sphere)), the constant Isoperimetry consumes.
- **The BV Sobolev inequality** — an integrable function in dimension `n ≥ 2` has `L^(n/(n−1))` norm bounded by a constant times its total variation ([`TauCeti.eLpNorm_le_totalVariationOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.eLpNorm_le_totalVariationOn)), which gives a [nonsharp isoperimetric inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.measure_rpow_le_perimeter) for finite-volume sets.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Distributional variation and perimeter** — [`TauCeti.totalVariationOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/TotalVariation.html#TauCeti.totalVariationOn) is a supremum over unit test vector fields, and [`TauCeti.perimeter`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Perimeter.html#TauCeti.perimeter) is the variation of an indicator. Both are extended-valued and need neither finite volume nor a derivative measure, which is what lets coarea, compactness and Poincaré be proved before `Du` exists.
- **Arclength factorization** — every continuous rectifiable curve factors through a 1-Lipschitz curve on its length interval, constant pieces included ([`BoundedVariationOn.exists_arclength_factorization`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MetricSpace/Arclength/Basic.html#BoundedVariationOn.exists_arclength_factorization)). This is the interface DifferentialGeometry's line integrals consume.
- **Directional derivative measures** — through a signed Riesz–Markov–Kakutani theorem, [each directional derivative of a function of locally finite variation is a signed Radon measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/DerivativeMeasure.html#TauCeti.exists_regular_lineDeriv_ofFun_apply_eq). These are the components from which the vector measure `Du` is to be built.

## The frontier

- **Relative perimeter estimate on balls** — `min(|E ∩ B_r|, |B_r \ E|)^((n−1)/n) ≤ C_n P(E; B_r)`. The global inequality and the ball Poincaré inequality are both in place; what remains is to localize. This is the analytic input to G5.
- **The vector derivative measure** — assemble the directional signed measures into a locally finite vector Radon measure `Du` whose total variation is `totalVariationOn`, then the normed `BV(Ω)` quotient and the polar decomposition.
- **Smooth approximation on open sets** — strict approximation on an arbitrary open set by a partition of unity, and approximation of finite-volume finite-perimeter sets by bounded smooth sets. Whole-space mollification is done, and Sard is available for regular levels.
- **Nonlinear coarea formula** — extend linear coarea and Eilenberg's inequality to Lipschitz `f : ℝⁿ → ℝᵐ`, with fibre measurability and real integrable forms.
- **Tangent planes on rectifiable sets** — approximate tangent planes, the density theorem and tangential differentiation. De Giorgi's structure theorem depends on these and on the relative perimeter estimate.
