## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The metric curve theory (G1) is essentially in place, and G2's area formula, sphere measure and polar integration are proved. Nonlinear coarea and the tangent-plane theory of rectifiable sets are still open. BV and perimeter (G3–G5) have a variational definition with its first properties, but no derivative measure, no structure theorem, and nothing yet of G6.

### Named results

- **The area formula with multiplicity** — for a Lipschitz map between finite-dimensional inner product spaces and a Lebesgue-measurable set, the weighted `normDet` Jacobian integrates to the Hausdorff integral of the weighted fibre sums, with infinite fibres counted as infinity ([`LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Multiplicity.html#LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum)); a real-valued form, the injective case and graph surface measure accompany it.
- **Length equals Hausdorff measure of the trace** — for a continuous curve injective on the open interval (arcs and simple closed curves), variation equals H¹ of the image, finite or infinite, so finite geometric length and rectifiability agree ([`ContinuousOn.eVariationOn_eq_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_image`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/EMetricSpace/CurveLength.html#ContinuousOn.eVariationOn_eq_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_image)).
- **Surface area of the sphere** — `Hⁿ⁻¹(sphere c r) = n ω_n r^(n−1)` ([`TauCeti.euclideanHausdorffMeasure_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Sphere.html#TauCeti.euclideanHausdorffMeasure_sphere)), with polar integration against Hausdorff measure on spheres ([`TauCeti.integral_eq_integral_Ioi_setIntegral_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Polar.html#TauCeti.integral_eq_integral_Ioi_setIntegral_sphere)).
- **Eilenberg's inequality** — a Lipschitz map's fibre measures integrate to at most a constant times the Hausdorff measure of the source, the first nonlinear step toward coarea ([`LipschitzOnWith.lintegral_hausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Eilenberg.html#LipschitzOnWith.lintegral_hausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_le)); the full coarea formula is proved only for linear maps ([`LinearMap.lintegral_setLIntegral_preimage_singleton`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Linear.html#LinearMap.lintegral_setLIntegral_preimage_singleton)).
- **Variation of a Sobolev function** — if `u` has weak derivative `U` on `Ω`, then `|Du|(Ω) = ∫_Ω ‖U‖` ([`TauCeti.HasWeakFDerivOn.totalVariationOn_eq_setLIntegral_enorm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/TotalVariation.html#TauCeti.HasWeakFDerivOn.totalVariationOn_eq_setLIntegral_enorm)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Arclength factorization** — every continuous rectifiable curve factors through a 1-Lipschitz curve on its length interval, constant pieces included ([`BoundedVariationOn.exists_arclength_factorization`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MetricSpace/Arclength/Basic.html#BoundedVariationOn.exists_arclength_factorization)). This is the interface DifferentialGeometry's line integrals consume.
- **Distributional variation and perimeter** — [`TauCeti.totalVariationOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/TotalVariation.html#TauCeti.totalVariationOn) is defined as a supremum over unit test vector fields, and [`TauCeti.perimeter`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Perimeter.html#TauCeti.perimeter) is the variation of an indicator. Both are extended-valued and need neither finite volume nor a derivative measure.
- **Countable rectifiability** — [`TauCeti.CountablyRectifiable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable.html#TauCeti.CountablyRectifiable) means covered by Lipschitz patches up to a `μHE[k]`-null set. It is closed under countable unions, subsets, null modification, Lipschitz images and isometries.

### Roadmap coverage

G1 is nearly complete. It has the factorization, the homeomorphism for injective arcs, the unit-speed simple closed parametrization, length as the integral of the metric derivative and of `‖γ'‖` for absolutely continuous curves, the two-sided H¹ comparison, path reparametrization invariance, and null planar traces. What remains is the multiplicity formula for nonsimple curves beyond the Lipschitz case, along with the worked examples. G2 is partial. Its area formulas, graph and sphere measure, `H⁰` as counting measure, polar integration and linear slicing are done. Approximate tangent planes, the density theorem, tangential differentiation, the tangential area formula and nonlinear coarea are not. G3 is partial: it has the variational total variation and the `W¹,¹` variation identity, but no measure-valued derivative, Radon representation or normed `BV(Ω)`. G4 has only lower semicontinuity under `L¹_loc` convergence. G5 has the elementary perimeter API (complements, null modification, monotonicity, translation, dilation, lower semicontinuity) but no reduced boundary or De Giorgi theorem. G6 is untouched.

## The frontier

- **Nonlinear coarea formula** — extend linear coarea and Eilenberg's inequality to Lipschitz `f : ℝⁿ → ℝᵐ`, with fibre measurability and real integrable forms; this unblocks scalar BV coarea in G4.
- **Measure-valued BV derivative** — construct `Du` as a locally finite vector Radon measure whose total variation is `totalVariationOn`, then the normed `BV(Ω)` quotient and polar decomposition.
- **Tangent planes on rectifiable sets** — approximate tangent planes, the density theorem and tangential differentiation, leading to the tangential area formula; De Giorgi's structure theorem depends on these.
- **Multiplicity formula for rectifiable curves** — transfer the Lipschitz-curve multiplicity identity through the arclength factorization to general rectifiable curves, and use it to compute the twice-traversed circle.
- **Compactness, strict approximation and relative isoperimetry** — the remaining G4 analysis, which is the input to G5's boundary structure.
