## GeometricMeasureTheory: 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-19 (`7503d78` to `9fc5d5a`)

The headline of this first window is the [area formula with multiplicity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Multiplicity.html#LipschitzWith.lintegral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_lintegral_tsum): for a Lipschitz map between finite-dimensional inner product spaces and a Lebesgue-measurable parameter set, integrating the `normDet` Jacobian against a weight equals the Hausdorff integral of the weighted fibre sums, with infinite fibres counted as infinity. It comes with [Sard's lemma for Lipschitz maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Lipschitz.html#LipschitzWith.euclideanHausdorffMeasure_image_setOf_normDet_fderiv_eq_zero), a [real-valued version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Real.html#LipschitzWith.integral_normDet_fderiv_mul_eq_integral_tsum) under a finite-integral hypothesis, and surface measure on graphs with the classical factor √(1 + ‖Dφ‖²). On the sphere side, [the sphere of radius r has (n−1)-dimensional measure n ω_n r^(n−1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Sphere.html#TauCeti.euclideanHausdorffMeasure_sphere), and [polar integration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Polar.html#TauCeti.integral_eq_integral_Ioi_setIntegral_sphere) is now written against Hausdorff measure on spheres. Coarea exists so far only for [linear maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Linear.html#LinearMap.lintegral_setLIntegral_preimage_singleton), together with [Eilenberg's inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Eilenberg.html#LipschitzOnWith.lintegral_hausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_le). The nonlinear coarea formula is still to come.

Curves came most of the way. A continuous rectifiable curve now [factors through a 1-Lipschitz curve on its length interval](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MetricSpace/Arclength/Basic.html#BoundedVariationOn.exists_arclength_factorization), and simple closed curves get a unit-speed parametrization. For arcs and simple closed curves, [length equals the H¹ measure of the trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/EMetricSpace/CurveLength.html#ContinuousOn.eVariationOn_eq_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_image), finite or infinite, so finite H¹ trace and rectifiability coincide. For Lipschitz curves, [length is the integral of fibre multiplicity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Curve.html#LipschitzOnWith.eVariationOn_eq_lintegral_encard). Path lengths also behave correctly under concatenation, reversal and reparametrization.

The BV side has begun with [distributional total variation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/TotalVariation.html#TauCeti.totalVariationOn), defined as a supremum over test vector fields, and [perimeter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Perimeter.html#TauCeti.perimeter) as the variation of an indicator. Both have lower semicontinuity, locality and translation invariance, and perimeter is unchanged under complements (TauCeti#17656). For weakly differentiable functions, [variation is the integral of the gradient norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/TotalVariation.html#TauCeti.HasWeakFDerivOn.totalVariationOn_eq_setLIntegral_enorm). There is no measure-valued derivative yet.

## GeometricMeasureTheory: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25 (`9fc5d5a` to `69cd1ab`)

The scalar coarea formula for BV functions is in: for `u` locally integrable on an open set Ω, [the total variation is the integral of the perimeters of its superlevel sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Coarea.html#TauCeti.totalVariationOn_eq_lintegral_perimeter), `|Du|(Ω) = ∫ P({u > t}; Ω) dt`, finite or infinite (TauCeti#19785). It comes with measurability of the perimeter in the level, [finite perimeter for almost every superlevel set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Coarea.html#TauCeti.ae_perimeter_setOf_lt_lt_top) when the variation is finite, and a [locally Lipschitz form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Coarea.html#LocallyLipschitzOn.lintegral_perimeter_eq_setLIntegral_enorm_fderiv) with `∫ ‖Du‖` on the left. More of G4's analysis arrived with it. Mollification [recovers the variation on the whole space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Convolution.html#TauCeti.tendsto_totalVariationOn_normed_convolution), families bounded in `L¹` and in variation are [precompact in `L¹` of each compact subset](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Compactness.html#TauCeti.isCompact_closure_of_setLIntegral_le_of_totalVariationOn_le), and there is a [BV Poincaré inequality on balls](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Poincare.html#TauCeti.setLIntegral_enorm_sub_setAverage_le_mul_totalVariationOn_of_eq_ball). The [Sobolev inequality for BV functions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.eLpNorm_le_totalVariationOn) gives a [nonsharp isoperimetric inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.measure_rpow_le_perimeter) for finite-volume sets. It is global; the relative estimate on balls that G5 needs is not there yet.

For curves, the [Banach indicatrix formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Curve.html#ContinuousOn.eVariationOn_eq_lintegral_encard) now holds for every continuous curve: length is the H¹-integral of fibre multiplicity, with no rectifiability hypothesis. As a check, [a twice-traversed circle has twice the length](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/Circle.html#TauCeti.eVariationOn_circleMap_Icc_zero_four_pi) of its trace. The [Cantor–Lebesgue function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/CantorFunction.html#TauCeti.cantorFunction) has everything the roadmap's acceptance example asks for: it is continuous and monotone with variation one and derivative zero almost everywhere, and its Stieltjes measure is a probability measure [singular to Lebesgue measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/CantorFunction.html#TauCeti.mutuallySingular_cantorStieltjesFunction_measure_volume).

On the way to a measure-valued derivative, a [signed Riesz–Markov–Kakutani theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/RieszMarkovKakutani/Signed.html#TauCeti.RealRMK.integral_rieszMeasure_posPart_sub_integral_rieszMeasure_negPart) shows that [each directional derivative of a function of locally finite variation is a signed Radon measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/DerivativeMeasure.html#TauCeti.exists_regular_lineDeriv_ofFun_apply_eq). The vector measure `Du` is not yet assembled from these. Perimeter also gained [submodularity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Submodularity.html#TauCeti.perimeter_union_add_perimeter_inter_le) and [invariance under isometries](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Isometry.html#LinearIsometryEquiv.perimeter_preimage).

## GeometricMeasureTheory: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-06 (`69cd1ab` to `385ec2d`)

The [Gauss–Green formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.integral_trace_fderiv_mul_indicator_eq_integral_inner_outerNormal) now holds for every set of locally finite perimeter: `∫_s div φ = ∫ ⟪φ, ν_s⟫ d|Dχ_s|` for compactly supported `C¹` fields, with the [outer normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.outerNormal) defined on a [reduced boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.reducedBoundary) that [carries the whole perimeter measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.html#TauCeti.ae_mem_reducedBoundary). This rests on the BV derivative finally being a measure. A function of locally finite variation has a [variation measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/VariationMeasure.html#TauCeti.variationMeasure) `|Du|`, and the [polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/PolarDecomposition.html#TauCeti.integral_divergenceCLM_mul_eq_neg_integral_inner_polarDensity) `Du = σ_u |Du|` represents its derivative. A [Lebesgue decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/LebesgueDecomposition.html#TauCeti.integral_divergenceCLM_mul_eq_neg_integral_inner_acDerivativeDensity_sub_singularPart) splits it, and [`u` is weakly differentiable exactly when `|Du|` is absolutely continuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/WeakDerivative.html#TauCeti.exists_hasWeakFDerivOn_iff). Integrable BV functions form a [complete normed space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Space.html#TauCeti.BV.instCompleteSpace), with restriction and Lipschitz composition.

De Giorgi's structure theorem is close but not proved. Lebesgue's density theorem gives the [essential boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Covering/EssentialBoundary.html#TauCeti.essentialBoundary), which [contains the reduced boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/LowerDensityEstimates.html#TauCeti.reducedBoundary_subset_essentialBoundary). The lower density estimates come from the [relative isoperimetric inequality in a ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/RelativeIsoperimetric.html#TauCeti.min_measure_inter_measure_sdiff_rpow_le_perimeter). [Blow-up limits have outer normal `ν`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/BlowUp.html#TauCeti.integral_divergenceCLM_mul_indicator_nonneg_of_tendsto_measure_symmDiff_inter), and [a set with constant normal is a half-space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/HalfSpace.html#TauCeti.ae_eq_setOf_inner_lt_of_ae_outerNormal_eq). Still missing are the assembled blow-up theorem, rectifiability of the reduced boundary and `|Dχ_E| = Hⁿ⁻¹|∂*E`, so Gauss–Green is stated against the perimeter measure rather than surface measure.

On the rectifiability side, the [coarea formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Basic.html#MeasureTheory.lintegral_euclideanHausdorffMeasure_inter_preimage_eq_lintegral_normDet_adjoint_fderiv) now covers nonlinear maps, so far only those with surjective derivative. [Approximate tangent planes exist](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/ApproximateTangent.html#TauCeti.CountablyRectifiable.ae_exists_submodule_tendsto_measure_outside_cone_div) almost everywhere on countably rectifiable sets, though their uniqueness is not yet proved. The approximation and compactness work brought weak convergence of derivative measures, compactness for sets of locally finite perimeter, and [approximation of finite-volume sets by bounded smooth sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/SmoothApproximation.html#MeasureTheory.NullMeasurableSet.exists_smooth_perimeter_approximation).
