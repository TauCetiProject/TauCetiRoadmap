## CharacteristicClasses: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-09 (`329ed53` to `d92acc5`)

The roadmap's first characteristic class has landed. An oriented real vector bundle over an arbitrary base now has an integral [Thom class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/ThomClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.thomClass), the unique class in the top relative cohomology of the deleted total space that restricts on each fibre to the generator chosen by the orientation ([uniqueness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/ThomClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eq_thomClass_iff)). It is built by gluing local fibre classes over a trivializing cover, using Mayer–Vietoris and directed unions, and the same argument gives every bundle, oriented or not, a [unique mod 2 Thom class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/MayerVietoris/DirectedUnion.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isThomClass_of_subsingleton_units). The [Euler class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass) is its pullback along the zero section. It is natural, changes sign when the orientation is reversed, is [2-torsion in odd rank](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.two_smul_eulerClass), and [vanishes when there is a nowhere-zero section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass_eq_zero_of_forall_ne_zero), so it vanishes for oriented line bundles (TauCeti#26601, TauCeti#28465). The [Thom map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Thom/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.thomMap) a ↦ π\*a ⌣ u is defined and is linear over the cohomology of the base. Nobody has yet proved that it is an isomorphism, and there is no Gysin sequence.

Underneath this sits the frame and orientation infrastructure. The [orthonormal frame bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/OrthonormalFrame/Basic.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle) is built by Gram–Schmidt, and it [recovers the bundle as an associated bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/OrthonormalFrame/Basic.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.associatedHomeomorph). Its [quotient by SO(n) is the orientation double cover](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/OrthonormalFrame.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.orientationCoverHomeomorph). Two metrics are compared through the inverse square root of a Riesz operator, the [metric change transport](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Positive/MetricChange/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.metricChangeTransport), with an explicit correction for composing two such changes. Orientation covers of direct sums are handled, and so is the [orientation local system](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/LocalSystem.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientationCover.signLocalSystem). Global trivializations of a principal bundle correspond to its continuous sections.

On the formal side, Hirzebruch's [multiplicative sequences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MultiplicativeSequence.html#PowerSeries.multiplicativeSequence) arrived with their root description and [Whitney formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MultiplicativeSequence.html#PowerSeries.aeval_multiset_esymm_add_multiplicativeSequence). So did the [L series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/CharacteristicSeries.html#TauCeti.hirzebruchLSeries) and the [Â series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/CharacteristicSeries.html#TauCeti.aHatSeries), the relation Td(X) = e^{X/2}Â(X), and the descent of the even series to squared roots. None of this is attached to a bundle yet, because no Chern or Pontryagin classes exist to feed it.
