## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** For real bundles, the Thom class, the Thom isomorphism over compact bases and the Euler number as a sum of local indices are proved. The Whitney product formula, the complex classes and Leray–Hirsch (products only) are partial. Higher characteristic classes, Chern–Weil theory and the compact-Lie layers have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Thom class and the Thom isomorphism** — an oriented real vector bundle over any base has a unique integral [Thom class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/ThomClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.thomClass), and every bundle has a unique mod 2 one. The Thom map a ↦ π\*a ⌣ u is proved bijective in rank 0 and in rank at least 2 over any base covered by finitely many trivializing opens, such as a compact one.
- **The Euler number is the sum of the local indices** — on a compact oriented (n + 1)-manifold, a continuous section with finitely many zeros of an oriented rank-(n + 1) bundle [computes ⟨e(E), [M]⟩ as the sum of its local indices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/SectionIndex.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.evalFundamentalClass_eulerClass_eq_sum_sectionIndex). At a transverse zero, [the index is the sign of the Jacobian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/SectionIndex/Degree.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.sectionIndex_eq_sign_det_of_hasFDerivAt).
- **The Euler class and its first properties** — the [Euler class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass) is [natural under bundle maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/FiberwiseEquiv.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass_comap). It changes sign when the orientation is reversed and is [2-torsion in odd rank](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.two_smul_eulerClass). A nowhere-zero section makes it vanish, and its [Whitney product formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/Whitney.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass_thomClassOfModel_prod) is proved for normalized classes.
- **The Euler class of a complex bundle** — the [complex Euler class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/Complex/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.complexEulerClass) uses the canonical orientation (v₁, iv₁, …). On a line it is the roadmap's c₁, and it [changes sign under conjugation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/Complex/FiberwiseEquiv.html#TauCeti.complexEulerClass_eq_neg_of_finrank_eq_one_of_conjugateFiberwise).
- **Leray–Hirsch for a product** — when the fibre's homology is projective and finitely generated, [H\*(X × F) is free over H\*(X)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/LerayHirsch/FiberwiseBasis.html#TopCat.lerayHirschProdOfClasses_bijective) on any classes that restrict to a basis on every fibre.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The orthonormal frames [form a fibre bundle with unitary fibre](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/OrthonormalFrame/Principal.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.instFiberBundleFiber), and the oriented frames form [a principal SO(n)-bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/OrientedFrame.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.exists_trivialization_orientedFrames). The canonical metric change transport, which is continuous and smooth in the metric, makes them independent of the chosen metric. These are the carriers the universal classifiers will need.
- Given a reference trivialization of a principal bundle, homotopy classes of trivializations [correspond to homotopy classes of maps to the structure group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/FiberBundle/Principal/Trivialization/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.PrincipalBundle.GlobalTrivialization.homotopyClassEquiv), with a relative version. This is the input C1.1d needs for clutching.
- The formal multiplicative side is ready for bundles. It has Hirzebruch's [multiplicative sequences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MultiplicativeSequence.html#PowerSeries.multiplicativeSequence) with their Whitney formula, the L, Â and Todd series, and the [Chern character polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/ChernCharacter.html#TauCeti.chernCharacterPolynomial) with root, additivity, multiplicativity, dual and line-twist identities.

## The frontier

- **The genuine Whitney product formula** — prove that the cross product of fibre generators generates the cohomology of the product fibre pair. The normalized product formulas then give U(E ⊕ F) = U(E) × U(F) and e(E ⊕ F) = e(E) ⌣ e(F).
- **e(TM)[M] = χ(M)** — the sum-of-indices formula is in place. What remains is the tangent-bundle Thom class with the matching orientation, and the diagonal and Künneth argument the roadmap prescribes.
- **Thom isomorphism in general, and the Gysin sequence** — extend bijectivity to rank 1 and to bases without a finite trivializing cover, then combine it with the existing disc–sphere comparison to get the sphere-bundle Gysin sequence.
- **Leray–Hirsch for locally trivial bundles** — the cell-by-cell and Milnor-sequence argument for general bases, and then the projective-bundle specialization, are the gate to Stiefel–Whitney, Chern and Pontryagin classes.
- **c₁ beyond its definition** — construct the determinant line, then prove c₁(L\*) = −c₁(L) and tensor additivity c₁(L ⊗ L′) = c₁(L) + c₁(L′).
