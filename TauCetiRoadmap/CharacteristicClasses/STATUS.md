## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Thom and Euler classes of oriented real vector bundles exist and have their basic properties. They rest on a substantial layer of metric, frame and orientation infrastructure. The formal Todd, L and Â series and Hirzebruch's multiplicative sequences are in place. The Thom isomorphism, the Gysin sequence, and every class beyond Euler (Stiefel–Whitney, Chern, Pontryagin, Chern–Weil, the Chern character) have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Thom class of an oriented bundle** — over an arbitrary base, an oriented real vector bundle has an integral [Thom class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/ThomClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.thomClass). It is [characterized uniquely](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/ThomClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eq_thomClass_iff) by restricting on every fibre to the generator chosen by the orientation.
- **The mod 2 Thom class** — every real vector bundle, oriented or not, has [exactly one Thom class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/MayerVietoris/DirectedUnion.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isThomClass_of_subsingleton_units) over any coefficient ring whose only unit is 1. The proof glues compatible local classes over a trivializing cover ([gluing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/MayerVietoris/DirectedUnion.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_singularCohomology_thomPair_of_iUnion_eq_univ)).
- **The Euler class and its first properties** — the [Euler class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass) is the zero-section pullback of the Thom class. It is [natural](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass_pullback), [changes sign when the orientation is reversed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass_neg), and is [2-torsion in odd rank](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.two_smul_eulerClass). It [vanishes when there is a nowhere-zero section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass_eq_zero_of_forall_ne_zero), and therefore for [oriented line bundles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/EulerClass.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientation.eulerClass_eq_zero_of_finrank_eq_one) over normal paracompact bases.
- **The orientation cover as a frame quotient** — the orientation double cover is the [quotient of the orthonormal frame bundle by SO(n)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/OrthonormalFrame.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.orientationCoverHomeomorph), and the frame bundle [recovers the vector bundle as an associated bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/OrthonormalFrame/Basic.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.associatedHomeomorph).
- **The Whitney formula for multiplicative sequences** — [Hirzebruch's polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MultiplicativeSequence.html#PowerSeries.multiplicativeSequence) evaluated on a union of two root families are [the convolution of their values on each family](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MultiplicativeSequence.html#PowerSeries.aeval_multiset_esymm_add_multiplicativeSequence). A [squared-root version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MultiplicativeSequence.html#PowerSeries.expand_mk_aeval_multiplicativeSequence) handles the even L and Â series.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [metric change transport](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Positive/MetricChange/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.metricChangeTransport) is the inverse square root of the Riesz operator of a second inner product. It carries orthonormal frames for one metric to orthonormal frames for another, and an explicit [composition correction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Positive/MetricChange/Composition.html#ContinuousLinearMap.metricChangeCompositionCorrection) is supplied. Together these make frame-bundle constructions independent of the chosen metric.
- The [orientation local system](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/LocalSystem.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientationCover.signLocalSystem) is the sign system of the orientation double cover. It is trivialized by an orientation and has a canonically constant tensor square, which makes it the twisting coefficient for the unoriented Thom theory.
- The [Thom map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Thom/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.thomMap) a ↦ π\*a ⌣ u is natural and [linear over the cohomology of the base](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Thom/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.thomMap_singularCup). It is the map whose bijectivity the Thom isomorphism will assert. A supporting result shows that the [Mayer–Vietoris connecting map commutes with cup products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/MayerVietoris/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCohomologyPairMayerVietorisδ_singularCup).

### Roadmap coverage

**C1** is partial. Most of C1.0 is in place: the Gram–Schmidt orthonormal frame bundle and its trivializations, pullbacks, associated-bundle reconstruction, the metric change transport, the SO(n) quotient description of the orientation cover, and a continuous-fibre-norm setting for Hermitian frames. From C1.1a, direct-sum orientation covers, the sign local system and the tautological orientation over the cover have landed. The universal classifiers, the Lie-group inputs and the classification of line bundles have not. **C2** is partial. Milestone C2.1 has its oriented integral and mod 2 Thom classes, but not the Thom module isomorphism or the local-coefficient form. Milestone C2.2 has Thom and Euler naturality, orientation reversal, odd-rank 2-torsion and section vanishing, but not Whitney products, the Gysin sequence or the obstruction-theoretic identification. The disk/sphere cell model, transverse zero loci, embedding Gysin maps and fibre integration are not established here. **C6** is partial, and on the formal side only: C6.1's series and multiplicative sequences exist, but nothing is evaluated on bundles. **C3, C4, C5, C5a and C7** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **The Thom isomorphism** — prove that the [Thom map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Thom/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.thomMap) is bijective, first over trivializing opens and then by the same Mayer–Vietoris and directed-union gluing that produced the Thom class.
- **The sphere-bundle Gysin sequence** — derive it from the Thom isomorphism together with the long exact sequence of the Thom pair. It depends on the Thom isomorphism.
- **Euler class of a Whitney sum** — prove that the Euler class is multiplicative under direct sums with ordered orientations. The direct-sum orientation covers already exist, so what is missing is the Thom-class product formula.
- **Leray–Hirsch and projective splitting (C3)** — nothing has landed yet. This is the gate for Stiefel–Whitney, Chern and Pontryagin classes, and through them for evaluating the existing L, Â and Todd sequences on bundles.
- **Euler class as primary obstruction** — compare the Euler class with the obstruction cocycle on a CW base. This needs the C2.0 disk/sphere cell model, which is not established here.
