### Named results

- **Unramified means étale normalization** — a finite separable extension of the fraction field of a discrete valuation ring is unramified with respect to it exactly when the integral closure is étale over it ([`isUnramifiedWRT_iff_etale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/TamelyRamified.html#TauCeti.Algebra.isUnramifiedWRT_iff_etale)).
- **Kummer extensions of a discrete valuation ring** — adjoining an `n`-th root of a uniformizer gives integral closure `A[π^{1/n}]`, which is a discrete valuation ring, totally ramified with index `n` ([`integralClosure_eq_adjoin_of_pow_eq_of_irreducible`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Kummer.html#TauCeti.IsDiscreteValuationRing.integralClosure_eq_adjoin_of_pow_eq_of_irreducible)). In the tame case every intermediate field is `K(π^{1/d})` ([`eq_adjoin_pow_finrank_of_pow_eq_of_irreducible`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Kummer.html#TauCeti.IsDiscreteValuationRing.eq_adjoin_pow_finrank_of_pow_eq_of_irreducible)).
- **Local rings of the Abhyankar model** — an integrally closed local domain over a discrete valuation ring that contains an `ed`-th root of `uϖ^e` is a localization of the Abhyankar model. It is therefore a discrete valuation ring with that root as uniformizer and a separable residue extension ([`exists_algEquiv_localization_abhyankarModel`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/AbhyankarModel/Localization.html#TauCeti.exists_algEquiv_localization_abhyankarModel)).
- **Regularity lifts along a nonzerodivisor** — a Noetherian local ring whose quotient by a nonzerodivisor in the maximal ideal is regular is itself regular, and the same holds for regular sequences. This is Stacks 00NU ([`of_quotient_nonZeroDivisor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/OfQuotient.html#TauCeti.IsRegularLocalRing.of_quotient_nonZeroDivisor)).
- **Finite étale covers split over strictly henselian rings** — over a henselian local ring with separably closed residue field, every finite étale algebra is a finite product of copies of the base ([`IsFiniteSplit.of_henselianLocalRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Etale.html#Algebra.IsFiniteSplit.of_henselianLocalRing)). Its global form says a finite étale morphism [splits étale locally](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/EtaleLocallySplit.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.exists_split_etaleLocally).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Tame ramification with respect to a discrete valuation ring** ([`IsTamelyRamifiedWRT`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/TamelyRamified.html#TauCeti.Algebra.IsTamelyRamifiedWRT)). With its unramified and totally ramified siblings, it is the language of Layers 0, 1 and 3. Its codimension-one version, [`IsTamelyRamifiedInCodimOne`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/Ramification.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.IsTamelyRamifiedInCodimOne), is the hypothesis of the end theorems.
- **The Abhyankar model** ([`abhyankarModel`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/AbhyankarModel/Basic.html#TauCeti.abhyankarModel)), the ring `(B[y]/(y^e − u))[x]/(x^d − ϖy)`. It is regular and is the integral closure of the root extraction. Its maximal localizations are [formally unramified over the Kummer root ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/AbhyankarModel/Unramified.html#TauCeti.formallyUnramified_abhyankarModelKummerHom), which is the local content of Abhyankar's lemma for discrete valuation rings.
- **Finite products of standard tame covers**. They are free and finite, and they are [the integral closure of the base in their localization at `f`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdjoinRoot/StandardTameCover/Normalization.html#TauCeti.IsFiniteProductOfStandardTameCovers.isIntegralClosure). The reduced preimage of a regular divisor in them is regular. This is the local model that the normalization theorem will be compared against.
