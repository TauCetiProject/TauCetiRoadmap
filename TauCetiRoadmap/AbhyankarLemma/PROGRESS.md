## AbhyankarLemma: 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-08 (`3f2c0ab` to `d2083f0`)

The first window of this roadmap built most of the local theory over a discrete valuation ring. The Stacks notions of an extension being unramified, [tamely ramified](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/TamelyRamified.html#TauCeti.Algebra.IsTamelyRamifiedWRT) or totally ramified with respect to a discrete valuation ring are now defined, with the criterion that [an extension is unramified exactly when the integral closure is étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/TamelyRamified.html#TauCeti.Algebra.isUnramifiedWRT_iff_etale), and a [comparison with tameness for local fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/TamelyRamified/Comparison.html#TauCeti.isTamelyRamifiedWRT_iff_isTamelyRamified). For Kummer extensions `K(π^{1/n})`, all six clauses of Stacks 09EV are proved as separate lemmas: the [integral closure is `A[π^{1/n}]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Kummer.html#TauCeti.IsDiscreteValuationRing.integralClosure_eq_adjoin_of_pow_eq_of_irreducible), it is a discrete valuation ring with ramification index `n`, and in the tame case [every intermediate field is `K(π^{1/d})`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Kummer.html#TauCeti.IsDiscreteValuationRing.eq_adjoin_pow_finrank_of_pow_eq_of_irreducible). They have not been bundled into a single `kummer_dvr` statement. Unramified extensions are [stable under base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/BaseChange/Unramified.html#TauCeti.Algebra.IsUnramifiedWRT.baseChange). The tame case is not proved yet.

Toward the scheme-level statements, covers [tamely ramified in codimension one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/Ramification.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.IsTamelyRamifiedInCodimOne) are defined, and the unramified version is shown to be [stable under base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/Ramification.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.IsUnramifiedInCodimOne.baseChange). A local ring is [regular when its quotient by a nonzerodivisor is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/OfQuotient.html#TauCeti.IsRegularLocalRing.of_quotient_nonZeroDivisor) (Stacks 00NU). Several splitting results also landed: [finite étale algebras over a strictly henselian local ring split](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Etale.html#Algebra.IsFiniteSplit.of_henselianLocalRing), finite étale morphisms [split étale locally](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/EtaleLocallySplit.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.exists_split_etaleLocally), and the [strict henselisation of a quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StrictHenselization/Quotient.html#TauCeti.StrictHenselization.quotientAlgEquiv) is identified.

The local model of Abhyankar's lemma, the [Abhyankar model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/AbhyankarModel/Basic.html#TauCeti.abhyankarModel), is shown to be regular, the integral closure of the root extraction, and [unramified over the Kummer base](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/AbhyankarModel/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ramificationIdx_abhyankarModelKummerHom). These are the ingredients of Abhyankar's lemma for discrete valuation rings, but that lemma itself is not stated yet. The extension theorems of Layer 2 and the main theorems are still to come.
