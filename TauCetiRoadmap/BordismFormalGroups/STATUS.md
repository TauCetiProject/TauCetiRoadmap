### Named results

- **Lazard's theorem** — the Lazard ring is the polynomial ring ℤ[x₁, x₂, …] with xₙ in degree 2n, as a graded isomorphism ([`LazardRing.mvPolynomialEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/LazardRing/Polynomial.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.mvPolynomialEquiv)). - **Lazard's classification by height** — over a separably closed field of characteristic p, two commutative formal group laws are isomorphic exactly when their heights agree ([`height_eq_height_iff_nonempty_formalGroupIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Height/Classification.html#FormalGroup.height_eq_height_iff_nonempty_formalGroupIso)). - **The symmetric 2-cocycle lemma** — over every commutative ring, torsion included, a homogeneous symmetric additive 2-cocycle of degree n ≥ 2 is r · Cₙ for a unique r ([`existsUnique_eq_smul_lazardCocycle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/SymmetricCocycle/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_eq_smul_lazardCocycle)). It drives both Lazard's theorem and the exponent-two logarithm.
- **The universal exponent-two formal group law** — laws with [2]_F = 0 are classified by the polynomial ring 𝔽₂[aᵢ : i ≠ 2ᵏ − 1], gradedly and as [the quotient of L by the 2-series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/ExponentTwo/Lazard.html#TauCeti.ExponentTwoLazardRing.ker_ofLazardRing) ([`ExponentTwoLazardRing.gradedLift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/ExponentTwo/Universal.html#TauCeti.ExponentTwoLazardRing.gradedLift)). The aᵢ are the coefficients of Quillen's normalised logarithm, now [a strict isomorphism to 𝔾ₐ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/ExponentTwo/Iso.html#FormalGroup.exponentTwoLogIso).
- **Integrality of Hazewinkel's formal group law** — if σ lifts Frobenius and sends each vᵢ to vᵢᵖ, the law with Hazewinkel's logarithm Σ mₙ(v) X^(pⁿ) has integral coefficients ([`coeff_hazewinkelFormalGroup_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Hazewinkel/FunctionalEquation.html#TauCeti.coeff_hazewinkelFormalGroup_mem)), by [Hazewinkel's functional equation lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/FunctionalEquation.html#TauCeti.coeff_inv_smul_𝔾ₐ_toPowerSeries_mem).
