### Named results

- **Lazard's theorem** — the Lazard ring is the polynomial ring ℤ[x₁, x₂, …] with xₙ in degree 2n, as a graded isomorphism ([`LazardRing.mvPolynomialEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/LazardRing/Polynomial.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.mvPolynomialEquiv)). This is the algebraic half of Quillen's theorem.
- **Lazard's classification by height** — over a separably closed field of characteristic p, two commutative formal group laws are isomorphic exactly when their heights agree ([`height_eq_height_iff_nonempty_formalGroupIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Height/Classification.html#FormalGroup.height_eq_height_iff_nonempty_formalGroupIso)); infinite height is [exactly the additive class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Logarithm/InfiniteHeight.html#FormalGroup.height_eq_top_iff_nonempty_formalGroupIso_𝔾ₐ).
- **The symmetric 2-cocycle lemma** — over every commutative ring, torsion included, a homogeneous symmetric additive 2-cocycle of degree n ≥ 2 is r · Cₙ for a unique r ([`existsUnique_eq_smul_lazardCocycle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/SymmetricCocycle/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_eq_smul_lazardCocycle)). It drives Lazard's theorem, the exponent-two logarithm and the deformation theory below.
- **The universal exponent-two formal group law** — laws with [2]_F = 0 are classified by the polynomial ring 𝔽₂[aᵢ : i ≠ 2ᵏ − 1], gradedly and as [the quotient of L by the 2-series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/ExponentTwo/Lazard.html#TauCeti.ExponentTwoLazardRing.ker_ofLazardRing) ([`ExponentTwoLazardRing.gradedLift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/ExponentTwo/Universal.html#TauCeti.ExponentTwoLazardRing.gradedLift)).
- **Lubin–Tate's coboundary lemma** — when p = 0 and the p-series of F starts in degree pʰ with a unit coefficient, a symmetric 2-cocycle vanishing through the degrees pⁱ, 0 < i < h, is a coboundary ([`IsSymmetricCocycle.exists_eq_homDefect_𝔾ₐ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/SymmetricCocycle/Height.html#FormalGroup.IsSymmetricCocycle.exists_eq_homDefect_𝔾ₐ)). This is the computation behind the h − 1 deformation parameters.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Hopf algebroid (L, L[b₁, b₂, …]) and its height ideals.** Strict coordinate changes are [represented and graded](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/CoordinateChange/Grading.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.gradedCoordinateChangeLift). The height ideals Iₙ = (p, v₁, …, vₙ₋₁) are [invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/CoordinateChange/HopfAlgebroid.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.map_rightUnit_heightIdeal), and [the right unit fixes vₙ modulo Iₙ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/CoordinateChange/HopfAlgebroid.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.rightUnit_sub_C_mem_map_heightIdeal). These are the inputs for classifying invariant primes, stated over L rather than BP_*.
- **Deformation inputs.** [Lubin–Tate rigidity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Rigidity.html#TauCeti.FormalGroupHom.eq_of_map_residue_eq), laws over adically complete rings, and [the universal property of Witt vectors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/WittVector/AdicLift.html#TauCeti.WittVector.adicLiftEquiv) are in place. The last makes every complete local ring with residue field k a W(k)-algebra, which supplies the base ring of the Lubin–Tate ring.
- **Cartier projectors on curves.** The [elementary projector](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Curve/Projector.html#FormalGroup.cartierProjectorCurve) 1 − Vₙ[n]⁻¹fₙ is an idempotent splitting curves into ker fₙ and the image of Vₙ. Its infinite product over primes q ≠ p is the route to p-typicalization.
