## BordismFormalGroups: 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-21 (`d0aec02` to `273ea28`)

The Lazard ring arrived: [`LazardRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/LazardRing.html#TauCeti.LazardRing) is built as ℤ[aᵢⱼ] modulo the associativity and commutativity relations of X + Y + Σ aᵢⱼ XⁱYʲ, carries a [universal formal group law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/LazardRing.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.formalGroup), and its [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/LazardRing.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.lift) identifies ring maps L → R with commutative formal group laws over R, naturally in R. This is the ungraded bijection only; the graded version and Lazard's theorem that L is polynomial are still ahead.

Toward that theorem, the [Lazard 2-cocycle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/SymmetricCocycle.html#TauCeti.lazardCocycle) Cₙ = ((X+Y)ⁿ − Xⁿ − Yⁿ)/dₙ is now defined integrally, with dₙ the gcd of the inner binomial coefficients, shown to be [p for prime powers pᵏ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/SymmetricCocycle.html#TauCeti.lazardGcd_eq_minFac_of_isPrimePow) and 1 otherwise. Cₙ is a symmetric homogeneous cocycle, and [r • Cₙ = 0 forces r = 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/SymmetricCocycle.html#TauCeti.smul_lazardCocycle_eq_zero_iff) over every commutative ring, torsion included. The converse half of the cocycle lemma, that every normalized symmetric cocycle of degree n is a multiple of Cₙ, has not landed.

The general formal-group API also grew. The [formal inverse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/AddInv.html#FormalGroup.addInvX) i(X), with F(i(X), X) = 0 and uniqueness, makes the power-series points of a formal group law into a group. Base change now covers formal group laws and their n-series, [multiplication by n scales the invariant differential by n](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/NSMul/Basic.html#FormalGroup.subst_nsmulSeries_invariantDifferential_mul_derivative) (Silverman IV.4.3), and the additive and multiplicative groups have their logarithms computed, including [log(1 + T)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Logarithm.html#FormalGroup.log_𝔾ₘ) for 𝔾ₘ.

## BordismFormalGroups: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

The symmetric 2-cocycle lemma is now complete: over every commutative ring, torsion included, a homogeneous symmetric additive 2-cocycle of degree n ≥ 2 is [a unique multiple of the Lazard cocycle Cₙ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/SymmetricCocycle/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_eq_smul_lazardCocycle). It went straight to work twice. With the Lazard ring now graded and given its [graded universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/LazardRing/Basic.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.gradedLift), the lemma yields [Lazard's computation of the indecomposables](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/LazardRing/Indecomposable.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.cotangentBasis): I/I² is free on explicit generators xₙ in degree 2n. That is the key step toward L ≅ ℤ[x₁, x₂, …], but the polynomial theorem itself has not landed. In characteristic two, the lemma's mod-2 form feeds Quillen's [normalized logarithm of an exponent-two formal group law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/ExponentTwo.html#FormalGroup.existsUnique_logarithm_of_nsmulSeries_two_eq_zero), which exists and is unique when 2 = 0 and [2]_F = 0. It is not yet bundled as a strict isomorphism, and its base-change naturality is still open.

Morphisms arrived as well. [Formal group homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Hom.html#TauCeti.FormalGroupHom) and [isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Hom.html#TauCeti.FormalGroupIso) now exist, and over a ℚ-algebra the logarithm is [the unique strict isomorphism to 𝔾ₐ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Logarithm.html#FormalGroup.logIso). Strict coordinate changes are [represented by L[b₁, b₂, …]](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/CoordinateChange/Lazard.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.coordinateChangeLift), which now carries the full Hopf-algebroid structure: counit, [conjugation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/CoordinateChange/HopfAlgebroid.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.conjugation) and a [coassociative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/CoordinateChange/HopfAlgebroid.html#TauCeti.LazardRing.coproduct_coassoc) coproduct.

[Height](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Height.html#FormalGroup.height) is defined over fields of characteristic p, using the fact that the [p-series begins in degree a power of p](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/Height.html#FormalGroup.exists_order_nsmulSeries_eq_pow). It is computed for 𝔾ₐ and 𝔾ₘ and shown to be invariant under field extension. Coordinate independence and the classification by height have not landed. Points of a formal group law in [topologically nilpotent elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGroup/TopNilpotentPoint.html#FormalGroup.TopNilpotentPoint) of a topological ring now form a group. Smaller additions describe additive power series in characteristic p and add rescaling of formal group laws.
