## PositiveScalarCurvature: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`8ec63e2` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's opening layer took shape in this window. Positive scalar curvature now exists as a property of a smooth metric, [`HasPositiveScalarCurvature`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Basic.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.HasPositiveScalarCurvature), with scalar curvature proved smooth and, on a compact manifold, bounded below by a [positive constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Basic.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.HasPositiveScalarCurvature.exists_pos_le). The headline is that [concordance is an equivalence relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Concordance.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.equivalence_concordant) on such metrics. Here a [concordance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Concordance.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.IsConcordance) is a PSC metric on M×ℝ that is the cylinder g₀+dt² for t≤0 and g₁+dt² for t≥1. Concatenation glues two concordances and compresses the time window with a reparametrisation that is a translation near both ends, so the product ends survive (TauCeti#19138).

Around this sit the basic laws of scalar curvature. It is [invariant under diffeomorphism pullback](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Pullback.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.scalarCurvature_pullback) and [local](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Restriction.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.scalarCurvature_restrictOpenTangentSpace), with both facts routed through a new notion of [local Riemannian isometry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Local.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRiemannianIsometry). It [scales by c⁻¹](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Scaling.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.scalarCurvature_rescale_const) when the metric is multiplied by c. It is [additive on products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Prod.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.scalarCurvature_prodTangentSpace), which yields the trick of [shrinking a compact PSC factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Basic.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.exists_pos_hasPositiveScalarCurvature_prodTangentSpace_rescale) until it dominates a factor whose scalar curvature is bounded below. PSC also behaves correctly on [disjoint unions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Basic.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.hasPositiveScalarCurvature_sumTangentSpace_iff). The round sphere of radius r has [scalar curvature n(n−1)/r²](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Sphere/Radius.html#TauCeti.scalarCurvature_sphereRoundMetricOfRadius). A symmetrisation projection on smooth bilinear fields is the first step towards Sym²(T*M).

P1 is still missing several pieces: the metric bundle as an actual subbundle, the C² and C∞ topologies on metrics with openness of positivity, uniform bounds for compact families, the CP and HP examples, collared bordisms, and isotopy implies concordance. Nothing from P2 to P6 has landed.

## PositiveScalarCurvature: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`c1c6773` to `93d1fe5`)

This window filled in three of the gaps left in P1. The first is a uniform scalar gap for families: on a compact manifold, a compact family of PSC metrics has [scalar curvature bounded below by one positive constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Family.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_pos_forall_le_scalarCurvature). Behind it, scalar curvature is written in a chart as an [explicit function of the metric's 2-jet](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Jet.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.scalarCurvature_eq_scalarCurvatureOfJet), built from the index formulas for Christoffel symbols and curvature, and that function is [continuous wherever the Gram matrix is invertible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Jet.html#ContinuousAt.scalarCurvatureOfJet) (TauCeti#20789). The family hypothesis is that the chart Gram matrices and their first and second derivatives vary continuously. There is still no C² topology on the space of metrics, so the theorem is not yet stated for a continuous path in that space, and the version relative to a closed subset is still missing.

The second is the bundle Sym²(T*M). The symmetric bilinear forms on the fibres of a smooth vector bundle now [form a smooth vector bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Bilinear/Symmetric.html#ContMDiffVectorBundle.symmetricBilinearMaps), and native smooth metrics are [exactly its positive-definite sections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Section.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.positiveDefiniteSymmetricSectionEquiv). That cone is convex.

The third concerns collars. A [collared concordance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Concordance.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.IsCollaredConcordance) is a PSC metric on the manifold with boundary M×[0,1] that is a product near both ends, and two metrics are [concordant exactly when one exists](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Concordance.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.concordant_iff_exists_isCollaredConcordance). This is the comparison between M×ℝ and M×[0,1] that the roadmap asks for. Nothing from P2 to P6 has landed.

## PositiveScalarCurvature: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-08 (`93d1fe5` to `d2083f0`)

The headline result is the Layer 1 milestone that [isotopic metrics are concordant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Isotopy.html#JoinedIn.concordant): on a closed manifold, two metrics joined by a path of PSC metrics are concordant. The path only has to be continuous in a new [chart Cᵏ topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Riemannian/Topology.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.chartCkTopology) on metrics, which is [atlas-independent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Pullback/Topology.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.chartCkTopology_eq_induced). The proof follows the roadmap's route. The path is replaced by smooth finite convex combinations with exact endpoint tails, and the time parameter is then [slowed down](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/SmoothPath.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.eventually_hasPositiveScalarCurvature_compTime_mul) until the cylinder keeps positive scalar curvature (TauCeti#23599). Along the way, PSC became [open in the C² topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Topology.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.isOpen_setOf_hasPositiveScalarCurvature_chartC2) and in the C∞ topology on closed manifolds, and the uniform scalar gap now holds for [C²-compact sets of metrics](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Topology.html#IsCompact.exists_pos_forall_le_scalarCurvature_chartC2) and for [families fixed near a closed set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Family.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_pos_forall_le_scalarCurvature_of_eventually_inner_eq). Concordance also became natural under pullback, scaling and products with a nonnegatively curved factor, and PSC descends to quotients by free, properly discontinuous isometric actions.

Layer 3 has started. The [torpedo metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Torpedo.html#TauCeti.torpedoMetric) is a round cap joined to a cylinder, and it [has PSC](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Torpedo.html#TauCeti.hasPositiveScalarCurvature_torpedoMetric) in dimension at least three. Its product with a small round sphere gives [the Gromov–Lawson handle metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Handle.html#TauCeti.hasPositiveScalarCurvature_torpedoMetric_prodTangentSpace_sphereRoundMetricOfRadius). The [bending term](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Rotational.html#TauCeti.scalarCurvature_rotationalMetric_log) of rotationally symmetric metrics is also computed. A [connected sum has PSC](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/ConnectedSum.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.HasPositiveScalarCurvature.connectedSum), but only when the two metrics already agree on a cylindrical neck. Deforming a metric into that form, the tube estimates and the surgery theorem itself are still missing.

Layer 2 also has a first step. By [van Dantzig–van der Waerden](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MetricSpace/Isometry/Group.html#IsometryEquiv.instCompactSpace), the isometry group of a compact metric space is a compact topological group, and a [Riemannian isometry is determined by its 1-jet](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Ext.html#TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.ext_of_mfderiv_eq) at a point. Myers–Steenrod is not yet proved.
