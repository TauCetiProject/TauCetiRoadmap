## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 1 (metrics, positivity and families) is nearly done. It has topologies on metrics, openness of PSC, uniform gaps for families, and isotopy implies concordance. What is missing there is the projective examples and the boundary and section-space versions of the topology. Layers 2 and 3 have their first ingredients: compactness of isometry groups, and the torpedo and handle metrics. Layers 4, 5 and 6, which cover bordism, the α-obstruction and exotic spheres, are untouched.

### Named results

- **Isotopic metrics are concordant** — On a closed manifold, two metrics joined by a path of PSC metrics that is continuous in the C² topology are concordant: [`JoinedIn.concordant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Isotopy.html#JoinedIn.concordant). The proof smooths the path by finite convex combinations and then slows its parameter (TauCeti#23599).
- **Positive scalar curvature is an open condition** — On a closed manifold, PSC metrics form an open set in the C² topology, and hence also in the C∞ topology: [`isOpen_setOf_hasPositiveScalarCurvature_chartC2`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Topology.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.isOpen_setOf_hasPositiveScalarCurvature_chartC2).
- **Uniform scalar gap for compact families** — A C²-compact set of PSC metrics on a closed manifold, such as the image of a path, has scalar curvature bounded below by one positive constant: [`exists_pos_forall_le_scalarCurvature_chartC2`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Topology.html#IsCompact.exists_pos_forall_le_scalarCurvature_chartC2). A relative version covers families that are [fixed near a closed set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Family.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_pos_forall_le_scalarCurvature_of_eventually_inner_eq).
- **The Gromov–Lawson handle metric** — The torpedo metric, a round cap joined to a cylinder, has PSC in dimension at least three. Its product with a round sphere Sᵐ, m≥2, of any radius has PSC: [`hasPositiveScalarCurvature_torpedoMetric_prodTangentSpace_sphereRoundMetricOfRadius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Handle.html#TauCeti.hasPositiveScalarCurvature_torpedoMetric_prodTangentSpace_sphereRoundMetricOfRadius).
- **Concordance is an equivalence relation** — Two PSC metrics are concordant when a PSC metric on M×ℝ ends in the cylinders g₀+dt² and g₁+dt². This relation is an equivalence: [`equivalence_concordant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Concordance.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.equivalence_concordant). It is equivalent to [collared concordance on M×[0,1]](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Concordance.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.concordant_iff_exists_isCollaredConcordance), and it is natural under pullback, scaling and products.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [chart Cᵏ topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Riemannian/Topology.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.chartCkTopology) on smooth metrics is uniform convergence of coordinate derivatives up to order k on compact chart sets. It is [atlas-independent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Pullback/Topology.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.chartCkTopology_eq_induced), and on compact M it is given by finitely many charts. Every continuity statement about families of metrics is stated in this topology.
- [`scalarCurvatureOfJet`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Jet.html#TauCeti.Manifold.scalarCurvatureOfJet) expresses scalar curvature in a chart through the metric's 2-jet. This is what makes scalar curvature continuous in the C² topology.
- The [torpedo metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/PositiveScalarCurvature/Torpedo.html#TauCeti.torpedoMetric) is a conformally flat metric that is round near the centre and cylindrical outside a ball. With the [connected-sum gluing along a cylindrical neck](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/ConnectedSum.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.connectedSum), it is the model piece for surgery.

### Roadmap coverage

Layer 1 is partial. The following are done: Sym²(T*M) with its rank n(n+1)/2 and its identification with metrics, chart C² and C∞ topologies with atlas independence, and openness of PSC on closed M. Also done are uniform gaps for a single metric, for compact families and for families relative to a closed set, the pullback, scaling, product, union and quotient laws, the round sphere, and concordance with collars and isotopy implies concordance. Still missing are Scal(CP^d) and Scal(HP^d), the C^k topology on compact manifolds with boundary, and the identification of the chart topologies with K5a's Fréchet section topology. Layer 2 is partial: the isometry group of a compact metric space is a compact topological group, and Riemannian isometries are determined by their 1-jet, but Myers–Steenrod, associated bundles and the O'Neill formula are absent. Layer 3 is partial: the torpedo and handle metrics, the bending-term formula, and PSC connected sums of metrics that already match on a cylindrical neck are in place, but the tube estimates, the surgery theorem and its relative versions are not. Layers 4, 5 and 6 are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Projective examples** — Scal(CP^d)=4d(d+1) and Scal(HP^d)=16d(d+2) on RiemannianGeometry R1's projective carriers. This is the last model computation in Layer 1.
- **Topologies on boundaries and section spaces** — The C^k topology on compact manifolds with boundary, and the comparison of the chart topologies with K5a's Fréchet section topology. The relative surgery concordances of 3.3 measure their margins in the C^k topology.
- **Gromov–Lawson surgery** — The tube principal-curvature estimates, and PSC through the bending region in codimension q≥3. The handle and torpedo pieces are ready. Deforming a PSC metric to a torpedo near a point would also make the connected-sum theorem unconditional.
- **Myers–Steenrod** — Smoothness of metric isometries, no small subgroups and the Lie structure of the isometry group. This needs HopfRinow Layer 4, CompactGroups and LieGroups.
- **Connection metrics on bundles (2.2)** — The O'Neill formula Scal(g_ε)=Scal(g_B)+ε⁻²Scal(g_F)−ε²|A|², and shrinking a PSC fibre. This waits on associated bundles and on Myers–Steenrod for the structure group.
