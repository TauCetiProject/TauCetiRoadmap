## LocalGaloisGroups: 2026-09-27 to 2026-09-30 (`9146745` to `e440f4e`)

This was the roadmap's first window, and it opened on two fronts. On the arithmetic side, the maximal pro-`p` Galois group [`absoluteGaloisGroupProP`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/ProP.html#TauCeti.absoluteGaloisGroupProP) now exists. So does the finite cyclic group of `p`-power roots of unity, with its order [`q(K)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/RootsOfUnity.html#TauCeti.localRootOfUnityOrder) and the dyadic criterion that [`q(K) ≠ 2` exactly when `K` contains a fourth root of unity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/RootsOfUnity.html#TauCeti.localRootOfUnityOrder_ne_two_iff). The cyclotomic character, pinned to Mathlib's, descends to the [cyclotomic orientation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/Orientation.html#TauCeti.cyclotomicOrientation) of `G_K(p)` when `μ_p ⊆ K`. Its image is [all of `ℤ₂ˣ` in odd degree over `ℚ₂`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/OddDegree.html#TauCeti.range_localCyclotomicCharacter_of_degree_odd), and every extension of `ℚ₂` falls into [exactly one dyadic branch](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/MarkedCase.html#TauCeti.dyadic_markedCase_exists_unique) of the marked classification. Inflation from `G_K(p)` is [an isomorphism on `H¹`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Inflation.html#TauCeti.inflH1AbsoluteGaloisProP) and [injective on `H²`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Inflation.html#TauCeti.inflH2AbsoluteGaloisProP_injective) (TauCeti#9822). Surjectivity has to wait for the local cohomology counts, which have not started.

The other front is the integral machinery behind the rank of the full `G_K`. The completed unit group `A(L)` is now a [finitely generated `ℤ_p[Gal(L/K)]`-module](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/Finite.html#TauCeti.padicCompletionUnits_module_finite) whose torsion [has order `q(L)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/Torsion.html#TauCeti.natCard_torsion_padicCompletionUnits). The [sharp tame-frame exponents](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/TameFrameExponents.html#TauCeti.exists_tameFrame_exponents) realise that torsion as `ℤ_p[G]/(σ - a, τ - b)`. The cancellation library has also begun, with [Krull–Schmidt cancellation over `ℤ_p[G]`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/GroupAlgebra/Cancellation.html#TauCeti.nonempty_linearEquiv_of_prod_linearEquiv) and [detection of projectives modulo `p`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/GroupAlgebra/Projective.html#TauCeti.nonempty_linearEquiv_of_projective_of_reduction). Around these sit relation modules, Schanuel's lemma and first steps toward the Auslander–Reiten transpose. So far the tame-frame module is identified with the regular representation only over a field in which `#G` is invertible, not yet as `M₀ ⊗ ℚ_p ≃ ℚ_p[G]`.

## LocalGaloisGroups: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-04 (`e440f4e` to `41e5e49`)

The integral cancellation library over `ℤ_p[G]` gained its two hardest pieces. [Swan's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/GroupAlgebra/Swan.html#TauCeti.nonempty_linearEquiv_of_projective_of_tensorRat) says that finitely generated projective `ℤ_p[G]`-modules with isomorphic rationalizations are isomorphic. It is proved by counting maps into simple modules and by lifting projectives from `𝔽_p[G]`. The [torsion criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/GroupAlgebra/Transpose.html#TauCeti.exists_projective_prod_linearEquiv_of_torsion) says that modules of projective dimension one with isomorphic `p`-power torsion are stably isomorphic, by way of the Auslander–Reiten transpose. On the arithmetic side, `A(L)` now has its [rational decomposition](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/Rational.html#TauCeti.nonempty_padicCompletionUnits_tensorRat_linearEquiv) `A(L) ⊗ ℚ_p ≃ ℚ_p[G]^N ⊕ ℚ_p` over a finite Galois layer, and its [free quotient](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/FreeQuotient.html#TauCeti.padicCompletionUnits_quotient_torsion_linearEquiv) is `ℤ_p`-free of rank `[L:ℚ_p]+1`. The tame-frame module's rationalization and torsion also landed, with the non-Galois cubic and `ℚ₂(ζ₃)` as rejection tests. What is still missing before the integral decomposition is the step from a stable isomorphism to an isomorphism, the Tate module, and the reciprocity identification of `A(L)`.

The maximal pro-`p` quotient is now [topologically finitely generated](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/Rank.html#TauCeti.isTopologicallyFinitelyGenerated_absoluteGaloisGroupProP), and it has [exactly `N+2` generators when `μ_p ⊆ K`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/Rank.html#TauCeti.topologicalGeneratorRankNat_absoluteGaloisGroupProP_of_mu). This rests on the Kummer count [`dim H¹(G_K, 𝔽_p) = N+2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.finrank_cohomFp_one_absoluteGaloisGroup_of_exists_isPrimitiveRoot). The `N+1` count of the free case still waits for the vanishing of `H²`. [`Φ_{p^n}` is irreducible over `ℚ_p`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/Irreducible.html#TauCeti.irreducible_cyclotomic_prime_pow_ratPadic), which gives `ℚ_p(μ_p)` [rank `p+1`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/Rank.html#TauCeti.topologicalGeneratorRankNat_cyclotomic_prime_ratPadic). The two even-degree dyadic images are [`{±1} × U^(f)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/EvenDegree.html#TauCeti.range_localCyclotomicCharacter_of_degree_even_plusMinus) and [`U^[f]`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/EvenDegree.html#TauCeti.range_localCyclotomicCharacter_of_degree_even_principal) for some `f ≥ 2`, with `f` not yet computed from `K`. For the full `G_K`, the tame frame is in place: the [tame quotient needs two generators](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Tame/Quotient.html#TauCeti.topologicalGeneratorRankNat_tameQuotient_le_two), and a [Frattini reduction along wild inertia](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/WildInertia.html#TauCeti.topologicalClosure_eq_top_of_sup_wildInertiaCommutator) has landed.

## LocalGaloisGroups: 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-06 (`41e5e49` to `9048b60`)

The headline is [Demushkin's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/Demushkin.html#TauCeti.isDemushkin_absoluteGaloisGroupProP_of_mu): when a nonarchimedean local field `K` contains a primitive `p`th root of unity, `G_K(p)` is a Demushkin group, and over `ℚ_p` its [Demushkin rank](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/Demushkin.html#TauCeti.demushkinRank_absoluteGaloisGroupProP) is `[K:ℚ_p] + 2` (TauCeti#12171). The proof goes through a general [criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Demushkin/CupForm.html#TauCeti.IsDemushkin.of_cupFp_injective): a pro-`p` group with one-dimensional `H²` and an injective cup form is Demushkin. The degree-two inputs came together in TauCeti#12139. `H²(G_K, ℤ/n)` is [free of rank one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Brauer/RootsOfUnity.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.finrank_cohomFp_two_absoluteGaloisGroup_of_isPrimitiveRoot) and [spanned by cup products](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/TrivialZMod.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.map₂_cupFp_absoluteGaloisGroup_eq_top_of_isPrimitiveRoot), so degree-two inflation is [surjective](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/Inflation.html#TauCeti.inflH2AbsoluteGaloisProP_surjective_of_mu). Inflation is therefore an isomorphism in degree two, and the [cup square on `H¹(G_K(p), 𝔽_p)` is perfect](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/Inflation.html#TauCeti.cupFp_bijective_absoluteGaloisGroupProP_of_mu).

On the cyclotomic side, the [cyclotomic orientation of `G_K(p)` has Labute's prescription property](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/Prescription.html#TauCeti.cyclotomicOrientation_hasPrescriptionProperty): every reduction `H¹(I(χ)/pⁱ) → H¹(I(χ)/p)` is onto. This is inherited from the [same property for the cyclotomic character of `G_K`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/Prescription.html#TauCeti.continuousLocalCyclotomicCharacter_hasPrescriptionProperty), which is pure Kummer theory, by way of power maps `μₙ → μₘ` on Kummer classes. The first of the Artin-map evaluations has also landed: over `ℚ_p`, the [Artin symbol of `p` has trivial cyclotomic character](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Cyclotomic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localCyclotomicCharacter_artinMap_padic_uniformizer). The unit and general-uniformizer evaluations have not.

For the full `G_K`, the cancellation library is now complete with [the step from a stable isomorphism to an isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/GroupAlgebra/StableIsomorphism.html#TauCeti.nonempty_linearEquiv_prod_of_stable) (NSW (5.6.11)). It feeds the [integral decomposition `(∗∗)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.nonempty_linearEquiv_tameFrameModule_prod) `Y ≃ M₀ ⊕ ℤ_p[G]^N`. That result is proved for any extension `Y` of `I_G` by `A(L)` of projective dimension at most one, so the existence of the Tate module itself, which needs Tate's theorem, is still a hypothesis.

The two dyadic instances, `ℚ₂` and `ℚ₂(√-2)`, have not started.

## LocalGaloisGroups: 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-19 (`b8db047` to `a5cde7b`)

Both summits of the roadmap landed in this window. [The absolute Galois group of a finite extension `K/ℚ_p` needs exactly `[K:ℚ_p] + 2` topological generators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Generation/AbsoluteGaloisGroup.html#TauCeti.topologicalGeneratorRankNat_absoluteGaloisGroup), whether or not `μ_p ⊆ K` (NSW (7.4.1), TauCeti#17388). And [`G_{ℚ₂}(2)` is isomorphic to `D₀ = ⟨A, S, Y ∣ A²S⁴(S,Y)⟩`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/RatPadic.html#TauCeti.absoluteGaloisGroupProP_two_ratPadic_marked), with the standard orientation of `D₀` pulling back to the cyclotomic one. The other dyadic instance is in too: [`G_{ℚ₂(√-2)}(2)` is `⟨x₁,…,x₄ ∣ x₁⁶(x₁,x₂)x₃⁸(x₃,x₄)⟩`, marked](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/SqrtNegTwo.html#TauCeti.absoluteGaloisGroupProP_two_ratPadicSqrtNegTwo_marked), its branch chosen because the [cyclotomic image is `closure⟨3⟩`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ProP/SqrtNegTwo.html#TauCeti.range_localCyclotomicCharacter_ratPadicSqrtNegTwo) and so misses `-1`.

Most of the window's work went into the upper bound for the rank. Local reciprocity now [identifies `A(L)` with `G_L^ab(p)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/Reciprocity.html#TauCeti.padicCompletionUnitsEquivAbelianizationProP), and [the Tate module of a finite layer exists](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/LayerTateModule/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.nonempty_layerTateModule), so the integral decomposition and the relation-module surjection no longer take it as a hypothesis. Then came a long run of class-module extensions, generation of their classes, and transport of cocycles through Lyndon's isomorphism. It ends in a passage from finite quotients to `G_K` by compactness and a [profinite Gaschütz lifting lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Gaschutz.html#TauCeti.exists_comp_eq_and_topologicalClosure_closure_range_eq_top). As the roadmap asks, the [relation-module sequence on an arbitrary layer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/RelationModule/Surjection.html#TauCeti.exists_relationModule_surjective) (NSW (7.4.2)(i)) is derived from the rank theorem, not used to prove it.

The older loose ends are also tied off. The Artin map is normalized: [`χ_cyc(Art_K(u)) = N(u)⁻¹` for a unit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ExplicitCyclotomicSymbol/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localCyclotomicCharacter_artinMap_unit), together with the [uniformizer value](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/ExplicitCyclotomicSymbol/ArtinMap.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.localCyclotomicCharacter_artinMap_uniformizer) and the [image theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Cyclotomic.html#TauCeti.ClassFieldTheory.range_localCyclotomicCharacter). The [Euler-characteristic identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassFieldTheory/Local/Duality/CohomFp.html#TauCeti.finrank_cohomFp_one_absoluteGaloisGroup) is now stated. The cyclotomic orientation is [natural along finite extensions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/Map.html#TauCeti.cyclotomicOrientation_maximalProPQuotient_map_absoluteGaloisGroupExtend), but the marked presentations themselves have not been restated functorially.
