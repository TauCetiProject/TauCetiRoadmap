### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The local root-of-unity order** [`localRootOfUnityOrder`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/RootsOfUnity.html#TauCeti.localRootOfUnityOrder) — `q(K)`, gated on the finiteness proof as the README requires. It is proved to be a positive power of `p`, it detects `μ_{p^n} ⊆ K` by divisibility, and at `p = 2` it detects the fourth roots of unity. This is the arithmetic `q` that Layer 5 must match with `demushkinQ`.
- **The cyclotomic orientation** [`cyclotomicOrientation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Cyclotomic/Orientation.html#TauCeti.cyclotomicOrientation) — the cyclotomic character descended to `G_K(p)` under `μ_p ⊆ K`, continuous and with the same image as the character. It is the object the marked theorems will compare with the Demushkin character.
- **The completed unit group** [`padicCompletionUnits`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/Basic.html#TauCeti.padicCompletionUnits) — `A(L) = lim Lˣ/(Lˣ)^{p^m}` as data, with compatible `ℤ_p` and `ℤ_p[Gal(L/K)]` structures, and [finitely generated](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/MultiplicativeCompletion/Finite.html#TauCeti.padicCompletionUnits_module_finite) over the group ring, which is the hypothesis every cancellation step needs.
