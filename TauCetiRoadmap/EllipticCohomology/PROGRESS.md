## EllipticCohomology: 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-09 (`478c117` to `d92acc5`)

The headline is Deligne's computation of integral modular forms in its Hopf-algebroid form: a polynomial in the Weierstrass coefficients transforms with weight k under every change of variables over every ring exactly when it lies in ℤ[c₄, c₆, Δ] and is weighted homogeneous of weight k ([`isIntegralModularForm_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/ModularForm.html#WeierstrassCurve.isIntegralModularForm_iff), TauCeti#24488). That subring is presented by the single relation c₄³ − c₆² = 1728Δ ([`ker_aeval_c₄_c₆_Δ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Invariants/Presentation.html#WeierstrassCurve.ker_aeval_c₄_c₆_Δ)), because c₄ and c₆ are algebraically independent over ℤ. The proof runs through the cuspidal locus V(c₄, Δ), which now has codimension two ([`height_span_c₄_Δ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Invariants/CuspidalLocus.html#WeierstrassCurve.Universal.height_span_c₄_Δ)). The Tate curve then links these forms to the analytic ones: its c₄ and Δ are the q-expansions of E₄ and the discriminant, and a level-one form with integral q-expansion comes from an integral modular form ([`exists_isIntegralModularForm_specialize_tateCurve_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateCurve/QExpansion.html#ModularForm.exists_isIntegralModularForm_specialize_tateCurve_eq)). The theorem is still about the cubic atlas. The stack form on Mbar_ell is not here.

The Weierstrass Hopf algebroid now exists. Its coproduct is coassociative ([`coproduct_coassoc`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/VariableChange/HopfAlgebroid.html#TauCeti.WeierstrassHopfAlgebroid.coproduct_coassoc)), it satisfies the counit and antipode laws, and its source and target maps are smooth of relative dimension four. The coordinate-change group is corepresentable and splits as the Heisenberg group semidirect the units ([`semidirectProductEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/VariableChange/SemidirectProduct.html#WeierstrassCurve.VariableChange.semidirectProductEquiv)). For formal groups, characteristics 2 and 3 are settled: there an elliptic curve is ordinary exactly when its formal group has height one, and supersingular exactly when it has height two ([`isSupersingular_iff_height_formalGroup_eq_two_of_char_three`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/FormalGroup/Char/Three.html#WeierstrassCurve.isSupersingular_iff_height_formalGroup_eq_two_of_char_three)). The Tate curve's formal group at q = 0 is multiplicative.

Layer 2 gained its first algebraic inputs. The translation-difference exact sequence on continuous functions on ℤ_p is now proved from the Mahler basis ([`exact_const_fwdDiffCLM`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/TranslationDifference/Basic.html#TauCeti.exact_const_fwdDiffCLM)) and carried over to the p-adic units modulo torsion, at every prime. There are also two completion lemmas: principal-adic completion preserves exactness when the cokernel has no torsion, with no Noetherian hypothesis, and the localization–completion square of a regular element is cartesian ([`existsUnique_localization_lift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Fracture.html#AdicCompletion.existsUnique_localization_lift)).
