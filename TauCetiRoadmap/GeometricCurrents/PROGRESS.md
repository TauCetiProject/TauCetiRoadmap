## GeometricCurrents: 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-24 (`3150404` to `5d671b9`)

This first window for the roadmap puts the Euclidean pieces of the constancy theorem in place. On an open set of an oriented finite-dimensional real space there is now the oriented integration current [`integrationCurrent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/IntegrationCurrent.html#Orientation.integrationCurrent), the functional that integrates a compactly supported top-degree form, and it has zero boundary: by [`integrationCurrent_extDerivCLM`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/IntegrationCurrent.html#Orientation.integrationCurrent_extDerivCLM) it kills every exact test form, which is Stokes' theorem for compactly supported forms (TauCeti#20490). It changes sign when the orientation is reversed and is a Dirac mass in dimension zero. Under it sits the exterior derivative on test forms as a continuous linear map, [`extDerivCLM`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/ExtDeriv.html#TestFunction.extDerivCLM), with d∘d = 0, together with the reduction showing that a top-degree current with zero boundary kills every derivative of a scalar test function.

For the other half of the argument, regularization arrived: pairing a distribution with translates of a fixed test function gives a smooth function whose derivative is the regularization of the distributional derivative ([`fderiv_translatePairing_apply`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Regularization.html#Distribution.fderiv_translatePairing_apply)). So a distribution with vanishing derivatives has [locally constant regularizations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Regularization.html#Distribution.translatePairing_eq_of_lineDerivOp_eq_zero) (TauCeti#20243). On the way came the fact that every distribution has [finite order on each compact set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Order.html#Distribution.exists_norm_apply_le). Separately, [`dualEquivDistribution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/DualEquiv.html#TestFunction.dualEquivDistribution) identifies maps on vector-valued tests with operator-valued distributions, compatibly with derivatives.

Two things are still missing: the scalar criterion itself, that a distribution with vanishing derivatives on a connected open set is a constant multiple of Lebesgue measure, and the constancy theorem built from it. Everything so far lives on Euclidean open sets. The manifold carrier of test forms and currents, and mass, have not been started.

## GeometricCurrents: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-12 (`5d671b9` to `941bb58`)

The Euclidean constancy theorem landed in this window. On a connected open subset of an oriented real vector space, a top-degree current with zero boundary is a unique constant multiple of the integration current ([`existsUnique_eq_smul_integrationCurrent_of_comp_extDerivCLM_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Constancy/Current.html#Orientation.existsUnique_eq_smul_integrationCurrent_of_comp_extDerivCLM_eq_zero), TauCeti#23379). On an arbitrary open set the same holds on each component. Underneath is the scalar criterion: a distribution on a connected open set whose directional derivatives all vanish is integration against a unique constant ([`existsUnique_eq_ofFun_const_of_forall_lineDerivOp_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Constancy/Basic.html#Distribution.existsUnique_eq_ofFun_const_of_forall_lineDerivOp_eq_zero)). It is proved with mollification that converges in the test topology, and then the local constants are glued.

Mass arrived in the same window. There is extended [`mass`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Mass/Basic.html#Distribution.mass) and local mass, which are weakly lower semicontinuous, and the Radon [mass measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Mass/Measure.html#Distribution.massMeasure) ‖T‖ is characterised as the least measure dominating evaluation. The [polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Mass/PolarDensity.html#Distribution.apply_eq_integral_polarDensity) T = τ‖T‖ has a unit-norm density. Borel restriction, gluing, and [normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Mass/Normal.html#Distribution.IsNormal) and locally normal currents are built on these. The comass side proves that a simple k-vector has mass equal to its volume. It also settles the README's check that e₀∧e₁+e₂∧e₃ has [mass 2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HodgeStar/Kahler/Mass.html#OrthonormalBasis.norm_formDualEquiv_ιMulti_add_ιMulti_finFour) but Hilbert norm √2.

The rest of the window builds the operations that deformation needs. It adds pushforwards, which multiply mass by at most Lᵏ when ‖DΦ‖ ≤ L, segment and [simplex currents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/SimplexCurrent.html#TauCeti.simplexCurrent_comp_extDerivCLM) with their boundary formulas, and the [homotopy formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/Homotopy.html#ContinuousLinearMap.comp_homotopyPullbackCLM_comp_extDerivCLM). Products with an interval meet the README's bounds M([[a,b]]×T) ≤ |b−a|M(T) and [M(∂([[a,b]]×T)) ≤ 2M(T)+|b−a|M(∂T)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/CylinderHomotopy.html#Distribution.mass_comp_intervalCylinderHomotopyCLM_comp_extDerivCLM_le). The [cone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/Cone.html#Distribution.mass_cone_le) satisfies M ≤ rM(T)/(k+1). A real [flat norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/Flat/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.normalFlatMass) comes with its completion. There is also weak compactness for real currents with bounded mass and boundary mass, but that is not Federer–Fleming, since integral currents do not exist yet. Everything is still on Euclidean open sets, and the manifold carrier has not begun.
