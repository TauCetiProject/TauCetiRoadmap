## AlgebraicCurves: 2026-08-18 to 2026-09-01 (`557387b` to `8745177`)

The roadmap's first report covers the function-field foundation up to the doorstep of Riemann–Roch. The **product formula** ([`TauCeti.Divisor.degree_principal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Divisor/ProductFormula.html#TauCeti.Divisor.degree_principal)) says every principal divisor has degree zero, via deg (z)₀ = deg (z)∞ = [F : k(z)] ([`TauCeti.Divisor.degree_poles`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Divisor/ProductFormula.html#TauCeti.Divisor.degree_poles)). **Riemann's theorem** ([`TauCeti.Divisor.degree_add_one_sub_genus_le_dim`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/RiemannRoch/Genus.html#TauCeti.Divisor.degree_add_one_sub_genus_le_dim)) gives ℓ(D) ≥ deg D + 1 − g, with equality in large degree, for the genus defined as Stichtenoth's supremum ([`TauCeti.genus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/RiemannRoch/Genus.html#TauCeti.genus)). The **index of specialty is a dimension**, i(D) = dim_k A_F/(A_F(D) + F) ([`TauCeti.finrank_quotient_repartitionSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Repartition/IndexOfSpecialty.html#TauCeti.finrank_quotient_repartitionSpace)), and the **Weil differentials are one-dimensional over F** ([`TauCeti.finrank_weilDifferentialSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_weilDifferentialSpace)). Riemann–Roch itself has not landed: the divisor of a Weil differential, the canonical class and the duality theorem are missing, though the **uniqueness of the Riemann–Roch data** ([`TauCeti.Divisor.IsRiemannRochDivisor.genus_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/RiemannRoch/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Divisor.IsRiemannRochDivisor.genus_eq)) shows any divisor satisfying the identity fixes the genus and the canonical class.

Beneath this sit normalized places ([`TauCeti.Place`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Place/Basic.html#TauCeti.Place)) with finite degree, **weak approximation** ([`TauCeti.Place.exists_forall_ord_sub_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Place/Approximation.html#TauCeti.Place.exists_forall_ord_sub_eq)) and **strong approximation** ([`TauCeti.Place.exists_forall_mem_ord_sub_eq_and_forall_mem_integers`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Consequences/StrongApproximation.html#TauCeti.Place.exists_forall_mem_ord_sub_eq_and_forall_mem_integers)), the classification of the places of k(x) ([`TauCeti.Place.ratFuncEquivMonicIrreducible`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Place/RatFunc/Basic.html#TauCeti.Place.ratFuncEquivMonicIrreducible)), the places-to-height-one-primes dictionary of a Dedekind model ([`TauCeti.Place.heightOneSpectrumEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/AffineModel/Prime.html#TauCeti.Place.heightOneSpectrumEquiv)), the bridge Cl⁰(F) ≅ ClassGroup R for a model with one rational place at infinity ([`TauCeti.Divisor.degreeZeroClassGroupEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Divisor/AffineModel.html#TauCeti.Divisor.degreeZeroClassGroupEquiv)), and finiteness of Cl⁰ over a finite constant field ([`TauCeti.Divisor.finite_ker_degreeClass`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/RiemannRoch/ClassNumber.html#TauCeti.Divisor.finite_ker_degreeClass)). The generic weak-approximation engine is TauCeti#3732.

Extension theory ran in parallel: the **fundamental identity** ([`TauCeti.Place.sum_ramificationIdx_mul_relativeDegree_eq_finrank_of_isSeparable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Place/Extension/Fundamental.html#TauCeti.Place.sum_ramificationIdx_mul_relativeDegree_eq_finrank_of_isSeparable)) — unconditional at places of an affine model, only for separable F′/F elsewhere — the conorm, **Kummer's theorem** ([`TauCeti.Place.exists_injective_restrict_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Place/Extension/Kummer.html#TauCeti.Place.exists_injective_restrict_eq)), transitivity of the Galois action ([`TauCeti.Place.exists_smul_eq_of_restrict_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Place/Extension/Galois.html#TauCeti.Place.exists_smul_eq_of_restrict_eq)), and the different exponent with Dedekind's bound d ≥ e − 1 ([`TauCeti.Place.ramificationIdx_le_differentExponent_add_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Basic.html#TauCeti.Place.ramificationIdx_le_differentExponent_add_one)). Kähler differentials of a separably generated F/k are one-dimensional ([`TauCeti.finrank_kaehlerDifferential_eq_one_of_separating`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Kaehler.html#TauCeti.finrank_kaehlerDifferential_eq_one_of_separating)). Absent: the different divisor and its tame value, the cotrace and Hurwitz, constant-field extensions, model classes, the scheme dictionary. Affine models are hypotheses throughout — the finite-normalization theorem producing them is absent — and extensions of places exist only when both F′/F and k′/k are integral.
