## AlgebraicCurves: 2026-08-18 to 2026-09-01 (`557387b` to `8745177`)

The roadmap's first report covers the function-field foundation up to the doorstep of Riemann–Roch. The **product formula** ([`TauCeti.Divisor.degree_principal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Divisor/ProductFormula.html#TauCeti.Divisor.degree_principal)) says every principal divisor has degree zero, via deg (z)₀ = deg (z)∞ = [F : k(z)] ([`TauCeti.Divisor.degree_poles`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Divisor/ProductFormula.html#TauCeti.Divisor.degree_poles)). **Riemann's theorem** ([`TauCeti.Divisor.degree_add_one_sub_genus_le_dim`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/RiemannRoch/Genus.html#TauCeti.Divisor.degree_add_one_sub_genus_le_dim)) gives ℓ(D) ≥ deg D + 1 − g, with equality in large degree, for the genus defined as Stichtenoth's supremum ([`TauCeti.genus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/RiemannRoch/Genus.html#TauCeti.genus)). The **index of specialty is a dimension**, i(D) = dim_k A_F/(A_F(D) + F) ([`TauCeti.finrank_quotient_repartitionSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Repartition/IndexOfSpecialty.html#TauCeti.finrank_quotient_repartitionSpace)), and the **Weil differentials are one-dimensional over F** ([`TauCeti.finrank_weilDifferentialSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_weilDifferentialSpace)). Riemann–Roch itself has not landed: the divisor of a Weil differential, the canonical class and the duality theorem are missing, though the **uniqueness of the Riemann–Roch data** ([`TauCeti.Divisor.IsRiemannRochDivisor.genus_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/RiemannRoch/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Divisor.IsRiemannRochDivisor.genus_eq)) shows any divisor satisfying the identity fixes the genus and the canonical class.

## AlgebraicCurves: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-21 (`8745177` to `a3c29b5`)

Riemann–Roch is now proved in the function-field Weil-differential formalism: a nonzero differential has a greatest divisor and canonical class, multiplication gives the duality between L(W − D) and the filtered Weil differentials, and that divisor satisfies Riemann–Roch with degree 2g − 2 ([the Riemann–Roch theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.html#TauCeti.isRiemannRochDivisor_weilDifferential), [duality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.html#TauCeti.riemannRochSpaceEquivWeilDifferentialFiltration), [canonical degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.html#TauCeti.degree_weilDifferentialDivisor)). This unlocks the high-degree formula and the Weierstrass gap theorem ([high-degree Riemann–Roch](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Consequences/HighDegree.html#TauCeti.Divisor.dim_eq_degree_add_one_sub_genus_of_two_mul_genus_sub_one_le_degree), [gap theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Consequences/WeierstrassGaps.html#TauCeti.Place.card_weierstrassGaps)).

The extension lane now reaches its main ramification summit. The fundamental identity holds without separability ([fundamental identity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Place/Extension/Fundamental.html#TauCeti.Place.sum_ramificationIdx_mul_relativeDegree_eq_finrank_of_isFunctionField)); the finite different divisor has the tame coefficient e − 1 ([different](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Divisor.html#TauCeti.Divisor.different), [tame equality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Tame.html#TauCeti.Divisor.coeff_different_add_one_eq_ramificationIdx_iff)); and cotrace has divisor Con(ω) + Diff, yielding the cross-multiplied Hurwitz formula ([cotrace divisor](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Cotrace.html#TauCeti.weilDifferentialDivisor_weilDifferentialCotrace), [Hurwitz](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Hurwitz.html#TauCeti.hurwitz_genus_formula)).
