## AlgebraicCurves: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-21 (`8745177` to `93386bc`)

**Riemann–Roch** landed. For a nonzero Weil differential `ω` with divisor `W = (ω)`, multiplication by `ω` is a `k`-linear isomorphism `L(W − D) ≃ Ω_F(D)` ([`TauCeti.riemannRochSpaceEquivWeilDifferentialFiltration`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.html#TauCeti.riemannRochSpaceEquivWeilDifferentialFiltration)), so `(ω)` is a Riemann–Roch divisor: `ℓ(D) − ℓ(W − D) = deg D + 1 − g` ([`TauCeti.isRiemannRochDivisor_weilDifferentialDivisor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.html#TauCeti.isRiemannRochDivisor_weilDifferentialDivisor), TauCeti#5903). The canonical class has degree `2g − 2` with `ℓ(W) = g`, and a divisor represents it exactly when it meets those two conditions ([`TauCeti.divisorClass_eq_canonicalClass_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.html#TauCeti.divisorClass_eq_canonicalClass_iff)). The consequences followed: `ℓ(D) = deg D + 1 − g` once `deg D ≥ 2g − 1`, the **Weierstrass gap theorem**, exactly `g` gaps at a rational place ([`TauCeti.Place.card_weierstrassGaps`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Consequences/WeierstrassGaps.html#TauCeti.Place.card_weierstrassGaps)), rationality of a genus-zero field carrying a divisor of degree one, and **Clifford's theorem**, proved only over an infinite constant field ([`TauCeti.Divisor.two_mul_dim_le_degree_add_two_of_infinite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Consequences/Clifford.html#TauCeti.Divisor.two_mul_dim_le_degree_add_two_of_infinite)).

The different lane closed alongside it. The cotrace of Weil differentials exists and is unique ([`TauCeti.weilDifferentialCotrace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Cotrace.html#TauCeti.weilDifferentialCotrace)), its divisor is `Con (ω) + Diff(F'/F)`, and from that comes the **Hurwitz genus formula** `[k' : k] · (2g' − 2) = [F' : F] · (2g − 2) + [k' : k] · deg Diff(F'/F)`, for `F'/F` finite separable ([`TauCeti.hurwitz_genus_formula`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Hurwitz.html#TauCeti.hurwitz_genus_formula), TauCeti#7306). Dedekind's different theorem is complete, tame equality included ([`TauCeti.Place.ramificationIdx_eq_differentExponent_add_one_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Tame.html#TauCeti.Place.ramificationIdx_eq_differentExponent_add_one_iff)). Galois ramification theory arrived with it: decomposition fields, inertia groups with their unconditional order `e · f_insep`, and the ramification filtration, elementary abelian quotients included.

Model classes opened. Elliptic function fields are defined as genus one plus a divisor of degree one, their degree-one places carry the group law of `Cl⁰(F)` ([`TauCeti.Place.degreeOneAddEquivDegreeZeroClassGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Elliptic.html#TauCeti.Place.degreeOneAddEquivDegreeZeroClassGroup)), and the function field of a Weierstrass curve is one of them ([`WeierstrassCurve.Affine.genus_functionField`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Affine/FunctionField/Genus.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.genus_functionField)). Hyperelliptic fields have their intrinsic characterization away from characteristic two. Absent: normal forms, Kummer and Artin–Schreier covers, residues and the Kähler comparison, and finite normalization, so affine models are still hypotheses.

## AlgebraicCurves: 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-26 (`93386bc` to `759eb3e`)

The **Kähler–Weil comparison** and the **divisor of `dx`** landed. For a separating element `x` of a function field with exact constants, the Weil differential `dx` is the cotrace of the standard differential of `k(x)`; an `F`-linear isomorphism from the Kähler differentials sends the Kähler `dx` to it ([`TauCeti.kaehlerDifferentialEquivWeilDifferentialOfSeparating`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Comparison.html#TauCeti.kaehlerDifferentialEquivWeilDifferentialOfSeparating)), and its divisor is `−2 (x)_∞ + Diff(F/k(x))` ([`TauCeti.weilDifferentialDivisor_weilDifferentialOfSeparating`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Comparison.html#TauCeti.weilDifferentialDivisor_weilDifferentialOfSeparating)), which is therefore canonical. The isomorphism depends on the chosen `x`, and residues and the residue theorem are still missing.

**The Weierstrass equation of a genus-one field**, the first half of Stichtenoth's Proposition 6.1.2, is proved: at every rational place `P` of a genus-one field with exact constants, some `x` and `y` with pole divisors `2P` and `3P` satisfy a Weierstrass equation over `k` and generate the field ([`TauCeti.Place.exists_isWeierstrassCoordinates_of_genus_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Elliptic/WeierstrassEquation.html#TauCeti.Place.exists_isWeierstrassCoordinates_of_genus_eq_one)), in the form `y² = x³ + a₂x² + a₄x + a₆` away from characteristic two. Nonsingularity of that equation, the characteristic-two forms and the converse remain. **Rigidity** holds too: over an exact constant field, an automorphism fixing `2g + 3` rational places is the identity ([`TauCeti.eq_one_of_two_mul_genus_add_three_le_card`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Automorphism/Rigidity.html#TauCeti.eq_one_of_two_mul_genus_add_three_le_card)), so an automorphism-invariant set of that many rational places makes `Aut(F/k)` finite ([`TauCeti.finite_algEquiv_of_invariant_rational_places`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Automorphism/Finite.html#TauCeti.finite_algEquiv_of_invariant_rational_places)). Weierstrass gap sets are invariant and fixed fields of finite groups are function fields, but finiteness for `g ≥ 2` still needs the Weierstrass-point count.

The integral closure of `k[x]` became an affine model, Dedekind with fraction field `F` and no separability assumption ([`TauCeti.isDedekindDomain_integralClosure_adjoin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/AffineModel/IntegralClosure.html#TauCeti.isDedekindDomain_integralClosure_adjoin)), whose charts for `x` and `x⁻¹` glue along their common localization; this completes Layer 2. Radical extensions `yⁿ = u` with `n` invertible in `k` got their ramification index and different exponent wherever `n` divides `ord_P u` or is prime to it, hence everywhere when `n` is prime ([`TauCeti.Place.differentExponent_eq_of_pow_eq_of_prime`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Radical.html#TauCeti.Place.differentExponent_eq_of_pow_eq_of_prime)). Smaller additions: the valuation criterion for separating elements over a perfect field, and positive ramification groups are `p`-groups.

The genus of a hyperelliptic curve given by its equation is now proved: away from characteristic two, if `F = k(x, y)` with `y² = f(x)` and `f` squarefree of degree `m`, then `F` has genus `⌊(m − 1)/2⌋` ([`TauCeti.genus_eq_of_sq_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Hyperelliptic/Genus.html#TauCeti.genus_eq_of_sq_eq), TauCeti#10058). The ramified places are those over the zeros of `f`, together with the place at infinity when `m` is odd ([`TauCeti.Place.one_lt_ramificationIdx_iff_of_sq_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Hyperelliptic/Genus.html#TauCeti.Place.one_lt_ramificationIdx_iff_of_sq_eq)), and the constants stay exact because a ramified extension of prime degree acquires no new ones. Constant-field extensions have started. For `k` exact in `F` and `k'/k` finite separable, the compositum `F·k'` is unramified at every place, and a `k`-basis of `L(D)` stays a `k'`-basis of `L(Con D)`, so `ℓ(Con D) = ℓ(D)` ([`TauCeti.Divisor.dim_conorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/ConstantExtension/RiemannRoch.html#TauCeti.Divisor.dim_conorm), TauCeti#10059).
