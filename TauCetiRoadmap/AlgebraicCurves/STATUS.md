### Named results

- **The Riemann–Roch theorem** — for `W` the divisor of a nonzero Weil differential, `ℓ(D) − ℓ(W − D) = deg D + 1 − g` for every divisor `D`, over any exact constant field ([`TauCeti.isRiemannRochDivisor_weilDifferentialDivisor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.html#TauCeti.isRiemannRochDivisor_weilDifferentialDivisor)).
- **The Hurwitz genus formula** — `[k' : k] · (2g' − 2) = [F' : F] · (2g − 2) + [k' : k] · deg Diff(F'/F)` for `F'/F` finite separable with exact constants and `k'/k` finite separable ([`TauCeti.hurwitz_genus_formula`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Hurwitz.html#TauCeti.hurwitz_genus_formula)).
- **The divisor of `dx`** — for a separating element `x` of a field with exact constants, the Weil differential `dx` has divisor `−2 (x)_∞ + Diff(F/k(x))`, which is therefore canonical ([`TauCeti.weilDifferentialDivisor_weilDifferentialOfSeparating`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Comparison.html#TauCeti.weilDifferentialDivisor_weilDifferentialOfSeparating)).
- **The Weierstrass equation of a genus-one field** — at each rational place `P` of a genus-one field with exact constants, some `x` and `y` with pole divisors `2P` and `3P` satisfy a Weierstrass equation over `k` and generate the field, in the form `y² = x³ + a₂x² + a₄x + a₆` away from characteristic two ([`TauCeti.Place.exists_isWeierstrassCoordinates_of_genus_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Elliptic/WeierstrassEquation.html#TauCeti.Place.exists_isWeierstrassCoordinates_of_genus_eq_one)).
- **Rigidity of automorphisms** — over an exact constant field, an automorphism of `F/k` fixing `2g + 3` distinct rational places is the identity ([`TauCeti.eq_one_of_two_mul_genus_add_three_le_card`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Automorphism/Rigidity.html#TauCeti.eq_one_of_two_mul_genus_add_three_le_card)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Affine models from any transcendental element** ([`TauCeti.isDedekindDomain_integralClosure_adjoin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/AffineModel/IntegralClosure.html#TauCeti.isDedekindDomain_integralClosure_adjoin)) — the integral closure `R_x` of `k[x]` in `F` is Dedekind with fraction field `F`, without separability, and its height-one primes are the places where `x` has no pole; the charts for `x` and `x⁻¹` glue along their overlap. This is the input for normalizing `ℙ¹` in `F`.
- **The Kähler–Weil comparison** ([`TauCeti.kaehlerDifferentialEquivWeilDifferentialOfSeparating`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Comparison.html#TauCeti.kaehlerDifferentialEquivWeilDifferentialOfSeparating)) — for a separating `x`, an `F`-linear isomorphism `Ω[F⁄k] ≃ Ω_F` sends the Kähler `dx` to the cotrace of the standard differential of `k(x)`. It depends on the chosen `x` and involves no residues yet.
- **The cotrace** ([`TauCeti.weilDifferentialCotrace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/Cotrace.html#TauCeti.weilDifferentialCotrace)) — carries a Weil differential `ω` of `F` to one of `F'` with divisor `Con (ω) + Diff(F'/F)`. Hurwitz is the degree of this identity, and the divisor of `dx` is its case `F/k(x)`.

## The frontier

- **Riemann–Hurwitz for `y² = f(x)` (Layers 7 and 10)** — radical extensions now have `e` and `d` at every place for a prime exponent invertible in `k`, and Hurwitz over `k(x)` is proved. What remains is the hyperelliptic genus formula with its ramified places, which also needs the exact-constant-field criterion for such covers.
- **Elliptic models (Layer 10)** — prove the Weierstrass equation found is nonsingular, so that `F` is the function field of an elliptic curve, then the characteristic-two normal forms and the converse, Proposition 6.1.3.
- **Finiteness of `Aut(F/k)` for `g ≥ 2` (Layer 11)** — rigidity, gap invariance and finiteness from an invariant set are in. Missing are the Wronskian count giving at least `2g + 3` Weierstrass points when `F` is not hyperelliptic, uniqueness of the degree-two rational subfield, and three-point rigidity on `ℙ¹`.
- **Normalizing `ℙ¹` in `F` (Layer 12B)** — its Layer 2 inputs are complete. Missing are the scheme gluing, the finite map to `ℙ¹`, projectivity, and 12A's proof that `k(X)` is a function field, which the existing scheme-side comparisons assume.
- **Residues (Layer 9, with Layer 5's completion milestone)** — local expansions, `res_P` with its change-of-uniformizer formula, local components as residues, the residue theorem, and independence of the comparison from `x`.
