## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The function-field theory through Riemann–Roch and Riemann–Hurwitz is complete, as are the model classes, the automorphism theory and the curve–function-field anti-equivalence in its projective form. What remains partial is narrow: completion compatibility of the different at inseparable residue fields, the residue comparison beyond separable residues, and the characteristic-zero finite-map contracts of the scheme dictionary. No layer is untouched.

### Named results

- **The Riemann–Roch theorem** — Over any exact constant field, `ℓ(D) − ℓ(W − D) = deg D + 1 − g` for a canonical divisor `W` ([`TauCeti.isRiemannRochDivisor_weilDifferentialDivisor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Differential/CanonicalDivisor.html#TauCeti.isRiemannRochDivisor_weilDifferentialDivisor)).
- **The Hurwitz genus formula** — For finite separable extensions, `[k′ : k](2g′ − 2) = [F′ : F](2g − 2) + [k′ : k] deg Diff` ([`TauCeti.hurwitz_genus_formula`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/Different/Hurwitz.html#TauCeti.hurwitz_genus_formula)).
- **The curve–function-field anti-equivalence** — Regular projective curves over any field, with dominant morphisms, are contravariantly equivalent to one-variable function fields with embeddings ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.RegularProjectiveCurveCat.equivFunctionFieldCat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/TwoChartModel/AntiEquivalence.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.RegularProjectiveCurveCat.equivFunctionFieldCat)).
- **The genus-change formula** — Under a finite linearly disjoint constant extension with exact constants on both sides, the genus drops by the sum of the local singularity degrees, which vanish at almost every place ([`TauCeti.genus_eq_genus_add_finsum_singularityDegree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/ConstantExtension/Genus/Change.html#TauCeti.genus_eq_genus_add_finsum_singularityDegree)). When no genus is lost, the conorm is injective on divisor classes and preserves the canonical class, with no separability of `k′/k` ([`TauCeti.conormClassGroup_injective_of_genus_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/ConstantExtension/Genus/Change.html#TauCeti.conormClassGroup_injective_of_genus_eq)).
- **The fierce biquadratic different** — Over `𝔽₂(s, u)`, a biquadratic extension with `e = 1` and a purely inseparable residue extension of degree four has different exponent two, while Hilbert's group sum is three, so the classical formula genuinely needs separable residues ([`TauCeti.FierceBiquadratic.multiplicity_differentIdeal_ne_finsum_ringYZ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/FierceBiquadratic/Different.html#TauCeti.FierceBiquadratic.multiplicity_differentIdeal_ne_finsum_ringYZ)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Singularity degrees** — `δ_P = dim (𝒪′_P / 𝒪_P ⊗ k′)` measures how far the base-changed local ring is from normal. It locates an inseparable genus drop at finitely many places, and the genus is unchanged exactly when every `δ_P` vanishes ([`TauCeti.Place.singularityDegree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/FunctionField/ConstantExtension/Genus/Change.html#TauCeti.Place.singularityDegree)).
- **Smoothness of regular curves over perfect fields** — A curve whose local rings at codimension-one points are discrete valuation rings is smooth of relative dimension one over a perfect field ([`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IsCurveOver.smoothOfRelativeDimension_one_of_perfectField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/Smooth.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IsCurveOver.smoothOfRelativeDimension_one_of_perfectField)). This connects the regular model to the smooth-model branch of constant extension, where Riemann–Roch dimensions and divisor classes survive every finite constant extension.
- **Projective plane curves as schemes** — The `Proj` of `R[X₀, X₁, X₂]/(G)`, with its three charts identified with dehomogenized coordinate rings and its function field with the affine one. This is the interface the plane-curve genus and the Klein quartic are proved through ([`TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.projectivePlaneCurve`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/PlaneCurve/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.projectivePlaneCurve)).

## The frontier

- **The different under completion** — Show that the trace-dual different, and its length formula, are unchanged on passing to the completion at a place with inseparable residue field.
- **Characteristic-zero finite maps and descent** — Compare the ramification divisor of the cotangent map with the trace-dual different, and descend finite models to a finitely generated field, as the BelyiMaps consumer requires.
- **Genus change beyond the linearly disjoint case** — The singularity-degree formula assumes finite `k′/k`, exact constants in `F·k′` and `[F·k′ : F] = [k′ : k]`. Algebraic extensions where the constants grow are not covered by it.
- **Inseparable residue fields** — The residue comparison cannot simply drop separability: completions are Laurent-series fields over their residue fields, but not always compatibly with the base field. The target needs revising rather than proving.
