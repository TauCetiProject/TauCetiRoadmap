## The frontier

- **Finite normalization of affine models** — prove that the integral closure of `k[x]` in a function field is finite and supplies the missing unconditional Dedekind models; this is the prerequisite for the remaining Layer 2 bridge and later dictionary work.
- **Constant-field extensions** — establish genus, degree, and `ℓ` invariance under the honest perfect/separably generated hypotheses, and prove the inseparable genus-drop counterexample; the missing constant-field identification is the immediate blocker.
- **Completion and residue comparison** — construct the arbitrary-residue complete-DVR comparison for repartitions, then the residue map and Kähler–Weil comparison in Layer 9.
- **Model classes** — add elliptic normal forms and converses, hyperelliptic cover calculations, and smooth plane-curve genus bounds; these require the extension/different results and the later differential comparison.
- **Automorphisms and the curve dictionary** — prove the characteristic-sensitive Hurwitz automorphism bound and then identify regular projective curves with the function-field carriers; both remain beyond the present Galois and model-class groundwork.
