Across all thirteen constructors, nine untwisted families and four graph-twisted ones, the candidate is the derived subgroup of the Steinberg fixed points modulo its centre ([`TauCeti.GraphTwistedIndex.Group`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/Assembly/GraphTwisted.html#TauCeti.GraphTwistedIndex.Group)), and the pinned equation `x_i(u) ↦ x_{σ i}(u ^ q)` holds on every simple root subgroup of every branch ([`steinberg_simpleRootSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/Assembly/GraphTwisted.html#TauCeti.GraphTwistedIndex.steinberg_simpleRootSubgroup), TauCeti#7561).
