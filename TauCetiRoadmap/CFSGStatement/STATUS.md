## The frontier

- **The special isogenies of the short-root carriers (L2)** — the carriers exist in characteristic two and three; missing is `τ` itself, its action sending long simple roots across with exponent one and short ones with exponent `p`, and `τ ^ 2 = Frob_p`. `²G₂`, `²F₄` and the Tits group then follow the Suzuki pattern.
- **The uniform `ValidLieTypeIndex.steinberg` and `.Group` (L3)** — the case split over thirteen graph-twisted branches plus Suzuki is ready to write; it waits only on the three branches above.
- **Assembly (A0)** — `CFSGIndex.Group`, `ClassificationStatement` and `classificationStatement_of_zero`. The cyclic, alternating and sporadic branches have carriers and the universe step is proved abstractly, so this is blocked only on the uniform L3.
- **Agreement of the explicit carriers with the pinned groups (L5)** — nothing has begun, and nothing can: every branch built so far owes an isomorphism to the pinned Chevalley--Demazure points, which the reductive-groups roadmap's Layer 9 has yet to supply.
- **The Suzuki identification (L4)** — what remains is that the Steinberg fixed points are generated by the standard unipotent and Weyl matrices, the containment opposite to the one now proved.
