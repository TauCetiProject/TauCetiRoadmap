## The frontier

- **Carrier agreement for the spin families (L5)** — B in rank at least three, D and ²D, all on full-weight spin carriers. Each needs an isomorphism to the pinned points that matches the numbered simple root subgroups and intertwines an independently built Steinberg map. The pinned spin group schemes belong to Layer 9 of the reductive-groups roadmap.
- **Carrier agreement for the exceptional families (L5)** — the same is needed for ³D₄, E₆, ²E₆, E₇, E₈, F₄ and G₂ on their tripled, minuscule, doubled and Geck carriers. It is also needed for Ree G₂, Ree F₄ and Tits on the short-root carriers over 𝔽₃ and 𝔽₂, against Layer 9's pinned points and, for those last three, its special isogenies.
- **The Suzuki identification against Mathlib (L4)** — the Suzuki candidate is already isomorphic to the group generated by the standard four-by-four unipotent and Weyl matrices, but in Tau Ceti's copy of that model rather than Mathlib's `suzukiGroup`, the README's target.
