## SpinGeometry: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`9bb1cd0` to `c1c6773`)

The first work on this roadmap is all algebra in its opening layer, G1, setting up the sign conventions that geometric Clifford multiplication will need. The compact group Spin(n) is built from the positive-definite form, but geometric Clifford multiplication uses the negative one, where c(v)² = −|v|². A [group isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Neg.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinGroupEquivNeg) between the Spin groups of Q and −Q now links the two, together with its [real-signature version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Neg.html#CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinGroupEquivNeg) from Spin(p,q) to Spin(q,p). It goes through the even subalgebras, sends a bivector to minus the corresponding bivector, preserves the vector actions and sends −1 to −1 (TauCeti#18313).

The other main thread is the Clifford scalar trace τ, [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace) as the degree-zero part under the exterior-algebra identification. It is [identified with the normalized regular trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.trace_mulLeft_eq_two_pow_mul_scalarTrace) 2^(−dim V)·tr(L_a) and shown to be cyclic, invariant under involute, reverse and algebra isomorphisms, and compatible with base change. For an orthogonal basis, the ordered products of basis vectors [are the Clifford basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/OrthogonalBasis.html#CliffordAlgebra.basis_eq_prod_map_sort). The pairing τ(reverse x · y) is diagonal in that basis, which gives [positivity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/OrthogonalBasis.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_reverse_mul_self_nonneg) and [faithfulness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/OrthogonalBasis.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_reverse_mul_self_eq_zero_iff) over ordered rings. On the complexified algebra, coefficient [conjugation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Complexification.html#CliffordAlgebra.complexConj) is characterized by fixing the real vectors, and its composite with Clifford conjugation gives the conjugate-linear [involution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Complexification.html#CliffordAlgebra.conjStar) that makes c(v) skew-Hermitian. That involution is matched with the tensor-product star. Positivity is not yet stated for complex coefficients, and the semidefinite null-ideal characterization has not landed.

## SpinGeometry: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`c1c6773` to `93d1fe5`)

The main result of this window is the Fock model of G1.2. Take a real quadratic space with a compatible complex structure J. The (1,0) and (0,1) parts of vectors span two isotropic eigenspaces, and these now give a [spin polarization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/ComplexStructure.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.spinPolarizationData). The [√2-normalized Fock action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Fock.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.fockAction) is defined by conjugating the native spinor action with the scaling by 2^(p/2) on Λ^pW, and the [intertwining equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Fock.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.degreeScaling_comp_spinAction_eq_fockAction_comp) is proved. A real vector acts by [√2 times creation plus a √2/2-weighted contraction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Fock.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.fockAction_ι_one_tmul), and this action satisfies the Clifford relation. On the metric side, the [sesquilinear complexification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/BilinearForm/Complexification.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.sesqComplexification) of g gives [|v^(1,0)|² = |v|²/2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Complex/HermitianForm.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.sesqComplexification_holomorphicPart_self). The Hermitian form h_J is [twice its pullback](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Complex/HermitianForm.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.Invariant.complexAssociatedForm_eq_two_mul_sesqComplexification) along v ↦ v^(1,0). Some of G1.2 has not landed: the creation/annihilation anticommutators, adjointness of wedge and contraction, skew-Hermitian c(v), and the 2^p comparison of Fock metrics.

The Morita evaluation of G1.3 also landed, in a general form. If an algebra acts on S as all of End S, then every module M is [S ⊗ Hom(S, M) by evaluation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Morita/ModuleEnd.html#TauCeti.isotypicTensorEquivOfBijective). The inverse is written in a basis, and the window adds naturality and a [dimension formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Morita/ModuleEnd.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_finrank_mul_finrank_linearMap_of_bijective_lsmul) (TauCeti#19663). It is not yet tied to a Hermitian spinor metric. The even and odd parts are now characterized as [eigenspaces of the grade involution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Grading.html#CliffordAlgebra.involute_eq_self_iff), [compatibly with base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/BaseChange.html#CliffordAlgebra.toBaseChange_mem_baseChange_evenOdd_iff). For G1.4, the equivalence Cl(V) ≃ Cl⁰(V ⊕ ℝ) now has its element dictionary: [odd x goes to e₀x](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Even/EquivEven.html#CliffordAlgebra.coe_toEven_of_mem_odd), and the [grade involution becomes conjugation by e₀](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Even/EquivEven.html#CliffordAlgebra.coe_toEven_involute). The odd-dimensional factors labelled by ω_ℂ are still to come.
