## SpinGeometry: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`9bb1cd0` to `c1c6773`)

The first work on this roadmap is all algebra in its opening layer, G1, setting up the sign conventions that geometric Clifford multiplication will need. The compact group Spin(n) is built from the positive-definite form, but geometric Clifford multiplication uses the negative one, where c(v)² = −|v|². A [group isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Neg.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinGroupEquivNeg) between the Spin groups of Q and −Q now links the two, together with its [real-signature version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Neg.html#CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinGroupEquivNeg) from Spin(p,q) to Spin(q,p). It goes through the even subalgebras, sends a bivector to minus the corresponding bivector, preserves the vector actions and sends −1 to −1 (TauCeti#18313).

The other main thread is the Clifford scalar trace τ, [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace) as the degree-zero part under the exterior-algebra identification. It is [identified with the normalized regular trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.trace_mulLeft_eq_two_pow_mul_scalarTrace) 2^(−dim V)·tr(L_a) and shown to be cyclic, invariant under involute, reverse and algebra isomorphisms, and compatible with base change. For an orthogonal basis, the ordered products of basis vectors [are the Clifford basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/OrthogonalBasis.html#CliffordAlgebra.basis_eq_prod_map_sort). The pairing τ(reverse x · y) is diagonal in that basis, which gives [positivity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/OrthogonalBasis.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_reverse_mul_self_nonneg) and [faithfulness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/OrthogonalBasis.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_reverse_mul_self_eq_zero_iff) over ordered rings. On the complexified algebra, coefficient [conjugation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Complexification.html#CliffordAlgebra.complexConj) is characterized by fixing the real vectors, and its composite with Clifford conjugation gives the conjugate-linear [involution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Complexification.html#CliffordAlgebra.conjStar) that makes c(v) skew-Hermitian. That involution is matched with the tensor-product star. Positivity is not yet stated for complex coefficients, and the semidefinite null-ideal characterization has not landed.

## SpinGeometry: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`c1c6773` to `93d1fe5`)

The main result of this window is the Fock model of G1.2. Take a real quadratic space with a compatible complex structure J. The (1,0) and (0,1) parts of vectors span two isotropic eigenspaces, and these now give a [spin polarization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/ComplexStructure.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.spinPolarizationData). The [√2-normalized Fock action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Fock.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.fockAction) is defined by conjugating the native spinor action with the scaling by 2^(p/2) on Λ^pW, and the [intertwining equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Fock.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.degreeScaling_comp_spinAction_eq_fockAction_comp) is proved. A real vector acts by [√2 times creation plus a √2/2-weighted contraction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Fock.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.fockAction_ι_one_tmul), and this action satisfies the Clifford relation. On the metric side, the [sesquilinear complexification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/BilinearForm/Complexification.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.sesqComplexification) of g gives [|v^(1,0)|² = |v|²/2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Complex/HermitianForm.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.sesqComplexification_holomorphicPart_self). The Hermitian form h_J is [twice its pullback](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Complex/HermitianForm.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.Invariant.complexAssociatedForm_eq_two_mul_sesqComplexification) along v ↦ v^(1,0). Some of G1.2 has not landed: the creation/annihilation anticommutators, adjointness of wedge and contraction, skew-Hermitian c(v), and the 2^p comparison of Fock metrics.

The Morita evaluation of G1.3 also landed, in a general form. If an algebra acts on S as all of End S, then every module M is [S ⊗ Hom(S, M) by evaluation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Morita/ModuleEnd.html#TauCeti.isotypicTensorEquivOfBijective). The inverse is written in a basis, and the window adds naturality and a [dimension formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Morita/ModuleEnd.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_finrank_mul_finrank_linearMap_of_bijective_lsmul) (TauCeti#19663). It is not yet tied to a Hermitian spinor metric. The even and odd parts are now characterized as [eigenspaces of the grade involution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Grading.html#CliffordAlgebra.involute_eq_self_iff), [compatibly with base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/BaseChange.html#CliffordAlgebra.toBaseChange_mem_baseChange_evenOdd_iff). For G1.4, the equivalence Cl(V) ≃ Cl⁰(V ⊕ ℝ) now has its element dictionary: [odd x goes to e₀x](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Even/EquivEven.html#CliffordAlgebra.coe_toEven_of_mem_odd), and the [grade involution becomes conjugation by e₀](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Even/EquivEven.html#CliffordAlgebra.coe_toEven_involute). The odd-dimensional factors labelled by ω_ℂ are still to come.

## SpinGeometry: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-09 (`93d1fe5` to `7ca783d`)

The headline of this window is the spinor metric of G1.3. In even dimension, every spinor module of the complexified negative-metric Clifford algebra now [carries a positive Hermitian metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Hermitian/Spinor.html#CliffordAlgebra.exists_sesqForm_map_eq_map_conjStar) for which real vectors act skew-Hermitianly, and any two such metrics [differ by a unique positive scalar](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Hermitian/Complex.html#CliffordAlgebra.existsUnique_pos_mul_of_surjective) (TauCeti#24019). The existence proof uses the Fock metric, and the rest of G1.2's metric side was finished along the way: wedge and contraction are adjoint, Clifford multiplication by a real vector is [skew-Hermitian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Fock/Metric.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.toLinearMapₛₗ₂_fockAction_ι_one_tmul_left), and the native metric carries the [factor 2^p in degree p](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Fock/Metric.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.toLinearMapₛₗ₂_degreeScaling_of_mem_exteriorPower). Elsewhere in G1, the complex-coefficient trace is [positive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Positivity.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_conjStar_mul_self_nonneg) and its null set is the [ideal generated by the radical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Positivity.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_conjStar_mul_self_eq_zero_iff_mem_span_radical). The eight-fold classification has been transported to −‖v‖² through an orthonormal coordinate model (for instance [dimension ≡ 0 mod 8](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/RealForm/Orthonormal.html#TauCeti.cliffordAlgebra_classification_of_finrank_mod_eight_eq_zero)). In odd dimension, modules now split into their [two ω-labelled multiplicity spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/Multiplicity.html#CliffordAlgebra.oddVolumeMultiplicityEquiv).

G2 also began, and most of it has landed. The [normalized complex volume element](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/Complex.html#CliffordAlgebra.complexVolume) Γ is frame independent, squares to 1 and is self-adjoint. Every module is the [direct sum of its ±1 eigenspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/Eigenspace.html#CliffordAlgebra.isCompl_eigenspace_complexVolume), and on the Fock module Γ is [exterior parity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Chirality.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.fockAction_complexVolume). On forms, a vector acts by [ε(v) − ι(v)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/ExteriorAlgebra/CliffordAction.html#CliffordAlgebra.exteriorAction_ι) and Γ acts by [i^(ℓ+k(k−1)) times the Hodge star](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/HodgeStar.html#CliffordAlgebra.baseChangeExteriorAction_complexVolume_of_eq_two_mul). There is also a [graded tensor product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Basic.html#AlgHom.cliffordProdTensor) of Clifford modules, with associativity and the [Koszul-signed symmetry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Comm.html#AlgHom.koszulComm_cliffordProdTensor).

Toward G4.1, the Spin projection along an orthonormal frame is a [covering map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Frame.html#OrthonormalBasis.isCoveringMap_realCliffordSpinFrameProjection) and a [local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LocalDiffeomorph.html#TauCeti.CliffordAlgebra.isLocalDiffeomorph_realCliffordSpinToSpecialOrthogonalSmoothRange). Spin(2) → SO(2) is [the squaring map of the circle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Two.html#CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpecialOrthogonalTwoContinuousMulEquivCircle_spinToSpecialOrthogonal), and the ranks zero and one are worked out. All of this is still fibrewise: no Clifford bundle, spin structure or Dirac operator has landed yet.

## SpinGeometry: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`7ca783d` to `a6027b1`)

The main result of this window completes the restriction half of Layer 1's odd-dimensional comparisons (G1.4). In dimension n = 2ℓ+1 the map c(v) ↦ c(e₀)c(v) sends the Clifford algebra of V into the even part of that of V ⊕ ℝ, so it acts on each half-spinor space of V ⊕ ℝ. The [odd spinor modules are exactly these restricted half-spinors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/HalfSpinRestriction.html#TauCeti.SpinPolarizationData.exists_linearEquiv_eigenspaceComplexVolume): with V ⊕ ℝ oriented by (e₁, …, eₙ, e₀), the module on which the central volume element ω_ℂ acts by +1 is the restriction of S⁻, and the one with ω_ℂ = −1 is the restriction of S⁺. The intertwiners are unique up to a scalar, and [putting e₀ first swaps the labels](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/HalfSpinRestriction.html#TauCeti.SpinPolarizationData.exists_linearEquiv_eigenspaceComplexVolume_of_cons) (TauCeti#33785). The proof compares even parts. Two general facts landed with it: a [Schur lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/End/Intertwiner.html#AlgHom.exists_eq_smul_of_apply_eq_apply), saying that every intertwiner out of a representation onto a full endomorphism algebra is a scalar multiple of a given isomorphism, and the fact that such a representation is [determined by its dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/End/Intertwiner.html#AlgEquiv.exists_linearEquiv_apply_eq_apply).

In G2.3 the graded tensor product of Clifford modules is now matched with Mathlib's description of the Clifford algebra of an orthogonal sum as a graded tensor product of the two algebras. Under `CliffordAlgebra.prodEquiv`, a pure tensor a ⊗ b with b of parity j [acts on E ⊗ F by ρ₁(a)γʲ ⊗ ρ₂(b)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Basic.html#AlgHom.cliffordProdTensor_toProd_tmul), which is the Koszul-signed action. The other two pull requests rework existing statements about the positivity and faithfulness of the scalar trace and add nothing new. The unit, orientation-reversal and half-spin laws of the graded product are still open, as are Layer 1's Pauli and Gram-determinant comparisons. Nothing on bundles has begun.
