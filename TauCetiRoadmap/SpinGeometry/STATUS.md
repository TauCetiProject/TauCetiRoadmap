### Named results

- **Positive Hermitian spinor metrics**: in even dimension, every spinor module of the complexified negative-metric Clifford algebra [carries a positive Hermitian metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Hermitian/Spinor.html#CliffordAlgebra.exists_sesqForm_map_eq_map_conjStar) that makes it a *-representation, [unique up to a positive scalar](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Hermitian/Complex.html#CliffordAlgebra.existsUnique_pos_mul_of_surjective).
- **The chirality decomposition**: every module is the [sum of the ±1 eigenspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/Eigenspace.html#CliffordAlgebra.isCompl_eigenspace_complexVolume) of the normalized volume element Γ. For a real action in the geometric convention, Γ acts by [i^⌊(n+1)/2⌋ c(e₁)⋯c(eₙ)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/RealAction.html#CliffordAlgebra.liftBaseChange_complexVolume_eq) in any positive orthonormal frame, and the half-spinors are [orthogonal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/RealAction.html#AlgHom.IsGeometricClifford.isOrtho_eigenspace_liftBaseChange_complexVolume).
- **Odd spinors as restricted half-spinors**: in dimension 2ℓ+1, the odd spinor module with ω_ℂ = ±1 is [the restriction of the ∓ half-spinors of V ⊕ ℝ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/HalfSpinRestriction.html#TauCeti.SpinPolarizationData.exists_linearEquiv_eigenspaceComplexVolume), uniquely up to a scalar.
- **Forms as endomorphisms of spinors**: complexified forms are [identified with End(S)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/SpinorForms.html#AlgEquiv.cliffordFormsEquivEnd) and with S ⊗ S*, intertwining the exterior Clifford action with left multiplication; exterior parity [becomes conjugation by Γ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/SpinorForms.html#AlgEquiv.cliffordFormsEquivEnd_involute).
- **The frame Spin cover**: along any orthonormal frame, Spin(n) → SO(n) is a [covering map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Frame.html#OrthonormalBasis.isCoveringMap_realCliffordSpinFrameProjection) and a [local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LocalDiffeomorph.html#TauCeti.CliffordAlgebra.isLocalDiffeomorph_realCliffordSpinToSpecialOrthogonalSmoothRange), with [smooth evenly covered sections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Covering.html#TauCeti.CliffordAlgebra.exists_realCliffordSpinSmoothCoveringTrivialization).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- A [Spin structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure.html#TauCeti.SpinStructure) of rank n ≥ 1 is a principal Spin(n)-bundle with an equivariant frame map. It has a groupoid of [equivalences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure.html#TauCeti.SpinStructure.Equiv) and a [pullback](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure.html#TauCeti.SpinStructure.pullback), which together are the setting for the obstruction and torsor theorems of G4.3–G4.4.
- The [geometric convention](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Hermitian/Geometric.html#AlgHom.IsGeometricClifford) for real Clifford actions requires Q(v) = −‖v‖² and every c(v) to be skew-Hermitian. With the [complex-linear extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/BaseChange.html#CliffordAlgebra.liftBaseChangeEquiv) of real Clifford representations, it gives Γ and S^± in the form that G3–G6 will use on bundles.
- In the [graded tensor product of Clifford modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Basic.html#AlgHom.cliffordProdTensor), (v, w) acts by c(v) ⊗ 1 + γ ⊗ c(w). It is associative and has the [Koszul symmetry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Comm.html#AlgHom.koszulComm_cliffordProdTensor). Direct-sum spin lifts and twisted Dirac operators will be built on it.
