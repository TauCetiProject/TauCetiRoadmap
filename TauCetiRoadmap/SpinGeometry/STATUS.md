## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The fibrewise algebra of Layers 1 and 2 is mostly in place, and so are the topological and smooth frame adapters of G4.1. Each of Layers 1 and 2 still lacks some of the comparisons the README adds on top of Tau Ceti's library. Nothing on bundles has begun: there are no Clifford bundles (Layer 3), no spin lifts beyond G4.1, and no spinor bundles (Layer 5) or Dirac operators (Layer 6).

### Named results

- **Positive Hermitian spinor metrics**: in even dimension, every spinor module of the complexified negative-metric Clifford algebra [carries a positive Hermitian metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Hermitian/Spinor.html#CliffordAlgebra.exists_sesqForm_map_eq_map_conjStar) that makes it a *-representation, [unique up to a positive scalar](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Hermitian/Complex.html#CliffordAlgebra.existsUnique_pos_mul_of_surjective).
- **The chirality decomposition**: the [normalized complex volume element](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/Complex.html#CliffordAlgebra.complexVolume) Γ is frame independent, involutive and self-adjoint. Every module is the [sum of its ±1 eigenspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/Eigenspace.html#CliffordAlgebra.isCompl_eigenspace_complexVolume), and on complex k-forms in dimension 2ℓ, Γ [acts by i^(ℓ+k(k−1)) times the Hodge star](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/HodgeStar.html#CliffordAlgebra.baseChangeExteriorAction_complexVolume_of_eq_two_mul).
- **Odd spinors as restricted half-spinors**: in dimension 2ℓ+1, the odd spinor module with ω_ℂ = ±1 is [the restriction of the ∓ half-spinors of V ⊕ ℝ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/HalfSpinRestriction.html#TauCeti.SpinPolarizationData.exists_linearEquiv_eigenspaceComplexVolume) along c(v) ↦ c(e₀)c(v), uniquely up to a scalar. Putting e₀ first [swaps the labels](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/HalfSpinRestriction.html#TauCeti.SpinPolarizationData.exists_linearEquiv_eigenspaceComplexVolume_of_cons).
- **Positivity and null ideal of the complex trace**: τ_ℂ(a*a) is [nonnegative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Positivity.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_conjStar_mul_self_nonneg) for semidefinite forms and vanishes exactly on the [ideal generated by the radical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Positivity.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_conjStar_mul_self_eq_zero_iff_mem_span_radical).
- **The frame Spin cover**: along any orthonormal frame, Spin(n) → SO(n) is a [covering map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Frame.html#OrthonormalBasis.isCoveringMap_realCliffordSpinFrameProjection) and a [local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LocalDiffeomorph.html#TauCeti.CliffordAlgebra.isLocalDiffeomorph_realCliffordSpinToSpecialOrthogonalSmoothRange). In rank two it is [the squaring map of the circle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Two.html#CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpecialOrthogonalTwoContinuousMulEquivCircle_spinToSpecialOrthogonal).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- In the [graded tensor product of Clifford modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Basic.html#AlgHom.cliffordProdTensor), (v, w) acts by c(v) ⊗ 1 + γ ⊗ c(w). It now has associativity, [Koszul symmetry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Comm.html#AlgHom.koszulComm_cliffordProdTensor), compatibility with reverse duals, and the [comparison with Mathlib's `prodEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Basic.html#AlgHom.cliffordProdTensor_toProd_tmul). Direct-sum spin lifts and twisted Dirac operators will be built on it.
- The [Clifford regular norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Regular/Norm.html#CliffordAlgebra.regularNorm) is invariant under isometries and left Spin multiplication. It is the model fibre norm that the G3.1 algebra bundle needs.
- The [Schur lemma for full endomorphism representations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/End/Intertwiner.html#AlgHom.exists_eq_smul_of_apply_eq_apply) makes Clifford-module isomorphisms unique up to a scalar. This is the uniqueness every "unique up to scalar" identification of spinor modules relies on.

## The frontier

- **Close G2.2**: build the Clifford-module isomorphism Λ*V_ℂ ≃ S ⊗ S* ≃ End(S), show that it is an isometry to the normalized Hilbert–Schmidt metric, and separate the chiral grading Γ_S ⊗ Id from exterior parity.
