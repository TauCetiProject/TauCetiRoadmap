### Named results

- **The chirality decomposition** — the [normalized complex volume element](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/Complex.html#CliffordAlgebra.complexVolume) Γ is frame independent, involutive and self-adjoint. Every module is the [sum of its ±1 eigenspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/Eigenspace.html#CliffordAlgebra.isCompl_eigenspace_complexVolume), and on the Fock module Γ is [exterior parity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Polarization/Chirality.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.fockAction_complexVolume).
- **Chirality on forms is the Hodge star** — on complex forms in dimension 2ℓ, [Γ acts by i^(ℓ+k(k−1)) times ⋆](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/VolumeElement/HodgeStar.html#CliffordAlgebra.baseChangeExteriorAction_complexVolume_of_eq_two_mul) on k-forms.
- **Positivity and null ideal of the complex trace** — τ_ℂ(a*a) is [nonnegative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Positivity.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_conjStar_mul_self_nonneg) for semidefinite forms and vanishes exactly on the [ideal generated by the radical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/ScalarTrace/Positivity.html#CliffordAlgebra.scalarTrace_conjStar_mul_self_eq_zero_iff_mem_span_radical).
- **The frame Spin cover** — along any orthonormal frame, Spin(n) → SO(n) is a [covering map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Frame.html#OrthonormalBasis.isCoveringMap_realCliffordSpinFrameProjection) and a [local diffeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LocalDiffeomorph.html#TauCeti.CliffordAlgebra.isLocalDiffeomorph_realCliffordSpinToSpecialOrthogonalSmoothRange). ### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [graded tensor product of Clifford modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Basic.html#AlgHom.cliffordProdTensor) has (v, w) acting by c(v) ⊗ 1 + γ ⊗ c(w). It has associativity and [Koszul symmetry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Prod/Comm.html#AlgHom.koszulComm_cliffordProdTensor), and Γ_(V⊕W) = Γ_V ⊗ Γ_W. This is what direct-sum spin lifts and twisted Dirac operators will be built on.
- The [Clifford regular norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Regular/Norm.html#CliffordAlgebra.regularNorm) is invariant under isometries, involute, reverse and left Spin multiplication. It is the model norm the G3 algebra bundle needs, and together with the [invariant-tensor criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/EquivariantModule.html#CliffordAlgebra.endValuedTensorEquiv_ι_mem_invariants_iff) for equivariant Clifford actions it prepares the associated-bundle construction.
- The [parity-twisted right action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Regular/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.parityTwistedRightAction) of the opposite-sign Clifford algebra on the regular module is the fibre model of G5.0's external right action. It is Spin-equivariant, anticommutes with the left action and is symmetric for the trace pairing.
