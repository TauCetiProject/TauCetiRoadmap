## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The general-level valence formula is proved, newforms span the cusp forms and are full Hecke eigenforms with an Euler product, and in weight at least two the coefficient field of a newform is a number field. The conductor, the bad-prime theory, Eisenstein spanning, Galois stability, the LMFDB invariants and the trace formula are partial. Weight one is untouched by the integrality argument.

### Named results

- **The general-level valence formula** — on a finite-index subgroup, the stabilizer-weighted interior and cusp orders of a nonzero form sum to `k[SL₂(ℤ):Γ]/12` ([`valence_formula_finiteIndex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Norm/Cusps.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.valence_formula_finiteIndex)).
- **Newform spanning** — every cusp form on `Γ₁(N)` is a combination of level-raised newforms from divisor levels ([`span_levelRaise_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/Decomposition.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.span_levelRaise_eq_top)).
- **The Atkin–Lehner–Li theorem** — at each prime `p ∣ N` a newform is a `Uₚ`-eigenvector with eigenvalue `aₚ`, so it is an eigenform of every `Tₙ` ([`heckeUCuspNat_eq_qExpansion_coeff_smul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/FullEigenform.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.heckeUCuspNat_eq_qExpansion_coeff_smul)).
- **The newform Euler product** — for `Re s > k/2 + 1`, `L(s,f) = ∏ₚ (1 − aₚp^{−s} + χ(p)p^{k−1−2s})^{−1}`, with `χ` extended by zero ([`L_eulerProduct`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/LFunction/EulerProduct.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.L_eulerProduct)).
- **The coefficient field is a number field** — for a newform of weight `k ≥ 2`, `ℚ(aₙ : n)` is a number field, because the integral Hecke algebra on cusp forms is a finite `ℤ`-module and so the coefficients are algebraic integers ([`numberField_CoefficientField`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/NumberField.html#TauCeti.numberField_CoefficientField)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

The [Satake angle](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/Satake.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.satakeAngle) exists only for real `aₚ`, with Ramanujan–Deligne as a hypothesis.

## The frontier

- **Galois stability.** The coefficients are now algebraic, so the semisimple Hecke-algebra correspondence and the conjugate newform `f^σ` can be built. Orbit sizes and rationality of characteristic polynomials follow from these, and Layer 9's charpolys and orbits consume them.
- **Cross-level strong multiplicity one.** Compare newforms of unrelated levels. Uniqueness of the primitive pair needs this.
- **The level-one trace formula.** Evaluate the elliptic, hyperbolic and parabolic contributions against Hurwitz class numbers, which are not yet defined. Then make the period-polynomial isomorphism Hecke-equivariant and isolate `tr(Tₙ | S_k)`.
- **The Eisenstein decomposition.** The constant-term maps at every cusp now exist. What remains is to identify their common kernel with `S_k(N, χ)` and show that the series span the image.
- **The remaining bad-prime cases.** Prove the `p ‖ N` and `p ∣ cond χ` cases of the `aₚ` classification.
