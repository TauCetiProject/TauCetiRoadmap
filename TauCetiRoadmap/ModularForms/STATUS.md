### Roadmap coverage

Layer 0 has diamonds, character spaces, parity, and their decomposition, not Eisenstein series with character; Layer 1 is done. Layer 2 has the abstract and `Γ₀(N)` Hecke rings, uniform classical `Tₙ`, the full multiplication table, and the character-space action with its prime-power coefficient formulas; the composite identification with `Tₙ`, the general-rank presentation, and the local–global hand-off are not established. Layer 3 has the Petersson pairing, character orthogonality, old/new complements, old-part stability under good `Tₚ`, and the slash adjoint in involution form, not the adjoint formula `Tₙ* = ⟨n⟩⁻¹Tₙ`, normality, simultaneous diagonalization, the exact conductor-indexed oldspace, or bad-prime newspace stability. Layers 4–5 have good eigenforms, newforms, the per-character Main Lemma, multiplicity one, and fixed-space strong multiplicity one, not the conductor decomposition, full eigenforms, bad-prime eigenvalues, cross-level strong multiplicity one, or the newforms as an orthogonal basis. Layer 6 has the normalized Fricke and Atkin–Lehner involutions with their composition law and eigenspace splittings, not commutation with `Tₙ` or newform signs. Layer 7 has analytic continuation and abscissa bounds, not the Euler product or functional equation. Layers 8, 8G, 9, and 11 are untouched; Layer 10 has only Sturm-bound preliminaries.

## The frontier

- **Petersson adjoints.** Prove `Tₙ* = ⟨n⟩⁻¹Tₙ` at good indices from the slash adjoint and double-coset tiling now available, then obtain normality, simultaneous diagonalization, and the newforms as an orthogonal basis of the newspace.
- **The conductor decomposition.** Assemble the level-lowering dichotomy and the Main Lemma into the existence of a primitive pair `(M, g)` for every normalized good Hecke eigenform; uniqueness needs cross-level, newform-against-newform strong multiplicity one, a separate theorem from the fixed-space one now proved.
- **Bad-prime newform theory.** Establish newspace stability under `Uₚ`, upgrade newforms to full eigenforms, and prove the bad-prime eigenvalue classification; the Euler product of Layer 7 needs this at every prime.
- **Atkin–Lehner signs.** Show the `𝒲_Q` commute with the good `Tₙ` and act on a trivial-nebentypus newform by `ε_Q = ±1`, the signs multiplying to the Fricke eigenvalue.
- **The Main Lemma at bare `Γ₁(N)`.** Sum the per-character statement over the nebentypus decomposition to reach the roadmap's stated form.
