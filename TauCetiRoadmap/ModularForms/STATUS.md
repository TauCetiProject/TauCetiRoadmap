## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The general-level valence formula and the old/new theory of `S_k(N, χ)` are done. That theory includes stability of both spaces under every `T_n`. Newforms are full Hecke eigenforms with an Euler product, and in weight at least two their coefficient fields are number fields. Eisenstein spanning, the bad-prime eigenvalue cases, Galois stability, the LMFDB invariants, the dimension formulas and the trace formula are partial. The weight-one integrality argument has not begun.

### Named results

- **The general-level valence formula** — on a finite-index subgroup, the stabilizer-weighted interior and cusp orders of a nonzero form sum to `k[SL₂(ℤ):Γ]/12` ([`valence_formula_finiteIndex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Norm/Cusps.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.valence_formula_finiteIndex)).
- **The Atkin–Lehner–Li theorem** — at each prime `p ∣ N` a newform is a `Uₚ`-eigenvector with eigenvalue `aₚ`, so it is an eigenform of every `Tₙ` ([`heckeUCuspNat_eq_qExpansion_coeff_smul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/FullEigenform.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.heckeUCuspNat_eq_qExpansion_coeff_smul)).
- **The newform Euler product** — for `Re s > k/2 + 1`, `L(s,f) = ∏ₚ (1 − aₚp^{−s} + χ(p)p^{k−1−2s})^{−1}`, with `χ` extended by zero ([`L_eulerProduct`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/LFunction/EulerProduct.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.L_eulerProduct)).
- **The coefficient field is a number field** — for a newform of weight `k ≥ 2`, `ℚ(aₙ : n)` is a number field, because the integral Hecke algebra is a finite `ℤ`-module ([`numberField_CoefficientField`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/NumberField.html#TauCeti.numberField_CoefficientField)).
- **The Fricke involution on a newform of any nebentypus** — `𝒲_N f = λ_N(f)·f_ρ`, a multiple of the conjugate newform (Miyake 4.6.15(2)). The pseudo-eigenvalue `λ_N(f)` has modulus one ([`exists_normalizedFrickeOperatorCusp_eq_smul_conj`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/Fricke.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.exists_normalizedFrickeOperatorCusp_eq_smul_conj)).

## The frontier

- **Galois stability.** Give the newspace Hecke algebra its `ℚ(χ)`-structure. Then define `f^σ` by conjugating the newform's character through an embedding. Orbit sizes and rational characteristic polynomials follow from this, and Layer 9 consumes them.
- **The Eisenstein decomposition.** Constant terms at every cusp exist, including for raised series. Identify their common kernel with `S_k(N, χ)` and show the series span the image. This also unlocks the sharp non-cuspidal abscissa.
- **Cross-level strong multiplicity one.** Compare newforms at unrelated levels. The divisor-level case is done, and uniqueness of the primitive pair needs the general case.
- **Atkin–Lehner pseudo-eigenvalues.** Extend `λ_N` to `λ_Q` for proper exact divisors. The conjugation theory and `W_Q` on `Γ₁(N)` are now in place.
- **The level-one trace formula.** Define Hurwitz class numbers, evaluate the remaining conjugacy contributions, and make the period-polynomial isomorphism Hecke-equivariant.
