## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The general-level valence formula and the old/new theory of `S_k(N, χ)` are done; newforms are full eigenforms with an Euler product and, in weight `≥ 2`, number-field coefficients. The cusp–Eisenstein decomposition holds in every weight `k ≥ 2`. Weight one, Atkin–Li unimodularity, Galois stability, the LMFDB invariants, Riemann–Roch, the dimension formulas and the trace formula are partial; weight-one integrality has not begun.

### Named results

- **The general-level valence formula** — on a finite-index subgroup, the stabilizer-weighted interior and cusp orders of a nonzero form sum to `k[SL₂(ℤ):Γ]/12` ([`valence_formula_finiteIndex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Norm/Cusps.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.valence_formula_finiteIndex)).
- **The Atkin–Lehner–Li theorem** — at each prime `p ∣ N` a newform is a `Uₚ`-eigenvector with eigenvalue `aₚ`, so it is an eigenform of every `Tₙ` ([`heckeUCuspNat_eq_qExpansion_coeff_smul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/FullEigenform.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.heckeUCuspNat_eq_qExpansion_coeff_smul)).
- **The newform Euler product** — for `Re s > k/2 + 1`, `L(s,f) = ∏ₚ (1 − aₚp^{−s} + χ(p)p^{k−1−2s})^{−1}` ([`L_eulerProduct`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/LFunction/EulerProduct.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.L_eulerProduct)).
- **The coefficient field is a number field** — for a newform of weight `k ≥ 2`, `ℚ(aₙ : n)` is a number field, because the integral Hecke algebra is a finite `ℤ`-module ([`numberField_CoefficientField`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/NumberField.html#TauCeti.numberField_CoefficientField)).
- **The cusp–Eisenstein decomposition** — the Eisenstein series of nebentypus `χ` span a complement of `S_k(N, χ)` in `M_k(N, χ)` in weight `k ≥ 3` ([`isCompl_eisensteinSubspace_range_cuspToModFormCharSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/EisensteinSeries/Dimension.html#TauCeti.isCompl_eisensteinSubspace_range_cuspToModFormCharSpace)) and, with the corrected `E₂(z) − tE₂(tz)`, in weight two (Diamond–Shurman 4.5.2 and 4.6.2; [`isCompl_weightTwoEisensteinSubspace_range_cuspToModFormCharSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/EisensteinSeries/WeightTwo/Dimension.html#TauCeti.isCompl_weightTwoEisensteinSubspace_range_cuspToModFormCharSpace)).

### Roadmap coverage

- **Done.** Layers 1 and 3.
- **Layer 0** is done in every weight `k ≥ 2`. In weight one the Eisenstein span has the largest dimension a complement of the cusp forms can have, away from levels 1, 2 and 4, but is not yet shown to meet the cusp forms only in zero. **Layer 2** has [Shimura's Theorem 3.20 at every rank](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/HeckeRing/GLn/PolynomialRing/Presentation.html#HeckeRing.GLn.polynomialRingEquiv). It lacks the local–global hand-off: the comparison with the `p`-adic Hecke ring and the inversion of `T(p,…,p)`.
- **Layers 4–5** have the newform basis and fixed-level strong multiplicity one. They lack the `p ‖ N` and `p ∣ cond χ` eigenvalue cases, cross-level strong multiplicity one, and uniqueness of the primitive pair.
- **Layer 6** has the trivial-character signs, the Fricke theorem, the square of `W_Q` and `λ_Q` with its product law; `|λ_Q| = 1` is proved only when the nebentypus is trivial at `Q` or away from `Q`.
- **Layer 7** has the sharp abscissa `≤ k` in weight `≥ 3`, but not in weights one and two, and no signs beyond trivial character.
- **Layer 8** is done in weight `≥ 2` except Hecke commutativity on symbols at general indices; weight one (8W) has not begun. **Layers 8G and 9** have the character field, the complex Hecke correspondence, integral newspace characteristic polynomials, Satake parameters, and Conrey and Galois-orbit labels of characters.
- **Layer 10** has the Sturm bound, the cusp and elliptic counts, the degree of `X₀(N) → X(1)`, and the start of 10B (divisor sheaves, residues, canonical divisors, acyclic discs), but no finiteness of `H¹`, Riemann–Roch or dimension formula. **Layer 11** has the period-polynomial machinery, Popa–Zagier's coset identity and the count of split conjugacy classes, but no Hurwitz class numbers and no assembled formula.

## The frontier

- **The weight-one decomposition.** Show that the weight-one Eisenstein span meets `S₁(N, χ)` only in zero; the dimension count already matches. The sharp abscissa in weights one and two waits on this.
- **Unimodularity of `λ_Q`.** Show that `f` and its transported newform `g` have equal Petersson norm for general nebentypus; that is all that remains of `|λ_Q(f)| = 1`.
- **Galois stability.** Give the newspace Hecke algebra its `ℚ(χ)`-structure, then define `f^σ`. Orbit sizes and rational characteristic polynomials follow, for Layer 9.
- **Riemann–Roch on `X(Γ)`.** Prove `dim H¹(X, 𝒪_D) < ∞` (compact Bergman restriction maps are in place), then Riemann–Roch along the point-evaluation sequence and Serre duality; the dimension formulas need these and the genus comparison.
- **The level-one trace formula.** Define Hurwitz class numbers, evaluate the remaining conjugacy contributions, and make the period-polynomial isomorphism Hecke-equivariant.
