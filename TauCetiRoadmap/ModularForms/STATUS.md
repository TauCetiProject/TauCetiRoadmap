## The frontier

- **The full Hecke action.** Upgrade the current linear extensions and special multiplication identities to the ring homomorphism on each character space, with the arbitrary-index coefficient formula, coprime multiplicativity, and prime-power recurrence.
- **Petersson adjoints and normality.** Prove `T_n* = ⟨n⟩⁻¹T_n` away from the level and derive simultaneous diagonalization; this depends on completing the uniform character-space action.
- **The Atkin–Lehner Main Lemma.** Build from the new coprime-index filter to the theorem that cusp forms whose coprime coefficients vanish are old, then construct newforms and the existence half of the primitive-level decomposition.
- **Normalized Atkin–Lehner operators.** Normalize the Fricke operator, extend it to every exact divisor, and prove the newform sign or pseudo-eigenvalue statements; the present result only transports `χ` to `χ⁻¹`.
- **Eisenstein series with character.** Construct the parity-correct series, including the exceptional weights, and prove the cusp–Eisenstein decomposition needed for the sharp noncuspidal coefficient bound.
