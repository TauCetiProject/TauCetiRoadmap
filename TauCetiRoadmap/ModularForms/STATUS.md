## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The general-level valence formula is proved, newforms span the cusp forms and are full Hecke eigenforms with an Euler product, and the Eichler–Shimura period map is built and Hecke-equivariant. The conductor, the bad-prime theory, Eisenstein spanning, the period map's injectivity, Galois stability and the trace formula are partial, and the LMFDB invariant layer has not begun.

### Named results

- **The general-level valence formula** — on a finite-index subgroup, the stabilizer-weighted interior and cusp orders of a nonzero form sum to `k[SL₂(ℤ):Γ]/12` ([`valence_formula_finiteIndex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Norm/Cusps.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.valence_formula_finiteIndex)).
- **Newform spanning** — every cusp form on `Γ₁(N)` is a combination of level-raised newforms from divisor levels ([`span_levelRaise_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/Decomposition.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.span_levelRaise_eq_top)).
- **The Atkin–Lehner–Li theorem** — at each prime `p ∣ N` a newform is a `Uₚ`-eigenvector with eigenvalue `aₚ`, so it is an eigenform of every `Tₙ` ([`heckeUCuspNat_eq_qExpansion_coeff_smul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/FullEigenform.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.heckeUCuspNat_eq_qExpansion_coeff_smul)).
- **The newform Euler product** — for `Re s > k/2 + 1`, `L(s,f) = ∏ₚ (1 − aₚp^{−s} + χ(p)p^{k−1−2s})^{−1}`, with `χ` extended by zero ([`L_eulerProduct`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/LFunction/EulerProduct.html#HeckeRing.GL2.Newform.L_eulerProduct)).
- **Hecke equivariance of the period map** — the functional `{α, β} ⊗ P ↦ ∫_β^α f(z) P(z, 1) dz` that a cusp form `f` defines on modular symbols turns `Tₙ` on forms of level `Γ₁(N)` into the transpose of `Tₙ` on symbols ([`periodMap_heckeTCuspNat`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ModularSymbols/Period/Hecke.html#TauCeti.ModularSymbols.periodMap_heckeTCuspNat)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [character field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/Fields.html#TauCeti.CharacterField) of the nebentypus [lies inside the coefficient field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/Fields.html#TauCeti.CharacterField_le_CoefficientField) of a newform, since `χ(p) = (a_p² − a_{p²})/p^{k−1}` at good primes. ## The frontier

- **The coefficient field is a number field.** Prove the period map injective by the Eichler-integral route: Bol's identity, boundedness of the Eichler integral at every cusp, and the lemma that a bounded holomorphic invariant function of weight `≤ 0` is constant. Hecke commutativity on symbols is the other input to the transfer. This is for weight `≥ 2`; weight one is a separate sublayer.
- **Cross-level strong multiplicity one.** Compare newforms of unrelated levels. Uniqueness of the primitive pair needs it, and so does Layer 8G's conjugate newform `f^σ`, which is defined by uniqueness.
- **The remaining bad-prime cases.** Prove the `p ‖ N` and `p ∣ cond χ` cases of the `aₚ` classification.
- **The Eisenstein decomposition.** Show that the defined Eisenstein subspace is a complement to the cusp forms in `M_k(N, χ)`.
- **The level-one trace formula.** Sum the upper-triangular contributions, evaluate the remaining conjugacy types, the elliptic ones against Hurwitz class numbers, and transfer from binary forms to `S_k` by the dimension count.
