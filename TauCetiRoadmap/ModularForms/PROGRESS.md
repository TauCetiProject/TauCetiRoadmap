## ModularForms: 2026-08-03 to 2026-08-10 (`5b40eb9` to `5c3a971`)

The Petersson inner product is now a genuine positive-definite Hermitian pairing at every finite level: a sum, over the cosets of `Γ·{±I}` in `SL₂(ℤ)`, of level-one fundamental-domain integrals ([`peterssonInnerCosets`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Petersson/FiniteIndex.html#CuspForm.peterssonInnerCosets)), definite because every coset summand is a nonnegative real ([`peterssonInnerCosets_definite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Petersson/FiniteIndex.html#CuspForm.peterssonInnerCosets_definite)). That buys the orthogonal-complement calculus Layer 3 was waiting on, and with it the old/new decomposition: the oldspace spanned by the level-raising images from proper divisor levels, the newspace its Petersson complement, the two meeting only in `0` and spanning `S_k(Γ₁(N))` ([`sup_cuspFormsOld_cuspFormsNew_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/Basic.html#TauCeti.sup_cuspFormsOld_cuspFormsNew_eq_top), TauCeti#2475). The degeneracy operators `V_d` came first, with `aₙ(V_d f) = a_{n/d}(f)` and their nebentypus transport. The fixed-character refinement of the oldspace is not there, and neither is any Hecke operator acting on forms, so the adjoint `Tₙ* = ⟨n⟩⁻¹Tₙ` and `Tₙ`-stability remain open.

Layer 0's diamond and nebentypus half is done: the diamond operators, the character spaces as their joint eigenspaces with the classical transformation law proved rather than assumed ([`mem_modFormCharSpace_iff_nebentypus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/DiamondOperators.html#mem_modFormCharSpace_iff_nebentypus)), the internal direct sum over characters ([`isInternal_modFormCharSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/CharacterDecomp.html#isInternal_modFormCharSpace)), the parity lemma, and the promised isomorphism between the two spellings of `M_k(Γ₀(N))`. Eisenstein series with character have not been started.

At level one, `E₄` and `E₆` generate the graded ring freely ([`mvPolynomialEquivModularForms`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/LevelOne/GradedRing.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.mvPolynomialEquivModularForms)), and the valence-formula contour has its generalized winding numbers: `-1` inside the truncated domain, `-1/2` at `i`, `-1/6` at each of `ρ` and `ρ+1` ([`windingNumber_fdBoundary_I`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/LevelOne/FundamentalDomainBoundary/Winding/I/Value.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.windingNumber_fdBoundary_I)), with the excised assembly of `∮ f′/f` in which the verticals cancel, the arc collapses to the elliptic weight and the ceiling reads the cusp order. The `k/12` identity itself is not yet assembled. Elsewhere: the Sturm bound and finite-dimensionality at finite relative index, the abstract Hecke ring's convolution product with commutativity from the transpose anti-involution at general `n`, and the entire continuation of a cusp form's L-series.
