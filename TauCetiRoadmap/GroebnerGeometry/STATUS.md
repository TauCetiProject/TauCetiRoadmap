## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Gröbner-basis layer (G1) is complete except for compatibility with field extensions. Normality of semigroup algebras is now proved, and flat weight-initial families exist for ideals and for monic SAGBI bases. Semigroup geometry (G2), SAGBI theory (G3) and degenerations (G4) are each partial. The minor algebras (G5) have only their leading-term and pattern foundations, and the explicit calculations (G6) have not begun.

### Named results

- **Buchberger's criterion and completion** ([`isGroebnerBasis_span_iff_isRemainder_sPolynomial_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Buchberger.html#MonomialOrder.isGroebnerBasis_span_iff_isRemainder_sPolynomial_zero)) — a set with unit leading coefficients is a Gröbner basis exactly when every S-polynomial reduces to zero. Over a field, S-polynomial completion [terminates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/SPolynomialCompletion.html#MonomialOrder.wellFounded_sPolynomialStep) however its steps are chosen.
- **Binomial generation of toric ideals** ([`toricIdeal_eq_span`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ToricIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.toricIdeal_eq_span)) — over any commutative ring the kernel of a monomial map is generated by finitely many binomials, and the quotient is the semigroup algebra.
- **Normality of semigroup algebras** ([`isIntegrallyClosed_iff_isIntegrallyClosed_and_nsmulSaturation_inf_closure_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/IntegrallyClosed.html#TauCeti.AddMonoidAlgebra.isIntegrallyClosed_iff_isIntegrallyClosed_and_nsmulSaturation_inf_closure_eq)) — over a domain `R`, `R[S]` is integrally closed exactly when `R` is and `S` is saturated in the group it generates. The algebra of the saturation is [the integral closure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/IntegrallyClosed.html#TauCeti.AddMonoidAlgebra.isIntegralClosure_nsmulSaturation_inf_closure_fractionRing) of `R[S]` in its fraction field.
- **The SAGBI criterion** ([`isSAGBIBasis_range_iff_forall_exists_aeval_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/SAGBI/Criterion.html#MonomialOrder.isSAGBIBasis_range_iff_forall_exists_aeval_eq)) — a monic family is a SAGBI basis exactly when each binomial relation among its leading terms has a strictly bounded zero subduction. The lifted relations then present the subalgebra.
- **The flat weight-initial degeneration** ([`flat_quotient_weightReesIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Initial/Degeneration.html#Ideal.flat_quotient_weightReesIdeal)) — over a field, every ideal is the fibre at `t = 1` of a flat family over `k[t]` whose fibre at `t = 0` is [its weight-initial ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Initial/Degeneration.html#Ideal.map_constantCoeff_weightReesIdeal).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The SAGBI Rees family** ([`IsSAGBIBasis.exists_pos_weight_range_weightReesAeval`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/SAGBI/Weight.html#MonomialOrder.IsSAGBIBasis.exists_pos_weight_range_weightReesAeval)) — for a finite monic SAGBI basis, positive weights make the Rees presentation the Rees algebra of the induced filtration, which is [flat](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Subalgebra/WeightFiltration.html#Subalgebra.flat_weightReesAlgebra) and degenerates the subalgebra to its initial algebra.
- **Flag-minor exponents as Gelfand–Tsetlin patterns** ([`GTPattern.flagExponentEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/FlagMinor.html#TauCeti.GTPattern.flagExponentEquiv)) — partial column sums identify the monoid generated by the diagonal exponents of the flag minors with the nonnegative patterns.
- **Monomial initial ideals as weight-initial ideals** ([`exists_pos_weight_weightInitialIdeal_eq_span_leadingTerm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Initial/Groebner.html#MonomialOrder.exists_pos_weight_weightInitialIdeal_eq_span_leadingTerm)) — every monomial-order initial ideal is the weight-initial ideal of some positive integral weight, so Gröbner degenerations are instances of weight degenerations.

## The frontier

- **Flag minors form a SAGBI basis** — the leading terms and the isomorphism of their exponent monoid with the nonnegative patterns are proved. What remains is that every leading exponent of the flag-minor algebra lies in that monoid, then standard monomials and the Rees degeneration of §4.5.
- **Plücker relations and straightening** — the exchange relations exist as determinant identities. The Plücker presentation, its `degRevLex` Gröbner basis with incomparable-pair leading terms, and the standard-monomial basis indexed by tableaux are still to be proved. The `SL₂` invariants of §5.5 wait on this.
- **Faces, orbits and Proj of semigroup algebras** — face ideals are prime. Still to come are the orbit decomposition, closures and stabilizers, then Proj, the Hilbert series and the Ehrhart degree formula, which now can use the finite normalization.
- **SAGBI completion and scalar extension** — prove that fair completion terminates when the initial algebra is finitely generated, and carry SAGBI bases along field extensions and renamings.
- **The ideal-adic associated graded ring and projective families** — build `gr_I R` and the extended Rees ring (§4.2), then the relative-Proj families with constant Hilbert functions (§4.6).
