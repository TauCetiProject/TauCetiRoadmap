## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Gröbner-basis layer (G1) is essentially complete. Semigroup presentations (G2) and SAGBI theory (G3) have their central theorems but not their geometric or completion parts. Rees degenerations (G4) have only their first algebraic pieces, and the minor and flag-minor algebras (G5) and explicit calculations (G6) have not begun.

- **The standard-monomial basis** ([`IsGroebnerBasis.basisQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/NormalForm.html#MonomialOrder.IsGroebnerBasis.basisQuotient)) — the monomials outside the leading-term ideal give a basis of the quotient ring, and there is a [graded version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Homogeneous.html#MonomialOrder.IsGroebnerBasis.basisMapWeightedHomogeneousSubmodule) for homogeneous ideals.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Weight realization** ([`exists_pos_weight_lt_iff_lt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/Finsupp/MonomialOrder/Weight.html#MonomialOrder.exists_pos_weight_lt_iff_lt)) — any finite set of comparisons in a monomial order is induced by a positive integral weight. This is the bridge from Gröbner and SAGBI certificates to the one-parameter degenerations in G4.
- **The weight Rees algebra** ([`weightReesAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/WeightFiltration.html#TauCeti.MvPolynomial.weightReesAlgebra)) — for the weight filtration of a polynomial ring it is identified with a polynomial ring in one more variable, with symbol map at `t = 0` and evaluation at `t = 1`. It is the carrier for the flat initial families.
- **The integral semigroup model** ([`affineToricIntegralModelBaseChangeIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/IntegralModel.html#TauCeti.Toric.affineToricIntegralModelBaseChangeIso)) — `Spec ℤ[S]` base-changes to every coefficient ring, and over ℂ to the native affine toric scheme of a dual semigroup.

### Roadmap coverage

**G1** is done apart from executable algorithms that return certificates, which are not established here. That covers the definitions, membership, normal forms, Buchberger's criterion and certified completion, finite and reduced bases, elimination, intersection, saturation and colon ideals, graded bases, coefficient change, graded reverse lexicographic order, weight realization, weight initial ideals and refinements. **G2** is partial. The binomial presentation, its elimination computation, units, group completion, saturation and its integrality, the integral model and the Hilbert-function count have landed. One direction of normality is proved: an integrally closed algebra forces saturation. Its converse, the identification with the integral closure, orbit–face geometry, Proj, Hilbert series, Ehrhart and degree have not. **G3** is partial. Initial algebras, subduction, the SAGBI criterion and the presentation hold for monic families, but the nonmonic transport, completion with termination and the regression examples are not established. **G4** has only the weight filtration, its Rees algebra and weight initial ideals. **G5** and **G6** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Normality iff saturation** — prove that the algebra of a saturated semigroup is integrally closed and equals the integral closure of `k[S]`, and identify saturation with the integral points of the cone.
- **Nonmonic SAGBI transport and completion** — carry the monic criterion and presentation back to an arbitrary family through the rescaling `D_ell`, and prove completion terminates when the initial algebra is finitely generated.
- **Flat initial families** — use weight realization and the weight Rees algebra to build the flat weight-initial family for ideals, then the subalgebra Rees family from SAGBI certificates. Flatness over `k[t]` and the fibre identifications are the substance.
- **Faces, orbits and Proj of semigroup algebras** — the orbit–face bijection, stabilizers and graded Proj with its Hilbert polynomial and degree. These open the G6 examples.
- **Minor and flag-minor algebras** — generic minors, the Plücker presentation and straightening, and the flag-minor SAGBI basis. Nothing has started, and these need the G3 and G4 tools above.
