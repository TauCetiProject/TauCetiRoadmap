## GroebnerGeometry: 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-08 (`a4a3682` to `d2083f0`)

The roadmap's first window built a Gröbner-basis layer on Mathlib's monomial orders. Its centre is [Buchberger's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Buchberger.html#MonomialOrder.isGroebnerBasis_span_iff_isRemainder_sPolynomial_zero) (TauCeti#22030): a set with unit leading coefficients is a Gröbner basis of the ideal it spans exactly when every S-polynomial reduces to zero. Alongside it come [finite Gröbner bases](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Finite.html#MonomialOrder.exists_finset_isGroebnerBasis) over Noetherian coefficients, [the unique reduced monic basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Reduced.html#MonomialOrder.existsUnique_isReducedGroebnerBasis) over a field, and [the standard-monomial basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/NormalForm.html#MonomialOrder.IsGroebnerBasis.basisQuotient) of the quotient with its normal-form map. The usual applications followed: elimination, intersections, [saturation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Colon.html#MonomialOrder.IsGroebnerBasis.comap_map_algebraMap_away) and colon ideals via an extra variable, graded bases for homogeneous ideals, change of coefficients, a [graded reverse lexicographic order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/Finsupp/MonomialOrder/DegRevLex.html#TauCeti.MonomialOrder.degRevLex), and [realization of finitely many comparisons by a positive weight](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/Finsupp/MonomialOrder/Weight.html#MonomialOrder.exists_pos_weight_lt_iff_lt).

For semigroups, [toric ideals are generated by binomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ToricIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.toricIdeal_eq_span) over any commutative ring, and finitely many of them suffice. The quotient is [the semigroup algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ToricIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.quotientToricIdealAlgEquiv), and its [Hilbert function counts semigroup elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ToricIdeal/HilbertFunction.html#TauCeti.finrank_map_weightedHomogeneousSubmodule_toricIdeal) of each degree. Units, group completion as a localization, saturation and an integral model whose complex base change is [the native affine toric scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/IntegralModel.html#TauCeti.Toric.affineToricIntegralModelBaseChangeIso) also landed. The SAGBI layer has initial algebras, subduction certificates, and [the SAGBI criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/SAGBI/Criterion.html#MonomialOrder.isSAGBIBasis_range_iff_forall_exists_aeval_eq) for monic families: bounded zero-subductions of the binomial relations certify a SAGBI basis. It also has [the resulting presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/SAGBI/Presentation.html#MonomialOrder.ker_aeval_eq_span) of the subalgebra.

Degenerations have begun. The [Rees algebra of a weight filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/WeightFiltration.html#TauCeti.MvPolynomial.weightReesAlgebra) is identified with a polynomial ring in one more variable, with its fibres at 0 and 1, and [weight initial ideals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Initial/Ideal.html#Ideal.weightInitialIdeal) and weight refinements of monomial orders landed with it. Flat initial families, minor algebras and the explicit examples have not started.
