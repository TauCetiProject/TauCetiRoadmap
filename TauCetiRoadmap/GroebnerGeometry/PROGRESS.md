## GroebnerGeometry: 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-08 (`a4a3682` to `d2083f0`)

The roadmap's first window built a Gröbner-basis layer on Mathlib's monomial orders. Its centre is [Buchberger's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Buchberger.html#MonomialOrder.isGroebnerBasis_span_iff_isRemainder_sPolynomial_zero) (TauCeti#22030): a set with unit leading coefficients is a Gröbner basis of the ideal it spans exactly when every S-polynomial reduces to zero. Alongside it come [finite Gröbner bases](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Finite.html#MonomialOrder.exists_finset_isGroebnerBasis) over Noetherian coefficients, [the unique reduced monic basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Reduced.html#MonomialOrder.existsUnique_isReducedGroebnerBasis) over a field, and [the standard-monomial basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/NormalForm.html#MonomialOrder.IsGroebnerBasis.basisQuotient) of the quotient with its normal-form map. The usual applications followed: elimination, intersections, [saturation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/Colon.html#MonomialOrder.IsGroebnerBasis.comap_map_algebraMap_away) and colon ideals via an extra variable, graded bases for homogeneous ideals, change of coefficients, a [graded reverse lexicographic order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/Finsupp/MonomialOrder/DegRevLex.html#TauCeti.MonomialOrder.degRevLex), and [realization of finitely many comparisons by a positive weight](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/Finsupp/MonomialOrder/Weight.html#MonomialOrder.exists_pos_weight_lt_iff_lt).

For semigroups, [toric ideals are generated by binomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ToricIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.toricIdeal_eq_span) over any commutative ring, and finitely many of them suffice. The quotient is [the semigroup algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ToricIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.quotientToricIdealAlgEquiv), and its [Hilbert function counts semigroup elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/ToricIdeal/HilbertFunction.html#TauCeti.finrank_map_weightedHomogeneousSubmodule_toricIdeal) of each degree. Units, group completion as a localization, saturation and an integral model whose complex base change is [the native affine toric scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/IntegralModel.html#TauCeti.Toric.affineToricIntegralModelBaseChangeIso) also landed. The SAGBI layer has initial algebras, subduction certificates, and [the SAGBI criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/SAGBI/Criterion.html#MonomialOrder.isSAGBIBasis_range_iff_forall_exists_aeval_eq) for monic families: bounded zero-subductions of the binomial relations certify a SAGBI basis. It also has [the resulting presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/SAGBI/Presentation.html#MonomialOrder.ker_aeval_eq_span) of the subalgebra.

Degenerations have begun. The [Rees algebra of a weight filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/WeightFiltration.html#TauCeti.MvPolynomial.weightReesAlgebra) is identified with a polynomial ring in one more variable, with its fibres at 0 and 1, and [weight initial ideals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Initial/Ideal.html#Ideal.weightInitialIdeal) and weight refinements of monomial orders landed with it. Flat initial families, minor algebras and the explicit examples have not started.

## GroebnerGeometry: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-19 (`d2083f0` to `b235000`)

The normality theorem for semigroup algebras landed. Over an integral domain `R`, the algebra `R[S]` of a submonoid of a torsion-free abelian group is integrally closed exactly when `R` is integrally closed and `S` is saturated in the group it generates. This is [Gilmer's Theorem 12.11](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/IntegrallyClosed.html#TauCeti.AddMonoidAlgebra.isIntegrallyClosed_iff_isIntegrallyClosed_and_nsmulSaturation_inf_closure_eq) (TauCeti#39696). The normalization is identified too: the algebra of the saturation is [the integral closure of `R[S]` in its fraction field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/IntegrallyClosed.html#TauCeti.AddMonoidAlgebra.isIntegralClosure_nsmulSaturation_inf_closure_fractionRing), and it is a finite module when `S` is finitely generated. The proof goes through [Gordan's lemma for the saturation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Submonoid/Finiteness.html#AddSubmonoid.FG.nsmulSaturation) and its description as [the lattice points of a rational cone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Convex/Cone/Saturation.html#PointedCone.latticePoints_hull_rat_image_eq_nsmulSaturation). On the geometric side, `Spec R[S]` is normal under the same conditions, and so is [every toric scheme of a finite fan](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Toric/Algebraic/Fan/Normal.html#TauCeti.Toric.Fan.isNormalScheme_algebraicRealization).

Degenerations now have their first flat families. For an ideal `I` and a weight `w`, the homogenized family over `k[t]` is `t`-saturated and [flat](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Initial/Degeneration.html#Ideal.flat_quotient_weightReesIdeal). Its fibre at `t = 1` is `I`, and at `t = 0` it is [the weight-initial ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Initial/Degeneration.html#Ideal.map_constantCoeff_weightReesIdeal). A finite monic SAGBI basis gets [positive weights](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/SAGBI/Weight.html#MonomialOrder.IsSAGBIBasis.exists_pos_weight_range_weightReesAeval) for which its Rees presentation is the Rees algebra of the induced filtration, with the initial algebra as special fibre, and that Rees algebra is [flat](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Subalgebra/WeightFiltration.html#Subalgebra.flat_weightReesAlgebra). For Gröbner bases, S-polynomial completion [terminates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Groebner/SPolynomialCompletion.html#MonomialOrder.wellFounded_sPolynomialStep) however its steps are chosen.

The minor algebras have begun. [Flag minors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/FlagMinor.html#TauCeti.leadingTerm_rename_toLex_flagMinor), like all sorted minors, have their diagonal as leading term, and their diagonal exponents generate [a monoid isomorphic to the nonnegative Gelfand–Tsetlin patterns](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/FlagMinor.html#TauCeti.GTPattern.flagExponentEquiv). The [exchange relations among minors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Determinant/PluckerRelation.html#Matrix.det_submatrix_mul_det_submatrix_eq_sum) also landed. That the flag minors form a SAGBI basis is not yet proved. Some of this work landed earlier and is recorded here late: the pattern monoid, the diagonal leading terms, prime ideals of faces, and monic normalization with the rescaling that [carries SAGBI presentations to nonmonic families](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/SAGBI/Monic.html#MonomialOrder.ker_aeval_eq_span_image).
