## NumberFieldArithmetic: 2026-08-28 to 2026-09-01 (`5cfdca0` to `8745177`)

The first pieces of Layer 2 are in place. **Relative Frobenius elements exist** ([`NumberField.exists_isArithFrobAt_of_isGalois`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius.html#NumberField.exists_isArithFrobAt_of_isGalois)): for a finite Galois extension `L/K` of number fields and a nonzero prime `Q` of `𝓞 L`, some element of `Gal(L/K)` is an arithmetic Frobenius at `Q`. **Frobenius elements are unique at an unramified prime** ([`NumberField.isArithFrobAt_eq_of_isUnramifiedAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius.html#NumberField.isArithFrobAt_eq_of_isUnramifiedAt)), now as an equality in the Galois group rather than of algebra homomorphisms, with the subsingleton instance ([`NumberField.subsingleton_isArithFrobAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius.html#NumberField.subsingleton_isArithFrobAt)) recorded. Underneath sits a ring-generic group-level uniqueness ([`IsArithFrobAt.eq_of_isUnramifiedAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Frobenius.html#IsArithFrobAt.eq_of_isUnramifiedAt)), stated for a Noetherian ring and a prime containing all zero-divisors; the number-field statement is its specialisation. On top of these is the **Artin symbol** ([`NumberField.artinSymbol`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArtinSymbol.html#NumberField.artinSymbol)), the conjugacy class in `Gal(L/K)` attached to a nonzero prime `𝔭` of `𝓞 K` unramified in `L`, together with the fact that every arithmetic Frobenius at a prime over `𝔭` represents it ([`NumberField.artinSymbol_eq_mk_of_isArithFrobAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArtinSymbol.html#NumberField.artinSymbol_eq_mk_of_isArithFrobAt)).

Layer 1.1's relative splitting criterion ([`NumberField.ncard_primesOver_eq_finrank_iff_of_isGalois`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SplitsCompletely.html#NumberField.ncard_primesOver_eq_finrank_iff_of_isGalois)) is now public: in a Galois extension over an arbitrary Dedekind base, the number of primes over a maximal ideal equals the relative degree exactly when the common ramification index and inertia degree are both `1`. This is the planned change of visibility, not new mathematics.

What is not here: the rest of the Artin symbol's basic API, namely the conjugation formula, `orderOf Frob = f`, the identification with the residue Frobenius, and the `K = ℚ` corollary; all of the functoriality of Layer 2.4; and the decomposition-group form of the splitting criterion, which these declarations do not show. Every other layer is untouched.
