## NumberFieldArithmetic: 2026-08-28 to 2026-09-01 (`5cfdca0` to `8745177`)

The first pieces of Layer 2 are in place. **Relative Frobenius elements exist** ([`NumberField.exists_isArithFrobAt_of_isGalois`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius.html#NumberField.exists_isArithFrobAt_of_isGalois)): for a finite Galois extension `L/K` of number fields and a nonzero prime `Q` of `𝓞 L`, some element of `Gal(L/K)` is an arithmetic Frobenius at `Q`. **Frobenius elements are unique at an unramified prime** ([`NumberField.isArithFrobAt_eq_of_isUnramifiedAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius.html#NumberField.isArithFrobAt_eq_of_isUnramifiedAt)), now as an equality in the Galois group rather than of algebra homomorphisms, with the subsingleton instance ([`NumberField.subsingleton_isArithFrobAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius.html#NumberField.subsingleton_isArithFrobAt)) recorded. Underneath sits a ring-generic group-level uniqueness ([`IsArithFrobAt.eq_of_isUnramifiedAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Frobenius.html#IsArithFrobAt.eq_of_isUnramifiedAt)), stated for a Noetherian ring and a prime containing all zero-divisors; the number-field statement is its specialisation. On top of these is the **Artin symbol** ([`NumberField.artinSymbol`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArtinSymbol.html#NumberField.artinSymbol)), the conjugacy class in `Gal(L/K)` attached to a nonzero prime `𝔭` of `𝓞 K` unramified in `L`, together with the fact that every arithmetic Frobenius at a prime over `𝔭` represents it ([`NumberField.artinSymbol_eq_mk_of_isArithFrobAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArtinSymbol.html#NumberField.artinSymbol_eq_mk_of_isArithFrobAt)).

Layer 1.1's relative splitting criterion ([`NumberField.ncard_primesOver_eq_finrank_iff_of_isGalois`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SplitsCompletely.html#NumberField.ncard_primesOver_eq_finrank_iff_of_isGalois)) is now public: in a Galois extension over an arbitrary Dedekind base, the number of primes over a maximal ideal equals the relative degree exactly when the common ramification index and inertia degree are both `1`. This is the planned change of visibility, not new mathematics.

What is not here: the rest of the Artin symbol's basic API, namely the conjugation formula, `orderOf Frob = f`, the identification with the residue Frobenius, and the `K = ℚ` corollary; all of the functoriality of Layer 2.4; and the decomposition-group form of the splitting criterion, which these declarations do not show. Every other layer is untouched.

## NumberFieldArithmetic: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-18 (`8745177` to `ecb4a7b`)

Dedekind's theorem has landed: the multiset of degrees of the monic irreducible factors of `minpoly ℤ θ` modulo `p` is the cycle type of a Frobenius at a prime above `p` acting on the roots of `minpoly ℚ θ`, with one part `1` restored for each fixed root ([`factorizationType_eq_cycleType_isArithFrobAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/DedekindTheorem.html#TauCeti.NumberField.factorizationType_eq_cycleType_isArithFrobAt), TauCeti#7269). Two inputs arrived with it: Dedekind's criterion, which decides from the factorization of the minimal polynomial modulo `p` whether `p` divides the index `[𝓞 K : ℤ[θ]]` ([`not_dvd_index_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Index/DedekindCriterion.html#TauCeti.NumberField.IntegralPrimitiveElement.not_dvd_index_iff)), and the instance recognising the splitting field of a rational polynomial as a number field. The polynomial-side corollary for arbitrary monic `f`, which downstream Galois-group work consumes, is not here.

The theory of the order `ℤ[θ]` inside `𝓞 K` grew around it: the index as a definition, the index formula relating the discriminant of `minpoly ℤ θ` to that of `K` ([`discr_minpoly_eq_index_sq_mul_discr`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Index/Discriminant.html#TauCeti.NumberField.IntegralPrimitiveElement.discr_minpoly_eq_index_sq_mul_discr)), and the agreement between the prime divisors of the index and of the conductor exponent. On top sit monogenicity, with quadratic and cyclotomic fields shown monogenic ([`isMonogenic_of_quadratic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Monogenic.html#TauCeti.NumberField.isMonogenic_of_quadratic)), and common index divisors, where having more primes of residue degree `d` than there are monic irreducibles of that degree over `𝔽ₚ` rules monogenicity out ([`isCommonIndexDivisor_of_ncard_lt_ncard`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Index/CommonIndexDivisor.html#TauCeti.NumberField.isCommonIndexDivisor_of_ncard_lt_ncard), TauCeti#6955).

Elsewhere: the relative discriminant ideal became a real object, the relative norm of the different, with transitivity in towers, localization, and the criterion that a prime divides it exactly when some prime above it ramifies ([`dvd_relDiscr_iff_exists_not_isUnramifiedAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/Discriminant/Ramification.html#TauCeti.dvd_relDiscr_iff_exists_not_isUnramifiedAt)); Stickelberger's congruence, that a field discriminant is `0` or `1` modulo `4`, came out of the Leibniz expansion of an embedding determinant ([`discr_emod_four_eq_zero_or_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Discriminant/Stickelberger.html#TauCeti.NumberField.discr_emod_four_eq_zero_or_one)); the splitting dictionary gained the double-coset law, indexing the primes of a non-Galois subfield by `H \ G / D` ([`doubleCosetQuotientEquivPrimesOver`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/RamificationInertia/DoubleCoset/Basic.html#Ideal.doubleCosetQuotientEquivPrimesOver)); and adic completions are now nonarchimedean local fields carrying a canonical map `K_v → L_w`.
