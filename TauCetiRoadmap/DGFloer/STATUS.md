### Named results

- **The cubical–simplicial comparison**: normalized cubical singular chains are naturally chain homotopy equivalent to simplicial ones, so [cubical homology is singular homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Comparison/Basic.html#TauCeti.singularCubicalHomologyNatIso). The equivalence extends to local coefficients and to pairs, where it gives the [relative Künneth theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Comparison/Relative/Kunneth.html#TopPair.isIso_cubicalHomologyKunneth).
- **Hofer–Zehnder–Struwe almost existence** (Proposition 6.3): if a region has finite `π₁`-sensitive Hofer–Zehnder capacity, almost every regular level inside it carries a contractible closed characteristic.
- **Lemma 3.41**: [a path-connected space is aspherical exactly when its double loop space has the homology of a point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/LoopSpace/Aspherical.html#TauCeti.MooreLoopSpace.isAspherical_iff_double_singularHomology).
- **Proposition 6.21**: the explicit double loops of Example 6.20 [generate the homology of `Ω²Sⁿ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Sphere/DoubleTangentCircle.html#TauCeti.isIso_singularHomologyFunctor_map_tangentCircleDoubleLoop).
- **Lemma 7.6**: a sphere meeting the core of a framed tube once, where its Pontryagin–Thom collapse is a local homeomorphism, [induces an injection on the homology of double loop spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PontryaginThom/DoubleLoop.html#TauCeti.IsFramedTube.injective_singularHomologyMap_map_map).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The chain algebra of Moore loops** [`loopChainAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGLocalSystem/LoopAlgebra.html#TauCeti.loopChainAlgebra): strict Moore concatenation makes `C_*(Ω_b B)` a DG algebra with no Eilenberg–Zilber correction, so DG local systems are simply right modules over it.
- **Twisting chain systems** [`TwistingChainSystem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/MonoidAlgebra/TwistingChainSystem.html#TauCeti.TwistingChainSystem): chains on abstract moduli spaces satisfying the twisting equation. Representing systems exist under a gluing condition and are [unique up to homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/MonoidAlgebra/ChainSystemHomotopy.html#TauCeti.TwistingChainSystem.IsRepresenting.nonempty_homotopy).
- **The Serre spectral sequence over an arbitrary base** [`arbitraryBaseSerreSpectralSequence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Fibration/ArbitraryBase.html#TopCat.Hom.arbitraryBaseSerreSpectralSequence): pulled back along a CW approximation, first-quadrant and [strongly convergent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Fibration/ArbitraryBase.html#TauCeti.TopCat.arbitraryBaseSerreSpectralSequencePageXIsoGraded) with no hypothesis on the base.
