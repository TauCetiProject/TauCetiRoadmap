## DGFloer: 2026-10-09 to 2026-10-19 (`0cd818b` to `a5cde7b`)

The cubical foundation is in place. Normalized cubical singular homology is now [naturally isomorphic to singular homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Comparison/Basic.html#TauCeti.singularCubicalHomologyNatIso), through an acyclic-models comparison that also covers relative chains, [excision](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Comparison/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_homologyMap_cubicalChainComplexMap_excisionMap) and local coefficients. The cubical cross product is strictly associative, satisfies [Eilenberg–Zilber](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Kunneth.html#TauCeti.quasiIso_cubicalCrossProduct), and gives the [Künneth theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Kunneth.html#TauCeti.isIso_cubicalHomologyKunneth). It also makes the chains on Moore loops a strict [DG algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGLocalSystem/LoopAlgebra.html#TauCeti.loopChainAlgebra). Alongside came [Hurewicz in degree one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/One/Basic.html#TauCeti.HurewiczOne.hurewiczAddEquiv), the computation [H₁(Ω²S³) ≅ ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Sphere/HomotopyGroup.html#TauCeti.MooreLoopSpace.doubleFirstHomologySphereAddEquiv), and the fact that [weak homotopy equivalences induce isomorphisms on singular homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Homotopy/WeakHomotopyEquivalence.html#ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.isIso_singularHomologyMap). The fundamental chains of manifolds with corners (Layer 0's last item) have not started, and neither has CW approximation.

DG local systems now exist as right modules over the loop algebra. A map has its [DG local system](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGLocalSystem/Fibration.html#TauCeti.fibrationLocalSystem) built from the Moore-path replacement, and when a transitive lifting function is given, [transport is a quasi-isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGLocalSystem/Transport.html#TauCeti.isQuasiIso_transportLocalSystemHom). [Classical local systems are exactly the DG local systems concentrated in degree zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGLocalSystem/Classical/EssImage.html#TauCeti.classicalDGLocalSystemEquivalence). On the algebraic side, twisted complexes come with continuation and parametrized cocycles, and [homologous cocycles give homotopy-equivalent complexes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Twisted/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.Homologous.homotopyEquiv). They also carry an index spectral sequence, built on a new spectral sequence of filtered complexes with a [comparison theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Filtered/Comparison.html#CochainComplex.isIso_homologyMap_of_isIso_spectralSequence_page).

The later layers got first steps: Serre classes of torsion and power-torsion groups, the first stage of the mod-2 vanishing argument, quaternionic projective space as a manifold, and [time-dependent Hamiltonian flows](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Hamiltonian/TimeDependent.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.timeDependentHamiltonianFlow), so far only on symplectic vector spaces with a global Lipschitz hypothesis.

## DGFloer: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25 (`a5cde7b` to `69cd1ab`)

Two named results landed. Every path-connected space now has a [CW approximation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/CWApproximation/Basic.html#TauCeti.isWeakHomotopyEquivalence_cwApproximationMap) (Hatcher, Proposition 4.13), built as a tower of cell attachments, and [weak homotopy equivalences induce isomorphisms on homology with local coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Homotopy/WeakHomotopyEquivalence.html#ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.isIso_twistedHomologyMap). The approximation is not yet natural, and the Serre spectral sequence over an arbitrary base that the roadmap wants from it is still missing. The second result is Lemma 3.41 of Barraud–Damian–Humilière–Oancea: [a path-connected space is aspherical exactly when its double loop space has the homology of a point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/LoopSpace/Aspherical.html#TauCeti.MooreLoopSpace.isAspherical_iff_double_singularHomology). It is proved for both the Moore and the fixed-interval loop models, through an inductive Hurewicz vanishing criterion (TauCeti#18106). This is the first milestone of Layer 10, the first application layer to start.

On the algebraic side, the [second page of the index spectral sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Twisted/SpectralSequence/E2.html#TauCeti.TwistingCocycle.indexSpectralSequenceSecondPageXIso) is now identified with the homology of the classical complex, built from `H(𝓕)` and the degree-zero entries of the cocycle. That leaves only the Brown universal cocycle in Layer 4. DG local systems gained a [representation of the fundamental group on their homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGLocalSystem/Homology.html#TauCeti.IsDGRightModule.cohomologyRepresentation), and with it [`H(𝓕 ⊗ L) ≅ H(𝓕) ⊗ L`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/DGLocalSystem/RankOne.html#TauCeti.RankOneLocalSystem.cohomologyRepresentationEquivTprod) for a rank-one system `L`. Cubical and simplicial chains with local coefficients are now [chain homotopy equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/LocalCoefficients/Comparison/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem.twistedCubicalSingularHomotopyEquiv), and a Hurewicz fibration with a lifting function now has its [local system of fibre homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Fibration/LocalSystem.html#TauCeti.fiberLocalSystem).

Layer 6 reached manifolds. The [unit cosphere bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Codisc.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.cosphereBundle) of a Riemannian manifold is a smooth hypersurface of `T*M`, compact when `M` is, and the radial field `p ∂_p` is a [Liouville vector field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/EulerField.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isLiouvilleVectorField_vectorBundleEulerField), which makes `S*M` [of restricted contact type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/ContactType.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isRestrictedContactType_cosphereBundle). Periodic trajectories of time-periodic fields now [correspond to fixed points of the return map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Flow/Periodic.html#TauCeti.periodicSolutionEquivFixedPoints), but only on Banach spaces under a global Lipschitz hypothesis. Liouville domains and the action functional are not yet there.

## DGFloer: 2026-10-25 to 2026-10-26 (`69cd1ab` to `938acbb`)

Liouville domains arrived in Layer 6, and the unit codisc bundle is the first example: for a compact Riemannian manifold `M` without boundary, [`D*M` is a Liouville domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Domain.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isLiouvilleDomain_codiscBundle) in `T*M` for `ω_can` with primitive `−λ_can`, its Liouville field `p ∂_p` pointing strictly outwards along the unit cosphere bundle (TauCeti#21371). This is the roadmap's own formulation of the cotangent milestone in Layer 6, item 3.

The [definition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Liouville/Domain.html#TauCeti.IsLiouvilleDomain) treats a Liouville domain as a compact sublevel set `{f ≤ c}` inside an ambient manifold, with nondegenerate exact form and a Liouville field transverse to the level, rather than as a manifold with boundary in its own right. From it follow that [the boundary is of restricted contact type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Liouville/Domain.html#TauCeti.IsLiouvilleDomain.exists_isRestrictedContactType), with the level as the domain's frontier, and some small facts about sublevel sets crossed outwards by a vector field. Completions, Hamiltonians linear at infinity and exact embeddings, the rest of item 2, have not started.

## DGFloer: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-06 (`938acbb` to `385ec2d`)

Floer theory has started. For a weakly exact symplectic form, [the energy of a Floer cylinder is the drop in the contractible action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/CylinderAction.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.integral_floerEnergyDensity_eq_contractibleLoopAction_sub), so [the action decreases along Floer cylinders](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/CylinderAction.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.contractibleLoopAction_le_contractibleLoopAction) ([TauCeti#28417](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/28417)). Underneath it is the [action functional](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Hamiltonian/Action.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.contractibleAction) itself, measured against smooth caps or against cylinders to a reference loop, and independent of the choice when the form vanishes on spheres or tori. Alongside came [finiteness of nondegenerate 1-periodic orbits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Hamiltonian/PeriodicOrbit.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.finite_periodicSolutions_hamiltonianVectorField) on compact manifolds. The PSS planes have their [perturbation data](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Plane/Basic.html#TauCeti.Floer.PSSPlane.perturbation) with a curvature bound ([TauCeti#27415](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27415)). The Conley–Zehnder index, compactness, transversality and gluing are still missing; only the pointwise linear algebra of the transversality argument is there.

Liouville domains now have [completions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Liouville/Completion.html#TauCeti.IsLiouvilleCompletion), with a radial coordinate on the cylindrical end, [Hamiltonians linear at infinity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Liouville/LinearAtInfinity.html#TauCeti.IsLinearAtInfinity) and [exact codimension-zero embeddings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Liouville/Embedding.html#TauCeti.IsExactLiouvilleEmbedding), and [`T*M` is the completion of `D*M`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/Bundle/Domain.html#TauCeti.CotangentBundle.isLiouvilleCompletion_codiscBundle). On the capacity side came the [π₁-sensitive Hofer–Zehnder capacity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Capacity/HoferZehnder.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.contractibleHZCapacity) and its relative version, the almost existence property, and the monotonicity argument as far as [dense existence of contractible closed characteristics](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Capacity/Levels.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.exists_mem_Ioo_hasContractibleClosedCharacteristic) under a finite capacity. Proposition 6.3, finite capacity implies almost existence, is not yet stated as one theorem. The [Liouville-flow argument](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Liouville/ClosedCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.IsLiouvilleVectorField.exists_hasContractibleClosedCharacteristic_image_flowOf) that turns almost existence into existence on levels swept out by the flow, the step from Theorems A–C to Corollary E, is in ([TauCeti#27444](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27444)).

On the topological side, [CW approximation is natural up to homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/CWApproximation/Sigma.html#TauCeti.comp_sigmaCWApproximationLift_homotopic) for every space, Serre fibrations have their [boundary map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/SerreFibration/Boundary.html#TauCeti.TopCat.fibrationBoundaryAtZeroHom) and their [exact homotopy sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/SerreFibration/Exact.html#TauCeti.TopCat.mem_range_fibrationBoundary_iff) down to `π₀` ([TauCeti#26266](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26266)), locally trivial bundles over paracompact bases are [Hurewicz fibrations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/HurewiczFibration/LocallyTrivial.html#FiberBundle.isHurewiczFibration_proj) ([TauCeti#27365](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27365)), an `(n − 1)`-connected space gives a [`K(πₙ, n)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/KillHomotopyGroups.html#TauCeti.isEilenbergMacLaneSpace_killHomotopyGroups) ([TauCeti#27607](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27607)), framed compact submanifolds have a [Pontryagin–Thom collapse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PontryaginThom/Existence.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.exists_pontryaginThomCollapse), and [Stiefel manifolds are smooth manifolds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/Stiefel.html#TauCeti.stiefel.instIsManifold). Twisted complexes were rebuilt over arbitrary generator sets, with a strongly convergent index spectral sequence, in preparation for the Brown cocycle on arbitrary CW complexes. Fundamental chains rel boundary have started with the [fundamental cycle of the cube pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Comparison/Relative/CubePair.html#TauCeti.bijective_zsmul_homologyπ_cubeBoundaryPairCycle) ([TauCeti#27581](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27581)) and the [lift of relative classes to chains with a prescribed boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Comparison/Excision.html#TopPair.exists_boundary_eq_of_singularHomologyδ_eq) ([TauCeti#25549](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25549)). In Layer 11, [`π₂(SO(3)) = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/HomotopyGroup.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_homotopyGroup_specialOrthogonalGroup_three) ([TauCeti#27414](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27414)) is the first explicit value of Lemma 6.35.
