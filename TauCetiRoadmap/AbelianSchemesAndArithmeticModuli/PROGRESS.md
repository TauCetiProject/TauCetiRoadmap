## AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-25 (`1c62aac` to `522d48f`)

The roadmap now has its carrier. An [abelian scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianScheme/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianScheme) over an arbitrary base is a group object in schemes over `S` that is proper and smooth with geometrically connected fibres, with no commutativity or dimension built in. It comes with base change, the dimension-zero identity scheme, and a [field equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianScheme/AbelianVariety.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianScheme.fieldEquivalence) with Tau Ceti's abelian varieties over `Spec K` (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/1457). The README's sanity checks are formalized alongside: [`𝔾_m` is not an abelian scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/MultiplicativeGroup/NotProper.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianScheme.isEmpty_toOver_iso_Gm) because it is not proper, nontrivial constant groups are disconnected, and a constant group of a nonabelian finite group is noncommutative. A [points criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Cartesian/Mon.html#CategoryTheory.isMonHom_iff_forall_one_comp_mul_comp) tests homomorphisms on `T`-valued points.

On the complex side, a [connected commutative complex Lie group is its tangent space modulo the kernel of the exponential](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Exponential/CommutativeGroup/ComplexTorus.html#TauCeti.mulInvariantExpQuotientKerDiffeomorph). In the compact case that kernel is a [full lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Exponential/CommutativeGroup/TangentLattice.html#TauCeti.isZLattice_ker_mulInvariantExpAddMonoidHom), and it is [first homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Exponential/CommutativeGroup/Homology.html#TauCeti.lieExpFirstHomologyEquiv). The `p`-adic [Tate module of the torus is `ℤ_p ⊗ Λ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Torsion/TateModule/OrbitRelQuotient.html#LinearMap.orbitRelQuotientTateModuleδEquivTensor). None of this is yet specialized to the analytification of an abelian variety. Riemann forms gained their [Hermitian form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/WeightOne/RiemannForm/Hermitian.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.RiemannForm.hermitian), the [homological polarization `Q = −E`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/WeightOne/RiemannForm/Homological.html#TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.IsRiemannForm.isPolarization_homological), and the fact that the [scaled dual form polarizes the dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/Dual/Polarization.html#TauCeti.Hodge.IsPolarization.scaledDualForm).

Two pieces of algebra for later layers also landed. The first is the [elementary-divisor normal form of an alternating form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/BilinearForm/ElementaryDivisors.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.IsAlt.exists_basis_toMatrix_eq_fromBlocks_pos_of_nondegenerate) over `ℤ`, with [determinant `(d₁ ⋯ d_g)²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/BilinearForm/ElementaryDivisors.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.det_toMatrix_eq_prod_sq_of_toMatrix_eq_fromBlocks); uniqueness of the divisors is not proved. The second is [Milne's Lemma 10.21](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/RootProducts.html#Polynomial.Monic.eq_of_forall_norm_prod_aroots_aeval_eq): two monic polynomials over `ℚ_ℓ` are equal if their roots give products of equal absolute value under every integral test polynomial.
