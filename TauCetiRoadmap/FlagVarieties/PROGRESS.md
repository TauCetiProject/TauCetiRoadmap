## FlagVarieties: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`7bbe688` to `7ca783d`)

The general-linear case of the first headline is now in place: over a field, the reduced closed subgroup schemes of GLₙ₊₁ containing the upper-triangular Borel are exactly the block-triangular weight parabolics of decreasing integer weights ([`isReduced_and_le_upperTriangular_definingHopfIdeal_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Classification.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.isReduced_and_le_upperTriangular_definingHopfIdeal_iff)), each of them parabolic, and in characteristic zero the reducedness hypothesis disappears by Cartier's theorem ([`exists_antitone_eq_weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_of_charZero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Classification.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.exists_antitone_eq_weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_of_charZero)). The proof classifies the point group through the GL Tits system, showing that every subgroup of GLₙ₊₁(k) containing the upper-triangular matrices is block triangular ([`exists_antitone_weightParabolicPoints_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/TitsSystem.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.exists_antitone_weightParabolicPoints_eq)), then recovers the ideal from its points. The labels are the normalized [`standardWeight`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/BlockWeight.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.standardWeight) block weights, which drop by one exactly at the adjacent indices outside J (TauCeti#29810, TauCeti#28718).

Two pieces of general infrastructure arrived alongside. Reduced finite-type closed subgroups now embed, with their geometric inclusion order, into the lattice of subgroups of geometric points ([`quotientPointsOrderEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Points/Embedding.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.quotientPointsOrderEmbedding)), with smooth and characteristic-zero variants; this is the point-detection adapter of Layer 0. And for closed subgroups P ⊆ Q there is now a canonical projection G/P → G/Q of fppf homogeneous quotients ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/Map.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientMap)), locally surjective, functorial, and unique among maps compatible with the projections from G. These are sheaf-level maps only; the represented quotient schemes and the equivariant-morphism classification are still to come.

## FlagVarieties: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-13 (`7ca783d` to `aa6e43e`)

Over an algebraically closed field k, the k-points of the fppf homogeneous quotient G/N are now identified with the cosets G(k)/N(k) ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientRationalPointsEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/RationalPoints.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientRationalPointsEquiv)), with no finiteness, smoothness or normality assumed of G or N. The key input is that every fppf cover of Spec k splits ([`eq_top_of_mem_fppfTopology_of_isAlgClosed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/FppfAlgClosed.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.eq_top_of_mem_fppfTopology_of_isAlgClosed)), so sheafification changes nothing over k. In particular every k-point of G/N lifts to G ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientProjection_app_surjective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/RationalPoints.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientProjection_app_surjective)). The comparison commutes with the projections G/P → G/Q and with left translation by k-points, which is itself new: it is obtained by sheafifying translation on cosets ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientTranslation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/Basic.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientTranslation)), and the projections commute with it. This is the coset half of the Layer 0 quotient comparison. The morphism between the representing schemes, and the orbit-to-quotient isomorphism for homogeneous varieties, are still to come.

In the general-linear case, the standard block parabolics now form a lattice matching their labels. Their scheme-theoretic intersection is the parabolic of the intersected label ([`weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_standardWeight_inter`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Lattice.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_standardWeight_inter)), and the smallest closed subgroup containing two of them is the parabolic of the union ([`le_weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_standardWeight_union_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Lattice.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.le_weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_standardWeight_union_iff)). The simple reflections that a standard weight selects are also shown to be exactly its label J. Separately, Lie–Kolchin gained a refinement: a given invariant line can be taken as the first vector of the triangularizing basis ([`exists_basis_coefficientMatrix_isUpperTriangular_first_span_of_isSolvable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Solvable/LieKolchin.html#TauCeti.Comodule.exists_basis_coefficientMatrix_isUpperTriangular_first_span_of_isSolvable)).
