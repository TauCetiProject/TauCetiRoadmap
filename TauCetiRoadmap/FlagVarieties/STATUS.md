## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The general-linear case (Layer 1) is done. Layer 0 is done except for the orbit-to-quotient isomorphism (0.3). Represented quotients now have their scheme-level universal property, projections and translations, which gives Layers 3, 4 and 5 their first proved steps. None of the five headline theorems for a general reductive group is proved yet. Most of them wait on ReductiveGroups for the Tits system on G(k), the standard parabolics P_J and the completeness of G/B.

### Named results

- **Classification of parabolics containing B in GLₙ₊₁** — a closed subgroup scheme of GLₙ₊₁ is reduced and contains the upper-triangular subgroup exactly when it is the weight parabolic of a decreasing integer weight ([`isReduced_and_le_upperTriangular_definingHopfIdeal_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Classification.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.isReduced_and_le_upperTriangular_definingHopfIdeal_iff)). The normalized label J is unique, and in characteristic zero the reducedness hypothesis can be dropped.
- **Rational points of a homogeneous quotient** — over an algebraically closed field, (G/N)(k) = G(k)/N(k) for any closed subgroup N, with no finiteness or smoothness hypotheses ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientRationalPointsEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/RationalPoints.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientRationalPointsEquiv)). The identification is compatible with translation and with the projections G/P → G/Q.
- **Universal property of a represented quotient (Milne 5.22)** — morphisms out of a scheme representing G/N correspond to right N-invariant morphisms out of G ([`representedQuotientHomEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/Scheme.html#CommHopfAlgCat.representedQuotientHomEquiv)). From this, each inclusion P ⊆ Q gives a unique surjective, translation-equivariant projection of schemes G/P → G/Q ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientSchemeMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/SchemeMap.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientSchemeMap)).
- **Properness descends to larger subgroups** — if P ⊆ Q and G/P is proper, then so is any separated, locally finite-type scheme representing G/Q ([`isProper_hom_of_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/SchemeMap.html#CommHopfAlgCat.isProper_hom_of_le)). A projective orbit is proper exactly when it is closed in projective space ([`isProper_projectiveOrbitToSpec_iff_isClosed`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/ProjectiveOrbit/Scheme.html#TauCeti.Comodule.isProper_projectiveOrbitToSpec_iff_isClosed)).
- **Fixed points of P on G/Q** — the point gQ is fixed by P, over every algebra, exactly when g⁻¹Pg ⊆ Q ([`conjugate_le_iff_forall_fppfHomogeneousQuotientProjection_app_mul_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/RightMul.html#CommHopfAlgCat.conjugate_le_iff_forall_fppfHomogeneousQuotientProjection_app_mul_eq)). This is the stabilizer criterion of §5.1.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Point detection of closed subgroups** — reduced finite-type closed subgroups embed, preserving order, into the subgroups of geometric points ([`quotientPointsOrderEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Points/Embedding.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.quotientPointsOrderEmbedding)). This lets a classification of point groups be lifted back to subgroup schemes.
- **The fppf functor of points is fully faithful** — schemes over Spec R are determined by their fppf sheaves of affine points ([`fullyFaithfulPointsSheafFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/CommAlgCat/Fppf.html#TauCeti.CommAlgCat.fullyFaithfulPointsSheafFunctor)). Sheaf-level constructions on quotients can therefore be transferred to their representing schemes.
- **The map xP ↦ xgQ** — for g⁻¹Pg ⊆ Q, this is the sheaf morphism G/P → G/Q that sends eP to gQ and depends only on gQ ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientRightMul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/RightMul.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientRightMul)). These are the candidate equivariant morphisms of Layer 5.

## The frontier

- **Projective implies parabolic (4.2)** — show that if G/H is proper then H contains a conjugate of B. The orbit is then closed in projective space, so the inputs the README names are all in the library: the projective Borel fixed-point theorem, comodule corestriction and the lifting of rational points.
- **Equivariant morphisms (5.1)** — show that every G-equivariant scheme morphism G/P → G/Q is xP ↦ xgQ for a P-fixed point gQ. The criterion and the construction exist on sheaves. What remains is surjectivity of evaluation at eP, and transferring the statement to schemes through the fully faithful functor of points.
- **Orbit-to-quotient comparison (0.3)** — identify a homogeneous variety with G/Stab(x) when the stabilizer is smooth. The recognition theorem for represented quotients is now available, but this still needs ReductiveGroups 3A.1's scheme orbit maps and stabilizers.
- **Borel containment implies projective (4.1)** — blocked only on completeness of G/B (`gap:complete-borel-quotient`).
- **Standard parabolics and their types (Layers 2–3)** — blocked on ReductiveGroups 7/7b: the Tits system on G(k), the scheme P_J with P_J(k) = B(k)W_J B(k), and N_G(B)(k) = B(k) (`gap:tits-system-of-points`, `gap:standard-parabolic-scheme`).
