## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The general-linear case (Layer 1) is done, and the standard GL block parabolics now also have their lattice structure. Layer 0 has its point-detection embedding and the identification of rational points of G/H with cosets, but not the orbit-to-quotient isomorphism. None of the five headline classifications for a general reductive group has begun: the standard parabolics P_J, their conjugacy classes, projectivity of G/H and the classification of projective homogeneous spaces still wait on suppliers in ReductiveGroups or on Layers 0 and 5.

### Named results

- **Classification of parabolics containing B in GLₙ₊₁** — a closed subgroup scheme of GLₙ₊₁ is reduced and contains the upper-triangular subgroup exactly when it is the weight parabolic of a decreasing integer weight ([`isReduced_and_le_upperTriangular_definingHopfIdeal_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Classification.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.isReduced_and_le_upperTriangular_definingHopfIdeal_iff)). Over an algebraically closed field the normalized label J is unique, which gives the roadmap's order isomorphism with subsets of the simple reflections.
- **The characteristic-zero GL case** — in characteristic zero every closed subgroup scheme containing B is a standard weight parabolic, with no reducedness hypothesis, by Cartier's theorem ([`exists_antitone_eq_weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_of_charZero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Classification.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.exists_antitone_eq_weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_of_charZero)).
- **The lattice of GL standard parabolics** — P_J ∩ P_K = P_(J∩K) scheme-theoretically ([`weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_standardWeight_inter`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Lattice.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_standardWeight_inter)), and P_(J∪K) is the smallest closed subgroup containing both ([`le_weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_standardWeight_union_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Lattice.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.le_weightParabolicDefiningHopfIdeal_standardWeight_union_iff)). This is the type-A instance of what §2.2 asks for in general.
- **Rational points of a homogeneous quotient** — over an algebraically closed field, (G/N)(k) = G(k)/N(k) for any closed subgroup N, with no finiteness or smoothness hypotheses ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientRationalPointsEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/RationalPoints.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientRationalPointsEquiv)). The identification is compatible with the projections G/P → G/Q and with left translation, and it rests on the fact that fppf covers of Spec k split ([`eq_top_of_mem_fppfTopology_of_isAlgClosed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/FppfAlgClosed.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.eq_top_of_mem_fppfTopology_of_isAlgClosed)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Point detection of closed subgroups** — reduced finite-type closed subgroups embed, order-preservingly, into the subgroups of geometric points ([`quotientPointsOrderEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Points/Embedding.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.quotientPointsOrderEmbedding)). This is what lets a classification of point groups be lifted back to subgroup schemes, as in the GL case.
- **Projections of homogeneous quotients** — for P ⊆ Q, the canonical map G/P → G/Q of fppf sheaves ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/Map.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientMap)). It is locally surjective, functorial, and the unique map compatible with the projections from G; it is the starting point for Layer 5.
- **Left translation on G/N** — translation by a k-point, obtained by sheafifying translation on cosets ([`fppfHomogeneousQuotientTranslation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/Basic.html#CommHopfAlgCat.fppfHomogeneousQuotientTranslation)). It is a left action, and the projections G/P → G/Q commute with it, which makes them equivariant for k-points at the sheaf level.

### Roadmap coverage

Layer 1 is done. Layer 0 is partial: point detection (0.1) is proved, and so is the coset half of 0.2, with rational points, translation and projections compatible. The morphism of representing schemes G/N → G/N′ is not established here, and nor is the orbit-to-quotient isomorphism for homogeneous varieties (0.3). Layer 5 is partial only in a weak sense: the sheaf-level projections are functorial and translation-equivariant, but neither the base-point evaluation criterion (5.1) nor the classification of equivariant maps G/P_J → G/P_K (5.2) is proved. Layers 2, 3, 4 and 6 are untouched for a general reductive group. The GL lattice results above anticipate §2.2 only in type A.

## The frontier

- **Projective implies parabolic (4.2)** — show that if G/H is proper then H contains a conjugate of B. The inputs the README names are now in the library: the represented quotient as a projective orbit, the projective Borel fixed-point theorem, comodule corestriction, and the lifting of rational points of G/H to G.
- **Orbit-to-quotient comparison (0.3)** — identify a homogeneous variety with G/Stab(x) when the stabilizer is smooth. This needs ReductiveGroups 3A.1's scheme orbit maps and stabilizers.
- **Equivariant maps (5.1)** — show that evaluation at eP identifies equivariant scheme morphisms G/P → G/Q with P-fixed points. The sheaf-level projections and translations are in place, but the scheme-level statement needs quotient descent.
- **Standard parabolics and their types (Layers 2–3)** — blocked on ReductiveGroups 7/7b: the Tits system on G(k) and the scheme P_J with P_J(k) = B(k)W_J B(k) (`gap:tits-system-of-points`, `gap:standard-parabolic-scheme`). Borel conjugacy and the parabolic-conjugate-contains-B lemma are already pinned.
- **Borel containment implies projective (4.1)** — needs completeness of G/B (`gap:complete-borel-quotient`).
