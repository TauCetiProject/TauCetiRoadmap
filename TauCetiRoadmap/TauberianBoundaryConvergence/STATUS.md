## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All three targets are proved: Newman's bounded-complex Tauberian theorem (N1), the ordered Dirichlet endpoint at `s = 1` (N2), and the ordered Euler product of a nontrivial Dirichlet `L`-function at `s = 1`, with its finite-deletion variant (N3). The only open points are small gaps against the README's wording for cutoff comparisons, not missing theorems.

### Named results

- **Newman's Tauberian theorem** — if `f` is measurable and bounded on `[0, ∞)` and its Laplace transform extends holomorphically to an open set containing the closed right half-plane, then `∫₀ᵀ f → G(0)`. This is [`newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Tauberian.html#TauCeti.LSeries.newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral). A variant, [`newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral_of_analyticAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Tauberian.html#TauCeti.LSeries.newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral_of_analyticAt), asks only for analyticity at each point of the imaginary axis.
- **The bounded Dirichlet endpoint** — if `a` is bounded from `n = 1` on and its `L`-series continues to a holomorphic `F` on a neighbourhood of `Re s ≥ 1`, then the ordered sums `∑_{1≤n≤N} a n / n` tend to `F(1)`. This is [`boundedDirichlet_tendsto_ordered_at_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/Endpoint.html#TauCeti.LSeries.boundedDirichlet_tendsto_ordered_at_one). The conclusion is an ordered `Tendsto`, exactly as the README requires.
- **The ordered Euler product at `s = 1`** — for a nontrivial character `χ` of positive level, `∏_{p≤B} (1 − χ(p)/p)⁻¹ → L(χ, 1)`. This is [`tendsto_prod_primesLE_inv_LFunction_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/DirichletEulerProduct.html#DirichletCharacter.tendsto_prod_primesLE_inv_LFunction_one). Deleting the primes in a finite set `S` gives the limit `L(χ, 1) · ∏_{p∈S} (1 − χ(p)/p)`, proved as [`tendsto_prod_primesLE_filter_notMem_inv_LFunction_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/DirichletEulerProduct.html#DirichletCharacter.tendsto_prod_primesLE_filter_notMem_inv_LFunction_one).
- **The Euler logarithm across `Re s = 1`** — for nontrivial `χ`, some open neighbourhood of `Re s ≥ 1` carries a holomorphic `F` with `exp F = L(χ, ·)` that agrees with the Euler-log series for `Re s > 1`. This is [`exists_differentiableOn_exp_eq_LFunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/DirichletEulerLog.html#DirichletCharacter.exists_differentiableOn_exp_eq_LFunction). It comes from a general extension theorem for logarithms across a boundary line, [`exists_differentiableOn_eqOn_exp_comp_le_re`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/BranchLogRoot.html#TauCeti.exists_differentiableOn_eqOn_exp_comp_le_re), rather than the README's ball-by-ball patching.
- **The ordered character series at `s = 1`** — for nontrivial `χ`, `∑_{n≤K} χ(n)/n → L(χ, 1)`. This is [`tendsto_sum_Icc_div_LFunction_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/DirichletEulerProduct.html#DirichletCharacter.tendsto_sum_Icc_div_LFunction_one). The ordered prime sums `∑_{p≤B} χ(p)/p` [converge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/DirichletEulerProduct.html#DirichletCharacter.exists_tendsto_sum_primesLE_div) as well.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Euler-log coefficients** — [`eulerLogCoeff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/DirichletEulerLog.html#DirichletCharacter.eulerLogCoeff) is `χ(n)Λ(n)/log n`. It equals `χ(p)^k/k` on prime powers, vanishes elsewhere and is bounded by `1`, so the bounded endpoint applies to it directly.
- **From ordered sums to ordered products** — [`tendsto_prod_primesLE_inv_of_tendsto_sum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/OrderedEulerProduct.html#TauCeti.LSeries.tendsto_prod_primesLE_inv_of_tendsto_sum) turns any convergent ordered Euler-log sum into a convergent Euler product with limit `exp L`. It works for arbitrary local coefficients of modulus at most `1`, using the uniform bound `1` on the higher-prime-power terms.
- **Rejection tests** — the [alternating coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/RejectionTests.html#TauCeti.LSeries.not_LSeriesHasSum_alternatingCoeff_one) have convergent ordered sums but no unconditional sum at `1`. [Constant coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/RejectionTests.html#TauCeti.LSeries.not_differentiableOn_of_LSeriesHasSum_one) cannot satisfy the continuation hypothesis. The trivial character's Euler products [have no limit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/TrivialCharacter.html#DirichletCharacter.not_tendsto_prod_primesLE_inv_one).
