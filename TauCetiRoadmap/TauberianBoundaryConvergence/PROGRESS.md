## TauberianBoundaryConvergence: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-24 (`9312dc7` to `e177885`)

Newman's Tauberian theorem is now proved in the generality the roadmap asks for, as [`newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Tauberian.html#TauCeti.LSeries.newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral): if `f` is measurable and bounded on `[0, ∞)` and its Laplace transform extends holomorphically to an open set containing the closed right half-plane, then `∫₀ᵀ f` converges to the extension's value at `0` (TauCeti#20461). The proof follows Newman's contour argument. It uses the fixed-radius estimate [`eventually_norm_sub_intervalIntegral_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Tauberian.html#TauCeti.LSeries.eventually_norm_sub_intervalIntegral_le) and then lets the radius grow. No uniform width of continuation is needed.

The supporting work builds that contour argument piece by piece. Truncated Laplace transforms are shown to be entire, the full transform holomorphic on `Re z > 0`, and both get the tail bounds the argument needs. Cauchy's formula is applied with Newman's kernel `exp(Tz)(1 + z²/R²)/z`, and both semicircle contributions are bounded by `M/R`. The left semicircle is swapped for an explicit parabolic arc, [`newmanDetour`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Detour.html#TauCeti.LSeries.newmanDetour), which compactness places inside the continuation domain and whose contribution vanishes as `T → ∞`. Along the way the library gained a general fact: [holomorphic functions have primitives on star-shaped open sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Primitive.html#TauCeti.Contour.isExactOn_of_starConvex).

The Dirichlet-series endpoint (N2) has begun with its correction term. This is the [step correction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/StepCorrection.html#TauCeti.LSeries.stepCorrection) `H`, which is [holomorphic on `Re z > −1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/StepCorrection.html#TauCeti.LSeries.differentiableOn_stepCorrection) and has the expected [value at `0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/StepCorrection.html#TauCeti.LSeries.stepCorrection_zero). Still missing: the Laplace-transform identity for the step function `a ⌊eᵗ⌋₊`, the ordered endpoint `boundedDirichlet_tendsto_ordered_at_one`, and all of the Dirichlet-character work in N3.
