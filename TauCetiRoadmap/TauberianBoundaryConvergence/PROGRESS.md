## TauberianBoundaryConvergence: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-24 (`9312dc7` to `da4d8dc`)

The first target of this roadmap is complete: [Newman's Tauberian theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Tauberian.html#TauCeti.LSeries.newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral) is proved for arbitrary bounded complex `f` on `[0, ∞)`. It says that if the Laplace transform of `f` continues holomorphically to an open set containing the closed right half-plane, then `∫₀ᵀ f` converges to the continuation's value at `0` (TauCeti#20461). The hypothesis is measurability plus a bound, which is all the roadmap's local integrability assumption asks for, and the theorem uses no sign condition or zero-free strip. The proof follows Newman's contour argument as the roadmap describes it. Truncated transforms are [entire](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/LaplaceTransform.html#TauCeti.differentiable_intervalIntegral_mul_cexp_neg_mul), and the full transform is holomorphic on the open half-plane. Each semicircle of the kernel `exp(Tz)(1+z²/R²)/z` contributes at most `M/R`. On the left, an explicit parabolic [detour](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Detour.html#TauCeti.LSeries.newmanDetour) inside the continuation domain replaces the semicircle, and its contribution vanishes as `T → ∞`. Along the way the library gained a general fact that a [holomorphic function on a star-shaped open set has a primitive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Primitive.html#TauCeti.Contour.isExactOn_of_starConvex).

The Dirichlet endpoint has begun. The [step correction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/StepCorrection.html#TauCeti.LSeries.stepCorrection) `H(z)` compares the step function `a ⌊eᵗ⌋₊` with the Dirichlet series. It is now known to converge absolutely and to be [holomorphic on `Re z > −1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/StepCorrection.html#TauCeti.LSeries.differentiableOn_stepCorrection), with its value at `0` worked out explicitly. Still missing are the identity between the step function's Laplace transform and `F(z+1)+H(z)`, and the ordered limit at `s = 1` itself. Nothing has landed yet for the Dirichlet-character Euler-product comparison.
