## TauberianBoundaryConvergence: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-24 (`9312dc7` to `e177885`)

Newman's Tauberian theorem is now proved in the generality the roadmap asks for, as [`newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Tauberian.html#TauCeti.LSeries.newmanLaplace_tendsto_integral): if `f` is measurable and bounded on `[0, ∞)` and its Laplace transform extends holomorphically to an open set containing the closed right half-plane, then `∫₀ᵀ f` converges to the extension's value at `0` (TauCeti#20461). The proof follows Newman's contour argument. It uses the fixed-radius estimate [`eventually_norm_sub_intervalIntegral_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Tauberian.html#TauCeti.LSeries.eventually_norm_sub_intervalIntegral_le) and then lets the radius grow. No uniform width of continuation is needed.

The supporting work builds that contour argument piece by piece. Truncated Laplace transforms are shown to be entire, the full transform holomorphic on `Re z > 0`, and both get the tail bounds the argument needs. Cauchy's formula is applied with Newman's kernel `exp(Tz)(1 + z²/R²)/z`, and both semicircle contributions are bounded by `M/R`. The left semicircle is swapped for an explicit parabolic arc, [`newmanDetour`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Newman/Detour.html#TauCeti.LSeries.newmanDetour), which compactness places inside the continuation domain and whose contribution vanishes as `T → ∞`. Along the way the library gained a general fact: [holomorphic functions have primitives on star-shaped open sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Primitive.html#TauCeti.Contour.isExactOn_of_starConvex).

The Dirichlet-series endpoint (N2) has begun with its correction term. This is the [step correction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/StepCorrection.html#TauCeti.LSeries.stepCorrection) `H`, which is [holomorphic on `Re z > −1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/StepCorrection.html#TauCeti.LSeries.differentiableOn_stepCorrection) and has the expected [value at `0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/StepCorrection.html#TauCeti.LSeries.stepCorrection_zero). Still missing: the Laplace-transform identity for the step function `a ⌊eᵗ⌋₊`, the ordered endpoint `boundedDirichlet_tendsto_ordered_at_one`, and all of the Dirichlet-character work in N3.

## TauberianBoundaryConvergence: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-13 (`e177885` to `aa6e43e`)

The ordered Dirichlet endpoint and the ordered Euler product at `s = 1` both landed, so all three of the roadmap's targets are now in place. The [bounded Dirichlet endpoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/Endpoint.html#TauCeti.LSeries.boundedDirichlet_tendsto_ordered_at_one) says that if bounded coefficients have an `L`-series that continues holomorphically to a neighbourhood of `Re s ≥ 1`, then the ordered sums `∑_{n≤N} a n / n` converge to the continuation's value at `1` (TauCeti#20964). The proof follows the plan: the Laplace transform of the step function `a ⌊eᵗ⌋₊` is [identified](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/Endpoint.html#TauCeti.LSeries.integral_floor_exp_mul_cexp_neg_mul) with `F(z+1)` plus the step correction, and Newman's theorem is applied. For a nontrivial Dirichlet character this gives the [ordered Euler product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/DirichletEulerProduct.html#DirichletCharacter.tendsto_prod_primesLE_inv_LFunction_one) `∏_{p≤B} (1 − χ(p)/p)⁻¹ → L(χ, 1)` (TauCeti#23021), and also the [ordered series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/DirichletEulerProduct.html#DirichletCharacter.tendsto_sum_Icc_div_LFunction_one) `∑ χ(n)/n → L(χ, 1)`.

The route to the product goes through a [holomorphic logarithm of `L(χ, s)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/DirichletEulerLog.html#DirichletCharacter.exists_differentiableOn_exp_eq_LFunction) on a neighbourhood of the closed half-plane. It is built from a general fact: [a logarithm on an open half-plane extends across the boundary line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/BranchLogRoot.html#TauCeti.exists_differentiableOn_eqOn_exp_comp_le_re) when the function is zero-free there. This replaces the ball-by-ball patching the README sketched. The higher prime powers are split off using their absolute bound of `1`, and truncation estimates for the Taylor series of `log(1 − z)⁻¹` compare the local factors. Around these results sits the comparison API the README asks for. It covers linearity and uniqueness of ordered limits, finite coefficient changes, [deleting finitely many Euler factors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/DirichletEulerProduct.html#DirichletCharacter.tendsto_prod_primesLE_filter_notMem_inv_LFunction_one), conjugation, change of level, and reindexing prime cutoffs.

The README's rejection tests are formal too. The [alternating coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/RejectionTests.html#TauCeti.LSeries.not_LSeriesHasSum_alternatingCoeff_one) have convergent ordered sums but no unconditional sum at `1`, and the [Euler products of the trivial character diverge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/BoundaryConvergence/TrivialCharacter.html#DirichletCharacter.not_tendsto_prod_primesLE_inv_one).
