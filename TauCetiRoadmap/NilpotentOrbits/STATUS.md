## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The orbit theory of Layers 0, 2 and 4 is largely in place: the internal Jordan decomposition, sl₂-triples, finiteness and weighted-diagram classification of nilpotent orbits, the closure order, and a complete type A chapter. The nilpotent cone, the unipotent variety and the Slodowy slice are partial, the dense regular orbit is not proved, and the subregular stratum, the Springer resolution and the ADE surfaces (Layers 3 and 7–9) have not begun.

### Named results

- **Finiteness and classification of nilpotent orbits** — over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, an affine group of finite type with Killing Lie algebra has [finitely many nilpotent adjoint orbits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/WeightedDiagram.html#TauCeti.Comodule.VectorOrbit.finite_setOf_isNilpotentAd), at most 3ʳ, and for connected G [the weighted Dynkin diagram determines the orbit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/WeightedDiagram.html#RootPairing.Base.nilpotentOrbitWeightedDynkinDiagram_injective). The acceptance file's statement for Int(L)-orbits of an abstract Lie algebra is not yet derived from it.
- **Jacobson–Morozov and Kostant conjugacy** — every nonzero ad-nilpotent e lies in an sl₂-triple (`TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad`), and two triples sharing e are [conjugate under the connected centralizer C_G(e)°](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Exponential.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_mem_quotientPointsSubgroup_centralizer_identityComponent_eq).
- **The abstract Jordan decomposition** — in a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form over an algebraically closed field, every element is a sum of commuting ad-nilpotent and ad-semisimple parts ([`TauCeti.exists_isNilpotent_ad_isSemisimple_ad`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Killing/JordanDecomposition/Basic.html#TauCeti.exists_isNilpotent_ad_isSemisimple_ad)), uniquely when the center is trivial.
- **Gerstenhaber's theorem** — a matrix Y lies in the closure of the class of a nilpotent X exactly when rank Yᵏ ≤ rank Xᵏ for all k, that is, when the Jordan partition of X dominates that of Y ([`Matrix.mem_closure_orbit_iff_forall_rank_pow_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Rank/OrbitClosure.html#Matrix.mem_closure_orbit_iff_forall_rank_pow_le)).
- **Adjoint orbits as homogeneous spaces** — for a reduced finite-type group over an algebraically closed field, the orbit of x is G/C_G(x) ([`TauCeti.fppfHomogeneousQuotientIsoAdjointOrbitPoints`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Orbit.html#TauCeti.fppfHomogeneousQuotientIsoAdjointOrbitPoints)), smooth of dimension dim G − dim C_G(x).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Rational orbits with the closure order** ([`TauCeti.Comodule.VectorOrbit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Orbit/Basic.html#TauCeti.Comodule.VectorOrbit)) for any representation: orbits are [partially ordered by closure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Orbit/ClosureOrder.html#TauCeti.Comodule.VectorOrbit.instPartialOrder), strictly smaller orbits have strictly smaller dimension, and a nilpotent orbit closure is the union of the finitely many orbits below it.
- **Centralizer component groups** π₀(C_G(x)), with [conjugation isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer/ComponentGroup.html#TauCeti.centralizerComponentGroupConjugationEquiv), maps induced by homomorphisms and surjectivity along isogenies in characteristic zero.
- **The ideal of the nilpotent cone**, generated by the invariants vanishing at the origin. Its rational points are the ad-nilpotent elements, and for a reductive group [the ad-nilpotent elements of the derived algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Reductive/NilpotentCone.html#TauCeti.reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty.nilpotentConeIdeal_le_ker_lift_eval_iff_mem_derivedSeries_and_isNilpotent_ad). Its scheme theory, reducedness and primality, is not developed.

## The frontier

- **The dense regular orbit (§2.3)**: identify the regular nilpotent elements with FormalConnectionsOpers §4.3's principal orbit, then prove density in 𝒩 and dim 𝒩 = dim 𝔤 − r. A regular element in an orbit closure is already known to lie in the orbit.
- **Finiteness for an abstract Lie algebra (§2.4)**: the group statement and the inclusion of Int(𝔤) in the adjoint image of G°(k) are proved; what remains is the acceptance file's form, finitely many Int(L)-orbits of ad-nilpotent elements of L.
- **Jacobson–Morozov in the group (§§2.1–2.2)**: integrate a triple to SL₂ → G with central kernel in {±I}, then build the parabolic of its cocharacter and show G(0) = C_G(h)°.
- **The minimal centralizer dimension (§0.4)**: prove it equals r. It also feeds §2.3.
- **Reducedness of the zero fibre (§5.0)**: the complete-intersection and reducedness argument for the cone's ideal. It needs irreducibility from §1.3 and the dimension from §2.3, and it blocks the exponential isomorphism 𝒩 ≃ 𝒰_G.
