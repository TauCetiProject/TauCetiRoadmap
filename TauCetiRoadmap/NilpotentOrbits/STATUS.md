## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Lie-algebraic theory of sl₂-triples is in place: Jacobson–Morozov, Kostant conjugacy, the transverse decomposition and the {0, 1, 2} bound. The Slodowy slice exists as an affine subspace with its contracting action. Group-level work has started with algebraic centralizers and their dimensions. There is still no internal Jordan decomposition and no nilpotent cone as a scheme. The flag variety, exponential, Springer resolution and subregular surfaces have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Jacobson–Morozov theorem** — in a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form over a field of characteristic zero, every nonzero ad-nilpotent e lies in an sl₂-triple (h, e, f) ([`TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad)). Equivalently, e is the image of E under an injective homomorphism from sl₂ ([`TauCeti.exists_injective_lieHom_slFinTwo_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/Embedding.html#TauCeti.exists_injective_lieHom_slFinTwo_iff)).
- **Kostant's conjugacy theorem** — two triples with the same e are conjugate by an automorphism generated by the exp(ad y) with y ∈ ker(ad e) ∩ [e, L] ([`IsSl2Triple.exists_lieEquiv_apply_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_lieEquiv_apply_eq)).
- **The transverse decomposition** — L = [L, e] ⊕ ker(ad f) for a triple (h, e, f). This is the linear algebra under the Slodowy slice ([`IsSl2Triple.isCompl_range_ad_e_ker_ad_f`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/RangeKer.html#IsSl2Triple.isCompl_range_ad_e_ker_ad_f)).
- **The {0, 1, 2} bound** — if h is dominant in a Cartan subalgebra, every simple root takes the value 0, 1 or 2 at h ([`IsSl2Triple.root_apply_eq_zero_or_eq_one_or_eq_two`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/WeightedDiagram.html#IsSl2Triple.root_apply_eq_zero_or_eq_one_or_eq_two)).
- **The centralizer dimension formula** — in characteristic zero, dim G = dim C_G(x) + dim [𝔤, x] for x in the Lie algebra of a finite-type affine group G. The Lie algebra of C_G(x) is ker(ad x) ([`TauCeti.ringKrullDim_quotient_centralizerHopfIdeal_add_finrank_range_ad`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer.html#TauCeti.ringKrullDim_quotient_centralizerHopfIdeal_add_finrank_range_ad)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The algebraic centralizer** ([`TauCeti.centralizerHopfIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer.html#TauCeti.centralizerHopfIdeal)) is the stabilizer of x under the adjoint comodule. It gives N0.3 a genuine closed subgroup with a computed Lie algebra and dimension, which is where the orbit G/C_G(x) will come from.
- **Regular elements** ([`LieAlgebra.IsCentralizerRegular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/AdjointAction/Regular.html#LieAlgebra.IsCentralizerRegular)) are defined by minimal centralizer dimension, as N0.4 asks. They are Zariski dense, the nonregular locus is Zariski closed, and the definition agrees with minimality of the group centralizer. This is the regular locus that N2.3 must compare with the principal export from FormalConnectionsOpers.
- **The Slodowy slice and its contracting action** ([`TauCeti.slodowySlice`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/SlodowySlice.html#TauCeti.slodowySlice), [`IsSl2Triple.contractingAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/SlodowySlice.html#IsSl2Triple.contractingAction)). The action is built from a grading automorphism of the Lie algebra rather than a cocharacter of G. It preserves the slice, has positive weights n + 2 on it, and extends to the limit e.

## The frontier

- **Orbits as homogeneous spaces (N0.3)** — construct the orbit as G/C_G(x), with tangent space [𝔤, x]. Its dimension count dim G − dim C_G(x) is already proved, so what remains is the quotient and the orbit map.
- **Internal Jordan decomposition (N0.2)** — show that the semisimple part of a Lie derivation is a derivation, and split x = xₛ + xₙ inside 𝔤 with uniqueness. Nothing for this has landed.
- **Weighted diagrams and finiteness (N2.2, N2.4)** — the open orbit of the grade-zero centralizer on 𝔤(2) and the injection of orbits into weighted diagrams. The {0, 1, 2} bound, [𝔤(0), e] = 𝔤(2) and the centralizer dimension formula are in hand. The open-orbit step needs the orbit dictionary above.
- **Type A partitions and dominance (N4)** — Jordan-chain bases, partitions from dim ker(Xᵏ), the centralizer dimension Σ(λ′ⱼ)², and explicit degenerations for the converse of the closure rank bounds.
- **The nilpotent cone as a scheme (N1)** — the zero fiber of the adjoint quotient χ, which consumes ReductiveGroups 11C and the FormalConnectionsOpers invariant adapter. N5 and N7–N9 wait on it.
