## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The linear-algebraic floor is largely in place: the internal Jordan decomposition, sl₂-triples, adjoint orbits as homogeneous spaces, and a complete type A chapter (N4) through Gerstenhaber's theorem. Weighted diagrams, the nilpotent cone, the unipotent variety and the Slodowy slice are partial. The flag variety, the Springer resolution and the subregular surfaces (N7–N9) have not begun.

### Named results

- **The abstract Jordan decomposition** — in a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form over an algebraically closed field, every element is a sum of commuting ad-nilpotent and ad-semisimple parts ([`TauCeti.exists_isNilpotent_ad_isSemisimple_ad`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Killing/JordanDecomposition/Basic.html#TauCeti.exists_isNilpotent_ad_isSemisimple_ad)), uniquely when the center is trivial. It is built from the Jordan–Chevalley decomposition of a derivation, as N0.2 asks.
- **The Jacobson–Morozov theorem** — in characteristic zero, every nonzero ad-nilpotent e of a Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form lies in an sl₂-triple (h, e, f) (`TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad`).
- **Gerstenhaber's theorem** — a matrix Y lies in the closure of the conjugacy class of a nilpotent X exactly when rank Yᵏ ≤ rank Xᵏ for every k, that is, when the Jordan partition of X dominates that of Y, in the analytic and the Zariski topology ([`Matrix.mem_closure_orbit_iff_forall_rank_pow_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Rank/OrbitClosure.html#Matrix.mem_closure_orbit_iff_forall_rank_pow_le)).
- **Adjoint orbits as homogeneous spaces** — for a reduced finite-type group over an algebraically closed field, the orbit of x is G/C_G(x) ([`TauCeti.fppfHomogeneousQuotientIsoAdjointOrbitPoints`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Orbit.html#TauCeti.fppfHomogeneousQuotientIsoAdjointOrbitPoints)), smooth of dimension dim G − dim C_G(x).
- **Conjugacy by weighted Dynkin diagrams** — over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, two triples with neutral elements dominant for the same base and equal weighted Dynkin diagrams are conjugate under the group ([`IsSl2Triple.exists_mem_quotientPointsSubgroup_identityComponent_of_weightedDynkinDiagram_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer/GradeTwoOrbit.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_mem_quotientPointsSubgroup_identityComponent_of_weightedDynkinDiagram_eq)). It rests on the open C_G(h)°-orbit of e in the grade-two space.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Jordan partition** ([`Module.End.jordanPartition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/End/JordanChain/Partition.html#Module.End.jordanPartition)) of a nilpotent endomorphism, read off from Jordan-chain bases and recovered from the dimensions of ker Xᵏ. It is a complete conjugacy invariant and carries every type A statement.
- **The unipotent variety** ([`TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.unipotentVarietyIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/UnipotentPoint/Variety.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.unipotentVarietyIdeal)) is a reduced closed subscheme, stable under inversion and isomorphisms. For any faithful representation it is the radical of the unipotence ideal, which is the defining ideal I_U of N5.1.
- **The ideal of the nilpotent cone**, `nilpotentConeIdeal`, is generated by the invariant polynomials vanishing at the origin. It is homogeneous and adjoint-stable, and for a connected group over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero its rational points are exactly the ad-nilpotent elements. This is N1's zero fiber as an ideal; its scheme theory is not yet developed.

## The frontier

- **Finiteness of nilpotent orbits (N2.2)**: dominant normalization, diagram conjugacy and finitely many dominant neutral elements are all proved. What remains is the theorem that chains them into an injection of orbits into weighted diagrams.
- **The dense regular orbit (N2.3)**: compare the minimal-centralizer regular locus with the principal export of FormalConnectionsOpers F4.3. A regular element in an orbit closure is already known to lie in the orbit.
- **Closure order (N2.4)**: centralizers strictly grow at boundary points; the partial order and the decomposition of closures into lower orbits remain.
- **Centralizer component groups (N0.4)**: the finite component group of C_G(x) and its maps under inclusion, conjugation and isogeny.
- **Reducedness of the zero fiber (N5.0)**: the complete-intersection and reducedness argument for the cone's ideal. It needs N1's irreducibility and N2.3's dimension, and blocks the exponential isomorphism 𝒩 ≃ 𝒰_G.
