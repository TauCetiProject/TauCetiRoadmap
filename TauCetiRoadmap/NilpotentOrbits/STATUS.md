## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Lie-algebraic theory of sl₂-triples is in place: Jacobson–Morozov, Kostant's conjugacy of triples, the transverse decomposition behind the Slodowy slice, and the {0, 1, 2} bound on weighted diagrams. All of it is stated for an abstract finite-dimensional Lie algebra in characteristic zero. Nothing algebro-geometric has begun. There is no nilpotent cone as a scheme, no flag variety, no exponential, no Springer resolution and no subregular surfaces.

### Named results

- **The Jacobson–Morozov theorem** — in a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form over a field of characteristic zero, every nonzero ad-nilpotent e sits in an sl₂-triple (h, e, f) ([`TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad)). The converse is also proved, so the nilpositive elements are exactly the nonzero ad-nilpotent ones ([`TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_iff)).
- **Kostant's conjugacy theorem** — two triples with the same e are conjugate by an automorphism fixing e, generated by the exp(ad y) with y ∈ ker(ad e) ∩ [e, L] ([`IsSl2Triple.exists_lieEquiv_apply_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_lieEquiv_apply_eq)). The possible neutral elements form the affine space h + (ker(ad e) ∩ [e, L]) ([`IsSl2Triple.exists_isSl2Triple_iff_sub_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_isSl2Triple_iff_sub_mem)).
- **The transverse decomposition** — for a triple (h, e, f), L = [L, e] ⊕ ker(ad f), which is the linear-algebra input to the Slodowy slice e + ker(ad f) ([`IsSl2Triple.isCompl_range_ad_e_ker_ad_f`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/RangeKer.html#IsSl2Triple.isCompl_range_ad_e_ker_ad_f)).
- **The {0, 1, 2} bound** — if h is dominant in a Cartan subalgebra, every simple root takes the value 0, 1 or 2 at h ([`IsSl2Triple.root_apply_eq_zero_or_eq_one_or_eq_two`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/WeightedDiagram.html#IsSl2Triple.root_apply_eq_zero_or_eq_one_or_eq_two)). Together with [e, L(0)] = L(2) ([`IsSl2Triple.map_ad_e_eigenspace_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/EigenspaceMap.html#IsSl2Triple.map_ad_e_eigenspace_zero)), it starts the weighted-diagram argument.
- **SL and GL conjugacy agree** — over an algebraically closed field a square matrix has the same conjugation orbit under SL(n) as under GL(n) ([`Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.orbit_conjAct_specialLinearGroup_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/SpecialLinear.html#Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.orbit_conjAct_specialLinearGroup_eq)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Killing-orthogonality of kernels and ranges** of powers of ad x ([`TauCeti.range_ad_pow_eq_orthogonal_ker_ad_pow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Killing/AdNilpotent.html#TauCeti.range_ad_pow_eq_orthogonal_ker_ad_pow)), together with Jacobson's lemma, supplies the neutral element h ∈ [e, L] that the Jacobson–Morozov proof starts from.
- **The nilpotent cone of sl n on points** — over a perfect field in which n is nonzero, the ad-nilpotent elements of sl n are exactly the nilpotent matrices ([`TauCeti.image_setOf_isNilpotent_ad_sl`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/GeneralLinear/AdNilpotent.html#TauCeti.image_setOf_isNilpotent_ad_sl)). Centralizers in sl n have codimension one in those in gl n.
