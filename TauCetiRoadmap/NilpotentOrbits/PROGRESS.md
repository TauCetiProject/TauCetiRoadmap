## NilpotentOrbits: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`cc6c96d` to `ddc3f97`)

The first work on this roadmap is the Lie-algebraic theory of sl₂-triples, and it lands two named theorems. [The Jacobson–Morozov theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad) says that every nonzero ad-nilpotent element e of a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form, in characteristic zero, is the nilpositive element of an sl₂-triple (h, e, f). [Kostant's conjugacy theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_lieEquiv_apply_eq) says that two triples sharing e are conjugate by an automorphism in the subgroup generated by the exp(ad y) with y in ker(ad e) ∩ [e, L] (TauCeti#19650). Both live in a Lie algebra over a field. The roadmap also asks for an algebraic SL₂ homomorphism into the group G and for conjugacy by its unipotent centralizer, and neither is here yet. The supporting work came first: Killing-orthogonality of kernels and ranges of powers of ad x, a trace criterion for nilpotence, Jacobson's lemma on commutators, and [Morozov's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/Morozov.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad) on the uniqueness of f given h and e.

The same sl₂ toolkit gives [the transverse decomposition L = [L, e] ⊕ ker(ad f)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/RangeKer.html#IsSl2Triple.isCompl_range_ad_e_ker_ad_f), which is the linear algebra under the Slodowy slice, and [the identity [e, L(0)] = L(2)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/EigenspaceMap.html#IsSl2Triple.map_ad_e_eigenspace_zero) for the grading by h. It also gives [the {0, 1, 2} bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/WeightedDiagram.html#IsSl2Triple.root_apply_eq_zero_or_eq_one_or_eq_two): when h is dominant in a Cartan subalgebra, each simple root takes the value 0, 1 or 2 at h. This is the first step toward weighted diagrams, though the open-orbit argument and finiteness of orbits are still to come.

Type A got its first pieces. Over a perfect field the ad-nilpotent elements of sl n are exactly [the nilpotent matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/GeneralLinear/AdNilpotent.html#TauCeti.image_setOf_isNilpotent_ad_sl), and over an algebraically closed field [SL and GL conjugacy orbits agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/SpecialLinear.html#Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.orbit_conjAct_specialLinearGroup_eq).

## NilpotentOrbits: 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-26 (`ddc3f97` to `b257e0f`)

The roadmap now has its first group-level objects. For an element x of the Lie algebra of a finite-type affine group, [the centralizer C_G(x)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer.html#TauCeti.centralizerHopfIdeal) is built as the stabilizer of x under the adjoint comodule. Its Lie algebra is [the kernel of ad x](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer.html#TauCeti.lieSubalgebra_centralizerHopfIdeal), and in characteristic zero [dim G = dim C_G(x) + dim [𝔤, x]](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer.html#TauCeti.ringKrullDim_quotient_centralizerHopfIdeal_add_finrank_range_ad). That is the dimension count behind the orbit dimension formula, but the orbit itself is not yet constructed. The supporting results are that every closed subgroup has the dimension of its Lie algebra, and that stabilizers of vectors work in any comodule. On the Lie side, [regular elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/AdjointAction/Regular.html#LieAlgebra.IsCentralizerRegular) are defined by minimal centralizer dimension. Over an infinite field they are [Zariski dense](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/AdjointAction/Regular.html#Module.Basis.vanishingIdeal_setOfPred_isCentralizerRegular_eq_bot), and they [agree with minimality of the group centralizer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Regular.html#Module.Dual.isCentralizerRegular_iff_forall_ringKrullDim_le).

[The Slodowy slice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/SlodowySlice.html#TauCeti.slodowySlice) e + ker(ad f) arrived together with its [contracting action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/SlodowySlice.html#IsSl2Triple.contractingAction), built from a grading automorphism of the Lie algebra. The action [preserves the slice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/SlodowySlice.html#IsSl2Triple.contractingAction_apply_mem_slodowySlice) and [has positive weights n + 2 on it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/SlodowySlice.html#IsSl2Triple.contractingAction_apply_of_mem_ker_inf). Transversality of G × S → 𝔤 is still missing. Triples are now also [Lie embeddings of sl₂](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/Embedding.html#IsSl2Triple.toLieHom), and Jacobson–Morozov and Kostant conjugacy are restated in that form (TauCeti#20235). An algebraic SL₂ homomorphism into G is not among them.

In type A, the ranks of all powers of a matrix can [only drop on the closure of its conjugacy class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Rank/OrbitClosure.html#Matrix.rank_pow_le_of_mem_closure_orbit). This holds in both the analytic and the Zariski topology. It is the easy half of the dominance criterion for orbit closures.

## NilpotentOrbits: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-12 (`b257e0f` to `941bb58`)

Two named theorems lead this window. [The abstract Jordan decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Killing/JordanDecomposition/Basic.html#TauCeti.exists_isNilpotent_ad_isSemisimple_ad) splits every element of a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form, over an algebraically closed field, into commuting ad-nilpotent and ad-semisimple parts, [uniquely](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Killing/JordanDecomposition/Basic.html#TauCeti.isNilpotent_ad_isSemisimple_ad_unique) when the center is trivial. It rests on [the Jordan–Chevalley decomposition of a derivation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Derivation/JordanChevalley.html#LieDerivation.exists_isNilpotent_isSemisimple), and the [canonical parts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Killing/JordanDecomposition/Canonical.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebra.jordanDecomposition) are compatible with isomorphisms, products and faithful representations. In type A, [Gerstenhaber's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Rank/OrbitClosure.html#Matrix.mem_closure_orbit_iff_forall_rank_pow_le) says that Y lies in the closure of the class of a nilpotent X exactly when rank Yᵏ ≤ rank Xᵏ for all k, equivalently when [the Jordan partition of X dominates that of Y](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Rank/OrbitClosure.html#Matrix.mem_closure_orbit_iff_jordanPartition_dominates), in the analytic and the Zariski topology. Jordan-chain bases, [the centralizer dimension Σⱼ(λ′ⱼ)²](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/End/NilpotentCentralizer.html#Module.End.finrank_centralizer_eq_sum_sq_conjugate_jordanPartition) and [orbit dimension n² − Σⱼ(λ′ⱼ)²](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Adjoint/NilpotentOrbit.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.topologicalKrullDim_range_projectiveOrbitMap_add_sum_sq_conjugate_parts) for GLₙ and SLₙ complete the type A picture.

For a reduced group over an algebraically closed field, [the adjoint orbit is now the homogeneous space G/C_G(x)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Orbit.html#TauCeti.fppfHomogeneousQuotientIsoAdjointOrbitPoints), [smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Smooth.html#TauCeti.smoothOfRelativeDimension_adjointOrbit) of dimension dim G − dim C_G(x). That drives the weighted-diagram argument: two triples sharing h are [conjugate under the connected centralizer C_G(h)°](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer/GradeTwoOrbit.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_mem_quotientPointsSubgroup_identityComponent_basePointsRepresentation_eq), the orbit of e is [open in grade two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer/GradeTwoOrbit.html#IsSl2Triple.isOpen_range_projectiveOrbitMap_eigenspace_two_identityComponent), and dominant triples with [equal weighted Dynkin diagrams are conjugate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Lie/Adjoint/Centralizer/GradeTwoOrbit.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_mem_quotientPointsSubgroup_identityComponent_of_weightedDynkinDiagram_eq). With [finitely many dominant neutral elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/WeightedDiagram/Finite.html#RootPairing.Base.finite_dominantNeutralElements), finiteness of nilpotent orbits is proved in steps, not yet chained into one theorem.

Toward the cone and the exponential, [ad-nilpotent elements are the common zeros of the positive-degree invariants](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/AdjointAction/InvariantZeroLocus.html#TauCeti.isNilpotent_ad_iff_forall_lift_eval_eq_zero), [every nilpotent lies in a Borel nilradical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/Borel.html#TauCeti.exists_mem_positiveNilradical_of_isNilpotent_ad), the unipotence ideal of a faithful representation has [a radical independent of the representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/UnipotentPoint/Ideal.html#TauCeti.Comodule.radical_unipotenceIdeal_eq), and [binomial powers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nilpotent/BinomialPow.html#TauCeti.binomialPow) give the one-parameter curve through a unipotent element, with the logarithm as tangent. The Slodowy slice is [transverse to every orbit it meets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/SlodowySlice/Basic.html#IsSl2Triple.codisjoint_range_ad_ker_ad_f) at the level of tangent spaces, and isogenies now transport Lie algebras, centralizers and orbit dimensions.
