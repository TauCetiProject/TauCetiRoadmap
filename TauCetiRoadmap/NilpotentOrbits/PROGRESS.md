## NilpotentOrbits: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`cc6c96d` to `ddc3f97`)

The first work on this roadmap is the Lie-algebraic theory of sl₂-triples, and it lands two named theorems. [The Jacobson–Morozov theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#TauCeti.exists_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad) says that every nonzero ad-nilpotent element e of a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form, in characteristic zero, is the nilpositive element of an sl₂-triple (h, e, f). [Kostant's conjugacy theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/JacobsonMorozov.html#IsSl2Triple.exists_lieEquiv_apply_eq) says that two triples sharing e are conjugate by an automorphism in the subgroup generated by the exp(ad y) with y in ker(ad e) ∩ [e, L] (TauCeti#19650). Both live in a Lie algebra over a field. The roadmap also asks for an algebraic SL₂ homomorphism into the group G and for conjugacy by its unipotent centralizer, and neither is here yet. The supporting work came first: Killing-orthogonality of kernels and ranges of powers of ad x, a trace criterion for nilpotence, Jacobson's lemma on commutators, and [Morozov's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/Morozov.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isSl2Triple_of_isNilpotent_ad) on the uniqueness of f given h and e.

The same sl₂ toolkit gives [the transverse decomposition L = [L, e] ⊕ ker(ad f)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/RangeKer.html#IsSl2Triple.isCompl_range_ad_e_ker_ad_f), which is the linear algebra under the Slodowy slice, and [the identity [e, L(0)] = L(2)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/EigenspaceMap.html#IsSl2Triple.map_ad_e_eigenspace_zero) for the grading by h. It also gives [the {0, 1, 2} bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Sl2/WeightedDiagram.html#IsSl2Triple.root_apply_eq_zero_or_eq_one_or_eq_two): when h is dominant in a Cartan subalgebra, each simple root takes the value 0, 1 or 2 at h. This is the first step toward weighted diagrams, though the open-orbit argument and finiteness of orbits are still to come.

Type A got its first pieces. Over a perfect field the ad-nilpotent elements of sl n are exactly [the nilpotent matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/GeneralLinear/AdNilpotent.html#TauCeti.image_setOf_isNilpotent_ad_sl), and over an algebraically closed field [SL and GL conjugacy orbits agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/SpecialLinear.html#Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.orbit_conjAct_specialLinearGroup_eq).
