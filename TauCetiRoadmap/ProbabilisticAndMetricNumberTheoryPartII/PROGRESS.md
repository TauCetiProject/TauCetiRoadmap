## ProbabilisticAndMetricNumberTheoryPartII: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-19 (`c91a4aa` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first work lays down the geometry of Layer 0. The [ordered projective simplex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.orderedSimplex), the chart image of the positive-ray quotient of the [ordered cone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.orderedCone) `H_n`, is now defined in its `(n − 2)`-dimensional chart, together with the [normalisation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.simplexChart) from the cone, which is constant exactly on positive rays. The simplex is [compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.isCompact_orderedSimplex) and convex, has [nonempty interior](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.interior_orderedSimplex_nonempty) and unique within-derivatives, and lies in the cube `[0, 1/2]^d`.

On top of it come the accelerated branches `F_a` and their denominators `q_a`. The [homogeneous identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Branch.html#TauCeti.linearMove_last_pow_linearMove_of_mem_orderedCone) shows that each [branch](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Branch.html#TauCeti.branchMap) really is the normalised action of the semigroup element `γ_{n−1}^A γ_j` on the cone. Each branch [maps the simplex into itself](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Branch.html#TauCeti.mapsTo_branchMap) and is smooth there. The denominators satisfy `(A + 3)/2 ≤ q_a ≤ A + 2`, so every delay [is at least log(3/2)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Branch.html#TauCeti.log_three_halves_le_log_branchDenom), and the [logarithmic derivative of `q_a` has norm below 2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/OrderedSimplex/Branch.html#TauCeti.norm_fderiv_log_branchDenom_lt_two) uniformly in the letter (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/602). These are the uniform estimates that the contraction argument of Layer 1 is built on.

The rest of Layer 0 has not started. That means the bound `|q_a^{−s}| = q_a^{−Re s}` for the complex weight, the words, delays and renewal count `N(w, r)` with the renewal equation and freeness, and the C¹ jet spaces with their pullback calculus and compact inclusion.
