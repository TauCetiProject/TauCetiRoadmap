## FormalConnectionsOpers: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (`52a7fa5` to `522d48f`)

The cyclic-vector theorem is proved in full: over a differential field of characteristic zero with nonzero derivation, every finite-dimensional differential module has a vector `v` with `v, Dv, …, D^(n−1)v` linearly independent ([`exists_cyclicVector`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Derivation/CyclicVector.html#TauCeti.exists_cyclicVector)), following Deligne's argument (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/1548). This is one of the three theorems `Suggested.lean` states, and the `GL_n` case of the Frenkel–Zhu oper-reduction theorem, which is still open.

Most of the rest builds formal loop groups over arbitrary, possibly nonreduced, coefficient algebras. The functors `LG` and `L⁺G` now exist on native Hopf points ([`loopGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/LoopGroup/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.loopGroup)), with congruence subgroups that intersect trivially ([`iInf_congruenceSubgroup_span_X_pow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/LoopGroup/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.iInf_congruenceSubgroup_span_X_pow)). `L⁺G` is the inverse limit of its jet groups ([`positiveLoopJetLimitMulEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/LoopGroup/Jet.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.positiveLoopJetLimitMulEquiv)), and for formally smooth `G` the quotient `L⁺G/K_N` is the group of `N`-jets. The pole-bounded pieces of `LG` are affine ([`poleBoundedEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/LoopGroup/PoleBounded/Representable.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.poleBoundedEquiv)), and loops satisfy faithfully flat descent in the coefficients ([`loopFaithfullyFlatDescentMulEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/LoopGroup/Descent.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.loopFaithfullyFlatDescentMulEquiv)). The README's warning examples are formalized as well: over a nonreduced ring, `1 + εt⁻¹` is not `tⁿ` times a positive loop of `𝔾ₘ`, and a pole-bounded piece of `𝔾ₐ` is not reduced.

On the connection side, the gauge action `gauge(g, A) = Ad(g)A − dlog g` is defined for points of any affine group along any derivation ([`pointGauge`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Tangent/Gauge.html#Derivation.pointGauge)). It is a left action, `dlog` is a crossed homomorphism, and the action specializes to matrices for `GL_n` and to `A − u′/u` for `𝔾ₘ`. Formal coordinate changes now act on Laurent series over every commutative ring ([`substAlgEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Substitution/Coordinate.html#TauCeti.LaurentSeries.substAlgEquiv)), with the chain rule and invariance of the residue, and they act on loop groups as well.
