## CalibratedGeometry: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-13 (`4bcb355` to `aa6e43e`)

The roadmap's first end theorem is in: comass, defined as the supremum of a real alternating form's absolute values on orthonormal frames, [equals the native operator norm](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Orthonormal/Alternating.html#ContinuousAlternatingMap.norm_eq_sSup_abs_orthonormal), in every degree, including degree zero and degrees above the dimension, where both sides vanish (TauCeti#39191). It rests on a [bound criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Orthonormal/Alternating.html#ContinuousAlternatingMap.opNorm_le_iff_le_orthonormal): a bound on all orthonormal frames bounds the operator norm, for maps into any real normed space and with no finite-dimensionality assumption. It is proved by restricting to the span of an independent frame and using the top-degree formula there. This is a more direct route than the README's, which went through the comass form of the linear calibration inequality. On a finite-dimensional space, compactness of the Stiefel manifold shows that [the norm is attained](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/Stiefel/Comass.html#ContinuousAlternatingMap.exists_orthonormal_norm_eq) on some orthonormal frame whenever the degree is at most the dimension, so comass is a maximum.

The rest of Layer 1 has not started. That covers the norm-determinant form of frame volume, the upper bound comparing comass with the Hilbert norm, the oriented Grassmannian of a general space, equality planes and external products. Nothing has landed yet for the later layers.

## CalibratedGeometry: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`aa6e43e` to `b235000`)

The fundamental theorem of calibrations has landed in its closed-source, real-homology form. [An immersion calibrated by a smooth calibration has volume at most that of any compact oriented competitor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Calibrated/FundamentalTheorem.html#TauCeti.immersionVolume_le_of_isCalibratedImmersion) whose integrals agree with its own on every smooth closed form of that degree (TauCeti#39992). The competitor may have boundary or corners. In [the equality case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Calibrated/FundamentalTheorem.html#TauCeti.immersionVolume_eq_iff_isCalibratedImmersion), the volumes are equal exactly when the competitor is calibrated as well. Both statements hold in every degree, including zero. The theorem needed three new definitions: [smooth calibrations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Calibrated/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsCalibration), which are smooth closed forms of pointwise comass at most one; [calibrated immersions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Calibrated/Immersion.html#TauCeti.IsCalibratedImmersion); and [the volume of an immersion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/Volume.html#TauCeti.immersionVolume). Around them came the pointwise and integrated calibration inequalities, together with supporting lemmas on pullback metrics and Riemannian volume forms. The chain form of the theorem, where the competitor differs from the calibrated immersion by the boundary of an integral smooth chain, is not there yet. Neither is the integral-homology version. Both wait on Layer 2's chain machinery.

Most of Layer 1's linear algebra is now in place. [Calibrated frames](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Orthonormal/CalibratedFrame.html#ContinuousAlternatingMap.IsCalibratedFrame) are orthonormal frames on which a form takes the value one. They come with their isometry and SO(k) invariance, and [a form of comass one calibrates some frame](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/Stiefel/CalibratedFrame.html#ContinuousAlternatingMap.exists_isCalibratedFrame_of_norm_eq_one). The linear calibration inequality now has [its Jacobian form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ExteriorPower/NormDet.html#ContinuousAlternatingMap.le_opNorm_mul_normDet). Comass is also compared with the Hilbert norm of forms in both directions; [the upper bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Comass.html#ContinuousAlternatingMap.sqrt_alternatingFormInner_self_le) carries the factor √(n choose k). Still missing are the oriented Grassmannian of a general space, and with it the equality locus on that Grassmannian, as well as external products of forms.
