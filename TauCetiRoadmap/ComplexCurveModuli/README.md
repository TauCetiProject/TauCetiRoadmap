# Analytic families and moduli of complex curves

Construct compact marked nodal complex curves, their actual analytic families and deformation groupoids, universal stable-pointed unfoldings, a countable étale chart presentation, analytic compactness/properness and coherent marking operations. The domain carrier is **prestable**. Its genus-zero components with at most two special flags and genus-one components with none have positive-dimensional automorphisms. They are needed by map moduli but are not orbifold points of the stable-pointed domain moduli. This roadmap supplies domain geometry, not map-dependent stability, moving-map compactness or virtual classes.

A smooth component uses native `ChartedSpace ℂ` and complex manifold/holomorphic-map vocabulary; no second Riemann-surface type is introduced. A curve has a finite nonempty collection of compact connected normalized components, a finite set of distinct node flags with a fixed-point-free pairing, and ordered distinct smooth marks. Its dual graph G is connected, equivalently the normalization quotient is connected; empty or disconnected curves are outside this carrier. Its arithmetic genus is `g=Σg_v+b₁(G)`. A self-node uses two different flags on the same component. Stable pointedness means `2g_v−2+n_v>0` on **each** normalized component, with node branches counted as flags. Isomorphisms retain graph/branch/mark permutations when permitted, and every actual automorphism, including ineffective isotropy.

Bases are finite-dimensional Hausdorff second-countable complex manifolds. A restricted analytic nodal family is proper with compact connected reduced nodal fibres, disjoint holomorphic smooth-locus sections, and local models `U×Δ` and `N_s={ (b,x,y)∈U×Δ² | xy=s(b) }` with their quotient **sheaves** of holomorphic germs. Here Δ is an open coordinate disc. Closed discs/annuli used in a Hardy decomposition are compact subdomains of larger open charts. The total space of N_s can be singular. The universal one-node smoothing is `xy=a` with a a base coordinate; a one-dimensional-base pullback has `a=s(t)`, with s either zero or a unit times `t^m` after centring. Thus permanent nodes and `xy=b²` are legal families. Use ComplexComparisonPartII C−1.1’s generic `ComplexAnalyticSpace` carrier and D2.0’s native-manifold/zero-ideal instance adapter: each restricted chart is its coherent analytic closed quotient, and its open gluing/fibre product is the supplied generic operation. This roadmap constructs the specific N_s instances and their family theorems, not a parallel general analytic-space category. Its node equations are proved reduced; the generic carrier still retains nilpotents on other analytic spaces.

Family morphisms over a holomorphic base map φ:A→B are **Cartesian isomorphisms** `P≅φ*Q` preserving sections and designated flags, with the induced total map P→Q. They compose by the proved base-change associators. A contraction is a different kind of map, built in D7. Composition of two curve projections is not a curve family. For each fixed base germ, families and isomorphisms form a groupoid; arbitrary base maps act contravariantly by pullback, not as invertible arrows in that groupoid.
