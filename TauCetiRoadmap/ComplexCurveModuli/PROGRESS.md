## ComplexCurveModuli: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`bdc7005` to `273ea28`)

The first work on this roadmap lays down three independent pieces of groundwork for nodal curves and their deformations. The most substantial is the fractional Sobolev space [`H^s` on the circle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev), defined by Fourier coefficients with the weight `(1+|n|)^s` for real `s`, exactly as D3.1 asks for boundary circles. It comes with the isometry to `ℓ²(ℤ)`, completeness, [density of trigonometric polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.dense_finite_support_coeff), the continuous inclusions `H^s → H^t` for `t ≤ s`, and [Rellich's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.isCompactOperator_inclusion), which says these inclusions are compact for `t < s` when the target is finite-dimensional. For `s > 1/2` an element is a [continuous function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.toContinuousMap) whose Fourier series converges uniformly (TauCeti#19036). The product estimates, Nemytskii maps, reparametrization invariance and Hilbert manifolds of boundary maps that D3.1 also asks for have not started.

On the D1 side, the [local ring of a node](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/NodeLocalRing.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.nodeLocalRing) is now defined the way the roadmap prescribes, as pairs of branch germs with equal values, with no hypersurface chart. It is a local ring with residue field `ℂ`, and the two branch coordinates satisfy [`xy = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/NodeLocalRing.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.nodeLocalRing.fstCoordinate_mul_sndCoordinate) and generate the maximal ideal. The D2 comparison with `ℂ{x,y}/(xy)` is still to come. Separately, quotients that glue only finitely many points are shown to be [Hausdorff](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Constructions/FiniteGluing.html#Setoid.t2Space_quotient), with a closed relation and an open embedding of the complement of the glued points. This is the topology of the nodal-curve carrier, though the carrier itself is not yet defined.

## ComplexCurveModuli: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

The nodal curve now exists as an object. From [flag-pair data](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/Basic.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.FlagPairData) (normalization components plus pairwise distinct glued points) the [glued curve](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/Basic.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.GluedCurve) is the [Hausdorff](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/Basic.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.t2Space_gluedCurve) quotient. Its structure sheaf is holomorphic functions agreeing at glued points, and its stalks are now identified: the holomorphic stalk of the normalization [away from the nodes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/StructureSheaf.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.quotientStalkBranchEquiv), and the branch-pair node ring [at a node](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/StructureSheaf.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.quotientStalkToNodeEquiv) (TauCeti#19955). [Isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/Iso.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.Iso) are biholomorphisms of normalizations that respect flag pairs. They induce the dual-graph maps and descend [faithfully](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/Iso.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.Iso.gluedCurveHomeomorph_injective) to the glued curve, and the [automorphism group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/Iso.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.Aut) acts on branches, nodes and components. On top of these sit D1's two auxiliary-mark groupoids: the [unordered](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/AuxiliaryMarks.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.AuxiliaryMarkedCurve.unorderedAuxiliaryIsomorphismGroupoid) one, with [finitely many arrows](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/AuxiliaryMarks.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.AuxiliaryIso.finite) between two curves, and its [colour-preserving](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/PartitionedAuxiliaryMarks.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.AuxiliaryMarkedCurve.partitionedAuxiliaryIsomorphismGroupoid) wide subgroupoid. Lifting of isomorphisms and stability-iff-finite-automorphisms are still missing. On the dual graph, [smoothing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/DualGraph/Smoothing.html#TauCeti.DualGraph.arithmeticGenus_smoothing) and [cutting](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/DualGraph/Cutting.html#TauCeti.DualGraph.sum_arithmeticGenus_cutComponent_add_card) nodes now account correctly for the arithmetic genus.

The circle space `H^s` for `s > 1/2` became a Banach algebra, with the [product estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Algebra.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.norm_mul_le) and [Wiener's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Spectrum.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.isUnit_iff_forall_isUnit): an element is invertible exactly when its values are. Holomorphic functions act on it by a [Nemytskii map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Nemytskii.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.analyticOnNhd_nemytskii) that is itself analytic. For every real `s`, multiplication by a nowhere-vanishing smooth function is an [automorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.mulFourierSeriesEquiv), and [rotations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Rotation.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.rotate) act with derivative given by [differentiation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Derivative.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.derivative). General circle diffeomorphisms, smooth Nemytskii maps and Hilbert manifolds of boundary maps are still to come.

## ComplexCurveModuli: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-09 (`e8ea0d1` to `7ca783d`)

The headline is D4.3's [nonlinear Hardy graph theorem at a plumbing node](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/HardyGraph.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.exists_analyticAt_hardyGraph) (TauCeti#24570). Near the trivial data, boundary maps on the two circles of `xy = a` that extend holomorphically across the neck are determined by their positive Laurent modes, and depend holomorphically on those modes and on `a`, including at the node `a = 0`. It sits on the linear [neck graph](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Neck.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.isCompl_neckGraph), a complement of the negative Laurent subspaces, and on an ambient neck evaluation that is [jointly holomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Plumbing/Basic.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.analyticAt_neckEvaluation_mul) and has a [local inverse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Plumbing/Inverse.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.neckEvaluationChart) at the node. The global Hardy matching is not started. Circle diffeomorphisms now act on `H^s` for every real `s` ([fractional](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/FractionalPrecomp.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.precompFractionalEquivOfLift), [positive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Diffeomorphism.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.precompSmoothEquivOfLift) and [negative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/NegativeOrder.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.precompNonposEquivOfLift) orders).

D1 is close to done. A node-preserving homeomorphism of glued curves [comes from a unique analytic isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/HolomorphicLift.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.existsUnique_iso_gluedCurveHomeomorph_eq_iff) exactly when its normalization lift is holomorphic off the flag points. The curve is a [locally ringed space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/RingedSpace.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.locallyRingedSpace) whose nodes are intrinsic, and it has a dual graph satisfying the [genus formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/DualGraph.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.arithmeticGenus_dualGraph_add_card). Whether the automorphism group is finite is now [decided component by component](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/FiniteAutomorphisms.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.AuxiliaryAut.finite_ordered_iff), and the question is settled for rational and elliptic components. Genus at least two is still open. On the D2 side, the node ring is [`ℂ{x,y}/(xy)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/PlaneNode.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.nodeLocalRing.planeNodeEquiv), and `N_s` has [product charts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/OffNodeChart.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.offNodeChartFst_bijective) away from the node.

Groundwork for D0 and D3.6 arrived as well. On a closed surface the hyperbolic metric in a conformal class [is unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/ClosedSurface.html#TauCeti.HyperbolicMetric.eq_of_metric_eq_rescale), and the Liouville energy is [coercive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/LiouvilleEnergy.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_le_liouvilleEnergy_add). Existence is not yet proved. Boundary-circle maps [extend across collars](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Diffeotopy/BoundaryCircle.html#TauCeti.exists_diffeotopy_extend_boundaryCircles_of_lifts), and ordered disc configurations [are isotopic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeomorphism/Diffeotopy/DiscConfiguration.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.exists_diffeotopy_eqOn_of_pairwise_disjoint). For product families, [integrability is now equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Family/Nijenhuis.html#TauCeti.ComplexStructureFamily.nijenhuis_eq_zero_iff) to the corrected mixed and horizontal Robbin–Salamon equations.
