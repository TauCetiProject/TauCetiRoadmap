## ComplexCurveModuli: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`bdc7005` to `273ea28`)

The first work on this roadmap lays down three independent pieces of groundwork for nodal curves and their deformations. The most substantial is the fractional Sobolev space [`H^s` on the circle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev), defined by Fourier coefficients with the weight `(1+|n|)^s` for real `s`, exactly as D3.1 asks for boundary circles. It comes with the isometry to `ℓ²(ℤ)`, completeness, [density of trigonometric polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.dense_finite_support_coeff), the continuous inclusions `H^s → H^t` for `t ≤ s`, and [Rellich's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.isCompactOperator_inclusion), which says these inclusions are compact for `t < s` when the target is finite-dimensional. For `s > 1/2` an element is a [continuous function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.toContinuousMap) whose Fourier series converges uniformly (TauCeti#19036). The product estimates, Nemytskii maps, reparametrization invariance and Hilbert manifolds of boundary maps that D3.1 also asks for have not started.

On the D1 side, the [local ring of a node](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/NodeLocalRing.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.nodeLocalRing) is now defined the way the roadmap prescribes, as pairs of branch germs with equal values, with no hypersurface chart. It is a local ring with residue field `ℂ`, and the two branch coordinates satisfy [`xy = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/NodeLocalRing.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.nodeLocalRing.fstCoordinate_mul_sndCoordinate) and generate the maximal ideal. The D2 comparison with `ℂ{x,y}/(xy)` is still to come. Separately, quotients that glue only finitely many points are shown to be [Hausdorff](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Constructions/FiniteGluing.html#Setoid.t2Space_quotient), with a closed relation and an open embedding of the complement of the glued points. This is the topology of the nodal-curve carrier, though the carrier itself is not yet defined.
