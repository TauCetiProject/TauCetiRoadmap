## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Nodal curves (Layer 1) are built and their isomorphisms lift, and the local algebra of the node charts is largely done: they are reduced and flat. The node-local Hardy theory of Layer 4, the circle `H^s` theory and the variational existence of hyperbolic metrics are substantial, but nothing has been assembled into families, a universal unfolding or a smooth uniformization theorem. Layers 6–8 have not begun.

### Named results

- **The nonlinear Hardy graph theorem at a node.** Near the trivial data, boundary maps on the two circles of the plumbing `xy = a` that extend holomorphically across the neck are fixed by their positive Laurent modes. They depend holomorphically on those modes and on `a`, including at the node `a = 0` ([`CircleSobolev.exists_analyticAt_hardyGraph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/HardyGraph.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.exists_analyticAt_hardyGraph)).
- **Lifting isomorphisms of nodal curves.** A node-preserving homeomorphism of glued curves comes from a unique analytic isomorphism exactly when its normalization lift is holomorphic off the flag points ([`NodalGluing.existsUnique_iso_gluedCurveHomeomorph_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/HolomorphicLift.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.existsUnique_iso_gluedCurveHomeomorph_eq_iff)).
- **Flatness of the node charts.** For every holomorphic `s`, the local rings of `xy = s(b)` are flat over the base, including permanent nodes and singular total spaces such as `xy = b²` ([`flat_quotientMk_comp_stalkPrecomp_smoothingEquation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/Node/Flat.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.flat_quotientMk_comp_stalkPrecomp_smoothingEquation)). The node equation is also [radical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/Node/Reduced.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.isRadical_span_nodeEquation).
- **A weak solution of the curvature equation.** On a closed connected surface with `∫ K < 0`, the Liouville energy has a minimizer in `H¹`, and it solves `Δw + K + e^(2w) = 0` weakly ([`SobolevH1.exists_forall_dirichletForm_eq_integral_neg_add_exp_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/LiouvilleEnergy/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.Manifold.SobolevH1.exists_forall_dirichletForm_eq_integral_neg_add_exp_mul)). The hyperbolic metric in a conformal class is already known to be [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/ClosedSurface.html#TauCeti.HyperbolicMetric.eq_of_metric_eq_rescale).
- **Stability of rational and elliptic components.** A nodal curve has finitely many automorphisms exactly when each component does after fixing its special points ([`AuxiliaryAut.finite_ordered_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/FiniteAutomorphisms.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.AuxiliaryAut.finite_ordered_iff)). The cases of spheres and tori are settled.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`NodalGluing.locallyRingedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/RingedSpace.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.locallyRingedSpace) makes the glued curve a locally ringed space with intrinsic nodes.
- The [neck smoothing chart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Plumbing/Smoothing.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.neckSmoothingChart) is a holomorphic change of smoothing coordinate at a node. Combined with ambient neck evaluation, it carries any source equation `xy = s(t)` to the target node equation. This is the single-node form of §4.5's pullback of the universal Hardy graph.
- The [Cayley chart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Algebra/CayleyChart.html#TauCeti.cayleyHomeomorph) `η ↦ (1 + η)⁻¹ j (1 + η)` parametrizes the square roots of `−1` near `j` in a Banach algebra, with analytic transitions. It is the algebraic core of §3.5's chart of complex structures.

## The frontier

- **Smooth hyperbolic metrics on closed surfaces (§0.4).** A weak `H¹` solution exists, and weak semilinear solutions are smooth on Euclidean domains of dimension at most three. What remains is to transfer that regularity through charts to the surface and conclude through the existing reduction of existence to the curvature equation. Cusps and geodesic boundary follow.
- **Families as analytic spaces (§§2.0–2.3).** The node charts need to become objects of ComplexComparisonPartII §C-1.1's category, with fibre products and Cartesian morphisms. The external category is the prerequisite.
- **The `W^(k,p)` chart of complex structures (§3.5).** Extend the Cayley chart to Sobolev endomorphism fields, then prove the offset implicit function theorem and the holomorphic marked slices.
- **Global Hardy matching (§§4.2–4.3).** This needs the `U` and `V` Hilbert submanifolds, injectivity of the trace on the core, and the kernel and cokernel restrictions. It needs §3.2's boundary Cauchy–Riemann estimate, which has not started.
- **Stability in genus at least two (§0.5).** Properness of the diffeomorphism action needs the canonical metrics of §0.4.
