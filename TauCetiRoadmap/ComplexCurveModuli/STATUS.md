### Named results

- **The nonlinear Hardy graph theorem at a node** — near the trivial data, boundary maps on the two circles of the plumbing `xy = a` that extend holomorphically across the neck are fixed by their positive Laurent modes. They depend holomorphically on those modes and on `a`, also at the node `a = 0` ([`CircleSobolev.exists_analyticAt_hardyGraph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/HardyGraph.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.exists_analyticAt_hardyGraph)).
- **Lifting isomorphisms of nodal curves** — a node-preserving homeomorphism of glued curves comes from a unique analytic nodal isomorphism exactly when its normalization lift is holomorphic off the flag points ([`NodalGluing.existsUnique_iso_gluedCurveHomeomorph_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/HolomorphicLift.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.existsUnique_iso_gluedCurveHomeomorph_eq_iff)). The same holds for isomorphisms of the ringed spaces ([`NodalGluing.existsUnique_iso_sheafedSpaceIso_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/RingedLift.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.existsUnique_iso_sheafedSpaceIso_eq_iff)).
- **Stability of rational and elliptic components** — a nodal curve has finitely many automorphisms exactly when each component does after fixing its special points ([`AuxiliaryAut.finite_ordered_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/FiniteAutomorphisms.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.AuxiliaryAut.finite_ordered_iff)). For tori this means each component carries a special point ([`finite_ordered_iff_forall_specialPoints_nonempty_of_complexTorus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/FiniteAutomorphisms.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.AuxiliaryAut.finite_ordered_iff_forall_specialPoints_nonempty_of_complexTorus)), and projective lines need more than two.
- **The node ring is `ℂ{x,y}/(xy)`** — restriction to the two axes identifies the branch-pair ring with the plane-curve quotient ([`nodeLocalRing.planeNodeEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/PlaneNode.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.nodeLocalRing.planeNodeEquiv)). - **Uniqueness of the hyperbolic metric in a conformal class** — on a closed surface two conformal hyperbolic metrics coincide ([`HyperbolicMetric.eq_of_metric_eq_rescale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/ClosedSurface.html#TauCeti.HyperbolicMetric.eq_of_metric_eq_rescale)), and existence is equivalent to solving the curvature equation ([`exists_hyperbolicMetric_metric_eq_rescale_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic/ClosedSurface.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.exists_hyperbolicMetric_metric_eq_rescale_iff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`NodalGluing.locallyRingedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/RingedSpace.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.locallyRingedSpace) makes the glued curve a locally ringed space. Nodes are intrinsic, being exactly the points where the stalk [is not a domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Curves/NodalGluing/IntrinsicNodes.html#TauCeti.NodalGluing.isDomain_quotientStalk_iff). This is the object that D2's families will have as fibres.
- The [neck graph](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Neck.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.neckGraph) of `xy = a` and the [ambient neck chart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Circle/Plumbing/Inverse.html#TauCeti.CircleSobolev.neckEvaluationChart) are the linear and coordinate halves of D4.3. Together they turn boundary data into holomorphic maps near the node, and D4.5's singular pullbacks will reuse them.
- The block form of almost complex structures on product families comes with [the Nijenhuis criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Family/Nijenhuis.html#TauCeti.ComplexStructureFamily.nijenhuis_eq_zero_iff) in the corrected Robbin–Salamon mixed and horizontal equations, and with the [marked-section equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Family/Section.html#TauCeti.ComplexStructureFamily.isPseudoholomorphic_graph_iff). These are D3.6 and the inputs to D4.1's universality proof.

## The frontier

- **Existence of the hyperbolic metric (D0.4)**: coercivity of the Liouville energy, its Euler–Lagrange equation, barriers and the two-dimensional exponential estimate are proved. What remains is a minimizer in the right Sobolev class and its elliptic bootstrap to a smooth solution. Cusps and geodesic boundary come after that.
- **Global Hardy matching (D4.3)**: the single-node graph exists near the trivial data. The `U`/`V` Hilbert submanifolds, trace injectivity on the core and the kernel/cokernel restriction isomorphisms remain.
- **The `N_s` charts at the node (D2.1)**: radicality of `(xy − s)` at the node, and the restricted families as objects of the ComplexComparisonPartII C−1.1 analytic-space category. The D2.0 native-manifold instance is also needed.
- **Hilbert manifolds of boundary maps (D3.1)**: smooth Nemytskii maps and variable reparametrizations with their derivative offsets, which D4.2 needs.
- **Smooth normal forms and classification (D0.1)**: the handle-reduction normal form and the Euler-characteristic invariants. The extension and isotopy pieces it composes with are in place.
