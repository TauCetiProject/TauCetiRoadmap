## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-27 (`9df480c` to `cc3d579`)

The roadmap's central one-variable theorems landed: [Weierstrass division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq) and [Weierstrass preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq) over a complete ultrametric Banach ring, at every positive radius, for a divisor whose distinguished coefficient is a multiplicative unit (Martin, Proposition 1.27 and Corollary 1.28). The coefficient norm need not be multiplicative. Division comes with [the norm identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DivMod.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.gaussNorm_eq_max) `‖f‖ = max(‖g‖ ‖q‖, ‖r‖)`, bounded quotient and remainder operators, and [naturality under bounded coefficient maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Map.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.map_weierstrassDiv). [Division in several variables](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/WeierstrassDivision.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished) follows by iterating over the coefficient restricted algebra (TauCeti#20363). Preparation in several variables has not landed yet.

On the Tate-algebra side, [Tate algebras over a complete nonarchimedean field are unique factorisation domains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid). A restricted series is [a unit exactly when its constant coefficient is a unit that strictly dominates every other weighted coefficient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Units.html#MvPowerSeries.isUnit_iff_isUnit_constantCoeff_and_norm_coeff_mul_prod_lt), the Gauss unit ball is [the power-bounded subring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UnitBall.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.powerBoundedSubring_eq_unitClosedBall), and [reduction identifies `T°/T°°` with polynomials over the residue field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UnitBall.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedReductionQuotientEquiv). [Strassmann's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le) bounds the number of zeros of a one-variable series in a closed disc by its distinguished degree. At radii in the divisible hull of the value group, the restricted algebra is [finite over the unit Tate algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Expand.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.exists_injective_finite_algHom_of_norm_mul_pow_eq_one).

Supporting work built out the other layers: [analytic substitution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst) with isometric translations, Hasse derivatives in one and several variables with their Gauss-norm estimate, radius restriction and rescaling, and modules of restricted coefficient families. The [entire](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Basic.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire) and [open-disc](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/Basic.html#PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc) series algebras arrived too. Entire series can now be evaluated in any complete normed algebra and divided uniquely by a polynomial with invertible leading coefficient, and they have [the entire resultant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.exists_C_entireResultant_eq) with its Bézout identity and [normalised entire preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.exists_isEntirePreparation).
