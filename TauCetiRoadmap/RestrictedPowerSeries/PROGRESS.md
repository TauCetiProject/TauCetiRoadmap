## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-11-06 (`9df480c` to `a67a650`)

The Tate algebra K⟨X₁, …, Xₙ⟩ over a complete nonarchimedean field now has most of its classical algebraic structure. It is a [unique factorisation domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid), its [maximal ideals are generated by polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/MaximalIdeal.html#Ideal.map_comap_polynomialToRestricted_of_isMaximal), and on the completed Huber carrier it satisfies the [Nullstellensatz](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/TateAlgebra.html#Ideal.finite_quotient_of_isMaximal_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion) (residue fields are finite over K) and is [Jacobson](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/TateAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Huber.isJacobsonRing_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion) and [regular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/TateAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Huber.isRegularRing_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion) of Krull dimension n (TauCeti#25579). Underneath this is Weierstrass theory over Banach rings, with no multiplicative norm required, only a divisor whose distinguished coefficient is a multiplicative unit: [division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq) and [preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq) at every positive radius, iterated to [several variables](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished), with the norm identities and naturality under bounded coefficient maps. A dual-number example checks it all over a ring with nilpotents.

The analysis around these results also filled in: [analytic substitution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst) with translations, affine changes of coordinates and the chain rule; multivariable Hasse derivatives and Taylor expansion; [Strassmann's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le); and reduction of the unit ball to polynomials over the residue field. For modules, submodules of free Tate modules get [strict generators of norm one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Submodule.html#Submodule.exists_finset_norm_eq_one_isRestricted_subring), and finite presentations stay exact after adjoining restricted variables.

Open-disc and entire series arrived with their topologies, evaluation and translation, along with division of entire series by polynomials, the [entire resultant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.entireResultant) (which is a unit exactly when the pair is coprime), and [normalised entire preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isEntirePreparation). Overconvergent series gained analytic substitution. Still missing: the maximum modulus principle over finite extensions, least-norm representatives for quotients by submodules, and the adic-ring comparisons.
