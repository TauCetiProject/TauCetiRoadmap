## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-27 (`9df480c` to `dd217c4`)

Weierstrass division and preparation now hold over an ultrametric Banach ring at every positive radius, with no multiplicative norm on the coefficients. Dividing by a series whose distinguished coefficient is a multiplicative unit gives a unique quotient and polynomial remainder ([`existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq)), and such a series is uniquely a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq)); these are Martin's Proposition 1.27 and Corollary 1.28. Quotient and remainder are bounded operators satisfying the norm identity `‖f‖ = max (‖g‖ ‖q‖) ‖r‖` ([`gaussNorm_eq_max`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DivMod.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.gaussNorm_eq_max)). They commute with bounded coefficient maps when both divisors are distinguished, and division iterates to several variables ([`existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/WeierstrassDivision.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished)). Several-variable preparation has not landed.

Tate algebras over a complete nonarchimedean field are now unique factorisation domains ([`uniqueFactorizationMonoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid)). Strassmann's theorem bounds the number of zeros of a restricted series in a closed disc by its distinguished degree ([`encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le)). The integral theory at unit radius also arrived, ending in the reduction isomorphism `T° ⧸ T°° ≃ 𝓀[K][X]` ([`restrictedReductionQuotientEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UnitBall.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedReductionQuotientEquiv)).

A third strand defined [entire](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Basic.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire) and [open-disc](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/Basic.html#PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc) series on native power series. Entire series gained unique division by a polynomial with invertible leading coefficient ([`existsUnique_entire_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.existsUnique_entire_mul_add_eq)), a monic resultant that detects coprimality ([`isUnit_entireResultant_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff)), and normalised preparation ([`existsUnique_isEntirePreparation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isEntirePreparation)). Supporting work added analytic substitution and translation, Hasse derivatives with Gauss-norm bounds, radius restriction and rescaling, and module-valued restricted families.
