## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-11-06 (`9df480c` to `afa08a3`)

The main work of this window was Weierstrass theory over Banach rings at every positive radius, with no multiplicativity assumed of the coefficient norm. Division by a multiplicatively distinguished series gives a unique quotient and polynomial remainder with the expected norm bounds ([`existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq)), and such a series factors uniquely as a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq)); these are Martin's Proposition 1.27 and Corollary 1.28. Both were iterated to several variables, in any chosen variable ([division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished), [preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished)), and shown to commute with bounded coefficient maps. The groundwork arrived alongside: multiplicative units and the unit criterion for restricted series, analytic substitution and translation, Hasse derivatives with a chain rule, and module-valued restricted series with their `c₀` coefficient model.

The Tate algebra `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` gained most of its structure theory. It is a [unique factorisation domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid), its [maximal ideals are generated by polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/MaximalIdeal.html#Ideal.map_comap_polynomialToRestricted_of_isMaximal), one triangular automorphism makes finitely many nonzero series distinguished at once, and ideals and submodules of finite free modules have strict generating systems. On the completed Huber carrier the algebra is Jacobson and [regular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/TateAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Huber.isRegularRing_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion), of Krull dimension `n`, with residue fields at maximal ideals finite over `K`. The Jacobson property is so far stated only on that completed carrier.

Beyond the closed disc, open-disc and entire series became subrings carrying the topology of all their Gauss norms, complete with dense polynomials. Entire series admit unique division by polynomials with invertible leading coefficient, a normalised preparation, and an entire resultant which [is a unit exactly when the two arguments are coprime](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff). Overconvergent series gained analytic substitution and translation. Still absent: the maximum modulus principle at finite-extension points, quotient norms on finite Tate modules, and the adic and tensor comparisons of Layer 6.
