## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-27 (`9df480c` to `dd217c4`)

[Weierstrass division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq) and [preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq) now work at every positive real radius over complete ultrametric normed commutative rings, assuming a multiplicatively distinguished divisor. Division gives a unique restricted quotient and a polynomial remainder of smaller degree; preparation gives a unique multiplicative-unit factor times a normalised monic polynomial. Neither requires a globally multiplicative coefficient norm. [Several-variable division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/WeierstrassDivision.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished) is also available when the divisor is multiplicatively distinguished over the coefficient restricted algebra.

Finite-variable Tate algebras over complete nonarchimedean fields are now [unique factorisation domains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid). On the analytic side, [Strassmann’s theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le) bounds the number of distinct zeros in a closed disc by the largest index attaining the Gauss norm, including zeros in complete isometrically normed field extensions.

[Entire division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.existsUnique_entire_mul_add_eq) retains an entire quotient when the polynomial divisor has invertible leading coefficient. The [entire resultant detects coprimality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff): for monic P and entire F, its value is a unit exactly when P and F are coprime in the entire-series ring. [Normalised entire preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isEntirePreparation) gives F = PG with P(0) = G(0) = 1 when F(0) = 1 and F is multiplicatively distinguished at the chosen radius; G is entire and invertible on that closed disc. These results establish substantial parts of the roadmap, but the partly truncated evidence does not establish the remaining comparison and finite-module interfaces.
