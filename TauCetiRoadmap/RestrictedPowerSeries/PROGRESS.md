## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-27 (`9df480c` to `cc3d579`)

[Weierstrass division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.exists_mul_add_eq) and [preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.exists_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq) now work at arbitrary positive radii over complete ultrametric Banach rings with a multiplicatively distinguished divisor: division gives a unique restricted quotient and polynomial remainder, while preparation factors the divisor into a multiplicative unit and a monic polynomial. The distinguished coefficient must be a multiplicative unit; the coefficient norm need not be globally multiplicative. [Several-variable division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/WeierstrassDivision.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished) carries the division theorem and its norm estimates through the isometric splitting of a variable.

Finite-variable Tate algebras over complete nonarchimedean fields are [unique factorisation domains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid). [Strassmann’s theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le) bounds the number of distinct zeros in a closed disc by the greatest index attaining the Gauss norm. These are substantial algebraic and function-theoretic milestones, without establishing the roadmap’s full dimension theory or module-valued coefficient recovery.

The entire-series algebra has unique [entire polynomial division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.existsUnique_entire_mul_add_eq) for divisors with invertible leading coefficient, and its [entire resultant detects coprimality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff). [Normalised entire preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isEntirePreparation) factors an entire series with constant coefficient one into polynomial and entire factors, both normalised at zero, with the entire factor invertible on the chosen closed disc. The topology of all Gauss norms on the entire algebra is not established by the supplied record.
