## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-27 (`9df480c` to `cc3d579`)

[Weierstrass division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq) and [preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq) now hold at every positive radius over a complete ultrametric Banach ring, provided the divisor is multiplicatively distinguished. Division gives a unique restricted quotient and a polynomial remainder of smaller degree; preparation gives a unique monic polynomial times a multiplicative unit. [Division in several variables](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/WeierstrassDivision.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished) is available by separating one variable. The corresponding several-variable preparation interface is not established in the supplied record.

Tate algebras in finitely many variables over a complete nonarchimedean field are [unique factorisation domains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid). [Strassmann’s theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le) bounds the number of distinct zeros in a closed ball by the greatest index attaining the Gauss norm, including zeros in complete normed field extensions.

For entire series, [polynomial division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.existsUnique_entire_mul_add_eq) retains an entire quotient when the leading coefficient is invertible. The [monic entire resultant detects coprimality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff), and [normalised entire preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isEntirePreparation) gives a unique polynomial-times-entire factorisation for a multiplicatively distinguished entire series with constant coefficient one. Open-disc series also acquired [completeness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/Topology.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc.instCompleteSpaceSubtypePowerSeriesMemSubringSubring) and [polynomial approximation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/Topology.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc.denseRange_polynomialHom) for the topology of all smaller-radius Gauss norms; this does not establish the corresponding topology for entire series.
