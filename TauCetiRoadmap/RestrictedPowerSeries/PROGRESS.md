## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-11-06 (`9df480c` to `afa08a3`)

The main work of this window was Weierstrass theory over Banach rings at every positive radius, with no multiplicativity assumed of the coefficient norm. Division by a multiplicatively distinguished series gives a unique quotient and polynomial remainder ([`existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq)), with the norm identity `‖g q + r‖ = max (‖g‖ ‖q‖) ‖r‖` ([`gaussNorm_mul_add_eq_max`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.gaussNorm_mul_add_eq_max)), and such a series factors uniquely as a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq)); these are Martin's Proposition 1.27 and Corollary 1.28. Both were iterated to several variables, in any chosen variable ([division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished), [preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished)), and shown to commute with bounded coefficient maps. The groundwork arrived alongside: multiplicative units and the unit criterion for restricted series, analytic substitution and translation, Hasse derivatives, a chain rule for partial derivatives under analytic substitution, coherence laws for iteration in two families of variables ([TauCeti#26352](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26352)), and module-valued restricted series with their `c₀` coefficient model, at unit radius and, by scalar rescaling, at radii realised by scalars ([TauCeti#25350](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25350)).

The Tate algebra `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` gained most of its structure theory. It is a [unique factorisation domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid), its [maximal ideals are generated by polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/MaximalIdeal.html#Ideal.map_comap_polynomialToRestricted_of_isMaximal), one triangular automorphism makes finitely many nonzero series distinguished at once, and ideals and submodules of finite free modules have strict generating systems. These submodules are closed and strictly closed, with [least-norm coset representatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Submodule.html#Submodule.exists_mk_eq_and_norm_eq_isRestricted_subring) and quotient norms in `‖K‖`, and linear maps of finite modules are [strict](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/FiniteModuleTopology.html#LinearMap.isStrictMap_of_isRestricted_subring) for a topology independent of the presentation ([TauCeti#27371](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27371)). On both the Gauss-normed and the completed Huber carriers the algebra is [Jacobson](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Jacobson.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.isJacobsonRing) and [regular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/TateAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Huber.isRegularRing_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion), of Krull dimension `n`, with residue fields at maximal ideals finite over `K`. The [maximum modulus principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximumModulus.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.exists_pointNorm_eq_norm) also landed ([TauCeti#21050](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/21050)): every series attains its Gauss norm at a maximal ideal.

Beyond the closed disc, open-disc and entire series became subrings carrying the topology of all their Gauss norms, complete with dense polynomials. Entire series admit unique division by polynomials with invertible leading coefficient, a normalised preparation, and an entire resultant which [is a unit exactly when the two arguments are coprime](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff). Overconvergent series gained analytic substitution and translation. For §6.4, the finite-coordinate comparisons of module-valued series were made isometric ([TauCeti#27297](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27297), [TauCeti#27301](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27301)), and closed coefficient quotients [lift with bounds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Quotient.html#Submodule.restrictedFamilyMkQ_surjective) ([TauCeti#27175](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27175)). Still absent: the presentation `T_n⟨Y⟩^a → T_n⟨Y⟩^b → M⟨Y⟩ → 0` of §6.4 and the adic comparisons of §6.3.
