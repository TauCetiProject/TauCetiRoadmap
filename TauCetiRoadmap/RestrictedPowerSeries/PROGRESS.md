## RestrictedPowerSeries: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-27 (`9df480c` to `dd217c4`)

The Weierstrass theory now works over Banach rings. If a restricted series is [multiplicatively distinguished](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MulDistinguished.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished) at some positive radius, it gives [unique Weierstrass division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq) (Martin's Proposition 1.27) and [unique Weierstrass preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq) (Corollary 1.28). Neither needs a multiplicative coefficient norm. Quotient and remainder are packaged as bounded operators with the [norm identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DivMod.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.gaussNorm_eq_max), and they commute with coefficient maps that keep the divisor distinguished. Iterating gives [several-variable division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/WeierstrassDivision.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_mul_add_eq_of_isMulDistinguished), but several-variable preparation has not landed yet. On the function side, [Strassmann's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le) bounds the number of zeros of a series over a complete field by its distinguished degree, and the [identity principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#PowerSeries.IsRestricted.eq_zero_of_infinite_of_evalAt_eq_zero) follows from it.

Entire series arrived with most of the API that Fredholm theory needs. They are [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Basic.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire) and can be evaluated in any complete normed algebra. They admit [unique division](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.existsUnique_entire_mul_add_eq) by a polynomial whose leading coefficient is invertible. There is a monic [entire resultant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.entireResultant), with a Bézout identity and a [coprimality criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff), and a unique [normalised entire preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isEntirePreparation). The [open-disc algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/Basic.html#PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc) carries the topology of all its smaller Gauss norms. It is complete in that topology and polynomials are dense.

The rest built the layers below. This includes multiplicative units, and the units of restricted algebras through Neumann series together with the converse criterion. The [reduction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UnitBall.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedReductionQuotientEquiv) `T°/T°° ≃ k̃[X]` landed. So did [analytic substitution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst) and translation, Hasse derivatives with their Gauss-norm estimate, and radius restriction, rescaling and the unit-radius `c₀` comparison. At radii in the divisible hull of the value group, restricted series are now [finite over the unit Tate algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Expand.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.exists_injective_finite_algHom_of_norm_mul_pow_eq_one). Noetherianity at those radii is not yet deduced.
