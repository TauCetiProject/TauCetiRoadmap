## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The normed foundations (Layers 0 and 1), multiplicative units and reduction (Layer 3) and the Banach-ring Weierstrass theory (Layer 4) are in place. The Tate-algebra structure theory, functions and evaluation, open-disc, entire and overconvergent series are each well advanced but incomplete, and the adic comparisons of Layer 6 have barely begun.

### Named results

- **Weierstrass division over a Banach ring** — dividing by a multiplicatively distinguished series at any positive radius gives a unique quotient and polynomial remainder with the norm identity, with no multiplicative norm assumed on the coefficients ([`IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq)).
- **Weierstrass preparation in several variables** — a series distinguished in a chosen variable over the coefficient restricted algebra is uniquely a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial in that variable ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished)).
- **Tate algebras are unique factorisation domains** — over a complete nonarchimedean field, `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` is factorial ([`MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid)).
- **Maximal ideals are generated by polynomials** — every maximal ideal of `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` is generated by its polynomial part, which is maximal in `K[X]` ([`Ideal.map_comap_polynomialToRestricted_of_isMaximal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/MaximalIdeal.html#Ideal.map_comap_polynomialToRestricted_of_isMaximal)); on the completed carrier the residue fields are finite over `K` ([`finite_quotient_of_isMaximal_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/TateAlgebra.html#Ideal.finite_quotient_of_isMaximal_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion)).
- **The entire resultant detects coprimality** — for monic `P` and entire `F`, the determinant of multiplication by `F` on `R[T]/(P)` is a unit exactly when `P` and `F` are coprime among entire series ([`Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Multiplicatively distinguished series** ([`IsMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MulDistinguished.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished)), built on [multiplicative units](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Monoid/MulUnit.html#TauCeti.IsNormMulUnit). This is the hypothesis that replaces a multiplicative coefficient norm, and it is what lets Weierstrass theory run over coefficient rings with nilpotents.
- **Analytic substitution** ([`restrictedAnalyticSubst`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst)), the unique continuous algebra map sending each variable to a series of small enough norm. Translation, affine changes of coordinates, the distinguishing automorphisms and the overconvergent substitutions are all instances of it.
- **Module-valued restricted series** ([`IsRestrictedFamily`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.IsRestrictedFamily)), with the convolution action of restricted scalars, their Gauss norm, iteration in disjoint variables, and the unit-radius isometry with `c₀`. They support coefficient recovery on `ℤ_pⁿ` and give the carrier that §6.4 needs.

## The frontier

- **Maximum modulus (§2.4)**: show that `‖f(x)‖ = ‖f‖` at some point over a finite extension of `K`, using the reduction of Layer 3. The Jacobson criterion on the Gauss-normed carrier depends on it.
- **Closed submodules and quotient norms (§5.3)**: strict generators exist; least-norm coset representatives, completeness of the quotient norm with values in `|K|`, and independence of the topology from the presentation remain.
- **Noetherianity at general radii (§5.4)**: the finite injective map from the unit Tate algebra at radii in `√|K×|` is proved ([`exists_injective_finite_algHom_of_norm_mul_pow_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Expand.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.exists_injective_finite_algHom_of_norm_mul_pow_eq_one)); the noetherian conclusion is the remaining step.
- **Quotient and tensor interfaces (§6.4)**: exactness of restricted series along a finite presentation, using the module-valued series and the §5.3 strictness results.
- **Adic restricted series (§6.3)**: compatibility of the AdicSpaces supplier's `A{X}` with the normed comparisons of §6.2, including reduction modulo `I^m` and the continuous universal property.
