## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The central Banach-ring Weierstrass theory of Layer 4 is proved in one variable at every positive radius, and division (but not preparation) is proved in several variables. Layer 3 is complete apart from a few compatibility statements. Layer 5 has unique factorisation but not the Jacobson, normality or dimension theorems, and the entire and open-disc algebras of Layer 7 are well under way. Nothing here establishes the Layer 6 comparisons or the overconvergent series of §7.3.

### Named results

- **Weierstrass division over a Banach ring** — a series whose distinguished coefficient is a multiplicative unit divides every restricted series with unique quotient and polynomial remainder, at any positive radius and without a multiplicative coefficient norm ([`existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq); Martin, Proposition 1.27).
- **Weierstrass preparation over a Banach ring** — such a series is uniquely a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial of the distinguished degree ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq); Martin, Corollary 1.28).
- **Tate algebras are factorial** — over a complete nonarchimedean field, `K⟨X₁, …, Xₙ⟩` is a unique factorisation domain ([`uniqueFactorizationMonoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid)).
- **Strassmann's theorem** — a restricted series distinguished of degree `s` at radius `c` has at most `s` zeros in the closed disc of radius `c`, in any complete normed extension field ([`encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le)).
- **Normalised entire preparation** — an entire series with constant term one that is multiplicatively distinguished at some radius factors uniquely as a polynomial times an entire series that is invertible at that radius, with coprime factors ([`existsUnique_isEntirePreparation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isEntirePreparation)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Multiplicative distinguishedness** ([`IsMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MulDistinguished.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished), built on [`IsNormMulUnit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Monoid/MulUnit.html#TauCeti.IsNormMulUnit)) moves the multiplicativity hypothesis from the whole coefficient ring onto one coefficient. This is what lets Weierstrass theory run over rings with nilpotents.
- **Analytic substitution** ([`restrictedAnalyticSubst`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst)) is defined by a convergent sum and is characterised as the unique continuous algebra map with given variable images. It agrees with formal substitution where that is defined, and it gives translations as isometric automorphisms.
- **The monic entire resultant** ([`entireResultant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.entireResultant)) comes with a Bézout identity and a coprimality criterion. It is the interface that the Fredholm-theory consumers need.

### Roadmap coverage

The earliest layers are all partial. **Layer 0** has the module-valued restricted families with their convolution action and bounded linear maps, but the bounded-lift surjectivity and quotient-map statement of §0.2 is not established here. **Layer 1** has radius restriction (contractive, injective, dense), rescaling by multiplicative units, the unit-radius `c₀` isometry and the isometric splitting-off of one variable; disjoint-sum and module iteration and the weighted `c₀` comparison are not established. **Layer 2** has evaluation, analytic substitution, translation, Hasse derivatives in one and several variables, Strassmann and the identity principle; affine maps, the chain rule, Taylor expansion, `ℤ_p` coefficient recovery and maximum modulus are missing. **Layer 3** lacks only compatibility of reduction with evaluation and valued-field coefficient maps. **Layer 4** is done in one variable, including naturality under bounded coefficient maps; in several variables only division and its norm estimate have landed. **Layer 5** has unique factorisation, distinguishing automorphisms and the finite map from the unit Tate algebra at radii in `√|K×|`. **Layer 7** has the open-disc algebra with its complete topology and polynomial density, plus the entire-series subring with evaluation, division, resultant and preparation; §7.3 has not started. **Layer 6** is unassessed.

## The frontier

- **Several-variable preparation**: apply one-variable preparation over the coefficient Tate algebra through the existing iteration equivalence, and add naturality and reindexing compatibility for several-variable division.
- **Maximum modulus on Tate algebras**: find finite-extension points where `‖f(x)‖ = ‖f‖` (§2.4). This is the prerequisite for the Jacobson property.
- **Rest of Layer 5**: normality, the Jacobson property, Krull dimension `n`, polynomial generators of maximal ideals with finite residue fields, and noetherianity at radii in `√|K×|`.
- **Strict closedness**: closed submodules of `T_n^m` with least-norm representatives and norm-controlled generators (§5.3). The quotient interfaces of §6.4 depend on these.
- **Entire topology and overconvergence**: completeness and polynomial density of entire series for all Gauss norms, evaluation on the open disc, and the §7.3 radius-controlled substitution on `W_n` supplied by AdicSpaces.
