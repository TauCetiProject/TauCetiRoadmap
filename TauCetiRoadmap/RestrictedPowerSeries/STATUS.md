## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The roadmap's main theorems are proved: Weierstrass division and preparation over Banach rings, the structure theory of Tate algebras, and entire division with its resultant. The README has since been rewritten, and every layer now ends with refinement targets: compatibilities, sharper forms and a few new statements. Most of these are still open, so every layer is now partial. The adic comparisons of §6.3 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Weierstrass division over a Banach ring**: dividing by a multiplicatively distinguished series at any positive radius gives a unique quotient and polynomial remainder ([`IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq)), with `‖g q + r‖ = max (‖g‖ ‖q‖) ‖r‖` and no multiplicative norm assumed on the coefficients.
- **Weierstrass preparation in several variables**: a series distinguished in a chosen variable is uniquely a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial in that variable ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished)).
- **Rückert's descent**: an injective map `A[X] → B` satisfying the Rückert hypotheses passes [unique factorisation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Rueckert.html#RingHom.IsRueckert.uniqueFactorizationMonoid), noetherianity and the Jacobson property from `A` to `B`. Tate algebras [satisfy these hypotheses](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Rueckert.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.isRueckert_polynomialToRestricted), so `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` is a [unique factorisation domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid).
- **Maximum modulus for Tate algebras**: every `f ∈ K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` attains its Gauss norm at a maximal ideal whose residue field is finite over `K` ([`MvPowerSeries.exists_pointNorm_eq_norm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximumModulus.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.exists_pointNorm_eq_norm)).
- **Strassmann's theorem**: a series that is restricted and distinguished of degree `s` at radius `c` has at most `s` zeros of norm at most `c` in any complete extension field ([`IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Multiplicatively distinguished series** ([`IsMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MulDistinguished.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished)) take the place of a multiplicative coefficient norm. This lets Weierstrass theory run over coefficient rings with nilpotents.
- **Analytic substitution** ([`restrictedAnalyticSubst`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst)) is the continuous algebra map sending each variable to a series of small enough norm. It has an [open-disc counterpart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc.analyticSubst), which now satisfies the [chain rule](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/Derivative.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc.derivative_analyticSubst) and includes the [affine isomorphisms between open discs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/Rescale.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc.affineEquiv).
- **Module-valued restricted series** ([`IsRestrictedFamily`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.IsRestrictedFamily)) carry §6.4. A Tate relation matrix presents the series with coefficients in the presented module, and these series are [independent of the presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Presentation.html#LinearEquiv.mapGaussFamily) up to topological isomorphism.

### Roadmap coverage

Every layer is partial. Each layer's main construction or theorem is proved, but some of the targets the rewritten README added are missing. Layer 0 lacks only injectivity of coefficient maps along injective `φ`. Layer 1 lacks the finiteness of the indices where the Gauss norm is attained, truncation convergence for families at general radii, density for polynomials with coefficients in a dense subset, and the `Fin (n+1)` form of splitting off a variable. Layer 2 lacks the algebra- and module-valued evaluation forms, the multi-index factorial comparison for Hasse derivatives, and the maximum principle at points of finite extensions. Layer 3 lacks the compatibility of inverses with evaluation and the ideal-level description of topologically nilpotent elements. Layer 4 lacks the two §4.3 statements relating preparation to division. Layer 5 lacks the §5.1 statements on `T_n` itself, the chain of coordinate ideals, and §5.4 with exponents chosen per variable. Layer 6 has §6.1, most of §6.2 and the §6.4 presentation. It lacks compatibility of the comparison with iteration, the transport of kernels and lifts in §6.4, and all of §6.3. Layer 7 has open-disc calculus, entire division, the resultant and preparation. It lacks the four §7.2 refinements, and neither the sequential description of §7.1 nor the polyradius comparison of §7.3 is stated as a theorem.

## The frontier

- **Entire-series refinements (§7.2)**: the norm identity for entire division, division along bounded coefficient maps by polynomials with invertible leading coefficient, coprimality as invertibility in `R[T]/(P)`, and non-normability of the topology of entire series.
- **Preparation versus division (§4.3)**: show that the Weierstrass polynomial is `Tˢ − b` with `b` the remainder of `Tˢ`, compare the quotients, and treat the polynomial case.
- **Comparison compatibilities (§§6.2, 6.4)**: show that the Gauss comparison commutes with iteration, and transport coefficientwise kernels and bounded lifts to the topological carriers.
- **Adic restricted series (§6.3)**: match the reductions modulo `I^m` of the AdicSpacesPartIII series and their universal property with the normed comparisons of §6.2.
- **Several-variable refinements (Layers 2 and 5)**: the multi-index factorial identity for Hasse derivatives, the maximum principle at points of finite extensions, freeness of `T_n/(w)` over `T_(n−1)`, and §5.4 with exponents chosen per variable.
