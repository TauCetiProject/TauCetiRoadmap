## Where this roadmap stands

### Named results

- **Weierstrass division over a Banach ring** — dividing by a multiplicatively distinguished series at any positive radius gives a unique quotient and polynomial remainder ([`IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq)), with the norm identity `‖g q + r‖ = max (‖g‖ ‖q‖) ‖r‖` and no multiplicative norm assumed on the coefficients.
- **Weierstrass preparation in several variables** — a series distinguished in a chosen variable over the coefficient restricted algebra is uniquely a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial in that variable ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished)).
- **Rückert's descent** — an injective map `A[X] → B` satisfying the Rückert hypotheses passes unique factorisation ([`IsRueckert.uniqueFactorizationMonoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Rueckert.html#RingHom.IsRueckert.uniqueFactorizationMonoid)), noetherianity and the Jacobson property from `A` to `B`; Tate algebras [satisfy these hypotheses](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Rueckert.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.isRueckert_polynomialToRestricted), and `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` is a [unique factorisation domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid).
- **Maximum modulus for Tate algebras** — every `f ∈ K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` attains its Gauss norm at a maximal ideal whose residue field is finite over `K` ([`MvPowerSeries.exists_pointNorm_eq_norm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximumModulus.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.exists_pointNorm_eq_norm)).
- **Strassmann's theorem** — a series restricted and distinguished of degree `s` at radius `c` has at most `s` zeros of norm at most `c` in any complete extension field ([`IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Multiplicatively distinguished series** ([`IsMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MulDistinguished.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished)), built on [multiplicative units](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Monoid/MulUnit.html#TauCeti.IsNormMulUnit). This hypothesis replaces a multiplicative coefficient norm and lets Weierstrass theory run over coefficient rings with nilpotents, as the worked examples over [dual numbers with the maximum norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Ring/DualNumber.html#TauCeti.MaxDualNumber) show.
- **Analytic substitution** ([`restrictedAnalyticSubst`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst)), the unique continuous algebra map sending each variable to a series of small enough norm. Translation, affine coordinate changes, distinguishing automorphisms and overconvergent substitution are instances, and it now has an [open-disc counterpart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/OpenDisc/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.OnOpenDisc.analyticSubst).
- **Module-valued restricted series** ([`IsRestrictedFamily`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.IsRestrictedFamily)), with their Gauss norm, `c₀` model, isometric finite-coordinate comparisons and bounded quotient lifts. These carry §6.4: the cokernel of a Tate relation matrix on restricted series is [topologically the series with coefficients in the presented module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Presentation.html#Matrix.restrictedFamilyPresentationEquiv).
