## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The normed foundations (Layers 0 and 1), evaluation, substitution and the maximum principles (Layer 2), multiplicative units and reduction (Layer 3) and the Banach-ring Weierstrass theory (Layer 4) are in place. The Tate-algebra structure theory of Layer 5 now includes closed submodules, quotient norms and noetherianity at every polyradius, but not the general Rückert interface. ### Named results

- **Weierstrass division over a Banach ring** — dividing by a multiplicatively distinguished series at any positive radius gives a unique quotient and polynomial remainder ([`IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq)), with the norm identity `‖g q + r‖ = max (‖g‖ ‖q‖) ‖r‖` ([`IsMulDistinguished.gaussNorm_mul_add_eq_max`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.gaussNorm_mul_add_eq_max)), and no multiplicative norm assumed on the coefficients.
- **Weierstrass preparation in several variables** — a series distinguished in a chosen variable over the coefficient restricted algebra is uniquely a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial in that variable ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isMulDistinguished)).
- **Tate algebras are unique factorisation domains** — over a complete nonarchimedean field, `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` is factorial ([`MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid)).
- **Maximum modulus for Tate algebras** — every `f ∈ K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` attains its Gauss norm at a maximal ideal, whose residue field is finite over `K` ([`MvPowerSeries.exists_pointNorm_eq_norm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximumModulus.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.exists_pointNorm_eq_norm)), so the Gauss norm is the supremum over such points ([`supSeminorm_eq_norm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximumModulus.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.supSeminorm_eq_norm)). The point can be taken with roots of unity as coordinates ([`exists_norm_restrictedEval₂_eq_norm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/MaximumModulus.html#MvPowerSeries.exists_norm_restrictedEval₂_eq_norm)).
- **Maximal ideals are generated by polynomials** — every maximal ideal of `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` is generated by its polynomial part, which is maximal in `K[X]` ([`Ideal.map_comap_polynomialToRestricted_of_isMaximal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/MaximalIdeal.html#Ideal.map_comap_polynomialToRestricted_of_isMaximal)); on the completed carrier the residue fields are finite over `K` ([`finite_quotient_of_isMaximal_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/TateAlgebra.html#Ideal.finite_quotient_of_isMaximal_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion)).
- **The entire resultant detects coprimality** — for monic `P` and entire `F`, the determinant of multiplication by `F` on `R[T]/(P)` is a unit exactly when `P` and `F` are coprime among entire series ([`Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Multiplicatively distinguished series** ([`IsMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MulDistinguished.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished)), built on [multiplicative units](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Monoid/MulUnit.html#TauCeti.IsNormMulUnit). This is the hypothesis that replaces a multiplicative coefficient norm, and it is what lets Weierstrass theory run over coefficient rings with nilpotents.
- **Analytic substitution** ([`restrictedAnalyticSubst`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst)), the unique continuous algebra map sending each variable to a series of small enough norm. Translation, affine changes of coordinates, the distinguishing automorphisms and the overconvergent substitutions are all instances of it.
- **Module-valued restricted series** ([`IsRestrictedFamily`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Basic.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.IsRestrictedFamily)), with the convolution action of restricted scalars, their Gauss norm, iteration in disjoint variables, and the isometry with `c₀` at unit radius and, by scalar rescaling, at radii realised by scalars. They support coefficient recovery on `ℤ_pⁿ` and are the carrier of §6.4: finite-coordinate comparisons are [isometric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Pi/Norm.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.IsRestrictedFamily.Gauss.piRingLinearIsometryEquiv), and closed coefficient quotients [lift with bounds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Module/Quotient.html#Submodule.restrictedFamilyMkQ_surjective).

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 0, 1, 3 and 4 are done: coefficient maps with bounded lifts give quotient maps, Gauss norms come with radius restriction, rescaling, polynomial completion and isometric iteration, the unit and reduction theory of the Gauss unit ball is complete, and division and preparation hold in one and several variables with naturality. Layer 2 is done: Strassmann's bound, coefficient recovery and the maximum modulus principle at finite-extension points are present. Layer 5 has distinguishing automorphisms, Weierstrass quotient bases, unique factorisation, Krull dimension, polynomial generation of maximal ideals and strict generators. Both the Gauss-normed and the completed carriers are Jacobson ([`MvPowerSeries.isJacobsonRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Jacobson.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.isJacobsonRing)), proved without maximum modulus. The theory of §5.3 is present: submodules of `T_nᵐ` are [closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Submodule.html#Submodule.isClosed_pi_isRestricted_subring) and strictly closed, with [least-norm representatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Submodule.html#Submodule.exists_mk_eq_and_norm_eq_isRestricted_subring) and [quotient norms in `‖K‖`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Submodule.html#Submodule.exists_norm_eq_norm_quotient_isRestricted_subring), and finite modules have a [presentation-independent topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Module/ModuleTopology.html#LinearMap.presentationContinuousLinearEquiv) in which [linear maps are strict](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/FiniteModuleTopology.html#LinearMap.isStrictMap_of_isRestricted_subring). For §5.4, the finite injective map from the unit Tate algebra at radii in `√|K×|` is [proved](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Expand.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.exists_injective_finite_algHom_of_norm_mul_pow_eq_one), and Tate algebras are [noetherian at every positive polyradius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Groebner.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.isNoetherianRing_isRestricted_subring).
