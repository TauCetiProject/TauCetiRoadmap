## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** One-variable Weierstrass division and preparation over Banach rings are done at every positive radius, and the multiplicative units and integral structure of Tate algebras are essentially complete. Several-variable Weierstrass theory, the algebraic structure of Tate algebras, functions on the disc, and entire and open-disc series are all partial. Nothing establishes the topological and adic comparisons of Layer 6.

### Named results

- **Weierstrass division over a Banach ring** — a restricted series whose distinguished coefficient is a multiplicative unit divides every restricted series uniquely, with a polynomial remainder of smaller degree, at any positive radius and without a multiplicative coefficient norm ([`IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Division.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_mul_add_eq)).
- **Weierstrass preparation over a Banach ring** — such a series is uniquely a multiplicative unit times a monic polynomial of Gauss norm `c^s` ([`existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Preparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished.existsUnique_isNormMulUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq)).
- **Factoriality of Tate algebras** — `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` over a complete nonarchimedean field is a unique factorisation domain ([`uniqueFactorizationMonoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/UniqueFactorization.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.uniqueFactorizationMonoid)).
- **Strassmann's theorem** — a one-variable restricted series has at most as many zeros in the closed disc, in any complete extension field, as its distinguished degree ([`IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Strassmann.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.encard_setOf_evalAt_eq_zero_le)).
- **The entire resultant detects coprimality** — for monic `P` and entire `F`, `Res(P, F)` is a unit exactly when `P` and `F` are coprime among entire series ([`isUnit_entireResultant_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Resultant.html#Polynomial.isUnit_entireResultant_iff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Multiplicatively distinguished series** ([`IsMulDistinguished`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/MulDistinguished.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsMulDistinguished), built on [`IsNormMulUnit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Monoid/MulUnit.html#TauCeti.IsNormMulUnit)) put the multiplicativity hypothesis on the one distinguished coefficient rather than on the whole norm. This is what lets the Weierstrass theory accept coefficient rings with nilpotents.
- **Analytic substitution** ([`restrictedAnalyticSubst`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/AnalyticSubst.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.restrictedAnalyticSubst)) is defined by a convergent sum, so the substituted series may have nonzero constant terms. It is characterised as the unique continuous algebra map with the given variable images, agrees with formal substitution where both are defined, and yields translation automorphisms.
- **Entire series** ([`IsEntire`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Basic.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire)) form a subring that can be evaluated in any complete normed algebra, including noncommutative ones. The subring supports explicit monic division and the resultant interface that Fredholm theory consumes.
