## ThetaSeries: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (`d88e389` to `273ea28`)

The first work on this roadmap builds the arithmetic vocabulary of a lattice in a real inner product space. The headline is the determinant–index identity: for an integral lattice L, the determinant [`ZLattice.det`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Determinant.html#ZLattice.det), defined as the squared covolume, is [the order of the discriminant group L^∨/L](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Determinant.html#ZLattice.IsIntegral.det_eq_card_discriminantGroup). From it the four faces of unimodularity (L equal to its dual, covolume one, determinant one, trivial discriminant group) are proved equivalent, for instance [via the covolume](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Determinant.html#ZLattice.IsIntegral.isUnimodular_iff_covolume_eq_one) and [via the discriminant group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Determinant.html#ZLattice.IsIntegral.isUnimodular_iff_subsingleton_discriminantGroup). Along the way the dual lattice has [the inverse covolume](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Covolume.html#ZLattice.covolume_dual), and the determinant is the Gram determinant of any ℤ-basis (TauCeti#19104).

Underneath this sits [the dual lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Dual.html#ZLattice.dual), taken literally as Mathlib's dual submodule of the inner product, with [biduality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Dual.html#ZLattice.dual_dual) and an instance making it a full lattice again. Integral, even and unimodular lattices are defined against it, with the expected relations: evenness implies integrality by polarization, and all three predicates are invariant under isometry. Preservation under orthogonal sums is not yet proved, and scaling, the bridge to the rational integral-lattice library, the level and the Kronecker symbol have not started.

On the analytic side, [the Fourier change-of-variables formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/ChangeOfVariables.html#ContinuousLinearEquiv.fourier_comp) for a linear automorphism of the space has landed: the transform picks up the inverse absolute determinant and is composed with the adjoint of the inverse. This is one of the steps that reduce Poisson summation on a general lattice to ℤⁿ. Poisson summation itself and the theta series have not begun.

## ThetaSeries: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

The headline is [Poisson summation over a full lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/PoissonSummation.html#SchwartzMap.tsum_eq_tsum_dual_fourier): for a Schwartz function f on a finite-dimensional real inner product space, the sum of f over a translate v + L equals the inverse covolume times the sum over the dual lattice of the Fourier transform twisted by e(⟪v, m⟫). The roadmap's two sign-corrected companions landed with it, [the reflected form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/PoissonSummation.html#SchwartzMap.tsum_eq_tsum_dual_fourier_neg) for every f and [the minus-phase form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/PoissonSummation.html#SchwartzMap.tsum_eq_tsum_dual_fourier_of_even) for even f. The proof takes a different route from the one the roadmap sketched: [the case of a lattice spanned by an orthonormal basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/PoissonSummation.html#SchwartzMap.tsum_eq_tsum_fourier_span_orthonormalBasis) comes from multivariable Fourier series on the torus, not from iterating the one-dimensional theorem. A change of variables then transports it, using the fact that [every full lattice is a linear image of that one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/ChangeOfVariables.html#ZLattice.exists_comap_span_orthonormalBasis_eq) with |det A| equal to the covolume, together with [the transport of duals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Dual.html#ZLattice.dual_comap_continuousLinearEquiv). Both sides are also proved summable as separate statements. The Gaussian transforms are still missing, so there is no inversion law yet.

The theta series of a lattice and of its cosets now exist ([`ZLattice.thetaSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Basic.html#ZLattice.thetaSeries), [`ZLattice.thetaCoset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Basic.html#ZLattice.thetaCoset)), proved holomorphic on the upper half-plane and bounded at the cusp with constant term 1. For an even lattice, [the q-expansion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/QExpansion.html#ZLattice.IsEven.hasSum_thetaSeries_qParam) has the representation numbers r_L(2m) as coefficients, and coset series have q_N-expansions with the expected support. The dual's series [splits over the discriminant group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/DiscriminantGroup.html#ZLattice.IsIntegral.thetaSeries_dual_eq_sum). The translation law is also in: [an even lattice's series is T-invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Translation.html#ZLattice.IsEven.thetaSeries_slash_T), and coset series pick up e(q_L(γ)). Supporting this are shells and representation numbers, with finiteness and [the bound r_L(t) = O(t^{n/2})](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Shell.html#ZLattice.representationNumber_isBigO). The level is not yet defined, so the coset results take any N with N‖γ‖²/2 ∈ ℤ as a hypothesis.

## ThetaSeries: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-06 (`e8ea0d1` to `385ec2d`)

The roadmap now reaches its first modularity theorem and the classical identities. Once the Gaussian Fourier transform was in place, Poisson summation gives [the inversion law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Inversion.html#ZLattice.thetaSeries_S_smul) Θ_L(−1/τ) = (covol L)⁻¹ (−i)^k τ^k Θ_{L^∨}(τ) in rank 2k. It also gives [its vector-valued form over the discriminant group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Inversion.html#ZLattice.IsIntegral.thetaCoset_S_smul), proved for integral lattices and not only even ones. Together with translation, this makes the theta series of an even unimodular lattice [a level-one modular form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/LevelOne.html#ZLattice.thetaForm), and [its rank is divisible by 8](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/LevelOne.html#ZLattice.IsEven.eight_dvd_finrank). Dimension counting then gives [Θ = E₄ in rank 8](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Eisenstein.html#ZLattice.IsEven.thetaForm_eq_E₄), in particular [for Tau Ceti's E₈](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/E8.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.thetaSeries_realModel_typeE₈RootLattice), and with it 240 roots. Rank 16 gives E₄², and rank 24 gives [E₄³ + (r_L(2) − 720)Δ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/RankTwentyFour.html#ZLattice.IsEven.thetaSeries_eq_E₄_cube_add_mul_discriminant). The Leech lattice has [theta series E₄³ − 720Δ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Leech.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.thetaSeries_realModel_leechLattice_eq_E₄_cube_sub_mul_discriminant), and its kissing number 196560 is derived, not assumed. Because E₈ ⊕ E₈ and D₁₆⁺ share the theta series E₄² without being isometric, [the theta series does not determine the lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/RankSixteen.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.not_forall_exists_map_eq_of_thetaSeries_eq).

Underneath this, the lattice vocabulary is now complete. It covers orthogonal sums, scaling, [the level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Level.html#ZLattice.level), and the Fricke partner, whose [exact level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/FrickePartner.html#ZLattice.IsEven.level_sqrt_level_smul_dual) is N/gcd(N, c_L). It also covers the passage between real lattices and Tau Ceti's rational ones in both directions ([TauCeti#21332](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/21332), [TauCeti#21517](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/21517)), [the Kronecker symbol](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LegendreSymbol/KroneckerSymbol.html#TauCeti.kroneckerSym), and [the signed discriminant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/SignedDiscriminant.html#ZLattice.signedDiscr) D_L, whose Kronecker character at modulus |D_L| [takes the value (−1)^k at −1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/SignedDiscriminant.html#ZLattice.IsEven.kroneckerChar_signedDiscr_neg_one).

Work toward the Hecke–Schoeneberg theorem is well advanced but not finished. Lattice Gauss sums are defined, with vanishing and completing the square. They satisfy [the reciprocity law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Reciprocity.html#ZLattice.IsEven.gaussSum_eq_mul_dualGaussSum), proved from the asymptotics of theta series, and its case a = c = 1 gives [Milgram's formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/Reciprocity.html#ZLattice.IsEven.dualGaussSum_one_one). They are also [evaluated at an odd modulus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/GaussSum.html#ZLattice.IsEven.dualGaussSum_eq_jacobiSym). Coset splitting gives [Θ_L ∣ A as a multiple of Θ_L for A in Γ₀(N) with c > 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ThetaSeries/CosetSplitting.html#ZLattice.IsEven.thetaSeries_slash_eq_gaussSum_smul), and the analogous statement holds for coset series on Γ(N). Theta series are bounded at every cusp, and the Fricke law is proved. What is missing is the evaluation of that multiple as χ_L(d), because the closed form of G_L(a, c) and the character χ_L at the level have not landed.
