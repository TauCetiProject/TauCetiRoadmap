## ThetaSeries: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (`d88e389` to `273ea28`)

The first work on this roadmap builds the arithmetic vocabulary of a lattice in a real inner product space. The headline is the determinant–index identity: for an integral lattice L, the determinant [`ZLattice.det`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Determinant.html#ZLattice.det), defined as the squared covolume, is [the order of the discriminant group L^∨/L](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Determinant.html#ZLattice.IsIntegral.det_eq_card_discriminantGroup). From it the four faces of unimodularity (L equal to its dual, covolume one, determinant one, trivial discriminant group) are proved equivalent, for instance [via the covolume](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Determinant.html#ZLattice.IsIntegral.isUnimodular_iff_covolume_eq_one) and [via the discriminant group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Determinant.html#ZLattice.IsIntegral.isUnimodular_iff_subsingleton_discriminantGroup). Along the way the dual lattice has [the inverse covolume](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Covolume.html#ZLattice.covolume_dual), and the determinant is the Gram determinant of any ℤ-basis (TauCeti#19104).

Underneath this sits [the dual lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Dual.html#ZLattice.dual), taken literally as Mathlib's dual submodule of the inner product, with [biduality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Dual.html#ZLattice.dual_dual) and an instance making it a full lattice again. Integral, even and unimodular lattices are defined against it, with the expected relations: evenness implies integrality by polarization, and all three predicates are invariant under isometry. Preservation under orthogonal sums is not yet proved, and scaling, the bridge to the rational integral-lattice library, the level and the Kronecker symbol have not started.

On the analytic side, [the Fourier change-of-variables formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/ChangeOfVariables.html#ContinuousLinearEquiv.fourier_comp) for a linear automorphism of the space has landed: the transform picks up the inverse absolute determinant and is composed with the adjoint of the inverse. This is one of the steps that reduce Poisson summation on a general lattice to ℤⁿ. Poisson summation itself and the theta series have not begun.
