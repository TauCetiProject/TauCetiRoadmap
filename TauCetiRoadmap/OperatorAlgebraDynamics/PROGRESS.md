## OperatorAlgebraDynamics: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`a863f31` to `c1c6773`)

This first window opened both ends of the roadmap. On the equilibrium side, [β-KMS states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState) of a real C⋆-dynamics are now defined by bounded holomorphic functions on the strip, with the KMS function of each pair [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSFunction.eqOn) by Schwarz reflection and [bounded by ‖ω‖‖a‖‖b‖](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSFunction.norm_le) by Phragmén–Lindelöf. The [analytic KMS identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState.apply_mul_entireExtension_mul_I) ω(a α_{iβ}(b)) = ω(ba) holds for entire analytic b, and [the 0-KMS states are exactly the tracial states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.isKMSState_zero_iff). The converse, that the analytic identity characterises KMS states, is proved only when every element is entire analytic, not yet on a dense invariant subalgebra (TauCeti#18766). Underneath sit the [infinitesimal generator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Generator.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.generator) of a real action, a closed, densely defined derivation satisfying Leibniz and star rules, and the [entire analytic subalgebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Analytic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.entireAnalytic) with uniqueness of continuation, the star relation and strip bounds. Density of entire elements by Gaussian smearing is still to come.

On the crossed-product side, the work laid D1's groundwork: [point-norm continuous actions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction), equivariant maps and invariant subalgebras, the [induced action on multipliers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.multiplier), and the [extension of nondegenerate representations to the multiplier algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Representation/Nondegenerate.html#NonUnitalStarAlgHom.multiplierExtension). Twisted convolution on C_c(G,A) is [associative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Convolution.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.convolution_assoc) and [L¹-submultiplicative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Convolution.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.integral_norm_convolution_le), and comes with the modular-character-twisted involution. Covariant pairs have an [integrated form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Covariant/Integrated.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.CovariantRepresentation.integratedOperator), built by vector integration and bounded by the L¹ norm. Its multiplicativity and star law, the L¹ completion and the full crossed product itself have not landed.
