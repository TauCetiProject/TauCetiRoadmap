## OperatorAlgebraDynamics: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`a863f31` to `c1c6773`)

This first window opened both ends of the roadmap. On the equilibrium side, [β-KMS states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState) of a real C⋆-dynamics are now defined by bounded holomorphic functions on the strip, with the KMS function of each pair [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSFunction.eqOn) by Schwarz reflection and [bounded by ‖ω‖‖a‖‖b‖](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSFunction.norm_le) by Phragmén–Lindelöf. The [analytic KMS identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState.apply_mul_entireExtension_mul_I) ω(a α_{iβ}(b)) = ω(ba) holds for entire analytic b, and [the 0-KMS states are exactly the tracial states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.isKMSState_zero_iff). The converse, that the analytic identity characterises KMS states, is proved only when every element is entire analytic, not yet on a dense invariant subalgebra (TauCeti#18766). Underneath sit the [infinitesimal generator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Generator.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.generator) of a real action, a closed, densely defined derivation satisfying Leibniz and star rules, and the [entire analytic subalgebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Analytic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.entireAnalytic) with uniqueness of continuation, the star relation and strip bounds. Density of entire elements by Gaussian smearing is still to come.

On the crossed-product side, the work laid D1's groundwork: [point-norm continuous actions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction), equivariant maps and invariant subalgebras, the [induced action on multipliers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.multiplier), and the [extension of nondegenerate representations to the multiplier algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Representation/Nondegenerate.html#NonUnitalStarAlgHom.multiplierExtension). Twisted convolution on C_c(G,A) is [associative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Convolution.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.convolution_assoc) and [L¹-submultiplicative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Convolution.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.integral_norm_convolution_le), and comes with the modular-character-twisted involution. Covariant pairs have an [integrated form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Covariant/Integrated.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.CovariantRepresentation.integratedOperator), built by vector integration and bounded by the L¹ norm. Its multiplicativity and star law, the L¹ completion and the full crossed product itself have not landed.

## OperatorAlgebraDynamics: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`c1c6773` to `e8ea0d1`)

The equilibrium side of D7 filled in most of its basic KMS theory. Gaussian smearing shows that [entire analytic elements are dense](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/GaussianSmearing.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.dense_entireAnalytic), and with it the [dense analytic KMS criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/Dense.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.isKMSState_iff_of_dense) now holds: a state is β-KMS exactly when ω(a α_{iβ}(b)) = ω(ba) for a and b in any norm-dense invariant set of entire elements. This removes the restriction from the previous window, where the converse needed every element to be entire. For β ≠ 0, [β-KMS states are invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState.apply_action) under the dynamics. For unital algebras the set K_β is [convex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/StateSpace.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.convex_kmsStates) and [weak-* compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/StateSpace.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.isCompact_kmsStates), and KMS states [pull back along quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState.comp_of_surjective) and [restrict to invariant subalgebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState.restrict) whenever the restriction has norm one. The simplex structure, the modular characterization and the Gibbs example are still to come.

On the crossed-product side, the integrated form of a covariant pair is now [multiplicative for twisted convolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Covariant/Integrated.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.CovariantRepresentation.integratedOperator_convolution), and the twisted involution [reverses convolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Convolution/Algebra.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.twistedInvolution_convolution). Together these make C_c(G,A) into a [convolution ⋆-algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Convolution/Algebra.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.ConvolutionAlgebra). The [regular covariant representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Covariant/Regular.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.CovariantRepresentation.regular) on L²(G,H) also landed, built from operator-valued multiplication operators on L² and a [strongly continuous left regular representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/LeftRegular.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.continuous_leftRegular_apply), with no separability or σ-finiteness assumed (TauCeti#20626). Three things are still missing: the star law for the integrated form, nondegeneracy and faithfulness of the regular representation, and every norm completion.

## OperatorAlgebraDynamics: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-09 (`e8ea0d1` to `7ca783d`)

The full crossed product now exists. [`FullCrossedProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Full.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.FullCrossedProduct) completes C_c(G,A) in the full C⋆-seminorm, the supremum over all integrated covariant representations, and is a C⋆-algebra for any locally compact G, with no separability or σ-finiteness (TauCeti#24505). The seminorm is a norm because the regular representation of a faithful ρ is [injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Covariant/Regular.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.CovariantRepresentation.injective_integratedOperator_regular). Every covariant pair [extends](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Full.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.CovariantRepresentation.integratedFull) to it, and it carries canonical coefficient and unitary group maps into its multiplier algebra that are covariant, strictly continuous and [nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Nondegenerate.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.FullCrossedProduct.dense_span_range_coefficientMultiplier_fst). Equivariant maps induce crossed-product maps, which are surjective for quotients by invariant ideals. The supporting work gave C_c(G,A) an isometric involution and a bounded approximate unit. D2 also began: a concrete [regular algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Regular.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.regularAlgebra) receives the full product densely, and for discrete G its [Fourier coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Fourier.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.RegularCrossedProduct.fourierCoeff) determine elements, with the identity coefficient [faithful](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Expectation.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.RegularCrossedProduct.fourierCoeff_one_star_mul_self_eq_zero_iff). Still missing are the universal property into multiplier algebras and the ideal exact sequence.

In D7, ground states are now characterised in all three ways the roadmap asks for: by bounded continuation of correlations to the upper half-plane, by the [generator inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/GroundState/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.isGroundState_iff_neg_I_mul_apply_star_mul_generator_nonneg) −iω(a*δ(a)) ≥ 0, and by a [nonnegative GNS Hamiltonian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/GroundState/GNS.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.isGroundState_iff_exists_inner_gnsHamiltonian_nonneg). Zero-temperature limits of KMS states are [ground states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/GroundState/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsGroundState.of_tendsto_isKMSState). For a Hermitian matrix K the Gibbs state is the [unique β-KMS state](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Gibbs.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.isKMSState_iff_eq_gibbsState). [Bounded perturbations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Perturbation.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.perturbed) come from a Dyson-series cocycle, and the GNS vector of a KMS state is [separating](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/Separating.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState.eq_zero_of_apply_toPreGNS_one_eq_zero). The modular characterization has not landed.

D9 opened with local matrix algebras, partial traces, interactions and finite-volume Gibbs states, which are the [unique KMS states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/QuantumSpin/Interaction.html#TauCeti.QuantumSpin.Interaction.isKMSState_localDynamics_iff) of the local dynamics. Matrix entropy came with it: Klein's inequality, [subadditivity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Entropy.html#Matrix.PosSemidef.vonNeumannEntropy_le_partialTraceRight_add_partialTraceLeft) and concavity, but not yet strong subadditivity.
