### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The full crossed product** ([`FullCrossedProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Full.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.FullCrossedProduct)) — the completion of C_c(G,A) in the supremum of all integrated covariant norms, for arbitrary locally compact G. It comes with a covariant pair of [coefficient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.FullCrossedProduct.coefficientMultiplier) and [group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.FullCrossedProduct.groupMultiplier) maps into its multiplier algebra, which are strictly continuous and nondegenerate.
- **Perturbed dynamics** ([`perturbed`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/Perturbation.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.perturbed)) — α^P_t = Γ_t α_t(·) Γ_t*, built from a unitary cocycle given by a norm-convergent [Dyson series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/DysonSeries.html#TauCeti.dysonSeries), with generator δ + i[P,·] on the same domain.
- **The concrete regular crossed product** ([`regularAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Regular.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.regularAlgebra)) — the norm closure of the regular representation of C_c(G,A) induced by a faithful ρ, which receives the full product densely. For discrete G it carries [Fourier coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Fourier.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.RegularCrossedProduct.fourierCoeff), and the identity coefficient is positive and faithful, which is the substance of the canonical conditional expectation.

## The frontier

- **The universal property of A⋊_αG** — show that every nondegenerate representation of the full crossed product comes from a covariant pair through the canonical multipliers, then state it for multiplier algebras and Hilbert-module adjointables. The multipliers and their strict continuity are in place.
- **The ideal exact sequence** — surjectivity onto (A/I)⋊G is proved. What remains is injectivity of I⋊G → A⋊G, exactness in the middle, and identifying the image as the ideal generated by C_c(G,I).
- **The reduced crossed product** — prove the regular norm is independent of the faithful ρ and package the identity Fourier coefficient as the faithful conditional expectation A⋊_rG → A. The regular Hilbert module waits on gap:hilbert-module-adjointables.
- **The modular characterization of KMS states** — the separating vector and the GNS implementing group are ready. Identifying σ_t^ω with τ_{−βt} needs V10's modular operator.
- **The thermodynamic limit** — build the quasi-local algebra and the infinite-volume dynamics from an interaction bound. Then show that cluster points of the finite-volume Gibbs states are KMS states. In parallel, strong subadditivity can build on the operator-Jensen inequality for −log already in the library.
