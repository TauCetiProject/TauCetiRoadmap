### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The full crossed product** ([`FullCrossedProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Full.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.FullCrossedProduct)) — the completion of C_c(G,A) in the universal norm, for any locally compact G. Its canonical coefficient and group multipliers now realise every nondegenerate representation, so later layers can define maps out of A⋊_αG by giving covariant pairs.
- **Central multiplier cocycles** ([`CentralCocycle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CentralTwist/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.CentralCocycle)) — 2-cocycles with values in the unitary centre of M(A). They come with associative, L¹-submultiplicative twisted convolution and [twisted covariant pairs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CentralTwist/Covariant.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.CentralCovariantPair) whose integrated forms are multiplicative and preserve the star. This is the start of the twisted algebras C*(G,ω).
- **The concrete regular crossed product** ([`regularAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Regular.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.regularAlgebra)) — the closure of the regular representation induced by a faithful ρ. For discrete G its identity Fourier coefficient is faithful, and the coefficient algebra is [exactly the elements with vanishing nonidentity coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/CrossedProduct/Coefficient.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.RegularCrossedProduct.mem_range_coefficient_iff).

### Roadmap coverage

**D1** is partial. The convolution algebra, the full product, functoriality, the commutant of integrated forms and the universal property in both forms are done. For the ideal sequence, injectivity of I⋊G → A⋊G and surjectivity onto (A/I)⋊G are proved, but exactness in the middle is not. Still missing are the identification of the L¹ completion with Bochner L¹(G,A), the identifications A⋊_idG ≅ A⊗_max C*(G) and of the free-group algebra, and most of the central-twist and Borel sublanes. **D2** is partial. It has the concrete regular algebra, the discrete Fourier coefficients with the coefficient algebra identified, and Powers' projection estimate. There is no regular Hilbert module, no proof that the regular norm is independent of ρ, no Powers property for free groups, and no amenability. **D7** is partial: it has generators, analytic elements, KMS basics, ground states, the matrix Gibbs state and bounded perturbations, but no modular characterization or simplex theory. **D9** is partial: it has finite-volume spin systems and matrix entropy up to strong subadditivity, but no quasi-local algebra or infinite-volume dynamics. **D3–D6** and **D8** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Exactness of the ideal sequence** — both ends are in place. What remains is that the kernel of A⋊G → (A/I)⋊G is the range of I⋊G.
- **The reduced crossed product** — prove that the regular norm does not depend on the faithful ρ, and package the identity Fourier coefficient as the faithful conditional expectation A⋊_rG → A. The regular Hilbert module waits on gap:hilbert-module-adjointables.
- **The twisted crossed product** — complete the twisted convolution algebra of a central cocycle in the supremum of twisted integrated forms, as the untwisted case was. Its universal property should then follow the pattern now in place for A⋊_αG.
- **The modular characterization of KMS states** — the separating GNS vector and the implementing group are ready. Identifying σ_t^ω with τ_{−βt} needs V10's modular operator.
- **The thermodynamic limit** — build the quasi-local algebra and the infinite-volume dynamics from an interaction bound, then show that cluster points of finite-volume Gibbs states are KMS states. Strong subadditivity is now available for the entropy-density arguments.
