- **Unimodularity of the 23 lattices** — every row lattice is unimodular ([`isUnimodular_lattice`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Niemeier.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.isUnimodular_lattice)), because every glue subgroup [is Lagrangian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Glue/Lagrangian.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.isLagrangian_glueSubgroup) of [the displayed order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Glue/Lagrangian.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.natCard_glueSubgroup). The proof runs through the [congruence model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech/Minimum.html#TauCeti.mem_leechLattice_carrier_iff_exists) and a four-case Golay argument, with no theta series.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The rootful table** — [`RootfulNiemeierType`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/RootSystems.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType) has 23 constructors carrying components, Coxeter number, glue patterns, span mode, glue order and root count. Its [finite eliminator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/RootSystems.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.forall) lets one theorem over the index serve every row.
- **Uniform gluing** — [`RootfulNiemeierType.lattice`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Niemeier.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.lattice) glues every row by one construction, so `D₂₄`, `A₂₄` and the unglued `E₈³` go through the same API as the rest.
- **Shortest norms of glue words** — [`isLeast_sum_glueMinimum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/GlueAlphabet/CosetMinimum.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.isLeast_sum_glueMinimum) proves that a word's coset minimum is attained and is the sum of its labels' minima. This is what makes the `μ(w) ≥ 4` checks say something about the glued lattice.
