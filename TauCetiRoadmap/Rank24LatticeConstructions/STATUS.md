It is proved [unimodular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech.html#TauCeti.isUnimodular_leechLattice), [even](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech.html#TauCeti.isEven_leechLattice), positive definite, and of rank 24, and it is the union of Construction B and its [shifted coset](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech.html#TauCeti.mem_leechLattice_carrier_iff).
- **The Leech norm bound in congruence coordinates** — if `a = 2b + m𝟙`, where `m ∈ {0,1}`, `b` reduces to a Golay codeword, `∑ aᵢ ≡ 4m (mod 8)` and `a ≠ 0`, then `∑ aᵢ² ≥ 32` ([`thirtyTwo_le_sum_sq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionB/Golay.html#TauCeti.BinaryGolay.thirtyTwo_le_sum_sq)). This is the roadmap's four-case argument, using Golay minimum distance 8 and doubly-evenness.
- **Coset minima of `Aₙ`** — the discriminant class labelled `a` has least norm `a(n+1-a)/(n+1)`, attained ([`isLeast_form_self_typeADiscriminantGroupEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeA/CosetMinimum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isLeast_form_self_typeADiscriminantGroupEquiv)). - **Additivity of coset minima** — attained least norms of two discriminant cosets add to the attained least norm of the coset of the orthogonal sum ([`isLeast_form_self_orthogonalSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Orthogonal/CosetMinimum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isLeast_form_self_orthogonalSum)).
