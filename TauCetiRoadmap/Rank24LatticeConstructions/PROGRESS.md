## Rank24LatticeConstructions: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`cd70bab` to `b4b3ce5`)

The [Leech lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech.html#TauCeti.leechLattice) is now constructed the way the roadmap asks. Take the [Construction B lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionB/Golay.html#TauCeti.BinaryGolay.constructionBLattice) of the extended binary Golay code, which has index two in Construction A and discriminant four. Then glue it along the order-two class of the odd shift `(-3/2, 1/2, …, 1/2)`, which is Lagrangian. The result is proved [even](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech.html#TauCeti.isEven_leechLattice), [unimodular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech.html#TauCeti.isUnimodular_leechLattice), positive definite and of rank 24 (TauCeti#20032). The four-case rootlessness argument has also landed, as [a bound in congruence coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionB/Golay.html#TauCeti.BinaryGolay.thirtyTwo_le_sum_sq): every nonzero admissible vector has `∑ aᵢ² ≥ 32`. What is still missing is the theorem identifying the Leech lattice with that congruence model, so minimum norm 4 and the absence of roots are not yet stated for the lattice itself.

On the rootful side, every simply-laced Dynkin type now has a [positive root lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/Dynkin.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.rootLattice) built from its Cartan matrix. It comes with its Gram matrix, rank, evenness and positive-definiteness, the fact that its norm-2 vectors span it, and identifications with the existing `A`, `D` and `E` models. The coset-minimum programme also advanced. The shortest vector in each discriminant class of `Aₙ` [has norm `k(n+1-k)/(n+1)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeA/CosetMinimum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isLeast_form_self_typeADiscriminantGroupEquiv), proved with attainment. The `E₆`, `E₇` and `E₈` classes are settled through their minuscule weights. Coset minima now [add over orthogonal sums](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Orthogonal/CosetMinimum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isLeast_form_self_orthogonalSum), which is the step from components to glue words. The 23-row table and its glued lattices have not started.

## Rank24LatticeConstructions: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-09 (`b4b3ce5` to `7ca783d`)

Both ends of the roadmap closed in this window. On the Leech side, the [congruence model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech/Minimum.html#TauCeti.mem_leechLattice_carrier_iff_exists) identifies the glued lattice with the vectors `a/2`, where `a = m𝟙 + 2b`, `b mod 2` is a Golay word and `∑ aᵢ ≡ 4m (mod 8)`. The earlier four-case bound then gives [minimum norm four](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech/Minimum.html#TauCeti.minimum_leechLattice) and [no roots](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Leech/Minimum.html#TauCeti.vectorsOfNorm_two_leechLattice) (TauCeti#20430). On the rootful side, the 23-row [Conway–Sloane table](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/RootSystems.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType) is now Lean data, and [one definition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Niemeier.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.lattice) glues every row. Each glued lattice is even, positive definite, unimodular and of rank 24, and gluing adds no roots: [its root sublattice is exactly the displayed one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Niemeier.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.span_vectorsOfNorm_two_lattice). The order, Lagrangian and `μ(w) ≥ 4` checks are each one theorem over the index. Nineteen rows are checked by proof-producing enumeration of their glue words, and four by code identifications.

Those four rows are the cross-checks. The `A₁²⁴` table lattice is [explicitly isometric to binary Golay Construction A](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/TableRow/A1TwentyFour/Basic.html#TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.a1TwentyFourthLatticeConstructionAIsometry). The `A₂¹²` glue [is the extended ternary Golay code](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/A2Twelfth.html#TauCeti.map_a2TwelfthGlueSubgroup_eq_ternaryGolay). The `D₄⁶` glue [is the hexacode](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/D4Six/Glue.html#TauCeti.map_d4SixGlueSubgroup_d4SixHexacodeIsometry), and the additive span of its printed words [is a proper subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/D4Six/AdditiveSpan.html#TauCeti.closure_d4SixPrintedDigits_lt_d4SixGlueSubgroup), which confirms that this row needs its `𝔽₄`-linear span mode. `A₁₁ D₇ E₆` was built end to end.

Underneath sit the remaining component-layer pieces: products over ordered component lists, [attained shortest norms of glue words](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/GlueAlphabet/CosetMinimum.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.isLeast_sum_glueMinimum), the [pattern constructors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Rank24/Glue/Pattern.html#TauCeti.GluePattern), and the naturality that turns component permutations and code automorphisms into lattice isometries. One caveat concerns the README's symmetry list. In `A₅⁴ D₄`, rotating the marked block of `[2(024)0]` does not preserve the glue subgroup, so that row is excluded from the pattern-symmetry action.
