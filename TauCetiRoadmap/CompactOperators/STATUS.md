## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** On `c₀` model spaces the determinant theory is essentially complete through the spectrum. The Fredholm determinant is entire, multiplicative and cyclic, and it decides invertibility of `1 − au`. Every `1 − au` is Fredholm of index zero, and Layer 3 has its first piece, the Riesz projector at a good zero. Coleman's transform, the polynomial-factor projectors and all slope theory have not begun.

### Named results

- **Serre's criterion** — over a complete ultrametric field, a map between `c₀` model spaces is row compact exactly when it is completely continuous ([`isRowCompact_iff_isCompletelyContinuous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isRowCompact_iff_isCompletelyContinuous)). It is also exactly when the image of the unit ball is compactoid ([`isRowCompact_iff_hasCompactoidImage`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/Compactoid.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isRowCompact_iff_hasCompactoidImage)).
- **Entirety of the Fredholm determinant** — for a row-compact `u`, the principal-minor series `det(1 − Tu)` converges at every radius ([`isEntire_charPowerSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isEntire_charPowerSeries)), and so does its resolvent.
- **Invertibility and the nonzero spectrum** — `1 − au` is invertible exactly when `H_u(a)` is a unit, over any coefficient ring ([`isUnit_one_sub_smul_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isUnit_one_sub_smul_iff)). Over a field, a nonzero `λ` is in the spectrum exactly when `H_u(λ⁻¹) = 0` ([`mem_spectrum_iff_evalAt_inv_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.mem_spectrum_iff_evalAt_inv_eq_zero)).
- **The Fredholm alternative** — on a Banach space over any complete nontrivially normed field, `1 − u` is Fredholm of index zero for completely continuous `u` ([`isFredholm_one_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsCompletelyContinuous.isFredholm_one_sub)). Hence every zero of `H_u` is the inverse of a genuine eigenvalue ([`evalAt_charPowerSeries_eq_zero_iff_hasEigenvalue`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Eigenvalues.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.evalAt_charPowerSeries_eq_zero_iff_hasEigenvalue)).
- **The Riesz projector at a good zero** — over a coefficient ring, a good zero of order `h` gives a row-compact idempotent ([`isRieszProjection_rieszProjection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isRieszProjection_rieszProjection)). It lies in the closure of `R[u]`, is unique, and its finite projective range is the generalised eigenspace `ker (1 − au)^h`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Row compactness** ([`IsRowCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact)) — the roadmap's working notion of compact operator. It is a closed ideal, approximated by finite output truncations. A row-compact idempotent has [projective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/Idempotent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.projective_range_of_isIdempotentElem), finitely generated range, which is what makes spectral summands finite.
- **The Fredholm series on (Pr) modules** ([`charPowerSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.charPowerSeries)) — defined through a retract onto a model space and [independent of the presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.charPowerSeries_eq). On finite modules it agrees with the finite projective determinant. This is the carrier on which rank and determinant statements for spectral summands can be phrased.
- **Good zeros** ([`IsGoodZero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/GoodZero.html#PowerSeries.IsGoodZero)) — Hasse derivatives vanish below order `h` and the `h`-th is a unit. They come with the factorisation `F = (T − a)^h G` and additivity of orders, and they are the input to the Riesz projector.

## The frontier

- **Rank and determinant of the Riesz projector (§3.1)** — show `range E` has constant rank `h` and determinant `(1 − a⁻¹T)^h`, with the complementary factor. The commuting-idempotent splitting and the (Pr) determinant are now both in hand.
- **Field eigenspaces (§3.2)** — the decomposition `ker C^h ⊕ range C^h` with `dim = h`, algebraic multiplicity, and finiteness of eigenvalues of norm bounded below. This mostly specialises the previous item to a field.
- **Coleman's transform (§3.3)** — `colemanDBounded`, the majorants and Gauss-norm convergence. Nothing exists yet. It is largely finite polynomial algebra, and §3.4's polynomial-factor projectors and Layer 4's finite-slope projectors wait on it.
- **Compactoid image implies complete continuity (§0.5)** — on arbitrary Banach spaces, via the countable-type reduction the README prescribes, plus the locally compact converse.
- **Kernels and radius improvement (§0.3)** — strict radius inclusions and the locally analytic transition maps are compact.
