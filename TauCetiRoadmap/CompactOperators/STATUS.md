### Named results

- **Coleman's spectral mapping theorem**: for a row-compact `u` and an entire `B` with `B(0) = 0`, the operator `B(u)` is row compact and its Fredholm series is Coleman's transform of `H_u` ([`charPowerSeries_eq_colemanDEntire`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/FunctionalCalculus.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.charPowerSeries_eq_colemanDEntire)).
- **Multiplicity of a good zero**: at a good zero of order `h`, the Riesz summand has rank `h` at every prime of the coefficient ring ([`rankAtStalk_range`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection/Multiplicity.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszProjection.rankAtStalk_range)). `H_u` also factors as `(1 − a⁻¹T)^h` times a complementary unit ([`charPowerSeries_matrixCoeff_eq_pow_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection/Multiplicity.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszProjection.charPowerSeries_matrixCoeff_eq_pow_mul)). Over a field this gives generalised eigenspaces of dimension `h`, on (Pr) modules too ([`finrank_ker_pow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.finrank_ker_pow)).
- **Invertibility and the nonzero spectrum**: `1 − au` is invertible exactly when `H_u(a)` is a unit, over any coefficient ring ([`isUnit_one_sub_smul_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isUnit_one_sub_smul_iff)). Over a field, the nonzero spectrum is the set of inverses of the zeros of `H_u`, on (Pr) modules as well ([`mem_spectrum_iff_evalAt_inv_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.mem_spectrum_iff_evalAt_inv_eq_zero)). On model spaces these spectral values are isolated, and only finitely many have norm bounded below.
- **The Fredholm alternative**: `1 − u` is Fredholm of index zero for every completely continuous `u` on a Banach space over a complete field ([`isFredholm_one_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsCompletelyContinuous.isFredholm_one_sub)).
- **Serre's criterion and its Banach-space form**: on `c₀` model spaces, row compactness, compactoid image and complete continuity coincide ([`isRowCompact_iff_isCompletelyContinuous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isRowCompact_iff_isCompletelyContinuous)). On arbitrary ultrametric Banach spaces, compactoid image is complete continuity ([`hasCompactoidImage_iff_isCompletelyContinuous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Compactoid/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.hasCompactoidImage_iff_isCompletelyContinuous)). Over locally compact fields it also agrees with topological compactness.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Row compactness** ([`IsRowCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact)) is the working notion of compact operator. It forms a closed ideal, and it is stable under entire functions with zero constant term. Row-compact idempotents have finite projective range, which is what makes spectral summands finite.
- **The Fredholm series on (Pr) modules** (`charPowerSeries`) is defined through any retract onto a model space and does not depend on the retract. Spectrum, Riesz projectors, rank statements and slope multiplicities have all been transported along it.
- **The entire Coleman transform** ([`colemanDEntire`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Coleman/Entire.html#PowerSeries.colemanDEntire)) is the Gauss-norm limit of finite resultant transforms of truncations, controlled by Newton and Coleman majorants. It is the input that the polynomial-factor projectors of §3.4 need.
