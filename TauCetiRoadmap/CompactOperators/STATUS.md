### Named results

- **The Riesz–Coleman decomposition**: suppose `H_u = QS`, where `Q` is a polynomial with `Q(0) = 1` and unit leading coefficient, and `S` is entire and coprime to `Q`. Then a row-compact `u` has a unique projector splitting off `ker Q*(u)`, a finite projective summand of constant rank `deg Q` with Fredholm series `Q`, while the complement has series `S`. This holds on model spaces ([`isRieszColemanProjection_rieszColemanProjection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszColeman/Decomposition.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isRieszColemanProjection_rieszColemanProjection)) and on (Pr) modules ([`exists_isRieszColemanProjection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszColeman/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.exists_isRieszColemanProjection)).
- **Slopes are valuations of eigenvalues**: over a complete algebraically closed field, the multiplicity of a slope of the Fredholm polygon is the total algebraic multiplicity of the eigenvalues of that valuation ([`newtonSlopeMultiplicity_charPowerSeries_eq_finsum_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Slopes.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.newtonSlopeMultiplicity_charPowerSeries_eq_finsum_finrank)). On model spaces this is independent of the splitting extension ([`finsum_finrank_maxGenEigenspace_mapCoeffs_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Slopes/BaseChange.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.finsum_finrank_maxGenEigenspace_mapCoeffs_eq)).
- **Coleman's spectral mapping theorem**: for a row-compact `u` and an entire `B` with `B(0) = 0`, the Fredholm series of `B(u)` is Coleman's transform of `H_u` ([`charPowerSeries_eq_colemanDEntire`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/FunctionalCalculus.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.charPowerSeries_eq_colemanDEntire)).
- **Invertibility and the nonzero spectrum**: `1 − au` is invertible exactly when `H_u(a)` is a unit ([`isUnit_one_sub_smul_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isUnit_one_sub_smul_iff)). More generally, `Q*(u)` is invertible exactly when `Q` is coprime to `H_u` ([`isUnit_aeval_reverse_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule/Coprime.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.isUnit_aeval_reverse_iff)).
- **The Fredholm alternative**: `1 − u` is Fredholm of index zero for every completely continuous `u` on a Banach space over a complete field ([`isFredholm_one_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsCompletelyContinuous.isFredholm_one_sub)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Row compactness** ([`IsRowCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact)) is the working notion of compact operator. It coincides with complete continuity on `c₀` model spaces, and its idempotents have finite projective range, which is what makes every spectral summand finite.
- **Riesz–Coleman projections** ([`IsRieszColemanProjection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszColeman/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszColemanProjection)) are idempotents in the closure of `R[u]` that cut out `ker Q*(u)`. They commute with everything that commutes with `u`, and they pass through retracts and bounded base change. They are the interface that the finite-slope projectors of §4.3 will consume.
- **Completed base change of model spaces** ([`zeroAtInftyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/BanachModule/ZeroAtInfty.html#TauCeti.Huber.BanachModule.CompletedTensor.zeroAtInftyEquiv)) identifies `C₀(I, R) ⊗̂_R E` with `C₀(I, E)` on Part II's carrier, and turns tensor extension of operators into coefficientwise extension.
