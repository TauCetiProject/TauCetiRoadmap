## CompactOperators: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`020a54d` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's opening run built the Fredholm determinant of a nonarchimedean compact operator and took it as far as the resolvent. On the compactness side, [Serre's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isRowCompact_iff_isCompletelyContinuous) now says that over a complete ultrametric field a map between `c₀` model spaces is [row compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact) exactly when it is a norm limit of finite-rank maps. Over Tate rings the same equivalence holds when finitely generated submodules of the target are closed (TauCeti#19117). Alongside it came the [identity criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isCompletelyContinuous_id_iff): the identity is completely continuous only on a finitely generated module.

The determinant `det(1 − Tu)` is the [principal-minor series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/CharPowerSeries/Basic.html#Matrix.charPowerSeries), and it is [entire](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/CharPowerSeries/Basic.html#Matrix.isRestricted_charPowerSeries) whenever the row norms tend to zero. It also [varies continuously in every Gauss norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/CharPowerSeries.html#TauCeti.tendsto_gaussNorm_charPowerSeries_sub_charPowerSeries_of_tendsto_ringOpNorm) along norm-convergent sequences of row-compact operators, and it satisfies [cyclicity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Cyclicity.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.charPowerSeries_matrixCoeff_comp_comm), `det(1 − Tuv) = det(1 − Tvu)` when one factor is row compact. Supporting work covered finite matrices, block-triangular factorisation, transposition, diagonal intertwining, coefficientwise base change, and the two-sided weighted estimates of §4.4. The [Fredholm resolvent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.resolventSeries) satisfies [`(1 − Tu)V(T) = det(1 − Tu)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.one_sub_C_mul_X_mul_resolventSeries) and is [entire in operator norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isRestricted_resolventSeries) (TauCeti#19178).

Everything so far lives on `c₀` model spaces. Determinants on (Pr) modules, the tensor base change, good zeros, the invertibility criterion and all of Riesz and Coleman theory have not started. The [lattice gauge norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/LatticeGauge.html#TauCeti.latticeGauge) of §0.1 exist, but the comparison with affinoid complete continuity does not yet.

## CompactOperators: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`c1c6773` to `69cd1ab`)

The Fredholm determinant now controls the spectrum. For a row-compact `u` on a `c₀` model space, [`1 − au` is invertible exactly when `H_u(a)` is a unit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isUnit_one_sub_smul_iff). Over a field this makes [the nonzero spectrum the inverses of the zeros of `H_u`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.mem_spectrum_iff_evalAt_inv_eq_zero). The Fredholm alternative also landed: [`1 − u` is Fredholm of index zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsCompletelyContinuous.isFredholm_one_sub) for every completely continuous `u` on a Banach space over any complete field. It follows that [every zero of `H_u` is the inverse of an actual eigenvalue](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Eigenvalues.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.evalAt_charPowerSeries_eq_zero_iff_hasEigenvalue) (TauCeti#20411). Layer 3 has begun as well. At a good zero of order `h` there is now a [Riesz projector](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isRieszProjection_rieszProjection), built from Hasse derivatives of the resolvent. Its range is [the generalised eigenspace `ker (1 − au)^h`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszProjection.range_eq_ker), the projector is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszProjection.unique), and it is row compact, so its range is finite projective. Neither its rank `h` nor its determinant factor `(1 − a⁻¹T)^h` is proved yet.

The supporting work prepared those results. The [Fredholm determinant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/FredholmDet.html#ContinuousLinearMap.fredholmDet) `H_u(1)` is [multiplicative for `(1 − u)(1 − v)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/FredholmDet.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.fredholmDet_add_sub_mul). [Good zeros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/GoodZero.html#PowerSeries.IsGoodZero) of entire series come with the factorisation `F = (T − a)^h G`. Determinants also split along commuting idempotents and finite partitions. Row compactness and [the Fredholm series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.charPowerSeries) now extend to (Pr) modules, and both are [independent of the chosen presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.charPowerSeries_eq).

On the compactness side, [compactoid sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Compactoid.html#TauCeti.IsCompactoidSet) arrived, and on model spaces [row compactness is compactoid image of the unit ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/Compactoid.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isRowCompact_iff_hasCompactoidImage). Diagonal operators got [Serre's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/Diagonal.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.isCompletelyContinuous_diagonalCLM_iff), and they give the roadmap's check that [complete continuity is strictly weaker than finite rank](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/Diagonal.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.exists_isCompletelyContinuous_not_isFiniteRank).

## CompactOperators: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-09 (`69cd1ab` to `7ca783d`)

Coleman's spectral mapping theorem landed. For a row-compact `u` and an entire `B` with `B(0) = 0`, [the Fredholm series of `B(u)` is Coleman's transform of `H_u`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/FunctionalCalculus.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.charPowerSeries_eq_colemanDEntire), which replaces each inverse root `α` by `B(α)`. Getting there took the whole of §3.3: the finite resultant transform [`colemanDBounded`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Resultant/Coleman.html#Polynomial.colemanDBounded) with its finite matrix version, Newton and Coleman majorants, a Cauchy estimate, and the [entire transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/Coleman/Entire.html#PowerSeries.colemanDEntire) as a Gauss-norm limit. The Riesz projector is finished as well. At a good zero of order `h` its summand [has rank `h` at every prime](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection/Multiplicity.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszProjection.rankAtStalk_range), and [`H_u` factors as `(1 − a⁻¹T)^h` times a complementary unit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection/Multiplicity.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszProjection.charPowerSeries_matrixCoeff_eq_pow_mul). Over a field the generalised eigenspaces have dimension `h`, and this carries over to [(Pr) modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.finrank_ker_pow). Nonzero spectral values are [isolated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.eventually_notMem_spectrum), and only finitely many have norm bounded below. Commuting families have joint eigenvectors in each Riesz summand.

Layer 0 got its comparison theorems. On any ultrametric Banach space, [compactoid image is the same as complete continuity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Compactoid/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.hasCompactoidImage_iff_isCompletelyContinuous). Compact operators are completely continuous, and the converse holds [over locally compact fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Compactoid/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isCompactOperator_iff_isCompletelyContinuous). Kernels and radius improvement (§0.3) went from nothing to substantially done: coefficient kernels, row-compact weighted substitutions, [completely continuous radius inclusions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/RestrictedRadius.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.isCompletelyContinuous_restrictedRadiusCLM) and the locally analytic transition maps.

On the slope side, the Fredholm polygon now has [locally finite slopes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Slopes.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.finite_setOf_unitSlope_newtonPolygon_charPowerSeries_le) with multiplicities. Finite DVR lattices have [Newton above Hodge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Lattice.html#Matrix.prefixSum_le_newtonPolygon_charpolyRev_of_mul_mul_eq_diagonal), proved via Smith normal form. Still missing are the Riesz–Coleman projector for coprime polynomial factors and everything downstream of it: finite-slope projectors and the slope–eigenvalue comparison.

## CompactOperators: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The Riesz–Coleman decomposition of §3.4 landed (TauCeti#40247). Suppose `H_u = QS`, where `Q` is a polynomial with `Q(0) = 1` and unit leading coefficient, and `S` is entire and coprime to `Q`. Then a row-compact `u` has a [Riesz–Coleman projector](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszColeman/Decomposition.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isRieszColemanProjection_rieszColemanProjection), and it is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszColeman/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszColemanProjection.unique). It splits off `ker Q*(u)`, a finite projective summand of [constant rank `deg Q`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszColeman/Decomposition.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszColemanProjection.rankAtStalk_range) on which [the Fredholm series is `Q`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszColeman/Decomposition.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszColemanProjection.charPowerSeries_matrixCoeff_mul), with `S` on the complement. The construction carries over to [(Pr) modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszColeman/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.exists_isRieszColemanProjection) and through bounded base change. A companion criterion says that [`Q*(u)` is invertible exactly when `Q` is coprime to `H_u`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule/Coprime.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.isUnit_aeval_reverse_iff).

Slopes are now tied to eigenvalues. Over a complete algebraically closed field, [the multiplicity of a slope `σ` in the Fredholm polygon is the total algebraic multiplicity of the eigenvalues of valuation `σ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Slopes.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.newtonSlopeMultiplicity_charPowerSeries_eq_finsum_finrank). The proof goes through two facts: [algebraic multiplicities are orders of Fredholm zeros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.finrank_maxGenEigenspace_eq_order_taylor), and Newton slope multiplicities count zeros. Cutoff counts and an eigenvalue at every finite slope come with it. On model spaces, the counts [do not depend on the splitting extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Slopes/BaseChange.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.finsum_finrank_maxGenEigenspace_mapCoeffs_eq).

This section also records, late, several pull requests that landed earlier. The main one is the [completed base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/BanachModule/ZeroAtInfty.html#TauCeti.Huber.BanachModule.CompletedTensor.zeroAtInftyEquiv) `C₀(I, R) ⊗̂_R E ≃ C₀(I, E)` on Part II's carrier. It identifies tensor extension of operators with coefficientwise extension, but determinants have not yet been transported to abstract (Pr) modules. The others brought the row Hodge polygon, and an explicit matrix whose minor series is entire although it is [not row compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/Matrix/TwoSided.html#TauCeti.TwoSidedExample.not_isRowCompact_operator). The rest of the window tightened existing estimates for the resolvent and Coleman's transform. Finite-slope projectors (§4.3) have not started, even though their input from §3.4 is now available.
