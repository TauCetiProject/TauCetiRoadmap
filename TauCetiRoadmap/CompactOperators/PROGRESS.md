## CompactOperators: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`020a54d` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's opening run built the Fredholm determinant of a nonarchimedean compact operator and took it as far as the resolvent. On the compactness side, [Serre's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isRowCompact_iff_isCompletelyContinuous) now says that over a complete ultrametric field a map between `c₀` model spaces is [row compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact) exactly when it is a norm limit of finite-rank maps. Over Tate rings the same equivalence holds when finitely generated submodules of the target are closed (TauCeti#19117). Alongside it came the [identity criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isCompletelyContinuous_id_iff): the identity is completely continuous only on a finitely generated module.

The determinant `det(1 − Tu)` is the [principal-minor series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/CharPowerSeries/Basic.html#Matrix.charPowerSeries), and it is [entire](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/CharPowerSeries/Basic.html#Matrix.isRestricted_charPowerSeries) whenever the row norms tend to zero. It also [varies continuously in every Gauss norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/CharPowerSeries.html#TauCeti.tendsto_gaussNorm_charPowerSeries_sub_charPowerSeries_of_tendsto_ringOpNorm) along norm-convergent sequences of row-compact operators, and it satisfies [cyclicity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Cyclicity.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.charPowerSeries_matrixCoeff_comp_comm), `det(1 − Tuv) = det(1 − Tvu)` when one factor is row compact. Supporting work covered finite matrices, block-triangular factorisation, transposition, diagonal intertwining, coefficientwise base change, and the two-sided weighted estimates of §4.4. The [Fredholm resolvent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.resolventSeries) satisfies [`(1 − Tu)V(T) = det(1 − Tu)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.one_sub_C_mul_X_mul_resolventSeries) and is [entire in operator norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isRestricted_resolventSeries) (TauCeti#19178).

Everything so far lives on `c₀` model spaces. Determinants on (Pr) modules, the tensor base change, good zeros, the invertibility criterion and all of Riesz and Coleman theory have not started. The [lattice gauge norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/LatticeGauge.html#TauCeti.latticeGauge) of §0.1 exist, but the comparison with affinoid complete continuity does not yet.
