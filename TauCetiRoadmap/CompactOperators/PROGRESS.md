## CompactOperators: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`020a54d` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's opening run built the Fredholm determinant of a nonarchimedean compact operator and took it as far as the resolvent. On the compactness side, [Serre's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isRowCompact_iff_isCompletelyContinuous) now says that over a complete ultrametric field a map between `c₀` model spaces is [row compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact) exactly when it is a norm limit of finite-rank maps. Over Tate rings the same equivalence holds when finitely generated submodules of the target are closed (TauCeti#19117). Alongside it came the [identity criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isCompletelyContinuous_id_iff): the identity is completely continuous only on a finitely generated module.

The determinant `det(1 − Tu)` is the [principal-minor series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/CharPowerSeries/Basic.html#Matrix.charPowerSeries), and it is [entire](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/CharPowerSeries/Basic.html#Matrix.isRestricted_charPowerSeries) whenever the row norms tend to zero. It also [varies continuously in every Gauss norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/CharPowerSeries.html#TauCeti.tendsto_gaussNorm_charPowerSeries_sub_charPowerSeries_of_tendsto_ringOpNorm) along norm-convergent sequences of row-compact operators, and it satisfies [cyclicity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Cyclicity.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.charPowerSeries_matrixCoeff_comp_comm), `det(1 − Tuv) = det(1 − Tvu)` when one factor is row compact. Supporting work covered finite matrices, block-triangular factorisation, transposition, diagonal intertwining, coefficientwise base change, and the two-sided weighted estimates of §4.4. The [Fredholm resolvent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.resolventSeries) satisfies [`(1 − Tu)V(T) = det(1 − Tu)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.one_sub_C_mul_X_mul_resolventSeries) and is [entire in operator norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Resolvent.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isRestricted_resolventSeries) (TauCeti#19178).

Everything so far lives on `c₀` model spaces. Determinants on (Pr) modules, the tensor base change, good zeros, the invertibility criterion and all of Riesz and Coleman theory have not started. The [lattice gauge norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/LatticeGauge.html#TauCeti.latticeGauge) of §0.1 exist, but the comparison with affinoid complete continuity does not yet.

## CompactOperators: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`c1c6773` to `69cd1ab`)

The Fredholm determinant now controls the spectrum. For a row-compact `u` on a `c₀` model space, [`1 − au` is invertible exactly when `H_u(a)` is a unit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isUnit_one_sub_smul_iff). Over a field this makes [the nonzero spectrum the inverses of the zeros of `H_u`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.mem_spectrum_iff_evalAt_inv_eq_zero). The Fredholm alternative also landed: [`1 − u` is Fredholm of index zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fredholm/CompletelyContinuous.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsCompletelyContinuous.isFredholm_one_sub) for every completely continuous `u` on a Banach space over any complete field. It follows that [every zero of `H_u` is the inverse of an actual eigenvalue](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/Eigenvalues.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.evalAt_charPowerSeries_eq_zero_iff_hasEigenvalue) (TauCeti#20411). Layer 3 has begun as well. At a good zero of order `h` there is now a [Riesz projector](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.isRieszProjection_rieszProjection), built from Hasse derivatives of the resolvent. Its range is [the generalised eigenspace `ker (1 − au)^h`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszProjection.range_eq_ker), the projector is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/RieszProjection.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRieszProjection.unique), and it is row compact, so its range is finite projective. Neither its rank `h` nor its determinant factor `(1 − a⁻¹T)^h` is proved yet.

The supporting work prepared those results. The [Fredholm determinant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/FredholmDet.html#ContinuousLinearMap.fredholmDet) `H_u(1)` is [multiplicative for `(1 − u)(1 − v)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/FredholmDet.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsRowCompact.fredholmDet_add_sub_mul). [Good zeros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/GoodZero.html#PowerSeries.IsGoodZero) of entire series come with the factorisation `F = (T − a)^h G`. Determinants also split along commuting idempotents and finite partitions. Row compactness and [the Fredholm series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.charPowerSeries) now extend to (Pr) modules, and both are [independent of the chosen presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nonarchimedean/PrModule.html#ContinuousLinearMap.IsPrCompact.charPowerSeries_eq).

On the compactness side, [compactoid sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Compactoid.html#TauCeti.IsCompactoidSet) arrived, and on model spaces [row compactness is compactoid image of the unit ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/Compactoid.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isRowCompact_iff_hasCompactoidImage). Diagonal operators got [Serre's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/Diagonal.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.isCompletelyContinuous_diagonalCLM_iff), and they give the roadmap's check that [complete continuity is strictly weaker than finite rank](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RowCompact/Diagonal.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.exists_isCompletelyContinuous_not_isFiniteRank).
