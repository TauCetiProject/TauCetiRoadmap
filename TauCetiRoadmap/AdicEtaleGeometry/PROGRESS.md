## AdicEtaleGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (`9b98520` to `273ea28`)

The roadmap's first results are the split-ring vocabulary at the base of its finite étale layer, the conclusion of Scholze's Lemma 15.3 in *Étale cohomology of diamonds*. A ring [has no nonsplit finite étale covers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/FiniteEtaleSplit.html#TauCeti.IsFiniteEtaleSplit) when every finite étale algebra over it is [totally split](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/FiniteEtaleSplit.html#TauCeti.Algebra.IsTotallySplit), a finite product of quotients by idempotents, so the number of sheets may vary over clopen pieces. The [section criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/FiniteEtaleSplit.html#TauCeti.isFiniteEtaleSplit_iff_exists_section) says this happens exactly when every faithfully flat finite étale algebra has a section. For a field the condition is [separable closedness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/FiniteEtaleSplit.html#TauCeti.isFiniteEtaleSplit_iff_isSepClosed). In the form the perfectoid application uses, every monic separable polynomial of positive degree [has a root](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/FiniteEtaleSplit.html#TauCeti.IsFiniteEtaleSplit.exists_root) (TauCeti#18924). A follow-up shows that the property passes to [arbitrary products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/FiniteEtaleSplit.html#TauCeti.IsFiniteEtaleSplit.pi). It is also [invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/FiniteEtaleSplit.html#TauCeti.isFiniteEtaleSplit_iff_of_isEquivalence) under ring maps along which base change is an equivalence on finite étale algebras.

Separately, [henselian pairs are stable under directed colimits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/DirectLimit.html#Ring.DirectLimit.henselianRing). The proof comes with lemmas that pull ideal membership, units modulo an ideal and monic polynomials back from a direct limit to a finite stage. Supporting work refines covering idempotents to orthogonal families and shows that the support of a finitely presented flat module is clopen.

These close the first two subsections of the finite étale layer. Henselianity of power-bounded subrings, descent of finite étale algebras from filtered colimits and everything beyond them have not started. Neither have layers A0, A2 and A3, and the torsor layer A4 has only the split-ring vocabulary it shares with this one.

## AdicEtaleGeometry: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

Finite étale geometry now reaches Huber pairs. For a Huber pair `(A, A⁺)` and a finite `A`-algebra `B` with its natural topology, [the Huber pair of a finite algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Pair.html#TauCeti.Huber.Pair.finiteAlgebra) is `(B, B⁺)`, with `B⁺` the integral closure of the image of `A⁺`. Its [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Pair.html#TauCeti.Huber.Pair.finiteAlgebra.homEquiv) says that morphisms of Huber pairs out of it under `(A, A⁺)` are exactly the `A`-algebra maps, and when `B` is finite étale the structure morphism is [finite étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/Pair.html#TauCeti.Huber.Pair.finiteAlgebra.isFiniteEtale_algebraHom) (TauCeti#20608). Underneath this, a finite algebra over a Huber ring [is Huber](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/FiniteAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsHuberRing.of_isModuleTopology) and over a Tate ring [is Tate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/ModuleTopology/FiniteAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsTateRing.of_isModuleTopology). The argument goes through multiplier rings: the multiplier ring of a finitely generated submodule containing `1` [is integral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Submodule/MultiplierRing.html#Submodule.isIntegral_of_mem_multiplierRing). The fibre bounds, surjectivity on spectra and base change of that roadmap target are still to come.

Two more layers have begun. [Pseudocoherent modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/PseudoCoherent.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsPseudoCoherentOver) are defined by finite free resolutions. In degree 0 they are the finitely generated modules and in degree 1 the finitely presented ones, and the condition can be checked [one degree at a time](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/PseudoCoherent.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsPseudoCoherentOver.succ_iff). Over a noetherian ring every finitely generated module [is pseudocoherent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/PseudoCoherent.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsPseudoCoherentOver.of_isNoetherianRing). The [analytic points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PreAdicSpace/Analytic.html#TauCeti.PreAdicSpace.analyticPoints) of a pre-adic space [form an open set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PreAdicSpace/Analytic.html#TauCeti.PreAdicSpace.isOpen_analyticPoints) that is compatible with restriction, and on `Spa(A, A⁺)` they [recover the analytic locus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PreAdicSpace/Analytic.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.analyticPoints_presentationLimitPreAdicSpace). They are not yet packaged as an open adic subspace.

For filtered colimits, a map from a finitely presented algebra into a directed colimit [factors through a stage](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Colimit/FinitePresentation.html#TauCeti.DirectLimit.Algebra.exists_eq_comp_of_finitePresentation), and base change [commutes with directed colimits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/TensorProduct/DirectLimit.html#TauCeti.directLimitLeft). That is the morphism half of finite étale descent; descent of objects is still open. Strictly henselian local rings [have no nonsplit finite étale covers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Etale.html#TauCeti.IsFiniteEtaleSplit.of_henselianLocalRing), which completes the checks for split rings.
