## TopologicalSheafCohomology: 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-09 (`7ad4bea` to `7ca783d`)

The roadmap's finite-fiber end theorem landed. For a closed map out of a Hausdorff space whose fiber over y is finite, the canonical map from the stalk of the direct image at y to the product of the stalks over the fiber is an isomorphism ([`isIso_fiberStalkMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/FiberStalk.html#TopCat.Presheaf.isIso_fiberStalkMap), TauCeti#24063). Proper maps are closed, so this covers the proper case the roadmap asks for. The same work gives the map's germ formula, its naturality, and its behaviour under identities and composites.

Čech cohomology now exists as a colimit over point-neighborhood covers on an arbitrary space ([`cechCohomology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.Topology.cechCohomology)). It equals global sections in degree zero and vanishes on the empty space. Mutually cofinal cover systems give canonically isomorphic colimits, and on a compact space the finite covers already compute it ([`finiteCechCohomologyIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/Cech/Cofinal.html#TauCeti.Topology.finiteCechCohomologyIso)). Over a linearly ordered index type, the ordered complex on strictly increasing tuples is homotopy equivalent to the tuple complex ([`strictMonoCechComplexHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Cech/Ordered/HomotopyEquiv.html#TauCeti.CategoryTheory.strictMonoCechComplexHomotopyEquiv)).

On compact normal spaces, short exact sequences of module sheaves now have a long exact Čech sequence, with connecting map [`cechCohomologyδ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/Cech/LongExactSequence.html#CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.ShortExact.cechCohomologyδ). The proof goes through vanishing for presheaves that are zero on the empty open and have zero sheafification. Flasque sheaves have no higher Čech cohomology, so the connecting map shifts dimension. On the derived side, a resolution by objects adapted to a functor computes its right derived functors ([`rightDerivedIsoOfResolution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Abelian/AcyclicResolution.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.rightDerivedIsoOfResolution)). In particular, Godement sections compute derived global sections of module sheaves ([`rightDerivedΓIsoGodement`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/Cohomology/Godement.html#TauCeti.Topology.rightDerivedΓIsoGodement)). What is still missing is the isomorphism that joins the two sides, Čechⁿ ≅ RⁿΓ on compact Hausdorff spaces.
