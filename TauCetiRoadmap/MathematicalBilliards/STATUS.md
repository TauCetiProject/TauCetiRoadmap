### Named results

- **Wiener's lemma on the line** — for a finite measure `σ` on ℝ, the time averages of `|charFun σ|²` tend to the sum of the squared masses of its atoms ([`tendsto_average_norm_charFun_sq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/CharacteristicFunction/Wiener.html#TauCeti.tendsto_average_norm_charFun_sq)).
- **The continuous-time twisted mean ergodic theorem** — for a unitary C₀-group on a Hilbert space, the averages twisted by a frequency converge to the projection onto that eigenspace, whose squared norm is the spectral mass at that frequency ([`exists_tendsto_twistedAverage`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/SpectralMeasure/Atoms.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousGroup.IsUnitary.exists_tendsto_twistedAverage)).
- **Strong continuity of the Koopman representation** — a jointly measurable action of ℝ preserving an s-finite measure acts strongly continuously on every `Lᵖ`, `p ≠ ∞`, with no topology on the space, and unitarily on `L²` ([`continuous_compMeasurePreserving_vadd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Ergodic/Action/Koopman.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.continuous_compMeasurePreserving_vadd)).
- **Rotation numbers of invariant graphs** — for a `C²` table, an invariant graph has rotation number in `(0, 1)`, and it equals `p/q` exactly when some state on the graph returns after `q` bounces having gone `p` times around ([`rotationNumber_eq_div_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/RotationNumber.html#TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.IsInvariantGraph.rotationNumber_eq_div_iff)). Every such graph is also Lipschitz ([`exists_lipschitzWith`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/InvariantGraph/Lipschitz.html#TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.IsInvariantGraph.exists_lipschitzWith)).
- **The corner bound** — in a wedge of angle `α`, a billiard trajectory whose unfolded line misses the vertex makes at most `⌈π/α⌉` reflections ([`encard_wedgeReflectionTimes_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/Wedge.html#TauCeti.Billiard.encard_wedgeReflectionTimes_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Convex tables** ([`ConvexTable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/ConvexTable.html#TauCeti.ConvexTable)) — the roadmap's carrier of `C^k` tables in ℂ. A [bridge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/ConvexTable.html#TauCeti.ConvexTable.toStrictConvexBilliardTable) makes the existing next-collision map, its inverse, smoothness, generating function and invariant measure for strictly convex tables available to it.
- **Rotational invariant curves** ([`IsRotationalInvariantCurve`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Annulus/RotationalInvariantCurve.html#TauCeti.IsRotationalInvariantCurve)) — invariant Jordan curves that are not null-homotopic in the annulus. These are the hypothesis of Birkhoff's graph theorem.
- **Polygonal tables** ([`PolygonalTable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/PolygonalTable.html#TauCeti.PolygonalTable)) — compact regions bounded by simple polygons, holes allowed, with each frontier point off the vertices in exactly one side.
