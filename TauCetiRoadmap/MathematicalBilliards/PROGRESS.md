## MathematicalBilliards: 2026-11-24 to 2026-11-25 (`ace3bd3` to `0253c4f`)

The first window of this roadmap put most of the analytic core of weak mixing in place. [Wiener's lemma on the line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/CharacteristicFunction/Wiener.html#TauCeti.tendsto_average_norm_charFun_sq) says that the time averages of `|charFun σ|²` tend to the sum of the squared atom masses of `σ`. The [continuous-time twisted mean ergodic theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/SpectralMeasure/Atoms.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousGroup.IsUnitary.exists_tendsto_twistedAverage) shows that twisted averages of a unitary C₀-group converge to the projection onto an eigenspace, whose squared norm is the spectral mass at that frequency (TauCeti#43173). The [Koopman group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Ergodic/Action/Koopman.html#TauCeti.koopmanGroup) of a jointly measurable action of ℝ is [strongly continuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Ergodic/Action/Koopman.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.continuous_compMeasurePreserving_vadd) on every `Lᵖ`, `p ≠ ∞`, for s-finite invariant measures, more general than the probability case the roadmap needs. Measurable eigenfunctions of ergodic actions are now [its L² eigenvectors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Ergodic/Action/Eigenfunction.html#TauCeti.toLp_mem_eigenspace_koopmanGroup). Weak mixing itself, the eigenfunction criterion and the multiplier property are not yet stated.

On convex tables, the roadmap's [carrier](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/ConvexTable.html#TauCeti.ConvexTable) landed with its basic geometry and a [bridge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/ConvexTable.html#TauCeti.ConvexTable.toStrictConvexBilliardTable) to the strictly convex tables already in the library. Most of the billiard work runs through that bridge, in the coordinates `(s, cos δ)`. The billiard map extends to a [homeomorphism of the closed cylinder](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/ClosedCylinder.html#TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.closedBilliardHomeomorph). For `C²` tables, invariant graphs have a [rotation number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/RotationNumber.html#TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.IsInvariantGraph.rotationNumber) in `(0, 1)`. It is [rational exactly when the graph carries a periodic orbit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/RotationNumber.html#TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.IsInvariantGraph.rotationNumber_eq_div_iff) of that type. Every invariant graph is [Lipschitz](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/InvariantGraph/Lipschitz.html#TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.IsInvariantGraph.exists_lipschitzWith), and on the circle the invariant graphs are [exactly the constant levels](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/Circle.html#TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.IsInvariantGraph.exists_eq_const_circle). All of this assumes the curve is already a graph. Birkhoff's graph theorem, which would derive that for every [rotational invariant curve](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Annulus/RotationalInvariantCurve.html#TauCeti.IsRotationalInvariantCurve), is not there yet.

Polygonal billiards have started. The [polygonal table](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/PolygonalTable.html#TauCeti.PolygonalTable) may have holes, and side reflections preserve arclength on directions. In a wedge of angle `α`, a trajectory that misses the vertex makes at most `⌈π/α⌉` reflections: this is the [corner bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/Wedge.html#TauCeti.Billiard.encard_wedgeReflectionTimes_le).
