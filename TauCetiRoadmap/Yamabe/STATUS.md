## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The smooth conformal geometry is largely in place. Y0's conformal identities, the Weyl–Schouten theorem on manifolds, the smooth variational theory of the Yamabe quotient and Aubin's bound Y(M) ≤ Y(Sⁿ) are all proved. The weak H¹ theory, the sharp Sobolev inequality, the developing map and every existence theorem are still open. The local expansions, Aubin's strict inequality, the low-dimensional and locally conformally flat positive-mass lanes, and the examples (Y4, Y5, Y7, Y8, Y10) have not begun.

### Roadmap coverage

Y0 is partial. The conformal laws are proved, and so are the smooth quotient identities with the Euler–Lagrange equation. Y0.5's converse Weyl–Schouten theorem and its comparison with conformal Euclidean charts are proved on boundaryless manifolds. Three things are missing: the H¹ statements of Y0.2–Y0.3, Y0.5's smooth dependence of flattening solutions on the initial data, and the developing map of Y0.6. Of Y0.6, only Liouville's theorem and bijectivity of a local diffeomorphism from a closed connected manifold onto Sⁿ are present. For Y9 it is uniqueness of conformal constant-scalar-curvature metrics of equal volume when the scalar curvature is nonpositive (Y9.2). Y4, Y5, Y7, Y8 and Y10 are untouched.

## The frontier

- **The sharp Euclidean Sobolev inequality** — prove a_n∫|∇u|² ≥ Y(Sⁿ)‖u‖²_(p_n) on D¹,² and classify its equality cases (Y2.1–Y2.2). The extremal energy and the D¹,² carrier are in place.

The manifold Weyl–Schouten theorem and Liouville's theorem are now available; the UniversalCovers lane is still needed.

- **Theorem A** — subcritical minimizers and the compactness argument below Y(Sⁿ) (Y3.2–Y3.3). These need the first eigenvalue, the critical bootstrap (Y1.2–Y1.3) and the ε-transfer of Y2.4.
