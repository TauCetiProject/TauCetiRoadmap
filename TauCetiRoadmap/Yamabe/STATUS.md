## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The smooth foundations are largely in place. Y0's conformal identities, the Yamabe quotient's smooth variational theory and Aubin's bound Y(M) ≤ Y(Sⁿ) are proved, and the Weyl–Schouten theorem holds for metrics on a vector space. The weak H¹ theory, the sharp Sobolev inequality and every existence theorem are still open. The local expansions, Aubin's strict inequality, the positive-mass lane and the examples (Y4, Y5, Y7, Y8, Y10) have not begun.

## The frontier

- **Weyl–Schouten on manifolds** — move the vector-space theorem through charts, using the new invariance of the Weyl, Cotton and Schouten tensors under local isometries. Then compare local conformal flatness with conformally Euclidean charts.
- **The sharp Euclidean Sobolev inequality** — prove a_n∫|∇u|² ≥ Y(Sⁿ)‖u‖²_(p_n) on D¹,² and classify its equality cases (Y2.1–Y2.2). The extremal energy and the D¹,² carrier are in place.
- **The weak domain** — extend the C¹ Sobolev inequality to H¹ and restate Q's conformal invariance and Euler–Lagrange equation there (Y1.1, Y0.2–Y0.3). This waits on the K5a and PDE supplier carriers.
- **The developing map** — construct the developing map of a locally conformally flat manifold by continuation, and prove it is a conformal diffeomorphism onto Sⁿ for compact simply connected M. This needs the manifold Weyl–Schouten theorem and the UniversalCovers lane.
- **Theorem A** — subcritical minimizers and the compactness argument below Y(Sⁿ) (Y3.2–Y3.3). These need the first eigenvalue, the critical bootstrap (Y1.2–Y1.3) and the ε-transfer of Y2.4.
