## Yamabe: 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-21 (`323ba49` to `c1c6773`)

The Euclidean side of the Yamabe problem now has its extremal functions. The bubbles U_{t,b}(x) = (2t/(t² + |x−b|²))^((n−2)/2) are shown to solve the sphere's Yamabe equation ([`conformalLaplacian_bubble`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/ConformalLaplacian.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.conformalLaplacian_bubble)), to be stereographic pullbacks of the round metric, and to attain the sphere's Yamabe constant Y(Sⁿ) = n(n−1)|Sⁿ|^(2/n) in the Sobolev quotient ([`conformalCoefficient_mul_integral_norm_fderiv_bubble_sq_eq_sphereYamabeConstant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/Bubble/SobolevQuotient.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.conformalCoefficient_mul_integral_norm_fderiv_bubble_sq_eq_sphereYamabeConstant)). That is the equality case of the sharp Sobolev inequality; the inequality itself, and the classification of its extremals, are not yet proved. Along the way came the area of the round sphere, 2π^((n+1)/2)/Γ((n+1)/2) ([`roundSphereVolume_eq_two_mul_pi_rpow_div_Gamma`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Sphere.html#TauCeti.roundSphereVolume_eq_two_mul_pi_rpow_div_Gamma)), and radial moment integrals of the bubbles, the first of the estimates the later test-function work needs.

On the manifold side, the [`conformal Laplacian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/ConformalLaplacian.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.conformalLaplacian) is defined, and the [`Weyl tensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Weyl.html#CovariantDerivative.weylTensor) is built from the Schouten tensor and the Kulkarni–Nomizu product, with its symmetries and its vanishing in dimension three ([`weylTensor_eq_zero_of_finrank_eq_three`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Weyl.html#CovariantDerivative.IsMetricCompatible.weylTensor_eq_zero_of_finrank_eq_three)). For conformal change, the window supplies the transformed Levi-Civita connection ([`isLeviCivitaConnection_rescale_addOneForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/LeviCivita/Conformal.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.isLeviCivitaConnection_rescale_addOneForm)), the identity Vol(ĝ) = ∫u^(p_n) dV_g ([`riemannianTotalVolume_rescale_rpow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Conformal.html#TauCeti.WithMetric.riemannianTotalVolume_rescale_rpow)), the conformal class with its positive-function parametrisation, and the metric-indexed copy [`WithMetric`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/WithMetric.html#TauCeti.WithMetric), which lets two metrics on one manifold be compared without clashing instances. The central transformation law Scal_ĝ = u^(−(n+2)/(n−2)) L_g u has not landed yet.

## Yamabe: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`c1c6773` to `93d1fe5`)

The central transformation law of the Yamabe problem has landed: for ĝ = u^(4/(n−2))g with u positive, the scalar curvature is Scal_ĝ = u^(−(n+2)/(n−2)) L_g u ([`scalarCurvature_rescale_eq_rpow_mul_conformalLaplacian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/ConformalLaplacian.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.scalarCurvature_rescale_eq_rpow_mul_conformalLaplacian)), so ĝ has constant scalar curvature c exactly when u solves the Yamabe equation L_g u = c u^(p_n−1) ([`scalarCurvature_rescale_eq_iff_conformalLaplacian_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/ConformalLaplacian.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.scalarCurvature_rescale_eq_iff_conformalLaplacian_eq)). It rests on chain rules for the Laplace–Beltrami operator and the gradient of a logarithm. Integrating it gives ∫Scal_ĝ dV_ĝ = ∫u L_g u dV_g and its volume-normalised form on compact M ([`integral_scalarCurvature_rescale_div_riemannianTotalVolume_rpow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/TotalScalarCurvature.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.integral_scalarCurvature_rescale_div_riemannianTotalVolume_rpow)). These identities hold only for smooth positive u. The operator identity for L_ĝ, the integration-by-parts form of the Yamabe quotient and its conformal invariance on H¹ are still missing.

On the conformal-tensor side, the Weyl tensor transforms as W^(fg) = f W^g away from dimension two ([`weylTensor_leviCivitaConnection_rescale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Conformal.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.weylTensor_leviCivitaConnection_rescale)), and the Schouten tensor gets its conformal law. Local conformal flatness is now [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/ConformallyFlat.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.IsLocallyConformallyFlat), and it is shown to depend only on the conformal class and to force the Weyl tensor to vanish ([`IsLocallyConformallyFlat.weylTensor_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/ConformallyFlat.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.IsLocallyConformallyFlat.weylTensor_eq_zero)). That is the easy half of the Weyl–Schouten theorem. The Frobenius converse is not there. The [Cotton tensor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Cotton.html#CovariantDerivative.cottonTensor) is defined, with its antisymmetry, cyclic identity and vanishing in constant curvature. Its conformal law and div W = (n−3)C have not been proved.

## Yamabe: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-13 (`93d1fe5` to `aa6e43e`)

The converse of the Weyl–Schouten theorem has landed, for metrics on a vector space. Such a metric is locally conformally flat exactly when its Weyl tensor vanishes, in dimension at least four ([`isLocallyConformallyFlat_iff_weylTensor_eq_zero_model_space`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/ConformallyFlat/ModelSpace.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.isLocallyConformallyFlat_iff_weylTensor_eq_zero_model_space)), and exactly when its Cotton tensor vanishes, in dimension three ([`isLocallyConformallyFlat_iff_cottonTensor_eq_zero_model_space`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/ConformallyFlat/ModelSpace.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.isLocallyConformallyFlat_iff_cottonTensor_eq_zero_model_space)). The proof solves the conformal flattening equation Hess φ = P + dφ⊗dφ − |dφ|²g/2 by a Frobenius argument. It rests on the contracted second Bianchi identity, the conformal law of the Cotton tensor and div W = (n−3)C ([`sum_derivWeylTensor_self_eq_mul_cottonTensor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/Curvature/Weyl/Divergence.html#CovariantDerivative.IsMetricCompatible.sum_derivWeylTensor_self_eq_mul_cottonTensor)), all new in this window. The statement on a general manifold is not there yet; invariance of these tensors under local isometries is the first step towards it. Liouville's theorem also landed: a C³ conformal map on a connected open set, in dimension at least three, is a similarity, possibly composed with an inversion ([`exists_eq_smul_linearIsometry_add_or_inversion_of_conformalAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Conformal/Liouville.html#TauCeti.exists_eq_smul_linearIsometry_add_or_inversion_of_conformalAt)).

The [Yamabe quotient](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/Quotient.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.yamabeQuotient) is now defined, and its smooth variational theory is largely in place. Its critical points are exactly the solutions of L_g u = λ|u|^(p_n−2)u ([`forall_hasDerivAt_yamabeQuotient_add_smul_iff_exists_conformalLaplacian_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/Quotient.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.forall_hasDerivAt_yamabeQuotient_add_smul_iff_exists_conformalLaplacian_eq)), and it is conformally invariant. Aubin's bound Y(M) ≤ Y(Sⁿ) is proved by concentrating cutoff bubbles in a coordinate ball ([`le_sphereYamabeConstant_of_forall_le_yamabeQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/UpperBound.html#TauCeti.Yamabe.le_sphereYamabeConstant_of_forall_le_yamabeQuotient)). Conformal constant-scalar-curvature metrics of equal volume coincide when the scalar curvature is nonpositive ([`eq_of_mem_conformalClass_of_scalarCurvature_nonpos_of_volume_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Yamabe/Uniqueness.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.eq_of_mem_conformalClass_of_scalarCurvature_nonpos_of_volume_eq)). All of this is for smooth functions; the H¹ statements are still missing.

Supporting work covered the critical Sobolev and Trudinger inequalities for C¹ functions on closed manifolds, the homogeneous space D¹,² with its translations and dilations, finer bubble moments (including dimension six's logarithmic terms), and the fact that deleting a point from a manifold of dimension at least three leaves its fundamental group unchanged ([`fundamentalGroup_map_subtypeVal_compl_singleton_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroup/ComplSingleton.html#TauCeti.fundamentalGroup_map_subtypeVal_compl_singleton_bijective)).
