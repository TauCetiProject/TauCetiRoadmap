## TangentialBordism: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`1a7a225` to `7433fc3`)

The first work on this roadmap is in its differential-topology layer. It starts with **Sard's theorem for manifolds with boundary**: for a `C^k` map from a Lindelöf manifold modelled on the half-space, the critical values are [null](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Sard/HalfSpace.html#ContMDiff.isNullSet_image_criticalPoints_halfSpace) and the regular values [dense](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Sard/HalfSpace.html#ContMDiff.dense_isRegularValue_halfSpace), with no relation imposed between the two dimensions (TauCeti#41008). The proof splits the source into its interior and its boundary and applies the boundaryless theorem to each piece. Next to it is the **parametric transversality theorem**: if a family `F : M × S → N` is transverse to an embedding `Z → N`, then the parameters whose slice fails to be transverse form a [null set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Sard/Transversality.html#ContMDiff.isNullSet_setOf_exists_not_transverse), so the good ones are [dense](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Sard/Transversality.html#ContMDiff.dense_setOf_forall_transverse) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/2916). For now this holds only when all four manifolds are boundaryless. The facewise version for half-space and cornered sources, which the roadmap needs, is still to come.

Manifolds with corners now have [depth strata](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Corners/Stratum.html#ModelWithCorners.depthStratum). On a manifold modelled on the quadrant `[0, ∞)ⁿ`, the depth-`k` stratum is a [boundaryless (n−k)-manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Corners/Stratum.html#TauCeti.isManifold_depthStratum) whose inclusion is a [smooth embedding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Corners/Stratum.html#TauCeti.isSmoothEmbedding_subtypeVal_depthStratum). The strata partition the manifold, and the depth-zero stratum is its interior. The construction covers the global quadrant model only, not DifferentialGeometry's face-structured manifolds. Every compact subset of a boundaryless σ-compact manifold now lies inside a [compact regular sublevel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Scalar/RegularDomain.html#IsCompact.exists_isRegularValue_isCompact_setOfPred_le) of a smooth function, which is a compact manifold with boundary; the interior, closure and frontier of such sublevels are computed as well. Smaller pieces include a time collar for homotopies and a positive continuous radius around the graph of a map, which feed the smoothing theorem.
