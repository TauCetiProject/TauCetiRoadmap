## VariationalMethods: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-13 (`a7fe63e` to `aa6e43e`)

The roadmap opens with the direct method of the calculus of variations in its topological form ([`exists_isMinOn_of_weak_closed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/DirectMethod.html#TauCeti.exists_isMinOn_of_weak_closed)): an energy that is lower semicontinuous for the weak topology and has norm-bounded sublevels attains its minimum on any nonempty weakly closed set in a reflexive space (TauCeti#39025). No lower bound on the energy is assumed, since one nonempty bounded sublevel is weakly compact and supplies it. The proof works over any proper nontrivially normed field, with values in any linear order. Along the way the sublevels and the whole set of minimizers are shown to be weakly compact ([`isCompact_toWeakSpace_minimizers`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/DirectMethod.html#TauCeti.isCompact_toWeakSpace_minimizers)).

The supporting work is the bounded-sublevel predicate [`HasBoundedSublevelsOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/BoundedSublevels.html#TauCeti.HasBoundedSublevelsOn), relative to an admissible set. It is characterized by sequences, by divergence of the energy at infinity and by a quantitative lower bound outside large balls, and it is stable under shrinking the set, positive scaling and adding an energy bounded below. What Layer 1 still lacks is the sequential direct method, the characterization of weak lower semicontinuity by sublevels, the sequential-closure form of Eberlein–Šmulian, the real/complex dictionary, convex energies and compact maps on bounded sets.

## VariationalMethods: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-25 (`aa6e43e` to `58174b5`)

The spectral half of the roadmap has begun. A [compact form pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/CompactFormPair.html#TauCeti.CompactFormPair) bundles a compact, injective inclusion of dense range with a bounded Hermitian form satisfying a shifted coercivity bound. For it, the existence of constrained Rayleigh minima is now proved ([`exists_isMinOn_energy_unitConstraint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/CompactFormPair.html#TauCeti.CompactFormPair.exists_isMinOn_energy_unitConstraint)): for a closed subspace `W`, the energy attains its minimum on the constrained sphere `{‖j u‖ = 1, j u ∈ W}`, and that minimum bounds the Rayleigh quotient on `W` (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/3600). The sphere is not weakly closed, so the proof runs the direct method on its bounded parts, which are weakly compact. Variational eigenspaces are shown to be finite dimensional and mutually orthogonal, with [finitely many eigenvalues below any bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/FormEigenspace.html#TauCeti.CompactFormPair.finite_setOf_formEigenspace_ne_bot_le). The constrained Euler–Lagrange equation and the successive construction of the eigenbasis are not yet there.

Layer 2 gained most of its content. For a quadratic energy of a Hermitian form, the library now has the [first variation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/QuadraticEnergy.html#ContinuousLinearMap.hasFDerivAt_quadraticEnergy) and the [equivalence of minimizers with weak solutions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/QuadraticEnergy.html#ContinuousLinearMap.forall_le_quadraticEnergy_add_iff). Under coercivity it also has the energy gap, convergence of near-minimizers and agreement with the Lax–Milgram solution. Alongside these come the [variational inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/FirstVariation.html#IsLocalMinOn.hasFDerivWithinAt_apply_sub_nonneg) on convex sets, the [energy space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/EnergySpace.html#TauCeti.CoerciveHermitianForm.EnergySpace) of a coercive Hermitian form, and its semibounded shifts. Normal cones, the subdifferential sum rule and the two forcing maps are still missing.

Layer 1 was filled in around the direct method. It now has the real/complex dictionary, including [scalar independence of reflexivity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/Reflexive/RestrictScalars.html#TauCeti.surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual_iff_real), and weak lower semicontinuity of norms, norm powers and `‖D u‖²`. Compact operators are now [weak-to-norm continuous on bounded sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Compact/WeakSpace.html#IsCompactOperator.continuousOn_comp_toWeakSpace_symm_of_isBounded), with the matching counterexample that [`j`-spheres are not weakly closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/WeakSpace/Sphere.html#TauCeti.not_isClosed_toWeakSpace_setOf_norm_eq). The bounded-sublevel API is invariant under translation and linear change of variables, and the README's `ℓ²` checks are formalized.
