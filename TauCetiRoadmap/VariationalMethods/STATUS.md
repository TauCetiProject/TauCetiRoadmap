## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Both forms of the direct method, the first end theorem, are proved. Layer 1 is done apart from a few secondary items, and Layer 2's quadratic-energy theory and energy space are in place. The spectral side has started: constrained Rayleigh minima exist on compact form pairs and variational eigenvalues have finite multiplicity. The Euler–Lagrange equation, the ordered eigenbasis, Courant–Fischer, the represented operator and Ritz–Galerkin (Layers 4–6 beyond those multiplicity facts) have not begun.

### Named results

- **The direct method** — a weakly lower semicontinuous energy with norm-bounded sublevels attains its minimum on any nonempty weakly closed set in a reflexive space, with no lower bound assumed ([`exists_isMinOn_of_weak_closed`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/DirectMethod.html#TauCeti.exists_isMinOn_of_weak_closed)).
- **The sequential direct method** — an energy that is bounded below and sequentially weakly lower semicontinuous on a sequentially weakly closed set attains its minimum once one minimizing sequence is norm bounded ([`exists_isMinOn_of_bounded_minimizing_sequence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/MinimizingSequence.html#TauCeti.exists_isMinOn_of_bounded_minimizing_sequence)). It predates the previous report, which listed it as open.
- **Constrained Rayleigh minima** — on a compact form pair with complete form domain, the energy attains its minimum on the constrained sphere `{‖j u‖ = 1, j u ∈ W}` of every closed `W` for which the sphere is nonempty ([`exists_isMinOn_energy_unitConstraint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/CompactFormPair.html#TauCeti.CompactFormPair.exists_isMinOn_energy_unitConstraint)). The minimum bounds the Rayleigh quotient on `W`, and the set of minimizers is weakly compact.
- **Minimizers are weak solutions** — for a Hermitian form whose energy is nonnegative on `W`, a point minimizes the quadratic energy on `u + W` exactly when it solves the weak equation there ([`forall_le_quadraticEnergy_add_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/QuadraticEnergy.html#ContinuousLinearMap.forall_le_quadraticEnergy_add_iff)). Over `ℝ` the minimizer is identified with the Lax–Milgram solution ([`isMinOn_quadraticEnergy_iff_eq_solutionOfFunctional`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Variational/QuadraticEnergy.html#IsCoercive.isMinOn_quadraticEnergy_iff_eq_solutionOfFunctional)).
- **Finite multiplicity of variational eigenvalues** — every variational eigenspace of a compact form pair is finite dimensional, distinct eigenspaces are orthogonal in `H`, and only finitely many eigenvalues lie below any bound ([`finite_setOf_formEigenspace_ne_bot_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/FormEigenspace.html#TauCeti.CompactFormPair.finite_setOf_formEigenspace_ne_bot_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Compact form pairs** ([`CompactFormPair`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/CompactFormPair.html#TauCeti.CompactFormPair)) — the setting of Layers 3–6. Its constrained spheres are sequentially weakly closed but not weakly closed, and their bounded parts are weakly compact, which is exactly what the direct method needs.
- **The energy space** ([`EnergySpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/EnergySpace.html#TauCeti.CoerciveHermitianForm.EnergySpace)) — a coercive Hermitian form turned into a Hilbert-space inner product equivalent to the original norm. Weak solutions become Riesz representatives in this space, and [semibounded shifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/Shift.html#TauCeti.CoerciveHermitianForm.ofSemibounded) feed into it with the same coercivity constant.
