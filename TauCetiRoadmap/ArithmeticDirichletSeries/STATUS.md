### Named results

- **The Euler product of the Dedekind zeta function** — for `Re s > 1`, `ζ_K` is the unrestricted product over the height-one primes of `(1 - N(𝔭)⁻ˢ)⁻¹` ([`TauCeti.dedekindZeta_eulerProduct_hasProd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Analytic.html#TauCeti.dedekindZeta_eulerProduct_hasProd)), a case of the analytic Euler product available wherever the ideal-indexed series converges absolutely ([`TauCeti.EulerProductData.hasProd_eulerFactor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Analytic.html#TauCeti.EulerProductData.hasProd_eulerFactor)); for a completely multiplicative weight the same hypothesis forces nonvanishing, so `ζ_K` has no zero on `Re s > 1` ([`TauCeti.dedekindZeta_ne_zero_of_one_lt_re`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Analytic.html#TauCeti.dedekindZeta_ne_zero_of_one_lt_re)).

- **The arithmetic Perron formula** — away from integer `x`, the truncated vertical integral of an absolutely convergent `L`-series differs from `∑_{n < x} f n` by a summable series of smoothed-step errors ([`TauCeti.norm_truncatedPerron_LSeries_sub_sum_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/Perron/Formula.html#TauCeti.norm_truncatedPerron_LSeries_sub_sum_le)), with the half-weight limit at an integer endpoint separate ([`TauCeti.tendsto_truncatedPerron_LSeries_natCast`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/Perron/Formula.html#TauCeti.tendsto_truncatedPerron_LSeries_natCast)).

## The frontier

- **Wiener-Ikehara (9.1, 9.2)** — the smoothed limit against a smooth compactly supported test function is proved, and the `O(t log t)` growth bound it needs is derived rather than assumed (TauCeti#6399). What remains is the approximation step to `x⁻¹ ∑_{n ≤ x} a n → κ`, the `κ = 0` case and the variants of 9.2. Nothing arithmetic blocks it.
- **The boundary package and the summit (10.1, 10.3)** — `PrimeBoundaryRemainder K S δ`, `primePsi_asymptotic_of_boundary`, and `primeNumberTheoremTransfer` returning `ψ`, `ϑ` and `π` together. Both transfer legs and the coefficient system exist, so this is assembly once Layer 9 closes; 10.4 also waits on the external `TauCeti.LFunctions.primeIdealVonMangoldtBoundary`.
- **Dirichlet density (Layer 7)** — untouched; the README makes updating the Mathlib pin its first task. The normalization `P_all(s) = log(1/(s-1)) + O(1)` of 7.2 is now unblocked: both its inputs, the analytic Euler product and the higher-prime-power bound, are available.
- **Cancellation and a named continuation (6.5)** — `HasCancellation` as the uniform `O(X^(1-1/[K:ℚ]))` bound on ideal partial sums, then `continuedLFunctionOfWeight`, agreeing with the regrouped series on `Re s > 1` and analytic on `Re s > 1-1/[K:ℚ]`. The Abel-summation interface is in place.
- **A logarithm past absolute convergence (3.4, 2.3)** — the branch and the logarithmic derivative exist only where the ideal series converges absolutely, so they say nothing on a zero-free region reaching `Re s = 1`, which is what consumers need.
