## ArithmeticDirichletSeries: 2026-08-20 to 2026-09-01 (`4239bee` to `8745177`)

This first report covers the roadmap's whole history, and the analytic headline is **Landau's theorem** ([`TauCeti.LSeries.landau`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Landau.html#TauCeti.LSeries.landau)): a Dirichlet series with nonnegative coefficients and abscissa of absolute convergence exactly `σ` has no analytic continuation across `σ`, with a half-plane form ([`TauCeti.LSeries.abscissaOfAbsConv_le_of_differentiableOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Landau.html#TauCeti.LSeries.abscissaOfAbsConv_le_of_differentiableOn)). Beside it, the **3-4-1 inequality** ([`TauCeti.LSeries.threeFourOne_re_neg_log_one_sub_nonneg`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/ThreeFourOne.html#TauCeti.LSeries.threeFourOne_re_neg_log_one_sub_nonneg)) is packaged as a nonnegative trigonometric combination on the closed disk, with no character data attached.

On the arithmetic side, the carriers of Layer 0 (TauCeti#3903), ideal Dirichlet convolution, and **regrouping by norm** ([`TauCeti.regroupByNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/Regroup.html#TauCeti.regroupByNorm)) connect ideal-indexed series to Mathlib's `LSeries`; the converse holds only under nonnegativity. **Ideal Möbius inversion** ([`TauCeti.IdealArithmeticFunction.convolution_one_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/Moebius.html#TauCeti.IdealArithmeticFunction.convolution_one_eq_iff)) and the **finite Euler product** ([`TauCeti.IdealArithmeticFunction.normCoeff_supportedPart`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct.html#TauCeti.IdealArithmeticFunction.normCoeff_supportedPart)) landed, the latter passing to Mathlib's formal Euler product coefficientwise only. The **exact abscissa of the trivial weight** ([`TauCeti.abscissaOfAbsConv_normCoeff_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/Estimates.html#TauCeti.abscissaOfAbsConv_normCoeff_one)) is `1`, from two-sided linear ideal counts and without the Dedekind pole.

The counting carriers (TauCeti#4495) support **negligible higher prime powers** ([`TauCeti.higherPrimePowerTheta_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/HigherPrimePowers.html#TauCeti.higherPrimePowerTheta_le)) with an explicit constant, the **transfer from ψ to ϑ** ([`TauCeti.primeTheta_asymptotic_of_primePsi`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/PrimePsi.html#TauCeti.primeTheta_asymptotic_of_primePsi)), and the **transfer from ϑ to π** ([`TauCeti.primeCount_asymptotic_of_primeTheta`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/Transfer.html#TauCeti.primeCount_asymptotic_of_primeTheta)) via the logarithmic integral. The **truncated Perron kernel** ([`TauCeti.truncatedPerronKernel`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/Perron.html#TauCeti.truncatedPerronKernel)) is a smoothed step with proved error and value `π⁻¹ arctan(T/c)` at `x = 1`. Absent: Wiener–Ikehara, Dirichlet density, the arithmetic Perron formula, cancellation and continuation, the logarithmic-derivative identity, and the summit `primeNumberTheoremTransfer`.

## ArithmeticDirichletSeries: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-14 (`8745177` to `6bc3780`)

The Euler product stopped being formal: where the ideal-indexed Dirichlet series converges absolutely, the local Euler factors have an unrestricted infinite product over the height-one primes equal to the `LSeries` of the norm coefficients ([`TauCeti.EulerProductData.hasProd_eulerFactor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Analytic.html#TauCeti.EulerProductData.hasProd_eulerFactor), TauCeti#5562); the contract's missing `EulerProductData` package, bundling local power series with the coprime-multiplicativity proof, landed just before it (TauCeti#5143). The trivial weight gives the **Euler product of the Dedekind zeta function** ([`TauCeti.dedekindZeta_eulerProduct_hasProd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Analytic.html#TauCeti.dedekindZeta_eulerProduct_hasProd)), and absolute convergence puts every local ratio strictly inside the unit disc, so **`ζ_K` does not vanish on `Re s > 1`** ([`TauCeti.dedekindZeta_ne_zero_of_one_lt_re`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Analytic.html#TauCeti.dedekindZeta_ne_zero_of_one_lt_re)). For a completely multiplicative weight the logarithm side followed: the series is the exponential of a prime-power sum, a holomorphic logarithm exists on any simply connected region of absolute convergence, and the **logarithmic derivative is the von Mangoldt series** `-∑' A, χ(A) Λ(A) N(A)⁻ˢ` ([`TauCeti.MultiplicativeIdealWeight.logDeriv_LSeries_eq_neg_tsum_vonMangoldtTransform`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Logarithm/VonMangoldtCoeff.html#TauCeti.MultiplicativeIdealWeight.logDeriv_LSeries_eq_neg_tsum_vonMangoldtTransform)), the identity Layer 2.3 owed.

The **arithmetic Perron formula** arrived (TauCeti#6126): the truncated integral of an `LSeries` absolutely convergent on the line is the series of truncated kernels at the ratios `x / n` ([`TauCeti.truncatedPerron_LSeries`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/Perron/Formula.html#TauCeti.truncatedPerron_LSeries)), with an off-norm error bound against the sharp partial sum and the half-weight limit at an integer endpoint. Wiener–Ikehara got its analytic core but not its statement: for nonnegative coefficients with a continuous boundary remainder, testing against a smooth compactly supported function on `Re s = 1` gives the limit `2π A ψ(0)` ([`TauCeti.LSeries.tendsto_tsum_term_mul_fourier_atTop_of_nonneg`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/WienerIkehara/BoundaryGrowth.html#TauCeti.LSeries.tendsto_tsum_term_mul_fourier_atTop_of_nonneg)), with the Chebyshev-type growth bound derived rather than assumed. Passing from smooth test functions to the sharp cutoff remains, so there is no `wienerIkehara`.

A bijection between prime-power ideals and pairs `(𝔭, k)` made the prime-power sums tractable, log-weighted ideal terms were shown summable strictly to the right, and `normCoeff` was proved compatible with norm twists and with transport along an isomorphism of number fields. Landau's theorem picked up its meromorphic-order corollary. Dirichlet density remains untouched.
