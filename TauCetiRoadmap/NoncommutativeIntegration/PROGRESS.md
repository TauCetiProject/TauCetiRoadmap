## NoncommutativeIntegration: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`b89abf4` to `1c6a1a4`)

The headline of this first window is the formula for Peetre's K-functional of the couple (L¹, L∞): for an a.e. strongly measurable f, [the integral of the decreasing rearrangement of ‖f‖ over (0, t)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/DecreasingRearrangement.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.lintegral_decreasingRearrangement_enorm_eq_iInf_eLpNorm) is the infimum of ‖f₀‖₁ + t‖f₁‖_∞ over splittings f = f₀ + f₁, attained by the radial retraction at level f*(t) (TauCeti#33838). It rests on a new scalar [decreasing rearrangement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/DecreasingRearrangement.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.decreasingRearrangement) for an arbitrary measure, with the generalized-inverse relation to the distribution function, right continuity, [equimeasurability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/DecreasingRearrangement.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.volume_restrict_setOf_lt_decreasingRearrangement) and hence equal Lᵖ norms, and the shifted bounds [(f + g)*(s + t) ≤ f*(s) + g*(t)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/DecreasingRearrangement.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.decreasingRearrangement_add_le) and its multiplicative twin. This is the commutative model of the singular numbers μ_t of Layer 1; the operator-algebraic μ_t itself has not been started, and the K-functional formula is stated directly for Lᵖ seminorms rather than through the library's compatible-couple K-functional.

On the abstract side, the real interpolation spaces now [embed continuously](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Interpolation/Exponent.html#TauCeti.CompatibleCouple.KMethodSpace.inclusion) as the secondary exponent grows, (X₀, X₁)_{θ,p} ⊆ (X₀, X₁)_{θ,q} for p ≤ q including q = ∞, with a norm bound independent of the couple. The key step bounds every weighted value of the K-functional by the interpolation norm. A small comparison of Lᵖ seminorms across exponents on infinite measure spaces came along with it.

## NoncommutativeIntegration: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

The headline is [Kahane's inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/RademacherSum/Kahane.html#TauCeti.rademacherMoment_le_kahaneConst_mul): in any real normed space, all moments (E‖∑ε_k x_k‖^q)^(1/q) of a finite Rademacher sum, 0<q<∞, are comparable with constants depending only on the exponents, here the explicit 2^(1/q)(8·3^q)^(1/p) when p<q (TauCeti#41344). The proof follows the route the roadmap prescribes for §6.2: [Lévy's reflection inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/RademacherSum/Levy.html#TauCeti.ncard_exists_lt_norm_sum_Iic_div_le) on the sign cube, the tail iteration [P(‖S‖>3t) ≤ 4P(‖S‖>t)²](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/RademacherSum/Levy.html#TauCeti.ncard_three_mul_lt_norm_sum_div_le) together with the [Hoffmann–Jørgensen inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/RademacherSum/Levy.html#TauCeti.ncard_two_mul_add_norm_toLp_top_lt_norm_sum_div_le), and tail integration. The [contraction principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/RademacherSum/Contraction.html#TauCeti.expect_norm_sum_smul_le) is proved separately, along with truncation to infinite families, and Rademacher type and cotype can now be [tested with any moment](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/RademacherSum/Kahane.html#TauCeti.hasType_of_rademacherMoment_le) instead of the second. All of this concerns Banach-space-valued sums. Nothing has yet been applied to the random-sum norm on noncommutative Lp, which does not exist yet.

On the interpolation side, the K-functional formula for (L¹, L∞) from the previous window is now stated for the library's own [compatible-couple K-functional](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Interpolation/L1Linfty.html#TauCeti.CompatibleCouple.ofReal_kFunctional_l1Linfty): K(t, f) = ∫₀ᵗ f*. Previously it was stated only through Lᵖ seminorms. The same work shows that f lies in L¹ + L∞ [exactly when ∫₀ᵗ f* is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Interpolation/L1Linfty.html#TauCeti.L1AddLinfty.exists_toAEEqFun_ae_eq_iff_lintegral_decreasingRearrangement_lt_top). The complex method gained the [symmetry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Interpolation/ComplexMethod/Symmetry.html#TauCeti.CompatibleCouple.ComplexMethodSpace.swap) [E₀, E₁]_θ = [E₁, E₀]_(1−θ), one of the clauses of §2.3, and a [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Interpolation/ComplexMethod/Basic.html#TauCeti.CompatibleCouple.ComplexMethodSpace.lift) for maps out of the interpolation space.
