## PseudodifferentialOperators: 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09 (`d99a4fc` to `d92acc5`)

The headline is the Sobolev mapping theorem for pseudodifferential operators with compact spatial support: a symbol of order m that vanishes outside a fixed compact set of positions quantizes to a bounded operator H^s → H^(s−m) for every real s ([`exists_quantizeSobolev`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_quantizeSobolev), TauCeti#28893). The proof uses the rapid decay of the symbol's Fourier transform in position. Around it, the Kohn–Nirenberg quantization of Schwartz functions got a working API. It is smooth, satisfies a Leibniz rule, and is recovered as a cutoff-regularized oscillatory double integral ([`tendsto_integral_cutoff_smul_quantizeSchwartzValue`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/OscillatoryIntegral.html#TauCeti.tendsto_integral_cutoff_smul_quantizeSchwartzValue)). It determines its symbol, and every output lies in a closed set K exactly when the symbol vanishes at positions outside K ([`tsupport_quantizeSchwartzValue_subset_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/OutputSupport.html#TauCeti.tsupport_quantizeSchwartzValue_subset_iff)). Symbols now carry their seminorm topology and homogeneous terms with a uniqueness theorem, but classical symbols, Borel summation and composition have not started.

On the geometric side, derivations of C^∞(M) are identified with smooth vector fields, with no continuity assumed ([`exists_derivationEquivSmoothVectorField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/Derivation.html#TauCeti.exists_derivationEquivSmoothVectorField)), and commutators of derivations go to Lie brackets. Grothendieck's algebraic differential operators arrived along with their principal symbol, defined by iterated commutators ([`commutatorSymbol`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Derivation/PrincipalSymbol.html#TauCeti.commutatorSymbol)). So did smooth section modules and first-jet coordinate changes.

Microlocal analysis began with a Euclidean wave front set. It is computed for a point mass and for the surface measure of a linear subspace, whose wave front set is its conormal set ([`waveFrontSet_ofFun_one_map_subtype`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet.html#Distribution.waveFrontSet_ofFun_one_map_subtype)). The analytic base also grew. It gained real K-method interpolation with its exactness bound, Banach spaces W^(k,p) of bundle sections ([`SobolevSection.instCompleteSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Sobolev/Basic.html#TauCeti.SobolevSection.instCompleteSpace)), and the weak gradient as a closed, unbounded operator.

## PseudodifferentialOperators: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`d92acc5` to `1b19ebb`)

The headline is the Calderón–Vaillancourt theorem: an order-zero symbol whose estimates hold uniformly in x quantizes to a bounded operator on L², with no spatial support condition ([`exists_quantizeUniformOrderZero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/CalderonVaillancourt.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_quantizeUniformOrderZero), TauCeti#35397). The proof uses unit-scale pieces in position and frequency and the Cotlar–Stein lemma. The symbol calculus also arrived. The composition symbol a#b has its asymptotic expansion with remainders of the stated order ([`sharp_eq_sum_sharpTerm_add_sharpRemainder`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/Expansion.html#TauCeti.Symbol.sharp_eq_sum_sharpTerm_add_sharpRemainder)). Classical symbols compose to classical symbols, and commutators satisfy the Poisson bracket formula ([`exists_inclusion_eq_sharpCommutator_of_scalar`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/Classical.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.exists_inclusion_eq_sharpCommutator_of_scalar)). The adjoint symbol has a matching expansion. All of this still requires compact spatial support, of the right factor for composition and of the symbol for the adjoint.

Classical symbols now exist, with the principal-symbol exact sequence and Borel summation for any orders decreasing to −∞, including continuous families ([`exists_asymptoticSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/AsymptoticSum.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_asymptoticSum)). On top of them sits ellipticity. An elliptic classical symbol has formal left and right inverses ([`exists_sharpTerms_eq_left`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Symbol/Parametrix.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.exists_sharpTerms_eq_left)), and these agree modulo smoothing symbols. This is a parametrix at the level of symbols only; operator parametrices, regularity and Fredholm theory have not started. Quantizations with compact spatial support are now compact H^s → H^r for r < s − m.

Elsewhere, Peetre's theorem landed in Euclidean form ([`exists_apply_eq_apply_of_iteratedFDeriv_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/LocalOperator.html#LinearMap.exists_apply_eq_apply_of_iteratedFDeriv_eq)), along with the complex derivation dictionary, the first-jet bundle and the rough Laplacian. Other additions were a Schwartz kernel theorem for small compact sets, tensor products of distributions, and wave front sets under linear coordinate changes, restriction and smooth multiplication. Interpolation gained the complex method with its exact bound, and there are now Besov inclusions B^s_(p,1) ⊂ H^(s,p) ⊂ B^s_(p,∞) and little Hölder spaces.
