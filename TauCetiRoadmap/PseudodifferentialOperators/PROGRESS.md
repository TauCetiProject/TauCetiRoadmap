## PseudodifferentialOperators: 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09 (`d99a4fc` to `d92acc5`)

The headline is the Sobolev mapping theorem for pseudodifferential operators with compact spatial support: a symbol of order m that vanishes outside a fixed compact set of positions quantizes to a bounded operator H^s → H^(s−m) for every real s ([`exists_quantizeSobolev`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_quantizeSobolev), TauCeti#28893). The proof uses the rapid decay of the symbol's Fourier transform in position. Around it, the Kohn–Nirenberg quantization of Schwartz functions got a working API. It is smooth, satisfies a Leibniz rule, and is recovered as a cutoff-regularized oscillatory double integral ([`tendsto_integral_cutoff_smul_quantizeSchwartzValue`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/OscillatoryIntegral.html#TauCeti.tendsto_integral_cutoff_smul_quantizeSchwartzValue)). It determines its symbol, and every output lies in a closed set K exactly when the symbol vanishes at positions outside K ([`tsupport_quantizeSchwartzValue_subset_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/OutputSupport.html#TauCeti.tsupport_quantizeSchwartzValue_subset_iff)). Symbols now carry their seminorm topology and homogeneous terms with a uniqueness theorem, but classical symbols, Borel summation and composition have not started.

On the geometric side, derivations of C^∞(M) are identified with smooth vector fields, with no continuity assumed ([`exists_derivationEquivSmoothVectorField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/Derivation.html#TauCeti.exists_derivationEquivSmoothVectorField)), and commutators of derivations go to Lie brackets. Grothendieck's algebraic differential operators arrived along with their principal symbol, defined by iterated commutators ([`commutatorSymbol`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Derivation/PrincipalSymbol.html#TauCeti.commutatorSymbol)). So did smooth section modules and first-jet coordinate changes.

Microlocal analysis began with a Euclidean wave front set. It is computed for a point mass and for the surface measure of a linear subspace, whose wave front set is its conormal set ([`waveFrontSet_ofFun_one_map_subtype`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet.html#Distribution.waveFrontSet_ofFun_one_map_subtype)). The analytic base also grew. It gained real K-method interpolation with its exactness bound, Banach spaces W^(k,p) of bundle sections ([`SobolevSection.instCompleteSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Sobolev/Basic.html#TauCeti.SobolevSection.instCompleteSpace)), and the weak gradient as a closed, unbounded operator.

## PseudodifferentialOperators: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`d92acc5` to `1b19ebb`)

The headline is the Calderón–Vaillancourt theorem: an order-zero symbol whose estimates hold uniformly in x quantizes to a bounded operator on L², with no spatial support condition ([`exists_quantizeUniformOrderZero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/CalderonVaillancourt.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_quantizeUniformOrderZero), TauCeti#35397). The proof uses unit-scale pieces in position and frequency and the Cotlar–Stein lemma. The symbol calculus also arrived. The composition symbol a#b has its asymptotic expansion with remainders of the stated order ([`sharp_eq_sum_sharpTerm_add_sharpRemainder`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/Expansion.html#TauCeti.Symbol.sharp_eq_sum_sharpTerm_add_sharpRemainder)). Classical symbols compose to classical symbols, and commutators satisfy the Poisson bracket formula ([`exists_inclusion_eq_sharpCommutator_of_scalar`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/Classical.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.exists_inclusion_eq_sharpCommutator_of_scalar)). The adjoint symbol has a matching expansion. All of this still requires compact spatial support, of the right factor for composition and of the symbol for the adjoint.

Classical symbols now exist, with the principal-symbol exact sequence and Borel summation for any orders decreasing to −∞, including continuous families ([`exists_asymptoticSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/AsymptoticSum.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_asymptoticSum)). On top of them sits ellipticity. An elliptic classical symbol has formal left and right inverses ([`exists_sharpTerms_eq_left`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Symbol/Parametrix.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.exists_sharpTerms_eq_left)), and these agree modulo smoothing symbols. This is a parametrix at the level of symbols only; operator parametrices, regularity and Fredholm theory have not started. Quantizations with compact spatial support are now compact H^s → H^r for r < s − m.

Elsewhere, Peetre's theorem landed in Euclidean form ([`exists_apply_eq_apply_of_iteratedFDeriv_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/LocalOperator.html#LinearMap.exists_apply_eq_apply_of_iteratedFDeriv_eq)), along with the complex derivation dictionary, the first-jet bundle and the rough Laplacian. Other additions were a Schwartz kernel theorem for small compact sets, tensor products of distributions, and wave front sets under linear coordinate changes, restriction and smooth multiplication. Interpolation gained the complex method with its exact bound, and there are now Besov inclusions B^s_(p,1) ⊂ H^(s,p) ⊂ B^s_(p,∞) and little Hölder spaces.

The headline is interior elliptic regularity on ℝⁿ: if p is elliptic of order m and Op(p)u is H^s near the support of a cutoff φ, then φu lies in H^(s+m) ([`IsEllipticAbove.memSobolev_smulLeftCLM`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/EllipticRegularity.html#TauCeti.Symbol.IsEllipticAbove.memSobolev_smulLeftCLM), TauCeti#38550). It rests on a two-sided parametrix symbol for any symbol that is elliptic at large frequencies ([`exists_isAsymptoticSum_sharpTerm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/Parametrix.html#TauCeti.Symbol.IsEllipticAbove.exists_isAsymptoticSum_sharpTerm)), a local parametrix modulo smoothing operators, and composition of quantizations on Sobolev spaces. The operator is localized by a symbol of compact spatial support, and u is taken in a Sobolev space from the start. The version for arbitrary distributions, Pu ∈ H^s_loc ⇒ u ∈ H^(s+m)_loc, is still open, although one of its ingredients also arrived: every distribution has finite Sobolev order on compact sets ([`exists_forall_isLocallySobolevAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/Sobolev/SingularSupport.html#Distribution.exists_forall_isLocallySobolevAt)).

Classical symbols now restrict to open sets ([`LocalClassicalSymbol`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/LocalSymbol/Classical.html#TauCeti.LocalClassicalSymbol)), the last missing piece of Layer 2. Differential operators became classical symbols whose quantization is exactly the operator, and the Laplace-type predicate arrived with −Δ as its model ([`isLaplaceType_neg_smoothLaplaceBeltrami`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialOperator/LaplaceType.html#TauCeti.isLaplaceType_neg_smoothLaplaceBeltrami)). On the microlocal side, the wave front set projects onto the singular support ([`fst_image_waveFrontSet`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/SingularSupport.html#Distribution.fst_image_waveFrontSet)), with an H^s analogue. A differential operator of order k costs at most k orders of microlocal Sobolev regularity ([`sobolevWaveFrontSet_sum_smul_smulLeftCLM_iteratedLineDerivOp_subset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/Sobolev/LineDeriv.html#Distribution.sobolevWaveFrontSet_sum_smul_smulLeftCLM_iteratedLineDerivOp_subset)). Interpolation gained Stein's theorem for analytic families ([`norm_analyticFamilyMap_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Interpolation/ComplexMethod/Basic.html#TauCeti.CompatibleCouple.ComplexMethodSpace.norm_analyticFamilyMap_le)). The cubic shear (x, y) ↦ (x, y + x³) is now proved to give an unbounded pullback on W^(1,p)(ℝ²). For Layer 8, Riemannian volume now descends along finite coverings, and isometries now act on H¹.

## PseudodifferentialOperators: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`aa6e43e` to `b235000`)

The headline is distributional elliptic regularity for differential operators on open sets of ℝⁿ. If P has smooth coefficients and is elliptic of order M at x, then for any distribution T, PT is H^s near x exactly when T is H^(s+M) near x ([`isLocallySobolevAt_sum_smul_smulLeftCLM_iteratedLineDerivOp_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/DistributionalRegularity.html#Distribution.isLocallySobolevAt_sum_smul_smulLeftCLM_iteratedLineDerivOp_iff)). The microlocal version also landed: WF(T) ⊆ WF(PT) ∪ Char(P), as in Hörmander's Theorem 8.3.1 ([`waveFrontSet_subset_waveFrontSet_sum_smul_smulLeftCLM_iteratedLineDerivOp_union`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/EllipticRegularity.html#Distribution.waveFrontSet_subset_waveFrontSet_sum_smul_smulLeftCLM_iteratedLineDerivOp_union), https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/184). Both are stated for scalar differential operators only. The same statements for general elliptic pseudodifferential operators are still open.

The calculus also lost much of its dependence on compact spatial support. Every symbol is now properly supported up to a smoothing operator ([`exists_isSmoothing_sub_and_hasPropagationLE`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/ProperSupport.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_isSmoothing_sub_and_hasPropagationLE)). A properly supported left factor composes with any symbol, and the composite has the full asymptotic expansion ([`isAsymptoticSum_properSharp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/ProperSupport.html#TauCeti.Symbol.isAsymptoticSum_properSharp)). Symbols with estimates uniform in x now compose and have uniform parametrices. They also map H^s → H^(s−m) for every real s ([`exists_quantizeUniformSobolev`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/UniformSobolev.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_quantizeUniformSobolev)). The Schwartz kernel theorem now holds on arbitrary open sets of Euclidean space, by gluing ([`existsUnique_schwartzKernel_distribution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/SchwartzKernel.html#LinearMap.existsUnique_schwartzKernel_distribution)). Interior regularity extended to H^(s,p).

On the function-space side, both interpolation identities arrived. Complex interpolation of the H^s scale gives H^(s_θ) with equal norms ([`besselPotentialSpaceComplexMethodEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/BesselPotential/ComplexMethod.html#TauCeti.CompatibleCouple.besselPotentialSpaceComplexMethodEquiv)), and real interpolation of H^(s,p) gives Besov spaces. Other additions were the Mihlin–Hörmander multiplier theorem, Sobolev embeddings, and pullback by diffeomorphisms on Besov spaces. Wave front sets gained Hörmander's pushforward bound along linear maps and frame independence for vector-valued distributions. The Fredholm index is now independent of the regularity level. Some work landed earlier and is recorded here late: the Littlewood–Paley partition, the Cotlar–Stein lemma and Atkinson's theorem.

## PseudodifferentialOperators: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`b235000` to `5b5d515`)

The headline is distributional elliptic regularity for properly supported pseudodifferential operators. If a classical symbol a of order m is elliptic and Op(a) has its kernel near the diagonal, then Op(a)T in H^s near x forces an arbitrary distribution T into H^(s+m) near x ([`IsElliptic.isLocallySobolevAt_of_quantizeDistribution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/ProperSupport/EllipticRegularity.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.IsElliptic.isLocallySobolevAt_of_quantizeDistribution)). Previously this needed T in a Sobolev space from the start. The proof rests on a two-sided properly supported parametrix for symbols elliptic at large frequencies ([`exists_hasPropagationLE_quantizeSchwartzValue_eq_add_and_eq_add`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/ProperParametrix.html#TauCeti.Symbol.IsEllipticAbove.exists_hasPropagationLE_quantizeSchwartzValue_eq_add_and_eq_add)). It also uses properly supported operators acting on all distributions, where they compose and map H^s_loc to H^(s−m)_loc. The same machinery gives microlocality, WF(Op(a)T) ⊆ WF(T), with equality for elliptic operators ([`waveFrontSet_quantizeDistribution_subset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/ProperSupport/Microlocality.html#TauCeti.Symbol.waveFrontSet_quantizeDistribution_subset)).

Two other open problems moved. Order-zero symbols with estimates uniform in x are bounded on L^p for 1 < p < ∞, via the Calderón–Zygmund theorem for their kernels ([`exists_eLpNorm_quantizeSchwartzValue_le_of_one_lt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Lp/CalderonZygmund.html#TauCeti.Symbol.IsUniform.exists_eLpNorm_quantizeSchwartzValue_le_of_one_lt)). Kuranishi's trick gives nonlinear changes of coordinates. Classical symbols stay classical, and their principal symbols transform by the cotangent map ([`ClassicalSymbol.exists_quantizeSchwartzValue_comp_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/CoordinateChange.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.exists_quantizeSchwartzValue_comp_eq)). This is still Euclidean: κ is a diffeomorphism of all of ℝⁿ, the symbol has compact spatial support, and κ's averaged derivative must be invertible. Global bundle quantization has not started.

Wave front sets gained Hörmander's product of distributions with no opposite covectors ([`waveFrontSet_product_subset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/Product.html#Distribution.waveFrontSet_product_subset)). They also gained invariance under changes of variables, submersion pullbacks, and continuity of pushforward in the normal topology. Other work covered averaging over compact groups, Besov and Schauder mapping, parameter-dependent symbols and the de Rham symbol complex. Sunada's theorem now holds for weak Laplace eigenspaces ([`nonempty_linearEquiv_weakEigenspace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/Isospectral/Weak.html#Subgroup.AlmostConjugate.nonempty_linearEquiv_weakEigenspace)). ## PseudodifferentialOperators: 2026-11-24 to 2026-11-28 (`5b5d515` to `b4f0735`)

Hörmander's pullback theorem now applies to smooth maps between Euclidean open sets. The pullback of a distribution exists when its wave front set avoids the map's normal set, and its wave front covectors lie in the cotangent pullback of the original ones ([`pullback`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/Pullback/SmoothMap.html#Distribution.pullback), [`waveFrontSet_pullback_subset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/Pullback/SmoothMap.html#Distribution.waveFrontSet_pullback_subset)). For a fixed closed admissible cone, pullback is continuous in the normal topology ([`pullbackCLM`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/NormalTopology/SmoothMap.html#TauCeti.WaveFrontSpace.pullbackCLM)).

Microlocal elliptic regularity now includes systems. For a matrix-valued symbol, every singularity of the input is either a singularity of the output or a characteristic covector ([`vectorWaveFrontSet_toDistribution_subset_quantizeSobolev_union_characteristicSet`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/CharacteristicSet.html#TauCeti.Symbol.vectorWaveFrontSet_toDistribution_subset_quantizeSobolev_union_characteristicSet)). This statement assumes compact spatial support of the symbol and an input already in some H^t, rather than an arbitrary distribution on a bundle.

The little Hölder–Zygmund space also gained a concrete characterization. At noninteger positive order, membership means little Hölder regularity of the highest derivative together with vanishing of the function at infinity ([`mem_littleBesov_iff_memLittleHolder_and_tendsto_cocompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Holder/Zygmund/Little.html#TauCeti.BesovSpace.mem_littleBesov_iff_memLittleHolder_and_tendsto_cocompact)). Composition and adjoint expansions gained continuity estimates for symbol families with common compact spatial support. The extract is truncated. These results do not establish global bundle quantization.
