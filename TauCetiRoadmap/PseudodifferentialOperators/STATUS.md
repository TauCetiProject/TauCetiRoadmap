## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Euclidean symbol calculus is largely in place: classical symbols, Borel summation, composition and adjoint expansions, L² boundedness, and a symbol-level parametrix for elliptic symbols. No layer is complete. Everything global is still missing: bundle quantization, coordinate changes, proper support, operator parametrices, elliptic regularity and Fredholm theory.

### Named results

- **The Calderón–Vaillancourt theorem**: an order-zero symbol with estimates uniform in x quantizes to a bounded operator on L², with no support condition in x ([`exists_quantizeUniformOrderZero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/CalderonVaillancourt.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_quantizeUniformOrderZero)).
- **The composition expansion**: when b has compact spatial support, the symbol of Op(a)∘Op(b) is Σ_(k<N) of the terms (−i)^k/k! ∂_ξ^α a ∂_x^α b plus a remainder of order m′+m−N ([`sharp_eq_sum_sharpTerm_add_sharpRemainder`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/Expansion.html#TauCeti.Symbol.sharp_eq_sum_sharpTerm_add_sharpRemainder)). Classical symbols compose to classical symbols, and commutators of scalar symbols have principal symbol −i times the Poisson bracket ([`exists_inclusion_eq_sharpCommutator_of_scalar`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/Classical.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.exists_inclusion_eq_sharpCommutator_of_scalar)).
- **Borel summation**: symbols of real orders decreasing to −∞ have an asymptotic sum, unique modulo smoothing, also for continuous families over a compact parameter space ([`exists_asymptoticSum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/AsymptoticSum.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_asymptoticSum)).
- **The formal parametrix**: an elliptic classical symbol of order m has classical left and right inverses of order −m under formal composition ([`exists_sharpTerms_eq_left`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Symbol/Parametrix.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.exists_sharpTerms_eq_left)), and any left and right parametrix symbols agree modulo smoothing ([`isSmoothing_sub_of_isAsymptoticSum_sharpTerm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Composition/Associativity.html#TauCeti.Symbol.isSmoothing_sub_of_isAsymptoticSum_sharpTerm)).
- **Sobolev mapping and compactness with compact spatial support**: such a symbol of order m gives a bounded operator H^s → H^(s−m) for every real s ([`exists_quantizeSobolev`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_quantizeSobolev)), which is compact into H^r for r < s − m ([`exists_isCompactOperator_quantizeSobolev`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/CompactOperator.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_isCompactOperator_quantizeSobolev)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Classical symbols** ([`ClassicalSymbol`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Symbol/Classical.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol)): these carry actual remainder symbols, unique homogeneous terms and the exact sequence 0 → S^(m−1)_cl → S^m_cl → H^m → 0. They are the carrier for ellipticity, composition and the parametrix recursion.
- **The complex interpolation method** ([`ComplexMethodSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Interpolation/ComplexMethod.html#TauCeti.CompatibleCouple.ComplexMethodSpace)): the strip-quotient space of a compatible couple, with the exact bound ‖T‖_θ ≤ ‖T₀‖^(1−θ)‖T₁‖^θ. Together with the K-method, it is the base for the interpolation identities.
- **The first-jet bundle** ([`FirstJetBundle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/FirstJet.html#TauCeti.FirstJetBundle)): a smooth vector bundle J¹V with jet extensions of sections and exactness of 0 → Hom(TM, V) → J¹V → V in the middle. Higher jets will extend it.

### Roadmap coverage

All nine layers are partial.

- **Layer 1** has the cotangent identification, the real and complex derivation dictionaries ([`exists_complexDerivationEquivComplexifiedVectorField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/ComplexDerivation.html#TauCeti.exists_complexDerivationEquivComplexifiedVectorField)), section modules, the first-jet bundle, and algebraic differential operators with coordinate expressions and a multiplicative principal symbol. It also has the rough Laplacian with its symbol identity, and Peetre's theorem for open sets of ℝⁿ. It lacks higher jets, formal adjoints, the Laplace-type and Bochner theory, and Peetre on section spaces.
- **Layer 2** lacks only restriction of classical symbols to open sets.
- **Layers 3 and 4** have quantization, kernels smooth off the diagonal, the reduction of an amplitude with compact input support to a left symbol, and a Schwartz kernel theorem on small compact sets ([`exists_schwartzKernel_supportedIn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction/SchwartzKernel.html#LinearMap.exists_schwartzKernel_supportedIn)). They also have composition and adjoints under compact spatial support, symbol recovery and output support. They lack the patched kernel theorem, proper support, coordinate and frame changes, and global bundle quantization.
- **Layer 5** adds Calderón–Vaillancourt, both interpolation methods and the Besov inclusions to the compact-support results. It lacks bundle H^(s,p), the interpolation identities, Zygmund mapping, embeddings and group averaging.
- **Layer 6** has ellipticity, the inverse principal symbol (as [`HomogeneousSymbol.inverse`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Elliptic.html#TauCeti.HomogeneousSymbol.inverse)), equal fibre ranks and the formal parametrix. Nothing operator-level exists yet.
- **Layers 7 and 8** have only preparatory steps. Layer 7 has equivariant kernel representations and the cut-off external product symbol with its ellipticity bound. Layer 8 has equal eigenspace multiplicities for almost-conjugate subgroups ([`finrank_invariants_inf_eigenspace_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/AlmostConjugate.html#Subgroup.AlmostConjugate.finrank_invariants_inf_eigenspace_eq)).
- **Layer 9** has the Euclidean wave front set with linear coordinate changes, restriction and smooth multiplication, plus tensor products of distributions. It lacks diffeomorphism and frame invariance, pseudolocality, microlocal regularity, pullbacks and products.

## The frontier

- **Operator parametrix and elliptic regularity**: the symbol-level parametrix needs to be quantized and patched, which requires composition without compact spatial support, through proper support. Local regularity, Pu ∈ H^s_loc ⇒ u ∈ H^(s+m)_loc, follows from that.
- **The uniform calculus**: Calderón–Vaillancourt covers only L². All-order H^s mapping for uniform symbols needs composition with Bessel multipliers in the uniform class.
- **Proper support and coordinate changes**: amplitude reduction exists for compact input support. Layer 3.2's near-diagonal localization and Layer 4.2's transformation formulas are next.
- **The Schwartz kernel theorem**: the small-compact-set case is done. Patching by partitions of unity and the bundle and density version remain.
- **Higher jets and formal adjoints**: these block the Laplace-type predicate, ∇*∇ as a composition of ∇ with its adjoint, and the Bochner connection.
