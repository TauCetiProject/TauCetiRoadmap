## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Euclidean theory is close to complete. It has the symbol calculus (Layer 2 is done), distributional elliptic regularity, microlocality, L^p bounds, nonlinear coordinate changes and now Hörmander's pullback and product of distributions. Layer 1's differential operators on bundles are nearly finished. Nothing pseudodifferential has reached a manifold yet: bundle quantization, the patched parametrix, Fredholm theory, elliptic powers and the product index have not started.

### Named results

- **Hörmander's pullback theorem**: a distribution whose wave front set misses the normal set of a smooth map f pulls back along f, with WF(f^*T) in the transposed image of WF(T) ([`Distribution.pullback`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/Pullback/SmoothMap.html#Distribution.pullback), [`waveFrontSet_pullback_subset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Distribution/WaveFrontSet/Pullback/SmoothMap.html#Distribution.waveFrontSet_pullback_subset)). Both are Euclidean only.
- **Distributional elliptic regularity**: if a classical symbol of order m is elliptic and its operator P is properly supported, then Pu ∈ H^s near x forces an arbitrary distribution u into H^(s+m) near x ([`IsElliptic.isLocallySobolevAt_of_quantizeDistribution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/ProperSupport/EllipticRegularity.html#TauCeti.ClassicalSymbol.IsElliptic.isLocallySobolevAt_of_quantizeDistribution)).
- **Microlocality**: a properly supported pseudodifferential operator does not enlarge the wave front set, and an elliptic one preserves it exactly ([`waveFrontSet_quantizeDistribution_subset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/ProperSupport/Microlocality.html#TauCeti.Symbol.waveFrontSet_quantizeDistribution_subset)). - **L^p boundedness of order-zero operators**: a symbol of order 0 with estimates uniform in x gives a bounded operator on L^p for 1 < p < ∞ ([`exists_quantizeUniformOrderZeroLp_of_one_lt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Pseudodifferential/Lp/CalderonZygmund.html#TauCeti.Symbol.exists_quantizeUniformOrderZeroLp_of_one_lt)).
- **Sunada's theorem**: for almost-conjugate subgroups acting freely by isometries on a closed manifold, the two quotients have isomorphic Laplace eigenspaces ([`nonempty_linearEquiv_weakEigenspace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/Isospectral/Weak.html#Subgroup.AlmostConjugate.nonempty_linearEquiv_weakEigenspace)). ### Roadmap coverage

Layer 2 is done; the other eight layers are partial.

- **Layer 1** is nearly complete. It lacks the Sym^j∇^j connection expansion of symbols, metric formal adjoints for general bundle operators, and the weighted-circle Bochner counterexample as a theorem.
- **Layers 3, 4 and 5** are essentially complete on ℝⁿ, now including the Hölder–Zygmund identification B^α_(∞,∞) = C^(0,α) ([`memBesov_iff_exists_holderSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Holder/Zygmund/Basic.html#TemperedDistribution.memBesov_iff_exists_holderSpace)). Every bundle form is missing. That means the density Schwartz kernel theorem, patched global quantization with atlas independence, and the global principal-symbol sequence. It also means bundle H^(s,p), Besov and Zygmund spaces, operator mapping on compact bundles, and equivariant averaging on manifolds.
- **Layer 6** has parametrices and regularity on ℝⁿ. The patched parametrix, Fredholm theory on compact bases, solvability and index independence are missing.
- **Layer 7** has ingredients only: powers of principal symbols, parameter-dependent calculi, the external product symbol and the de Rham symbol complex.
- **Layer 8** has Sunada isospectrality and normalized pullback on L² and H¹. It lacks the H² realization of the scalar Laplacian and the resolvent and spectral-projection intertwining.
- **Layer 9** has the full Euclidean calculus of §9.1 and §9.2: pseudolocality, microlocal elliptic regularity, invariance under changes of variables, admissible pullback, tensor products, products and proper pushforward, with continuity in the normal topology. The manifold and bundle versions are missing.

## The frontier

- **Global bundle quantization**: patch chart quantizations into a class on vector bundles, then prove atlas independence and the principal-symbol exact sequence. The local-to-global diffeomorphism extension is in place, but Kuranishi's trick has not yet been restated for chart maps between open sets.
- **Patched parametrix and Fredholm theory**: assemble Σ ψ_j Q_j φ_j from the properly supported local parametrices. This needs bundle quantization first.
- **Sobolev scales on bundles**: H^(s,p)(E) for p ≠ 2, and Besov and Zygmund spaces on bundles, plus operator mapping on all of them. Local Besov regularity is now invariant under changes of variables, a chart-compatibility step.
- **Manifold and bundle wave front sets**: the Euclidean pullback, product and pushforward theorems still have to be carried to manifolds and finite-rank bundles by localization in charts.
- **Elliptic powers**: A^z still has to be built as a classical operator of order dz via the resolvent contour integral and matched with the spectral power.
