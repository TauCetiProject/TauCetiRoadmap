### Named results

- **Existence and uniqueness of the Jones–Wenzl projection** — over any commutative ring where `[1], …, [n]` are units, exactly one element of the Temperley–Lieb algebra on `n` strands is idempotent, has augmentation `1` and is killed by every generator ([`existsUnique_jonesWenzl`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/JonesWenzl.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.existsUnique_jonesWenzl)). It is built by Wenzl's quadratic recursion and also satisfies the linear [single-clasp recursion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/SingleClasp.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.jonesWenzl_add_two_eq_strandIncl_mul).
- **The quantum dimension of the Jones–Wenzl projection** — every unnormalized Markov trace takes the value `[n+1]` on `f_n`, even when `[n+1]` itself vanishes ([`IsMarkovTrace.apply_jonesWenzl`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/QuantumDimension.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.apply_jonesWenzl)).
- **Jones' tower normal form and uniqueness of the Markov trace** — the algebra on `n + 2` strands is spanned by `strandIncl a` and `strandIncl a · e_last · strandIncl b` ([`span_strandIncl_union_strandIncl_mul_e_last_mul_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Tower.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.span_strandIncl_union_strandIncl_mul_e_last_mul_eq_top)), so there is at most one Markov trace at each `δ`. At `δ = -(q + q⁻¹)` it is therefore the spin-model trace ([`IsMarkovTrace.eq_markovTrace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/MarkovTrace.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.eq_markovTrace)).
- **The root-of-unity dictionary** — over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, some `[n]` with `n ≥ 1` vanishes at `δ` iff `δ = ζ + ζ⁻¹` for a root of unity `ζ ≠ ±1` ([`exists_qInt_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/RootOfUnity.html#TauCeti.exists_qInt_eq_zero_iff)).
- **The Catalan count of planar matchings** — there are `catalan n` planar matchings of `2n` points ([`card_planarMatching`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/DyckWord.html#TauCeti.PlanarMatching.card_planarMatching)), proved through an explicit [Dyck-word equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/DyckWord.html#TauCeti.PlanarMatching.dyckWordEquiv).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The tensor-ideal quotient** — [`MonoidalIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Quotient.html#TauCeti.MonoidalIdeal) is a morphism ideal closed under whiskering. Its quotient is [monoidal `S`-linear](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Quotient.html#TauCeti.MonoidalQuotient.instMonoidalLinear), and tensor ideals extend along `Mat_` and `Karoubi`. This is the route to a semisimplified quotient.
- **Rigid monoidal envelopes** — `Mat_ C` is [monoidal via Kronecker products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Mat/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.Mat_.instMonoidalCategory), and both `Mat_` and `Karoubi` inherit linear and [rigid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Karoubi/Rigid.html#CategoryTheory.Idempotents.Karoubi.instRigidCategory) structure. So `Karoubi (Mat_ C)` is a rigid monoidal linear category for any rigid monoidal linear `C`.
- **Cellular bases** — the Graham–Lehrer notion over a commutative ring, with cell representations, symmetric Gram matrices and cell modules with their radical quotients ([`CellularBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cellular/Basic.html#TauCeti.CellularBasis)).

## The frontier

- **Composition of Temperley–Lieb diagrams and `TLDiagCat`** — gluing `n → m → p` with its circle count, then associativity. This is the main blocker: Layers 3–5 and the Temperley–Lieb side of 6–7 all wait on it.
- **`exists_basis_planarMatching` and `existsUnique_isMarkovTrace`** — uniqueness is already done, so existence of a Markov trace at every `δ` needs the diagram basis. The trace is currently available only at `δ = -(q + q⁻¹)` with `q` a unit.
- **The Morrison coefficient formula** — the single-clasp recursion is in place. What remains is the sum over admissible removal sequences and an `#eval`-able coefficient check against `f_2`, `f_3` and a diagram with two removal choices.
- **The Temperley–Lieb cell structure and Gram determinants** — the through-strand cell structure, `CellModule R δ n k` with the half-diagram basis, and `gramPoly` in `ℤ[X]`. Over a field this also needs the general Graham–Lehrer simple-module classification and semisimplicity criterion, neither of which is proved yet.
- **Descent of rigid structure to the monoidal quotient and the abelian bridge** — the envelope pieces that remain before any fusion quotient can be stated.
