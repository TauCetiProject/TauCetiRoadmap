## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Temperley–Lieb diagram category now exists as a monoidal category, with associative composition and the free self-duality's universal property, so the main blocker of earlier reports is gone. The Jones–Wenzl projection, uniqueness of the Markov trace and the general cellular theory are in place. The linear category `TL(R, δ)`, the diagram basis, Gram determinants and every fusion result have not begun.

### Named results

- **Associativity of diagram composition** — gluing three Temperley–Lieb diagrams along their two middle boundaries gives the same matching and the same total circle count either way ([`TLDiagram.comp_assoc`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Associativity.html#TauCeti.TLDiagram.comp_assoc)). This is the load-bearing combinatorial theorem behind the diagram category.
- **The universal property of the free self-duality** — every self-dual object `V` of a monoidal category is the image of the generator of the free monoidal category on a self-dual object, under a strong monoidal functor unique up to monoidal isomorphism ([`FreeSelfDualityCat.lift_unique`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Free/SelfDuality.html#TauCeti.FreeSelfDualityCat.lift_unique)).
- **Existence and uniqueness of the Jones–Wenzl projection** — over any commutative ring where `[1], …, [n]` are units, exactly one element of the presented algebra on `n` strands is idempotent, has augmentation `1` and is killed by every generator ([`existsUnique_jonesWenzl`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/JonesWenzl.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.existsUnique_jonesWenzl)). Every Markov trace takes the value `[n+1]` on it ([`IsMarkovTrace.apply_jonesWenzl`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/QuantumDimension.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.apply_jonesWenzl)).
- **Uniqueness of the Markov trace** — Jones' tower normal form allows at most one Markov trace at each `δ`, so at `δ = -(q + q⁻¹)` it is the spin-model trace ([`IsMarkovTrace.eq_markovTrace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/MarkovTrace.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.eq_markovTrace)).
- **Graham–Lehrer theory over a field** — the source has the classification of simple modules of a cellular algebra and the criterion that it is semisimple exactly when every Gram determinant is nonzero (`isSemisimpleRing_iff_forall_det_gramMatrix_ne_zero`). These are general results, not yet applied to Temperley–Lieb.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The diagram category** — [`TLDiagCat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Category.html#TauCeti.TLDiagCat) keeps circles as data, so the endomorphisms of the empty boundary are [freely generated by the circle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Category.html#TauCeti.TLDiagCat.endZeroMulEquiv), and it is [monoidal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Monoidal.html#TauCeti.TLDiagCat.instMonoidalCategory) under juxtaposition. Linearization at every `δ` will factor through it.
- **Rigid envelopes and quotients** — `Karoubi` and `Mat_` inherit [rigid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Karoubi/Rigid.html#CategoryTheory.Idempotents.Karoubi.instRigidCategory) structure, and a quotient by a tensor ideal is now [rigid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Quotient/Rigid.html#TauCeti.MonoidalQuotient.instRigidCategory) too. These are the pieces a semisimplified quotient is built from.
- **Cell modules** — the [cell module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cellular/CellModule.html#TauCeti.CellularBasis.CellModule) of a cellular basis, with its invariant form and radical, is what Layer 5's Gram determinants will be computed on.

## The frontier

- **Rigidity and the universal property of `TLDiagCat`** — the nested cup and cap self-dualities, the necessity of circle centrality, and identifying `TLDiagCat` with the free self-duality category modulo that relation through the innermost-cup normal form.
- **The linear category `TL(R, δ)`** — now unblocked. It feeds `TLAlg`, the comparison with the presented algebra, the planar-matching basis and existence of the Markov trace at every `δ`.
- **The Temperley–Lieb cell structure and Gram determinants** — the general Graham–Lehrer theorems are available. What is missing is the through-strand cell datum on the diagram basis, which needs Layer 4.
- **The Morrison coefficient formula** — the single-clasp recursion is in place; the sum over admissible removal sequences is not.
- **The remaining envelope descent** — `MonoidalLinear` on `Mat_` and `Karoubi`, pivotal, spherical and ribbon descent to tensor-ideal quotients, and the abelian bridge of §7.3.
