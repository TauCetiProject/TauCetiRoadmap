## TemperleyLieb: 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-09 (`888e64c` to `d92acc5`)

The Jones–Wenzl projections arrived on Tau Ceti's presented Temperley–Lieb algebra. [Existence and uniqueness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/JonesWenzl.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.existsUnique_jonesWenzl) says that once `[1], …, [n]` are units, exactly one element is idempotent, has augmentation `1` and is killed by every generator. It is built by [Wenzl's recursion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/JonesWenzl.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.jonesWenzl_add_two) and also satisfies the [single-clasp recursion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/SingleClasp.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.jonesWenzl_add_two_eq_strandIncl_mul), the linear form that leads toward Morrison's coefficient formula. Every Markov trace [takes the value `[n+1]` on it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/QuantumDimension.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.apply_jonesWenzl). Jones' [tower normal form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Tower.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.span_strandIncl_union_strandIncl_mul_e_last_mul_eq_top) gives uniqueness of Markov traces, so any trace at `δ = -(q + q⁻¹)` [is the spin-model one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/MarkovTrace.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.eq_markovTrace). A Markov trace at an arbitrary `δ` still has not been constructed.

Underneath, the quantum integers are now Chebyshev evaluations with the [root-of-unity dictionary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/RootOfUnity.html#TauCeti.exists_qInt_eq_zero_iff): in characteristic zero, some `[n]` vanishes exactly when `δ = ζ + ζ⁻¹` for a root of unity `ζ ≠ ±1`. Planar matchings got rotation, reflection and a [Dyck-word model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/DyckWord.html#TauCeti.PlanarMatching.dyckWordEquiv), which yields the [Catalan count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/DyckWord.html#TauCeti.PlanarMatching.card_planarMatching), plus half-diagrams with the ballot formula. Temperley–Lieb diagrams carrying a circle count now exist, with through-strands and an embedding into the planar Brauer diagrams. There is no composition of diagrams yet, so the diagram category, the linear category `TL(R, δ)` and its universal property have not begun.

On the categorical side, the envelope infrastructure Layer 7 asks for is largely in place: monoidal, linear and rigid structures on `Mat_` and on `Karoubi` (for instance [rigidity of the idempotent completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Karoubi/Rigid.html#CategoryTheory.Idempotents.Karoubi.instRigidCategory)), plus [tensor ideals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Quotient.html#TauCeti.MonoidalIdeal) with their monoidally linear quotients (TauCeti#24293). A general [cellular basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cellular/Basic.html#TauCeti.CellularBasis) with cell representations and Gram matrices begins Layer 5. The Temperley–Lieb cell structure and the Gram determinants have not been started.
