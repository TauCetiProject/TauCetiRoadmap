## TemperleyLieb: 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-09 (`888e64c` to `d92acc5`)

The Jones–Wenzl projections arrived on Tau Ceti's presented Temperley–Lieb algebra. [Existence and uniqueness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/JonesWenzl.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.existsUnique_jonesWenzl) says that once `[1], …, [n]` are units, exactly one element is idempotent, has augmentation `1` and is killed by every generator. It is built by [Wenzl's recursion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/JonesWenzl.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.jonesWenzl_add_two) and also satisfies the [single-clasp recursion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/SingleClasp.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.jonesWenzl_add_two_eq_strandIncl_mul), the linear form that leads toward Morrison's coefficient formula. Every Markov trace [takes the value `[n+1]` on it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/QuantumDimension.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.apply_jonesWenzl). Jones' [tower normal form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Tower.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.span_strandIncl_union_strandIncl_mul_e_last_mul_eq_top) gives uniqueness of Markov traces, so any trace at `δ = -(q + q⁻¹)` [is the spin-model one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/MarkovTrace.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.eq_markovTrace). A Markov trace at an arbitrary `δ` still has not been constructed.

Underneath, the quantum integers are now Chebyshev evaluations with the [root-of-unity dictionary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/RootOfUnity.html#TauCeti.exists_qInt_eq_zero_iff): in characteristic zero, some `[n]` vanishes exactly when `δ = ζ + ζ⁻¹` for a root of unity `ζ ≠ ±1`. Planar matchings got rotation, reflection and a [Dyck-word model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/DyckWord.html#TauCeti.PlanarMatching.dyckWordEquiv), which yields the [Catalan count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/DyckWord.html#TauCeti.PlanarMatching.card_planarMatching), plus half-diagrams with the ballot formula. Temperley–Lieb diagrams carrying a circle count now exist, with through-strands and an embedding into the planar Brauer diagrams. There is no composition of diagrams yet, so the diagram category, the linear category `TL(R, δ)` and its universal property have not begun.

On the categorical side, the envelope infrastructure Layer 7 asks for is largely in place: monoidal, linear and rigid structures on `Mat_` and on `Karoubi` (for instance [rigidity of the idempotent completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Karoubi/Rigid.html#CategoryTheory.Idempotents.Karoubi.instRigidCategory)), plus [tensor ideals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Quotient.html#TauCeti.MonoidalIdeal) with their monoidally linear quotients (TauCeti#24293). A general [cellular basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cellular/Basic.html#TauCeti.CellularBasis) with cell representations and Gram matrices begins Layer 5. The Temperley–Lieb cell structure and the Gram determinants have not been started.

## TemperleyLieb: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`d92acc5` to `b235000`)

The Temperley–Lieb diagram category now exists. [Composition of diagrams](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Compose.html#TauCeti.TLDiagram.comp) glues one diagram on top of another and counts the circles closed in the middle, and its [associativity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Associativity.html#TauCeti.TLDiagram.comp_assoc), the hard theorem of Layer 2, is proved (TauCeti#38316). On top of it sits [`TLDiagCat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Category.html#TauCeti.TLDiagCat), where a cup followed by a cap is the closed circle and the endomorphisms of the empty boundary are [freely generated by that circle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Category.html#TauCeti.TLDiagCat.endZeroMulEquiv). Juxtaposition makes it [monoidal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Monoidal.html#TauCeti.TLDiagCat.instMonoidalCategory), with the [interchange law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Tensor.html#TauCeti.TLDiagram.tensor_comp_tensor) proved for diagrams. Rigidity, the universal property of `TLDiagCat` and the comparison of composition with Brauer stacking are not there yet. That stacking two planar Brauer diagrams [gives a planar diagram](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Brauer/Planar.html#TauCeti.BrauerDiagram.IsPlanar.composeDiagram) is in.

The free half of the presentation theorem landed. [`FreeSelfDualityCat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Free/SelfDuality.html#TauCeti.FreeSelfDualityCat) is presented by one object with a cup, a cap and the two snake relations, and every self-duality in a monoidal category is the image of its generator under a strong monoidal functor that is [unique up to monoidal isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Free/SelfDuality.html#TauCeti.FreeSelfDualityCat.lift_unique). On the envelope side, quotients by tensor ideals of rigid categories are now [rigid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Envelope/Quotient/Rigid.html#TauCeti.MonoidalQuotient.instRigidCategory), and [braidings descend](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Quotient/Braided.html#CategoryTheory.Quotient.braidedCategory) to quotients by monoidal relations.

Layer 1's [product formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/HalfDiagram/Glue.html#TauCeti.PlanarMatching.card_through_eq) is proved: cutting along the through-strands shows that the matchings with `k` of them number `halfCount n k * halfCount m k`. Two pieces that landed earlier are recorded here late: the [half-diagram convolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PlanarMatching/Counting.html#TauCeti.sum_halfCount_mul), whose squares sum to the Catalan number, and the [cell modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cellular/CellModule.html#TauCeti.CellularBasis.CellModule) of a cellular basis. A cell module's quotient by the radical is [simple exactly when the cell form is nonzero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Cellular/CellModule.html#TauCeti.CellularBasis.isSimpleModule_cellModule_quotient_iff).
