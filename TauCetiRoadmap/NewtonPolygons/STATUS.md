## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The arithmetic core is in place. That covers the root-valuation dictionary, the product formula for polynomials and entire series, slope factorisation, disc preparation, the irreducibility and ramification consequences, and the `μ`/`λ` preparation for Iwasawa theory. Every layer has work in it, but none is fully closed: exact zero counts on discs, convergence in extensions, entire products over zeros, the `ℂ_p` specialisations and several worked checks are still missing.

### Named results

- **Slopes are negated root valuations** — if a polynomial splits in a valued extension, its finite slopes are the values `-w(α)` over its nonzero roots, with multiplicity, and face endpoints count roots in open and closed balls ([`Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Roots.html#Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map)).
- **The product formula** — the Newton polygon of a product is the Minkowski sum of the factors' polygons, for polynomials ([`Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Mul.html#Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul)) and for entire series ([`PowerSeries.IsEntire.newtonPolygon_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Entire.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire.newtonPolygon_mul)).
- **The pure slope factorisation** — over a complete ultrametric field, a polynomial with constant coefficient one factors uniquely into coprime constant-one pieces, each pure of one slope; in particular distinct slopes force splitting over the base ([`Polynomial.existsUnique_isPureSlopeFactorization`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Factorization.html#Polynomial.existsUnique_isPureSlopeFactorization)).
- **The Newton polygon irreducibility criterion** — for an integer-valued valuation on a field, a polynomial with nonzero constant coefficient, pure of slope `q`, whose degree is the denominator of `q`, is irreducible, and Eisenstein's criterion follows ([`Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Irreducible.html#Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq)).
- **Weierstrass preparation with a uniformiser power** — over a complete DVR, a nonzero series is `π^μ(f)` times a unit times a distinguished polynomial of degree `λ(f)`, uniquely, with `μ` and `λ` additive on products ([`PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DiscreteValuationRing.html#PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The lower convex envelope** ([`TauCeti.lowerConvexEnvelope`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/Envelope.html#TauCeti.lowerConvexEnvelope)) is the greatest convex minorant of a height sequence, defined independently of coefficients. Newton polygons are its application to coefficient valuations, so supporting values, faces, Minkowski sums and comparison theorems are proved once and reused.
- **Slope presentations** ([`TauCeti.FiniteSlopeData`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/SlopeData/Basic.html#TauCeti.FiniteSlopeData), [`TauCeti.InfiniteSlopeData`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/SlopeData/Basic.html#TauCeti.InfiniteSlopeData)) build polygons from prescribed slopes, with compressed and terminal-ray variants. Through them, Hodge lower bounds become partial-sum inequalities, including the bound for `det(1 - XA)`.
- **The normalised disc preparation** ([`TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDiscPreparation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DiscPreparation.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDiscPreparation)) writes `f = P_r U_r` with constant-one factors and `‖U_r - 1‖_r < 1`. Its polynomial is unique, carries every zero in the closed disc, and divides the polynomial at any larger radius.

Layer 6 lacks the `ℚ₂(i)` check, the checks on `X^p − p`, `Φ_p(X+1)/p` and `p + X + X²`, and the valuation-ring form of the preparation contract.

## The frontier

- **Analytic multiplicity and exact disc counts** — zero multiplicity by translation and order exists for entire series; extend it to restricted series, match it with disc-polynomial root multiplicity, and prove that the closed disc, open disc and sphere carry `u_r`, `l_r` and `u_r - l_r` zeros. This needs translation and Hasse derivatives from RestrictedPowerSeries §2.
- **Entire factorisation** — show that the disc polynomials `P_r` converge to `f` in every Gauss norm, then deduce `f = ∏ (1 - X/α)` over an algebraically closed complete field, with the converse and uniqueness. The entire product formula and the nested disc polynomials are already available.
- **Algebraic extensions and `ℂ_p`** — the spectral-norm valuation on an algebraic closure, the rational range on `ℂ_p`, density of its norms, and the corresponding root-dictionary and convergence-point statements (§§1.3, 3.3, 5.2).
- **Checks and the Iwasawa contract** — the Layer 0 examples (`n²`, `⌈n√2⌉`), the Layer 2 and Layer 6 checks, the `ξ₂` factorisation over `ℚ₂(i)`, and the valuation-ring form of preparation consumed by PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras.
