## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The polynomial core is in place: most of the generic convex-polygon geometry, polynomial Newton polygons with their slopes, products and root valuations, the irreducibility criterion, and formal Weierstrass preparation with the μ- and λ-invariants. Normalisation has only its multiplicative-to-additive dictionary, and the analytic layers (disc factors, zero counts, radius of convergence, entire products) have not begun.

### Named results

- **Slopes are negated root valuations** — the finite slopes of a split polynomial are the values -v(α) over its nonzero roots α, with multiplicity, also for roots in a valued extension whose valuation restricts to the given one ([`Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Roots.html#Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots), [`Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Roots.html#Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map)).
- **Root counts from face endpoints** — for a split polynomial, the right and left endpoints of the face of slope σ count roots with v(α) ≥ -σ and v(α) > -σ, including the root zero, so the face width counts roots of valuation exactly -σ ([`Polynomial.card_filter_roots_le_addValuation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Roots.html#Polynomial.card_filter_roots_le_addValuation)).
- **The polynomial product formula** — the Newton polygon of a product is the Minkowski sum of the factors' polygons, and finite slope multisets add ([`Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Mul.html#Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul)).
- **The Newton polygon irreducibility criterion** — for an integer-valued valuation, a polynomial with nonzero constant term, all slopes equal to q, and degree equal to the denominator of q is irreducible; more generally each factor's degree is a multiple of that denominator ([`Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Irreducible.html#Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq)).
- **Weierstrass preparation with a uniformiser power** — over a complete discrete valuation ring a nonzero power series is π^μ(f) times a unit times a distinguished polynomial of degree λ(f), uniquely for fixed π ([`PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DiscreteValuationRing.html#PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [lower convex envelope](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/Envelope.html#TauCeti.lowerConvexEnvelope) of a height sequence `ℕ → WithTop ℝ`, the greatest convex minorant with an all-`∞` fallback for inadmissible input. With supporting values and the `ConvexOn` interpolation equivalence, it makes the Newton polygon one instance of a coefficient-free geometry.
- [Finite slope data](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/SlopeData.html#TauCeti.FiniteSlopeData) and its half-line counterpart present a polygon by an anchor and nondecreasing unit slopes, with both round trips. Polygon order becomes comparison of partial sums ([`FiniteSlopeData.heights_le_heights_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/Order.html#TauCeti.FiniteSlopeData.heights_le_heights_iff)), which is the interface Hodge lower bounds need.
- The [Minkowski sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/Minkowski/Basic.html#TauCeti.minkowskiSum) by min-convolution, under which supporting values, finite-face endpoints and slope multisets all add.
