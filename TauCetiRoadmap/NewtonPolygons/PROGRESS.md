## NewtonPolygons: 2026-10-16 to 2026-11-06 (`91933c3` to `385ec2d`)

The roadmap's central theorems have landed. The finite Newton slopes of a split polynomial are the negated valuations of its nonzero roots, with multiplicity ([`Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Roots.html#Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map)). The polygon of a product is the Minkowski sum of the factors' polygons, for polynomials ([`Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Mul.html#Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul)) and for entire series ([`PowerSeries.IsEntire.newtonPolygon_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Entire.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire.newtonPolygon_mul)). Over a complete field, a polynomial with constant coefficient one has a unique factorisation into pure-slope pieces ([`Polynomial.existsUnique_isPureSlopeFactorization`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Factorization.html#Polynomial.existsUnique_isPureSlopeFactorization)). So a polynomial whose slopes are all distinct splits over the base field, and the same factorisation carries the zeros of a restricted series on any closed disc ([`PowerSeries.existsUnique_isUnit_X_pow_mul_finprod_eq_of_pos`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Factorization.html#PowerSeries.existsUnique_isUnit_X_pow_mul_finprod_eq_of_pos)).

For integer-valued valuations on a field, the Newton polygon irreducibility criterion ([`Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Irreducible.html#Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq)) recovers Eisenstein's criterion, and slope denominators are shown to divide ramification indices. Over a complete field, irreducible polynomials are pure, and the slope of a [minimal polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Minpoly.html#minpoly.newtonSlopes_eq_replicate_logb_spectralNorm) is the base-`b` logarithm of the spectral norm ([TauCeti#25678](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25678)). On the Iwasawa side, a nonzero series over a complete DVR factors as `π^μ` times a unit times a distinguished polynomial of degree `λ` ([`PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DiscreteValuationRing.html#PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit)). Both invariants are additive and can be read off the polygon, and at radii just inside one the formal preparation agrees with normed preparation. For a series with infinitely many nonzero coefficients, the radius of convergence is `b` raised to the supremum of the finite slopes ([`PowerSeries.radius_eq_exp_iSup_unitSlope_newtonPolygon`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Radius.html#PowerSeries.radius_eq_exp_iSup_unitSlope_newtonPolygon)), and the Cauchy–Hadamard formula landed alongside it.

All of this rests on a construction-independent geometry of discrete convex height functions: lower convex envelopes, supporting values, faces, Minkowski sums, slope presentations and two-sided versions. Polynomials over Laurent series use the same API, through Mathlib's order valuation read in `ℝ`, with [multiplicative initial polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/LaurentSeries.html#Polynomial.initialPolynomial_mul) ([TauCeti#23449](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/23449), [TauCeti#24512](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/24512)). Some milestones are not established yet: analytic multiplicity for restricted series and exact zero counts on a disc, convergence in extensions, the entire product over zeros, and the `ℂ_p` specialisations.

## NewtonPolygons: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-19 (`385ec2d` to `b235000`)

The Weierstrass product for non-archimedean entire functions has landed. Over an algebraically closed complete ultrametric field, an entire series with constant coefficient one is the product of `1 - X/α` over its zeros with multiplicity, converging unconditionally in every Gauss norm and independently of how the zeros are enumerated ([`PowerSeries.IsEntire.hasProd_polynomialHom_one_sub_C_inv_mul_X`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Product.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire.hasProd_polynomialHom_one_sub_C_inv_mul_X)). The proof shows that the normalised disc polynomials converge to the series as the radius grows. The converse is also there: such a product converges exactly when the prescribed zeros tend to infinity in norm ([`TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsEntire.multipliable_polynomialHom_one_sub_C_inv_mul_X_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Product.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsEntire.multipliable_polynomialHom_one_sub_C_inv_mul_X_iff)), and then it has exactly those zeros. Normalised entire series with the same zeros are equal ([`PowerSeries.IsEntire.eq_of_order_taylor_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Entire/Factorization.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire.eq_of_order_taylor_eq)), entire units are constants, and an entire series with finitely many zeros is a polynomial.

The exact disc counts arrived alongside. Multiplicity is now defined for restricted series by the order of the Taylor translate, giving a [zero multiset](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/ZeroCount.html#PowerSeries.zeroMultiset) on each closed disc that equals the root multiset of the disc polynomial. In an algebraically closed complete extension, the closed disc carries exactly `u_r` zeros ([`TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.card_zeroMultiset_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/ZeroCount.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.IsDistinguished.card_zeroMultiset_eq)), the open disc carries `l_r`, and the sphere carries `u_r - l_r`. The theorem that small perturbations preserve these counts has not been proved yet.

On the valuation side, `ℂ_p` now has its rational valuation normalised by `v(p) = 1`, with value group `ℚ` and uniqueness ([`PadicComplex.addValuation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Complex/Valuation.html#PadicComplex.addValuation)). It comes from the fact that commensurability passes to algebraic extensions and completions. Several Layer 6 checks also landed: `X^p - p` forces total ramification, `Φ_p(X+1)/p` is pure of slope `-1/(p-1)`, and `p + X + X²` has `λ = 1` but unit-disc Weierstrass degree two. Smaller pieces include realisability of slope multisets by split polynomials, rational slope data, and slope presentations with an integer anchor (TauCeti#28478).

## NewtonPolygons: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (`b235000` to `522d48f`)

This window closed most of the gaps the last report listed. Zero counts on a disc are now stable under perturbation: if `‖f - g‖_r < ‖f‖_r`, then `f` and `g` have the same number of zeros in the closed disc of radius `r` ([`TauCeti.PowerSeries.card_zeroMultiset_eq_of_gaussNorm_sub_lt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Perturbation/ZeroCount.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.card_zeroMultiset_eq_of_gaussNorm_sub_lt)), and the same holds in the open disc and on the sphere. Convergence in extensions is also done. A series converges at a point of norm `r` exactly when it is restricted at `r` ([`PowerSeries.summable_smul_pow_iff_isRestricted`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Convergence.html#PowerSeries.summable_smul_pow_iff_isRestricted)). The [pointwise radius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Convergence.html#PowerSeries.convergenceRadius) equals the intrinsic one when the norms of the field are dense, as they are for `ℂ_p`, and can be strictly smaller over a discretely valued field (TauCeti#39627).

On the valuation side, an algebraic extension of a complete field now carries the [additive spectral valuation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Valuation/Spectral.html#TauCeti.spectralAddValuation). In an algebraically closed extension, the slopes of a polynomial are [the negated values of its roots](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Spectral.html#Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map_spectralNorm_of_isAlgClosed) under this valuation. Over a complete field, a slope's multiplicity is [the total degree of the irreducible factors of that slope](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Minpoly.html#Polynomial.count_newtonSlopes_eq_sum_natDegree_filter_normalizedFactors). First breaks of a polygon got a coefficient-line criterion and a [first-segment factorisation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/FirstBreak.html#PowerSeries.existsUnique_isUnit_isMonicOfDegree_mul_eq_of_isFirstBreak).

Many of the roadmap's worked checks landed too. They include `ξ₂ = X² + 2X + 2` [splitting](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/RamifiedQuadratic.html#TauCeti.NewtonPolygonRamifiedQuadraticExample.X_sq_add_two_mul_X_add_two_eq_mul) over a ring containing `i`, and the [Weierstrass product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Complex/QuadraticSeries.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.hasProd_mk_padicComplex_pow_sq) of `Σ p^(n²) Xⁿ` over `ℂ_p`. The [Newton polygon of the `p`-adic exponential](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Exponential.html#TauCeti.NewtonPolygonExponentialExample.newtonPolygon_exp) is computed, with no zeros in its disc of convergence. The envelope of `⌈n√2⌉` is a ray of [irrational slope](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/Envelope.html#TauCeti.LowerConvexEnvelopeExample.irrational_unitSlope_lowerConvexEnvelope_ceilHeights_sqrt_two). Two series with the same polygon have [different numbers of zeros on the unit sphere](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/BoundaryZeros.html#TauCeti.NewtonPolygonBoundaryZeroExample.card_filter_zeroMultiset_norm_eq_boundaryZeroSeries).
