## NewtonPolygons: 2026-10-16 to 2026-11-06 (`91933c3` to `385ec2d`)

The roadmap's central theorems have landed. The finite Newton slopes of a split polynomial are the negated valuations of its nonzero roots, with multiplicity ([`Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Roots.html#Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots_map)). The polygon of a product is the Minkowski sum of the factors' polygons, for polynomials ([`Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Mul.html#Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul)) and for entire series ([`PowerSeries.IsEntire.newtonPolygon_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Entire.html#PowerSeries.IsEntire.newtonPolygon_mul)). Over a complete field, a polynomial with constant coefficient one has a unique factorisation into pure-slope pieces ([`Polynomial.existsUnique_isPureSlopeFactorization`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Factorization.html#Polynomial.existsUnique_isPureSlopeFactorization)). So a polynomial whose slopes are all distinct splits over the base field, and the same factorisation carries the zeros of a restricted series on any closed disc ([`PowerSeries.existsUnique_isUnit_X_pow_mul_finprod_eq_of_pos`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Factorization.html#PowerSeries.existsUnique_isUnit_X_pow_mul_finprod_eq_of_pos)).

For integer-valued valuations on a field, the Newton polygon irreducibility criterion ([`Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Irreducible.html#Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq)) recovers Eisenstein's criterion, and slope denominators are shown to divide ramification indices. Over a complete field, irreducible polynomials are pure, and the slope of a [minimal polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Minpoly.html#minpoly.newtonSlopes_eq_replicate_logb_spectralNorm) is the base-`b` logarithm of the spectral norm ([TauCeti#25678](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25678)). On the Iwasawa side, a nonzero series over a complete DVR factors as `π^μ` times a unit times a distinguished polynomial of degree `λ` ([`PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DiscreteValuationRing.html#PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit)). Both invariants are additive and can be read off the polygon, and at radii just inside one the formal preparation agrees with normed preparation. For a series with infinitely many nonzero coefficients, the radius of convergence is `b` raised to the supremum of the finite slopes ([`PowerSeries.radius_eq_exp_iSup_unitSlope_newtonPolygon`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Radius.html#PowerSeries.radius_eq_exp_iSup_unitSlope_newtonPolygon)), and the Cauchy–Hadamard formula landed alongside it.

All of this rests on a construction-independent geometry of discrete convex height functions: lower convex envelopes, supporting values, faces, Minkowski sums, slope presentations and two-sided versions. Polynomials over Laurent series use the same API, through Mathlib's order valuation read in `ℝ`, with [multiplicative initial polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/LaurentSeries.html#Polynomial.initialPolynomial_mul) ([TauCeti#23449](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/23449), [TauCeti#24512](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/24512)). Some milestones are not established yet: analytic multiplicity for restricted series and exact zero counts on a disc, convergence in extensions, the entire product over zeros, and the `ℂ_p` specialisations.
