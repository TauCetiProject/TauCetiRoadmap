## NewtonPolygons: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-27 (`91933c3` to `dd217c4`)

The Newton polygon of a polynomial now reads off its roots. When the polynomial splits, its finite slopes are exactly the negated valuations of its nonzero roots, counted with multiplicity ([`Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Roots.html#Polynomial.newtonSlopes_eq_map_roots)), and the endpoints of the face of slope σ count the roots of valuation at least, or strictly above, -σ, the root zero included ([`Polynomial.card_filter_roots_le_addValuation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Roots.html#Polynomial.card_filter_roots_le_addValuation)). Behind this sits the product formula: the polygon of a product is the Minkowski sum of the polygons of its factors ([`Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Mul.html#Polynomial.newtonPolygon_mul)). Its first arithmetic consequence is the Newton polygon irreducibility criterion: for an integer-valued valuation, a polynomial with nonzero constant term whose slopes all equal q, and whose degree is the denominator of q, is irreducible ([`Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NewtonPolygon/Irreducible.html#Polynomial.irreducible_of_forall_mem_newtonSlopes_eq)).

On the formal side, Weierstrass preparation with a uniformiser power landed: over a complete discrete valuation ring, a nonzero power series is π^μ times a unit times a distinguished polynomial of degree λ ([`PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/DiscreteValuationRing.html#PowerSeries.exists_pow_mul_distinguished_mul_unit)), uniquely for a fixed uniformiser, and both invariants are additive on products. This is the abstract complete-DVR form; the valuation-ring specialisation and the reading of μ and λ off the polygon are still to come.

All of this rests on a coefficient-free geometry of convex height sequences: the [lower convex envelope](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Sequence/Envelope.html#TauCeti.lowerConvexEnvelope), its reconstruction from supporting values, finite faces, Minkowski sums, unit-slope presentations with both round trips, and the partial-sum criterion for comparing polygons. Nothing analytic has started: Gauss norms, active indices, disc factors and the radius of convergence are all ahead, as are the integer and rational valuation normalisations.
