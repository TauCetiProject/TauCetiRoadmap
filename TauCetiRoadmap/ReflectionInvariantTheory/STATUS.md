### Named results

- **The Chevalley–Shephard–Todd theorem** — a finite group acting faithfully on a finite-dimensional space in characteristic zero is generated by pseudoreflections exactly when its invariant polynomial functions are a polynomial algebra on homogeneous generators ([`pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top_iff_exists_bijective_aeval_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_dual`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/ShephardTodd.html#Representation.pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top_iff_exists_bijective_aeval_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_dual)). The forward direction needs only that `|G|` be invertible.
- **Chevalley's regular-representation theorem** — for a pseudoreflection group, the coinvariant algebra is the regular representation ([`nonempty_equiv_coinvariantAlgebra_dual_leftRegular_of_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/RegularRepresentation.html#Representation.nonempty_equiv_coinvariantAlgebra_dual_leftRegular_of_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top)), and its graded character is computed as well.
- **The degree identities** — the product of the degrees of basic invariants is `|G|` ([`prod_eq_card_of_bijective_aeval_symmetricAlgebraInvariants`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/ProductOfDegrees.html#Representation.prod_eq_card_of_bijective_aeval_symmetricAlgebraInvariants)), and their sum is `dim V` plus the number of pseudoreflections.
- **The Jacobian formula** — the Jacobian of basic invariants is a nonzero scalar times `∏_H ℓ_H^(e_H − 1)` ([`exists_jacobian_eq_algebraMap_mul_prod_dual_inertia_sub_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Hyperplane/Jacobian.html#Representation.exists_jacobian_eq_algebraMap_mul_prod_dual_inertia_sub_one)).
- **Exceptional Coxeter eigenvalues** — every Coxeter element of type `E₈` has eigenvalues `exp(2πi e/30)` for the eight exponents ([`eigenvalues_of_hasCartanType_E8`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Coxeter/Element/Eigen.html#TauCeti.Coxeter.eigenvalues_of_hasCartanType_E8)). The same is proved for `E₆`, `E₇`, `F₄` and `G₂`, by explicit cyclotomic factorisation of characteristic polynomials.

## The frontier

- **The coprime exponents and uniform eigenvalues** — construct, for a Coxeter element, an eigenvector for `exp(2πi/h)` that lies on no reflecting hyperplane. With the coprime step and Springer's regular-eigenvector computation, this gives §4.3 uniformly and, once the exponents are known to be less than `h`, §4.6.
- **The Coxeter number and root heights** — `h = |Φ|/r` by Steinberg's orbit count, then Macdonald's product formula for the height partition and `ht(θ) = h − 1`.
- **Chambers and the length identity** — a Coxeter system on a general finite real reflection group from its wall reflections, which §3.5's `Σ t^{ℓ(w)}` and §4.1 both need.
- **The geometric converse and Steinberg's theorem** — étale covers of affine space are trivial, plus purity from PurityOfBranchLocus §2.2. These give the converse for arbitrary generators and then the stabilizer theorem through the completed local comparison.
- **Layer 1's new targets** — Fogarty's route to Noether's bound in positive characteristic, and Hochster–Eagon, whose regular-local-ring and graded Nakayama inputs are already in the library.
