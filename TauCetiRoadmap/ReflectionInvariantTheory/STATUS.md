## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Both directions of the Chevalley–Shephard–Todd theorem are proved, and so is most of the coinvariant package, in characteristic zero for pseudoreflection groups. Two parts of the README remain open: the geometric proof of the converse (purity and étale covers) and the real chamber geometry. The Weyl/Coxeter dictionary is done only for the exceptional types and type `A`, by explicit computation. The topological consumers have not begun.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [Faithful flatness of `Sym(V*)` over the invariants](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Flat.html#Representation.faithfullyFlat_symmetricAlgebra_dual_of_finrank_quotient_eq_one) comes from killing `Tor′` with divided differences and Reynolds averaging. With faithfully flat descent of global dimension ([`globalDimension_le_of_faithfullyFlat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GlobalDimension.html#TauCeti.globalDimension_le_of_faithfullyFlat)), it carries the homological proof of polynomiality.
- [Invariants commute with flat scalar extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/BaseChange.html#Representation.symmetricAlgebraInvariantsBaseChangeEquiv), and so does the Reynolds operator. This lets degrees move between `ℚ`, `ℝ` and `ℂ`.
- The [pseudoreflection subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Inertia.html#Representation.inertia_quotient_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_bot) is a normal subgroup. Its residual quotient has trivial inertia and is [unramified in codimension one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Unramified.html#Representation.isUnramifiedAt_quotient_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_of_height_eq_one), the first half of the geometric converse.

### Roadmap coverage

**§1, the finite-group invariant algebra, is done.** The Reynolds/base-change comparison now holds. The only Cohen–Macaulay consequence recorded is the regular-sequence statement for reflection groups.

**§2, the complex branch, is partial.** Both implications, the Jacobian formula, the cyclic normal-coordinate model, the inertia correspondence and the `S_n` degree examples are proved. The converse was proved by comparing degrees rather than by the route the README specifies, so the following are still missing:

- purity of the branch locus;
- triviality of connected finite étale covers of affine space;
- the point-stabilizer and completed-local APIs.

**§3, the real and Weyl package, is partial.** Its algebra is proved for all pseudoreflection groups: freeness of rank `|W|`, regular sequences, a homogeneous complement and the coinvariant Hilbert series `∏(1−t^{dᵢ})/(1−t)^r`. The real-specific part is missing:

- chambers and the native Coxeter system from wall reflections;
- length as the number of separating hyperplanes;
- the identity with `Σ t^{ℓ(w)}`;
- the flag Poincaré polynomial and the character-lattice bridge.

**§4, the Weyl/Coxeter dictionary, is partial.** It is complete for `E₆`–`G₂` and type `A`, and the Coxeter elements of a Weyl group are conjugate. Types `B`–`D` are missing, as are `h = |Φ|/r`, the height partition, the highest-root height and the general coprime theorem.

**§5, the source examples, is partial.** The `S_n` degrees, the Coxeter cycle and the bipartite Coxeter element are proved. The `U(n)` and `SU(n)` cohomology checks and the affine-diagram colouring classification are missing.

## The frontier

- **The regular-eigenvector theorem** — at an eigenvector where the Jacobian is nonzero, [the degrees give the eigenvalues](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/RegularEigenvector.html#Representation.charpoly_eq_prod_of_eigenvector_of_jacobian_ne_zero). The remaining step is a primitive `h`-eigenvector of a Coxeter element that lies off the reflecting hyperplanes. That would give the uniform `eᵢ = dᵢ − 1` theorem and, with the cyclotomic computations, the coprime-exponent theorem.
- **The classical types and root heights** — Coxeter numbers and eigenvalues for `B`, `C` and `D`, then `h = |Φ|/r`, the height partition and `ht(θ) = h − 1`.
- **The length-sum Hilbert series** — the product side of `Hilb(C_W)` is proved. Equating it with `Σ t^{ℓ(w)}` needs the chamber geometry and the Coxeter system of a real reflection group.
- **The geometric converse** — purity for the residual map `V/H → V/G` and triviality of connected finite étale covers of affine space. The theorem itself is already proved, by counting degrees.
- **The topological consumers** — the flag Poincaré polynomial, the rational character-lattice presentation and the `U(n)`/`SU(n)` checks. None has started.
