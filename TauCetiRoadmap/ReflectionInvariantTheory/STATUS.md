### Named results

- **Molien's formula** — over a field in which `|G|` is invertible, the Hilbert series of `Sym(M)^G` equals `|G|⁻¹ Σ_g det(1 − t g)⁻¹`, with dimensions read in the field, so it determines the series in characteristic zero ([`mk_finrank_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_inf_homogeneousSubmodule_eq_sum_inv_reverse_charpoly`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Molien.html#Representation.mk_finrank_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_inf_homogeneousSubmodule_eq_sum_inv_reverse_charpoly)).
- **Noether's finiteness theorem** — over a Noetherian base, the invariants of a finite group acting on a finite-type algebra form a finite-type algebra, and the algebra is a finite module over them ([`FixedPoints.finiteType_subalgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Invariant/FiniteType.html#TauCeti.FixedPoints.finiteType_subalgebra)).
- **Noether's degree bound** — over a ℚ-algebra, the invariants are generated by orbit sums of monomials of total degree at most `|G|` ([`FixedPoints.subalgebra_eq_adjoin_sum_smul_prod_pow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Invariant/FiniteType.html#TauCeti.FixedPoints.subalgebra_eq_adjoin_sum_smul_prod_pow)).
- **Orbit separation** — two vectors agree on every invariant polynomial exactly when they lie in the same orbit. As a consequence, the only point where every positive-degree homogeneous invariant vanishes is the origin ([`forall_mem_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_lift_eval_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/OrbitSeparation.html#Representation.forall_mem_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_lift_eval_eq_iff), [`forall_homogeneous_mem_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_lift_eval_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/OrbitSeparation.html#Representation.forall_homogeneous_mem_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_lift_eval_eq_zero_iff)).
- **The radical of the Hilbert ideal** — over a reduced base, the ideal `A₊·S` has the irrelevant ideal as its radical, so the coinvariant algebra `S/A₊S` is a finite module ([`radical_symmetricAlgebraHilbertIdeal_of_isReduced`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/HilbertIdeal.html#Representation.radical_symmetricAlgebraHilbertIdeal_of_isReduced), [`moduleFinite_quotient_symmetricAlgebraHilbertIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/HilbertIdeal.html#Representation.moduleFinite_quotient_symmetricAlgebraHilbertIdeal)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [invariant subalgebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Basic.html#Representation.symmetricAlgebraInvariants) of `Sym M` is defined as Mathlib's fixed subalgebra and shown to be graded, with `Sym M` integral over it, so Mathlib's `Algebra.IsInvariant` API applies to it directly.
- The [Reynolds operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Reynolds.html#Representation.symmetricAlgebraReynolds) is a degree-preserving projection onto invariants that is linear over them. Its trace in each degree is what turns the [graded trace formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/Trace.html#Module.End.mk_trace_homogeneousSubmoduleMapₛₗ_mul_reverse_charpoly_of_pow_eq_one) into Molien's formula.
- The [divided-difference operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/DividedDifference.html#SymmetricAlgebra.dividedDifference) `δ(p) = (p − σp)/ℓ` lowers degree by one, is linear over the invariants, and satisfies `1 − σ = m_ℓ ∘ δ`. This is the operator that the Tor′ argument for Shephard–Todd applies to each pseudoreflection.

## The frontier

- **Tor′ vanishing for reflection groups** — build a graded free `A`-resolution of `k`, push `δ_s` and multiplication by `ℓ` through to `Tor′_1^A(k,S)`, and use Reynolds invariants on the resolution to show the group is zero. The divided-difference operator and the Hilbert ideal are ready for this.
- **Forward Shephard–Todd** — from Tor′ vanishing, deduce that `S` is free over `A`, then prove faithfully flat regularity descent and the vertex cotangent-dimension count. That gives polynomiality on `dim V` generators. The regularity-descent lemma is a separate target the README flags explicitly.
- **Reynolds and scalar extension** — show that the invariant subalgebra and the Reynolds projector commute with base change in the finite-free setting. This is the last named §1 construction, and §3 needs it to move degrees from ℝ to ℂ and from ℚ to ℝ.
- **Converse Shephard–Todd** — the inertia/stabilizer correspondence in codimension one, purity of the branch locus for the residual map `V/H → V/G`, and triviality of connected finite étale covers of affine space. None of these has been started.
- **Coinvariant package for real reflection groups** — the Jacobian as a product of reflecting-hyperplane forms, the harmonic complement, freeness of rank `|W|`, and the identities `∏ dᵢ = |W|` and `Σ dᵢ = r + |Ref(W)|`. All of this waits on polynomiality.
