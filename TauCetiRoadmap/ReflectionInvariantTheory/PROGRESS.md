## ReflectionInvariantTheory: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`89e60de` to `bfcff79`)

The first window of this roadmap built most of its finite-group invariant layer. [Molien's formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Molien.html#Representation.mk_finrank_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_inf_homogeneousSubmodule_eq_sum_inv_reverse_charpoly) now holds in its determinant form: the Hilbert series of `Sym(M)^G` is the group average of `det(1 − t g)⁻¹` (TauCeti#19837). It holds over any field in which `|G|` is invertible, with the dimensions read in that field, so it pins down the series only in characteristic zero. The proof goes through the trace of the [Reynolds operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Reynolds.html#Representation.symmetricAlgebraReynolds) on each degree and a [graded trace formula for finite-order endomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/Trace.html#Module.End.mk_trace_homogeneousSubmoduleMapₛₗ_mul_reverse_charpoly_of_pow_eq_one). [Noether's finiteness theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Invariant/FiniteType.html#TauCeti.FixedPoints.finiteType_subalgebra) landed alongside it, as did [Noether's degree bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Invariant/FiniteType.html#TauCeti.FixedPoints.subalgebra_eq_adjoin_sum_smul_prod_pow), which says that over a ℚ-algebra the invariants are generated by orbit sums of monomials of degree at most `|G|`.

The rest of the window prepared the forward direction of Shephard–Todd. [Invariant polynomials separate orbits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/OrbitSeparation.html#Representation.forall_mem_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_lift_eval_eq_iff), and [the only point where every positive-degree homogeneous invariant vanishes is the origin](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/OrbitSeparation.html#Representation.forall_homogeneous_mem_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_lift_eval_eq_zero_iff). The [Hilbert ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/HilbertIdeal.html#Representation.symmetricAlgebraHilbertIdeal) `A₊·S` has [the irrelevant ideal as its radical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/HilbertIdeal.html#Representation.radical_symmetricAlgebraHilbertIdeal_of_isReduced), which makes the coinvariant algebra finite. The [divided-difference operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/DividedDifference.html#SymmetricAlgebra.dividedDifference) `δ(p) = (p − σp)/ℓ` lowers degree by one, is linear over the invariants, and satisfies [the identity `1 − σ = m_ℓ ∘ δ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricAlgebra/DividedDifference.html#SymmetricAlgebra.mulLeft_comp_dividedDifference).

Neither direction of Shephard–Todd has been proved yet. The Tor′ argument, regularity descent and the polynomiality conclusion are all still missing, and so are the real reflection, Weyl and Coxeter-exponent branches.

## ReflectionInvariantTheory: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-11 (`bfcff79` to `1b19ebb`)

The [Chevalley–Shephard–Todd theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/ShephardTodd.html#Representation.pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top_iff_exists_bijective_aeval_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_dual) landed in both directions: a finite group acting faithfully on a finite-dimensional space over a field of characteristic zero is generated by pseudoreflections exactly when its invariant polynomial functions form a polynomial algebra (TauCeti#29146). The [forward direction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/ShephardTodd.html#Representation.exists_bijective_aeval_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_dual_of_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top) needs only that `|G|` be invertible. It follows the README's homological route: divided differences and Reynolds averaging kill `Tor′`, so `Sym(V*)` is [faithfully flat over the invariants](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Flat.html#Representation.faithfullyFlat_symmetricAlgebra_dual_of_finrank_quotient_eq_one), regularity descends, and a basis of the vertex cotangent space lifts to generators. The converse is proved differently, by comparing degrees with the subgroup generated by pseudoreflections. The residual quotient is shown [unramified in codimension one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Unramified.html#Representation.isUnramifiedAt_quotient_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_of_height_eq_one), but the purity and étale-cover argument the README asks for is not there.

Most of the coinvariant package followed, stated for pseudoreflection groups in general. It includes [Chevalley's theorem that the coinvariant algebra is the regular representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/RegularRepresentation.html#Representation.nonempty_equiv_coinvariantAlgebra_dual_leftRegular_of_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top) and its [graded character](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Coinvariant/Character.html#Representation.mk_character_coinvariantAlgebraHomogeneous_dual_of_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top). The product of the degrees [is `|G|`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/ProductOfDegrees.html#Representation.prod_eq_card_of_bijective_aeval_symmetricAlgebraInvariants), and their sum is the dimension plus [the number of pseudoreflections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/ProductOfDegrees.html#Representation.sum_eq_finrank_add_card_of_bijective_aeval_symmetricAlgebraInvariants_dual). The [Jacobian formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/SymmetricAlgebra/Hyperplane/Jacobian.html#Representation.exists_jacobian_eq_algebraMap_mul_prod_dual_inertia_sub_one) is exact. Freeness, regular sequences, intrinsic degrees and the `S_n` degree examples also landed, along with the missing Reynolds/base-change comparison.

On the Coxeter side, every Coxeter element of type `E₈` [has order 30](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Coxeter/Element/Every.html#TauCeti.Coxeter.orderOf_wordProd_of_hasCartanType_E8) and [eigenvalues `exp(2πi e/30)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Coxeter/Element/Eigen.html#TauCeti.Coxeter.eigenvalues_of_hasCartanType_E8) for the eight exponents, and the same holds for `E₆`, `E₇`, `F₄` and `G₂`. All of these are explicit characteristic-polynomial computations. For `S_n`, the Coxeter eigenvalues are [read off from the invariant degrees](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Symmetric/CoxeterElement.html#TauCeti.roots_charpoly_standardRepresentation_wordProd_permCoxeterSystem_eq_map_degrees). Coxeter elements of a Weyl group are [conjugate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Coxeter/Element/Conj.html#RootPairing.isConj_wordProd_of_perm). Still missing are the uniform degree–exponent theorem, the root-height partition, the classical types `B`–`D` and the coprime-exponent theorem.
