### Named results

- **The finite triangle groups** — `Δ(a, b, c)` with sorted signature is finite exactly when the signature is a spherical row, meaning `(1, m, m)`, `(2, 2, m)`, `(2, 3, 3)`, `(2, 3, 4)` or `(2, 3, 5)` ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.finite_iff_isSphericalParameterSignature`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Signatures.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.finite_iff_isSphericalParameterSignature)). The last three are `A₄`, `S₄` and `A₅` ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.equivIcosahedral`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Polyhedral.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.equivIcosahedral)).
- **Regular triples are normal subgroups** — for `n ≠ 0`, isomorphism classes of regular degree-`n` triples with orders dividing `(a, b, c)` correspond to the normal subgroups of index `n` in `Δ(a, b, c)` ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.regularIsoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Regular.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.regularIsoClassEquiv)), and the automorphism group is the quotient.
- **The passport-size formula** — for a transitive monodromy group `G`, `passportSize P = genCountType · |C_{Sₙ}(G)| / |N_{Sₙ}(G)|`, where the right-hand side divides exactly ([`TauCeti.PassportSpec.passportSize_eq_genCountType_mul_card_centralizer_div_card_normalizer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Passport/GeneratingCount.html#TauCeti.PassportSpec.passportSize_eq_genCountType_mul_card_centralizer_div_card_normalizer)). Here `genCountType` sums generating counts over the `G`-classes each cycle type meets, and the product-one counts come from the Frobenius character formula ([`TauCeti.card_productOneTriples_eq_sum_characterTable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/ProductOne.html#TauCeti.card_productOneTriples_eq_sum_characterTable)).
- **Dessins and triples classify each other** — isomorphism classes of bipartite ribbon graphs with `n` edges correspond to those of degree-`n` permutation triples, with matching automorphism groups ([`TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/RibbonGraph/Classification.html#TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv)).
- **Covers are determined by their triples** — a connected degree-`n` cover of `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}` is determined up to isomorphism by the class of its monodromy triple ([`TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClass_injective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Classification.html#TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClass_injective)). Surjectivity is open.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The monodromy triple of a cover** ([`IsCoveringMap.monodromyTriple`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/MonodromyTriple.html#IsCoveringMap.monodromyTriple)) — the monodromy around `0`, `1` and `∞` of a cover with a numbered fibre. It is connected exactly when the total space is, and Layer 6 classifies covers by it.
- **Coset triples** ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.cosetTriple`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/PermutationRepresentation.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.cosetTriple)) — the triple of the action of `Δ(a, b, c)` on the cosets of a subgroup. Every connected triple is the coset triple of a sheet stabiliser.
- **Certified coset tables** ([`TauCeti.CosetTable.index_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Coset/Table.html#TauCeti.CosetTable.index_le)) — a table of words whose certificate checks bounds a subgroup's index. This supplies upper bounds on group orders from a presentation, which representations alone cannot give.
