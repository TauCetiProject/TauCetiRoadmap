### Named results

- **Covers are determined by their triples** — a connected degree-`n` cover of `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}` is determined up to isomorphism by the isomorphism class of its monodromy triple ([`TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClass_injective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Classification.html#TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClass_injective)), and likewise with a numbered fibre or a marked point; surjectivity is open.
- **Dessins and triples classify each other** — isomorphism classes of bipartite ribbon graphs with `n` edges correspond to isomorphism classes of degree-`n` permutation triples ([`TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/RibbonGraph/Classification.html#TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv)), with automorphism groups matching ([`TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.autEquivAutomorphismGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/RibbonGraph/Classification.html#TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.autEquivAutomorphismGroup)).
- **The Frobenius product-one formula** — in a finite group `G`, the number of `(x, y, z) ∈ C₀ × C₁ × C∞` with `z y x = 1` is `(|C₀| |C₁| |C∞| / |G|) Σ_χ χ(C₀) χ(C₁) χ(C∞) / χ(1)` ([`TauCeti.card_productOneTriples_eq_sum_characterTable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/ProductOne.html#TauCeti.card_productOneTriples_eq_sum_characterTable)), with the roadmap's `S₃` checks.
- **Non-spherical triangle groups are infinite** — `Δ(a, b, c)` is infinite whenever `1/a + 1/b + 1/c ≤ 1` ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.infinite_of_inv_add_inv_add_inv_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.infinite_of_inv_add_inv_add_inv_le_one)): the commutator of the generators has infinite order, acting as a translation of `ℂ` or as a hyperbolic element of `PSL(2, ℝ)`.
- **The combinatorial Euler bound** — a connected triple satisfies `χ = c(σ0) + c(σ1) + c(σinf) − n ≤ 2`, where `c` counts cycles ([`TauCeti.PermutationTriple.IsConnected.eulerChar_le_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.PermutationTriple.IsConnected.eulerChar_le_two)), proved by induction along transpositions rather than through a surface.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The monodromy triple of a cover** ([`IsCoveringMap.monodromyTriple`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/MonodromyTriple.html#IsCoveringMap.monodromyTriple)) — the monodromy around `0`, `1` and `∞` of a cover of `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}` with numbered fibre over `1/2`. It is connected exactly when the total space is path connected, and Layer 6 classifies covers by it.
- **Passports** ([`TauCeti.PassportSpec`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Passport/Basic.html#TauCeti.PassportSpec)) — ordered cycle data with a reference monodromy group up to conjugacy. For a transitive reference group, the passport size is the number of normalizer orbits on generating triples ([`TauCeti.PassportSpec.passportSize_eq_card_generatingTripleOrbits`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Passport/Normalizer.html#TauCeti.PassportSpec.passportSize_eq_card_generatingTripleOrbits)), where Layer 3's counts will enter.
- **Triples as representations of triangle groups** ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.permutationTripleEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/PermutationRepresentation.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.permutationTripleEquiv)) — homomorphisms `Δ(a, b, c) →* Perm (Fin n)` are exactly the degree-`n` triples whose component orders divide `(a, b, c)`, with relabeling as conjugation and connectedness as transitivity.
