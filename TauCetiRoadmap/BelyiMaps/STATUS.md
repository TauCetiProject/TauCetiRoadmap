### Named results

- **Covers are classified by their triples** — connected degree-`n` covers of `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}` up to isomorphism correspond bijectively to isomorphism classes of connected degree-`n` triples ([`TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Classification.html#TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClassEquiv)). Numbered covers correspond to triples ([`TauCeti.ConnectedFiberNumberedCoverClass.tripleEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Classification.html#TauCeti.ConnectedFiberNumberedCoverClass.tripleEquiv)), and pointed covers to triples with a marked label. Pulling back along the anharmonic maps is the branch-point action on triples.
- **Deck groups and regular covers** — the deck group of a numbered cover is the automorphism group of its triple, acting on the labelled fibre ([`TauCeti.ConnectedFiberNumberedCover.deckMulEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Deck.html#TauCeti.ConnectedFiberNumberedCover.deckMulEquiv)). A cover is regular exactly when its deck group has order the degree ([`TauCeti.ConnectedFiberNumberedCover.isRegular_iff_card_deck`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Regular.html#TauCeti.ConnectedFiberNumberedCover.isRegular_iff_card_deck)), and regular covers then match the normal subgroups of triangle groups.
- **The finite triangle groups** — `Δ(a, b, c)` with sorted signature is finite exactly when the signature is `(1, m, m)`, `(2, 2, m)`, `(2, 3, 3)`, `(2, 3, 4)` or `(2, 3, 5)` ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.finite_iff_isSphericalParameterSignature`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Signatures.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.finite_iff_isSphericalParameterSignature)). The last three are `A₄`, `S₄` and `A₅`.
- **Dessins and triples classify each other** — isomorphism classes of bipartite ribbon graphs with `n` edges correspond to those of degree-`n` triples, with matching automorphism groups ([`TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/RibbonGraph/Classification.html#TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv)). The correspondence restricts to connected classes and respects the branch-point exchanges.
