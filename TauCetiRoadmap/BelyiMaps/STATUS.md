### Named results

- **Analytic Riemann existence for three-point covers** — isomorphism classes of analytic Belyi pairs of degree `n` correspond bijectively to isomorphism classes of connected degree-`n` covers of `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}`, by restricting the Belyi map and filling the punctures ([`TauCeti.AnalyticBelyiPair.isoClassEquivConnectedCoverClass`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/FilledCover/AnalyticBelyiPair.html#TauCeti.AnalyticBelyiPair.isoClassEquivConnectedCoverClass)). Forgetting the complex structure is a bijection onto topological branched covers ([`TauCeti.AnalyticBelyiPair.toTopBranchedCoverIsoClass_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/FilledCover/AnalyticBelyiPair.html#TauCeti.AnalyticBelyiPair.toTopBranchedCoverIsoClass_bijective)).
- **Covers are classified by their triples** — connected degree-`n` covers of `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}` up to isomorphism correspond bijectively to isomorphism classes of connected degree-`n` triples, and likewise for numbered and pointed covers ([`TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Classification.html#TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClassEquiv)). It rests on the freeness of `π₁(ℂ ∖ {0, 1})` on the peripheral loops around `0` and `1` ([`TauCeti.ThricePuncturedSphere.fundamentalGroupMulEquivFreeGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.ThricePuncturedSphere.fundamentalGroupMulEquivFreeGroup)).
- **Uniqueness of the compactification** — a topological branched cover is determined up to isomorphism by its unbranched part, and every connected finite cover extends to one ([`TauCeti.TopBranchedCover.IsoClass.restrict_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/FilledCover/TopBranchedCover.html#TauCeti.TopBranchedCover.IsoClass.restrict_bijective)).
- **The finite triangle groups** — `Δ(a, b, c)` with sorted signature is finite exactly when the signature is `(1, m, m)`, `(2, 2, m)`, `(2, 3, 3)`, `(2, 3, 4)` or `(2, 3, 5)` ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.finite_iff_isSphericalParameterSignature`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Signatures.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.finite_iff_isSphericalParameterSignature)).
- **The complete table through degree four** — there are 1, 3, 7 and 26 connected classes in degrees one to four ([`TauCeti.ConnectedIsoClass.card_four`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/SmallDegrees.html#TauCeti.ConnectedIsoClass.card_four)). The thirteen frozen LMFDB records of these degrees are pairwise distinct, and their unordered cycle data are exactly the branch-point orbits of the table ([`TauCeti.PermutationTriple.LMFDB.unorderedCycleData_eq_classification`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/LMFDB/SmallDegrees.html#TauCeti.PermutationTriple.LMFDB.unorderedCycleData_eq_classification)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The filled cover** ([`TauCeti.FilledCover`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/FilledCover/Basic.html#TauCeti.FilledCover)) — a cover of `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}` with one point added for each cycle of `σ0`, `σ1` and `σinf`. It is compact and Hausdorff, and it is a [complex manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/FilledCover/ChartedSpace.html#TauCeti.FilledCover.isManifold) on which the extended projection is holomorphic.
- **Topological branched covers** ([`TauCeti.TopBranchedCover`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/TopBranchedCover.html#TauCeti.TopBranchedCover)) — compact surfaces over the sphere that are locally power maps over `0`, `1` and `∞`. Their local degrees reproduce the cycle types of the triple, and they satisfy the [Riemann–Hurwitz formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/FilledCover/Ramification.html#TauCeti.FilledCover.two_mul_genus_sub_two) with the combinatorial genus.
