## BelyiMaps: 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-26 (`6efdf55` to `759eb3e`)

Connected finite covers of the thrice-punctured sphere `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}` are now determined up to isomorphism by the isomorphism class of their monodromy triple ([`TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClass_injective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Classification.html#TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClass_injective)), and the Frobenius product-one formula counts triples in prescribed conjugacy classes with product one by characters ([`TauCeti.card_productOneTriples_eq_sum_characterTable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/ProductOne.html#TauCeti.card_productOneTriples_eq_sum_characterTable)). The cover statement also holds with a numbered fibre or a marked point. Its converse, that every connected triple comes from a cover, waits on the freeness of `π₁(ℂ ∖ {0, 1})`, of which only generation by the two peripheral loops is proved. Bipartite ribbon graphs and permutation triples classify each other, with matching automorphism groups ([`TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/RibbonGraph/Classification.html#TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv)).

Triangle groups have their presentation, universal property, and the dictionary between triples of bounded order and permutation representations. The infinite half of the trichotomy is proved: `Δ(a, b, c)` is infinite whenever `1/a + 1/b + 1/c ≤ 1` ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.infinite_of_inv_add_inv_add_inv_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.infinite_of_inv_add_inv_add_inv_le_one)), through an affine representation in the Euclidean case and one into `PSL(2, ℝ)`, using the Fricke trace identity, in the hyperbolic case. Of the finite spherical groups only the cyclic and dihedral families are identified; `A₄`, `S₄` and `A₅` remain.

Underneath, permutation triples gained cycle data, the transposition step lemma, a combinatorial proof that connected triples have Euler characteristic at most two (hence a genus), orbit decomposition, block quotients, regular triples, and the `S₃`-action on the branch points. Passports have their specification, class sets, normalizer formulation and label semantics through degree five, and connected triples up to isomorphism number `1, 3, 7` in degrees one to three by kernel computation ([`TauCeti.ConnectedIsoClass.card_three`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Enumeration.html#TauCeti.ConnectedIsoClass.card_three)). The thrice-punctured sphere has its two-set cover, peripheral loops and anharmonic homeomorphisms, and `periphInf` is identified with the loop around `∞`. The analytic, algebraic and arithmetic layers have not started.

## BelyiMaps: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

The finite triangle groups are classified: a sorted signature gives a finite `Δ(a, b, c)` exactly when it is a row of the spherical table, meaning the cyclic and dihedral families together with `(2, 3, 3)`, `(2, 3, 4)` and `(2, 3, 5)` ([`finite_iff_isSphericalParameterSignature`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Signatures.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.finite_iff_isSphericalParameterSignature)). The three polyhedral groups are identified with `A₄`, `S₄` and `A₅`, of orders 12, 24 and 60 ([`equivIcosahedral`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Polyhedral.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.equivIcosahedral)). Explicit permutation representations give the lower bounds, and certified coset tables give the upper ones: a table on `k` points whose certificate checks bounds the subgroup's index by `k` ([`CosetTable.index_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Coset/Table.html#TauCeti.CosetTable.index_le)). Regular triples of degree `n` whose component orders divide `(a, b, c)` now correspond to the normal subgroups of index `n` in `Δ(a, b, c)` ([`regularIsoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Regular.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.regularIsoClassEquiv)). Along the way, every connected triple is shown to be the coset triple of a sheet stabiliser. With these results the triangle-group layer is complete.

Passport sizes now have the roadmap's exact formula, `passportSize P = genCountType · |C(G)| / |N(G)|` with centralizer and normalizer taken in `Sₙ`, together with the divisibility that makes it an equation of natural numbers ([`passportSize_eq_genCountType_mul_card_centralizer_div_card_normalizer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Passport/GeneratingCount.html#TauCeti.PassportSpec.passportSize_eq_genCountType_mul_card_centralizer_div_card_normalizer)). The first passport with more than one class has also landed: in degree five, monodromy `C₅` with partitions `([5], [5], [5])` has three classes ([`passportSize_cyclicTotallyRamified_five`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Passport/Cyclic.html#TauCeti.PassportSpec.passportSize_cyclicTotallyRamified_five)), a case of `p − 2` classes for every prime `p`. Primitivity is decidable by a finite block test that is proved sound ([`isPrimitiveBool_eq_true_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Primitivity/Basic.html#TauCeti.PermutationTriple.isPrimitiveBool_eq_true_iff)).

The rest of the window went into ribbon-graph infrastructure: composition of morphisms, the automorphism group, faces and components carried along isomorphisms, and the graph's Euler characteristic. It also showed that the graph of a triple is connected exactly when the triple is. The complete tables in degree at most four are still missing.
