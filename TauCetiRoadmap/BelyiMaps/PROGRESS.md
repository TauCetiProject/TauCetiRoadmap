## BelyiMaps: 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-26 (`6efdf55` to `759eb3e`)

Connected finite covers of the thrice-punctured sphere `ℂ ∖ {0, 1}` are now determined up to isomorphism by the isomorphism class of their monodromy triple ([`TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClass_injective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ThricePuncturedSphere/Classification.html#TauCeti.ConnectedCoverClass.isoClass_injective)), and the Frobenius product-one formula counts triples in prescribed conjugacy classes with product one by characters ([`TauCeti.card_productOneTriples_eq_sum_characterTable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/ProductOne.html#TauCeti.card_productOneTriples_eq_sum_characterTable)). The cover statement also holds with a numbered fibre or a marked point. Its converse, that every connected triple comes from a cover, waits on the freeness of `π₁(ℂ ∖ {0, 1})`, of which only generation by the two peripheral loops is proved. Bipartite ribbon graphs and permutation triples classify each other, with matching automorphism groups ([`TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/RibbonGraph/Classification.html#TauCeti.BipartiteRibbonGraph.isoClassEquiv)).

Triangle groups have their presentation, universal property, and the dictionary between triples of bounded order and permutation representations. The infinite half of the trichotomy is proved: `Δ(a, b, c)` is infinite whenever `1/a + 1/b + 1/c ≤ 1` ([`TauCeti.TriangleGroup.infinite_of_inv_add_inv_add_inv_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/TriangleGroup/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.TriangleGroup.infinite_of_inv_add_inv_add_inv_le_one)), through an affine representation in the Euclidean case and one into `PSL(2, ℝ)`, using the Fricke trace identity, in the hyperbolic case. Of the finite spherical groups only the cyclic and dihedral families are identified; `A₄`, `S₄` and `A₅` remain.

Underneath, permutation triples gained cycle data, the transposition step lemma, a combinatorial proof that connected triples have Euler characteristic at most two (hence a genus), orbit decomposition, block quotients, regular triples, and the `S₃`-action on the branch points. Passports have their specification, class sets, normalizer formulation and label semantics through degree five, and connected triples up to isomorphism number `1, 3, 7` in degrees one to three by kernel computation ([`TauCeti.ConnectedIsoClass.card_three`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Enumeration.html#TauCeti.ConnectedIsoClass.card_three)). The thrice-punctured sphere has its two-set cover, peripheral loops and anharmonic homeomorphisms, and `periphInf` is identified with the loop around `∞`. The analytic, algebraic and arithmetic layers have not started.
