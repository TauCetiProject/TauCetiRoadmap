## FiniteSamplingDiscrepancy: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`4c6a9bc` to `e06d672`)

The headline of this window is that the exact collision bound is sharp. When a population has distinct values and the sample is no larger than the population, the largest gap between sampling without and with replacement is exactly the collision probability `1 - (n)_m / n^m` ([`iSup_sampleWithoutReplacement_sub_sampleWithReplacement_dirac_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Distinct.html#TauCeti.Probability.iSup_sampleWithoutReplacement_sub_sampleWithReplacement_dirac_eq)), and Mathlib's total variation distance between the two laws is twice that ([`etvDist_sampleWithoutReplacement_sampleWithReplacement_dirac_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Distinct.html#TauCeti.Probability.etvDist_sampleWithoutReplacement_sampleWithReplacement_dirac_eq)). The event that attains it is "all sampled values are distinct": it has probability one without replacement ([`sampleWithoutReplacement_dirac_injective_apply_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Distinct.html#TauCeti.Probability.sampleWithoutReplacement_dirac_injective_apply_eq_one)) and probability `(n)_m / n^m` with replacement ([`sampleWithReplacement_dirac_injective_apply_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Distinct.html#TauCeti.Probability.sampleWithReplacement_dirac_injective_apply_eq)). The value space is arbitrary, but the all-distinct event has to be assumed measurable.

The rest of the window reorganised the sampling constructions into a population-sampling library and filled in the deterministic case: sampling a fixed population is a pushforward of the selection law ([`samplePopulation_dirac`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.samplePopulation_dirac)), with versions without replacement (uniform injective selections) and with replacement (all selections). A finiteness instance and measurability lemmas round this out (TauCeti#34311, TauCeti#33068). Nothing yet treats populations with repeated values, the subject of the roadmap's later layers.
