## FiniteSamplingDiscrepancy: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`4c6a9bc` to `e06d672`)

The headline of this window is that the exact collision bound is sharp. When a population has distinct values and the sample is no larger than the population, the largest gap between sampling without and with replacement is exactly the collision probability `1 - (n)_m / n^m` ([`iSup_sampleWithoutReplacement_sub_sampleWithReplacement_dirac_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Distinct.html#TauCeti.Probability.iSup_sampleWithoutReplacement_sub_sampleWithReplacement_dirac_eq)), and Mathlib's total variation distance between the two laws is twice that ([`etvDist_sampleWithoutReplacement_sampleWithReplacement_dirac_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Distinct.html#TauCeti.Probability.etvDist_sampleWithoutReplacement_sampleWithReplacement_dirac_eq)). The event that attains it is "all sampled values are distinct": it has probability one without replacement ([`sampleWithoutReplacement_dirac_injective_apply_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Distinct.html#TauCeti.Probability.sampleWithoutReplacement_dirac_injective_apply_eq_one)) and probability `(n)_m / n^m` with replacement ([`sampleWithReplacement_dirac_injective_apply_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Distinct.html#TauCeti.Probability.sampleWithReplacement_dirac_injective_apply_eq)). The value space is arbitrary, but the all-distinct event has to be assumed measurable.

The rest of the window reorganised the sampling constructions into a population-sampling library and filled in the deterministic case: sampling a fixed population is a pushforward of the selection law ([`samplePopulation_dirac`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.samplePopulation_dirac)), with versions without replacement (uniform injective selections) and with replacement (all selections). A finiteness instance and measurability lemmas round this out (TauCeti#34311, TauCeti#33068). Nothing yet treats populations with repeated values, the subject of the roadmap's later layers.

## FiniteSamplingDiscrepancy: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`e06d672` to `b235000`)

This window began the repeated-value half of the roadmap by working out the combinatorics of the one-pair population, the population that sends one index `b` to another index `a` and fixes the rest. The two-hit mass ([`uniformOn_injective_mem_range_and_mem_range`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/UniformSampling.html#TauCeti.Probability.uniformOn_injective_mem_range_and_mem_range)) says that a uniform injective sample of size `m` from `n` indices contains two given indices with probability `m(m-1)/(n(n-1))`, the quantity `t` in the roadmap's one-pair formula. The fibre counts come with it (TauCeti#34138). A word of the one-pair population is produced by `2^r` index maps, where `r` counts the positions landing on `a` or `b` ([`natCard_update_id_comp_eq_update_id_comp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/OnePair.html#TauCeti.Probability.natCard_update_id_comp_eq_update_id_comp)), but by only one or two embeddings: `f` itself, and `f` composed with the swap of `a` and `b` ([`natCard_embedding_update_id_comp_eq_update_id_comp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/OnePair.html#TauCeti.Probability.natCard_embedding_update_id_comp_eq_update_id_comp)). The one-pair formula itself, the exact excess `1 - p - t·min(p, 1-p)`, is not proved yet. It still needs the singleton masses of the two sampling laws and the finite-type excess sum.

Observing population values through a measurable map now commutes with sampling, both along an arbitrary selection law ([`samplePopulation_map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.samplePopulation_map)) and for sampling without and with replacement ([`sampleWithoutReplacement_map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Finite.html#TauCeti.Probability.sampleWithoutReplacement_map), [`sampleWithReplacement_map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling/Finite.html#TauCeti.Probability.sampleWithReplacement_map)). This is the step that will carry the one-pair bound over to every population with a repeated value. The rest of the window was supporting work on the collision lemmas for uniform sampling. It added an equivalence between functions that agree at two given points and functions on the remaining points, which is used to count such functions.
