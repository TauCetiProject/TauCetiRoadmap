## FreeProbability: 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-21 (`454bc91` to `273ea28`)

The roadmap's first window builds the algebraic core of free independence. [Freeness of unital subalgebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent) is defined by the vanishing of every centred alternating word, and its basic structural theorem is in: [a normalised functional that makes a family free is determined on the algebra the family generates by its restrictions to the members](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.eqOn_iSup). The proof shows that [the generated algebra is spanned by 1 and the centred alternating words](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.toSubmodule_iSup_eq_span_insert_one_centeredAlternatingProds), and the same expansion gives [freeness of the algebras obtained by grouping members along disjoint label sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.iSup_fiber) (TauCeti#18651). Around this sit [freeness of elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Variables.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependentElements) with a [criterion by centred polynomial words](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Variables.html#TauCeti.isFreelyIndependentElements_iff_polynomial), the mixed moments of orders two to four, and a clean separation from classical independence: [if two free variables commute, the product of their variances is zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.sub_mul_sub_eq_zero_of_commute). The [joint algebraic distribution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Distribution.html#TauCeti.jointDistribution), as φ composed with evaluation on the free algebra, comes with relabelling, polynomial substitution and determination by word moments.

On the combinatorial side, [noncrossing partitions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Basic.html#Finpartition.IsNoncrossing) are now defined on Mathlib's `Finpartition` and form a [lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Basic.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition.instLattice) with a [locally finite order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Basic.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition.instLocallyFiniteOrder), so the incidence algebra's Möbius function is available on NC(n). Still missing: Catalan enumeration, the Kreweras complement and free cumulants. Everything so far is algebraic. There are no *-distributions, positivity, GNS realisations or scalar laws yet.

## FreeProbability: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

Free cumulants have arrived, with the moment–cumulant formula in both directions. The [free cumulant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Basic.html#TauCeti.freeCumulant) of a word is defined by Möbius inversion of block moments on the noncrossing-partition lattice, so that [a moment is the sum over NC(n) of products of cumulants along the blocks](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Basic.html#TauCeti.apply_prod_ofFn_eq_sum_prod_freeCumulant) and, conversely, [κ_n is the Möbius-weighted sum of block moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Basic.html#TauCeti.freeCumulant_eq_sum_mu_mul_partitionMoment) (TauCeti#19384). [Multiplicativity over blocks](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Basic.html#TauCeti.partitionCumulant_eq_prod_freeCumulant) comes from [factoring each lower interval of NC(s) as a product of smaller NC lattices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Interval.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition.iicOrderIso). The [formula for cumulants with products as arguments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Product.html#TauCeti.freeCumulant_fiberProd_eq_sum_prod) sums over the noncrossing π whose join with the grouping partition is the top. Cumulants are bundled as multilinear maps and [vanish when an entry is 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Basic.html#TauCeti.freeCumulant_eq_zero_of_apply_eq_one). The Catalan counts are in: [NC(n) has catalan n elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Catalan.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition.card_fin_eq_catalan), as do [the noncrossing pairings of 2n points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Catalan.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition.card_isPairing_fin_eq_catalan).

F1 gained its first analytic piece. The [law of a selfadjoint element](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Law.html#PositiveLinearMap.law) for a positive functional on a C*-algebra is the measure on its spectrum with [∫ f dμ_a = φ(f(a))](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Law.html#PositiveLinearMap.ofReal_integral_law), compatible with affine maps and functional calculus. Laws are [determined by moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Law.html#PositiveLinearMap.law_eq_law_iff_forall_apply_pow), and [every compactly supported law is realised in C(K)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Law.html#PositiveLinearMap.law_integralPositiveLinearMap_comap_restrict_id). The [method of moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Moments/WeakConvergence.html#MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure.tendsto_of_tendsto_integral_pow) gives weak convergence to a compactly supported limit without a common support bound on the approximants. Smaller work transports freeness along equal joint distributions.

Not yet here: the Kreweras complement, the Möbius values on NC(n), the criterion that freeness means vanishing mixed cumulants, and the comparison of μ_a with a GNS spectral measure.
