## FreeProbability: 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-21 (`454bc91` to `273ea28`)

The roadmap's first window builds the algebraic core of free independence. [Freeness of unital subalgebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent) is defined by the vanishing of every centred alternating word, and its basic structural theorem is in: [a normalised functional that makes a family free is determined on the algebra the family generates by its restrictions to the members](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.eqOn_iSup). The proof shows that [the generated algebra is spanned by 1 and the centred alternating words](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.toSubmodule_iSup_eq_span_insert_one_centeredAlternatingProds), and the same expansion gives [freeness of the algebras obtained by grouping members along disjoint label sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.iSup_fiber) (TauCeti#18651). Around this sit [freeness of elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Variables.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependentElements) with a [criterion by centred polynomial words](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Variables.html#TauCeti.isFreelyIndependentElements_iff_polynomial), the mixed moments of orders two to four, and a clean separation from classical independence: [if two free variables commute, the product of their variances is zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.sub_mul_sub_eq_zero_of_commute). The [joint algebraic distribution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Distribution.html#TauCeti.jointDistribution), as φ composed with evaluation on the free algebra, comes with relabelling, polynomial substitution and determination by word moments.

On the combinatorial side, [noncrossing partitions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Basic.html#Finpartition.IsNoncrossing) are now defined on Mathlib's `Finpartition` and form a [lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Basic.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition.instLattice) with a [locally finite order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Basic.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition.instLocallyFiniteOrder), so the incidence algebra's Möbius function is available on NC(n). Still missing: Catalan enumeration, the Kreweras complement and free cumulants. Everything so far is algebraic. There are no *-distributions, positivity, GNS realisations or scalar laws yet.
