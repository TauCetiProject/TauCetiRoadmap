## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Work has begun on the algebraic part of F1, free independence for linear functionals on unital algebras, and on the opening definitions of F2, the noncrossing partition lattice. Both layers are partial. No free cumulants, positivity or operator-algebraic constructions exist yet, and F3–F8 are untouched.

### Named results

- **Freeness determines the joint law** — two normalised functionals that make a family free and agree on each member agree on the whole generated algebra ([`IsFreelyIndependent.eqOn_iSup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.eqOn_iSup)). For elements, this says one-variable moments determine the functional ([`IsFreelyIndependentElements.eqOn_adjoin_range`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Variables.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependentElements.eqOn_adjoin_range)).
- **The centred-word decomposition** — for a normalised functional, the generated algebra is spanned by 1 and the values of centred alternating words ([`toSubmodule_iSup_eq_span_insert_one_centeredAlternatingProds`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.toSubmodule_iSup_eq_span_insert_one_centeredAlternatingProds)). This is the multilinear reduction that the other structural results rest on.
- **Grouping of free families** — the algebras generated by members over disjoint sets of labels are again free ([`IsFreelyIndependent.iSup_fiber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.iSup_fiber)).
- **Freeness is not classical independence** — if two free variables commute, the product of their variances vanishes, so over ℂ commuting variables of nonzero variance are never free ([`IsFreelyIndependent.sub_mul_sub_eq_zero_of_commute`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.sub_mul_sub_eq_zero_of_commute)). It is derived from the computed alternating fourth moment ([`IsFreelyIndependent.apply_mul_mul_mul_of_ne`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.apply_mul_mul_mul_of_ne)).
- **The polynomial-word criterion** — elements are free exactly when every nonempty centred alternating word of polynomials in them has expectation zero ([`isFreelyIndependentElements_iff_polynomial`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Variables.html#TauCeti.isFreelyIndependentElements_iff_polynomial)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`IsFreelyIndependent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Independence.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent) states freeness of subalgebras for an arbitrary linear functional over a commutative semiring. Normalisation is a separate hypothesis, and positivity and traciality are not assumed, matching the README's insistence that algebraic expectations are not silently states.
- [`jointDistribution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Distribution.html#TauCeti.jointDistribution) is φ composed with evaluation on the free algebra, as the README prescribes. It is determined by word moments and is compatible with relabelling, subfamilies, homomorphic transport and polynomial substitution.
- [`NoncrossingPartition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Basic.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition) is NC(s) as a bounded lattice of `Finpartition`s with a [locally finite order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/Basic.html#TauCeti.NoncrossingPartition.instLocallyFiniteOrder). This makes `IncidenceAlgebra.mu` available for the Möbius inversion that defines free cumulants.

### Roadmap coverage

**F1** is partial. The algebraic free-independence theory is largely in place: the definition, subfamilies, monotonicity, transport, grouping, uniqueness of the joint law, first mixed moments and the commuting-variable obstruction. Joint algebraic distributions have their basic API. Still missing are the *-polynomial synonym and *-distributions, GNS realisations and boundedness criteria, laws of selfadjoint elements in C*-probability spaces, convergence in distribution, *-freeness, and closure under norm and weak limits. **F2** is partial, with only the noncrossing predicate, restriction, the lattice and its incidence-algebra setup. Catalan enumeration, the Kreweras complement, Möbius values, cumulants, the moment–cumulant formulas and the vanishing-mixed-cumulant criterion have not been started. **F3–F8** are untouched: free products, Fock space, amalgamation, free convolution and transforms, limit theorems, R-diagonal elements and random-matrix asymptotics.

## The frontier

- **Free cumulants and the moment–cumulant formula (F2)** — define block moments and κ_π by Möbius inversion on NC(n), and prove both directions of the moment–cumulant relation. The NC lattice and its incidence algebra are ready. Expanding the lattice API with joins and interval factorisation is the immediate prerequisite.
- **Catalan count and Kreweras complement (F2)** — `card NC(n) = catalan n`, the permutation model and the complement with its block-count identity. These are needed for the multiplicative cumulant formula and later for F8's genus bound.
- **Freeness ⇔ vanishing mixed cumulants (F2)** — this comes after cumulants and uses the centred-word decomposition that has already landed. Additivity of cumulants for free sums follows from it.
- **\*-distributions and positivity (F1)** — the `FreeAlgebra ℂ (ι×Bool)` synonym with its conjugate-linear involution, *-freeness, and the GNS realisation with the bounded-realisation criterion. Nothing here yet touches positivity.
- **Laws of selfadjoint elements (F1)** — the CFC construction of μ_a with `φ(f(a)) = ∫ f dμ_a`, moment determinacy via the existing compact-support determinacy theorem, and moment-to-weak convergence. This is the bridge that F5 and F6 depend on.
