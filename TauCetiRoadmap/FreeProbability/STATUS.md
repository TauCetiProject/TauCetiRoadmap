### Named results

- **The S-transform is multiplicative** — if `a` and `b` are free for a normalised functional and have invertible means, then S_ab = S_a S_b, with no trace and no positivity ([`sSeries_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/S/Free.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.sSeries_mul)).
- **Freeness is the vanishing of mixed cumulants** — unital subalgebras are free for a normalised functional exactly when every free cumulant with labels that are not all equal vanishes ([`isFreelyIndependent_iff_freeCumulant_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Free.html#TauCeti.isFreelyIndependent_iff_freeCumulant_eq_zero)).
- **The free central limit theorem** — normalised sums of centred free variables with bounded moments and converging average variance have semicircular limiting moments. For selfadjoint elements of a C*-algebra, their spectral laws converge weakly ([`tendsto_law_inv_sqrt_smul_sum_semicircleReal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Law/CentralLimit.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependentElements.tendsto_law_inv_sqrt_smul_sum_semicircleReal)).
- **Cumulants of products of free families** — κ_n(a₁b₁, …, aₙbₙ) is the sum over NC(n) of κ_π(a)κ_K(π)(b), with K the Kreweras complement ([`freeCumulant_mul_eq_sum_partitionCumulant_mul_partitionCumulant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Kreweras.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.freeCumulant_mul_eq_sum_partitionCumulant_mul_partitionCumulant)).
- **The bounded GNS realisation criterion** — a positive star-distribution comes from a family in a C*-algebra with generator norms at most Rᵢ exactly when the README's quadratic-form inequalities hold ([`boundedGNS_TFAE`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Distribution/GNS.html#TauCeti.StarPositiveFunctional.boundedGNS_TFAE)). An exponential bound on word moments implies those inequalities.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`freeCumulant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Basic.html#TauCeti.freeCumulant) is defined by Möbius inversion on NC(s), with the moment–cumulant formula in both directions. Semicircular, free Poisson, circular and R-diagonal elements are all characterised through it.
- The [reduced C*-free product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/FreeProduct/Reduced.html#TauCeti.ReducedFreeProduct.algebra) is the norm closure of the GNS representation of the positive [free-product state](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/FreeProduct/Basic.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_freeProductExpectation). Its vacuum state restricts to the factor states and makes the factors free. It supplies free elements with prescribed laws in a C*-probability space.
- [Full Fock space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/FullFockSpace/Defs.html#TauCeti.FullFockSpace) has creation, annihilation and gauge operators with exact norms and the [Cuntz–Toeplitz relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/FullFockSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.FullFockSpace.annihilation_comp_creation). It is the model space for the free Gaussian functor, semicircular systems and the Fock realisation of freely infinitely divisible laws.

## The frontier

- **Faithfulness of the reduced product (§§3.3–3.5)** — identify the GNS space of the reduced product with the reduced-word Hilbert space, compute the kernels of the factor maps, and prove Dykema's theorem and traciality for tracial inputs.

What remains is the complexification of a real Hilbert space, the noncrossing Wick formula for s(h) = ℓ(h) + ℓ(h)*, and freeness for orthogonal vectors, which give the Fock realisations of semicircular laws and systems.
