## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The algebraic and combinatorial foundation is essentially finished: F1 has all its major milestones and F2 lacks only the plane-tree model. F3–F7 are all partial. Their algebraic and formal parts are in place, but Hilbert-space and C*-free products, free convolution of measures and von Neumann algebras are missing. F8 (random matrices) is untouched.

### Named results

- **Freeness is the vanishing of mixed cumulants** — unital subalgebras are free for a normalised functional exactly when every free cumulant whose labels are not all equal vanishes ([`isFreelyIndependent_iff_freeCumulant_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Free.html#TauCeti.isFreelyIndependent_iff_freeCumulant_eq_zero)). Additivity of cumulants and of the R-series over free sums follows.
- **The free central limit theorem** — centred free variables with moments bounded in each degree and converging average variance v have normalised sums whose moments tend to those of the semicircle law of variance v ([`tendsto_apply_inv_sqrt_smul_sum_pow_semicircleReal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/CentralLimit.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependentElements.tendsto_apply_inv_sqrt_smul_sum_pow_semicircleReal)). For selfadjoint elements of a C*-algebra the spectral laws converge weakly to `semicircleReal 0 v` ([`tendsto_law_inv_sqrt_smul_sum_semicircleReal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Law/CentralLimit.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependentElements.tendsto_law_inv_sqrt_smul_sum_semicircleReal)).
- **Cumulants of products of free families** — κ_n(a_1b_1,…,a_nb_n) is the sum over NC(n) of κ_π(a)κ_{K(π)}(b) for the Kreweras complement K, with no trace hypothesis ([`freeCumulant_mul_eq_sum_partitionCumulant_mul_partitionCumulant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Kreweras.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependent.freeCumulant_mul_eq_sum_partitionCumulant_mul_partitionCumulant)). The companion moment formula is also proved.
- **The bounded GNS realisation criterion** — a positive functional on *-polynomials comes from a family in a C*-algebra, with generator norms at most R_i, exactly when the README's quadratic-form inequalities hold ([`boundedGNS_TFAE`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Distribution/GNS.html#TauCeti.StarPositiveFunctional.boundedGNS_TFAE)). Positivity alone [does not suffice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Distribution/GNS.html#TauCeti.exists_starPositiveFunctional_apply_one_not_exists_cstar_starDistribution).
- **The free-product state** — the algebraic free product carries a unique normalised functional that extends the factor functionals and makes the factors free ([`existsUnique_freeProductExpectation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/FreeProduct/Basic.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_freeProductExpectation)). It is tracial for tracial inputs and [positive on squares for positive inputs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/FreeProduct/Positive.html#TauCeti.freeProductExpectation_star_mul_self_nonneg).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`freeCumulant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Basic.html#TauCeti.freeCumulant) is defined by Möbius inversion on NC(s), with [the moment–cumulant formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/Basic.html#TauCeti.apply_prod_ofFn_eq_sum_prod_freeCumulant) in both directions. Semicircular, free Poisson, circular and R-diagonal elements are all characterised through it.
- `PositiveLinearMap.law` is the spectral law μ_a of a selfadjoint element, [the unique measure with ∫ f dμ_a = φ(f(a))](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Law/Basic.html#PositiveLinearMap.eq_law_iff), now [identified with the GNS spectral measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Law/GNS.html#PositiveLinearMap.law_eq_map_gns_diagMeasure). It turns moment statements into weak convergence, and free convolution of measures will be defined through it.
- [`PowerSeries.boxedConvolution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/NoncrossingPartition/BoxedConvolution.html#PowerSeries.boxedConvolution) is the NC/Kreweras coefficient sum. It is associative and commutative, has the zeta and Möbius series as mutual inverses, and computes [the cumulant series of a product of free elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/FreeProbability/Cumulant/BoxedConvolution/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsFreelyIndependentElements.freeCumulantSeries_mul).

### Roadmap coverage

**F1** is essentially done: *-polynomials on the prescribed synonym, *-distributions, *-freeness, bounded GNS realisations, convergence in (*-)distribution, and closure of freeness under norm and weak-operator limits. **F2** is done apart from the plane-tree correspondence. **F3** is partial: only the algebraic free product and its state exist. Hilbert, C* and von Neumann products, Fock space and the Gaussian functor are untouched. **F4** is partial: C*-conditional expectations are defined, with contractivity, the Schwarz inequality and positive B-valued Gram forms, but there is no amalgamated freeness or product. **F5** is partial and purely formal: the R-series with its functional equation and convergence on a disk, word series, the joint R-series, boxed convolution and an S-series. It lacks μ⊞ν, μ⊠ν, the analytic transforms and S-multiplicativity. **F6** is partial. Proved: semicircular laws and systems, the free CLT, the free law of large numbers, and free Poisson elements with their limit theorem in moments. Missing: the free Poisson density, compound laws, infinite divisibility and the Gaussian von Neumann algebra. **F7** is partial. Proved: the compression formula κ^{pAp}_n = t^(n−1)κ_n, circular elements and the basic R-diagonal theory. Missing: Haar unitaries, matrix-unit compressions and the polar-decomposition results. **F8** is untouched.

## The frontier

- **Reduced free products on Hilbert space (F3)** — the positive free-product state now exists algebraically. What remains is its GNS completion as the reduced C*-product, faithfulness, and the full product. This unblocks the analytic layers.
- **Free additive convolution of measures (F5)** — define μ⊞ν through a bounded free realisation and the existing spectral law. Then compare the formal R-series with the analytic R-transform built from the resolvent transform. This waits on the F3 reduced product.
- **S-multiplicativity and μ⊠ν (F5)** — boxed convolution already computes cumulants of free products. What is missing is the boxed-convolution/S-series group isomorphism giving S_ab = S_aS_b, then the multiplicative convolution of positive laws.
- **Measure-level free Poisson and infinite divisibility (F6)** — identify the free Poisson moments with the explicit density and upgrade the Poisson limit to weak convergence. Then compound laws and the free Lévy–Khintchine criterion.
- **Gaussian matrix models (F8)** — the F2 inputs (the Kreweras permutation formula and the geodesic characterisation) are ready. The GUE genus expansion and expected-moment asymptotic freeness have not been started.
