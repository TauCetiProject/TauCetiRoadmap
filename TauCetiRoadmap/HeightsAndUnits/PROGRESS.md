## HeightsAndUnits: 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-19 (`d3dc3fb` to `9fc5d5a`)

The `S`-unit half of the roadmap is now complete. The `S`-unit group of a number field now has a fundamental system with unique decomposition [`exist_unique_eq_mul_prod`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Units/SUnit/FundamentalSystem.html#TauCeti.NumberField.SUnit.exist_unique_eq_mul_prod), an `S`-logarithmic lattice identified with the `S`-units modulo torsion ([`logEmbeddingEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SUnits/Lattice.html#TauCeti.NumberField.SUnit.logEmbeddingEquiv)), and an `S`-regulator with its determinant formula on any fundamental system and any omitted infinite place ([`regulator_eq_abs_det`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/SUnits/Regulator.html#TauCeti.NumberField.SUnit.regulator_eq_abs_det)). At `S = ∅` it recovers the classical regulator, and over `ℚ` it is the product of the logarithms of the primes in `S`. Underneath sits the height of an `S`-unit as Bombieri–Gubler 1.5.10 states it ([`mulHeight₁_eq_of_mem_unit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/SInteger.html#NumberField.mulHeight₁_eq_of_mem_unit)).

For ordinary units, height and logarithmic embedding are now matched exactly: twice the height is the ℓ¹ norm of the embedding plus the absolute value of the dropped coordinate, and the torsion units are exactly the units of height one. Hadamard's bound, regulator ≤ `(2d)^r` times the product of the heights of any fundamental system, is [`regulator_le_prod_absLogHeight₁`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Units/Regulator/Height.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Units.regulator_le_prod_absLogHeight₁). The reverse bound, a fundamental system of controlled height ([`exists_fundSystem_prod_absLogHeight₁_le_of_isMaxRank`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Units/FundamentalSystem.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Units.exists_fundSystem_prod_absLogHeight₁_le_of_isMaxRank)), is stated against an independent family of units rather than the regulator: replacing one by the other needs Minkowski's second theorem. `ℚ(√2)` checks both constants, and the reduced one is attained there.

The height estimates arrived as well: the affine height with its linear-form and determinant bounds, projective and subspace Northcott instances, and Kronecker's theorem for projective tuples ([`absoluteProjectiveTupleHeight_eq_one_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Kronecker.html#TauCeti.absoluteProjectiveTupleHeight_eq_one_iff)) with the ineffective height gap at bounded degree. On the geometry-of-numbers side, Cassels' lattice basis of controlled gauge ([`exists_basis_gauge_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/GaugeBasis.html#ZLattice.exists_basis_gauge_le)) and the optimality of Siegel's exponent landed, along with log-concavity and Prékopa–Leindler material (TauCeti#18787). The integral kernel basis has not yet landed.
