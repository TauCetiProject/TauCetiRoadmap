## PeripheralActions: 2026-09-29 to 2026-09-30 (`912f4de` to `0d3161a`)

This first window lays the ground floor of the roadmap and one wall of the second storey. The peripheral system of a free pro-`p` group of finite rank is now in place: the [basis](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.basis) transported along a marked isomorphism, the [cusp](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.cusp) as the inverse of the ordered product of the basis, the [peripheral tuple](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.peripheralTuple), and the predicate [peripheral of exponent `u`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.IsPeripheralAut), stated with conjugacy rather than named conjugators. Inner automorphisms are peripheral of exponent one, and in positive rank [the exponent is determined by the automorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.IsPeripheralAut.exponent_unique) (TauCeti#9857).

Separately, the automorphism criterion that Layer 2 needs has landed: for any units `u i` and conjugators `c i`, [some continuous automorphism sends each basis element `x i` to `(c i)⁻¹ x i ^ u i c i`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.IsProP.exists_continuousAut_apply_eq_conj_padicPow). It rests on two general facts about pro-`p` groups: [conjugates of unit powers of topological generators still generate](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/Surjective.html#TauCeti.IsProP.topologicalClosure_closure_range_eq_top_of_isConj_padicPow), and in a free pro-`p` group of finite rank [every generating family is the image of the basis under an automorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exists_continuousAut_of_topologicallyGenerates) (TauCeti#9869).

The criterion says nothing about the cusp, so the peripheral-power theorem does not follow yet. That needs the peripheral product identity of Layer 1, the central-series lifting and compactness argument, and none of it has started.
