## PeripheralActions: 2026-09-29 to 2026-09-30 (`912f4de` to `0d3161a`)

This first window lays the ground floor of the roadmap and one wall of the second storey. The peripheral system of a free pro-`p` group of finite rank is now in place: the [basis](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.basis) transported along a marked isomorphism, the [cusp](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.cusp) as the inverse of the ordered product of the basis, the [peripheral tuple](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.peripheralTuple), and the predicate [peripheral of exponent `u`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.IsPeripheralAut), stated with conjugacy rather than named conjugators. Inner automorphisms are peripheral of exponent one, and in positive rank [the exponent is determined by the automorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Basic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.IsPeripheralAut.exponent_unique) (TauCeti#9857).

Separately, the automorphism criterion that Layer 2 needs has landed: for any units `u i` and conjugators `c i`, [some continuous automorphism sends each basis element `x i` to `(c i)⁻¹ x i ^ u i c i`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.IsProP.exists_continuousAut_apply_eq_conj_padicPow). It rests on two general facts about pro-`p` groups: [conjugates of unit powers of topological generators still generate](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/Surjective.html#TauCeti.IsProP.topologicalClosure_closure_range_eq_top_of_isConj_padicPow), and in a free pro-`p` group of finite rank [every generating family is the image of the basis under an automorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exists_continuousAut_of_topologicallyGenerates) (TauCeti#9869).

The criterion says nothing about the cusp, so the peripheral-power theorem does not follow yet. That needs the peripheral product identity of Layer 1, the central-series lifting and compactness argument, and none of it has started.

## PeripheralActions: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02 (`0d3161a` to `d449639`)

The roadmap's core theorem has landed. [The peripheral product identity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Identity.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_defect_eq_one) says that for every `p`-adic unit `u`, the `u`-th powers of the basis and the cusp of a free pro-`p` group of finite rank can be conjugated, the first conjugator trivial, so that their ordered product is `1` (TauCeti#10313). It is proved as the roadmap planned, without any arithmetic: the defect's level sets in the closed lower central series are closed and decreasing, a [correction step](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Lifting.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_mem_level_succ) pushes a solution one level deeper using the spanning theorem for the graded brackets, and compactness passes to the limit. Combined with the earlier automorphism criterion, it gives [the peripheral-power theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_peripheralAut): for every unit `u`, a continuous automorphism sending each basis element and the cusp to a conjugate of its `u`-th power. A [version for every conjugate of the cusp](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_peripheralAut_conj_cusp) follows.

The group of peripheral automorphisms followed. [`peripheralAut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Group.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.peripheralAut) is a subgroup, and its [exponent character](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Group.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exponent) is [surjective](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Group.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exponent_surjective) and [continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Topology.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.continuous_exponent). The subgroup is [closed](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Topology.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.isClosed_peripheralAut) in the congruence topology. The [reflection](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Reflection.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.isPeripheralAut_reflect) is an explicit element of exponent `-1` in every rank, and plain inversion of the generators is [proved not peripheral in rank at least three](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Reflection.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.not_isPeripheralAut_of_apply_basis_eq_inv). Over the principal units the exponent has [a continuous homomorphic section](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Section.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_section_principalUnits), built from a new [identification of the principal units with `ℤ_p`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/PadicUnits.html#TauCeti.principalUnitsEquiv).

The dyadic instance is stated in consumer shape: [the identity form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Dyadic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_peripheral_identity_two) for `P`, `T`, `C`, and the automorphism in [both](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Dyadic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_peripheralPowerAutomorphism_two) [conventions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Dyadic.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_peripheralPowerAutomorphism_two_opposite) for the third element. The only part of the roadmap still missing is the permutation symmetries of Layer 3.4.
