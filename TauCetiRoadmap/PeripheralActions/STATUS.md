## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The roadmap is complete. The peripheral product identity and the peripheral-power theorem hold for every `p`-adic unit. The group of peripheral automorphisms has its exponent character, closedness, explicit elements of exponent `-1`, permutation symmetries and principal-unit section. The dyadic instance is stated in the shape its consumer needs. Only Layer 5 is left, and it is by design not built here.

### Named results

- **The peripheral product identity** — for every unit `u` there are conjugators, the first one trivial, such that the conjugated `u`-th powers of the basis and of the cusp multiply to `1` in order ([`exists_defect_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Identity.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_defect_eq_one)). It is proved by lifting through the closed lower central series and passing to the limit by compactness, with no Galois group involved. The unit hypothesis is necessary: at exponent `2` in rank two over `p = 2`, [the identity fails even modulo `γ_2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Nonunit.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.not_exists_peripheral_identity_two).
- **The peripheral-power theorem** — for every unit `u`, some continuous automorphism of a free pro-`p` group of finite rank sends each basis element and the cusp to a conjugate of its `u`-th power, and fixes the first basis element's power exactly ([`exists_peripheralAut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_peripheralAut)). The conjugators cannot be taken equal: in rank at least two [some conjugator differs from the cusp's](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Conjugator.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_ne_of_apply_eq_conj).
- **The permutation-and-exponent homomorphism** — in rank at least two, an automorphism that permutes the peripheral classes up to a common power [determines the permutation and the exponent](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Permutation.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.IsPeripheralPermAut.unique), giving a homomorphism [`permData`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Permutation.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.permData) to `Perm (Fin (r + 1)) × ℤ_pˣ`. In rank one [this fails](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Permutation.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_isPeripheralPermAut_swap_rank_one).
- **The section over the principal units** — in positive rank, the exponent character has a continuous homomorphic section over `1 + p^k ℤ_p`, for `k ≥ 1`, with `k ≥ 2` when `p = 2` ([`exists_section_principalUnits`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Section.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_section_principalUnits)). No section over all of `ℤ_pˣ` is claimed.
- **Closedness of the peripheral automorphisms** — they form a closed subgroup of the continuous automorphisms in the congruence topology ([`isClosed_peripheralAut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Topology.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.isClosed_peripheralAut)), and the exponent is continuous on it ([`continuous_exponent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Topology.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.continuous_exponent)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The peripheral automorphism group and its exponent** ([`peripheralAut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Group.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.peripheralAut), [`exponent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Group.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exponent)) — the automorphisms that are peripheral of some unit exponent. The exponent is a surjective homomorphism to `ℤ_pˣ`, and its kernel contains the inner automorphisms. In rank one this group is [all of the continuous automorphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/RankOne.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.peripheralAut_eq_top_rank_one).
- **The peripheral levels** ([`level`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Lifting.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.level)) — the closed sets of conjugators whose defect lies in the `n`-th term of the closed lower central series. Together with the [correction step](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Peripheral/Lifting.html#TauCeti.Peripheral.exists_mem_level_succ), they drive the proof of the identity.
- **The principal units as a copy of `ℤ_p`** ([`principalUnitsEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/PadicUnits.html#TauCeti.principalUnitsEquiv)) — the `p`-adic power map of a unit of exact level `f` is a topological isomorphism onto `1 + p^f ℤ_p`. This is what parameterizes the section.

### Roadmap coverage

Layer 5 is untouched, and the README marks it as not work for this roadmap.

## The frontier

- **The arithmetic route (Layer 5)** — a Galois homomorphism into the peripheral automorphisms, with the Grothendieck–Teichmüller relations. The README assigns this to the arithmetic successor of BelyiMaps, not to this roadmap.
- **A section over all of `ℤ_pˣ`** — explicitly not a milestone. Over the torsion units, a section would need peripheral automorphisms of finite order that are compatible with the principal-unit section. Nothing here addresses that compatibility.
- **Generation by the explicit elements** — the README says that the swap, rotation, reflection and inner automorphisms generate the image of the mapping class group of the thrice-punctured sphere. That claim is not a milestone, and it is not proved here.
