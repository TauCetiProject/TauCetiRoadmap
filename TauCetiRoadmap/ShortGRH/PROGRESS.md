## ShortGRH: 2026-11-26 to 2026-11-27 (`c9e9c7b` to `dde09e0`)

This is the first report for the shorter route to the generalized Riemann hypothesis, and both ends of its analytic frame are now in place. The one-sided criterion, in its form for one prime ([`LFunction_ne_zero_of_primeRemainder_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/OneSidedBound/Criterion.html#TauCeti.OneSidedBound.LFunction_ne_zero_of_primeRemainder_le)), says that if the signed prime remainder `R_q(y)` is eventually at most `y^(β−1)`, and the quadratic twists by a chosen prime `d` have no zero in the box `β < Re z < 1`, `|Im z| ≤ |Im ρ|`, then `L(ρ, χ) ≠ 0`. A [reduction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/OneSidedBound/Reduction.html#TauCeti.OneSidedBound.forall_zeroFreeRightOf_LFunction_iff_forall_parameters) shows that moduli divisible by 18480 suffice. The last step of the main inequality is proved as [plain linear algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/OneSidedBound/Covolume.html#TauCeti.OneSidedBound.weightedRemainder_le_of_covolume_bounds): covolume and index bounds of the shape Theorems A, B and C will supply force the weighted remainder below `A·(log b)⁵`. Those three theorems are not proved, so the end theorems are not reached.

On the analytic side, a [zero-free region with a bound for the logarithmic derivative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/LFunction/LogDeriv/ZeroFreeRegion.html#DirichletCharacter.eventually_entireLFunction_ne_zero_and_norm_logDeriv_le) landed for every Dirichlet L-function. So did the theta-tail estimates that detect zeros of quadratic twists, a mean square built on Pólya–Vinogradov, and the [choice of a prime with zero-free twists](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Quadratic/TwistSelection.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_quad_twist_zero_free_of_density). That choice still assumes the zero-density count it needs.

The rest is groundwork for the bounds themselves. At the real places, the [cocycle of the Fock model is trivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/HeisenbergModel/Fock/Basic.html#TauCeti.Fock.cocycle_multiplier), so the Weil representation there is a genuine representation, and its polynomial vectors are [dense](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/HeisenbergModel/Fock/Fischer.html#TauCeti.Fock.denseRange_polyVector). Appell–Humbert theta functions now have [Frobenius' dimension formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/AppellHumbert/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.AppellHumbertData.IsPositive.finrank_thetaSpace_sq) (TauCeti#46905). Weierstrass `σ` has its [duplication formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Elliptic/Weierstrass/Addition.html#PeriodPair.weierstrassSigma_two_mul). Also new: the second-moment argument behind Theorem B ([one translate spreads](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/NearIsometry/SecondMoment.html#TauCeti.isSpread_of_sum_sq_frobenius_le)), [Hua's integral bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HuaIntegral.html#Matrix.lmarginal_norm_det_add_sum_vecMulVec_inv_pow_le), polar decomposition in indefinite unitary groups, and Ext groups of functors on test rings (TauCeti#46286).
