- **Frobenius' dimension formula** — for an Appell–Humbert datum with positive Riemann form, `(dim Θ)² = [L^⊥ : L]` ([`finrank_thetaSpace_sq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/AppellHumbert/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.AppellHumbertData.IsPositive.finrank_thetaSpace_sq)).
- **The Fock-model cocycle is trivial** — at the real places the Weil representation of `U(ε)` is a genuine, jointly continuous representation ([`Fock.cocycle_multiplier`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/HeisenbergModel/Fock/Basic.html#TauCeti.Fock.cocycle_multiplier)).

### Roadmap coverage

Work has begun in 24 of the 25 sub-roadmaps, but "partial" often means only a few supporting lemmas. Furthest along are the one-sided reduction, where every layer has begun and Layers 5–6 wait on Theorems A to C, and the zero-free regions, where only the zero-density count of §3.5 visibly remains. The other well-advanced layers are the reproducing and Fock models (Weil representations, Layers 1–2), the spreading argument (second moments, Layer 1) and quotient integrals and class sums (adelic unitary groups, Layer 1). Of the route's own roadmaps, these have not begun: the norms of theta lifts (all of it), theta kernels and the seesaw identity (Layers 1 and 3 of theta lift chains), most of congruences of twisted lifts, Layers 3–8 of pairs of frames, and the orbit-bound and counting layers of the remaining terms of second moments. Layers of background roadmaps with no work citing the source proof are left unassessed, since the library may cover them elsewhere. These include moduli of abelian schemes beyond complex tori, crystalline representations beyond period rings, most of the p-adic comparison, and much of the Galois representations of unitary eigenforms.

## The frontier

- **Zero density for quadratic twists** (ZeroFree §3.5 (a)) — a power-saving count of the primes of a class whose twists vanish in a box. The selection of a twist-free prime is proved from it, and it turns the fixed-prime criterion into the class form of OneSided §0.2.
- **Theorem B** (OneSided §5.4) — the spreading linear algebra is done; what remains is equality of multiplicities, Bessel functions at the real places, the unfolded second moment and its remaining and main terms (second moments, Layers 2–5, with the remaining and main terms roadmap).
- **Theorem A** (OneSided §5.3) — needs source and target forms, the seesaw identity (theta lift chains, untouched beyond §2.3) and the divisibility and size of the composite lift (composite lifts, Layers 1–5, begun).
- **Theorem C** (Determinants §6.3) — the lower bound. The arrow-triangle Gram algebra and grid paths have begun, but it rests on the norms of single lifts (untouched) and on congruences and Selmer bounds (barely begun).
- **The end theorems** (OneSided §6.4) — the passage from the half-plane to the critical line and Mathlib's `RiemannHypothesis`. It is short once the one-sided bound of §6.2 holds, through RiemannHypothesisStatementsAndConsequences §§9.3–9.4.
