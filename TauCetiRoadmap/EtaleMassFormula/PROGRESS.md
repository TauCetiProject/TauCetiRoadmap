## EtaleMassFormula: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`96bf548` to `7ca783d`)

The first window of this roadmap built most of its carrier layer. Labelled [Galois sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/GaloisSet.html#TauCeti.GaloisSet), permutation representations of `Gal(K^sep/K)` on `Fin n` with open kernel, now exist alongside their discriminant weight and the [étale mass](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/Mass.html#TauCeti.etaleMass) `M_n(K) = (1/n!) ∑_ρ q^{-d(ρ)}`, the quantity Bhargava's formula evaluates. The headline is [the transitive bridge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/GaloisSet.html#TauCeti.GaloisSet.tsum_isTransitive): summing any weight of the stabilizer's fixed field over the transitive Galois sets of size `n` gives `(n − 1)!` times its sum over the degree-`n` subextensions of `SeparableClosure K`. Specialised to `q^{-d}`, this is [the transitive mass by subextensions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/Mass.html#TauCeti.tsum_isTransitive_one_div_natCard_residueField_pow_discriminantExponent). The weight itself is an [orbit discriminant exponent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/OrbitWeight.html#TauCeti.orbitDiscriminantExponent), additive on disjoint unions and independent of the chosen orbit representatives.

On the Layer 1 side, the residue degree is now totalized on subextensions as [`IntermediateField.inertiaDegree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/IntermediateField.html#IntermediateField.inertiaDegree) and is invariant under `K`-isomorphism. A finite subextension's unramified part is [the canonical unramified extension of its residue degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/IntermediateField.html#IntermediateField.inf_maximalUnramifiedExtension), so `K_{f(L/K)} ≤ L`.

Still missing are the bridge from Galois sets to étale algebras (§0.4), the automorphism count and the general orbit count of Layer 1, and everything from Serre's formula over `K_f` onward.

## EtaleMassFormula: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-24 (`7ca783d` to `d25d526`)

This window reached the summit. [Bhargava's mass formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/Main.html#TauCeti.etaleMass_eq) is now proved for every non-archimedean local field: the étale mass `M_n(K)` equals `∑_{k ≤ n} q(k, n − k) · q^{−k}`, where `q(k, m)` counts the partitions of `k` into at most `m` parts. It is also stated in Bhargava's own form, as [a sum of `1/(#Aut_K(A) · q^{v(Disc A)})` over the isomorphism classes of étale algebras of degree `n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/Main.html#TauCeti.tsum_etaleDiscriminantExponent_eq) (TauCeti#40204). That form rests on the new bridge [identifying the étale-algebra sum with the mass of Galois sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/EtaleAlgebra.html#TauCeti.tsum_etaleDiscriminantExponent). Every Galois set is realised as the points of an étale algebra, and the algebra's automorphisms are read off as the relabellings that fix it (TauCeti#38285). Both of Bhargava's refinements landed on the way: the [mass by splitting type](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/Main.html#TauCeti.etaleMass_splittingSymbol), `1/(#Aut(σ) · q^{∑ f_i(e_i−1)})`, and the [mass by ramification partition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/Main.html#TauCeti.etaleMass_ramificationPartition), `q^{−k}`. The second rests on the combinatorial fact that [the automorphism weights of the splitting symbols with a given partition sum to one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/SplittingSymbol.html#TauCeti.tsum_one_div_splittingSymbolAut_ramificationPartition).

Underneath, the [subfield count by residue degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/SubfieldCount.html#TauCeti.tsum_inertiaDegree_eq) transports Serre's formula to each unramified extension `K_f` and gives `e · q^{−f(e−1)}`. With splitting symbols, this yields the [transitive mass](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/Transitive.html#TauCeti.etaleMass_isTransitive_splittingSymbol) `q^{−f(e−1)}/f`. Layer 1 is finished too. It now has the [automorphism count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/IntermediateField.html#IntermediateField.natCard_algEquiv_eq_mul) `#Aut_K(L) = #Aut_{K_f}(L) · #Stab([L])`. It also has the [classification of finite extensions by their unramified part](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/EtaleMassFormula/Classification.html#TauCeti.totallyRamifiedOrbitEquiv), as Galois orbits on classes of totally ramified extensions of `K_f`, which the roadmap had left to its README. The rest of the work was supporting material: disjoint unions and block decompositions of Galois sets, the relabelling action, and automorphism counts in towers with a normal bottom step.
