## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 1 is done apart from one identification, and amenability is defined for arbitrary topological groups. Layers 2–5 are partial: finite groups, directed unions and colimits, and the implication from Følner sets to amenability are proved, but the reverse Følner implication, the mean–content correspondence, subgroup and extension closure, and nonamenability of `F₂` are not. Layers 6–8 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Følner sets imply amenability** — [`Amenable.of_hasFoelnerSets`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Average.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_hasFoelnerSets): a group with a nontrivial counting-measure Følner filter is amenable for every group topology. Averages over Følner sets are approximately invariant, and a weak-* cluster point of them is an invariant mean.
- **The cluster-point and finite-intersection criteria** — [`Amenable.of_tendsto`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/WeakStar.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_tendsto) and [`amenable_iff_forall_finset_exists`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/WeakStar.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_forall_finset_exists): a group is amenable if it has means that are approximately invariant along a nontrivial filter, and exactly when every finite family of invariance equations has a solution.
- **Directed unions and filtered colimits of amenable groups are amenable** — [`Amenable.of_directed_of_iSup_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/DirectedUnion.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_directed_of_iSup_eq_top) and [`Amenable.of_isColimit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Colimit.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_isColimit). The colimit version needs no injectivity of the transition maps, and it gives amenability of a group all of whose finitely generated subgroups are amenable.
- **Reiter implies Følner** — [`exists_finset_card_smul_symmDiff_lt_of_sum_tsum_lt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Reiter.html#TauCeti.exists_finset_card_smul_symmDiff_lt_of_sum_tsum_lt): an almost-invariant finitely supported function has an almost-invariant superlevel set. This is the layer-cake half of the converse.
- **Finite groups are amenable** — [`Amenable.of_finite`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Finite.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_finite), for any group topology. The uniform mean is the [only invariant mean](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Finite.html#TauCeti.Mean.isLeftInvariant_iff_eq_uniform).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Amenability and the weak-* space of means** — [`TauCeti.Amenable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Basic.html#TauCeti.Amenable) asks for a left-invariant mean on `UCB(G)`, with left translation acting by [linear isometries](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/UCB/Translation.html#TauCeti.UCB.leftTranslation). The means form a [compact space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/WeakStar.html#TauCeti.Mean.instCompactSpace) by Banach–Alaoglu, and every existence argument so far runs through that compactness.
- **Equidecompositions as partitions** — [`Equidecomp.exists_iff_exists_finpartition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Action/Equipartition.html#Equidecomp.exists_iff_exists_finpartition) shows that Mathlib's `Equidecomp` is the same as a finite partition moved piece by piece. It goes through [`Equidecomp.Equipartition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Action/Equipartition.html#Equidecomp.Equipartition), with the shape of Mathlib PR #30330. This is the bridge that paradoxicality needs.
- **The finite-test form of Følner sets** — [`hasFoelnerSets_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Foelner.html#TauCeti.hasFoelnerSets_iff) turns the filter definition into "for every finite `K` and `ε` there is a finite `F` with `|gF △ F| < ε|F|`". It needs no topology and no countability.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 1** is done except one part of item 2: the identification, by inversion, of `UCB(G)` with the space defined through `f(xu)`, exchanging left and right translation. Translations, invariant means and their convexity, weak-* compactness, pullback and transport, and the [real/complex correspondence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Complex.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_exists_complexMean) are all in. **Layer 2** is partial: only the finite-group example. There is no identification of `UCB` with all bounded functions on a discrete group, no content API, no mean–content correspondence, and no general bridge from abstract to topological amenability. **Layer 3** is partial: the equipartition bridge and the free-group prefix sets `W(c)`, with `F = W(a) ⊔ a·W(a⁻¹)`, are in. The relation `∼_G`, Schröder–Bernstein, paradoxical sets, the content obstruction and nonamenability of `F₂` are not. **Layer 4** is partial: directed unions, filtered colimits and the finitely-generated reduction are in, and images follow from Layer 1. Subgroups, quotient packaging, extensions, finite products and the `F₂`-subgroup corollary are missing. **Layer 5** is partial: the Følner API, `hasFoelnerSets_iff`, the Følner-to-amenable direction and Reiter-to-Følner are in. The separation argument and the full equivalence are not. **Layers 6, 7 and 8** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Amenable implies Reiter** — produce finitely supported almost-invariant probabilities from an invariant mean by Hahn–Banach separation in a finite product of `ℓ¹(G)`. Combined with Reiter-to-Følner, this closes `amenable_iff_hasFoelnerSets` and unlocks Layers 6 and 8.
- **Paradoxical sets and `F₂`** — define `∼_G` and paradoxicality on top of the equipartition bridge, prove the subcopy-to-partition theorem, and apply it to the prefix sets `W(c)` to show `F₂` is not amenable. The content obstruction also needs Layer 2's contents.
- **The discrete mean–content correspondence** — identify `UCB` with all bounded functions on a discrete group, and build the normalized `AddContent` API and the mutually inverse correspondence with means.
- **Subgroups, extensions and products** — the coset-retraction construction for subgroups, and the iterated-mean constructions for extensions and finite products, of discrete amenable groups.
- **Inversion symmetry of `UCB`** — the remaining Layer 1 item, which identifies the left and right versions of the space.
