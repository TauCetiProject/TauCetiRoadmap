## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All eight layers are now proved. The discrete theory culminates in Tarski's alternative and the Følner characterization, and the locally compact branch in the Haar `L∞` characterization. The library source has no open `sorry` in this area. What remains is checking that every connecting lemma in the README exists in the form it asks for, not proving new theorems.

### Named results

- **Tarski's alternative** — [`amenable_iff_not_paradoxical_univ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Tarski.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_not_paradoxical_univ): a discrete group is amenable exactly when it is not paradoxical for its left regular action, with no countability assumption. A [topology-free form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Tarski.html#TauCeti.hasFoelnerSets_iff_not_paradoxical_univ) states it for Følner sets.
- **The Følner characterization** — [`amenable_iff_hasFoelnerSets`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/DiscreteFoelner.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_hasFoelnerSets): a discrete group is amenable exactly when it has Følner sets, equivalently [Reiter's condition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/DiscreteFoelner.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_exists_sum_tsum_abs_sub_lt), equivalently the [finite-test condition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/DiscreteFoelner.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_exists_finset) `|gF △ F| < ε|F|`.
- **Nonamenability of free groups** — [`not_amenable_freeGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/FreeGroup.html#TauCeti.not_amenable_freeGroup): a free group of rank at least two is not amenable as a discrete group, and neither is [any discrete group containing one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/FreeGroup.html#TauCeti.not_amenable_of_injective_freeGroup).
- **The Haar `L∞` characterization** — [`Amenable.exists_lpTop_invariant`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/LocallyCompact.html#TauCeti.Amenable.exists_lpTop_invariant): an amenable locally compact group has a left-invariant mean on `L∞` of any regular left Haar measure. Together with [the converse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Linfty.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_lpTop), this characterizes amenability with no σ-compactness or σ-finiteness hypothesis.
- **Commutative groups are amenable** — [`Amenable.of_commGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Abelian.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_commGroup): every commutative topological group is amenable, for any topology, rank or torsion. Each finite subset lies in the image of a finite power of `ℤ`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Amenability through invariant means on `UCB(G)`** — [`TauCeti.Amenable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Basic.html#TauCeti.Amenable), with the [weak-* compact space of means](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/WeakStar.html#TauCeti.Mean.instCompactSpace). Every existence argument in the discrete layers goes through this compactness. On discrete groups the definition agrees with [invariant finitely additive probabilities on all subsets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Content.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_exists_invariantContent).
- **Equidecomposability and paradoxical sets** — [`Equidecomposable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Action/Equidecomp.html#TauCeti.Equidecomposable) and [`Paradoxical`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Action/Paradoxical.html#TauCeti.Paradoxical), built on Mathlib's `Equidecomp`. They come with [Schröder–Bernstein](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Action/Equidecomp.html#TauCeti.EquidecomposableLE.antisymm) and the [disjoint-subcopy criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Action/Paradoxical.html#TauCeti.paradoxical_iff_exists_subset), which together make paradoxical decompositions easy to produce.
- **Convolution of `L¹` with `L∞`** — [`Lp.convolutionUCB`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/Convolution/UCB.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.convolutionUCB) smooths an essentially bounded class into an honest `UCB` function. This is how an invariant `UCB` mean is lifted to `L∞`. It rests on a reduction of countably many integrable functions to [one σ-compact open subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/OpenSubgroup/SigmaCompact.html#IsSigmaCompact.exists_openSubgroup).

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 1** is done: the inversion identification of `UCB(G)` with its [right-handed counterpart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/UCB/Inversion.html#TauCeti.UCB.invLinearIsometryEquiv) closed the last item. **Layers 2–5 and 8** are done: discrete `UCB` as all bounded functions, the content API and mean–content correspondence; equidecompositions, paradoxicality and `F₂`; subgroups, quotients, extensions, finite products, directed unions and colimits; the Følner, Reiter and countable-sequence criteria; and Tarski's alternative. **Layer 6** is done, although `ℤ`-powers and directed unions give all commutative groups directly, and the README's separate finitely-generated abelian statement is subsumed rather than stated. **Layer 7** is done: the `L∞` characterization, independence of the regular Haar measure and its scaling, agreement with the discrete case, the correspondence with invariant Borel contents and its completion, σ-finite local nullity, and compact groups. The README's Baire-representative route to Fubini was not followed. The proof goes through σ-compact open subgroups instead, so some connecting targets of item 1 exist only in that form.
