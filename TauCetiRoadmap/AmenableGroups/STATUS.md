## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All four headline theorems are proved: Følner's theorem, Tarski's alternative, the paradoxical free group, and the Haar `L∞` characterization. Layer 7 now also has the Baire-measurable route the README describes. The rewritten README added a few small connecting lemmas to Layers 2 and 5, and these are not yet stated. Every other layer is done.

### Named results

- **Tarski's alternative** — [`amenable_iff_not_paradoxical_univ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Tarski.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_not_paradoxical_univ): a discrete group is amenable exactly when it is not paradoxical for its left regular action. No countability assumption is needed.
- **Følner's theorem** — [`amenable_iff_hasFoelnerSets`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/DiscreteFoelner.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_hasFoelnerSets): a discrete group is amenable exactly when it has Følner sets. Equivalently it satisfies Reiter's condition, or the [finite test](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/DiscreteFoelner.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_exists_finset) `|gF △ F| < ε|F|`.
- **Nonamenability of free groups** — [`not_amenable_freeGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/FreeGroup.html#TauCeti.not_amenable_freeGroup): a free group of rank at least two is not amenable, and neither is any discrete group containing one.
- **The Haar `L∞` characterization** — [`amenable_iff_exists_lpTop_invariant_of_regular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/LocallyCompact.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_exists_lpTop_invariant_of_regular): a locally compact group is amenable exactly when `L∞` of a regular left Haar measure carries a left-invariant mean. No σ-compactness or σ-finiteness is assumed.
- **Completion regularity of Haar measure** — [`NullMeasurableSet.exists_baire_sandwich`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/CompletionRegular.html#MeasureTheory.NullMeasurableSet.exists_baire_sandwich): on a σ-compact locally compact group, every null-measurable set lies between two Baire sets that differ by a null set. It is proved through [the Kakutani–Kodaira theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/KakutaniKodaira.html#TauCeti.exists_compact_normal_subgroup_quotient_metrizable).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Amenability through invariant means on `UCB(G)`** — [`TauCeti.Amenable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Basic.html#TauCeti.Amenable), with the [weak-* compact space of means](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/WeakStar.html#TauCeti.Mean.instCompactSpace). Every existence argument in the discrete layers goes through this compactness. An additive twin, [`AddAmenable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Additive.html#TauCeti.AddAmenable), carries the closure theory over to additive groups.
- **Convolution of `L¹` with `L∞`** — [`Lp.convolutionUCB`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/Convolution/UCB.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.convolutionUCB) smooths an essentially bounded class into a `UCB` function, which is how an invariant `UCB` mean reaches `L∞`. The `L¹` convolution now has its [pointwise formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/Convolution/Pointwise.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.convolutionL_ae_eq) on every locally compact Hausdorff group.
- **The Baire σ-algebra** — [`TauCeti.baire`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Constructions/Baire/Basic.html#TauCeti.baire), generated by the continuous real functions. Its Haar [Baire representatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Constructions/Baire/Haar.html#MeasureTheory.AEStronglyMeasurable.exists_stronglyMeasurable_baire) make convolution integrands measurable on products without assuming that the Borel σ-algebra of `G × G` is the product σ-algebra.

## The frontier

- **Naturality of the mean–content correspondence (§2.3)** — push-forward along a map of discrete spaces, and equivariance for an arbitrary group action. The group-level versions for `Mean G` already exist, so this is a generalization of known arguments.
- **Elementary finite-test lemmas (§5.1)** — monotonicity in `K` and `ε`, and the bound `|ghF △ F| ≤ |gF △ F| + |hF △ F|`. Both are short set-cardinality arguments.
- **Følner families under eventual equality (§5.3)** — if two families agree eventually along a filter, one is Følner exactly when the other is.
- **Naming the `L∞` headline** — the README calls the theorem `amenable_iff_exists_lpTop_invariant_of_isHaarMeasure`, while the library proves it as `amenable_iff_exists_lpTop_invariant_of_regular`. Either an alias or a README edit closes the gap.
- **The `Equipartition` shim** — the equipartition bridge copies the shape of Mathlib PR #30330 and becomes redundant if that PR is merged.
