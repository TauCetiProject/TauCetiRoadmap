## AmenableGroups: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`94514ad` to `273ea28`)

The roadmap's first window laid down the function space and the notion of a mean that everything else is built on. [`TauCeti.UCB`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/UCB.html#TauCeti.UCB) is the real Banach algebra of bounded functions on a topological group that are uniformly continuous under small left multiplication, with the three equivalent descriptions the roadmap asks for: the neighbourhood condition, convergence of left translates in supremum norm, and uniform continuity for the right uniformity ([`mem_ucb_iff_uniformContinuous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/UCB.html#TauCeti.mem_ucb_iff_uniformContinuous)). It is closed under uniform limits, a lattice with a solid norm, and complete (TauCeti#18461).

On top of it, a [`Mean`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Mean.html#TauCeti.Mean) is a positive linear functional taking the value 1 at 1, with continuity derived rather than assumed: every mean lies between the infimum and supremum of its argument, is 1-Lipschitz, and has [operator norm one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Mean.html#TauCeti.Mean.norm_toContinuousLinearMap). The means form a convex set, nonempty because evaluation at a point is one. The supporting lemmas, that a positive functional dominated at an order unit is continuous with norm its value there, were stated for general ordered normed spaces so Layers 2 and 7 can reuse them. Invariance has not appeared yet: there is no translation action on `UCB`, no invariant mean and no definition of amenability.

## AmenableGroups: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

Amenability now exists as a definition, and the first half of the Følner theorem is proved on top of it: [a group with Følner sets is amenable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Average.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_hasFoelnerSets), for every group topology. The proof averages over Følner sets and takes a weak-* cluster point, using what Layer 1 still lacked: left and right translation on `UCB(G)` as [linear isometries](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/UCB/Translation.html#TauCeti.UCB.leftTranslation), [amenability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Basic.html#TauCeti.Amenable) as existence of a left-invariant mean, and the [weak-* compact space of means](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/WeakStar.html#TauCeti.Mean.instCompactSpace) with its [cluster-point criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/WeakStar.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_tendsto). Having Følner sets is also given its [finite-test form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Foelner.html#TauCeti.hasFoelnerSets_iff). For the converse, the combinatorial half is in: [Reiter implies Følner](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Reiter.html#TauCeti.exists_finset_card_smul_symmDiff_lt_of_sum_tsum_lt), by a layer-cake argument over superlevel sets. The Hahn–Banach step that turns an invariant mean into Reiter functions is still missing, so amenability is not yet known to imply Følner sets.

The closure theory got its first results. [Finite groups are amenable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Finite.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_finite), and the uniform mean is their only invariant mean. Amenability passes to images under surjective continuous homomorphisms, to [unions of directed families of amenable subgroups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/DirectedUnion.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_directed_of_iSup_eq_top) (so to groups whose finitely generated subgroups are amenable), and to [filtered colimits in `GrpCat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Colimit.html#TauCeti.Amenable.of_isColimit). Subgroups, extensions and products have not been done yet. The real and complex definitions now [agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Amenable/Complex.html#TauCeti.amenable_iff_exists_complexMean).

On the paradoxical side, so far there are only preparations. [Equidecompositions are now the same as finite partitions moved piece by piece](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Action/Equipartition.html#Equidecomp.exists_iff_exists_finpartition), using the equipartitions of Mathlib PR #30330. Free groups got the reduced-word prefix sets `W(c)`, together with the [decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/FreeGroup/StartsWith.html#FreeGroup.isCompl_startsWith_of_smul) `F = W(a) ⊔ a·W(a⁻¹)`. Paradoxical sets have not been defined, and `F₂` is not yet shown to be nonamenable.
