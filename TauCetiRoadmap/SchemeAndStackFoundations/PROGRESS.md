## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-19 (`84255bb` to `9fc5d5a`)

The main theorem of the henselization strand landed: the [henselization of a pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Henselian.html#TauCeti.Henselization.henselian) is a henselian pair, with no hypothesis on the ring or the ideal. The [henselization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Algebra.html#TauCeti.Henselization.algebra) is built as a colimit over a small model of the filtered category of étale neighbourhoods of `(R, I)`, and it keeps the [same quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Residue.html#TauCeti.Henselization.quotient_bijective) `R/I` (TauCeti#17460, TauCeti#18534). Its universal property, and the fact that a henselian pair is its own henselization, are not yet proved. Groundwork for them came alongside: [idempotents lift uniquely along a henselian pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Idempotent.html#TauCeti.HenselianRing.existsUnique_isIdempotentElem_mk_eq), and sections of unramified algebras split off idempotent factors.

Scheme Frobenius grew from a single map into a working API. There is now the [iterated relative Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Twist.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.iterateRelativeFrobenius), which [commutes with base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Twist.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isPullback_iterateRelativeFrobenius) and is a homomorphism on group schemes, and a [`q`-power Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Iterate.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.qPowerFrobenius) over a finite field. The [absolute Frobenius is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Finite.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isFinite_frobenius) on a scheme locally of finite type over a perfect ring. [Normal schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Normal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsNormalScheme) arrived too. On a locally Noetherian one, the [local rings at codimension-one points are discrete valuation rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Normal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsNormalScheme.isDiscreteValuationRing_stalk). They come with a [ramification index at a point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationIdx.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationIdxAt) and with the [surjectivity on codimension-one points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.codimensionOneMap_surjective) of surjective quasi-finite morphisms.

The commutative algebra underneath grew as well. Universally catenary rings appeared, among them [polynomial rings over universally catenary rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/UniversallyCatenary.html#MvPolynomial.isUniversallyCatenary), along with depth bounds and [Cohen–Macaulay modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CohenMacaulay/Basic.html#Module.IsCohenMacaulay). Also new: [a regular domain is integrally closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Normal.html#TauCeti.IsRegularRing.isIntegrallyClosed), a regular locus, flat base change of annihilators and cokernels, and Proj under arbitrary extension of coefficients.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`9fc5d5a` to `c1c6773`)

Japanese and Nagata rings arrived, opening §0.20. A domain is [Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Japanese.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsJapanese) when its integral closure in every finite extension of its fraction field, inseparable ones included, is a finite module. It is N-1 when this holds for the fraction field itself, and a ring is [Nagata](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsNagata) when it is Noetherian and each quotient by a prime is Japanese. Both properties [pass to localizations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Japanese.html#TauCeti.Ring.isJapanese_of_isLocalization), and [finite algebras over a Nagata ring are Nagata](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsNagata.of_finite). In dimension at most one, [Nagata is the same as Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.isNagata_iff_isJapanese), which already covers `ℤ`, rings of integers and Dedekind domains with perfect fraction field. The harder Stacks results are still missing: Nagata rings are universally Japanese, finite-type algebras over them are Nagata, and complete local rings are Nagata.

The normal-schemes strand of §0.30 is now nearly complete. [Ramification indices multiply in towers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationIdx/Normal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationIdxAt_comp_of_coheight_eq_one) at points lying over a codimension-one point of a normal target. Finite dominant maps [preserve codimension-one points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/FiniteType.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.coheight_apply_eq_one) when the target is of finite type over a field, and [also when it is normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/Normal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.coheight_apply_eq_one_of_coheight_eq_one), by going down. A [regular quotient of a regular local ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Quotient.html#Ideal.exists_regularSystem_of_quotient) is cut out by part of a regular system of parameters. The parabola and cusp serve as the check.

[Regular schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular) also appeared, the first piece of Layer 4 (§4.13). They come with affine and open-cover characterisations, are [normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular.isNormal), and [stay regular under smooth morphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular.of_smooth). The henselization gained its uniqueness half: two maps out of it agree once the ideal lands in the Jacobson radical of the target. Existence of lifts is still open.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`c1c6773` to `69cd1ab`)

The henselization of a pair now has its [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_lift): every map of pairs from `(R, I)` to a henselian pair factors uniquely through it. Two consequences follow. A henselian pair is [its own henselization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.algEquivOfHenselianRing), and for a local ring the construction along the maximal ideal [is the usual henselization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Local.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_isLocalHom), with the same residue field. The existence of lifts rests on a [global form of Zariski's main theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/QuasiFinite/ZariskisMainTheorem.html#Algebra.QuasiFinite.exists_fg_subalgebra_and_span_eq_top) for quasi-finite algebras (TauCeti#18880). Henselization is also [functorial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Map.html#TauCeti.Henselization.map) in maps of pairs. The ind-étale and flatness properties of §0.13 are still to come.

Resolution of curves landed in the form the roadmap asks for. Let `Y` be integral, locally Noetherian and of dimension at most one, with finite normalization in its function field. Then that normalization is [regular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint) and [a modification of `Y`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Modification.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isModification_fromNormalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint). [Modifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Modification.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsModification) were introduced for this, though so far only with birationality and the identity case.

The commutative algebra filled in around this. Excellence (§0.21) has begun with [geometrically regular algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/GeometricallyRegular.html#TauCeti.Excellence.GeometricallyRegular) and [regular ring maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/RegularAlgebraMap.html#TauCeti.Excellence.RegularAlgebraMap). Serre's conditions `(R_n)` and `(S_n)` arrived, with [reduced as `(R_0)` plus `(S_1)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/SerreCondition.html#TauCeti.isReduced_iff_satisfiesSerreR_zero_and_satisfiesSerreS_one); Serre's normality criterion is not yet there. [Polynomial rings over a field are Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/MvPolynomial.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsJapanese.mvPolynomial), and [being Nagata is local on `Spec R`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.isNagata_iff_of_span_eq_top). The [absolute](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/UniversalHomeomorphism.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.universally_isHomeomorph_frobenius) and [relative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/UniversalHomeomorphism.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.universally_isHomeomorph_relativeFrobenius) Frobenius are now universal homeomorphisms. A height-one ideal with regular quotient [is principal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Quotient.html#Ideal.isPrincipal_of_height_eq_one), which completes §0.30.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-06 (`69cd1ab` to `a67a650`)

Perfect schemes arrived, together with the perfection of a scheme. A scheme over `𝔽_p` is [perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfect.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IsPerfect) when its absolute Frobenius is an isomorphism. The [perfection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.perfection) `X^perf` is [perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isPerfect_perfection), maps to `X` by a [universal homeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isUniversalHomeomorphism_fromPerfection), and has the [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.bijective_comp_fromPerfection): for perfect `T`, maps `T → X^perf` are the same as maps `T → X` (TauCeti#27900). Underneath sits the criterion that a morphism is a universal homeomorphism [exactly when it is integral, universally injective and surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/UniversalHomeomorphism.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isUniversalHomeomorphism_iff_isIntegralHom_universallyInjective_surjective). Relative Frobenius is also finished: it [is an isomorphism for étale morphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Etale.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isIso_relativeFrobenius) and [finite flat of rank `p^n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Smooth.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.finrank_relativeFrobenius_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension) for smooth morphisms of relative dimension `n`. What perfection reflects and preserves, and the equivalence of étale sites, are still to come.

Every later layer now has a first foothold. [Chow groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup) are defined as `k`-cycles modulo rational equivalence, with [pushforward along closed immersions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/ClosedImmersion.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup.pushforward); the divisor class comparison is not there yet. [Algebraic spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace) are defined. Layer 2 gained the [Nisnevich topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Nisnevich/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.nisnevichTopology) and [classes of torsors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/Torsor.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1). Layer 3 has a [degree of vector bundles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Degree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.FixedRank.vectorBundleDegree), defined through `h⁰ − h¹`. Layer 4 has the [Artinian coefficient category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Deformation/ArtinLocalAlg.html#TauCeti.Deformation.ArtinLocalAlg).

In commutative algebra, [Cohen–Macaulay rings are universally catenary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CohenMacaulay/Catenary.html#TauCeti.Excellence.isUniversallyCatenary_of_forall_isCohenMacaulay), and [bijectivity can be tested at depth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Depth/Bijectivity.html#LinearMap.bijective_of_depth). The henselization is [ind-étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/IndEtale.html#TauCeti.Henselization.indEtale). It is [faithfully flat](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/IndEtale.html#TauCeti.Henselization.faithfullyFlat_of_le_jacobson) when `I` lies in the Jacobson radical. Twisting sheaves on `Proj`, pseudo-coherent modules and quasi-coherent cohomology filled in. The declaration survey was truncated, so later ring-theory modules are not reflected here.
