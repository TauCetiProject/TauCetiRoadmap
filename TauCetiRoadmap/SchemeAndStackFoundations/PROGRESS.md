## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-19 (`84255bb` to `9fc5d5a`)

The main theorem of the henselization strand landed: the [henselization of a pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Henselian.html#TauCeti.Henselization.henselian) is a henselian pair, with no hypothesis on the ring or the ideal. The [henselization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Algebra.html#TauCeti.Henselization.algebra) is built as a colimit over a small model of the filtered category of étale neighbourhoods of `(R, I)`, and it keeps the [same quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Residue.html#TauCeti.Henselization.quotient_bijective) `R/I` (TauCeti#17460, TauCeti#18534). Its universal property, and the fact that a henselian pair is its own henselization, are not yet proved. Groundwork for them came alongside: [idempotents lift uniquely along a henselian pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Idempotent.html#TauCeti.HenselianRing.existsUnique_isIdempotentElem_mk_eq), and sections of unramified algebras split off idempotent factors.

Scheme Frobenius grew from a single map into a working API. There is now the [iterated relative Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Twist.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.iterateRelativeFrobenius), which [commutes with base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Twist.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isPullback_iterateRelativeFrobenius) and is a homomorphism on group schemes, and a [`q`-power Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Iterate.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.qPowerFrobenius) over a finite field. The [absolute Frobenius is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Finite.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isFinite_frobenius) on a scheme locally of finite type over a perfect ring. [Normal schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Normal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsNormalScheme) arrived too. On a locally Noetherian one, the [local rings at codimension-one points are discrete valuation rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Normal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsNormalScheme.isDiscreteValuationRing_stalk). They come with a [ramification index at a point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationIdx.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationIdxAt) and with the [surjectivity on codimension-one points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.codimensionOneMap_surjective) of surjective quasi-finite morphisms.

The commutative algebra underneath grew as well. Universally catenary rings appeared, among them [polynomial rings over universally catenary rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/UniversallyCatenary.html#MvPolynomial.isUniversallyCatenary), along with depth bounds and [Cohen–Macaulay modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CohenMacaulay/Basic.html#Module.IsCohenMacaulay). Also new: [a regular domain is integrally closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Normal.html#TauCeti.IsRegularRing.isIntegrallyClosed), a regular locus, flat base change of annihilators and cokernels, and Proj under arbitrary extension of coefficients.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`9fc5d5a` to `c1c6773`)

Japanese and Nagata rings arrived, opening §0.20. A domain is [Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Japanese.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsJapanese) when its integral closure in every finite extension of its fraction field, inseparable ones included, is a finite module. It is N-1 when this holds for the fraction field itself, and a ring is [Nagata](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsNagata) when it is Noetherian and each quotient by a prime is Japanese. Both properties [pass to localizations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Japanese.html#TauCeti.Ring.isJapanese_of_isLocalization), and [finite algebras over a Nagata ring are Nagata](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsNagata.of_finite). In dimension at most one, [Nagata is the same as Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.isNagata_iff_isJapanese), which already covers `ℤ`, rings of integers and Dedekind domains with perfect fraction field. The harder Stacks results are still missing: Nagata rings are universally Japanese, finite-type algebras over them are Nagata, and complete local rings are Nagata.

The normal-schemes strand of §0.30 is now nearly complete. [Ramification indices multiply in towers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationIdx/Normal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationIdxAt_comp_of_coheight_eq_one) at points lying over a codimension-one point of a normal target. Finite dominant maps [preserve codimension-one points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/FiniteType.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.coheight_apply_eq_one) when the target is of finite type over a field, and [also when it is normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/Normal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.coheight_apply_eq_one_of_coheight_eq_one), by going down. A [regular quotient of a regular local ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Quotient.html#Ideal.exists_regularSystem_of_quotient) is cut out by part of a regular system of parameters. The parabola and cusp serve as the check.

[Regular schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular) also appeared, the first piece of Layer 4 (§4.13). They come with affine and open-cover characterisations, are [normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular.isNormal), and [stay regular under smooth morphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular.of_smooth). The henselization gained its uniqueness half: two maps out of it agree once the ideal lands in the Jacobson radical of the target. Existence of lifts is still open.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`c1c6773` to `69cd1ab`)

The henselization of a pair now has its [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_lift): every map of pairs from `(R, I)` to a henselian pair factors uniquely through it. Two consequences follow. A henselian pair is [its own henselization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.algEquivOfHenselianRing), and for a local ring the construction along the maximal ideal [is the usual henselization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Local.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_isLocalHom), with the same residue field. The existence of lifts rests on a [global form of Zariski's main theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/QuasiFinite/ZariskisMainTheorem.html#Algebra.QuasiFinite.exists_fg_subalgebra_and_span_eq_top) for quasi-finite algebras (TauCeti#18880). Henselization is also [functorial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Map.html#TauCeti.Henselization.map) in maps of pairs. The ind-étale and flatness properties of §0.13 are still to come.

Resolution of curves landed in the form the roadmap asks for. Let `Y` be integral, locally Noetherian and of dimension at most one, with finite normalization in its function field. Then that normalization is [regular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint) and [a modification of `Y`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Modification.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isModification_fromNormalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint). [Modifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Modification.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsModification) were introduced for this, though so far only with birationality and the identity case.

The commutative algebra filled in around this. Excellence (§0.21) has begun with [geometrically regular algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/GeometricallyRegular.html#TauCeti.Excellence.GeometricallyRegular) and [regular ring maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/RegularAlgebraMap.html#TauCeti.Excellence.RegularAlgebraMap). Serre's conditions `(R_n)` and `(S_n)` arrived, with [reduced as `(R_0)` plus `(S_1)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/SerreCondition.html#TauCeti.isReduced_iff_satisfiesSerreR_zero_and_satisfiesSerreS_one); Serre's normality criterion is not yet there. [Polynomial rings over a field are Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/MvPolynomial.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsJapanese.mvPolynomial), and [being Nagata is local on `Spec R`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.isNagata_iff_of_span_eq_top). The [absolute](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/UniversalHomeomorphism.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.universally_isHomeomorph_frobenius) and [relative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/UniversalHomeomorphism.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.universally_isHomeomorph_relativeFrobenius) Frobenius are now universal homeomorphisms. A height-one ideal with regular quotient [is principal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Quotient.html#Ideal.isPrincipal_of_height_eq_one), which completes §0.30.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-06 (`69cd1ab` to `a67a650`)

Perfect schemes arrived, together with the perfection of a scheme. A scheme over `𝔽_p` is [perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfect.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IsPerfect) when its absolute Frobenius is an isomorphism. The [perfection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.perfection) `X^perf` is [perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isPerfect_perfection), maps to `X` by a [universal homeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isUniversalHomeomorphism_fromPerfection), and has the [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.bijective_comp_fromPerfection): for perfect `T`, maps `T → X^perf` are the same as maps `T → X` (TauCeti#27900). Underneath sits the criterion that a morphism is a universal homeomorphism [exactly when it is integral, universally injective and surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/UniversalHomeomorphism.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isUniversalHomeomorphism_iff_isIntegralHom_universallyInjective_surjective). Relative Frobenius is also finished: it [is an isomorphism for étale morphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Etale.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isIso_relativeFrobenius) and [finite flat of rank `p^n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Smooth.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.finrank_relativeFrobenius_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension) for smooth morphisms of relative dimension `n`. What perfection reflects and preserves, and the equivalence of étale sites, are still to come.

Every later layer now has a first foothold. [Chow groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup) are defined as `k`-cycles modulo rational equivalence, with [pushforward along closed immersions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/ClosedImmersion.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup.pushforward); the divisor class comparison is not there yet. [Algebraic spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace) are defined. Layer 2 gained the [Nisnevich topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Nisnevich/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.nisnevichTopology) and [classes of torsors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/Torsor.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1). Layer 3 has a [degree of vector bundles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Degree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.FixedRank.vectorBundleDegree), defined through `h⁰ − h¹`. Layer 4 has the [Artinian coefficient category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Deformation/ArtinLocalAlg.html#TauCeti.Deformation.ArtinLocalAlg).

In commutative algebra, [Cohen–Macaulay rings are universally catenary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CohenMacaulay/Catenary.html#TauCeti.Excellence.isUniversallyCatenary_of_forall_isCohenMacaulay), and [bijectivity can be tested at depth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Depth/Bijectivity.html#LinearMap.bijective_of_depth). The henselization is [ind-étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/IndEtale.html#TauCeti.Henselization.indEtale). It is [faithfully flat](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/IndEtale.html#TauCeti.Henselization.faithfullyFlat_of_le_jacobson) when `I` lies in the Jacobson radical. Twisting sheaves on `Proj`, pseudo-coherent modules and quasi-coherent cohomology filled in. The declaration survey was truncated, so later ring-theory modules are not reflected here.

The Nisnevich topology now has its [henselization criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Nisnevich/Henselization.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isNisnevichCovering_iff_henselization): a family of étale maps is a Nisnevich covering exactly when, at every point `s`, the map from the spectrum of the henselized local ring `𝒪ʰ_{S,s}` lifts through one of them. Descent and cohomology moved together. ## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-11 (`d9fbe7b` to `a6027b1`)

Leray acyclicity for a morphism of sites landed: if an abelian sheaf `F` has no higher direct images, the [edge map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Leray/Edge.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H.lerayEdge) `Hⁿ(C, f_* F) → Hⁿ(D, F)` [is bijective in every degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Leray/Edge.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H.lerayEdge_sheafAdjunctionContinuous_bijective) (TauCeti#35717). The proof shifts dimension through injective sheaves, which pushforward sends to sheaves with no higher cohomology, so it never builds the Leray spectral sequence. The same work gives the partial-vanishing version and [injectivity in degree one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Leray/Edge.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H.lerayEdge_one_injective), the first map of the five-term exact sequence. The spectral sequence itself, and the Čech one, are still to come.

On the descent side, the comparison functor from quasi-coherent modules to descent data is now [faithful for every fpqc covering](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/QuasicoherentSheaf/Separated.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Descent.qcohPseudofunctor_faithful_toDescentData), so a descended morphism is unique, and being an isomorphism [can be checked on an fpqc cover](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/QuasicoherentSheaf/Separated.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.Hom.isIso_of_fpqc). Effective descent of objects, which would make quasi-coherent modules an fpqc stack, is not there yet. Higher direct images of schemes now keep their module structure: the cohomology presheaf `U ↦ Hⁿ(f⁻¹U, M)` is built as a [presheaf of `𝒪_Y`-modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/HigherDirectImage/Presheaf.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.higherDirectImagePresheaf), and [its sheafification is `Rⁿf_* M`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/HigherDirectImage/Presheaf.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.higherDirectImagePresheafSheafificationIso) as a module, not just as an abelian sheaf.

Torsor classes are now tied to sheaf cohomology: for an abelian sheaf `F`, isomorphism classes of `F`-torsors are [in bijection with `H¹(F)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/SheafH.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1.equivSheafH), and the bijection sends the torsor of lifts of a global section to [its connecting class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/SheafH.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1.equivSheafH_connecting) (TauCeti#38564). The proof rests on [torsors under an injective sheaf being trivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/Abelian.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1.subsingleton_of_injective). Pullback of torsor classes along morphisms of sites is still missing. The henselization is now [recognised](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Recognition.html#TauCeti.Henselization.algEquivOfIndEtale) as any ind-étale `R`-algebra `S` with `(S, IS)` henselian and `R/I → S/IS` bijective, and [henselian pairs pass to integral extensions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Integral.html#Ideal.henselianRing_map_of_isIntegral).

Along a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism, [higher direct images of quasi-coherent modules are quasi-coherent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/HigherDirectImage/Quasicoherent.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isQuasicoherent_higherDirectImage), with [sections `Hⁿ(f⁻¹V, M)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/HigherDirectImage/Quasicoherent.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.higherDirectImageBasicOpenSectionsAddEquiv) over basic opens of an affine base; compatibility with filtered colimits is not yet there. An fppf sheaf with representable diagonal and a jointly surjective family of étale maps from schemes [is an algebraic space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/EtaleCover.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.isAlgebraicSpace_of_etale_cover), and [stacks in groupoids](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Stack/StackInGroupoids.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.StackInGroupoids) are defined. Quasi-coherent modules form [an fpqc prestack](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/QuasicoherentSheaf/Prestack.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Descent.qcohPseudofunctor_isPrestack_fpqcTopology); effective descent of objects remains open. [Every affine scheme is a limit of affine schemes of finite presentation over `ℤ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Limits/NoetherianApproximation.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.exists_isLimit_finitePresentation_of_isAffine), though the quasi-compact quasi-separated case is still to come. [Formally smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/FormallySmooth.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FormallySmooth) morphisms of schemes are defined, but not yet the lifting criterion.

For intersection theory, the [localization sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/OpenRestriction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.range_cyclesPushforward_eq_ker_cyclesRestrict) `Z_n(Z) → Z_n(X) → Z_n(U)` is exact on cycles but not yet on Chow groups. Fulton's [order of a norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Length/Norm.html#TauCeti.ordFrac_norm_eq_finprod), the algebra behind proper pushforward of rational equivalence, also landed. On surfaces, an [intersection number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/SurfaceIntersection.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.intersectionNumber) of line bundles built from Euler characteristics gives the [numerical classes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/SurfaceIntersection.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.Num) of §5.10.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`aa6e43e` to `b235000`)

The affine criterion for pseudo-coherence landed: a module sheaf on a scheme is [pseudo-coherent exactly when](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/PseudoCoherent/Affine.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isPseudoCoherent_iff_forall_affineOpens) it is quasi-coherent and its sections over every affine open are pseudo-coherent (Stacks, Lemma 36.10.2; TauCeti#40176). Quasi-coherence cannot be dropped: over any local domain that is not a field there is a [sheaf with pseudo-coherent affine sections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/PseudoCoherent.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.exists_isPseudoCoherentOver_and_not_isQuasicoherent_of_not_isField) that is not quasi-coherent. Algebraic spaces gained [étale equivalence relations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/EtaleEquivRel.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.EtaleEquivRel), with kernel pairs of étale maps as the basic example, and their [diagonal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/Diagonal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace.diagonal_locallyQuasiFinite) is now known to be locally quasi-finite and separated; the quotient sheaf of §1.5 is not yet there. Smaller additions: the torsor–`H¹` comparison is [natural in the coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/SheafH.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1.equivSheafH_map), the [étale-to-Nisnevich comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Nisnevich/EtaleComparison.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.etaleToNisnevichAdjunction) of topoi exists, the top cohomology of `𝒪(m)` on projective space is [dual to a module of forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/ProjectiveSpace/TopDual.html#Module.Basis.nonempty_linearEquiv_cohomology_top_twistingSheaf_dual), and vector-bundle degree is [additive on direct sums](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/VectorBundle/Degree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.FixedRank.vectorBundleDegree_biprod).

A batch of work that landed earlier, recorded here late, fills out the henselian chapter. A pair `(R, I)` is henselian exactly when [idempotents lift bijectively](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Integral.html#TauCeti.henselianRing_iff_forall_bijOn_isIdempotentElem) through every finite `R`-algebra, or when [coprime factorizations lift](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Factorization.html#TauCeti.henselianRing_iff_forall_exists_monic_mul_eq); base change to `R/I` is an [equivalence on finite étale algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/FiniteEtale.html#CommAlgCat.FiniteEtale.isEquivalence_baseChange_of_surjective); and the henselization along pairwise coprime ideals [is the product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Product.html#TauCeti.Henselization.coprimeEquiv) of the separate henselizations. Elkik's lifting of smooth algebras is still open. The same batch proved the [divisor class comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/DivisorClass.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.classGroupEquivChowGroup) `Cl(X) ≅ CH_{n-1}(X)` for integral Noetherian schemes of finite type over a field, showed [smooth algebras over Cohen–Macaulay rings are Cohen–Macaulay](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CohenMacaulay/Smooth.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsCohenMacaulay.of_smooth), introduced the [formal spectrum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicThickeningSystem/Spf.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spf) of an adic ring, and built out relative `Proj`, including [`𝒪(m) ⊗ 𝒪(n) ≅ 𝒪(m + n)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeProj/TwistingSheaf/TensorProduct.html#CategoryTheory.CommMon.relativeProjTwistingSheafTensorProductIso) when degree-one elements cover.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-26 (`b235000` to `01ef2fa`)

Quasi-coherent modules now descend along faithfully flat maps of affine schemes: for `R → S` faithfully flat, [the comparison with descent data along `Spec S → Spec R`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/QuasicoherentSheaf/Affine.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Descent.isEquivalence_toDescentData_specMap) is an equivalence, so descent data are effective (TauCeti#44999). The proof identifies descent data with coalgebras. With quasi-coherent modules also [a stack for the Zariski topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/QuasicoherentSheaf/Zariski.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Descent.ZariskiGlue.isStack_zariskiTopology), what §1.1 still lacks is gluing the two into effectivity along arbitrary fpqc coverings. Along the same lines, [torsors under an affine group scheme are representable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/Torsor.html#TauCeti.GroupScheme.exists_isTorsor_nonempty_toTorsor_iso) by schemes affine over the base, with no Noetherian hypotheses. [Algebraic spaces over a base scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/Over.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpaceOver) are set up, and the [quotient sheaf](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/QuotientSheaf.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.quotientSheaf) of §1.5 is defined, though it is not yet shown to be an algebraic space.

In cohomology, [Serre's criterion for affineness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/AffinenessCriterion.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isAffine_of_subsingleton_cohomology_one) landed: a quasi-compact scheme on which `H¹` of every quasi-coherent module vanishes is affine (TauCeti#38440). On a quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme, [cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/FilteredColimit.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isColimitMapCoconeCohomologyFunctor) and [higher direct images](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/HigherDirectImage/FilteredColimit.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isColimitMapCoconeHigherDirectImage) commute with filtered colimits of quasi-coherent sheaves. Right derived functors built from K-injective replacements give the [total direct image](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Derived/TotalDirectImage.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.totalDirectImage) `Rf_*` on unbounded complexes, alongside [`D_QCoh`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Derived/DQCoh.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Coherent.DQCoh). For Chow groups, the [localization sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Localization.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup.exact_pushforward_restrict) `CH_n(Z) → CH_n(X) → CH_n(U) → 0` is now exact. [Flat pullback](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/FlatPullback.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.cyclesPullback) exists on cycles but does not yet pass to Chow groups.

Smaller pieces: [Chow's lemma over an affine base](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.exists_chowModification_of_finite_irreducibleComponents); [coherent formal modules on `Spf A`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicThickeningSystem/CoherentFormalModule/Spf.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AdicThickeningSystem.CoherentFormalModule.spfEquivFGModuleCat) are finite `A`-modules; the [Picard groupoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/PicardGroupoid.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.picardGroupoid); line bundles are [determined by their `𝔾_m`-frame torsors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/FrameTorsor.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.frameTorsorClass_injective); and perfection [preserves fibre products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isPullback_perfection). The declaration survey was truncated, so this account is not exhaustive.
