## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-19 (`84255bb` to `9fc5d5a`)

The main theorem of the henselization strand landed: the [henselization of a pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Henselian.html#TauCeti.Henselization.henselian) is a henselian pair, with no hypothesis on the ring or the ideal. The [henselization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Algebra.html#TauCeti.Henselization.algebra) is built as a colimit over a small model of the filtered category of étale neighbourhoods of `(R, I)`, and it keeps the [same quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Residue.html#TauCeti.Henselization.quotient_bijective) `R/I` (TauCeti#17460, TauCeti#18534). Its universal property, and the fact that a henselian pair is its own henselization, are not yet proved. Groundwork for them came alongside: [idempotents lift uniquely along a henselian pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/Idempotent.html#TauCeti.HenselianRing.existsUnique_isIdempotentElem_mk_eq), and sections of unramified algebras split off idempotent factors.

Scheme Frobenius grew from a single map into a working API. There is now the [iterated relative Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Twist.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.iterateRelativeFrobenius), which [commutes with base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Twist.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isPullback_iterateRelativeFrobenius) and is a homomorphism on group schemes, and a [`q`-power Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Iterate.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.qPowerFrobenius) over a finite field. The [absolute Frobenius is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Finite.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isFinite_frobenius) on a scheme locally of finite type over a perfect ring. [Normal schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Normal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsNormalScheme) arrived too. On a locally Noetherian one, the [local rings at codimension-one points are discrete valuation rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Normal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsNormalScheme.isDiscreteValuationRing_stalk). They come with a [ramification index at a point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationIdx.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationIdxAt) and with the [surjectivity on codimension-one points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.codimensionOneMap_surjective) of surjective quasi-finite morphisms.

The commutative algebra underneath grew as well. Universally catenary rings appeared, among them [polynomial rings over universally catenary rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/UniversallyCatenary.html#MvPolynomial.isUniversallyCatenary), along with depth bounds and [Cohen–Macaulay modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CohenMacaulay/Basic.html#Module.IsCohenMacaulay). Also new: [a regular domain is integrally closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Normal.html#TauCeti.IsRegularRing.isIntegrallyClosed), a regular locus, flat base change of annihilators and cokernels, and Proj under arbitrary extension of coefficients.

## SchemeAndStackFoundations: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`9fc5d5a` to `c1c6773`)

Japanese and Nagata rings arrived, opening §0.20. A domain is [Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Japanese.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsJapanese) when its integral closure in every finite extension of its fraction field, inseparable ones included, is a finite module. It is N-1 when this holds for the fraction field itself, and a ring is [Nagata](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsNagata) when it is Noetherian and each quotient by a prime is Japanese. Both properties [pass to localizations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Japanese.html#TauCeti.Ring.isJapanese_of_isLocalization), and [finite algebras over a Nagata ring are Nagata](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsNagata.of_finite). In dimension at most one, [Nagata is the same as Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.isNagata_iff_isJapanese), which already covers `ℤ`, rings of integers and Dedekind domains with perfect fraction field. The harder Stacks results are still missing: Nagata rings are universally Japanese, finite-type algebras over them are Nagata, and complete local rings are Nagata.

The normal-schemes strand of §0.30 is now nearly complete. [Ramification indices multiply in towers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationIdx/Normal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationIdxAt_comp_of_coheight_eq_one) at points lying over a codimension-one point of a normal target. Finite dominant maps [preserve codimension-one points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/FiniteType.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.coheight_apply_eq_one) when the target is of finite type over a field, and [also when it is normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/Normal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.coheight_apply_eq_one_of_coheight_eq_one), by going down. A [regular quotient of a regular local ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Quotient.html#Ideal.exists_regularSystem_of_quotient) is cut out by part of a regular system of parameters. The parabola and cusp serve as the check.

[Regular schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular) also appeared, the first piece of Layer 4 (§4.13). They come with affine and open-cover characterisations, are [normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular.isNormal), and [stay regular under smooth morphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular.of_smooth). The henselization gained its uniqueness half: two maps out of it agree once the ideal lands in the Jacobson radical of the target. Existence of lifts is still open.
