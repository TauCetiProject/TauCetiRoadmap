### Named results

- **The henselization of a pair is henselian** — for any ring `R` and ideal `I`, the colimit of the étale neighbourhoods of `(R, I)` is henselian along the extended ideal, with no Noetherian or local hypothesis ([`Henselization.henselian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Henselian.html#TauCeti.Henselization.henselian)).
- **The universal property of the henselization** — every map of pairs from `(R, I)` to a henselian pair factors uniquely through the henselization ([`Henselization.existsUnique_lift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_lift)). In particular a henselian pair is its own henselization, and for a local ring the construction is the classical henselization ([`existsUnique_isLocalHom`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Local.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_isLocalHom)).
- **Resolution of curves** — if `Y` is integral, locally Noetherian, of dimension at most one, and its normalization in its function field is finite, then that normalization is regular ([`isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint)) and a modification of `Y` ([`isModification_fromNormalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Modification.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isModification_fromNormalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint)). As the roadmap intends, finiteness is a hypothesis carrying the Nagata input.
- **Zariski's main theorem, global algebraic form** — a quasi-finite finite-type algebra is covered, up to localization, by a module-finite subalgebra ([`Algebra.QuasiFinite.exists_fg_subalgebra_and_span_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/QuasiFinite/ZariskisMainTheorem.html#Algebra.QuasiFinite.exists_fg_subalgebra_and_span_eq_top)). It is the input for lifting maps out of the henselization.
- **Frobenius is a universal homeomorphism** — the absolute, relative and iterated scheme Frobenius are integral and universal homeomorphisms, with no finiteness hypothesis ([`Scheme.universally_isHomeomorph_frobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/UniversalHomeomorphism.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.universally_isHomeomorph_frobenius)). The absolute Frobenius is also finite over a perfect base ([`Scheme.isFinite_frobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Finite.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isFinite_frobenius)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Japanese and Nagata rings** ([`Ring.IsJapanese`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Japanese.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsJapanese), [`Ring.IsNagata`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsNagata)) are stable under localization, quotients and finite extensions, and both properties are local on `Spec R`. Polynomial rings over a field are Japanese, and in characteristic `p` the Japanese test reduces to purely inseparable extensions. These are the hypotheses that make normalization finite.
- **Serre's conditions** ([`Module.SatisfiesSerreS`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/SerreCondition.html#Module.SatisfiesSerreS), [`Ring.SatisfiesSerreR`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/SerreCondition.html#Ring.SatisfiesSerreR)) come with `(S_1)` as "no embedded primes", "all `(S_n)`" as Cohen–Macaulay, and reduced as `(R_0)` plus `(S_1)`. With these in place, the next step is Serre's normality criterion.
- **Geometrically regular algebras and regular ring maps** ([`Excellence.GeometricallyRegular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/GeometricallyRegular.html#TauCeti.Excellence.GeometricallyRegular), [`Excellence.RegularAlgebraMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/RegularAlgebraMap.html#TauCeti.Excellence.RegularAlgebraMap)) are the first two definitions of §0.21. They cover smooth algebras and localizations and agree with each other over a field. G-rings, J-2 rings and excellence are built from them.

## The frontier

- **Serre's normality criterion (§4.13)** — `(R_n)` and `(S_n)` are defined and regular rings satisfy every `(R_n)`. What remains is "normal iff `(R_1)` and `(S_2)`" for Noetherian rings.
- **Henselization properties (§0.13)** — the universal property is done. Ind-étale algebras, flatness of `R → R^h`, `R/I^n ≅ R^h/(I^h)^n` and Noetherianity of `R^h` are not yet started.

- **Excellent rings (§0.21)** — G-rings, J-2 rings, quasi-excellence and excellence remain, along with the preservation of reducedness and normality under regular maps.
- **Rest of relative Frobenius (§0.29)** — it remains to show that relative Frobenius is an isomorphism for étale morphisms and finite locally free of rank `p^d` for smooth ones. These gate perfect schemes (§§0.24–0.26).
