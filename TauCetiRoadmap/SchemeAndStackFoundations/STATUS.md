## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Most of the work is in Layer 0, which is partial. The henselization of a pair is henselian, and normal schemes with codimension-one points and ramification are nearly complete. Frobenius, catenary rings and Cohen–Macaulay modules are largely in place, and Japanese and Nagata rings have begun. Layer 4 has its first piece, regular schemes. Layers 1, 2, 3 and 5 are untouched.

### Named results

- **The henselization of a pair is henselian** — for any ring `R` and ideal `I`, the colimit of the étale neighbourhoods of `(R, I)` is henselian along the extended ideal, with no Noetherian or local hypothesis ([`Henselization.henselian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Henselian.html#TauCeti.Henselization.henselian)).
- **Ramification indices multiply in towers** — for a dominant map from an integral scheme, at a point over a codimension-one point of a locally Noetherian normal target, `e_x(f ≫ g) = e_x(f) · e_{f(x)}(g)` ([`ramificationIdxAt_comp_of_coheight_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationIdx/Normal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationIdxAt_comp_of_coheight_eq_one)).
- **Finite maps preserve codimension-one points** — a finite dominant map of integral schemes sends coheight-one points to coheight-one points when the target is normal and locally Noetherian, by going down ([`coheight_apply_eq_one_of_coheight_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/CodimensionOne/Normal.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.coheight_apply_eq_one_of_coheight_eq_one)). The same holds when the target is locally of finite type over a field.
- **Finiteness of Frobenius** — on a scheme locally of finite type over a perfect ring of characteristic `p`, the absolute Frobenius is finite ([`Scheme.isFinite_frobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Finite.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isFinite_frobenius)).
- **Regular quotients of regular local rings** — if `R` and `R ⧸ I` are both regular local, then `I` is generated by an initial segment of a regular system of parameters (Stacks 00NR, [`exists_regularSystem_of_quotient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Quotient.html#Ideal.exists_regularSystem_of_quotient)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The henselization algebra** ([`Henselization.algebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Algebra.html#TauCeti.Henselization.algebra)) is built as a colimit over a small model of the étale neighbourhoods. Maps out of it are now known to be unique once `I` lands in the Jacobson radical, which is the uniqueness half of the universal property.
- **Japanese and Nagata rings** ([`Ring.IsJapanese`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Japanese.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsJapanese), [`Ring.IsNagata`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Nagata.html#TauCeti.Ring.IsNagata)) are stable under localization, quotients and finite extensions. In dimension at most one, Nagata coincides with Japanese. These are the finiteness hypotheses that resolution of curves (§4.13) and finite normalization need.
- **Regular schemes** ([`IsRegular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular/Scheme.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsRegular)) are locally Noetherian with regular stalks, characterised on affine opens and open covers. They are normal and stable under smooth morphisms.

## The frontier

- **Existence in the henselization's universal property (§0.12)** — uniqueness of maps is done. Lifting a map to a henselian pair through an étale neighbourhood is what remains, and §0.13's ind-étale and flatness results wait on it.
- **Nagata rings at full strength (§0.20)** — the open results are that finite-type algebras over Nagata rings are Nagata, that Nagata equals universally Japanese for Noetherian rings, and the complete local case. Together they feed `nagata_normalization_finite` and the finiteness hypothesis in resolution of curves.
- **Excellent rings (§0.21)** — geometric regularity, G-rings, J-2 rings and excellence are still to come. So is "Cohen–Macaulay rings are universally catenary", which closes §0.16.
- **Rest of scheme Frobenius (§0.29)** — the universal-homeomorphism, étale and smooth-rank statements are open. They gate perfect schemes (§§0.24–0.26).
- **Resolution of curves (§4.13)** — regular and normal schemes and DVR stalks in codimension one are all available. What remains is proving that a finite normalization of a one-dimensional integral Noetherian scheme is regular.
