## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All six layers are partial. Layer 0 is large: henselization, perfection, scheme Frobenius, normal schemes, and the Cohen–Macaulay and Nagata predicates. The later layers have their first real theorems: torsor classes compared with `H¹`, Leray acyclicity, quasi-coherence of higher direct images, fpqc descent of morphisms, and the localization sequence for cycles. None of the summit theorems (Keel–Mori, Grothendieck duality, Schlessinger, de Jong, Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch) has begun.

### Named results

- **The universal property of the henselization.** For any ring and ideal, the henselization is a henselian pair through which every map to a henselian pair factors uniquely ([`Henselization.existsUnique_lift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_lift)).
- **Torsor classes and `H¹`.** For an abelian sheaf `F` on a site, isomorphism classes of `F`-torsors are in bijection with `H¹(F)` (Stacks, Lemma 21.4.3), compatibly with the connecting map of a short exact sequence ([`NonabelianH1.equivSheafH`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/SheafH.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1.equivSheafH)).
- **Quasi-coherence of higher direct images.** For a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism, every `Rⁿf_*` of a quasi-coherent module is quasi-coherent ([`isQuasicoherent_higherDirectImage`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/HigherDirectImage/Quasicoherent.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isQuasicoherent_higherDirectImage)).
- **Leray acyclicity.** For a morphism of sites and an abelian sheaf with no higher direct images, the edge map `Hⁿ(C, f_* F) → Hⁿ(D, F)` is bijective in every degree ([`lerayEdge_sheafAdjunctionContinuous_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Leray/Edge.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H.lerayEdge_sheafAdjunctionContinuous_bijective)).
- **The perfection of a scheme.** For `X` over `𝔽_p`, `X^perf` is perfect, maps to `X` by a universal homeomorphism, and every map from a perfect scheme to `X` lifts uniquely to it ([`Scheme.bijective_comp_fromPerfection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.bijective_comp_fromPerfection)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Chow groups** ([`Chow.ChowGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup)) are `k`-cycles modulo rational equivalence. Cycles now have pushforward, restriction to opens, and the [localization sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/OpenRestriction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.range_cyclesPushforward_eq_ker_cyclesRestrict) `Z_n(Z) → Z_n(X) → Z_n(U)`.
- **fpqc descent of morphisms** ([`qcohPseudofunctor_isPrestack_fpqcTopology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/QuasicoherentSheaf/Prestack.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Descent.qcohPseudofunctor_isPrestack_fpqcTopology)): quasi-coherent modules form a prestack for the fpqc topology. Together with sections forming an fpqc sheaf on the big site, this is the morphism half of §1.1's stack property.
- **The formal completion** ([`Scheme.formalCompletion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicThickeningSystem/FormalCompletion.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.formalCompletion)) of a scheme along a closed subscheme is an adic thickening system, agreeing for `Spec R` with the formal spectrum of the completion. Coherent formal modules on it pull back, and isomorphisms between them can be checked on an open cover.

## The frontier

- **Effective fpqc descent of quasi-coherent modules (§1.1).** Morphisms descend along every fpqc covering. What remains is effectivity of descent data for objects, which would make `QCoh` an fpqc stack.
- **Proper pushforward on Chow groups (§5.2).** Cycle pushforward and the order of a norm are in place. What remains is that proper pushforward preserves rational equivalence, then flat pullback, so that the cycle-level localization sequence passes to `CH_k`.
- **The Leray spectral sequence (§2.2).** What remains is `H^p(D, R^q f_* F) ⇒ H^{p+q}(C, F)` itself, its relative form, and the Čech-to-cohomology spectral sequence.
- **Noetherian approximation (§0.9).** The affine case is done. What remains is the quasi-compact quasi-separated case, then descent of finitely presented schemes and their properties to a finite stage.
- **Topological invariance of the étale site (§0.23a).** Base change along a universal homeomorphism is full and faithful on étale schemes. What remains is essential surjectivity, then §0.24's equivalence of étale sites for the perfection.
