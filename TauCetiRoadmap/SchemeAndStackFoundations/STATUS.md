### Named results

- **The affine criterion for pseudo-coherence** — a module sheaf is pseudo-coherent exactly when it is quasi-coherent and its sections over every affine open are pseudo-coherent (Stacks, Lemma 36.10.2). An example over any non-field local domain shows the quasi-coherence clause is needed ([`isPseudoCoherent_iff_forall_affineOpens`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/PseudoCoherent/Affine.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isPseudoCoherent_iff_forall_affineOpens)).
- **Finite étale algebras over a henselian pair** — for `(R, I)` henselian, base change to `R/I` is an equivalence of categories of finite étale algebras (Stacks, Lemma 15.13.2). In particular, finite étale algebras over any henselization are those over `R/I` ([`isEquivalence_baseChange_of_surjective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselian/FiniteEtale.html#CommAlgCat.FiniteEtale.isEquivalence_baseChange_of_surjective)).
- **Torsor classes and `H¹`** — for an abelian sheaf `F` on a site, isomorphism classes of `F`-torsors are in bijection with `H¹(F)`, compatibly with connecting maps and with change of coefficients ([`NonabelianH1.equivSheafH`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/SheafH.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1.equivSheafH)).
- **Quasi-coherence of higher direct images** — for a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism, every `Rⁿf_*` of a quasi-coherent module is quasi-coherent ([`isQuasicoherent_higherDirectImage`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/HigherDirectImage/Quasicoherent.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isQuasicoherent_higherDirectImage)).
- **The divisor class comparison** — for an integral Noetherian scheme of dimension `n` locally of finite type over a field, the Weil divisor class group is the Chow group `CH_{n-1}` ([`classGroupEquivChowGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/DivisorClass.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.classGroupEquivChowGroup)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Chow groups** ([`Chow.ChowGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup)), `k`-cycles modulo rational equivalence. Cycles have pushforward and the [localization sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/OpenRestriction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.range_cyclesPushforward_eq_ker_cyclesRestrict) `Z_n(Z) → Z_n(X) → Z_n(U)`, which is what proper pushforward and flat pullback on `CH_k` will build on.
- **fpqc descent of morphisms** ([`qcohPseudofunctor_isPrestack_fpqcTopology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/QuasicoherentSheaf/Prestack.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Descent.qcohPseudofunctor_isPrestack_fpqcTopology)): quasi-coherent modules form an fpqc prestack, the morphism half of §1.1's stack property.
- **Formal spectra and completions.** The [formal spectrum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicThickeningSystem/Spf.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spf) of an adic ring and the [formal completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicThickeningSystem/FormalCompletion.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.formalCompletion) of a scheme along a closed subscheme are adic thickening systems. On `Spf`, the global sections of a coherent formal module form a finite adic system, the input to the theorem on formal functions.

## The frontier

- **Effective fpqc descent of quasi-coherent modules (§1.1).** Morphisms descend along every fpqc covering. What remains is effectivity of descent data for objects.
- **Proper pushforward on Chow groups (§5.2).** Cycle pushforward and Fulton's order-of-a-norm lemma are in place. What remains is that proper pushforward preserves rational equivalence, then flat pullback, so the localization sequence passes to `CH_k`.
- **Quotients by étale equivalence relations (§1.5).** Étale equivalence relations and their restrictions along étale maps exist. What remains is the quotient sheaf and the proof that it is an algebraic space.
- **The Leray spectral sequence (§2.2).** What remains is `H^p(D, R^q f_* F) ⇒ H^{p+q}(C, F)` itself, its relative form, and the Čech-to-cohomology spectral sequence.
- **Elkik's smooth lifting (§0.14).** Henselian pairs are characterised and finite étale algebras lift. What remains is lifting `R`-algebra maps `A → R/I` to `A → R` for smooth `A`.
