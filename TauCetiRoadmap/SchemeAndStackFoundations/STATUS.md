## Where this roadmap stands

The henselization of a pair, perfection of schemes, scheme Frobenius, normal schemes and the Cohen–Macaulay and Nagata predicates are in place, and the rewritten README now treats them as Tau Ceti's carriers. Every later layer is partial, with its first theorems: fpqc descent of sections, the Nisnevich henselization criterion, degree laws for vector bundles, formal completion, and cycle pushforward. None of the summit theorems (Keel–Mori, Grothendieck duality, Schlessinger, de Jong, Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch) has begun.

### Named results

- **The universal property of the henselization.** For any ring and ideal, the henselization is a henselian pair through which every map to a henselian pair factors uniquely ([`Henselization.existsUnique_lift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_lift)). It also commutes with integral base change ([`Henselization.integralBaseChangeEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Integral.html#TauCeti.Henselization.integralBaseChangeEquiv)).
- **The perfection of a scheme.** For `X` over `𝔽_p`, `X^perf` is perfect, maps to `X` by a universal homeomorphism, and every map from a perfect scheme to `X` lifts uniquely to it ([`Scheme.bijective_comp_fromPerfection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.bijective_comp_fromPerfection)).
- **The henselization criterion for Nisnevich coverings.** A family of étale maps is a Nisnevich covering exactly when, at each point, the spectrum of the henselized local ring lifts through one of them ([`isNisnevichCovering_iff_henselization`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Nisnevich/Henselization.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isNisnevichCovering_iff_henselization)).
- **fpqc descent of sections.** For quasi-coherent `F`, the presheaf `T ↦ Γ(T, h^*F)` on the big site is an fpqc sheaf ([`isSheaf_bigPresheaf_fpqcTopology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Big/Sheaf.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.isSheaf_bigPresheaf_fpqcTopology)).
- **Resolution of curves.** For `Y` integral, locally Noetherian, of dimension at most one, with finite normalization, that normalization is regular ([`isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint)). Finiteness of the normalization is still a hypothesis.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Chow groups** ([`Chow.ChowGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup)) are `k`-cycles modulo rational equivalence on locally Noetherian schemes. The library also identifies the divisor class group of an integral scheme of finite type over a field with `CH_{n−1}` (`classGroupEquivChowGroup`). The functorial [pushforward of cycles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Pushforward.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.cyclesMap) is the start of proper pushforward.
- **The formal completion** ([`Scheme.formalCompletion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicThickeningSystem/FormalCompletion.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.formalCompletion)) of a scheme along a closed subscheme is an adic thickening system, and for `Spec R` it agrees with the formal spectrum of the completion. This is the object for the theorem on formal functions and algebraization.
- **Functorial torsor classes** ([`NonabelianH1.map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/Map.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1.map)) via the contracted product. With the exact sequences of a group extension, they make `H¹` usable as the roadmap's single carrier of torsor classes.

## The frontier

- **Topological invariance of the étale site (§0.23a).** What remains is essential surjectivity of base change along a universal homeomorphism, cover preservation, and the comparison data. This then gives §0.24's equivalence of étale sites for the perfection.
- **Proper pushforward on Chow groups (§5.2).** Cycle pushforward exists. What remains is that it respects rational equivalence for proper maps, then flat pullback and the localisation sequence.
- **fpqc descent of quasi-coherent modules (§1.1).** Descent of morphisms along an affine faithfully flat map is proved. What remains is effective descent of objects and gluing to general fpqc coverings.
- **Torsor classes and `H¹` (§2.3).** What remains is pullback along morphisms of sites and the comparison `equivSheafH` with abelian sheaf cohomology.
- **Degree laws on curves (§3.3).** What remains is the tensor, dual, determinant and pullback formulas, and removing the finite-cohomology hypotheses. Riemann–Roch for vector bundles on curves comes after these.
