## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is partial but substantial: henselization, scheme Frobenius, universal homeomorphisms, catenary rings and normal schemes are finished, while excellence, Nagata rings at full strength and perfection are open. Every other layer has now started, but each has only its first definitions and a few results.

### Named results

- **The universal property of the henselization** — every map of pairs from `(R, I)` to a henselian pair factors uniquely through the henselization, with no Noetherian or local hypothesis ([`Henselization.existsUnique_lift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_lift)). The henselization is also [ind-étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/IndEtale.html#TauCeti.Henselization.indEtale) and [Noetherian when `R` is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Henselization.isNoetherianRing).
- **Resolution of curves** — for `Y` integral, locally Noetherian, of dimension at most one, with finite normalization in its function field, that normalization is regular ([`isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint)) and a modification of `Y`. Finiteness is a hypothesis carrying the Nagata input. It holds when the [affine coordinate rings are Japanese](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Finite.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isFinite_fromNormalization_of_isJapanese).
- **Relative Frobenius of étale and smooth morphisms** — for an étale morphism it is an [isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Etale.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isIso_relativeFrobenius), and for a smooth morphism of relative dimension `n` it is finite and flat of [rank `p^n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Smooth.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.finrank_relativeFrobenius_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension).
- **Cohen–Macaulay rings are universally catenary** — a Noetherian ring whose local rings are Cohen–Macaulay is universally catenary ([`isUniversallyCatenary_of_forall_isCohenMacaulay`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CohenMacaulay/Catenary.html#TauCeti.Excellence.isUniversallyCatenary_of_forall_isCohenMacaulay)). In particular every Noetherian domain of dimension at most one is.
- **The criterion for universal homeomorphisms** — a morphism of schemes is a universal homeomorphism exactly when it is integral, universally injective and surjective ([`isUniversalHomeomorphism_iff_isIntegralHom_universallyInjective_surjective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/UniversalHomeomorphism.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isUniversalHomeomorphism_iff_isIntegralHom_universallyInjective_surjective)). Frobenius and thickenings are instances.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Chow groups** ([`Chow.ChowGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup)) are `k`-cycles modulo locally finite sums of pushforwards of [principal cycles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicCycle/Principal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AlgebraicCycle.div), on any locally Noetherian scheme. Pushforward along closed immersions descends to them. Proper pushforward and flat pullback (§5.2) can be built on this.
- **Algebraic spaces** ([`Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace)) are fppf sheaves on schemes with representable diagonal and an étale atlas, and every scheme is one. The rest of Layer 1 builds on this definition.
- **Torsors and their classes** ([`Sheaf.Torsor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/Torsor.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.Torsor), [`Sheaf.NonabelianH1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/Torsor.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1)) give the roadmap's single carrier of torsor classes, with a torsor trivial exactly when it has a global section. Layer 1's geometric torsors and Layer 3's Picard torsors are meant to be instances.

## The frontier

- **Serre's normality criterion (§4.13)**: `(R_n)`, `(S_n)` and Cohen–Macaulay rings are in place. "Normal iff `(R_1)` and `(S_2)`" for Noetherian rings remains.
- **The rest of §0.13**: the recognition theorem, integral base change in general, henselization at a prime, and `ℤ_{(p)}^h` inside `ℤ_p`.
- **Nagata rings at full strength (§0.20)**: finite-type algebras over Nagata rings and complete local rings. These would remove the finiteness hypothesis from resolution of curves.
- **Formal smoothness and the lifting criterion (§4.1)**: thickenings are defined, but formally smooth and étale morphisms and torsors of lifts are not. Deformation functors (§4.3) also need the category of complete local algebras from §4.2.
- **Degrees on curves (§3.3)**: tensor, determinant and dual laws and Riemann–Roch remain. The degree truncates `χ` at `H¹`, so it needs vanishing above `H¹` on curves to match the classical one.
