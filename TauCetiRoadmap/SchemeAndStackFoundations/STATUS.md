## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is partial but now very substantial: the henselization of a pair, Cohen–Macaulay catenarity, scheme Frobenius and normal schemes are complete, and perfect schemes have their perfection and its universal property. Every other layer has now started, but only with its opening definitions: algebraic spaces, the Nisnevich topology and torsor classes, vector-bundle degrees, the Artinian coefficient category and Chow groups.

### Named results

- **The perfection of a scheme** — for `X` over `𝔽_p`, the perfection `X^perf` is perfect, maps to `X` by a universal homeomorphism, and every map from a perfect scheme to `X` lifts uniquely to `X^perf` ([`Scheme.bijective_comp_fromPerfection`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Perfection.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.bijective_comp_fromPerfection)).
- **Relative Frobenius for étale and smooth morphisms** — relative Frobenius is an isomorphism for étale morphisms ([`Scheme.Hom.isIso_relativeFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Etale.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isIso_relativeFrobenius)), and finite flat of rank `p^n` for smooth morphisms of relative dimension `n` ([`finrank_relativeFrobenius_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Frobenius/Smooth.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.finrank_relativeFrobenius_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension)), proved through étale coordinates.
- **The universal property of the henselization** — for any ring and ideal, the henselization is a henselian pair through which every map of pairs to a henselian pair factors uniquely ([`Henselization.existsUnique_lift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/Lift.html#TauCeti.Henselization.existsUnique_lift)). It is also ind-étale over the base ([`Henselization.indEtale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Henselization/IndEtale.html#TauCeti.Henselization.indEtale)).
- **Cohen–Macaulay rings are universally catenary** — a Noetherian ring whose local rings are all Cohen–Macaulay is universally catenary ([`Excellence.isUniversallyCatenary_of_forall_isCohenMacaulay`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/CohenMacaulay/Catenary.html#TauCeti.Excellence.isUniversallyCatenary_of_forall_isCohenMacaulay)).
- **Resolution of curves** — for `Y` integral, locally Noetherian, of dimension at most one, with finite normalization, that normalization is regular ([`isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint)) and a modification of `Y`. Finiteness is still a hypothesis carrying the Nagata input.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Chow groups** ([`Chow.ChowGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Chow.ChowGroup)) are `k`-cycles modulo the cycles `Σ (i_j)_* div(r_j)` of locally finite families of integral subschemes, exactly the roadmap's contract. They have pushforward along closed immersions, the first piece of proper pushforward.
- **Algebraic spaces** ([`Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace)) are fppf sheaves on schemes with representable diagonal and an étale atlas. Schemes are examples. This opens the `Spaces` strand of Layer 1.
- **Torsor classes** ([`Sheaf.NonabelianH1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/NonabelianCohomology/Torsor.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.NonabelianH1)) form the pointed set of isomorphism classes of torsors under a sheaf of groups, with a torsor trivial exactly when it has a global section. The roadmap makes this the single carrier for torsor classes in Layers 1 and 3.

## The frontier

- **Properties of the perfection (§0.24)** — what remains is that perfection reflects quasi-compactness, separatedness, affineness and integrality and preserves immersions and étale maps, plus the equivalence of small étale sites, which needs §0.23a.
- **Henselization properties (§0.13)** — what remains is `R/I^n ≅ R^h/(I^h)^n`, Noetherianity of `R^h` for Noetherian `R`, henselization at a prime, and the `ℤ_(p)` example.
