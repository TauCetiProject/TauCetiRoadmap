### Named results

- **Extension of tracial states from closed ideals** — every tracial state of a closed two-sided ideal extends uniquely to a tracial state of the whole algebra; [`existsUnique_isTracialState_apply_coe_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/IdealExtension.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsTracialState.existsUnique_isTracialState_apply_coe_eq).
- **Faithfulness on simple algebras** — the trace kernel `N_τ` is a closed ideal equal to the kernel of the GNS representation, so every tracial state of a simple C*-algebra is faithful; [`isFaithfulTrace_of_forall_isClosed_eq_bot_or_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/TraceKernel.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsTracialState.isFaithfulTrace_of_forall_isClosed_eq_bot_or_eq_top).
- **The vector trace** — for a tracial state of a unital algebra, `x ↦ ⟪Ω, xΩ⟫` is a tracial state of the GNS von Neumann algebra `π_τ(A)''`, and it is faithful; [`isKMSState_gnsVectorState`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/VectorState.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState.isKMSState_gnsVectorState).
- **Pullback of amenable traces** — composing an amenable, quasidiagonal or uniform amenable trace with a star homomorphism gives one of the same kind whenever the result is a state, which covers restriction to unital subalgebras and lifting from quotients; [`pullbackFunctional_mem_amenableTraces`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/AmenableTrace.html#TauCeti.pullbackFunctional_mem_amenableTraces).
- **The generating-set criterion** — a net of completely positive contractions into matrices that is asymptotically multiplicative in 2-norm on `(s, s*)` for a generating set is asymptotically multiplicative everywhere; [`tendsto_normalizedTwoNorm_map_mul_sub_of_adjoin`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap/AsymptoticDomain.html#TauCeti.tendsto_normalizedTwoNorm_map_mul_sub_of_adjoin).

## The frontier

- **Convexity and closure of `AT`, `AT_QD`, `UAT` (§3.3)** — block sums of approximants into `M_{k₁} ⊕ M_{k₂}`. The block-additivity lemmas for the trace and 2-norm are already there, but the c.p.c. block sum of §2.1 is not.

The u.c.p. case needs the Choi–Effros factorization and the unitalization estimates of §2.1. The norm version needs the `ψ` with the inverse square root, beyond the existing `QφQ` rounding bound.
- **Finite-dimensional algebras with tracial states (§1.6)** — rational embeddings into matrix algebras and trace-preserving conditional expectations. These feed §3.2's finite-dimensional approximants and Layer 7.
- **The rest of the GNS data (§1.4)** — `JM_τJ = M_τ'`, normality and uniqueness of the vector trace, and the nonunital case. These lean on the standard-form and normal-functional layers of VonNeumannAlgebras.
- **Compressions and Ozawa's lemma (§2.3–§2.4)** — the Hilbert–Schmidt commutator identity and matrix maps with prescribed trace, the route to the hypertrace direction of Connes–Kirchberg. Ozawa's lemma also needs the Powers–Størmer inequality from VonNeumannAlgebras V13b.
