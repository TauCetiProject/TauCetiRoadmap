## AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-19 (`349dfaa` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first piece has landed: the [tracial state space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.tracialStates) `T(A)` of a unital or nonunital C*-algebra, with [tracial states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsTracialState) defined as the `0`-KMS states of the trivial dynamics (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/617). That choice is identified with [the KMS state space at every inverse temperature](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.tracialStates_eq_kmsStates), so [convexity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.convex_tracialStates), the norm-one closure statement, and [weak-* compactness for unital algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.isCompact_tracialStates) all come straight from the existing KMS theory.

The two characterizations the roadmap asks for in §1.1 are proved: the [polarization criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.isTracialState_iff_map_star_mul_self), that a state is tracial exactly when `τ(a*a) = τ(aa*)` for every `a`, and the [unitary criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.isTracialState_iff_map_unitary_conj), invariance under inner automorphisms on unital algebras. Tracial states [pull back along unital star homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsTracialState.comp), so they restrict to unital subalgebras, and on commutative algebras [every state is tracial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.tracialStates_eq_stateSpace). One of the roadmap's checks is also in: [a unital algebra containing two isometries with complementary range projections has no tracial state](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.not_isTracialState_of_mul_star_add_mul_star_eq_one), which is the usual argument that `B(ℓ²)` has none. The rest of Layer 1 has not started, including the trace kernel, the unitization and ideal extensions, the GNS data and the matrix 2-norm.

## AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (`b235000` to `522d48f`)

The roadmap's central objects now exist: [amenable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/AmenableTrace.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsAmenableTrace), [quasidiagonal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/AmenableTrace.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsQuasidiagonalTrace) and [uniform amenable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/AmenableTrace.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsUniformAmenableTrace) traces, each defined by completely positive contractions into matrix algebras that are almost multiplicative and almost recover the trace (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/2458). They come with the [inclusion of quasidiagonal traces among amenable ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/AmenableTrace.html#TauCeti.quasidiagonalTraces_subset_amenableTraces), [pullback along star homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/AmenableTrace.html#TauCeti.pullbackFunctional_mem_amenableTraces), and the matrix algebras as [the first algebras admitting a faithful amenable trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/AmenableTrace.html#CStarMatrix.admitsFaithfulAmenableTrace). The net, sequence and unital reformulations, convexity, closure and tensor products are not there yet.

Most of Layer 1 filled in around them. The [trace kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/TraceKernel.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.traceKernel) is a closed ideal equal to [the kernel of the GNS representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/TraceKernel.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.traceKernel_eq_ker_gnsNonUnitalStarAlgHom), so [tracial states of simple algebras are faithful](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/TraceKernel.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsTracialState.isFaithfulTrace_of_forall_isClosed_eq_bot_or_eq_top). [Every tracial state of a closed ideal extends uniquely to the whole algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/IdealExtension.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsTracialState.existsUnique_isTracialState_apply_coe_eq). On the GNS side there are the [GNS von Neumann algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/GNS/VonNeumannAlgebra.html#PositiveLinearMap.gnsVonNeumannAlgebra) with its [vector trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/VectorState.html#TauCeti.CStarAction.IsKMSState.isKMSState_gnsVectorState) (unital algebras only so far), the right representation, the [modular conjugation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/GNS/Conjugation.html#PositiveLinearMap.gnsConj) and the [tensor functional](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/GNS/ProductRepresentation.html#PositiveLinearMap.gnsTensorFunctional). The [normalized matrix 2-norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Matrix/Trace.html#CStarMatrix.normalizedTwoNorm) has its operator-norm comparisons, block additivity and tensor multiplicativity, and [norming sets of states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Norming.html#TauCeti.closedConvexHull_eq_stateSpace_iff) are characterized.

Layer 2 has begun with multiplicativity defects, the [Kadison–Schwarz inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap/Defect.html#CompletelyPositiveMap.star_map_mul_map_le_of_norm_map_le) for contractions into matrices, and asymptotic multiplicative domains with [the generating-set criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap/AsymptoticDomain.html#TauCeti.tendsto_normalizedTwoNorm_map_mul_sub_of_adjoin). Compressions of `C(𝕋)` to trigonometric polynomials show [the norm domain can be proper](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap/CircleCompression.html#TauCeti.asymptoticNormDomain_circleCompression_ne_top) when the 2-norm domain is everything.
