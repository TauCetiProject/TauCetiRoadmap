## AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-19 (`349dfaa` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first piece has landed: the [tracial state space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.tracialStates) `T(A)` of a unital or nonunital C*-algebra, with [tracial states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsTracialState) defined as the `0`-KMS states of the trivial dynamics (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/617). That choice is identified with [the KMS state space at every inverse temperature](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.tracialStates_eq_kmsStates), so [convexity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.convex_tracialStates), the norm-one closure statement, and [weak-* compactness for unital algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.isCompact_tracialStates) all come straight from the existing KMS theory.

The two characterizations the roadmap asks for in §1.1 are proved: the [polarization criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.isTracialState_iff_map_star_mul_self), that a state is tracial exactly when `τ(a*a) = τ(aa*)` for every `a`, and the [unitary criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.isTracialState_iff_map_unitary_conj), invariance under inner automorphisms on unital algebras. Tracial states [pull back along unital star homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.IsTracialState.comp), so they restrict to unital subalgebras, and on commutative algebras [every state is tracial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#TauCeti.tracialStates_eq_stateSpace). One of the roadmap's checks is also in: [a unital algebra containing two isometries with complementary range projections has no tracial state](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Tracial.html#PositiveContinuousLinearMap.not_isTracialState_of_mul_star_add_mul_star_eq_one), which is the usual argument that `B(ℓ²)` has none. The rest of Layer 1 has not started, including the trace kernel, the unitization and ideal extensions, the GNS data and the matrix 2-norm.
