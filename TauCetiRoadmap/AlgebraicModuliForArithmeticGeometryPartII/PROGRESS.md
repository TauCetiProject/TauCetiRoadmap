## AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`d760e08` to `522d48f`)

The first work on this roadmap sets up numerical equivalence of line bundles. A class on a proper scheme over a field is [numerically trivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/NumericallyTrivial.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.NumericallyTrivial) when it has degree zero on every integral closed curve, and [relatively numerically trivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/NumericallyTrivial.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.RelNumericallyTrivial) over a base when this holds on every fibre. The two notions [agree over Spec k](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/NumericallyTrivial.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.relNumericallyTrivial_iff_numericallyTrivial), and pullbacks from the base are relatively trivial. The quotients Num(X/k) and Num(X/S) follow. Num(X/S) [embeds into the product of its fibre groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/NumericalClassGroup.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.numericalFiberRestriction_injective), so it is torsion-free and [injects into N¹(X/S)_ℚ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/NumericalSpace.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.toRelRatNumericalClasses_injective). The rational degree pairing with curves in fibres is [nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/NumericalSpace.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.eq_zero_of_forall_relNumericalIntersectionNumberBaseChange_eq_zero), and [Num(ℙ¹_k/k) is ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/NumericalClassGroup.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.projectiveLine.numericalClassDegreeEquiv) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/2759). The finiteness theorems, invariance under extension of the base field and testing over closed points are not here yet. The only comparison across fields is for one scheme viewed over a finite subextension.

The curve pairing extends to Cartier divisors with rational or real coefficients. Principal divisors pair to zero, so [linear equivalence implies numerical equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/CartierDivisor.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.CartierDivisor.principalSubmodule_le_numericallyTrivialSubmodule). On a Noetherian normal integral scheme the [Cartier-to-Weil map is injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/CartierDivisor/Factorial.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.CartierDivisor.toWeilDivisor_eq_zero_iff), by an [algebraic Hartogs principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme/Regular.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.exists_germToFunctionField_eq_of_ord_nonneg_of_isIntegrallyClosed). As a result a ℚ-Cartier Weil divisor has a [well-defined rational degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/CartierDivisor.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.CartierDivisor.qCartierIntersectionNumber_eq_div) on each curve. [Nefness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/Nef.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.IsNef) over a field arrived with its basic closure properties. A class is numerically trivial exactly when it and its inverse are both nef.

The descent part of the roadmap begins with [contractions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Contraction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsContraction), proper maps with O_Y ≅ f_*O_X. They are shown to be surjective epimorphisms that are closed under composition and restriction, and an affine contraction is [an isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Contraction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.isContraction_iff_isIso). Supporting work covers fibres of composite morphisms and presents an algebraic extension as the [filtered colimit of its finite subextensions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/IntermediateField/FilteredColimit.html#TauCeti.IntermediateField.isColimitFiniteSubextensionCocone).
