## FormalLaurentPuiseux: 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-19 (`9ea32b0` to `9fc5d5a`)

The Puiseux field now exists on the roadmap's chosen carrier. The [Puiseux series field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField) is the subfield of rational-exponent Hahn series whose exponents share a common denominator, and it is [the colimit of the ramified levels](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Colimit.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.levelCoconeIsColimit) K((t^(1/n))) in commutative K-algebras, indexed by ramification indices ordered by divisibility (TauCeti#17548). Level n [has degree n](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Level/Basis.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.level_finrank) over K((t)), with basis 1, u, …, u^(n−1) obtained by splitting exponents into congruence classes, and is generated by a root of the [irreducible polynomial X^n − t](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Level/Adjoin.html#TauCeti.LaurentSeries.irreducible_X_pow_sub_C_single). So the Puiseux field is [algebraic over K((t))](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Level/Basis.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.isAlgebraic_laurentSeries) in every characteristic, and every polynomial over it [descends to a single level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.exists_map_levelEmbedding_eq). For Newton–Puiseux itself only the [coprime-factor Hensel lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Hensel.html#Polynomial.Monic.exists_monic_mul_eq_of_isCoprime) has landed, over R⟦t⟧ for any commutative ring and with unique factors; Newton polygons and the root theorem have not started.

The differential calculus on K((t)) is largely in place. Every continuous K-derivation is [a multiple of d/dt](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/ContinuousDerivation.html#Derivation.eq_smul_laurentSeriesDerivation), and the [continuous Kähler differentials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/KaehlerDifferential.html#TauCeti.ContinuousKaehlerDifferential) are free of rank one on dt, with [the universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/KaehlerDifferential.html#TauCeti.ContinuousKaehlerDifferential.existsUnique_compDer_eq) for continuous derivations. In characteristic zero the residue-zero series are [exactly the derivatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Primitive.html#TauCeti.mem_range_laurentSeries_derivative_iff). The residue form gives [perfect pairings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/CoefficientDuality.html#TauCeti.LaurentSeries.residueFormQuotientPairing.instIsPerfPair) between the quotients U_a/U_b and U_(−b)/U_(−a). Around these sit the quotient and chain rules, the kernel of the derivative, and the valuation-ring and residue-field identifications.

On the coordinate side, formal coordinates φ = uX + ⋯ form a group [anti-isomorphic to the automorphisms of K⟦X⟧](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Coordinate/Basic.html#TauCeti.FormalCoordinate.substAlgEquivMulEquiv). As a topological group it is [the inverse limit of its discrete jet groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Coordinate/Jet.html#TauCeti.FormalCoordinate.jetLimitEquiv), and the [commutator of infinitesimal substitutions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Coordinate/Infinitesimal.html#TauCeti.infinitesimalSubstAlgEquiv_commutator_apply) fixes the bracket sign. Still missing: the residue formula for substitutions of order e, and vector- and Lie-valued Laurent series.

## FormalLaurentPuiseux: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25 (`9fc5d5a` to `69cd1ab`)

The formal Newton–Puiseux theorem has landed. Over an algebraically closed field K of characteristic zero, every nonconstant polynomial over the Puiseux field [has a root in a single ramified level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/AlgClosure.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.exists_isRoot_levelEmbedding), so the Puiseux field is [algebraically closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/AlgClosure.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.instIsAlgClosed) and [an algebraic closure of K((t))](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/AlgClosure.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.instIsAlgClosure) (TauCeti#20575). The proof follows the roadmap's route: a Tschirnhaus shift, a [least-slope ramify-and-rescale normalization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/SlopeNormalization.html#Polynomial.Monic.exists_map_expand_eq_scaleRoots), and a coprime splitting of the reduction lifted by Hensel. It does not go through a Newton-polygon API with faces and initial polynomials, and none was built. The finite extensions followed at once. Every extension of K((t)) of degree e is [isomorphic to K((t^(1/e)))](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/FiniteExtension.html#TauCeti.LaurentSeries.Level.algEquivOfFinrankEq), and a root of a degree-d polynomial [lies at level d!](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/FiniteExtension.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.mem_level_factorial_of_aeval_eq_zero).

Galois theory has begun. Each level is [Galois with group μ_n(K)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Level/Kummer.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.levelGalEquivRootsOfUnity), acting by u ↦ ζu. The Tate module lim μ_n(K) acts [faithfully on the whole Puiseux field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/Kummer.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.kummer), with [fixed fields the levels](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/Kummer.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.fixedField_zpowers_kummer_pow). The Puiseux field also got its valuation and topology, with level embeddings that are [topological embeddings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/Valuation.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.isEmbedding_levelEmbedding), and a proof that it is [not complete](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/Valuation.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.not_completeSpace).

Back on K((t)), the [finite-map residue formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Substitution.html#TauCeti.LaurentSeries.coeff_neg_one_substAlgHom_mul_derivative_eq_nsmul) Res(f(φ)Dφ dt) = e·Res(f dt) now holds in all characteristics, through the [universal coefficient identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Derivative.html#PowerSeries.mul_coeff_val_zpow_neg_add_coeff_val_zpow_neg_sub_one_mul_derivative_eq_zero) (TauCeti#18268). Coordinate changes [act on one-forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/KaehlerDifferential.html#TauCeti.ContinuousKaehlerDifferential.substEquiv) by f dt ↦ f(φ) dφ. Substitution and jets are now natural in the coefficient field. Vector- and Lie-valued series have started: there is the [convolution Lie algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HahnModule.html#HahnModule.instLieRing) L((t)), the [polynomial-loop embedding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Loop/HahnModule.html#LieAlgebra.LoopAlgebra.toHahnModule) into it, and F ⊗ V ≃ V((t)) for finite free V. The continuous dual, D on L((t)) and the comparison with the loop residue pairing are still missing.
