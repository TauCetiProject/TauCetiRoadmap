## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The coordinate-level calculus on K((t)) (L1–L2) is essentially complete, and the Puiseux field has been built on its Hahn-series carrier together with its ramified levels, their degrees and its colimit presentation (L4, without valuation or differentials). L3 is partial. L5 has only its coprime-factor Hensel lemma, and the Galois layers L6–L7 have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Puiseux field as a colimit of ramified levels** — the subfield of rational-exponent Hahn series with a common exponent denominator is the colimit in commutative K-algebras of the levels K((t^(1/n))), indexed by divisibility ([`levelCoconeIsColimit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Colimit.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.levelCoconeIsColimit)).
- **Ramified levels have degree n** — level n has basis 1, u, …, u^(n−1) over K((t)), from splitting exponents into congruence classes, and is generated by a root of the irreducible X^n − t ([`level_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Level/Basis.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.level_finrank)). Hence the Puiseux field is algebraic over K((t)) in every characteristic.
- **Hensel's lemma for coprime factors** — a monic polynomial over R⟦t⟧ whose reduction is a product of coprime factors, one of them monic, factors uniquely into monic lifts, for any commutative ring R ([`exists_monic_mul_eq_of_isCoprime`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Hensel.html#Polynomial.Monic.exists_monic_mul_eq_of_isCoprime)).
- **Continuous derivations and differentials of K((t))** — every continuous K-derivation is a d/dt for a unique a ([`continuousDerivationsEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/ContinuousDerivation.html#TauCeti.continuousDerivationsEquiv)). The continuous Kähler differentials are free on dt and universal for continuous derivations ([`existsUnique_compDer_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/KaehlerDifferential.html#TauCeti.ContinuousKaehlerDifferential.existsUnique_compDer_eq)).
- **Residue duality on lattices** — the residue form Res(fg dt) is nondegenerate, the orthogonal of U_a is U_(−a), and it induces perfect pairings U_a/U_b × U_(−b)/U_(−a) ([`instIsPerfPair`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/CoefficientDuality.html#TauCeti.LaurentSeries.residueFormQuotientPairing.instIsPerfPair)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The formal coordinate group** ([`FormalCoordinate`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Coordinate/Basic.html#TauCeti.FormalCoordinate)) — series uX + ⋯ under composition. It is anti-isomorphic to the automorphisms of K⟦X⟧, and as a topological group it is the inverse limit of its discrete jet groups ([`jetLimitEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Coordinate/Jet.html#TauCeti.FormalCoordinate.jetLimitEquiv)). This is the parameter-change group later layers act by.
- **Laurent substitution** ([`substAlgHom`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Substitution.html#TauCeti.LaurentSeries.substAlgHom)) — f ↦ f(φ) for nonzero φ ∈ tK⟦t⟧. It multiplies orders, is continuous and injective, composes correctly and satisfies the chain rule. It also gives the ramified maps t ↦ s^e used to build the levels.
- **The Puiseux carrier and its level embeddings** ([`puiseuxField`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField)) — with intersections at the gcd, composita at the lcm, and a common-level lift of any polynomial's coefficients ([`exists_map_levelEmbedding_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.exists_map_levelEmbedding_eq)). The Newton–Puiseux reduction to a single level will run through this.

### Roadmap coverage

**L1** is done in substance: the algebra tower, the Laurent-polynomial embedding with density and truncation convergence, the valuation ring and residue field, substitution, the scoped valuation topology on K⟦t⟧ with automatic continuity of automorphisms, jets, and infinitesimal substitutions with their commutator formula. Naturality under coefficient-field embeddings K → K′ was not found in the source. **L2** is done except coordinate covariance du = Dφ dt and coefficient-field functoriality of one-forms. **L3** is partial. Done: primitives, the derivative image as the residue-zero forms, scalar lattice duality and residue invariance under order-one coordinate changes. Missing: the order-e formula Res(f(φ)Dφ dt) = e·Res(f dt), the universal coefficient identity, V((t)) and its continuous dual, and the loop Lie algebra L((t)) with its residue-pairing comparison. **L4** is partial. The levels, basis, degree, X^n − t, the common level and the colimit are done. Missing: the Puiseux valuation and topology, the non-completeness example, D_t, and Puiseux one-forms and residues. **L5** is partial, with only the coprime-factor Hensel lemma. **L6 and L7** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Formal Newton–Puiseux root theorem (L5)** — Newton polygons and initial polynomials, the Tschirnhaus shift, ramify-and-rescale normalization, and the degree induction that splits off a coprime factor. The Hensel step it needs is in place, as are the common-level lift and the algebraicity needed to conclude algebraic closure.
- **Order-e residue formula and universal coefficient identity (L3)** — Res(f(φ)Dφ dt) = e·Res(f dt) for φ of order e in all characteristics, via the identity in ℤ[x₀,…,x_(ek)][x₀⁻¹]. Only the order-one case exists. AlgebraicCurves Layer 9 consumes this.
- **Puiseux valuation and topology (L4)** — restrict the Hahn additive valuation, prove the level embeddings are homeomorphisms onto their images, and exhibit Σ t^(j+1/(j+1)) as a Cauchy sequence with no Puiseux limit.
- **Vector- and Lie-valued Laurent series (L3)** — HahnModule ℤ K V with lattice topology, its continuous dual V*((t))dt, and the coefficient-convolution Lie algebra L((t)) compared with LieAlgebra.LoopAlgebra.residuePairing.
- **Kummer structure of the levels (L6)** — identify level n as the splitting field of X^n − t and consume Mathlib's Kummer theory; this waits on L5 for the finite-extension classification.
