It is proved through the universal coefficient identity over an arbitrary commutative ring ([`coeff_neg_one_substAlgHom_mul_derivative_eq_nsmul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Substitution.html#TauCeti.LaurentSeries.coeff_neg_one_substAlgHom_mul_derivative_eq_nsmul)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Puiseux field as a valued colimit of levels** — the field is the colimit of the levels K((t^(1/n))) ([`levelCoconeIsColimit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Colimit.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.levelCoconeIsColimit)). Its valuation is in `WithTop ℚ`, and each level embeds topologically. It is [not complete](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Puiseux/Valuation.html#TauCeti.puiseuxField.not_completeSpace).
- **The formal coordinate group** ([`FormalCoordinate`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Coordinate/Basic.html#TauCeti.FormalCoordinate)) — it is the inverse limit of its discrete jet groups ([`jetLimitEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Coordinate/Jet.html#TauCeti.FormalCoordinate.jetLimitEquiv)), it acts on series and forms, and it is natural in the coefficient field. This is the parameter-change group the L7 independence statements quantify over.
- **Lie-valued Laurent series** ([`instLieRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HahnModule.html#HahnModule.instLieRing)) — L((t)) under the coefficient-convolution bracket, which receives the polynomial loop algebra by [a Lie embedding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Loop/HahnModule.html#LieAlgebra.LoopAlgebra.toHahnModule). It is the carrier for the formal loop cocycles L3 is meant to supply.
