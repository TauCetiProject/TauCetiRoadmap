## DimerModelsAndLozengeTilings: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`64919fe` to `522d48f`)

Most of the abstract Gibbs layer is now in place. Under a proper action of an infinite group, [invariant events are almost surely tail events](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Tail/ProperAction.html#TauCeti.Probability.exists_measurableSet_tailEvents_ae_eq_of_proper). Combined with [Kolmogorov's zero-one law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Tail/InfinitePi.html#TauCeti.Probability.infinitePi_eq_zero_or_one_of_measurableSet_tailEvents), this makes [every Bernoulli product measure ergodic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Ergodic/Bernoulli/Action.html#TauCeti.Probability.ergodicSMul_arrowAction_infinitePi_const_of_proper). [Specifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Gibbs/Specification.html#TauCeti.Specification) now live on a [subshift carrier](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/SymbolicDynamics/Subshift.html#TauCeti.Subshift) whose index set may have several orbits (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/2278). A measure is Gibbs exactly when [the kernels give its conditional probabilities](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Gibbs/Specification.html#TauCeti.Specification.isGibbs_iff_ae_eq_condExp). [Reweighting by a tail density](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Gibbs/Specification.html#TauCeti.Specification.IsGibbs.withDensity) keeps it Gibbs, and for a quasilocal specification the [invariant Gibbs measures are weakly compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Gibbs/Closed.html#TauCeti.Specification.isCompact_setOf_mem_gibbsInvariant). The face property itself, and with it "extreme means ergodic", is not yet proved.

On the lattice side, the edges and faces of the triangular lattice and the [dual hexagonal graph](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Tiling/Lozenge/HexGraph.html#TauCeti.hexGraph) are in place. So is the dimer model on any locally finite graph: [dimer configurations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Matching/Dimer.html#SimpleGraph.dimerSubshift) form a closed subshift matching perfect matchings, and there are [dimer Gibbs measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Gibbs/Dimer.html#SimpleGraph.IsDimerGibbs) in cylinder form, which are gauge invariant. Regions, tilings and height functions have not started.

At the far end, the Okounkov–Reshetikhin [incomplete beta kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteBeta/Kernel.html#TauCeti.incompleteBetaKernel) is defined (TauCeti#43636). Its value on the diagonal is the slope `s`. Along a row it is [a scaled discrete sine kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteBeta/Kernel.html#TauCeti.incompleteBetaKernel_add_row), and by the homology form of Cauchy's theorem it [depends only on the side of crossing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/IncompleteBeta/PathIndependence.html#TauCeti.incompleteBetaKernel_eq_inv_mul_intervalIntegral_of_lt). The [good torus sizes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Tiling/Lozenge/GoodTorusSizes.html#TauCeti.eventually_mem_goodTorusSizes_or_add_one) lemma also landed, in the corrected "`n` or `n + 1`" form.
