## HabiroCyclotomicCompletions: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (`9615f7d` to `522d48f`)

The classical cyclotomic completion `R[q]^S` now exists as a complete Hausdorff topological ring with its universal property: a polynomial ring map into a complete Hausdorff ring that sends some cyclotomic ideal into every neighbourhood of zero extends uniquely to a continuous map on the completion ([existsUnique_continuous_comp_fromPoly_eq](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Completion/Topology.html#TauCeti.CycloCompletion.existsUnique_continuous_comp_fromPoly_eq), TauCeti#42927). Around it came the finite quotient isomorphism `R[q]^S/(f) ≃ R[q]/(f)` ([quotientSpanAlgEquiv](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Completion/Basic.html#TauCeti.CycloCompletion.quotientSpanAlgEquiv)), and Habiro's Lemma 3.1, that an injective or surjective coefficient map stays [injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Completion/Map.html#TauCeti.CycloCompletion.map_injective) or [surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Completion/Map.html#TauCeti.CycloCompletion.map_surjective) on completions. Changes of presentation also landed. Mutually cofinal polynomial families give canonically isomorphic limits ([cofinalEquiv](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/FamilyLimit.html#TauCeti.PolyFamilyLimit.cofinalEquiv)), and for finite `S` the completion is the adic completion at `∏Φ_n` ([isAdicComplete_span_cycloProd](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Completion/Topology.html#TauCeti.CycloCompletion.isAdicComplete_span_cycloProd)). For any `S` it is the limit of its finite-subset completions ([finiteSubsetEquiv](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Completion/FiniteSubset.html#TauCeti.CycloCompletion.finiteSubsetEquiv)).

The factorial polynomial `P_N = (q;q)_N` was given its signed cyclotomic factorization ([neg_one_pow_mul_factorialPoly_eq_prod_cyclotomic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Factorial.html#TauCeti.neg_one_pow_mul_factorialPoly_eq_prod_cyclotomic)). In characteristic zero, `Φ_d` occurs in it with multiplicity exactly `⌊N/d⌋` ([cyclotomic_pow_dvd_factorialPoly_iff](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Factorial.html#TauCeti.cyclotomic_pow_dvd_factorialPoly_iff)). This yields the normalized factorial expansion: every element of the full completion is uniquely `Σ a_n P_n` with `deg a_n ≤ n` ([existsUnique_factorialExpansion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Completion/FactorialExpansion.html#TauCeti.CycloCompletion.existsUnique_factorialExpansion), https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/3176). Underneath sit the q-factorial and the finite [q-Pochhammer product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Ring/QPochhammer.html#TauCeti.qPochhammer), with `(1-q)^n[n]_q! = (q;q)_n`.

Evaluation at a root `ζ` of `Φ_n` and the additive [Taylor expansion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Completion/Taylor.html#TauCeti.CycloCompletion.taylorAt) in `q-ζ` are now algebra maps for any coefficient algebra, with Hasse-derivative coefficients. Still missing are the Kontsevich series, the inverse of `q`, digit multiplication, close-root re-expansion, and all of the rigidity theory.
