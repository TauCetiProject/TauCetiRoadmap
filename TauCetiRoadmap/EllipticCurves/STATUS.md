## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Mordell–Weil, the Hasse bound, finiteness of the Selmer group, Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich, the classification of twists and the Tate curve's uniformisation are all proved, and isogenous curves have equal `L`-functions. Eleven of the twelve layers are complete. The one open milestone is Cassels' isogeny invariance of the BSD quotient, a stretch goal in Layer 7, whose local ingredients are now largely in place.

### Named results

- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField), and the worked example shows that [`y² = x³ − x + 1` has group `ℤ` over `ℚ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/WorkedExamples/X3SubXAddOne/Rank.html#TauCeti.WorkedExamples.nonempty_addEquiv_int_point_curveX3SubXAddOne).
- **The Hasse bound** — over a field with `q` elements the Frobenius trace satisfies [`a_q² ≤ 4q`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/HasseBound.html#WeierstrassCurve.frobeniusTrace_sq_le_four_mul_card), proved through positivity of the degree form.
- **Finiteness of the Selmer group** — over a number field, [`Sel_m(E/K)` is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Finite.html#WeierstrassCurve.finite_selmerGroup) (AEC X.4.2), by passing to the `m`-division field and applying Hermite's theorem.
- **The Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criteria** — for `ℓ` prime to the residue characteristic, potential good reduction holds [exactly when inertia has finite image on `T_ℓ E`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/PotentialGoodReduction.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasPotentialGoodReduction_iff_finite_image_tateModuleGaloisRepresentation), and good reduction exactly when inertia acts trivially.
- **Isogeny invariance of the `L`-function** — isogenous curves over a number field have [the same `L`-function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/LFunction/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.LFunction_eq) as a formal Dirichlet series, Euler factors at bad places included.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The BSD quotient and the statement of BSD over `ℚ`.** The [quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Quotient.html#WeierstrassCurve.bsdQuotient) is defined when `Ш` is finite, from the real period of a global minimal equation, the regulator and the Tamagawa product. [BSD itself](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Statement.html#TauCeti.BirchSwinnertonDyerConjecture) is stated against a continuation of the actual `L`-series. It is a statement only, as the roadmap intends.
- **The duality toolkit for Cassels' theorem.** [`Sel_φ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Isogeny/Basic.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.selmerGroup) for an arbitrary isogeny has its descent sequence. [Local Tate duality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/WeilPairing/Local.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.localTateKerWeilPairing_bijective) pairs `H¹(K, E₁[φ])` perfectly with `H¹(K, E₂[φ̂])`, and under it [the Kummer images of `φ` and `φ̂` are orthogonal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/WeilPairing/Orthogonality.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.localTateKerWeilPairing_kummerMap_kummerMap_eq_zero) at every place. Local Kummer indices are known at finite and real places, and [global reciprocity for the Weil cup product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/WeilPairing/Reciprocity.html#WeierstrassCurve.finsum_toRatAddCircle_localTateWeilPairing_add_sum_infiniteInvMap_eq_zero) makes the sum of local invariants vanish.
- **The local filtration of `E(K)`.** The formal group is the kernel of reduction, and its levels have their indices computed, with [the component-group factor equal to the Tamagawa number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/FormalGroup/Point/Index.html#WeierstrassCurve.index_formalPointFiltration_eq_mul_tamagawaNumber). This is how the `c_p` enter the BSD quotient and Cassels' local factors.

## The frontier

- **Exact local annihilation for `φ` and `φ̂`.** The local Kummer images are orthogonal, the local count [`#H²(K, E₁[φ]) = #E₂(K)[φ̂]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/WeilPairing/Cardinality.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.natCard_H2_kerCoeff) is proved, and a [counting criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZMod/Annihilator.html#AddMonoidHom.eq_ker_compl₂_subtype_iff_forall_eq_zero_and_natCard_mul_eq_zmod) turns orthogonality into exact annihilation when the orders multiply to `#H¹`. Matching those orders at each place is the step that does not yet appear.
- **The global Selmer ratio for `φ` and `φ̂`.** With exact local annihilators, reciprocity and the local Kummer indices, what remains is Cassels' formula expressing `#Sel_φ / #Sel_φ̂` through local indices.
- **Cassels' isogeny invariance over `ℚ`.** The remaining work is to assemble the Selmer ratio with the existing transformation laws for `Ш` finiteness, the regulator, the real period and the Tamagawa product into equality of BSD quotients for isogenous curves. Since the `L`-functions already agree, this would also make the truth of BSD isogeny-invariant.
