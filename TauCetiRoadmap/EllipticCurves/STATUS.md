## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Mordell–Weil, the Hasse bound, [finiteness of the Selmer group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Finite.html#WeierstrassCurve.finite_selmerGroup), Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich, the classification of twists and the Tate curve's uniformisation are all proved, and eleven of the twelve layers are complete. The one open milestone is Cassels' isogeny invariance of the BSD quotient, a stretch goal in Layer 7. It is now reduced to a single unproved identity, Cassels' formula for the ratio of the Selmer groups of an isogeny and its dual.

### Named results

- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField).
- **The Hasse bound** — over a field with `q` elements the Frobenius trace satisfies [`a_q² ≤ 4q`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/HasseBound.html#WeierstrassCurve.frobeniusTrace_sq_le_four_mul_card), proved through positivity of the degree form.
- **The Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criteria** — for `ℓ` prime to the residue characteristic, potential good reduction holds [exactly when inertia has finite image on `T_ℓ E`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/PotentialGoodReduction.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasPotentialGoodReduction_iff_finite_image_tateModuleGaloisRepresentation), and good reduction holds exactly when inertia acts trivially.
- **Isogeny invariance of the `L`-function** — isogenous curves over a number field have [the same `L`-function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/LFunction/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.LFunction_eq) as a formal Dirichlet series, including the Euler factors at bad places.
- **The BSD quotient along an isogeny** — over `ℚ`, with `Ш` finite, [`BSD(E₁)/BSD(E₂)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.bsdQuotient_div_bsdQuotient) is expressed through `#Sel_ψ / #Sel_ψ̂`, kernel orders, periods and Tamagawa products, for an isogeny `ψ` that kills `Ш` in both directions. [Every nonzero morphism has such a multiple](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.exists_isogeny_bsdQuotient_div_bsdQuotient).

## The frontier

- **Exact local annihilation for `φ` and `φ̂`.** Orthogonality of the two Kummer images and the order of `H¹(K, E₁[φ])` are both known. So is a counting criterion that upgrades orthogonality to exact annihilation. What remains is to check at each place, the real one included, that the two Kummer images have orders whose product is `#H¹`.
- **Cassels' Selmer-ratio formula.** The identity `#Sel_ψ / #Sel_ψ̂ = (#E₁(ℚ)[ψ] / #E₂(ℚ)[ψ̂]) · ∏_v (local Kummer ratio)` is still to be proved. It depends on exact annihilation, global reciprocity, and the new comparison lemmas for Selmer structures, which bound relative indices by local quotients.
- **Cassels' isogeny invariance over `ℚ`.** Once the Selmer-ratio formula is proved, this should be immediate: substituting it, together with the product formula, into the BSD ratio along `ψ` gives `BSD(E₁) = BSD(E₂)`. Since the `L`-functions already agree, this would make the truth of BSD isogeny-invariant.
