## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The two classical summits within reach are proved: the Mordell–Weil theorem and the Hasse bound. Layers 0 and 4.5b are complete, and Layer 2 lacks only functoriality of the Weil pairing under change of field. The general dual isogeny, Tate's algorithm and the zeta function are still open, and work on Selmer groups and Sha has not begun.

### Named results

- **The Hasse bound** — an elliptic curve over `𝔽_q` satisfies [`(#E(𝔽_q) − (q + 1))² ≤ 4q`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/HasseBound.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasse_bound), where the count includes the point at infinity. It is proved through the degree form: the Frobenius pencil has [`deg(rπ − s) = qr² − a_q rs + s²`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Frobenius/Trace.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.degree_zsmul_ofIsogeny_baseChangeFrobenius_sub_zsmul_id_eq_frobeniusTrace) when `p ∤ s`, and that form is non-negative.
- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField), proved through a [general version over Dedekind fraction fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point).
- **The determinant of the Tate module is cyclotomic** — for `ℓ` invertible over a separably closed field, `T_ℓ E` is [free of rank 2 over `ℤ_ℓ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/Basic.html#WeierstrassCurve.nonempty_linearEquiv_tateModule), and the [determinant of its Galois representation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/Determinant.html#TauCeti.det_tateModuleGaloisRepresentation) is the cyclotomic character.
- **The Weil pairing** — over a separably closed field with `N` invertible, `e_N` is bilinear, alternating, nondegenerate, [Galois-equivariant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/MulByInt/WeilPairing/Galois.html#WeierstrassCurve.weilPairing_torsionGaloisAction) and [adjoint to the dual](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Dual/WeilPairing.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.weilPairing_eq_weilPairing_dual). It is built from a Kummer character, not from Weil reciprocity.
- **Global minimal models and Kraus's criterion** — over a Dedekind domain, the global-minimality class [is trivial exactly when](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MinimalModel/GlobalExistence.html#WeierstrassCurve.globalMinimalityClass_eq_one_iff) a globally minimal equation exists (VIII.8.2). [Kraus's criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GlobalModels/Kraus.html#TauCeti.krausGlobalCondition_iff_exists_integralModel) decides which pairs `(c₄, c₆)` come from an integral equation, away from characteristic 2 and 3.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The dual isogeny, in two cases.** A separable isogeny over a separably closed field has a [unique dual](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Dual/Separable.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.dual) with `φ̂ ∘ φ = [deg φ]`. Over a finite field, so does the purely inseparable Frobenius: [`π̂`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Frobenius/Dual.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.dualFrobeniusIsogeny), the `q`-power Verschiebung. The general inseparable case is still missing.
- **The endomorphism ring acting on torsion.** `End(E)` is a [ring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Hom/Ring.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.instRing) that [acts on `E[N]`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Hom/Torsion.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.torsionRepresentation). A separable isogeny [acts with determinant equal to its degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Hom/Determinant.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.det_torsionLinearMap_ofIsogeny) modulo `N` (III.8.6). This is how degrees of Frobenius pencils were computed, and it is the natural home for the characteristic polynomial of Frobenius.
- **The canonical height.** The Néron–Tate height vanishes exactly on torsion and is the [limit of `h(nP)/n²`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/CanonicalHeight/Limit.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.Point.tendsto_naiveHeight_nsmul_div_sq). The [regulator](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/Regulator.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.regulator) that Layer 7 will need is its Gram determinant.

## The frontier

- **The Frobenius identities and the zeta function.** `π + π̂ = [a_q]` and `π² − [a_q]π + [q] = 0` are next. From them come the trace recursion for `#E(𝔽_{qⁿ})` and rationality of `Z(E/𝔽_q, T)`.
- **Supersingularity over finite fields.** `IsSupersingular E ↔ p ∣ a_q`, together with its companion criteria, now that the dual of Frobenius and the trace are in place.
- **The dual in general.** The Frobenius case is done. The relative-Frobenius case gives Verschiebung, and Galois descent then brings `φ̂` from `Kˢᵉᵖ` down to `K`.
- **The reduction filtration and Tate's algorithm.** Next come `E₀(K)` and `0 → E₁ → E₀ → Ẽ_ns → 0`. With VII.3.1 in hand, these give Layer 6's injectivity of reduction on prime-to-`p` torsion. Tate's algorithm then unblocks Layer 4.5a's semistability tests and Layer 8's Szpiro block.
- **The Weil pairing under change of field.** This is the last Layer 2 milestone.
