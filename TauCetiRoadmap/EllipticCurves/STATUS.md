### Named results

- **The Hasse bound** — an elliptic curve over `𝔽_q` satisfies [`(#E(𝔽_q) − (q + 1))² ≤ 4q`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/HasseBound.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasse_bound). The zeta function is rational, `Z(E/𝔽_q, T) = (1 − a_q T + q T²)/((1 − T)(1 − qT))` (`WeierstrassCurve.zetaFunction_eq_mul_inv`), and [it is an isogeny invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Frobenius/Isogenous.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.zetaFunction_eq).
- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField). The worked example computes [the group of `y² = x³ − x + 1` over `ℚ` as `ℤ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/WorkedExamples/X3SubXAddOne/Rank.html#TauCeti.WorkedExamples.nonempty_addEquiv_int_point_curveX3SubXAddOne).
- **Finiteness of the Selmer group** — over a number field, [`Sel_m(E/K)` is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Finite.html#WeierstrassCurve.finite_selmerGroup) (AEC X.4.2). The proof reduces to the `m`-division field and finishes with Hermite's theorem on extensions of bounded degree.
- **The Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criteria** — `ℓ` is prime to the residue characteristic throughout. Good reduction holds exactly when inertia acts trivially on `T_ℓ E` (`WeierstrassCurve.hasGoodReduction_iff_tateModuleGaloisRepresentation_eq_one`). Potential good reduction holds [exactly when the inertia image is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/PotentialGoodReduction.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasPotentialGoodReduction_iff_finite_image_tateModuleGaloisRepresentation). Under potential good reduction, good reduction holds [exactly when inertia fixes `E[N]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/TorsionCriterion.html#WeierstrassCurve.HasPotentialGoodReduction.hasGoodReduction_iff_forall_mem_inertiaSubgroup).
- **The classification of twists** — for a Galois extension `L/K`, [pointed twists split by `L` correspond to `H¹(Gal(L/K), Aut(W_L))`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Twist/Classification.html#WeierstrassCurve.pointedTwistEquivNonabelianH1) (AEC X.5).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The dual isogeny in general.** Every isogeny has a dual (`TauCeti.Isogeny.dual`), built by factoring it as a separable map after a power of Frobenius. With it, the degree is an unconditional quadratic form, and a nonzero isogeny is injective on points modulo torsion, so isogenous curves have the same rank.
- **Selmer groups, Sha and the descent sequences.** `Sel_m` and `Ш` are defined through general Selmer structures on continuous cohomology. The sequence `0 → E(K)/mE(K) → Sel_m → Ш[m] → 0` is exact, and the [`φ`-descent sequence of an arbitrary isogeny](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/IsogenyDescent.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.exact_kummerClassMap_kerCoeffInclH1) is exact too. Cassels' theorem will need this.
- **Tamagawa indices from Tate's algorithm.** Over a Henselian valuation ring with finite residue field, [`[E(K) : E₀(K)]` equals the algorithm's local index](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Affine/Point/Index/TateAlgorithm.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.index_nonsingularReduction_eq_tateLocalIndex) at every symbol. That is the `c_p` a BSD quotient consumes.

## The frontier

- **The Tate curve's uniformisation.** The map [`Kˣ/q^ℤ → E_q(K)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateCurve/Uniformization.html#TauCeti.tateUniformization) is defined and respects inversion and base change. What remains is to show it is a homomorphism and a bijection, and then to make it compatible in `L` and Galois-equivariant.
- **The formal logarithm and exponential.** The formal group law and its torsion results are in place, but neither series is defined over a `ℚ`-algebra yet.
- **The BSD quotient over `ℚ`.** The real period (`WeierstrassCurve.realPeriod`), the regulator, the indices `c_p` and `Ш` all exist. The quotient itself, defined under finiteness of `Ш`, has not been assembled.
- **Cassels' isogeny invariance.** The `φ`-descent sequence, the regulator formula and the equality of local polynomials for isogenous curves are proved. What is still missing is `Sel_φ` and `Sel_φ̂` with their local Kummer-cokernel indices, local Tate duality, and the transfer of `Ш` finiteness across an isogeny.
- **The statement of BSD over `ℚ`.** A statement-only milestone, with the analytic hypothesis pinned to the actual Dirichlet series. Nothing towards it is visible in the supplied material.
