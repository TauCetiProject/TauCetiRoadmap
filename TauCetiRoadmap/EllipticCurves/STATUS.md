### Named results

- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField), proved through a [general version over Dedekind fraction fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point).
- **The determinant of the Tate module is cyclotomic** — over a separably closed field in which the prime `ℓ` is invertible, `T_ℓ E` is [free of rank 2 over `ℤ_ℓ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/Basic.html#WeierstrassCurve.nonempty_linearEquiv_tateModule), Galois acts on it continuously, and the [determinant of that representation is the cyclotomic character](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/Determinant.html#TauCeti.det_tateModuleGaloisRepresentation). This is read off from the [`ℓ`-adic Weil pairing](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/WeilPairing.html#WeierstrassCurve.tateModuleWeilPairing) into `ℤ_ℓ(1)`.
- **The Weil pairing** — over a separably closed field in which `N` is invertible, `e_N` is bilinear, alternating, nondegenerate, [Galois-equivariant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/MulByInt/WeilPairing/Galois.html#WeierstrassCurve.weilPairing_torsionGaloisAction), compatible across levels, and [adjoint to the dual](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Dual/WeilPairing.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.weilPairing_eq_weilPairing_dual). It is built from a Kummer character, not from Weil reciprocity.
- **The dual isogeny** — over a separably closed field, a separable `φ` has a [unique `φ̂` with `φ̂ ∘ φ = [deg φ]`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Dual/Separable.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.dual), of the same degree, additive, and reversing composites (Silverman III.6.1–2). Here the [degree polarisation is `φ̂ψ + ψ̂φ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Dual/Degree.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.ofIsogeny_dual_comp_add_dual_comp_eq_polar_degree) (III.6.3).
- **Global minimal models and Kraus's criterion** — over any Dedekind domain, the global-minimality class [is trivial exactly when](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MinimalModel/GlobalExistence.html#WeierstrassCurve.globalMinimalityClass_eq_one_iff) a globally minimal equation exists (VIII.8.2). A [sharp semi-global model](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MinimalModel/GlobalExistence.html#WeierstrassCurve.globalMinimalityClass_eq_classGroupMk_iff) exists at any prime representing that class. [Kraus's global criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GlobalModels/Kraus.html#TauCeti.krausGlobalCondition_iff_exists_integralModel) decides which pairs `(c₄, c₆)` are the invariants of an integral equation, away from characteristic 2 and 3.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The endomorphism ring and rigidity.** Morphisms [act on points](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Hom/PointMap.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.pointMap) and are [determined by that action](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Hom/PointMap.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.ext_pointMap), and `End(E)` is a [ring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Hom/Ring.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.instRing) and a domain. Together with [`deg(1 − π_q) = #E(𝔽_q)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/OneSubFrobenius/Degree.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.degree_oneSubFrobeniusIsogeny_eq_pointCount), this is the setting the Hasse bound will be proved in.
- **Galois actions and descent.** Coefficient automorphisms act on function fields, places, divisors and isogenies, and an isogeny [descends to the ground field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Descent.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.existsUnique_map_eq_iff_galoisFixed) exactly when every conjugation fixes it. This is what the descended dual and Verschiebung need.
- **The canonical height.** The Néron–Tate height vanishes exactly on torsion and is the [limit of `h(nP)/n²`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/CanonicalHeight/Limit.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.Point.tendsto_naiveHeight_nsmul_div_sq) along all multiples. The [regulator](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/Regulator.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.regulator) Layer 7 will need is its Gram determinant.

## The frontier

- **The Hasse bound.** The point count is a degree and `End(E)` is a ring. The degree polarisation is bilinear only when every sum involved is a separable isogeny. Removing that hypothesis, which in practice needs the general dual, is what stands between this and Cauchy–Schwarz.
- **The dual in general.** The inseparable case goes through the Frobenius factorisation. The new isogeny-descent criterion then brings `φ̂` down from `Kˢᵉᵖ` to `K`, which gives Verschiebung as the dual of relative Frobenius.
- **The Weil pairing under change of field.** This is the last Layer 2 milestone. Galois equivariance, the base change of morphisms and point maps are all in place.
- **The reduction filtration and Tate's algorithm.** Next come `E₀(K)` and `0 → E₁ → E₀ → Ẽ_ns → 0`, then Tate's algorithm. Layer 4.5a's semistability tests and Layer 8's Szpiro block both wait on it.
- **Vélu quotients.** The quotient by a finite subgroup, and the descent of its symmetric coefficients for Galois-stable kernels, are the remaining Layer 1 and Layer 0.5 constructions.
