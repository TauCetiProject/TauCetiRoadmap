## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Mordell–Weil, the Hasse bound, finiteness of the Selmer group, Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich, the classification of twists and the Tate curve's uniformisation are all proved. Ten of the twelve layers are complete. Still open are the formal exponential in Layer 1 and Cassels' isogeny invariance of the BSD quotient in Layer 7.

### Named results

- **The Hasse bound** — an elliptic curve over `𝔽_q` satisfies [`(#E(𝔽_q) − (q + 1))² ≤ 4q`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/HasseBound.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasse_bound), and its zeta function is rational and [an isogeny invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Frobenius/Isogenous.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.zetaFunction_eq).
- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField). The worked example shows that [`y² = x³ − x + 1` has group `ℤ` over `ℚ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/WorkedExamples/X3SubXAddOne/Rank.html#TauCeti.WorkedExamples.nonempty_addEquiv_int_point_curveX3SubXAddOne).
- **Finiteness of the Selmer group** — over a number field, [`Sel_m(E/K)` is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Finite.html#WeierstrassCurve.finite_selmerGroup) (AEC X.4.2). The proof passes to the `m`-division field and uses Hermite's theorem.
- **The Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criteria** — for `ℓ` prime to the residue characteristic, good reduction holds exactly when inertia acts trivially on `T_ℓ E`, and potential good reduction holds [exactly when the inertia image is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/PotentialGoodReduction.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasPotentialGoodReduction_iff_finite_image_tateModuleGaloisRepresentation).
- **The Tate uniformisation** — for `|q| < 1` over a complete non-archimedean field, [`Kˣ/q^ℤ ≃ E_q(K)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateCurve/Surjectivity.html#TauCeti.tateUniformizationEquiv) as groups. Over every complete extension `L`, the [isomorphism for `Lˣ/q^ℤ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateCurve/Surjectivity.html#TauCeti.tateUniformizationBaseChangeEquiv) is compatible in `L` and [Galois-equivariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateCurve/Surjectivity.html#TauCeti.tateUniformizationBaseChangeEquiv_symm_map_algEquiv).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The BSD quotient and the statement of BSD over `ℚ`.** The [quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Quotient.html#WeierstrassCurve.bsdQuotient) is defined when `Ш` is finite, using the [real period of a global minimal equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MinimalModel/RealPeriod.html#WeierstrassCurve.minimalRealPeriod), the regulator and the Tamagawa product. [BSD itself](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Statement.html#TauCeti.BirchSwinnertonDyerConjecture) is stated against a continuation of the actual `L`-series, as the roadmap requires. It is a statement only.
- **Selmer groups for an arbitrary isogeny.** [`Sel_φ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Isogeny/Basic.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.selmerGroup) has its descent sequence, and the exact sequence `Sel_φ → Sel_{deg φ} → Sel_φ̂` holds. Local conditions satisfy a [product formula against those of the dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Isogeny/Local.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.natCard_localCondition_selmerStructure_mul_natCard_localCondition_dual), and [local Tate duality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/WeilPairing/Local.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.localTateKerWeilPairing_bijective) pairs `H¹(K, E₁[φ])` perfectly with `H¹(K, E₂[φ̂])`. These are the local inputs Cassels' theorem needs.
- **The dual isogeny in general.** Every isogeny has a dual, built by factoring through a power of Frobenius. With it, the degree is a quadratic form and isogenous curves have the same rank. It is also what makes [finiteness of `Ш` isogeny-invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Isogeny/Sha.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.finite_sha_iff).

## The frontier

- **Cassels' isogeny invariance.** Several ingredients are proved: `Sel_φ` and `Sel_φ̂` with their local index product, local Tate duality, isogeny invariance of `Ш` finiteness, and the behaviour of the regulator and minimal real period under isogeny. What remains is the global duality step the proof uses and the assembly into equality of BSD quotients for isogenous curves over `ℚ`. Nothing for either appears in the supplied material.
- **Equal `L`-functions for isogenous curves.** Isogenous curves have [equal local factors at potentially good reduction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.localPolynomial_eq_of_hasPotentialGoodReduction), and good reduction is the special case. The multiplicative-reduction case is not visible here, so equality of the full `L`-functions is not yet established.
- **The formal exponential.** The formal group law, its filtration indices and its logarithm are in place. The exponential, the compositional inverse of the logarithm over a `ℚ`-algebra, is what Layer 1 still lacks.
