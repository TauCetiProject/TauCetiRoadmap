### Named results

- **The Hasse bound** — an elliptic curve over `𝔽_q` satisfies [`(#E(𝔽_q) − (q + 1))² ≤ 4q`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/HasseBound.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasse_bound). The zeta function is rational and [isogeny-invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Frobenius/Isogenous.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.zetaFunction_eq).
- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField). The worked example shows [the group of `y² = x³ − x + 1` over `ℚ` is `ℤ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/WorkedExamples/X3SubXAddOne/Rank.html#TauCeti.WorkedExamples.nonempty_addEquiv_int_point_curveX3SubXAddOne).
- **Finiteness of the Selmer group** — over a number field, [`Sel_m(E/K)` is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Finite.html#WeierstrassCurve.finite_selmerGroup) (AEC X.4.2). Over a number field, finiteness of `Ш` is also [invariant under isogeny](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Isogeny/Sha.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.finite_sha_iff).
- **The Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criteria** — with `ℓ` prime to the residue characteristic, good reduction holds exactly when inertia acts trivially on `T_ℓ E`. Potential good reduction holds [exactly when the inertia image is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/PotentialGoodReduction.html#WeierstrassCurve.hasPotentialGoodReduction_iff_finite_image_tateModuleGaloisRepresentation). As a consequence, [isogenous curves have good reduction together](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateModule/Isogeny.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.hasGoodReduction_iff).
- **Local Tate duality for `E[m]`** — pairing through the Weil pairing and the local invariant is [perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/WeilPairing/Local.html#WeierstrassCurve.localTateWeilPairing_bijective): `H¹(K, E[m])` is identified with its own `ℤ/m`-dual.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The BSD quotient over `ℚ`.** [`Ω(E) · Reg(E/ℚ) · #Ш(E/ℚ) · ∏_p c_p / #E(ℚ)_tors²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Quotient.html#WeierstrassCurve.bsdQuotient) is defined under finiteness of `Ш`. It brings together the period of the global minimal equation, the regulator, `Ш`, and the Tamagawa numbers from Tate's algorithm. It is the quantity that Cassels' theorem must show is unchanged by isogeny.
- **The BSD statement.** [BSD over `ℚ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Statement.html#TauCeti.BirchSwinnertonDyerConjecture) is stated as the roadmap asks. The analytic hypothesis is a continuation of the actual `L`-series to a connected open set meeting its half-plane of convergence. The conjecture then asserts the rank equality and the leading-coefficient identity. A second formulation, [`WeierstrassCurve.BirchSwinnertonDyerConjecture`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Conjecture.html#WeierstrassCurve.BirchSwinnertonDyerConjecture), also exists. It is weaker, since it assumes `Ш` finite for the rank equality too.
- **Selmer groups of an isogeny.** `Sel_φ` sits in the [`φ`-descent sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Isogeny/Basic.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.exact_kummerSelmerMap_selmerShaMap) and is [finite over a number field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Isogeny/Finite.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.finite_selmerGroup_place). It also fits into the [exact sequence `Sel_φ → Sel_{deg φ} → Sel_φ̂`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Isogeny/Basic.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.exact_selmerGroupToTorsion_selmerGroupTorsionToDual). Cassels' theorem needs all of these.

## The frontier

- **Surjectivity of the Tate uniformisation.** The uniformisation is an injective homomorphism over `K` and over every complete extension `L`. It is onto each of the three reduction strata: the kernel of reduction, the points with nonsingular reduction, and the points reducing to the node. What remains is to assemble these into surjectivity, and to bundle the group isomorphism `L^×/q^ℤ ≅ E_q(L)` that the roadmap names.
- **Cassels' isogeny invariance of the BSD quotient.** Several inputs are in place: `Sel_φ` and its exact sequences, the [orders of its local conditions as cokernel indices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/Isogeny/Local.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.natCard_localCondition_selmerStructure), the local cokernel/kernel ratios at places prime to the degree, local Tate duality for `E[m]`, and the transfer of `Ш` finiteness. The regulator's transformation and equal local factors at good and potential good reduction are also proved. So is the [real period under any isogeny over `ℝ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/RealPeriod.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.abs_mul_realPeriod_mul_index_eq_card_ker_mul): `|c| · Ω(W₁) · [W₂(ℝ) : φ(W₁(ℝ))] = #ker φ(ℝ) · Ω(W₂)`, where `φ^*ω₂ = c ω₁`. Four things are still missing: the global duality or product formula, the local cokernel indices at places dividing `deg φ`, the move from this period identity to the global minimal models, and the theorem itself.
- **Consolidating the BSD statement.** Two formulations of the conjecture now coexist, and they are not equivalent. [`WeierstrassCurve.BirchSwinnertonDyerConjecture`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Conjecture.html#WeierstrassCurve.BirchSwinnertonDyerConjecture) assumes `Ш` finite for the rank equality too, while [`WeierstrassCurve.BirchSwinnertonDyer`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Statement.html#WeierstrassCurve.BirchSwinnertonDyer) does not. The second implies the first; the converse would need `Ш` to be finite. Choosing one as the roadmap's milestone would keep later consumers from splitting.
