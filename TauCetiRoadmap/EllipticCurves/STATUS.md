## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Mordell–Weil theorem is proved, and Layer 0's function-field foundation is complete. The dual isogeny now exists for separable isogenies over a separably closed field, and the Weil pairing is alternating and compatible with it. Torsion structure, global minimal models and the first local theory are also in. The Hasse bound, the Tate module and Tate's algorithm have not landed, and work on Selmer groups and Sha has not begun.

### Named results

- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField), proved through a [general version over Dedekind fraction fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point).
- **The dual isogeny** — over a separably closed field, a separable `φ` has a [unique `φ̂` with `φ̂ ∘ φ = [deg φ]`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Dual/Separable.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.dual), of the same degree, additive, and reversing composites (Silverman III.6.1–2). It rests on the kernel [having `deg φ` points](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/PointHom/Kernel.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.card_ker_eq_degree).
- **The Weil pairing** — over a separably closed field in which `N` is invertible, [`e_N`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/MulByInt/WeilPairing.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.weilPairing) is bilinear, [alternating](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/MulByInt/WeilPairing.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.weilPairing_self), nondegenerate, and [adjoint to the dual](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Dual/WeilPairing.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.weilPairing_eq_weilPairing_dual): `e_N(φS, T) = e_N(S, φ̂T)`. It is built from a Kummer character, not from Weil reciprocity.
- **The structure of the `N`-torsion** — over a separably closed field in which `N` is invertible, [`E[N] ≅ (ℤ/N)²`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/MulByInt/Torsion/Structure.html#WeierstrassCurve.torsion_addEquiv_prod).
- **Global minimal models** — over any Dedekind domain, the global-minimality class [is trivial exactly when](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MinimalModel/GlobalExistence.html#WeierstrassCurve.globalMinimalityClass_eq_one_iff) the curve has a globally minimal equation (Silverman VIII.8.2).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Points as the action of morphisms.** Every morphism [acts on points](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Hom/PointMap.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.pointMap), and over a separably closed field [is determined by that action](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Hom/PointMap.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.ext_pointMap). This rigidity is how identities between isogenies, such as the dual's, are now proved. Together with [`deg(1 − π_q) = #E(𝔽_q)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/OneSubFrobenius/Degree.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.degree_oneSubFrobeniusIsogeny_eq_pointCount), it is the setting the Hasse bound will be proved in.
- **Galois actions.** Coefficient automorphisms now act on function fields, places, [divisors](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Affine/FunctionField/Galois/Divisor.html#WeierstrassCurve.divisorGaloisAction) and [isogenies](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/Galois.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.galoisConj), respecting principal divisors and translations. This is what descent and Galois equivariance of the pairing need.
- **The canonical height and regulator.** The Néron–Tate height [vanishes exactly on torsion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/CanonicalHeight.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.Point.canonicalHeight_eq_zero_iff_isOfFinAddOrder), and the [regulator](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/Regulator.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.regulator) that Layer 7 will need is its Gram determinant.

## The frontier

- **The Hasse bound.** The point count is a degree and rigidity is available. What remains is the degree form on `Hom`: bilinearity of `deg(φ + ψ) − deg φ − deg ψ` and Cauchy–Schwarz. Composition is so far additive in the inner variable only when the outer isogeny is separable.
- **The Weil pairing's Galois equivariance.** The Galois actions on points, places and divisors are all in place. After it comes the Tate module and its Galois representation.
- **The dual in general.** The inseparable case through Frobenius, and descent from the separable closure to the ground field, which also gives Verschiebung as the dual of relative Frobenius.
- **Kraus's global criterion.** The local criterion is proved. The finite-approximation lemma and the ordered patching of `a₁`, `a₃` and `b₂` remain, and then the sharp semi-global model at a prime `v₀ ∤ 6`.
- **The reduction filtration and Tate's algorithm.** Next come `E₀(K)` and `0 → E₁ → E₀ → Ẽ_ns → 0`, then Tate's algorithm, which Layer 4.5a's semistability test and Layer 8's Szpiro block both wait on.
