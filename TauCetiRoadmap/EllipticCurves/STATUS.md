### Named results

- **The Mordell–Weil theorem** — the points of an elliptic curve over a number field form a [finitely generated group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point_of_numberField). The proof goes through a [general version over Dedekind fraction fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/FinitelyGenerated.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.fg_point).
- **The Weil pairing** — over a separably closed field in which `N` is invertible, [`e_N(S, T) = τ_S g / g`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/MulByInt/WeilPairing.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.weilPairing) is additive in both variables and [nondegenerate in the second](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/MulByInt/WeilPairing.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.eq_zero_of_forall_weilPairing_eq_zero). It is built from a Kummer character, not from Weil reciprocity.
- **The structure of the `N`-torsion** — over a separably closed field in which `N` is invertible, [`E[N] ≅ (ℤ/N)²`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/MulByInt/Torsion/Structure.html#WeierstrassCurve.torsion_addEquiv_prod).
- **Global minimal models** — over any Dedekind domain, the global-minimality class [is trivial exactly when](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MinimalModel/GlobalExistence.html#WeierstrassCurve.globalMinimalityClass_eq_one_iff) the curve has a globally minimal equation (Silverman VIII.8.2).
- **The point count as a degree** — over `𝔽_q`, [`deg(1 − π_q) = #E(𝔽_q)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/OneSubFrobenius/Degree.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.degree_oneSubFrobeniusIsogeny_eq_pointCount), the identity that makes `q + 1 − #E(𝔽_q)` a trace.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The map on points of an isogeny.** [`toPointHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/PointHom/Basic.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.toPointHom) is defined through the class group, so it is a homomorphism by construction. For a separable isogeny over a separably closed field it is [surjective](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/PointHom/Fiber.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.toPointHom_surjective), and its kernel [has `deg φ` elements](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Isogeny/PointHom/Fiber.html#TauCeti.Isogeny.card_ker_toPointHom_eq_degree).
- **The canonical height and regulator.** The Néron–Tate height [vanishes exactly on torsion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/CanonicalHeight.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.Point.canonicalHeight_eq_zero_iff_isOfFinAddOrder). The [regulator](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/MordellWeil/Regulator.html#WeierstrassCurve.Affine.regulator) that Layer 7's BSD quotient will need is the Gram determinant of the height pairing.
- **Reduction and the Tate curve.** Over completions of Dedekind domains, the formal group is [the kernel of reduction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/FormalGroup/Point/KerReduction.html#WeierstrassCurve.formalPointAddEquivKerReduction). The Tate curve, evaluated at a unit `q` with `‖q‖ < 1`, [is elliptic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/TateCurve/Specialization.html#TauCeti.isElliptic_tateCurveAt) in any complete non-archimedean ring.

## The frontier

- **The dual isogeny in general.** The kernel of the class-group point map has `deg φ` elements. What remains is to identify that kernel with the isogeny's own kernel, which discharges the hypothesis of the conditional construction.
- **The Hasse bound.** The point count is already a degree. What remains is the degree form on `Hom`: bilinearity of `deg(φ + ψ) − deg φ − deg ψ` and Cauchy–Schwarz. Composition is now additive in the inner variable for Frobenius only, not in general.
- **The Weil pairing's theory.** Next are alternation (which gives nondegeneracy in the first variable), Galois equivariance, and `e_N(φP, Q) = e_N(P, φ̂Q)` once the general dual exists. After that comes the Tate module.
- **The reduction filtration and Tate's algorithm.** Next come `E₀(K)` and the exact sequence `0 → E₁ → E₀ → Ẽ_ns → 0`, then Tate's algorithm, which Layer 4.5a's semistability test and Layer 8's Szpiro block both wait on.
- **Kraus's criteria.** The local and global criteria on `(c₄, c₆)` remain, and with them the sharp semi-global model at a prime `v₀ ∤ 6`.
