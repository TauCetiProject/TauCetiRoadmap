## DysonBrownianMotion: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`75982fc` to `d25d526`)

The limit object of the hydrodynamic limit is now pinned down. A [Burgers flow](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Burgers.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsBurgersFlow) is a family of measures whose transforms solve the complex Burgers equation in integrated form, and flows of probability measures are [determined by their initial measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Burgers.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsBurgersFlow.eq), by constancy along characteristics and Stieltjes inversion. Every such flow satisfies the [Pastur equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Burgers.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsBurgersFlow.resolventTransform_eq_resolventTransform_add_mul) `m_t(w) = m_0(w + t m_t(w))`, and the flow from a point mass is the [semicircle family](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Burgers.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsBurgersFlow.eq_semicircleReal) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/2289). Convergence of the empirical measures to this flow has not begun.

For the particle system, the Dyson equation is set up pathwise, with [pathwise uniqueness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsDysonPath.eq), `ℓ²` stability in the start, the [comparison principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsDysonPath.le_of_le) of AGZ Lemma 4.3.6, and a Rogers–Shi bound on the integrated inverse gaps that holds even from coincident starts. The route to existence is in place but not completed: globally Lipschitz [truncated trajectories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Truncation.html#TauCeti.Probability.truncatedDysonPath) paste into a solution once they avoid the chamber walls, the [Lyapunov bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Lyapunov.html#TauCeti.Probability.dysonGenerator_dysonLyapunov_le) holds for `β ≥ 1`, and the truncations are [bounded uniformly in the cutoff](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/Nonexplosion.html#TauCeti.Probability.exists_forall_norm_truncatedDysonPath_le). Noncollision is still missing, so existence is proved only for at most one particle. An [exponential time change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DysonBrownianMotion/OrnsteinUhlenbeck.html#TauCeti.Probability.isDysonPath_iff_isDysonOUPath_ouTimeChange) identifies Dyson trajectories with Ornstein–Uhlenbeck ones in both directions.

On the matrix side, matrix Brownian motion gained [independent increments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/MatrixBrownian.html#TauCeti.Probability.hasIndepIncrements_matrixBrownian) and invariance in law under [unitary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/RandomMatrices/MatrixBrownian.html#TauCeti.Probability.identDistrib_mul_matrixBrownian_two_mul_conjTranspose) and orthogonal conjugation. [Hadamard's first variation formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Eigenvalues/Smooth.html#Matrix.hasFDerivAt_eigenvalues₀) for simple ordered eigenvalues is the first step of the eigenvalue equation. Supporting work added global solutions of nonautonomous Lipschitz ODEs and identities for sums over ordered index pairs.
