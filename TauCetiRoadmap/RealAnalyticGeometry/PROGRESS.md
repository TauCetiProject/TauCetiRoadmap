## RealAnalyticGeometry: 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-24 (`42da0da` to `271e1af`)

The roadmap's first window defines its central objects. A set is [semianalytic at a point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.SemianalyticAt) if, near that point, it is a finite Boolean combination of zero sets and positivity sets of analytic functions. It is [semianalytic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsSemianalytic) if this holds at every point of the ambient space. These come with the basic theory the roadmap asks for (TauCeti#20126): the sets form an algebra and are closed under [locally finite unions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#LocallyFinite.isSemianalytic_iUnion), [analytic preimages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsSemianalytic.preimage) and analytic isomorphisms. The window also proves that a presentation can be shrunk, that the different presentations are equivalent, and that [semialgebraic sets are semianalytic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsSemialgebraic.isSemianalytic). All of this lives on a normed space. Nothing yet works on manifolds through charts.

On the chart side, the window proves [the identity theorem on analytic manifolds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/Analytic.html#ContMDiff.eq_of_eventuallyEq): two analytic functions on a connected analytic manifold that agree near one point agree everywhere. It also shows that [changes of coordinates are analytic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/Analytic.html#ModelWithCorners.analyticOnNhd_extendCoordChange) and that a map is [analytic exactly when its chart expressions are](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/Analytic.html#TauCeti.contMDiffAt_omega_iff). A real-analytic function on an open set of ℝⁿ now [extends holomorphically](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Complexification/Neighborhood.html#AnalyticOnNhd.exists_complexification) to a conjugation-invariant complex neighbourhood, and the extension commutes with conjugation and is [unique near the real points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Complexification/Neighborhood.html#AnalyticOnNhd.exists_eqOn_of_forall_real).

Two first pieces of the triangulation theorem also landed. Łojasiewicz's Lemma 6 says that [Lipschitz analytic submanifolds are topographic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Submanifold/Topographic.html#TauCeti.IsAnalyticSubmanifold.exists_topographic): such a submanifold is the graph of a Lipschitz function, and that function is analytic. It comes with the estimate for small shears. Separately, [coning a triangulation of the boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SimplicialComplex/Cone.html#Geometry.SimplicialComplex.space_cone_of_space_eq_intrinsicFrontier) of a compact convex set to an interior point triangulates the whole set. This is the inductive step of the polyhedral subdivision lemma. The local normal decomposition has not been started.
