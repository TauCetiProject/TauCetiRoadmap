## RealAnalyticGeometry: 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-24 (`42da0da` to `5d671b9`)

The roadmap's foundational definition is now in place: [semianalytic sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsSemianalytic), those that near every point agree with a finite Boolean combination of zero sets and positivity sets of real-analytic functions, together with the pointwise notion [semianalytic at a point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.SemianalyticAt) (TauCeti#20126). Around it comes the basic calculus the roadmap's first semianalytic layer asks for: presentations can be shrunk into any neighbourhood, every presentation can be put in the form "one equation and finitely many strict inequalities" ([equivalence of presentations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.semianalyticAt_iff_exists_iUnion)), semianalytic sets form an algebra of sets, and they are stable under [analytic preimages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsSemianalytic.preimage), [analytic isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#Equiv.isSemianalytic_image_iff) and [locally finite unions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#LocallyFinite.isSemianalytic_iUnion). [Semialgebraic sets are semianalytic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsSemialgebraic.isSemianalytic), via a sign-condition normal form proved for both. All of this lives in a normed space; the manifold-level notion and the local normal decomposition have not started.

The neighbourhood complexification adapter also landed: a real-analytic function on an open subset of ℝⁿ [extends holomorphically](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Complexification/Neighborhood.html#AnalyticOnNhd.exists_complexification) to an open set whose real points are exactly the original set, compatibly with conjugation, and such extensions are [unique near the real locus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Complexification/Neighborhood.html#AnalyticOnNhd.exists_eqOn_of_forall_real) (TauCeti#19958). On the triangulation side, the [cone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SimplicialComplex/Cone.html#Geometry.SimplicialComplex.cone) on a geometric simplicial complex is now available, with the theorem that [coning a triangulated boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SimplicialComplex/Cone.html#Geometry.SimplicialComplex.space_cone_of_space_eq_intrinsicFrontier) of a compact convex set to an interior point triangulates the whole set. That is the inductive step of the convex-polyhedral subdivision lemma that comes before Łojasiewicz's theorem, though the lemma itself is not yet stated.

## RealAnalyticGeometry: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-09 (`5d671b9` to `7ca783d`)

The [real analytic Weierstrass preparation theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Complexification/Weierstrass.html#AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation_real) is now proved: a real-analytic function whose vertical slice vanishes to finite order is, near the point, a unit times a real Weierstrass polynomial. The proof complexifies, prepares over ℂ and descends by conjugation symmetry, and comes with the matching division theorem and uniqueness (TauCeti#28359). This is the input the local normal decomposition has been waiting for. Several of Łojasiewicz's lemmas also landed: Lemma 5, that [a set with nonsingular vertical direction is partially semialgebraic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/PartiallySemialgebraic/NonsingularDirection.html#TauCeti.IsNonsingularDirection.isPartiallySemialgebraic); Lemma 6, that [Lipschitz analytic submanifolds are topographic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Submanifold/Topographic.html#TauCeti.IsAnalyticSubmanifold.exists_topographic); the [Lefschetz–Whitehead rank estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/FDeriv/RankDrop.html#HasFDerivAt.finrank_range_lt_of_infinite); and Lemma 10's [straightening of an ordered family of graphs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/GraphStraightening.html#TauCeti.exists_straightening_graphOn) by an analytic automorphism. They rest on a theory of partially semialgebraic sets, whose [projections to the base are semianalytic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/PartiallySemialgebraic/Projection.html#TauCeti.IsPartiallySemialgebraic.isSemianalytic_image_fst).

On the polyhedral side, the [relative subdivision of a locally finite family of compact convex cells](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SimplicialComplex/Subdivision.html#Geometry.SimplicialComplex.exists_subdivision_extending) and Lemma 15's [triangulation of the rectilinear cells](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SimplicialComplex/Rectilinear.html#TauCeti.exists_subdivision_rectilinearCells) are both done, as are the analytic and partially semialgebraic band maps of Lemmas 13–14.

The Nash layer went from one inclusion to most of its API: [Nash germs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Nash/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsNashAt) with composition, derivatives and an implicit function theorem, the [local graph criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Nash/Graph.html#TauCeti.isNashAt_iff_exists_isSemialgebraicOn), and [locally semialgebraic sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/Local.html#TauCeti.IsLocallySemialgebraic), which are globally semialgebraic when bounded. Semianalytic sets are now defined [on manifolds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Manifold.html#TauCeti.IsMSemianalytic), and the chart-local germ API is in place. Lemmas 3–4 and 7–9, the normal decomposition and Theorem 1 itself are still missing.
