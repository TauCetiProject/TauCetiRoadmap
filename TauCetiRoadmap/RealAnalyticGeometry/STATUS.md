## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The local foundations are largely in place. R0's germ API, semianalytic sets on manifolds, most of the Nash layer R1.1 and about half of the lemmas in Łojasiewicz's affine proof are done, along with the convex-polyhedral subdivision lemma. The local normal decomposition, dimension theory, the remaining affine lemmas and Łojasiewicz's Theorem 1 are not. Stein theory (R3), Grauert's embedding beyond dimension zero (R4) and manifold triangulation (R5) have not begun.

### Named results

- **The real analytic Weierstrass preparation theorem** — near a point where the vertical slice of a real-analytic function vanishes to finite order, the function is a unit times a real Weierstrass polynomial. It comes with real division and uniqueness, and is proved by complexifying and descending through conjugation symmetry ([`AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation_real`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Complexification/Weierstrass.html#AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation_real)).
- **Łojasiewicz's Lemma 5** — a subset of a cylinder with bounded heights whose vertical direction is nonsingular is partially semialgebraic, that is, locally polynomial in the height with analytic coefficients ([`TauCeti.IsNonsingularDirection.isPartiallySemialgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/PartiallySemialgebraic/NonsingularDirection.html#TauCeti.IsNonsingularDirection.isPartiallySemialgebraic)).
- **Łojasiewicz's straightening of graphs (Lemma 10)** — an ordered, locally finite two-sided family of Lipschitz graphs near a vertical line is carried, by an analytic automorphism of the cylinder, to disjoint bounded graphs with a common Lipschitz bound and unbounded tails, as Step I of the proof requires ([`TauCeti.exists_straightening_graphOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/GraphStraightening.html#TauCeti.exists_straightening_graphOn)).
- **The relative convex-polyhedral subdivision lemma** — a locally finite family of compact convex cells with disjoint intrinsic interiors has a simplicial subdivision extending a prescribed complex on their boundaries ([`Geometry.SimplicialComplex.exists_subdivision_extending`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SimplicialComplex/Subdivision.html#Geometry.SimplicialComplex.exists_subdivision_extending)). This is the single subdivision supplier that R2 and R2.1 both consume.
- **The local Nash graph criterion** — a function is a Nash germ exactly when it is analytic and its graph is semialgebraic on some open semialgebraic neighbourhood ([`TauCeti.isNashAt_iff_exists_isSemialgebraicOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Nash/Graph.html#TauCeti.isNashAt_iff_exists_isSemialgebraicOn)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Partially semialgebraic sets**: [`TauCeti.IsPartiallySemialgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/PartiallySemialgebraic.html#TauCeti.IsPartiallySemialgebraic), the class of Łojasiewicz's §1, which is closed under Boolean operations, locally finite unions and analytic base changes. Its [projection to the base is semianalytic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/PartiallySemialgebraic/Projection.html#TauCeti.IsPartiallySemialgebraic.isSemianalytic_image_fst). Property (A) and every projected family in R2 are expressed through it.
- **Nash germs and locally semialgebraic sets**: [`TauCeti.IsNashAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Nash/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsNashAt) and [`TauCeti.IsLocallySemialgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Semialgebraic/Local.html#TauCeti.IsLocallySemialgebraic). Together they give the Nash category in which R2.1's bounded triangulation will be stated.
- **Semianalytic sets on manifolds**: [`TauCeti.IsMSemianalytic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/Manifold.html#TauCeti.IsMSemianalytic), tested in any chart of the maximal analytic atlas, alongside [analytic submanifolds of a manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/AnalyticSubmanifold/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsMAnalyticSubmanifold). These are the carriers R4 and R5 will need.

### Roadmap coverage

**R0** is done. The neighbourhood complexification adapter is joined by the [analytic germ ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Germ.html#TauCeti.analyticGerms) with pullback, its canonical [complexification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Complexification/Germ.html#TauCeti.analyticGerms.complexify), [chart transport](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/Germ.html#ModelWithCorners.analyticGermsEquiv) with its cocycle law, and the [identity theorem on analytic manifolds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/Analytic.html#ContMDiff.eq_of_eventuallyEq). **R1** is partial. Its first paragraph is done, now at manifold level as well, together with the one-dimensional case and real Weierstrass preparation. The normal decomposition, dimension theory, the frontier condition and component finiteness are untouched. **R1.1** is partial. Nash germs, the Nash implicit function theorem, derivatives, the graph criterion, Nash presentations, and locally semialgebraic sets with their components and the bounded-implies-semialgebraic theorem are all proved. The Nash normal decomposition, algebraic realification and Nash maps on semialgebraic submanifolds are not. **R2** is partial. The subdivision lemma, Lemmas 1, 2, 5, 6, 10, 15 and the band calculus of Lemmas 11–14 are proved. Lemmas 3–4 and 7–9, the normal-box API, Steps 5–8 and Theorem 1 are missing. **R2.1** has only the [projector embedding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Projectivization/Projector.html#Projectivization.isSmoothEmbedding_projector) of real projective space and its [semialgebraic subsets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/ProjectorEmbedding.html#TauCeti.isSemialgebraic_image_projector_iff); Theorems 3 and 4 are untouched. **R4** has only the [zero-dimensional case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothEmbedding/ZeroDimensional.html#TauCeti.exists_isProperMap_isSmoothEmbedding_real_two_of_subsingleton). **R3 and R5** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **R1 local normal decomposition**: real Weierstrass preparation is now available. What remains is the bad-set induction: squarefree reduction, subresultant and discriminant cells, ordered root sections described by Thom signs, and the dimension-drop termination. It still draws on the RealAlgebraicGeometry Layers 1, 2 and 4 suppliers.
- **Lemmas 3–4, singular directions are meagre**: needs the common-complex-neighbourhood reduction for the directional-derivative equations, via CK K3's Noetherian germs. Lemmas 1 and 2, which it uses, are proved.
- **Lemmas 7–9 and the normal-box API**: the property-(P) hypersurface lemma and the adapted normal boxes, which depend on the normal decomposition. With them in place, Lemmas 5, 6 and 10 can be assembled into Step I.
- **R2.1 bounded affine triangulation (Theorem 3)**: the subdivision lemma, band maps and Nash graph criterion it uses are ready. It still needs A4.1's parametrized finite lifting data and the Nashness of the auxiliary-section extensions.
- **R3.0 global complexification**: nothing has started. It blocks R3's Stein theory and, through it, R4 in positive dimension.
