## Where this roadmap stands

R1's local normal decomposition and dimension theory, and in R2 Lemmas 3–4, 7–9 and Theorem 1, are partial at best: route pieces exist, the theorems do not. ### Named results

- **The real analytic Weierstrass preparation theorem** — near a point where the vertical slice of a real-analytic function vanishes to finite order, the function is a unit times a real Weierstrass polynomial, with real division and uniqueness, proved by complexifying and descending through conjugation symmetry ([`AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation_real`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/Complexification/Weierstrass.html#AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation_real)).
- **The Nash inverse function theorem** — a map with Nash coordinate germs and invertible derivative restricts, inside any prescribed neighbourhood, to a local homeomorphism with open semialgebraic source and target and semialgebraic graphs in both directions ([`TauCeti.exists_openPartialHomeomorph_of_forall_isNashAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/Nash/Inverse.html#TauCeti.exists_openPartialHomeomorph_of_forall_isNashAt)).
- **Łojasiewicz's Lemma 5** — a subset of a cylinder with bounded heights whose vertical direction is nonsingular is partially semialgebraic, that is, locally polynomial in the height with analytic coefficients ([`TauCeti.IsNonsingularDirection.isPartiallySemialgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RealAnalytic/Semianalytic/PartiallySemialgebraic/NonsingularDirection.html#TauCeti.IsNonsingularDirection.isPartiallySemialgebraic)).
- **Łojasiewicz's straightening of graphs (Lemma 10)** — an ordered, locally finite two-sided family of Lipschitz graphs near a vertical line is carried, by an analytic automorphism of the cylinder, to disjoint bounded graphs with a common Lipschitz bound, as Step I of the proof requires ([`TauCeti.exists_straightening_graphOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Analytic/GraphStraightening.html#TauCeti.exists_straightening_graphOn)).
- **The relative convex-polyhedral subdivision lemma** — a locally finite family of compact convex cells with disjoint intrinsic interiors has a simplicial subdivision extending a prescribed complex on their boundaries, the one subdivision supplier for both R2 and R2.1 ([`Geometry.SimplicialComplex.exists_subdivision_extending`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SimplicialComplex/Subdivision.html#Geometry.SimplicialComplex.exists_subdivision_extending)).
