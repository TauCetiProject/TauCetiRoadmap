## Chebotarev: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-01 (`7201851` to `8745177`)

The roadmap's first window laid foundations at its two ends. On the analytic side, the **Frobenius orientation on roots of unity** ([`AlgHom.IsArithFrobAt.apply_eq_pow_absNorm_of_pow_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Frobenius.html#AlgHom.IsArithFrobAt.apply_eq_pow_absNorm_of_pow_eq_one)) landed: for a prime `𝔭` of a number field `K` not dividing `m`, every arithmetic Frobenius at a prime above `𝔭` sends an `m`-th root of unity in an extension field `F` to its `𝔑𝔭`-th power. This is the orientation fact the roadmap's acceptance tests insist on (Frobenius corresponds to `p mod m`, not its inverse), stated for an arbitrary arithmetic Frobenius element rather than for the Artin class. It came with the instance that the Galois action on `𝓞 F` commutes with the `𝓞 K`-action ([`NumberField.RingOfIntegers.smulCommClass`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/AutomorphismAction.html#NumberField.RingOfIntegers.smulCommClass)).

On the group-theoretic side, **powers of conjugacy classes** ([`ConjClasses.pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Conj.html#ConjClasses.pow)) is the `Pow (ConjClasses M) ℕ` operation the roadmap's von Mangoldt fibre will test against, with the membership law `τ ∈ C ^ j ↔ ∃ σ ∈ C, σ ^ j = τ` ([`ConjClasses.mem_pow_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Conj.html#ConjClasses.mem_pow_iff)), the identity and composition laws, and naturality in the monoid ([`ConjClasses.map_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Group/Conj.html#ConjClasses.map_pow)). Finally, the finite set of **primes of `K` ramifying in `L`** ([`NumberField.Chebotarev.ramifiedPrimes`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/RamifiedPrimes.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.ramifiedPrimes)) exists as a `Finset (HeightOneSpectrum (𝓞 K))`, with its membership characterisation (TauCeti#5400).

Most of the roadmap is still absent. `frobeniusPrimeSet` itself, the transport lemmas along towers, and the split-completely and class-cardinal facts of Layer 1 have not landed; nothing from Layer 3 onward has begun. The root-of-unity theorem is only the first half of Layer 4: abelianity of the cyclotomic Galois group and the character weight `cyclotomicCharacterWeight` are not yet there.

## Chebotarev: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-11 (`8745177` to `94050b6`)

The Frobenius fibres now form the promised partition of the unramified primes: [`frobeniusPrimeSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/FrobeniusPrimeSet.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.frobeniusPrimeSet) is proof-independent and equivariant under isomorphisms, distinct classes give disjoint fibres, and their union is exactly the complement of the ramified set ([`NumberField.Chebotarev.iUnion_frobeniusPrimeSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/FrobeniusPrimeSet.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.iUnion_frobeniusPrimeSet)). The Artin symbol also acquired both tower laws: restriction to a normal subextension is unpowered, while raising the base raises it to the intermediate residue degree ([`NumberField.artinSymbol_map_restrictNormalHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArtinSymbol.html#NumberField.artinSymbol_map_restrictNormalHom), [`NumberField.artinSymbol_map_restrictScalarsHom_eq_pow_inertiaDeg`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArtinSymbol.html#NumberField.artinSymbol_map_restrictScalarsHom_eq_pow_inertiaDeg)).

The crossing route gained its [`auxiliary-prime theorem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/AuxiliaryPrime.html#NumberField.exists_auxiliaryPrime), including the congruence, unramifiedness, and cyclotomic irreducibility conclusions. Cyclotomic composita and tagged fixed fields can now be constructed, and the latter carry the required cyclotomic extension ([`TauCeti.fixedField_zpowers_isCyclotomicExtension`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/TaggedFixedField.html#TauCeti.fixedField_zpowers_isCyclotomicExtension)); the exact fixed-field fibre count and its exceptional-set statement have not yet landed.

In particular, the higher-prime-power contribution to Frobenius `ψ` is now [`o(x)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/PrimeCounting/VonMangoldt.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.frobeniusPsi_sub_frobeniusTheta_isLittleO). The continuation, boundary-value, crossing, and density theorems remain absent.

## Chebotarev: 2026-09-11 to 2026-09-28 (`94050b6` to `8339b5a`)

The density spine reached its summit: **the Chebotarev density theorem** now holds in the form the roadmap specifies, that for a finite Galois extension `L / K` of number fields and a conjugacy class `C` of `Gal(L/K)`, the primes of `𝓞 K` whose Frobenius class is `C` have Dirichlet density `#C / #Gal(L/K)` ([`hasDirichletDensity_frobeniusPrimeSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Density/Chebotarev.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasDirichletDensity_frobeniusPrimeSet), TauCeti#8380). It arrives through the abelian case for a single automorphism ([`hasDirichletDensity_abelianFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Density/Abelian.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasDirichletDensity_abelianFrobenius)), contracted across the cyclic fixed field of a representative. This is the Dirichlet-density statement alone: no asymptotic for `ψ_C`, `ϑ_C` or `π_C`, and no natural-density form, is established by the declarations added here.

Two counts carry that contraction. Over an unramified prime with Artin class `C`, the primes of the fixed field `L^⟨σ⟩` of residue degree one whose relative Frobenius is `σ` number exactly `#G / (#C · orderOf σ)` ([`fixedField_frobenius_fiber_card`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/FixedField/FiberCount.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.fixedField_frobenius_fiber_card)), resting on the description of the absolute fibre as a single centralizer orbit ([`frobenius_fiber_card_eq_card_centralizer_div_orderOf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius/FiberCount.html#IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum.frobenius_fiber_card_eq_card_centralizer_div_orderOf)); and the auxiliary cyclotomic group supplies the crossing constant with the uniform bound `(1 - 2^(-r))^(#f.primeFactors) / #G` that makes the congruence level load-bearing ([`le_crossingConstant`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Crossing/CrossingConstant.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Chebotarev.le_crossingConstant)). The analytic side got its ray-class route: the cyclotomic Artin map factors a Galois character weight through a ray class character ([`cyclotomicArtin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/GaloisCharacter/Cyclotomic/Basic.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.cyclotomicArtin)), and a `3-4-1` nonvanishing criterion ([`ne_zero_of_threeFourOne`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Nonvanishing.html#TauCeti.LSeries.ne_zero_of_threeFourOne)).

The promised corollaries came with it. Completely split primes have density `1 / [L : K]`, the non-Galois case follows through a Galois closure ([`hasDirichletDensity_setOf_ncard_primesOver_eq_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Density/SplitsCompletely.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasDirichletDensity_setOf_ncard_primesOver_eq_finrank)), every Frobenius class is infinite, and Dirichlet's theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions appears in density form over `ℚ` ([`hasDirichletDensity_primesCongruent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Density/PrimesCongruent.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.hasDirichletDensity_primesCongruent)). The roadmap's tower-exponent regression, `ℚ(ζ₇) ⊃ ℚ(√-7) ⊃ ℚ` at `p = 3`, is discharged (TauCeti#9593). Many of the window's pull requests added no declarations at all, being proof work behind statements already in place.

## Chebotarev: 2026-09-28 to 2026-09-30 (`8339b5a` to `835fbbd`)

This window was about the roadmap's acceptance tests, the counterexamples and worked cases that pin down its conventions, rather than new theorems. The most substantial is the rational specialization of the counting side. Over `ℚ(ζₙ)` with `n` not congruent to `2` mod `4`, the Frobenius von Mangoldt coefficient of the class tagged by a unit `a` is exactly the von Mangoldt function restricted to `m ≡ a (mod n)` ([`frobeniusVonMangoldtCoeff_galEquivZMod_symm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/PrimeCounting/ArithmeticProgression.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.frobeniusVonMangoldtCoeff_galEquivZMod_symm), TauCeti#9773). The mandatory degree-four test of the powered filter comes with it ([`frobeniusVonMangoldtCoeff_cyclotomic_five_of_galEquivZMod_eq_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/PrimeCounting/ArithmeticProgression.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.frobeniusVonMangoldtCoeff_cyclotomic_five_of_galEquivZMod_eq_two)): in `ℚ(ζ₅)` the term at `4 = 2²` lies in the fibre of `[g]²`, not in that of the Frobenius `g` at `2`.

The other nearby false statements are now refuted by named witnesses. Column orthogonality fails on `S₃` ([`sum_inv_mul_monoidHom_apply_finRotate_three_ne_ite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/FinThree/Character.html#TauCeti.sum_inv_mul_monoidHom_apply_finRotate_three_ne_ite)), and in fact in every non-commutative group. Over `ℚ(√5)` the fifth cyclotomic extension has degree two, not four ([`finrank_adjoin_primitiveRoot_eq_two_of_sq_eq_five`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/FifthIntersection.html#TauCeti.NumberField.finrank_adjoin_primitiveRoot_eq_two_of_sq_eq_five)), even though `K(√2)` meets `K(ζ₅)` only in `K`. In the splitting field of `X³ - 2`, a prime above `2` must be discarded on the fixed-field side even though it is unramified there ([`exists_mem_primesAboveRamifiedPrimes_not_mem_ramifiedPrimes_X_pow_three_sub_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/FixedField/S3Contraction.html#TauCeti.exists_mem_primesAboveRamifiedPrimes_not_mem_ramifiedPrimes_X_pow_three_sub_two)). For a cyclic group of order four the crossing constant counts three tags ([`crossingConstant_two_of_card_four`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/Crossing/CyclicFour.html#TauCeti.NumberField.Chebotarev.crossingConstant_two_of_card_four)), and above a completely split prime a nonidentity `σ` has an empty fixed-field fibre ([`fixedField_frobenius_fiber_eq_empty_of_mem_frobeniusPrimeSet_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/FixedField/IdentityFiber.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.fixedField_frobenius_fiber_eq_empty_of_mem_frobeniusPrimeSet_one)). Supporting this, the exceptional set for the contraction is now described through inertia groups ([`under_notMem_ramifiedPrimes_iff_disjoint_inertia`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Chebotarev/FixedField/ExceptionalPrimes.html#NumberField.Chebotarev.under_notMem_ramifiedPrimes_iff_disjoint_inertia)).
