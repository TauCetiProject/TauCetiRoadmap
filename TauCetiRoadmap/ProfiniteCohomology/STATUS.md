## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The low-degree theory is essentially complete, including its comparisons, its degree-two colimit theorem, the five-term sequence, Hilbert 90 and Kummer theory with its norm square. The all-degree theory has the long exact sequence, dimension shifting, torsion and a large part of cohomological dimension. Still missing are all-degree Shapiro, corestriction, the canonical colimit theorem, graded commutativity of the cup product beyond degree one, and the general Evens norm.

### Named results

- **[Hilbert 90](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Hilbert90.html#TauCeti.hilbert90)**: continuous `H¹` of the absolute Galois group with coefficients in the units of the separable closure vanishes.
- **[The Kummer isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.html#TauCeti.kummerIso) and [its norm square](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.html#TauCeti.kummerIso_norm)**: for `n` invertible in `K`, `Kˣ/(Kˣ)ⁿ ≅ H¹(G_K, μₙ)`, and corestriction from a finite extension corresponds to the field norm.
- **[Dimension shifting](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/DimensionShifting/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dimensionShiftIso)**: for a compact group, `Hⁱ⁺¹(G, M) ≅ Hⁱ(G, Coind_1^G M ⧸ M)` in every degree `i ≥ 1`, because `Coind_1^G` is [acyclic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Coinduced/Acyclic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.coindAcyclic).
- **[Torsion of positive-degree cohomology](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Torsion.html#TauCeti.ContinuousCohomology.isAddTorsion_continuousCohomology)**: NSW (1.6.1). Every class in positive degree is torsion, and cohomology with `ℚ`-vector-space coefficients vanishes there.
- **[The degree-two finite-quotient colimit theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/FiniteQuotient/Colimit.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitFiniteQuotientColimit2)**: `H²(G, M)` is the colimit over inflation of `H²(G/U, M^U)`, and degrees zero and one have parallel results.

- **[The discrete-module dictionary](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/SmoothDiscrete.html#TauCeti.discreteRepEquivSmoothTopRep)**: identifies discrete `G`-modules with smooth discrete topological representations, which is where the explicit and canonical theories meet.

## The frontier

- **`cd_p ℤ̂ = 1`**: prove the upper bound and `H²(ℤ̂, M) = 0` for finite `M`. The lower bound and the single-degree finite test are in place.
- **All-degree Shapiro and corestriction**: build the Shapiro cochain isomorphism, then corestriction through the existing coinduced trace. `cor ∘ res = (G : U)`, Mackey and the projection formula follow from these.
- **The canonical colimit theorem**: prove the existing cocone is a colimit and that cohomology preserves filtered coefficient colimits. The dévissage and closed-subgroup steps of Layer 11 wait on this.
- **Graded commutativity in all bidegrees**: construct the commutativity homotopy. Only the `(1,1)` case exists.
- **Field-extension adapters and the Evens norm**: `galoisCor`, `galoisEvens` and embedding independence need all-degree corestriction. The general norm needs the wreath-product construction on top of Layer 12.
