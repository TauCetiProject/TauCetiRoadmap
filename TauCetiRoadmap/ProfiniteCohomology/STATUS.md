### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dimension shifting** ([`dimensionShiftIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/DimensionShifting/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dimensionShiftIso)), `Hⁱ⁺¹(G, M) ≅ Hⁱ(G, Coind_1^G M ⧸ M)`. Together with Shapiro's lemma in every degree, it drives the cohomological-dimension arguments.
- **The Evens norm** ([`evensNorm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Evens/Norm/Basic.html#OpenSubgroup.evensNorm)), the function `Hⁿ(U, 𝔽₂) → H^{l n}(G, 𝔽₂)` for `U` open of index `l`, sending a class to the class of the diagonal tensor of a representing cocycle. At index two it [is the explicit index-two norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Evens/Norm/IndexTwo.html#OpenSubgroup.evensNormIndexTwo_eq), which makes the graph cocycle a standard construction.
- **Evens's twisted multiplication** ([`tensorInducedMulAlgHom`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Induction/Ring/Basic.html#Subgroup.tensorInducedMulAlgHom)), `a₁ ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ a_l ↦ t₁a₁ ⋯ t_l a_l` from the tensor-induced `G`-ring to a commutative `G`-algebra. It is equivariant, independent of the transversal, and compatible with cup products. This is the coefficient map that the norm for general coefficients will push forward along.

## The frontier

- **The double-coset formula over several double cosets** — express the restriction of `N_U^G x` to `K` as a cup product of norms over `K \ G / U`. The orbitwise tensor complex now has [a contraction compatible with restriction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Induction/DoubleCoset/Contraction.html#TauCeti.res_gradedCohomologyClass_smoothTensorInducedGradedResolutionContraction_mackey). What remains is splitting the orbitwise class as a cup product.
- **Normalization and expansion at `𝔽₂`** — prove `N_U^G 1 = 1`, then that the norm of `1 + χ` expands as `∑ Nᵢ(χ)` with `N₁ = cor` and `N_l = N_U^G`.
- **The norm for a discrete commutative `G`-ring** — milestones 3 to 7 for general `A`: the diagonal-tensor cocycle with values in the tensor-induced ring, pushed forward along [the twisted multiplication](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Induction/Ring/Discrete.html#TauCeti.smoothTensorInducedMulHom). Then descent to classes and the laws. Outside characteristic two this needs the sign-correct even-degree construction.
- **The `R`-linear cochain functor** — a one-line instance from `cochainsMap_smul` at `φ = id`.
- **Universe independence of `cd_p`** — build the chain isomorphism between the homogeneous cochain complexes at the two universe parameters, as 11.2 describes, and conclude that both invariants agree.
