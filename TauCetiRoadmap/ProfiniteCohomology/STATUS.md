## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The low-degree theory is essentially complete. So are Layer 3's comparisons, Layer 5's exact sequences, Layer 10's all-degree toolkit and Layer 12's graded cup product. Strict dimension is now computed from ordinary dimension. Still partial are the class module of strict dimension two, the reduction to simple modules, and the index-two Evens norm as a function on classes together with its Galois transport. The general Evens norm has its ingredients but no norm.

### Named results

- **Hilbert 90 and the Kummer isomorphism**: continuous `H¹(G_K, (Kˢ)ˣ)` vanishes, so `Kˣ/(Kˣ)ⁿ ≅ H¹(G_K, μₙ)` for `n` invertible in `K` ([`TauCeti.hilbert90`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Hilbert90.html#TauCeti.hilbert90), [`TauCeti.kummerIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.html#TauCeti.kummerIso)).
- **The cohomological dimension of `ℤ̂`**: `cd_p ℤ̂ = 1` for every prime `p`. The upper bound holds because profinite extensions of `ℤ̂` split ([`zHat.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/CohomologicalDimension.html#TauCeti.zHat.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_one)).
- **The strict-dimension criterion** (NSW (3.3.4)): `scd_p G ≤ n` if and only if `cd_p G ≤ n` and the `p`-primary part of `H^{n+1}(U, ℤ)` vanishes for every open `U` ([`strictCohomologicalDimensionLE_iff_forall_openSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/IntegralCriterion.html#TauCeti.strictCohomologicalDimensionLE_iff_forall_openSubgroup)).
- **The Mackey double-coset formula in every degree** (NSW (1.5.6)): restriction after corestriction is a sum over double cosets of restriction, conjugation and corestriction ([`corestriction_mackey`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Corestriction/Mackey/AllDegrees.html#TauCeti.ContinuousCohomology.corestriction_mackey)).
- **The kernel of restriction at index two**: for an open subgroup `U` of index two, a class of `H²(G, 𝔽₂)` dies on `U` exactly when it is `χ_U ⌣ y` ([`exact_cup_res2_of_index_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Corestriction/IndexTwo/CupExact.html#OpenSubgroup.exact_cup_res2_of_index_two)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dimension shifting** ([`dimensionShiftIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/DimensionShifting/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dimensionShiftIso)), `Hⁱ⁺¹(G, M) ≅ Hⁱ(G, Coind_1^G M ⧸ M)`. Together with Shapiro's lemma in every degree, this is what the cohomological-dimension arguments run on.
- **The integral Bockstein** ([`integralBockstein`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Bockstein/Integral.html#TauCeti.integralBockstein)) of `0 → ℤ → ℤ → ℤ/n → 0`. It identifies `ℤ/n`-characters with the `n`-torsion of `H²(G, ℤ)`, so `p`-primary integral classes can be read as characters. The class-module argument uses it to read (3.3.11) at `ℤ` coefficients on characters.
- **Tensor induction** ([`Subgroup.tensorInductionFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Induction.html#Subgroup.tensorInductionFunctor)), along the monomial homomorphism into the wreath product. The general Evens norm is to be built through it.

## The frontier

- **The index-two norm on classes**: descend the graph class to `H¹(U, 𝔽₂)` and prove `N(α) = (Ind α)^* c_{D₁₆}` on classes. This is now unblocked, since Layer 3's canonical class of an inhomogeneous 2-cocycle has landed.
- **`galoisEvens`**: transport the norm and identities 1, 2 and 5 to a quadratic `L/K`, and prove embedding independence. The Quadratic Form Invariants roadmap consumes these.
- **The class module of strict dimension two** (NSW (3.6.4)): the transfer and `Ver : G^ab(p) ≅ V^ab(p)^{G/V}`, the injectivity half of (3.3.11), the order-`p` step, and the change-of-group and Sylow steps. Surjectivity in (3.3.11), the integral Bockstein, character separation, cyclic `H²` and the trace-kernel cover sequence are in place.
- **Reduction to simple modules**: test `cd_p G ≤ n` on finite simple `p`-primary modules, including independence of the chosen open normal subgroup.
- **The general Evens norm**: with the wreath product, monomial homomorphism and tensor induction built, what remains is the norm at resolution level, transversal independence, multiplicativity and transitivity, and agreement with the index-two graph class.
