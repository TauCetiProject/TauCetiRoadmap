### Named results

- **The class module of strict dimension two** — NSW (3.6.4) in `p`-primary form: if `scd_p G ≤ 2` and `V` is open and normal, then `H¹(G ⧸ V, V^ab(p)) = 0` and `u_{G/V}(p)` generates `H²(G ⧸ V, V^ab(p))`, a group whose order is the `p`-part of `#(G ⧸ V)` (`abelianizationProPClass_generates`). The cyclic case is [`abelianizationProPClass_generates_of_isCyclic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/ClassModule/Cyclic/SecondCohomology.html#TauCeti.abelianizationProPClass_generates_of_isCyclic).
- **The strict-dimension criterion** — NSW (3.3.4): `scd_p G ≤ n` exactly when `cd_p G ≤ n` and the `p`-primary part of `H^{n+1}(U, ℤ)` vanishes for every open `U` ([`strictCohomologicalDimensionLE_iff_forall_openSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/IntegralCriterion.html#TauCeti.strictCohomologicalDimensionLE_iff_forall_openSubgroup)).
- **Hilbert 90 and the Kummer isomorphism** — continuous `H¹(G_K, (Kˢ)ˣ)` vanishes, so `Kˣ/(Kˣ)ⁿ ≅ H¹(G_K, μₙ)` for `n` invertible in `K` ([`TauCeti.hilbert90`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Hilbert90.html#TauCeti.hilbert90), [`TauCeti.kummerIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.html#TauCeti.kummerIso)). Over `ℚ`, [every class of `H¹(G_ℚ, 𝔽₂)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/MuTwo/Rational.html#TauCeti.exists_squarefree_int_kummerClass) is the Kummer class of a squarefree integer.
- **The mod-2 Bockstein is a derivation** — for the Bockstein of `0 → ℤ/2 → ℤ/4 → ℤ/2 → 0`, `β(x ⌣ y) = βx ⌣ y + x ⌣ βy` in every degree ([`cyclicBockstein_two_cup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Bockstein/Leibniz.html#TauCeti.cyclicBockstein_two_cup)).
- **The index-two Evens norm** — for an open subgroup `U` of index two, a map `H¹(U, 𝔽₂) → H²(G, 𝔽₂)` defined by descending the graph class ([`evensNormIndexTwo`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Evens/IndexTwoNorm.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.evensNormIndexTwo)). On classes it equals the pullback of the `D₁₆` extension class.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dimension shifting** ([`dimensionShiftIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/DimensionShifting/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dimensionShiftIso)), `Hⁱ⁺¹(G, M) ≅ Hⁱ(G, Coind_1^G M ⧸ M)`. With Shapiro's lemma in every degree, it drives the cohomological-dimension arguments.
- **Tensor induction** ([`tensorInducedTransitivity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Induction/Transitivity/Basic.html#Subgroup.tensorInducedTransitivity)), along the monomial homomorphism into the wreath product. It is now transitive in towers, satisfies a [Mackey formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Induction/DoubleCoset/Basic.html#Subgroup.tensorInducedMackey) and does not depend on the transversal up to natural isomorphism. These are the properties the general Evens norm's transitivity and double-coset formula will rest on.
- **Contractions of resolutions** ([`TopRep.ResolutionContraction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Resolution/Comparison.html#TopRep.ResolutionContraction)). A complex contracted onto its coefficients maps canonically to continuous cohomology, independently of the contraction. Applied to the coinduced resolution summed over all degrees, it lets cocycles built there be read as ordinary classes.
