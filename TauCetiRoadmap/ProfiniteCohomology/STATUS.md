## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The low-degree theory is essentially complete, including Hilbert 90 and Kummer theory. The all-degree theory now has its core: Shapiro, corestriction, dimension shifting, torsion and the finite-quotient colimit theorem, all in every degree. Still missing are filtered coefficient colimits, `cd_p ℤ̂ = 1`, graded commutativity of the cup product in general, and the Evens norm.

### Named results

- **[Hilbert 90](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Hilbert90.html#TauCeti.hilbert90) and [the Kummer isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.html#TauCeti.kummerIso)**: continuous `H¹(G_K, (Kˢ)ˣ)` vanishes. Hence, for `n` invertible in `K`, `Kˣ/(Kˣ)ⁿ ≅ H¹(G_K, μₙ)`, with corestriction matching the field norm.
- **[`cor ∘ res = [G : U]` in every degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Corestriction/AllDegrees.html#TauCeti.ContinuousCohomology.res_comp_corestriction)**: NSW (1.5.7). Corestriction from an open finite-index subgroup is built through Shapiro and the trace, and is natural, transitive and compatible with connecting maps.
- **[The finite-quotient colimit theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/FiniteQuotient/AllDegreeColimit.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.continuousFiniteQuotientColimit)**: for a compact group, `Hⁿ(G, M)` is the colimit of `Hⁿ(G/U, M^U)` over open normal `U`, in every degree.
- **[Dimension shifting](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/DimensionShifting/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dimensionShiftIso)**: `Hⁱ⁺¹(G, M) ≅ Hⁱ(G, Coind_1^G M ⧸ M)` for `i ≥ 1`. Together with [torsion in positive degree](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Torsion.html#TauCeti.ContinuousCohomology.isAddTorsion_continuousCohomology), it carries the cohomological-dimension arguments.
- **[The projection formula](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Cup/ProjectionFormula.html#TauCeti.TopPairing.cup_projection)**: the all-degree cup product and corestriction satisfy `cor(res a ⌣ b) = a ⌣ cor b` in every bidegree.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[The discrete-module dictionary](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/SmoothDiscrete.html#TauCeti.discreteRepEquivSmoothTopRep)**: identifies discrete `G`-modules with smooth discrete topological representations, which is where the explicit and canonical theories meet.
- **[The all-degree cup product](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Cup/Assoc.html#TauCeti.TopPairing.cup_assoc)**: the Alexander–Whitney product. It is associative, unital and functorial, and it matches the six explicit low-degree cups.

## The frontier

- **`cd_p ℤ̂ = 1`**: prove the upper bound, i.e. `H²(ℤ̂, M) = 0` for finite `p`-primary `M`. The lower bound and the finite tests are in place.
- **Filtered coefficient colimits**: prove that continuous cohomology preserves filtered colimits of smooth discrete coefficients. Layer 4's remainder and Layer 11's reduction to simple modules wait on it.
- **Graded commutativity in all bidegrees**: construct the commutativity homotopy. Only `(0, n)` and `(1, 1)` exist.
- **All-degree Mackey and degree-two corestriction agreement**: lift the degree 0–2 Mackey formula to all-degree corestriction, and identify it with the explicit `cor²`.
- **The Evens norm and `galoisEvens`**: build the general multiplicative norm on the wreath products now available, then match it with the index-two graph class and its field adapter.
