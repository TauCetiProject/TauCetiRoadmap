## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The low-degree theory is essentially complete, including Hilbert 90 and Kummer theory, and Layer 5's exact sequences are done. The all-degree theory has Shapiro, corestriction, dimension shifting, torsion and the finite-quotient colimit theorem, and `cd_p` is unchanged by passing to subgroups of index prime to `p`. Still missing are filtered coefficient colimits, `cd_p ℤ̂ ≤ 1`, the strict-dimension theory, general graded commutativity, and the Evens norm beyond its index-two graph class.

### Named results

- **Hilbert 90 and the Kummer isomorphism** — continuous `H¹(G_K, (Kˢ)ˣ)` vanishes, so for `n` invertible in `K`, `Kˣ/(Kˣ)ⁿ ≅ H¹(G_K, μₙ)`, with corestriction matching the field norm ([`TauCeti.hilbert90`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Hilbert90.html#TauCeti.hilbert90), [`TauCeti.kummerIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.html#TauCeti.kummerIso)).
- **`cor ∘ res = [G : U]` in every degree** — corestriction from an open subgroup, built through Shapiro and the trace, satisfies NSW (1.5.7) and is natural, transitive and compatible with connecting maps ([`res_comp_corestriction`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Corestriction/AllDegrees.html#TauCeti.ContinuousCohomology.res_comp_corestriction)).
- **The finite-quotient colimit theorem** — for a compact group, `Hⁿ(G, M)` is the colimit of `Hⁿ(G/U, M^U)` over open normal `U`, in every degree ([`continuousFiniteQuotientColimit`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/FiniteQuotient/AllDegreeColimit.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.continuousFiniteQuotientColimit)).
- **The projection formula** — the all-degree cup product satisfies `cor(res a ⌣ b) = a ⌣ cor b` in every bidegree ([`cup_projection`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Cup/ProjectionFormula.html#TauCeti.TopPairing.cup_projection)).
- **The prime-to-`p` equality** — an open subgroup of index prime to `p` has the same `cd_p` as the profinite group (NSW (3.3.5)), and so does a closed subgroup whose open neighbourhoods all have index prime to `p`, which covers Sylow pro-`p` subgroups ([`cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_not_dvd_index`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/IndexNotDvd.html#TauCeti.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_not_dvd_index), [`cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_isClosed_of_forall_not_dvd_index`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/IndexNotDvd.html#TauCeti.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_isClosed_of_forall_not_dvd_index)).

## The frontier

- **`cd_p ℤ̂ = 1`**: prove `H²(ℤ̂, M) = 0` for finite `p`-primary `M`. The lower bound and the finite tests are in place.
- **Filtered coefficient colimits**: continuous cohomology commutes with filtered colimits of smooth discrete coefficients. Layer 4's remainder, the reduction to simple modules and the strict-dimension criterion wait on it.
- **The index-two package**: the exact sequence `H¹(G) → H¹(U) → H¹(G) → H²(G) → H²(U) → H²(G)` built from restriction, corestriction and cup with `χ_U`, the identity `N(res y) = y ⌣ y + χ_U ⌣ y`, and the norm as the pullback of the `D₁₆` extension class, then their transport to `L/K` through `galoisEvens`. The Quadratic Form Invariants roadmap consumes these.
- **Groups of strict dimension two**: the criterion `scd_p G ≤ n` iff `cd_p G ≤ n` and the `p`-part of `H^{n+1}(U, ℤ)` vanishes for open `U`, then the class module of NSW (3.6.4): for `scd_p G ≤ 2`, `H²(G ⧸ V, V^ab(p))` is cyclic of order the `p`-part of `#(G ⧸ V)`, generated by the extension class.
- **The general Evens norm**: tensor induction is next, then the norm, its laws, and its agreement with the index-two graph class.
