### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dimension shifting** ([`dimensionShiftIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/DimensionShifting/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dimensionShiftIso)), `Hⁱ⁺¹(G, M) ≅ Hⁱ(G, Coind_1^G M ⧸ M)`. Together with Shapiro's lemma in every degree, it drives the cohomological-dimension arguments.
- **The Evens norm in characteristic two** ([`evensNormCharTwo`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Evens/Norm/CharTwo.html#OpenSubgroup.evensNormCharTwo)), `Hⁿ(U, A) → H^{ln}(G, A)` for a discrete commutative `G`-ring `A` with `2 = 0`. It takes the diagonal tensor of a cocycle and pushes it forward along Evens's twisted multiplication, it is independent of the cocycle and the transversal, and it sends `1` to `1`. This is milestone 7 of 13.2, with the action on `A` arbitrary.
- **Block tensors and cup products** ([`gradedCohomologyClass_smoothPiTensorProductLexPairing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Pi/Cup.html#TauCeti.gradedCohomologyClass_smoothPiTensorProductLexPairing)): the class of a tensor of cocycles over two blocks of a contracted tensor product is the cup product of their classes. Iterating it splits an orbitwise diagonal class into a cup product, which is the missing step of the double-coset formula.

## The frontier

- **The double-coset formula over several double cosets** — express the restriction of `N_U^G x` to `K` as a cup product over `K \ G / U` of norms from `K ∩ τUτ⁻¹`. The orbitwise contraction compatible with restriction and the block-tensor cup identity are both in place. What remains is to identify each orbitwise diagonal class as a norm from its stabilizer and chain these steps into the formula.
- **The expansion** — the norm of `1 + χ` is `∑ Nᵢ(χ)` with `N₀ = 1`, `N₁ = cor` and `N_l = N_U^G`. It is open even at `𝔽₂`.
- **The remaining laws of the characteristic-two norm** — transitivity, naturality, `N_G^G = id` and the double-coset formula for general `A` with `2 = 0`, with agreement with the `𝔽₂` norm as a check.
- **The even-degree norm when `2 ≠ 0`** — milestones 3 to 6 for a general discrete commutative `G`-ring. This needs the sign-correct construction on even degrees, because the tensor-induced resolution is a complex only up to sign there. The `𝔽₃` check in 13.2 needs exactly this case.
