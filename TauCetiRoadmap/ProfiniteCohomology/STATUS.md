## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The low-degree theory is essentially complete, and so are Layer 5's exact sequences and Layer 12's graded cup product. The all-degree theory now has filtered coefficient colimits, and `cd ℤ̂ = 1` is proved. Still partial are the strict-dimension theory and the class module, the index-two Evens norm as a function on classes together with its Galois transport, and the general Evens norm. The last of these has its ingredients but no norm.

### Named results

- **Hilbert 90 and the Kummer isomorphism**: continuous `H¹(G_K, (Kˢ)ˣ)` vanishes, so `Kˣ/(Kˣ)ⁿ ≅ H¹(G_K, μₙ)` for `n` invertible in `K` ([`TauCeti.hilbert90`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Hilbert90.html#TauCeti.hilbert90), [`TauCeti.kummerIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.html#TauCeti.kummerIso)).
- **The cohomological dimension of `ℤ̂`**: `cd_p ℤ̂ = 1` for every prime `p`. The upper bound comes from `H²(ℤ̂, M) = 0`, which holds because profinite extensions of `ℤ̂` split ([`zHat.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/CohomologicalDimension.html#TauCeti.zHat.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_one)).
- **Filtered coefficient colimits**: for a compact group, `Hⁿ(G, -)` preserves filtered colimits of smooth discrete coefficients in every degree ([`continuousCohomology_preservesFilteredColimits`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/FilteredColimits.html#TauCeti.ContinuousCohomology.continuousCohomology_preservesFilteredColimits)).
- **Graded commutativity of the cup product**: `a ⌣ b = (-1)^{mn} (b ⌣ᵒᵖ a)` on classes in every bidegree, with Steenrod's cup-one product as the homotopy ([`cup_gradedComm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Cup/Graded/Comm.html#TauCeti.TopPairing.cup_gradedComm)).
- **The kernel of restriction at index two**: for an open subgroup `U` of index two, a class of `H²(G, 𝔽₂)` dies on `U` exactly when it is `χ_U ⌣ y` ([`exact_cup_res2_of_index_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Corestriction/IndexTwo/CupExact.html#OpenSubgroup.exact_cup_res2_of_index_two)). Its Galois form for a quadratic extension is [`galoisRes_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Corestriction/Exact.html#TauCeti.galoisRes_eq_zero_iff).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dimension shifting** ([`dimensionShiftIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/DimensionShifting/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dimensionShiftIso)), `Hⁱ⁺¹(G, M) ≅ Hⁱ(G, Coind_1^G M ⧸ M)` for `i ≥ 1`, with Shapiro's lemma in every degree. This is what the cohomological-dimension arguments run on.
- **The mod-2 cohomology algebra** ([`gradedCohomF2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Cup/TrivialF2/GradedAlgebra.html#TauCeti.gradedCohomF2)), `⨁ₙ Hⁿ(G, 𝔽₂)` as a graded commutative `𝔽₂`-algebra. With it, the degree multiplication of the Evens norm can be typed.
- **Tensor induction** ([`Subgroup.tensorInductionFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Induction.html#Subgroup.tensorInductionFunctor)) restricts the tensor power with its wreath-product action along the [monomial homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/WreathProduct/Monomial.html#Subgroup.monomialContinuousHom). The general norm is to be built through it.

## The frontier

- **The index-two norm on classes**: descend the graph class to a function on `H¹(U, 𝔽₂)` and turn the cochain-level `D₁₆` identity into `N(α) = (Ind α)^* c_{D₁₆}`. This needs Layer 3's canonical class of an inhomogeneous 2-cocycle.
- **`galoisEvens`**: transport the norm and identities 1, 2 and 5 to a quadratic `L/K`, and prove embedding independence. The Quadratic Form Invariants roadmap consumes these.
- **Strict dimension from ordinary dimension**: the reduction to finite simple modules and the criterion `scd_p G ≤ n` iff `cd_p G ≤ n` and the `p`-part of `H^{n+1}(U, ℤ)` vanishes for open `U`. Filtered colimits, the bounded-exponent test and `scd_p ≤ cd_p + 1` are now in place.
- **The class module of strict dimension two**: NSW (3.6.4). The surjectivity half of (3.3.11) is [proved](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/Corestriction.html#TauCeti.corestriction_surjOn_primaryComponent_of_strictCohomologicalDimensionAt_le). The injectivity half, the transfer, and the cyclic, `p`-group and Sylow steps remain.
- **The general Evens norm**: with the wreath product, monomial homomorphism and tensor induction built, what remains is the norm at resolution level, transversal independence, multiplicativity and transitivity, and agreement with the index-two graph class.
