The cyclic case is [`abelianizationProPClass_generates_of_isCyclic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/ClassModule/Cyclic/SecondCohomology.html#TauCeti.abelianizationProPClass_generates_of_isCyclic).
- **The strict-dimension criterion** — NSW (3.3.4): `scd_p G ≤ n` exactly when `cd_p G ≤ n` and the `p`-primary part of `H^{n+1}(U, ℤ)` vanishes for every open `U` ([`strictCohomologicalDimensionLE_iff_forall_openSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/IntegralCriterion.html#TauCeti.strictCohomologicalDimensionLE_iff_forall_openSubgroup)).

Together with `β ∘ β = 0`, this completes Layer 8.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dimension shifting** ([`dimensionShiftIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/DimensionShifting/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.dimensionShiftIso)), `Hⁱ⁺¹(G, M) ≅ Hⁱ(G, Coind_1^G M ⧸ M)`. Together with Shapiro's lemma in every degree, it drives the cohomological-dimension arguments.
- **The index-two Evens norm** ([`evensNormIndexTwo`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Evens/IndexTwoNorm.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.evensNormIndexTwo)) maps `H¹(U, 𝔽₂) → H²(G, 𝔽₂)` for `U` of index two by descending the graph class. On classes it is the pullback of the `D₁₆` extension class. It is the value the general norm must reproduce.
- **Tensor induction with its Mackey formula** ([`smoothTensorInducedMackeyIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Tensor/Induction/DoubleCoset/Discrete.html#TauCeti.smoothTensorInducedMackeyIso)). Tensor induction is also independent of the transversal, transitive, and compatible with tensor products and inflation. It is applied to the total coinduced resolution, which has an explicit contraction. These are the inputs for building the general norm at resolution level and proving its double-coset and transitivity formulas.

## The frontier

- **The general Evens norm, at resolution level**: tensor-induce the contracted total resolution and define the norm on cochains with degree `q ↦ l q`. Show it sends cocycles to cocycles and that its class does not depend on the representative or the transversal. This is milestones 4 to 6.
- **The norm as a function on classes and its laws**: the public `N_U^G : H^q(U, 𝔽₂) → H^{l q}(G, 𝔽₂)`, with multiplicativity, transitivity, the restriction and double-coset formula, and inflation compatibility. The tensor-induction transitivity and Mackey isomorphisms already exist for these.
- **Agreement with the index-two norm**: `evensNormIndexTwo_eq`, identifying the general norm in index two and degree one with the graph-class construction.
- **Finite products of discrete modules**: state the closure that Layer 0 lists. It is the one item outside Layer 13 that is not established here.
