## ProfiniteCohomology: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-01 (`22df7c0` to `8a32441`)

The prime-to-`p` equality of cohomological dimension landed: an open subgroup of a profinite group whose index is prime to `p` has the same `cd_p` as the group ([`cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_not_dvd_index`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/IndexNotDvd.html#TauCeti.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_not_dvd_index), NSW (3.3.5)), and so does a closed subgroup all of whose open neighbourhoods have index prime to `p` ([`cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_isClosed_of_forall_not_dvd_index`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/IndexNotDvd.html#TauCeti.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq_of_isClosed_of_forall_not_dvd_index)) (TauCeti#10489). A Sylow pro-`p` subgroup satisfies exactly that hypothesis, so the closed form yields the Sylow equality [`cd_p G = cd_p P`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Sylow/CohomologicalDimension.html#TauCeti.IsProPSylow.cohomologicalDimensionAt_eq) of NSW (3.3.6). With monotonicity for closed subgroups already in place, Layer 11 still lacks the reduction to simple modules, the upper bound in `cd_p ℤ̂ = 1`, the criterion computing `scd_p` from `cd_p`, and the class module of a group of strict dimension two.

The general Evens norm has its second ingredient. For an open subgroup `U` and a chosen transversal, the monomial homomorphism from `G` into the permutation wreath product of `U` over the cosets `G ⧸ U` is defined ([`Subgroup.monomialHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/WreathProduct/Monomial.html#Subgroup.monomialHom)), injective, and continuous ([`Subgroup.monomialContinuousHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/WreathProduct/Monomial.html#Subgroup.monomialContinuousHom)), with a variant that indexes the cosets by `Fin (G : U)`. Underneath it, permutation wreath products over a discrete index set are now [topological groups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/WreathProduct/Basic.html#TauCeti.WreathProduct.instIsTopologicalGroup) (TauCeti#9294). Tensor induction, the norm itself and its agreement with the index-two graph class have not landed. Neither have the index-two exact sequence, the fifth Evens identity or the `D₁₆` pullback formula of the explicit half.

Facts about continuous actions on finite discrete sets were also reorganised, among them the [open kernel](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/GroupAction/Discrete.html#TauCeti.openActionKernel) of such an action and the theorem that a pro-`p` group [fixes a nonzero element](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/FixedPoints.html#TauCeti.exists_ne_zero_invariant_of_isProP) of a nontrivial finite `p`-primary module.
