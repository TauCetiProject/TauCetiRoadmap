## ProfiniteCohomology: 2026-08-19 to 2026-09-01 (`e2fd9e9` to `8745177`)

**Shapiro's lemma** landed in degrees zero and one: for a profinite group and a *closed* subgroup `U`, evaluation at `1` identifies `H¹(G, Coind_U^G A)` with `H¹(U, A)` ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitShapiro1`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Shapiro.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitShapiro1)). Around it the explicit low-degree theory took shape: `H⁰, H¹, H²` as subquotients of continuous cochains ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.H1`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/LowDegree.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.H1)), compatible-pair pullback in all three degrees with named restriction, inflation and coefficient maps, and the **inflation-restriction sequence** ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitInfRes_exact`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Inflation.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitInfRes_exact)). A short exact sequence of discrete modules yields `δ⁰`, `δ¹` and exactness at all eight nodes from `H⁰(G, A)` to `H²(G, B)` ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.DiscreteShortExact.explicitLongExact_H2B`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/LongExact.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.DiscreteShortExact.explicitLongExact_H2B)). **Corestriction** exists in degrees 0, 1 and 2 through the transversal word, with `cor ∘ res = (G : U)` on cohomology ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitCor1_comp_res1`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Corestriction.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitCor1_comp_res1)). The six low-degree **cup products** carry associativity in all ten tridegrees, graded commutativity ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitCup11_eq_neg_flip`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CupProduct.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitCup11_eq_neg_flip)) and the projection formula ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitCup_projection11`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/ProjectionFormula.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitCup_projection11)).

On the canonical side, the smooth discrete objects of `TopRep R G` are equivalent to the discrete `G`-modules ([`TauCeti.discreteRepEquivSmoothTopRep`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/SmoothDiscrete.html#TauCeti.discreteRepEquivSmoothTopRep)), Mathlib's `map` acquired named restriction, inflation and coefficient maps, and continuous cohomology of a discrete representation of a compact group is discrete (TauCeti#4070). Over a discrete group the explicit complex is Mathlib's `groupCohomology` in degrees 0 to 2 ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitH1IsoGroupCohomology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/GroupCohomologyIso.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitH1IsoGroupCohomology)); against the canonical object the comparison exists in degree zero only ([`TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitH0IsoContinuousCohomology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/ContinuousCohomologyIso.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitH0IsoContinuousCohomology)). The finite-quotient system is a functor on `(OpenNormalSubgroup G)ᵒᵖ`, and the Galois layer fixed `G_K = Gal(Kˢ/K)` and assembled the **Kummer sequence** ([`TauCeti.kummerShortExact`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Coefficients.html#TauCeti.kummerShortExact)).

Not here yet: the canonical comparison in degrees 1 and 2; the colimit theorem, of which only the system exists; conjugation, transgression and the five-term sequence; Shapiro in degree 2; Hilbert 90 and the Kummer isomorphism; the worked examples of §6; and all of Layers 10 to 13. Shapiro consumes the continuous section of `G → G ⧸ H` for closed `H`, but no declaration for it appears among what landed, only its companion extension lemma ([`TauCeti.exists_continuous_eqOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Separation/Profinite.html#TauCeti.exists_continuous_eqOn)).

## ProfiniteCohomology: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-26 (`8745177` to `9f176e4`)

[Hilbert 90](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Hilbert90.html#TauCeti.hilbert90) now gives the vanishing of continuous `H¹(G_K, (Kˢ)ˣ)`, and the [Kummer isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.html#TauCeti.kummerIso) identifies `Kˣ/(Kˣ)ⁿ` with `H¹(G_K, μₙ)` when `n` is invertible in `K`. The Kummer class has its root-cocycle description and is compatible with restriction to a finite separable extension; the norm square and mod-2 specialization remain open.

The explicit theory now [agrees with canonical continuous cohomology in degree two](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/CohomologyComparison.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitH2IsoContinuousCohomology), as it does in degrees zero and one. Its [finite-quotient colimit theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/FiniteQuotient/Colimit.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitFiniteQuotientColimit2) reaches degree two. [Transgression and five-term exactness](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Transgression.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.fiveTerm_exact_H2Q), [Shapiro's lemma in degree two](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Shapiro.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.explicitShapiro2), and the [long exact sequence in all degrees](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/HomologySequence.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.DiscreteShortExact.longExact_exact₃) extend the exact and coinduced side of the theory.

The index-two Evens calculation now has a [choice-free graph class](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Evens/Class.html#TauCeti.ContCohomology.graphClass) and its restriction, polarization and inflation identities. It is still an explicit low-degree construction: the general Evens norm and the graded cup product in all degrees have not landed.
