## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The classification of continuous central extensions of formal loops (A1–A2) and the completion Ûκ with its module equivalence (A4.1, A5) are in place. Formal vertex algebras now have the Borcherds identity, but without reconstruction there is still no vertex algebra on Vκ. The loop lift, integration to K_N and all of A7 have not begun, and neither center theorem has started, although Segal–Sugawara modes are now known to be central at the critical level.

### Named results

- **Classification of continuous central extensions of formal loops**: for a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form in characteristic zero, Φ ↦ [cΦ] is a linear equivalence from invariant symmetric forms onto H²_cont(g((t)), 𝕜) ([`invariantFormsResidueClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/InvariantForms.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.invariantFormsResidueClassEquiv)). For simple g it holds with every trivial coefficient space Z ([`residueClassEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/ContinuousCohomology.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.residueClassEquiv)).
- **Universality of the residue extension**: the nonzero scalar residue extension is initial among extensions given by continuous two-cocycles with arbitrary trivial coefficients ([`existsUnique_residueExtensionLift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Universal.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.existsUnique_residueExtensionLift)).
- **Smooth modules are discrete modules over the completion**: modules of level a on which each vector is killed by currents of high order form a category equivalent to discrete Ûκ-modules ([`smoothModulesEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/CompletedModules.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.smoothModulesEquivalence)).
- **The Borcherds identity**: in a formal vertex algebra, the Borcherds identity holds for all states and integer modes, with the commutator formula and skew symmetry as consequences ([`FormalVertexAxioms.borcherds`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Vertex/Borcherds.html#TauCeti.FormalVertexAxioms.borcherds)).
- **Centrality of the Segal–Sugawara modes at the critical level**: the modes Sₙ of the normally ordered Casimir field lie in the center of Û when the level is critical ([`sugawaraMode_mem_center`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Sugawara.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.sugawaraMode_mem_center)). These are the first nonscalar central elements, and nothing yet says they generate the center.

## The frontier

- **Reconstruction (A6.1)**: the extension theorem from local, covariant, generating fields to a unique formal vertex algebra. Goddard uniqueness, Dong's lemma and the creation recursion are already proved.
- **The weight-one Lie algebra (A6.4)**: this needs only A6.1. Skew symmetry of the zero product modulo T(V), and the fact that the zero mode acts as a derivation of every product, already exist.
- **The vacuum vertex algebra Vκ and Sugawara (A6.2)**: apply reconstruction to the vacuum module, identify its center with the g[[t]]-invariants, and build the noncritical conformal vector with central charge k·dim g/(k + h∨).
- **The affine loop lift (A2.3, A4.2)**: lift Ad_g to ĝκ with the residue Maurer–Cartan term, then to Uκ and Ûκ. This is blocked on the pinned G((t)) and Maurer–Cartan differential from FormalConnectionsOpers.
- **Rational integration to K_N (A3.2)**: the finite presentation step is done, but the finite-jet nilpotence estimates and the exponential to K_N remain. These need the supplier's congruence subgroups.
