## AffineLieAlgebras: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`d133437` to `273ea28`)

The first window on this roadmap proved the finite-dimensional lemmas behind the classification of central extensions of loop algebras. Whitehead's first and second lemmas landed: over a field of characteristic zero, for a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form and a finite-dimensional module, every 1-cocycle is [principal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Whitehead.html#LieModule.Cohomology.exists_eq_lie_of_d₁₂_eq_zero) and every [2-cocycle is a coboundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Whitehead.html#LieModule.Cohomology.twoCocycle_eq_range_d₁₂). Alongside them, [every invariant form on a simple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field is a multiple of the Killing form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Killing/InvariantForm.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebra.exists_eq_smul_killingForm). That result feeds the mode-recurrence step for polynomial loops: a trivial-coefficient [2-cocycle on the loop algebra of a simple Lie algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Loop/Cocycle.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebra.LoopAlgebra.exists_eq_smul_twoCocycleOfBilinear_killingForm_iff) is a multiple of the residue cocycle exactly when it vanishes on constant loops (TauCeti#18706). Nothing yet joins the two halves. Normalising an arbitrary cocycle to vanish on constant loops needs the mode-block adapters, so the polynomial H² classification is still unassembled, and the continuous version has not begun.

Supporting work started the A1 carriers and the A5 module-category layer. [Formal currents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Basic.html#TauCeti.currentAlgebra) now come with coefficients, an order filtration satisfying [a_M, a_N] ⊆ a_(M+N), truncation and the polynomial embedding, though there is still no topology. The Laurent [residue pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/ResiduePairing.html#LaurentSeries.residuePairing) Res(f dg) was shown to be alternating and jointly continuous, and it satisfies the cyclic identity behind the cocycle law. [Smooth modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Smooth/Basic.html#TauCeti.smoothModules) for a family of left ideals were matched with discrete modules and shown to be closed under subobjects, quotients, colimits and finite limits. Low-degree first cohomology also got a bundled API.

## AffineLieAlgebras: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

The headline result of this window is the classification of central extensions of the formal loop algebra. For a finite-dimensional Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero with irreducible adjoint module and nondegenerate Killing form, so for instance a simple one, sending an invariant symmetric form to its residue cocycle Res(f dg)·κ(x, y) is [a linear equivalence onto continuous second cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/InvariantForms.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.invariantFormsResidueClassEquiv), and that cohomology is [one-dimensional](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/ContinuousCohomology.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.finrank_continuousSecondCohomology_eq_one). Along the way the polynomial version was assembled too: [the second cohomology of the polynomial loop algebra is spanned by the Killing residue class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Loop/Cocycle.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebra.LoopAlgebra.twoCocycle_eq_span_killingForm_sup_range_d₁₂). The classification is for scalar coefficients only. The version with an arbitrary discrete coefficient space, which A2's universality needs, is still open, as is the semisimple product case.

The classification needed formal currents to become a topological Lie algebra. They now carry the t-adic linear topology, which is Hausdorff and [complete](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Topology.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.instCompleteSpace), with a [jointly continuous bracket](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Topology.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.continuous_lie) and polynomial currents dense. Continuous cochains with discrete coefficients were set up in degrees one and two, together with the [joint-continuity criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/Continuous.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.mem_continuousTwoCochain_iff_of_hasBasis). The residue pairing was shown to be [invariant under formal coordinate changes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Substitution.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.residuePairing_substLieEquiv). A2 has started: the residue extension of a perfect Lie algebra is [perfect exactly when its form is nonzero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Perfect.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.derivedSeries_ofTwoCocycle_eq_top_iff), and it comes with level rescaling and the polynomial embedding.

On the module side, smooth modules got their [smooth-part coreflection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Smooth/Coreflection.html#TauCeti.smoothPartAdjunction) and continuous [coinduction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Smooth/Coinduction.html#TauCeti.coinduction). Together these give the smooth category [enough injectives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Smooth/Coinduction.html#TauCeti.enoughInjectives_smoothModules) and Ext groups in the right universe (TauCeti#20436).

## AffineLieAlgebras: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-08 (`e8ea0d1` to `a4fcb57`)

The classification of central extensions of formal loops now holds with coefficients. For a simple Lie algebra and any vector space Z with trivial action, of any dimension, the residue class map is [a linear equivalence from Z onto continuous H²(g((t)), Z)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/ContinuousCohomology.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.residueClassEquiv) (TauCeti#20348). The scalar classification by invariant forms was also [extended to every Lie algebra with nondegenerate Killing form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/InvariantForms.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.invariantFormsResidueClassEquiv), so semisimple ones are covered. With these, A2 gained its universal property: the nonzero residue extension is [initial among continuous cocycle extensions with arbitrary trivial coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Universal.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.existsUnique_residueExtensionLift). The extensions now carry a topology and continuous sections. The [dual Coxeter number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/DualCoxeterNumber.html#RootPairing.Base.dualCoxeterNumber) is defined as 1 + ht(θ∨), and the Killing form is [2h∨ times the invariant form normalised at the highest coroot](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Killing/DualCoxeterNumber.html#RootPairing.Base.killingForm_eq_smul_iff_apply_coroot_highestRoot_eq_two), which pins the critical level.

The largest body of work built the completed enveloping algebra. The level quotient U(ĝ)/(K − a) got its left ideals I_N, which [intersect in zero by PBW](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/LeftIdeal.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.iInf_orderLeftIdeal). Their inverse limit Û is [a ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Completion.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.instRingCompletedEnveloping) with continuous multiplication, and [smooth modules are exactly the discrete Û-modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/CompletedModules.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.smoothModulesEquivalence). The completed center is closed, [centrality in it is detected on currents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Center.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.mem_center_iff_forall_commute), and it acts on every smooth module. Coordinate changes and the level comparison extend to Û. Smooth representations of polynomial and formal currents were [identified](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/SmoothAction.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.smoothLieHomEquiv).

A6 has begun. The vacuum module has its [PBW identification with U(t⁻¹g[t⁻¹])](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Vacuum/Basic.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.vacuumPBWEquiv) at every level, plus translation and degree operators, and any two current fields are [mutually local of order 2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Current/Field.html#TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.isLocalOfOrder_currentField). There is no vertex algebra structure yet, and the affine loop lift and central-character quotients are still absent.
