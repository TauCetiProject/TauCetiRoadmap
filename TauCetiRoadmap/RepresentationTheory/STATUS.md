### Named results

- **The Weyl character and [dimension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div) formulas** — a highest-weight module's character times the Weyl denominator is the alternating Weyl numerator. Its dimension is the resulting product over the positive coroots.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)** — the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations of a compact group form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **Schur–Weyl duality** — the actions of `GLₙ` and `S_d` on `(kⁿ)^{⊗d}` are each other's centralizers. In characteristic zero, the tensor power is a sum of Specht modules tensored with Schur modules.
- **The [Cartan–Killing classification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/FiniteType/RationalClassification.html#RootPairing.existsUnique_nonempty_equiv_rationalRootSystem)** — every irreducible reduced crystallographic root system over `ℚ` is isomorphic to the model of exactly one Dynkin type. The type can be read off any base.

## The frontier

- **The Harish-Chandra isomorphism** — show that the linear projection hits every Weyl invariant. Orbit separation is already proved, so this would also give the converse: equal central characters force a single dot orbit.
- **Brauer–Schur–Weyl duality** — show that the orthogonal Brauer image fills the commutant of `O(n)`, now that the inclusion and the harmonic–trace splitting exist. Then assemble the symplectic generators, which already satisfy the Brauer relations, into an algebra action.
