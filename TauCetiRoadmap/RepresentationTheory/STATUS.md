### Named results

- **The [Harish–Chandra isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.harishChandraLinearIso)** — for a finite-dimensional Killing-semisimple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, it identifies the centre of the enveloping algebra with the Weyl invariants of `S(H)`.
- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)** — a finite connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **Kostant's isotypy theorem** — for a Killing-semisimple Lie algebra of dimension `d` and rank `l`, the Clifford algebra of the Killing form under left multiplication is `2^((d+l)/2)` copies of `L(ρ)`, and its [spinor module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Kostant.html#TauCeti.SpinPolarizationData.kostantSpinRep_multiplicity) is `2^⌊l/2⌋` copies.
- **The [first Brauer–Thrall conjecture](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/BrauerThrall.html#TauCeti.isRepresentationFinite_of_length_le)** — a finite-dimensional algebra whose indecomposable modules have bounded length has only finitely many of them up to isomorphism.
- **[Conjugacy of maximal tori](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.exists_map_conj_eq)** — in a compact Lie group any two maximal tori are conjugate, and in a connected one a maximal torus is [its own centralizer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Centralizer.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.centralizer_eq).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[Krull–Schmidt categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/KrullSchmidt/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsKrullSchmidt)**: every object is a finite biproduct of objects with local endomorphism rings, [uniquely](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/KrullSchmidt/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.exists_equiv_iso_of_biproduct_iso_of_isLocalRing). This is the setting for the functorial approach to almost-split sequences.
- **The [Yoneda `Ext¹` of an exact category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.Ext1)**: an abelian group under Baer sum, with its exact sequences, and [equal to derived `Ext`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/Abelian.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.Ext1.abelianEquiv) in an abelian category. It lets Auslander–Reiten theory leave module categories.
- **[Matlis duality for Artin algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/MatlisDuality.html#TauCeti.IsInjectiveEnvelope.fgModuleCatMatlisDuality)**: the anti-equivalence between finitely generated left and right modules. It is the base for translates and almost-split sequences over a commutative Artinian base.
