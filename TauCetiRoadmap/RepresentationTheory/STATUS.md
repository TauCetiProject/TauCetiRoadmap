## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Root systems and semisimple algebras are complete. So are the Weyl character formula, Peter–Weyl, Brauer's induction theorem, the Clifford correspondence, the closed-subgroup theorem and the character table of `GL₂(𝔽_q)`, which lacks only its `GL₂(𝔽₃)` instance. Gabriel's theorem, Ado–Iwasawa, Schur–Weyl duality, the spin fundamentals and the modular Artin theorem are partial. Triality and the Lie-group layers from Lie's third theorem onward have not begun.

### Named results

- **The [Cartan–Killing classification](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/FiniteType/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_dynkinType)**: every irreducible reduced crystallographic finite root system has a unique Dynkin type.
- **The Weyl character and [dimension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div) formulas**: multiplying a highest-weight module's character by the Weyl denominator gives the alternating Weyl numerator, and the dimension is the resulting product over positive coroots.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)**: the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations of a compact group form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **[Brauer's induction theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Brauer/Induction.html#TauCeti.ClassFunction.indVirtualCharacters_eq_virtualCharacters_isElementary)**: over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, the virtual characters induced from elementary subgroups are exactly the virtual characters.
- **The [character table of `GL₂(𝔽_q)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/GL2/Table.html#TauCeti.GL2CharacterTable)**: a square table of size `q² − 1`, indexed by the linear, Steinberg, principal-series and cuspidal parameters. It is the group's character table with its rows relabelled, and its rows are [orthonormal](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/GL2/Table.html#TauCeti.GL2CharacterParam.characterPairing_classFunction).

## The frontier

- **The `GL₂(𝔽₃)` instance**: specialise the assembled table to the uniform case of order `48`. The degenerate `GL₂(𝔽₂) ≅ S₃` is already checked.
- **Gabriel's dichotomy**: prove that a connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite. Finite type already passes to subquivers and excludes loops and parallel arrows. The remaining extended Dynkin subquivers and the reflection-functor direction are still open.
- **The Grothendieck group of `k[G]`**: build the dictionary `FDRep k G ≌ FGModuleCat k[G]`, then `G₀(k[G])` with its universal property, its ring structure, and restriction, induction and permutation classes. The generic simple-class basis is available. This group is the one prerequisite blocking every `G₀` statement of the modular Artin theorem.
- **Positive-characteristic Ado**: apply Krull intersection over the central subalgebra to separate `L` inside some finite-dimensional quotient `U(L)/(UC)ⁿ`, then assemble the faithful nilpotence-preserving representation.
- **Spin modules as fundamental representations**: in type `B`, the all-coordinate vector is a highest-weight vector of weight `ωₗ`. What remains is to state `S ≅ L(ωₗ)` and to do the same for the type-`D` half-spin modules.
