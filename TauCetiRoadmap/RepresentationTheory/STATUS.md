### Named results

- **The [Harish–Chandra isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.harishChandraLinearIso)**: for a finite-dimensional Killing-semisimple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, it identifies the centre of the enveloping algebra with the Weyl invariants of `S(H)`.
- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)**: a finite connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **The [Auslander–Reiten existence theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/Sequence.html#ModuleCat.exists_isAlmostSplit_X₃_eq_iff)**: over a finite-dimensional algebra, every finitely generated indecomposable non-projective module ends an almost-split sequence.
- **The [first Brauer–Thrall conjecture](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/BrauerThrall.html#TauCeti.isRepresentationFinite_of_length_le)**: a finite-dimensional algebra whose indecomposable modules have bounded length has only finitely many of them up to isomorphism.
- **[Conjugacy of maximal tori](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.exists_map_conj_eq)**: in a compact Lie group any two maximal tori are conjugate. In a connected group a maximal torus is also [its own centralizer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Centralizer.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.centralizer_eq).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[Krull–Schmidt categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/KrullSchmidt/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsKrullSchmidt)**: categories in which every object is a finite biproduct of objects with local endomorphism rings. These decompositions are now [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/KrullSchmidt/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.exists_equiv_iso_of_biproduct_iso_of_isLocalRing), and [Krause's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/KrullSchmidt/Semiperfect.html#TauCeti.isKrullSchmidt_iff_isIdempotentComplete_and_isSemiperfectRing_end) recognizes such categories. This is the setting for the functorial approach to almost-split sequences.
- **The [Yoneda `Ext¹` of an exact category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.Ext1)**: its Baer sum [makes it an abelian group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/BaerSum.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.Ext1.instAddCommGroup). Almost-split conflations are detected by their extension classes, which lets Auslander–Reiten theory leave module categories.
- **[Matlis duality for Artin algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/MatlisDuality.html#TauCeti.IsInjectiveEnvelope.fgModuleCatMatlisDuality)**: the anti-equivalence between finitely generated left and right modules. Translates and almost-split sequences over a commutative Artinian base, not just a field, are built on it.

## The frontier

- **Auslander's theorem on bounded components**: show that a weakly connected component of bounded length is the whole valued Auslander–Reiten quiver of a connected algebra. Brauer–Thrall I should then follow for general Artin algebras, together with the `rad^∞` criterion.
- **Almost-split existence for Artin algebras**: Matlis duality and uniqueness of almost-split conflations are now in place. What remains is Auslander–Reiten duality and existence in exact categories, and the Matlis-based translate over a commutative Artinian base.
- **The Weyl group of a compact Lie group**: identify the faithful action of `N(T)/T` with the Weyl group of the root system, then prove the Weyl integration formula. The centre-plus-derived-algebra splitting of Layer 5A comes first.
- **Kostant's isotypy corollary**: a highest weight of Kostant's module with the Casimir scalar of `ρ` [must be `ρ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Quadratic/Lie/KostantWeights.html#CliffordAlgebra.eq_weylVector_of_isHighestWeightVector_kostant_of_casimirScalar_eq). It remains to combine this with the Casimir action and state the isotypy for a general semisimple Lie algebra.
- **Brauer–Schur–Weyl duality**: show that the orthogonal and symplectic Brauer images fill their commutants. [Semisimplicity of `B_k(n)` for `n ≥ k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ClassicalGroups/BrauerGenerators/Orthogonal/Faithful.html#TauCeti.BrauerAlgebra.isSemisimpleRing_of_le) does not give these equalities.
