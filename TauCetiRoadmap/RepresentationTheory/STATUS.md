### Named results

- **The [Harish–Chandra isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.harishChandraLinearIso)**: for a finite-dimensional Killing-semisimple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, it identifies the centre of the enveloping algebra with the Weyl invariants of `S(H)`.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)**: for a compact group, the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)**: a finite connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **The [Auslander–Reiten existence theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/Sequence.html#ModuleCat.exists_isAlmostSplit_X₃_eq_iff)**: over a finite-dimensional algebra, every finitely generated indecomposable non-projective module ends an almost-split sequence.
- **[Burnside–Brauer tensor generation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/TensorGeneration.html#FDRep.exists_pos_finrank_hom_tensorPower)**: every nonzero complex representation maps nontrivially into a tensor power of any faithful one. The multiplicity series is now known to be rational, given by [an explicit character fraction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/TensorGeneration.html#FDRep.tensorMultiplicitySeries_eq_characterFractions).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[Krull–Schmidt categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/KrullSchmidt/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsKrullSchmidt)**: categories in which indecomposables are exactly the objects with local endomorphism rings. They are the setting for lifting Auslander–Reiten theory from finite-dimensional algebras to Artin algebras.
- **The [PBW Rees algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/Rees/Basic.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.reesAlgebra)**: it connects filtered enveloping-algebra calculations with commutative symbols.
- **[Isotypic projections by central idempotents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/IsotypicProjection.html#Representation.asAlgebraHom_primitiveCentralIdempotent_eq_starProjection)**: on a unitary representation, a primitive central idempotent acts as the orthogonal projection onto its isotypic component. The spring-system applications rest on this.
