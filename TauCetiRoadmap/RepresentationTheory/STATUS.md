## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The highest-weight lane finished its character layer: the Weyl character, Weyl
dimension and Kostant multiplicity formulas are all proved, joining the Cartan-Killing
classification, the character tables of a finite group and of `Sₙ`, Peter-Weyl and Schur-Weyl
duality. Partial are the character-table algorithm, `GL₂(𝔽_q)`, the classical groups, the spin

Ado-Iwasawa, with foundations but neither proof track. Symmetric functions,
Murnaghan-Nakayama and Brauer induction are absent.

### Named results

- **The Weyl character and dimension formulas** — for a finite-dimensional module generated by a highest weight vector of weight `λ`, the formal character times the Weyl denominator is [`∑_w sgn(w) e^{w(λ+ρ)-ρ}`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/WeylCharacter.html#TauCeti.formalCharacter_mul_weylDenominator_eq_weylNumerator) and the dimension is [`∏_{α>0} ⟨λ+ρ, α^∨⟩ / ⟨ρ, α^∨⟩`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div).
- **Kostant's multiplicity formula** — the multiplicity of `μ` in that module is [`∑_w sgn(w) P(w(λ+ρ) - (μ+ρ))`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/KostantMultiplicity.html#TauCeti.finrank_weightSpace_eq_kostantMultiplicity), the character identity divided by the denominator.
- **The Clifford correspondence** — over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, induction from the inertia group of an irreducible `V` of a normal subgroup [stays irreducible](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Clifford/Correspondence.html#FDRep.simple_indFDRep_of_inertia) and is [injective on isomorphism classes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Clifford/Injectivity.html#FDRep.nonempty_iso_of_liesOver_inertia_of_nonempty_iso_indFDRep); surjectivity is not yet here.
- **Schur-Weyl duality** — over a field in which `d!` is invertible, the images of `k[S_d]` and of `k[GLₙ]` in [`(kⁿ)^{⊗d}`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Symmetric/TensorAction/GeneralLinear.html#TauCeti.centralizer_range_tensorPowerRep_asAlgebraHom_eq_range_permTensorActionAlgHom) are each other's centralizers.
- **The character table of a finite group** — the irreducible characters are a basis of the class functions, with both orthogonality relations, as a [table](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Table.html#TauCeti.characterTable); the [table of `Sₙ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Symmetric/Specht/Character.html#TauCeti.symmetricCharacterTable) and [Peter-Weyl](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis) sit beside it.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[The nilradical of a Lie algebra](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Nilradical.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebra.nilradical)** — the supremum of the nilpotent ideals, nilpotent itself over a Noetherian algebra and strictly larger than the ideal the whole algebra acts nilpotently on. The Ado construction is organized around it.
- **[Derivations of the universal enveloping algebra](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/Derivation.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.envelopingDerivation)** — every Lie derivation extends uniquely to `U(L)`, and descends to quotients by ideals it stabilizes: the machinery for refining a cofinite kernel to a derivation-stable one.
- **[The representation ring, with restriction and induction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/RepresentationRing/Induction.html#TauCeti.repRingInd_mul_repRingRes)** — `Ind(x · Res y) = (Ind x) · y`, so the image of induction is an ideal: the frame Artin and Brauer induction are stated in.

## The frontier

- **Ado's theorem** — what remains is the characteristic-zero assembly, a derivation-stable cofinite refinement then extension across a split ideal extension, and in positive characteristic central `p`-polynomials and Krull intersection.
- **Gabriel's theorem** — reflection at a source preserves indecomposability and acts on dimension vectors by the simple reflection, and the positive roots of a positive definite Tits form are known; carrying an indecomposable along a whole Coxeter word is missing.
- **Artin and Brauer induction** — the `p`-section counting is done; next is the class function that is `1` on a `p`-section and congruent modulo `p` to characters induced from `p`-elementary subgroups.
- **The character table of `GL₂(𝔽_q)`** — the cuspidal virtual character has its four class values; its irreducibility when `θ^q ≠ θ`, the principal series and Steinberg are absent.
- **The spin and half-spin modules as `L(ω)`** — the weights and their count `2^{l-1}` are settled; irreducibility and the type `B` and `D` fundamental weights are not.
