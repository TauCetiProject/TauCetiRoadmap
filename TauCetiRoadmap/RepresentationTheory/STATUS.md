## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Root systems, semisimple algebras, compact groups and induction–restriction are complete, Gabriel's theorem is proved, and Ado–Iwasawa holds over every field. Harish-Chandra, the character-table algorithm, the exceptional Lie algebras, Schur–Weyl duality, the spin fundamentals, Auslander–Reiten theory, the classical groups and modular Artin are partial. Triality has not begun, and neither have the Lie-group layers from maximal tori onward.

### Named results

- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)** — a finite connected quiver has finite representation type over any field exactly when its Tits form is positive definite, that is, exactly when it is an [oriented simply-laced Dynkin diagram](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_card_hom_add_card_hom_le_one_and_exists_dynkinType_iso).
- **The Ado–Iwasawa theorem** — every finite-dimensional Lie algebra over a field has a faithful finite-dimensional representation, even one in which ad-nilpotent elements act nilpotently.
- **The Weyl character and [dimension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div) formulas** — a highest-weight module's character times the Weyl denominator is the alternating Weyl numerator, and its dimension is the resulting product over positive coroots.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)** — the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations of a compact group form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **[Brauer's characterization of characters](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Brauer/Characterization.html#TauCeti.mem_virtualCharacters_iff_forall_comp_subtype_isElementary)** — a class function is a virtual character exactly when its restriction to every elementary subgroup is one.

## The frontier

- **The spin fundamental representations** — in type `D`, irreducibility and the fork highest-weight vectors are proved, so `S⁺ ≅ L(ωₗ)` and `S⁻ ≅ L(ωₗ₋₁)` need only be assembled. In type `B`, a top-wedge vector killed by the positive simple generators is in place; `S ≅ L(ωₗ)` is not.
- **The Harish-Chandra isomorphism** — show that the projection hits all the dot-invariants. Then derive `χ_λ = χ_μ` forces `μ ∈ W·λ`.
- **The modular Artin theorem** — what remains is Artin's identity `|G| • x = ∑ m_C |C| • Ind Res x` in `G₀` in characteristic `ℓ`. The multiplier [must contain the `p`-part of `|G|`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/GroupAlgebra/PrimePart.html#TauCeti.ordProj_dvd_of_nsmul_one_mem_iSup_range_indK0).

What is missing is its bijectivity onto non-injective indecomposables, the existence theorem, and then the AR quiver of a representation-finite algebra.
- **The Dixon algorithm always succeeds** — completeness holds whenever the central coefficients lie in the residue window, and `A₅` [has been checked](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Dixon/Cyclotomic/AlternatingFive/Solver.html#TauCeti.isSome_dixonCyclotomicCharacterTable_alternatingGroupFive).
