## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Cartan-Killing classification, the character tables of a finite group and of
`Sₙ`, Peter-Weyl, Weyl complete reducibility, the highest-weight classification, Schur-Weyl duality
and the hook-length formula were already established; this window adds the Weyl character formula
and the Mackey decomposition. Partial are the character-table algorithm, the classical groups, the
spin representations, Gabriel's theorem, Auslander-Reiten theory, `GL₂(𝔽_q)` and the Lie-group
correspondence; symmetric functions, Murnaghan-Nakayama and Brauer induction have not begun.

### Named results

- **[The Weyl character formula](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/WeylCharacter.html#TauCeti.formalCharacter_mul_weylDenominator_eq_weylNumerator)** — for a finite-dimensional module generated by a highest weight vector of weight `λ`, the formal character times the Weyl denominator is `∑_w sgn(w) e^{w(λ+ρ)-ρ}`, in the division-free form the roadmap asked for.
- **[The Mackey decomposition](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Mackey/Decomposition.html#Rep.mackeyDecomposition)** — restricting `Ind_H^G A` to `K` is the direct sum over the double cosets `K \ G / H` of the induced restrictions of the conjugates of `A`.
- **[Schur-Weyl duality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Symmetric/TensorAction/GeneralLinear.html#TauCeti.centralizer_range_tensorPowerRep_asAlgebraHom_eq_range_permTensorActionAlgHom)** — over a field in which `d!` is invertible, the images of `k[S_d]` and of `k[GLₙ]` in `(kⁿ)^{⊗d}` are each other's centralizers.
- **[The Cartan-Killing classification](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/FiniteType/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_dynkinType)** — every irreducible reduced crystallographic finite root system has a unique valid Dynkin type.
- **[The character table of a finite group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Table.html#TauCeti.characterTable)** — the irreducible characters are a basis of the class functions, with both orthogonality relations; the [table of `Sₙ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Symmetric/Specht/Character.html#TauCeti.symmetricCharacterTable) and [Peter-Weyl](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis) sit beside it.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[The coroot pairing and the integral weight lattice](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Weights/WeightLattice.html#TauCeti.coweightPairing)** — `⟨λ, αᵢ^∨⟩` as an honest integer, which makes dominance an inequality in `ℤ` over a field carrying no order. It is the arithmetic the character formula runs on.
- **[The representation ring, with restriction and induction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/RepresentationRing/Induction.html#TauCeti.repRingInd_mul_repRingRes)** — restriction a ring homomorphism, induction additive, and `Ind(x · Res y) = (Ind x) · y`, so the image of induction is an ideal: the frame Artin and Brauer induction are stated in.
- **[Minimal projective presentations over a semiprimary ring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/MinimalProjectivePresentation/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isMinimalProjectivePresentation)** — every module has one, which is what lets the Auslander-Reiten transpose and the translate `τ = D Tr` exist; both vanish exactly on the projectives.

Ado-Iwasawa Layer 0 only, where a faithful finite-dimensional representation exists exactly when
the enveloping algebra has a finite-dimensional associative target separating `L`.

## The frontier

- **The Weyl dimension and Kostant multiplicity formulas** — both specialize the character formula now in hand, at the trivial character and against the Kostant partition function.
- **The Mackey irreducibility criterion in general** — the decomposition is proved; the intertwining-number formula over `H \ G / H` and the disjointness criterion read off it remain.
- **Gabriel's theorem** — reflection at a source is fully faithful and the positive roots of a positive definite Tits form are known; carrying indecomposables along a Coxeter word is missing.
- **The character table of `GL₂(𝔽_q)`** — the conjugacy normal forms are classified and two induced families have character values; the principal series, Steinberg and cuspidal rows are absent.
- **The spin and half-spin modules as `L(ω)`** — irreducibility, the dimension `2^{l-1}` and the type-`B` statement are still missing.
