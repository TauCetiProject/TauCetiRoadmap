## Where this roadmap stands

Induction–restriction is complete apart from its Gassmann layers, the first of which is now mostly built. ### Named results

- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)**: a finite connected quiver has finite representation type over any field exactly when its Tits form is positive definite. It is also stated for rings: the path algebra is [representation-finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/PathAlgebra.html#TauCeti.isRepresentationFinite_pathAlgebra_iff_titsForm_posDef) under the same condition.
- **The Weyl character and [dimension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div) formulas**: a highest-weight module's character times the Weyl denominator is the alternating Weyl numerator, and its dimension is the resulting product over positive coroots.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)**: the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations of a compact group form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **Schur–Weyl duality**: the actions of `GLₙ` and `S_d` on `(kⁿ)^{⊗d}` are each other's centralizers. In characteristic zero the tensor power splits as a sum, over partitions of `d`, of a Specht module tensored with a Schur module.
- **Gassmann's criterion**: two subgroups of a finite group [meet every conjugacy class equally often exactly when their permutation representations are equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/FixedPointCount.html#TauCeti.almostConjugate_iff_nonempty_equiv_ofMulAction) in characteristic zero.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The [Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/PBW/Injective.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.pbwAssociatedGradedEquiv)**: for a Lie algebra that is free as a module, `gr U(L)` is the symmetric algebra.
- **The [Harish-Chandra projection](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/HarishChandra.html#TauCeti.hcProjection)**: it reads the centre of `U(L)` as polynomial functions on weights, landing in the dot-invariants. Those invariants [separate dot orbits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/OrbitSeparation.html#TauCeti.forall_mem_dotInvariants_lift_eq_iff).
- **The [Auslander–Reiten quiver](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/Radical/IrreducibleMorphismQuiver/Basic.html#TauCeti.IrreducibleMorphismQuiver)**: vertices are indecomposables up to isomorphism, and arrows count irreducible morphisms. The translate acts on it [as a partial translation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/Radical/IrreducibleMorphismQuiver/Translation.html#TauCeti.ARTranslation), and an almost-split sequence matches the arrows at its two ends.

## The frontier

- **Almost-split sequences**: make the left almost-split map left minimal. Then [the cokernel completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AlmostSplit/Completion.html#TauCeti.IsLeftAlmostSplit.isAlmostSplit_cokernelSequence) gives an almost-split sequence ending at every non-projective indecomposable, and the irreducible-morphism quiver becomes a translation quiver.
- **Brauer–Schur–Weyl duality**: show that the orthogonal Brauer action commutes with `O(n)` and fills its commutant. Then build the symplectic action, whose cap and cup conventions are sign-sensitive.
- **The Harish-Chandra isomorphism**: show that the projection hits all dot-invariants. With orbit separation already proved, this also gives the converse that equal central characters force one dot orbit.
- **The Gassmann pair in `S₁₆`**: realise `C₄ × C₄` and `Q₈ × C₂` by their regular actions and use the new cycle-type criterion to show that they are almost conjugate but not conjugate.
- **Triality for `Spin₈`**: the cyclic intertwiners exist only on Lie-algebra generators over `ℚ`. Still missing are an order-three automorphism of the group and the proof that it is outer.
