## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Root systems and semisimple algebras are complete. So are the Weyl character formula, Peter–Weyl, Brauer's induction theorem, the Clifford correspondence and the classification of the irreducible characters of `GL₂(𝔽_q)`. Gabriel's theorem, Ado–Iwasawa, Schur–Weyl duality, the spin fundamentals, the Lie-group correspondence and the modular Artin theorem are partial. Triality and the Lie-group layers from Lie's third theorem onward have not begun.

### Named results

- **The [Cartan–Killing classification](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/FiniteType/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_dynkinType)**: every irreducible reduced crystallographic finite root system has a unique Dynkin type.
- **The Weyl character and [dimension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div) formulas**: multiplying a highest-weight module's character by the Weyl denominator gives the alternating Weyl numerator, and the dimension is the resulting product over positive coroots.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)**: the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations of a compact group form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **[Brauer's induction theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Brauer/Induction.html#TauCeti.ClassFunction.indVirtualCharacters_eq_virtualCharacters_isElementary)**: over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, the virtual characters induced from elementary subgroups are all the virtual characters.
- **The [irreducible characters of `GL₂(𝔽_q)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/GL2/Classification.html#TauCeti.irreducibleCharacters_GL2_eq_union)**: they are exactly the linear, Steinberg, principal-series and cuspidal characters.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[Gabriel's correspondence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Correspondence.html#TauCeti.gabrielIndecomposableEquivPositiveRoot)**: for a positive definite Tits form, it identifies indecomposable classes with positive roots by dimension vector. Only the finite-type dichotomy stands between it and Gabriel's theorem.
- **The [central augmentation ideal](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/HopfIdeal/Augmentation.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.centralAugmentationIdeal)**: adjoint-nilpotent elements of `U(L)` are nilpotent modulo its powers, which is the engine of positive-characteristic Ado.
- **The [Artin coefficients](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/ArtinCoefficient.html#Subgroup.artinCoeff)**: Möbius sums over the cyclic subgroups above a subgroup. They satisfy [Artin's identity for fixed points](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/ArtinCoefficient.html#TauCeti.sum_artinCoeff_mul_card_fixedBy) in `ℤ`, the first step of the modular Artin theorem.

## The frontier

- **The `GL₂(𝔽_q)` character table**: assemble the `(q²−1)`-square table from the existing value formulas, prove its rows orthonormal, and check `GL₂(𝔽₃)` and the degenerate `GL₂(𝔽₂)`.
- **Gabriel's dichotomy**: prove that a connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite, and use it to exclude the Kronecker quiver.
- **The Grothendieck group of `k[G]`**: build `G₀(k[G])` with its ring structure, restriction, induction and permutation classes. It is the one prerequisite blocking every `G₀` statement of the modular Artin theorem, whose group-theoretic inputs are now in place.
- **Positive-characteristic Ado**: apply Krull intersection over the central subalgebra to find a finite-dimensional quotient of `U(L)` separating `L`, then assemble the faithful nilpotence-preserving representation.
- **Spin modules as fundamental representations**: identify `S`, `S⁺` and `S⁻` with the irreducibles of highest weight `ωₗ` and `ωₗ₋₁` for types `B` and `D`. Irreducibility and the weights are available.
