### Named results

- **The [Harish–Chandra isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.harishChandraLinearIso)**: for a finite-dimensional Killing-semisimple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, it identifies the centre of the enveloping algebra with the Weyl invariants of `S(H)`.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)**: for a compact group, the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **[Conjugacy of maximal tori](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.exists_map_conj_eq)**: in a compact Lie group any two maximal tori are conjugate. For a connected group their conjugates [cover the group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Exhaustion.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.iUnion_map_conj_eq_univ).
- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)**: a finite connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **The [Auslander–Reiten existence theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/Sequence.html#ModuleCat.exists_isAlmostSplit_X₃_eq_iff)**: over a finite-dimensional algebra, every finitely generated indecomposable non-projective module ends an almost-split sequence.

Finitely generated functors on them now have [projective covers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/FinitelyPresentedFunctor/ProjectiveCover.html#TauCeti.IsKrullSchmidt.exists_isRepresentableProjectiveCover), the starting point of the functorial approach to almost-split sequences.

## The frontier

- **Kostant's isotypy corollary**: a highest weight of Kostant's module with the Casimir scalar of `ρ` [must be `ρ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Quadratic/Lie/KostantWeights.html#CliffordAlgebra.eq_weylVector_of_isHighestWeightVector_kostant_of_casimirScalar_eq). What remains is to combine this with the scalar action of the Casimir and state the isotypy for a general semisimple Lie algebra.
- **The first Brauer–Thrall theorem**: with Harada–Sai in hand, prove Auslander's theorem that a connected component of bounded length is the whole Auslander–Reiten quiver, and deduce that bounded representation type means finite type.
- **Almost-split existence for Artin algebras**: lift the field-case theorem to a commutative Artinian base. This needs almost-split conflations and Auslander–Reiten duality in exact categories, Matlis duality for Artin algebras, and uniqueness of Krull–Schmidt decompositions.
- **The Weyl group of a compact Lie group**: prove `C_G(T) = T`, identify `N(T)/T` with the Weyl group of the root system, and prove the Weyl integration formula. The derived-algebra splitting of Layer 5A comes first.
- **Brauer–Schur–Weyl duality**: show that the orthogonal and symplectic Brauer images fill their commutants. [Semisimplicity of `B_k(n)` for `n ≥ k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ClassicalGroups/BrauerGenerators/Orthogonal/Faithful.html#TauCeti.BrauerAlgebra.isSemisimpleRing_of_le) does not give these equalities.
