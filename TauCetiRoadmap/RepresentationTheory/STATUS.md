### Named results

- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)**: a finite connected quiver has finite representation type over any field exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **The Weyl character and [dimension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div) formulas**: a highest-weight module's character times the Weyl denominator is the alternating Weyl numerator, and its dimension is the resulting product over positive coroots.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)**: the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations of a compact group form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **Schur–Weyl duality**: the actions of `GLₙ` and `S_d` on `(kⁿ)^{⊗d}` are each other's centralizers. In characteristic zero the tensor power splits as a sum, over partitions of `d`, of a Specht module tensored with a Schur module.
- **The modular Artin theorem**: in characteristic `p`, `|G|` times any class in `G₀(k[G])` lies in the span of classes induced from cyclic subgroups of order prime to `p`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The [Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/PBW/Injective.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.pbwAssociatedGradedEquiv)**: for a Lie algebra that is free as a module, `gr U(L)` is the symmetric algebra.
- **The [Harish-Chandra projection](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/HarishChandra.html#TauCeti.hcProjection)**: it reads the centre of `U(L)` as polynomial functions on weights, landing in the dot-invariants. Those invariants [separate dot orbits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/OrbitSeparation.html#TauCeti.forall_mem_dotInvariants_lift_eq_iff).
- **The [Auslander–Reiten translate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/AuslanderReiten/Surjectivity.html#ModuleCat.exists_auslanderReitenTranslate)**: computed from minimal projective presentations, it is a bijection from non-projective to non-injective indecomposables. Over a finite-dimensional algebra it [starts a left almost-split map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/LeftAlmostSplit.html#LinearMap.exists_isLeftAlmostSplit_translate).

## The frontier

- **Almost-split sequences**: make the left almost-split map left minimal. Then [the cokernel completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AlmostSplit/Completion.html#TauCeti.IsLeftAlmostSplit.isAlmostSplit_cokernelSequence) gives an almost-split sequence ending at every non-projective indecomposable. After that, build the AR quiver of `kQ/I`.
- **The Harish-Chandra isomorphism**: show that the projection hits all dot-invariants. With orbit separation already proved, this also gives the converse that equal central characters force one dot orbit.
- **Triality for `Spin₈`**: the cyclic intertwiners and the octonion trilinear form exist only on Lie-algebra generators over `ℚ`. Still missing are an order-three automorphism of the group and the proof that it is outer.
- **`Der 𝕆 ≅ g₂`**: the map from split `g₂` is surjective and its target is simple of rank 14. What remains is injectivity, and then `f₄` from the Albert algebra.
- **Lie's third theorem**: give integral leaves their immersed Lie structure, match subalgebras with connected subgroups, and integrate an arbitrary real Lie algebra using the Ado embedding.
