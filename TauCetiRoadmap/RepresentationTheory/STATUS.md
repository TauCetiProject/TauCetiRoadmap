## The frontier

- **Ado–Iwasawa** — refine a cofinite ideal stably under derivations and extend the resulting nilrepresentation across split ideals in characteristic zero; in characteristic p, combine central polynomials with Krull separation to obtain a faithful finite quotient.
- **Gabriel's theorem** — connect positive-root reduction to admissible reflection words in both directions, then prove the finite-type equivalence and the indecomposable–positive-root bijection.
- **Artin and Brauer induction** — turn the local p-section and primewise induction results into the stated rational and integral induction theorems.
- **The GL₂(𝔽_q) character table** — prove the cuspidal virtual characters irreducible when appropriate, add the Steinberg constituent and assemble the complete table; principal-series isomorphism classes are known.
- **Spin highest weights** — prove the spin and half-spin modules irreducible and identify their type B and D fundamental highest weights; their sign-vector weights are known.
