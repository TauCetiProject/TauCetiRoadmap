### Named results

- **The Auslander–Reiten theorem** — over an Artin algebra, every finitely generated indecomposable non-projective module [ends an almost-split sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/Sequence.html#ModuleCat.exists_isAlmostSplit_arTranslate) `0 → τM → Y → M → 0`, and dually.
- **Auslander's theorem on bounded components** — over a connected finite-dimensional algebra, a class of indecomposables closed under irreducible maps and of bounded length [contains every indecomposable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/Component.html#TauCeti.forall_prop_and_isRepresentationFinite_of_length_le), so the algebra is representation-finite.
- **The [Harish–Chandra isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.harishChandraLinearIso)** — for a Killing-semisimple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, the centre of the enveloping algebra is identified with the Weyl invariants of `S(H)`.
- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)** — a finite connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **The [Frobenius characteristic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Symmetric/Stable/FrobeniusCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.stableFrobeniusCharacteristic)** — the class functions of `Sₙ` are isomorphic to the degree-`n` symmetric functions, with each irreducible character going to its Schur function.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The translate as a [stable equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/AuslanderReiten/StableEquivalence.html#TauCeti.stableTranslateEquivalence)**: `τ = D Tr` identifies finitely presented modules modulo projectives with finitely generated modules modulo injectives. Both Auslander–Reiten formulas are read off from it.
- **[Auslander–Reiten duality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/AuslanderReitenDuality.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.AuslanderReitenDuality) of an exact category**: this is the form of Auslander–Reiten theory that works outside module categories. For a Krull–Schmidt category with finite-length `Ext¹`, it exists exactly when almost-split conflations do.
- **The [dominant dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Abelian/DominantDimension.html#CategoryTheory.dominantDimension)**: the length of the longest coresolution by projective-injectives, and one side of the Morita–Tachikawa correspondence.
