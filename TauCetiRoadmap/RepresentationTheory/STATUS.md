## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Root systems, semisimple algebras and compact groups are complete. Ado–Iwasawa now holds over every field, Gabriel's theorem is proved for quivers without a pair of opposite arrows, and the induction–restriction roadmap is done apart from its Mackey layer. The executable character-table algorithm, the exceptional Lie algebras, Schur–Weyl duality, the classical-group characters and the modular Artin theorem are genuinely partial. Triality has not begun, and neither have the Lie-group layers from maximal tori onward.

### Named results

- **The Ado–Iwasawa theorem** — every finite-dimensional Lie algebra over a field has a faithful finite-dimensional representation, and by Hochschild's strengthening one in which ad-nilpotent elements act nilpotently. Equivalently, every such algebra is a Lie subalgebra of some `𝔤𝔩ₙ`.
- **Gabriel's theorem** — a finite connected quiver with no pair of opposite arrows has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite, that is, exactly when it is an oriented simply-laced Dynkin diagram.
- **The Weyl character and [dimension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div) formulas** — multiplying a highest-weight module's character by the Weyl denominator gives the alternating Weyl numerator, and the dimension is the resulting product over positive coroots.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)** — the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations of a compact group form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`.
- **[Brauer's characterization of characters](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Brauer/Characterization.html#TauCeti.mem_virtualCharacters_iff_forall_comp_subtype_isElementary)** — a class function is a virtual character exactly when its restriction to every elementary subgroup is one. Its corollary is in as well: `ℚ(ζ_e)` realizes every irreducible representation.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The [Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/PBW/Injective.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.pbwAssociatedGradedEquiv)** — for a Lie algebra free as a module, `gr U(L)` is the symmetric algebra. This makes `U(L)` a [domain](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/PBW/Domain.html#TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.instIsDomain) containing `L`, which is what positive-characteristic Ado was waiting on.
- **[Central characters](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Basic.html#TauCeti.vermaCentralCharacter)** — the centre of `U(L)` acts on each highest-weight module by a scalar character, and it acts [semisimply](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isotypic.html#TauCeti.isInternal_centerEigenspace) on every finite-dimensional module. This is the entry point to the Harish-Chandra isomorphism.
- **The [Burnside–Dixon–Schneider algorithm](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Dixon/Cyclotomic/Solver.html#TauCeti.ClassData.characterTableDixon?)** — it is executable, chooses its own prime, and is [sound](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Dixon/Cyclotomic/Solver.html#TauCeti.ClassData.isCharacterTableSpec_of_characterTableDixon?_eq_some): any table it returns is the character table. It has been run on `C₃` and `A₄`.
