### Named results

- **The [Harish–Chandra isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.harishChandraLinearIso)**: for a finite-dimensional Killing-semisimple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, it identifies the centre of the enveloping algebra with the Weyl invariants of `S(H)`.
- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)**: a finite connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **`G₂` as the [derivations of the octonions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Octonion/G2.html#TauCeti.Octonion.g₂EquivDerivationLieAlgebra)**: over a field of characteristic zero, the Serre-presented split Lie algebra of type `G₂` is the derivation algebra of the split octonions.
- **The [Frobenius characteristic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Symmetric/Stable/FrobeniusCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.stableFrobeniusCharacteristic)**: the class functions of `Sₙ` are isomorphic to the degree-`n` symmetric functions, with [each irreducible character going to its Schur function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Symmetric/Stable/FrobeniusCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.stableFrobeniusCharacteristic_spechtCharacter).
- **[Conjugacy of maximal tori](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.exists_map_conj_eq)**: in a compact Lie group any two maximal tori are conjugate, and in a connected one the conjugacy classes [are the normalizer orbits on a maximal torus](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Weyl/ConjugacyClasses.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.normalizerOrbitHomeomorph).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The [dominant dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Abelian/DominantDimension.html#CategoryTheory.dominantDimension)** of an object of an abelian category: the length of its longest coresolution by projective-injective objects. Minimal injective coresolutions [compute it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Abelian/DominantDimension.html#CategoryTheory.ComposableArrows.IsMinimalInjectiveCoresolution.natCast_le_dominantDimension_iff). With the next entry, it is one of the two sides of the Morita–Tachikawa correspondence.
- **[Generator-cogenerators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Generator/GeneratorCogenerator.html#TauCeti.IsGeneratorCogenerator)**: over an Artin algebra these are [the modules containing every indecomposable projective and injective as a summand](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Generator/Cogenerator.html#TauCeti.isGeneratorCogenerator_iff_forall_indecomposable). Together with [additive generators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/AdditiveGenerator.html#TauCeti.IsAdditiveGenerator) and projectivization, they supply what the Auslander and Morita–Tachikawa correspondences take as input.
- **The [Yoneda `Ext¹` of an exact category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.Ext1)**: it carries [the local theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/LocalDuality.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.tfae_exists_isAlmostSplit) on almost-split conflations, the objectwise step towards Auslander–Reiten duality outside module categories.
