- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)** — a finite connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **Kostant's isotypy theorem** — for a Killing-semisimple Lie algebra of rank `l`, the [spinor module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Kostant.html#TauCeti.SpinPolarizationData.kostantSpinRep_multiplicity) of a polarization of the Killing form is `2^⌊l/2⌋` copies of `L(ρ)`.
- **[Conjugacy of maximal tori](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.exists_map_conj_eq)** — in a compact Lie group any two maximal tori are conjugate, and in a connected one the conjugacy classes [are the normalizer orbits on a maximal torus](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Weyl/ConjugacyClasses.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.normalizerOrbitHomeomorph).
- **The [classification of irreducible `gl_N`-modules](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/GeneralLinear/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isGlDominantIntegral_and_nonempty_lieModuleEquiv_glIrreducible)** — in characteristic zero, a finite-dimensional irreducible module with triangularizable diagonal action is `L(μ)` for exactly one dominant integral `μ`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[Krull–Schmidt categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/KrullSchmidt/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsKrullSchmidt)**: every object is a finite biproduct of objects with local endomorphism rings, uniquely. With `mod A` now Krull–Schmidt over any left Artinian ring, this carries Auslander's criterion that [representation-finiteness is the existence of an additive generator](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/RepresentationFinite.html#TauCeti.isRepresentationFinite_iff_exists_isAdditiveGenerator).
- **The [Yoneda `Ext¹` of an exact category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.Ext1)**: an abelian group under Baer sum, equal to derived `Ext` in abelian categories. It now supports [the local theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/LocalDuality.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.tfae_exists_isAlmostSplit) on almost-split conflations, the objectwise step towards Auslander–Reiten duality outside module categories.
- **[The group of inner automorphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/AdjointAction/InnerAutomorphismGroup.html#TauCeti.intLieAlgebra)** `Int(L)`, generated by exponentials of nilpotent adjoints. The Weyl group acts on a Cartan subalgebra through it, which ties Weyl invariants to Lie invariants.
