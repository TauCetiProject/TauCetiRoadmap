The Weyl character formula, Brauer induction and Brauer's characterization, the Clifford correspondence, the closed-subgroup theorem and the character table of `GL₂(𝔽_q)` are also done; the table still lacks its `GL₂(𝔽₃)` instance. ### Named results

- **The [Cartan–Killing classification](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/FiniteType/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_dynkinType)**: every irreducible reduced crystallographic finite root system has a unique Dynkin type.
- **The Weyl character and [dimension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Weyl/Dimension.html#TauCeti.finrank_eq_prod_coweightPairing_div) formulas**: multiplying a highest-weight module's character by the Weyl denominator gives the alternating Weyl numerator, and the dimension is the resulting product over positive coroots.
- **The [Peter–Weyl theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)**: the normalized matrix coefficients of the irreducible unitary representations of a compact group form a Hilbert basis of `L²(G)`. Restricted to class functions, the irreducible characters form a Hilbert basis of the closed subspace of class functions.
- **[Brauer's characterization of characters](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Brauer/Characterization.html#TauCeti.mem_virtualCharacters_iff_forall_comp_subtype_isElementary)**: over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, a class function is a virtual character exactly when its restriction to every elementary subgroup is one. [Brauer's induction theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Brauer/Induction.html#TauCeti.ClassFunction.indVirtualCharacters_eq_virtualCharacters_isElementary), that induction from elementary subgroups yields every virtual character, is also in.
- **The [character table of `GL₂(𝔽_q)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/GL2/Table.html#TauCeti.GL2CharacterTable)**: a square table of size `q² − 1`, indexed by the linear, Steinberg, principal-series and cuspidal parameters. It is the group's character table with its rows relabelled, and its rows are [orthonormal](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/GL2/Table.html#TauCeti.GL2CharacterParam.characterPairing_classFunction).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

In that case every indecomposable is a [brick](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Reflection/Brick.html#TauCeti.finrank_end_eq_one_of_indecomposable). - **The [central augmentation ideal](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/HopfIdeal/Augmentation.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.centralAugmentationIdeal)**: this is the engine of positive-characteristic Ado. This is the generic half of the modular Artin theorem's `G₀(k[G])`.

## The frontier

- **The `GL₂(𝔽₃)` instance**: specialise the assembled table to the uniform case of order `48`. The degenerate `GL₂(𝔽₂) ≅ S₃` is already checked.
- **Positive-characteristic Ado**: prove that `U(L)` has no zero divisors and that `ι` is injective for nonabelian `L`. These are the PBW consequences that AdoIwasawa Layer 1 expects from the highest-weight roadmap. After that, state the faithful nilpotence-preserving theorem and `adoCharP`.
- **Gabriel's dichotomy**: show that finite representation type forces a positive definite Tits form. Positive definiteness already gives finite type, finite type passes to subquivers and to reflected quivers, and it rules out loops and parallel arrows. The extended Dynkin quivers still have to be shown to be of infinite type.
- **The Grothendieck group of `k[G]`**: build the dictionary `FDRep k G ≌ FGModuleCat k[G]`, then `G₀(k[G])` with its universal property, ring structure, and restriction, induction and permutation classes. This is the one prerequisite blocking every `G₀` statement of the modular Artin theorem.
- **The cyclotomic splitting field**: show that `ℚ(ζ_e)` realizes every irreducible representation. The README warns that this needs character-field and Schur-index descent, not just Brauer's theorem.
