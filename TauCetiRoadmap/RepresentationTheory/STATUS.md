## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Ado–Iwasawa, semisimple algebras, compact groups (through [Peter–Weyl](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/PeterWeyl.html#TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis)) and modular induction are complete. ### Named results

- **The [Harish–Chandra isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isomorphism.html#TauCeti.harishChandraLinearIso)**: for a finite-dimensional Killing-semisimple Lie algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, it identifies the centre of the enveloping algebra with the Weyl invariants of `S(H)`.
- **[Gabriel's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/FiniteRepType/Gabriel.html#TauCeti.isFiniteRepType_iff_titsForm_posDef)**: a finite connected quiver has finite representation type exactly when its Tits form is positive definite.
- **`G₂` as the [derivations of the octonions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Octonion/G2.html#TauCeti.Octonion.g₂EquivDerivationLieAlgebra)**: over a field of characteristic zero, the Serre-presented split Lie algebra of type `G₂` is isomorphic to the derivation algebra of the split octonions, and so is 14-dimensional with nondegenerate Killing form.
- **Kostant's isotypy theorem**: for a Killing-semisimple Lie algebra of rank `l`, the [spinor module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Spin/Kostant.html#TauCeti.SpinPolarizationData.kostantSpinRep_multiplicity) of a polarization of the Killing form is `2^⌊l/2⌋` copies of `L(ρ)`.
- **[Conjugacy of maximal tori](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Torus/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.exists_map_conj_eq)**: in a compact Lie group any two maximal tori are conjugate, and in a connected one the conjugacy classes [are the normalizer orbits on a maximal torus](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Weyl/ConjugacyClasses.html#TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus.normalizerOrbitHomeomorph).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[Additive generators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/AdditiveGenerator.html#TauCeti.IsAdditiveGenerator)**, defined by `add G = C` and, in a Krull–Schmidt category, [characterised by indecomposables](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/KrullSchmidt/AdditiveGenerator.html#TauCeti.isAdditiveGenerator_iff_forall_indecomposable). With [projectivization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/Projectivization.html#TauCeti.instIsEquivalenceOppositeFullSubcategoryAdditiveClosureModuleCatEndFiniteProjectiveModulesContravariantProjectivizationFunctorOfIsIdempotentComplete), which identifies `add M` with the projective `End(M)`-modules, they are the inputs to the Auslander correspondence.
- **The [Yoneda `Ext¹` of an exact category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.Ext1)**: it carries [the local theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/YonedaExt/LocalDuality.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.tfae_exists_isAlmostSplit) on almost-split conflations, the objectwise step towards Auslander–Reiten duality outside module categories.
- **[Matlis duality for Artin algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/MatlisDuality.html#TauCeti.IsInjectiveEnvelope.fgModuleCatMatlisDuality)**: an anti-equivalence between finitely generated left and right modules. It is now also available [for finite-length modules over any algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/MatlisDuality/FiniteLength.html#ModuleCat.matlisDuality).

## The frontier

- **`F₄` from the Albert algebra**: the derivations of the split Albert algebra [have dimension 52](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlbertAlgebra/Derivation/Finrank.html#TauCeti.AlbertAlgebra.finrank_derivationLieAlgebra). What remains is simplicity and a split Cartan subalgebra, after which the `G₂` argument should transfer. The `E`-series models have not started.
- **The Auslander correspondence**: projectivization and additive generators are in. What remains is the converse (an Auslander algebra is `End` of an additive generator), Morita classes, dominant dimension and Morita–Tachikawa.
- **The classical Schur–Weyl commutants**: show that matching tensors span the `O(n)`-invariants of every tensor power. On the symplectic side the commutant is known only on two strands, and the three-strand trace analysis has begun.
- **The Weyl group of a compact Lie group**: identify `N(T)/T` with the root-system Weyl group, then prove the Weyl integration formula.
- **Blocks of finite groups**: Layer 0 of ModularCharactersAndBlocks (p-sections, block idempotents, block splitting of modules) can start on the existing primitive-idempotent and p-part API.
