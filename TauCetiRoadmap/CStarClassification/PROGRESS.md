## CStarClassification: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-13 (`2bbe126` to `aa6e43e`)

The classification roadmap has its first foundation stone: [approximate unitary equivalence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateUnitaryEquivalence/Basic.html#TauCeti.ApproximatelyUnitarilyEquivalent) of *-homomorphisms into a unital C*-algebra, where on every finite set one map is approximated by the other conjugated by a unitary of the target (TauCeti#39205). The source and the maps may be nonunital. It is [an equivalence relation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateUnitaryEquivalence/Basic.html#TauCeti.ApproximatelyUnitarilyEquivalent.equivalence) and is preserved by [precomposition](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateUnitaryEquivalence/Basic.html#TauCeti.ApproximatelyUnitarilyEquivalent.precomp) with any map and by postcomposition with a unital one. Equivalent maps have equal pointwise norms, so one is injective exactly when the other is. For a separable source, the relation is [equivalent to pointwise convergence along a sequence of unitaries](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateUnitaryEquivalence/Sequential.html#TauCeti.ApproximatelyUnitarilyEquivalent.iff_exists_unitary_seq). That is the sequential form the intertwining arguments will use.

This is only the unital-target version of §1.1. The versions with unitaries in the multiplier algebra or the minimal unitization are still missing, as are asymptotic unitary equivalence and the fact that equivalent maps agree on K-theory. Nothing yet touches the finite-dimensional classification of maps, Elliott's intertwining, or anything beyond Layer 1.

## CStarClassification: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-25 (`aa6e43e` to `7433fc3`)

Layer 1 now ends where it was meant to: [Elliott's approximate intertwining theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateIntertwining/Unitary.html#TauCeti.elliott_intertwining) is proved (TauCeti#40052). For separable unital A and B, *-homomorphisms φ : A → B and ψ : B → A whose two composites are approximately unitarily equivalent to the identities yield a *-isomorphism A ≅ B that is approximately unitarily equivalent to φ. It is deduced from a [version for sequential inductive systems](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateIntertwining/Unitary.html#TauCeti.elliott_intertwining_of_isSeqColimitCocone) whose connecting maps need not be injective. Underneath is a general [approximate intertwining](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateIntertwining/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsApproximateIntertwining) with summable errors, which comes with the limit isomorphism and tail-error bounds. The K-theory maps of the limit isomorphism are not yet identified.

Maps between full matrix algebras are classified. The [multiplicity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/HomMultiplicity.html#Matrix.homMultiplicity) of a map Mₙ → Mₘ multiplies under composition, and a map of multiplicity μ [exists exactly when nμ ≤ m](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/HomMultiplicity.html#Matrix.exists_nonUnitalStarAlgHom_homMultiplicity_eq_iff), with equality exactly for unital maps. Two maps are [unitarily conjugate exactly when their multiplicities agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HomMultiplicity.html#NonUnitalStarAlgHom.exists_mem_unitary_mul_map_mul_star_eq_iff). Direct sums of matrix algebras, where multiplicities form a matrix, are still to come. On the §1.1 side, [asymptotic unitary equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateUnitaryEquivalence/Asymptotic.html#TauCeti.AsymptoticallyUnitarilyEquivalent) arrived, with paths of unitaries in the target or in the multiplier algebra, as did comparisons with unitaries of the unitization.

Layers 3 and 4 have their first definitions. [Scales](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Order/Group/DimensionGroup/Scale.html#TauCeti.IsScale) of ordered groups come with their domination form, and [Cuntz subequivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Cuntz/Subequivalence.html#TauCeti.CuntzLE) is a preorder preserved by *-homomorphisms, with the [cutdown](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Cuntz/Subequivalence.html#TauCeti.cuntzLE_cfcₙ_max_sub_zero) (a − ε)₊ ≼ a. [Infinite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Projection/Infinite.html#TauCeti.IsInfiniteProjection) and [properly infinite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Projection/Infinite.html#TauCeti.IsProperlyInfiniteProjection) projections are defined too, and no projection in Mₙ is infinite.
