### Named results

- **Elliott's approximate intertwining theorem** — for separable unital A and B, *-homomorphisms φ : A → B and ψ : B → A whose composites are approximately unitarily equivalent to the identities give a *-isomorphism A ≅ B approximately unitarily equivalent to φ ([`elliott_intertwining`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateIntertwining/Unitary.html#TauCeti.elliott_intertwining)). It is deduced from a [version for sequential inductive systems](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateIntertwining/Unitary.html#TauCeti.elliott_intertwining_of_isSeqColimitCocone) whose connecting maps may fail to be injective.
- **Classification of maps between full matrix algebras** — two *-homomorphisms Mₙ → Mₘ over ℝ or ℂ are unitarily conjugate exactly when they have the same multiplicity ([`exists_mem_unitary_mul_map_mul_star_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/HomMultiplicity.html#NonUnitalStarAlgHom.exists_mem_unitary_mul_map_mul_star_eq_iff)), and multiplicity μ is [realised exactly when nμ ≤ m](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/HomMultiplicity.html#Matrix.exists_nonUnitalStarAlgHom_homMultiplicity_eq_iff), with equality exactly for unital maps.
- **The Effros–Handelman–Shen theorem** — a partially ordered abelian group is a countable dimension group exactly when it is a sequential limit of simplicial groups ℤʳ along positive maps ([`effros_handelman_shen`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Order/Group/DimensionGroup/EffrosHandelmanShen.html#TauCeti.effros_handelman_shen)).
- **AF algebras as inductive limits** — an algebra is AF exactly when it is the C*-inductive limit of a sequence of finite-dimensional algebras, connecting maps possibly noninjective ([`isAF_iff_nonempty_starAlgEquiv_cStarInductiveLimit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/AF/InductiveLimit.html#TauCeti.isAF_iff_nonempty_starAlgEquiv_cStarInductiveLimit)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Approximate and asymptotic unitary equivalence** ([`ApproximatelyUnitarilyEquivalent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateUnitaryEquivalence/Basic.html#TauCeti.ApproximatelyUnitarilyEquivalent), [`AsymptoticallyUnitarilyEquivalent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ApproximateUnitaryEquivalence/Asymptotic.html#TauCeti.AsymptoticallyUnitarilyEquivalent)): the relations every uniqueness theorem is stated in. They come in target-unitary and multiplier-unitary versions, with comparisons to unitaries of the unitization, and each is an equivalence relation compatible with composition.
- **Cuntz subequivalence** ([`CuntzLE`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Cuntz/Subequivalence.html#TauCeti.CuntzLE)): the comparison order for positive elements that pure infiniteness is built on. It is a preorder, preserved by *-homomorphisms, with the cutdown (a − ε)₊ ≼ a.
- **Scales** ([`IsScale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Order/Group/DimensionGroup/Scale.html#TauCeti.IsScale)): the extra datum in the AF invariant. Under interpolation the domination form is proved in both directions, and an interval [0, u] is a scale exactly when u is an order unit.

## The frontier

- **Multiplicity matrices (§1.2)**: extend the full-matrix classification to finite direct sums, with composition as matrix multiplication and the induced K₀ map equal to the multiplicity matrix. The K₀ part needs OperatorKTheory K1.
- **Projections and Cuntz comparison (§4.1–§4.2)**: prove that p ≼ q for projections means Murray–von Neumann subequivalence, along with the matrix form p ⊕ p ≼ p of proper infiniteness and the compatibility with hereditary subalgebras.
- **Semiprojectivity and cancellation (§1.3–§1.4)**: lifting finite-dimensional maps through increasing ideals, and lifting scaled K₀ maps into targets with cancellation. These need CStarAlgebras C9 and OperatorKTheory K₀, and they lead into Bratteli's local characterization (§2.1).
- **Finishing §1.1**: invariance under restriction and stabilisation, and equal K₀ and K₁ maps for approximately unitarily equivalent maps, which needs OperatorKTheory K1–K2.
- **The scaled range theorem (§3.3)**: realise each countable scaled dimension group as an AF invariant. This builds on the simplicial presentations now available, but it also needs Bratteli diagrams and the AF classification of §2.2 and §2.6.
