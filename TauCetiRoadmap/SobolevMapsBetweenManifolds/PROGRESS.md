## SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds: 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-19 (`ba82f2a` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first landed work is the linear algebra at the base of its Jacobian layer: the [oriented Jacobian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/OrientedJacobian.html#Orientation.orientedJacobian) of a linear map between oriented real inner product spaces of the same dimension, the scalar by which the map pulls the target volume form back to a multiple of the source one (TauCeti#41492). It comes with [Hadamard's inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/OrientedJacobian.html#Orientation.abs_orientedJacobian_le), `|J(A)| ≤ ‖A‖ⁿ`, and its [equality case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/OrientedJacobian.html#Orientation.abs_orientedJacobian_toLinearMap_eq_norm_pow_iff): equality holds exactly when `A` is zero or conformal. The other properties the roadmap asks of `J` are also proved. It is [multiplicative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/OrientedJacobian.html#Orientation.orientedJacobian_comp) under composition and changes sign when exactly one orientation is reversed. For an endomorphism with a single orientation it is the determinant. It is [positive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/OrientedJacobian.html#Orientation.orientedJacobian_pos_iff) exactly when `A` is invertible and carries one orientation to the other, and in dimension zero it is `±1` according to whether the orientations agree.

The same work goes beyond §6.1. It computes `J` in arbitrary bases and through linear identifications with a common model space, and proves a [metric-rescaling formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/OrientedJacobian.html#Orientation.orientedJacobian_comp_eq_div_mul_of_volumeForm_compLinearMap) for rescaled volume forms; these are the linear-algebra forms of the chart formula and change-of-metric rule that §6.2 needs. It also shows that `J(A)²` is a Gram determinant and that `J` depends smoothly on `A`. The Jacobian of a Sobolev map is not defined yet. It needs the manifold class and weak differential of Layer 4, and none of Layers 1–5 has landed.
