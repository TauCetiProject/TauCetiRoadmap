## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0, 1, 3 and 4 are done. Layer 2 lacks only the almost-everywhere-finite case of Borel-cost duality. Layers 5, 6 and 8–15 are partial: Brenier, Benamou–Brenier, the Gaussian barycenter and EVI flows under compactness are proved, while Caffarelli regularity, JKO convergence and `RCD` are not. Layers 7 and 16 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Brenier's theorem**: for an absolutely continuous source on a finite-dimensional inner product space at finite quadratic cost, the optimal plan is unique and is induced by the gradient of a convex potential ([`TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Brenier.html#TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient)).
- **Pratelli's theorem**: for an atomless finite source and a continuous cost with values in `[0, ∞]` on second-countable standard Borel spaces, the Monge and Kantorovich values agree, whether finite or not ([`TauCeti.mongeCost_eq_transportCost_of_continuous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Pratelli.html#TauCeti.mongeCost_eq_transportCost_of_continuous)).
- **The Beiglböck–Léonard–Schachermayer duality theorem**: for every Borel cost `c : X × Y → [0, ∞]` on Polish spaces, the relaxed transport value equals the dual supremum over integrable potentials ([`TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_integrable_relaxedTransportCost`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Relaxed.html#TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_integrable_relaxedTransportCost)). Without relaxation the gap [can be strict](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Relaxed.html#TauCeti.sSup_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_integrable_lt_transportCost_borelGapCost).
- **The Benamou–Brenier formula**: for `1 ≤ p < ∞` on a finite-dimensional normed space, `W_p^p` is the attained minimum of the action over solutions of the continuity equation joining the two laws ([`TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/ContinuityEquation/BenamouBrenier.html#TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation)).
- **Minimizing movements converge to EVI flows**: for a lower semicontinuous, coercive energy that is penalized convex for small steps, every generalized minimizing movement is the unique `EVI_m` solution from its starting point ([`TauCeti.IsGeneralizedMinimizingMovement.isEVISolution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/EVI/MinimizingMovement.html#TauCeti.IsGeneralizedMinimizingMovement.isEVISolution)). The flow therefore [exists](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/EVI/MinimizingMovement.html#TauCeti.exists_isEVISolution) whenever bounded parts of sublevel sets are relatively compact.

### Roadmap coverage

- **Layers 0, 1, 3 and 4** are done. **Layer 2** has the relaxed Borel duality, unrelaxed duality for bounded costs and under an integrable split envelope, and the strict-gap example. It lacks the case of costs finite almost everywhere and the Beiglböck–Schachermayer Borel dual maximizers.
- **Layer 5** has Brenier, the quadratic polar factorization and uniqueness of potentials. It lacks sources null on Lipschitz hypersurfaces, Gangbo–McCann, and the Sobolev polar factorization.
- **Layer 6** has most of the Aleksandrov and transport-equation tiers, convex sections, a Hölder gradient *given* a uniform section height, and the Perron envelope. It lacks viscosity-to-Aleksandrov, Dirichlet existence, unconditional Caffarelli estimates, the higher tiers, MTW and partial regularity.
- **Layer 8** lacks Lagrangian superposition, `CBB(κ)` beyond `κ = 0`, the `p = 1` and `W_∞` theories and the Riemannian formula. **Layer 9** lacks the restriction and reparameterization lemmas along generalized geodesics, and agreement with Layer 7.
- **Layer 10** has slopes, curves of maximal slope, fixed-carrier Γ-stability of EVI flows, and convergence of minimizing movements to EVI flows under sublevel compactness. It lacks the compactness-free existence and the varying-carrier theory. **Layer 11** has entropies, the JKO step and parts of 11.3–11.5, but not JKO convergence or the PDE flows.
- **Layer 12** has Fréchet barycenters, finitely supported and population laws, and the Gaussian case. It lacks general stability, multi-marginal duality and Agueh–Carlier.
- **Layers 13–15**: entropic duality covers only bounded costs, and Sinkhorn lacks kernels with zero entries. Layer 14 has no `RCD` or heat flow. Layer 15 lacks separability and measured Gromov–Hausdorff convergence.
- **Layers 7 and 16** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Caffarelli interior regularity** (Layer 6.8): a Hölder gradient already follows from a uniform height of compactly contained sections. What remains is the local strict convexity that supplies that height, and then `W²,1+ε`.
- **JKO convergence** (Layer 11.7): the abstract EVI limit now exists under sublevel compactness. The Wasserstein instance needs that compactness or the Ambrosio–Gigli–Savaré Cauchy estimate for discrete solutions (Layer 10.5), then convergence to Fokker–Planck and porous-medium solutions.
- **The Monge–Ampère Dirichlet problem** (Layer 6.4): show that the Perron envelope attains the boundary data and solves the equation on strictly convex domains.
- **Borel duality for almost-everywhere-finite costs** (Layer 2.5): show that relaxation is exact when `c` is finite `μ ⊗ ν`-almost everywhere, and construct the Borel contact potentials of Beiglböck–Schachermayer.
- **Infinitesimal Hilbertianity and `RCD(K, ∞)`** (Layer 14.3): the quadratic Cheeger case, the `L²` heat flow and the EVI characterization.
