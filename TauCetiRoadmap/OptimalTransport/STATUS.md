### Named results

- **Brenier's theorem** — for an absolutely continuous source on a finite-dimensional inner product space and finite quadratic optimal cost, the optimal plan is unique and is induced by the a.e.-unique gradient of a lower semicontinuous convex potential ([`TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Brenier.html#TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient)).
- **Kantorovich duality on Polish spaces** — for a lower semicontinuous cost bounded below by `a ⊕ b`, with `a, b` upper semicontinuous and integrable, the optimal cost is the supremum of the dual values ([`TauCeti.isLUB_kantorovichDualValue_of_lowerSemicontinuous_bddBelow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/BoundedBelow.html#TauCeti.isLUB_kantorovichDualValue_of_lowerSemicontinuous_bddBelow)). Dual attainment is proved only for bounded continuous costs.
- **Lisini's superposition principle** — on a complete separable metric space, an absolutely continuous curve of laws is the family of time marginals of a law on absolutely continuous paths with the same action ([`TauCeti.exists_map_IccExtend_eq_lintegral_curveAction_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Superposition.html#TauCeti.exists_map_IccExtend_eq_lintegral_curveAction_eq)).
- **McCann's displacement convexity** — on a finite-dimensional inner product space with Haar reference, internal energies satisfying McCann's condition are convex along the interpolation by the gradient of a convex function ([`TauCeti.internalEnergy_map_smul_add_smul_gradient_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/InternalEnergy/DisplacementConvexity.html#TauCeti.internalEnergy_map_smul_add_smul_gradient_le)).
- **The Le Gouic–Loubes existence theorem** — every population law on a Wasserstein space has a Wasserstein barycenter ([`TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.exists_isFrechetBarycenter`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/PopulationBarycenter.html#TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.exists_isFrechetBarycenter)), with Agueh–Carlier uniqueness for an absolutely continuous input.

## The frontier

- **The Benamou–Brenier lower bound** (Layer 8, item 8): show every continuity-equation solution has action at least `W_p^p`, and prove attainment. The upper bound and the Hopf–Lax tools are in place.
- **The JKO step** (Layer 11, item 6): the penalized `W₂` minimization with its Euler–Lagrange condition. Uniqueness needs generalized-geodesic convexity (Layer 9, item 4), which is missing.
- **Dual attainment for unbounded costs** (Layer 2, item 4): attainment for real costs under an integrable split upper envelope `c ≤ c_X ⊕ c_Y`.

- **The Gangbo–McCann theorem** (Layer 5, item 8): construct the `c`-concave potential and map for strictly convex superlinear `h`; the conjugate calculus and contact-set map theorem exist.
