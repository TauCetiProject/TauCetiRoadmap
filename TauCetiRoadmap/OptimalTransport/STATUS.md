### Named results

- **Brenier's theorem** — for an absolutely continuous source on a finite-dimensional inner product space at finite quadratic cost, the optimal plan is unique and induced by the gradient of a convex potential ([`TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Brenier.html#TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient)).
- **Pratelli's theorem** — for an atomless finite source and a continuous cost with values in `[0, ∞]` on second-countable standard Borel spaces, the Monge and Kantorovich values agree, whether finite or not ([`TauCeti.mongeCost_eq_transportCost_of_continuous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Pratelli.html#TauCeti.mongeCost_eq_transportCost_of_continuous)).
- **The Benamou–Brenier formula** — for `1 ≤ p < ∞` on a finite-dimensional normed space, `W_p^p` is the attained minimum of the action over continuity-equation solutions joining the two laws ([`TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/ContinuityEquation/BenamouBrenier.html#TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation)).
- **The Gaussian barycenter** — if some positively weighted input covariance is positive definite, the quadratic Wasserstein barycenter of Gaussian laws is the unique Gaussian with the averaged mean and the positive-definite solution of `S = ∑ⱼ wⱼ (S^½ Cⱼ S^½)^½`, and that solution [exists](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/BuresFixedPoint.html#TauCeti.exists_posDef_eq_sum_smul_sqrt_sqrt_mul_mul_sqrt) and is unique.
- **Minimizing movements converge to curves of maximal slope** — for a lower semicontinuous energy that is bounded below and whose descending slope is a lower semicontinuous strong upper gradient, every pointwise limit of implicit Euler interpolants from a fixed finite-energy start is a curve of maximal slope ([`TauCeti.isCurveOfMaximalSlope_of_forall_mem_moreauYosidaResolvent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/MaximalSlope/MinimizingMovement.html#TauCeti.isCurveOfMaximalSlope_of_forall_mem_moreauYosidaResolvent)).

The EVI convergence theorem will rest on it.

## The frontier

- **Caffarelli interior regularity** (Layer 6.8): a Hölder gradient already follows from a uniform height of compactly contained sections. What remains is local strict convexity, which supplies it, and then `W²,1+ε`.
- **Minimizing movements to EVI flows** (Layer 10.5): the discrete EVI holds under penalized convexity and has to be passed to the limit. After that, JKO schemes have to converge to Fokker–Planck and porous-medium solutions (Layer 11.7).
- **The Monge–Ampère Dirichlet problem** (Layer 6.4): show that the Perron envelope attains the boundary data and that its Monge–Ampère measure equals the datum on strictly convex domains.
- **Infinitesimal Hilbertianity and `RCD(K, ∞)`** (Layer 14.3): the quadratic Cheeger case, the `L²` heat flow and the EVI characterization.
- **Entropic duality beyond bounded costs** (Layer 13.3): Nutz's potentials under finite-entropy feasibility.
