## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0, 1, 3 and 4 are done. Layer 2 lacks only the almost-everywhere-finite case of Borel-cost duality. Layers 5, 6 and 8–15 are partial. Brenier, Gangbo–McCann, Benamou–Brenier, compactness-free EVI flows and convergence of the JKO scheme are proved. Caffarelli regularity, the PDE identification of JKO limits and `RCD` are not. Layers 7 and 16 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Brenier's theorem**: for an absolutely continuous source on a finite-dimensional inner product space at finite quadratic cost, the optimal plan is unique and is induced by the gradient of a convex potential ([`TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Brenier.html#TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient)).
- **Gangbo–McCann's theorem**: for a strictly convex, superlinear cost `h(x − y)` with their cone condition at infinity, an absolutely continuous source and finite cost, there is an optimal map. Its graph plan is the only optimal coupling, and the map is unique almost everywhere ([`TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_of_strictConvexOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/GangboMcCann.html#TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_of_strictConvexOn)).
- **The Beiglböck–Léonard–Schachermayer duality theorem**: for every Borel cost `c : X × Y → [0, ∞]` on Polish spaces, the relaxed transport value equals the dual supremum over integrable potentials ([`TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_integrable_relaxedTransportCost`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Relaxed.html#TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_integrable_relaxedTransportCost)). Without relaxation the gap [can be strict](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Relaxed.html#TauCeti.sSup_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_integrable_lt_transportCost_borelGapCost).
- **The Benamou–Brenier formula**: for `1 ≤ p < ∞` on a finite-dimensional normed space, `W_p^p` is the minimum of the action over solutions of the continuity equation joining the two laws, and the minimum is attained ([`TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/ContinuityEquation/BenamouBrenier.html#TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation)).
- **Convergence of the JKO scheme**: on `P₂(E)` for a separable Hilbert space `E`, a lower semicontinuous energy that is `λ`-convex along generalized geodesics has an `EVI_λ` flow from each point of finite energy. For every real `λ`, the JKO interpolants converge to that flow uniformly on bounded time intervals ([`TauCeti.exists_isEVISolution_tendstoUniformlyOn_of_generalizedGeodesicallyConvex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/GeneralizedGeodesic/EVI.html#TauCeti.exists_isEVISolution_tendstoUniformlyOn_of_generalizedGeodesicallyConvex)). It instantiates Ambrosio–Gigli–Savaré's [compactness-free EVI theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/EVI/MinimizingMovement.html#TauCeti.exists_isEVISolution_tendstoUniformlyOn_of_penalizedConvex).

## The frontier

- **Fokker–Planck identification** (Layer 11.7, item 2): the JKO limit now exists as an `EVI_λ` flow. What remains is to show that it solves `∂ₜρ = Δρ + div(ρ∇V)` in distributions, which needs the subdifferential and first-variation theory of 11.4–11.5.
- **Regular sources** (Layer 5.7): prove that the quadratic optimal plan is unique and induced by a map exactly when the source is null on `c − c` hypersurfaces. Then extend Gangbo–McCann to rectifiable-null sources.
- **Borel duality for almost-everywhere-finite costs** (Layer 2.5): show that relaxation is exact when `c` is finite `μ ⊗ ν`-almost everywhere, and construct the Borel contact potentials of Beiglböck–Schachermayer.
- **Caffarelli interior regularity** (Layer 6.8): a Hölder gradient already follows from a uniform height of sections. The local strict convexity that supplies that height is missing, and so is `W²,1+ε`.
- **Infinitesimal Hilbertianity and `RCD(K, ∞)`** (Layer 14.3): the quadratic Cheeger case, the `L²` heat flow and the EVI characterization.
