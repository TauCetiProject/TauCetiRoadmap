### Named results

- **Brenier's theorem** — for an absolutely continuous source on a finite-dimensional inner product space at finite quadratic cost, the optimal plan is unique and induced by the gradient of a convex potential ([`TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Brenier.html#TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient)).
- **The Benamou–Brenier formula** — for `1 ≤ p < ∞` on a finite-dimensional normed space, `W_p^p` is the attained minimum of the action `∫₀¹ ∫ ‖vₜ‖^p dμₜ dt` over solutions of the continuity equation joining the two laws ([`TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/ContinuityEquation/BenamouBrenier.html#TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation)).
- **Lisini's superposition principle** — for `1 < p < ∞` on a complete separable metric space, every absolutely continuous curve in `P_p(X)` is the curve of time marginals of a law on absolutely continuous paths with the same averaged speed ([`TauCeti.exists_map_IccExtend_eq_lintegral_curveAction_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Superposition.html#TauCeti.exists_map_IccExtend_eq_lintegral_curveAction_eq)).
- **McCann's displacement convexity** — an internal energy satisfying McCann's condition is convex along the Brenier interpolation from an absolutely continuous law ([`TauCeti.internalEnergy_map_smul_add_smul_gradient_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/InternalEnergy/DisplacementConvexity.html#TauCeti.internalEnergy_map_smul_add_smul_gradient_le)).
- **Existence of population Wasserstein barycenters** — the Le Gouic–Loubes theorem, assembled from finitely supported laws by approximation, with almost-sure consistency of empirical barycenters ([`TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.exists_isFrechetBarycenter`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/PopulationBarycenter.html#TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.exists_isFrechetBarycenter)).
