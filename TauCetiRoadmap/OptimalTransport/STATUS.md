- **Strong Kantorovich duality on compact spaces** — for a continuous nonnegative cost on compact pseudometric spaces the primal value is the supremum of the continuous dual values, unchanged by admitting integrable potentials; monotone convergence extends this to lower semicontinuous costs on compact metrizable spaces ([`TauCeti.isLUB_kantorovichDualValue_continuous`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Compact.html#TauCeti.isLUB_kantorovichDualValue_continuous), [`TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_continuous_of_lowerSemicontinuous`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/LowerSemicontinuous.html#TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_continuous_of_lowerSemicontinuous)).
- **The gluing lemma** — two plans sharing a middle marginal have a joint law with both prescribed two-coordinate marginals, in both standard-Borel-sided forms, with countable chains glued through a Kolmogorov extension theorem on countable index types ([`TauCeti.Measure.exists_glue_of_standardBorel_right`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Gluing.html#TauCeti.Measure.exists_glue_of_standardBorel_right), [`TauCeti.Measure.exists_isProjectiveLimit_of_countable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ProjectiveLimit/Countable.html#TauCeti.Measure.exists_isProjectiveLimit_of_countable)).
- **Complementary slackness and optimality certificates** — a coupling and an integrable dual feasible pair certify each other exactly when the plan is concentrated on the contact set; a certified plan is then optimal, with no duality gap, and concentrated on a measurable `c`-cyclically monotone set ([`TauCeti.isDualCertificate_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Certificate.html#TauCeti.isDualCertificate_iff), [`TauCeti.IsDualCertificate.exists_isCyclicallyMonotone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Certificate.html#TauCeti.IsDualCertificate.exists_isCyclicallyMonotone)).

## The frontier

- **Polish lower-semicontinuous Kantorovich duality** (Layer 2, item 4) — the theorem for `c ≥ a ⊕ b` with `a, b` upper semicontinuous and integrable, comparing bounded-continuous and integrable dual classes, then dual attainment under an integrable split upper envelope. The compact theorem and the bounded-below interface are in place; the passage to the Polish space remains. This is Layer 2's named summit.
- **The `W_p` distance** (Layer 3, item 1) — define it over couplings through `eLpNorm` of `edist` for `p : ℝ≥0∞`, and prove the triangle inequality by gluing plus Minkowski. Gluing and existence for `edist ^ p` are ready; every later layer waits on this.
- **Schachermayer–Teichmann on Polish spaces** (Layer 2, item 7) — the converse: a finite-cost plan concentrated on a `c`-cyclically monotone set is optimal, for finite-valued lower-semicontinuous costs. The forward direction and the finite case are done; the converse needs a contact-potential construction not yet started.
- **Small Layer 1 and 2 gaps** — finite multi-marginal dual attainment, the lower-semicontinuity lemma for costs bounded below by integrable marginal terms, and the Layer 0 and Layer 1 acceptance checks. All self-contained.
- **Borel-cost regimes** (Layer 2, item 5) — Beiglböck–Schachermayer for finite nonnegative Borel costs and the relaxed primal of Beiglböck–Léonard–Schachermayer. Untouched; depends on the Polish duality theorem above.
