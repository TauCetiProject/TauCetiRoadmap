### Named results

- **Brenier's theorem** — for an absolutely continuous source on a finite-dimensional inner product space at finite quadratic cost, the optimal plan is unique and induced by the gradient of a convex potential ([`TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Brenier.html#TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient)).
- **Pratelli's theorem** — for an atomless finite source and a continuous cost with values in `[0, ∞]` on second-countable standard Borel spaces, the Monge and Kantorovich values agree, whether finite or not ([`TauCeti.mongeCost_eq_transportCost_of_continuous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Pratelli.html#TauCeti.mongeCost_eq_transportCost_of_continuous)).
- **The Benamou–Brenier formula** — for `1 ≤ p < ∞` on a finite-dimensional normed space, `W_p^p` is the attained minimum of the action over continuity-equation solutions joining the two laws ([`TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/ContinuityEquation/BenamouBrenier.html#TauCeti.isLeast_lintegral_isContinuityEquation)).
- **Uniqueness of Wasserstein geodesics** — a unique optimal coupling together with almost surely unique base geodesics forces a unique geodesic in `P_p(X)` ([`TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.eqOn_of_isGeodesicSegment_of_ae_subsingleton`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Geodesic/Unique.html#TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.eqOn_of_isGeodesicSegment_of_ae_subsingleton)).
- **Minimizing movements converge to curves of maximal slope** — for a lower semicontinuous energy that is bounded below and whose descending slope is a lower semicontinuous strong upper gradient, every pointwise limit of implicit Euler interpolants from a fixed finite-energy start, built from given resolvent selections, is a curve of maximal slope ([`TauCeti.isCurveOfMaximalSlope_of_forall_mem_moreauYosidaResolvent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/GradientFlow/MaximalSlope/MinimizingMovement.html#TauCeti.isCurveOfMaximalSlope_of_forall_mem_moreauYosidaResolvent)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- Displacement interpolations ([`TauCeti.IsDisplacementInterpolation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Geodesic/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsDisplacementInterpolation)) are the generic relation from optimal dynamic plans. They exist on Polish geodesic spaces and contain the curves induced by Euclidean optimal maps.
- Sturm's `CD(K, N)`, the reduced `CD*(K, N)` ([`TauCeti.MetricMeasureSpace.ReducedCurvatureDimension`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/MetricMeasure/CurvatureDimension/Finite.html#TauCeti.MetricMeasureSpace.ReducedCurvatureDimension)) and a dynamic-plan form of `CD(K, N)` sit beside `MCP` and `CD(K, ∞)`, with no bridge theorems among the three finite-dimensional predicates yet. The first two have Bonnet–Myers bounds; for the dynamic-plan form, Haar measure on an `n`-dimensional space is `CD(0, N)` exactly when `N ≥ n`, given `N ≥ 1`. Together with the complete Cheeger–Sobolev space they are the starting point for `RCD`.
- The Gromov–Wasserstein space ([`TauCeti.GromovWassersteinSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/GromovWasserstein/Space.html#TauCeti.GromovWassersteinSpace)) of measured kernels modulo weak isomorphism is a metric space with optimal couplings on Polish carriers, and it is [complete exactly when the target is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/GromovWasserstein/Complete.html#TauCeti.GromovWassersteinSpace.completeSpace_iff).

- **Gaussian barycenter existence** (Layer 12, item 8): show the fixed-point equation has a positive-definite solution when some positively weighted input is nondegenerate.
