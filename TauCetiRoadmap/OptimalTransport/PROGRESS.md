## OptimalTransport: 2026-08-08 to 2026-09-01 (`1a83584` to `8745177`)

The primal Kantorovich problem is now solved in Polish generality: for a lower semicontinuous cost `c : X × Y → ℝ≥0∞`, two probability measures on Polish spaces admit an **optimal transport plan** ([`TauCeti.exists_isOptimalCoupling`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isOptimalCoupling)), by weak compactness of the coupling set ([`TauCeti.isCompact_setOfPred_isCoupling_of_polishSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Compactness.html#TauCeti.isCompact_setOfPred_isCoupling_of_polishSpace)) and lower semicontinuity of `π ↦ ∫⁻ c ∂π` ([`TauCeti.lowerSemicontinuous_lintegral_probabilityMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/LowerSemicontinuousLintegral.html#TauCeti.lowerSemicontinuous_lintegral_probabilityMeasure)). **Stability** follows: under a liminf hypothesis on the moving costs and a recovery-plan hypothesis, optimal values converge ([`TauCeti.tendsto_transportCost`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Stability.html#TauCeti.tendsto_transportCost)) and optimal plans converge subsequentially to an optimal plan of the limit ([`TauCeti.exists_isOptimalCoupling_tendsto`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Stability.html#TauCeti.exists_isOptimalCoupling_tendsto)). **Strong Kantorovich duality** holds for continuous nonnegative costs on compact pseudometric spaces ([`TauCeti.isLUB_kantorovichDualValue_continuous`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Compact.html#TauCeti.isLUB_kantorovichDualValue_continuous)) and, by monotone convergence, for lower semicontinuous costs on compact metrizable spaces ([`TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_continuous_of_lowerSemicontinuous`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/LowerSemicontinuous.html#TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_continuous_of_lowerSemicontinuous)); on finite types both problems are attained with equal value ([`TauCeti.exists_cost_eq_finiteDualValue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Finite/Duality.html#TauCeti.exists_cost_eq_finiteDualValue)).

Beneath these sit the coupling foundations: the raw coupling relation and bundled probability couplings, graph plans with the Monge-dominates-Kantorovich inequality ([`TauCeti.transportCost_le_lintegral_of_hasLaw`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Cost/Basic.html#TauCeti.transportCost_le_lintegral_of_hasLaw)), disintegration of plans, the **gluing lemma** ([`TauCeti.Measure.exists_glue_of_standardBorel_right`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Gluing.html#TauCeti.Measure.exists_glue_of_standardBorel_right)) with countable chains and a Kolmogorov extension theorem on countable index types ([`TauCeti.Measure.exists_isProjectiveLimit_of_countable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ProjectiveLimit/Countable.html#TauCeti.Measure.exists_isProjectiveLimit_of_countable)), finite multi-marginal couplings, and transportation matrices. The `c`-transform calculus comes with **Lusin's theorem** on universal measurability of analytic sets ([`TauCeti.MeasureTheory.AnalyticSet.nullMeasurableSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/MeasurableSpace/Analytic.html#TauCeti.MeasureTheory.AnalyticSet.nullMeasurableSet)), and complementary slackness is packaged as an optimality certificate ([`TauCeti.isDualCertificate_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Certificate.html#TauCeti.isDualCertificate_iff)) whose plans are concentrated on `c`-cyclically monotone sets.

The gaps are visible. Strong duality is compact-only: the Polish theorem for costs bounded below by `a ⊕ b`, dual attainment, and the Borel-cost regimes have not begun, so optimality forces contact-set concentration only under an assumed dual optimizer, and the Schachermayer–Teichmann equivalence exists only for finite spaces ([`TauCeti.TransportMatrix.isCyclicallyMonotone_toPMF_support_of_forall_cost_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Finite/CyclicalMonotonicity.html#TauCeti.TransportMatrix.isCyclicallyMonotone_toPMF_support_of_forall_cost_le)). Finite multi-marginal duality has weak duality and slackness but no attainment. Nothing from Layer 3 onward exists: there is no Wasserstein distance yet.

## OptimalTransport: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30 (`8745177` to `e440f4e`)

The **Wasserstein distance** arrived, and with it most of Layer 3. It is defined for every exponent `1 ≤ p ≤ ∞` as an infimum over couplings ([`TauCeti.wassersteinEDist`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Basic.html#TauCeti.wassersteinEDist)), satisfies the triangle inequality by gluing ([`TauCeti.wassersteinEDist_triangle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Basic.html#TauCeti.wassersteinEDist_triangle)), and makes the finite-moment space `P_p(X)` complete and separable over a Polish base. Its topology is pinned down: relatively compact sets are the tight ones with uniformly integrable moments ([`TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.isCompact_closure_iff_isTightMeasureSet_and_exists_setLIntegral_edist_rpow_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Compactness.html#TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.isCompact_closure_iff_isTightMeasureSet_and_exists_setLIntegral_edist_rpow_le)), and empirical measures of i.i.d. samples converge almost surely ([`TauCeti.tendsto_wassersteinEDist_empiricalMeasure_ae`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Empirical.html#TauCeti.tendsto_wassersteinEDist_empiricalMeasure_ae)). **Kantorovich–Rubinstein duality** writes `W_1` as a supremum over 1-Lipschitz test functions ([`TauCeti.wassersteinEDist_one_eq_iSup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/KantorovichRubinstein.html#TauCeti.wassersteinEDist_one_eq_iSup)), and on the line `W_p` is the `Lᵖ` distance of quantile functions ([`TauCeti.wassersteinEDist_eq_eLpNorm_quantile_sub`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Rearrangement.html#TauCeti.wassersteinEDist_eq_eLpNorm_quantile_sub)). The endpoint `W_∞` gets attainment, the limit `W_p → W_∞`, and counterexamples to separability and to density of finite support; pushforward, product, mixture, convolution and Markov-kernel estimates round out the layer.

Strong duality now reaches Polish spaces with an attained integrable dual optimum, but only for bounded continuous costs ([`TauCeti.isGreatest_kantorovichDualValue_integrable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Attainment.html#TauCeti.isGreatest_kantorovichDualValue_integrable)). The Monge problem opened: source atoms make it infeasible, graph plans are dense for atomless sources so Monge and Kantorovich values agree for bounded continuous costs, and the **twist theorem** ([`TauCeti.ae_subsingleton_setOf_mem_contactSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Twist/Basic.html#TauCeti.ae_subsingleton_setOf_mem_contactSet)) turns a certified plan into an a.e.-unique measurable map. Toward Brenier came Legendre–Fenchel conjugates, subdifferentials and **Rockafellar's theorem** ([`TauCeti.IsCyclicallyMonotone.exists_fenchelConjugate_subset_subdifferential`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/CTransform/Rockafellar.html#TauCeti.IsCyclicallyMonotone.exists_fenchelConjugate_subset_subdifferential)), matched to quadratic-cost cyclical monotonicity, plus the matrix geometric mean carrying one Gaussian to another.

Still missing: Polish duality for costs bounded below by `a ⊕ b`, the Borel-cost regimes, the Schachermayer–Teichmann converse, Pratelli's theorem for unbounded costs, and Brenier itself. Smaller pieces closed old gaps: finite multi-marginal dual attainment, `2 × 2` problems solved outright, barrier costs, and a finite entropy minimiser as a first step toward Sinkhorn.

## OptimalTransport: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02 (`e440f4e` to `d449639`)

**Brenier's theorem** landed in its strong form: if `μ` is absolutely continuous and the quadratic optimal cost is finite (as it is for finite second moments), the optimal plan is unique and is the graph of `∇u` for a lower semicontinuous convex `u`, finite and differentiable `μ`-a.e., and that map is unique a.e. ([`TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Brenier.html#TauCeti.exists_isKantorovichOptimalTransportMap_gradient)). Around it came the **Knott–Smith criterion** characterising quadratic optimality by concentration on a subdifferential graph ([`TauCeti.IsCoupling.isOptimalCoupling_iff_exists_ae_mem_subdifferential`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Brenier.html#TauCeti.IsCoupling.isOptimalCoupling_iff_exists_ae_mem_subdifferential)), the inverse map `∇u*` when the target is also absolutely continuous, and uniqueness of the potential up to a constant on a connected open set ([`IsOpen.exists_eq_add_of_gradient_ae_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SameDerivative.html#IsOpen.exists_eq_add_of_gradient_ae_eq)). The missing prerequisite was **Rademacher's theorem** for extended-real convex functions ([`TauCeti.ae_eventually_ne_top_and_differentiableAt_toReal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Differentiability.html#TauCeti.ae_eventually_ne_top_and_differentiableAt_toReal)), built on a bridge from effective domains to real-valued convexity.

Kantorovich duality now holds for every nonnegative lower semicontinuous cost on Polish spaces ([`TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_integrable_of_lowerSemicontinuous`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/LowerSemicontinuous.html#TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_integrable_of_lowerSemicontinuous)), as equality of values only; the `c ≥ a ⊕ b` version and dual attainment for unbounded costs remain open. The graph-plan theorem closes a Monge item: graph plans converge narrowly exactly when the maps converge in measure ([`TauCeti.tendsto_map_prodMk_self_iff_tendstoInMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/GraphPlan/Convergence.html#TauCeti.tendsto_map_prodMk_self_iff_tendstoInMeasure)), with a metric-valued Vitali theorem for the `Lᵖ` upgrade and Lusin's theorem along the way.

Two later layers opened. Fréchet barycenters exist, and form a compact set, for laws on a proper metric space with finite radius somewhere ([`TauCeti.exists_isFrechetBarycenter`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/FrechetMean.html#TauCeti.exists_isFrechetBarycenter)), with Chebyshev centers at `p = ∞`. Entropic transport and the static Schrödinger problem are defined, linked by the Gibbs reformulation ([`TauCeti.entropicTransportCost_eq_mul_schroedingerValue_add`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Entropic.html#TauCeti.entropicTransportCost_eq_mul_schroedingerValue_add)), but there is no existence of entropic minimisers yet.
