## OptimalTransport: 2026-08-08 to 2026-09-01 (`1a83584` to `8745177`)

The primal Kantorovich problem is now solved in Polish generality: for a lower semicontinuous cost `c : X × Y → ℝ≥0∞`, two probability measures on Polish spaces admit an **optimal transport plan** ([`TauCeti.exists_isOptimalCoupling`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isOptimalCoupling)), by weak compactness of the coupling set ([`TauCeti.isCompact_setOfPred_isCoupling_of_polishSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Compactness.html#TauCeti.isCompact_setOfPred_isCoupling_of_polishSpace)) and lower semicontinuity of `π ↦ ∫⁻ c ∂π` ([`TauCeti.lowerSemicontinuous_lintegral_probabilityMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/LowerSemicontinuousLintegral.html#TauCeti.lowerSemicontinuous_lintegral_probabilityMeasure)). **Stability** follows: under a liminf hypothesis on the moving costs and a recovery-plan hypothesis, optimal values converge ([`TauCeti.tendsto_transportCost`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Stability.html#TauCeti.tendsto_transportCost)) and optimal plans converge subsequentially to an optimal plan of the limit ([`TauCeti.exists_isOptimalCoupling_tendsto`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Stability.html#TauCeti.exists_isOptimalCoupling_tendsto)). **Strong Kantorovich duality** holds for continuous nonnegative costs on compact pseudometric spaces ([`TauCeti.isLUB_kantorovichDualValue_continuous`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Compact.html#TauCeti.isLUB_kantorovichDualValue_continuous)) and, by monotone convergence, for lower semicontinuous costs on compact metrizable spaces ([`TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_continuous_of_lowerSemicontinuous`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/LowerSemicontinuous.html#TauCeti.isLUB_ofReal_kantorovichDualValue_continuous_of_lowerSemicontinuous)); on finite types both problems are attained with equal value ([`TauCeti.exists_cost_eq_finiteDualValue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Finite/Duality.html#TauCeti.exists_cost_eq_finiteDualValue)).

Beneath these sit the coupling foundations: the raw coupling relation and bundled probability couplings, graph plans with the Monge-dominates-Kantorovich inequality ([`TauCeti.transportCost_le_lintegral_of_hasLaw`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Cost/Basic.html#TauCeti.transportCost_le_lintegral_of_hasLaw)), disintegration of plans, the **gluing lemma** ([`TauCeti.Measure.exists_glue_of_standardBorel_right`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Gluing.html#TauCeti.Measure.exists_glue_of_standardBorel_right)) with countable chains and a Kolmogorov extension theorem on countable index types ([`TauCeti.Measure.exists_isProjectiveLimit_of_countable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/ProjectiveLimit/Countable.html#TauCeti.Measure.exists_isProjectiveLimit_of_countable)), finite multi-marginal couplings, and transportation matrices. The `c`-transform calculus comes with **Lusin's theorem** on universal measurability of analytic sets ([`TauCeti.MeasureTheory.AnalyticSet.nullMeasurableSet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/MeasurableSpace/Analytic.html#TauCeti.MeasureTheory.AnalyticSet.nullMeasurableSet)), and complementary slackness is packaged as an optimality certificate ([`TauCeti.isDualCertificate_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Certificate.html#TauCeti.isDualCertificate_iff)) whose plans are concentrated on `c`-cyclically monotone sets.

The gaps are visible. Strong duality is compact-only: the Polish theorem for costs bounded below by `a ⊕ b`, dual attainment, and the Borel-cost regimes have not begun, so optimality forces contact-set concentration only under an assumed dual optimizer, and the Schachermayer–Teichmann equivalence exists only for finite spaces ([`TauCeti.TransportMatrix.isCyclicallyMonotone_toPMF_support_of_forall_cost_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Finite/CyclicalMonotonicity.html#TauCeti.TransportMatrix.isCyclicallyMonotone_toPMF_support_of_forall_cost_le)). Finite multi-marginal duality has weak duality and slackness but no attainment. Nothing from Layer 3 onward exists: there is no Wasserstein distance yet.
