Strong duality now reaches Polish spaces with an attained integrable dual optimum, but only for bounded continuous costs ([`TauCeti.isGreatest_kantorovichDualValue_integrable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Duality/Attainment.html#TauCeti.isGreatest_kantorovichDualValue_integrable)). Smaller pieces closed old gaps: finite multi-marginal dual attainment, `2 × 2` problems solved outright, barrier costs, and a finite entropy minimiser as a first step toward Sinkhorn.

Wasserstein geodesics are now known to be unique exactly when the two obvious obstructions are absent: on a complete separable metric space and for `1 < p < ∞`, if the optimal coupling is unique and almost every pair it matches is joined by only one geodesic, then any two geodesic segments of `P_p(X)` between the same laws agree ([`TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.eqOn_of_isGeodesicSegment_of_ae_subsingleton`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Geodesic/Unique.html#TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.eqOn_of_isGeodesicSegment_of_ae_subsingleton)). The proof runs through Lisini's superposition principle and the fact that a dynamic plan on unique geodesics is determined by its endpoint law. Combined with Brenier's theorem, it shows that every `W₂` geodesic out of an absolutely continuous law on a finite-dimensional inner product space is McCann's displacement interpolation ([`TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.isGeodesicSegment_iff_coe_eq_map_smul_add_smul_of_absolutelyContinuous`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Geodesic/Unique.html#TauCeti.WassersteinSpace.isGeodesicSegment_iff_coe_eq_map_smul_add_smul_of_absolutelyContinuous)) (TauCeti#21462).

On the topological side, weak convergence of probability measures on a Polish space is now characterised as convergence to zero of transport cost with the ground distance capped at any fixed level ([`TauCeti.tendsto_probabilityMeasure_iff_transportCost_truncated_dist`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/BoundedLipschitz.html#TauCeti.tendsto_probabilityMeasure_iff_transportCost_truncated_dist)), equivalently by uniform convergence against bounded `1`-Lipschitz tests, with a bound on the Lévy–Prokhorov distance. Separation of measures by `W_p` was extended to finite measures and to every exponent, including `p = ∞` ([`TauCeti.wassersteinEDist_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/OptimalTransport/Wasserstein/Basic.html#TauCeti.wassersteinEDist_eq_zero_iff)).

The rest was supporting work. The Monge–Ampère files gained bounds on subgradient images, including local boundedness of the subgradients of a convex function ([`TauCeti.exists_norm_le_of_mem_subdifferential`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SubgradientImage.html#TauCeti.exists_norm_le_of_mem_subdifferential)), and these support the Aleksandrov maximum principle and a mass comparison for ordered convex functions. Viscosity solutions are packaged as a structure. Only the Aleksandrov-to-viscosity direction is proved; the converse is still missing. Limits of minimizing movements are shown to be absolutely continuous with finite action, but they are not yet shown to converge to a gradient flow. Zero Gromov–Wasserstein distance is identified with weak isomorphism, provided an optimal coupling exists. Entropic duality, with potentials unique up to a constant, still covers only bounded costs.
