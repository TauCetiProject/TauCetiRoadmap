## AlgebraicCodingTheory: 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-26 (`66cca66` to `759eb3e`)

The [MacWilliams identity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/MacWilliams/Basic.html#Submodule.natCard_mul_weightEnumerator_euclideanDual) is proved for linear codes over every finite field in the division-free form `#C · W_{C⊥}(X, Y) = W_C(X + (q - 1)Y, X - Y)` in `ℤ[X, Y]`, through a [Poisson summation formula for codes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/CharacterSum.html#Submodule.sum_sum_addChar_dotProduct_smul) rather than theta series; the [Krawtchouk coefficient form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Krawtchouk.html#TauCeti.natCard_mul_weightDistribution_euclideanDual), the [rational normalization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/MacWilliams/Basic.html#Submodule.map_weightEnumerator_euclideanDual) and [self-dual invariance](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/MacWilliams/Basic.html#Submodule.aeval_inv_smul_weightEnumerator_of_eq_euclideanDual) follow from it. For binary codes it gives the classical fact that [a Type II code has length divisible by eight](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Binary/TypeII.html#TauCeti.BinaryCode.IsTypeII.eight_dvd_card). Beneath these sits the general code API, from matrix presentations and puncturing to Hermitian duals and semilinear automorphism groups.

All four named codes are built from their displayed matrices and checked by exact computation: [the tetracode](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Tetracode.html#TauCeti.tetracode); the hexacode, [Hermitian self-dual](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Hexacode/Basic.html#TauCeti.Hexacode.galoisDual_galoisFieldCode) over a field of four elements, whose Frobenius conjugate is a different code [carried back by a coordinate reordering](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Hexacode/Basic.html#TauCeti.Hexacode.map_code_omega_sq_conjugatePerm); [the extended ternary Golay code](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/TernaryGolay.html#TauCeti.TernaryGolay.code); and the [extended binary Golay code](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Binary/Golay/Basic.html#TauCeti.BinaryGolay.weightEnumerator_code), a doubly even self-dual `[24, 12, 8]` code with enumerator `X^24 + 759 X^16 Y^8 + 2576 X^12 Y^12 + 759 X^8 Y^16 + Y^24`, every puncture of which is [a `[23, 12, 7]` code](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Binary/Golay/Punctured.html#TauCeti.BinaryGolay.parameters_punctureAt_code).

Construction A lives in `ℚ^ι` with the dot product divided by `m`; it comes with the literal identity [`P_m(C)^∨ = P_m(C^⊥)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionA/Basic.html#TauCeti.ConstructionA.dualSubmodule_lattice), the [discriminant `m^n / #C²`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionA/Discriminant.html#TauCeti.ConstructionA.integralLattice_discriminant_mul_natCard_sq), the [evenness criteria](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionA/Even.html#TauCeti.ConstructionA.isEven_integralLattice_iff_isIsotropic), and the higher-modulus Type II theorem as an equivalence: [over `ℤ/2^r` a self-orthogonal code has an even unimodular lattice exactly when it is Type II](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionA/Even.html#TauCeti.ConstructionA.isEven_and_isUnimodular_integralLattice_iff_isTypeII). ## AlgebraicCodingTheory: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `0d3161a`)

This window closes the last gaps in Layer 2, so every layer of the roadmap now has its milestones proved. The Hamming support lemmas are in, for a general alphabet: [weight is the size of the support](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Hamming.html#TauCeti.hammingNorm_eq_ncard_support), [the distance from `x` to `y` is the weight of `y - x`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Hamming.html#TauCeti.hammingDist_eq_hammingNorm_sub') in the pinned orientation, and there is an exact [formula for the weight of a sum](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Hamming.html#TauCeti.hammingNorm_add_add_ncard_inter_add_ncard_sdiff) together with [coordinatewise scaling](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Hamming.html#TauCeti.hammingNorm_smul_eq_ncard_inter) (TauCeti#9220). The Frobenius of a field of order `p²` is now [an involution](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Finite/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.FiniteField.frobeniusEquiv_involutive) in its own right, and the Hermitian dual of a row space is [the kernel of the generator's entrywise `p`-th power](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/GaloisDual/Frobenius.html#RingEquiv.galoisDual_range_vecMulLinear_frobeniusEquiv). At `p = 2` this is Hermitian duality over `F₄`, and the hexacode's self-duality is now proved through it.

On the lattice side, the Golay Construction A lattice is now a named object, [even](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionA/Golay.html#TauCeti.BinaryGolay.isEven_constructionALattice) and [unimodular](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/ConstructionA/Golay.html#TauCeti.BinaryGolay.isUnimodular_constructionALattice), because the code is [Type II](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Binary/Golay/Basic.html#TauCeti.BinaryGolay.isTypeII_code). The tetracode, the ternary Golay code and the hexacode are now also [quadratic Lagrangians](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/RootDiscriminant.html#TauCeti.Hexacode.isLagrangian_codeInTypeD4CoordinatePowerDiscriminant) in the discriminant groups of orthogonal powers of `A₂` and `D₄`.

The rest of the window filled in weight-enumerator details. It adds [the enumerator of a coset of the parity-check code](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Elementary/WeightEnumerator.html#TauCeti.natCard_mul_weightEnumerator_setOf_sum_eq) for any sum over any finite additive alphabet, the [convolution of weight distributions under direct sum](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Weight/Enumerator.html#Submodule.weightDistribution_directSum), and MacWilliams checks for the zero, whole and single-parity-check codes. Two smaller pieces are a transport of codes to Mathlib's Hamming space, which shows that minimum distance is the metric separation there, and the `Y ↦ iY` invariance of a doubly even enumerator.

## AlgebraicCodingTheory: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02 (`0d3161a` to `d449639`)

With every layer already done, this window carried Layer 1's code operations over from linear codes to additive codes over any finite alphabet. Additive codes now have [puncturing](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Puncture.html#TauCeti.AdditiveCode.puncture), [shortening](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Puncture.html#TauCeti.AdditiveCode.shorten) and [direct sums](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Additive/DirectSum.html#AddSubgroup.directSum), with the same minimum-distance statements as linear codes and the same hypotheses: [shortening cannot decrease distance](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/MinimumDistance/Operations.html#TauCeti.AdditiveCode.hammingMinDist_le_hammingMinDist_shorten) unless the code collapses to zero, and the [distance of a direct sum](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/MinimumDistance/Operations.html#AddSubgroup.hammingMinDist_directSum) is the smaller of the two only when both summands are nonzero. Over a nondegenerate finite bilinear alphabet, the [dual of a punctured code is the shortened dual](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Additive/Duality.html#TauCeti.FiniteBilinearModule.orthogonalComplement_puncture), and a direct sum is [Lagrangian exactly when both summands are](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Additive/Duality.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.isLagrangian_directSum_iff). That is the form the discriminant-glue side of the roadmap uses.

The linear theory gained some counting results. A [rank-nullity formula](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Puncture.html#TauCeti.finrank_shorten_add_finrank_puncture_compl) says that shortening to `s` and puncturing to its complement have dimensions adding to `dim C`, and its additive analogue is a product of cardinalities. Single-coordinate rules say when deleting a coordinate lowers the dimension, and that it [lowers the minimum distance by exactly one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/MinimumDistance/Operations.html#TauCeti.hammingMinDist_punctureAt_add_one_eq) at a nonzero coordinate of a minimum-weight word, if the distance is at least two. On the matrix side, [every parity-check presentation can be cut down](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/ParityCheck/RedundantRows.html#TauCeti.LinearCode.IsParityCheckMatrix.exists_submatrix) to exactly `n - k` independent rows by deleting redundant ones. The product formula for weight enumerators of direct sums now [holds for concatenations of any two finite sets of words](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/InformationTheory/Coding/Weight/DirectSum.html#Set.weightEnumerator_sumElim), so it covers additive codes too.
