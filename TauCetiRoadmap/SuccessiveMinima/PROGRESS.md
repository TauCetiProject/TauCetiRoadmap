## SuccessiveMinima: 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-26 (`842d789` to `938acbb`)

The first piece of this roadmap is in: the [volume of a weighted cross-polytope](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/WeightedCrosspolytope.html#OrthonormalBasis.volume_weighted_crosspolytope) (TauCeti#21412). For an orthonormal basis `o`, any real basis `b` and positive weights `a_i`, the body `Σ a_i·|b.repr(x)_i| ≤ 1` has canonical Euclidean volume `(2^n/n!)·|det_o(b)|/∏ a_i`, including the empty case. This is target 2.1.1, stated over any finite index type rather than `Fin n`, and it is the volume computation in the lower half of Minkowski's second theorem. The other two ingredients of that proof, containment of the cross-polytope in the body and the covolume bound by a basis determinant, have not landed, and nor has the minima calculus of Layer 1 it relies on.

## SuccessiveMinima: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-09 (`938acbb` to `d92acc5`)

The end theorem is in: the lower half of Minkowski's second theorem, [`ZLattice.two_pow_div_factorial_mul_covolume_le_prod_successiveMin_mul_measureReal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GeometryOfNumbers/MinkowskiSecond.html#ZLattice.two_pow_div_factorial_mul_covolume_le_prod_successiveMin_mul_measureReal), which says that `(2^d/d!)·covolume(L,μ) ≤ λ₀⋯λ_{d−1}·μ(K)` for a symmetric convex body, a full lattice and any additive Haar measure (TauCeti#21796). It is sharp on both sides. Weighted cross-polytopes attain [equality in the lower bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GeometryOfNumbers/BoxMinima.html#ZLattice.prod_successiveMin_mul_measureReal_crosspolytope) and weighted boxes attain [equality in the upper one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GeometryOfNumbers/BoxMinima.html#ZLattice.prod_successiveMin_mul_measureReal_box), because the minima of both bodies are exactly their weights. The proof's ingredients also landed: a weighted cross-polytope inside the body, and the [covolume bounded by any lattice parallelepiped](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Covolume.html#ZLattice.covolume_le_abs_det).

The rest of the roadmap arrived alongside it. There is [Minkowski's linear forms theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GeometryOfNumbers/LinearForms.html#Matrix.exists_ne_zero_int_abs_sum_mul_le), with non-strict bounds; [Hadamard's inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Gram/Hadamard.html#OrthonormalBasis.norm_det_le_prod_norm) and its Gram-determinant forms over any `RCLike` field; and [Minkowski's first theorem on successive minima](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GeometryOfNumbers/MinkowskiSecond.html#ZLattice.successiveMin_pow_mul_measureReal_le_two_pow_mul_covolume), `λ₀^d·μ(K) ≤ 2^d·covolume`. The minima calculus is complete as well. The foundational [`MeasureTheory.successiveMin`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/GeometryOfNumbers/SuccessiveMinima.html#MeasureTheory.successiveMin) of Mathlib's proposal is built with that proposal's lemmas and identified with the body minima. On top of it come the rank thresholds, monotonicity in the body and the lattice, transport along linear maps, a [real basis attaining all minima](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/SuccessiveMinima.html#ZLattice.exists_basis_gauge_eq_successiveMin) and an [integral basis adapted to them](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/SuccessiveMinima.html#ZLattice.exists_basis_mem_flag_of_gauge_lt_successiveMin) (Cassels VIII, Lemma 2).

The supporting work covers volumes of convex sets with disjoint interiors, which give the translate lemmas of Layer 3, together with the roadmap's worked examples of box minima and a few counting bounds for discrete subgroups.
