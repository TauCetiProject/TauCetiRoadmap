## MorseNovikov: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`4142c09` to `273ea28`)

The first work on this roadmap starts at its two algebraic ends. On the torsion side, based Reidemeister torsion of a ℤ-graded chain complex of vector spaces is now defined as [`reidemeisterTorsion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion): the alternating product of determinants built from boundary bases and their lifts, set to zero when the complex is not acyclic or not bounded. It is [independent of the boundary bases and lifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion_eq_finprod), [nonzero exactly on bounded acyclic complexes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion_eq_zero_iff), and on a two-term complex it is a [power of the determinant of the differential](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion_eq_det_zpow), which matches the roadmap's convention that a differential a from degree 1 to degree 0 has torsion a⁻¹. A follow-up proves the [graded basis-change formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion_eq_mul_finprod_det), with the permutation-sign and rescaling rules as corollaries (TauCeti#18666, TauCeti#18886). The supporting linear algebra is a basis of the middle term of a short exact sequence assembled from a basis of the sub and lifts of a basis of the quotient, [`ofExactLift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Basis/Exact.html#Module.Basis.ofExactLift).

On the completion side, the [Novikov series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/Basic.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries) carrier exists for an arbitrary abelian exponent group and period homomorphism, with the roadmap's exact support condition, least periods on nonzero supports and the additive and module structure. The finiteness lemmas that convolution needs, including the uniform cutoff lemma, are proved, but there is no multiplication yet, so Nov is not yet a ring. Nothing geometric (one-forms, covers, trajectories, orbits) has started.

## MorseNovikov: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

The Novikov ring is now a complete topological ring. The period topology has the subgroups U_C as a neighbourhood basis, and Nov is [the completion of the group ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/Topology.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.completionPkg) for it, with finite truncations dense and multiplication [continuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/TopologicalRing.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.instIsTopologicalRing). For H=ℤ it is [identified with Laurent series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/LaurentSeries.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.toLaurentSeries), coefficient by coefficient, and over a field the period topology matches the X-adic one. The roadmap's functoriality is in place: the [pushforward ring map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/Functoriality.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.mapRingHom) along a coefficient map and a period-rescaling exponent map, which sums over fibres and is continuous, and [restriction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/Map.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.comap) along a map with finite kernel. Positive rescaling of N gives the same topological algebra, and reversing N corresponds to negating exponents. Several analytic tools also landed: geometric series in the positive ideal, [exp and log](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/ExpLog.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.expMulEquiv) between that ideal and its principal units over a ℚ-algebra, and convergent [filtered infinite products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/InfiniteProduct.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.multipliable_of_nonneg_finite_cutoffs), which the zeta function's product formula will use. A leading-term argument shows that Nov is [a domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/LeadingTerm.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.isDomain_of_fg) for finitely generated torsion-free H over a domain, for every period map, so ties in N are allowed. Uniform cutoff estimates for Novikov matrices prepare for completed chain complexes.

On the torsion side, [multiplicativity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion_eq_mul_of_shortExact) now holds for a short exact sequence of complexes with acyclic ends and compatible bases, with the explicit Koszul sign. Torsion also [commutes with scalar extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion.html#HomologicalComplex.map_reidemeisterTorsion) along any field homomorphism.

Still missing from N1 is the Hahn-series comparison through a splitting of the period kernel. In N4, the determinant line and the long-exact-sequence correction for non-acyclic complexes are still to come. Nothing geometric has started.

## MorseNovikov: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-08 (`e8ea0d1` to `d2083f0`)

The algebraic half of the dynamical zeta function is in place. The [orbit zeta function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/OrbitZeta/Basic.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.orbitZeta) is the exponential of the rational log series Σ ε/k·e^(kγ) over orbit classes, and when the signs of the iterates form a geometric sequence it equals an Euler product over primitive orbits, so its [coefficients are integral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/OrbitZeta/Basic.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.orbitZeta_eq_mapRingHom_eulerProduct). The sign rule sgn det(1−A^k) = (−1)^(i₊)·(−1)^((k+1)i₋) is [proved for real matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Charpoly/DetSign.html#Matrix.sign_det_one_sub_pow), which gives the [product formula for linearized return maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/OrbitZeta/ReturnMap.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.orbitZeta_sign_det_one_sub_pow_eq_eulerProduct). Under a finite cyclic cover the [norm of the zeta function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/OrbitZeta/Norm.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.algebraNorm_orbitZeta) is computed orbit by orbit, using coset bases, trace as index times restriction, and log Norm = Tr log. All of this takes the orbits as abstract data. No orbits of an actual flow feed into it yet, although [closed orbits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Dynamics/Flow/ClosedOrbit.html#Flow.ClosedOrbit) of a flow and their multiplicities are now defined.

Torsion gained the [square-minor formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion/SquareMinor.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion_eq_finprod_det_torsionMinor), direct sums, split exact sequences, the [mapping-cone formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion/MappingCone.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion_homotopyCofiber), torsion with homology bases, and [componentwise torsion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion/Componentwise.html#HomologicalComplex.componentwiseReidemeisterTorsion), which vanishes on exactly the non-acyclic field components. The long-exact-sequence correction is still missing. The total quotient ring of ℤ[H] is [a finite product of characteristic-zero fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/TotalQuotient.html#AddMonoidAlgebra.exists_ringEquiv_pi_field_of_isFractionRing). Layer 1 closed with the [Hahn-series comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/HahnSeries.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.hahnSeriesEquiv) through a period-kernel splitting and the irrational-period example.

The geometry has started. [Morse one-forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/OneForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsMorseOneForm) have finitely many zeros on a compact manifold, and their indices do not depend on the local potential. The [period homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Period.html#TauCeti.periodHom) is natural under pullback, and deck transformations of the period cover shift primitives by periods. Trajectory spaces and the Novikov complex have not begun.

## MorseNovikov: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-11 (`d2083f0` to `e06d672`)

The headline is the [Hopf trace formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ModuleCat/HopfTrace.html#ChainComplex.sum_range_trace_f_eq_sum_range_trace_homologyMap): for a chain endomorphism of a complex that is finite-dimensional through degree n, with zero differential into degree n, the alternating sum of its traces on the chains equals the alternating sum of its traces on homology. This is the algebraic step in the Lefschetz fixed-point formula of Layer 6. The geometric side has started too. The [fixed-point derivative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/FixedPoint.html#TauCeti.fixedPointDerivative) treats df_x at a fixed point as an endomorphism of T_xM. A fixed point is [nondegenerate exactly when det(1−df_x) ≠ 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/FixedPoint.html#TauCeti.isNondegenerateFixedPt_iff_det_id_sub_ne_zero), that determinant does not depend on the chart, and the derivative of an iterate is the corresponding power. The roadmap's `fixedPointDet` and `fixedPointSign`, and the Lefschetz formula itself, are not there yet.

For torsion, the long exact homology sequence of a short exact sequence of complexes is now [a single chain complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Interleave.html#CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.ShortExact.homologySequenceComplex), with the three homologies in degrees 3i+2, 3i+1 and 3i as the roadmap prescribes. It is [acyclic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Interleave.html#CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.ShortExact.acyclic_homologySequenceComplex), and with homology bases it has [nonzero torsion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion/HomologySequence.html#CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.ShortExact.reidemeisterTorsion_homologySequenceComplex_ne_zero). The multiplicativity formula that uses this correction term is still to come. The least period of a Novikov series is now a [valuation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/Valuation.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.periodValuation) whenever the group ring has no zero divisors. It extends to fractions, and an [estimate for inverses of high-order perturbations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/Valuation.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.fractionValuation_inv_sub_inv_le) comes with it. This is groundwork for the period-order topology, though it does not yet cover total quotients with torsion in the deck group.

On the geometric side, a closed one-form is [Morse exactly when it is transverse to the zero section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/OneForm/Linearization.html#TauCeti.isMorseOneForm_iff_surjective_sectionLinearization), and it has [finitely many zeros on a compact manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/OneForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsMorseOneForm.finite_zeroSet). Smaller additions include chart formulas for pullbacks of forms and the conjugate root pair of a real quadratic.
