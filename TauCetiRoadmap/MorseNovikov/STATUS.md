### Named results

- **The product formula and integrality of the orbit zeta function**: if the signs of the iterates of each orbit form a geometric sequence, the exponential of the rational orbit log series is an Euler product over primitive orbits, so its coefficients are integral ([`orbitZeta_eq_mapRingHom_eulerProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/OrbitZeta/Basic.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.orbitZeta_eq_mapRingHom_eulerProduct)). The real-matrix sign rule sgn det(1−A^k) = (−1)^(i₊)·(−1)^((k+1)i₋) ([`Matrix.sign_det_one_sub_pow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Charpoly/DetSign.html#Matrix.sign_det_one_sub_pow)) supplies that hypothesis for linearized return maps.
- **The norm of the zeta function under a finite cover**: under a finite-index subgroup, the norm of an orbit zeta function is again an orbit zeta function, built from the lifted orbits and their multiplicities ([`algebraNorm_orbitZeta`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/OrbitZeta/Norm.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.algebraNorm_orbitZeta)).
- **Multiplicativity of torsion**: for a short exact sequence of based complexes with acyclic ends, τ(C₂) = ε·τ(C₁)·τ(C₃), with an explicit Koszul sign ([`reidemeisterTorsion_eq_mul_of_shortExact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion/Basic.html#HomologicalComplex.reidemeisterTorsion_eq_mul_of_shortExact)).
- **Total quotients of abelian group rings**: for finitely generated abelian H, the total quotient ring of ℤ[H], torsion included, is a finite product of characteristic-zero fields ([`exists_ringEquiv_pi_field_of_isFractionRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/TotalQuotient.html#AddMonoidAlgebra.exists_ringEquiv_pi_field_of_isFractionRing)). For the Novikov ring, the total quotient is [Artinian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/TotalQuotient.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.isArtinianRing_of_isFractionRing).
- **The Hopf trace formula**: for a chain endomorphism of a complex that is finite-dimensional through degree n, with zero differential into degree n, the alternating traces on chains and on homology agree ([`sum_range_trace_f_eq_sum_range_trace_homologyMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ModuleCat/HopfTrace.html#ChainComplex.sum_range_trace_f_eq_sum_range_trace_homologyMap)). This is the homological half of the Lefschetz formula.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Componentwise torsion** ([`componentwiseReidemeisterTorsion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion/Componentwise.html#HomologicalComplex.componentwiseReidemeisterTorsion)) is defined over a reduced Artinian ring, one field component at a time, and is zero on exactly the non-acyclic components.
- **Morse one-forms and the period cover.** [`IsMorseOneForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/OneForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsMorseOneForm) has potential-independent indices and a quadratic normal form. It is equivalent to [transversality to the zero section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/OneForm/Linearization.html#TauCeti.isMorseOneForm_iff_surjective_sectionLinearization) and forces finitely many zeros on a compact manifold. Deck transformations of the period cover [shift primitives by periods](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Cover.html#TauCeti.PeriodCover.apply_deck_apply).
- **The fixed-point derivative** ([`fixedPointDerivative`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/FixedPoint.html#TauCeti.fixedPointDerivative)) treats df_x as an endomorphism of T_xM. Nondegeneracy is equivalent to det(1−df_x) ≠ 0, the determinant does not depend on the chart, and the derivative of an iterate is the corresponding power. This supplies the substance of the API for the roadmap's `fixedPointDet`.

## The frontier

- **Long-exact-sequence torsion (Layer 4)**: the interleaved homology sequence and its nonzero torsion are in place. What remains is multiplicativity for non-acyclic sequences, with that torsion as the correction term.
- **Period-order topology on total quotients (Layer 4)**: extend the period valuation to each field component of the total quotient, including the case where the deck group has torsion. Then prove continuity of inversion and combine it with the existing square-minor continuity to get cutoff continuity of torsion.
- **The Lefschetz fixed-point formula (Layer 6)**: the Hopf trace formula and the intrinsic det(1−df_x) are proved. What remains is `fixedPointDet` and `fixedPointSign`, the simplicial chain adapter, simplicial approximation with Hopf's modification, and the local fixed-point index of Layer 5.
- **Geometric orbit counting (Layer 5)**: apply the return germ to −α♯, build the local fixed-point index, and prove that only finitely many orbits lie below each cutoff.
- **Cutoff trajectory spaces (Layer 2)**: this needs stable and unstable manifolds of −α♯ and lifted moduli with a bounded drop in potential. It depends on HeegaardFloer Lane M and blocks all of Layer 3.
