- **The norm of the zeta function under a finite cover**: under a finite-index subgroup, the norm of an orbit zeta function is again an orbit zeta function, built from the lifted orbits and their multiplicities ([`algebraNorm_orbitZeta`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/OrbitZeta/Norm.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.algebraNorm_orbitZeta)).

- **Total quotients of abelian group rings**: for finitely generated abelian H, the total quotient ring of ℤ[H], torsion included, is a finite product of characteristic-zero fields ([`exists_ringEquiv_pi_field_of_isFractionRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/TotalQuotient.html#AddMonoidAlgebra.exists_ringEquiv_pi_field_of_isFractionRing)). ### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Componentwise torsion** ([`componentwiseReidemeisterTorsion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/ReidemeisterTorsion/Componentwise.html#HomologicalComplex.componentwiseReidemeisterTorsion)) is defined over a reduced Artinian ring, one field component at a time. It is zero on exactly the non-acyclic components, so partial acyclicity is kept. Torsion classes modulo signs and deck monomials sit on top of it. This is the target ring for both Novikov and cellular torsion.
- **Morse one-forms and the period cover.** [`IsMorseOneForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/OneForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsMorseOneForm) has potential-independent indices, finite zero sets and a quadratic normal form. The period cover is a regular cover on which the form becomes exact, and its deck transformations shift primitives by periods ([`PeriodCover.apply_deck_apply`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Cover.html#TauCeti.PeriodCover.apply_deck_apply)).
- **Finite-cover norms and traces.** Nov(H) is finite free over Nov(K) for a finite-index K, and the trace is the index times restriction. Over ℚ, the [logarithm of the norm is the trace of the logarithm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovSeries/Norm.html#TauCeti.NovikovSeries.log_normNonnegSubring). The cover formulas of Layers 5 and 6 rest on these.

## The frontier

- **Long-exact-sequence torsion (Layer 4)**: torsion with homology bases is defined and transforms correctly under a change of basis. What remains is multiplicativity for non-acyclic sequences, with its homology correction term.
- **Period-order topology on total quotients (Layer 4)**: put the order topology on each field component and prove continuity of inversion. Combined with the existing square-minor continuity, this gives the cutoff continuity of torsion.
- **Cutoff trajectory spaces (Layer 2)**: stable and unstable manifolds of −α♯, then lifted moduli with bounded potential drop. These depend on HeegaardFloer Lane M. They block the whole of Layer 3.
- **Geometric orbit counting (Layer 5)**: apply the return germ to −α♯, build the local fixed-point index, and prove that only finitely many orbits lie below each cutoff. The existing algebraic product formula can then be applied directly.
- **Poincaré–Hopf and Euler chains (Layer 7)**: the chart derivative is in place, so the next step is the signed zero-cycle, the index theorem Σ sgn det(dV) = χ(M), and the Euler-chain torsor.
