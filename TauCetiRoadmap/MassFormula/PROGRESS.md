## MassFormula: 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-21 (`c249a80` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap opened with everything below its summit except the integration. The family σ_K(n) of totally ramified degree-n subextensions of a separable closure is [`totallyRamifiedOfDegree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Basic.html#TauCeti.totallyRamifiedOfDegree), and it is now known to be [countable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Countable.html#TauCeti.countable_totallyRamifiedOfDegree) in every characteristic. It is also [infinite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Infinite.html#TauCeti.infinite_totallyRamifiedOfDegree) when K has characteristic p dividing n, through Serre's family X^n + π^m X + π, whose discriminant exponents are unbounded (TauCeti#19033). Serre's [orbit count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Orbit.html#TauCeti.ncard_isomorphic_mul_natCard_algEquiv) also landed: the isomorphism class of L inside σ_K(n), times #Aut_K(L), has n elements. The [wild exponent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Basic.html#IntermediateField.wildExponent) c(L) comes with its invariance under K-isomorphism and the [tame criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Basic.html#IntermediateField.wildExponent_eq_zero_iff), c(L) = 0 exactly when the residue characteristic does not divide n.

Both general layers are in. [Hensel's lemma with an estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Hensel.html#Polynomial.exists_isRoot_addVal_eval_eq) holds over any Henselian discrete valuation ring, which is more general than the complete case the roadmap asked for, and it yields the [local constancy of the root count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/RootCount.html#TauCeti.exists_card_aroots_eq) under small coefficient perturbations. On the measure side there is the [Haar scaling law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/LocalFieldScaling.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.addHaar_image_mulVec) μ(A·S) = q^{−k} μ(S), resting on the [index of an image lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FreeModule/CardQuotient.html#Matrix.card_quotient_range_mulVecLin_eq_pow) over a discrete valuation ring.

For the parametrization, the Eisenstein region has its expected [volume](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Eisenstein/Region.html#TauCeti.measure_eisensteinSet) q^{−n}(1 − q^{−1}), and at each separable Eisenstein polynomial the [root counts over σ_K(n) sum to n](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/RootCount.html#TauCeti.tsum_rootCount_eq_of_separable). Still missing are Serre's local fibre count, the integral of the root count, almost-everywhere separability and measurability, and with them both mass formulas.

## MassFormula: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`c1c6773` to `93d1fe5`)

The quadratic worked examples are now done, so the definitions of σ_K(n) and the wild exponent c have been tested against Serre's formula in the cases the roadmap uses as acceptance tests. In odd residue characteristic, σ_K(2) consists of exactly [two fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Quadratic/Basic.html#TauCeti.totallyRamifiedOfDegree_two_eq), K(√ϖ) and K(√(uϖ)) for a non-square unit u, and the [mass formula holds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Quadratic/Basic.html#TauCeti.tsum_one_div_natCard_residueField_pow_wildExponent_two) with sum 2; the ℚ_p case is a [corollary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Quadratic/Basic.html#TauCeti.Padic.tsum_one_div_pow_wildExponent_two). The harder wild case is also in. σ_{ℚ_2}(2) is [the six fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Quadratic/PadicTwo.html#TauCeti.totallyRamifiedOfDegree_two_eq_padicTwo) ℚ_2(√−1), ℚ_2(√−5), ℚ_2(√±2), ℚ_2(√±10), and [the sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Quadratic/PadicTwo.html#TauCeti.tsum_one_div_pow_wildExponent_two_padicTwo) 2·2⁻¹ + 4·2⁻² = 2 comes out right. That rules out an off-by-one in c (TauCeti#20206). Its inputs are the discriminant exponents of roots of Eisenstein quadratics, [min(2v(b), 2v(2)+1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Eisenstein/Basic.html#TauCeti.natCast_discriminantExponent_adjoin_eq_min_of_sq_add_mul_add_eq_zero). These examples are computed directly. They do not come from a general Theorem 1.

Two pieces of Layer 4 now wait only on Theorem 1. Theorem 2 is [equivalent to Theorem 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Representative.html#TauCeti.IsRepresentativeSet.tsum_one_div_natCard_algEquiv_mul_natCard_residueField_pow_wildExponent_eq_one_iff) for any [set of representatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Representative.html#TauCeti.IsRepresentativeSet), by regrouping along the orbit count. Outside equal characteristic p ∣ n, the [uniform bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Finite.html#IntermediateField.wildExponent_le_mul_natCastValuation) c(L) ≤ n·v_K(n) makes the weighted sum [finite exactly when σ_K(n) is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Finite.html#TauCeti.tsum_one_div_natCard_residueField_pow_wildExponent_ne_top_iff_finite). In the tame case that sum is [the cardinality of σ_K(n)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Finite.html#TauCeti.tsum_one_div_natCard_residueField_pow_wildExponent_eq_encard). A smaller addition partitions valuation spheres into finitely many balls, so that a root count becomes a finite sum of indicators, a step toward integrating the root count. Layer 3's fibre count, integral, almost-everywhere separability and measurability are still missing.
