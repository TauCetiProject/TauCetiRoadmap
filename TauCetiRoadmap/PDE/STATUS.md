### Named results

- **Existence and uniqueness for the Dirichlet problem** — on a bounded open set, `-∂ⱼ(aⁱʲ∂ᵢu) + b·∇u + cu = f` with measurable, uniformly elliptic `a`, bounded drift and `c ≥ 0` has exactly one weak solution in `H¹₀(Ω)` ([`existsUnique_isWeakSolutionDirichlet_of_mass_nonneg`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/MaximumPrinciple/Drift.html#TauCeti.PDE.UniformlyEllipticOn.existsUnique_isWeakSolutionDirichlet_of_mass_nonneg)).
- **De Giorgi's theorem** — weak solutions of divergence-form equations with merely measurable, uniformly elliptic coefficients are locally Hölder continuous in every dimension, with an exponent depending only on `λ`, `Λ` and `n` ([`exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/HolderContinuity.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative)). Moser's [Harnack inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/Harnack.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_ae_ae_value_le_mul_value) holds in the same generality.
- **The Mihlin-Hörmander multiplier theorem** — a symbol with `‖Dᵏm(ξ)‖ ≤ A‖ξ‖⁻ᵏ` for every `k ≤ N`, where `2N > n`, gives a Fourier multiplier bounded on `Lᵖ` for `1 < p < ∞` ([`exists_fourierMultiplierCLM_eq_and_norm_le_of_norm_iteratedFDeriv_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/MihlinHormander.html#TauCeti.exists_fourierMultiplierCLM_eq_and_norm_le_of_norm_iteratedFDeriv_le)).
- **The Calderón-Zygmund estimate** — `‖D²u‖_p ≤ C‖Δu‖_p` for `1 < p < ∞` ([`exists_eLpNorm_iteratedFDeriv_two_le_eLpNorm_laplacian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/Hessian/Lp.html#TauCeti.exists_eLpNorm_iteratedFDeriv_two_le_eLpNorm_laplacian)). It now holds on compact sets for non-divergence operators with [continuous coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/Hessian/ContinuousCoefficient.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_lpNorm_iteratedGradient_le_of_sum_mul_inner_eq_of_continuousAt).
- **Existence for linear parabolic equations** — in a Gelfand triple `V ↪ H ↪ V*`, the problem `u' + A(t)u = f`, `u(a) = u₀` has a weak solution whenever `A(t)` satisfies Gårding's inequality ([`exists_hasWeakLineDerivOn_gelfandDual`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_hasWeakLineDerivOn_gelfandDual)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`Wkp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Basic.html#TauCeti.Wkp) is `W^{k,p}(Ω)` built from weak derivatives and complete at every order, and it is [invariant under `Cᵏ` diffeomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/ChangeOfVariables.html#TauCeti.Wkp.compLEquiv).
- The [trace operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/Trace/Frontier.html#TauCeti.W1p.frontierTrace) of a bounded Lipschitz domain maps `W^{1,p}(Ω)` to `Lᵖ(∂Ω)` for `1 ≤ p < ∞`, and its [kernel is exactly `W^{1,p}_0(Ω)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/Trace/Frontier.html#TauCeti.W1p.ker_frontierTrace). This lets Dirichlet data be stated as a trace condition.
- [`ParabolicC2HolderSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Holder/Two.html#TauCeti.ParabolicC2HolderSpace) is `C_b^{2+α,1+α/2}` on a slab. It has interpolation, absorption, traces and products, and the [Cauchy heat potential](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Holder/CauchyPotential.html#TauCeti.heatCauchyPotentialL) maps into it. It carries the classical Schauder isomorphisms.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 1** has the boundary package on bounded Lipschitz domains (trace with its kernel, extension, Rellich, Poincaré-Wirtinger) and `C^{k,α}` on the whole space at every order. Not established: Meyers-Serrin beyond order one, Calderón's `W^{k,p} = H^{k,p}`, the `p = ∞` cases, Campanato spaces. **Layer 2** has the maximal function, Mihlin-Hörmander, the Calderón-Zygmund decomposition, John-Nirenberg and the sharp-function Fefferman-Stein inequality. It lacks Riesz-Thorin, Calderón-Zygmund operators, Gehring and VMO. **Layer 3** has harmonic-function theory, Green kernels and Perron's method, but not the general Hopf lemmas or the Aleksandrov-Bakelman-Pucci estimate. **Layer 4** has coercive existence, `H¹` boundary data, the mass-shift Fredholm alternative and the spectrum, but not `H⁻¹` liftings or the adjoint compatibility condition. **Layer 5** has the `Hᵏ` bootstrap, De Giorgi-Nash-Moser with drift and right-hand side, and interior `W^{2,p}` and `C^{2,α}` estimates on compact sets. It lacks Meyers' estimate, divergence-form `C^{1,β}`, Krylov-Safonov, VMO coefficients and all global estimates. **Layer 6** has weak parabolic existence, whole-space comparison, parabolic Hölder spaces of orders one and two, the heat potentials and the heat flow on `BUC`. The heat generator and the isomorphisms of F27.2 to F27.6 are not there. **Layers 7 to 9** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **The constant-coefficient parabolic isomorphism (F27.2).** Package the Duhamel potential as a bounded map into `C_b^{2+α,1+α/2}`. With the Cauchy potential, uniqueness and the reduction to the heat operator, show that `u ↦ (u_t − a₀D²u, u(·,0))` is an isomorphism onto `C_b^{α,α/2} × C_b^{2,α}`.
- **Variable parabolic coefficients (F27.3).** Localise the freezing estimate into a global a priori bound, then run the [method of continuity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/MethodOfContinuity.html#IsPreconnected.surjective_iff_of_forall_norm_le) from the heat operator. Backward, `BUC` and weighted versions follow. Needs the item above.
- **Liftings and the Fredholm compatibility condition (§4.2, §4.3).** Solve with `H⁻¹` forcing and `H¹` boundary data, state the boundary condition as a trace condition, and prove solvability exactly when the datum annihilates the adjoint kernel.
- **Global elliptic Schauder theory (§5.6).** Prove boundary and global estimates on `C^{2,α}` domains, then deduce `C^{2,α}` solvability of the Dirichlet problem by the method of continuity.
- **Interpolation and the BMO toolkit (§2.2 to §2.4).** Riesz-Thorin, Calderón-Zygmund operators with their weak `(1,1)` bound, Gehring's lemma and the commutator theorem feed Meyers' estimate and the VMO theory of Layer 5.
