## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The roadmap was rewritten into nine layers, and each section now targets what lies beyond the material the library already had. By that measure every layer is partial. The core of the Dirichlet theory is in place: the energy method with the Fredholm compatibility condition, De Giorgi-Nash-Moser, Harnack, Perron, and interior `W^{2,p}` and Schauder estimates. Missing are the Aleksandrov-Bakelman-Pucci and Krylov-Safonov track, boundary and global regularity, the parabolic Schauder isomorphisms, Besov spaces, Strichartz estimates and quasilinear existence.

### Named results

- **The Fredholm alternative with its compatibility condition**: on a bounded open set with bounded measurable coefficients and no sign condition on `c`, the Dirichlet problem with `H⁻¹` data is solvable exactly when the data annihilates the kernel of the adjoint problem ([`exists_forall_energyFormH1_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/FredholmAlternative.html#TauCeti.PDE.UniformlyEllipticOn.exists_forall_energyFormH1_eq_iff)).
- **De Giorgi's theorem**: weak solutions of divergence-form equations with merely measurable, uniformly elliptic coefficients are locally Hölder continuous ([`exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/HolderContinuity.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative)), with Moser's Harnack inequality beside it.
- **The Mihlin-Hörmander multiplier theorem**: a symbol with `‖Dᵏm(ξ)‖ ≤ A‖ξ‖⁻ᵏ` gives a multiplier bounded on `Lᵖ` for `1 < p < ∞` ([`exists_fourierMultiplierCLM_eq_and_norm_le_of_norm_iteratedFDeriv_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/MihlinHormander.html#TauCeti.exists_fourierMultiplierCLM_eq_and_norm_le_of_norm_iteratedFDeriv_le)). It yields the Calderón-Zygmund estimate `‖D²u‖_p ≤ C‖Δu‖_p`.
- **Interior `W^{2,p}` estimates for continuous coefficients**: strong solutions of non-divergence equations with coefficients continuous on a compact set obey the Calderón-Zygmund bound there ([`exists_lpNorm_iteratedGradient_le_of_sum_mul_inner_add_inner_add_mul_eq_of_continuousAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/Hessian/ContinuousCoefficient.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_lpNorm_iteratedGradient_le_of_sum_mul_inner_add_inner_add_mul_eq_of_continuousAt)). The interior Schauder estimate for Hölder coefficients is its [`C^{2,α}` counterpart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/Hessian/Schauder/HolderCoefficient.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_holderOnWith_iteratedFDeriv_two_of_sum_smul_fderiv_fderiv_of_holderOnWith).
- **Regular boundary points**: a bounded open set has a harmonic solution of the Dirichlet problem, continuous up to the boundary, for all continuous data exactly when every boundary point admits a barrier ([`forall_mem_frontier_exists_isBarrier_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Perron/Regular.html#TauCeti.forall_mem_frontier_exists_isBarrier_iff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [trace operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/Trace/Frontier.html#TauCeti.W1p.frontierTrace) `W^{1,p}(Ω) → Lᵖ(∂Ω)` of a bounded Lipschitz domain now has [kernel exactly `W^{1,p}_0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/Trace/Frontier.html#TauCeti.W1p.ker_frontierTrace) for finite `p`. Together with extension and Rellich, this lets boundary conditions be stated by traces.
- [`ParabolicC2HolderSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Holder/Two.html#TauCeti.ParabolicC2HolderSpace) is the Banach space `C_b^{2+α,1+α/2}` on a slab, with traces and interpolation. The [Cauchy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Holder/CauchyPotential.html#TauCeti.heatCauchyPotentialL) and [Duhamel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Holder/DuhamelPotential/Basic.html#TauCeti.heatDuhamelPotentialL) heat potentials map boundedly into it, and parabolic operators with Hölder coefficients act on it. It is the stage for the classical parabolic Schauder theory.
- The [method of continuity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/MethodOfContinuity.html#IsPreconnected.surjective_iff_of_forall_norm_le) turns a uniform a priori estimate along a path of operators into solvability. Neither the elliptic nor the parabolic Schauder theory applies it yet.

### Roadmap coverage

All nine layers are partial. **Layer 1** has interval representatives, Meyers-Serrin at every order, `H^{k,p} = W^{k,p}` as sets, and trace, extension and Rellich on Lipschitz domains. The `p = ∞` cases, Hölder and Campanato spaces on domains, and the norm equivalences are missing. **Layer 2** has Calderón-Zygmund theory, Mihlin-Hörmander, Riesz-Thorin and John-Nirenberg. It lacks the uncentred and dyadic maximal functions, Fefferman-Stein, off-diagonal Marcinkiewicz, Gehring, BMO, VMO and the commutator theorem. **Layer 3** is done except for the Aleksandrov-Bakelman-Pucci estimate, the variable-coefficient strong maximum principle and Hopf lemmas, higher-order harmonic estimates, and a few Green-function cases. **Layer 4** has the compatibility condition but lacks `H⁻¹` liftings and the ordered spectrum of `−Δ`. **Layer 5** has De Giorgi-Nash-Moser and the interior estimates. Section 5.5 (ABP, Krylov-Safonov) is untouched, and Meyers' theorem, variable-coefficient `C^{1,β}`, VMO and all boundary and global estimates are missing. **Layer 6** has the weak theory and the order-two parabolic Hölder spaces, but no isomorphism theorems, and §6.8 is untouched. **Layers 7–9** have only first pieces: Littlewood-Paley blocks, the Schrödinger dispersive estimate, and Schauder's fixed-point theorem.

## The frontier

- **The constant-coefficient heat isomorphism (§6.5)**: assemble the Cauchy and Duhamel potentials into the inverse of `u ↦ (Pu, u(·, 0))` on `C_b^{2+α,1+α/2}`. The homogeneous solution operator and both potentials already exist.
- **Variable-coefficient parabolic Schauder theory (§6.6–6.9)**: the global a priori estimate from freezing and absorption, then existence by the method of continuity. BUC traces, weights and the order-`k` spaces follow it.
- **Boundary Schauder and `C^{2,α}` solvability (§5.6–5.7)**: the boundary estimate, the Dirichlet problem on `C^{2,α}` domains by continuity, then VMO coefficients and the global `W^{2,p}` theorem on `C^{1,1}` domains.
- **Aleksandrov-Bakelman-Pucci and Krylov-Safonov (§3.2, §5.5)**: untouched. This needs the convex envelope, the contact-set lemma and the gradient-image measure bound.
- **Gehring, BMO and VMO (§2.4)**: these are prerequisites for Meyers' `W^{1,p}` theorem, which now has its sharpness example in place, and for the VMO estimates of Layer 5.
