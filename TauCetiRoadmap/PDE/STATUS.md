### Named results

- **Existence and uniqueness for the Dirichlet problem** — on a bounded open set, `-∂ⱼ(aⁱʲ∂ᵢu) + b·∇u + cu = f` with measurable, uniformly elliptic `a`, bounded drift and `c ≥ 0` has exactly one weak solution in `H¹₀(Ω)` ([`existsUnique_isWeakSolutionDirichlet_of_mass_nonneg`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/MaximumPrinciple/Drift.html#TauCeti.PDE.UniformlyEllipticOn.existsUnique_isWeakSolutionDirichlet_of_mass_nonneg)).
- **De Giorgi's theorem** — weak solutions of divergence-form equations with merely measurable, uniformly elliptic coefficients are locally Hölder continuous in every dimension, with an exponent depending only on `λ`, `Λ` and `n` ([`exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/HolderContinuity.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative)). There are also versions with a bounded drift and with an `Lʳ` right-hand side for `r > n/2`, and Moser's Harnack inequality.
- **The Mihlin-Hörmander multiplier theorem** — a symbol with `‖Dᵏm(ξ)‖ ≤ A‖ξ‖⁻ᵏ` for every `k ≤ N`, where `2N > n`, gives a Fourier multiplier bounded on `Lᵖ` for `1 < p < ∞` ([`exists_fourierMultiplierCLM_eq_and_norm_le_of_norm_iteratedFDeriv_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/MihlinHormander.html#TauCeti.exists_fourierMultiplierCLM_eq_and_norm_le_of_norm_iteratedFDeriv_le)).
- **The Calderón-Zygmund estimate** — `‖D²u‖_p ≤ C‖Δu‖_p` for `1 < p < ∞` ([`exists_eLpNorm_iteratedFDeriv_two_le_eLpNorm_laplacian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/Hessian/Lp.html#TauCeti.exists_eLpNorm_iteratedFDeriv_two_le_eLpNorm_laplacian)). It extends to constant coefficients and to `W^{2,p}(ℝⁿ)` solutions whose coefficients have small oscillation.
- **Existence for linear parabolic equations** — in a Gelfand triple `V ↪ H ↪ V*`, the problem `u' + A(t)u = f`, `u(a) = u₀` has a weak solution whenever `A(t)` satisfies Gårding's inequality ([`exists_hasWeakLineDerivOn_gelfandDual`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_hasWeakLineDerivOn_gelfandDual)). Together with the energy estimate, this gives the well-posed parabolic Dirichlet problem.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`Wkp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Basic.html#TauCeti.Wkp) is `W^{k,p}(Ω)` built from weak derivatives and complete at every order. Meyers-Serrin and agreement with the Bessel-potential scale `H^{k,2}(ℝⁿ)` tie it to Mathlib, and it is the common ground for the energy method, spectral theory and regularity.
- [Lipschitz domains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/Basic.html#Set.IsLipschitzDomain) are open sets that are locally the region above a Lipschitz graph after a rigid motion. They come with finite chart covers and partitions of unity, which carry half-space results to curved boundaries: [extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/GlobalExtension.html#TauCeti.W1p.exists_extensionL_of_isLipschitzDomainWith), [Rellich-Kondrachov](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/RellichKondrachov.html#TauCeti.W1p.isCompactOperator_valueL_of_isLipschitzDomainWith) and the zero-trace characterisation of `H¹₀`.
- [`EvolutionSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/EvolutionSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.EvolutionSpace) is `L²(V) ∩ H¹(V*)` with its graph norm. Its elements have continuous time traces and an energy identity, the setting for parabolic existence and uniqueness.

## The frontier

- **`W^{2,p}` estimates for continuous coefficients** — patch the small-oscillation estimate together with a partition of unity into an interior estimate on domains, then handle VMO coefficients and the boundary.
- **Schauder `C^{2,α}` estimates for Hölder coefficients** — localize the global small-oscillation estimate by cutoffs and covering, then deduce classical solvability of the Dirichlet problem in `C^{2,α}`.
- **The trace operator on Lipschitz domains** — glue the local chart traces into one map into `Lᵖ(∂Ω)` with surface measure, for general `p`. The chart area formula is already in place, so this is assembly work.
- **Bounded `C_b^{k,α}(ℝⁿ)` at every order** — the spaces exist through `k = 2`. Lane F's trace spaces need the general order with the iterated-derivative norm the README specifies.
- **Whole-space parabolic Schauder theory (F27)** — parabolic Hölder spaces, heat potentials, variable coefficients, BUC traces and weights. Not started; it depends on the item above.
