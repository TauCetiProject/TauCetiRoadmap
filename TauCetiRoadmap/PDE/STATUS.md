## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 2, 3 and 7 are done, and with the mixed and Neumann spectra in place Layer 4 lacks only the pure Neumann problem beyond one dimension. The other layers are partial with named gaps; the largest is global Schauder theory on `C^{2,α}` domains, which Layers 5 and 9 both wait on.

### Named results

- **The endpoint Strichartz estimate of Keel and Tao** — in dimension `n ≥ 3`, `‖e^{itΔ}f‖_{L²_t L^{2n/(n−2)}_x} ≲ ‖f‖₂`, with dual and retarded forms, now the `q = 2` case of the general Schrödinger estimate `exists_eLpNorm_eLpNorm_schrodingerPropagator_le` and derived from an [abstract endpoint theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Strichartz/Endpoint.html#TauCeti.exists_enorm_integral_adjoint_le_of_endpoint) for dispersive operator families.
- **De Giorgi's theorem** — weak solutions of divergence-form equations with measurable, uniformly elliptic coefficients are locally Hölder continuous in every dimension ([`exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/HolderContinuity.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative)).
- **The Krylov-Safonov Harnack inequality** — nonnegative `W^{2,n}` strong solutions of `aⁱʲ∂ᵢⱼu = f` with bounded measurable, uniformly elliptic coefficients satisfy `sup u ≤ C(inf u + r‖f‖_n)` on half the ball ([`exists_preciseRepresentative_le_mul_add_of_sum_mul_iteratedGradient_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/KrylovSafonov/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_preciseRepresentative_le_mul_add_of_sum_mul_iteratedGradient_eq)).
- **The parabolic Cauchy problem with variable coefficients** — for coefficients in `C_b^{α,α/2}`, `u ↦ (Pu, u(·,0))` is an isomorphism from `C_b^{2+α,1+α/2}` onto `C_b^{α,α/2} × C_b^{2,α}` ([`isInvertible_euclideanParabolicOperatorL_prod_spatialTraceL`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Holder/CauchyProblem.html#TauCeti.ParabolicC2HolderSpace.isInvertible_euclideanParabolicOperatorL_prod_spatialTraceL)).
- **The Courant-Fischer min-max principle for mixed eigenvalues** — for a symmetric energy form, coercive on `H¹_Γ(Ω)` after a shift, on a domain with the Rellich property, the min-max values `λₖ(Γ)` carry an [orthonormal eigenbasis of `L²(Ω)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Spectrum/Mixed/MinMax.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_hilbertBasis_mixedEigenvalue) and are exactly the spectrum ([`mixedEigenvalue_eq_of_hilbertBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Spectrum/Mixed/MinMax.html#TauCeti.PDE.mixedEigenvalue_eq_of_hilbertBasis)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [Admissible boundary parts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/BoundaryPart.html#Set.IsAdmissibleBoundaryPart.mem_w1pVanishingOnSubmodule_iff_frontierTraceOfIsLipschitzDomain_ae_eq_zero) carry the trace characterization `W^{1,p}_Γ = {u : Tu = 0 on Γ}` on Lipschitz domains, so a mixed problem can state its Dirichlet condition on the trace, as the classical theory does.

## The frontier

- **Global Schauder theory**: the global `C^{2,α}` estimate on a `C^{2,α}` domain and solvability by the method of continuity. Flattening charts, `C^{2,α}` regularity up to a flat boundary and the Hessian's mean oscillation at a flat boundary are in place; Layers 5 and 9 wait on it.
- **The pure Neumann problem in every dimension** (§4.5): solvability exactly when `∫f + ∫h = 0`, and uniqueness up to constants. The Fredholm alternative for every boundary part and the Neumann kernel are both available.
- **Parabolic F27.5-F27.6**: the order-`k` variable-coefficient isomorphism with its estimate, then the weighted theory at those orders.
- **Below `p = 2`**: the VMO gradient estimates on domains and the Hessian estimates for `1 < p < 2`; the whole-space gradient bound already covers that range.
- **Lipschitz shears** (§1.8): Sobolev composition with the shear `(x, t) ↦ (x, t − γ(x))` for Lipschitz `γ`.
