## PDE: 2026-06-09 to 2026-08-03 (`4eba9f5` to `11ef09d`)

The weak maximum principle arrived for the full second-order operator. A continuous, twice-differentiable subharmonic function on a compact set is bounded there by its frontier values ([`le_of_laplacian_nonneg_le_frontier`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Laplacian/WeakMaximumPrinciple.html#TauCeti.le_of_laplacian_nonneg_le_frontier)), and the same conclusion now holds for `Δ + b·∇` with drift bounded on the interior, and for `-Δ - b·∇ + c` once `c ≥ 0` ([`le_of_mul_le_laplacian_add_fderiv_le_frontier`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Laplacian/LowerOrderMaximumPrinciple.html#TauCeti.le_of_mul_le_laplacian_add_fderiv_le_frontier), TauCeti#1093, TauCeti#1111), each with a comparison principle and Dirichlet uniqueness beside it. The proofs are the classical ones: an `ε‖·‖²` perturbation for the bare Laplacian, an exponential barrier once there is drift. That the sign condition on `c` is load-bearing is now also a theorem, via `sin` on `[0, π]`, which solves `-Δu = -u`, vanishes on the frontier and is positive inside.

In the plane the theory goes further. Harnack's inequality holds in its sharp centred form for a nonnegative harmonic function on a disk ([`harnack_inequality_center`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Harnack/Planar.html#TauCeti.harnack_inequality_center)), and with it the strong maximum principle: harmonic on a preconnected planar set with an interior maximum forces constancy ([`eqOn_const_of_harmonicOnNhd_of_isMaxOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Harnack/StrongPrinciple.html#TauCeti.eqOn_const_of_harmonicOnNhd_of_isMaxOn)). The logarithmic Newtonian kernel is harmonic away from its pole and has outward flux `-1` through every circle around it. All of this is two-dimensional, resting on Mathlib's complex harmonic theory, and the distributional identity `-ΔG = δ` is not among it.

The other half of the window built the divergence-form energy form: a bundled pointwise jet form ([`energyIntegrand`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/EnergyForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.PDE.energyIntegrand)) with its symmetry, linearity and boundedness API, a pointwise Gårding bound, and integrated `L²` versions for constant and variable coefficients. These live on square-integrable value-gradient jets rather than on `W^{1,2}(Ω)`, which is not yet defined, so the existence theorem the Lax-Milgram wrapper (TauCeti#517) is waiting for cannot yet be stated.

## PDE: 2026-08-03 to 2026-09-07 (`11ef09d` to `bc61e7b`)

The Dirichlet problem now has an end-to-end existence theorem. For a divergence-form operator whose energy form is bounded and coercive on `H¹₀(Ω)`, there is exactly one weak solution of `L u = f` in `Ω`, `u = 0` on `∂Ω` ([`existsUnique_isWeakSolutionDirichlet`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/DirichletProblem.html#TauCeti.PDE.existsUnique_isWeakSolutionDirichlet), TauCeti#4188), with the energy estimate `‖u‖_{H¹} ≲ ‖f‖_{L²}` beside it. Coercivity is supplied concretely by Gårding's inequality on `H¹(Ω)` ([`garding_energyFormH1_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/EnergyForm/Sobolev.html#TauCeti.PDE.UniformlyEllipticOn.garding_energyFormH1_self)) together with a Poincaré inequality, so the Poisson problem `-Δu = f` on any domain contained in a ball is unconditionally solvable ([`existsUnique_isWeakSolutionDirichlet_laplacian_of_subset_ball`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/DirichletProblem.html#TauCeti.PDE.existsUnique_isWeakSolutionDirichlet_laplacian_of_subset_ball)), one of the roadmap's acceptance criteria.

The spectral theory followed. Rellich-Kondrachov, in the form that the value map `W^{1,p}_0(Ω) → Lᵖ(Ω)` is compact for bounded `Ω` ([`isCompactOperator_valueL`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/RellichKondrachov.html#TauCeti.W1p0.isCompactOperator_valueL)), makes the solution operator compact; from there come the Fredholm alternative for `L u - κu = f`, positivity and discreteness of the Dirichlet spectrum, the Rayleigh principle identifying the first eigenvalue as the optimal Poincaré constant, and an orthonormal basis of `L²(Ω)` of Dirichlet eigenfunctions ([`exists_hilbertBasis_forall_isDirichletEigenvalue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Spectrum.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_hilbertBasis_forall_isDirichletEigenvalue), TauCeti#5670).

All of this rests on the Sobolev spaces built in the same window: weak Fréchet derivatives with their uniqueness and Leibniz rule, `W^{k,p}(Ω)` as an iterated graph space, complete at every order ([`Wkp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Basic.html#TauCeti.Wkp)), `W^{k,p}_0` as the test-function closure, Poincaré, the Gagliardo-Nirenberg-Sobolev embedding, and extension by zero as an isometry. Alongside, the Hardy-Littlewood maximal inequality landed in both its weak `(1,1)` and strong `(p,p)` forms, and the Newtonian kernel was built in every dimension. Meyers-Serrin density, the trace operator and `-ΔG = δ` did not arrive, and Rellich is proved for the zero-boundary space only.

## PDE: 2026-09-07 to 2026-09-28 (`bc61e7b` to `8339b5a`)

Harmonic-function theory left the plane. The mean-value property now holds in every dimension, over spheres and over balls ([`setAverage_ball_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Harmonic/MeanValue.html#InnerProductSpace.HarmonicOnNhd.setAverage_ball_eq), TauCeti#8183), and it carries the strong maximum principle with it: a harmonic function on a preconnected open set that attains its maximum at an interior point is constant there ([`eqOn_const_of_harmonicOnNhd_of_isMaxOn_of_isOpen`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Laplacian/StrongMaximumPrinciple.html#TauCeti.eqOn_const_of_harmonicOnNhd_of_isMaxOn_of_isOpen)), with the subharmonic, comparison and up-to-the-boundary forms beside it. Harnack's inequality followed in the same generality, comparing the values of a nonnegative harmonic function on a compact subset of a preconnected open set ([`harnack_inequality`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Harnack/Basic.html#IsCompact.harnack_inequality)). All three had been two-dimensional, resting on Mathlib's complex harmonic theory.

On the weak side the maximum principle reached divergence-form operators with bounded measurable coefficients: for `-div(a∇u) + b·∇u + cu` with `c ≥ 0`, a weak subsolution whose excess `(u - k)⁺` lies in `W^{1,2}_0(Ω)` is at most `k` almost everywhere ([`value_le_const_of_small_drift_of_poincare`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/MaximumPrinciple/Weak.html#TauCeti.PDE.UniformlyEllipticOn.value_le_const_of_small_drift_of_poincare), TauCeti#9065), but only when the drift is small against the Poincaré constant, `βP < λ`. The [Caccioppoli inequality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Caccioppoli/Basic.html#TauCeti.PDE.UniformlyEllipticOn.setIntegral_sq_mul_norm_gradient_sq_le) arrived for weak solutions and for truncations of subsolutions, together with the [fast-geometric iteration lemma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecificLimits/FastGeometric.html#TauCeti.tendsto_atTop_zero_of_le_mul_pow_mul_rpow) that drives De Giorgi's scheme; the regularity theorem itself did not land.

Supporting this, `W^{1,p}(Ω)` gained the calculus those truncation arguments need: composition with Lipschitz `C¹` functions, and positive parts and truncations above a level, each with weak gradient `1_{u>k}∇u`. Morrey's inequality became a continuous linear [embedding of `W^{1,p}(ℝⁿ)` into a Hölder Banach space](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/HolderEmbedding.html#TauCeti.W1p.morreyEmbedding) for `p > n`, on top of new [`C^{k,α}` Banach spaces](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Holder/Two.html#TauCeti.C2HolderSpace) through second order. Also landing: Poincaré-Wirtinger on bounded convex domains, Rellich compactness on relatively compact subdomains, Marcinkiewicz interpolation between two finite exponents, and the Green's function of a disk with the Poisson kernel as its normal derivative, still only in the plane.

## PDE: 2026-09-28 to 2026-09-30 (`8339b5a` to `0d3161a`)

The first half of De Giorgi-Nash-Moser landed: [local boundedness of weak subsolutions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/LocalBoundedness.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_ae_value_le_mul_rpow_mul_sqrt_setIntegral) (TauCeti#9933). For `-div(a∇u)` with `a` measurable and uniformly elliptic, a weak subsolution is bounded above on a half ball by a constant times the `L²` norm of its positive part on the full ball. The constant depends only on the ellipticity constants and a Sobolev inequality on `W^{1,2}_0(Ω)`, which Gagliardo-Nirenberg-Sobolev supplies once `n ≥ 3`. Lower-order terms are not covered. [De Giorgi's isoperimetric inequality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Wirtinger/DeGiorgi.html#TauCeti.W1p.sub_mul_measureReal_mul_measureReal_le_of_eq_ball), which bounds how fast a Sobolev function can pass between two levels on a ball, also arrived. It is the level-set input for oscillation decay, but Hölder continuity itself has not been proved.

Potential theory now works in every dimension. The [Green's function of the unit ball](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/GreenFunction/Ball.html#TauCeti.ballGreenKernel) is built by reflecting the pole through the sphere. It vanishes on the boundary, and for `n ≥ 3` its [distributional Laplacian is `-δ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/GreenFunction/DistributionalLaplacian.html#TauCeti.integral_laplacian_mul_ballGreenKernel). The Poisson kernel is its [normal derivative](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/GreenFunction/Ball.html#TauCeti.fderiv_ballGreenKernel_normal), and the [half-space Green kernel](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/GreenFunction/HalfSpace.html#TauCeti.halfSpaceGreenKernel) comes with its own Poisson kernel. Only part of the proof that the Poisson integral takes the right boundary values is there: the mass away from a boundary point is shown to vanish. [Hopf's boundary-point lemma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Laplacian/HopfLemma.html#TauCeti.fderiv_pos_of_mul_le_laplacian_add_fderiv_of_lt_ball_of_le_sphere) holds on balls for `-Δ - b·∇ + c` with `c ≥ 0`, and so does the [strong maximum principle](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Laplacian/StrongMaximumPrinciple.html#TauCeti.eqOn_const_of_mul_le_laplacian_add_fderiv_of_isMaxOn) for the same operator.

On the Sobolev side, translation is an isometry of `W^{k,p}(ℝⁿ)` at every order. [Mollification converges to the identity there](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/ApproximateIdentity.html#TauCeti.Wkp.tendsto_normedBumpL), so a compactly supported function lies in the closure of the test functions. The `C^{0,α}` spaces became a normed algebra under pointwise multiplication, and on a domain of finite measure `W^{1,q}(Ω)` now includes into `W^{1,p}(Ω)` whenever `p ≤ q`.

## PDE: 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-05 (`d449639` to `1d09589`)

De Giorgi's theorem landed. Take a weak solution `u ∈ H¹(Ω)` of `-div(a∇u) = 0`, where `a` is measurable and uniformly elliptic and nothing more. Then [`exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Regularity/HolderContinuity.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_holderOnWith_preciseRepresentative) says its precise representative is Hölder continuous on every set whose `R`-thickening lies in `Ω`. The exponent depends only on `λ`, `Λ` and the dimension, and the constant is `C R^(-α-n/2) ‖u‖_{L²}`. The representative is the limit of averages over shrinking balls, and it is a new general [construction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/PreciseRepresentative.html#TauCeti.MeasureTheory.preciseRepresentative) that turns power-law oscillation decay into a Hölder bound. The theorem still needs `n ≥ 3`, covers only the homogeneous equation, and admits no lower-order terms.

The Dirichlet problem is now solved on every ball in every dimension. For [continuous data on the sphere](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/PoissonIntegral/Ball.html#TauCeti.exists_harmonicOnNhd_ball_continuousOn_closedBall_eqOn_sphere) there is a function harmonic inside the ball, continuous up to the boundary and equal to the data there. It is given by the [Poisson integral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/PoissonIntegral/Ball.html#TauCeti.ballPoissonIntegral), which is harmonic because its kernel is harmonic in the pole, and which takes the right boundary values because the kernel has mass one. The [converse of the mean-value property](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Harmonic/MeanValue/Converse.html#TauCeti.harmonicOnNhd_iff_continuousOn_setAverage_ball_eq) also arrived, with maximum principles for sub-mean-value functions. These are the ingredients Perron's method needs, but Perron's method itself has not landed.

On the Sobolev side, [Meyers-Serrin](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/MeyersSerrin.html#TauCeti.W1p.dense_contDiffOn_representatives) now holds on `W^{1,p}(Ω)` for an arbitrary open `Ω`, though only at first order. On the whole space, `W^{k,p}_0 = W^{k,p}` at every order. The first link to the Bessel-potential scale also landed: weak derivatives coincide with tempered-distributional ones, so a function in `H^{1,2}(ℝⁿ)` [lies in `W^{1,2}(ℝⁿ)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/BesselPotential.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.exists_w1p_value_eq_of_memSobolev_one). The reverse inclusion is still missing.

## PDE: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07 (`1d09589` to `3b48739`)

Perron's method landed. For a bounded open `Ω` and boundary data bounded on its frontier, the [Perron solution](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Perron/Basic.html#TauCeti.perronSolution), the supremum of all subharmonic functions that lie below the data on the boundary, is harmonic in `Ω` ([`harmonicOnNhd_perronSolution`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Perron/Basic.html#TauCeti.harmonicOnNhd_perronSolution), TauCeti#11908). No regularity of the boundary and no continuity of the data is assumed. The other half of the classical story, that the Perron solution attains continuous boundary values at points admitting a barrier, has not arrived, so this does not yet solve the Dirichlet problem on any domain beyond the ball.

The proof brought its own tools, all in every dimension. [Subharmonic functions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Harmonic/Subharmonic.html#TauCeti.SubharmonicOn) are defined by a continuous local sub-mean-value inequality, closed under maxima, and satisfy the [comparison principle](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Harmonic/Subharmonic.html#TauCeti.SubharmonicOn.le_of_le_frontier) against harmonic functions on bounded open sets. [Harnack's convergence theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Harnack/Convergence.html#TauCeti.tendstoLocallyUniformlyOn_iSup_of_monotone) says that an increasing family of harmonic functions on a preconnected open set, bounded at one point, converges locally uniformly, and [locally uniform limits of harmonic functions are harmonic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Harmonic/Convergence.html#TauCeti.harmonicOnNhd_of_tendstoLocallyUniformlyOn).

The rest of the window reorganised the Sobolev spaces rather than adding theorems. [`Wkp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Wkp/Basic.html#TauCeti.Wkp) is now built by iterating one generic weak-derivative graph step, and it agrees with the first-order API at order one. Convergence in it is characterised componentwise, and on the whole space translation is shown to preserve the norm and to vary continuously at every order.

## PDE: 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-13 (`3b48739` to `c7ae9d7`)

The weak maximum principle for the heat equation landed. On a cylinder `[0, T] × K` over a compact `K`, a subsolution of `∂ₜu ≤ Δu + b·∇u` that is continuous on the closed cylinder is bounded everywhere by any bound it satisfies on the parabolic boundary, the bottom and the lateral sides ([`le_of_deriv_le_laplacian_add_fderiv_le_parabolicBoundary`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/MaximumPrinciple/Parabolic.html#TauCeti.le_of_deriv_le_laplacian_add_fderiv_le_parabolicBoundary), TauCeti#12641). No bound on the drift is needed. On bounded Lipschitz domains, `W^{k,p}` now has [higher-order Sobolev embeddings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/Wkp/Embedding.html#TauCeti.Wkp.exists_sobolevEmbeddingL_of_isLipschitzDomainWith), Morrey embeddings, [Rellich-Kondrachov at every order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/LipschitzDomain/Wkp/RellichKondrachov.html#TauCeti.Wkp.isCompactOperator_lowerOrderL_of_isLipschitzDomainWith) and density of functions smooth up to the boundary. The energy method added [continuous dependence on the coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/DirichletProblem/ContinuousDependence.html#TauCeti.PDE.tendsto_of_isWeakSolutionDirichlet) and an [enumeration of the Dirichlet eigenvalues](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/Spectrum/Sequence.html#TauCeti.PDE.exists_hilbertBasis_nat_isDirichletEigenvalue_monotone) as `0 < λ₀ ≤ λ₁ ≤ ⋯ → ∞`.
