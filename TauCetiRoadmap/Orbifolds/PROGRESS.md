## Orbifolds: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`b2c8dd5` to `273ea28`)

This first window laid finite-group groundwork for the local theory rather than any orbifold construction itself. The headline is a generic-stabilizer theorem for finite linear actions: for a finite group acting linearly and continuously on, for instance, a finite-dimensional real vector space, the points whose stabilizer is exactly the kernel of the action are dense ([`dense_setOf_stabilizer_eq_ker`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/GroupAction/GenericStabilizer.html#TauCeti.dense_setOf_stabilizer_eq_ker)), with the faithful case giving a dense free locus ([`dense_freeLocus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/GroupAction/GenericStabilizer.html#TauCeti.dense_freeLocus)). This is the linear calculation behind the roadmap's dense principal stratum; the statement on an orbifold chart is not yet there. Alongside it, summing a form over its conjugates now gives an invariant positive-definite inner product ([`posDef_toQuadraticMap_sumOfConjugatesForm_inner`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/InvariantForm/InnerProduct.html#Representation.posDef_toQuadraticMap_sumOfConjugatesForm_inner)), whose sublevel sets are invariant open balls fitting inside any neighbourhood of the origin, as the invariant-ball step of the local linear chart needs.

On the diffeology side, the quotient diffeology arrived together with coinduced diffeologies and subductions: a plot of a quotient is exactly a map that locally lifts ([`isPlot_quotient_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeology/Quotient.html#TauCeti.Diffeology.isPlot_quotient_iff)), a function out of it is smooth exactly when its lift is, and its D-topology is the quotient topology ([`isQuotientMap_dTopology_quotient_mk`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeology/Quotient.html#TauCeti.Diffeology.isQuotientMap_dTopology_quotient_mk)). These are the generic laws the coarse orbifold diffeology will be built from. The manifold diffeology, chart gluing and Morita independence are still to come.

Finally, the action groupoid got its basic dictionary (TauCeti#18639): stabilizers are automorphism groups, keeping elements that act trivially ([`stabilizerMulEquivAut`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/ActionCategory.html#CategoryTheory.ActionCategory.stabilizerMulEquivAut)), orbits are isomorphism classes, and arrows are pairs of a label and a source. The roadmap's sanity check that `[*/G]` with `G` nontrivial is not equivalent to a point is also proved.

## Orbifolds: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

The local theory of finite smooth actions moved from linear algebra onto manifolds. The headline is finite Bochner linearization on a manifold ([`exists_equivariant_chart_of_contMDiffAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/GroupAction/GermKernel.html#TauCeti.exists_equivariant_chart_of_contMDiffAt)): a finite group fixing a point by maps that are `C¹` there acts linearly in some chart, through its derivative representation. That chart can then be shrunk to an invariant ball for an averaged inner product ([`exists_equivariant_chart_target_eq_sumOfConjugatesInnerSublevel_of_contMDiffAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/GroupAction/GermKernel.html#TauCeti.exists_equivariant_chart_target_eq_sumOfConjugatesInnerSublevel_of_contMDiffAt)), on which the derivative representation detects exactly the elements acting trivially. This is the analytic content of the O2.1 local model, still for a group acting on a manifold rather than for a proper étale groupoid, which does not yet exist here.

Around it came the ineffective kernel in the same setting. The [germ kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/GroupAction/GermKernel.html#TauCeti.MulAction.germKernel) at a point collects the elements acting as the identity nearby, and an identity principle for finite-order `C¹` maps ([`isClosed_interior_fixedBy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/GroupAction/GermKernel.html#TauCeti.isClosed_interior_fixedBy)) makes it locally constant and, on a connected manifold, equal to the kernel of the action. The principal part is dense: points whose stabilizer is the kernel are dense for a finite `C¹` action on a connected manifold ([`dense_setOf_stabilizer_eq_ker_of_contMDiff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/GroupAction/GermKernel.html#TauCeti.dense_setOf_stabilizer_eq_ker_of_contMDiff)). The effective quotient presentation is not attempted.

Two further pieces serve later layers. When the group order is invertible, the identity on inhomogeneous cochains is homotopic to averaging onto invariants in degree zero ([`averagingHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/GroupCohomology/Averaging.html#TauCeti.groupCohomology.averagingHomotopy)), so every positive-degree cocycle has an explicit primitive. This is the finite-group contraction O2.4 asks for. And for the classifying-space topology, attaching along closed subspaces and sequential colimits along closed embeddings preserve normality, `T1` and the Lindelöf property ([`normalSpace_of_isPushout`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Category/TopCat/Normal.html#TauCeti.TopCat.normalSpace_of_isPushout)), using a Tietze extension with prescribed values (TauCeti#19604). Paracompactness of the realization does not follow yet.
