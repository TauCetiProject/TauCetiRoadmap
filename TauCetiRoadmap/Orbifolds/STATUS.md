## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The local theory of Layer 2 has its central results on proper étale groupoids: retained-arrow linear charts, effective reduction with its kernel, and cutoff contractions. Effective reduction (§2.3) is complete apart from its face statement. Layers 1, 4 and 5 have foundations only. Atlases (§2.2), triangulations (Layer 3) and the laws of Layer 6 have not begun.

### Named results

- **The retained-arrow linear chart**: near any object of a Hausdorff proper étale groupoid, the groupoid is its finite isotropy group acting linearly on an invariant ball, through a fully faithful chart that keeps every arrow ([`exists_linear_ball_isotropy_chart`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Etale/Linearization.html#TauCeti.exists_linear_ball_isotropy_chart)).
- **Effective reduction is again a proper étale groupoid**: the [effective quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Effective/Basic.html#TauCeti.EffectiveQuotient) has the same coarse space ([`isomorphismClassesHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Effective/Basic.html#TauCeti.EffectiveQuotient.isomorphismClassesHomeomorph)) and proper `(s, t)` ([`isProperMap_source_target`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Effective/Topology.html#TauCeti.EffectiveQuotient.isProperMap_source_target)). In the smooth case its arrows form a Hausdorff manifold with étale structure maps ([`t2Space_arrows`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Etale/Effective.html#TauCeti.EffectiveQuotient.t2Space_arrows)).
- **Local constancy of the kernel order**: in a proper étale Lie groupoid the order of the ineffective kernel is locally constant, being the degree of a covering by ineffective arrows ([`isLocallyConstant_card_ineffectiveKernel`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Etale/IneffectiveKernel.html#TauCeti.isLocallyConstant_card_ineffectiveKernel)).
- **Density of the principal stratum**: the objects whose whole isotropy group acts ineffectively form an open dense set ([`dense_setOf_ineffectiveKernel_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Effective/Principal.html#TauCeti.dense_setOf_ineffectiveKernel_eq_top)).
- **The cutoff contraction of a nerve**: a cutoff function makes the identity on nerve cochains homotopic to averaging onto arrow-invariant functions ([`cutoffHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Nerve/Cutoff.html#TauCeti.nerveCochainComplex.cutoffHomotopy)), and proper étale groupoids have smooth cutoffs ([`exists_isCutoff_contMDiff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Groupoid/Cutoff.html#TauCeti.Quiver.exists_isCutoff_contMDiff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The fat classifying space** ([`fatClassifyingSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Nerve.html#TauCeti.fatClassifyingSpace)): the fat realization of the topological nerve, with its coarse map. Over a saturated open set it is the classifying space of the restricted groupoid ([`fatClassifyingSpaceRestrictionHomeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/CoarseSpace/Restrict.html#CategoryTheory.ObjectProperty.fatClassifyingSpaceRestrictionHomeomorph)). This is §4.3's primary model.
- **Invariant forms** ([`invariantForms`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Category/InvariantForm.html#TauCeti.Quiver.invariantForms)): forms with `s*ω = t*ω`, closed under `d`, wedge and pullback along smooth functors. These are the carrier for §4.2 and §4.5.
- **The manifold diffeology** ([`manifoldDiffeology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Diffeology/Manifold.html#TauCeti.manifoldDiffeology)): with the quotient diffeology it gives the coarse diffeology local finite-quotient charts.

## The frontier

- **The retained-arrow atlas (§2.2)**: atlas data with overlap-arrow torsors, the presenting groupoid, and its Morita comparison with the original presentation, built on the existing linear charts.
- **Proper étale presentations and generalized maps (§§1.1–1.2)**: bundle the hypotheses that the chart and effective-reduction theorems carry into one class closed under smooth equivalences, then add bibundles and their coarse maps.
- **Paracompact classifying spaces (§4.9a)**: combine the clopen latching loci with colimit paracompactness, which needs the skeletal pushout description of the fat realization.
- **The collared corner carrier (§1.6)**: collars, quadrant charts and NDR data on the corner domains. The face-kernel equality of §2.3 then follows from its chart lemma.
- **Gluing the coarse diffeology (§2.6)**: glue the local quotient charts, then prove Morita independence and functoriality.
