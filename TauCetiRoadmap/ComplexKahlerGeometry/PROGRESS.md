## ComplexKahlerGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (`e475758` to `273ea28`)

The foundational analytic step of the roadmap's K3.0 milestone landed in this window. **Osgood's lemma** is now proved: a function of finitely many complex variables that is continuous and holomorphic in each variable separately is jointly analytic, both at a point ([`ContinuousOn.analyticAt_of_differentiableAt_update`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CauchyIntegralPolydisc.html#ContinuousOn.analyticAt_of_differentiableAt_update)) and on open sets, with the resulting characterisation of analyticity on open subsets of ℂⁿ ([`TauCeti.analyticOnNhd_iff_continuousOn_and_differentiableAt_update`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CauchyIntegralPolydisc.html#TauCeti.analyticOnNhd_iff_continuousOn_and_differentiableAt_update)). The proof goes through a version of the polydisc Cauchy integral formula for separately holomorphic functions (TauCeti#18983).

The manifold consequences followed in TauCeti#19067. On a boundaryless finite-dimensional complex model, the `C^m` groupoids agree for all nonzero `m`. So a complex manifold of class `C^1` is automatically of class `C^ω`, and with the same charts it is a real-analytic manifold modelled on the underlying real space ([`IsManifold.restrictScalars_of_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Analytic.html#IsManifold.restrictScalars_of_ne_zero)). This realified atlas is the smooth structure that the roadmap's real differential geometry of complex manifolds is meant to sit on. The same work shows that a map between complex manifolds is holomorphic exactly when it is `C^n` for some (equivalently every) nonzero `n` ([`TauCeti.contMDiff_iff_mdifferentiable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Analytic.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_iff_mdifferentiable)), which is the comparison the roadmap's conventions rely on when they take holomorphic to mean `MDifferentiable ℂ`.

## ComplexKahlerGeometry: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

The one-variable meromorphic germ field from K3.MeromorphicGermField landed in this window. The germs at a point of ℂ of functions meromorphic there, taken on punctured neighbourhoods, form a [field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Germ.html#TauCeti.meromorphicGerms.instField). That field is [the fraction field of the ring of holomorphic germs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/MeromorphicGerm.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.isFractionRing_meromorphicGerms) at the point, and every meromorphic germ is [a holomorphic germ divided by a power of `z - a`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/MeromorphicGerm.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.exists_eq_toMeromorphicGerms_div) (TauCeti#20252). The same work compares stalk germs with ordinary function germs. What the target still asks for is the pullback along local biholomorphisms, the Laurent principal-part comparison and regular evaluation.

K1 started with its complex scalar action on smooth forms. Smooth forms with complex coefficients are now a [complex module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/MapCoeff.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.instModule), built from coefficient maps, and the exterior derivative and pullback are [complex-linear](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/MapCoeff.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.mextDerivₗ_smul). The paired wedge is [linear over the pairing's scalars](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Wedge/MapCoeff.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.wedgeWith_bilinearRestrictScalars_smul_left) in each factor. Coefficient conjugation is [conjugate-linear](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/MapCoeff.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.mapCoeff_conj_smul) and multiplicative for the wedge product. This is the carrier the roadmap's `(p,q)` calculus is meant to sit on. That calculus itself has not begun.

On the K7.1 side, projective space `ℙ 𝕜 (Fin (n+1) → 𝕜)` over any nontrivially normed field now has its standard affine charts and is [an analytic manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/Projectivization.html#Projectivization.instIsManifold) with them. Over ℂ this gives ℂPⁿ on the roadmap's chosen carrier. Connectedness, the comparison with the complex orientation and the biholomorphism with the Riemann sphere are not part of this work.

## ComplexKahlerGeometry: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-06 (`93d1fe5` to `385ec2d`)

The Dolbeault calculus arrived in this window. On any almost complex manifold, complex-valued smooth forms now split into [types `(p,q)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/AlmostComplexType.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.typeProjection) compatible with wedge, pullback and conjugation, and the operators ∂ and [∂̄](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/Basic.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.delBar) are defined. The [Nijenhuis tensor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Nijenhuis.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.nijenhuis) comes with the theorem that it vanishes exactly when [`T^(1,0)` is involutive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Eigenbundle.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.isInvolutiveComplexDistribution_eigenspace_I_iff), and with the roadmap's pinned formula [`∂̄²f(v,w) = -∂f(N(v,w))/4`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/Basic.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.delBar_delBar_apply). On a complex manifold the [Nijenhuis tensor vanishes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/AlmostComplexStructure.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.nijenhuis_ofComplexManifold), so [`d = ∂ + ∂̄`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/ComplexManifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.mextDerivₗ_eq_del_add_delBar_ofComplexManifold) and [`∂̄² = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/ComplexManifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.delBar_delBar_ofComplexManifold), and [the ∂̄-closed functions are exactly the holomorphic ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/Holomorphic.html#TauCeti.kerDelBarZeroEquiv). These operators now form the [Dolbeault complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/Complex.html#TauCeti.dolbeaultComplex) of `(p,•)`-forms in `ModuleCat ℂ`, with [cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/Complex.html#TauCeti.dolbeaultCohomology) `H^(p,q)_∂̄` ([TauCeti#25294](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25294)), and `dᶜ = i(∂̄ − ∂)` satisfies `ddᶜ = 2i∂∂̄` ([TauCeti#26148](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/26148)). The Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma is not here yet. Its analytic input has begun: [the Cauchy transform solves `∂̄u = f`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Pompeiu/Basic.html#HasCompactSupport.conjWirtingerDeriv_integral_sub_inv_smul_apply_one), with [local solutions depending smoothly and holomorphically on parameters](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/Potential.html#TauCeti.exists_contDiffOn_conjWirtingerDeriv_apply_one_eventually_eq_with_holomorphic_param), and without parameters on [discs of any radius and on ℂ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/DolbeaultLemma.html#TauCeti.exists_contDiffOn_conjWirtingerDeriv_apply_one_eq_of_eball) ([TauCeti#27339](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27339)). The solver on general plane domains with parameters is still to come.

The Hodge star now exists [on forms of an oriented inner product space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HodgeStar/AlternatingForm.html#Orientation.formHodgeStar) and [on Riemannian manifolds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/HodgeStar.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.hodgeStar_hodgeStar). Its complex extension, so far only pointwise, satisfies [the `(p,q)` type law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HodgeStar/Complex.html#Orientation.complexHodgeStar_mem_typeSubmodule_iff), and [the codifferential is the formal adjoint of `d`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Codifferential.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.integralTopForm_inner_mextDeriv_eq_inner_codifferential). The [Hodge Laplacian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Codifferential.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.hodgeLaplacian) `d*d + dd*` follows, with Green's formulas and formal self-adjointness ([TauCeti#27227](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27227)). Separately, [complex de Rham cohomology is the complexification of the real one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/ComplexCoefficients.html#TauCeti.complexDeRhamCohomology.complexificationEquiv).

In several variables, [Weierstrass preparation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Weierstrass.html#AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation) and division are proved by the contour and Newton-identity route, with uniqueness in the holomorphic stalk ([TauCeti#27243](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27243)). A holomorphic stalk modulo a coordinate is the stalk of the hyperplane ([TauCeti#25889](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25889)). A regular level set of a holomorphic map is an [embedded complex submanifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Level.html#TauCeti.isSmoothEmbedding_coe_regularLevel) ([TauCeti#27582](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27582)), and the canonical complex orientation is natural under local biholomorphisms and compatible with products ([TauCeti#21146](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/21146), [TauCeti#25506](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25506)).

The one-variable meromorphic germ field gained its [principal-part splitting](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/MeromorphicGerm.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.evalInvSub_principalPart_add_toMeromorphicGerms_regularPart), [Laurent coefficients as contour integrals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Residue/PrincipalPart.html#TauCeti.meromorphicGerms.coeff_principalPart_succ_eq_circleIntegral), [regular evaluation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/RegularEval.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.regularEval) and [pullback along local biholomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Pullback.html#OpenPartialHomeomorph.meromorphicGermsEquiv). Elsewhere: the [joint holomorphic implicit function theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/ImplicitFunction.html#TauCeti.exists_jointImplicitFunction_of_eventually_differentiableAt), [connectedness of projective space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Projectivization.html#Projectivization.instPathConnectedSpace), and an [analytic identification of `OnePoint 𝕜` with the projective line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/OnePoint/Projectivization.html#OnePoint.diffeomorphProjectivization).
