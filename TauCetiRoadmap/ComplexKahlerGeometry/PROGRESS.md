## ComplexKahlerGeometry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (`e475758` to `273ea28`)

The foundational analytic step of the roadmap's K3.0 milestone landed in this window. **Osgood's lemma** is now proved: a function of finitely many complex variables that is continuous and holomorphic in each variable separately is jointly analytic, both at a point ([`ContinuousOn.analyticAt_of_differentiableAt_update`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CauchyIntegralPolydisc.html#ContinuousOn.analyticAt_of_differentiableAt_update)) and on open sets, with the resulting characterisation of analyticity on open subsets of ℂⁿ ([`TauCeti.analyticOnNhd_iff_continuousOn_and_differentiableAt_update`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CauchyIntegralPolydisc.html#TauCeti.analyticOnNhd_iff_continuousOn_and_differentiableAt_update)). The proof goes through a version of the polydisc Cauchy integral formula for separately holomorphic functions (TauCeti#18983).

The manifold consequences followed in TauCeti#19067. On a boundaryless finite-dimensional complex model, the `C^m` groupoids agree for all nonzero `m`. So a complex manifold of class `C^1` is automatically of class `C^ω`, and with the same charts it is a real-analytic manifold modelled on the underlying real space ([`IsManifold.restrictScalars_of_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Analytic.html#IsManifold.restrictScalars_of_ne_zero)). This realified atlas is the smooth structure that the roadmap's real differential geometry of complex manifolds is meant to sit on. The same work shows that a map between complex manifolds is holomorphic exactly when it is `C^n` for some (equivalently every) nonzero `n` ([`TauCeti.contMDiff_iff_mdifferentiable`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Analytic.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_iff_mdifferentiable)), which is the comparison the roadmap's conventions rely on when they take holomorphic to mean `MDifferentiable ℂ`.

## ComplexKahlerGeometry: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

The one-variable meromorphic germ field from K3.MeromorphicGermField landed in this window. The germs at a point of ℂ of functions meromorphic there, taken on punctured neighbourhoods, form a [field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Germ.html#TauCeti.meromorphicGerms.instField). That field is [the fraction field of the ring of holomorphic germs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/MeromorphicGerm.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.isFractionRing_meromorphicGerms) at the point, and every meromorphic germ is [a holomorphic germ divided by a power of `z - a`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/MeromorphicGerm.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.exists_eq_toMeromorphicGerms_div) (TauCeti#20252). The same work compares stalk germs with ordinary function germs. What the target still asks for is the pullback along local biholomorphisms, the Laurent principal-part comparison and regular evaluation.

K1 started with its complex scalar action on smooth forms. Smooth forms with complex coefficients are now a [complex module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/MapCoeff.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.instModule), built from coefficient maps, and the exterior derivative and pullback are [complex-linear](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/MapCoeff.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.mextDerivₗ_smul). The paired wedge is [linear over the pairing's scalars](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Wedge/MapCoeff.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.wedgeWith_bilinearRestrictScalars_smul_left) in each factor. Coefficient conjugation is [conjugate-linear](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/MapCoeff.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.mapCoeff_conj_smul) and multiplicative for the wedge product. This is the carrier the roadmap's `(p,q)` calculus is meant to sit on. That calculus itself has not begun.

On the K7.1 side, projective space `ℙ 𝕜 (Fin (n+1) → 𝕜)` over any nontrivially normed field now has its standard affine charts and is [an analytic manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/Projectivization.html#Projectivization.instIsManifold) with them. Over ℂ this gives ℂPⁿ on the roadmap's chosen carrier. Connectedness, the comparison with the complex orientation and the biholomorphism with the Riemann sphere are not part of this work.
