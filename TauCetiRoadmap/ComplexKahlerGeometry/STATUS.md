## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The several-variable foundations are done. Hartogs extension across points and discrete sets is proved, and holomorphic stalks are regular local rings of the right dimension. Every layer is now partial: the Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma covers only `(0,q)`-forms on smaller polydiscs, the real Hodge theorem holds only in degrees zero and top, and Layer 6 has only its Kähler condition.

### Named results

- **Hartogs' extension theorem across a discrete set** — on a complex manifold of dimension at least two, a function holomorphic off a closed discrete set extends holomorphically, without a boundedness hypothesis ([`exists_mdifferentiableOn_eqOn_sdiff_of_isDiscrete`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Hartogs.html#TauCeti.exists_mdifferentiableOn_eqOn_sdiff_of_isDiscrete)).
- **Holomorphic stalks are regular local rings** — the ring of holomorphic germs at a point of an n-dimensional complex manifold is Noetherian of Krull dimension n, with maximal ideal generated by n elements ([`stalkIsRegularLocalRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/Regular.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.stalkIsRegularLocalRing)).
- **The Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma on polydiscs, for `(0,q)`-forms** — a smooth ∂̄-closed `(0,q+1)`-form on a polydisc in ℂⁿ is ∂̄-exact on every smaller concentric polydisc ([`exists_delBar_eq_of_polydisc`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/LocalExactness.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.IsOfType.exists_delBar_eq_of_polydisc)).
- **Weierstrass preparation and division** — near a point where `w ↦ f(x₀, w)` has order d, f is a unit times a monic Weierstrass polynomial of degree d, and every germ divides by f with a remainder of degree below d ([`AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Weierstrass.html#AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation)).
- **The real Hodge theorem in degrees zero and top** — on a compact oriented Riemannian manifold, harmonic representatives are always [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Harmonic.html#TauCeti.harmonicForms.toDeRhamCohomology_injective). In degrees zero and top they also exist ([`toDeRhamCohomology_bijective_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Harmonic.html#TauCeti.harmonicForms.toDeRhamCohomology_bijective_top)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dolbeault cohomology** ([`dolbeaultCohomology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/Complex.html#TauCeti.dolbeaultCohomology)), with holomorphic pullback, a wedge product and `H^(p,0)` identified with holomorphic p-forms. ∂̄² = 0 and `d = ∂ + ∂̄` hold on complex manifolds, so this is the carrier the Dolbeault theorem and Hodge decomposition will land in.
- **The Hodge star, Hodge Laplacian and harmonic forms** ([`harmonicForms`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Harmonic.html#TauCeti.harmonicForms)). The Laplacian now has its principal symbol computed, which is the Laplace-type input the elliptic estimates need.
- **The line bundles 𝒪(k) on projective space** ([`twistCore`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Projectivization/LineBundle.html#Projectivization.twistCore)), analytic, with 𝒪(−1) tautological and homogeneous polynomials as global sections. This is the base for Fubini–Study and for `H⁰(𝒪(k))`.

### Roadmap coverage

Layer 1 has the type calculus, the Nijenhuis tensor, smooth eigenbundles and holomorphic submersions. It lacks parametrized submanifolds, the normal sequences and Newlander–Nirenberg. Layer 2 has the ∂̄ calculus, Dolbeault cohomology, the `(0,q)` polydisc lemma and the ℂP¹ check. It lacks the finite-rank Runge operator (only simple-fraction approximation is in), the parameter solver, the whole-polydisc `(p,q)` lemma and the Dolbeault theorem. Layer 3 has K3.0, Weierstrass theory and the meromorphic germ field. From 3.4 it has regularity and dimension of stalks, and from 3.5 Hartogs across points and discrete sets. Factoriality, local parametrization, the Nullstellensatz, coherence, singular loci, Riemann extension and codimension-two Hartogs are missing. Layer 4 has only the ring-sheaf restriction. Layer 5 has the Hodge star, codifferential, Laplacian and the Hodge theorem's uniqueness half plus degrees zero and top. Of the Sobolev scale it has only Schwartz multipliers on `H^s`; it has no elliptic estimates and no K5b. Layer 6 has the real-carrier Kähler condition, the fact that Kähler forms are symplectic, and flat space. Layer 7 has projective charts, 𝒪(k), the additive cohomology and Euler characteristic of ℂPⁿ, and finiteness for maps of compact Riemann surfaces. It lacks Fubini–Study, the cup ring, tori, genus and the Hopf surface. Layer 8 has only the Iwasawa manifold.

## The frontier

- **Factorial holomorphic stalks**: regularity and Krull dimension n are done. Factoriality should follow from Weierstrass preparation, and it opens the hypersurface Nullstellensatz.
- **The whole-polydisc Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma**: the finite-rank Runge operator and parameter solver are needed to reach the whole polydisc and `(p,q)`-forms.
- **Riemann and codimension-two Hartogs extension**: the point and discrete cases are done. The general statements need 3.4's local parametrization and singular locus.
- **Sections of 𝒪(k)**: polynomial sections are built and injective. Surjectivity onto `H⁰(𝒪(k))` uses the now-available point Hartogs theorem. Fubini–Study and the tensor laws are also open.
- **Existence in the real Hodge theorem in middle degrees**: this needs Layer 5's Sobolev scale and elliptic estimates.
