### Named results

- **∂̄² = 0 on complex manifolds**: on the complex-valued smooth forms of a complex manifold, [`d = ∂ + ∂̄`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/ComplexManifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.mextDerivₗ_eq_del_add_delBar_ofComplexManifold) and [`∂̄∂̄ = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/ComplexManifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.delBar_delBar_ofComplexManifold).
- **Integrability of `T^(1,0)`**: for a smooth almost complex structure, the `i`-eigenbundle of the complexified tangent bundle is involutive exactly when the Nijenhuis tensor vanishes ([`isInvolutiveComplexDistribution_eigenspace_I_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Eigenbundle.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.isInvolutiveComplexDistribution_eigenspace_I_iff)).
- **Osgood's lemma**: a function of finitely many complex variables that is continuous and holomorphic in each variable separately is jointly analytic ([`ContinuousOn.analyticOnNhd_of_differentiableAt_update`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CauchyIntegralPolydisc.html#ContinuousOn.analyticOnNhd_of_differentiableAt_update)).
- **The codifferential is the formal adjoint of `d`**: on an oriented boundaryless Riemannian manifold, `∫⟨dα, β⟩ = ∫⟨α, d*β⟩` for smooth forms, one of them compactly supported ([`integralTopForm_inner_mextDeriv_eq_inner_codifferential`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Codifferential.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.integralTopForm_inner_mextDeriv_eq_inner_codifferential)).
- **Weierstrass preparation and division**: if `w ↦ f(x₀, w)` has order d at w₀, then near `(x₀, w₀)` f is a unit times a monic degree-d polynomial in `w − w₀` with coefficients vanishing at x₀, and any analytic g divides by f with remainder of degree below d ([`AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Weierstrass.html#AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation)). Both are unique in the holomorphic stalk.
- **The principal-part splitting**: a meromorphic germ at a point of ℂ is uniquely its principal part, a polynomial in `(z - a)⁻¹`, plus a holomorphic germ ([`evalInvSub_principalPart_add_toMeromorphicGerms_regularPart`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/MeromorphicGerm.html#TauCeti.holomorphicStructureSheaf.evalInvSub_principalPart_add_toMeromorphicGerms_regularPart)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Type projections of smooth forms**: for any smooth almost complex structure, the [projections onto type `(p,q)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/AlmostComplexType.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.typeProjection) are complementary idempotents, built as polynomials in the derivation that `J` induces on forms. ∂, ∂̄ and the four bidegrees of `d` are stated on them.
- **The Dolbeault complex** ([`dolbeaultComplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/Complex.html#TauCeti.dolbeaultComplex)): the `(p,•)`-forms of a complex manifold with differential ∂̄, a cochain complex in `ModuleCat ℂ`, with cohomology [`H^(p,q)_∂̄`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/Complex.html#TauCeti.dolbeaultCohomology) identified with closed modulo exact forms.
- **The Hodge star on Riemannian manifolds**: the [Hodge star of smooth forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/HodgeStar.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.hodgeStar) is built pointwise from the inner-product-space star, and satisfies `α ∧ ⋆β = ⟨α, β⟩ vol` and `⋆⋆ = (-1)^{kl}`. It carries the codifferential and the [Hodge Laplacian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Codifferential.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.hodgeLaplacian), with Green's formulas and formal self-adjointness. The complex star and its type law are only pointwise so far.
- **Complex de Rham cohomology**: the [complex de Rham complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/ComplexCoefficients.html#TauCeti.complexDeRhamComplex) is a cochain complex of complex vector spaces, and its cohomology is [the complexification of real de Rham cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/ComplexCoefficients.html#TauCeti.complexDeRhamCohomology.complexificationEquiv).

### Roadmap coverage

K3.0 is done, though the local defining-equation comparisons K6 asks of it are not established. K1 is partial. Done: the complex module of forms, the flat `dz` criterion for holomorphy, the joint holomorphic implicit map with local uniqueness, the eigenbundles, `(p,q)` types with wedge, pullback and conjugation, the four bidegrees of `d`, and the Nijenhuis tensor with its type identities, the ∂̄² and ∂² formulas and the involutivity equivalence. A complex orientation lift with positive chart signs exists; the canonical orientation is preserved by local biholomorphisms, [pulls back along them](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#IsLocalDiffeomorph.complexManifoldOrientation_eq_pullback), restricts to open subsets and is compatible with products. A regular level set of a holomorphic map is an [embedded complex submanifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Level.html#TauCeti.isSmoothEmbedding_coe_regularLevel) whose tangent space is the kernel; parametrized submanifolds, their comparison with zero sets, the normal sequences and Newlander–Nirenberg are missing. K2 is partial: ∂ and ∂̄ have their bidegrees, squares, anticommutation, Leibniz rule, conjugation and pullback naturality, ∂̄-closed functions are holomorphic, and `dᶜ = i(∂̄ − ∂)` satisfies `ddᶜ = 2i∂∂̄`. The Dolbeault complex and its cohomology exist. The Cauchy transform solves `∂̄u = f` locally with parameters, and on discs of any radius and on ℂ without them. The whole-domain Runge solver with parameters, the Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma, the Dolbeault sheaves and the Dolbeault theorem are absent. K3.MeromorphicGermField is done except for transporting products of Laurent series and an explicit comparison with GlobalHolomorphicFunctions' coefficients. K3.HolomorphicStalk has domain stalks, coordinate generation of the maximal ideal, coordinate-hyperplane quotients and Weierstrass preparation and division with uniqueness; the rest of K3 is untouched. K5a is partial: it has the real Hodge star and the pointwise complex star with their identities, the codifferential and Hodge Laplacian, the complex de Rham complex, the delta-series failure, chart `C^k` seminorms and the topology on smooth sections, and Peetre's inequality on `H^s`, but not the orientation line, density bundle, bundle distributions or Sobolev scale. K7.1 is done except for comparing its atlas and orientation with K1's. K4, K5b, K6, K7.2–K7.4 and K8 are untouched.

## The frontier
