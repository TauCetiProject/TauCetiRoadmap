## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The several-variable foundations (K3.0) are done, and the almost complex type calculus, ∂ and ∂̄ and Dolbeault cohomology are in place. The Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma holds for `(0,q)`-forms on polydiscs, but not yet in the roadmap's whole-polydisc `(p,q)` form. Layers 1–5, 7 and 8 are partial, Layer 4 and Layer 8 only barely. The Kähler layer (Layer 6) has not begun.

### Named results

- **The Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma on polydiscs, for `(0,q)`-forms** — a smooth ∂̄-closed `(0,q+1)`-form on a polydisc in ℂⁿ, with Banach-valued coefficients, is ∂̄-exact on every smaller concentric polydisc ([`exists_contDiffOn_delBarCoeff_eq_of_polydisc`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/Polydisc.html#TauCeti.exists_contDiffOn_delBarCoeff_eq_of_polydisc)).
- **∂̄² = 0 on complex manifolds** — on complex-valued smooth forms, [`d = ∂ + ∂̄`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/ComplexManifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.mextDerivₗ_eq_del_add_delBar_ofComplexManifold) and [`∂̄∂̄ = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/ComplexManifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.delBar_delBar_ofComplexManifold).
- **Weierstrass preparation and division** — near a point where `w ↦ f(x₀, w)` has order d, f is a unit times a monic Weierstrass polynomial of degree d, and every analytic germ divides by f with remainder of degree below d ([`AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Weierstrass.html#AnalyticAt.exists_weierstrassPreparation)).
- **Osgood's lemma** — a continuous function of several complex variables that is holomorphic in each variable separately is jointly analytic ([`ContinuousOn.analyticOnNhd_of_differentiableAt_update`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CauchyIntegralPolydisc.html#ContinuousOn.analyticOnNhd_of_differentiableAt_update)).
- **Uniqueness of harmonic representatives** — on a compact oriented Riemannian manifold, a harmonic form with zero de Rham class vanishes ([`toDeRhamCohomology_injective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Harmonic.html#TauCeti.harmonicForms.toDeRhamCohomology_injective)), and the harmonic, exact and coexact summands are independent.

This is Layer 8's test case for non-Kähler behaviour.

## The frontier

- **The whole-polydisc Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma**: build the finite-rank Runge operator and the whole-domain parameter solver, then pass from smaller concentric polydiscs to the whole polydisc and from `(0,q)` coordinate forms to `(p,q)` forms in the manifold calculus.
- **Analytic local rings**: prove the holomorphic stalks Noetherian, of Krull dimension exactly n with the coordinates as regular parameters, and factorial.
- **Existence in the real Hodge theorem**: every de Rham class has a harmonic representative. Uniqueness and independence of the summands are done; existence needs Layer 5's Sobolev scale and elliptic estimates.
- **Cohomology of the Iwasawa manifold**: compute `h^(1,0)=3`, `h^(0,1)=2` and `b₁=4`. This needs the Nomizu and Sakane comparisons with invariant forms.
- **The cup ring of ℂPⁿ**: the additive cohomology is computed, but `H*(ℂPⁿ;ℤ)=ℤ[u]/(u^(n+1))` and the Hodge numbers are not.
