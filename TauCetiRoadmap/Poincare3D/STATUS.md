### Named results

- **Sphere homotopy equivalence forces compactness** — a Hausdorff `n`-manifold homotopy equivalent to `Sⁿ` is compact (and second countable), because a connected noncompact `n`-manifold has vanishing `Hₙ` ([`compactSpace_of_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PoincareConjecture.html#ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv.compactSpace_of_sphere), [`isZero_singularHomology_of_noncompactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/LocalHomology/Noncompact.html#TauCeti.isZero_singularHomology_of_noncompactSpace)).
- **Reduction of the Poincaré statements to closed simply connected manifolds** — for `n ≥ 2`, the homeomorphism and diffeomorphism conclusions for a manifold homotopy equivalent to `Sⁿ` follow once they hold under compactness, second countability and simple connectedness ([`nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_compactSpace_simplyConnectedSpace_imp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PoincareConjecture.html#ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv.nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_compactSpace_simplyConnectedSpace_imp), [`nonemptyDiffeomorphSphere_of_compactSpace_simplyConnectedSpace_imp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PoincareConjecture.html#ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv.nonemptyDiffeomorphSphere_of_compactSpace_simplyConnectedSpace_imp)).
- **The scalar extinction-time bound** — a continuous width obeying the forward Dini inequality, with weighted jump errors at finitely many events, becomes negative after an explicit time; this is the arithmetic of Morgan–Tian Theorem 18.1 for real functions ([`ricciWidth_time_le_of_forward_le_of_jump_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RicciFlow/WidthComparison.html#TauCeti.ricciWidth_time_le_of_forward_le_of_jump_le)).

## The frontier

- **The standard cap on ℝ³ (6.1)** — prove the punctured metric extends smoothly across the tip, giving a complete nonnegatively curved metric on ℝ³ with tip sectional curvature `1/4`. The curvature computations are in place.
- **Closed simply connected three-manifold topology (1.1)** — `H₁=H₂=0`, `H₃≅ℤ`, `π₂=0`, `π₃≅ℤ` and the homotopy equivalence with `S³`. This needs the AlgebraicTopology suppliers (Poincaré duality, Hurewicz, Whitehead).

- **Localized Shi estimates (2.2)** — the first genuinely parabolic estimate, needed by pointed compactness and the cap flow. The new maximum-principle and cutoff tools are inputs, not the estimate.
