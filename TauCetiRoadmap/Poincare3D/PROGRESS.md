## Poincare3D: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`fc5db6f` to `e06d672`)

The first pieces of the Poincaré programme to land are its two scalar ends. The extinction argument's one-variable core is now proved. A nonnegative continuous width obeying the forward Dini inequality `D⁺W ≤ -2π + 3W/(1+4t)` cannot survive for all time ([`not_forall_ricciWidth_forward_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RicciFlow/WidthComparison.html#TauCeti.not_forall_ricciWidth_forward_le)). The estimate also carries through finitely many surgery events, each jump error weighted by `(1+4tⱼ)^(-3/4)`, and this gives an explicit extinction-time bound in terms of the initial width and the error budget ([`ricciWidth_time_le_of_forward_le_of_jump_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RicciFlow/WidthComparison.html#TauCeti.ricciWidth_time_le_of_forward_le_of_jump_le)). All of this is about real functions, not flows: no geometric width or Ricci flow exists yet to feed it.

On the surgery side, standard cap profiles are defined and shown to exist ([`exists_isStandardCapProfile`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RicciFlow/StandardCap/Profile.html#TauCeti.exists_isStandardCapProfile)). These are the radial functions with a round tip `2 sin(r/2)` and a cylindrical end `√2`, and they satisfy the bounds `0 ≤ f′ ≤ 1` and `f″ ≤ 0` that make the cap nonnegatively curved. The cap metric on ℝ³ and its flow are not built yet.

The rest of the window is general analysis aimed at later layers: a two-sided Grönwall inequality from an arbitrary interior time, `Cᵏ` extension across a half-space by higher-order reflection ([`exists_contDiff_extension_halfSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Trace/Reflection/HalfSpace.html#TauCeti.HalfSpaceExtension.exists_contDiff_extension_halfSpace)), uniform jet bounds for families of maps, polar-coordinate integration, which shows that radial graphs are Haar-null, simultaneous isometric limits of compact metric charts, and completeness criteria for comparable metrics.

## Poincare3D: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`e06d672` to `b235000`)

The topological side of the roadmap opened this window. A Hausdorff `n`-manifold homotopy equivalent to `Sⁿ` is compact ([`compactSpace_of_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PoincareConjecture.html#ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv.compactSpace_of_sphere)), because the top singular homology of a connected noncompact manifold vanishes ([`isZero_singularHomology_of_noncompactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/LocalHomology/Noncompact.html#TauCeti.isZero_singularHomology_of_noncompactSpace), Hatcher's Proposition 3.29). That in turn rests on singular homology being the colimit of the homology of compact subsets ([`isColimitMapCoconeSingularHomologyCompacts`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/CompactSupport.html#TauCeti.isColimitMapCoconeSingularHomologyCompacts)). With simple connectedness transported from the sphere, the homotopy-equivalence forms of the Poincaré statement now [reduce](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PoincareConjecture.html#ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv.nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_compactSpace_simplyConnectedSpace_imp) to closed simply connected manifolds in every dimension `n ≥ 2`, smooth and topological alike (TauCeti#41690). This is all done in general dimension, and the closed case itself is of course untouched.

On the surgery side the standard cap became a metric. The [punctured cap metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RicciFlow/StandardCap/PuncturedMetric.html#TauCeti.IsStandardCapProfile.puncturedMetric) `dr² + f(r)² g_(Sⁿ)` has nonnegative [sectional curvature](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RicciFlow/StandardCap/SectionalCurvature.html#TauCeti.IsStandardCapProfile.sectionalCurvature_puncturedMetric_nonneg), round scalar curvature near the tip and scalar curvature `n(n-1)/2` on the cylindrical end. Smoothness across the tip is not yet proved, so there is no metric on ℝ³ itself. The scalar maximum principle behind `R_min(t) ≥ -6/(1+4t)` is also [proved](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/RicciFlow/ScalarCurvatureComparison.html#TauCeti.neg_six_div_one_add_four_mul_le_of_mul_sq_le_deriv_of_isLocalMin_of_neg_six_le), for a function obeying the scalar-curvature inequality at its minima rather than for an actual flow.

The rest is analysis for the parabolic layers: Hessian sign conditions at extrema of functions on Riemannian manifolds, upper-support (barrier) calculus, point selection with doubling control, subsequence compactness of smooth maps in all derivatives, cutoff diffeomorphisms correcting nearly inverse maps, ODE uniqueness up to closed endpoints, and second-order trajectory estimates.
