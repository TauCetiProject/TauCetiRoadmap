## AffineMorseLefschetz: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`2c51628` to `c1c6773`)

The first window of this roadmap proved the index bounds that drive complex Morse theory. A strongly q-pseudoconvex function, one whose Levi form is positive on a complex subspace of codimension at most q, has Morse index at most n+q at a critical point ([`IsStronglyPseudoconvexAt.morseIndex_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Pseudoconvex.html#TauCeti.IsStronglyPseudoconvexAt.morseIndex_le)). For q=0 this is the classical bound λ≤n for strictly plurisubharmonic functions. Both bounds also hold on complex manifolds, for the intrinsic manifold Morse index ([`IsManifoldStronglyPseudoconvexAt.manifoldMorseIndex_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/LeviForm.html#TauCeti.IsManifoldStronglyPseudoconvexAt.manifoldMorseIndex_le)) (TauCeti#17543, TauCeti#17946).

Underneath this sits a full calculus for the [Levi form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/LeviForm.html#TauCeti.leviForm): sum, product and convex-composition rules, holomorphic pullback, and chart independence on manifolds. Strong pseudoconvexity is open in the fine C² topology, with a positive tolerance that varies continuously ([`exists_continuousOn_pos_forall_isStronglyPseudoconvexAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Pseudoconvex.html#TauCeti.exists_continuousOn_pos_forall_isStronglyPseudoconvexAt)). There are also worked inertia examples: squared norms, real parts of holomorphic quadratic forms, and their sums. On the linear-algebra side, an invertible operator anticommuting with a symmetric one pairs its eigenvalues λ and −λ, multiplicities included ([`genEigenspaceEquivOfAnticommute`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Eigenspace/Anticommute.html#Module.End.genEigenspaceEquivOfAnticommute)).

The second stage, on closed embeddings, has begun in flat coordinates. The second fundamental form of a holomorphic map satisfies B(Jv,Jw)=−B(v,w) ([`secondFundamentalForm_I_smul_I_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SecondFundamentalForm.html#ContDiffAt.secondFundamentalForm_I_smul_I_smul)), and for squared distance the real Hessians on v and Jv sum to 4‖di(v)‖² ([`fderiv_fderiv_norm_sq_sub_comp_add_I_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SquaredDistance.html#TauCeti.fderiv_fderiv_norm_sq_sub_comp_add_I_smul)). Shape operators, focal distances and the CW models are still to come. Nothing has landed yet for projective zero loci, exhaustions, duality or the Lefschetz theorems.

## AffineMorseLefschetz: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`c1c6773` to `93d1fe5`)

The closed-embedding stage now has its shape operator, and with it Milnor's paired-curvature calculation. For a holomorphic map the [shape operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ShapeOperator.html#TauCeti.shapeOperator) anticommutes with J ([`shapeOperator_apply_I_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ShapeOperator.html#ContDiffAt.shapeOperator_apply_I_smul)), so principal curvatures come in pairs κ and −κ of equal multiplicity ([`finrank_genEigenspace_shapeOperator_neg`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ShapeOperator.html#ContDiffAt.finrank_genEigenspace_shapeOperator_neg)). Hence i(x)+ν and i(x)−ν are focal points of the same multiplicity ([`finrank_ker_fderiv_fderiv_norm_sq_sub_sub_comp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ShapeOperator.html#ContDiffAt.finrank_ker_fderiv_fderiv_norm_sq_sub_sub_comp)). At a critical point the Hessian of squared distance is 2g((1−S)v,w) ([`fderiv_fderiv_norm_sq_sub_comp_apply_eq_inner_shapeOperator`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ShapeOperator.html#TauCeti.fderiv_fderiv_norm_sq_sub_comp_apply_eq_inner_shapeOperator)). All of this is still for maps between vector spaces, not on the manifold normal bundle.

The projective stage has begun. [`complexProjectiveZeroLocus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/ZeroLocus.html#TauCeti.complexProjectiveZeroLocus) cuts a polynomial family out of ℙ(ℂᴺ). It comes with chart equations by dehomogenization, invariance under powers and under the generated ideal, and pullback along injective linear maps. For the Jacobian matrix, Euler's identity puts the radial direction in the kernel, and the rank is a projective invariant equal to the rank in each affine ratio chart ([`rank_jacobiMatrixAt_dehomogenize_div`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Jacobian.html#TauCeti.rank_jacobiMatrixAt_dehomogenize_div)). Spaces of homogeneous coefficients get the monomial topology, in which evaluation and the Jacobian are jointly continuous (TauCeti#20265). The zero locus is still a bare set: no subspace topology, closedness, incidence variety or induced submanifold yet.

The Levi stage gained Wirtinger derivatives, with the Levi matrix entries identified as ∂²f/∂z_j∂z̄_k ([`toMatrix₂_leviForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/LeviForm.html#TauCeti.toMatrix₂_leviForm)). It also gained the Morse index of every function a‖z‖²+Re(Σc_j⟨b_j,z⟩²), and index exactly n at a nondegenerate Levi-flat critical point. Its maximum principle is still missing.
