## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All four summits are proved: model existence, the omitting types theorem, interpolation with PC separation and its Lyndon and Malitz refinements, and the boundedness of well-orderings. Two small items are still open: `expandSymbStructure` in the Layer 0 signature calculus, and `craig_of_lyndon_interpolation`, which recovers Craig's theorem from Lyndon's.

### Named results

- **Model existence** — over a language with countably many symbols, a countable member of a consistency property has a countable model in any universe, and every element of that model names a Henkin constant: [`ConsistencyProperty.model_existence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/ModelExistence.html#FirstOrder.Language.ConsistencyProperty.model_existence). A [variant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/ModelExistence.html#FirstOrder.Language.ConsistencyProperty.model_existence_omitting) also omits countably many types.
- **The omitting types theorem** — a satisfiable theory in a countable formation-closed fragment has a countable model that omits countably many non-isolated partial types of the fragment: [`Theoryω.exists_countable_model_omitting`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/OmittingTypes.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theoryω.exists_countable_model_omitting). Its [first-order form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/OmittingTypes.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theory.exists_countable_model_omitting) follows by taking the first-order image as the fragment.
- **Craig interpolation and PC separation** — if one Lω₁ω sentence entails another, over a language of any cardinality, some sentence in their shared symbols lies between them: [`craig_interpolation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Interpolation/Craig.html#FirstOrder.Language.craig_interpolation). As a consequence, two projective classes with no countable common member are separated by one sentence: [`pcSeparation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Interpolation/Craig.html#FirstOrder.Language.pcSeparation). The CountableStructureSpaces roadmap consumes this.
- **Lyndon and Malitz interpolation** — [Lyndon's form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Interpolation/Lyndon.html#FirstOrder.Language.lyndon_interpolation) holds over arbitrary languages: each relation symbol of the interpolant occurs with a polarity it has in both sentences. [Malitz's form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Interpolation/Malitz.html#FirstOrder.Language.malitz_interpolation) gives a universal interpolant when the consequent is universal, over relational languages, as the roadmap specifies.
- **Boundedness of well-orderings** — if a sentence forces `lt` to well-order its models, their order types are bounded below ω₁ ([`Sentenceω.wellOrder_type_boundedness`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/WellOrdering.html#FirstOrder.Language.Sentenceω.wellOrder_type_boundedness)). It follows that [no sentence defines well-ordering](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/WellOrdering.html#FirstOrder.Language.wellOrdering_undefinable).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Fragments and Löwenheim–Skolem** — a countable set generates a countable fragment over any language, and this is what makes [downward Löwenheim–Skolem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/LowenheimSkolem.html#FirstOrder.Language.exists_aElementary_substructure) work. Löwenheim–Skolem in turn shows that [entailment does not depend on the universe of models](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/CountableModel.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theoryω.Entails.realize).
- **The first-order image** — the [first-order image fragment](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/FirstOrderImage.html#FirstOrder.Language.Fragment.firstOrder) is the least formation-closed fragment. Through it the infinitary theory meets first-order logic: [elementarity for it is exactly Mathlib's elementarity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/ElementaryEmbedding.html#FirstOrder.Language.aElementary_firstOrder_iff), and it is the fragment from which first-order omitting types is derived.
- **The graph translation** — [`relationalize`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/GraphTranslation/Relationalize.html#FirstOrder.Language.BoundedFormulaInf.relationalize) replaces function symbols by their graphs while preserving the exact signed occurrences of the base relations. That is how both Craig and Lyndon reach languages with function symbols.
