## InfinitaryModelExistence: 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-26 (`df756f6` to `938acbb`)

The roadmap's first code is the vocabulary of Lω₁ω theories: a [theory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/Theory.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theoryω) is a set of Lω₁ω sentences, a [model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/Theory.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theoryω.Model) satisfies all of them, and the expected bookkeeping is there: monotonicity, empty, singleton, insert and union theories, and [invariance under isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/Theory.html#FirstOrder.Language.StrongHomClass.theoryω_model_iff) (TauCeti#21392). The one substantive piece is the reduction of a countable theory to a single sentence: its [conjunction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/Theory.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theoryω.conjunction), coded along the parent roadmap's index coding, [holds in a structure exactly when the structure models the theory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/Theory.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theoryω.realize_conjunction_iff). That is how the later sentence-level theorems will apply to countable theories.

What's missing is the rest of Layer 1: satisfiability, entailment, omitted types, transfer along expansions, and all of fragments. Transfer along expansions needs the language maps of Layer 0, which has not started.

## InfinitaryModelExistence: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-08 (`938acbb` to `d2083f0`)

This window took the roadmap from its first definitions to three of its four summits. The engine is [model existence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/ModelExistence.html#FirstOrder.Language.ConsistencyProperty.model_existence): over a language with countably many symbols, a countable member of a [consistency property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/ConsistencyProperty.html#FirstOrder.Language.ConsistencyProperty) has a countable model in which every element is named by a Henkin constant, built by a fair enumeration and a two-sided truth lemma on the term model (TauCeti#24643). A [variant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/ModelExistence.html#FirstOrder.Language.ConsistencyProperty.model_existence_omitting) also omits countably many types. Model existence feeds three results: [the omitting types theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/OmittingTypes.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theoryω.exists_countable_model_omitting) for countable formation-closed fragments; [Craig interpolation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Interpolation/Craig.html#FirstOrder.Language.craig_interpolation) for sentences of a language of any cardinality, together with [PC separation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Interpolation/Craig.html#FirstOrder.Language.pcSeparation) in the sum-language form that López–Escobar's theorem will consume (TauCeti#29276); and Marker's [bound on the order types of well-ordered models](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/WellOrdering.html#FirstOrder.Language.Sentenceω.wellOrder_type_boundedness), with the [undefinability of well-ordering](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/WellOrdering.html#FirstOrder.Language.wellOrdering_undefinable) as a corollary.

Most of the supporting layers landed alongside. They cover language maps, symbol occurrences, sublanguages and bound substitution; satisfiability, entailment and the required non-compactness example; fragments, where the fragment generated by a countable set is countable over any language; [downward Löwenheim–Skolem for fragments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/LowenheimSkolem.html#FirstOrder.Language.exists_aElementary_substructure), which shows that [entailment does not depend on the universe of models](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/ModelExistence/CountableModel.html#FirstOrder.Language.Theoryω.Entails.realize); complete types and isolation; Morleyization with its first-order presentation; and the graph translation into relational languages.

Lyndon and Malitz interpolation are still missing. The signed polarity and the universal and existential classes they need are in place, but neither theorem is. The first-order omitting types corollary and the first-order image fragment are also missing, along with a few Layer 0 items. The well-ordering theorems take a different route from the README: they are proved directly over an arbitrary language, so the relational core is not stated separately.
