## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Hives are built, along with their boundaries, finite fibres and `hiveCount`, and they are compared exactly with Gelfand–Tsetlin patterns, so §§1.1–1.2 are essentially complete and so is §2.1. The rest is open. Interpolation and the concavity criterion are missing, the generic polyhedral and counting lanes of §§1.3–1.6 have not started, and nothing from Littlewood–Richardson tableaux onwards has landed. In particular, no representation-theoretic hive formula has been proved, and neither have saturation or Horn.

### Named results

- **The hive–Gelfand–Tsetlin equivalence** — integer hives with boundary (λ, μ, γ*) correspond bijectively to Gelfand–Tsetlin patterns with top row μ and weight γ − λ that satisfy the one remaining rhombus family ([`fixedIntegerHiveEquivGTPattern`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.fixedIntegerHiveEquivGTPattern), [`gt_reconstruction_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.gt_reconstruction_iff)). The equivalence is a bijection of objects, not just an equality of counts.
- **The Henriques–Kamnitzer quasi-hive theorem** — the same equivalence in the cyclic orientation, with top row λ and weight μ* − ν, as in their Theorem 7.4 ([`cyclicFixedIntegerHiveEquivGTPattern`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.cyclicFixedIntegerHiveEquivGTPattern)).
- **The stable range** — when every adjacent gap of λ is at least μ₁ − μₙ, the remaining family holds automatically and hives correspond to the whole weight fibre ([`exists_stable_gt_equiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.exists_stable_gt_equiv)). Without that gap the equivalence fails: in rank 3 the adjoint weight has two weight-zero patterns and [no hive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.hiveCount_three_zero_adjoint_zero).
- **The GL₂ Clebsch–Gordan rule for hives** — in rank 2, the hive count of (λ, μ, γ*) is 1 exactly in the Clebsch–Gordan range and 0 otherwise ([`hiveCount_two_productBoundary`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.hiveCount_two_productBoundary)).
- **Finite output support** — for fixed λ and μ, only finitely many γ have a hive. This follows from degree conservation, the dominance bound γ ≤ λ + μ and the lowest-coordinate bound ([`finite_support_hiveCount_productBoundary`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.finite_support_hiveCount_productBoundary)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Hives and their boundaries** — [`Hive`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Basic.html#TauCeti.Hive) is a normalized rhombus-concave function on the triangular lattice. Its sides are [antitone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Basic.html#TauCeti.Hive.boundary_antitone), so each side of an integer hive is a dominant weight. Rotation, reflection, gauge and subtriangle restriction all have explicit effects on the boundary.
- **The hive count** — integer fibres are [finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.finite_fixedIntegerHive) and enumerable from an explicit bounding box, which defines [`hiveCount`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.hiveCount) together with its rotation, reflection and duality symmetries. Real fibres are [compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.isCompact_realFibre) convex polyhedra, and the boundary projection on the real cone is [proper](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.isProperMap_boundary_properCone). This is what saturation and the Hermitian layer will need.
- **Gelfand–Tsetlin weights** — every pattern now has a [torus weight](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ClassicalGroups/GelfandTsetlin/Basic.html#TauCeti.GTPattern.rowWeight), with a [branching recursion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ClassicalGroups/GelfandTsetlin/Weight.html#TauCeti.GTPattern.card_topRow_rowWeight_succ) for weight-fibre counts and agreement with tableau content. This is how the hive comparison reaches Gelfand–Tsetlin patterns.

## The frontier

- **Interpolation and concavity** — define the piecewise-affine extension of a hive and prove that it is concave exactly when all rhombus inequalities hold. This closes §1.1, and Layers 4 and 7 rely on it.
- **The Littlewood–Richardson bijection** — §2.2 needs an object-level equivalence between hives and LR tableaux of shape γ/λ and content μ that carries the ballot condition to the third rhombus family. It is blocked on SchurWeyl's skew LR tableaux.
- **The hive Littlewood–Richardson rule** — `schur_mul_eq_finsum_hiveCount` and `finrank_hom_eq_hiveCount` need §2.2 plus ClassicalGroups' identification of Schur structure constants with tensor multiplicities. `hiveCount_comm` is also still open.
- **The polyhedral adapters** — the Farkas certificate with unrestricted equality multipliers, exact projected cones and largest-lift selections (§§1.3–1.4). Saturation in Layer 4 needs them.
- **Vector-partition counting** — the chamber polynomials of §1.5 are independent of hives and could proceed in parallel. §2.6's type-A Kostant specialization depends on them.
