## TensorProductCombinatorics: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`91c8998` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap's first code is the triangular lattice that hives will live on (TauCeti#33128). Its points are the nonnegative barycentric triples summing to n, packaged as [`Vertex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Lattice.html#TauCeti.Hive.Vertex) with a computable enumeration, the third-turn [rotation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Lattice.html#TauCeti.Hive.Vertex.rotate) and a [reflection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Lattice.html#TauCeti.Hive.Vertex.reflect), and an injective equilateral [planar embedding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Lattice.html#TauCeti.Hive.Vertex.plane) whose squared distances are half the barycentric ones. Unit rhombi are [`IsRhombus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Lattice.html#TauCeti.Hive.IsRhombus), recorded in perimeter order with the obtuse corners singled out so that the hive inequality can later be read off directly; the diagonals have the expected planar squared lengths 1 and 3, and rhombi are preserved by rotation, reflection and translation into a larger triangle.

This is lattice geometry only. Hives themselves, their boundaries and the rhombus inequalities, and everything built on them, have not started.

## TensorProductCombinatorics: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

Hives arrived, and with them the roadmap's first real comparison theorem: integer hives with a fixed product boundary are in bijection with Gelfand–Tsetlin patterns of top row μ and weight γ − λ that satisfy one remaining family of rhombus inequalities ([`fixedIntegerHiveEquivGTPattern`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.fixedIntegerHiveEquivGTPattern), with the criterion itself as [`gt_reconstruction_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.gt_reconstruction_iff)). The same statement in the cyclic orientation is the quasi-hive form of Henriques–Kamnitzer's Theorem 7.4 ([`cyclicFixedIntegerHiveEquivGTPattern`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.cyclicFixedIntegerHiveEquivGTPattern)). When the input λ has adjacent gaps at least μ₁ − μₙ, the extra condition is automatic and hives match the whole weight fibre ([`exists_stable_gt_equiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.exists_stable_gt_equiv)). A consequence is the [closed-form GL₂ Clebsch–Gordan rule](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.hiveCount_two_productBoundary). The README's checks are proved as well, including the rank-3 adjoint case, where two weight-zero patterns give [no hive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/GelfandTsetlin.html#TauCeti.Hive.hiveCount_three_zero_adjoint_zero) (TauCeti#40166).

Underneath this is most of Layer 1. [Hives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Basic.html#TauCeti.Hive) are now defined, along with their boundaries, and [each boundary side is antitone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Basic.html#TauCeti.Hive.boundary_antitone), so it is a dominant weight. Fixed-boundary integer fibres are [finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.finite_fixedIntegerHive), which defines [`hiveCount`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.hiveCount). Real fibres are [compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.isCompact_realFibre) convex polyhedra. The dominance and lowest-coordinate bounds give [finite output support](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hive/Fibre.html#TauCeti.Hive.finite_support_hiveCount_productBoundary) for fixed λ and μ (TauCeti#38555). Supporting work added the dominance order and contragredient duality on dominant weights, plus a weight theory for Gelfand–Tsetlin patterns: a branching recursion for weight-fibre counts, and agreement with tableau content.

Some things are still missing. The interpolation of a hive and its concavity criterion have not landed, nor has any of the polyhedral, largest-lift, vector-partition or cone-series machinery of §§1.3–1.6. On the Layer 2 side, the Littlewood–Richardson tableau comparison and the representation-theoretic hive formulas have not started.
