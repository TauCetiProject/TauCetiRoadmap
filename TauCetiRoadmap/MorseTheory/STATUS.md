## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The ordinary Morse theory of compact manifolds is complete through the strong Morse inequalities. That includes the local theory, band deformation, cell attachment, a finite CW model, Morse approximation, the Lusternik–Schnirelmann bound and exponential gradient convergence. Three things are partial: the CW model for proper functions on noncompact manifolds, the two-critical-point sphere theorem with degenerate critical points, and Morse–Bott theory, which has its normal splitting and local normal form but no attachment theorem yet.

### Named results

- **The strong Morse inequalities** — for a Morse function on a compact boundaryless manifold, bₙ − bₙ₋₁ + ⋯ ± b₀ ≤ cₙ − cₙ₋₁ + ⋯ ± c₀ over any field ([`IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le)), with the polynomial form Σcᵢtⁱ = Σbᵢtⁱ + (1+t)Q(t), and versions for regular bands and cobordisms.
- **Milnor's cell attachment theorem** — crossing a critical level with compact band attaches one λ-cell for each critical point of index λ, up to homotopy relative to the lower sublevel ([`IsMorse.exists_morseCellAttachment_deformation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Comparison.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_morseCellAttachment_deformation)). Iterated, it shows that a compact manifold is homotopy equivalent to a finite CW complex with one cell per critical point (`IsMorse.exists_finite_cwComplex_homotopyEquiv`).
- **Index one and the fundamental group** — a connected manifold with a proper, bounded-below Morse function that has no index-one critical points is simply connected ([`IsMorse.simplyConnectedSpace_of_manifoldMorseIndex_ne_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.simplyConnectedSpace_of_manifoldMorseIndex_ne_one)). So a compact manifold with nontrivial π₁ and b₁ = 0 has no perfect Morse function.
- **The Lusternik–Schnirelmann theorem** — on a compact boundaryless manifold every smooth function, degenerate or not, has at least as many critical points as the Lusternik–Schnirelmann count of M ([`lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/LusternikSchnirelmann.html#TauCeti.lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount)).
- **Exponential convergence of gradient trajectories** — a convergent negative-gradient trajectory of a Morse function approaches its limit at rate C·e^{−ct} in Riemannian distance ([`IsMorse.exists_edist_le_exp_of_tendsto_neg_mgradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Trajectory.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_edist_le_exp_of_tendsto_neg_mgradient)). Worked examples show that the rate fails at a [degenerate minimum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Examples/ExponentialConvergence.html#TauCeti.not_exists_riemannianEDist_le_mul_exp_maximalIntegralCurve_neg_mgradient_pow_four).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [The Morse relative CW complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/RelCWComplex.html#TauCeti.morseRelCWComplex) of a critical level, whose relative homology is [free on the critical points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Homology.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_singularHomology_sublevelPair_iso_sigma). The inequalities, the CW model and the [spectral sequence of the critical-band filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/SpectralSequence.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.nonempty_filteredSpaceSpectralSequence_page_two_X_iso_sigma) are all built on it.
- [The strong Whitney topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Strong.html#ContMDiffMap.manifoldStrongWhitneyTopology), in which Morse functions are dense, with [relative approximation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Generic/Strong.html#ContMDiffMap.exists_isMorse_mem_ball_sub_compl) on noncompact manifolds.
- [The negative normal space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Splitting.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.IsCriticalSubmanifold.negativeNormalSpace) of a Morse–Bott submanifold. Its rank is the index and is locally constant, the splitting varies smoothly, and there is a [normalized Morse–Bott lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Lemma.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.IsNondegenerateCriticalSubmanifold.exists_normalized_morseBott_chart) in slice charts. This is what a negative disc-bundle attachment would use.

## The frontier

- **The degenerate two-critical-point sphere theorem (§4.4)** — the cover by two Euclidean open sets exists, along with most of Brown's argument: cellular-set collapse, [Brown's theorem on inverse sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CellularSet/InverseSet.html#TauCeti.isCellularSet_closedBall_inter_preimage), the bicollar collapse, and the [one-dimensional case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Circle/Schoenflies.html#TauCeti.exists_closure_homeomorph_closedBall_of_finrank_eq_one). The generalized Schoenflies theorem in all dimensions and the final two-disc gluing remain.
- **Morse–Bott attachment (Layer 5)** — glue the slice-chart normal forms into a Morse lemma on the actual normal bundle, using the natural parametric coordinate. Then attach negative disc bundles across a critical band and identify the relative homology with Thom coefficients.
- **Morse–Bott inequalities** — the critical-band spectral sequence exists, with its first page computed for Morse functions. It still needs the Morse–Bott first page, which depends on the attachment theorem.
- **The proper CW model (§2.4)** — extend the level-by-level compact model to a compatible tower over a regular exhaustion, with cells labelled by critical points. AlgebraicTopology's tower comparisons are the inputs.
- **Parametric chart comparisons (§2.3)** — extend the chart-change comparison continuously over compact CW parameter spaces.
