## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The ordinary Morse theory of the roadmap is complete. That includes the parametric chart comparison of Layer 2, which was the last ordinary piece outstanding. Morse–Bott theory is the only partial layer. Its attachment theorem now holds on compact manifolds, but the twisted Thom identification, the Morse–Bott spectral sequence and the Morse–Bott inequalities have not been built.

### Named results

- **Milnor's CW model** — a manifold with a proper, bounded-below Morse function is homotopy equivalent to a CW complex with one m-cell for each critical point of index m ([`IsMorse.exists_cwComplex_homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/CWModel/Proper.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_cwComplex_homotopyEquiv)).
- **The strong Morse inequalities** — on a compact boundaryless manifold, bₙ − bₙ₋₁ + ⋯ ± b₀ ≤ cₙ − cₙ₋₁ + ⋯ ± c₀ over any field, with the polynomial form Σcᵢtⁱ = Σbᵢtⁱ + (1+t)Q(t) ([`IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le)).
- **Reeb's sphere theorem for degenerate critical points** — a compact boundaryless manifold with a smooth function that has exactly two critical points, however degenerate, is homeomorphic to a sphere ([`nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_two_criticalPoints`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/TwoCriticalPoints.html#TauCeti.nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_two_criticalPoints)).
- **The Lusternik–Schnirelmann theorem** — on a compact boundaryless manifold, every smooth function has at least as many critical points as the Lusternik–Schnirelmann count of M ([`lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/LusternikSchnirelmann.html#TauCeti.lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount)).
- **Morse–Bott attachment** — crossing a critical level of a Morse–Bott function on a compact manifold attaches the negative disc bundles of all the critical submanifolds on that level, up to a deformation that fixes the lower sublevel ([`IsMorseBottFamily.exists_sublevel_union_iUnion_negativeDiscBundle_deformation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/DiscBundle.html#TauCeti.IsMorseBottFamily.exists_sublevel_union_iUnion_negativeDiscBundle_deformation)). The relative homology of the band is [carried by those disc bundles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Homology.html#TauCeti.IsMorseBottFamily.nonempty_singularHomology_sublevelPair_iso_iUnion_negativeDiscBundle).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [The Morse relative CW complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/RelCWComplex.html#TauCeti.morseRelCWComplex) of a critical level. The global CW model, the inequalities and the critical-band spectral sequence all rest on it.
- [The Morse–Bott lemma on the normal bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Tubular.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.IsNondegenerateCriticalSubmanifold.exists_morseBott_partialDiffeomorph_normalBundle) writes f near a critical submanifold as its critical value plus half the normal Hessian. This is the quadratic normal form that the disc-bundle attachment consumes.
- [Milnor's Lemma 3.6 over a compact parameter space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Parametric.html#TauCeti.exists_fiberHomotopyEquiv_fiberwiseCellAttachment_morseFamilyAttachingMap) is a fibre homotopy equivalence over P between the cell attachments of two continuous families of Morse charts, relative to the lower sublevel. With [fiberwise reattachment maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CWComplex/Abstract/FiberwiseCellAttachment.html#TauCeti.fiberwiseCellReattachment), it makes the handle decomposition natural in families.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 are done. Layer 2 now includes the §2.3 parametric families: the comparison over a compact parameter space comes with an inverse and homotopies over P, and it restricts along maps of parameter spaces. The other done layers include the [strong Whitney topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Strong.html#ContMDiffMap.manifoldStrongWhitneyTopology), the ℝP² Lusternik–Schnirelmann count of 3, and [exponential convergence of gradient trajectories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Trajectory.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_edist_le_exp_of_tendsto_neg_mgradient). Layer 5 is partial. It has the definitions, the normal-bundle splitting, the normal-bundle Morse lemma and the attachment for finitely many compact critical components on one level. It also identifies the band's relative homology with that of the attached disc bundles, which is the homology of their Thom pairs. Three acceptance tests pass: the y² and y⁴ tests, the ℝP² band across a critical circle with H₁ ≅ ℤ/2 instead of the untwisted ℤ, and the ℝP² spectral sequence with its ±2 differential. What Layer 5 lacks is the identification with H_{j−λ}(C) twisted by the orientation of ν⁻, and the Morse–Bott spectral sequence and inequalities.

## The frontier

- **The twisted Thom identification (Layer 5)** — identify the Thom-pair homology of each negative disc bundle with H_{j−λ}(C; o(ν⁻)). The [homology Thom map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Thom/Homology.html#TauCeti.homologyThomMap) and the orientation local system with its [zeroth homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Orientation/TwistedHomology.html#TauCeti.VectorBundleOrientationCover.twistedHomology₀EquivQuotientTwo) are in place. The twisted Thom isomorphism itself comes from CharacteristicClasses C2.
- **The Morse–Bott spectral sequence** — the critical-band spectral sequence exists for Morse functions and [does not depend on where the bands are cut](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/SpectralSequence.html#TauCeti.isIso_filteredSpaceSpectralSequenceMap_sublevel_hom_f). Its Morse–Bott first page needs the Thom identification above.
- **The Morse–Bott inequalities** — deduce the strong inequalities with shifted, orientation-twisted Betti polynomials of the critical components, and show that they specialise to the ordinary Morse inequalities. The [filtration form of the strong inequalities](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sublevel.html#TauCeti.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le_sum_sublevelPair) is already available as the template.
