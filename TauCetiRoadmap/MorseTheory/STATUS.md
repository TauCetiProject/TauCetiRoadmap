## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The ordinary Morse theory of the roadmap is complete. That covers the local theory, deformation and cell attachment, the CW model for proper bounded-below Morse functions, Morse approximation, the strong inequalities, the Lusternik–Schnirelmann bound, the degenerate two-critical-point sphere theorem and exponential gradient convergence. Two things are partial: the parametric chart comparison of Layer 2, and Morse–Bott theory, which has its normal-bundle Morse lemma and a model attachment theorem but no Thom identification, spectral sequence or inequalities.

### Named results

- **Milnor's CW model** — a manifold with a proper, bounded-below Morse function is homotopy equivalent to a CW complex with one m-cell for each critical point of index m, infinitely many when there are infinitely many such points ([`IsMorse.exists_cwComplex_homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/CWModel/Proper.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_cwComplex_homotopyEquiv)). A compact manifold gets [a finite model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/CWModel.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_finite_cwComplex_homotopyEquiv).
- **The strong Morse inequalities** — for a Morse function on a compact boundaryless manifold, bₙ − bₙ₋₁ + ⋯ ± b₀ ≤ cₙ − cₙ₋₁ + ⋯ ± c₀ over any field, with the polynomial form Σcᵢtⁱ = Σbᵢtⁱ + (1+t)Q(t) ([`IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le)).
- **Reeb's sphere theorem for degenerate critical points** — a compact boundaryless manifold carrying a smooth function with exactly two critical points, however degenerate, is homeomorphic to a sphere ([`nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_two_criticalPoints`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/TwoCriticalPoints.html#TauCeti.nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_two_criticalPoints)). The proof goes through [Brown's generalized Schoenflies theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Schoenflies/Disc.html#TauCeti.IsBicollar.exists_homeomorph_closure_connectedComponentIn_compl_range).
- **The Lusternik–Schnirelmann theorem** — on a compact boundaryless manifold every smooth function has at least as many critical points as the Lusternik–Schnirelmann count of M ([`lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/LusternikSchnirelmann.html#TauCeti.lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount)). On the torus Tᵐ that count is [m + 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/LusternikSchnirelmann/Torus.html#TauCeti.lsCategoryCount_univ_pi_addCircle).
- **Exponential convergence of gradient trajectories** — a convergent negative-gradient trajectory of a Morse function approaches its limit at rate C·e^{−ct} in Riemannian distance ([`IsMorse.exists_edist_le_exp_of_tendsto_neg_mgradient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Trajectory.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_edist_le_exp_of_tendsto_neg_mgradient)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

## The frontier

- **Morse–Bott attachment on the manifold** — combine the normal-bundle Morse lemma with the tube attachment theorem to attach the negative disc bundles of compact critical components across an isolated critical band, several components at once, compatibly with regular-band deformation.
- **The Thom identification (Layer 5)** — identify the relative homology of a Morse–Bott attachment with H_{j−λ}(C) twisted by the orientation of the negative bundle. The relative isomorphism for [disc-bundle attachments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Attachment.html#TauCeti.isIso_singularHomologyMap_of_attach_discBundle) and [the homology Thom map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Thom/Homology.html#TauCeti.homologyThomMap) are in place. The twisted Thom isomorphism itself comes from CharacteristicClasses C2.
- **Morse–Bott spectral sequence and inequalities** — the critical-band spectral sequence exists for Morse functions. It needs the Morse–Bott first page, which waits on the two items above.
- **Parametric chart comparisons (§2.3)** — assemble the family homotopies and fiberwise reattachment maps into the comparison over a compact CW parameter space, with inverse and homotopies over P, and restriction to subcomplexes.
