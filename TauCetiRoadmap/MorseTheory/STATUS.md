## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The ordinary Morse theory of Layers 1–4 and 6 is complete. Morse–Bott theory, Layer 5, now has its attachment theorem, Bott's twisted description of each critical level, the first page of its spectral sequence, and the Morse–Bott inequalities over every field. The one piece still missing is the integral twisted Thom identification for non-orientable negative bundles of rank above one.

### Named results

- **Milnor's CW model** — a manifold with a proper, bounded-below Morse function is homotopy equivalent to a CW complex with one m-cell for each critical point of index m ([`IsMorse.exists_cwComplex_homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/CWModel/Proper.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_cwComplex_homotopyEquiv)).
- **The strong Morse inequalities** — on a compact boundaryless manifold, bₙ − bₙ₋₁ + ⋯ ± b₀ ≤ cₙ − cₙ₋₁ + ⋯ ± c₀ over any field, with the polynomial form Σcᵢtⁱ = Σbᵢtⁱ + (1+t)Q(t) ([`IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le)).
- **Reeb's sphere theorem for degenerate critical points** — a compact boundaryless manifold with a smooth function that has exactly two critical points, however degenerate, is homeomorphic to a sphere ([`nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_two_criticalPoints`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/TwoCriticalPoints.html#TauCeti.nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_two_criticalPoints)).
- **The Lusternik–Schnirelmann theorem** — on a compact boundaryless manifold, every smooth function has at least as many critical points as the Lusternik–Schnirelmann count of M ([`lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/LusternikSchnirelmann.html#TauCeti.lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount)).
- **The Morse–Bott inequalities** — for a Morse–Bott function on a compact manifold and a field in which 2 is invertible, the Betti numbers of M satisfy the strong inequalities against cₖ = Σ dim H_{k−λ}(C; o(ν⁻)), with no orientation of the negative bundles assumed ([`IsMorseBottFamily.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_bettiNumber_le_sum_twistedBettiNumber`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/TwistedInequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorseBottFamily.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_bettiNumber_le_sum_twistedBettiNumber)). In characteristic two the [untwisted form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorseBottFamily.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_bettiNumber_le_sum) holds.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [The Morse relative CW complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/RelCWComplex.html#TauCeti.morseRelCWComplex) of a critical level. The global CW model, the inequalities and the critical-band spectral sequence all rest on it.
- [Morse–Bott attachment](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/DiscBundle.html#TauCeti.IsMorseBottFamily.exists_sublevel_union_iUnion_negativeDiscBundle_deformation): crossing a critical level attaches the negative disc bundles of its critical submanifolds. Through the twisted Thom isomorphism this makes [the band's relative homology the shifted twisted homology of those submanifolds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Thom/TwistedHomology.html#TauCeti.IsMorseBottFamily.nonempty_singularHomology_sublevelPair_iso_sigma_twistedHomology), which every Morse–Bott inequality is built from.
- [The Morse–Bott lemma on the normal bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Tubular.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.IsNondegenerateCriticalSubmanifold.exists_morseBott_partialDiffeomorph_normalBundle) writes f near a critical submanifold as its critical value plus half the normal Hessian. This is the normal form that the disc-bundle attachment uses.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 are done: normal forms and persistence, handles and the CW model, strong approximation and exhaustions, the inequalities, the Lusternik–Schnirelmann bound and both sphere theorems, and exponential convergence of gradient trajectories. Layer 5 is partial. It has the definitions, the smooth normal splitting, the normal-bundle Morse lemma and the attachment for several critical components on one level. The band's homology is computed through the Thom pairs. The spectral sequence's [first page](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/SpectralSequence.html#TauCeti.IsMorseBottFamily.nonempty_filteredSpaceSpectralSequence_page_two_X_iso_sigma_twistedHomology) is identified with twisted homology. The inequalities hold over every field, and the Morse case specializes to the ordinary identity. The integral twisted identification holds only in index one, or with a Thom class as for an oriented ν⁻. The general rank over ℤ without orientation is still open.

## The frontier

- **The integral twisted Thom identification (Layer 5)** — identify the relative homology across a Morse–Bott level with H_{j−λ}(C; o(ν⁻)) over ℤ, or any ring in which 2 is not a unit, when ν⁻ is non-orientable of rank at least two. It is proved over rings with 2 invertible, in characteristic two, for oriented ν⁻, and in [index one over every ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Index/One.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.IsNondegenerateCriticalSubmanifold.nonempty_singularHomology_sublevelPair_succ_iso_twistedHomology). The README routes the general case through CharacteristicClasses C2's twisted Thom isomorphism. That same step would give the integral first page of the Morse–Bott spectral sequence without an orientation hypothesis.
