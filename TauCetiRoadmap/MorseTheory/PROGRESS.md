## MorseTheory: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-20 (`063858c` to `68a457e`)

This first window put most of Milnor's opening chapters on manifolds. The headline is the deformation across a critical-free band: if f⁻¹[a,b] is compact and has no critical points, then [the sublevel {f ≤ a} is a deformation retract of {f ≤ b}](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_sublevelDeformation), [the inclusion is a homotopy equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_sublevelHomotopyEquiv), and [an ambient diffeomorphism carries one sublevel onto the other](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_diffeomorph_sublevel). Alongside these come [the band as a product with an interval](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_bandHomeomorph), as a homeomorphism, and [a deformation near a degenerate critical level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_criticalLevelDeformation) that is meant for the Lusternik–Schnirelmann bound. The handle step exists only in the quadratic model: [Milnor's handle retraction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/HandleRetraction.html#TauCeti.exists_inter_quadraticSublevelUnionCell_deformation) of a sublevel onto the sublevel plus a cell. It has not yet been glued into a manifold.

Local theory filled in around this. There is now [an intrinsic critical Hessian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Hessian.html#TauCeti.criticalHessian), with its index readable in any chart, in matrices, and through definite subspaces. There is [the normalized Morse lemma on a manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/NormalizedChart.html#TauCeti.IsManifoldNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_normalized_morseChart), with its two-dimensional saddle and the closed-disc sublevels near a minimum. Nondegenerate critical points were shown to be isolated, and [the critical counts of f and −f are dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/CriticalPoints.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.criticalCount_neg). A nondegenerate critical point also [persists, with its index, under small C² perturbations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Persistence.html#TauCeti.IsManifoldNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_mem_nhds_eventually_criticalPoint), and it continues smoothly in parameters. A [self-modelled copy of a boundaryless-model manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ModelTransport/Boundaryless.html#TauCeti.BoundarylessModel) carries these results to arbitrary models.

On the flow side, [negative gradient trajectories of a proper, bounded-below Morse function converge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/GradientFlow.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_tendsto_maximalIntegralCurve_neg_mgradient) to a critical point, for any C¹ metric. More generally, a trapped trajectory converges whenever the compact set it stays in holds finitely many critical points (TauCeti#18840). The exponential rate is still open.
