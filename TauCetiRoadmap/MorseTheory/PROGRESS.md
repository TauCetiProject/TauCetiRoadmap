## MorseTheory: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-20 (`063858c` to `68a457e`)

This first window put most of Milnor's opening chapters on manifolds. The headline is the deformation across a critical-free band: if f⁻¹[a,b] is compact and has no critical points, then [the sublevel {f ≤ a} is a deformation retract of {f ≤ b}](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_sublevelDeformation), [the inclusion is a homotopy equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_sublevelHomotopyEquiv), and [an ambient diffeomorphism carries one sublevel onto the other](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_diffeomorph_sublevel). Alongside these come [the band as a product with an interval](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_bandHomeomorph), as a homeomorphism, and [a deformation near a degenerate critical level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Deformation.html#TauCeti.exists_criticalLevelDeformation) that is meant for the Lusternik–Schnirelmann bound. The handle step exists only in the quadratic model: [Milnor's handle retraction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/HandleRetraction.html#TauCeti.exists_inter_quadraticSublevelUnionCell_deformation) of a sublevel onto the sublevel plus a cell. It has not yet been glued into a manifold.

Local theory filled in around this. There is now [an intrinsic critical Hessian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Hessian.html#TauCeti.criticalHessian), with its index readable in any chart, in matrices, and through definite subspaces. There is [the normalized Morse lemma on a manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/NormalizedChart.html#TauCeti.IsManifoldNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_normalized_morseChart), with its two-dimensional saddle and the closed-disc sublevels near a minimum. Nondegenerate critical points were shown to be isolated, and [the critical counts of f and −f are dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/CriticalPoints.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.criticalCount_neg). A nondegenerate critical point also [persists, with its index, under small C² perturbations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Persistence.html#TauCeti.IsManifoldNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_mem_nhds_eventually_criticalPoint), and it continues smoothly in parameters. A [self-modelled copy of a boundaryless-model manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ModelTransport/Boundaryless.html#TauCeti.BoundarylessModel) carries these results to arbitrary models.

On the flow side, [negative gradient trajectories of a proper, bounded-below Morse function converge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/GradientFlow.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_tendsto_maximalIntegralCurve_neg_mgradient) to a critical point, for any C¹ metric. More generally, a trapped trajectory converges whenever the compact set it stays in holds finitely many critical points (TauCeti#18840). The exponential rate is still open.

## MorseTheory: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `e8ea0d1`)

The headline is [the Lusternik–Schnirelmann theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/LusternikSchnirelmann.html#TauCeti.lsCategoryCount_univ_le_totalCriticalCount): on a compact boundaryless manifold, the Lusternik–Schnirelmann count of M is at most the number of critical points of any smooth function, with degenerate critical points allowed and an infinite critical set counting as ∞ (TauCeti#20709). The proof is the critical-level induction the roadmap asks for. Each [crossing of a critical level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/LusternikSchnirelmann.html#TauCeti.exists_lsCategoryCount_sublevel_le_add) raises the count by at most the number of critical points in the band, and a critical-free band raises it not at all. Still missing are the cup-length and normalized-category corollaries, the component-sum formula and the minimum c(M).

The second result is [exponential convergence of gradient trajectories on a manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/ExponentialConvergence.html#TauCeti.exists_riemannianEDist_le_mul_exp_maximalIntegralCurve_neg_mgradient). If a negative gradient trajectory converges to a nondegenerate critical point, its Riemannian distance to that point decays like C·e^{−μt}, for any C¹ metric. The route is the one M6 lays out: the Morse form of [Łojasiewicz's inequality on a Riemannian manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/ExponentialConvergence.html#TauCeti.IsManifoldNondegenerateCriticalPoint.exists_mul_abs_sub_le_norm_mgradient_sq), exponential energy decay, and a tail-length bound by the square root of the energy.

Regular levels are now manifolds. A regular level f⁻¹(c) [carries a manifold structure of dimension dim E − 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Scalar/Level.html#TauCeti.isManifold_scalarRegularLevel) and [embeds smoothly in M](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Scalar/Level.html#TauCeti.isSmoothEmbedding_coe_scalarRegularLevel), with tangent space the kernel of df, and [a regular point is a submersion point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Scalar/Submersion.html#TauCeti.IsRegularPoint.isSubmersionAtOfComplement), with a chart that restricts to the level chart. The sublevel and band boundary atlases are still absent. Groundwork for handle attachment arrived as [admissible Morse charts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/AdmissibleCharts.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_admissible_morseCharts) at a critical level, but the attachment itself has not. Every sigma-compact manifold also gets [a smooth proper exhaustion function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Exhaustion.html#TauCeti.exists_contMDiffMap_isProperMap_nonneg).

## MorseTheory: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-09 (`e8ea0d1` to `7ca783d`)

The Morse inequalities are now proved on compact manifolds. For a Morse function with cᵢ critical points of index i and Betti numbers bᵢ over a field, the [strong Morse inequalities](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_le) hold, together with [the Morse polynomial identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_sum_range_monomial_eq_add_mul) Σcᵢtⁱ = Σbᵢtⁱ + (1+t)Q(t) with Q having natural-number coefficients, and the weak inequalities and Euler's equality (TauCeti#26473). The proof works one critical level at a time, using that [the relative homology across a level is free on its critical points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Homology.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_singularHomology_sublevelPair_iso_sigma). It does not go through a CW model of the whole manifold, which has still not been built. The same statements hold for regular bands and cobordisms. Corollaries include [the lacunary principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.finrank_singularHomology_eq_of_forall_ne_add_one) and, for manifolds of finite CW type, [the signed critical count is the Euler characteristic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_toNat_criticalCount_eq_eulerChar).

Underneath sits [Milnor's cell attachment theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Comparison.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_morseCellAttachment_deformation). Crossing a critical level is, up to homotopy relative to the lower sublevel, attaching one λ-cell per critical point of index λ, and these cells form [a relative CW complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/RelCWComplex.html#TauCeti.morseRelCWComplex). A [handle version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Attachment.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_diffeomorph_sublevel_eq_union_handles) holds up to an ambient diffeomorphism, and [orientation-preserving chart changes give homotopic attaching maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Map.html#TauCeti.homotopic_morseAttachingMap_of_det_eq_one). [Reeb's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Reeb.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.nonempty_homeomorph_sphere_of_totalCriticalCount_eq_two) follows: a compact manifold with a Morse function that has exactly two critical points is homeomorphic to a sphere. The degenerate version is not proved yet. So far it has only its first step, [a cover by two Euclidean open sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/TwoCriticalPoints.html#TauCeti.exists_twoEuclideanOpenCover_of_two_criticalPoints), and the first pieces of Brown's Schoenflies argument.

Other work in the window: [the strong Whitney topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Strong.html#ContMDiffMap.manifoldStrongWhitneyTopology) and [density of Morse functions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Generic/Basic.html#TauCeti.dense_setOf_isMorse) on compact manifolds, [the minimal critical count c(M)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/MinCriticalCount.html#TauCeti.minCriticalCount), regular sublevels and bands as [manifolds with boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Scalar/Sublevel.html#TauCeti.isManifold_scalarRegularSublevel), and a first definition of [Morse–Bott functions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Family.html#TauCeti.IsMorseBottFamily). There are also worked examples: [the cosine function on a torus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Torus.html#TauCeti.criticalCount_torusCos), with binomial critical counts, and moment functions on projective spaces.

## MorseTheory: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`7ca783d` to `d9fbe7b`)

Morse functions now see the fundamental group. A connected manifold with a proper, bounded-below Morse function that has [no critical points of index one is simply connected](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.simplyConnectedSpace_of_manifoldMorseIndex_ne_one), so a nontrivial fundamental group [forces an index-one critical point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_manifoldMorseIndex_eq_one). On a compact manifold whose fundamental group is nontrivial but whose first Betti number vanishes over k, [no Morse function is perfect over k](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.finrank_singularHomology_one_lt_toNat_criticalCount) (TauCeti#33447). On the torus Tᵐ every Morse function has [at least (m choose j) critical points of index j](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Perfect.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.choose_card_le_criticalCount_torus), and the sum of cosines is [perfect](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Perfect.html#TauCeti.isPerfectMorse_torusCos). The sublevel filtration cut at regular values now has a spectral sequence whose [first page is free on the critical points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/SpectralSequence.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.nonempty_filteredSpaceSpectralSequence_page_two_X_iso_sigma), and it [degenerates in the lacunary case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/SpectralSequence.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.filteredSpaceSpectralSequence_page_d_eq_zero_of_forall_ne_add_one). The Morse inequalities also hold for a cobordism [relative to its outgoing boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Inequalities.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_upperBandPair_le).

Most of the remaining work went into Morse–Bott theory. The normal space of a Morse–Bott submanifold [splits into negative and positive parts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Splitting.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.IsNondegenerateCriticalSubmanifold.isCompl_negativeNormalSpace_positiveNormalSpace) of the index and coindex dimensions, the [spectral splitting is smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/NormalSplitting.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.IsNondegenerateCriticalSubmanifold.exists_contMDiffOn_starProjection_pointSpectralSubspace), and there is a [normalized Morse–Bott lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Bott/Lemma.html#TauCeti.SmoothEmbedding.IsNondegenerateCriticalSubmanifold.exists_normalized_morseBott_chart) in slice charts. A [canonical parametric Morse coordinate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Parametric.html#TauCeti.parametricMorseCoord) that is [natural under fibrewise linear changes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Morse/Parametric.html#TauCeti.eventually_parametricMorseCoord_comp) is in place to glue these charts. The Morse lemma on the actual normal bundle and the disc-bundle attachment have not landed yet.

On the cell side, [handle attachment is now a pushout](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/HandlePushout.html#TauCeti.IsMorse.exists_isPushout_sublevel_handles). A chart change [multiplies the attaching map in homology by its orientation sign](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Degree.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologyFunctor_map_morseAttachingMap_eq_sign_det_smul), and the roadmap's ℝP² test passes: the two attaching maps of the top cell, of degrees 2 and −2, [are not homotopic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Morse/Cell/Examples/RealProjectivePlane.html#TauCeti.not_homotopic_morseAttachingMap_realProjPlaneTopChart_realProjPlaneTopReflChart). Brown's Schoenflies argument gained several more pieces, but the theorem itself has not been proved yet.
