## RigidAnalyticGeometry: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`3501c1a` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's first results are about affinoid algebras themselves. [`IsAffinoidAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra) is the property of being a quotient of a Tate algebra, with no chosen norm attached, and on top of it came [affinoid Noether normalisation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/NoetherNormalization.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.exists_finite_injective): every nonzero affinoid algebra is finite over a Tate algebra embedded in it (TauCeti#17189). The basic algebra followed. Affinoid algebras are [noetherian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/FiniteQuotient.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.isNoetherianRing), their maximal ideals have [finite residue fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximalSpectrum.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.finite_quotient_of_isMaximal), and a [finite algebra over an affinoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Integral.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.of_finite) is again affinoid.

The topology is settled too. [Automatic continuity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Continuity.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.continuous_algHom) says every algebra map from a noetherian Banach algebra into an affinoid is continuous. Two consequences follow: [independence of presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/QuotientNorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.presentationContinuousAlgEquiv), and the fact that [all complete algebra norms are equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/QuotientNorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.toContinuousAlgEquiv). A third is that a [finite injective map is a closed embedding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/FiniteMap.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.isClosedEmbedding_of_finite_injective).

The supremum seminorm arrived, built from [point norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SupSeminorm.html#TauCeti.pointNorm) on the finite residue fields. It is power-multiplicative, its [kernel is the nilradical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SupSeminorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.supSeminorm_eq_zero_iff), and it is [computed on minimal-prime quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SupSeminorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.supSeminorm_eq_iSup_minimalPrimes). There is also the [integral-equation bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SupSeminorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.supSeminorm_le_iSup_coeff_rpow_of_eval_eq_zero): a root of a monic equation is bounded by the coefficients. Still missing are the maximum modulus principle, the identification of the power-bounded elements with the unit ball of the supremum seminorm, and the reduction functor.

## RigidAnalyticGeometry: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`c1c6773` to `e8ea0d1`)

The headline is the [maximum modulus principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximumModulus.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.exists_pointNorm_eq_supSeminorm): on a nonzero affinoid algebra, the supremum seminorm of every element is attained at a classical point (TauCeti#21050). On a Tate algebra the [supremum seminorm equals the Gauss norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximumModulus.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.supSeminorm_eq_norm). The proof evaluates at roots of unity in a cyclotomic extension and uses character orthogonality. It passes to the general case through Noether normalisation and a second new result: the supremum seminorm is the [spectral value of the minimal polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MinimalPolynomial.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.supSeminorm_eq_iSup_minpoly_coeff_rpow) over an integrally closed base, which is BGR 3.8.1/7 with a torsion-free, possibly non-domain target. The value-group statement and the description of power-bounded elements by the supremum seminorm are not yet there.

Dimension theory moved forward too. In an [affinoid domain every maximal ideal has height `dim A`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/KrullDimension.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.height_eq_ringKrullDim_of_isDomain), and for general affinoids there are componentwise statements. For Tate algebras, maximal ideals have height `n` and are generated by `n` elements, so [their localisations are regular local rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/TateAlgebra/Regular.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.isRegularLocalRing_localization_atPrime_of_isMaximal_isRestricted_subring). This is the first of the supporting targets for excellence.

The relative algebra `A⟨X₁, …, Xₘ⟩` over an affinoid [is again affinoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Restricted.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.isRestricted_subring), with iteration and its [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Restricted.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.existsUnique_algHom_isRestricted_subring) at power-bounded images. That fills the gap left in §1.1. The first geometric layer has also opened. [G-topologies](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/GTopology/Basic.html#TauCeti.GTopology) now have a site on their admissible opens, whose [sheaves are exactly the presheaves with unique gluing on admissible covers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/GTopology/Basic.html#TauCeti.GTopology.isSheaf_iff), and ordinary open covers give back the usual open-set site.

## RigidAnalyticGeometry: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-12 (`e8ea0d1` to `941bb58`)

The supremum seminorm now does what [BGR] asks of it. An element is [power-bounded exactly when `|f|sup ≤ 1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/PowerBounded.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.isPowerBounded_iff_supSeminorm_le_one), and topologically nilpotent exactly when `|f|sup < 1`. The [spectral-radius formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SpectralRadius.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.pow_norm_pow_one_div_tendsto_nhds_supSeminorm) gives `|f|sup` as the limit of `‖fⁿ‖^(1/n)` for any complete algebra norm. By the [value-group theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/ValueGroup.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.exists_supSeminorm_pow_eq_norm), one exponent depending only on `A` sends every `|f|sup` into `|K|`. Two more criteria landed: `|·|sup` is [multiplicative exactly when `A` is reduced and `Ã` is a domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Multiplicative.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.exists_absoluteValue_coe_eq_supSeminorm_iff), and positive polyradii give [strictly affinoid algebras exactly when they lie in `√|K×|`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Polyradius.html#TauCeti.isAffinoidAlgebra_isRestricted_iff_pow_eq_norm). The reduction functor arrived with [BGR 6.3.1/6](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Reduction.html#AlgHom.injective_reductionMap_iff_injective) and a [surjection that is not surjective on reductions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/NonsurjectiveReduction.html#TauCeti.MvPowerSeries.not_surjective_reductionMap_restrictedSquareSubst). The Banach function algebra theorem is still missing.

The geometry opened properly. [Affinoid subdomains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Subdomain/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidSubdomainMap) are defined by their universal property, with uniqueness, transitivity, intersections and inverse images. [Rational](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/RationalFraction.html#TauCeti.RationalFraction) and [Laurent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/LaurentFraction/Basic.html#TauCeti.LaurentFraction) fraction algebras represent their domains, and rational fractions are [Huber's rational localisations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/RationalFraction.html#TauCeti.RationalFraction.completionLocEquiv). Restrictions are [flat](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Subdomain/Flat.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidSubdomainMap.flat), and finite covers are faithfully flat. Tate acyclicity holds for the two-piece Laurent cover, both for the algebra and for [every algebraic module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Rigid/Affinoid/LaurentCover/Module.html#TauCeti.LaurentCover.exact_moduleAugmentation_moduleDiff), flat or not (TauCeti#33681). General covers are not done. The affinoid pushout is [the completed tensor product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Pushout/CompletedTensor.html#TauCeti.AffinoidPushout.completedTensorEquiv).

G-topologies gained continuous maps, induced topologies, the G0–G2 conditions, [pasting](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/GTopology/Paste.html#TauCeti.GTopology.paste), points with stalks, and [unique extension of sheaves](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/GTopology/SlightlyFiner.html#TauCeti.GTopology.sheafEquivFinestSlightlyFiner) to the finest slightly finer topology. Smaller pieces also landed: [weak stability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Field/WeaklyStable.html#TauCeti.IsWeaklyStable) of complete and of perfect fields, regular Tate algebras, universally catenary affinoids, contractive renorming, and the slope-decomposition interfaces of §7.2.
