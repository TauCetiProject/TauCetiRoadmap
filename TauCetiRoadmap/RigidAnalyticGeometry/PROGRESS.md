## RigidAnalyticGeometry: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-21 (`3501c1a` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's first results are about affinoid algebras themselves. [`IsAffinoidAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra) is the property of being a quotient of a Tate algebra, with no chosen norm attached, and on top of it came [affinoid Noether normalisation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/NoetherNormalization.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.exists_finite_injective): every nonzero affinoid algebra is finite over a Tate algebra embedded in it (TauCeti#17189). The basic algebra followed. Affinoid algebras are [noetherian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/FiniteQuotient.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.isNoetherianRing), their maximal ideals have [finite residue fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximalSpectrum.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.finite_quotient_of_isMaximal), and a [finite algebra over an affinoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Integral.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.of_finite) is again affinoid.

The topology is settled too. [Automatic continuity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Continuity.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.continuous_algHom) says every algebra map from a noetherian Banach algebra into an affinoid is continuous. Two consequences follow: [independence of presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/QuotientNorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.presentationContinuousAlgEquiv), and the fact that [all complete algebra norms are equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/QuotientNorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.toContinuousAlgEquiv). A third is that a [finite injective map is a closed embedding](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/FiniteMap.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.isClosedEmbedding_of_finite_injective).

The supremum seminorm arrived, built from [point norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SupSeminorm.html#TauCeti.pointNorm) on the finite residue fields. It is power-multiplicative, its [kernel is the nilradical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SupSeminorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.supSeminorm_eq_zero_iff), and it is [computed on minimal-prime quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SupSeminorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.supSeminorm_eq_iSup_minimalPrimes). There is also the [integral-equation bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/SupSeminorm.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.supSeminorm_le_iSup_coeff_rpow_of_eval_eq_zero): a root of a monic equation is bounded by the coefficients. Still missing are the maximum modulus principle, the identification of the power-bounded elements with the unit ball of the supremum seminorm, and the reduction functor.
