## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The algebra of affinoid algebras is nearly complete. It still lacks the Japanese property, excellence, completed field extension and the Banach function algebra theorem. Affinoid subdomains, rational and Laurent localisation, general G-topologies and Kiehl's inner subdomains are in place. Tate acyclicity is proved only for the two-piece Laurent cover, and Gerritzen–Grauert has not landed. Neither have rigid spaces, the identity principle or analytification.

### Named results

- **The maximum modulus principle**: on a nonzero affinoid algebra, the supremum seminorm of every element is attained at a classical point ([`exists_pointNorm_eq_supSeminorm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/MaximumModulus.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.exists_pointNorm_eq_supSeminorm)).
- **Power-bounded elements are the unit ball of the supremum seminorm**: for any complete algebra norm, `f` is power-bounded exactly when `|f|sup ≤ 1` ([`isPowerBounded_iff_supSeminorm_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/PowerBounded.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.isPowerBounded_iff_supSeminorm_le_one)).
- **The value-group theorem**: one exponent `m ≥ 1`, depending only on `A`, puts every `|f|sup^m` in `|K|` ([`exists_supSeminorm_pow_eq_norm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/ValueGroup.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.exists_supSeminorm_pow_eq_norm)).
- **Tate acyclicity for a Laurent cover**: for the cover by `|f| ≤ 1` and `|f| ≥ 1`, the augmented Čech sequence is exact for every algebraic module ([`exact_moduleAugmentation_moduleDiff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Rigid/Affinoid/LaurentCover/Module.html#TauCeti.LaurentCover.exact_moduleAugmentation_moduleDiff)). For finite modules it is also strictly exact, since the differential is an [open quotient map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Rigid/Affinoid/LaurentCover/Topology.html#TauCeti.LaurentCover.isOpenQuotientMap_moduleDiff).
- **Affinoid Noether normalisation**: every nonzero affinoid algebra is finite over an injectively embedded Tate algebra in `dim A` variables ([`exists_finite_injective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/NoetherNormalization.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidAlgebra.exists_finite_injective)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [Affinoid subdomains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/Subdomain/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAffinoidSubdomainMap) are characterised by their universal property. They have uniqueness, transitivity, intersections, inverse images and flat restrictions. The affinoid site and every gluing argument are built on them.
- [Rational fraction algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/RationalFraction.html#TauCeti.RationalFraction) are [identified with Huber's rational localisations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Affinoid/RationalFraction.html#TauCeti.RationalFraction.completionLocEquiv). That identification is the bridge by which classical Tate acyclicity is to be deduced from the adic one.
- [Inner subdomains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Rigid/Affinoid/Inner.html#TauCeti.AffinoidSubdomain.IsInner) `U ⋐_A Sp B` come with the single-radius and [relative-polydisc](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Rigid/Affinoid/Inner.html#TauCeti.AffinoidSubdomain.isInner_iff_exists_mapsTo) criteria. Their laws for restriction, closed subspaces, products and affinoid base change are in the library. Kiehl properness will be defined in these terms.
