## MarkovChainsAndRenewal: 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-24 (`fbbf903` to `271e1af`)

Renewal processes arrived this window, together with the first extensions of the Markov-chain path law. For i.i.d. nonnegative increments that are not almost surely zero, the renewal process [does not explode](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.ae_tendsto_arrival_atTop): the arrival times tend to infinity almost surely, so [every count is almost surely finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.ae_renewalCount_lt_top). The [count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.renewalCount) is an extended natural number, with the inversion relations to arrival times, monotonicity and right continuity, and a transfer of its law to the canonical product space. A Chernoff bound on the arrival times then gives [finite moments of every order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Moments.html#TauCeti.Probability.lintegral_renewalCount_pow_lt_top) for each count, with no assumption on the mean (TauCeti#20685). The roadmap's suggested proof compares with increments in {0, a}; the Laplace-transform argument replaces that comparison.

On the Markov-chain side, the path law now has its [time-n marginal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/MarkovChain.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.markovChainLaw_map_eval), the initial law propagated by the n-step kernel. It is [characterised uniquely](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/MarkovChain.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.eq_markovChainLaw) by its initial law and the prefix recursion, and it is packaged as a [path-law kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/MarkovChain.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.markovChainKernel), x ↦ ℙₓ, which depends measurably on the starting state. This kernel is the object the Markov property in Layer 1 will be stated against. Transition weights, samplers, stopping times, and everything about recurrence and renewal limits have not started.
