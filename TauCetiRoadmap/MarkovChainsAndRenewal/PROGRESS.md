## MarkovChainsAndRenewal: 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-24 (`fbbf903` to `5d671b9`)

Renewal processes now exist in the library, along with their first two theorems. [Nonexplosion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.ae_tendsto_arrival_atTop) says that if the i.i.d. nonnegative increments are not almost surely zero, the arrival times tend to infinity almost surely, so that [every count is finite at once](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.ae_renewalCount_lt_top) (TauCeti#20361). The proof uses the second Borel–Cantelli lemma. Under the same hypotheses each count has [finite moments of every order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Moments.html#TauCeti.Probability.lintegral_renewalCount_pow_lt_top), with no assumption on the mean of the increments. That proof goes through a [Chernoff bound for the arrival times](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Moments.html#TauCeti.Probability.measureReal_arrival_le_le_exp_mul_mgf_pow), not the two-point comparison the roadmap suggested. Around these sit the [extended-valued count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.renewalCount) with its monotonicity, right continuity and inverse relations to the arrival times, and a transfer of its law to the canonical product space. The renewal measure and the renewal function have not been started.

On the Markov-chain side, the path law gained what Layer 0 asked of it. It is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/MarkovChain.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.eq_markovChainLaw) given its initial law and the prefix recursion, its [time-n marginal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/MarkovChain.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.markovChainLaw_map_eval) is the initial law pushed through the n-th kernel power, and it depends measurably on the starting state. This assembles into the [path-law kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/MarkovChain.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.markovChainKernel) x ↦ ℙₓ that the Markov property in Layer 1 will be stated with (TauCeti#20290).

## MarkovChainsAndRenewal: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-07 (`5d671b9` to `e583e15`)

The renewal side now has its first limit theorems. The [elementary renewal theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/ElementaryTheorem.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.tendsto_renewalFunction_div) says m(t)/t tends to the reciprocal mean, including when the mean is infinite. Alongside it come the [almost-sure law of large numbers for the count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/StrongLaw.html#TauCeti.Probability.ae_tendsto_renewalCountNat_div), with uniform integrability and L¹ convergence. The [renewal central limit theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/CentralLimitTheorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.tendstoInDistribution_renewalCountNat_sub_div) proves the Gaussian limit of the standardized count, and needed a Helly–Bray theorem built along the way (TauCeti#27657). Beneath these sit the [renewal measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Measure.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.renewalMeasure) with U = δ₀ + F ∗ U, its Stieltjes form, and [existence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Equation.html#TauCeti.Probability.renewalConv_renewalMeasure_eq_add) and [uniqueness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Equation.html#TauCeti.Probability.eq_renewalConv_renewalMeasure) for the renewal equation. Most of the groundwork for the Blackwell and key renewal theorems has landed: the local renewal bound, vague compactness of translated renewal measures, the lattice span and countdown chain, and Darboux-sum approximations. The theorems themselves have not.

Markov chains moved from path laws to classification. Proved this window are the [strong Markov property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/StrongMarkov/Basic.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.condExp_stoppedValue_shift_iterate_markovChainLaw) at stopping times that may be infinite, a filtration-relative Markov property, and the [recurrence criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Visits.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.tsum_pow_apply_singleton_self_eq_top_iff) that a state is recurrent exactly when its Green function diverges. Also new are [long-run occupation frequencies](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Occupation.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.ae_tendsto_visitCount_div_markovChainLaw), periods as a class property with the additive-semigroup lemma, and coupling convergence in total variation. That last result assumes the product chain is recurrent, because positive recurrence and the invariant probability 1/mₓ are still missing. The integer walk is [recurrent exactly when symmetric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Examples/IntegerWalk/Recurrence.html#TauCeti.Probability.integerWalk_isRecurrent_iff), with infinite mean return time.

On the Poisson side, the [microscopic characterization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Process/Poisson/Microscopic.html#ProbabilityTheory.HasIndepIncrements.hasLaw_sub_poissonMeasure_iff_stationary_small_time) identifies rate-λ Poisson processes by stationary independent increments and the o(h) jump conditions.
