### Named results

- **The key renewal theorem**: for a nonlattice interarrival law and nonnegative, nonincreasing, integrable forcing h, (h ∗ U)(t) → (1/μ)∫h, with limit zero when the mean is infinite ([`tendsto_renewalConv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/KeyTheorem.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.tendsto_renewalConv)).
- **The Blackwell renewal theorems**: for a nonlattice law, m(t+u) − m(t) → u/μ ([`tendsto_renewalFunction_add_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Blackwell.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.tendsto_renewalFunction_add_sub)). For a lattice law of maximal span a, m(a(n+1)) − m(an) → a/μ ([`tendsto_renewalFunction_succ_mul_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Lattice/Blackwell.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.IsLatticeSpan.tendsto_renewalFunction_succ_mul_sub)).
- **Kac's formula and the invariant probability**: an irreducible chain has an invariant probability exactly when every state is positive recurrent ([`exists_invariant_probability_iff_forall_isPositiveRecurrent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Invariant.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.exists_invariant_probability_iff_forall_isPositiveRecurrent)). It is then unique and gives each state x the mass 1/mₓ ([`apply_singleton_eq_inv_meanReturnTime`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Invariant.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.Invariant.apply_singleton_eq_inv_meanReturnTime)).
- **Convergence to equilibrium**: an irreducible aperiodic chain converges to its invariant probability in total variation from every initial law ([`tendsto_tvDist_pow_comp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Equilibrium.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.Invariant.tendsto_tvDist_pow_comp)).
- **Exponential renewals are Poisson**: with independent rate-r exponential increments, the arrival measure ∑ δ_{S_n} is a homogeneous Poisson random measure ([`isPoissonRandomMeasure_renewalArrivalMeasure_of_expMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Exponential/Poisson.html#TauCeti.Probability.isPoissonRandomMeasure_renewalArrivalMeasure_of_expMeasure)). Conversely, a positive-rate homogeneous Poisson random measure is one whose interarrival gaps are independent exponentials ([`isPoissonRandomMeasure_smul_nnvolume_iff_iIndepFun_toReal_occurrenceGap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PointProcess/Poisson/Interarrival.html#TauCeti.Probability.isPoissonRandomMeasure_smul_nnvolume_iff_iIndepFun_toReal_occurrenceGap)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The cycle occupation measure** ([`cycleOccupation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Invariant.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.cycleOccupation)) counts expected visits in one return cycle. It is invariant at a recurrent state and minimal among subinvariant measures, which gives both existence and uniqueness of the invariant probability.
- **Markov chains relative to a filtration** ([`IsMarkovChain`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Filtered/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsMarkovChain)) state the Markov property for any filtration to which the process is adapted. For the natural filtration this is [equivalent to having the path law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Filtered/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.hasLaw_markovChainLaw_iff_isMarkovChain_natural), and the strong Markov property holds in this generality.
- **The renewal measure** U = ∑ F^{∗n} ([`renewalMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Measure.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.renewalMeasure)) satisfies U = δ₀ + F ∗ U. It solves the renewal equation uniquely and carries all the renewal limit theorems.

## The frontier

- **Unconditional excess-life limit**: feed the nonlattice interval limits ([`tendsto_renewalMeasure_real_Ioc`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Blackwell.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.tendsto_renewalMeasure_real_Ioc)) into [`tendstoInDistribution_renewalExcess_of_tendsto_measureReal_Ioc`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Excess/Limit.html#TauCeti.Probability.tendstoInDistribution_renewalExcess_of_tendsto_measureReal_Ioc). Then do the same for the nonlattice infinite-mean escape, using the zero-rate Blackwell limit. All the ingredients are present.
- **Thinning an unmarked Poisson process**: attach independent Bernoulli marks to a rate-λ process and show that the marked measure is Poisson with product intensity. The splitting results then give the rates pλ and (1 − p)λ and independence.
- **Multi-time cylinder probabilities**: prove the product formula for P_ν(X_{t₀} = y₀, …, X_{t_k} = y_k) with kernel powers between the listed times, by summing the prefix formula over intermediate states.
- **Return probability of the biased walk**: show ρ₀ = 1 − |p − q| for p ≠ 1/2. Transience is already proved, from the central-binomial Green series.
