That covers path laws, the strong Markov property, classification including positive recurrence, the invariant probability and convergence to equilibrium. ### Named results

- **The Blackwell renewal theorems**: for a nonlattice interarrival law, m(t+u) − m(t) → u/μ ([`tendsto_renewalFunction_add_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Blackwell.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.tendsto_renewalFunction_add_sub)). For a lattice law of maximal span a, m(a(n+1)) − m(an) → a/μ ([`tendsto_renewalFunction_succ_mul_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Lattice/Blackwell.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.IsLatticeSpan.tendsto_renewalFunction_succ_mul_sub)). Both include the infinite-mean case, where the limit is zero.
- **Kac's formula and the invariant probability**: an irreducible chain has an invariant probability exactly when every state is positive recurrent ([`exists_invariant_probability_iff_forall_isPositiveRecurrent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Invariant.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.exists_invariant_probability_iff_forall_isPositiveRecurrent)). It is then unique, with π({x}) = 1/mₓ ([`apply_singleton_eq_inv_meanReturnTime`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Invariant.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.Invariant.apply_singleton_eq_inv_meanReturnTime)).
- **Convergence to equilibrium**: for an irreducible aperiodic chain, every initial law converges to the invariant probability in total variation ([`tendsto_tvDist_pow_comp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Equilibrium.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.Invariant.tendsto_tvDist_pow_comp)). Without positive recurrence, the time-n mass of each state tends to zero ([`tendsto_pow_comp_real_singleton_of_isAperiodic_of_lintegral_return_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Pointwise.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.tendsto_pow_comp_real_singleton_of_isAperiodic_of_lintegral_return_eq_top)).
- **The elementary renewal theorem**: m(t)/t tends to 1/μ, including 0 when the mean is infinite ([`tendsto_renewalFunction_div`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/ElementaryTheorem.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.tendsto_renewalFunction_div)).
- **The renewal central limit theorem**: under finite positive variance, the standardized count (N_t − t/μ)/(σ√(t/μ³)) converges in distribution to a standard Gaussian ([`tendstoInDistribution_renewalCountNat_sub_div`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/CentralLimitTheorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.tendstoInDistribution_renewalCountNat_sub_div)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The cycle occupation measure** ([`cycleOccupation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Invariant.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.cycleOccupation)) counts expected visits in one return cycle. It is invariant at a recurrent state and minimal among subinvariant measures, so it gives both existence and uniqueness of the invariant probability.
- **Rigidity of harmonic measures** ([`eq_smul_volume_of_map_coe_conv_eq_self`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/HarmonicMeasure.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.eq_smul_volume_of_map_coe_conv_eq_self)) is a Choquet–Deny theorem on ℝ. For a nonlattice law, a locally bounded measure fixed by convolution is a multiple of Lebesgue measure. This is the step of Feller's argument that the local renewal bound alone could not supply.
- **The renewal measure** U = ∑ F^{∗n} ([`renewalMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Measure.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.renewalMeasure)) satisfies U = δ₀ + F ∗ U. It solves the renewal equation uniquely, and lattice renewal masses are return probabilities of the countdown chain.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 0 to 5 are done. The Markov layers include the restriction to closed classes. Positive recurrence is a class property, so null recurrence (recurrent but not positive recurrent) is one too. Finite irreducible chains are positive recurrent. Product chains preserve products of invariant measures. The hard-core heat bath satisfies detailed balance and converges to its Gibbs law, which is uniform at activity one. Layer 6 is partial. Items 1 and 2 are done, along with the excess-tail renewal equations and the equilibrium excess law. The key renewal theorem and the finite-mean excess-life limit are proved as consequences of the Blackwell interval limits, not yet as standalone statements. In the infinite-mean case the excess escape is unconditional for lattice laws and conditional for nonlattice ones. Layer 7 is partial. Items 2 and 3 are done. For item 1, the exponential renewal count path has the law of any process with independent Poisson increments, and N_t/t → λ almost surely. The identification with the PointProcesses point-measure construction is not established here, and item 4 has not started.
