- **The renewal central limit theorem**: under finite positive variance, (N_t − t/μ)/(σ√(t/μ³)) converges in distribution to a standard Gaussian ([`tendstoInDistribution_renewalCountNat_sub_div`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/CentralLimitTheorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.tendstoInDistribution_renewalCountNat_sub_div)).
- **The strong Markov property**: at a stopping time valued in ℕ ∪ {∞}, on the event that it is finite, the shifted path is the chain started from the stopped state, conditionally on the stopped σ-algebra ([`condExp_stoppedValue_shift_iterate_markovChainLaw`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/StrongMarkov/Basic.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.condExp_stoppedValue_shift_iterate_markovChainLaw)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The renewal measure** U = ∑ F^{∗n} ([`renewalMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Measure.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.renewalMeasure)) is locally finite and satisfies U = δ₀ + F ∗ U. With it, the renewal equation g = h + g ∗ F has the unique locally bounded solution h ∗ U ([`eq_renewalConv_renewalMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Equation.html#TauCeti.Probability.eq_renewalConv_renewalMeasure)). The uniform local bound U([t,t+h]) ≤ U([0,h]) ([`renewalMeasure_Icc_add_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/LocalBound.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.renewalMeasure_Icc_add_le)) drives all the remaining limit theorems.
- **The countdown chain** ([`countdownKernel`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Renewal/Countdown/Basic.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.countdownKernel)) is the Markov chain the lattice Blackwell proof runs on. It is irreducible on its natural states, its return time has the gap law, and its mean return time is the mean gap.
- **Coupling at the meeting time** gives total-variation convergence ([`tendsto_tvDist_pow_comp_of_parallelComp_isRecurrent`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Coupling/Convergence.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.tendsto_tvDist_pow_comp_of_parallelComp_isRecurrent)) whenever the product chain is irreducible and recurrent.

## The frontier

- **Positive recurrence and the invariant probability**: define positive and null recurrence through [`meanReturnTime`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/MarkovChain/Recurrence.html#ProbabilityTheory.Kernel.meanReturnTime). Then prove that an irreducible chain has an invariant probability exactly when it is positive recurrent, with π({x}) = 1/mₓ. This closes Layer 2.3 and unlocks Layer 3.5.
- **Convergence to equilibrium**: deduce tvDist(νκⁿ, π) → 0 from the existing coupling theorem, using π ⊗ π to make the product chain positive recurrent. Then assemble the null-recurrent pointwise limit from the averaged occupation bounds already present.
- **Lattice Blackwell theorem**: identify U({an}) with the countdown chain's return probabilities, then apply Layer 3 convergence. This waits on the previous item.
- **Nonlattice Blackwell theorem**: vague subsequence limits of translated renewal measures exist. What remains is that a bounded solution of η = η ∗ F is a multiple of Lebesgue measure, plus identifying the constant.
- **Key renewal theorem and excess-life limit**: the Darboux step approximations and tail truncations are ready, and both theorems need the nonlattice Blackwell theorem.
