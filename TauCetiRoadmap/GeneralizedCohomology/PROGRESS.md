## GeneralizedCohomology: 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-21 (`a525d7e` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's first work lays groundwork for its spectral model. The main piece is the pointed simplicial category. Pointed simplicial sets, taken as the undercategory of `Δ[0]`, are shown [equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Pointed/Basic.html#TauCeti.PointedSSet.simplicialPointedEquivalence) to simplicial pointed types. The degreewise smash product makes them [symmetric monoidal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Pointed/Smash.html#TauCeti.PointedSSet.symmetricCategory) and [closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Pointed/Closed.html#TauCeti.PointedSSet.monoidalClosed), with the pointed mapping simplicial set as internal hom. On top of this come [tensors and cotensors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Pointed/Tensor.html#TauCeti.PointedSSet.simplicialTensorCotensorAdjunction) by unpointed simplicial sets, the circle [as `Δ[1]/∂Δ[1]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Pointed/Circle.html#TauCeti.SSet.S1.isPushout), and the [suspension–loop adjunction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Pointed/Suspension.html#TauCeti.PointedSSet.suspensionLoopAdjunction). The same smash and closed structure was first built on pointed types. For symmetric spectra, the [finite-bijection groupoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/FiniteBijection.html#TauCeti.FiniteBijection) now exists, with symmetric groups as its automorphism groups. Symmetric sequences, Day convolution and the sphere module do not exist yet.

The second strand is the topological mapping-path construction. A map is a Hurewicz fibration [exactly when it has a continuous lifting function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/MappingPath/LiftingFunction.html#TauCeti.isHurewiczFibration_iff_nonempty_liftingFunction). Using this, the endpoint map of a mapping-path replacement is [a Hurewicz fibration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/MappingPath/LiftingFunction.html#TauCeti.MappingPathSpace.isHurewiczFibration_endpoint), and so is the fibre's [source projection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/MappingPath/PathFibration.html#TauCeti.MappingPathFiber.isHurewiczFibration_proj). Once a reference path is chosen, each fibre of that projection is [homotopy equivalent to the loop space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/MappingPath/Fiber.html#TauCeti.MappingPathFiber.projFiberHomotopyEquivLoopSpace). Functoriality in commutative squares and lemmas about prepending paths round this out.

## GeneralizedCohomology: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`c1c6773` to `938acbb`)

Symmetric spectra now exist in the form the roadmap asks for. A [symmetric spectrum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Spectra/SymmetricSpectrum.html#TauCeti.SymmetricSpectrum) is a module over the [sphere sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Spectra/SymmetricSpectrum.html#TauCeti.SymmetricSequence.sphere) `n ↦ (S¹)^∧n`, taken as a monoid among [symmetric sequences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Spectra/SymmetricSequence.html#TauCeti.SymmetricSequence), the functors from the finite-bijection groupoid to pointed simplicial sets under Day convolution. Unit, associativity and equivariance of the structure maps are therefore actual module laws. Getting there took three steps. First, Day convolution was made [symmetric monoidal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/DayConvolution/Braided.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.DayFunctor.symmetricCategory). Second, smash powers `X^∧n` were built, with permutation actions and block concatenation, as a [braided monoidal symmetric sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Pointed/Smash/Power.html#TauCeti.PointedSSet.instBraidedFiniteBijectionSmashPowFunctor). Third, a general theorem shows the Day monoid of a lax braided functor [is commutative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/DayConvolution/Monoid.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.DayFunctor.isCommMonObj) (TauCeti#20392). Since the forgetful functor from modules [creates limits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Mod/Limits.html#CategoryTheory.Mod.forgetCreatesLimits), limits of symmetric spectra are [computed levelwise](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Spectra/Limits.html#TauCeti.SymmetricSpectrum.isLimitOfLevelwise). Still missing are the wedge formula for Day convolution, free spectra, colimits and the smash product over the sphere.

On the fibration side, take a Hurewicz fibration and consider paths in the total space that start in a fibre and end at a chosen point of it. This space is now shown to be [homotopy equivalent to the loop space of the base](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/HurewiczFibration/FiberInclusion.html#TauCeti.FiberInclusionPathFiber.loopHomotopyEquiv), on every loop component and with no connectedness hypothesis. The comparison is in fact a fibre homotopy equivalence over the loop space. The proof rests on the fact that [lifts of the same family of paths are vertically homotopic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/OpenBoxLifting.html#TauCeti.IsHurewiczFibration.exists_homotopy_lifts).
