## GrothendieckEulerForms: 2026-08-14 to 2026-09-01 (`f6ce37b` to `8745177`)

This first stretch of work builds the spine from Quillen exact structures to numerical Grothendieck groups, and two named theorems sit on it. The **resolution theorem** for finite projective resolutions ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/ProjectiveResolution.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.resolutionEquiv)) says that when `P` consists of `E`-projectives containing zero and closed under biproducts, the inclusion of `P` into the objects of finite `P`-dimension induces an isomorphism on exact K₀, the inverse sending a class to the alternating class of any finite resolution; it rests on **Schanuel's lemma** ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.nonempty_iso_biprod_of_projective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Projective.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.nonempty_iso_biprod_of_projective)) and the **horseshoe lemma** ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.exists_conflation_biprod_of_conflation_of_projective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Projective.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.exists_conflation_biprod_of_conflation_of_projective)). The **Euler–Poincaré theorem** in abelian K₀ ([`TauCeti.AbelianK0.eulerChar_eq_homologyEulerChar`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.AbelianK0.eulerChar_eq_homologyEulerChar)) identifies the alternating class of a bounded cochain complex's terms with that of its cohomology, making the Euler class a quasi-isomorphism invariant.

Beneath these, Layer 0 is essentially complete: `ExactStructure` with E0/E1/E2 as instances, opposite and transported structures, split, abelian and induced structures, and conflation-exact functors compared with Mathlib's exactness ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.isConflationExact_abelian_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Functor.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.isConflationExact_abelian_iff)). One presentation engine ([`TauCeti.PresentedK0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Presentation.html#TauCeti.PresentedK0)) instantiates split, exact, abelian and triangulated K₀ with universal properties and functoriality; triangulated K₀ gets `[X⟦n⟧] = (-1)ⁿ[X]` ([`TauCeti.TriangulatedK0.of_shift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.TriangulatedK0.of_shift)). Graded exact categories put a ℤ-action on each K₀, normalised to `[M{n}] = qⁿ[M]` ([`TauCeti.LaurentK0.T_smul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Laurent.html#TauCeti.LaurentK0.T_smul)). Euler-admissibility keeps Ext-finiteness and eventual vanishing separate; the Ext-Euler characteristic is additive in both variables, descends to a biadditive pairing ([`TauCeti.extEulerPairing`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Descent.html#TauCeti.extEulerPairing)), evaluates on projectives as a Hom dimension, and is computed by any finite Hom-finite projective resolution ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.FiniteResolution.extEuler_eq_homEuler`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Resolution.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.FiniteResolution.extEuler_eq_homEuler)). Numerical quotients make it nondegenerate ([`TauCeti.extEulerNumericalPairing_nondegenerate`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Numerical.html#TauCeti.extEulerNumericalPairing_nondegenerate)); the Laurent-valued graded χ_q ([`TauCeti.gradedExtEuler`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Graded/Basic.html#TauCeti.gradedExtEuler)) is defined and descends, but only to ordinary exact K₀ (TauCeti#5105).

Absent: the resolution theorem covers only projective resolutions, not resolving subcategories; no bounded-complex comparisons with homotopy or derived K₀; Layer 4 (algebras, the Cartan map) untouched; χ_q has no sesquilinearity, no descent to `LaurentK0`, no specialization at `q = ±1`; graded and linear resolutions, the quiver comparison, and every worked example, including the mandatory dual-numbers rejection, remain undone.

## GrothendieckEulerForms: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30 (`8745177` to `e440f4e`)

Weibel's **resolution theorem** now holds in its general form: for a [resolving subcategory](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving) of an exact category, the inclusion induces an isomorphism on exact K₀, inverse to the alternating class of any finite resolution ([`resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv)). It is natural, invariant under equivalence, and has a graded version over the Laurent ring ([`laurentResolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Laurent/Resolving.html#TauCeti.GradedExactStructure.IsResolving.laurentResolutionEquiv)). For modules, it makes the Cartan map `K₀(proj R) → G₀(mod R)` an isomorphism whenever every finitely generated module has a finite projective resolution ([`cartanEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/CartanMap/Basic.html#TauCeti.cartanEquiv)). Under the same hypothesis, **Cartan unimodularity** follows, determinant ±1 ([`cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/UnimodularCartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one)), and the Ext-Euler matrix of the simples is the transposed inverse Cartan matrix ([`extEulerMatrix_eq_inverseCartanMatrix_transpose`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/ExtEulerMatrix.html#TauCeti.extEulerMatrix_eq_inverseCartanMatrix_transpose)). Morita invariance, bases of simple and indecomposable-projective classes, and the multiplicity formula for Hom out of projective covers came with it (TauCeti#8802).

The q-Euler form is now a sesquilinear form on graded K₀, q-antilinear in its first argument ([`gradedExtEulerSesquilinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Graded/Sesquilinear.html#TauCeti.gradedExtEulerSesquilinear)). It specializes at `q = ±1`, and at `q = 1` it recovers the ordinary Ext-Euler characteristic, under explicit graded-to-ungraded Ext comparison hypotheses ([`laurentEval_one_gradedExtEuler`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Graded/ForgetGrading.html#TauCeti.IsGradedExtComparison.laurentEval_one_gradedExtEuler)). For a finite acyclic quiver, the categorical Ext-Euler pairing **is the Ringel form** on dimension vectors ([`extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Quiver/ExtEuler.html#TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0)). Both bounded-complex comparisons landed: split K₀ is triangulated K₀ of the bounded homotopy category ([`boundedHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/BoundedHomotopy.html#TauCeti.SplitK0.boundedHomotopyEquiv)), and abelian K₀ is triangulated K₀ of the bounded derived category ([`boundedDerivedK0Equiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Derived.html#TauCeti.AbelianK0.boundedDerivedK0Equiv)). All five worked examples are in. They include the mandatory dual-numbers rejection ([`not_isEulerAdmissible_dualNumberResidue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/DualNumbers.html#TauCeti.not_isEulerAdmissible_dualNumberResidue)) and an oriented E₈ quiver whose symmetrized categorical Euler matrix is the E₈ Cartan matrix ([`extEulerMatrix_add_transpose`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/E8/ExtEuler.html#TauCeti.Quiver.E8.extEulerMatrix_add_transpose)).

Four pieces are still missing: the graded Cartan map, the graded quiver comparison with the path-length grading, linear resolutions, and comparison maps between projective resolutions up to chain homotopy.

## GrothendieckEulerForms: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (`e440f4e` to `8a32441`)

This window closes the product item in Layers 1 and 2. Exact K₀ now turns a product of exact categories, with the [componentwise exact structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Product.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.prod), into the product of the two exact Grothendieck groups ([`ExactK0.prodEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Product.html#TauCeti.ExactK0.prodEquiv)). Triangulated K₀ does the same for a product of pretriangulated categories ([`TriangulatedK0.prodEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Product.html#TauCeti.TriangulatedK0.prodEquiv)). Both equivalences are natural in exact or triangulated functors in each variable. Before the second could be stated, products needed their own triangulated structure: the componentwise shift, and the [pretriangulated structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Triangulated/Prod.html#TauCeti.instPretriangulatedProd) in which a triangle is distinguished exactly when both of its projections are (TauCeti#10422). Some general lemmas came with this, among them that pullback and pushout squares in a product category are detected componentwise.

The rest of the window tidied up the presentations. Additivity on conflations, or on distinguished triangles, now implies invariance under isomorphism ([`ExactK0.AdditiveInvariant.map_iso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Exact.html#TauCeti.ExactK0.AdditiveInvariant.map_iso)). Functors with isomorphic values induce the same map on K₀. Exact and triangulated K₀ are quotients of split K₀, and exact K₀ equals split K₀ when every conflation splits ([`fromSplitEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Exact.html#TauCeti.ExactK0.fromSplitEquiv)). From Layer 1 the canonical isomorphisms between presentations are still missing, along with their cocycle laws. From Layer 2 the complex-level Euler comparison for full exact subcategories is still missing.

## GrothendieckEulerForms: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05 (`8a32441` to `1d09589`)

The **graded Cartan map** `K₀^gr(proj A) → G₀^gr(mod A)` now exists as a map of `ℤ[q,q⁻¹]`-modules ([`gradedCartanMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Basic.html#TauCeti.gradedCartanMap)), built on a new abelian [category of graded modules](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/Basic.html#TauCeti.GradedModuleCat). Its matrix in arbitrary bases has a change-of-basis formula ([`gradedCartanMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Matrix.html#TauCeti.gradedCartanMatrix)), and after specializing at `q = 1`, forgetting the grading recovers the ungraded Cartan map ([`gradedFiniteModulesForgetK0_comp_gradedCartanMapSpecialized`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/ForgetGrading.html#TauCeti.gradedFiniteModulesForgetK0_comp_gradedCartanMapSpecialized)). Idempotent coordinates show that classes with Kronecker-delta graded dimensions, such as graded simples, are linearly independent over the Laurent ring. Graded simples and projective covers are not yet used as bases, and nothing yet relates the matrix to a graded Poincaré matrix.

Layer 3's projective-resolution calculus is in. The [comparison map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Resolution/Comparison.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.FiniteResolution.lift) into a finite resolution is unique up to chain homotopy, any two finite projective resolutions of an object are [homotopy equivalent](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Resolution/Comparison.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.FiniteResolution.homotopyEquiv), and the [finite projective horseshoe lemma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Resolution/Horseshoe.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.FiniteResolution.horseshoe) holds. Lifts also commute with the grading shift up to homotopy ([`liftShiftHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Graded/Projective.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.FiniteResolution.liftShiftHomotopy)). [Linear resolutions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/GradedModule/Resolution.html#TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear) are defined and shown to be [minimal](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/GradedModule/Resolution.html#TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear.isMinimal). They force `Extⁿ(M,N{j}) = 0` for `n ≠ j` ([`subsingleton_ext_shiftObj_of_ne`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/LinearResolution.html#TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear.subsingleton_ext_shiftObj_of_ne)), but the converse is not proved.

Two older gaps closed. Presentations over different models of the isomorphism classes now carry [canonical isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/IsoClassModel.html#TauCeti.IsoClassModel.K0.equiv) that are natural and satisfy the cocycle law. The Euler–Poincaré theorem now holds in exact K₀ of an extension-closed subcategory, provided the boundaries and cohomology lie in it ([`sum_negOnePow_of_X_eq_sum_negOnePow_of_homology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.ExactK0.sum_negOnePow_of_X_eq_sum_negOnePow_of_homology)). G₀ of an Artinian ring is also shown to be free on the simple classes (TauCeti#10712).
