### Named results

- **The resolution theorem** — for a resolving subcategory of an exact category, inclusion induces an isomorphism on exact K₀, whose inverse sends an object to the alternating class of any finite resolution. It is natural, invariant under equivalence, and has a graded Laurent version ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv)).
- **Cartan unimodularity** — if every finitely generated module has a finite projective resolution, the Cartan map `K₀(proj A) → G₀(mod A)` is an isomorphism and the Cartan matrix has determinant ±1. The dual numbers, with Cartan matrix `[2]`, show that the hypothesis is needed ([`TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/UnimodularCartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one)).
- **The Ext-Euler pairing of a quiver is the Ringel form** — for a finite acyclic quiver, the categorical pairing on `G₀` of the path algebra is the Euler form of dimension vectors ([`TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Quiver/ExtEuler.html#TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0)).
- **The sesquilinear q-Euler form** — `χ_q` descends to graded K₀ as a Laurent module, q-antilinear in the first argument and q-linear in the second ([`TauCeti.gradedExtEulerSesquilinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Graded/Sesquilinear.html#TauCeti.gradedExtEulerSesquilinear)).
- **Uniqueness of projective resolutions** — any two finite resolutions of an object by relative projectives are homotopy equivalent, since comparison maps are unique up to chain homotopy. A finite projective horseshoe lemma accompanies it ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.FiniteResolution.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Resolution/Comparison.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.FiniteResolution.homotopyEquiv)).

## The frontier

- **The graded quiver comparison** — for `kQ` with the path-length grading, identify the bigraded Ext-Euler form with the graded Ringel form, then specialize it to the ungraded form at `q = 1` through the Ext comparison hypotheses. The zigzag roadmap compares against this statement.
- **Graded bases and the Cartan matrix** — make graded simples and indecomposable graded projectives into Laurent bases, using the linear-independence criterion already proved. Then show that the graded Cartan matrix becomes the ungraded one at `q = 1`, and relate it to the graded Ext-Euler matrix by the conjugate-transpose inverse.
- **The converse linearity criterion** — show that diagonal vanishing of `Ext^{i,j}(S,S)` forces a linear resolution. Also show that it is equivalent to the graded Cartan and Poincaré series being inverse under `q ↦ -q`. This needs the graded-basis work above.
- **A graded horseshoe** — a horseshoe statement compatible with the shift `{1}`, to match the graded comparison theorem that is already proved.
