### Named results

- **The resolution theorem** — for a resolving subcategory of an exact category, inclusion induces an isomorphism on exact K₀, inverse to the alternating class of any finite resolution; it is natural, equivalence-invariant, and has a graded Laurent version ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv)).
- **Cartan unimodularity** — if every finitely generated module has a finite projective resolution, the Cartan map `K₀(proj A) → G₀(mod A)` is an isomorphism and the Cartan matrix has determinant ±1; the dual numbers, with Cartan matrix `[2]`, show that the hypothesis is needed ([`TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/UnimodularCartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one)).
- **The Ext-Euler pairing of a quiver is the Ringel form** — for a finite acyclic quiver, the categorical pairing on `G₀` of finitely generated path-algebra modules is the Euler form of dimension vectors, and its symmetrization is the polarized Tits form ([`TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Quiver/ExtEuler.html#TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0)).
- **The sesquilinear q-Euler form** — `χ_q` descends to the Laurent-module graded K₀, where it is q-antilinear in the first argument and q-linear in the second ([`TauCeti.gradedExtEulerSesquilinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Graded/Sesquilinear.html#TauCeti.gradedExtEulerSesquilinear)).
- **The derived comparison** — abelian K₀ is triangulated K₀ of the bounded derived category, a complex going to the alternating sum of its cohomology classes; the companion result identifies split K₀ with triangulated K₀ of the bounded homotopy category ([`TauCeti.AbelianK0.boundedDerivedK0Equiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Derived.html#TauCeti.AbelianK0.boundedDerivedK0Equiv)).
