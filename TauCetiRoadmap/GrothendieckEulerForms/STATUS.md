### Named results

- **The resolution theorem** — for a resolving subcategory of an exact category, inclusion induces an isomorphism on exact K₀, inverse to the alternating class of any finite resolution. There is a graded Laurent version ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv)).
- **Cartan unimodularity** — when modules have finite projective resolutions, the Cartan matrix has determinant ±1 ([`TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/UnimodularCartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one)), and the graded matrix has determinant ±qⁿ. The dual numbers, with Cartan matrix `[2]`, show that the hypothesis is needed.
- **The graded quiver comparison** — for `kQ` with the path-length grading, the graded Cartan matrix is `(1 - qAᵀ)⁻¹` and the q-Euler matrix of the vertex simples is `I - q⁻¹A`, which recovers the Ringel form at `q = 1` ([`TauCeti.PathAlgebra.gradedExtEulerForm_gradedVertexSimpleClassBasis_eq_inv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/PathAlgebra/GradedExtEuler/CartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.PathAlgebra.gradedExtEulerForm_gradedVertexSimpleClassBasis_eq_inv)).
- **Linearity as a Cartan–Poincaré identity** — over a finite-dimensional, nonnegatively graded algebra with semisimple degree-zero part, the simples have linear resolutions exactly when `Cᵀ · P_tot(-q)` is the diagonal of the `dim End(Sᵢ)`. This is an identity of formal power series, with no bound on Ext ([`TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_iff_cartan_poincare_maslovEulerPowerSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/ExtEuler/PowerSeries.html#TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_iff_cartan_poincare_maslovEulerPowerSeries)).
- **The Betti criterion for linearity** — a minimal resolution is linear exactly when its Betti Poincaré series are supported on the diagonal ([`TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsMinimal.isLinear_iff_diagonal_bettiPoincarePowerSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/LinearResolution/Betti.html#TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsMinimal.isLinear_iff_diagonal_bettiPoincarePowerSeries)). This rests on Betti numbers being Ext dimensions against shifted simples, up to the factor `dᵢ = gdim(eᵢ • Sᵢ)`, which is assumed nonzero.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.bettiPoincarePowerSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/LinearResolution/Betti.html#TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.bettiPoincarePowerSeries) — the two-variable Betti series of a resolution by finitely generated terms. - [`TauCeti.gradedProjectiveSimplePairing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Idempotent/Projective/SimplePairing.html#TauCeti.gradedProjectiveSimplePairing) — the graded multiplicity pairing. It is perfect and identifies graded Cartan entries with graded multiplicities, and it agrees with the q-Euler form exactly when the simples are split.
- [`TauCeti.bigradedExtPoincarePowerSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Graded/Poincare/PowerSeries.html#TauCeti.bigradedExtPoincarePowerSeries) — the bigraded Ext Poincaré series with infinite cohomological support. It is the Ext-side counterpart of the Betti series and makes the Cartan identities meaningful for algebras whose simples have no finite resolution.
