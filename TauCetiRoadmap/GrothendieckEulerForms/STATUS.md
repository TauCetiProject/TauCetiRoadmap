### Named results

- **The resolution theorem** — for a resolving subcategory of an exact category, inclusion induces an isomorphism on exact K₀ whose inverse is the alternating class of any finite resolution. It is natural, equivalence-invariant, and has a graded Laurent version ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv)).
- **Cartan unimodularity** — if every finitely generated module has a finite projective resolution, the Cartan map `K₀(proj A) → G₀(mod A)` is an isomorphism and the Cartan matrix has determinant ±1. The dual numbers, whose Cartan matrix is `[2]`, show that the hypothesis is needed ([`TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/UnimodularCartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one)).
- **The Ext-Euler pairing of a quiver is the Ringel form** — for a finite acyclic quiver, the categorical pairing on `G₀` of finitely generated path-algebra modules is the Euler form of dimension vectors ([`TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Quiver/ExtEuler.html#TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0)).
- **The sesquilinear q-Euler form** — `χ_q` descends to the Laurent-module graded K₀, where it is q-antilinear in the first argument and q-linear in the second ([`TauCeti.gradedExtEulerSesquilinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Graded/Sesquilinear.html#TauCeti.gradedExtEulerSesquilinear)).
- **The derived comparison** — abelian K₀ is the triangulated K₀ of the bounded derived category, with a complex going to the alternating sum of its cohomology classes. A companion result identifies split K₀ with the triangulated K₀ of the bounded homotopy category ([`TauCeti.AbelianK0.boundedDerivedK0Equiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Derived.html#TauCeti.AbelianK0.boundedDerivedK0Equiv)).

## The frontier

- **The graded Cartan map** — `K₀^gr(proj A) → G₀^gr(mod A)` as a map of Laurent modules, with its graded multiplicity matrix and recovery of the ungraded map at `q = 1`. This feeds the Layer 6 graded Cartan and Gram matrices, and it needs graded simples and graded projective covers.
- **The graded quiver comparison** — for `kQ` with the path-length grading, identify the bigraded Ext-Euler form with the graded Ringel form, and specialize it to the ungraded one through the Ext comparison hypotheses. The zigzag roadmap compares against this statement.
- **Linear resolutions and the Koszul boundary** — the source has only a generated-in-degree predicate for graded modules. The definition of a linear resolution, its equivalence with diagonal Ext vanishing, and minimality all remain.
- **Projective-resolution calculus** — comparison maps into a fixed projective resolution that are unique up to chain homotopy, and graded versions of the comparison and horseshoe lemmas.
- **Choice independence and the subcategory Euler comparison** — canonical isomorphisms between presentations, with their naturality and cocycle laws, and the complex-level Euler-class comparison for full exact subcategories with explicit closure hypotheses.
