### Named results

- **The resolution theorem** — for a resolving subcategory of an exact category, inclusion induces an isomorphism on exact K₀, whose inverse sends an object to the alternating class of any finite resolution. It is natural, invariant under equivalence, and has a graded Laurent version ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv)).
- **Cartan unimodularity** — if every finitely generated module has a finite projective resolution, the Cartan map `K₀(proj A) → G₀(mod A)` is an isomorphism and the Cartan matrix has determinant ±1. The dual numbers, with Cartan matrix `[2]`, show that the hypothesis is needed ([`TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/UnimodularCartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one)).
- **The graded Cartan isomorphism** — if every finite graded module has a finite resolution by finite graded projectives, the graded Cartan map is an isomorphism of `ℤ[q,q⁻¹]`-modules, and its inverse is the alternating class of a resolution ([`TauCeti.gradedCartanEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Resolution.html#TauCeti.gradedCartanEquiv)).
- **The Ext-Euler pairing of a quiver is the Ringel form** — for a finite acyclic quiver, the categorical pairing on `G₀` of the path algebra is the Euler form of dimension vectors ([`TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Quiver/ExtEuler.html#TauCeti.extEulerPairing_eq_pathAlgebraEulerPairingK0)).
- **Graded Cartan columns count paths** — for `kQ` with the path-length grading, the `qⁿ` coefficient of the `eᵢ`-coordinate of `[kQ eⱼ]` is the number of length-`n` paths from `j` to `i` ([`TauCeti.PathAlgebra.coeff_gradedCartanMap_gradedVertexProjectiveClass`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/PathAlgebra/GradedProjective.html#TauCeti.PathAlgebra.coeff_gradedCartanMap_gradedVertexProjectiveClass)).

## The frontier

- **The graded quiver comparison** — for `kQ` with the path-length grading, identify the bigraded Ext-Euler form with the graded Ringel form. Then specialize at `q = 1` through the Ext comparison hypotheses. The path-counting formula for graded Cartan columns is now available as input. The zigzag roadmap compares against this statement.
- **A graded projective basis** — prove that the classes of an exhaustive family of indecomposable graded projectives are linearly independent over `ℤ[q,q⁻¹]`, which completes the spanning result already proved.
- **Cartan versus Ext-Euler in graded bases** — show that the graded Ext-Euler matrix is the conjugate-transpose inverse of the graded Cartan matrix, and work out the projective, simple and dual bases of Layer 6. This needs the projective basis above.
- **The converse linearity criterion** — show that diagonal vanishing of `Ext^{i,j}(S,S)` forces a linear resolution, and that this is equivalent to the graded Cartan and Poincaré series being inverse under `q ↦ -q`.
- **A graded horseshoe** — a horseshoe statement compatible with the shift `{1}`, to match the graded comparison theorem that is already proved.
