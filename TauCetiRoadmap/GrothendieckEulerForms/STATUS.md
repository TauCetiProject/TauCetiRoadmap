### Named results

- **The resolution theorem** — for a resolving subcategory of an exact category, inclusion induces an isomorphism on exact K₀. Its inverse sends an object to the alternating class of any finite resolution, and there is a graded Laurent version ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv)).
- **Cartan unimodularity** — if every finitely generated module has a finite projective resolution, the Cartan map is an isomorphism and the Cartan matrix has determinant ±1. The dual numbers, with Cartan matrix `[2]`, show that the hypothesis is needed ([`TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/UnimodularCartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one)). In the graded case the dual numbers with `ε` in degree two play the same role: their Cartan matrix is [`1 + q²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/GradedDualNumbers.html#TauCeti.gradedCartanMatrix_dualNumber).
- **The graded Cartan isomorphism** — if every finite graded module has a finite resolution by finite graded projectives, the graded Cartan map is an isomorphism of `ℤ[q,q⁻¹]`-modules ([`TauCeti.gradedCartanEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Resolution.html#TauCeti.gradedCartanEquiv)). The q-Euler matrix of the graded simples is then the inverse of the conjugate transpose of the graded Cartan matrix.
- **The graded quiver comparison** — for `kQ` with the path-length grading, the graded Cartan matrix is `(1 - qAᵀ)⁻¹`. The q-Euler matrix of the vertex simples is `I - q⁻¹A`, which is the graded Ringel form. At `q = 1` this matrix becomes the ordinary Ext-Euler pairing, which is the Ringel form on dimension vectors ([`TauCeti.PathAlgebra.gradedExtEulerForm_gradedVertexSimpleClassBasis_eq_inv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/PathAlgebra/GradedExtEuler/CartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.PathAlgebra.gradedExtEulerForm_gradedVertexSimpleClassBasis_eq_inv), [`laurentSpecialize_gradedExtEulerForm_eq_extEulerPairing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/PathAlgebra/GradedExtEuler/ForgetGrading.html#TauCeti.PathAlgebra.laurentSpecialize_gradedExtEulerForm_eq_extEulerPairing)).
- **The linearity criterion** — take a nonnegatively graded algebra whose degree-zero part is semisimple. Minimal resolutions of its simples are linear exactly when their full bigraded Ext Poincaré matrix is diagonal, and that diagonality gives the Cartan–Poincaré identity `Cᵀ P(-q) = 1` ([`TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_iff_bigradedExtPoincareMatrix_isDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/LinearResolution/Poincare.html#TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_iff_bigradedExtPoincareMatrix_isDiagonal), [`cartan_poincare_of_bigradedExtPoincareMatrix_isDiagonal_of_semisimple`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/ExtEuler/Linearity.html#TauCeti.cartan_poincare_of_bigradedExtPoincareMatrix_isDiagonal_of_semisimple)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`TauCeti.gradedCartanMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Matrix.html#TauCeti.gradedCartanMatrix) — the graded Cartan matrix in arbitrary Laurent bases. Its change-of-basis formula is what lets the projective and simple bases and the q-Euler matrix be compared at all.
- [`TauCeti.gradedIdempotentProjectiveBasis`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Idempotent/Projective.html#TauCeti.gradedIdempotentProjectiveBasis) — the projectives `A eᵢ` as a Laurent basis of graded projective K₀, when the degree-zero part is spanned by orthogonal idempotents. With it, the Cartan matrix is square in canonical bases, so graded unimodularity and the conjugate-transpose relation can be stated.
- [`TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/GradedModule/Resolution.html#TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear) — linear graded resolutions, the shared vocabulary for the Koszul and zigzag roadmaps. Their existence is now detected by diagonal Ext.

## The frontier

- **The Cartan–Poincaré converse** — the README asks that linearity be *equivalent* to the graded Cartan and Poincaré series being inverse under `q ↦ -q`. Only the forward direction is proved. The single-variable identity cannot see Ext off the diagonal, and the source says outright that it does not claim the converse. For quadratic algebras, "numerically Koszul" is known not to imply Koszul, so the target looks unprovable as stated. The bigraded criterion, which is proved, is the natural replacement, and the README should be amended to match.
- **Non-split degree-zero parts** — the graded projective basis, graded unimodularity and the conjugate-transpose relation are proved only when the degree-zero part is spanned by orthogonal idempotents. A version that keeps the `dim_k End(Sᵢ)` factors, as the ungraded Layer 4 does, is not established here.
