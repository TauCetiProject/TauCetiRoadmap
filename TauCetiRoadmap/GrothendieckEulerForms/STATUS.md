### Named results

- **The resolution theorem** — for a resolving subcategory of an exact category, inclusion induces an isomorphism on exact K₀. Its inverse sends an object to the alternating class of any finite resolution, and there is a graded Laurent version ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/GrothendieckGroup/Resolving.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsResolving.resolutionEquiv)).
- **Cartan unimodularity** — when modules have finite projective resolutions, the Cartan matrix has determinant ±1 ([`TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/UnimodularCartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.cartanMatrix_det_eq_one_or_neg_one)) and the graded Cartan matrix has determinant ±qⁿ ([`TauCeti.exists_gradedCartanMatrix_det_eq_T_or_neg_T_of_le_admitsFiniteResolution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Resolution.html#TauCeti.exists_gradedCartanMatrix_det_eq_T_or_neg_T_of_le_admitsFiniteResolution)). The dual numbers, with Cartan matrix `[2]`, show that the hypothesis is needed.
- **Linearity as a Cartan–Poincaré identity** — over a finite-dimensional, nonnegatively graded algebra with semisimple degree-zero part, the simples have linear resolutions exactly when `Cᵀ · P_tot(-q)` is the diagonal of the `dim End(Sᵢ)`. Here `P_tot` is the total Ext Poincaré matrix ([`TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_iff_cartan_poincare_maslovEuler`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/ExtEuler/Linearity.html#TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_iff_cartan_poincare_maslovEuler)). The single-variable identity built from diagonal Ext is deliberately not claimed to imply linearity.
- **The graded quiver comparison** — for `kQ` with the path-length grading, the graded Cartan matrix is `(1 - qAᵀ)⁻¹` and the q-Euler matrix of the vertex simples is `I - q⁻¹A`. At `q = 1` this is the Ringel form ([`TauCeti.PathAlgebra.gradedExtEulerForm_gradedVertexSimpleClassBasis_eq_inv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/PathAlgebra/GradedExtEuler/CartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.PathAlgebra.gradedExtEulerForm_gradedVertexSimpleClassBasis_eq_inv)).
- **Euler duality over a general field** — the projective/module Euler form has zero radicals over every field, but it is perfect exactly when every simple is absolutely simple ([`TauCeti.projectiveExtEuler_isPerfPair_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/EulerDuality.html#TauCeti.projectiveExtEuler_isPerfPair_iff)). The graded q-Euler pairing behaves the same way.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`TauCeti.gradedCartanMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/CartanMap/Matrix.html#TauCeti.gradedCartanMatrix) — the graded Cartan matrix in arbitrary Laurent bases. Its change-of-basis formula is what allows the Cartan, q-Euler and Poincaré matrices to be compared.
- [`TauCeti.bigradedExtPoincarePowerSeries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/EulerCharacteristic/ExtEuler/Graded/Poincare/PowerSeries.html#TauCeti.bigradedExtPoincarePowerSeries) — the bigraded Ext Poincaré series, allowing infinite cohomological support. It is what makes linearity testable for algebras such as the dual numbers, whose simples have no finite resolution.
- [`TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/GradedModule/Resolution.html#TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear) — linear graded resolutions, the one notion of linearity shared with the Koszul and zigzag roadmaps.

## The frontier

- **The Betti Poincaré series** — define the series of a module from a minimal graded projective resolution, prove that it does not depend on the resolution, that diagonal support means linear, and that the series of `M{1}` is `q` times that of `M`. Then run the §3.7 checks for `1 ⟶ 2` and `k[x]/(x²)`. For minimal resolutions, the landed Ext Poincaré series against simples carry the same data, so comparing the two is the natural route.
- **The Betti form of the Cartan–Poincaré identity** — prove `C(q) · B(q, -1) = I` coefficientwise under local finiteness, and as a Laurent-matrix identity when the resolutions are finite. The q-Euler version `Cᵀ · Ē = D` and the linear case are already proved, so this is mostly translation once the Betti series exists.
- **Update the §3.7 status in the README** — it says only the forward direction of the diagonal criterion has landed. The converse is proved, for bounded-below minimal resolutions tested against concentrated targets, and over a semisimple degree-zero part it is proved [against simples](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/GradedModuleCat/Idempotent/MinimalResolution.html#TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.exists_isLinear_iff_subsingleton_ext_simple).
