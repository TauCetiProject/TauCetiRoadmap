## DeRhamCohomologyAndDuality: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`e5452b4` to `aa6e43e`)

This first report on the roadmap covers two foundational pieces, one at the start of each of its first two layers. For Layer 1, the predicate [`HasLocalPotentials`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/LocalPotentials.html#TauCeti.HasLocalPotentials) (TauCeti#39275) says that a covector field is, on a ball about each point, the derivative of a `C¹` function. It is stated on any real normed space rather than only the plane, and it requires no regularity of the field itself. The main input is [the closed-form case](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/DifferentialForm/LocalPotentials.html#TauCeti.hasLocalPotentials_of_extDeriv_eq_zero): a closed `C¹` one-form with complete coefficients has local potentials, by the flat Poincaré lemma on balls. Around it sit the locality statements the roadmap asks for: the predicate [can be checked on any open cover](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/LocalPotentials.html#TauCeti.hasLocalPotentials_iUnion_iff), restricts to open subsets, [pulls back along `C¹` maps](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/LocalPotentials.html#TauCeti.HasLocalPotentials.pullback), and is linear in the field. Uniqueness of potentials up to a constant is not yet stated, and the continuous path integral has not begun.

For Layer 2, the [strict inward cone](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/StrictInward.html#TauCeti.strictInwardCone) (TauCeti#35685) collects the directions along which a ball of proportionally growing radius stays inside a set. For a convex set, a direction belongs to the cone [exactly when its positive ray meets the interior](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/StrictInward.html#Convex.mem_strictInwardCone_iff). The cone is convex, open, and stable under positive scaling, and its ball estimates are [uniform near each point and direction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/StrictInward.html#Convex.exists_uniform_ball_subset_of_mem_strictInwardCone). The roadmap's half-line and quadrant checks are proved. All of this is flat. Invariance under chart changes and strictly inward vector fields on a manifold with corners are still to come.
