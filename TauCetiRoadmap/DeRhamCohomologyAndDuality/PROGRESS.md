## DeRhamCohomologyAndDuality: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`e5452b4` to `aa6e43e`)

This first report on the roadmap covers two foundational pieces, one at the start of each of its first two layers. For Layer 1, the predicate [`HasLocalPotentials`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/LocalPotentials.html#TauCeti.HasLocalPotentials) (TauCeti#39275) says that a covector field is, on a ball about each point, the derivative of a `C¹` function. It is stated on any real normed space rather than only the plane, and it requires no regularity of the field itself. The main input is [the closed-form case](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/DifferentialForm/LocalPotentials.html#TauCeti.hasLocalPotentials_of_extDeriv_eq_zero): a closed `C¹` one-form with complete coefficients has local potentials, by the flat Poincaré lemma on balls. Around it sit the locality statements the roadmap asks for: the predicate [can be checked on any open cover](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/LocalPotentials.html#TauCeti.hasLocalPotentials_iUnion_iff), restricts to open subsets, [pulls back along `C¹` maps](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/LocalPotentials.html#TauCeti.HasLocalPotentials.pullback), and is linear in the field. Uniqueness of potentials up to a constant is not yet stated, and the continuous path integral has not begun.

For Layer 2, the [strict inward cone](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/StrictInward.html#TauCeti.strictInwardCone) (TauCeti#35685) collects the directions along which a ball of proportionally growing radius stays inside a set. For a convex set, a direction belongs to the cone [exactly when its positive ray meets the interior](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/StrictInward.html#Convex.mem_strictInwardCone_iff). The cone is convex, open, and stable under positive scaling, and its ball estimates are [uniform near each point and direction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Cone/StrictInward.html#Convex.exists_uniform_ball_subset_of_mem_strictInwardCone). The roadmap's half-line and quadrant checks are proved. All of this is flat. Invariance under chart changes and strictly inward vector fields on a manifold with corners are still to come.

## DeRhamCohomologyAndDuality: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`aa6e43e` to `b235000`)

The strict inward cone now lives on manifolds, which finishes §2.1 of Layer 2. The [strict inward tangent cone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/StrictInward.html#TauCeti.strictInwardTangentCone) at a point reads a tangent vector in the preferred chart and asks that it be strictly inward to the range of the model. It does not matter which chart is used, because [derivatives of chart changes preserve strict inwardness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/StrictInward.html#TauCeti.tangentCoordChange_mem_strictInwardCone_iff). More generally, [any differential of dense range does](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/StrictInward.html#MDifferentiableAt.mapsTo_strictInwardTangentCone). The headline is the [existence of strictly inward vector fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/StrictInward.html#TauCeti.exists_contMDiffSection_mem_strictInwardTangentCone) (TauCeti#39010): a Hausdorff σ-compact manifold over a finite-dimensional model with corners carries a smooth vector field that is strictly inward at every point. It is built by gluing local constant fields that point at an interior anchor. The roadmap's interior check also holds: the cone is [the whole tangent space exactly at interior points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorField/StrictInward.html#TauCeti.strictInwardTangentCone_eq_univ_iff). The forward semiflow and inward domination of §2.2 have not started.

Layer 5 has begun (TauCeti#36574). The [incidence equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Incidence.html#TauCeti.incidenceEquation) `F(x, p) − j(y)` and [regular incidence parameters](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Incidence.html#TauCeti.IsRegularIncidenceParameter) are defined as the roadmap specifies, together with their calculus API. For differentiable maps, regularity is [surjectivity of `(u, v) ↦ D_xF u − Dj v`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Incidence.html#TauCeti.isRegularIncidenceParameter_iff_surjective). When `dim E + dim W < dim V`, a regular slice [misses the image of `j` entirely](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Incidence.html#TauCeti.IsRegularIncidenceParameter.disjoint_range). Several parts of §5.1 have not landed: universal transversality, avoidance for countable families, and invariance under open covers and affine isomorphisms. The Haar-null theorem of §5.2 is also still to come.
