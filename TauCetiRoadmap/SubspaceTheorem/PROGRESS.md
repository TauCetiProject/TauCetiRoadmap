## SubspaceTheorem: 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-08 (`e898f6d` to `a4fcb57`)

The first work on this roadmap is the geometry of numbers that Schmidt's proof stands on. None of its finiteness theorems has landed yet. The centrepiece is [Evertse's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Approximation/Evertse.html#NumberField.exists_evertse) (TauCeti#24839): a basis that a system of weighted forms controls at the infinite places and at `S₀` can be corrected, by triangular combinations that are integral outside `S₀`, so that every form is small on it simultaneously, with a constant that depends only on `K` and the dimension. It rests on [simultaneous approximation by `S₀`-integers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Approximation/SInteger.html#NumberField.exists_forall_apply_add_le). Approximation domains over `K` are now a lattice meeting a compact convex body. The body's [volume](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Subspace/Volume.html#TauCeti.NumberField.volume_approxBody) is computed exactly, and the volume-to-covolume ratio lies between two constants times `Q ^ weight` ([upper](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Subspace/Volume.html#TauCeti.NumberField.volume_div_covolume_le), [lower](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Subspace/Volume.html#TauCeti.NumberField.le_volume_div_covolume)). Beneath this sit [pseudo-bases](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/PseudoBasis.html#Submodule.exists_pseudoBasis) over a Dedekind domain, the [covolume of an `𝓞 K`-module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/CanonicalEmbedding/ModuleCovolume.html#Submodule.covolume_mixedImage) read place by place, and the linear algebra of wedge forms, including [Lemma 7.5.33](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/ExteriorPower/LinearForms.html#TauCeti.mem_span_iff_forall_plucker_mem_wedgeSpan).

The `S`-adic foundation is largely in place. Every absolute value lying over a finite place is [a root of a finite place of the extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/PlacesOver/FinitePlace.html#NumberField.exists_finitePlace_rpow_inv_eq_of_liesOver), and over a Galois extension these absolute values [form one orbit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/PlacesOver/Galois.html#NumberField.setOf_liesOver_finitePlace_eq_orbit). Every finite `S` enlarges to one over which [every height is a finite product of local sup norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Primitive.html#NumberField.exists_finset_superset_forall_exists_mulHeight_eq). Still missing are the matching classification over infinite places and Liouville's inequality over a number field.

Smaller pieces landed across the other layers. On the real side there is [Möbius invariance of the irrationality exponent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Exponent.html#TauCeti.irrationalityExponent_mul_add_div_mul_add), [Northcott for integer polynomials](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/SupNorm/Northcott.html#Polynomial.finite_setOf_natDegree_le_supNorm_le), and [Liouville's bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/MahlerExponent.html#IsAlgebraic.mahlerExponent_le) `w_n(ξ) ≤ D − 1`. For later layers there is the [simplex-volume tail bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/SimplexVolume/Basic.html#TauCeti.simplexVolume_le_exp_neg), approximation classes, and the multihomogeneous and index-along-forms groundwork for Layer 5.
