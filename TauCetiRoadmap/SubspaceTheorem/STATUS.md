### Named results

- **Evertse's lemma** — take a basis controlled by weighted linear forms at the infinite places and at `S₀`. Triangular corrections that are integral outside `S₀` make every form small on it at once, with a constant that depends only on `K` and the dimension ([`NumberField.exists_evertse`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Approximation/Evertse.html#NumberField.exists_evertse)).
- **The volume–covolume comparison for approximation domains** — for an approximation domain of level `Q`, the ratio of the body's volume to the lattice's covolume is `Q ^ approxWeight` up to two explicit constants. The finite-place factor is exact only after each bound is rounded down to a value of the place ([upper](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Subspace/Volume.html#TauCeti.NumberField.volume_div_covolume_le), [lower](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/Subspace/Volume.html#TauCeti.NumberField.le_volume_div_covolume)).
- **Absolute values above a finite place** — every absolute value of `F` lying over a finite place of `K` is the `(e f)`-th root of a unique finite place of `F` ([`NumberField.existsUnique_finitePlace_rpow_inv_eq_of_liesOver`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/PlacesOver/FinitePlace.html#NumberField.existsUnique_finitePlace_rpow_inv_eq_of_liesOver)), and over a Galois extension they form a single orbit ([`NumberField.setOf_liesOver_finitePlace_eq_orbit`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/PlacesOver/Galois.html#NumberField.setOf_liesOver_finitePlace_eq_orbit)).
- **Heights computed by finitely many places** — every finite `S` enlarges to an `S'` such that, after one scaling, every nonzero point's height is the product of its local sup norms over the infinite places and `S'` ([`NumberField.exists_finset_superset_forall_exists_mulHeight_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Height/Primitive.html#NumberField.exists_finset_superset_forall_exists_mulHeight_eq)).
- **Liouville's bound for Mahler's exponent** — a real algebraic number of degree `D` has `w_n ≤ D − 1` in every degree ([`IsAlgebraic.mahlerExponent_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DiophantineApproximation/MahlerExponent.html#IsAlgebraic.mahlerExponent_le)).
