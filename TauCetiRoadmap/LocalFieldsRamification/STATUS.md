## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** No layer is complete, but the arithmetic of one finite extension is largely built: finite extensions are local fields with a unique valuation and e·f = [L:K], the unit group is filtered and split, and unramified extensions have Frobenius and norms. Not begun: the deep-unit logarithm, the construction of unramified extensions, Herbrand's quotient theorem, Hasse–Arf, and Layer 4 beyond its ambient Galois model.

### Named results

- **The fundamental identity** — the intrinsic ramification index and residue degree of a finite extension of local fields satisfy e(L/K)·f(L/K) = [L:K] and agree with Mathlib's ideal-theoretic invariants ([`ramificationIndex_mul_inertiaDegree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/InertiaDegree.html#TauCeti.ramificationIndex_mul_inertiaDegree)).
- **Uniqueness of the extended valuation** — valuations on a finite extension that restrict to the valuation class of K are all equivalent, so the spectral-norm local-field structure is the only compatible one and K-automorphisms preserve it ([`finiteExtensionValuation_isEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/FiniteExtension/Basic.html#TauCeti.finiteExtensionValuation_isEquiv)).
- **The structure of the unit group** — a uniformizer splits Kˣ as ℤ × 𝒪[K]ˣ and the Teichmüller lift splits 𝒪[K]ˣ as μ_{q−1}(K) × U(K,1), both as topological groups ([`unitsEquivIntProd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/UnitsDecomposition.html#TauCeti.unitsEquivIntProd), [`unitFiltrationZeroEquivProd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/UnitsDecomposition.html#TauCeti.unitFiltrationZeroEquivProd)).
- **The sharp local square theorem** — outside characteristic 2, the filtration step U(K,n) consists of squares exactly when n ≥ 2v_K(2)+1 ([`unitFiltration_le_range_powMonoidHom_two_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Squares.html#TauCeti.unitFiltration_le_range_powMonoidHom_two_iff)).
- **Norms from an unramified extension** — the norm maps 𝒪[L]ˣ onto 𝒪[K]ˣ, so x ∈ Kˣ is a norm exactly when f(L/K) divides v_K(x) ([`mem_normGroup_iff_dvd_normalizedValuation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Norm/Unramified.html#TauCeti.mem_normGroup_iff_dvd_normalizedValuation)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Frobenius** — reduction identifies the Galois group of a finite unramified Galois extension with the residue Galois group ([`residueFieldAutEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ResidueCorrespondence.html#TauCeti.residueFieldAutEquiv)), and the lift of x ↦ x^q ([`frobeniusAlgEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ResidueCorrespondence.html#TauCeti.frobeniusAlgEquiv)) generates it, is pinned by its congruence modulo 𝓂[L], and restricts to Frobenius in towers.
- **The Herbrand function and upper numbering** — φ_{L/K} is an order automorphism of [−1, ∞) with its integral and finite-sum formulas ([`herbrandOrderIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.herbrandOrderIso)), and G^v = G_{ψ(v)} ([`upperRamificationGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.upperRamificationGroup)) is the filtration Herbrand's quotient theorem and Hasse–Arf concern.

### Roadmap coverage

All five layers are partial. **Layer 0** has the normalized valuation, the local-field structure on finite extensions with its tower and intermediate-field forms, and the invariants e, f, v_K(n) and the absolute ramification index; it lacks the unramified and totally ramified integral bases, a total-ramification predicate, and the worked ℚ_2(√2) values. **Layer 1** has the filtration and its graded pieces, the Teichmüller lift, both splittings, the square theorem, and the count and openness of n-th powers for n prime to the residue characteristic ([`card_powerClasses_of_isUnit`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/PowerSubgroup.html#TauCeti.card_powerClasses_of_isUnit)); it lacks the deep-unit logarithm and the mixed-characteristic results built on it, the pro-p property of U(K,1), and finiteness of μ(K). **Layer 2** has the predicate, residue correspondence and norms, but constructs no unramified extensions yet. **Layer 3** has the lower filtration, the Herbrand function, the norm on valuations and integral units, the different and discriminant core, and the quotient maps with the tame character (injective so far only when a uniformizer generates 𝒪[L] over a fixed subring); Eisenstein extensions, the tame structure theorem, Herbrand's quotient theorem, the shifted norm and Hasse–Arf are open. **Layer 4** has only the ambient model: the algebraic-closure and separable-closure models of G_K are identified as profinite groups, with the closed-subgroup correspondence and quotients by normal subextensions ([`absoluteGaloisGroupQuotientEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Galois/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Quotient.html#TauCeti.absoluteGaloisGroupQuotientEquiv)).

## The frontier

- **Local monogenicity for local fields** — proved for extensions of discrete valuation rings with separable residue extension ([`IsDiscreteValuationRing.exists_adjoin_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Monogenic.html#TauCeti.IsDiscreteValuationRing.exists_adjoin_eq_top)); the local-field form `exists_integerRing_adjoin_eq_top`, the Eisenstein power-basis valuation formula and K-isomorphism invariance of the different and discriminant exponents are among what Counting Totally Ramified Extensions still needs.
- **Herbrand's theorem** — (G/H)^v = G^v H/H for normal H and the tower laws for φ and ψ; with ψℕ and the shifted inclusion N(U(L, ψℕ(i))) ⊆ U(K,i) they feed Hasse–Arf.
- **Unramified extensions of each degree** — the four existence and uniqueness statements and Gal(K^ur/K) ≅ Ẑ; Layer 4's inertia group is defined through K^ur, and the Eisenstein factorization below needs the maximal unramified subextension.
- **Eisenstein and tame extensions** — a total-ramification predicate, the Eisenstein characterization, the factorization L/L_0/K, and L = K(π^{1/e}) when L/K is totally and tamely ramified; the factorization would also discharge the monogenicity hypothesis of the quotient embeddings.
- **The deep-unit logarithm** — log and exp on U(K,i) when (p−1)i > e, on which the roadmap builds the mixed-characteristic power-class count, openness of the power subgroup, and #(ℚ_2ˣ/(ℚ_2ˣ)²) = 8.
