## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All five layers are done. The summit, Iwasawa's presentation of the tame quotient, is proved, as is the general Hasse–Arf theorem. Recent work extends the valuation theory from finite to arbitrary algebraic extensions and makes tame inertia a named procyclic subgroup.

### Named results

- **Iwasawa's theorem** — the tame quotient G_K/P_K is isomorphic as a topological group to the profinite group ⟨σ, τ | στσ⁻¹ = τ^q⟩, via an isomorphism fixed by an arithmetic Frobenius lift and a tame inertia generator ([`nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_iwasawaGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Tame/Presentation.html#TauCeti.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_iwasawaGroup)).
- **The Hasse–Arf theorem** — every upper ramification break of a finite abelian extension of local fields is an integer (`hasseArf`, TauCeti#13558). The proof [detects upper breaks in a cyclic subextension](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand/CyclicReduction.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.upperJump_iff_exists_isCyclic_intermediateField) and then reduces the cyclic case to prime-power degree.
- **Herbrand's theorem** — for a normal subgroup H, restriction carries G^v onto (G/H)^v in the upper numbering ([`map_restrictNormalHom_upperRamificationGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand/UpperQuotient.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.map_restrictNormalHom_upperRamificationGroup)). The lower numbering fails this, and ℚ₂(ζ₈) gives a proved [witness](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/WorkedExamples/CyclotomicEight/Quotient.html#TauCeti.DyadicCyclotomicEight.map_restrictSqrtTwo_lowerRamificationGroup_three_ne).
- **Tame inertia is the prime-to-p Tate module** — the tame character gives I_K/P_K ≅ Ẑ^{(p')}(1), independently of the uniformizer and equivariantly for G_K ([`quotientWildInertiaSubgroupEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Tame/Character.html#TauCeti.quotientWildInertiaSubgroupEquiv)). As a subgroup of the tame quotient, tame inertia is [procyclic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Tame/Quotient.html#TauCeti.exists_topologicalClosure_zpowers_eq_tameInertia), generated by any element whose tame character generates.
- **The Galois group of the maximal unramified extension is Ẑ** — with arithmetic Frobenius as the canonical generator ([`maximalUnramifiedGaloisGroupEquivZHat`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/ZHat.html#TauCeti.maximalUnramifiedGaloisGroupEquivZHat)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Valuations on algebraic extensions.** The spectral norm makes any algebraic extension M/K, finite or infinite, a [normed field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/AlgebraicExtension/Basic.html#TauCeti.algebraicExtensionNormedField) whose valuation is [unique up to equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/AlgebraicExtension/Basic.html#TauCeti.algebraicExtensionValuation_isEquiv) and preserved by every K-automorphism. This lets K^alg, K^ur and K^t be handled as valued fields rather than only through their finite layers.
- **Wild inertia** [`wildInertiaSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/WildInertia.html#TauCeti.wildInertiaSubgroup) is the pro-p Sylow subgroup of inertia and cuts out the maximal tamely ramified extension. This is what makes the tame quotient a profinite group with a presentation.
- **The graded norm** [`normGradedMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Norm/Graded.html#TauCeti.normGradedMap) acts on the Herbrand-shifted pieces of the unit filtration. It is computed in every regime for cyclic extensions of prime degree, and it is how Hasse–Arf compares conductors with norms.
