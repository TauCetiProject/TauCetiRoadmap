## LocalFieldsRamification: 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-26 (`c31fdf0` to `759eb3e`)

The roadmap's first stretch built the arithmetic of a single finite extension of local fields. Its headline is the fundamental identity e(L/K)·f(L/K) = [L:K] for the intrinsic ramification index and residue degree ([`ramificationIndex_mul_inertiaDegree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/InertiaDegree.html#TauCeti.ramificationIndex_mul_inertiaDegree)), alongside a spectral-norm construction that makes every finite extension a local field and a proof that the extended valuation is unique ([`finiteExtensionValuation_isEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/FiniteExtension/Basic.html#TauCeti.finiteExtensionValuation_isEquiv)). The unit group is filtered and split, Kˣ ≅ ℤ × μ_{q−1}(K) × U(K,1) as topological groups ([`unitsEquivIntProd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/UnitsDecomposition.html#TauCeti.unitsEquivIntProd)), the local square theorem holds at its exact depth 2v_K(2)+1 ([`unitFiltration_le_range_powMonoidHom_two_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Squares.html#TauCeti.unitFiltration_le_range_powMonoidHom_two_iff)), and power classes are counted for exponents prime to the residue characteristic; the mixed-characteristic count waits on the deep-unit logarithm.

Unramified extensions have their predicate, compared with Mathlib's formally unramified algebras, the residue correspondence with its Frobenius ([`frobeniusAlgEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ResidueCorrespondence.html#TauCeti.frobeniusAlgEquiv)), and their norms: a Hensel lemma for norms makes the norm onto the integral units, so x is a norm exactly when f(L/K) divides v_K(x) ([`mem_normGroup_iff_dvd_normalizedValuation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Norm/Unramified.html#TauCeti.mem_normGroup_iff_dvd_normalizedValuation)).

On the ramification side, the lower filtration gained real indexing, normality, finiteness and compatibility with subgroups, and the Herbrand function arrived as an order automorphism of [−1, ∞) with the upper numbering ([`herbrandOrderIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.herbrandOrderIso)); Herbrand's quotient theorem is not yet proved. The quotients G_i/G_{i+1} map to the graded pieces of the unit filtration, giving the tame character; injectivity is proved only when a uniformizer generates 𝒪[L] over a subring the group fixes. The discriminant exponent satisfies δ = f·d and the different exponent its tower law ([`differentExponent_tower`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Different/Tower.html#TauCeti.differentExponent_tower)). Local monogenicity is proved for discrete valuation rings but not yet stated for local fields, and the absolute Galois group has its profinite structure and closed-subgroup correspondence.
