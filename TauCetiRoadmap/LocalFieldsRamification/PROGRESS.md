## LocalFieldsRamification: 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-26 (`c31fdf0` to `759eb3e`)

The roadmap's first stretch built the arithmetic of a single finite extension of local fields. Its headline is the fundamental identity e(L/K)·f(L/K) = [L:K] for the intrinsic ramification index and residue degree ([`ramificationIndex_mul_inertiaDegree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/InertiaDegree.html#TauCeti.ramificationIndex_mul_inertiaDegree)), alongside a spectral-norm construction that makes every finite extension a local field and a proof that the extended valuation is unique ([`finiteExtensionValuation_isEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/FiniteExtension/Basic.html#TauCeti.finiteExtensionValuation_isEquiv)). The unit group is filtered and split, Kˣ ≅ ℤ × μ_{q−1}(K) × U(K,1) as topological groups ([`unitsEquivIntProd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/UnitsDecomposition.html#TauCeti.unitsEquivIntProd)), the local square theorem holds at its exact depth 2v_K(2)+1 ([`unitFiltration_le_range_powMonoidHom_two_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Squares.html#TauCeti.unitFiltration_le_range_powMonoidHom_two_iff)), and power classes are counted for exponents prime to the residue characteristic; the mixed-characteristic count waits on the deep-unit logarithm.

Unramified extensions have their predicate, compared with Mathlib's formally unramified algebras, the residue correspondence with its Frobenius ([`frobeniusAlgEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ResidueCorrespondence.html#TauCeti.frobeniusAlgEquiv)), and their norms: a Hensel lemma for norms makes the norm onto the integral units, so x is a norm exactly when f(L/K) divides v_K(x) ([`mem_normGroup_iff_dvd_normalizedValuation`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Norm/Unramified.html#TauCeti.mem_normGroup_iff_dvd_normalizedValuation)).

On the ramification side, the lower filtration gained real indexing, normality, finiteness and compatibility with subgroups, and the Herbrand function arrived as an order automorphism of [−1, ∞) with the upper numbering ([`herbrandOrderIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.herbrandOrderIso)); Herbrand's quotient theorem is not yet proved. The quotients G_i/G_{i+1} map to the graded pieces of the unit filtration, giving the tame character; injectivity is proved only when a uniformizer generates 𝒪[L] over a subring the group fixes. The discriminant exponent satisfies δ = f·d and the different exponent its tower law ([`differentExponent_tower`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Different/Tower.html#TauCeti.differentExponent_tower)). Local monogenicity is proved for discrete valuation rings but not yet stated for local fields, and the absolute Galois group has its profinite structure and closed-subgroup correspondence.

## LocalFieldsRamification: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

Unramified extensions are now constructed, not just described. For each f ≥ 1 there is exactly one unramified extension of degree f inside a separably closed field, the splitting field of X^{q^f} − X ([`existsUnique_isUnramified_finrank_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Existence.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isUnramified_finrank_eq)). Their union is the maximal unramified extension, and its Galois group is identified with Ẑ, with arithmetic Frobenius sent to the generator ([`maximalUnramifiedGaloisGroupEquivZHat`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/ZHat.html#TauCeti.maximalUnramifiedGaloisGroupEquivZHat)). That was enough to start Layer 4. The inertia subgroup is defined, with G_K/I_K ≅ Gal(K^ur/K) as topological groups ([`quotientInertiaSubgroupEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Inertia.html#TauCeti.quotientInertiaSubgroupEquiv)), and the Frobenius lifts are exactly a coset of inertia. Around this sit the étale criterion for unramifiedness, stability under composita, and the worked example ℚ_2(√5), whose norms are the elements of even valuation.

On the ramified side, an extension is totally ramified exactly when an Eisenstein root generates it ([`isTotallyRamified_iff_exists_eisenstein_generator`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Eisenstein/TotallyRamified.html#TauCeti.isTotallyRamified_iff_exists_eisenstein_generator)). Totally and tamely ramified extensions have the form K(π^{1/e}) ([`exists_pow_eq_uniformizer_of_isTamelyRamified`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/TamelyRamified.html#TauCeti.IsTotallyRamified.exists_pow_eq_uniformizer_of_isTamelyRamified)). Herbrand's theorem is proved in its lower-numbering form: the image of G_u in G/H is (G/H)_{φ(u)} ([`map_restrictNormalHom_lowerRamificationGroupReal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand/Quotient.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.map_restrictNormalHom_lowerRamificationGroupReal)). The tower laws for φ, ψ and ψℕ come with it. Local monogenicity now holds for local fields, which makes Hilbert's formula for the different unconditional, and G_1 is shown to be the unique Sylow p-subgroup of inertia. The upper-numbering form of Herbrand's theorem, the shifted norm inclusion and Hasse–Arf are still open.

For units, n-th power classes are counted for every n with n ≠ 0 in K, including p ∣ n ([`card_powerClasses`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/PowerSubgroup/Basic.html#TauCeti.card_powerClasses)); ℚ_2ˣ has eight square classes, power subgroups are open, and U(K,1) is pro-p. The exponential and logarithm are mutually inverse on deep units, but that is not yet packaged as an isomorphism.

## LocalFieldsRamification: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05 (`8a32441` to `1d09589`)

Iwasawa's theorem landed: the tame quotient G_K/P_K is isomorphic, as a topological group, to the profinite group ⟨σ, τ | στσ⁻¹ = τ^q⟩ ([`nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_iwasawaGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Tame/Presentation.html#TauCeti.nonempty_continuousMulEquiv_iwasawaGroup)). The isomorphism is marked by an arithmetic Frobenius lift and a tame inertia generator, which determine it ([`tameQuotientEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Tame/Presentation.html#TauCeti.tameQuotientEquiv)). Its Ẑ coordinate agrees with the unramified one, and a translation to the geometric-Frobenius convention comes with it. Wild inertia now maps onto every finite G_1 ([`map_wildInertiaSubgroup_restrictNormalHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/WildInertia.html#TauCeti.map_wildInertiaSubgroup_restrictNormalHom)), so a finite Galois subextension lies in K^t exactly when it is tame.

For a cyclic extension of prime degree, the norm on the Herbrand-shifted graded pieces of the unit filtration is computed in all four regimes, including the additive map at a positive break ([`normGradedMap_at_break`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Norm/Graded.html#TauCeti.normGradedMap_at_break)). From this follow surjectivity above the break, index ℓ on units, and Hasse–Arf in prime degree: the upper break is an integer ([`exists_eq_intCast_of_finrank_prime`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Herbrand/HasseArf.html#TauCeti.LocalFieldsRamification.UpperJump.exists_eq_intCast_of_finrank_prime)). The general abelian case, by induction along a prime-degree tower, is not yet proved. The shifted inclusion N(U(L, ψℕ(i))) ⊆ U(K,i) holds for every Galois L/K. Also new are the conjugation action formula on G_i/G_{i+1}, the Galois criterion μ_e ⊆ K for tame radical extensions, and #G_1 = p^{v_p(e)}.

For units, the roots of unity μ(K) are finite, the product of the Teichmüller roots and the p-power roots ([`torsionUnitsEquivProd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/RootsOfUnity/Torsion.html#TauCeti.torsionUnitsEquivProd)), and deep units are free ℤ_p-modules of rank [K : ℚ_p]. Unramified extensions acquired rigidity: their embeddings correspond bijectively to embeddings of residue fields ([`residueFieldHomEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/Rigidity.html#TauCeti.IsUnramified.residueFieldHomEquiv)), and the finite ones form a lattice isomorphic to the positive integers under divisibility.
