# Hamiltonian Floer operations: geometric product and multiplicative PSS

Construct the geometric pair-of-pants product on capped Hamiltonian Floer complexes, its unit and sewing identities, and the multiplicative Piunikhin–Salamon–Schwarz comparison with GromovWittenTheory G7. The construction includes classical oriented counts on positive spherical-monotone and spherical-c1-zero targets, and the rational virtual punctured application on arbitrary closed symplectic targets. The definitive specification is this README; `Suggested.lean` gives nonexhaustive interface prototypes.

## H0. Scope and prerequisites

The main target is a closed connected boundaryless symplectic manifold (X^(2n),ω), n≥1, with its actual finite-dimensional Hausdorff second-countable smooth structure. Work componentwise for disconnected targets; the zero-dimensional case has the separate point/count model. Hamiltonians are smooth period-one functions; the contractible one-periodic orbits used as generators are nondegenerate. Their finiteness is the actual clopen-contractible-sector argument of DGF7.C.1. Degenerate Hamiltonians have an invariant homology interpretation through specified nondegenerate perturbations and continuation, not unperturbed generator counts.

There are two geometric coefficient branches. **Classical**: positive spherical-monotone or spherical-c1-zero targets with DGF7.C's actual regular count-dimensional cylinder/PSS package, first mod2 and then oriented integral counts and scalar extensions. This owner proves the additional punctured product/family and sphere-incidence theorems; a cylinder exclusion theorem is not a product exclusion theorem. **Rational virtual**: arbitrary closed symplectic targets, with a coherent punctured Hamiltonian atlas/count package constructed here, including cylinder, plane and comparison families outside DGF's classical hypotheses. G7's rational coefficients are the common comparison coefficients. An integral product in the classical branch is retained; an arbitrary closed integral virtual theory is not inferred from a rational implicit-atlas trace.

## H1. Coefficients, caps and degree dictionary before counts

Use DGF's contractible convention: i_(X_H)ω=dH, u_s+J(u_t−X_H)=0, positive ends are inputs, negative ends outputs, A_H(x,D)=∫Dω+∫Hdt, μ=−CZ_HF, μ(constant)=ind(H)−n. For D'−D=B, A increases by ω(B) and μ by 2c1(B). Let S_sph be the spherical Hurewicz image and Γ=S_sph/(kerω∩kerc1). Caps are actual Γ-torsors. The joint kernel, not area alone, preserves equal-energy different-degree classes. Construct class addition, cap gluing and cap/reference changes before matrices.

The Floer ring uses e^B with degree +2c1(B) and valuation ν(B)=−ω(B). For a punctured pair of pants u with input reference caps D1,D2 and output cap D0, put A=D0+u−D1−D2 using the oriented compactification. Its coefficient in the reference-output basis is e^(−A). The raw Fredholm index is

    ind D_u = μ1+μ2−μ0−n+2c1(A).

Thus a rigid coefficient satisfies μ0−2c1(A)=μ1+μ2−n: the product CF_i⊗CF_j→CF_(i+j−n) has degree −n. Define the comparison cochain regrading C^k=CF_(n−k), with differential d_C=d_CF; its product has degree zero and d_C has degree +1. The cap-dependent orientation lines are regraded at the same time. The regrading is an actual construction with sign/shift identities, not a renaming of an unrelated complex.

Let Γ_G be G7's chosen class group with energy and Chern maps. First work over a common class group retaining the classes needed by both constructions. Prove descent to any joint-period quotient and the finite coefficient-summing map, rather than assuming GW invariants are individually cap-quotient classes. The comparison sends q^A to e^(−A): ν(−A)=ω(A). G7's degree +2c1(A) becomes cochain coefficient degree −deg_CF(e^(−A))=+2c1(A). Its energy completion therefore maps in the correct direction. The map is a continuous unital coefficient map only with the actual positive-rescaling period identity and finite-sublevel hypotheses of N1. No t↦1 map on the entire completed Novikov ring is asserted.

General Γ need not have injective valuation, and the resulting graded completed algebra need not be a field. Positive monotonicity has the finite Laurent model before completion; c1-zero has its separate completed integer grading. For arbitrary classes use homogeneous graded pieces and the energy-compatible completion (or the resulting residue grading), not an integer direct sum containing every inhomogeneous infinite series. ## H3. Energy, support and raw compactness before regular counts

With compatible J put E=∫ω(u_s−X_(K_s),u_t−X_(K_t))≥0. The exact identity is

    E = ∫u*ω + ∫∂ K(u) − ∫R_K(u)
      = A1(x1,D1)+A2(x2,D2)−A0(x0,D0)+ω(A)−∫R_K(u).

If B=−A is the output monomial, ν(B)=ω(A). A bound ∫sup_X|R_K|≤C implies ν(B)≥−A1−A2+A0−C. Conversely ν(B)<L bounds E above by A1+A2−A0+L+C. On tame compact windows use the actual positive symmetric metric/coercivity and operator homotopy; do not call ω(·,J·) a symmetric metric for a merely tame J.
