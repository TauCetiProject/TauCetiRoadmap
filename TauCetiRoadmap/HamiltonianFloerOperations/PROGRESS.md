## HamiltonianFloerOperations: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (`cb38e65` to `273ea28`)

The first piece of this roadmap is the coefficient group for capped Hamiltonian Floer theory: the [period group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PeriodGroup.html#TauCeti.Floer.PeriodGroup) Γ, the quotient of the spherical classes by the joint kernel of symplectic area and first Chern number (TauCeti#19069). Quotienting by both kernels rather than by area alone keeps apart classes that have equal energy but different degree, and the file proves exactly that: a class is [determined by its area and Chern number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PeriodGroup.html#TauCeti.Floer.PeriodGroup.ext_iff), so Γ is torsion-free. It also identifies the special cases the roadmap's classical branch uses. For a spherically monotone pair Γ is [cyclic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PeriodGroup.html#TauCeti.Floer.PeriodGroup.isAddCyclic_of_area_eq_mul_chern). When the Chern number vanishes, [area embeds Γ into ℝ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PeriodGroup.html#TauCeti.Floer.PeriodGroup.area_injective_of_chern_eq_zero). And Γ is [trivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PeriodGroup.html#TauCeti.Floer.PeriodGroup.subsingleton_iff) exactly when both periods vanish, as they do on aspherical targets. Induced maps of period groups are functorial and always injective.

All of this is stated for an arbitrary additive group with two homomorphisms to ℝ and ℤ. It is not yet attached to the Hurewicz image of an actual symplectic manifold, and caps, the Novikov ring and every analytic layer of the roadmap remain to be built.

## HamiltonianFloerOperations: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `b257e0f`)

The headline of this window is the [Floer energy identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Energy.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.integral_floerEnergyDensity): for a Hamiltonian perturbation K = K_s ds + K_t dt, the energy of a map over a rectangle is its symplectic area, plus the boundary integral of K, minus the integral of the [curvature](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Energy.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.hamiltonianCurvature) R_K = ∂_s K_t − ∂_t K_s − {K_s, K_t}. This is the identity from which H3 gets its energy bounds (TauCeti#20092). The same file states the [Floer equation in coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Energy.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isComplexLinearMap_perturbedFDeriv_iff) and shows that [cylindrical perturbations have zero curvature](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Energy.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.hamiltonianCurvature_cylindrical). For now the target is a symplectic vector space and the domain is a coordinate rectangle. It is not yet a pair of pants mapping into a manifold.

The rest of H1's coefficient algebra arrived around it. [Cap classes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Cap.html#TauCeti.Floer.CapClass) form a torsor over the period group, and a cap class is [determined by its action and grading](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Cap.html#TauCeti.Floer.CapClass.mk_eq_mk_iff_action_eq_and_index_eq). The [product class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Cap.html#TauCeti.Floer.productClass) A = D₀ + u − D₁ − D₂ behaves correctly under recapping, so the product's [index does not depend on the caps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Cap.html#TauCeti.Floer.add_sub_add_two_mul_chern_productClass_eq). The [Floer Novikov ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/NovikovRing.html#TauCeti.Floer.NovikovRing) comes with its grading, and in the monotone case it [has the finite Laurent model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/NovikovRing.html#TauCeti.Floer.NovikovRing.forall_finite_lt_iff_bddAbove_degree). The [coefficient comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/CoefficientComparison.html#TauCeti.Floer.coefficientComparison) q^A ↦ e^(−A) is [continuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/CoefficientComparison.html#TauCeti.Floer.continuous_coefficientComparison), and it [descends to the joint-period quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/CoefficientComparison.html#TauCeti.Floer.periodGroupComparison) by finite summation. All of this is still abstract: the class groups are arbitrary, not yet the Hurewicz image of a manifold or G7's actual class group.

Looking ahead to H6, a [kernel product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovModule/Bilinear.html#TauCeti.NovikovModule.kernelProduct) turns any coefficient kernel obeying an energy-type bound into a [Novikov-bilinear map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovModule/Bilinear.html#TauCeti.NovikovModule.novikovKernelProduct) on completed modules, and computes its matrix in reference generators. This is the algebraic shell of the pair-of-pants product, waiting for actual counts.

The energy identity now holds on an actual pair of pants.
