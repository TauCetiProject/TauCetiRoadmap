## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** H1's coefficient algebra is essentially complete in abstract form, including the cochain regrading. H2 and H3 are well under way on the explicit domain ℂ ∖ {0, 1}, mostly for a symplectic vector space target; a manifold target has the integrated energy identity and the pair-of-pants `J`. H6's product laws and the algebraic half of H7's comparison exist as formal algebra waiting for counts. There are no moduli spaces, counts or gluing, compactness is only local and for a vector-space target, and H5 has not begun.

### Named results

- **The pair-of-pants energy identity** — for a Floer solution on the pair of pants with the standard end perturbation, the energy is the input actions minus the output action, corrected by curvature. This gives H3's energy and action bounds, but only for a vector-space target ([`lintegral_floerEnergyDensity_pairOfPantsPerturbation_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PairOfPants/Action.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.lintegral_floerEnergyDensity_pairOfPantsPerturbation_eq)).
- **The Floer energy identity on rectangles** — energy is symplectic area plus the boundary term of K minus the curvature R_K. The pair-of-pants version is assembled from it ([`integral_floerEnergyDensity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Energy.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.integral_floerEnergyDensity)). It also holds [on a manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Manifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.integral_floerEnergyDensity).
- **Uniform curvature bounds under neck-stretching** — inserting a neck of any length where K is cylindrical leaves the integrated curvature bounded independently of the length, as H3 requires ([`integral_abs_hamiltonianCurvature_neckStretch_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Neck.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.integral_abs_hamiltonianCurvature_neckStretch_le)).
- **Bubbles are holomorphic for the frozen structure** — rescaled limits of Floer solutions with domain-dependent J are holomorphic for J frozen at the bubbling point, and a bubble's area is at most the energy concentrating there ([`isComplexLinearMap_of_tendsto_rescale`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Rescaling.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isComplexLinearMap_of_tendsto_rescale)).
- **Associativity on cohomology from a kernel homotopy** — if the two broken triple products differ by the boundary of a homotopy kernel, the product is associative on cocycles up to coboundaries, with Koszul signs. Commutativity, unit and continuation laws have the same form ([`kernelProduct_kernelProduct_sub_kernelProduct_kernelProduct_mem_coboundaries`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovModule/Associativity.html#TauCeti.NovikovModule.kernelProduct_kernelProduct_sub_kernelProduct_kernelProduct_mem_coboundaries)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The pair-of-pants perturbation** — [`pairOfPantsPerturbation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PairOfPants/Basic.html#TauCeti.Floer.pairOfPantsPerturbation) is the one-form on ℂ ∖ {0, 1} equal to H_q dt far out on each cylindrical end. All estimates on the actual domain are stated for it, and a [plumbing map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/ThricePuncturedSphere/Plumbing.html#TauCeti.ThricePuncturedSphere.Puncture.plumbingMap_image_union) glues two such domains into the four-punctured sphere that associativity needs.
- **The cochain regrading** — [`regradeEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Embedding/Regrade.html#ChainComplex.regradeEquivalence) gives C^k = K_(n−k) as an equivalence of categories, with homology identified degree by degree. It moves the product from degree −n to degree zero.
- **Kernel products on completed modules** — [`kernelProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovModule/Bilinear.html#TauCeti.NovikovModule.kernelProduct) turns a supported coefficient kernel into an `R`-bilinear map, which is [Novikov-bilinear for an invariant kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovModule/Bilinear.html#TauCeti.NovikovModule.novikovKernelProduct). Its [operator readout](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovModule/Readout.html#TauCeti.NovikovModule.kernelReadout) converts a count functional into an operator with H4's graded sign. Rigid pair-of-pants counts are meant to supply the kernel.

### Roadmap coverage

H1 is done in abstract form: period group, caps and recapping, the graded Novikov ring with its monotone Laurent model and residue grading, the coefficient comparison and the regrading. A [period group from the spherical Hurewicz image](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/SphericalPeriodGroup.html#TauCeti.Floer.SphericalPeriodGroup) of a space exists, but not the area and Chern class of an actual manifold, actual caps, G7's class group or regraded orientation lines. H2 is partial. It has the three-ended domain with end charts, truncations and plumbing, the end perturbation, plumbed tame and compatible structures, Cayley paths, the Floer linearization and the asymptotic operator of an orbit. On a manifold target it has the [pair-of-pants `J`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PairOfPants/AlmostComplex/Manifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.Compatible.pairOfPantsAlmostComplex) and a jointly smooth plumbed perturbation. Stable domain charts, corner jets and the plumbed `J` on a manifold are missing. H3 is partial. For a vector-space target it has energy identities, bounds and coercivity on the pair of pants, the first bubbling step and [convergence off finitely many bubbling points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Compactness.html#TauCeti.Floer.exists_pos_forall_tendstoLocallyUniformlyOn_subseq_off_finset); for a manifold target, the integrated energy identity on rectangles and, for weakly exact forms, on cylinders; and uniform curvature bounds over stretched, interpolated and cutoff families. The pair-of-pants identity for a manifold target, breaking at the ends and parameter faces are missing. H4 has localized Hamiltonian variations realizing every direction of the Floer operator, and the graded readout of count functionals; there is no Fredholm theory, transversality, gluing or orientation. H6 has the bilinear product and its formal laws, but no counts, no m2 and no unit cycle. H7 is partial: the [algebraic half of the PSS comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/NovikovModule/Comparison.html#TauCeti.NovikovModule.cohomologyMap_cohomologyProduct_kernelProduct_of_homotopy_inverse) and the cutoff and neck-stretching perturbations exist, but not the Morse quantum operation or the comparison family's moduli spaces. H5 is untouched, and no H8 check has been done.

## The frontier

- **The pair-of-pants energy identity on a manifold target** — assemble the manifold identity on rectangles over the truncated pair of pants, so the action and energy bounds hold in a closed symplectic manifold.
- **Gromov–Floer compactness** — rescaling, the frozen-J limit, the area bound, point picking and, for a vector-space target, convergence off finitely many bubbling points exist. The manifold target, breaking at the ends and parameter faces remain.
- **Attaching Γ and caps to a manifold** — give the spherical Hurewicz period group the true area and Chern class of a closed symplectic manifold, and instantiate G7's class group. This waits on a Chern-class interface from lower roadmaps.
- **The Fredholm problem on the punctured domain** — extend the linearization to the weighted operator on S with end asymptotics, and use the localized variations for transversality. This consumes HeegaardFloer F0–F2.
- **Counts feeding H6** — the product laws hold for any kernels obeying the boundary identities. Supplying them needs rigid and one-dimensional moduli spaces with oriented boundary, so this is blocked on compactness and H4.
