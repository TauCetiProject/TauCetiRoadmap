## Where this roadmap stands

Compactness off bubbling points and energy theory now hold on a closed manifold, and the cylinder operator is Fredholm for a vector-space target. ### Named results

- **The linearized Floer operator is Fredholm** — along a trajectory between nondegenerate orbits, D_u : W^{1,2}(ℝ × S¹) → L² is [Fredholm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Sobolev/Parametrix.html#TauCeti.SymplecticForm.isFredholm_floerSobolevOperator), with index depending only on the asymptotic orbits. This holds in a unitary trivialization of a vector-space target, and no index formula has landed.
- **Gromov–Floer compactness off bubbling points in a closed manifold** — solutions of bounded energy [converge off finitely many points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Compactness/Closed.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.exists_pos_forall_tendstoLocallyUniformlyOn_comp_subseq_off_finset), each point carrying a fixed quantum of energy. The same holds for [Floer pairs of pants](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PairOfPants/Compactness/Closed.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.exists_pos_forall_tendstoLocallyUniformlyOn_comp_subseq_off_finset_pairOfPantsPerturbation).
- **Breaking at the ends in a closed manifold** — a finite-energy Floer pair of pants converges to a periodic orbit along each of its three ends ([`exists_mem_periodicSolutions_tendstoUniformly_endChart_of_floer`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PairOfPants/End/Manifold.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.exists_mem_periodicSolutions_tendstoUniformly_endChart_of_floer)).
- **The pair-of-pants energy identity** — on a manifold target, the energy of a Floer pair of pants is the input actions minus the output action, corrected by curvature ([`integral_floerEnergyDensity_pairOfPantsPerturbation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/PairOfPants/Energy/Asymptotic.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.integral_floerEnergyDensity_pairOfPantsPerturbation)), with analogues for every family the product uses.
- **The ring of class-indexed counts** — given counts satisfying the Leibniz, unit, exchange and associativity identities, the counts form a graded-commutative, associative, unital [cohomology ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/Cap/Ring.html#TauCeti.Floer.cohomologyGRingOfCounts). PSS maps are multiplicative given the comparison homotopies.
