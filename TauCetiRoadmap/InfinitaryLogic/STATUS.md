## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Internal stabilization (Layer 2) is done. Karp's theorem and every ingredient of Scott's isomorphism theorem are proved. What remains is to state the summit as a single theorem for countable models, plus a few API gaps in the syntax and back-and-forth layers.

### Named results

- **Karp's theorem** — relational structures are potentially isomorphic exactly when they are L∞ω-equivalent. It is proved at any common coded carrier ([`karp_theorem_at`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Karp.html#TauCeti.karp_theorem_at)) and packaged over all carriers ([`karp_theorem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Karp.html#TauCeti.karp_theorem)), with no countability hypothesis.
- **Karp's theorem for countable structures** — countable relational structures are isomorphic exactly when they satisfy the same Lω₁ω sentences ([`countable_infEquivAt_nat_iff_iso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Karp.html#TauCeti.countable_infEquivAt_nat_iff_iso)). It goes through the fact that countable potentially isomorphic structures are isomorphic ([`countable_potentialIso_iff_iso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Countable.html#TauCeti.countable_potentialIso_iff_iso)).
- **The characteristic property of Scott formulas** — for every structure, of any cardinality, a tuple satisfies the level-α Scott formula of a tuple of a countable structure exactly when the two tuples are back-and-forth equivalent at level α ([`realize_scottFormula_iff_bfEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Formula.html#TauCeti.realize_scottFormula_iff_bfEquiv)).
- **The Scott sentence pins down potential isomorphism** — a countable structure satisfies its [Scott sentence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Sentence.html#TauCeti.realize_scottSentence_self), and every model of that sentence, countable or not, is potentially isomorphic to it ([`potentialIso_of_realize_scottSentence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/PotentialIso.html#TauCeti.potentialIso_of_realize_scottSentence)).
- **Countable Scott rank** — the internal Scott rank of a countable structure is below ω₁ ([`internalScottRank_lt_omega_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Rank.html#TauCeti.internalScottRank_lt_omega_one)). It is sandwiched between the stabilization ordinal and its successor, and that ordinal is the supremum of the orbit ranks ([`selfStabilizationOrdinal_eq_iSup_orbitRank`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Rank.html#TauCeti.selfStabilizationOrdinal_eq_iSup_orbitRank)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The fixed-carrier infinitary syntax** [`BoundedFormulaInf`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/Syntax.html#FirstOrder.Language.BoundedFormulaInf), with Lω₁ω as its ℕ-carried case. Together with [`IndexCoding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/IndexCoding.html#TauCeti.IndexCoding) and [`reindex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Infinitary/Reindex.html#FirstOrder.Language.BoundedFormulaInf.reindex), it lets conjunctions indexed by structure elements be written at any coded carrier. That is how both directions of Karp and the Scott formulas are expressed.
- **Ordinal back-and-forth equivalence** [`BFEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.BFEquiv), together with [`PotentialIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/PotentialIso.html#TauCeti.PotentialIso) as a system of finitely generated partial isomorphisms. The two are linked by a tuple presentation, and [potential isomorphism is exactly a winning coherent infinite Ehrenfeucht-Fraïssé strategy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/Game/Infinite.html#TauCeti.potentialIso_iff_nonempty_coherentEFStrategy).
- **Internal stabilization**: the back-and-forth refinement of a countable structure's own tuples [stabilizes below ω₁](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Stabilization.html#TauCeti.exists_complete_self_stabilization). This is what makes the Scott sentence unconditional.

Every ingredient is proved: models are potentially isomorphic, countable potential isomorphism gives isomorphism, and isomorphic copies satisfy the sentence ([`Equiv.realize_scottSentence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Sentence.html#FirstOrder.Language.Equiv.realize_scottSentence)).
