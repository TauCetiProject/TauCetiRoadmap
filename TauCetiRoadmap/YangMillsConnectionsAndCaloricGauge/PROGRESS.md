## YangMillsConnectionsAndCaloricGauge: 2026-11-17 to 2026-11-19 (`02582d6` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first piece of work defines its structure group. A [compact matrix structure group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/YangMills/StructureGroup.html#TauCeti.CompactMatrixStructureGroup) is a closed subgroup of the unitary group `U(n)` together with an inner product on its Lie algebra that is invariant under conjugation by the group. Connectedness is not assumed, so this invariance is part of the data. From it follow the two facts every later norm estimate relies on: each `Ad(g)` is [an isometry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/YangMills/StructureGroup.html#TauCeti.CompactMatrixStructureGroup.innerForm_matrixAdjointRepresentation), and each `ad X` is [skew-adjoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/YangMills/StructureGroup.html#TauCeti.CompactMatrixStructureGroup.innerForm_lie_left) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/58).

The same work shows that [every closed subgroup of `U(n)` is a structure group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/YangMills/StructureGroup.html#TauCeti.CompactMatrixStructureGroup.ofTraceForm) for the trace form `−Re tr(XY)`, which is [positive definite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Unitary/Matrix.html#ClosedSubgroup.posDef_matrixTraceForm). It also shows that structure groups are closed under [restriction to a closed subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/YangMills/StructureGroup.html#TauCeti.CompactMatrixStructureGroup.restrict) and under [block-diagonal products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/YangMills/StructureGroup.html#TauCeti.CompactMatrixStructureGroup.prod) with the orthogonal sum of inner products. The supporting matrix work describes the Lie algebra of a closed subgroup of `U(n)` concretely, as the matrices whose exponential line stays in the group, with its adjoint representation. It computes [the Lie algebra of a block-diagonal product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Unitary/Matrix.html#ClosedSubgroup.mem_matrixLieAlgebra_fromBlocksProd_iff), and proves that [an `Ad`-invariant form is `ad`-invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Unitary/Matrix.html#ClosedSubgroup.lieInvariant_of_isInvariantForm). The one item of §1.1 still missing is the description `g𝔤 = 𝔤g` of the tangent space of `G` at `g`.
