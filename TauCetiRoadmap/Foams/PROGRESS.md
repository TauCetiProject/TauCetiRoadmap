## Foams: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`6ffddaf` to `aa6e43e`)

The first foam result is in: [Frobenius reconstruction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Coalgebra.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_frobeniusCoalgebra), the Section 0.8 headline from the roadmap's acceptance tests. It says that a perfect trace ε on a finite commutative algebra determines exactly one coalgebra structure with counit ε that satisfies the Frobenius law (TauCeti#36521). The proof is more general than the target: it assumes a finite projective algebra over a commutative semiring rather than a finite free one over a ring. The comultiplication is [built](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Coalgebra.html#LinearMap.frobeniusCoalgebra) without choosing a basis, by transposing multiplication through the trace pairing. The familiar dual-basis formula, Δ(a) as a sum over a basis and its [trace-dual basis](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Coalgebra.html#LinearMap.traceDualBasis), is then [proved for every finite basis](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Coalgebra.html#LinearMap.frobeniusCoalgebra_comul_eq_sum), so independence of the basis comes for free.

The rest of Section 0.8 is still open: transport along trace-preserving isomorphisms, tensor products and scalar extension, the handle element, the even-graded version, and the check that the reconstructed coproduct on the dual numbers is the chosen Khovanov one. Nothing has yet landed for webs, prefoams, evaluations or the higher-categorical layers.

## Foams: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`aa6e43e` to `b235000`)

Section 0.1 of the roadmap, unlabelled closed webs, landed in one piece (TauCeti#36510). A [closed web](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb) is a finite oriented trivalent graph together with a finite set of vertex-free circles. Each interval edge keeps distinct source and target ends even when both meet one vertex, and each vertex numbers its three ends in cyclic order starting from the end of odd orientation, which makes it a [merge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.IsMerge) or a [split](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.IsSplit). The record comes with [isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.Iso), [disjoint union](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.sum) with its unit, associativity and symmetry isomorphisms, and two involutions: [orientation reversal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.reverse) and [mirror reversal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.mirror) of the cyclic orders. The [oriented theta web](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.theta) is the first example. Planarity is deliberately not part of the record; it is left as a separate condition, which Section 2.4 will supply.

The roadmap's checks for this section are proved. The [empty web and a single circle are not isomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.not_nonempty_iso_circle_empty). A loop [contributes two ends](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.card_incident) to its vertex, so [three times the number of vertices is twice the number of edges](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Foam/ClosedWeb/Basic.html#TauCeti.ClosedWeb.three_mul_card_vertex_eq_two_mul_card_edge). Cyclic successor has order three and fixes no end, while mirroring is an involution that turns successor into predecessor. Labels are not yet introduced. Prefoams (Section 0.2 onward), the rest of Section 0.8 and every later layer remain open.
