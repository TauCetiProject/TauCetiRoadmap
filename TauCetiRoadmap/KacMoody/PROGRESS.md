## KacMoody: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`3d56e59` to `7ca783d`)

The Kac–Moody roadmap now has realizations of a generalized Cartan matrix, and singular matrices are handled from the start. A [realization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization) has independent roots and coroots in a full ambient space. The library proves Kac's dimension bound, that [every realization has dimension at least `2|I| − rank A`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.two_mul_card_le_rank_add_finrank), and that [every matrix has a realization attaining it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#Matrix.nonempty_cartanRealization). The [Cartan center](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.cartanCenter) is everything the simple roots kill, and its part inside the coroot span is [the left kernel of the matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.cartanCenter_inf_corootSpan). The central splitting [writes every finite-dimensional realization as a minimal sub-realization plus a central summand](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/CentralSplitting.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.exists_isMinimal_restrict_isCompl) (TauCeti#31371). The extra directions of a non-minimal realization are therefore central.

A separate file treats the cone spanned by an independent family. It is [pointed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/LinearIndependent/NatSpan.html#LinearIndependent.eq_zero_of_mem_natSpan_of_neg_mem), and it has finitely many elements of each bounded height. It also has [only finitely many pairs in two translated negative cones with a given sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/LinearIndependent/NatSpan.html#LinearIndependent.finite_pairs_sub_mem_natSpan), which is the finiteness needed to multiply characters later on. None of this is attached to an integral weight lattice yet. Isomorphism of minimal realizations is also still missing in the singular case.
