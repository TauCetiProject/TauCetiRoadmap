## KacMoody: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`3d56e59` to `7ca783d`)

The Kac–Moody roadmap now has realizations of a generalized Cartan matrix, and singular matrices are handled from the start. A [realization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization) has independent roots and coroots in a full ambient space. The library proves Kac's dimension bound, that [every realization has dimension at least `2|I| − rank A`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.two_mul_card_le_rank_add_finrank), and that [every matrix has a realization attaining it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#Matrix.nonempty_cartanRealization). The [Cartan center](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.cartanCenter) is everything the simple roots kill, and its part inside the coroot span is [the left kernel of the matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Basic.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.cartanCenter_inf_corootSpan). The central splitting [writes every finite-dimensional realization as a minimal sub-realization plus a central summand](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/CentralSplitting.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.exists_isMinimal_restrict_isCompl) (TauCeti#31371). The extra directions of a non-minimal realization are therefore central.

A separate file treats the cone spanned by an independent family. It is [pointed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/LinearIndependent/NatSpan.html#LinearIndependent.eq_zero_of_mem_natSpan_of_neg_mem), and it has finitely many elements of each bounded height. It also has [only finitely many pairs in two translated negative cones with a given sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/LinearIndependent/NatSpan.html#LinearIndependent.finite_pairs_sub_mem_natSpan), which is the finiteness needed to multiply characters later on. None of this is attached to an integral weight lattice yet. Isomorphism of minimal realizations is also still missing in the singular case.

## KacMoody: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The Kac–Moody algebra itself now exists. [The algebra of a realization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/KacMoody/Basic.html#TauCeti.KacMoody.PresentedLieAlgebra) is presented on the whole Cartan space and the Chevalley generators, modulo the Cartan and Serre relators. It comes with [its universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/KacMoody/Basic.html#TauCeti.KacMoody.lift) and a comparison with Serre's presentation: the image of Serre's algebra is [the derived algebra `[𝔤, 𝔤]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/KacMoody/Basic.html#TauCeti.KacMoody.range_serreHom_eq_derivedSeries), and [together with the Cartan elements it spans `𝔤`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/KacMoody/Basic.html#TauCeti.KacMoody.range_serreHom_sup_range_cartanElement) (TauCeti#31601). Cartan injectivity is not proved yet, and neither is the central splitting of the algebra.

The [Weyl group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Weyl.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.weylGroup) of a realization arrived as well. Over a field of characteristic zero, [the order of `sᵢsⱼ` is exactly the Coxeter matrix entry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Weyl.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.orderOf_simpleReflection_mul_simpleReflection): 2, 3, 4 or 6 for Cartan products 0 to 3, and infinite from 4 on. So [the Coxeter group maps onto the Weyl group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Weyl.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.range_coxeterHom). The proof runs through a general analysis of products of two reflections using Chebyshev polynomials (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/68). The Weyl group also [preserves the root lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Weyl.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.apply_mem_rootLattice_iff_of_mem_weylGroup) and the weight lattice. The map is not yet shown to be injective, so the Weyl group is not yet identified with the Coxeter group.

Layer 0 closed its last gaps. [Any two minimal realizations are isomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.IsMinimal.exists_map_eq) with no nonsingularity hypothesis, so affine matrices are covered. Realizations [extend to field extensions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.baseChange), and this [preserves minimality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.isMinimal_baseChange). The earlier abstract cone results now apply to the simple roots: the [root lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/RootLattice.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.rootLattice), a pointed positive cone, height, dominance, and [finite bounded-height intervals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Cartan/Realization/RootLattice.html#TauCeti.CartanRealization.finite_setOf_rootLE_height_le) below a weight.
