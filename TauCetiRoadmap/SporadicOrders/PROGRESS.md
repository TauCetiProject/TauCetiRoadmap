## SporadicOrders: 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-09 (`25a4576` to `d92acc5`)

The general theory this roadmap rests on landed in full. The [stabilizer-chain order formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupAction/Stabilizer/Chain.html#Subgroup.prod_ncard_orbit_mul_card_stabilizerChain) says the order of a permutation group is the product of its basic-orbit lengths times the order of the last pointwise stabilizer, with no finiteness hypothesis. Alongside it come the [membership criterion along a base](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupAction/Stabilizer/Chain.html#Subgroup.mem_iff_exists_prod_eq), which decides membership by sifting through orbit representatives, and the description of [the orbit of a generated group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupAction/Orbit/Closure.html#MulAction.isLeast_orbit_closure) as the least set closed under the generators and their inverses. When the type is finite, the generators alone are enough.

Four of the ten ATLAS models now have their orders: [M₁₁](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/Sporadic/Mathieu/Eleven.html#TauCeti.Sporadic.card_M11), [M₁₂](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/Sporadic/Mathieu/Twelve.html#TauCeti.Sporadic.card_M12), [M₂₂](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/Sporadic/Mathieu/TwentyTwo.html#TauCeti.Sporadic.card_M22) and [Higman–Sims](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/Sporadic/HigmanSims/Order.html#TauCeti.Sporadic.card_HS), the last of order 44352000. The Mathieu orders go through a reusable [order theorem for sifting](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupAction/Stabilizer/Sift.html#Subgroup.natCard_eq_prod_length_of_siftCheck). If lists of stabilizer-chain elements absorb each other's products under sifting, the group's order is the product of the list lengths, and every hypothesis is a finite check that `decide` runs. Higman–Sims was done one basic orbit at a time instead. McLaughlin has its first two basic orbits, 275 and 112 (TauCeti#26359).

On the presentation side, the presented groups of M₁₁, M₁₂, [M₂₄](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/CFSG/Sporadic/Mathieu/TwentyFour/PermutationModel.html#TauCeti.Sporadic.exists_surjective_M24), J₁, Higman–Sims and McLaughlin now map onto their models. In each case the transcribed relators hold of permutations that generate the model, so none of these presentations collapses to the trivial group. Where the order is known, this becomes a lower bound: the presented M₂₂ and Higman–Sims groups, if finite, are at least as large as their models. M₂₃ and Co₃ have no surjection yet.
