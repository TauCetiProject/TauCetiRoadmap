## HopfologicalAlgebra: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`0ef487c` to `7ca783d`)

The first work on this roadmap lays the opening definition of Layer 1: the [left](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/Basic.html#TauCeti.leftIntegral) and [right](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/Basic.html#TauCeti.rightIntegral) integral submodules of a bialgebra, defined over any commutative semiring, containing zero, and with no normalization of the counit (TauCeti#28771). They come with the transport lemmas the roadmap asks for. Bialgebra isomorphisms [restrict to the integral spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/Basic.html#BialgEquiv.leftIntegralEquiv). The antipode [sends left integrals to right ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/Basic.html#TauCeti.antipode_mem_rightIntegral) and back without any bijectivity hypothesis, and when it is bijective it [identifies the two spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/Basic.html#TauCeti.integralAntipodeEquiv).

The group algebra is worked out as the first example. An element is an integral exactly when [its coefficients are constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/MonoidAlgebra.html#MonoidAlgebra.mem_leftIntegral_iff_coeff), so left and right integrals [agree even for a noncommutative group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/MonoidAlgebra.html#MonoidAlgebra.leftIntegral_eq_rightIntegral). A finite group algebra [has nonzero integrals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/MonoidAlgebra.html#MonoidAlgebra.leftIntegral_ne_bot) even when the group order vanishes in the coefficients. This covers the roadmap's characteristic-two check for `k[C₂]`, where the integral `1 + g` has counit zero. The window contains no existence or dimension theorem for integrals of a general finite-dimensional Hopf algebra.

## HopfologicalAlgebra: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The main result this window is the general form of Khovanov's Proposition 1. Over a finite-dimensional algebra with a Frobenius functional, a module is [projective exactly when it is injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Injective/Frobenius.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleProjective_iff_moduleInjective), with no finiteness assumption on the module, so the abelian exact structure on the [category of all modules is Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Frobenius.html#ModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius) (TauCeti#34564). The proof identifies induced and coinduced modules: a Frobenius functional gives an [isomorphism `A ⊗ V ≅ Hom(A, V)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Injective/Frobenius.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.tensorEquivLinearMap), so [every induced module is injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Injective/Frobenius.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_tensor). This is what Layer 1's `hMod_abelian_isFrobenius` asks for, but only conditionally. For a finite-dimensional Hopf algebra it applies once a nonzero cointegral is known to exist, and that theorem has still not been proved.

Layer 3 has begun. [Left module algebras over a bialgebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Bialgebra/ModuleAlgebra/Basic.html#TauCeti.LeftModuleAlgebra) now come with equivariant algebra maps, [restriction along bialgebra maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Bialgebra/ModuleAlgebra/Basic.html#TauCeti.LeftModuleAlgebra.restrict) and the [invariant subalgebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Bialgebra/ModuleAlgebra/Basic.html#TauCeti.LeftModuleAlgebra.invariants). Here an element is invariant when `H` acts on it through the counit. The smash product is not built yet. Layer 7 gained its first obstruction: outside characteristic two, [a square-zero primitive element is zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Bialgebra/Primitive.html#Bialgebra.IsPrimitiveElem.eq_zero_of_sq_eq_zero). This is why an ordinary bialgebra cannot carry a square-zero differential, and why the exterior superalgebra and Sweedler's `H₄` are needed. Neither of those two repairs is built yet.
