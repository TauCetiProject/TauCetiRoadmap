## ChevalleyGroups: 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-08 (`48641d3` to `d2083f0`)

The roadmap's central object now exists: [the simply connected Chevalley group scheme `G_sc(t)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/SimplyConnected.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.chevalleyGroupScheme) is defined over ℤ for every valid Dynkin type, as the Kostant toral closure of the full-weight module `V_sc(t) = ⨁ᵢ L(ϖᵢ)` (TauCeti#29194). Most of the window went into the representation theory underneath it. The [fundamental modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/Fundamental/Basic.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.fundamentalModule) are finite-dimensional over ℚ, and their Kostant orbits `U_ℤ · v⁺ᵢ` are full admissible lattices with integral weights. Their direct sum, [the full-weight lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/SimplyConnectedModule/Lattice.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.scLattice), has weights that [span the whole weight lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/SimplyConnectedModule/Lattice.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.span_range_scWeight_eq_top), and the Lie algebra [acts on it faithfully](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/SimplyConnectedModule.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.instIsFaithfulScModule).

For a general Chevalley datum there are now root subgroups `x_γ` for every root, not only the simple ones. They satisfy [the pinning equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/RootSubgroup.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.torusPoints_conj_rootSubgroup) `t x_γ(u) t⁻¹ = x_γ(γ(t) u)`, and each one [lies in the group generated by the simple ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/RootSubgroup.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.rootSubgroup_mem_closure), by conjugation with Steinberg's elements `n_γ(s)`. The datum's own torus is kept separate from the full-weight parametrization, which [reaches every torus point once units are divisible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Torus.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.range_kostantTorusMatrix_weight_eq). A first piece of the Weyl group theory also landed: a [surjective projection onto `W`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Weyl.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.weylProjection) from the group generated by the torus and the Weyl representatives.

On endomorphisms, the diagram symmetries of `V_sc(t)` give [graph automorphisms `γ_σ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/DiagramAutomorphism/NumberedSymmetry.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.scElementaryDiagramAut). These renumber the simple root subgroups, commute with Frobenius, and [descend to the integral scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/DiagramAutomorphism/ToralClosure.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.scToralDiagramAut). [Character maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Basis/DiagonalTorus/CharacterMap.html#TauCeti.IsCharacterMap) are now explicit data, and [Lang's equation on the torus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/GeckLattice/CharacterMap.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.exists_geckWeightTorusPoints_eq_mul_inv) is proved for the adjoint form. Still missing: Serre's theorem for the pinned algebra, the full commutator formula, the Bruhat decomposition, and the exceptional isogenies.

## ChevalleyGroups: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-19 (`d2083f0` to `b235000`)

The group theory over a field arrived largely in one piece (TauCeti#41256). For every Chevalley datum over a field, the group `E(k)` generated by the root subgroups and the torus now has its [Bruhat decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Bruhat/Basic.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.existsUnique_mem_bruhatCell_of_mem_extendedElementarySubgroup): every point lies in exactly one double coset `B n_w B`, and distinct Weyl elements give distinct cells. This assembles into [a Tits system](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/TitsSystem.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.titsSystem) whose Weyl group is that of the root datum, with [a second one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/TitsSystem.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.elementaryTitsSystem) on the subgroup generated by root subgroups alone. On the way it proves [`N(k)/T(k) ≅ W`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/TitsSystem.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.weylSubgroupQuotientMulEquiv) and [`N(k) ∩ B(k) = T(k)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Weyl/Borel.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.mem_borelSubgroup_iff_mem_range_torusPoints_of_mem_weylSubgroup). The unique normal form `u n_w b` is proved only in rank one.

Chevalley's commutator formula now holds on the points of every datum over every commutative ring, with coefficients from the structure constants: root subgroups [commute](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Commutator/Basic.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.commute_rootSubgroup) when `α + β` is not a root, and the one- and two-factor formulas hold for longer strings, for example [along `α + β`, `2α + β`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Commutator/Basic.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.commutatorElement_rootSubgroup_of_root_eq_two_nsmul_add). These feed [Steinberg's relations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Presentation.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.IsSteinbergRelator), which [hold in every Chevalley group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Presentation.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.lift_eq_one_of_isSteinbergRelator), so the presented group [maps onto the elementary subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Presentation.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.range_steinbergLift). Steinberg's theorem, that this map is injective, is not proved. The pinned Weyl representatives satisfy [the braid relations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Braid/Coxeter.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.weylWord_braidWord_eq) at every bond.

Toward the big cell there is block `LDU` factorization, [characterized by leading block minors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/LDU.html#Matrix.exists_blockLDU_iff) and unique, and the test that a Weyl word [has invertible leading weight-block minors only when it spells the identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/BigCell/Weyl.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.isUnit_leading_weight_minors_weylWord_iff). The matrix factorization, together with the [Chevalley datum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Datum.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum) structure, root subgroups at every root and the ordered-product lemmas, landed earlier and is recorded here late. The big cell over `ℤ` itself is still to come.
