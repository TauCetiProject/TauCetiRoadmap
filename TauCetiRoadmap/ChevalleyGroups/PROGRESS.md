## ChevalleyGroups: 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-08 (`48641d3` to `d2083f0`)

The roadmap's central object now exists: [the simply connected Chevalley group scheme `G_sc(t)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/SimplyConnected.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.chevalleyGroupScheme) is defined over ℤ for every valid Dynkin type, as the Kostant toral closure of the full-weight module `V_sc(t) = ⨁ᵢ L(ϖᵢ)` (TauCeti#29194). Most of the window went into the representation theory underneath it. The [fundamental modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/Fundamental/Basic.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.fundamentalModule) are finite-dimensional over ℚ, and their Kostant orbits `U_ℤ · v⁺ᵢ` are full admissible lattices with integral weights. Their direct sum, [the full-weight lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/SimplyConnectedModule/Lattice.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.scLattice), has weights that [span the whole weight lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/SimplyConnectedModule/Lattice.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.span_range_scWeight_eq_top), and the Lie algebra [acts on it faithfully](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/SimplyConnectedModule.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.instIsFaithfulScModule).

For a general Chevalley datum there are now root subgroups `x_γ` for every root, not only the simple ones. They satisfy [the pinning equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/RootSubgroup.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.torusPoints_conj_rootSubgroup) `t x_γ(u) t⁻¹ = x_γ(γ(t) u)`, and each one [lies in the group generated by the simple ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/RootSubgroup.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.rootSubgroup_mem_closure), by conjugation with Steinberg's elements `n_γ(s)`. The datum's own torus is kept separate from the full-weight parametrization, which [reaches every torus point once units are divisible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Torus.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.range_kostantTorusMatrix_weight_eq). A first piece of the Weyl group theory also landed: a [surjective projection onto `W`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Weyl.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.weylProjection) from the group generated by the torus and the Weyl representatives.

On endomorphisms, the diagram symmetries of `V_sc(t)` give [graph automorphisms `γ_σ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/DiagramAutomorphism/NumberedSymmetry.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.scElementaryDiagramAut). These renumber the simple root subgroups, commute with Frobenius, and [descend to the integral scheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/DiagramAutomorphism/ToralClosure.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.scToralDiagramAut). [Character maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Basis/DiagonalTorus/CharacterMap.html#TauCeti.IsCharacterMap) are now explicit data, and [Lang's equation on the torus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/GeckLattice/CharacterMap.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.exists_geckWeightTorusPoints_eq_mul_inv) is proved for the adjoint form. Still missing: Serre's theorem for the pinned algebra, the full commutator formula, the Bruhat decomposition, and the exceptional isogenies.
