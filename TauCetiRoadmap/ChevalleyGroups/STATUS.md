### Named results

- **The structure-constant theorem** — for roots `α, β` with `α + β` a root, the named Chevalley system built from Geck's basis satisfies `⁅e_α, e_β⁆ = N_{α,β} e_{α+β}` with `N_{α,β} = ±(r + 1)`. It has the symmetries `N_{β,α} = -N_{α,β}` and `N_{-α,-β} = -N_{α,β}` (`structureConstant_eq_natCast_or_eq_neg_natCast`).
- **The full-weight module realizes the weight lattice** — the weights of `V_sc(t) = ⨁ᵢ L(ϖᵢ)` and its admissible lattice span all of `Fin t.rank → ℤ` ([`span_range_scWeight_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/SimplyConnectedModule/Lattice.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.span_range_scWeight_eq_top)), and the representation is faithful ([`instIsFaithfulScModule`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/LieAlgebra/SimplyConnectedModule.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.instIsFaithfulScModule)).
- **The pinning equation** — for every root `γ` of a Chevalley datum and every torus point `t`, `t x_γ(u) t⁻¹ = x_γ(γ(t) u)` over every commutative ring ([`torusPoints_conj_rootSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/RootSubgroup.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.torusPoints_conj_rootSubgroup)).
- **Root subgroups come from the simple ones** — conjugating by a simple Weyl element gives `nᵢ x_γ(u) nᵢ⁻¹ = x_{sᵢγ}(±u)` ([`weylElement_conj_rootSubgroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/RootSubgroup.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.weylElement_conj_rootSubgroup)), so every root subgroup lies in the group generated by the simple ones ([`rootSubgroup_mem_closure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/RootSubgroup.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.rootSubgroup_mem_closure)).
- **Lang's equation on the torus** — on the adjoint Geck carrier over a ring of characteristic `p` with divisible units, an endomorphism whose character map has a power equal to `p ^ r • 1` makes `u ↦ u F(u)⁻¹` reach every torus point ([`exists_geckWeightTorusPoints_eq_mul_inv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/GeckLattice/CharacterMap.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.exists_geckWeightTorusPoints_eq_mul_inv)).

It comes with composition, uniqueness over an infinite field, and surjectivity on the torus when units are divisible.
