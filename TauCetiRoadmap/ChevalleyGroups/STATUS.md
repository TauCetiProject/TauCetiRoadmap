### Roadmap coverage

## The frontier

- **Steinberg's presentation** — the relators hold and the lift is onto the elementary subgroup. What remains is injectivity for `G_sc(t)` over a field, and the resulting extension property for maps defined on generators. The exceptional isogenies of C5 depend on it.
- **The Bruhat normal form and the centre** — the decomposition is unique by cell, but the unique form `u n_w b` with `u ∈ U ∩ n_w U⁻ n_w⁻¹` is proved only in rank one. The centralizer of `T(k)` in all of `E(k)` and the statement that the centre lies in the torus are still open.
- **The big cell over `ℤ`** — the matrix factorization and the Weyl-word test are ready. Still needed are `Ω_D = G_D ∩ GLₙ^{LDU}` with `U⁻ × T_D × U ≅ Ω_D`, its Weyl translates covering `G_D`, smoothness with connected fibres, and `ChevalleyGroup t ht k = E(k)`.
- **Serre's theorem for the pinned algebra** — `DynkinType.serreLift_injective` is the last C2 item. It specializes LieHighestWeight Layer 7.
- **Exceptional isogenies and `γ_σ`'s character map** — C5 still needs the character map and root signs of the graph automorphisms on `G_sc(t)`, the `B₂`, `F₄`, `G₂` isogenies, and the standard-endomorphism and rigidity results.
