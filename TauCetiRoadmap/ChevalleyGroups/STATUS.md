### Named results

- **The Bruhat decomposition with Steinberg's normal form**: over every field, each element of `E(k)` is uniquely `u n_w b` with `b ∈ B(k)` and `u ∈ U(k) ∩ n_w U⁻(k) n_w⁻¹` ([`existsUnique_bruhatNormalForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Bruhat/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.existsUnique_bruhatNormalForm)), and `(B, N)` is [a Tits system](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/TitsSystem.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.titsSystem) with the root datum's Weyl group.
- **The big cell over a field**: a point of `E(k)` lies in `U⁻(k) T(k) U(k)` exactly when its leading weight-block minors are units ([`mem_range_bigCellMul_iff_forall_isUnit_det_toSquareBlockProp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/BigCell/Field.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.mem_range_bigCellMul_iff_forall_isUnit_det_toSquareBlockProp)), and the factorization is unique over every commutative ring.
- **Chevalley's commutator formula**: on every datum over every commutative ring, in Steinberg's sign convention, from [commuting root subgroups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Commutator/Basic.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.commute_rootSubgroup) through the doubly-laced chains in both directions, with the `G₂` families handled separately.
- **The Weyl group as `N/T`**: [`N(k)/T(k) ≅ W`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Weyl/Quotient.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.weylSubgroupQuotientMulEquiv), with inverse the Coxeter lift through [the Bourbaki-numbered Coxeter system](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/Coxeter.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.simplyConnectedWeylCoxeterSystem) of the Weyl group, which is injective over any nontrivial ring.
- **Serre's theorem for the pinned algebra**: the map from the Lie algebra presented by Serre's relations onto Geck's split Lie algebra [is injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/SimplyConnectedRootDatum/SerrePresentation.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.serreLift_injective), so representations of the Serre algebra are representations of the Lie algebra.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`chevalleyGroupScheme`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/SimplyConnected.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.chevalleyGroupScheme) is `G_sc(t)` over `ℤ`, the case of a general [Chevalley datum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Datum.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum) with full weight lattice. Each datum carries its own torus with character lattice `Λ(D)`, so adjoint forms see their whole torus.
- [Steinberg's lift](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Presentation/Basic.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.steinbergLift) from the presented group onto the elementary subgroup, now [an isomorphism on the positive part](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Presentation/Positive.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.steinbergPositiveSubgroupEquiv) for `G_sc(t)`. This sets up the presentation theorem that the exceptional isogenies and the comparison theorem need.
- [The torus extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/Torus/Extension.html#TauCeti.DynkinType.ChevalleyDatum.extendedElementaryLift) `e t ↦ f(e) res(t)` turns a root-preserving map on the elementary subgroup into one on `E(k)`. It is the second step of the comparison theorem.

## The frontier

- **Steinberg's presentation**: the relators hold, the lift is onto, and it is injective on the positive part. What remains is injectivity on all of the presented group for `G_sc(t)` over a field, and the resulting extension property for maps defined on generators. §5.4 and §6.1 both wait on it.
- **The centre and the torus centralizer**: the centralizer of `T(k)` in `E(k)` for infinite `k`, and the centre as `Hom(Λ(D)/Q, kˣ)`. The sharp Bruhat normal form, which this needs, is now in place.
- **The integral big cell and the points**: the field case is done. The open immersion `U⁻ × T_D × U → G_D` over `ℤ`, the fibres and the points over a field go through §2.9, so they wait on ReductiveGroups §9B.1.
- **Exceptional isogenies**: the `B₂`, `F₄` and `G₂` endomorphisms `τ` with `τ ∘ τ = Frob_p` and their character maps, then standard endomorphisms, rigidity and Steinberg endomorphisms (§§5.6–5.8).
- **The elementary comparison (§6.1)**: the map `x_α(u) ↦ x_α(u)` between elementary groups for `Λ(D′) ⊆ Λ(D)`. Once it exists, the torus extension already gives the map on `E(k)`.
