## AlgebraicKTheory: 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-19 (`e4c0a82` to `b235000`)

The first calculation of the roadmap's K₁ landed: [K₁(ℤ) ≅ {±1}](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.detEquivInt), the isomorphism being the determinant (TauCeti#40043). Behind it is the general machinery for commutative rings: the [determinant on K₁](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.det), split by the classes of 1×1 matrices, its kernel [SK₁(R)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.specialAlgebraicK1), and the [determinant splitting](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.equivUnitsProdSpecial) K₁(R) ≅ Rˣ × SK₁(R). SK₁(ℤ) vanishes because elementary integer matrices are [exactly those of determinant one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#TauCeti.elementarySubgroup_int_eq_ker_det), and the [same is proved over fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#TauCeti.elementarySubgroup_eq_ker_det_of_field). That makes K₁(F) = Fˣ one short step away, but it is not yet stated, and division rings, which need the Dieudonné determinant, are untouched.

On the K₀ side the roadmap's [K₀](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Defs.html#TauCeti.KZero) is now defined, as the split Grothendieck group of finitely generated projective right modules, and [computed as ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.FiniteProjectiveModules.rankEquiv) whenever the ring has invariant basis number and every finitely generated projective is stably free. The named values [K₀(D) = ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.kZero_divisionRing) for a division ring and [K₀(ℤ) = ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.kZero_int) follow, as does the [principal ideal domain case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.kZero_of_isPrincipalIdealRing) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/204). The induced map of K₀ along a ring map is still missing.

Elementary matrices now behave well under ring maps. A surjection carries Eₙ [onto Eₙ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#RingHom.map_elementarySubgroup_eq_of_surjective) at the same size, and [Whitehead's matrix u ⊕ u⁻¹ lifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#RingHom.exists_mem_elementarySubgroup_unitsMap_mapMatrix_eq_fromBlocksGL_inv) to an elementary matrix even when u itself has no invertible lift. This is the lifting input that Milnor patching will use.
