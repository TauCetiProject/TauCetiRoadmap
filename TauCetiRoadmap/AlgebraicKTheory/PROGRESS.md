## AlgebraicKTheory: 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-19 (`e4c0a82` to `b235000`)

The first calculation of the roadmap's K₁ landed: [K₁(ℤ) ≅ {±1}](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.detEquivInt), the isomorphism being the determinant (TauCeti#40043). Behind it is the general machinery for commutative rings: the [determinant on K₁](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.det), split by the classes of 1×1 matrices, its kernel [SK₁(R)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.specialAlgebraicK1), and the [determinant splitting](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.equivUnitsProdSpecial) K₁(R) ≅ Rˣ × SK₁(R). SK₁(ℤ) vanishes because elementary integer matrices are [exactly those of determinant one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#TauCeti.elementarySubgroup_int_eq_ker_det), and the [same is proved over fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#TauCeti.elementarySubgroup_eq_ker_det_of_field). That makes K₁(F) = Fˣ one short step away, but it is not yet stated, and division rings, which need the Dieudonné determinant, are untouched.

On the K₀ side the roadmap's [K₀](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Defs.html#TauCeti.KZero) is now defined, as the split Grothendieck group of finitely generated projective right modules, and [computed as ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.FiniteProjectiveModules.rankEquiv) whenever the ring has invariant basis number and every finitely generated projective is stably free. The named values [K₀(D) = ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.kZero_divisionRing) for a division ring and [K₀(ℤ) = ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.kZero_int) follow, as does the [principal ideal domain case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.kZero_of_isPrincipalIdealRing) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/204). The induced map of K₀ along a ring map is still missing.

Elementary matrices now behave well under ring maps. A surjection carries Eₙ [onto Eₙ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#RingHom.map_elementarySubgroup_eq_of_surjective) at the same size, and [Whitehead's matrix u ⊕ u⁻¹ lifts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#RingHom.exists_mem_elementarySubgroup_unitsMap_mapMatrix_eq_fromBlocksGL_inv) to an elementary matrix even when u itself has no invertible lift. This is the lifting input that Milnor patching will use.

## AlgebraicKTheory: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (`b235000` to `0253c4f`)

Steinberg groups arrived, together with the [Kervaire–Steinberg theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Steinberg/UniversalCentralExtension.html#TauCeti.steinberg_universalCentralExtension): for every ring, St(R) → E(R) is the universal central extension of the perfect group E(R) (TauCeti#40389). On top of it, [K₂](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Steinberg/K2.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK2) is defined as the kernel of St(R) → GL(R). It is abelian by [Milnor's centrality theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Steinberg/Centrality.html#TauCeti.SteinbergGroup.ker_toStableGL_le_center) and sits in the [exact sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Steinberg/K2.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK2.mulExact_subtype_toStableGL) 1 → K₂ → St → GL → K₁ → 1. It also has natural [Steinberg symbols](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Steinberg/Symbol.html#TauCeti.steinbergSymbol) and a relative version for unitizations. Matsumoto's theorem and K₂ of finite fields are not yet stated.

The stable-range layer also opened with named theorems. Bass [stable range](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StableRange.html#TauCeti.HasStableRangeLE) comes with Vaserstein's [longer-row](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StableRange.html#TauCeti.hasStableRangeLE_iff_forall_le) and [opposite-ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StableRange.html#TauCeti.hasStableRangeLE_mop_iff) theorems and with [Bass's bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StableRange/Commutative.html#TauCeti.hasStableRangeLE_of_ringKrullDim_le) sr ≤ d + 1 for commutative Noetherian rings. Both [surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Stability/Surjective.html#TauCeti.surjective_stability) and [injective stability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Stability/Injective.html#TauCeti.injective_stability) are proved, so K₁(R) is [GLₙ(R)/Eₙ(R)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Stability.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.quotientFinElementarySubgroupEquiv) once n ≥ s + 1. On the patching side, fibre products of rings got their matrix and unit descriptions. The [Mayer–Vietoris sequence for K₁](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Pullback.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.mayerVietoris_exact) is now exact at K₁(A₁) × K₁(A₂) for any square with one surjective leg. The boundary and the K₀ end still wait on Milnor patching.

On the K₀ side, K₀ is now the [group completion of the idempotent monoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Idempotent.html#TauCeti.IdempotentMonoid.kZeroEquiv) V(R). The [Möbius module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Instances/AddCircle/MobiusModule.html#TauCeti.mobiusSections_order_two) satisfies M ⊕ M ≅ R² but is never stably free. The sphere's tangent-section module becomes [free after complexification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sphere/TangentSections/Complex.html#TauCeti.tangentSectionsSphereTwoComplexTrivializationEquiv), through an explicit unitary frame. Supporting work added extension of scalars for right modules, the relative triple group of a functor, K₁ of products, perfectness of E∞ and the comparison with the stable component group. The induced map of K₀ along ring maps is still missing.
