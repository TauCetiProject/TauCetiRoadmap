## QuadraticFormInvariants: 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-26 (`f7761c5` to `759eb3e`)

The roadmap's first high point is proved: the four-fold splitting criterion ([`TauCeti.QuaternionAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_matrix_tfae`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Quaternion/SplittingCriterion.html#TauCeti.QuaternionAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_matrix_tfae)) says that for units `a, b` of a field in which 2 is invertible, `ℍ[K,a,b]` is a matrix algebra exactly when `b` is a norm from `K(√a)`, when `b = x² − a y²` is solvable, and when `⟨1, −a, −b⟩` is isotropic. Beneath it lie a carrier for isometry classes of regular forms ([`TauCeti.RegularFormClass`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/Basic.html#TauCeti.RegularFormClass)) and the elementary theory of forms: Witt's chain-equivalence theorem ([`TauCeti.diagonalChain_iff_equivalent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Diagonal/WittChain.html#TauCeti.diagonalChain_iff_equivalent)), in rank at least two since in rank one the chain relation is equality, Witt cancellation ([`TauCeti.equivalent_of_equivalent_prod`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Cancellation.html#TauCeti.equivalent_of_equivalent_prod)), Witt decomposition for regular forms, and Witt's extension theorem ([`QuadraticMap.IsometryEquiv.exists_extension`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Extension.html#QuadraticMap.IsometryEquiv.exists_extension)).

On this carrier sits the Witt ring ([`TauCeti.WittRing`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Ring.html#TauCeti.WittRing)), in which `Iⁿ` is additively generated by `n`-fold Pfister forms ([`TauCeti.fundamentalIdeal_pow_eq_addClosure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Pfister/Basic.html#TauCeti.fundamentalIdeal_pow_eq_addClosure)) and `I/I² ≅ Kˣ/(Kˣ)²` through the signed discriminant ([`TauCeti.fundamentalIdealCotangentEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Discriminant.html#TauCeti.fundamentalIdealCotangentEquiv)); two-fold Pfister forms are round, and isotropic exactly when hyperbolic, exactly when the quaternion algebra splits ([`TauCeti.pfisterFormClass_two_tfae`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Pfister/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.pfisterFormClass_two_tfae)). Quaternion algebras are central simple, with Brauer classes that are symmetric, bilinear ([`TauCeti.BrauerGroup.quaternionClass_mul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Quaternion/BrauerClass.html#TauCeti.BrauerGroup.quaternionClass_mul)) and 2-torsion. They define the Hasse invariant, and regular forms of dimension at most three are classified by discriminant and Hasse invariant ([`QuadraticForm.equivalent_iff_discr_eq_and_hasseInvariant_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/LowRank.html#QuadraticForm.equivalent_iff_discr_eq_and_hasseInvariant_eq)). The Clifford invariant has not started. Smaller changes generalized existing diagonal-form, quaternion and valuation lemmas.

Over a nonarchimedean local field came uniformizers, the four square classes in odd residue characteristic, and the unramified quadratic class ([`TauCeti.exists_unramified_class`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/UnramifiedClass.html#TauCeti.exists_unramified_class)), unique up to squares, against which the Hilbert symbol is `(Δ, b)_K = (−1)^{v_K(b)}` ([`TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_unramified`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/UnramifiedClass.html#TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_unramified)). Away from residue characteristic 2 the norm group of `K(√a)` has index two when `v_K(a)` is odd ([`TauCeti.quadraticNormSubgroup_index_eq_two_of_odd`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/OddValuation.html#TauCeti.quadraticNormSubgroup_index_eq_two_of_odd)), so the symbol is multiplicative for such `a`, as it is for the unramified class in every residue characteristic; the other dyadic cases are open. Layer 9 took its first step: two nonzero functionals on a finite extension differ by a unique unit ([`LinearMap.existsUnique_unit_apply_eq_apply_mul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Field.html#LinearMap.existsUnique_unit_apply_eq_apply_mul)).
