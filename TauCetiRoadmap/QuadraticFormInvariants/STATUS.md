### Named results

- **The four-fold splitting criterion** — for units `a, b` of a field in which 2 is invertible, `ℍ[K,a,b]` splits exactly when `b` is a norm from `K(√a)`, when `b = x² − a y²` is solvable, and when `⟨1, −a, −b⟩` is isotropic ([`TauCeti.QuaternionAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_matrix_tfae`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Quaternion/SplittingCriterion.html#TauCeti.QuaternionAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_matrix_tfae)).
- **Bimultiplicativity of the local Hilbert symbol** — over a nonarchimedean local field with `2 ≠ 0`, dyadic fields included, `(a, bc)_K = (a, b)_K (a, c)_K`. This follows from the norm group of `K(√a)` having index two for every nonsquare `a` ([`TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_mul_right`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/Bimultiplicativity.html#TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_mul_right)).
- **Every Brauer class is a crossed product** — each class in `Br(K)` is the class of the crossed product of a 2-cocycle of some finite Galois subextension of `Kˢ/K` ([`TauCeti.BrauerGroup.exists_galoisCocycle_brauerClass_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/CrossedProduct/GaloisCocycle/BrauerClass.html#TauCeti.BrauerGroup.exists_galoisCocycle_brauerClass_eq)).
- **Classification in dimension at most three** — regular forms of equal dimension at most three are isometric exactly when their discriminants and Hasse invariants agree (Lam V.3.21, [`QuadraticForm.equivalent_iff_discr_eq_and_hasseInvariant_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/LowRank.html#QuadraticForm.equivalent_iff_discr_eq_and_hasseInvariant_eq)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Isometry classes with a descent principle** — isometry classes of regular forms form a commutative semiring, and any function of diagonal presentations that survives the chain moves descends to it, which is how both Hasse invariants are defined ([`TauCeti.RegularFormClass.liftDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/Descent.html#TauCeti.RegularFormClass.liftDiagonal)).
- **The Witt ring, functorial, with Pfister generators** — `Iⁿ` is additively generated by `n`-fold Pfister classes, and scalar extension gives ring maps `W(K) → W(L)` that preserve every power of `I`. A homomorphism on `I²` can therefore be defined by its values on generators ([`TauCeti.fundamentalIdeal_pow_eq_addClosure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Pfister/Basic.html#TauCeti.fundamentalIdeal_pow_eq_addClosure), [`TauCeti.WittRing.baseChange`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.WittRing.baseChange)).
- **The Scharlau transfer on Witt rings** — transfer along a nonzero functional respects isometry, satisfies Frobenius reciprocity and the change-of-functional rule, and descends to a `W(K)`-linear map `W(L) → W(K)` ([`TauCeti.WittRing.scharlauTransfer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Transfer/Witt.html#TauCeti.WittRing.scharlauTransfer)).
