### Named results

- **The four-fold splitting criterion** — for units `a, b` of a field in which 2 is invertible, `ℍ[K,a,b]` splits exactly when `b` is a norm from `K(√a)`, when `b = x² − a y²` is solvable, and when `⟨1, −a, −b⟩` is isotropic ([`TauCeti.QuaternionAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_matrix_tfae`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Quaternion/SplittingCriterion.html#TauCeti.QuaternionAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_matrix_tfae)).
- **Witt's chain-equivalence theorem** — diagonal forms with unit coefficients and rank at least two are isometric exactly when a chain of permutations and binary replacements joins them (Lam I.5.2, [`TauCeti.diagonalChain_iff_equivalent`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Diagonal/WittChain.html#TauCeti.diagonalChain_iff_equivalent)).
- **Witt's extension theorem** — an isometry between regular subspaces of a finite-dimensional quadratic space extends to the whole space (Lam I.4.9, [`QuadraticMap.IsometryEquiv.exists_extension`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Extension.html#QuadraticMap.IsometryEquiv.exists_extension)).
- **`I/I²` is the square-class group** — the signed discriminant induces `I(K)/I(K)² ≅ Kˣ/(Kˣ)²` ([`TauCeti.fundamentalIdealCotangentEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Discriminant.html#TauCeti.fundamentalIdealCotangentEquiv)).
- **Classification in dimension at most three** — regular forms of equal dimension at most three are isometric exactly when their discriminants and Hasse invariants agree (Lam V.3.21, [`QuadraticForm.equivalent_iff_discr_eq_and_hasseInvariant_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/LowRank.html#QuadraticForm.equivalent_iff_discr_eq_and_hasseInvariant_eq)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Isometry classes with a descent principle** — the carrier is an additively cancellative commutative semiring under orthogonal sum and tensor product, and a function of diagonal presentations that survives permutations, binary steps and, in rank one, rescaling by a square descends to it, as the Hasse invariant does ([`TauCeti.RegularFormClass`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/Basic.html#TauCeti.RegularFormClass), [`TauCeti.RegularFormClass.liftDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/Descent.html#TauCeti.RegularFormClass.liftDiagonal)).
- **The Witt ring with Pfister generators** — `Iⁿ` is additively generated by `n`-fold Pfister classes for `n ≥ 1`, which lets Layer 5 define a homomorphism on `I²` by its values on generators ([`TauCeti.WittRing`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Ring.html#TauCeti.WittRing), [`TauCeti.fundamentalIdeal_pow_eq_addClosure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Witt/Pfister/Basic.html#TauCeti.fundamentalIdeal_pow_eq_addClosure)).
- **The quaternion symbol in the Brauer group** — `[(a, b)]` is symmetric, bilinear, 2-torsion and an invariant of the binary form `⟨a, b⟩`, which is what makes the Hasse invariant well defined ([`TauCeti.BrauerGroup.quaternionClass`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Quaternion/BrauerClass.html#TauCeti.BrauerGroup.quaternionClass), [`TauCeti.RegularFormClass.hasseInvariant`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/Hasse.html#TauCeti.RegularFormClass.hasseInvariant)).

## The frontier

- **Hilbert-symbol bimultiplicativity** — index two for the norm group of `K(√a)` remains open in residue characteristic 2, for odd valuation and for units of odd defect `d`, where `U(K, 2e−d+1)` is known to consist of norms but sharpness is not. The indicator criterion then gives bimultiplicativity ([`TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_mul_iff_quadraticNormSubgroup_index_dvd_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/HilbertSymbol/NormSubgroup.html#TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_mul_iff_quadraticNormSubgroup_index_dvd_two)).
- **Functoriality of the Witt ring** — the map `W(K) → W(L)` along a field embedding. Base change of form classes, compatible with sums, products and the hyperbolic class, is in, so only the descent remains.
- **The local Hasse invariant and the classification by `(dim, d, s)`** — after bimultiplicativity, `localHasse` can descend through the chain-induction lemmas the Hasse invariant already uses; then come the closed formulas and the `ℚ₂` table, the classification with `u(K) = 4`, and 6E. The dyadic count `4·q^e` waits on the local-fields-ramification roadmap's dyadic power-class count, not yet in Tau Ceti.
- **The Clifford invariant** — central simplicity of `C(q)` and of its even part, the Lam V.3.20 comparison, and `I² → Br(K)[2]` vanishing on `I³`; its generation inputs are in.
- **The Scharlau transfer** — the transfer along a nonzero functional with its basic theorems, and `Tr_*⟨1⟩ ≅ ⟨2, 2d⟩`, need only Layers 1 to 4. The relative Stiefel-Whitney formula waits on Layer 8 and on the profinite-cohomology roadmap's transfer along `L/K`, not yet in Tau Ceti.
