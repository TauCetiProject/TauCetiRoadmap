### Named results

- **The four-fold splitting criterion**: for units `a, b` of a field in which 2 is invertible, `ℍ[K,a,b]` splits exactly when `b` is a norm from `K(√a)`, exactly when `b = x² − a y²` is solvable, and exactly when `⟨1, −a, −b⟩` is isotropic ([`TauCeti.QuaternionAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_matrix_tfae`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Quaternion/SplittingCriterion.html#TauCeti.QuaternionAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_matrix_tfae)).
- **Bimultiplicativity of the local Hilbert symbol**: over a nonarchimedean local field with `2 ≠ 0`, dyadic fields included, `(a, bc)_K = (a, b)_K (a, c)_K` ([`TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_mul_right`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/Bimultiplicativity.html#TauCeti.hilbertSymbol_mul_right)).
- **Local isotropy by rank** (Serre IV Thm 6): in ranks three and four, isotropy is decided by the discriminant and the local Hasse invariant, and every form of rank at least five is isotropic ([`QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_iff_discr_ne_zero_or_localHasse_eq_of_finrank_eq_four`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/Isotropy.html#QuadraticForm.not_anisotropic_iff_discr_ne_zero_or_localHasse_eq_of_finrank_eq_four)). With an anisotropic quaternary form, this gives `u(K) = 4`.
- **Brauer classes are crossed products, up to cohomology**: every class in `Br(K)` is the class of a crossed product of a finite Galois cocycle ([`TauCeti.BrauerGroup.exists_galoisCocycle_brauerClass_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/CrossedProduct/GaloisCocycle/BrauerClass.html#TauCeti.BrauerGroup.exists_galoisCocycle_brauerClass_eq)), and two such classes agree exactly when the cocycles are cohomologous ([`TauCeti.BrauerGroup.crossedProductClass_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/CrossedProduct/Injectivity.html#TauCeti.BrauerGroup.crossedProductClass_eq_iff)).
- **Classification in dimension at most three** (Lam V.3.21): over any field, regular forms of equal dimension at most three are isometric exactly when their discriminants and Hasse invariants agree ([`QuadraticForm.equivalent_iff_discr_eq_and_hasseInvariant_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/LowRank.html#QuadraticForm.equivalent_iff_discr_eq_and_hasseInvariant_eq)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Isometry classes with a descent principle**: any function of diagonal presentations that survives the chain moves descends to isometry classes. Both Hasse invariants are defined this way ([`TauCeti.RegularFormClass.liftDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/RegularFormClass/Descent.html#TauCeti.RegularFormClass.liftDiagonal)).
- **Inflation of finite Galois cocycles**: this turns a cocycle of `Gal(L/K)` into a continuous class of `G_K`. It depends only on the cohomology class, reaches every continuous degree-two class, and is the bridge the comparison isomorphism needs ([`TauCeti.TwoCocycle.inflateClass`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/CrossedProduct/Inflation.html#TauCeti.TwoCocycle.inflateClass)).
- **The Scharlau transfer on Witt rings**: a `W(K)`-linear map `W(L) → W(K)` that satisfies Frobenius reciprocity. It is the transfer side of the relative Stiefel-Whitney formula ([`TauCeti.WittRing.scharlauTransfer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Transfer/Witt.html#TauCeti.WittRing.scharlauTransfer)).

## The frontier

- **The local classification (6D)**: extend the classification by `(dim, d, s)` from dimension two to all dimensions. Then prove realization of admissible triples and the uniqueness of the anisotropic quaternary form.
- **The comparison `Br(K) ≅ H²`**: every crossed-product and inflation input is in. What remains is to assemble the equivalence with its defining equation on crossed products, restrict it to `ι` on 2-torsion, and prove `ι[(a,b)] = (a) ∪ (b)` from the quadratic-cocycle computation. The cup-norm theorem of 7C follows.
- **The Clifford invariant**: prove centrality of `C⁰(q)` in odd dimension and invariance under isometry, then the Lam V.3.20 comparison and `I² → Br(K)[2]` vanishing on `I³`.
- **Descended Stiefel-Whitney classes**: `w₂` invariance under the binary step needs the cup identity `(a)(b) = (c)(d)` for `⟨a,b⟩ ≅ ⟨c,d⟩`, so it waits on 7C.
- **The relative Stiefel-Whitney formula**: the transfer and the Kummer restriction and corestriction formulas are in. It still needs Layer 8's descended classes and an Evens norm along `L/K`; only an abstract index-two Evens class exists so far.
