## AnalyticNumberTheoryPartIII: 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-19 (`88e3264` to `b235000`)

The first work on this roadmap fills in half of target 11.4, the Dirichlet series of the classical arithmetic functions. The Liouville series is now [summed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Liouville.html#TauCeti.LSeriesHasSum_liouville): `Σ λ(n) n^{−s} = ζ(2s)/ζ(s)` on `Re s > 1`. So is Euler's totient, with [`Σ φ(n) n^{−s} = ζ(s − 1)/ζ(s)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Totient.html#TauCeti.LSeriesHasSum_totient) on `Re s > 2` (TauCeti#42855, TauCeti#42858). Each comes with its exact region of absolute convergence, so the abscissas are pinned down as [one for `λ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Liouville.html#TauCeti.abscissaOfAbsConv_liouville) and [two for `φ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Totient.html#TauCeti.abscissaOfAbsConv_totient). The second confirms the roadmap's warning that a hypothesis `Re s > 1` would be wrong for the totient series.

The other two series in 11.4 have not landed: those of `2^ω` and of `μ²`, with their abscissas. Neither has the combined statement the roadmap names. Nothing has yet appeared for the other layers.

## AnalyticNumberTheoryPartIII: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`b235000` to `d25d526`)

The contour and smoothing machinery of Layers 8 and 9 arrived this window. The [rectangle residue theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Residue/Rectangle.html#TauCeti.Contour.rectangle_boundary_integral_eq_residues) is now stated in the four-edge form a contour shift needs: the boundary integral equals `2πi` times the sum of residues at finitely many poles of any order. It comes with its [U-shaped form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Residue/Rectangle.html#TauCeti.Contour.integral_vertical_eq_uShaped_add_two_pi_mul_sum_residue) for a full vertical line, a [circle-integral version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Cauchy/Rectangle.html#TauCeti.Contour.rectangle_boundary_integral_eq_sum_circleIntegral) that also allows essential singularities, and the [L-shaped shift to `Re s = −∞`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Cauchy/Rectangle.html#TauCeti.Contour.vertical_integral_eq_lShaped_add_sum_circleIntegral) (TauCeti#43070). On the smoothing side there are [Mellin convolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/MellinTransform/Convolution.html#TauCeti.mellinConv), delta spikes as an approximate identity for `dx/x`, and [smoothed cutoffs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/MellinTransform/SmoothCutoff.html#TauCeti.smoothCutoff). A cutoff's Mellin transform [is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/MellinTransform/SmoothCutoff.html#TauCeti.mellin_smoothCutoff) `mellin ν (εs)/s`, and it [decays](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/MellinTransform/SmoothCutoff.html#TauCeti.exists_norm_mellin_smoothCutoff_le) like `C/(|s|(1 + ε|s|)^k)`, uniformly in `ε`. The sine-integral bounds of 7.2, such as [`|Si x| ≤ Si π`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/SineIntegral.html#Real.abs_sineIntegral_le), also landed.

Layer 10 has started. A smoothed sum [is an inverse Mellin integral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/SmoothedSum/Basic.html#TauCeti.LSeries.hasSum_mul_smoothCutoff), and it [differs from the sharp sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/SmoothedSum/Basic.html#TauCeti.norm_sum_sub_tsum_mul_smoothCutoff_le) by `O(B(εX + 1) log X)`. Moving its contour across the pole at `1` [gives the residue plus three edge terms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/SmoothedSum/Contour.html#TauCeti.LSeries.tsum_mul_smoothCutoff_eq_residue_add_contour). The [Ingham exponent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/InghamExponent.html#TauCeti.inghamExponent) and bounds on the edges now exist as well, but the transfer theorem itself has not yet been assembled.

In Layer 11, [complete multiplicativity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticFunction/CompletelyMultiplicative.html#ArithmeticFunction.IsCompletelyMultiplicative) has its full API, including Euler products. The divisor sum `σ_ν` with complex `ν` has [series `ζ(s)ζ(s − ν)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Divisor/PowerSum.html#TauCeti.LSeries.LSeries_sigmaC) and [exact abscissa](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Divisor/PowerSum.html#TauCeti.LSeries.abscissaOfAbsConv_sigmaC) `max(1, 1 + Re ν)`. The [series of `2^ω`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/TwoPowOmega.html#TauCeti.LSeriesHasSum_two_pow_cardDistinctFactors) and the [abscissa of `μ²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Moebius.html#TauCeti.abscissaOfAbsConv_moebius_sq) mean all four series of 11.4 are now proved separately, though the combined statements are not. The `d_z` functions and Ramanujan's four-shift identity are still missing, but its [`τ(n²)` corollary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Divisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.LSeriesHasSum_card_divisors_comp_sq) is in.
