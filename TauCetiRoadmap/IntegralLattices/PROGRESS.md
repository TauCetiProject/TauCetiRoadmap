## IntegralLattices: 2026-08-14 to 2026-09-30 (`7759fe7` to `dec7a58`)

The overlattice theory now covers odd overlattices too. For an integral overlattice that need not be even, the discriminant bilinear module is the orthogonal quotient `H⊥/H` ([`discriminantBilinearOrthogonalQuotientIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Overlattice/OrthogonalQuotient/Bilinear.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IntermediateCarrier.discriminantBilinearOrthogonalQuotientIsometry)), and over an orthogonal sum both the bilinear and quadratic comparisons split componentwise ([`discriminantQuadraticOrthogonalQuotientIsometryOrthogonalSum`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Overlattice/OrthogonalQuotient/OrthogonalSum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IntermediateCarrier.discriminantQuadraticOrthogonalQuotientIsometryOrthogonalSum)). These were the two gaps left around the `D₈⁺ ≅ E₈` isometry and Nikulin's even-overlattice correspondence. The discriminant form of an even lattice is metabolic exactly when the lattice has an even unimodular overlattice ([`isMetabolic_discriminantQuadraticModule_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Overlattice/Metabolic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isMetabolic_discriminantQuadraticModule_iff)). The Smith invariant factors of `A_L` are now available for every nondegenerate Gram matrix, including indefinite ones ([`exists_gramSmithInvariantFactors_smith_normal_form`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Discriminant/Smith.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.exists_gramSmithInvariantFactors_smith_normal_form)).

The roadmap has been rewritten as a much larger programme that runs to genera, Nikulin's embedding theory and the unimodular classification, and the newer work has started on it. A positive definite lattice has finite shells, a minimum, a [kissing number](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Minimum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.kissingNumber) and [representation numbers](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/RepresentationNumber.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.representationNumber), and its isometry group is finite ([`IsPosDef.finite_isometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Finite.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPosDef.finite_isometry)), with `SO(L)` of index at most two. In a unimodular lattice the characteristic vectors form a single coset of `2L`, and their norms all agree mod 8 ([`characteristicVector_integralNorm_modEq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Characteristic/Unimodular.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsUnimodular.characteristicVector_integralNorm_modEq)). That is the setup for van der Blij's congruence, but the congruence with the signature itself has not landed. A vector of unit norm splits off an orthogonal summand ([`isCompl_span_singleton_orthogonal_of_isUnit`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Orthogonal/Splitting.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isCompl_span_singleton_orthogonal_of_isUnit)).

Layer 3 has begun with the bridge from `L` to `L_p = ℤ_p ⊗ L` and `V_p = ℚ_p ⊗ V`. The canonical map is injective, its extension to `ℚ_p` is an isomorphism ([`isBaseChange_localizationToCompletion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Localization.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.isBaseChange_localizationToCompletion)), and the local form is the restriction of the completed one, including at `p = 2`. Jordan splittings and the genus have not started.

## IntegralLattices: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-05 (`dec7a58` to `1d09589`)

The Gauss-sum invariant of a finite quadratic form is in. The Gauss sum of a nondegenerate module has absolute value `√#A` and its normalisation is an eighth root of unity, which defines [`sign q ∈ ℤ/8`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/GaussSum.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.gaussSign). The invariant is [additive over orthogonal sums](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/GaussSum.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.gaussSign_prod), vanishes on metabolic modules, survives isotropic reduction, and is therefore unchanged by passing to an even overlattice. Nikulin's values on the dyadic generators are proved: `θ + 4kω(θ)` for [`q_θ^{(2)}(2^k)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/Dyadic/GaussSum.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.gaussSign_dyadicCyclic), `4k` for `v`, and `0` for `u`. The values on the odd generators, Nikulin's Theorem 1.11.3 and Milgram's theorem have not landed.

The generator classification has begun. [Nikulin's decomposition in odd order](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/OddCyclic/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.exists_isometry_pi_oddCyclic) writes every nondegenerate form of odd order as a sum of odd cyclic forms `q_θ^{(p)}(p^k)`, which are isometric exactly when their coefficients differ by a unit square. At 2 the cyclic forms are classified, and an alternating form on the Klein four-group is [`u` or `v`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/Dyadic/KleinFour.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.nonempty_isometry_dyadicU_or_dyadicV_of_isAddKleinFour). The full dyadic decomposition and the relations are still to come.

Elsewhere, Layer 0 got its scale and norm ideals and the rational square-class comparison, and the coordinate model of `Aₙ` is now [isometric to the Cartan model](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeA/ZeroSum.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeAIsometryZeroSumLattice). The [level](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Level.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.level) is the annihilator of `q_L` and divides `2 det L`. The [covolume identity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Covolume.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.covolume_range_sq_eq_determinant) `covolume² = det L` is proved. At an odd prime the local form is [diagonal with entries unit times `pⁱ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Diagonalization.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.exists_basis_iIsOrtho_localIntegralForm_eq_unit_mul_pow), while over `ℤ₂` the hyperbolic plane [has no orthogonal basis](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/BilinearForm.html#TauCeti.not_exists_orthogonal_basis_hyperbolic_padicInt_two).

## IntegralLattices: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-07 (`1d09589` to `8334df2`)

Layer 2 now has successive minima (TauCeti#12942). The `i`-th [successive minimum](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/SuccessiveMinima.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.successiveMinimum) is the least norm bound below which the lattice has `i` independent vectors. The first is the ordinary minimum, the sequence is nondecreasing, and a positive semidefinite lattice has [independent vectors attaining all of them at once](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/SuccessiveMinima.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPosSemidef.exists_linearIndependent_integralNorm_eq_successiveMinimum). Minkowski's second theorem, which bounds their product by `γₙⁿ det L`, has not landed, and neither have the Minkowski and Hermite bounds.

On the finite-form side, the rank-two dyadic blocks are classified. If `x`, `y`, `q(x)` and `q(y)` are killed by `2^{k+1}` while `2^k b(x, y) ≠ 0`, the subgroup they generate is [isometric to `u^{(2)}(2^{k+1})` or `v^{(2)}(2^{k+1})`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/Dyadic/RankTwo/Classification.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule.nonempty_isometry_dyadicU_or_dyadicV_restrict_zmultiples_sup). The proof goes through a [normal form for binary forms with unit middle coefficient over `ℤ/2^{k+1}`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Data/ZMod/BinaryQuadraticForm.html#ZMod.BinaryQuadraticForm.exists_basis). This is the case that the dyadic step of Nikulin's generator decomposition needs beyond cyclic summands. The relations among the generators (Proposition 1.8.2) are still open.

Layer 0 got both of its restriction constructors. One restricts to an arbitrary submodule in [its own ambient space](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Restriction.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.restrict), the other to a full submodule in the original space, and a [canonical isometry](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Restriction.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.restrictFullIsometry) relates them when both apply. A submodule on which the form is unimodular now [splits off isometrically](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Orthogonal/Isometry.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.orthogonalSplittingIsometry) as `L ≅ M ⊕ M^⊥`. The standard lattice [`Iₙ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Unit.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.unitLattice) has its rank, determinant, signature and parity computed, and its level is 2. The Construction A lattice of the zero code is identified with a scaled `Iₙ`.

## IntegralLattices: 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-17 (`8334df2` to `c170031`)

Hermite's inequality is in (TauCeti#12901): a positive definite integral lattice of rank `n` has [`min L ≤ (4/3)^{(n−1)/2} (det L)^{1/n}`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Hermite.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPosDef.minimum_le_rpow_mul_determinant_rpow), also stated without roots as [`3^{C(n,2)} (min L)ⁿ ≤ 4^{C(n,2)} det L`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/Hermite.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPosDef.three_pow_mul_minimum_pow_le). The constant is sharp in rank two, since [`A₂` attains equality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeA/Hermite.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.minimum_typeARootLattice_two_eq_rpow_mul_determinant_rpow) with minimum 2 and determinant 3. Minkowski's convex-body bound on the minimum is also in the library, so milestone 2E is now complete.

The proof is Hermite's original induction on the rank, and it stays entirely integral, for [any positive definite integral bilinear form on a free `ℤ`-module](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/BilinearForm/Hermite.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.exists_ne_zero_three_pow_mul_pow_le_four_pow_mul_det). A minimal vector is [primitive](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/BilinearForm/Hermite.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.exists_isPrimitive_forall_apply_self_le), so it splits off a complement carrying the projected form scaled by the minimum. [Chiò's condensation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Condensation.html#Matrix.mul_det_condensation_eq_pow_mul_det) computes the determinant of that form, and a nearest-integer rounding step supplies the factor `4/3`.

## IntegralLattices: 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-24 (`c170031` to `271e1af`)

Cancellation fails over `ℤ`, and it is now a theorem: `E₈ ⊕ E₈` and `D₁₆⁺` are not isometric, yet [they become isometric after adding a hyperbolic plane](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Cancellation.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.nonempty_isometry_orthogonalSum_hyperbolicPlane_e8PowLattice) (TauCeti#18979). This follows from a general fact, that [Kneser neighbours become isometric after adding `U`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Neighbor/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.nonempty_isometry_orthogonalSum_hyperbolicPlane_neighbor), and it is backed by neighbours now being [built from isotropic vectors at any odd prime not dividing the discriminant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Neighbor/Isotropic.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.exists_isNeighbor_of_not_dvd_discriminant). The other headline is that [a nondegenerate indefinite lattice of rank at least three has infinitely many isometries](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/IndefiniteIsometry.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.infinite_isometry_of_isIndefinite). The isotropic case uses Eichler transvections. The anisotropic case reduces to a binary sublattice, whose [proper isometries are the norm-one units of its quadratic order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Binary/NormOne.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.specialOrthogonalGroupEquivNormOneUnits), and these are infinite by Pell's equation.

The local and genus theory also moved. [Odd unimodular forms over `ℤ₂` are classified](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/BilinearForm/OddUnimodular.html#LinearMap.BilinForm.IsSymm.equivalent_iff_isSquare_det_mul_det_and_eight_dvd_of_odd) by the determinant square class together with the norm of a characteristic vector mod 8. The [class number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/ClassNumber.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.classNumber) is defined, it is a genus invariant, and it is [finite for definite genera](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/ClassNumber.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPosDef.finite_genusClasses). The determinant, the completed rational spaces and the [dyadic density profile](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Dyadic/DensityExponent/JordanSplitting.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.SameGenus.dyadicJordanProfile_eq) are all shown to depend only on the genus.

For binary forms, Lagrange reduction gives [finitely many proper classes for every nonsquare discriminant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/LagrangeReduction.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.finite_orbitRel_quotient_primitiveForms). For negative discriminant, [equal Picard classes force proper equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/Ideal/Class.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.picClass_eq_picClass_iff_of_neg). For positive discriminant, a sign-corrected [map to the narrow Picard group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/Ideal/NarrowClass.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.properClassToNarrowPic) is defined and checked on the README's discriminant-12 trap. Neither map is yet shown to be bijective. Layer 7 has begun. The [local density counting function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/LocalDensity.html#Matrix.localDensityCount) is defined, and it [stabilizes at odd primes not dividing `det A`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/LocalDensity.html#Matrix.localDensityCount_succ_eq_pow_mul_one), with point counts on quadrics over finite fields alongside. Elsewhere the window filled in smaller gaps: `O(q)` for finite quadratic forms, and positive definiteness compared with the real form.
