## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The basic dictionary (Layer 0), positive definite reduction (Layer 2) and the LMFDB certificates (Layer 9) are done. The binary dictionary, the class and mass bookkeeping, the low-rank definite classification and the odd-prime local densities are now largely in place. The general dyadic theory, Nikulin's existence and uniqueness theorems, spinor genera and the indefinite unimodular classification have not landed.

### Named results

- **Unimodular lattices of rank at most nine**: a positive definite unimodular lattice of rank at most nine is [`Iₙ`, `E₈` or `I₁ ⊕ E₈`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/PosDef/SmallRankUnimodular.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.IsPosDef.nonempty_isometry_of_isUnimodular_of_finrank_le_nine). In particular `E₈` is the only even one in rank eight.
- **The order of `O(E₈)`**: the 240 root reflections [generate the whole isometry group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeE/WeylGroup.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.weylGroup_typeE₈RootLattice_eq_top), and it has [order 696729600](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/RootLattice/TypeE/WeylGroup.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.card_isometry_typeE₈RootLattice). This makes the genus of `E₈` the first one whose mass is computed.
- **Forms, ideal classes and the ring class field**: for `Δ < 0`, proper classes of primitive positive definite forms are [the Picard group of the order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/Ideal/PicEquivalence.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.posDefProperClassEquivPic) and hence [the Galois group of its ring class field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/Ideal/RingClassField.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.posDefProperClassEquivGal). For nonsquare `Δ > 0`, proper classes of primitive forms go to [the narrow Picard group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/Ideal/Narrow/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.properClassEquivNarrowPic), through the sign-corrected map.
- **The mass of a definite binary genus**: [`m⁺ = h⁺/w`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Binary/Mass.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.properMass_eq_properClassNumber_div_of_binaryDiscriminant_neg) and [`m = h⁺/(2w)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Binary/Mass.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.mass_eq_properClassNumber_div_of_binaryDiscriminant_neg), with `h⁺` the proper class number and `w` the number of units of the order.
- **Milgram's theorem**: for an even nondegenerate lattice, the Gauss-sum invariant of `q_L` is `t₊ − t₋ mod 8` (`TauCeti.IntegralLattice.gaussSign_discriminantQuadraticModule_eq_sigPos_sub_sigNeg`).
- **Failure of cancellation**: `E₈ ⊕ E₈` and `D₁₆⁺` are not isometric, but [they become isometric after adding `U`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Cancellation.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.nonempty_isometry_orthogonalSum_hyperbolicPlane_e8PowLattice).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/FiniteBilinearModule/Quadratic.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuadraticModule) carries the discriminant forms. It has Nikulin's generators and the Gauss-sum invariant, and it classifies the odd-primary and `2`-elementary cases. Layer 5 is stated on it.
- The [mass](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/Mass.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.mass) and the [local density](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/LocalDensity/Basic.html#Matrix.localDensity) are both genus-level quantities with proved structure: `m⁺ = 2m` in positive rank for a genus with finitely many classes, explicit stabilization of the density, its unramified values, and at odd `p` the Conway–Sloane dictionary [`m_p·α_p = p^{(n+1)v_p(det)/2}`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/LocalDensity/ConwaySloane.html#TauCeti.conwaySloanePMass_mul_localDensity_toMatrix). These are the inputs the successor's mass formula consumes.
- The [class-number certificate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/LMFDB/StoredClassNumberCertificate.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.StoredClassNumberCertificate) has a field for the mass of the genus and turns it into completeness of a stored list of classes. [For `E₈`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/IntegralLattice/LMFDB/E8.html#TauCeti.IntegralLattice.typeE₈StoredClassNumberCertificate) that field is the proved mass, so the instance assumes nothing.

## The frontier

- **Dyadic classification and genus symbols**: extend the type-II and unimodular classifications to mixed types, then build the Conway–Sloane symbols and the genus symbol (3D, 3E, 3G). Several sign-walking moves are already proved.
- **The genus from the discriminant form**: prove that signature and `q_L` determine the genus (5A), and then existence and uniqueness (5B, 5C). This needs the dyadic work above and the dyadic half of 1G.
- **The adelic dictionary and spinor genera**: identify classes in the genus with double cosets over the local stabilizers (4B), then define spinor genera through local spinor norms (4C).
- **Binary tori and genera**: show that a genus of binary forms is a coset of the squares (B3) and that distinct cycles give distinct classes (B5), then build the norm-one tori and their measures (B6, B7) and the `Δ = −23` check of B8.
- **The remaining unimodular classification**: the indefinite case (6A) is absent. The source contains glued constructions of the rootful rank-24 lattices and a Leech lattice, but whether they meet every clause of 6E, including pairwise non-isometry, was not established here.
