### Named results

- **The Bartlett decomposition** — the Cholesky factor of a standard Wishart matrix of degree `n` has independent entries: standard Gaussian below the diagonal, and chi with `n - i` degrees of freedom at diagonal entry `i` (counting from zero) ([`TauCeti.Probability.map_choleskyLowerCoordinates_comap_nonsingularWishartMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Bartlett.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_choleskyLowerCoordinates_comap_nonsingularWishartMeasure)).
- **Gaussian sums of squares are noncentral chi-squared** — for independent unit-variance Gaussians, the sum of squares is noncentral chi-squared with noncentrality the squared length of the mean vector; the noncentral F and Beta laws are built on it ([`TauCeti.Probability.map_sum_sq_pi_gaussianReal_noncentral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NoncentralChiSquared/Gaussian.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_sum_sq_pi_gaussianReal_noncentral)).
- **The theta-function identity for Gaussian lattice sums** — `∑ₙ exp(-(n - c)²/(2s²))` is `exp(-c²/(2s²))` times the real part of a value of Mathlib's two-variable Jacobi theta function, and Poisson summation gives its Fourier series ([`Real.gaussianLatticeSum_eq_jacobiTheta₂`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gaussian/LatticeSum.html#Real.gaussianLatticeSum_eq_jacobiTheta₂)). It normalizes the discrete Gaussian law.
- **Change of variables to partial correlations** — integrals over positive-definite correlation matrices become integrals over the open cube of partial correlations, weighted by an explicit product of powers of `1 - z²` ([`TauCeti.setIntegral_comp_correlationCoordinatesOfPartialCorrelation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/SymmetricMatrix/Correlation.html#TauCeti.setIntegral_comp_correlationCoordinatesOfPartialCorrelation)). This is the computation the LKJ normalizer rests on.
- **Lévy's continuity theorem with existence** — if characteristic functions converge pointwise to a function continuous at zero, that limit is the characteristic function of a probability measure, and the sequence converges weakly to it ([`TauCeti.exists_probabilityMeasure_of_tendsto_charFun`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/LevyConvergence.html#TauCeti.exists_probabilityMeasure_of_tendsto_charFun)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Integration over the positive-definite cone.** Symmetric Lebesgue measure and the Cholesky change of variables reduce cone integrals to one-dimensional Gamma and Gaussian integrals, giving the [multivariate Gamma integral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/MultivariateGamma/Integral.html#TauCeti.integral_posDef_multivariateGamma) that normalizes every Wishart density.
- **The correlation reference measure.** [`TauCeti.correlationLebesgue`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/SymmetricMatrix/Correlation.html#TauCeti.correlationLebesgue) is Lebesgue measure on unit-diagonal symmetric matrices, invariant under permutations and sign changes, and is what an LKJ density will be taken against.
- **Fourier inversion for measures.** [a finite measure on `ℝ` with integrable characteristic function has a bounded continuous density](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/CharFun/Inversion.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.eq_withDensity_charFunDensity) given by the inversion integral.

## The frontier

- **The LKJ law.** The normalizer `Z_p(η)` as a product of Beta functions from the partial-correlation change of variables, then `lkjMeasure` with its Beta marginals, independent partial correlations and determinant moments. The coordinates, determinant formula and Jacobian are in place.
- **Scalar stable laws.** The Lévy-measure integral `∫(e^{itx} - 1 - it sin x) dν = Ψ_{α,β}(t)`, then truncated compound-Poisson approximations whose limit exists by Lévy's continuity theorem. The sine-compensated Laplace transform and the Mellin transform of `1/(1 + y²)` are ready.
- **Circular and spherical laws.** The wrapped-normal density series and chart cdfs, now unblocked by the lattice sum, and the von Mises–Fisher normalizer, mean and second moment in dimension two and up.
- **Densities of the multivariate laws.** The multivariate t, matrix normal and logistic-normal densities, then the conditional multivariate t.
- **Remaining scalar and discrete families.** Truncated and skew normal, triangular, hurdle counts, the multivariate hypergeometric and the integer-interval uniform, plus the noncentral Beta density and cdf.
