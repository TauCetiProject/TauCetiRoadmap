### Named results

- **The Wick–Isserlis formula** — for a centred Gaussian vector with any positive-semidefinite covariance, the expectation of a product of coordinates, repeats allowed, is the sum over perfect matchings of the positions of the products of matched covariances, so odd products vanish ([`TauCeti.Probability.integral_prod_multivariateGaussian_eq_sum_perfectMatching`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Wick.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_prod_multivariateGaussian_eq_sum_perfectMatching)).
- **The Bartlett decomposition** — the Cholesky entries of a standard Wishart matrix are independent, with standard Gaussian strict-lower entries and chi diagonal entries of successively decreasing degrees ([`TauCeti.Probability.map_choleskyLowerCoordinates_comap_nonsingularWishartMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Bartlett.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_choleskyLowerCoordinates_comap_nonsingularWishartMeasure)).
- **Gaussian conditioning with singular covariance** — conditioning a jointly Gaussian vector uses pseudoinverse regression and a generalized Schur complement, including when the observed block is singular ([`TauCeti.Probability.condDistrib_multivariateGaussian`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Conditional/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.condDistrib_multivariateGaussian)).
- **Independence of the LKJ partial correlations** — the partial correlations of an LKJ matrix are independent centred symmetric Beta variables ([`ProbabilityTheory.HasLaw.iIndepFun_partialCorrelation_lkjMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/LKJ/PartialCorrelation.html#ProbabilityTheory.HasLaw.iIndepFun_partialCorrelation_lkjMeasure)). From this come the law's marginals, its quantiles, its exact mixed entry moments and its [determinant-power moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/LKJ/Moments.html#ProbabilityTheory.HasLaw.integral_rpow_det_lkjMeasure).
- **Stable laws in the S₀ convention** — compensated compound Poisson limits produce a law with characteristic function `exp(imt + Ψ(st))`, a bounded density, exact support (a half-line for fully skewed laws with α < 1), and the sharp moment threshold `q < α`. For α ≥ 1 the cdf is strictly increasing on the line, so quantiles are unique roots ([`TauCeti.Probability.strictMono_cdf_stableMeasure_of_one_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Stable/Cdf.html#TauCeti.Probability.strictMono_cdf_stableMeasure_of_one_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Lévy's continuity theorem, including existence** — if characteristic functions converge pointwise to a function continuous at zero, then a unique limiting probability law exists. This is the existence step for the stable laws ([`TauCeti.exists_probabilityMeasure_of_tendsto_charFun`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/LevyConvergence.html#TauCeti.exists_probabilityMeasure_of_tendsto_charFun)).
- **Change of variables to partial correlations** — integration over positive-definite correlation matrices reduces to an open cube with an explicit product Jacobian, which is what normalizes the LKJ law ([`TauCeti.setIntegral_comp_correlationCoordinatesOfPartialCorrelation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/SymmetricMatrix/Correlation.html#TauCeti.setIntegral_comp_correlationCoordinatesOfPartialCorrelation)).
- **Quantile construction of extreme order statistics** — the minimum and maximum of an iid sample from any law on the line, atoms included, are the images of Beta(1, n) and Beta(n, 1) under its quantile function. This gives the sample-extreme quantile formulas with no continuity hypothesis ([`TauCeti.Probability.map_orderStatistic_last_pi_eq_map_betaMeasure_quantile`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/OrderStatistic/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_orderStatistic_last_pi_eq_map_betaMeasure_quantile)).
