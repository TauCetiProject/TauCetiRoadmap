## StandardDistributions: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-10 (`8745177` to `14e3bf8`)

Layer 4, the web of relations among the families, is now complete. The square of a standard Gaussian is chi-squared with one degree of freedom, and a sum of independent squares carries one degree of freedom per summand ([`TauCeti.Probability.gaussianReal_map_sq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/ChiSquared.html#TauCeti.Probability.gaussianReal_map_sq)). The three classical ratios followed: a standard Gaussian over the root of an independent scaled chi-squared is Student's t ([`TauCeti.Probability.hasLaw_studentT_of_gaussian_chiSquared`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/StudentT/ChiSquared.html#TauCeti.Probability.hasLaw_studentT_of_gaussian_chiSquared)), a ratio of two scaled chi-squares is Fisher's F, and a ratio of two standard Gaussians is Cauchy. The Gamma-Beta change of variables sends a pair of independent gamma variables of common rate to a ratio and a sum that are independent, Beta and gamma ([`TauCeti.map_div_add_prod_gammaMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gamma/Beta.html#TauCeti.map_div_add_prod_gammaMeasure)), and a Gamma mixture of Poisson rates is negative-binomial ([`TauCeti.Probability.bind_gammaMeasure_poissonMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gamma/Poisson.html#TauCeti.Probability.bind_gammaMeasure_poissonMeasure)).

Layer 3's nine scalar families are all built. Fisher's F, the inverse-gamma law and the negative binomial arrived whole, each with its density or mass formula, its cdf through the regularized incomplete functions, sharp moment thresholds with the matching non-integrability, its exponential-integrability domain, and parameter measurability. Student's t gained its mean, variance and cdf; Weibull its three moment-generating regimes, including the Gamma-coefficient series above shape one; hypergeometric its mean, variance, symmetry in sample size and marked count, and its binomial limit.

Higher up, the moment-generating function of a Gaussian quadratic form landed with its exact domain, and the conditional law of one block of a jointly Gaussian vector is the Schur-complement Gaussian kernel ([`TauCeti.condDistrib_multivariateGaussian`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Conditional.html#TauCeti.condDistrib_multivariateGaussian)). Layer 6 opened with its carrier (TauCeti#5745): the Frobenius inner product on symmetric matrices, upper-triangular coordinates, [`TauCeti.symmetricLebesgue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/SymmetricMatrix/Lebesgue.html#TauCeti.symmetricLebesgue) as a Haar measure, the congruence Jacobian `(det C) ^ (p + 1)`, and the singular matrices as a null set. The multinomial has masses, binomial coordinate marginals and directional transforms but no mean or covariance yet; the multivariate Gaussian density, Dirichlet and Wishart remain open.

This rests on a completed matrix-integration route: the [Cholesky change of variables](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/SymmetricMatrix/Cholesky.html#TauCeti.map_cholesky_symmetricLebesgue) yields the [multivariate Gamma integral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/MultivariateGamma/Integral.html#TauCeti.integral_posDef_multivariateGamma), which normalizes the nonsingular Wishart density. Matrix inversion then supplies the inverse-Wishart family, with its sharp integrability threshold and [mean above that threshold](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Inverse.html#TauCeti.integral_id_inverseWishartMeasure).

Layer 5 also gained the [multivariate Gaussian density](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Density.html#TauCeti.multivariateGaussian_eq_withDensity), the Dirichlet chart density and moments, and the missing multinomial moments, characteristic function and aggregation law. The one-dimensional conditional-Gaussian specialization now has the usual regression formula. What is not established here is the multivariate Gaussian's Bochner mean, nor the real-degree nonsingular Wishart mean, covariance, and full-row-rank rectangular pushforward.

## StandardDistributions: 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-26 (`ecb4a7b` to `759eb3e`)

The real-degree Wishart law now has its moments and marginals, the last open targets of Layer 6. For positive-definite scale `S` and degree `n > p - 1` the [mean is `n • S`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Moments.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_id_nonsingularWishartMeasure) and entries `(i, j)` and `(k, l)` have [covariance `n (S i k * S j l + S i l * S j k)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Moments.html#TauCeti.Probability.covariance_coe_apply_nonsingularWishartMeasure), both read off the trace moment-generating function that the density and Gaussian-Gram families share. [Congruence by a matrix `M` of full row rank](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Marginal.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_symmetricCongruenceLinearMap_nonsingularWishartMeasure) carries the law of scale `S` to that of scale `M * S * Mᵀ`; an LQ factorization reduces it to reading off coordinates at identity scale, where the Bartlett coordinates settle it. So every [principal submatrix of a Wishart matrix is Wishart](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Marginal.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_symmetricCongruenceLinearMap_submatrix_one_nonsingularWishartMeasure), with the matching submatrix of `S` as scale. The nonsingular and inverse-Wishart laws also gained `HasPDF` and Radon–Nikodym theorems against symmetric Lebesgue measure, as did the Gaussian-Gram law at positive-definite scale and degree at least the dimension, for example [`TauCeti.Probability.rnDeriv_nonsingularWishartMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Nonsingular.html#TauCeti.Probability.rnDeriv_nonsingularWishartMeasure).

Layer 5 is closed as well. The multivariate Gaussian's Bochner mean, which the last report could not establish, is Mathlib's `ProbabilityTheory.integral_id_multivariateGaussian`; Tau Ceti adds its [random-variable form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Multivariate.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_of_hasLaw_multivariateGaussian) and the law's [measurability in the raw coordinates of its covariance](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Measurability.html#TauCeti.Probability.measurable_multivariateGaussian), the form a kernel needs. Relations previously stated only for random variables gained product-measure forms, among them the [difference of two independent exponentials of the same rate being Laplace](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Sums.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_sub_prod_expMeasure) and the negative-binomial and Erlang laws of geometric and exponential sums.

Most of the window consolidated existing code. The distribution APIs moved into one namespace, `TauCeti.Probability` (TauCeti#7983); shared density and measurability modules were split per family; and the exponential and geometric laws now derive their moments and transforms from the Gamma and negative-binomial results. With this, every layer of the roadmap is done.

The roadmap grew from seven layers to sixteen, and work went straight into the new ones. The headline is order statistics. The `k`-th smallest of `n` independent uniform variables, counting from zero, is [Beta with shapes `k + 1` and `n - k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/OrderStatistic/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_orderStatistic_pi_uniformMeasure), and the sorted sample has [density `n!` on the increasing cube](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/OrderStatistic/Joint.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_orderStatistics_pi_uniformMeasure). For any real law, the `k`-th order statistic is [that Beta law pushed through the source quantile](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/OrderStatistic/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_orderStatistic_pi_eq_map_betaMeasure_quantile). The log-normal law also got its classical counterexample: weighting it by `1 + ε sin (2π log x)` gives a [family of distinct laws](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/LogNormal/MomentIndeterminacy.html#TauCeti.Probability.injOn_logNormalMomentPerturbation) that all have [the log-normal moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/LogNormal/MomentIndeterminacy.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_pow_logNormalMomentPerturbation).

Over a dozen scalar families arrived, among them logistic, Gumbel, generalized extreme-value, generalized Pareto, chi, Nakagami, beta prime, Gompertz and generalized gamma. ## StandardDistributions: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-24 (`a5cde7b` to `271e1af`)

The noncentral families are the main story of this window. The noncentral chi-squared law is built as [a Poisson mixture of central ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NoncentralChiSquared/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.noncentralChiSquaredMeasure_eq_sum), and the classical description is now a theorem: [the sum of squares of independent unit-variance Gaussians is noncentral chi-squared](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NoncentralChiSquared/Gaussian.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_sum_sq_pi_gaussianReal_noncentral), with noncentrality the squared length of the mean vector. The law has a density, [a cdf that is a Poisson series of regularized Gamma functions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NoncentralChiSquared/Cdf.html#TauCeti.Probability.cdf_noncentralChiSquaredMeasure_eq), and quantiles. On top of it come the noncentral chi, the [noncentral F law as a variance ratio](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NoncentralF/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.hasLaw_noncentralF_of_noncentralChiSquared) and the [noncentral Beta law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NoncentralBeta/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.hasLaw_noncentralBeta_of_noncentralChiSquared). Compound Poisson laws also arrived. [A Poisson number of iid jumps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/CompoundPoisson/RandomSum.html#ProbabilityTheory.iIndepFun.hasLaw_sum_range_compoundPoissonMeasure) has the compound Poisson law, which comes with its transforms, moments and cdf, and [a Poisson sum of logarithmic-series variables is negative binomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/LogarithmicSeries/CompoundPoisson.html#ProbabilityTheory.iIndepFun.hasLaw_sum_range_negativeBinomialMeasure).

A multivariate log-normal law also landed. Elsewhere, [the gaps between sorted uniform points are Dirichlet with all shapes one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Dirichlet/Spacing.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_tupleSpacings_orderStatistics_pi_uniformMeasure), and the negative-multinomial law was built as [a Gamma mixture of Poisson counts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NegativeMultinomial/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.negativeMultinomialMeasure).

For the circular laws, Bessel `I_n` got [its circle integral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/BesselI.html#Real.integral_exp_mul_cos_mul_cos), and the unit sphere got [its surface area](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Euclidean/Volume/Sphere.html#TauCeti.toSphere_volume_apply_univ).

## StandardDistributions: 2026-10-24 to 2026-10-26 (`271e1af` to `938acbb`)

Layer 6 is finished. The last missing piece was the pair of inverse-Wishart marginal laws at a general positive-definite scale `S`. The [diagonal entries of an inverse-Wishart matrix are inverse gamma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Inverse/Marginal.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_coe_apply_inverseWishartMeasure), with shape `(n - p + 1)/2` and scale `S i i / 2`. Dually, for a Wishart matrix `W` of scale `S⁻¹`, the [reciprocal of each diagonal entry of `W⁻¹` is a scaled chi-squared variable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Wishart/Inverse/Marginal.html#TauCeti.Probability.map_inv_inv_apply_nonsingularWishartMeasure) with `n - p + 1` degrees of freedom. The bridge is a matrix identity: [the reciprocal of `(A⁻¹) i i` is a scalar Schur complement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/SchurComplement.html#Matrix.PosDef.inv_inv_apply_self_eq_sub). It is combined with a Cholesky-type factorization that isolates one coordinate.

The negative-multinomial law now has nearly everything Layer 9 asks of it. It has its [directional moment generating function on the exact domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NegativeMultinomial/Transforms.html#TauCeti.Probability.mgf_sum_mul_natCast_negativeMultinomialMeasure) and the [mixed falling-factorial moments](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NegativeMultinomial/FactorialMoments.html#TauCeti.Probability.integral_prod_descFactorial_negativeMultinomialMeasure) `r(r+1)⋯(r+N-1)∏(pᵢ/p₀)^kᵢ`, and [convolution at fixed weights adds shapes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NegativeMultinomial/Convolution.html#TauCeti.Probability.negativeMultinomialMeasure_conv_negativeMultinomialMeasure). Independent Poisson count vectors got the same treatment. Its characteristic function is still missing.

Smaller additions: the inverse Gaussian law got its [characteristic function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/InverseGaussian/CharFun.html#TauCeti.Probability.charFun_inverseGaussianMeasure) and a moment generating function that stays finite at the critical parameter. The asymmetric Laplace law got all its raw moments. The [matrix normal law is weakly continuous in its parameters](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/MatrixNormal/Continuity.html#TauCeti.Probability.continuous_matrixNormalMeasure), even at singular covariances, which fills one entry of the Layer 7 continuity table. There were also cdf and quantile facts for censored, hyperexponential and noncentral chi-squared laws.
