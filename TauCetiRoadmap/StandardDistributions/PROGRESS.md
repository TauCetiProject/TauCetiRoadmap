## StandardDistributions: 2026-08-17 to 2026-09-01 (`fdf68d6` to `8745177`)

Layer 2's closed-form cumulative distribution functions are all in place. The **Gaussian cdf** is `(1 + erf((x - m)/√(2v)))/2` ([`TauCeti.cdf_gaussianReal_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Cdf.html#TauCeti.cdf_gaussianReal_eq)), on top of a new error function ([`TauCeti.Real.erf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Erf.html#TauCeti.Real.erf)); the **gamma and beta cdfs** are the regularized incomplete gamma and beta functions ([`TauCeti.cdf_gammaMeasure_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gamma/Cdf.html#TauCeti.cdf_gammaMeasure_eq), [`TauCeti.cdf_betaMeasure_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Beta/Cdf.html#TauCeti.cdf_betaMeasure_eq)); and both discrete tail identities landed, the **binomial tail** `Bin(n,p){m ≤ k} = I_p(m, n - m + 1)` ([`TauCeti.binomial_tail_eq_regularizedIncompleteBeta`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Binomial/Tail.html#TauCeti.binomial_tail_eq_regularizedIncompleteBeta)) and the **Poisson tail** `Po(r){n < k} = P(n + 1, r)` ([`TauCeti.poissonMeasure_tail_eq_regularizedGamma`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Poisson/Tail.html#TauCeti.poissonMeasure_tail_eq_regularizedGamma)). The special functions come with continuity, monotonicity, the step recurrences, and the reflection formula.

Layers 0 and 1 are complete. Every Mathlib scalar family now has `HasPDF`, Radon–Nikodym, and parameter-measurability theorems (TauCeti#3752) together with its listed moments and transforms. Named results among them: **gamma convolution** adds shapes ([`TauCeti.gammaMeasure_conv_gammaMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gamma/Basic.html#TauCeti.gammaMeasure_conv_gammaMeasure)); the **Cauchy characteristic function** `exp(i x₀ t - γ|t|)` ([`TauCeti.charFun_cauchyMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Cauchy.html#TauCeti.charFun_cauchyMeasure)) and its consequence that the **sample mean of i.i.d. Cauchy variables is again Cauchy** ([`TauCeti.hasLaw_average_of_iIndepFun_cauchyMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Cauchy.html#TauCeti.hasLaw_average_of_iIndepFun_cauchyMeasure)); **exponential memorylessness** ([`TauCeti.Probability.memoryless_expMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Exponential.html#TauCeti.Probability.memoryless_expMeasure)); and **pgf uniqueness**, a probability measure on `ℕ` being determined by its generating function on `(-1, 1)` ([`TauCeti.Probability.measure_eq_of_pgf_eqOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/GeneratingFunction.html#TauCeti.Probability.measure_eq_of_pgf_eqOn)). The uniform law on an interval, the roadmap's model family, is fully developed.

Of Layer 3's new scalar families, Laplace, log-normal, and chi-squared are complete. Weibull has its density, cdf, and moments but none of its three mgf regimes; Student's t has only its measure, density, and `studentTMeasure 1 = cauchyMeasure 0 1` ([`TauCeti.Probability.studentTMeasure_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/StudentT.html#TauCeti.Probability.studentTMeasure_one)), with no moments or cdf; hypergeometric has its masses and finite-sum transforms but no mean, variance, symmetry, or binomial limit. Inverse-gamma, Fisher's F, and negative binomial have not begun. Beyond scalar laws there are the cdfs of i.i.d. maxima and minima ([`TauCeti.Probability.cdf_max_iid`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Relations.html#TauCeti.Probability.cdf_max_iid)), covariance matrices, and the affine image of a multivariate Gaussian ([`TauCeti.map_affine_multivariateGaussian`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Affine.html#TauCeti.map_affine_multivariateGaussian)); the rest of Layers 4–6 is untouched.

## StandardDistributions: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-09 (`8745177` to `d8476fa`)

The Student t cdf is now expressed through the regularized incomplete beta function, and the sharp thresholds for its mean, variance, and exponential integrability complete that family ([`TauCeti.Probability.cdf_studentTMeasure_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/StudentT/Cdf.html#TauCeti.Probability.cdf_studentTMeasure_eq)). The [inverse-gamma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/InverseGamma.html#TauCeti.Probability.inverseGammaMeasure), [Fisher's F](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/FisherSnedecor/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.fisherSnedecorMeasure), and [negative-binomial](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/NegativeBinomial/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.negativeBinomialMeasure) laws likewise acquired their elementary theories, while the Weibull mgf is a Gamma-coefficient series when the shape exceeds one ([`TauCeti.Probability.mgf_id_weibullMeasure_of_one_lt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Weibull/Transforms.html#TauCeti.Probability.mgf_id_weibullMeasure_of_one_lt)). Hypergeometric symmetry, moments, and the binomial limit complete Layer 3 ([`TauCeti.Probability.tendsto_hypergeometricMeasure_real_singleton`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Hypergeometric/Limit.html#TauCeti.Probability.tendsto_hypergeometricMeasure_real_singleton)).

The classical relations in Layer 4 are also in place. Independent gamma variables split into an independent beta ratio and gamma sum ([`TauCeti.map_div_add_prod_gammaMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gamma/Beta.html#TauCeti.map_div_add_prod_gammaMeasure)), while a Gamma mixture of Poisson laws is negative-binomial ([`TauCeti.Probability.bind_gammaMeasure_poissonMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gamma/Poisson.html#TauCeti.Probability.bind_gammaMeasure_poissonMeasure)). The remaining requested pushforward, ratio, finite-sum, and difference laws fill out the layer.

Beyond the scalar layers, the conditional multivariate Gaussian theorem gives the usual affine mean and Schur-complement covariance ([`TauCeti.condDistrib_multivariateGaussian`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Conditional.html#TauCeti.condDistrib_multivariateGaussian)), and the quadratic-form mgf has its exact positive-definite domain ([`TauCeti.mgf_inner_toEuclideanLin_multivariateGaussian`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/QuadraticForm.html#TauCeti.mgf_inner_toEuclideanLin_multivariateGaussian)). A [multinomial measure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Multinomial/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.multinomialMeasure), cell marginals, and directional mgf have begun Layer 5. Layer 6 now has [normalized symmetric Lebesgue measure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/SymmetricMatrix/Lebesgue.html#TauCeti.symmetricLebesgue) and its congruence change-of-variables theorem ([`Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.map_symmetricCongruence_symmetricLebesgue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/SymmetricMatrix/Congruence.html#Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.map_symmetricCongruence_symmetricLebesgue)); Cholesky, multivariate Gamma, and the Wishart families have not landed.
