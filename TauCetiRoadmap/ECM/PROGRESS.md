## ECM: 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-09 (`454fefe` to `7ca783d`)

All five of the roadmap's end theorems arrived in this window. Montgomery's two x-only formulas are now proved correct on Mathlib's native point group of the monic model: [doubling](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Montgomery/Double.html#TauCeti.Montgomery.double_coordinates) computes the x-coordinate of `[2]P` for every point, and [differential addition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Montgomery/DiffAdd.html#TauCeti.Montgomery.diffAdd_coordinates) computes that of `P + Q` from `P`, `Q` and `P − Q`, with output zero exactly when the difference is infinity or the distinguished 2-torsion point `(0, 0)`; [other points of order two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Montgomery/DiffAdd.html#TauCeti.Montgomery.diffAdd_coordinates_some_ne_zero) are shown not to be exceptional. [Suyama's ellipticity criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Montgomery/Suyama/Basic.html#TauCeti.Montgomery.isElliptic_model_suyama_iff) pins down exactly the five factors in `σ` at which Suyama's curve degenerates, and [stage-1 detection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Montgomery/Detection.html#TauCeti.Montgomery.prime_dvd_gcd_of_card_dvd) shows that a prime `p ∣ n` divides `gcd(Z, n)` once a pair over `ZMod n` reduces to a multiple of a point by a multiple of the group order.

Underneath sit the [monic model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Montgomery/Basic.html#TauCeti.Montgomery.model) with its discriminant and ellipticity criterion, raw coordinate pairs, and the projective x-coordinate, whose fibres are `{P, −P}` and whose image is [identified with the quotient by negation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Montgomery/Coordinates.html#TauCeti.Montgomery.orbitRelQuotientEquivRangeProjectiveX). The stage-2 side got its [cross-difference criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Montgomery/Coordinates.html#TauCeti.Montgomery.cross_coordinates_zsmul_eq_zero_iff): the pairs of `[m]Q` and `[n]Q` agree projectively exactly when `[m − n]Q` or `[m + n]Q` vanishes. A gcd lemma for [finite products of residues](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/ZMod/ProductGCD.html#List.prime_dvd_gcd_val_prod_iff) needs no elliptic-curve hypothesis. Further work made points, coordinates and the projective line compatible with field embeddings.

Not yet there: the description of the stage-1 multiplier `M(B₁)` as an lcm of prime powers.
