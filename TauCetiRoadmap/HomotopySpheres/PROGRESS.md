## HomotopySpheres: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-13 (`cbcf10e` to `aa6e43e`)

The first work for this roadmap is a small algebraic ingredient for the positive half of the parallelizable-spheres theorem, which says that Sⁿ has a smooth tangent frame exactly when n is 1, 3 or 7. Any Clifford module over a negative-definite Clifford algebra with a finite orthogonal basis squaring to −1 carries a [positive-definite symmetric form making every Clifford vector skew-adjoint](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Module/Metric.html#AlgHom.exists_posDef_isSymm_isSkewAdjoint_map_cliffordAlgebraι). It comes from averaging a starting form over the finite group of signed monomials, and needs neither irreducibility nor faithfulness. A companion lemma says that a skew-adjoint vector [scales the form by −Q(v)](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Module/Metric.html#AlgHom.bilinForm_map_cliffordAlgebraι_apply_map_cliffordAlgebraι_apply) when it acts on both arguments (TauCeti#38969). Over the reals, these are the facts that make the fields Aᵢx orthonormal and tangent on the unit sphere. The frames themselves are not built yet: that still needs the modules of dimensions 2, 4 and 8 and the tangent trivialization on the sphere.

## HomotopySpheres: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`aa6e43e` to `b235000`)

The algebraic core of the Adams–Atiyah proof that Hopf invariant one occurs only in dimensions 2, 4 and 8 has landed as the [Adams–Atiyah divisibility test](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/HopfInvariant/AdamsOperation.html#TauCeti.eq_one_or_eq_two_or_eq_four_of_adamsOperation). Take a free abelian group on x and y with a multiplication satisfying x² = h·y for odd h. Suppose two commuting additive maps ψ² and ψ³ act as Adams operations do on the K-theory of a two-cell complex, with ψˢx = sʳx + b_s·y and ψˢy = s²ʳy, and suppose ψ²x ≡ x² mod 2. Then r is 1, 2 or 4. The proof combines the [commutation identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/HopfInvariant/AdamsOperation.html#TauCeti.pow_mul_pow_sub_one_mul_eq_pow_mul_pow_sub_one_mul) 3ʳ(3ʳ−1)b₂ = 2ʳ(2ʳ−1)b₃ with the number-theoretic fact that [2ʳ divides 3ʳ − 1 exactly when r is 0, 1, 2 or 4](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiplicity.html#Nat.two_pow_dvd_three_pow_sub_one_iff) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/663). The test is purely formal so far. The Hopf invariant, the K-theory of the mapping cone and the Adams operations that would feed it are not built.

Layer 3's James construction also began. The [James reduced product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/JamesReducedProduct.html#TauCeti.JamesReducedProduct) is defined for an arbitrary pointed space and formed in compactly generated spaces. As a monoid it is free on the non-basepoint points, and it has the [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/JamesReducedProduct.html#TauCeti.JamesReducedProduct.lift) of extending based maps into topological monoids. A map out of it is [continuous exactly when](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/JamesReducedProduct.html#TauCeti.JamesReducedProduct.continuous_iff) it is continuous on families of words parametrized by compact Hausdorff spaces. The [word-length filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/JamesReducedProduct.html#TauCeti.JamesReducedProduct.filtration) is exhaustive and multiplicative, closed when the basepoint is closed, and compact for compact Hausdorff spaces. The construction is functorial and preserves path-connectedness (TauCeti#38519). Joint continuity of the multiplication is still missing. So are compatibility with CW structures, the identification of filtration quotients with smash powers, and the James map to ΩΣX.
