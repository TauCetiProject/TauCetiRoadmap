## JacobianChallenge: 2026-06-09 to 2026-08-03 (`9ffdcc6` to `11ef09d`)

Over an affine Dedekind chart the divisor class group is now identified with the ideal class group ([`classGroupAddEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Dedekind/ClassGroup.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.classGroupAddEquiv)), and invertible fractional ideals with the free Weil-divisor group on the height-one primes ([`fractionalIdealDivisorAddEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/FractionalIdealDivisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.fractionalIdealDivisorAddEquiv), TauCeti#1345): the affine case of the Weil-Cartier dictionary, carrying principal ideals to principal divisors and restricting to a correspondence between integral ideals and effective divisors. On the scheme side, a Weil divisor is now a finite formal sum of codimension-one points, matched with the algebraic cycles supported in codimension one ([`equivFiniteCodimensionOneCycles`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.equivFiniteCodimensionOneCycles)); the orders of a nonzero rational function on a Noetherian integral scheme were shown to be nonzero at only finitely many such points, which is what assembles them into an order system ([`OrderSystem.ofScheme`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/Principal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.OrderSystem.ofScheme), TauCeti#1799). Degree exists in relative form, weighted by residue degrees ([`relativeDegree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/Degree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.relativeDegree)), with the normalisation `D - (deg D) · x₀` landing in degree zero at a rational point.

Abelian varieties were built from the definition up: a proper geometrically integral group scheme over a field ([`AbelianVariety`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety)), from which smoothness, geometric connectedness and commutativity of the group law follow. They form a category with commutative-group hom-sets, finite products and a zero object, base change along a field extension is a functor, endomorphisms form a ring in which the integer `n` acts as `[n]` ([`End`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/End/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety.End)), and isogenies are the finite surjective homomorphisms.

What is not there: no coherent cohomology, hence no genus, no Euler characteristic and no Riemann-Roch; `Pic⁰` so far is the abstract quotient of degree-zero divisors by principal ones ([`weightedDegreeZeroQuotientEquivPicZero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/PicZeroQuotient.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.OrderSystem.weightedDegreeZeroQuotientEquivPicZero)), not a functor and not a scheme; and Abel-Jacobi appears only as a map on divisor classes, not as a morphism of schemes.

## JacobianChallenge: 2026-09-11 to 2026-09-30 (`2c0c80a` to `e440f4e`)

The Riemann–Roch theorem is now proved for curves: on a proper integral curve over `k` whose codimension-one local rings are discrete valuation rings and which has a `k`-rational point, every Weil divisor satisfies `χ(𝒪_X(D)) = deg D + 1 − g`, with the genus defined as `dim H¹(X, 𝒪_X)` ([`eulerCharBelow_sheaf_eq_relativeDegree_add_one_sub_genus`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/RiemannRoch/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.eulerCharBelow_sheaf_eq_relativeDegree_add_one_sub_genus), TauCeti#7715). It assumes `H¹(X, 𝒪_X)` is finite-dimensional, which is proved separately for such curves ([`finiteDimensional_cohomology_one_trivial_of_isProper`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/RiemannRoch/Finiteness.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.finiteDimensional_cohomology_one_trivial_of_isProper)). The proof computes cohomology through the short exact sequence of principal parts and the residue sequence at a closed point, and along the way shows that line bundles on a curve have no cohomology above degree one. Serre duality followed, `H¹(𝒪_X(D))^∨ ≅ H⁰(𝒪_X(K − D))` ([`cohomologyOneDualEquivCohomologyZero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/SerreDuality.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.cohomologyOneDualEquivCohomologyZero)), with a canonical divisor of degree `2g − 2`. It is stated with Weil differentials of the function field rather than a relative dualizing sheaf, and it assumes `k` is integrally closed in `k(X)`.

Layer A is now complete in the roadmap's setting. Line-bundle classes on an integral scheme form the Picard group ([`instCommGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/CartierDivisor/Picard.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.LineBundleClass.instCommGroup)). Every line bundle comes from a Cartier divisor, and a Cartier divisor gives the trivial bundle exactly when it is principal. On curves, `Cl(X) ≅ Pic(X)` ([`classGroupAddEquivLineBundleClass`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/Picard.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.classGroupAddEquivLineBundleClass)). The Euler-characteristic degree `χ(L) − χ(𝒪_X)` agrees with the weighted divisor degree, and `Pic⁰`, defined as its kernel, is identified with `Cl⁰` ([`classGroupPicZeroAddEquivPicZero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/PicZero.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.classGroupPicZeroAddEquivPicZero)).

Layer D has begun with the rigidified Picard functor ([`rigidifiedPicardFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/PicardFunctor/Rigidified.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.rigidifiedPicardFunctor)). It sends `T` to the line bundles on `X_T` trivialized along the base-changed section. Alongside it is a proof that automorphisms of a line bundle are global units, which is what rigidification kills. So far this is a functor of sets. There is no fppf sheafification, no `Pic⁰` subfunctor and no representability. Separately, a smooth geometrically connected morphism is now shown to be geometrically integral ([`Smooth.geometricallyIntegral`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Smooth/GeometricallyIntegral.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Smooth.geometricallyIntegral)), the standing-hypothesis lemma the README asks for.

Serre duality now holds for line bundles rather than only divisor sheaves: on a proper curve, `H¹(X, L)^∨ ≅ H⁰(X, ω ⊗ L⁻¹)` for every line bundle `L` ([`nonempty_cohomologyOneDualEquivCohomologyZero_tensor_dual`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/SerreDuality.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.InvertibleSheaf.nonempty_cohomologyOneDualEquivCohomologyZero_tensor_dual), TauCeti#11165), and the canonical bundle has degree `2g − 2` ([`eulerDegree_canonicalBundle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/SerreDuality.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.InvertibleSheaf.eulerDegree_canonicalBundle)). General coherent cohomology took its first real steps. Finiteness on proper schemes is still missing.

On the abelian-variety side, the rigidity lemma landed, and with it the fact that identity-preserving morphisms between abelian varieties are homomorphisms. The Albanese property, the universal property the Jacobian must eventually satisfy, is now defined, with uniqueness up to unique isomorphism ([`IsAlbanese`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/Albanese.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety.IsAlbanese)). Relative effective Cartier divisors, stable under flat pullback, open Layer C.

## JacobianChallenge: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25 (`a5cde7b` to `e8ea0d1`)

The Abel–Jacobi map of a curve of positive genus now separates points. On a smooth proper geometrically connected curve of genus `g ≥ 1` with a rational point, distinct `K`-points have distinct images in `Pic⁰` for every field extension `K` ([`abelJacobiMap_app_injective_of_genus_pos`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/PicardFunctor/Abel/PointSeparation.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.abelJacobiMap_app_injective_of_genus_pos), TauCeti#19587). A version holds on the field-valued fibres of a family. This is the pointwise half of the acceptance check that Abel–Jacobi is a closed immersion in positive genus. Injectivity on tangent vectors is still missing, and so is any scheme for the map to land in. Separately, on such a curve, sections of a line bundle `L` take arbitrary values along an effective Cartier divisor `D` once `deg L − deg D > 2g − 2` ([`surjective_tensorRestriction_of_high_degree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/LineBundle/Restriction.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.InvertibleSheaf.surjective_tensorRestriction_of_high_degree)).

Relative cohomology took its first finiteness steps. Over a Noetherian ring `R`, every quasi-coherent sheaf of finite type on `ℙ¹_R` has finitely generated `H⁰` and `H¹` and no higher cohomology ([`finite_cohomology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/ProjectiveLine.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.projectiveLine.finite_cohomology)). This took a good deal of module theory over `R[F, F⁻¹]`. Higher direct images vanish along affine morphisms, and along finite morphisms they are coherent in every degree ([`isFinitePresentation_higherDirectImage_of_isFinite`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/HigherDirectImage/Affine.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.isFinitePresentation_higherDirectImage_of_isFinite)), the situation of Layer C's first check. Coherence under general proper morphisms is still open. On the abelian-variety side, an abelian variety is the zero object exactly when it has dimension zero ([`isZero_iff_dim_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/DimensionZero.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety.isZero_iff_dim_eq_zero)). In that case every `[n]` is the identity, and `[0]` is an isogeny only then. These cover the dimension-zero checks on multiplication in Layer E.
