## JacobianChallenge: 2026-06-09 to 2026-08-03 (`9ffdcc6` to `11ef09d`)

Over an affine Dedekind chart the divisor class group is now identified with the ideal class group ([`classGroupAddEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Dedekind/ClassGroup.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.classGroupAddEquiv)), and invertible fractional ideals with the free Weil-divisor group on the height-one primes ([`fractionalIdealDivisorAddEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/FractionalIdealDivisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.fractionalIdealDivisorAddEquiv), TauCeti#1345): the affine case of the Weil-Cartier dictionary, carrying principal ideals to principal divisors and restricting to a correspondence between integral ideals and effective divisors. On the scheme side, a Weil divisor is now a finite formal sum of codimension-one points, matched with the algebraic cycles supported in codimension one ([`equivFiniteCodimensionOneCycles`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.equivFiniteCodimensionOneCycles)); the orders of a nonzero rational function on a Noetherian integral scheme were shown to be nonzero at only finitely many such points, which is what assembles them into an order system ([`OrderSystem.ofScheme`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/Principal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.OrderSystem.ofScheme), TauCeti#1799). Degree exists in relative form, weighted by residue degrees ([`relativeDegree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/Degree.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.relativeDegree)), with the normalisation `D - (deg D) · x₀` landing in degree zero at a rational point.

Abelian varieties were built from the definition up: a proper geometrically integral group scheme over a field ([`AbelianVariety`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety)), from which smoothness, geometric connectedness and commutativity of the group law follow. They form a category with commutative-group hom-sets, finite products and a zero object, base change along a field extension is a functor, endomorphisms form a ring in which the integer `n` acts as `[n]` ([`End`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/End/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety.End)), and isogenies are the finite surjective homomorphisms.

What is not there: no coherent cohomology, hence no genus, no Euler characteristic and no Riemann-Roch; `Pic⁰` so far is the abstract quotient of degree-zero divisors by principal ones ([`weightedDegreeZeroQuotientEquivPicZero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/PicZeroQuotient.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.OrderSystem.weightedDegreeZeroQuotientEquivPicZero)), not a functor and not a scheme; and Abel-Jacobi appears only as a map on divisor classes, not as a morphism of schemes.
