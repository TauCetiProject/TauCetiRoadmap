## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** No layer of the tower is finished and the Jacobian itself does not exist yet. Layer A is the one with real substance: divisors, degree and `Pic⁰` are built as group theory and have now been connected to actual schemes, while the line-bundle half is only started. Layer E has a usable category of abelian varieties but none of the geometry that makes them abelian varieties. ### Named results

- **The affine Weil-Cartier dictionary** — the invertible fractional ideals of a Dedekind domain are exactly the Weil divisors on its height-one spectrum, principal for principal, and the correspondence restricts to integral ideals against effective divisors ([`fractionalIdealDivisorAddEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/FractionalIdealDivisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.fractionalIdealDivisorAddEquiv), [`integralFractionalIdealDivisorAddEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/FractionalIdealDivisor/Effectivity.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.integralFractionalIdealDivisorAddEquiv)).
- **Divisor class group equals ideal class group** — on the affine Dedekind chart, the divisor class group of the height-one spectrum is the ideal class group of the ring ([`classGroupAddEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Dedekind/ClassGroup.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.classGroupAddEquiv)).
- **`Pic⁰` as a quotient** — degree-zero divisors modulo principal divisors is the degree-zero divisor class group, with every class having a degree-zero representative ([`weightedDegreeZeroQuotientEquivPicZero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/PicZeroQuotient.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.WeilDivisor.OrderSystem.weightedDegreeZeroQuotientEquivPicZero)). This is an abstract group, not a functor and not a scheme.
- **Principal divisors really are divisors** — a nonzero rational function on a Noetherian integral scheme has nonzero order at only finitely many codimension-one points, so orders of vanishing assemble into an order system on the scheme ([`finite_support_orderAt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/Scheme/Principal.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.SchemeWeilDivisor.finite_support_orderAt)). This is what lets the abstract divisor theory be about schemes.
- **Commutativity of an abelian variety** — a proper, geometrically integral group scheme over a field is a commutative group object ([`isCommMonObj`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/Basic.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety.isCommMonObj)); smoothness and geometric connectedness likewise come out of the definition rather than being assumed.

### Roadmap coverage

Layer A is partial and lopsided. The divisor side is well developed: the coefficientwise order and positive/negative parts, effective divisors of fixed degree with their lattice operations and inclusion-exclusion, divisors from finite sets with multiplicities, principal divisors, class groups, and Abel-Jacobi classes with base-point change, all at the level of divisor classes. Invertible sheaves are defined but `Pic X` under tensor product is not yet a group, there are no Cartier divisors on a general scheme, and no Weil-Cartier dictionary beyond the Dedekind chart. Degree exists only as the residue-degree-weighted sum; the Euler-characteristic definition, and the proof that the two agree, wait on Layer B. Layers B, C, D and F are untouched: nothing on coherent cohomology, genus, Riemann-Roch, Serre duality, relative pushforward or base change, the Picard functor, or Abel-Jacobi as a morphism. Layer E has its definitional and categorical core (category, commutative-group hom-sets, finite products, zero object, base-change functor, endomorphism ring, isogenies) but none of its substance: `dim` is defined as topological Krull dimension and nothing computes it, and there is no tangent space, dual abelian variety, theorem of the cube, or polarization. Supporting work sits underneath all of this: residue degrees of scheme morphisms, sections and rational points, and pullbacks of split epimorphisms.

## The frontier

- **Coherent cohomology over `k`.** The single largest gap, and it has not begun. Genus, Euler characteristic, Riemann-Roch and Serre duality all sit behind it, as does the acceptance check `dim (Jac X) = genus X`, and Layer C cannot start without it.
- **`Pic X` and the divisor-to-line-bundle dictionary.** Invertible sheaves and their local trivializations exist; what is missing is the group structure under tensor product and the correspondence with divisors on a scheme. This is the nearest piece of Layer A still open and needs no cohomology.
- **Degree via Euler characteristic.** The residue-degree sum is in place and behaves correctly at rational points. Defining `deg L := χ(L) − χ(𝒪_X)` and proving it agrees requires the cohomology above.
- **`Pic⁰` as a functor.** At present it is an abstract group quotient. Turning it into a functor of points, and then into a representable one, needs the whole of Layer C: flat base change, semicontinuity, relative effective Cartier divisors and symmetric powers.
- **Abel-Jacobi as a morphism of schemes.** Only the divisor-class version exists, with the dependence on the base point understood. The scheme morphism `X ⟶ Jac X`, its universal property and its base-change compatibility all wait on the Jacobian being constructed at all.
