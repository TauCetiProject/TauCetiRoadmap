## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The bicommutant theorem for the weak and strong operator topologies, Kaplansky density and the Gelfand–Naimark theorem are proved, so the core of Layers 0 and 1 is in place, though both still lack named pieces (the separable ℓ² refinement, the strong-star topology, the ultraweak and ultrastrong bicommutant closures). Layers 2, 8 and 10 are only begun, and Layers 3–7, 9 and 11–13 have not started.

### Named results

- **The bicommutant theorem** — a unital star subalgebra of operators on a Hilbert space equals its double commutant exactly when it is [weak operator closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Bicommutant.html#StarSubalgebra.double_commutant_iff_isClosed_image_ofCLM), or equivalently [strong operator closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Bicommutant.html#StarSubalgebra.double_commutant_iff_isClosed_image_toPointwiseConvergenceCLM); the von Neumann density theorem behind it approximates on finitely many vectors at once.
- **The Kaplansky density theorem** — [the strong closure of the unit ball of a unital star subalgebra is the unit ball of its double commutant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Kaplansky.html#StarSubalgebra.closure_unitBall_eq_unitBall_double_centralizer), with [self-adjoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Kaplansky.html#StarSubalgebra.closure_selfAdjoint_unitBall_eq_selfAdjoint_unitBall_double_centralizer) and [positive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Kaplansky.html#StarSubalgebra.closure_nonneg_unitBall_eq_nonneg_unitBall_double_centralizer) forms.
- **The Gelfand–Naimark theorem** — [every C*-algebra, possibly nonunital and nonseparable, has a faithful nondegenerate representation on a Hilbert space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Representation/GNS.html#TauCeti.CStarAlgebra.exists_faithful_nondegenerate_representation), the direct sum of the GNS representations of its positive functionals.
- **Ultraweakly continuous functionals** — [a functional on B(H) is ultraweakly continuous exactly when it is a square-summable vector functional](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UltraweakTopology.html#TauCeti.ContinuousLinearMapUltraweak.continuous_iff_exists_tsum_inner_apply_eq) A ↦ ∑ ⟪ηₙ, A ξₙ⟫, and on infinite-dimensional spaces such functionals [need not be weak operator continuous](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UltraweakTopology.html#TauCeti.exists_not_continuous_pointwiseConvergenceCLM_tsum_inner_apply).
- **Krein–Milman for states** — the state space of a unital C*-algebra is [weak-star compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/StateSpace.html#TauCeti.isCompact_stateSpace) and is [the closed convex hull of its pure states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/StateSpace.html#TauCeti.closure_convexHull_extremePoints_stateSpace).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Ultraweak and ultrastrong topologies.** The [ultraweak](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UltraweakTopology.html#TauCeti.ContinuousLinearMapUltraweak) and [ultrastrong](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/UltrastrongTopology.html#TauCeti.ContinuousLinearMapUltrastrong) topologies are built by ampliation to ℓ²(ℕ, H). They agree with the weak and strong operator topologies on bounded sets, and [give convex sets the same closures as each other](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UltraweakTopology.html#Convex.ultrastrongOfCLM_mem_closure_image_iff_ultraweak). Together with the functional characterisation, this is the entry point to the predual of Layer 4.
- **Matrix amplification.** The [matrix amplification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/MatrixAmplification.html#StarSubalgebra.piLpMatrix) of a star algebra on a finite Hilbert sum has double commutant [given entrywise by the double commutant of the algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/MatrixAmplification.html#StarSubalgebra.mem_centralizer_centralizer_piLpMatrix_iff). This is what drives Kaplansky density, and the same step is needed for its matrix form.
- **The bounded Borel calculus of a representation of `C(K)`.** It extends such a representation to bounded Borel symbols, commutes with the representation's commutant, and yields a projection-valued measure: the core of V2.BorelCalculus.

## The frontier

- **Ultraweak and ultrastrong bicommutant theorem** — show the ultrastrong closure of a unital star subalgebra is its double commutant by applying the density theorem to its ampliation on ℓ²(ℕ, H); the ultraweak closure then follows, since convex sets already have equal closures in the two topologies.
- **Strong-star Kaplansky and the unit-ball criterion** — define the strong-star topology and state Kaplansky density in its strong-star and matrix-amplified forms, then prove that a unital star subalgebra with weak operator compact unit ball is a von Neumann algebra.
- **Separable Gelfand–Naimark on ℓ²** — build a faithful state from a countable dense family and transport its amplified GNS representation to `lp (fun _ : ℕ => ℂ) 2` (`exists_injective_nondegenerate_representation_lp`). Separable GNS spaces and injectivity for faithful functionals are in place.
- **The predual of B(H)** — with ultraweakly continuous functionals characterised, the trace-class predual and normal functionals of Layer 4 are the next layer to open.
- **The rest of the closed-operator calculus** — TT*, the domain identity D(T) = D(|T|) and the polar decomposition of closed densely defined operators, building on T†T.
