## VonNeumannAlgebras: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`3160b49` to `7ca783d`)

The first window of this roadmap delivered von Neumann's bicommutant theorem: [a unital star subalgebra of bounded operators equals its double commutant exactly when it is closed in the weak operator topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Bicommutant.html#StarSubalgebra.double_commutant_iff_isClosed_image_ofCLM) (TauCeti#28517). It comes from the sharper identification of both the [weak](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Bicommutant.html#StarSubalgebra.closure_image_ofCLM) and the [strong](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Bicommutant.html#StarSubalgebra.closure_image_toPointwiseConvergenceCLM) operator closure of such an algebra with its double commutant, over real or complex Hilbert spaces. So every concrete von Neumann algebra is [weakly closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Bicommutant.html#VonNeumannAlgebra.isClosed_image_ofCLM). The closures are taken in Mathlib's separate topology copies, so the norm topology on operators is untouched. The ultraweak and ultrastrong closures that the roadmap also asks for are still missing, because those topologies have no wrappers yet.

Around this, the strong operator topology got the bounded-composition API it needed. By Banach–Steinhaus, [strongly convergent sequences are norm bounded](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/StrongOperatorTopology.html#ContinuousLinearMap.exists_norm_le_of_tendsto_pointwise), and composition preserves strong convergence once the outer operators are uniformly bounded. As an application, [a strong limit of orthogonal projections is again a projection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/StrongOperatorTopology.html#IsStarProjection.of_tendsto_pointwise), for arbitrary nets and with no separability assumption (TauCeti#31222). Kaplansky density is not yet stated.

## VonNeumannAlgebras: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`7ca783d` to `e06d672`)

The state space of a C*-algebra arrived, with its two classical facts: for a unital algebra it is [weak-star compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/StateSpace.html#TauCeti.isCompact_stateSpace) by Banach–Alaoglu, and [Krein–Milman for states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/StateSpace.html#TauCeti.closure_convexHull_extremePoints_stateSpace) makes it the weak-star closed convex hull of the pure states, which therefore [exist](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/StateSpace.html#TauCeti.extremePoints_stateSpace_nonempty) on every nonzero unital algebra (TauCeti#29833). States are the norm-one positive functionals, [equivalently those with φ(1) = 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/StateSpace.html#TauCeti.mem_stateSpace_iff_norm_eq_one_and_map_one) in the unital case. They pull back along unital star homomorphisms, and the β-KMS states of a C*-dynamical system are now known to be states. The Gelfand–Naimark faithful representation, which Layer 0 builds from these states, is not yet there.

Layer 1 gained its missing topologies. The [ultraweak](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UltraweakTopology.html#TauCeti.ContinuousLinearMapUltraweak) and [ultrastrong](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/UltrastrongTopology.html#TauCeti.ContinuousLinearMapUltrastrong) topologies are defined by ampliating operators to ℓ²(ℕ, H), and on norm-bounded sets they [agree with the weak operator topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UltraweakTopology.html#TauCeti.ContinuousLinearMapUltraweak.isInducing_restrict_toWOT) and [the strong one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/UltrastrongTopology.html#TauCeti.ContinuousLinearMapUltrastrong.isInducing_restrict_toPointwiseConvergenceCLM) respectively (TauCeti#33606). [Closed operator balls are weak operator compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/WeakOperatorTopology/Compact.html#ContinuousLinearMapWOT.isCompact_image_ofCLM_closedBall), and so are [the balls of every von Neumann algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Bicommutant.html#VonNeumannAlgebra.isCompact_image_ofCLM_inter_closedBall), one direction of the unit-ball characterisation. The ultraweak and ultrastrong bicommutant theorems and Kaplansky density are still to come.

Von Neumann algebras on a fixed Hilbert space now form a [complete lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Lattice.html#VonNeumannAlgebra.instCompleteLattice), with joins given by generation, on which [taking commutants is an order-reversing involution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Lattice.html#VonNeumannAlgebra.commutantOrderIso). The generated von Neumann algebra is identified with the [weak operator closure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/VonNeumannAlgebra/Bicommutant.html#VonNeumannAlgebra.image_ofCLM_adjoin_eq_closure) of the generated star algebra, and with its strong closure.
