## DiagrammaticCoherence: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`afa08a3` to `7ca783d`)

The functor-level Drinfeld compatibility of Layer 0.3 is now proved. Every strong braided functor between rigid braided categories carries the Drinfeld isomorphism of X to that of F(X) along the bidual comparison, [`map_drinfeldIso_hom_comp_mapDoubleRightDualIso_hom`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.map_drinfeldIso_hom_comp_mapDoubleRightDualIso_hom). That comparison, [`mapDoubleRightDualIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Rigid/Functor.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.mapDoubleRightDualIso), is built as the roadmap specifies, from the dual comparison at the dual followed by the inverse dual of the dual comparison. It comes with naturality, identity and composite laws, and compatibility with monoidal natural isomorphisms (TauCeti#29345). As a consequence, a braided functor preserves the twist determined by a pivotal structure exactly when it preserves the pivotal structure: [`isBalanced_iff_isPivotal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Balanced.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.isBalanced_iff_isPivotal), with the [converse direction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Balanced.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.isPivotal_pivotalOfBalanced_iff) for pivotal structures induced by twists.

To state this, balanced functors were introduced as [`IsBalanced`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Braided/Balanced/Functor.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.IsBalanced). They are closed under identities and composition, and both preservation conditions transport along natural isomorphisms (TauCeti#28498). The equivalence is proved for the preservation conditions themselves. It is not yet packaged as an equivalence of functor groupoids, the form Layer 7 will use. A third pull request added no new declarations. Nothing in this window touches the syntax of the five non-monoidal rows or any geometric diagram category.
