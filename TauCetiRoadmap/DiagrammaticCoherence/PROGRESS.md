## DiagrammaticCoherence: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`afa08a3` to `7ca783d`)

The functor-level Drinfeld compatibility of Layer 0.3 is now proved. Every strong braided functor between rigid braided categories carries the Drinfeld isomorphism of X to that of F(X) along the bidual comparison, [`map_drinfeldIso_hom_comp_mapDoubleRightDualIso_hom`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.map_drinfeldIso_hom_comp_mapDoubleRightDualIso_hom). That comparison, [`mapDoubleRightDualIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Rigid/Functor.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.mapDoubleRightDualIso), is built as the roadmap specifies, from the dual comparison at the dual followed by the inverse dual of the dual comparison. It comes with naturality, identity and composite laws, and compatibility with monoidal natural isomorphisms (TauCeti#29345). As a consequence, a braided functor preserves the twist determined by a pivotal structure exactly when it preserves the pivotal structure: [`isBalanced_iff_isPivotal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Balanced.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.isBalanced_iff_isPivotal), with the [converse direction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Balanced.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.isPivotal_pivotalOfBalanced_iff) for pivotal structures induced by twists.

To state this, balanced functors were introduced as [`IsBalanced`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Braided/Balanced/Functor.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.IsBalanced). They are closed under identities and composition, and both preservation conditions transport along natural isomorphisms (TauCeti#28498). The equivalence is proved for the preservation conditions themselves. It is not yet packaged as an equivalence of functor groupoids, the form Layer 7 will use. A third pull request added no new declarations. Nothing in this window touches the syntax of the five non-monoidal rows or any geometric diagram category.

## DiagrammaticCoherence: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The comparison of twists and pivotal structures in §0.3 now has the groupoid form that Layer 7 will use. For braided rigid categories with compatible pivotal and balanced structures, the category of twist-preserving strong braided functors is equivalent to the category of pivotal-preserving ones, [`braidedPivotalBalancedFunctorEquivOfEq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Groupoid.html#TauCeti.braidedPivotalBalancedFunctorEquivOfEq). The equivalence also holds on the groupoids of natural isomorphisms, [`braidedPivotalBalancedFunctorCoreEquivOfEq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Groupoid.html#TauCeti.braidedPivotalBalancedFunctorCoreEquivOfEq), and it commutes with forgetting to braided functors and with pre- and postcomposition (TauCeti#41027). Behind it are bundled categories of braided, balanced and pivotal-preserving braided functors, and a torsor calculation: modifying a pivotal structure by a monoidal automorphism u of the identity precomposes the induced twist with u, [`balancedOfBraidedPivotal_smul_twist`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Torsor.html#TauCeti.balancedOfBraidedPivotal_smul_twist). So the identity functor preserves either structure across such a modification only when u = 1, which is the abstract form of the README's order-three check. Between symmetric rigid categories with their canonical pivotal structures, every strong braided functor is pivotal, [`symmetricEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor/Braided.html#TauCeti.BraidedPivotalFunctor.symmetricEquiv).

The rigid row also gained its first object syntax. Formal rigid objects, [`RigidObject`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Diagram/RigidObject/Basic.html#TauCeti.RigidObject), normalize to [winding words](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Diagram/RigidObject/Basic.html#TauCeti.WindingWord), ordered letters each carrying an integer dual level. Right and left duals reverse the word and shift levels by ±1, with no reduction mod 2, and [every word is reached](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Diagram/RigidObject/Basic.html#TauCeti.RigidObject.normalize_surjective). Substitution of words for generators is associative, and [normalization commutes with it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Diagram/RigidObject/Substitution.html#TauCeti.RigidObject.normalize_subst). This is the combinatorics only. The categorical comparison isomorphisms from formal expressions to normal forms, rigid morphism syntax and congruences are not yet there, and nothing geometric has started.
