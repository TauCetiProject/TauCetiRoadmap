## FactorRigidity: 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-09 (`10302fd` to `7ca783d`)

Property (T) has its first footing in the library. A subgroup Λ of a discrete group now has [relative property (T)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/PropertyT.html#Subgroup.RelativePropertyT) when every unitary representation with [almost invariant vectors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/AlmostInvariant.html#ContRepresentation.HasAlmostInvariantVectors) has a nonzero Λ-invariant vector, and the [Kazhdan pair characterization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/PropertyT/KazhdanPair.html#Subgroup.relativePropertyT_iff_exists_isKazhdanPair) is proved: this holds exactly when a single finite set F and a δ > 0 suffice. The converse direction builds one bad representation from many by taking their [Hilbert direct sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/Unitary/HilbertSum.html#ContRepresentation.hilbertSum), whose invariant vectors are computed coordinatewise; because of that sum the equivalence is stated for representations in a universe large enough to hold both the group and the summands.

The remaining work covers the roadmap's sanity checks and basic permanence. The trivial subgroup and every finite subgroup have relative property (T), so [finite groups have property (T)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/PropertyT.html#Subgroup.relativePropertyT_top_of_finite), by an averaging argument that also gives (Γ, 1) as an explicit Kazhdan pair. The property passes to smaller subgroups and to images under homomorphisms. The quantitative and positive-definite forms of the characterization, finite-index transfer, and the action of SL₂(ℤ) on ℤ² are not yet here, so the pair (ℤ²⋊SL₂(ℤ), ℤ²) remains out of reach.

## FactorRigidity: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

Both of the roadmap's group-theoretic end theorems have landed. The [Kazhdan–Margulis theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/PropertyT/SpecialLinear.html#TauCeti.relativePropertyT_inl_slTwo) says the pair (ℤ²⋊SL₂(ℤ), ℤ²) has relative property (T). It comes as a case of [Burger's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/PropertyT/SpecialLinear.html#TauCeti.relativePropertyT_inl_of_not_amenable), which gives the same for every nonamenable G ≤ SL₂(ℤ), and the converse also holds, so relative (T) of (ℤ²⋊G, ℤ²) is [equivalent to nonamenability of G](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/PropertyT/SpecialLinear.html#TauCeti.relativePropertyT_inl_iff_not_amenable). The proof follows the roadmap's route. It uses the [spectral measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/Unitary/SpectralMeasure.html#ContRepresentation.spectralPVM) of a unitary representation of a discrete abelian group, with its uniqueness and its equivariance in semidirect products, and [Furstenberg's concentration lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/OnePoint/InvariantMeasure.html#OnePoint.exists_measure_compl_pair_eq_zero_of_tendsto_psl_cocompact) on the projective line. It also uses amenability of the point stabilizers, which needed amenability of affine groups and of semidirect products (TauCeti#40466).

[Haagerup's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/HaagerupProperty/FreeGroup.html#TauCeti.hasHaagerupProperty_freeGroup) is the other end theorem: the free group on any countable set of generators has the [Haagerup property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/HaagerupProperty/Basic.html#TauCeti.HasHaagerupProperty). It is proved through [Haagerup's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/FreeGroup/GeodesicEdges.html#FreeGroup.posSemidef_pow_norm_inv_mul) that r^|g| is positive definite for |r| ≤ 1, using geodesic edge sets in the Cayley tree, and works with weighted generators. Around it, Layer 4.1 gained [Schoenberg's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/Kernel/Schoenberg.html#Matrix.condNegSemidef_iff_forall_posSemidef_cexp_neg_mul) for conditionally negative semidefinite kernels and the [GNS characterization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/Unitary/PositiveDefinite.html#TauCeti.posSemidef_inv_mul_iff_exists_isUnitary) of positive-definite functions as unitary coefficients. It also gained [amenable groups have the Haagerup property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/HaagerupProperty/Amenable.html#TauCeti.hasHaagerupProperty_of_amenable), and the incompatibility of the two properties: in a Haagerup group every [relatively rigid subgroup is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/HaagerupProperty/PropertyT.html#TauCeti.HasHaagerupProperty.finite_of_relativePropertyT). That makes ℤ²⋊SL₂(ℤ) [non-Haagerup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/PropertyT/SpecialLinear.html#TauCeti.not_hasHaagerupProperty_semidirectProduct_slTwo).

Smaller pieces include the column-vector [`slTwoAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/SpecialLinearGroup/Action.html#TauCeti.slTwoAction) and the fact that [infinite groups with Følner sets lack property (T)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/PropertyT/Foelner.html#Subgroup.not_relativePropertyT_top_of_hasFoelnerSets). There is also induction of positive-definite functions from finite-index subgroups, but it is not yet assembled into finite-index permanence of the Haagerup property.
