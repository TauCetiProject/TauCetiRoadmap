Most of the Banach–Tate operator theory and the bounded-measure theory on `ℤ_p` (Layer 3) are also proved. ### Named results

- **Serre's orthonormalisation theorem** — over a complete ultrametric ring with a multiplicative pseudo-uniformiser `ϖ`, take a complete module whose norms lie in `‖ϖ‖^ℤ ∪ {0}`. It is orthonormalisable exactly when its residue module is free, and lifts of a residue basis are an orthonormal basis ([`isONable_iff_free_residueModule`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ResidueBasis.html#TauCeti.isONable_iff_free_residueModule), [`ResidueModule.isOrthonormalBasis_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ResidueBasis.html#TauCeti.ResidueModule.isOrthonormalBasis_iff)).
- **Orthogonal bases for spaces of countable type** — over any complete nontrivially normed ultrametric field, no discrete valuation needed, a space with a densely spanning sequence has a `t`-orthogonal basis for every `t < 1` ([`exists_isTOrthogonalBasis_of_dense_span`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/CountableType.html#TauCeti.exists_isTOrthogonalBasis_of_dense_span)). Hence it is potentially orthonormalisable ([`isPotentiallyONable_of_dense_span`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ModelSpace.html#TauCeti.isPotentiallyONable_of_dense_span)) and, if infinite-dimensional, isomorphic to `C₀(ℕ, K)`.
- **The open mapping theorem over Tate rings** — a continuous surjection of Banach modules over a Tate ring has lifts with `‖x‖ ≤ C‖y‖` ([`exists_preimage_norm_le_of_isTate`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RingOpNorm/OpenMapping.html#ContinuousLinearMap.exists_preimage_norm_le_of_isTate)). Bounded inverse, closed graph, strictness of closed-range maps and [Banach–Steinhaus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RingOpNorm/UniformBoundedness.html#TauCeti.NormedRing.IsTate.banach_steinhaus) come with it.
- **Mahler duality for measures** — the operator norm of a measure on `ℤ_p` is the supremum of its Amice coefficients ([`norm_toCLMEquiv_eq_iSup_coeff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.norm_toCLMEquiv_eq_iSup_coeff)), and a power series is an Amice transform exactly when its coefficients are bounded ([`mem_range_amiceTransform_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.mem_range_amiceTransform_iff)).
- **The integral dual unit ball** — a `ℚ_p`-valued measure has norm at most one exactly when it is the unique extension of a `ℤ_p`-valued measure ([`existsUnique_integral_iff_norm_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/IntegralUnitBall.html#AbstractMeasure.existsUnique_integral_iff_norm_le_one)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Multiplicative pseudo-uniformisers** ([`NormedRing.PseudoUniformizer`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Ring/PseudoUniformizer.html#TauCeti.NormedRing.PseudoUniformizer)) — these supply the exact shell scaling behind every quantitative estimate. They are bridged to topological Tate rings, and the converse runs through the [Tate gauge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TateGauge.html#TauCeti.Huber.tateGauge).
- **The model space `C₀(I, R)` and its matrices** — bounded families in a module correspond isometrically to maps out of `C₀(I, R)` ([`linearCombinationEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/ZeroAtInfty/LinearCombination.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.linearCombinationEquiv)). Operators have null-column matrices with the [sup-entry norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/ZeroAtInfty/Matrix.html#ContinuousLinearMap.ringOpNorm_eq_iSup_norm_matrixCoeff), and triangular perturbations of the identity are [isometric automorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/ZeroAtInfty/Triangular.html#Matrix.toZeroAtInftyLinearIsometryEquiv), which Amice's basis theorem needs.
- **Analytic stages** ([`AnalyticStage`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AnalyticStage.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage)) — the coefficient model `LA_h(E) = C₀(ZMod(p^h) × ℕ, E)`. It comes with norm-decreasing evaluation and a [transition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AnalyticStage.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage.transition) to level `h+1` that is compatible with evaluation.
