### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Normed models of Tate gauges.** A nonzero Hausdorff Tate ring with chosen gauge data gets a compatible ultrametric norm in which the chosen unit is a multiplicative pseudo-uniformiser ([`TauCeti.Huber.tateNormedCommRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TateGauge/Normed.html#TauCeti.Huber.tateNormedCommRing)), so the quantitative theory applies to topological Tate rings.
- **Locally analytic distributions.** Functionals on the algebraic union of the stages that are bounded on each stage, equivalently compatible families of stage functionals ([`TauCeti.LocallyAnalyticDistribution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/Dual.html#TauCeti.LocallyAnalyticDistribution)). This carrier holds the Amice correspondence; it has no global topology, by design.
- **Weights of ℤ_pˣ in coordinates.** The decomposition ℤ_pˣ ≅ (ℤ/qℤ)ˣ × ℤ_p at all primes ([`TauCeti.padicIntUnitsEquivZModUnitsProd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/UnitsDecomposition/Logarithm.html#TauCeti.padicIntUnitsEquivZModUnitsProd)) parametrises weights by a finite character and T_κ = κ(γ)−1, in normed and adic settings ([`TauCeti.weightSpaceEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/WeightSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.weightSpaceEquiv)). Analytic levels, conductor formulas and measure functions are built on it.
