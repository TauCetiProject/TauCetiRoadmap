## PadicFunctionalAnalysis: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-26 (`36093c0` to `938acbb`)

[Serre’s orthonormalisation theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ResidueBasis.html#TauCeti.ResidueModule.isOrthonormalBasis_iff) now identifies orthonormal bases with bases of the residue module, when both norms take values in powers of the chosen multiplicative pseudo-uniformiser. Over an arbitrary complete nontrivially normed ultrametric field, an infinite-dimensional Banach space of countable type also has a [continuous linear identification with c₀(ℕ)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ModelSpace.html#TauCeti.nonempty_continuousLinearEquiv_zeroAtInfty_nat_of_dense_span); continuous splitting of closed subspaces is not established here.

[Mahler duality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.mahlerMomentsEquiv) identifies bounded measures on ℤ_p with bounded moment sequences over Tate coefficients. For complete ultrametric field coefficients with contractive ℤ_p action, the [operator norm is the supremum of the moment norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.norm_toCLMEquiv_eq_iSup_coeff). The [integral dual unit ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/IntegralUnitBall.html#AbstractMeasure.existsUnique_integral_iff_norm_le_one) consists exactly of rational-valued measures extending integral-valued ones, uniquely. Continuous functions also acquire [van der Put coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/VanDerPut.html#PadicInt.vanDerPutEquiv), isometrically, for complete ultrametric group coefficients.

The analytic work supplies [contractive re-expansion between stages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AnalyticStage.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage.transition) and the factorial-weight estimates [S_h ≤ D_h](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AmiceNorm.html#TauCeti.gaussNorm_amiceRadius_le_amiceDualNorm) and [D_h ≤ p S_(h+1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AmiceNorm.html#TauCeti.amiceDualNorm_le_mul_gaussNorm_amiceRadius_succ), with boundedness hypotheses on the suprema. These are coefficient estimates: Amice’s analytic basis theorem and the distribution bijection are not established by the supplied declarations. Some declaration context was omitted, so this account is selective.

## PadicFunctionalAnalysis: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-13 (`938acbb` to `aa6e43e`)

Amice's basis theorem is now proved: at every level h, the polynomials ⌊n/pʰ⌋!·binom(x,n) give an [isometry from null sequences onto the analytic stage LA_h](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/Amice/Isometry.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage.amiceEquiv) and an [orthonormal basis of LA_h(ℤ_p)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/Amice/Isometry.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage.isOrthonormalBasis_amiceTerm). Because evaluation is diagonal in Amice and Mahler coordinates, a continuous function is [analytic on level-h discs exactly when ‖a_n‖/‖⌊n/pʰ⌋!‖ → 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/Amice/Mahler.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage.mem_range_eval_iff_tendsto_norm_div). For characters this becomes the [radius criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/Amice/Character.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage.exists_eval_eq_addChar_iff): x ↦ (1+t)^x lies in LA_h exactly when ‖t‖ < p^(−1/((p−1)pʰ)).

On top of this sit [locally analytic distributions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/Dual.html#TauCeti.LocallyAnalyticDistribution), functionals bounded on every stage, and [Amice's theorem for distributions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/Amice/Transform.html#TauCeti.LocallyAnalyticDistribution.mem_range_amiceTransform_iff): a power series is the Amice transform of a distribution exactly when it converges on the open unit disc, with the [stage seminorm equal to sup ‖b_n‖‖⌊n/pʰ⌋!‖](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/Amice/Transform.html#TauCeti.LocallyAnalyticDistribution.stageSeminorm_eq_amiceDualNorm). Additive convolution of distributions has not landed. On the Banach-space side, [strict short exact sequences out of a space of countable type split](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Splitting.html#TauCeti.exists_continuousLinearEquiv_prod_of_countableType) and [stay strictly exact after completed tensoring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/CompletedExtension.html#TauCeti.strictExact_completedTensor_of_countableType). Serre's [potential orthonormalisation theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/DiscreteNorm.html#TauCeti.isPotentiallyONable_of_discrete_norm) and Buzzard's [finite-coordinate bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/ZeroAtInfty/FiniteCoordinates.html#Submodule.exists_finset_norm_le_of_isNoetherianRing) for finite submodules of c₀ over Noetherian Tate rings arrived alongside rounded and adapted norms, normed models of Tate gauges, and Banach–Steinhaus over Tate rings.

Layer 5 has begun. The [decomposition ℤ_pˣ ≅ (ℤ/qℤ)ˣ × ℤ_p](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/UnitsDecomposition/Logarithm.html#TauCeti.padicIntUnitsEquivZModUnitsProd) holds at all primes, weights are [parametrised by a finite character and the coordinate T_κ = κ(γ)−1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/WeightSpace/Basic.html#TauCeti.weightSpaceEquiv) in normed and adic settings, and their analytic levels and conductor formulas are computed. The [annular ring H](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/AnnularRing/Basic.html#TauCeti.AnnularRing) exists as a ring with TU = p; its gauge norm, annulus evaluations, and the comparison of the completed group algebra with ℤ_p[Δ]⟦T⟧ are still to come.
