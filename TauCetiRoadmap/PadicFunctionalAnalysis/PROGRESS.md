## PadicFunctionalAnalysis: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-26 (`36093c0` to `938acbb`)

The headline is Serre's orthonormalisation theorem. [`isONable_iff_free_residueModule`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ResidueBasis.html#TauCeti.isONable_iff_free_residueModule) says that over a complete ultrametric ring with a multiplicative pseudo-uniformiser `ϖ`, a Banach module whose norms lie in `‖ϖ‖^ℤ ∪ {0}` is orthonormalisable exactly when its residue module is free (TauCeti#19918). Around it sits the countable-type theory over an arbitrary complete nontrivially normed field. For every `t < 1` there are [`t`-orthogonal bases](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/CountableType.html#TauCeti.exists_isTOrthogonalBasis_of_dense_span), every countable-type Banach space is [potentially orthonormalisable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ModelSpace.html#TauCeti.isPotentiallyONable_of_dense_span), and subspaces stay of countable type. The splitting theorem for countable-type spaces has not landed yet.

On the measure side, [Mahler duality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.norm_toCLMEquiv_eq_iSup_coeff) identifies the norm of a measure on `ℤ_p` with the supremum of its Amice coefficients, and [the range of the Amice transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.mem_range_amiceTransform_iff) is exactly the series with bounded coefficients. Also in are the [integral dual unit ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/IntegralUnitBall.html#AbstractMeasure.existsUnique_integral_iff_norm_le_one), van der Put's theorem as an [isometry](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/VanDerPut.html#PadicInt.vanDerPutEquiv), and the operators `φ, ψ` together with the [identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/PhiPsi.html#AbstractMeasure.amiceTransform_phiMeasure) `A_{φμ}(T) = A_μ((1+T)^p − 1)`. Work on distributions has begun. There are [analytic stages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AnalyticStage.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage) with their re-expansion maps, and the Amice norm comparison [`S_h ≤ D_h`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AmiceNorm.html#TauCeti.gaussNorm_amiceRadius_le_amiceDualNorm) `≤ p S_{h+1}` is proved for power series. Amice's basis theorem and the distribution carrier itself are not there yet.

Underneath this is a Banach–Tate foundation. It has multiplicative pseudo-uniformisers bridged to topological Tate rings, the [Tate gauge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TateGauge.html#TauCeti.Huber.tateGauge), and a ring-scalar operator norm. On that norm sit the [quantitative open mapping theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RingOpNorm/OpenMapping.html#ContinuousLinearMap.exists_preimage_norm_le_of_isTate), closed graph and [Banach–Steinhaus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RingOpNorm/UniformBoundedness.html#TauCeti.NormedRing.IsTate.banach_steinhaus) over Tate rings. A working API for `C₀(I, R)` covers its universal property, matrices, the `ℓ∞` dual and triangular perturbations. Nothing has started on characters and weights (Layer 5).
