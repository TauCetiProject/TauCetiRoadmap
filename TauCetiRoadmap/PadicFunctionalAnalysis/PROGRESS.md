## PadicFunctionalAnalysis: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-26 (`36093c0` to `938acbb`)

[Serre’s orthonormalisation theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ResidueBasis.html#TauCeti.ResidueModule.isOrthonormalBasis_iff) now identifies orthonormal bases with bases of the residue module, when both norms take values in powers of the chosen multiplicative pseudo-uniformiser. Over an arbitrary complete nontrivially normed ultrametric field, an infinite-dimensional Banach space of countable type also has a [continuous linear identification with c₀(ℕ)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ModelSpace.html#TauCeti.nonempty_continuousLinearEquiv_zeroAtInfty_nat_of_dense_span); continuous splitting of closed subspaces is not established here.

[Mahler duality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.mahlerMomentsEquiv) identifies bounded measures on ℤ_p with bounded moment sequences over Tate coefficients. For complete ultrametric field coefficients with contractive ℤ_p action, the [operator norm is the supremum of the moment norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.norm_toCLMEquiv_eq_iSup_coeff). The [integral dual unit ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/IntegralUnitBall.html#AbstractMeasure.existsUnique_integral_iff_norm_le_one) consists exactly of rational-valued measures extending integral-valued ones, uniquely. Continuous functions also acquire [van der Put coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/VanDerPut.html#PadicInt.vanDerPutEquiv), isometrically, for complete ultrametric group coefficients.

The analytic work supplies [contractive re-expansion between stages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AnalyticStage.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage.transition) and the factorial-weight estimates [S_h ≤ D_h](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AmiceNorm.html#TauCeti.gaussNorm_amiceRadius_le_amiceDualNorm) and [D_h ≤ p S_(h+1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AmiceNorm.html#TauCeti.amiceDualNorm_le_mul_gaussNorm_amiceRadius_succ), with boundedness hypotheses on the suprema. These are coefficient estimates: Amice’s analytic basis theorem and the distribution bijection are not established by the supplied declarations. Some declaration context was omitted, so this account is selective.
