## PadicFunctionalAnalysis: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-23 (`36093c0` to `bfcff79`)

The operator theory over Banach–Tate rings arrived in one piece. The quantitative open mapping theorem ([`exists_preimage_norm_le_of_isTate`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RingOpNorm/OpenMapping.html#ContinuousLinearMap.exists_preimage_norm_le_of_isTate)) says a continuous surjection of complete ultrametric modules over a Tate ring lifts every vector with norm at most a fixed constant times its own. The bounded inverse and closed graph theorems, strictness of closed-range maps and [Banach–Steinhaus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/RingOpNorm/UniformBoundedness.html#TauCeti.NormedRing.IsTate.banach_steinhaus) came with it (TauCeti#18165). On the `p`-adic side, van der Put's theorem landed as an isometry from `C(ℤ_p, E)` to null sequences ([`vanDerPutEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/VanDerPut.html#PadicInt.vanDerPutEquiv)). Mahler duality now computes the norm of a measure on `ℤ_p` as the supremum of its Amice coefficients ([`norm_toCLMEquiv_eq_iSup_coeff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.norm_toCLMEquiv_eq_iSup_coeff)), and a rational measure lies in the dual unit ball exactly when it comes from a unique integral one ([`existsUnique_integral_iff_norm_le_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/IntegralUnitBall.html#AbstractMeasure.existsUnique_integral_iff_norm_le_one)).

The model space `C₀(I, R)` gained the API the later layers will need: coordinate vectors, its universal property for bounded families, matrices with the sup-entry norm, transposes on `ℓ∞` and triangular isometric automorphisms. On that base, orthonormal bases were shown to be the same thing as isometries to `C₀(I, R)` ([`exists_isOrthonormalBasis_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Orthonormal.html#TauCeti.exists_isOrthonormalBasis_iff)), and property (Pr) was shown to mean that surjections onto the module split. An approximate Gram–Schmidt process gives `t`-orthogonal bases of spaces of countable type over any complete nontrivially normed field. Underneath all this sit multiplicative pseudo-uniformisers, the operator norm over ring scalars with Neumann inversion, the Tate gauge and rounding of norms to `c^ℤ`. The measure operators `φ` and `ψ` arrived with `A_(φμ)(T) = A_μ((1+T)^p − 1)`.

Serre's orthonormalisation theorem, the countable-type splitting theorem and the finite Banach module theory have not landed yet. Analytic stages, Amice's basis theorem and the character layer (Layers 4 and 5) have not been started.
