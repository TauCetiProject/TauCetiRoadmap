## PadicFunctionalAnalysis: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-26 (`36093c0` to `b257e0f`)

[Serre’s orthonormalisation theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ResidueBasis.html#TauCeti.ResidueModule.isOrthonormalBasis_iff) identifies orthonormal bases with lifts of residue-module bases when scalar and module norms lie in the powers of a multiplicative pseudo-uniformiser. Over arbitrary complete nontrivially normed ultrametric fields, Banach spaces of countable type are now [potentially orthonormalisable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/ModelSpace.html#TauCeti.isPotentiallyONable_of_dense_span); continuous splitting of their closed-subspace sequences is not established here.

[Mahler duality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.mahlerMomentsEquiv) identifies measures on ℤ_p with bounded moment sequences over Banach–Tate coefficient rings; for field coefficients, the operator norm is the [supremum of the moment norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/MahlerDuality.html#AbstractMeasure.norm_toCLMEquiv_eq_iSup_coeff). The [integral dual unit ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/IntegralUnitBall.html#AbstractMeasure.existsUnique_integral_iff_norm_le_one) consists exactly of the extensions of ℤ_p-valued measures to ℚ_p. Separately, [Van der Put’s theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/VanDerPut.html#PadicInt.vanDerPutEquiv) gives isometric null-sequence coordinates for continuous functions with values in any complete ultrametric normed group.

For power-series coefficients, the estimates [S_h ≤ D_h](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AmiceNorm.html#TauCeti.gaussNorm_amiceRadius_le_amiceDualNorm) and [D_h ≤ p S_(h+1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AmiceNorm.html#TauCeti.amiceDualNorm_le_mul_gaussNorm_amiceRadius_succ) compare factorial-weighted moment bounds with disc norms. Boundedness at every analytic level is equivalent to [convergence on every closed radius below one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AmiceNorm.html#TauCeti.onOpenDisc_iff_forall_bddAbove_norm_coeff_mul_norm_factorial). The [analytic stages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Distribution/AnalyticStage.html#TauCeti.AnalyticStage) also have contractive evaluation and re-expansion maps. These are ingredients for the unbounded Amice transform; its distribution bijection and the analytic basis theorem are not established here.
