## PurityOfBranchLocus: 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-08 (`d01fc7f` to `d2083f0`)

The local algebra under purity of the branch locus is now in place. The headline is [Serre's characterization of regular local rings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/ProjectiveDimension.html#TauCeti.isRegularLocalRing_tfae): a Noetherian local ring is regular exactly when its residue field has finite projective dimension, exactly when it has finite global dimension, and then the [global dimension equals the Krull dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/ProjectiveDimension.html#TauCeti.IsRegularLocalRing.globalDimension_eq_ringKrullDim). Getting there took the [Auslander–Buchsbaum formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Depth/AuslanderBuchsbaum.html#ModuleCat.projectiveDimension_add_depth_eq_depth), the fact that a [maximal Cohen–Macaulay module over a regular local ring is free](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Free.html#TauCeti.IsRegularLocalRing.free_of_isCohenMacaulay), minimal finite free resolutions, and a comparison of the Koszul complex with a minimal resolution that proves the residue field's projective dimension is [at least the embedding dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LocalRing/ProjectiveDimension.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRing.finrank_cotangentSpace_le_projectiveDimension_quotient_maximalIdeal) (TauCeti#24780).

On the geometric side, morphisms of schemes now have a [ramification locus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationLocus), a non-étale locus and a [branch locus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.branchLocus), with closedness and comparisons to the affine loci. Being étale or unramified at a point is now described by the stalk map, and [elementary étale localization of a quasi-finite point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/ElementaryEtale/Basic.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.exists_elementaryEtale_finite_of_isolated) (Stacks 02LK) has landed. Over a normal base, [the ramification and non-étale loci agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationLocus_eq_nonEtaleLocus), by the SGA 1 I 9.5 route instead of through purity.

Purity itself is only proved in low dimension so far: [in dimension one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/StalkMap.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.exists_etale_of_formallyUnramified_stalkMap_of_isDedekindDomain), and for [finite algebras over a regular local ring of dimension one or two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Purity.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_of_isUnramifiedAt_height_eq_one_of_ringKrullDim_le_two). In higher dimension there is only a [conditional form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/FiniteNormal.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_of_finiteNormal_of_purityHolds), which assumes that finite étale covers of the punctured spectrum extend. The general Zariski–Nagata theorem (Stacks 0BMB) and its codimension-one corollaries are still open.
