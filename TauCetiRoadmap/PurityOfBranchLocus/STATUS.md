## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0 and 1 are done: the stalk-form vocabulary, normality under étale maps, Serre's characterization of regular local rings with its consequence that their localizations are regular, the three loci, and elementary étale localization. Layer 2 is partial. Purity is proved over regular bases of dimension at most two, but the general Zariski–Nagata theorem and the results that rest on it have not landed.

### Named results

- **Serre's characterization of regular local rings** — a Noetherian local ring is regular iff its residue field has finite projective dimension, iff it has finite global dimension ([`TauCeti.isRegularLocalRing_tfae`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/ProjectiveDimension.html#TauCeti.isRegularLocalRing_tfae)). It follows that the global dimension is then the Krull dimension ([`globalDimension_eq_ringKrullDim`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/ProjectiveDimension.html#TauCeti.IsRegularLocalRing.globalDimension_eq_ringKrullDim)), and that every localization of a regular local ring is regular (Stacks 0AFS, as the instance `TauCeti.isRegularRing_of_isRegularLocalRing`).
- **The Auslander–Buchsbaum formula** — for a nonzero finite module of finite projective dimension over a Noetherian local ring, projective dimension plus depth equals the depth of the ring ([`ModuleCat.projectiveDimension_add_depth_eq_depth`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Depth/AuslanderBuchsbaum.html#ModuleCat.projectiveDimension_add_depth_eq_depth)).
- **Purity over a regular local ring of dimension one or two** — a finite torsion-free algebra with normal local rings, unramified at its height-one primes, is étale ([`TauCeti.Algebra.etale_of_isUnramifiedAt_height_eq_one_of_ringKrullDim_le_two`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Purity.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_of_isUnramifiedAt_height_eq_one_of_ringKrullDim_le_two)). This is the finite algebraic case of 0BMB in low dimension.
- **Elementary étale localization of a quasi-finite point** — at a quasi-finite point, an elementary étale neighbourhood of the image makes the morphism finite near a unique point with the same residue field (Stacks 02LK, [`Scheme.Hom.exists_elementaryEtale_finite_of_isolated`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/ElementaryEtale/Basic.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.exists_elementaryEtale_finite_of_isolated)). The persistence clause is a separate base-change lemma.
- **Unramified points are étale over a normal base** — for a dominant, locally finite type morphism of integral schemes onto a normal locally Noetherian scheme, the ramification locus equals the non-étale locus (SGA 1 I 9.5, [`Scheme.Hom.ramificationLocus_eq_nonEtaleLocus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationLocus_eq_nonEtaleLocus)). It is proved without purity.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The ramification, non-étale and branch loci** ([`Scheme.Hom.ramificationLocus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.ramificationLocus), [`Scheme.Hom.branchLocus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/RamificationLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.branchLocus)) — closed under the expected hypotheses and matched with Mathlib's affine loci, so the codimension-one statements of §2.3 have something to be about. The README's worked example, the squaring map of the plane, is computed.
- **Global dimension** ([`TauCeti.globalDimension`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/GlobalDimension.html#TauCeti.globalDimension)) — detected on finite and cyclic modules, not increased by localization. This is the bridge that turns Serre's criterion into regularity of localizations.
- **Minimal finite free resolutions** ([`ModuleCat.exists_projectiveResolution_finite_free_minimal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Projective/MinimalResolution/ChainComplex.html#ModuleCat.exists_projectiveResolution_finite_free_minimal)) — the finite-free realization and minimization interfaces. Together with the Koszul comparison they give the residue-field bound of Stacks 00OA.

## The frontier

- **0BMB in general dimension** — the scheme form `exists_etale_of_formallyUnramified_stalk_codimOne`. The reduction ingredients are in place: elementary étale localization, invariance of the codimension-one hypothesis under étale base change, and going down with dimension equality. The blocker is purity for regular local rings in dimension at least three (LocalPurity §2.1). Here it exists only conditionally on `IsLocalRing.PurityHolds` ([`etale_of_finiteNormal_of_purityHolds`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/FiniteNormal.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_of_finiteNormal_of_purityHolds)).
- **Purity of the ramification and branch loci** — `ringKrullDim_stalk_eq_one_of_maximal_ramificationLocus` and its branch-locus counterpart. The lifting step from a maximal branch point to a maximal ramified point is proved ([`exists_maximal_ramificationLocus_over_of_maximal_branchLocus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/BranchLocus.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.exists_maximal_ramificationLocus_over_of_maximal_branchLocus)). What remains needs 0BMB at the maximal points.
- **The global corollary** `etale_of_etaleAt_codimOne` — a finite dominant generically étale morphism from a normal to a regular scheme, étale in codimension one, is étale. This follows once 0BMB lands.
- **The Stacks and affine forms of 0BMB** — `etaleAt_of_unramifiedAt_codimOne` and `Algebra.isEtaleAt_of_purity`. Both should follow formally from the scheme form through the §0.1 comparisons.
