## RicciFlow: 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-13 (`96cef6f` to `aa6e43e`)

The first work on this roadmap supplies three of its starting pieces. The headline is [Nagumo's criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/ConvexInvariance.html#Convex.forall_mem_posTangentConeAt_iff_forall_mapsTo_Icc): for a locally Lipschitz autonomous field on a real Hilbert space, a closed convex set is invariant under the flow exactly when the field points into the set's positive tangent cone at each of its points (TauCeti#38492). This is the invariance test the roadmap will later apply to curvature cones under the reaction ODE. It currently holds for an abstract field; it has not yet been applied to the reaction ODE `dR/dt = Q(R)`, because that ODE isn't defined yet.

The metric variation formulas for families of nondegenerate metrics also landed. These include the [variation of the inverse metric](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/NondegenerateMetric/Variation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.ContMDiffNondegenerateMetric.hasDerivAt_inverse), the [first variation of the Levi-Civita connection](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/LeviCivita/Nondegenerate/Variation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.ContMDiffNondegenerateMetricFamily.two_mul_apply_deriv_difference_eq_derivBilinearTensor) as a tensor built from `∇h`, and the [first variation of curvature](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/LeviCivita/Nondegenerate/CurvatureVariation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.ContMDiffNondegenerateMetricFamily.hasDerivAt_curvatureOperator_of_isLeviCivitaConnection), with Jacobi's formula for determinants underneath. From these follows the [volume density along Ricci flow](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Variation.html#TauCeti.hasDerivWithinAt_chartVolumeDensity_of_ricciFlow), `∂_t dμ = −Scal dμ`, which is stated for the chart density at a point rather than as an intrinsic density. The Ricci and scalar variations have not landed.

On the algebraic side, [algebraic curvature tensors](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/AlgebraicCurvature/Basic.html#TauCeti.FourTensor.algebraicCurvatureSubmodule) now exist as a closed submodule of four-tensors that is stable under pullback. Alongside them come the Kulkarni–Nomizu product, the constant-curvature tensor, and the Ricci and scalar contractions, with the expected trace identities such as [`Ric(h ⊙ g) = (n − 2)h + (tr h)g`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/AlgebraicCurvature/Basic.html#TauCeti.FourTensor.ricci_kulkarniNomizu_innerₗ).
