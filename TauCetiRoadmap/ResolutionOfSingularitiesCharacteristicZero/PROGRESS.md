## ResolutionOfSingularitiesCharacteristicZero: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`8e5b2bc` to `58174b5`)

The headline is the roadmap's Hilbert–Samuel stabilization target, proved in full: in finitely many variables over a field, any sequence of ideals of the formal power series ring whose Hilbert–Samuel functions are lexicographically nonincreasing has eventually constant Hilbert–Samuel function ([`exists_forall_ge_localQuotientHilbertSamuel_eq_of_antitone`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/HilbertSamuel.html#MvPowerSeries.exists_forall_ge_localQuotientHilbertSamuel_eq_of_antitone), https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/3540). The pointwise version follows as a [corollary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/HilbertSamuel.html#MvPowerSeries.exists_forall_ge_localQuotientHilbertSamuel_eq_of_forall_le). The proof has two steps. First, using the formal division theorem already in the library, the Hilbert–Samuel function [counts the exponents of degree at most ℓ outside the graded-lexicographic initial diagram](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPowerSeries/HilbertSamuel.html#Ideal.localQuotientHilbertSamuel_eq_ncard_diff_initialDiagram). Second, such counting functions of upper sets of exponents [form a well-founded set in the lexicographic order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/Finsupp/UpperSet.html#TauCeti.isWF_range_toLex_ncard_sdiff).

The [Hilbert–Samuel function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LocalRing/HilbertSamuel.html#Ideal.localQuotientHilbertSamuel) itself also arrived, measured by local-ring length. It comes with finiteness over a noetherian local ring, monotonicity, and the value `(ℓ + n).choose n` for the full power series ring. Its invariance under completion is not yet proved.

On the Layer 1 side, [marked ideals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MarkedIdeal.html#TauCeti.MarkedIdeal) arrived in their affine form, each an ideal with a positive mark whose cosupport is the set of primes where the order reaches the mark. Over a regular ring, rescaling leaves the cosupport unchanged, and the cosupport of a [marked sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MarkedIdeal.html#TauCeti.MarkedIdeal.cosupport_sum) is the intersection of the two cosupports. Both rest on the new [multiplicativity of ideal order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Ideal/Order.html#Ideal.localOrder_mul) when the associated graded ring has no zero divisors. Closedness of the cosupport and the sheaf-level marked ideal are still to come.
