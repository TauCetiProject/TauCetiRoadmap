## KTheoryLowDegrees: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`a5b839b` to `d25d526`)

Over a Euclidean domain, an invertible matrix of any size is a product of elementary matrices exactly when its determinant is one ([elementary generation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#TauCeti.elementarySubgroup_eq_ker_det_of_euclideanDomain)). So SK₁ vanishes there and the determinant identifies K₁ with the unit group. Bass stable range reaches the same conclusion more widely: [SK₁ vanishes in stable range one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Stability/SpecialLinear.html#TauCeti.specialAlgebraicK1_eq_bot_of_hasStableRangeLE_one), semilocal rings have stable range one, and one-dimensional noetherian domains, Dedekind domains among them, [have stable range at most two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StableRange/Commutative.html#TauCeti.hasStableRangeLE_two_of_krullDimLE_one). The [Sylvester–Franke theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/ExteriorPower/Determinant.html#Matrix.det_compound) also landed: the r-th compound of an n×n matrix A has determinant (det A)^C(n−1, r−1). It comes with a version for endomorphisms of finite projective modules.

Two threads aimed at the roadmap's main results have begun. The [universal Mennicke group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MennickeSymbol/Basic.html#Ideal.MennickeGroup) is defined by generators and relations, with its universal property, functoriality, the near-unit reductions and triviality over Euclidean domains (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/3700). Kervaire reciprocity and the passage from matrices to symbols are still missing. For the real circle, the coordinate ring [is a Dedekind domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleRing.instIsDedekindDomain), and [the rotation loop is not nullhomotopic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/RotationLoop.html#TauCeti.not_homotopicRel_circleRotationLoop_const) in GL_k(ℝ) for k ≥ 2: its lift through the Spin double cover runs from 1 to −1. That is the topological half of the argument. Nothing yet shows that the rotation matrix has a nontrivial class in K₁.

The supporting work went into projective K₀, exterior powers and matrices. The map n ↦ n[A] is injective exactly under invariant basis number and surjective exactly when every projective is stably free. Matrix-ring Morita invariance is normalized by the column module. Also new are the degree-n exterior sum comparison for M × N, block sums that multiply classes in K₁, the signed Weyl word, the centralizers of elementary subgroups, and the index ideal of a map between invertible modules.
