### Named results

- **Elementary generation over a Euclidean domain**: an invertible matrix of any size is a product of elementary matrices exactly when its determinant is one, so SK₁ vanishes and [the determinant identifies K₁ with the unit group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/DeterminantK1.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicK1.detEquiv) ([`elementarySubgroup_eq_ker_det_of_euclideanDomain`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Elementary.html#TauCeti.elementarySubgroup_eq_ker_det_of_euclideanDomain)).
- **Vanishing of SK₁ in stable range one**: over a commutative ring of Bass stable range one, which includes every semilocal ring, every K₁ class comes from a 1×1 matrix ([`specialAlgebraicK1_eq_bot_of_hasStableRangeLE_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Stability/SpecialLinear.html#TauCeti.specialAlgebraicK1_eq_bot_of_hasStableRangeLE_one)). One-dimensional noetherian domains are shown to have [stable range at most two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/StableRange/Commutative.html#TauCeti.hasStableRangeLE_two_of_krullDimLE_one).
- **The Sylvester–Franke theorem**: the r-th compound of an n×n matrix has determinant (det A)^C(n−1, r−1). This is the compound-matrix identity that Layer 4 needs ([`Matrix.det_compound`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/ExteriorPower/Determinant.html#Matrix.det_compound)).
- **The rotation loop is not nullhomotopic**: for k ≥ 2, the loop of plane rotations in GL_k(ℝ) does not contract, because its Spin lift runs from 1 to −1. This is the topological input to the nonzero circle class ([`not_homotopicRel_circleRotationLoop_const`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/RotationLoop.html#TauCeti.not_homotopicRel_circleRotationLoop_const)).
- **The free-class map and invariant basis number**: n ↦ n[A] from ℤ to K₀(A) is injective exactly when A has invariant basis number ([`zsmul_of_finiteProjectiveModulesRegular_injective_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Rank.html#TauCeti.FiniteProjectiveModules.zsmul_of_finiteProjectiveModulesRegular_injective_iff)). It is surjective exactly when every projective is stably free. Over a matrix ring the free classes have [index card ι](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/FiniteProjective/KZero/Matrix.html#TauCeti.FiniteProjectiveModules.index_zmultiples_of_finiteProjectiveModulesRegular_matrix).
