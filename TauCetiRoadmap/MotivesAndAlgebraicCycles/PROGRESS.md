## MotivesAndAlgebraicCycles: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-25 (`e45d174` to `522d48f`)

Two of the roadmap's abstract headline statements landed. [Jannsen's criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/EndRing.html#TauCeti.isFiniteSemisimpleCategory_abelianOfIsSemisimpleRingEnd) says that an additive category with split idempotents whose endomorphism rings are semisimple is abelian, with the given addition, and that every object is a [finite biproduct of simple objects](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/EndRing.html#TauCeti.exists_biproduct_simples_of_isSemisimpleRing_end); no finite-dimensionality is assumed, and the converse comes too. The proof goes through von Neumann regularity of morphisms. The finite-algebra input to the numerical theorem also arrived: in characteristic zero the Jacobson radical of a finite-dimensional algebra [commutes with field extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Jacobson/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.restrictScalars_jacobson_tensorProduct_eq_baseChange). The second headline is that a commutative bialgebra over a field whose finite comodules have duals [has an antipode](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/OfRigidFiniteComodules.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.exists_antipode_of_rigid_finite_comodules) satisfying both convolution identities (TauCeti#44197).

Tannakian reconstruction is now proved one object at a time. For an exact faithful linear fibre functor and an object `X` with finite-dimensional fibre, the [hull](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Abelian/SubquotientHull.html#TauCeti.subquotientHull) of subquotients of finite sums of `X` is [equivalent to finite modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Tannaka/Hull/Equivalence.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.hullEquivalence) over the algebra of natural endomorphisms of the restricted fibre. The proof goes through a representing object. Gluing these into one coalgebra is still open, but its parts are here: modules over a finite algebra as [comodules over its dual coalgebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Convolution/DualCoalgebra.html#TauCeti.ComoduleCat.equivalenceModuleCat), directed colimits of coalgebras, descent of finite comodules to a stage, and Nori's [compatible endomorphism algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/CompatibleEnd.html#Prefunctor.compatibleEnd) of quiver representations.

For comodules over a given Hopf algebra, a Hopf algebra over a field is [finitely generated exactly when some comodule tensor-generates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Finite/TensorGenerator.html#TauCeti.FGComoduleCat.finiteType_iff_exists_isTensorGenerator). In characteristic zero over an algebraically closed field, [connectedness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Connected/HopfSubalgebra.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.connectedSpace_primeSpectrum_iff_forall_isHopfSubalgebra_eq_bot) means having no nontrivial finite-dimensional Hopf subalgebra. Monoidal functors between comodule categories [induce Hopf algebra maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Tannaka/Functoriality.html#TauCeti.Tannaka.bialgHomOfFunctor).
