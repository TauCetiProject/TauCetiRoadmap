## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Only the abstract algebra has started. Jannsen's category criterion and the theorem that rigidity of finite comodules forces an antipode are proved, and Tannakian reconstruction is proved for the hull of a single object. Every geometric layer is untouched: correspondences, pure motives, realizations, Voevodsky motives, Nori's pair diagrams and the cycle conjectures.

### Named results

- **Jannsen's criterion**: an additive category with split idempotents and semisimple endomorphism rings is abelian with its given addition, and is finite semisimple ([`isFiniteSemisimpleCategory_abelianOfIsSemisimpleRingEnd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/EndRing.html#TauCeti.isFiniteSemisimpleCategory_abelianOfIsSemisimpleRingEnd)). The statement is stronger than the roadmap's version because it needs no finite-dimensionality. The converse is [`isFiniteSemisimpleCategory_iff_isSemisimpleRing_end`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/EndRing.html#TauCeti.isFiniteSemisimpleCategory_iff_isSemisimpleRing_end).
- **Antipodes from rigidity**: a commutative bialgebra over a field whose finite comodules have duals carries an antipode satisfying both convolution identities ([`exists_antipode_of_rigid_finite_comodules`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/OfRigidFiniteComodules.html#TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.exists_antipode_of_rigid_finite_comodules)). This is the README's §6.2 statement, built without circular use of group reconstruction.
- **Finite Tannakian reconstruction**: for an exact faithful linear fibre functor to vector spaces, the subquotients of finite sums of an object `X` are equivalent to finite modules over the algebra of natural endomorphisms of the restricted fibre ([`hullEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Tannaka/Hull/Equivalence.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.hullEquivalence)).
- **Finite type is tensor generation**: a Hopf algebra over a field is finitely generated exactly when one finite comodule tensor-generates its finite comodules ([`finiteType_iff_exists_isTensorGenerator`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Finite/TensorGenerator.html#TauCeti.FGComoduleCat.finiteType_iff_exists_isTensorGenerator)).
- **Radicals under field extension**: over a field of characteristic zero, the Jacobson radical of a finite-dimensional algebra commutes with extension of scalars ([`restrictScalars_jacobson_tensorProduct_eq_baseChange`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Jacobson/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.restrictScalars_jacobson_tensorProduct_eq_baseChange)). It is also the kernel of the regular trace form.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The hull of an object and its stabiliser algebra.** These are the subquotient hull [`subquotientHull`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Abelian/SubquotientHull.html#TauCeti.subquotientHull), the algebra [`hullAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Tannaka/Hull/Algebra.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.hullAlgebra) of endomorphisms preserving the fibres of all subobjects of all finite powers, and the representing object [`hullRepresenting`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Tannaka/RepresentingObject.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.hullRepresenting). Together they are the finite pieces from which a reconstructed coalgebra is assembled.
- **Modules as comodules over the dual coalgebra.** Left modules over a finite projective algebra are equivalent to right comodules over its dual coalgebra ([`equivalenceModuleCat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Convolution/DualCoalgebra.html#TauCeti.ComoduleCat.equivalenceModuleCat)), compatibly with restriction of scalars. This is the dictionary that turns hull algebras into coalgebra pieces.
- **Directed colimits of coalgebras.** Coalgebras form [directed colimits](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/DirectLimit.html#Module.DirectLimit.instCoalgebra), and a finitely presented comodule over the colimit [descends to a stage](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/DirectLimit.html#TauCeti.Comodule.exists_comoduleFactor_ofCoalgHom). Both filtered reconstruction and Nori's coalgebra `A(D,T)` need this.

## The frontier

- **Coalgebra reconstruction (§5.2, §6.2).** The duals of the hull algebras still have to be glued along nested hulls into a filtered coalgebra, with a proof that the category is equivalent to its finite comodules over the fibre. Every ingredient above is present.
- **Neutral Tannakian recognition (§6.2).** The tensor product has to put a commutative bialgebra structure on that coalgebra, which the antipode theorem then makes Hopf, and the equivalence has to be shown symmetric monoidal. This waits on the previous item.
- **§6.3 for neutral categories.** The finite-type, connectedness and closed-immersion criteria are proved only for comodules of a given Hopf algebra; they transfer once recognition exists. The faithful-flatness criterion for tensor functors and "finite iff one generator" have not been seen.
- **Nori's diagram category (§§5.1–5.2).** The duals of the [compatible endomorphism algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/CompatibleEnd.html#Prefunctor.compatibleEnd) have to be assembled into `A(D,T)`, and its universal property proved.
- **Jannsen's theorem on numerical motives (§3.3).** This is blocked on correspondences and pure motives (Layers 0–1) and on a Weil-cohomology bound for §3.1.
