### Named results

- **The Lovász–Szegedy characterization** — a graph parameter is the homomorphism density of a unit-interval graphon exactly when it is isomorphism invariant, multiplicative, normalized, and reflection positive ([`lovasz_szegedy_representability`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Representability/Representation.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.lovasz_szegedy_representability)).
- **The Diaconis–Janson graphon-mixture correspondence** — probability measures on the graphon quotient correspond to exchangeable probability laws on infinite graphs, with homomorphism densities as the mixture coordinates ([`graphonMixtureLawEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/ExchangeableGraphLaw/InfiniteCorrespondence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.graphonMixtureLawEquiv)).
- **The convergence equivalence** — for graphons on arbitrary, possibly different, probability carriers, convergence in cut distance is the same as convergence of every homomorphism density ([`tendsto_cutDist_iff_forall_homDensity_tendsto`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.tendsto_cutDist_iff_forall_homDensity_tendsto)). Its corollary is that every convergent sequence of finite graphs has a limit graphon ([`exists_graphon_tendsto_cutDist_finiteGraphGraphon_iff_forall_homDensityFin_cauchySeq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.exists_graphon_tendsto_cutDist_finiteGraphGraphon_iff_forall_homDensityFin_cauchySeq)).
- **Lovász–Szegedy compactness** — the cut-distance quotient of graphons on any atomless standard Borel carrier is a compact metric space ([`GraphonSpace.instCompactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Compact.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.GraphonSpace.instCompactSpace)).
- **Cross-carrier separation** — two graphons on arbitrary probability carriers are at cut distance zero exactly when every finite homomorphism density agrees ([`cutDist_eq_zero_iff_forall_homDensity_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Separation/Inverse.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist_eq_zero_iff_forall_homDensity_eq)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The coupling cut distance** — [`cutDist`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Distance.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist) satisfies an [arbitrary-carrier triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Triangle.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist_triangle), so graphon space is a genuine metric quotient over every carrier.
- **The unit-interval representative** — [`unitIntervalRepr`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/UnitIntervalEmbedding.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.Graphon.unitIntervalRepr) puts every graphon at cut distance zero from one on `(I, volume)`. This yields an [isometric embedding of every graphon space into the unit-interval one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/UnitIntervalEmbedding.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.isometry_toGraphonSpaceI), and through it, total boundedness, compactness and convergence all transfer off the unit interval.
