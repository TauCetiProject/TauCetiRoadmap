Beneath these sit the strict carrier and its API: `SymmKernel` and `Graphon`, homomorphism densities with the Erdős–Rényi value, the edge and triangle integrals, multiplicativity over disjoint unions, and **finite-graph compatibility** ([`homDensity_finiteGraphGraphon`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/StepGraphon/FiniteGraph/Basic.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.homDensity_finiteGraphGraphon)) with the computed backstops t(K₂, W_{K₄}) = 3/4 and t(K₃, W_{C₅}) = 0; the cut norm with its seminorm laws, the factor-four sandwich against the signed form, and invariance under measure-preserving pullback ([`cutNorm_comap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Kernel/Pullback.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutNorm_comap)); step graphons and block averages; and the Layer 3 AE view ([`Graphon.toAEEqFun`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/AEEqFun.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.Graphon.toAEEqFun)) with its three invariance theorems and the reverse bridge. Supporting measure theory brought finite and countable-middle coupling gluing (TauCeti#5350, TauCeti#4365) and rectangle approximation of product sets.

Missing is the arbitrary-carrier triangle inequality for `cutDist`. Its two design-validation gates — finite gluing with explicit null atoms and stability under step approximation ([`abs_cutDist_sub_le_cutNorm_add_cutNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Stability.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.abs_cutDist_sub_le_cutNorm_add_cutNorm)) — are in, but `cutDist_triangle` is not, so `GraphonSpace` and everything quotient-level (the descent of t(F,·), total boundedness, compactness, the convergence equivalence) has not begun. The inverse counting converse, the sampling laws, and Layers 8–9 are untouched, save the finite estimators `homDensityFin` and `injHomDensity` with their copy-count bridge.

## DenseGraphLimits: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `0d3161a`)

The convergence equivalence no longer needs a fixed carrier: [cut-distance convergence is convergence of all homomorphism densities](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.tendsto_cutDist_iff_forall_homDensity_tendsto) for graphons on arbitrary, possibly different, probability carriers. With it comes the existence half of Lovász and Szegedy's theory, that [every convergent sequence of finite graphs has a limit graphon](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.exists_graphon_tendsto_cutDist_finiteGraphGraphon_iff_forall_homDensityFin_cauchySeq) on the unit interval (TauCeti#10258).

The route there is a [unit-interval representative](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/UnitIntervalEmbedding.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.Graphon.unitIntervalRepr) for every graphon, which makes [every graphon space embed isometrically in the unit-interval one](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/UnitIntervalEmbedding.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.isometry_toGraphonSpaceI). That gives [total boundedness over any probability carrier](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/TotallyBounded.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.totallyBounded_graphonSpace), which was the one piece Layer 2 still lacked. Over an atomless standard Borel carrier the embedding is onto, so [compactness](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Compact.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.GraphonSpace.instCompactSpace) holds there too. [Frieze–Kannan weak regularity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/StepGraphon/Regularity.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.weak_regularity_frieze_kannan) was sharpened to at most 4^⌈1/ε²⌉ parts, the bound the roadmap called a prospective improvement.

Exact cut distances were computed for constant graphons and for graphons on point masses, such as [the Dirac case](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Constant.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist_dirac_dirac). Among them is the value 1/8 between K₂ on two uniform points and the constant 1/2. These discharge Layer 5's Dirac, finite-atomic and mixed regressions against the coupling-to-map equality, where they appear as unnamed examples. Smaller additions: the counting lemma specialised to an edge and a triangle, and associativity of labeled-graph gluing.

## DenseGraphLimits: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-05 (`0d3161a` to `1d09589`)

The [Lovász–Szegedy characterization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Representability/Representation.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.lovasz_szegedy_representability) and the [existence of a limit graphon for every convergent graph sequence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.exists_graphon_tendsto_cutDist_finiteGraphGraphon_iff_forall_homDensityFin_cauchySeq) no longer single out the unit interval: both now produce the graphon on any atomless standard Borel carrier. The Diaconis–Janson correspondence gained its [integral form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/ExchangeableGraphLaw/Infinite/Mixture.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.InfiniteExchangeableGraphLaw.existsUnique_bind_infiniteSampleLawOnSpace): every exchangeable law on infinite graphs is a mixture of graphon sampling laws, for exactly one mixing measure on graphon space. The README asks that the generic array-level representation be compatible with this graph-level one, and that is now proved: [every exchangeable graph law has an Aldous–Hoover coding](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/ExchangeableGraphLaw/Compatibility.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.InfiniteExchangeableGraphLaw.exists_jointCoding_mixingMeasure) whose frozen coding graphons induce exactly its mixing measure (TauCeti#10630).

Behind this, homomorphism densities became coordinates in a precise sense. They form a [closed embedding of unit-interval graphon space](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Coordinates.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.isClosedEmbedding_homDensityCoords) into a product of real lines, and a map into graphon space is [measurable exactly when every density along it is](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Measurable.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.measurable_graphonSpace_iff_forall_homDensity). Cut distance was shown [complete across carriers](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/GraphonSpace/Convergence.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.exists_graphon_tendsto_cutDist_iff_tendsto_cutDist_prod), and almost-sure and in-measure sampling convergence now hold for graphons on any probability carrier, after embedding into the unit-interval space.

On the representability side, Möbius inversion now appears as a [factorization of the connection matrix](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Representability/Moebius.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.connectionMatrix_fullyLabeled), so that reflection positivity on fully labeled graphs is exactly nonnegativity of the Möbius masses. The parameter 2^e(F) shows that [reflection positivity does not follow from the other three axioms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Representability/Validation.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.exists_not_isReflectionPositive). Two further validation examples show that the almost-everywhere view and block averaging on atomic carriers both lose strict equality. Injective homomorphism counts were tied to Mathlib's labelled copy count.
