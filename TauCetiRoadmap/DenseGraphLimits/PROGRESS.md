## DenseGraphLimits: 2026-08-17 to 2026-09-01 (`70f6c4b` to `8745177`)

The **Frieze–Kannan weak regularity lemma** ([`weak_regularity_frieze_kannan`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/StepGraphon/Regularity.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.weak_regularity_frieze_kannan)) has landed: every graphon is within ε in cut norm of a block-averaged step graphon on a measurable partition with at most 4^(⌈1/ε²⌉+1) parts, driven by the L²-Pythagoras energy increment ([`graphonPartitionEnergy_increment`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/StepGraphon/Energy.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.graphonPartitionEnergy_increment)). The **counting lemma** ([`counting_lemma`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Counting.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.counting_lemma)) bounds |t(F,U) − t(F,W)| by e(F) times the cut norm of U − W; its coupling form ([`counting_lemma_coupling`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Counting.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.counting_lemma_coupling)) crosses carriers and yields **forward separation** ([`forall_homDensity_eq_of_cutDist_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Separation/Forward.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.forall_homDensity_eq_of_cutDist_eq_zero)): graphons at coupling cut distance zero, on arbitrary probability carriers, have equal homomorphism densities for every finite graph. The coupling-primary cut distance **agrees with the classical measure-preserving-map form** over standard Borel carriers, atoms allowed ([`cutDist_eq_cutDistPullback`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Pullback/Basic.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutDist_eq_cutDistPullback)), resting on **Janson's Theorem A.9** ([`exists_measurePreserving_from_unitInterval`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/UnitIntervalMap.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.exists_measurePreserving_from_unitInterval)). **Goodman's inequality** ([`goodman_triangle_density`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Applications.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.goodman_triangle_density)) and **Mantel's bound** ([`mantel_triangle_free`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Applications.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.mantel_triangle_free)) hold for every graphon.

Beneath these sit the strict carrier and its API: `SymmKernel` and `Graphon`, homomorphism densities with the Erdős–Rényi value, the edge and triangle integrals, multiplicativity over disjoint unions, and **finite-graph compatibility** ([`homDensity_finiteGraphGraphon`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/StepGraphon/FiniteGraph/Basic.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.homDensity_finiteGraphGraphon)) with the computed backstops t(K₂, W_{K₄}) = 3/4 and t(K₃, W_{C₅}) = 0; the cut norm with its seminorm laws, the factor-four sandwich against the signed form, and invariance under measure-preserving pullback ([`cutNorm_comap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/Kernel/Pullback.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.cutNorm_comap)); step graphons and block averages; and the Layer 3 AE view ([`Graphon.toAEEqFun`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/AEEqFun.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.Graphon.toAEEqFun)) with its three invariance theorems and the reverse bridge. Supporting measure theory brought finite and countable-middle coupling gluing (TauCeti#5350, TauCeti#4365) and rectangle approximation of product sets.

Missing is the arbitrary-carrier triangle inequality for `cutDist`. Its two design-validation gates — finite gluing with explicit null atoms and stability under step approximation ([`abs_cutDist_sub_le_cutNorm_add_cutNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/DenseGraphLimits/CutMetric/Stability.html#TauCeti.DenseGraphLimits.abs_cutDist_sub_le_cutNorm_add_cutNorm)) — are in, but `cutDist_triangle` is not, so `GraphonSpace` and everything quotient-level (the descent of t(F,·), total boundedness, compactness, the convergence equivalence) has not begun. The inverse counting converse, the sampling laws, and Layers 8–9 are untouched, save the finite estimators `homDensityFin` and `injHomDensity` with their copy-count bridge.
