### Named results

- **Ocneanu's theorem** — for every parameter `z` there is exactly one normalized Markov trace on the infinite type-A Hecke algebra ([`TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.existsUnique_isMarkovTrace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/OcneanuTrace.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.existsUnique_isMarkovTrace)). - **Cycle words determine traces** — two tracial functionals that are multiplicative on disjoint strand blocks agree as soon as they agree on every cycle word `ζₘ` ([`TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.towerTrace_eq_of_cycleChain_values`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Cocenter.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.towerTrace_eq_of_cycleChain_values)).
- **The skein characterization of the Hecke braid trace** — a Markov invariant of braids that satisfies the Hecke skein relation and the split-unknot normalization is the normalized Hecke trace ([`TauCeti.MarkovBraid.eq_heckeTrace_of_skein`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Hecke.html#TauCeti.MarkovBraid.eq_heckeTrace_of_skein)). - **The hyperbolic projection parameter** — for `q = e^(−θ)` and `cosh α cosh θ ≠ 0`, the positive and negative stabilization constants of a Markov trace agree exactly when `η = ½(1 − tanh α tanh θ)` ([`TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.IsMarkovTrace.map_hyperbolicGen_eq_map_inv_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.IsMarkovTrace.map_hyperbolicGen_eq_map_inv_iff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The infinite type-A Hecke algebra** ([`TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra)) is the `RingQuot` of the free algebra on ℕ, as the README prescribes. Every trace, quotient and comparison map is stated on it, and the finite algebras now [embed into it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Embedding.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.toInfinite_injective).
- **The regular inner product** ([`TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.regularInnerProductCore`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Regular/Form.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.regularInnerProductCore)) is the definite trace form `τ(x* y)` under the real-parameter involution, with self-adjoint generators. It yields [semisimplicity at every real nonzero parameter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Semisimple.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.instIsSemisimpleRing).
- **Atomic traces** ([`TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.atomicTrace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/AtomicModel/Basic.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.atomicTrace)) are density traces on signed word spaces. On finite alphabets they are [the Ocneanu trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/AtomicModel/Basic.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.atomicTrace_eq_ocneanuTrace) under the slice condition, and they satisfy the [cycle-trace generating identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/AtomicModel/CycleWord.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.atomicTrace_cycleWord_generating_function) without the exponential `γ` factor.

### Roadmap coverage

Missing are the GNS carriers, any positivity theorem, the classification, the `τ = 0` and collapse regimes, and countable models. **H4 and H4a are untouched.**

## The frontier

- **The cocenter basis and Ram's formula** — spanning is done. What remains is linear independence of the `ζ_λ` classes and then the q-Frobenius character formula on that basis, which H3's Schur weights consume.
- **Specht modules and branching** — permutation modules, row symmetrizers and the Jucys-Murphy elements are ready. The fixed Specht construction, its tableau basis, one-box branching and the eigenvalues `Q^(column−row)` all remain.
- **The positive-real Pieri rule** — the shuffle carrier, its induced-module property and continuous regular matrices exist. What remains is the continuous Young path projectors, the rank-constancy argument and the comparison with SchurWeyl's symmetric-function Pieri rule.
- **Positivity of Markov traces** — the involutions, the Wenzl parameters and the hyperbolic `η` are in place. The positive-state classification on the full tower and its GNS carriers have not been started. The atomic models also need positivity and the countable passage with the `exp((Q−1)γx)` factor.
- **Comparison with the Jimbo HOMFLY invariant** — the braid-closure invariant exists, but its source file states that its comparison with the invariant built from Jimbo representations is not made.
