### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The infinite type-A Hecke algebra** ([`TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra)) is the quotient of the free algebra on ℕ.
