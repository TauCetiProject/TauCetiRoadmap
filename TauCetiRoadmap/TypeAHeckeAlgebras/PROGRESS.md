## TypeAHeckeAlgebras: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`1e665a6` to `b4b3ce5`)

The roadmap's central carrier now exists: the [infinite type-A Hecke algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra), built as the actual quotient of the free algebra on ℕ by the quadratic, braid and distant-commutation relations, with its [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.lift) (TauCeti#19931). Around it sit the finite [stages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.stage) generated by the first few generators, which exhaust the algebra and are spanned by images of braids under the [braid homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.braidHom), together with the strand shift, the fact that [disjoint blocks commute](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.commute_shift_of_mem_stage), and the index and sign characters. The stages are still the adjoined subalgebras: the finite presentation, the basis theorem and the comparison between them have not landed.

On the representation side, the [signed checked R-matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/SignedCheckedR.html#TauCeti.signedCheckedR) on word spaces over an alphabet of positive and negative letters satisfies the Hecke quadratic relation, the [Yang-Baxter equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/SignedCheckedR.html#TauCeti.signedCheckedR_braid) and distant commutation. It therefore gives a [braid group representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/SignedCheckedR.html#TauCeti.signedCheckedRep), and it [reduces to the Jimbo R-matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/SignedCheckedR.html#TauCeti.signedCheckedR_eq_jimboGenerator) when every letter is positive. This is the building block for H3's atomic tensor models.

Two pieces of combinatorics that H1 asks for also landed. One is the separation of Jucys-Murphy eigenvalues: distinct addable cells of a Young diagram have contents at least 2 and at most one more than the diagram's size apart, and [split semisimplicity separates the Q-contents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/ContentSeparation.html#YoungDiagram.injOn_zpow_cellContent_of_prod_geom_sum_ne_zero), with a [sharp criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Young/Addable.html#YoungDiagram.injOn_zpow_cellContent_iff). The other is that over a ℚ-algebra, Newton's identities make the [power-sum products a basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Symmetric/PowerSum/Basis.html#TauCeti.psumPartBasis) of the symmetric polynomials of each degree.
