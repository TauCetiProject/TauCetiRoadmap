## TypeAHeckeAlgebras: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`1e665a6` to `b4b3ce5`)

The roadmap's central carrier now exists: the [infinite type-A Hecke algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra), built as the actual quotient of the free algebra on ℕ by the quadratic, braid and distant-commutation relations, with its [universal property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.lift) (TauCeti#19931). Around it sit the finite [stages](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.stage) generated by the first few generators, which exhaust the algebra and are spanned by images of braids under the [braid homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.braidHom), together with the strand shift, the fact that [disjoint blocks commute](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Infinite.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.commute_shift_of_mem_stage), and the index and sign characters. The stages are still the adjoined subalgebras: the finite presentation, the basis theorem and the comparison between them have not landed.

On the representation side, the [signed checked R-matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/SignedCheckedR.html#TauCeti.signedCheckedR) on word spaces over an alphabet of positive and negative letters satisfies the Hecke quadratic relation, the [Yang-Baxter equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/SignedCheckedR.html#TauCeti.signedCheckedR_braid) and distant commutation. It therefore gives a [braid group representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/SignedCheckedR.html#TauCeti.signedCheckedRep), and it [reduces to the Jimbo R-matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/SignedCheckedR.html#TauCeti.signedCheckedR_eq_jimboGenerator) when every letter is positive. This is the building block for H3's atomic tensor models.

Two pieces of combinatorics that H1 asks for also landed. One is the separation of Jucys-Murphy eigenvalues: distinct addable cells of a Young diagram have contents at least 2 and at most one more than the diagram's size apart, and [split semisimplicity separates the Q-contents](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/ContentSeparation.html#YoungDiagram.injOn_zpow_cellContent_of_prod_geom_sum_ne_zero), with a [sharp criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Young/Addable.html#YoungDiagram.injOn_zpow_cellContent_iff). The other is that over a ℚ-algebra, Newton's identities make the [power-sum products a basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Symmetric/PowerSum/Basis.html#TauCeti.psumPartBasis) of the symmetric polynomials of each degree.

## TypeAHeckeAlgebras: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-12 (`b4b3ce5` to `941bb58`)

The finite type-A Hecke algebra now exists by generators and relations, and it has its [basis theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Basis.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.basisTw): over every commutative ring and at every unit parameter, the elements `T_w` for `w ∈ Sₙ` form a basis, so the rank is `n!`. The presentation is [isomorphic to the stage](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Embedding.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.stageEquiv) of the infinite algebra, and at `q = ±1` it is the [group algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/GroupAlgebra.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.groupAlgebraEquiv). With the basis in hand, [Ocneanu's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/OcneanuTrace.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.existsUnique_isMarkovTrace) followed: for every `z` there is exactly one normalized Markov trace (TauCeti#24190). Coset bases over the previous stage and over parabolic subalgebras, finite trace radicals, and base change came with it.

The cycle-block elements `ζ_λ` [span the cocenter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Cocenter.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.span_cycleBlock_sup_stageCommutatorSpan), so a trace is [determined by its values on them](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Cocenter.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.trace_eq_of_cycleBlock_values). Their linear independence, the cocenter basis proper, is still open. The normalized trace of a braid descends to a [braid-closure invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Hecke/Trace.html#TauCeti.heckeBraidInvariant), and the [skein relation characterizes it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Hecke.html#TauCeti.MarkovBraid.eq_heckeTrace_of_skein). Its comparison with the Jimbo-built invariant has not been made. The course-note computation is also done: the two stabilization constants of a Markov trace [agree exactly when η = ½(1 − tanh α tanh θ)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Hyperbolic.html#TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.IsMarkovTrace.map_hyperbolicGen_eq_map_inv_iff).

Groundwork for positivity accumulated without reaching a positivity theorem. Under the real-parameter involution the canonical trace form is definite, which makes the finite algebra [semisimple at every real nonzero parameter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/Semisimple.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.instIsSemisimpleRing). The Temperley-Lieb algebra is [the Jones quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/TemperleyLieb.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.ker_toTemperleyLieb). Finite atomic tensor models [realize the Ocneanu trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TypeAHeckeAlgebra/AtomicModel/Basic.html#TauCeti.TypeAHeckeAlgebra.atomicTrace_eq_ocneanuTrace). Also new are Jucys-Murphy elements, which commute and are self-adjoint, the shuffle module shown to be induced, and Wenzl's parameters. Specht modules have not started.
