## LieGroupoidsPoisson: 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09 (`b93bbb0` to `7ca783d`)

Lie algebroids now exist. A [Lie algebroid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LieAlgebroid/Basic.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebroid) is a smooth real vector bundle with an anchor to the tangent bundle and a Lie bracket on sections satisfying the Leibniz rule. Its sections form a [Lie–Rinehart algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LieAlgebroid/Basic.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebroid.Sections.instLieRinehartAlgebra) over the smooth functions. The fact that the [anchor carries brackets of sections to brackets of vector fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LieAlgebroid/Basic.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebroid.anchor_bracket) is derived from Jacobi and Leibniz rather than assumed. The [tangent algebroid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LieAlgebroid/Tangent.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebroid.tangent) is the first example. Restriction to opens, local frames and the bracket-locality API that the roadmap also asks of this target are not yet there.

On the algebraic side, the [Chevalley–Eilenberg complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/ChevalleyEilenberg.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.cochainComplex) of a Lie algebra with coefficients in a module now exists in every degree. Its cochains are alternating maps, the [differential squares to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/ChevalleyEilenberg.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.d_d), and [Cartan's formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/ChevalleyEilenberg.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.curryLeft_d_succ) `ι_x d = L_x − d ι_x` is proved. In degrees one and two the differential agrees with Mathlib's `d₁₂` and [`d₂₃`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/ChevalleyEilenberg.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.d₂₃_twoCochainEquiv). Supporting this, a Lie algebra now acts on alternating maps, and interior products can be curried and uncurried. The algebroid version of the complex has not started.

In the foundations, the split-derivative submersion predicate is now compared with Mathlib's normal form: a smooth map between finite-dimensional boundaryless manifolds [is a submersion exactly when every differential has a continuous right inverse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Submersion/Split.html#TauCeti.submersion_iff_split_mfderiv) (TauCeti#27736). Separately, the [kernels of the differential of any smooth map form an involutive distribution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/Kernel.html#ContMDiff.isInvolutiveDistribution_ker_mfderiv), with no constant-rank hypothesis.
