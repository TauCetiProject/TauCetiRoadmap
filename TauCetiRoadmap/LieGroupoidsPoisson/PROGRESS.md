## LieGroupoidsPoisson: 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09 (`b93bbb0` to `7ca783d`)

Lie algebroids now exist. A [Lie algebroid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LieAlgebroid/Basic.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebroid) is a smooth real vector bundle with an anchor to the tangent bundle and a Lie bracket on sections satisfying the Leibniz rule. Its sections form a [Lie–Rinehart algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LieAlgebroid/Basic.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebroid.Sections.instLieRinehartAlgebra) over the smooth functions. The fact that the [anchor carries brackets of sections to brackets of vector fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LieAlgebroid/Basic.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebroid.anchor_bracket) is derived from Jacobi and Leibniz rather than assumed. The [tangent algebroid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/LieAlgebroid/Tangent.html#TauCeti.LieAlgebroid.tangent) is the first example. Restriction to opens, local frames and the bracket-locality API that the roadmap also asks of this target are not yet there.

On the algebraic side, the [Chevalley–Eilenberg complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/ChevalleyEilenberg.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.cochainComplex) of a Lie algebra with coefficients in a module now exists in every degree. Its cochains are alternating maps, the [differential squares to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/ChevalleyEilenberg.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.d_d), and [Cartan's formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/ChevalleyEilenberg.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.curryLeft_d_succ) `ι_x d = L_x − d ι_x` is proved. In degrees one and two the differential agrees with Mathlib's `d₁₂` and [`d₂₃`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/ChevalleyEilenberg.html#TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.d₂₃_twoCochainEquiv). Supporting this, a Lie algebra now acts on alternating maps, and interior products can be curried and uncurried. The algebroid version of the complex has not started.

In the foundations, the split-derivative submersion predicate is now compared with Mathlib's normal form: a smooth map between finite-dimensional boundaryless manifolds [is a submersion exactly when every differential has a continuous right inverse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Submersion/Split.html#TauCeti.submersion_iff_split_mfderiv) (TauCeti#27736). Separately, the [kernels of the differential of any smooth map form an involutive distribution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/Kernel.html#ContMDiff.isInvolutiveDistribution_ker_mfderiv), with no constant-rank hypothesis.

## LieGroupoidsPoisson: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The constant-rank theorem is now proved on manifolds. A smooth map from a finite-dimensional real or complex manifold to a Banach manifold, whose rank near a point never exceeds its rank at that point, [agrees with its derivative in suitable smooth local coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ConstantRank.html#ContMDiffOn.exists_openPartialHomeomorph_comp_eq_mfderiv_comp), and no Hausdorff or countability hypothesis is needed (TauCeti#36520). In those coordinates the [level set through the point is the kernel of the derivative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ConstantRank.html#ContMDiffOn.exists_openPartialHomeomorph_apply_eq_iff_mem_ker). The proof transports the [Euclidean rank theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/ConstantRank.html#ContDiffOn.exists_openPartialHomeomorph_comp_eq_fderiv_comp) through charts. That theorem now has its own level-set form, and is supported by continuous pseudo-inverses of finite-rank maps and a rank-nullity comparison of kernels. What the roadmap builds on top is still missing: level fibres as embedded submanifolds with their universal property, and the embedded local image.

On the groupoid side, the [topological groupoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsTopologicalGroupoid) foundations, together with the common-complement lemma below, landed earlier and are recorded here late. They include full restrictions, products, the [action groupoid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Constructions.html#TauCeti.ActionGroupoid.isTopologicalGroupoid) with arrow space `G × X`, and [openness of target from local source sections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsTopologicalGroupoid.isOpenMap_target_of_local_source_sections). This window adds the roadmap's other examples. The [unit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Examples.html#TauCeti.UnitGroupoid.isTopologicalGroupoid) and [pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Examples.html#TauCeti.PairGroupoid.isTopologicalGroupoid) groupoids and [a topological group over a point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CategoryTheory/Groupoid/Examples.html#TauCeti.OneObjectGroupoid.isTopologicalGroupoid) each come with explicit homeomorphisms describing their arrows and composable pairs. Continuous functors are not yet there.

The linear-algebra input for bisections is that [equal-dimensional subspaces have a common complement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Submodule/CommonComplement.html#Submodule.exists_isCompl_isCompl_of_finrank_eq). Submersions with split differentials are now [closed under composition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Submersion/Split.html#TauCeti.IsDiffSubmersionAt.comp), and they get a [projection normal form in a maximal-atlas chart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Submersion/Split.html#ContMDiffOn.exists_domChart_mem_maximalAtlas_of_surjective_mfderiv). Lie groupoids themselves are still undefined.
