## CategoricalOperatorAlgebras: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`0900b7c` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first layer got its foundation. There is now a class of [C*-categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Category/Basic.html#TauCeti.CStarCategory) on Mathlib's native linear categories: Banach Hom spaces, contractive composition, the C*-identity ‖f†f‖ = ‖f‖², and positivity of f†f. From these axioms, [each endomorphism algebra End X is a unital C*-algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Category/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.End.instCStarAlgebra), with the dagger as its star and its positive elements [exactly the composites f†f](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Category/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.End.nonneg_iff). A linear dagger functor between C*-categories is [automatically contractive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Category/Functor.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.norm_map_le), and [isometric when faithful](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Category/Functor.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.norm_map). The first example is [complex Hilbert spaces with operator norms and adjoints](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Category.html#TauCeti.HilbertCat.instCStarCategoryComplex) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/43).

Underneath this sits a purely algebraic layer (TauCeti#38275): [dagger categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Dagger/Basic.html#TauCeti.DaggerCategory) with additive and conjugate-linear compatibility, unitary arrows, dagger-preserving functors, and pointwise daggers of natural transformations. It also includes a category of Hilbert spaces in which [unitary arrows are exactly the linear isometric equivalences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Category.html#TauCeti.HilbertCat.unitaryEquivLinearIsometryEquiv). Still missing from the first layer are linking algebras, orthogonal sums and the Karoubi-type completion, Hilbert C*-modules as an example, and everything on W*-categories.
