## DiamondsAndVStacks: 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-19 (`3821a65` to `b235000`)

The spectral-topology strand of the foundations layer (D0) got its first results. The main one is Scholze's criterion from *Étale cohomology of diamonds* for when the quotient of a spectral space by an equivalence relation is again spectral. When the graph of the relation is pro-constructible and its projections are generalizing, the quotient is spectral as long as its topology has a basis with quasi-compact preimages ([`spectralSpace_quotient`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/Quotient.html#TauCeti.spectralSpace_quotient), Lemma 2.9). When the projection is open, the quotient is spectral outright ([`spectralSpace_quotient_of_isOpenMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/Quotient.html#TauCeti.spectralSpace_quotient_of_isOpenMap)). The locally spectral form of Lemma 2.10, the form later used to show that the underlying space of a spatial diamond is spectral, is there too: the quotient is [locally spectral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/Quotient.html#TauCeti.isLocallySpectralSpace_quotient_of_isOpenMap) and [quasi-separated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/Quotient.html#TauCeti.quasiSeparatedSpace_quotient_of_isOpenMap). So are the T₀ and invariant-neighbourhood statements of Lemma 2.7. To support this, TauCeti#41206 adds locally pro-constructible sets, products of (locally) spectral spaces, and open quotient maps.

Hochster duality also landed: the [inverse topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/InverseTopology.html#TauCeti.inverseTopology) of a spectral space is [spectral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/InverseTopology.html#TauCeti.spectralSpace_inverseTopology). Taking it twice gives back the original topology, it has the same constructible topology, and it reverses specialization. A map is spectral exactly when it is spectral for the inverse topologies. Finally, the [space of connected components](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/ConnectedComponents.html#ConnectedComponents.t2Space_of_prespectralSpace) of a spectral space is Hausdorff, and therefore profinite. Its clopens match those of the space, and [each component is the intersection of its clopen neighbourhoods](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/ConnectedComponents.html#PrespectralSpace.connectedComponent_eq_iInter_isClopen).
