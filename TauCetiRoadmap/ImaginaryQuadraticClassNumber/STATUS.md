## The frontier

- **The CM dictionary (H3.1)**: uniformization and the homothety classification are in place. What remains is to show that lattices with endomorphism ring O_f correspond to Pic(O_f), that ℤ + ℤτ_D has endomorphism ring O_K, and then to export the ideal-class action and H_K = K(j(τ_D)). The last step consumes ClassFieldTheory Layers 10–13.
- **Siegel functions at general level (H3.2)**: the congruence criterion is done. What remains is integrality over ℤ[j] at every level and the diagonal cyclotomic action.
- **Level-two norms (H3.3)**: show that x = Norm(α¹²) is an integer satisfying the two j-formulas, and prove the small-power inclusion for 2 inert. The group of quadratic multiplication matrices over 𝔽₂ [has order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticOrder/LevelTwoMatrix.html#QuadraticAlgebra.card_periodMatrixGroup_zmod_two) 2, 1 or 3 as 2 ramifies, splits or is inert. The rest needs CM reciprocity from H3.1.
- **Siegel's theorem (H2.3–H2.4)**: shift the smoothed Mellin contour with error estimates, prove the zero-free alternative and L(1, χ) ≥ c_ε q^(−ε). Finiteness then follows from the real form of the class number formula.
- **The field theorem (H3.6)**: assemble the branches and specialize the existing order classification to the thirteen orders.
