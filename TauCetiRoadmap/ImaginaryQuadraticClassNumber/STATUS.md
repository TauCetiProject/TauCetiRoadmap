## The frontier

- **Lattice comparisons (§§1.3–1.4)**: show that the ideal of a form maps to a(ℤ + ℤτ), that O_f maps to ℤ + ℤfτ_D, and that O_f is the endomorphism ring of that lattice. These are the inputs the CM dictionary consumes.
- **The CM dictionary (§3.1)**: match lattices with endomorphism ring O_f to Pic(O_f), then export the ideal-class action and H_K = K(j(τ_D)). The last step needs ClassFieldTheory Layers 10–13.
- **Siegel functions at general level (§3.2)**: prove integrality over ℤ[j] at every level, plus the diagonal cyclotomic action.
- **Level-two norms (§3.3)**: show that x = Norm(α¹²) is an integer satisfying the two j-formulas, and prove the small-power inclusion when 2 is inert. Both need the reciprocity of §3.1.
- **Siegel's theorem (§§2.3–2.4)**: shift the smoothed Mellin contour with error estimates, prove the zero-free alternative, and deduce L(1, χ) ≥ c_ε q^(−ε). Finiteness then follows from the real form of the class number formula.
