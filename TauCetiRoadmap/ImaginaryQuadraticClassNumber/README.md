# Imaginary quadratic fields: class numbers, finiteness and class number one

Construct the arithmetic of imaginary quadratic fields and orders on the existing number-field, ideal-class, binary-form and modular-function carriers. Prove both the finiteness of imaginary quadratic fields of bounded class number and the exact class-number-one classification. The two conclusions have distinct proofs: an ineffective uniform lower bound proves finiteness, while an effective CM and integer-equation argument proves exhaustion of the nine fields.

## Scope, conventions and supplier boundaries

The field classification concerns the **maximal order** O_K of every imaginary quadratic field K, up to Q-algebra isomorphism. Parametrize these fields uniquely by negative squarefree integers d, with K_d=Q(sqrt d), and equivalently by negative fundamental discriminants D. The radicand d and field discriminant D are different: D=d if d≡1 mod4, and D=4d otherwise. The positive radicands in the nine-field list are1,2,3,7,11,19,43,67,163; the corresponding field discriminants are−4,−8,−3,−7,−11,−19,−43,−67,−163. Multiplying a radicand by a rational square does not create a new field. Neither arbitrary presentations nor positive integers with repeated square factors form a finite classification set.

Use native NumberField, NumberField.RingOfIntegers, ClassGroup and NumberField.classNumber. Use native QuadraticAlgebra ℚ d 0 for the concrete two-dimensional model and native Algebra.IsQuadraticExtension Q K for the intrinsic predicate; introduce no competing quadratic-field property. Mathlib supplies the two-coordinate basis, multiplication, conjugation, norm and trace of QuadraticAlgebra, its Field instance for a nonsquare discriminant (`QuadraticAlgebra.isField_iff_not_isSquare_discr`) and its compatible rational scalar action. Tau Ceti supplies, on these carriers, the model equivalence `TauCeti.QuadraticOrder.algEquivOfSqrt` (evaluation at a square root of D identifies QuadraticAlgebra ℚ D 0 with a quadratic field containing one), the signature criterion `QuadraticAlgebra.isTotallyComplex_iff` and the quadratic integral-basis and discriminant theorems of the module TauCeti.NumberTheory.NumberField.Quadratic.RingOfIntegers. Build the remaining comparison interfaces in that shape.

[NumberFieldArithmetic](../../Completed/NumberFieldArithmetic/README.md) and the Tau Ceti quadratic modules supply integral generators, quadratic integral bases, discriminants, norms, finite-prime splitting and the class-number/Minkowski interfaces. Reuse their actual statements, including minpoly and generation hypotheses; construct the comparison from each concrete model to those presentations. [GlobalNumberFields Layer11](../../Completed/GlobalNumberFields/README.md#layer-11-orders-and-picard-groups) owns orders, conductors, invertible proper ideals and Picard groups. [ClassFieldTheory Layers10–13](../ClassFieldTheory/README.md) owns global reciprocity, existence, Hilbert and ray class fields; its actual arithmetic existence and ray-degree statements are required, not just a generic class-formation structure. This roadmap owns the imaginary-quadratic analytic and CM specializations and their comparison maps.

Use the primitive positive-definite part of Tau Ceti's binary quadratic forms and its reduced representatives. The combinatorial **weighted Hurwitz** class number in [ModularForms Layer 11](../ModularForms/README.md) is not NumberField.classNumber: exceptional automorphisms and imprimitive forms must be separated. [ModularForms](../ModularForms/README.md), [ModularCurves](../ModularCurves/README.md) and [EllipticCurves](../EllipticCurves/README.md) supply ordinary analytic modular curves, arithmetic moduli and algebraic torsion/isogeny APIs within their stated scopes. Complex elliptic uniformization and the needed analytic-to-algebraic comparison are built in H3.1; EllipticCurves expressly excludes them, and ModularCurves keeps its generic connectedness comparison conditional. They do not by their names supply CM reciprocity, integrality of singular values or the classification here. Build these comparisons in H3.

Generic ideal-series regrouping and Euler products use the [completed Arithmetic Dirichlet Series specification](../../Completed/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/README.md). H2 owns the quadratic specializations and additional real-character estimates.

H2.3–H2.4 state and prove the classical ineffective form of Siegel's theorem and its class-number consequences. Their effective forms are not stated here; both are stated after this roadmap and supply the effective forms to later roadmaps. AnalyticNumberTheoryPartI §0.9 states L(1,χ)≥c/log q with an effective absolute c, derived there from the uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros (AnalyticNumberTheoryPartI §0.34); it consumes only the class-number formula of H2.2 from this roadmap. ### H1.3. Primitive forms and ideal classes
