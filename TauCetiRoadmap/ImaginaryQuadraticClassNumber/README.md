# Imaginary quadratic fields: class numbers, finiteness and class number one

Construct the arithmetic of imaginary quadratic fields and orders on the existing number-field, ideal-class, binary-form and modular-function carriers. Prove both the finiteness of imaginary quadratic fields of bounded class number and the exact class-number-one classification. The two conclusions have distinct proofs: an ineffective uniform lower bound proves finiteness, while an effective CM and integer-equation argument proves exhaustion of the nine fields.

## Scope, conventions and supplier boundaries

The field classification concerns the **maximal order** O_K of every imaginary quadratic field K, up to Q-algebra isomorphism. Parametrize these fields uniquely by negative squarefree integers d, with K_d=Q(sqrt d), and equivalently by negative fundamental discriminants D. The radicand d and field discriminant D are different: D=d if d≡1 mod4, and D=4d otherwise. The positive radicands in the nine-field list are1,2,3,7,11,19,43,67,163; the corresponding field discriminants are−4,−8,−3,−7,−11,−19,−43,−67,−163. Multiplying a radicand by a rational square does not create a new field. Neither arbitrary presentations nor positive integers with repeated square factors form a finite classification set.

Use native NumberField, NumberField.RingOfIntegers, ClassGroup and NumberField.classNumber. Use native QuadraticAlgebra for the concrete two-dimensional algebra and native Algebra.IsQuadraticExtension Q K for the intrinsic predicate; introduce no competing quadratic-field property. [Mathlib PR36347](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/36347) and [PR36387](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/36387) supply the concrete Qsqrtd/parameter-comparison API shape. The current native Basic.lean already supplies the nonsquare-to-Field instance and compatible rational scalar parent. The active complementary sequence [PR43088](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/43088), [PR43490](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/43490), [PR43491](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/43491), [PR43493](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/43493) and [PR44156](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/44156) develops maximal quadratic orders, intrinsic discriminants, squarefree models, embeddings and prime splitting on the same carriers. Coordinate overlapping implementations with that work; consume the installed Tau Ceti integral-basis/presentation theorems now and port missing comparison interfaces in the existing shape, then delete them in favor of upstream imports when available. Implement the API here with that shape when unavailable in the installed dependency; adopt the upstream API by deletion and import when it becomes available. The model for a nonsquare rational parameter is a field; nonzero square parameters give a split algebra, and zero gives dual numbers. A bare type alias does not supply a field instance or identify two potentially different scalar actions.

[NumberFieldArithmetic](../NumberFieldArithmetic/README.md) and its existing Tau Ceti quadratic modules supply integral generators, quadratic integral bases, discriminants, norms, finite-prime splitting and the class-number/Minkowski interfaces. Reuse their actual statements, including minpoly and generation hypotheses; construct the comparison from each concrete model to those presentations. [GlobalNumberFields Layer11](../../Completed/GlobalNumberFields/README.md#layer-11-orders-and-picard-groups) owns orders, conductors, invertible proper ideals and Picard groups. [ClassFieldTheory Layers10–13](../ClassFieldTheory/README.md) owns global reciprocity, existence, Hilbert and ray class fields; its actual arithmetic existence and ray-degree statements are required, not just a generic class-formation structure. This roadmap owns the imaginary-quadratic analytic and CM specializations and their comparison maps.

Use the primitive positive-definite part of Tau Ceti's binary quadratic forms and its reduced representatives. The combinatorial **weighted Hurwitz** class number in [ModularForms Layer11](../ModularForms/README.md#layer-11-the-eichler–selberg-trace-formula-level-one) is not NumberField.classNumber: exceptional automorphisms and imprimitive forms must be separated. [ModularForms](../ModularForms/README.md), [ModularCurves](../ModularCurves/README.md) and [EllipticCurves](../EllipticCurves/README.md) supply ordinary analytic modular curves, arithmetic moduli and algebraic torsion/isogeny APIs within their stated scopes. Complex elliptic uniformization and the needed analytic-to-algebraic comparison are built in H3.1; EllipticCurves expressly excludes them, and ModularCurves keeps its generic connectedness comparison conditional. They do not by their names supply CM reciprocity, integrality of singular values or the classification here. Build these comparisons in H3.

Generic ideal-series regrouping and Euler products use the [completed Arithmetic Dirichlet Series specification](../../Completed/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/README.md). H2 owns the quadratic specializations and additional real-character estimates.

The effective integer-equation floor is the [DiophantineApproximationAndTranscendence proposal](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/pull/906), in particular §§3.6,4.1,4.3–4.5: explicit archimedean logarithm bounds, bounded divisor representatives, unit and Thue equations, S-unit/Thue–Mahler reduction, and the effective superelliptic bound. Its quantitative bounds are not implied by the qualitative Baker theorem. Consume the named endpoint with all degree, height, nonzero and distinct-root hypotheses. Build any unavailable supplied interface in Tau Ceti with the supplier's specification; there is no waiting or transfer of the work to Mathlib.

All embeddings into C used below carry a chosen positive imaginary square root. Use q=exp(2πiτ) on the native UpperHalfPlane. In finite-level modular-function actions, a row vector r acts on the right by matrices; the diagonal cyclotomic action sends ζ_N to ζ_N^d. Do not silently exchange action conventions.

The order O_f=Z+fO_K has discriminant f²D. Its Picard number counts invertible proper ideal classes. It is a separate invariant from the class number of K and from the ideal-class monoid of noninvertible ideals. The nine-field theorem does not classify all orders, and UFD language applies to the maximal Dedekind order. All conductor comparisons below retain f≥1 and the conductor-prime restrictions.
