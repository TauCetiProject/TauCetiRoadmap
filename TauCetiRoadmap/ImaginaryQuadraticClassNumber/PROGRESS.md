## ImaginaryQuadraticClassNumber: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-23 (`2148d49` to `b4b3ce5`)

The first window on this roadmap settled what an imaginary quadratic field is in the library. Every totally complex quadratic number field is isomorphic to ℚ(√d) for exactly one negative squarefree integer d ([`NumberField.existsUnique_neg_squarefree_algEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Quadratic/Radicand.html#NumberField.existsUnique_neg_squarefree_algEquiv)), and two squarefree radicands give isomorphic fields only when they are equal ([`NumberField.nonempty_algEquiv_iff_of_squarefree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Quadratic/Radicand.html#NumberField.nonempty_algEquiv_iff_of_squarefree)). The model ℚ(√d) now has a distinguished complex embedding sending √d into the upper half-plane ([`QuadraticAlgebra.complexEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Quadratic/ComplexEmbedding.html#QuadraticAlgebra.complexEmbedding)). Every complex embedding is either this one or its conjugate, complex conjugation restricts to the Galois involution, and the signature is (0, 1) ([`QuadraticAlgebra.nrComplexPlaces_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Quadratic/ComplexEmbedding.html#QuadraticAlgebra.nrComplexPlaces_eq_one)).

Three other parts of the roadmap got their first pieces. Sending a primitive positive definite form (a, b, c) to the ideal class of aℤ + ((−b + √D)/2)ℤ gives an injective map from proper equivalence classes of forms into the Picard group of the order ([`TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.posDefProperClassToPic_injective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/Ideal/Class.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.posDefProperClassToPic_injective)). Surjectivity, which would complete the correspondence, is not yet proved. On the analytic side, L(1, χ) is shown to be a positive real number for every nontrivial quadratic Dirichlet character ([`DirichletCharacter.LFunction_apply_one_pos`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/QuadraticCharacter.html#DirichletCharacter.LFunction_apply_one_pos)). Finally, the Siegel functions g_r are defined through a theta product ([`TauCeti.ModularForm.siegelFunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/SiegelFunction.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.siegelFunction)) and shown to be holomorphic and nowhere zero on the upper half-plane. Their behaviour under τ ↦ τ + 1 and under integral shifts of r is also proved, but the full SL₂(ℤ) transformation law is still open.

## ImaginaryQuadraticClassNumber: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-06 (`b4b3ce5` to `385ec2d`)

The analytic class number formula h = w√|D| L(1, χ_D)/(2π) for imaginary quadratic fields is now proved ([`classNumber_eq_card_units_mul_LFunction_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Quadratic/DedekindZeta.html#TauCeti.NumberField.classNumber_eq_card_units_mul_LFunction_one)). The proof factors the Dedekind zeta function as ζ(s) L(s, χ_D) ([`dedekindZeta_eq_riemannZeta_mul_LFunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Quadratic/DedekindZeta.html#TauCeti.NumberField.dedekindZeta_eq_riemannZeta_mul_LFunction)) and compares residues, treating D = −3 and −4 with their six and four units. The form–ideal correspondence is also complete: the primitive reduced forms of discriminant −n hit each Picard class exactly once ([`picClass_primitiveReducedForms_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/Ideal/ClassNumber.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.picClass_primitiveReducedForms_bijective)), and Dirichlet composition is the group law. Counting reduced forms then shows that the nine discriminants −3, −4, −7, −8, −11, −19, −43, −67, −163 have class number one ([`classNumber_eq_one_of_mem_heegnerDiscriminants`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassNumberOne/HeegnerNumbers.html#TauCeti.NumberField.classNumber_eq_one_of_mem_heegnerDiscriminants)), and that no other discriminant above −51 does. That is the easy direction of the class-number-one theorem. Showing the list is complete is still open.

Several separate pieces of the effective proof also landed. The Siegel functions now have their SL₂(ℤ) transformation law up to a twelfth root of unity ([`exists_siegelFunction_smul_eq_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/SiegelFunction/Transform.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.exists_siegelFunction_smul_eq_mul)). The level-two functions satisfy g₀g₁g₂ = 2ζ₈ and Weber's cubic (X + 16)³ − jX, and the explicit product bounds for the level-two and level-three branches are proved ([`norm_siegelFunction_le_of_le_half`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/SiegelFunction/Bounds.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.norm_siegelFunction_le_of_le_half)). The integer points of the Heegner quartic y² = 2u(u³ + 1) are determined exactly ([`sq_eq_two_mul_mul_pow_three_add_one_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassNumberOne/HeegnerQuartic.html#TauCeti.sq_eq_two_mul_mul_pow_three_add_one_iff)). From them follow the six possible coefficients γ ∈ {0, −32, −96, −960, −5280, −640320} ([`mem_image_intCast_of_tschirnhausPolynomial_X_pow_four_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassNumberOne/HeegnerQuartic.html#TauCeti.mem_image_intCast_of_tschirnhausPolynomial_X_pow_four_eq)). The holomorphic cube root γ₂ of j landed, with [γ₂³ = j](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/SiegelFunction/GammaTwo.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.gammaTwo_pow_three), γ₂ = (g_i¹² + 16)/g_i⁴ and leading term q^(−1/3) ([TauCeti#25623](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25623)). The level-two Siegel functions are integral over any ring over which j is integral ([TauCeti#27286](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27286)). In the six-value branch, j(τ) = v³ with Im τ = √n/2 and n ≥ 3 [forces n ∈ {3, 11, 19, 43, 67, 163}](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ClassNumberOne/SingularValue.html#TauCeti.ModularForm.mem_of_j_eq_intCast_pow_three), which recovers D ([TauCeti#25733](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25733)). The [Hilbert class polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BinaryQuadraticForm/HilbertClassPolynomial.html#TauCeti.BinaryQuadraticForm.hilbertClassPolynomial) is defined: monic and separable, of degree the class number, with real coefficients and exactly the singular moduli as roots ([TauCeti#27426](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27426)). What would connect these pieces, the level-two norms at CM points, has not started.

Supporting work covered integral models and prime splitting for ℚ(√d), the class number of the order of conductor f, the values of χ_D at primes, the first nonnegativity and residue facts toward Siegel's theorem, the bound [‖L(1, χ)‖ ≤ 2 + log N](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/LFunction/LogBound.html#DirichletCharacter.norm_LFunction_one_le_two_add_log) for nonprincipal χ of level N ([TauCeti#25473](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/25473)), the fields [F_N](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/CongruenceSubgroups/FunctionField.html#TauCeti.modularFunctionField) with their level maps ([TauCeti#27151](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/27151)), and F₁ = ℚ(j) ([`coeffSubfield_cuspDatumInfty_bot`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/LevelOne/FunctionField.html#TauCeti.ModularGroup.coeffSubfield_cuspDatumInfty_bot)). The Weierstrass map from ℂ to E(ℂ) is now a surjective holomorphic homomorphism with kernel the lattice.
