## PivotalSpherical: 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-20 (`622be3b` to `68a457e`)

The categorical core of the roadmap went up in its first window. Pivotal categories now exist as [`PivotalCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Basic.html#TauCeti.PivotalCategory), a monoidal trivialization of the double-dual functor, with the [Freyd–Yetter identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Basic.html#TauCeti.pivotalIsoApp_rightDual) `φ_{Xᘁ} = (φ_X⁻¹)ᘁ` proved as a theorem rather than carried as an axiom, and the [torsor of pivotal structures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Torsor.html#TauCeti.PivotalCategory.instTorsor): when one exists, the monoidal automorphisms of the identity act on them freely and transitively. On top of that sit left and right traces with their basic theory (cyclicity, linearity, additivity on biproducts, and the mate exchanging the two traces), [spherical categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Spherical.html#TauCeti.SphericalCategory) whose trace is [multiplicative on tensor products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Spherical.html#TauCeti.trace_tensorHom) with no braiding needed, and pivotal functors, which [preserve traces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.trace_map). The one-sided traces are multiplicative only under a hypothesis that scalars act alike on both sides, which a braiding supplies.

Underneath, the dual and double-dual functors were built as strong monoidal functors and shown to be equivalences, along with the monoidal trivializations of the mixed composites `ᘁ(Xᘁ) ≅ X` and `(ᘁX)ᘁ ≅ X` and an explicit-pairing mate API. A symmetric rigid category gets its [canonical pivotal structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Symmetric.html#TauCeti.symmetricPivotalCategory) from the Drinfeld isomorphisms, and its two traces [agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Symmetric.html#TauCeti.leftTrace_eq_rightTrace_of_symmetric), though no spherical instance is registered yet. Finite-dimensional vector spaces are now [rigid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Rigid.html#FGModuleCat.rigidCategory) with algebraic duals on both sides.

Separately, the semisimple strand began: [finite semisimple categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsFiniteSemisimpleCategory), simple isomorphism classes, and multiplicities that [classify objects up to isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/Multiplicity.html#TauCeti.nonempty_iso_iff_simpleMultiplicities_eq), computed as [Hom dimensions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/Finrank.html#TauCeti.finrank_hom_simple_eq_simpleMultiplicities) over an algebraically closed field.

## PivotalSpherical: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `69cd1ab`)

The fusion level arrived in this window, and with it the theorem of Etingof, Nikshych and Ostrik that [the global dimension of a pivotal fusion category is nonzero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Character.html#TauCeti.globalDim_ne_zero) in characteristic zero over an algebraically closed field (TauCeti#20727). The proof follows the roadmap's route: the left dimensions form a [character of the Grothendieck ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Character.html#TauCeti.SplitK0.leftDimChar) with algebraic-integer values, and every complex character of that ring is a [⋆-character](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/Character.html#TauCeti.SplitK0.ringHom_apply_dual), sending the dual of a simple class to the complex conjugate. The [global dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/GlobalDim.html#TauCeti.globalDim) is also [independent of the pivotal structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/GlobalDim.html#TauCeti.globalDim_pivotalCategory_eq).

Underneath this sits the Grothendieck ring as a based ring, with a [basis of simple classes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/GrothendieckRing.html#TauCeti.SplitK0.simpleBasis), [fusion coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/GrothendieckRing.html#TauCeti.fusionCoeff), the duality involution, Frobenius reciprocity and [transitivity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/Rigid.html#TauCeti.exists_fusionCoeff_pos_right), together with Schur's lemma as an [algebra isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/Schur.html#TauCeti.endAlgEquivOfSimple) with `k`. Perron–Frobenius landed [for strictly positive matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/PerronFrobenius/Positive.html#Matrix.exists_mulVec_eq_smul_of_pos), not yet for irreducible or primitive ones, which was enough for [the unique nonnegative character of a transitive based ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/PerronFrobenius/Character.html#Module.Basis.existsUnique_ringHom_nonneg). The [Frobenius–Perron dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/FrobeniusPerron.html#TauCeti.frobeniusPerronDim) is defined from it and shown multiplicative, nonnegative, and zero exactly on zero objects.

Several earlier gaps closed as well. `FDRep k G` now carries its [standard spherical structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Symmetric.html#TauCeti.symmetricSphericalCategory), whose [trace is the linear trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/FDRep/Pivotal.html#FDRep.trace_eq_smul) and whose [quantum dimension is the vector-space dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/FDRep/Pivotal.html#FDRep.quantumDim_eq_smul). Pivotal categories gained the [left-right dual comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/LeftDual.html#TauCeti.leftDualIsoRightDual) and a [monoidal isomorphism of the dual functors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/LeftDual.html#TauCeti.dualFunctorIso). On the synoptic chart, [balanced categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Braided/Balanced.html#TauCeti.BalancedCategory) now [correspond to pivotal structures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/OfBalanced.html#TauCeti.pivotalBalancedEquiv) on a braided rigid category, and the Drinfeld centre became [linear](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Center/Preadditive.html#CategoryTheory.Center.instLinear) with finite biproducts.
