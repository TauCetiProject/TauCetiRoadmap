## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The categorical core is in place: the monoidal double-dual functor, pivotal and spherical categories, and the trace and dimension API (Layers 0 to 2, with two small gaps in Layers 0 and 1), plus the finite-semisimple infrastructure of Layer 3. The `FDRep` example is partial. Nothing at the fusion level has begun: no Grothendieck ring, Perron–Frobenius, Frobenius–Perron dimension, `Vec^ω_G`, gradings, synoptic chart or centre.

### Named results

- **The Freyd–Yetter identity** — in a pivotal category the component of `φ` at `Xᘁ` is the mate of `φ_X⁻¹`, so the historical fourth axiom is a theorem ([`pivotalIsoApp_rightDual`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Basic.html#TauCeti.pivotalIsoApp_rightDual)); the companion [`rightAdjointMate_rightAdjointMate`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Basic.html#TauCeti.rightAdjointMate_rightAdjointMate) writes `fᘁᘁ` as `φ_X⁻¹ ≫ f ≫ φ_Y`.
- **The torsor of pivotal structures** — if a rigid category admits a pivotal structure, the monoidal automorphisms of the identity functor act on its pivotal structures freely and transitively ([`instTorsor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Torsor.html#TauCeti.PivotalCategory.instTorsor)).
- **Multiplicativity of the spherical trace** — in a spherical category `trace (f ⊗ g) = trace f ≫ trace g` with no braiding assumed ([`trace_tensorHom`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Spherical.html#TauCeti.trace_tensorHom)). The one-sided traces are multiplicative under a hypothesis on how scalars act, which holds [in the braided case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Tensor.html#TauCeti.leftTrace_tensorHom_eq_comp_of_braided).
- **Symmetric rigid categories are spherical** — the Drinfeld isomorphisms give a [canonical pivotal structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Symmetric.html#TauCeti.symmetricPivotalCategory) whose left and right traces [agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Symmetric.html#TauCeti.leftTrace_eq_rightTrace_of_symmetric). This is proved, but not yet registered as a `SphericalCategory` instance.
- **Multiplicities classify semisimple objects** — in a finite semisimple category two objects are isomorphic exactly when their simple multiplicities agree ([`nonempty_iso_iff_simpleMultiplicities_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/Multiplicity.html#TauCeti.nonempty_iso_iff_simpleMultiplicities_eq)), and over an algebraically closed field with finite-dimensional Hom spaces the multiplicity is a [Hom dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/Finrank.html#TauCeti.finrank_hom_simple_eq_simpleMultiplicities).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The monoidal double-dual functor** ([`doubleRightDualFunctorMonoidal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Rigid/DualFunctor.html#TauCeti.doubleRightDualFunctorMonoidal)), built through `Functor.opMop` in the shape of the in-flight Mathlib series. The dual functor itself is [an equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Rigid/DualFunctor.html#TauCeti.rightDualFunctorIsEquivalence), which closes the open `Future work` item in Mathlib's `Rigid/Functor.lean`. Everything pivotal is stated against this functor.
- **Pivotal functors** ([`Functor.IsPivotal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Functor.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.IsPivotal)), closed under identity and composition, with braided functors between symmetric categories pivotal for the canonical structures. They preserve left and right traces, dimensions, and the spherical trace.
- **Simple isomorphism classes** ([`SimpleClasses`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Simple/Classes.html#TauCeti.SimpleClasses)), a subtype of the skeleton with the full list of class and representative lemmas and invariance under equivalence. This is the index set the Grothendieck ring and every fusion-level sum will run over.

## The frontier

- **Closing Layers 0 and 1** — state the double left and right dual functors as inverse monoidal autoequivalences, and add `leftDualIsoRightDual` and `dualFunctorIso` from a pivotal structure. All the ingredients are already in place.
- **`FDRep k G` is spherical with the linear trace** — register `RigidCategory (FDRep k G)` from the new `FGModuleCat` left duals, register the symmetric spherical instance, and prove that `trace f` is the linear trace and `dim V = finrank k V`.
- **The fusion scalars and the Grothendieck based ring** — `End (𝟙_ C) ≃ₐ[k] k` under finite-dimensionality, then the based ring free on `SimpleClasses C` with fusion coefficients and the duality involution. Layer 3's multiplicity API is what this needs.
- **Perron–Frobenius** — the theorem for nonnegative and irreducible matrices, and the unique nonnegative character of a transitive based ring. This is independent of everything above and can start now.
- **`Vec^ω_G`** — the degree-3 cocycle predicate, the finitely supported twisted graded category, and the classification of its pivotal structures as a torsor over `Hom(G, kˣ)`. The general torsor from Layer 1 is already available to receive it.
