## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 2, 3, 5, 7 and 10 are done, and the first two steps of the pivot-free theory (squared norms and the global dimension built from them) are in place. Partial: the long road through the Radford isomorphism to nonvanishing without a pivotal structure, pivotal structures on `Vec^ω_G`, and the semisimplicity and dimension of the Drinfeld centre.

### Named results

- **Nonvanishing of the global dimension** — a pivotal fusion category over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero has `dim C ≠ 0` (Etingof–Nikshych–Ostrik) ([`globalDim_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Character.html#TauCeti.globalDim_ne_zero)); the pivot-free form is still open.
- **Müger's theorem** — the Drinfeld centre of a spherical category, with its canonical braiding and inherited pivotal structure, is ribbon ([`Center.ribbonCategory_of_sphericalCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Center/Ribbon.html#CategoryTheory.Center.ribbonCategory_of_sphericalCategory)).
- **Spherical implies ribbon** — in a braided spherical finite semisimple category whose simple objects have scalar endomorphisms over a commutative ring, the induced twist is ribbon, with no simple unit or finiteness of simple classes assumed (Henriques–Penneys–Tener) ([`ribbonCategory_of_sphericalCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Ribbon/OfSpherical.html#TauCeti.ribbonCategory_of_sphericalCategory)).
- **The canonical pivotal structure of a pseudo-unitary category** — a pseudo-unitary complex fusion category has exactly one pivotal structure whose left dimensions are the Frobenius–Perron dimensions, and it is spherical ([`IsPseudoUnitary.existsUnique_pivotalCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/PseudoUnitary/Existence.html#TauCeti.IsPseudoUnitary.existsUnique_pivotalCategory)). Pseudo-unitarity is still defined relative to a chosen pivotal structure.
- **Perron–Frobenius for primitive matrices** — the spectral radius of a primitive real matrix is positive and strictly exceeds the modulus of every other complex eigenvalue ([`Matrix.IsPrimitive.norm_lt_of_hasEigenvalue`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/PerronFrobenius/Primitive.html#Matrix.IsPrimitive.norm_lt_of_hasEigenvalue)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The [squared norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/SquaredNorm.html#TauCeti.squaredNorm)** of an object with respect to any `a : X ≅ Xᘁᘁ`, independent of `a` for objects with scalar endomorphisms, and the [global dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/SquaredNorm.html#TauCeti.normGlobalDim) summed from it, which [matches the pivotal one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/SquaredNorm.html#TauCeti.normGlobalDim_eq_globalDim). These are what OcneanuRigidity and the averaging identity in the centre consume.
- **The [Frobenius–Perron dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/FrobeniusPerron.html#TauCeti.frobeniusPerronDim)** on the Grothendieck based ring, which bounds every complex character of that ring and is what pseudo-unitarity compares `dim C` against.
- **The [induction functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Center/Induction/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.Center.induction)** `X ↦ ⨁ S ⊗ X ⊗ Sᘁ` into the centre, built from dual bases of morphisms, [right adjoint to the forgetful functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Center/Induction/Adjunction.html#CategoryTheory.Center.forgetInductionAdjunction) and [faithful](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Center/Induction/Faithful.html#CategoryTheory.Center.induction_faithful). This is the route to the centre's semisimplicity.
