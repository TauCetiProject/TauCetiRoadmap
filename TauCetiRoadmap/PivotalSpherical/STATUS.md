## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The pivotal, spherical and fusion core is in place: duals, traces, the Grothendieck based ring, Frobenius–Perron dimensions, pseudo-unitarity relative to a pivotal structure, the universal grading group, and a rigid, abelian, ribbon Drinfeld centre. Layer 7 is done. Still open: the commutative-ring forms of Layers 2 and 3, the pivot-free theory of Layer 6, pivotal structures on `Vec^ω_G`, and the second half of induction into the centre.

### Named results

- **Nonvanishing of the global dimension** — a pivotal fusion category over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero has `dim C ≠ 0` (Etingof–Nikshych–Ostrik) ([`globalDim_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Character.html#TauCeti.globalDim_ne_zero)).
- **Müger's theorem** — the Drinfeld centre of a spherical category, with its canonical braiding and inherited pivotal structure, is ribbon ([`Center.ribbonCategory_of_sphericalCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Center/Ribbon.html#CategoryTheory.Center.ribbonCategory_of_sphericalCategory)).
- **The canonical pivotal structure of a pseudo-unitary category** — a pseudo-unitary complex fusion category has exactly one pivotal structure whose left dimensions are the Frobenius–Perron dimensions, and it is spherical ([`IsPseudoUnitary.existsUnique_pivotalCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/PseudoUnitary/Existence.html#TauCeti.IsPseudoUnitary.existsUnique_pivotalCategory)).
- **Perron–Frobenius for primitive matrices** — if a primitive real matrix has a strictly positive eigenvector for `r`, every other point of its complex spectrum has modulus less than `r` ([`Matrix.IsPrimitive.norm_lt_of_mem_spectrum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/PerronFrobenius/Primitive.html#Matrix.IsPrimitive.norm_lt_of_mem_spectrum)); the irreducible case supplies such an eigenvector ([`Matrix.IsIrreducible.exists_mulVec_eq_smul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/PerronFrobenius/Irreducible.html#Matrix.IsIrreducible.exists_mulVec_eq_smul)).
- **Frobenius–Schur indicators of group representations** — in `FDRep k G` with its standard spherical structure, the categorical `n`-th indicator is `|G|⁻¹ ∑ χ_V(gⁿ)` whenever `|G|` is invertible in `k` ([`FDRep.frobeniusSchurIndicator_fdRep`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/FDRep/FrobeniusSchur.html#FDRep.frobeniusSchurIndicator_fdRep)), and for simple objects `ν₂` lies in `{1, 0, −1}` in any pivotal category ([`frobeniusSchurIndicator_two_eq_one_or_eq_zero_or_eq_neg_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/FrobeniusSchur/Simple.html#TauCeti.frobeniusSchurIndicator_two_eq_one_or_eq_zero_or_eq_neg_one)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The [Frobenius–Perron dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/FrobeniusPerron.html#TauCeti.frobeniusPerronDim)** on the Grothendieck based ring, which bounds every complex character of that ring and is what pseudo-unitarity compares `dim C` against.
- **The [universal grading group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Grading/Universal.html#TauCeti.universalGradingGroup)**, whose characters are [the monoidal automorphisms of the identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Grading/Classification.html#TauCeti.monoidalAutCharacterEquiv). This turns the torsor of pivotal structures into a concrete parametrization.
- **The [induction functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Center/Induction/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.Center.induction)** `X ↦ ⨁ S ⊗ X ⊗ Sᘁ` into the centre, with its half-braiding built from [dual bases of morphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Semisimple/DualBasis.html#Module.Basis.homDual), [right adjoint to the forgetful functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Center/Induction/Adjunction.html#CategoryTheory.Center.forgetInductionAdjunction). This is the route to the centre's semisimplicity.
