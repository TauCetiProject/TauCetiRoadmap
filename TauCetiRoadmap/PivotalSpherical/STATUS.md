- **The global dimension does not depend on the pivotal structure** ([`globalDim_pivotalCategory_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/GlobalDim.html#TauCeti.globalDim_pivotalCategory_eq)), since `dim_L S · dim_R S` is the same for any two structures on a simple `S`.
- **Traces in `FDRep k G` are linear traces** — under the [standard spherical structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Pivotal/Symmetric.html#TauCeti.symmetricSphericalCategory), the categorical trace of an equivariant map is its [ordinary trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/FDRep/Pivotal.html#FDRep.trace_eq_smul), so the quantum dimension is `finrank k V`. Earlier results (the Freyd–Yetter identity, the torsor of pivotal structures, multiplicativity of the spherical trace) stand.

## The frontier

- **Closing Layer 0** — state that the double left and double right dual functors are mutually inverse monoidal autoequivalences; the mixed trivializations already supply the ingredients.
- **Perron–Frobenius for irreducible and primitive matrices** — Perron root as spectral radius, simplicity, and a positive eigenvector. The fusion-level `FPdim` does not need it, but Layer 5 asks for it.
- **Pseudo-unitarity** — `|dim_L X| ≤ FPdim X` over `ℂ`, the property `dim C = FPdim C`, and the unique spherical pivotal structure with `dim_L = FPdim`, using the Layer 1 torsor for uniqueness.
