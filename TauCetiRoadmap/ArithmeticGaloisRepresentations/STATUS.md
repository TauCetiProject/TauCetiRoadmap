### Named results

- **The image algebra as a form of `End(V)`**: the algebra generated by a representation's image over its character field `k(ρ)` is central simple. When the image spans `End_K(V)` it is a [`k(ρ)`-form of `End_K(V)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ImageAlgebra.html#Representation.imageAlgebraBaseChangeAlgEquiv). Its [Schur index divides the dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ImageAlgebra.html#Representation.schurIndex_dvd_finrank), and its [Brauer class is trivial exactly when the Schur index is one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ImageAlgebra.html#Representation.brauerClass_eq_one_iff_schurIndex_eq_one).
- **Burnside's theorem**: over an algebraically closed field, the image of a finite-dimensional irreducible representation [spans all endomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Irreducible.html#Representation.span_range_eq_top_of_isIrreducible).
- **Skolem–Noether over a local ring**: every unital algebra endomorphism of `M_n(R)`, for `R` commutative local, is [conjugation by an invertible matrix](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/InnerAut.html#AlgHom.exists_innerAut_eq_of_isLocalRing), unique up to a unit.
- **The Haar measure of `GL_n`**: over a local ring with residue field of `q` elements, the invertible matrices have [Haar probability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Haar/MatrixUnits.html#Matrix.measureReal_setOf_isUnit) `∏_{i=1}^n (1 − q^{−i})`. A companion result says nonzero polynomials have [Haar-null zero sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Haar/ZeroLocus.html#Matrix.haarProb_setOf_eval_algebraMap_eq_zero) on compact open matrix groups.
- **Primes with prescribed quadratic residue symbols**: for square-class independent `d₁, …, dₙ` and any signs, the primes with `(dᵢ/p) = εᵢ` have [Dirichlet density `2^{−n}`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Legendre/Density.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.hasDirichletDensity_setOf_jacobiSym_eq).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Operations on continuous representations.** The [tensor product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/ContinuousRep/Operations.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.tprod), dual, Hom, twist and restriction are available, with [induction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/ContinuousRep/Induced.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.ind) from open subgroups and Shapiro's lemma on invariants.
- **The Frobenius polynomial.** It is [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GaloisRepresentation/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.frobCharpoly) for unramified local representations, is independent of the Frobenius lift, and is compatible with sums, duals and coefficient change. It is the input recognition needs, so far only locally.
- **Decomposition and inertia groups in `G_F`.** These are [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.GaloisRep.decompositionGroup) at maximal ideals of the integral closure of `𝓞_F` in `F̄`, together with wild inertia and arithmetic Frobenius elements, which [form one coset of inertia](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Frobenius.html#IsArithFrobAt.iff_mul_inv_mem_inertia).

## The frontier

- **Semisimplification and Brauer–Nesbitt (§§1.7–1.8).** Nothing has landed. These are the remaining prerequisite for recognition, since Layer 5 already has the dense-subset step: [characteristic polynomials agreeing on a dense set agree everywhere](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Instances/Matrix.html#Matrix.charpoly_eq_of_dense).

- **Global ramification (§2.2).** What remains: `IsUnramifiedAt` for a global representation, the ramification set and its finiteness for open kernel, and `IsUnramifiedOutside`. The local predicates and decomposition groups it needs are in place.
- **Weil–Deligne representations and local factors (§§2.4–2.5).** None of this has begun. It is the gateway to the conductors of Layer 3 and to Tate-curve monodromy in §6.3.
- **Swan and Artin conductors (Layer 3).** The first steps are §3.0's identification `|G_0| = e` over a perfect residue field and the definition of the Swan conductor. The supplier's abelian conductor exponents already exist.
