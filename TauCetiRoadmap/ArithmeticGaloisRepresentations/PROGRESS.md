## ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-24 (`55cccde` to `d25d526`)

This is the first report on this roadmap, and its most substantial new mathematics is the descent machinery of Layer 5. For a representation with character field `k(ρ)`, the [image algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ImageAlgebra.html#Representation.imageAlgebra) it generates over `k(ρ)` is central simple and, when the image spans all endomorphisms, a [`k(ρ)`-form of `End(V)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ImageAlgebra.html#Representation.imageAlgebraBaseChangeAlgEquiv); its [Schur index divides the dimension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/ImageAlgebra.html#Representation.schurIndex_dvd_finrank), and its Brauer class is trivial exactly when that index is one (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/3929). On the way came [Burnside's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Irreducible.html#Representation.span_range_eq_top_of_isIrreducible), in the form that an irreducible image over an algebraically closed field spans all endomorphisms, and [Skolem–Noether over a local ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/InnerAut.html#AlgHom.exists_innerAut_eq_of_isLocalRing). The layer also gained the [rank-two trace identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Trace/FinTwo.html#Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.det_mul_trace_inv_mul_sub_trace_mul_trace_add_trace_mul_fin_two), the [Haar measure of `GL_n` over a local ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Haar/MatrixUnits.html#Matrix.measureReal_setOf_isUnit), `∏ (1 − q^{−i})`, and the [density of primes with prescribed quadratic residue symbols](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Legendre/Density.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.hasDirichletDensity_setOf_jacobiSym_eq).

Most of the remaining work builds Layer 1's category of continuous representations: morphisms with automatic continuity, the [determinant character](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/Determinant.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.det), tensor products, duals, twists and restriction, [induction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/ContinuousRep/Induced.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.ind) from open subgroups with Shapiro's lemma on invariants, [reduction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/Reduction.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.reduction) with marked residual lifts, and [Frobenius twists](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/CoefficientTwist.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.frobTwist). Symplectic matrices now [lift along adically complete quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Symplectic/AdicLift.html#TauCeti.GLSymplectic.map_quotient_mk_surjective_of_isAdicComplete). Layer 2 gained [decomposition groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/AbsoluteGaloisGroup/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.GaloisRep.decompositionGroup) and wild inertia at the primes of `F̄` above a number field `F`, Frobenius elements as [one coset of inertia](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Frobenius.html#IsArithFrobAt.iff_mul_inv_mem_inertia), and [unramified](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GaloisRepresentation/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.IsUnramified) representations with a [Frobenius polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GaloisRepresentation/Unramified.html#TauCeti.ContinuousRep.frobCharpoly) independent of the lift.

What is missing shapes the next steps. Semisimplification and Brauer–Nesbitt have not landed, so the recognition theorem is still unproved: the dense-subset result shows only that characteristic polynomials agreeing on a dense set agree everywhere. Affine quotients `X//G`, with their universal property and the conjugation quotient of an affine group, are a first step toward §1.11's reductive quotients; finite generation is not yet proved.
