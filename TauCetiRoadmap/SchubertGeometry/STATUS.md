### Named results

- **Littelmann's crystal of integral paths** — every operator-closed set of integral paths is a normal crystal, with weight the endpoint and string lengths ε_i = −min H_i and φ_i = H_i(1) − min H_i ([`normalCrystalOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/Crystal/Basic.html#TauCeti.PLPath.normalCrystalOn)). Conversely, the crystal-admissible sets are exactly these ([`isCrystalSet_iff_subset_isIntegralPath_and_closed`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/Crystal/Basic.html#TauCeti.PLPath.isCrystalSet_iff_subset_isIntegralPath_and_closed)).
- **Concatenation is the crystal tensor product** — concatenating paths is a strict morphism from Kashiwara's tensor square of the integral-path crystal, compatible with the associator ([`mulHom`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/Crystal/Operations.html#TauCeti.PLPath.mulHom)); reversing them is a strict isomorphism from the dual crystal ([`reverseIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/Crystal/Operations.html#TauCeti.PLPath.reverseIso)).
- **Lowering preserves LS paths** — Littelmann's f_i sends a Lakshmibai–Seshadri path of dominant shape to another one ([`exists_toPLPath_eq_of_lower_eq_some`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/Lower.html#TauCeti.LSPath.exists_toPLPath_eq_of_lower_eq_some)). The same statement for raising is still missing.
- **Littelmann's a-chain criterion** — for comparable ends in W/W_λ, a saturated a-chain exists exactly when the denominator of a divides the interval gcd d_λ(x, y), and then every saturated chain is one ([`exists_isAChain_iff_den_dvd_intervalBondGcd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/IntervalBondGcd.html#TauCeti.exists_isAChain_iff_den_dvd_intervalBondGcd)).
- **Demazure's formula for the longest element** — D_{w₀}(e^λ) is Weyl's quotient ([`weylDenominator_mul_prod_map_demazureOperator_single`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/Demazure/LongestElement.html#RootPairing.weylDenominator_mul_prod_map_demazureOperator_single)). The roadmap now treats this, with the braid relations and word independence, as supplied input: what S3.4 owns is its geometric reading as a character of sections.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [interval bond gcd](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/IntervalBondGcd.html#TauCeti.intervalBondGcd) d_λ(x, y), chain-independent by proof. It is the arithmetic S7 will compare with Chevalley divisor multiplicities.
- The [Schubert rank locus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/RankLocus.html#Module.Grassmannian.schemeSchubertLocus), a closed subscheme of the Grassmannian over an affine base cut out by minors against a basis flag. Over a field its cells are [in bijection with affine space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Basis/Flag.html#Module.Basis.flagCellEquiv) through the reduced echelon basis.
- [Frobenius splittings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Frobenius/Splitting.html#TauCeti.FrobeniusSplitting) of rings, with compatible ideals and reduced quotients, and the affine Cartier trace. They are the ring-level starting point for splitting on root charts.

## The frontier

- **Raising stability and B(λ) (S4.2–4.3)** — prove that e_i preserves LS paths of dominant shape. The new dictionary then makes them a normal crystal. What remains after that is connectedness (Littelmann's Corollary 3) and the central-transport isomorphisms with their composition and inverse laws.
- **The path character and tensor-component theorems (S4.4)** — the isomorphism theorem for dominant paths, B(λ)*B(μ) ≅ B(λ)⊗B(μ) with its component decomposition, and Δ·char B(λ) = J(e^λ). The ambient concatenation morphism exists. Everything else waits on B(λ).
- **Fixed-chain simplices (S4.5)** — generalize the S₃ complex to arbitrary direction chains, with face identifications and the integral break test.
