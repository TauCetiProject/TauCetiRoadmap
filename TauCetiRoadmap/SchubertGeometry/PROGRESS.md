## SchubertGeometry: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`83eb372` to `273ea28`)

The first piece of the roadmap's finite-type path engine (S4.1) landed: an abstract category of normal crystals over a root datum with a base. A [normal crystal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Crystal/Basic.html#TauCeti.NormalCrystal) carries a weight map, partial raising and lowering operators and natural string lengths satisfying Kashiwara's axioms with normality. It comes with the string identities, for instance that raising n times is defined exactly when n ≤ ε_i ([`isSome_eIter_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Crystal/Basic.html#TauCeti.NormalCrystal.isSome_eIter_iff)), and the observation that a crystal is determined by its weights and raising operators ([`ext`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Crystal/Basic.html#TauCeti.NormalCrystal.ext)). It also has [strict morphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Crystal/Basic.html#TauCeti.NormalCrystal.StrictHom) and the dual crystal (TauCeti#18632).

The [tensor product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Crystal/Tensor.html#TauCeti.NormalCrystal.tensor) followed, with Kashiwara's tie convention, the one under which Littelmann's concatenation of paths corresponds to tensoring. It comes with Kashiwara's max formulas for the string lengths ([`tensor_ε_eq_max`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Crystal/Tensor.html#TauCeti.NormalCrystal.tensor_ε_eq_max)), an [associator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Crystal/Tensor.html#TauCeti.NormalCrystal.tensorAssoc) with explicit inverse, and the isomorphism [(B₁ ⊗ B₂)^∨ ≅ B₂^∨ ⊗ B₁^∨](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Crystal/Tensor.html#TauCeti.NormalCrystal.dualTensor) (TauCeti#19015). Connected components and transport along isomorphisms, which S4.1 also asks for, are not there yet, and no crystal of paths has been built.

## SchubertGeometry: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

Littelmann's root operators on piecewise-linear paths landed (S4.2). The [lowering operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/RootOperator.html#TauCeti.PLPath.lower) f_α is built from the running height ⟨π(t), α^∨⟩ with the last/first cut-time conventions, and the [raising operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/RootOperator.html#TauCeti.PLPath.raise) e_α is its conjugate by path reversal. Lowering is defined exactly when the final height exceeds the minimum by at least one, and raising exactly when the minimum is at most −1. Both [do not depend on the presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/RootOperator.html#TauCeti.PLPath.map_normalize_listLower_eq), shift the endpoint by ∓α, and are [partial inverses](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/LSPath/PartialInverse.html#TauCeti.PLPath.lower_eq_some_iff) of each other (TauCeti#19612, TauCeti#20592). Paths have not yet been assembled into a normal crystal: the string-length identities ε = −min H and φ = H(1) − min H are still to be proved.

On the Weyl-group side, the reflection test on W/W_λ that S4.3 asks for first is now in place. The stabilizer of a dominant weight [is a standard parabolic subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/ParabolicQuotient.html#RootPairing.parabolic_wallIndices_eq_stabilizer), its [cosets correspond to orbit weights](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/ParabolicQuotient.html#TauCeti.inv_mul_mem_parabolic_wallIndices_iff), and for a positive root β the sign of ⟨uλ, β^∨⟩ decides whether s_β u lies strictly below u, strictly above it, or in the same coset (TauCeti#19638). On top of this, each cover in W/W_λ has a [unique positive root label](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/CoverBond.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isPos_reflection_smul_eq_of_minimalCosetRepCovBy), and its [bond](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/ReflectionBond.html#RootPairing.reflectionBond) is [a positive integer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/CoverBond.html#TauCeti.exists_nat_reflectionBond_eq_of_minimalCosetRepCovBy) when λ is integral. Supporting Coxeter-group work covers minimal coset representatives (unique, and Bruhat-least in their coset), the [lifting property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Coxeter/Bruhat.html#CoxeterSystem.BruhatLE.bruhatLE_mul_simple), and the fact that covers in a parabolic quotient come from reflections. The chain gcd d_λ and the a-chain criterion are not started.

Finally, the integral [Demazure operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/Demazure.html#RootPairing.demazureOperator) D_i on ℤ[X] arrived, defined by finite signed root strings. It satisfies the [localization identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/Demazure.html#RootPairing.one_sub_single_neg_root_mul_demazureOperator) (1 − e^{−α_i}) D_i f = f − e^{−α_i} s_i f and is [idempotent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/Weyl/Demazure.html#RootPairing.demazureOperator_demazureOperator). This is the combinatorial part of S3.3–4 only. Braid relations, word-independence and any link to cohomology on ℙ¹ or on Schubert varieties are not yet there.
