# Four-dimensional Seiberg–Witten theory

Construct the Spinᶜ representation, monopole equations and their gauge quotient on a closed oriented smooth four-manifold, followed by compactness, universal regularity, determinant orientations, integral point and loop insertions, chamber invariance, and the geometric wall-crossing construction over the reducible Picard torus. Keep the full Spinᶜ structure, including torsion labels, throughout. The invariant takes values in integral alternating forms on H₁(X;ℤ)/torsion; its point-only component is not its entire content.

Use the existing principal/vector bundle, ordinary cohomology, Hodge, Sobolev, Dirac, elliptic/index and K-theory carriers. This area owns their rank-four Spinᶜ applications and nonlinear abelian gauge analysis. FourManifolds F5 owns SU(2) anti-self-dual gauge theory; its effective μ₂ quotient, bubbling compactification and reducible off-diagonal continuation theorem are distinct. SpinGeometry owns generic Clifford/Dirac geometry and IndexTheory owns the scalar analytical/topological index theorem. The constructions here do not include three-dimensional monopole Floer theory, Taubes's large-parameter/vortex correspondence, weighted pseudoholomorphic torus counts or mapping-torus zeta formulas.

## Conventions and prerequisite boundaries

For global statements X is a nonempty connected compact boundaryless oriented smooth real four-manifold, with native Mathlib manifold carriers, Hausdorffness, second countability and a smooth Riemannian metric g. Local algebraic statements use an oriented real inner-product space of dimension four. Local continuation statements use connected open smooth four-manifolds and require no compactness. All complex inner products are conjugate-linear in the first argument. Use increasing wedge monomials of an orthonormal real coframe as the orthonormal exterior basis, Clifford relation c(v)c(w)+c(w)c(v)=−2⟨v,w⟩ and c(v)*=−c(v).

Fix Γ=−c(e₁)c(e₂)c(e₃)c(e₄); W⁺ and W⁻ are its +1 and −1 eigenspaces. For an imaginary determinant connection A use ∇=d+A, active Spinᶜ gauges (A,ψ)↦(A−2u⁻¹du,uψ), and c₁(L)_ℝ=[iF_A/(2π)]. The scalar Hodge Laplacian is Δ=d*d≥0. The two-form action is c₂(α)=Σ_(i<j)α(eᵢ,eⱼ)c(eᵢ)c(eⱼ), equivalently half the ordered double sum. Frame orientation, chirality, target-factor order and all associated-line conventions are part of the statements.
