## SeibergWittenTheory: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-24 (`c65617c` to `fe8ca8b`)

The first window of this roadmap brought the pointwise algebra behind the curvature equation. The rank-one quadratic endomorphism [`quadraticEnd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/QuadraticEnd.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.quadraticEnd), Q₀(ψ) = ψψ* − (|ψ|²/2)·Id, now has essentially the full SW2.2 list: it is trace free in complex rank two, self-adjoint, invariant under unit phases, and has ψ as an eigenvector. It also has real polarization, sharp norm bounds and its real Fréchet derivative φψ* + ψφ* − Re⟨ψ,φ⟩·Id, proved in the strict sense in [`hasStrictFDerivAt_quadraticEnd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/QuadraticEnd.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.hasStrictFDerivAt_quadraticEnd) (TauCeti#19139). Alongside it came the real subspace of [trace-free self-adjoint endomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Trace/Free.html#TauCeti.traceFreeSelfAdjoint), of dimension n² − 1, and the trace pairings that make T ↦ 2Tψ the formal adjoint of the derivative of Q₀ ([`trace_quadraticEndPolar_mul_of_mem_traceFreeSelfAdjoint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/TracePairing.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.trace_quadraticEndPolar_mul_of_mem_traceFreeSelfAdjoint)).

On the Clifford side, an oriented four-space now has a [Hodge star](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HodgeStar.html#Orientation.hodgeStar), characterized by ⟨⋆x, y⟩ = vol(x ∧ y), and the [chirality element](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Chirality.html#CliffordAlgebra.chirality) Γ, with Γ² = 1, anticommutation with generators and [frame independence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Chirality.html#CliffordAlgebra.chirality_eq) (TauCeti#19436). The [self-duality relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Chirality.html#CliffordAlgebra.bivectorExterior_hodgeStar) c₂(⋆α) = Γc₂(α) follows. It gives the vanishing of anti-self-dual forms on positive spinors, and the anticommutator of two two-forms. Self-dual forms act faithfully on nonzero positive spinors. Sign rules for products of orthogonal Clifford basis monomials support these proofs.

All of this is linear algebra on a single four-space. No Spinᶜ representation, bundle, Dirac operator or two-form quadratic map q = C⁻¹Q₀ has been constructed yet.

## SeibergWittenTheory: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-13 (`fe8ca8b` to `aa6e43e`)

The half-spin representations of Spinᶜ(4) landed this window. The group [`spinCGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup), the quotient of Spin × U(1) by (−1, −1), now acts on each chirality eigenspace by [s, z] ↦ zρ(s). The action is unitary and Clifford-equivariant, and has [determinant z²](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/HalfSpin.html#CliffordAlgebra.det_spinCGroupHalfSpinRep) (TauCeti#23487). It also comes as a [smooth representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/HalfSpin.html#Orientation.realSpinCHalfSpinSmoothHom) of compact Spinᶜ(4) (TauCeti#30117). The map to SO × U(1) is shown to be a [two-sheeted quotient covering](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup.isQuotientCoveringMap_toSpecialOrthogonal_prod_det). The SU(2) factor labels are fixed against the Γ convention: the [second factor acts on W⁺](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/Chirality.html#CliffordAlgebra.halfSpinRep_one_realSpinGroupEquiv_eq_one_iff_specialUnitary). On the maximal torus the [character of W⁺ − W⁻](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/TorusCharacter.html#CliffordAlgebra.trace_spinCGroupHalfSpinRep_one_sub_neg_one_mk_spinTorus) is z(w₁ − w₁⁻¹)(w₂ − w₂⁻¹).

The two-form quadratic map [q = C⁻¹Q₀](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/QuadraticTwoForm.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.quadraticTwoForm) arrived with its pointwise identities: [|q(ψ)|² = |ψ|⁴/8](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/QuadraticTwoForm.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.norm_quadraticTwoForm_sq), C(q(ψ))ψ = (|ψ|²/2)ψ, phase invariance, a derivative that vanishes along iψ, and [Spinᶜ equivariance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/Naturality.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.quadraticTwoForm_positiveHalfSpinorsEquiv). The adjoint τ = C* satisfies C*C = 4 and CC* = 4 times the trace-free projection. On manifolds there are now [smooth self-dual forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/SelfDual.html#TauCeti.SmoothForm.selfDualForms) and the [abelian deformation operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/AbelianDeformation.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.abelianDeformationOperator) T a = (−2d*a, (da)⁺). Its kernel is the closed and coclosed one-forms, and its range is annihilated exactly by the closed functions and closed self-dual forms. Its ellipticity and its index b₁ − 1 − b₂⁺ are not yet proved.

Smaller pieces went into later layers. Closed imaginary one-forms with periods in 2πiℤ [are logarithmic derivatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/LogDeriv.html#TauCeti.exists_mlogDeriv_coe_eq) of circle-valued maps, and Δ + V is [coercive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Sobolev/Schrodinger.html#TauCeti.Manifold.SobolevH1.isCoercive_dirichletForm_add_potentialForm) on H¹. On the cohomology side there are integral cup products and alternating insertion forms, with a [half-integrality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/InsertionForm.html#TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.two_dvd_cupInsertionForm_two) result for characteristic classes. Spinor bundles, connections, Dirac operators and Sobolev gauge groups have not been started.
