## SeibergWittenTheory: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-24 (`c65617c` to `fe8ca8b`)

The first window of this roadmap brought the pointwise algebra behind the curvature equation. The rank-one quadratic endomorphism [`quadraticEnd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/QuadraticEnd.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.quadraticEnd), Q₀(ψ) = ψψ* − (|ψ|²/2)·Id, now has essentially the full SW2.2 list: it is trace free in complex rank two, self-adjoint, invariant under unit phases, and has ψ as an eigenvector. It also has real polarization, sharp norm bounds and its real Fréchet derivative φψ* + ψφ* − Re⟨ψ,φ⟩·Id, proved in the strict sense in [`hasStrictFDerivAt_quadraticEnd`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/QuadraticEnd.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.hasStrictFDerivAt_quadraticEnd) (TauCeti#19139). Alongside it came the real subspace of [trace-free self-adjoint endomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Trace/Free.html#TauCeti.traceFreeSelfAdjoint), of dimension n² − 1, and the trace pairings that make T ↦ 2Tψ the formal adjoint of the derivative of Q₀ ([`trace_quadraticEndPolar_mul_of_mem_traceFreeSelfAdjoint`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/TracePairing.html#TauCeti.SeibergWitten.trace_quadraticEndPolar_mul_of_mem_traceFreeSelfAdjoint)).

On the Clifford side, an oriented four-space now has a [Hodge star](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HodgeStar.html#Orientation.hodgeStar), characterized by ⟨⋆x, y⟩ = vol(x ∧ y), and the [chirality element](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Chirality.html#CliffordAlgebra.chirality) Γ, with Γ² = 1, anticommutation with generators and [frame independence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Chirality.html#CliffordAlgebra.chirality_eq) (TauCeti#19436). The [self-duality relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Chirality.html#CliffordAlgebra.bivectorExterior_hodgeStar) c₂(⋆α) = Γc₂(α) follows. It gives the vanishing of anti-self-dual forms on positive spinors, and the anticommutator of two two-forms. Self-dual forms act faithfully on nonzero positive spinors. Sign rules for products of orthogonal Clifford basis monomials support these proofs.

All of this is linear algebra on a single four-space. No Spinᶜ representation, bundle, Dirac operator or two-form quadratic map q = C⁻¹Q₀ has been constructed yet.
