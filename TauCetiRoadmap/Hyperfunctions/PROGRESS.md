## Hyperfunctions: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`8827df0` to `273ea28`)

The first piece of HF0 has landed: sheaf cohomology with closed supports, for sheaves of abelian groups. The group [`supportedH`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/SupportedCohomology.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.supportedH) H_Sⁿ(W, F) is defined as the relative cohomology of the inclusion W ∖ S ⊆ W, that is, Ext out of the cokernel of the map of free abelian sheaves, the construction the roadmap prescribes (TauCeti#18807). It comes with the facts one expects. In degree zero it is the sections supported in S ([`supportedHZeroEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/SupportedCohomology.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.supportedHZeroEquiv)). The long exact sequence of the pair gives the support sequence, as in `exact_toH'_cohomologyPresheaf_map`. A flasque sheaf has no supported cohomology in positive degrees. Excision ([`supportedHExcisionEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/SupportedCohomology.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.supportedHExcisionEquiv)) says that restricting to a smaller open which still contains S ∩ W is an isomorphism. Underneath all of this sits an identification of Mathlib's sheaf cohomology with Ext out of the free abelian sheaf, `H'AddEquivExt`.

For HF1, one-dimensional hyperfunctions now exist as the presheaf U ↦ O(ℂ ∖ ℝ)/O(W(U)) on ℝ ([`oneDimBoundaryPresheaf`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Hyperfunction/OneDimensional.html#TauCeti.Hyperfunction.oneDimBoundaryPresheaf)). Its restriction maps are surjective, and it is [separated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Hyperfunction/OneDimensional.html#TauCeti.Hyperfunction.oneDimBoundaryPresheaf_isSeparated), because a function on ℂ ∖ ℝ extends across a union of real opens exactly when it extends across each one. Gluing, which needs a Cousin-type vanishing of H¹ for holomorphic functions, is not proved, so this is not yet a sheaf.

What is missing: the supported theory has only abelian-group coefficients so far, with no complex-module version and no sheafified local cohomology sheaves. Nothing has landed for HF2 through HF5.

## Hyperfunctions: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`273ea28` to `93d1fe5`)

HF0 gained its local cohomology sheaves and the first theorem built on them. The sheaf 𝓗_Sⁿ(F) is defined as the sheafification of W ↦ H_Sⁿ(W, F) ([`supportedHSheaf`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/LocalCohomology.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.supportedHSheaf)). It is supported in S, it equals the sheaf of supported sections Γ_S(F) in degree zero, and it vanishes in positive degrees for flasque F. On top of this sits **support truncation** ([`subsingleton_supportedH_of_isZero_supportedHSheaf`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/LocalCohomology.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.subsingleton_supportedH_of_isZero_supportedHSheaf)): if 𝓗_S^q(F) = 0 for all q < n, then H_S^q(W, F) = 0 for all q < n and every open W. This is the step that will turn local purity into global vanishing (TauCeti#20020). The supporting work includes the long exact sequence of a triple of opens and the long exact sequence of supports for closed Z ⊆ S. **FlasqueLocality** is also done ([`map_surjective_of_locally_flasque`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Sheaves/Flasque/Locality.html#TopCat.Sheaf.map_surjective_of_locally_flasque)): if restrictions are surjective inside some neighbourhood of each point, they are surjective everywhere. The proof is the Zorn argument the roadmap asks for, and it covers sheaves of modules as well as sheaves of sets.

The first pieces of HF2's analytic preparation also landed. Every compact subset of ℝⁿ ⊆ ℂⁿ is [polynomially convex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/PolynomialHull.html#IsCompact.isPolynomiallyConvex_of_forall_im_eq_zero), and a compact polynomially convex set has [polynomial polyhedron neighbourhoods](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/PolynomialHull.html#TauCeti.IsPolynomiallyConvex.exists_subset_interior_polynomialPolyhedron) inside any open set containing it. Every open set in a proper real normed space has a [filled compact exhaustion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Exhaustion.html#IsOpen.exists_isFilledIn_compact_exhaustion), which is the opening step of the harmonic Runge route to Malgrange's theorem.

Still missing: everything is still stated for abelian-group coefficients only, with no `ModuleCat ℂ` version. The degenerate edge comparison is not there. The polyhedra are not yet shown to have Stein interiors, and nothing new happened in HF1.
