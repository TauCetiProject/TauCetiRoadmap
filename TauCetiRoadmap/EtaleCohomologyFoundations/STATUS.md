## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The compactification object layer (§G.1) appears complete, and the core of §F.0.2 is done: finite étale schemes over a connected scheme form a Galois category, with fundamental groups and étale paths. Nagata existence (§G.2) holds only over an affine base. The first étale-sheaf, inertia, lattice, finite-field and trace sections are partial. Everything from base change and finiteness through adic realization, duality and perverse sheaves has not begun.

### Named results

- **The Galois category of finite étale covers** — over a connected scheme, finite étale schemes [form a Galois category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/Category.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.instGaloisCategoryOfConnectedSpaceCarrierCarrierCommRingCat) in which every geometric fibre functor is a fibre functor, and [any two geometric points are joined by an étale path](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/Category.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.nonempty_fiber_iso).
- **The fundamental group of a field** — for a separable closure `Ω` of `K`, the automorphism group of the geometric fibre functor [is `Gal(Ω/K)` as a topological group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.galContinuousMulEquivAutFiber).
- **Affine Nagata compactification** — [a finite-type morphism between affine schemes has a dense compactification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/ProjectiveClosure.html#TauCeti.nonempty_denseCompactification_of_isAffine), given by the [projective closure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/ProjectiveClosure.html#TauCeti.DenseCompactification.projectiveClosure) of an affine presentation.
- **The stable-lattice criterion** — over a nonarchimedean local field, [a subgroup of `GL(V)` stabilizes a lattice exactly when its closure is compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/StableLattice.html#Subgroup.isCompact_closure_iff_exists_stable_lattice). Jointly continuous representations of compact groups also have [integral models](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/IntegralModel.html#TauCeti.GaloisLattice.IntegralModel.nonempty).
- **Decomposition modulo inertia** — when the fibres of `Y → X` on field-valued points are `G`-orbits, [`D_y/I_y` is the automorphism group of the residue extension `κ(y)/κ(x)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupAction/Inertia.html#TauCeti.SchemeInertia.decompositionQuotientInertiaEquiv).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [Dense replacement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/DenseReplacement/Basic.html#TauCeti.Compactification.denseReplacementAdjunction) replaces the ambient scheme by the schematic closure of the interior. It is right adjoint to the inclusion of dense compactifications, and its base-change comparison is [invertible exactly when density survives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/DenseReplacement/Basic.html#TauCeti.Compactification.isIso_denseReplacementBaseChange_iff). It returns nondense compactifications to the dense, thin category.
- [Étale stalks](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale/ModuleSheaf.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.etaleStalk) of sheaves of `Λ`-modules at geometric points. They are exact and [jointly conservative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale/ModuleSheaf.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isIso_of_forall_isIso_etaleStalk). A [finite lisse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale/ModuleSheaf.html#CategoryTheory.Sheaf.IsFiniteLisse) predicate is defined on top of them.
- The [Hattori-Stallings trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Trace/HattoriStallings.html#LinearMap.hattoriStallingsTrace) of an endomorphism of a finite projective module over any ring, with values in `R/[R,R]`. It is cyclic and additive, and over commutative rings it [agrees with the projective trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Trace/HattoriStallings.html#LinearMap.hattoriStallingsTrace_eq_projectiveTrace).

## The frontier

- **Nagata over a qcqs base**: glue across a dense common open after blowups, prove the two-open step and the Noetherian theorem, then reduce a qcqs base by Noetherian approximation. That last step needs SchemeAndStackFoundations §0.9.
- **Lisse monodromy**: identify finite lisse `Λ`-sheaves with continuous finite `π₁`-modules. The Galois category, the finite lisse predicate and the [fully faithful sheaf-of-sections functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Sheaf.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.toSheafFullyFaithful) are in place.
- **The fundamental group of `𝔾_m`**: show that every connected finite étale cover of `𝔾_m` over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero is Kummer, then identify `π₁` with the Tate module. §F.0.A2's exact sequence also needs descent from SchemeAndStackFoundations Layer 1.
- **Constructible étale sheaves**: stratifications, pullback and descent, extension by zero, and closure under limits, colimits and tensor products. Everything from §F.0.3 onward depends on this.
- **Completing integral models**: saturation in a stable subspace, the dual lattice and biduality. Commensurability, sums, intersections and homothety are already proved.
