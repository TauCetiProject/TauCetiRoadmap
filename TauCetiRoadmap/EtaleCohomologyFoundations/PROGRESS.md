## EtaleCohomologyFoundations: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`6ca7725` to `3150404`)

The roadmap's first window starts its two entry points: scheme compactifications and the Galois category of finite étale covers. The compactification package now has the category of [compactifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Basic.html#TauCeti.Compactification) of a morphism, its full subcategory of [dense compactifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Basic.html#TauCeti.DenseCompactification), base change along any map of bases (preserving density when the base change is flat), and the identity compactification of a proper morphism as an initial object. On top of this sits the [common refinement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Refinement.html#TauCeti.Compactification.commonRefinement), the schematic closure of the source in the fibre product of two compactifications. It is [the binary product of dense compactifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Refinement.html#TauCeti.DenseCompactification.isLimitCommonRefinementFan), and [every nonempty finite family has a dense common refinement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Refinement.html#TauCeti.Compactification.exists_forall_nonempty_hom). Maps out of a dense compactification are [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Compactification.hom_unique), so that category is thin. Dense replacement, Nagata's existence theorem and the comparison principle for refinements are still to come.

On the finite étale side, finite étale schemes over a base now have finite limits and are finitary extensive. Every monomorphism [splits off a direct summand](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Limits.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.exists_isColimit_binaryCofanMk_of_mono), and epimorphisms are exactly the surjections. The [geometric fibre functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Fiber.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.fiber) at a separably closed point is corepresented by that point and preserves finite limits, finite coproducts and epimorphisms (TauCeti#20152). Together these give most of Lenstra's Galois-category axioms. Quotients by finite groups and conservativity of the fibre functor are missing. The algebra needed for quotients has started: [invariants of a finite étale algebra under a finite monoid are étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/Equalizer.html#TauCeti.FixedPoints.etale_subalgebra).

## EtaleCohomologyFoundations: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-13 (`3150404` to `aa6e43e`)

Finite étale schemes over a connected scheme now [form a Galois category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/Category.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.instGaloisCategoryOfConnectedSpaceCarrierCarrierCommRingCat), with every geometric fibre functor a fibre functor. The missing pieces were [quotients by finite groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Quotient.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.quotient) and conservativity of the fibre functor. Over a locally connected base, geometric points are joined by an étale path [exactly when they share a connected component](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/Component.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.nonempty_fiber_iso_iff_mem_connectedComponent). [The étale fundamental group of a field is its absolute Galois group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.galContinuousMulEquivAutFiber), and π₁ is [functorial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Galois/Functoriality.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.mapAutFiber). For 𝔾_m only the [Kummer covers and their fibres μ_n](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Kummer.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.kummerCoverFiberEquivRootsOfUnity) are in. Nothing yet shows that every connected cover is Kummer.

Nagata's theorem holds [for finite-type morphisms between affine schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/ProjectiveClosure.html#TauCeti.nonempty_denseCompactification_of_isAffine), by projective closure. The blowup steps of the general proof have started, including [extending a blowup from an open subscheme](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Blowup/Extension.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.exists_blowup_extension) on locally Noetherian schemes, but the general theorem has not. [Dense replacement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/DenseReplacement/Basic.html#TauCeti.Compactification.denseReplacementAdjunction) is the coreflection onto dense compactifications. Its base-change comparison is [invertible exactly when density survives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/DenseReplacement/Basic.html#TauCeti.Compactification.isIso_denseReplacementBaseChange_iff). Compactifications form a [cofiltered category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Cofiltered.html#TauCeti.Compactification.instIsCofilteredOrEmpty), with [comparison isomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Filtered/Comparison.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.cofilteredComparison) that satisfy the cocycle law.

Several later sections also began. Stalks of étale module sheaves are exact and [jointly conservative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale/ModuleSheaf.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.isIso_of_forall_isIso_etaleStalk). Over a nonarchimedean local field, [a subgroup of GL(V) stabilizes a lattice exactly when its closure is compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/StableLattice.html#Subgroup.isCompact_closure_iff_exists_stable_lattice), and integral models [exist](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/IntegralModel.html#TauCeti.GaloisLattice.IntegralModel.nonempty). Rational-point counts over finite fields arrived, along with the [Hattori-Stallings trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Trace/HattoriStallings.html#LinearMap.hattoriStallingsTrace). Decomposition and inertia groups of points under a group action also landed.
