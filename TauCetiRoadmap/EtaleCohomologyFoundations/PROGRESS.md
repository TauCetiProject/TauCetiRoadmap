## EtaleCohomologyFoundations: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-23 (`6ca7725` to `3150404`)

The roadmap's first window starts its two entry points: scheme compactifications and the Galois category of finite étale covers. The compactification package now has the category of [compactifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Basic.html#TauCeti.Compactification) of a morphism, its full subcategory of [dense compactifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Basic.html#TauCeti.DenseCompactification), base change along any map of bases (preserving density when the base change is flat), and the identity compactification of a proper morphism as an initial object. On top of this sits the [common refinement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Refinement.html#TauCeti.Compactification.commonRefinement), the schematic closure of the source in the fibre product of two compactifications. It is [the binary product of dense compactifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Refinement.html#TauCeti.DenseCompactification.isLimitCommonRefinementFan), and [every nonempty finite family has a dense common refinement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Refinement.html#TauCeti.Compactification.exists_forall_nonempty_hom). Maps out of a dense compactification are [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Compactification/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Compactification.hom_unique), so that category is thin. Dense replacement, Nagata's existence theorem and the comparison principle for refinements are still to come.

On the finite étale side, finite étale schemes over a base now have finite limits and are finitary extensive. Every monomorphism [splits off a direct summand](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Limits.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.exists_isColimit_binaryCofanMk_of_mono), and epimorphisms are exactly the surjections. The [geometric fibre functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Fiber.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.fiber) at a separably closed point is corepresented by that point and preserves finite limits, finite coproducts and epimorphisms (TauCeti#20152). Together these give most of Lenstra's Galois-category axioms. Quotients by finite groups and conservativity of the fibre functor are missing. The algebra needed for quotients has started: [invariants of a finite étale algebra under a finite monoid are étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/Equalizer.html#TauCeti.FixedPoints.etale_subalgebra).
