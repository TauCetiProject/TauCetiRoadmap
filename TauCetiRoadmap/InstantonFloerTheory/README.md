# Instanton Floer theory

Construct three-dimensional Chern–Simons Floer theory from four-dimensional anti-self-dual connections. Build the nontrivial admissible theory over the integers, the separate reducible-aware theory of integral homology spheres, and the complex framed theory for every closed connected oriented three-manifold and every specified SO(3) bundle. Construct continuation, naturality, oriented cobordism maps, universal-class operations, exact surgery triangles, excision and the admissible mixed-equation Atiyah–Floer comparison. Keep the actual bundle, gauge group, coefficients and grading as part of every interface.

For finite disjoint unions construct the graded tensor product of the component chain complexes, with the actual componentwise bundle/admissibility data, tensor signs and empty object. Over Z retain the derived/Künneth Tor terms rather than identifying homology with an underived tensor product without hypotheses; over C give the resulting tensor comparison.

Use native smooth principal/associated bundles, connections, forms, Sobolev sections, continuous linear/Fredholm maps, determinant lines, quotients, singular (co)homology, chain complexes, modules and eigenspaces. The representative interfaces in Suggested.lean are not exhaustive; this README is the definitive specification.

## IF0. Bundles, groups, coefficients and signs

Let Y be a nonempty closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold, Hausdorff, second countable and finite dimensional, with a smooth Riemannian metric. Fix a Hermitian rank-two bundle E, a Hermitian line w, an isomorphism det E≃w and a connection on w. The traceless connection carrier is the affine space of SO(3) connections on ad E; compare it with U(2) connections inducing that fixed determinant connection. Keep the integral lift and its isomorphisms until independence of those choices is proved. A nontrivial admissible datum additionally includes an integral two-cycle S with ⟨c1(w),S⟩ odd. Prove its equivalent formulation using an embedded oriented detecting surface and w2(ad E). Nonzero w2 without such an integral detecting class is not this hypothesis.

Let G_det be determinant-one unitary automorphisms. Its constant ±1 acts trivially; on irreducibles the effective quotient G_det/{±1} is free. Retain the ineffective center in the groupoid and distinguish the based group and the full SO(3) gauge group. Construct the exact lifting obstruction to H¹(Y;Z/2); a chosen odd surface R gives a particular index-two extension via its dual real line, not a license to quotient by every extended gauge transformation. Prove its degree-four action and its compatibility with the perturbations/counts before defining the folded Z/4 theory. Do not identify invariant homology, invariant chains and orbit chains over Z by division by2. Construct their actual comparison in each claimed coefficient and grading scope.

Use skew-Hermitian traceless 2×2 matrices and B(X,Y)=−tr₂(XY). Fix product orientation ds followed by the orientation of Y. Use the left action g·A=gAg⁻¹−(dg)g⁻¹ and infinitesimal generator −d_Aξ; F_(A+a)=F_A+d_Aa+[a∧a]/2. Define CS relative to a fixed reference by the transgression with coefficient −1/(8π²) and the fundamental trace tr₂. Its variation is

`δ CS_A(a)=−(1/(4π²))∫_Y tr₂(a∧F_A)`.

For nontrivial bundles construct the global relative transgression/differential-character definition and all change-of-reference laws. The normalized L² pairing on one-forms is `(1/(4π²))∫B(a∧*b)`. Prove grad CS=*F_A. Thus its negative flow is `∂_s A=−*F_A`; in temporal gauge this is F_𝔄⁺=0 on R×Y. Construct temporal gauge and its residual gauges, rather than equating connection paths and four-dimensional connections without a gauge comparison.

For the traceless curvature convention prove `p1(ad E)=−(1/(2π²))[tr₂(F∧F)]`; put κ=−p1/4. An unperturbed ASD trajectory satisfies

`E_flow=(1/(4π²))∫|∂_s A|²_B=CS(A_−)−CS(A_+)=κ`,

and `E_YM=∫|F_𝔄|²_B=8π²κ`. On manifolds with ends κ is the relative transgression value with the stated end lifts, not automatically an integer. Derive both the factor2 between the spatial and four-dimensional curvature norms and the perturbation correction. Choose the determinant-one deck generator using the mapping-torus bundle with κ=1; prove it lowers the real CS lift by1 and contributes +8 to the ASD path index. Derive the obstruction/half-period law for the chosen extended gauge transformation separately. A sign in a cited opposite gauge convention must be transported through these definitions.

The admissible complex is integral and relatively Z/8 graded. Homology-sphere ordinary Floer has its own S³ normalization. The folded extended-gauge complex is relatively Z/4 graded and is a different construction. The framed eigensector below is over C. Rational μ=−p1/4 operations, their integral multiples and integer-valued counts must not be conflated.

## IF1. Nonlinear gauge completions and quotient topology

Construct H^k connections and H^(k+1) determinant-one gauges for every integer k≥2 in dimension3, and integer k≥3 on compact four-dimensional pieces; use weighted and local versions on ends. Form gauges as actual Sobolev sections of the associated SU(2) group bundle. Prove multiplication/inversion/exponential and composition estimates in charts, transition compatibility, smooth action and curvature map one derivative lower, separability and independence of atlas/reference/Sobolev order. Retain finite-regularity and compact-parameter estimates with explicit derivative budgets. Use the existing BundleSobolev carrier and prove its nonlinear gauge applications.

At any smooth reference invert d_A*d_A on the complement of H⁰_A, with the K5 Green comparison. Prove the nonlinear Coulomb slice and its uniqueness modulo the actual stabilizer, local properness against remote gauges, quotient topology, based/unbased comparison and smooth transition laws. At irreducibles H⁰_A=0; at reducibles keep the stabilizer and Kuranishi model. Stabilizers in the effective group and in G_det are different. Prove weak-gauge regularity between smooth fields, local bootstrap and bundle globalization; do not declare a newly smoothed bundle equal to the original without its actual gauge/isomorphism comparison. Families require common neighborhoods and estimates, not merely individual smoothing.

In dimension4 prove the small L²-curvature Coulomb theorem on balls, with actual boundary gauge condition, W^(1,2) bound, scaling and overlapping-gauge estimates. Supply its higher regularity when curvature/equation gains regularity. Prove gauge-patching, weak compactness and local compact embedding in the required ranges. The critical theorem is a distinct nonlinear application; the H^k Banach slice and the two-dimensional R5 theorem do not prove it. Establish the corresponding local three-dimensional curvature/slice estimates used for compact critical loci.

## IF2. Holonomy perturbations and regular critical points

Define cylinder functions by thickened embedded loops, holonomy in the fixed determinant bundle, conjugation-invariant smooth functions on the holonomy factors and smooth transverse measures. Construct a separable weighted Banach space of countably many such functions with summable all-required-derivative bounds. Prove differentiability, gauge covariance and the actual L² gradient/Hessian estimates, self-adjointness on a slice and compact lower-order comparison. State spatial/end support and fixed-collar requirements explicitly. For perturbation h use CS_h=CS+h and the perturbed flat equation *F+grad h=0.

Prove the odd-cycle obstruction to a reducible projectively flat connection by the integral line-splitting/curvature comparison; prove that a sufficiently controlled small perturbation retains irreducibility of the relevant critical set. Establish compactness modulo gauge before deriving this openness. Prove density of regular perturbations using the actual holonomy variation/annihilator/unique-continuation argument, with all incidence, end and parameter dimensions included. Include paths and two-parameter comparisons relative to already regular end collars; construct their nonempty allowable extension spaces before applying Sard–Smale. Fixed critical endpoints require the constrained perturbation argument, not arbitrary endpoint motion. For each finite index/energy/class/parameter list, let N bound the actual universal projection index including every parameter and incidence constraint. Choose k>max(N,0), choose a finite auxiliary regularity order covering the Sobolev/composition losses, and prove the universal section/projection is C^k at that order before invoking the generic theorem. Construct smooth choices by the actual density/openness argument in its stated topology; a Banach residual set alone is not a smooth choice theorem.

Prove the regular critical set is discrete and compact, hence finite, for the chosen admissible data. Do not infer finiteness from an elliptic index alone. For the extended-gauge branch choose compatible perturbations and prove free pairing on relevant critical generators and orientation-line action; handle equivariant regularity on actual solution strata. No freeness assertion on the entire affine configuration space is used without proof.

Prove local elliptic regularity, finite-energy limits modulo gauge, exponential convergence in all controlled derivatives and uniqueness of the limiting critical orbit. Give constants uniform on compact regular sectors, relative families and necks with a fixed common end gap. Distinguish uniform compact-sector estimates from arbitrary approaches to degeneracy. Quotient nonconstant trajectories by translation and prove its free/proper action with genuine slices; stationary trajectories have their separate carrier and are not divided by an R action as though free.

For the closed connected oriented four-dimensional ASD symbol, derive `ind_R(d_A*⊕d_A⁺)=−2⟨p1(ad P),[X]⟩−3(1−b1(X)+b⁺(X))=8κ−3(1−b1+b⁺)` from the actual symbol/index comparison, retaining stabilizer and obstruction spaces in the index. ## IF4. Uhlenbeck compactification, gluing and orientations

Prove epsilon-regularity, removable singularities for finite-L² ASD fields, energy concentration and quantization, convergence modulo gauges away from finitely many points, and the ideal-connection topology on compact cobordisms and cylinders. Include perturbed equations with the actual scaling behavior of holonomy perturbations, bounded-energy and fixed-end families. Retain multiplicities, lost relative charge and determinant bundle data. Prove fixed-index energy bounds, including the CS lift/path-class relation and the perturbation budget, before compactness is used for counts. For index1 and2 cylinder moduli, and the required low-dimensional continuation families, establish regularity of all lower-charge ideal strata and show bubbles cannot appear. The ideal-stratum dimension drops by4 per simple bubble after its center is retained, not by8. Construct pregluing for broken trajectories and cylindrical cobordisms, right inverses with norm/neck bounds, nonlinear contraction, uniqueness and neighborhood-surjectivity. Include all finite breaks, compatible corner/parameter descriptions and inverse charts needed to identify the full boundary; a formal broken configuration is not a gluing theorem. Retain scale/rotation/center variables in bubble charts whenever the operation uses them. Identify which regularity and compactness theorems control each chart.

Use real determinant lines det ker⊗(det coker)*. Construct coherent orientation lines o_α for critical points and ordered gluing maps, initially with the translation line first and quotient orientation defined by `det(D)=or(R_translation)⊗det(T M_hat)`. Prove gauge and extended-gauge orientation transport, stabilization independence and homology-orientation composition for cobordisms. At nontrivial stabilizers use their actual representation on these lines. A final free ± sign is not a normalization theorem.
