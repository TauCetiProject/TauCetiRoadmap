# Instanton Floer theory

Construct three-dimensional Chern–Simons Floer theory from four-dimensional anti-self-dual connections. Build the nontrivial admissible theory over the integers, the separate reducible-aware theory of integral homology spheres, and the complex framed theory for every closed connected oriented three-manifold and every specified SO(3) bundle. Construct continuation, naturality, oriented cobordism maps, universal-class operations, exact surgery triangles, excision and the admissible mixed-equation Atiyah–Floer comparison. Keep the actual bundle, gauge group, coefficients and grading as part of every interface.

For finite disjoint unions construct the graded tensor product of the component chain complexes, with the actual componentwise bundle/admissibility data, tensor signs and empty object. Over Z retain the derived/Künneth Tor terms rather than identifying homology with an underived tensor product without hypotheses; over C give the resulting tensor comparison.

Use native smooth principal/associated bundles, connections, forms, Sobolev sections, continuous linear/Fredholm maps, determinant lines, quotients, singular (co)homology, chain complexes, modules and eigenspaces. This README is the roadmap. Suggested.lean records its headline theorems and the carriers each lacks at the pinned Mathlib and Tau Ceti; none is statable there.

## IF0. Bundles, groups, coefficients and signs

Let Y be a nonempty closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold, Hausdorff, second countable and finite dimensional, with a smooth Riemannian metric. Fix a Hermitian rank-two bundle E, a Hermitian line w, an isomorphism det E≃w and a connection on w. The traceless connection carrier is the affine space of SO(3) connections on ad E; compare it with U(2) connections inducing that fixed determinant connection. Keep the integral lift and its isomorphisms until independence of those choices is proved. A nontrivial admissible datum additionally includes an integral two-cycle S with ⟨c1(w),S⟩ odd. Prove its equivalent formulation using an embedded oriented detecting surface and w2(ad E). Nonzero w2 without such an integral detecting class is not this hypothesis.

**Checks.**

- `T³` with `w` dual to one circle factor: `⟨c₁(w),[T²]⟩=1` on the transverse torus, so the pair is nontrivial admissible (Kronheimer–Mrowka §7.1, p. 52); the same `T³` with `w` trivial carries the reducible trivial connection and is not admissible.
- An integral homology sphere: `H₂(Y;Z)=0`, so no `w` has an odd pairing; its trivial SU(2) bundle is the separate IF7 input, never an admissible datum.
- `RP³` with the SO(3) bundle `R⊕L⊕L`, `L` the nontrivial real line bundle: `w₂=w₁(L)²≠0` in `H²(RP³;Z/2)=Z/2`, but `H₂(RP³;Z)=0`, so there is no integral detecting class; the flat connection with holonomy `diag(1,−1,−1)` is reducible. This is the nonzero-`w₂`-without-odd-class exclusion.
- Equivalence of the two formulations: an integral class `S` with `⟨c₁(w),S⟩` odd is represented by an embedded oriented surface `R`, and `⟨w₂(ad E),[R]⟩≡⟨c₁(w),[R]⟩ mod 2`; a class detected only modulo 2 does not give the integral oddness.

Let G_det be determinant-one unitary automorphisms. Its constant ±1 acts trivially; on irreducibles the effective quotient G_det/{±1} is free. Retain the ineffective center in the groupoid and distinguish the based group and the full SO(3) gauge group. Construct the exact lifting obstruction to H¹(Y;Z/2); a chosen odd surface R gives a particular index-two extension via its dual real line, not a license to quotient by every extended gauge transformation. Prove its degree-four action and its compatibility with the perturbations/counts before defining the folded Z/4 theory. Do not identify invariant homology, invariant chains and orbit chains over Z by division by2. Construct their actual comparison in each claimed coefficient and grading scope.

**Checks.**

- The constant `−1∈G_det` acts trivially on every connection, so on the irreducible locus the free group is `G_det/{±1}`; a count that divides by the order of a stabilizer in `G_det` instead of in the effective group is off by this center.
- `T³` with the odd bundle: the determinant-one quotient has two flat classes separated by degree 4, and the extended gauge transformation of the index-two group identifies them, so the folded complex has one generator (Kronheimer–Mrowka §7.1, p. 53; Scaduto §7.6, p. 34).
- On `T³`, `H¹(T³;Z/2)=(Z/2)³`: the full SO(3) gauge group of `ad E` modulo the image of `G_det` has order eight, while the index-two extension chosen by an odd surface `R` adds the single class dual to `R`; quotienting by all eight would identify the two flat classes and act on their orientation lines in ways the folded `Z/4` theory does not.

Use skew-Hermitian traceless 2×2 matrices and B(X,Y)=−tr₂(XY). Fix product orientation ds followed by the orientation of Y. Use the left action g·A=gAg⁻¹−(dg)g⁻¹ and infinitesimal generator −d_Aξ; F_(A+a)=F_A+d_Aa+[a∧a]/2. Define CS relative to a fixed reference by the transgression with coefficient −1/(8π²) and the fundamental trace tr₂ (Donaldson §2.5.2, p. 16, up to this normalization). Its variation is

`δ CS_A(a)=−(1/(4π²))∫_Y tr₂(a∧F_A)`.

For nontrivial bundles construct the global relative transgression/differential-character definition and all change-of-reference laws. The normalized L² pairing on one-forms is `(1/(4π²))∫B(a∧*b)`. Prove grad CS=*F_A. Thus its negative flow is `∂_s A=−*F_A`; in temporal gauge this is F_𝔄⁺=0 on R×Y. Construct temporal gauge and its residual gauges, rather than equating connection paths and four-dimensional connections without a gauge comparison.

**Checks.**

- Variation: on a trivial bundle `CS(A)=−(1/(8π²))∫_Y tr₂(A∧dA+(2/3)A∧A∧A)` and `A↦A+ta` give `(d/dt)CS=−(1/(8π²))·2∫tr₂(a∧F_A)`, the displayed `−(1/(4π²))∫tr₂(a∧F_A)`; the coefficient `+1/(8π²)` would turn the negative gradient flow into the self-duality equation.
- Gradient: with `B=−tr₂` and the pairing `(1/(4π²))∫B(a∧*b)`, the identity `(1/(4π²))∫(−tr₂)(a∧*grad CS)=−(1/(4π²))∫tr₂(a∧F_A)` forces `*grad CS=F_A`, and `**=1` on one-forms of a three-manifold gives `grad CS=*F_A`.
- Flow versus ASD: in temporal gauge `F_𝔄=ds∧∂_sA+F_A` and, for the orientation `ds` followed by `Y`, `*F_𝔄=*₃∂_sA+ds∧*₃F_A`, so `F_𝔄=−*F_𝔄` is exactly `∂_sA=−*₃F_A` (Donaldson §2.5.3, p. 20); with the opposite product orientation the same flow would be self-dual.

For the traceless curvature convention prove `p1(ad E)=−(1/(2π²))[tr₂(F∧F)]`; put κ=−p1/4. An unperturbed ASD trajectory satisfies

`E_flow=(1/(4π²))∫|∂_s A|²_B=CS(A_−)−CS(A_+)=κ`,

and `E_YM=∫|F_𝔄|²_B=8π²κ`. On manifolds with ends κ is the relative transgression value with the stated end lifts, not automatically an integer. Derive both the factor2 between the spatial and four-dimensional curvature norms and the perturbation correction. Choose the determinant-one deck generator using the mapping-torus bundle with κ=1; prove it lowers the real CS lift by1 and contributes +8 to the ASD path index. Derive the obstruction/half-period law for the chosen extended gauge transformation separately. A sign in a cited opposite gauge convention must be transported through these definitions.

**Checks.**

- `p₁` and `κ`: for an SU(2) bundle `c₂=(1/(8π²))∫tr₂(F∧F)` and `ad E⊗C≅Sym²E`, so `p₁(ad E)=−4c₂` and the displayed `−(1/(2π²))[tr₂(F∧F)]` gives `κ=−p₁/4=c₂`; the coefficient `−(1/(8π²))` would make `κ=c₂/4`.
- Energies: on `R×Y` an ASD trajectory has `|F_𝔄|²=|∂_sA|²+|F_A|²=2|∂_sA|²` pointwise, so `E_YM=8π²κ` and `E_flow=(1/(4π²))∫|∂_sA|²_B=κ`; this is the factor 2 between the spatial and four-dimensional norms. The charge-one instanton on `S⁴` has `E_YM=8π²`.
- Deck generator: a determinant-one gauge transformation of degree one on `Y` is the clutching map of the mapping-torus bundle over `S¹×Y` with `κ=1`; the CS lift drops by exactly 1 and the ASD path index along it is `8κ=8`, so the relative grading is `Z/8`; the half-period extended-gauge path of IF10b carries `κ=1/2` and index 4.

The admissible complex is integral and relatively Z/8 graded. Homology-sphere ordinary Floer has its own S³ normalization. The folded extended-gauge complex is relatively Z/4 graded and is a different construction. The framed eigensector below is over C. Rational μ=−p1/4 operations, their integral multiples and integer-valued counts must not be conflated.

*Needs:* SmoothBundlesConnections B2.5 (the adjoint bundle), B3.1–B3.3 (connections and induced connections), B4.1–B4.2 (curvature, the gauge group and its left action), B5.2 (holonomy and flat connections); DifferentialGeometry Layer 5 (integration and Stokes) and Layer 6 (de Rham classes); ComplexKahlerGeometry K5a (the Hodge star); CharacteristicClasses C3 items 2, 3 and 5 and C4 items 1–3 (`w₂`, `p₁` and their curvature representatives); AlgebraicTopology Stage 6 items 3–4 (orientation systems and duality for the detecting surface); Kronheimer–Mrowka §7.1 (pp. 52–53) for the admissible datum and the index-two extension; Donaldson §§2.5.2–2.5.3 (pp. 16–23) for the Chern–Simons functional and the instanton equation.

## IF2. Holonomy perturbations and regular critical points

Define cylinder functions by thickened embedded loops, holonomy in the fixed determinant bundle, conjugation-invariant smooth functions on the holonomy factors and smooth transverse measures (Donaldson §5.5, p. 134; Daemi–Fukaya–Lipyanskiy §6.1, p. 75). Construct a separable weighted Banach space of countably many such functions with summable all-required-derivative bounds. Prove differentiability, gauge covariance and the actual L² gradient/Hessian estimates, self-adjointness on a slice and compact lower-order comparison. State spatial/end support and fixed-collar requirements explicitly. For perturbation h use CS_h=CS+h and the perturbed flat equation *F+grad h=0.

Prove the odd-cycle obstruction to a reducible projectively flat connection by the integral line-splitting/curvature comparison; prove that a sufficiently controlled small perturbation retains irreducibility of the relevant critical set. Establish compactness modulo gauge before deriving this openness. Prove density of regular perturbations using the actual holonomy variation/annihilator/unique-continuation argument, with all incidence, end and parameter dimensions included. Include paths and two-parameter comparisons relative to already regular end collars; construct their nonempty allowable extension spaces before applying Sard–Smale. Fixed critical endpoints require the constrained perturbation argument, not arbitrary endpoint motion. For each finite index/energy/class/parameter list, let N bound the actual universal projection index including every parameter and incidence constraint. Choose k>max(N,0), choose a finite auxiliary regularity order covering the Sobolev/composition losses, and prove the universal section/projection is C^k at that order before invoking the generic theorem. Construct smooth choices by the actual density/openness argument in its stated topology; a Banach residual set alone is not a smooth choice theorem.

**Checks.**

- Reducibility obstruction: a projectively flat connection reducible to `U(1)×U(1)` splits `E=L₁⊕L₂` with equal curvature forms, so `c₁(L₁)=c₁(L₂)` in `H²(Y;R)` and `⟨c₁(w),S⟩=2⟨c₁(L₁),S⟩` is even for every integral `S`; the odd datum excludes it. On `S¹×S²` with `w` trivial the trivial connection is reducible and the argument has nothing to exclude.
- Unperturbed regular set: on `T³` with the odd bundle the two flat classes are nondegenerate and irreducible with `h=0` (Scaduto §7.6, p. 34), so the perturbation needed there is zero; a construction that always forced a nonzero perturbation would lose this computation.
- Cylinder functions: for a loop `γ` the holonomy of `A+ta` changes to first order by `−∫_γ a` conjugated along `γ`, so the gradient of a cylinder function is a one-form supported in the thickened loop and its Hessian is a lower-order compact perturbation of `*d_A`; a perturbation supported in a collar of the detecting surface, as IF9 needs, is a special case.

Prove the regular critical set is discrete and compact, hence finite, for the chosen admissible data. Do not infer finiteness from an elliptic index alone. For the extended-gauge branch choose compatible perturbations and prove free pairing on relevant critical generators and orientation-line action; handle equivariant regularity on actual solution strata. No freeness assertion on the entire affine configuration space is used without proof.

*Needs:* HeegaardFloer F0.S (finite-dimensional Sard), F0.0 (generic Fredholm sections and determinant lines), F0.1 (function spaces and generic functions), F0.3 (universal zero sets and the regular-parameter projection); SmoothBundlesConnections B5.2 (holonomy); IF1 (slices, quotient topology); Donaldson §5.5 and §5.5.1 (pp. 134–144) for the holonomy perturbations, the finite nondegenerate perturbed critical set and the transversality arguments; Kronheimer–Mrowka §7.1 (p. 52) for the admissible datum only; Frøyshov §2 (p. 4) for the finite nondegenerate perturbed critical set in the homology-sphere theory of IF7; Daemi–Fukaya–Lipyanskiy §6.1 (p. 75) for the thickened-loop holonomy traces and cylinder functions in the two-component setting.

## IF3. Cylinder ASD analysis, index and convergence

For a trajectory from α to β construct the gauge-fixed operator d_𝔄*⊕d_𝔄⁺ plus the holonomy Hessian on actual weighted Sobolev bundles. Choose weights strictly within the gaps of the nondegenerate end Hessians, identify kernel/cokernel with the deformation problem and prove Fredholmness, spectral-flow index, index gluing and determinant exact sequences. Build the cylindrical parametrix from end spectral decomposition and cutoffs, and compact-core comparison; neither a compact-base index theorem nor a weighted CR operator is already this realization.

For X_h=grad h, derive `∂_s A=−*F_A−X_h(A)` and, in temporal gauge, `F_𝔄⁺=−(ds∧X_h+*_3 X_h)/2`. Prove the perturbed flow energy equals the drop of CS_h for s-independent h. For varying h_s include the explicit integral of ∂_s h_s(A_s), with the correct sign and an integrable uniform bound on the parameter sector. Prove local elliptic regularity, finite-energy limits modulo gauge, exponential convergence in all controlled derivatives and uniqueness of the limiting critical orbit. Give constants uniform on compact regular sectors, relative families and necks with a fixed common end gap. Distinguish uniform compact-sector estimates from arbitrary approaches to degeneracy. Quotient nonconstant trajectories by translation and prove its free/proper action with genuine slices; stationary trajectories have their separate carrier and are not divided by an R action as though free.

For the closed connected oriented four-dimensional ASD symbol, derive `ind_R(d_A*⊕d_A⁺)=−2⟨p1(ad P),[X]⟩−3(1−b1(X)+b⁺(X))=8κ−3(1−b1+b⁺)` from the actual symbol/index comparison (Donaldson–Kronheimer Chapter 4; Donaldson §3.2, p. 47, for the tube version), retaining stabilizer and obstruction spaces in the index. - `π₂`: for `Σ=Σ_(g+1)⊔T²` with `g≥1`, `π₂(M)=π₂(M(Σ_(g+1)))⊕π₂(M(T²))=Z⊕0` (Daemi–Fukaya–Lipyanskiy §2.2, p. 8), so there is a single sphere generator to normalize against; for two positive-genus factors `π₂=Z⊕Z`, and compatibility with one summand does not give the other.

In the simply connected case prove the explicit comparison criterion: if both orientation systems respect gluing **every** sphere class in the relevant ambient factors, their ratio for `p:α→β` is independent of p. Strip concatenation then makes it `ε(β)−ε(α)`; the map on generator lines multiplying by `(-1)^ε(α)` intertwines differentials, continuation and mixed maps. Construct the sphere-gluing/cap maps used in that argument. For two positive-genus surface factors do not infer simultaneous sphere compatibility from compatibility with just one summand of π₂. The general coherent pair-strip theorem above needs no such universal brane identification.

For the framed Heegaard splitting `Σ_(g+1)⊔T²`, construct the actual holonomy models of the two supports, with the T² odd holonomies fixing the residual projective frame. Choose the anticommuting SU(2) lifts of the odd torus holonomies; their simultaneous centralizer is the ineffective ±1. The remaining g free handle holonomies give the explicit SU(2)^g support model, with central signs and boundary identifications retained. Identify each support by this model (including the g0 point), transport its product right-invariant tangent spin lift and choose the common zero background. Construct the fourfold grading lifts and the trivial integral coefficient lines; prove their comparisons under the framing/Heegaard/base-ball identifications. For g≥1 only the genus-(g+1) factor contributes nonzero π₂; the T² moduli is a point. Use the raw mixed shifting/determinant construction to normalize the gauge orientation system against that sphere generator and compare it with the spin-induced system. For g0 both relevant moduli are points and the constant-cap unit fixes the comparison without asserting a sphere generator. The generator-sign criterion then gives the actual decorated-Fukaya identification for framing, also on lifted action-filtered complexes. This consumes the precise framing topology and does not import a universal absolute-spin assertion about arbitrary gauge correspondences. Prove coherence of these brane/line/grading identifications under the declared comparisons. Unweighted integral, rational and complex scalar extensions retain their actual orientation and coefficient maps.

### IF10b. Lifted Laurent complexes and the deck-sign adapter

Construct an actual period cover of the selected index-two gauge model's critical set.     U : (i, A_h) ↦ (i+4, A_h + 1/2),   C̄_G = ⊕_(α in the finite folded set) Z[U,U⁻¹] ⊗ o_α̃.

### IF10c. Invariant enriched filtered framing

For the untwisted added-T³ model use its actual flat reference family and choose a base connection `α₀` in it. Transport gauge continuation by `g_i^j=N_j f_i^j N_i^−1` and conjugate its homotopies; this constructs the symplectic enriched system and the equivalence, rather than assuming it. **Checks.**

- Nilpotence: in a fixed Floer degree `i` each folded generator has exactly one lift (`U` shifts the degree by 4), so the degree-`i` piece is a finite free `Z`-module and a strictly action-lowering endomorphism of it is nilpotent; `id−K+K²−…` is a finite sum and `N_i⁻¹` is level 0 with integral entries.
- Levels compose additively: a level-`λ` map followed by a level-`μ` map has level `λ+μ`, so `g_i^j=N_j f_i^j N_i⁻¹` has the level of `f_i^j` once the `N` are level 0, and the tails `λ_n→0` are preserved.
- Rationalization: Definition 7.4 is over `Q[U^±1]` while the complexes are `Z[U^±1]`-modules (Daemi–Fukaya–Lipyanskiy p. 83 and Definition 7.4, p. 85); the integral enriched object maps to the rational one by base change, and nothing in the source asserts the integral statement.

**Checks.**

- A twist without a flat reference: on `S¹×S²` the SO(3) bundle with `w₂` the class dual to the circle factor, which pairs oddly with `[S²]`, carries no flat connection (a flat bundle is pulled back from `S¹` and has `w₂=0`), so for such a summand the reference connection is a nonflat choice and the action is a torsor, not a numerical filtration.
