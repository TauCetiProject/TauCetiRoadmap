## SpecialFunctionsParabolicCylinder: 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-13 (`2b93e72` to `aa6e43e`)

The first work toward this roadmap is supporting analysis for the endpoint case of the rotational addition theorem (DLMF 12.13.5, in Layer 8), not parabolic cylinder functions themselves. One piece is a Gaussian majorant for the half-Gamma series ∑ x^m/Γ(m/2 + 1). The series is evaluated in closed form as exp(x²)(1 + erf x) ([`tsum_pow_div_Gamma_div_two_add_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Erf/Series.html#TauCeti.Real.tsum_pow_div_Gamma_div_two_add_one), DLMF 7.6.3), giving the bound [`tsum_pow_div_Gamma_div_two_add_one_lt_two_mul_exp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Erf/Series.html#Real.tsum_pow_div_Gamma_div_two_add_one_lt_two_mul_exp), < 2 exp(x²) with no polynomial factor. That is the bound the README asks for, for every real x rather than only x ≥ 0. Along the way the real error function got its standard series: the Maclaurin series ([`hasSum_erf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Erf/Series.html#TauCeti.Real.hasSum_erf), DLMF 7.6.1), the double-factorial series for exp(x²) erf x ([`erf_eq_mul_exp_neg_sq_mul_tsum`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Erf/Series.html#TauCeti.Real.erf_eq_mul_exp_neg_sq_mul_tsum), DLMF 7.6.2), and erf x = x γ*(½, x²) ([`ofReal_erf_eq_mul_tricomiIncompleteGamma`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Erf/Series.html#TauCeti.Real.ofReal_erf_eq_mul_tricomiIncompleteGamma), DLMF 7.11.3).

The other piece is the Hermite–Gaussian Fourier identity. The Fourier integral of s^n e^{−s²/2} at any complex frequency y is √(2π) (−i)^n He_n(y) e^{−y²/2} ([`integral_pow_mul_cexp_neg_sq_div_two_sub_I_mul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/FourierIntegral.html#TauCeti.integral_pow_mul_cexp_neg_sq_div_two_sub_I_mul)). From it follows the moment bound |He_n(y)| e^{−y²/2}/n! ≤ 2^{−n/2}/Γ(n/2 + 1) ([`abs_aeval_hermite_mul_exp_div_factorial_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Hermite/Bound.html#TauCeti.abs_aeval_hermite_mul_exp_div_factorial_le)). Both are stated for Hermite polynomials, not for D_m, which does not exist yet. None of the roadmap's own definitions has landed: there is no Kummer derivative theory, no Weber solutions, and no U, V or D.
