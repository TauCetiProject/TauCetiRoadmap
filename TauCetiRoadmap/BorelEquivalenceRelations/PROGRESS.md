## BorelEquivalenceRelations: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-08 (`4769d20` to `d2083f0`)

The first summit is in: the Kechris–Solecki–Todorcevic [G₀ dichotomy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/GZeroDichotomy.html#SimpleGraph.g0_dichotomy) says that a simple graph on a Polish space with analytic edge relation has a countable Borel coloring or receives a continuous homomorphism from G₀, and never both. It also comes in an [oriented form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/GZeroDichotomy.html#TauCeti.g0_dichotomy_oriented) for arbitrary analytic relations (TauCeti#25447). The proof is the fusion argument, built on [Miller's ideals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/SmallFamily/Basic.html#SetRel.IsSmallFamily) of non-small families of assignments, with amalgamation across a fresh edge, vertex shrinking and witness extension, and on the [Kechris–Solecki–Todorcevic lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/BorelColoring.html#SetRel.exists_measurableSet_isIndependent_superset), which puts an analytic independent set inside a Borel one.

On the category side, the [Kuratowski–Ulam theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Baire/KuratowskiUlam.html#BaireMeasurableSet.eventually_eventually_left_iff_mem_residual) landed in the shapes of the Mathlib proposal, together with [Miller's independence lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/GSGraph.html#TauCeti.exists_gSGraph_adj_of_not_isMeagre) for the graphs G_S. These combine into the [category route](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/Silver.html#Setoid.hasCantorAntichainOn_range_of_forall_gSGraph_adj): a continuous map sending G_S-edges to inequivalent pairs carries a Cantor antichain. With Mycielski's theorem, this gives Silver's theorem, which the library now states in its Polish, standard Borel, cardinality and relative forms. Below this sits the Layer 0 infrastructure: word cylinders and coordinate flips on Cantor space, Gδ subspaces, closure properties of analytic sets, the [Borel graph theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Constructions/Polish/Graph.html#TauCeti.measurableSet_graph_iff_measurable), the [perfect set theorem for Borel sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Constructions/Polish/Perfect.html#MeasurableSet.exists_nat_bool_injection_of_not_countable), and the vocabulary of Cantor and perfect antichains.

Layer 5 has Borel reducibility, with smoothness characterised as [countable separation of the quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/BorelReducibility.html#Setoid.isSmooth_iff_countablySeparated), and the [trichotomy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/NormalForms.html#Setoid.borelReducibility_trichotomy) at the bottom of the reducibility order. E₀ is [generically ergodic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/EZero.html#TauCeti.isMeagre_or_mem_residual_of_eZero_invariant) and [not smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/EZero.html#TauCeti.not_isSmooth_eZero), which gives the [easy half of Glimm–Effros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/EZero.html#TauCeti.not_isSmooth_of_borelReducible_eZero).
