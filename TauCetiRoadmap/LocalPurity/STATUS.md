- **Extension is detected on the completion**: for a Noetherian local ring of depth at least two, a cover extends across the closed point if and only if its base change to the completion does (0BLL; [`extends_iff_completion_extends_of_two_le_depth`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Completion.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRing.extends_iff_completion_extends_of_two_le_depth)).

- **Lifting from compatible quotient classes**: a cohomology class on `M` is recovered uniquely from compatible classes on the `M/fⁿM` once `Hⁱ(X, M)` is `f`-adically complete and the `f`-power torsion of the next group is uniformly bounded ([`existsUnique_cohomologyMapLinear_eq_of_isAdicComplete`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/ScalarQuotient.html#AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.existsUnique_cohomologyMapLinear_eq_of_isAdicComplete)). ### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Purity for a local ring**: the predicate that every finite étale cover of the punctured spectrum extends ([`PurityHolds`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/PuncturedSpectrum.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRing.PurityHolds)).
- **Full faithfulness across a depth-two locus**: on a locally Noetherian scheme, restricting finite étale schemes to an open is fully faithful when every local ring off the open has depth at least two ([`fullyFaithfulPullbackιOfTwoLeDepthStalk`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/FullyFaithful.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.fullyFaithfulPullbackιOfTwoLeDepthStalk)). ## The frontier

- **Restriction to a hypersurface section is full** (0BLJ, through 0BM6, 0EKS and 0EL8): faithfulness is in, and morphisms of étale covers already lift to every infinitesimal thickening. What remains is algebraizing them, the completion criterion 0EKP/0EKS, which needs SchemeAndStackFoundations' local cohomology and formal completion. With it, the existing henselian reduction gives the local Lefschetz theorem 0BLM.
- **The formal-section comparison** (0EII): the lifting lemma for compatible families is proved. It remains to identify the cohomology of the complement of `V(𝔞)` with local cohomology (SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.7) and to show that the sections are complete.
- **The formal glueing equivalence and finite extension from the completion** (05ER, 0BLH): the category of glueing data and the equivalence with modules remain, then their specialization to finite algebras over the cover of the punctured spectrum by the `D(f_i)`.
- **Purity in dimension at least three** (0BMA): the inductive step reduces to full faithfulness of restriction to `A/(f)`, so it waits on 0BLJ.
- **Spreading out and global extension** (0BQ5/0EY3, 0EY7, regular codimension two): these need SchemeAndStackFoundations' finite-presentation limits. After that, the regular codimension-two case is a gluing argument with the depth-two full faithfulness already in place.
