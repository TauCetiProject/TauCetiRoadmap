## LocalPurity: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-24 (`67f09bd` to `e177885`)

The discriminant criterion of Layer 0 is in: a finite free algebra is étale exactly when the discriminant of a basis is a unit ([`etale_iff_isUnit_discr`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/Discriminant.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_iff_isUnit_discr), Stacks 0BJF), proved over an arbitrary commutative base rather than only the local one the roadmap asks for (TauCeti#19153). Its field case, that a finite-dimensional algebra over a field is étale iff its [trace form is nondegenerate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/Discriminant.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_iff_traceForm_nondegenerate), and the [localization form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/Discriminant.html#TauCeti.Algebra.isUnit_discr_localization_iff) came with it, along with base change of discriminants and trace matrices.

On top of this, Krull's principal ideal theorem now shows that étaleness of a finite free algebra over a Noetherian ring is [detected on fibres over primes of height at most one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/Purity.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_iff_etale_fiber_of_height_le_one). As a consequence, over a Noetherian local ring of dimension at least two, a [finite flat algebra unramified away from the closed point is étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/Purity.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_of_isUnramifiedAt_of_two_le_ringKrullDim). This is the discriminant step of purity for regular local rings, but it assumes flatness, which miracle flatness is supposed to supply and which has not landed.

Work toward that flatness has started. Regular local rings are now [Cohen–Macaulay](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/CohenMacaulay.html#TauCeti.IsRegularLocalRing.isCohenMacaulay): a regular system of parameters is a regular sequence, so depth equals dimension. Over a two-dimensional regular local ring, any pair generating the maximal ideal is [regular on a finite normal local algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/FiniteNormal.html#TauCeti.IsRegularLocalRing.isRegular_pair_of_finite_normal), which is the input for the dimension-two flatness lemma. Nothing about punctured spectra or extending covers has landed yet.

## LocalPurity: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-12 (`e177885` to `941bb58`)

Purity now holds for regular local rings of dimension two: every finite étale cover of the punctured spectrum of such a ring extends to a finite étale cover of the whole spectrum ([`purityHolds_of_ringKrullDim_eq_two`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Purity.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRing.purityHolds_of_ringKrullDim_eq_two), the dimension-two case of Stacks 0BMA). Its algebraic form, that a finite normal torsion-free algebra étale away from the closed point is [étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Purity.html#TauCeti.Algebra.etale_of_finiteNormal_of_ringKrullDim_eq_two), came with it. Two results of independent interest carry the proof. The first is [miracle flatness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/MiracleFlatness.html#TauCeti.Algebra.flat_of_isCohenMacaulay) in Stacks' general form (00R4): a local homomorphism from a regular local ring to a Cohen–Macaulay one that satisfies the dimension formula is flat. The second is the corrected 0BM9 anti-equivalence [`finiteNormalEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/FiniteNormal.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRing.finiteNormalEquiv), between finite étale covers of the punctured spectrum of a normal local domain of dimension at least two and finite normal torsion-free algebras étale off the closed point (TauCeti#28830).

Underneath, the punctured spectrum and [purity for a local ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/PuncturedSpectrum.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRing.PurityHolds) are now defined. Restriction to the punctured spectrum is faithful in positive depth, and a cover [extends exactly when its sections are finite étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Extension.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRing.extends_iff_sections_finiteEtale) (0BLK). A Hartogs principle for depth at least two makes restriction of finite étale schemes [fully faithful](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/FullyFaithful.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.fullyFaithfulPullbackιOfTwoLeDepthStalk) on a locally Noetherian scheme, which is the gluing input for the global extension theorem. The roadmap's counterexample [`A × κ(A)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Etale/ProdResidueField.html#TauCeti.IsLocalRing.not_etale_prod_residueField), which shows why torsion-freeness is needed, has landed, as has a square-zero example showing that miracle flatness needs the Cohen–Macaulay hypothesis.

Work toward dimension three and up has begun but is not finished. Formal gluing along a faithfully flat map, which is Stacks 05ER for [module homomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FormalGlueing/Morphisms.html#LinearMap.existsUnique_formalGlueing_lift), now has finiteness detection and a Koszul–Ext comparison. Separately, finite étale schemes over a henselian quotient [lift](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Henselian.html#TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.essSurj_pullback_quotient). The full faithfulness of restriction to a hypersurface section is still missing, and so are the local Lefschetz theorem and the global extension across points where purity holds.
