## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Kadeishvili's minimal-model theorem for `A∞` algebras over a field and his intrinsic formality criterion are both proved, and transferred `m₃` is identified with triple Massey products. Layers 0–5 and 8 are partial. They have `A∞` functors, DG bimodules, Hochschild cochains and twisted objects, but nothing triangulated or derived. Layers 6, 7 and 9–11 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Kadeishvili's theorem** — over a field, the cohomology of an `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) with the induced product and an `A∞` quasi-isomorphism to the algebra that is the identity on cohomology, unique up to a non-canonical `A∞` isomorphism.
- **Kadeishvili's intrinsic formality criterion** — over a field, a graded algebra `H` with `HH^{n,2-n}(H,H) = 0` for all `n ≥ 3` is [intrinsically formal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/IntrinsicFormality/Criterion.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIntrinsicallyFormal_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt), meaning every `A∞` algebra with that cohomology algebra is formal.
- **`m₃` and the triple Massey product** — when `xy = 0` and `yz = 0`, the transferred `m₃(x,y,z)` lies in `⟨x,y,z⟩`, and the [Massey product is exactly its coset](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Massey.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.mem_tripleMasseyProduct_iff_sub_minimalModel_m_three_mem) by the indeterminacy. For `Λ(a,b,c)` with `dc = ab` this gives a [nonzero `m₃([a],[a],[b]) = [ac]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Heisenberg.html#TauCeti.HeisenbergDGA.minimalModel_m_three), which is the README's acceptance example.
- **Homological transfer** — a special contraction [transfers](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer) an `A∞` structure. Over any commutative ring, this gives an `A∞` homotopy equivalence with an [explicit homotopy](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transferHomotopy). It now also [transfers `A∞` categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Transfer.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory.transfer) along contractions that respect Hom modules.
- **The Yoneda lemma for right `A∞` modules** — over a strictly unital `A∞` algebra, evaluation at the unit is a [homotopy equivalence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.yonedaHomotopyEquiv) from the morphism complex out of the free module to the module. On the DG side, representables over any DG category satisfy the [Yoneda lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Representable.html#TauCeti.dgYonedaEquiv).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **`A∞` functors** ([`AInfinityFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Functor/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityFunctor)) — object maps with `A∞` morphisms of total morphism algebras, carrying higher components. They compose, and unital ones induce functors on `H⁰`.
- **The Hochschild complex of an `A∞` algebra** ([`hochschildDifferential_hochschildDifferential`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.hochschildDifferential_hochschildDifferential)) — braces, the Gerstenhaber bracket, the differential `[m, -]` with `m{m} = 0`, and cohomology with its bracket. This is the language of obstruction theory and gauge equivalence.
- **Twisted objects of a DG category** ([`DGTwistedObject`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/Basic.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject)) — Maurer–Cartan twists on the additive closure of the shift closure. [Cones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/Cone.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject.cone) of homotopic morphisms are isomorphic in `H⁰`, and cones of isomorphisms are contractible.

## The frontier

- **The pretriangulated envelope** — show that `H⁰` of twisted objects is triangulated with cone triangles `X ⟶ Y ⟶ Cone(f) ⟶ X[1]`, then build `tria` and `Perf`.
- **`A∞` homotopy inverses** — show that over a field every `A∞` quasi-isomorphism is invertible up to homotopy.
- **`A∞` transformations and functor categories** — now that non-strict functors compose, build transformations, the `A∞` functor category, and the categorical Yoneda functor.
- **Bimodule composition** — give the new `A∞` bimodules composition by bar tensor product, and add a derived tensor on the DG side. Both are prerequisites for Layers 6 and 9.
- **Kadeishvili beyond algebras, and the module equivalence** — category and module minimal models, then `D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A, m₂, m₃, …)`. This also needs the derived invariance of Layer 6.
