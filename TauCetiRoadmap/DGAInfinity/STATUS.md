## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Kadeishvili's minimal-model theorem holds for `A∞` algebras, categories and right modules over a field, and the minimal model's higher operations now compute higher Massey products. Twisted objects give a triangulated envelope, and the cobar construction has its universal property. Measured against the rewritten README, Layers 0–5 and 8 are all partial. Nothing derived (`D(A)`, `Perf`, Morita theory) has begun, and Layers 6, 7 and 9–11 are untouched.

### Named results

- **Kadeishvili's theorem** — over a field, the cohomology of an `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) with the induced product, quasi-isomorphic to the algebra and unique up to `A∞` isomorphism. It also holds for [`A∞` categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory.minimalModel), where the inclusion is [a quasi-equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory.isQuasiEquivalence_minimalModelInclusion) in the cohomologically unital case, and for [right modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Minimal/Model.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.minimalModel).
- **Kadeishvili's intrinsic formality criterion** — over a field, a graded algebra `H` with `HH^{n,2-n}(H,H) = 0` for all `n ≥ 3` is [intrinsically formal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/IntrinsicFormality/Criterion.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIntrinsicallyFormal_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt).
- **Massey products from the minimal model** — the triple Massey product is [exactly the coset of the transferred `m₃`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Massey.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.mem_tripleMasseyProduct_iff_sub_minimalModel_m_three_mem) by its indeterminacy. Over a field, once `m_k` vanishes on all shorter consecutive blocks, `mₙ(x₁, …, xₙ)` [lies in the `n`-fold Massey product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Massey.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel_taylor_subword_mem_masseyProduct).
- **The triangulated pretriangulated envelope** — `H⁰` of twisted objects is [triangulated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject.isTriangulated), and up to isomorphism the one-sided twisted complexes are [the triangulated closure of `C`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/OneSided.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject.isLeast_isoClosure_oneSided).
- **The universal property of the cobar construction** — for a noncounital DG coalgebra `C`, DG algebra maps `ΩC → A` [correspond bijectively to twisting cochains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Coalgebra/Cobar/TwistingCochain.html#TauCeti.IsNonCounitalDGCoalgebra.cobarTwistingCochainEquiv) `C → A`, naturally in `A`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Homological transfer** ([`transfer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer)) moves an `A∞` structure along a special contraction by perturbing the bar construction. Every minimal model above comes from it.
- **The Hochschild complex** ([`hochschildDifferential_hochschildDifferential`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.hochschildDifferential_hochschildDifferential)) is built from braces and the Gerstenhaber bracket. Deformations, obstructions to formality and the intrinsic formality criterion are all stated in it.
- **The DG Karoubi envelope** ([`DGKaroubi`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Karoubi.html#TauCeti.DGKaroubi)) adjoins images of closed idempotents. `C` embeds in it isomorphically on Hom complexes, so this is the model on which `Perf(C)` is to be identified.

## The frontier

- **The bar side of bar–cobar, and `U(A)`.** The cobar universal property is in, and the bar construction is a noncounital DG coalgebra. Next comes identifying twisting cochains with coalgebra maps into the bar construction, then the quasi-isomorphism `A → ΩB∞A` over a field.
- **Derived categories and `Perf`.** Define `D(A)` and `D(C)` as localizations at quasi-isomorphisms. Then identify `H⁰` of the DG Karoubi envelope with `Perf(C)` and with the compact objects.
- **`A∞` homotopy inverses.** Over a field, every `A∞` quasi-isomorphism should be invertible up to homotopy. The module isomorphism criterion and the homotopy complex are in place.
- **Massey products, finished.** Compare the block-defined products with May's, show that `A∞` quasi-isomorphisms carry triple products onto each other, and prove that vanishing transferred operations give formality.
- **Bimodule composition and the derived tensor product.** `A∞` bimodules now have quasi-isomorphisms but no bar tensor product, and the DG side has no derived tensor. Layers 6 and 9 need both.
