## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 3 has reached its first summit: Kadeishvili's minimal-model theorem, for `A∞` algebras over a field, including uniqueness. Layers 0–4 are all partial. The single-object theory is far ahead of the many-object and module theory, since there are still no `A∞` categories, `A∞` modules or DG bimodules. Layers 5–11 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Kadeishvili's theorem** — over a field, the cohomology of an `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) with the induced product. It has an `A∞` quasi-isomorphism to the algebra that is the identity on cohomology, and it is unique up to a non-canonical `A∞` isomorphism ([`isIso_comp_minimalModelInclusion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIso_comp_minimalModelInclusion)).
- **Homological transfer** — a special contraction of `(A, m₁)` onto `H` transfers the `A∞` structure to `H` ([`TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer)). It does so by perturbing the bar construction, with quasi-isomorphisms in both directions and `m₃` in Merkulov's form ([`m_three_transfer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.m_three_transfer)).
- **`b² = 0` is the Stasheff identities** — the bar coderivation squares to zero exactly when every unsuspended Stasheff identity holds ([`TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff)).
- **Invertibility of `A∞` morphisms** — an `A∞` morphism is invertible exactly when its linear part is bijective ([`TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isIso_iff_linearPart_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Iso.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isIso_iff_linearPart_bijective)). This is what turns quasi-isomorphisms between minimal algebras into isomorphisms.
- **The basic perturbation lemma** — a special contraction perturbed by `δ`, with `1 + δh` invertible, gives a contraction with the classical formulas ([`TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.perturb`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction/Perturbation.html#TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.perturb)). It is the engine behind transfer.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 0** is partial. Gradings, Koszul-signed complexes and their enrichment, tensor coalgebras (reduced, coaugmented and completed), coderivations and the Stasheff bridge are in place, and so are operations along quiver paths. What is missing is signed substitution of operations along paths. **Layer 1** is partial. DG algebras and DG categories, now with tensor products, are done, and right DG modules have the free-module Yoneda lemma and a cohomology functor. Bimodules, extension of scalars, tensor and internal Hom of modules, and the Yoneda embedding are missing. **Layer 2** is partial: `A∞` algebras and morphisms exist, with opposites, but tensor products, homotopies, `A∞` categories, modules, bimodules and unit strictification are missing. **Layer 3** is partial: minimality, formality, normalized contractions, transfer and Kadeishvili's theorem for algebras over a field are in place. The tree-sum formulas, naturality, the complete-filtered perturbation lemma, intrinsic formality, the category, module and bimodule versions, and the module equivalence `D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A)` remain. **Layer 4** is partial, with only the bar construction and arity-nilpotent Maurer–Cartan elements. **Layers 5–11** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Kadeishvili beyond algebras** — the category, module and bimodule versions of the minimal model, then the module equivalence `D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A, m₂, m₃, …)`. These need `A∞` categories and modules, and the module equivalence also needs Layer 6.
- **`A∞` categories (Layer 2)** — define them on graded linear quivers using the new path operations. This first needs signed substitution of path operations, the last Layer 0 item.
- **`A∞` modules** — the comodule-coderivation structure `b^M`, whose square-zero criterion now exists for graded coderivations of comodules, with module morphisms and the Yoneda lemma.
- **`A∞` homotopies and homotopy inverses** — homotopies of `A∞` morphisms, and the theorem that quasi-isomorphisms are invertible up to homotopy over a field.
- **Tree formulas and filtered transfer** — identify the transferred operations with planar-tree sums in all arities and prove the perturbation lemma for complete filtered contractions.
