## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is nearly done. Its one open item is the dependent multilinear maps along paths in a graded quiver. Layers 1 and 2 are substantially started at the level of single-object DG and `A∞` algebras and DG categories, but bimodules, `A∞` categories and `A∞` modules are still missing. Layer 3 has minimality, formality and the perturbation lemma but not the transfer theorem, and Layer 4 has only the bar construction and Maurer–Cartan elements. Layers 5–11 have not begun.

### Named results

- **`b² = 0` is the Stasheff identities** — for an `A∞` algebra, the bar coderivation squares to zero exactly when every unsuspended Stasheff identity holds on homogeneous inputs, with the arity 1–4 equations stated verbatim ([`TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff)).
- **Invertibility of `A∞` morphisms** — an `A∞` morphism has a two-sided `A∞` inverse exactly when its linear part is bijective ([`TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isIso_iff_linearPart_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Iso.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isIso_iff_linearPart_bijective)). So between minimal algebras, quasi-isomorphisms are isomorphisms ([`TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isQuasiIso_iff_isIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Minimal.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isQuasiIso_iff_isIso)).
- **The basic perturbation lemma** — a special contraction perturbed by `δ`, with `1 + δh` invertible, gives a contraction with the classical formulas `i - hXi`, `p - pXh`, `h - hXh` ([`TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.perturb`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction/Perturbation.html#TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.perturb)). The companion **tensor trick** lifts contractions to reduced tensor coalgebras compatibly with coderivation perturbations ([`TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.reducedTensorWords`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction/TensorTrick.html#TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.reducedTensorWords)).
- **Quasi-equivalences induce equivalences on `H⁰`** — a quasi-equivalence of DG categories gives an equivalence of homotopy categories ([`CategoryTheory.EnrichedFunctor.IsQuasiEquivalence.isEquivalence_mapDGHomotopyCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/QuasiEquivalence.html#CategoryTheory.EnrichedFunctor.IsQuasiEquivalence.isEquivalence_mapDGHomotopyCategory)).
- **The Koszul braiding on cochain complexes** — `CochainComplex (ModuleCat R) ℤ` is symmetric monoidal with the sign `(-1)^{|x||y|}`, and cochain complexes are enriched over it through the `R`-linear Hom complex ([`TauCeti.koszulBraidedCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Monoidal/Braiding.html#TauCeti.koszulBraidedCategory)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **`A∞` algebras and morphisms** ([`TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra), [`TauCeti.AInfinityHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom)) — square-zero coderivations and bar-coalgebra maps, with unsuspended operations and components exposed. They come with strict, strictly unital and cohomologically unital variants, and DG algebras embed as the `m_{n≥3} = 0` case.
- **DG categories in two presentations** ([`TauCeti.DGCategoryData.equivDGCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/HomComplexData.html#TauCeti.DGCategoryData.equivDGCategory)) — enrichment in cochain complexes is equivalent to explicit Hom-complex data. The one-object category of a DG algebra and the DG category of right modules are built this way.
- **The completed tensor coalgebra** ([`TauCeti.CompletedTensorWords`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Completed.html#TauCeti.CompletedTensorWords)) — the product over tensor lengths, with coassociative deconcatenation and a complete separated length filtration. It is the carrier for the complete-filtration regime of Layers 3–4.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 0** is partial: only dependent multilinear maps along composable quiver paths remain. Graded duals exist for gradings with finitely many nonzero pieces, and graded linear quivers are defined. **Layer 1** is partial. DG algebras (nonunital, unital, augmented) are done with morphisms, opposites, tensor products and cohomology. DG categories are done with `Z⁰`, `H⁰`, functors, natural transformations, opposites, full subcategories and quasi-equivalences, and so are the one-object and endomorphism conversions. Right DG modules have their DG category, restriction of scalars and the Yoneda lemma for the free module. Tensor DG categories, bimodules, extension of scalars, tensor and internal Hom of modules, and the Yoneda embedding are missing. **Layer 2** is partial: it has `A∞` algebras, units, augmentations and morphisms, with composition, strictness, quasi-isomorphisms and isomorphisms. It lacks opposites and tensor products, homotopies, the inverse-up-to-homotopy theorem, `A∞` categories, functors, modules, bimodules and unit strictification. **Layer 3** is partial. It has normalized contractions, minimality and formality (but not intrinsic formality), and the perturbation lemma with the tensor trick. Tree transfer, Kadeishvili's theorem and the module equivalence remain. **Layer 4** is partial: it has the coaugmented bar construction with its square-zero differential and Maurer–Cartan elements in the arity-nilpotent regime (in the DG case, `dα = α²`). Cobar, twisting cochains, `U(A)`, twisted operations and the other regimes remain. **Layers 5–11** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Homological transfer** — assemble the perturbation lemma, the tensor trick and the proved invertibility for the higher bar differential into a transferred `A∞` structure on a contraction's target with an `A∞` quasi-isomorphism. Then prove Kadeishvili's theorem over a field.
- **`A∞` categories and modules (Layer 2)** — `A∞` categories on graded linear quivers are the next definition. They need the one missing Layer 0 item, dependent multilinear maps along composable paths.
- **DG bimodules and tensor products (Layer 1)** — `(A,B)`-bimodules, extension of scalars, tensor and internal Hom, the Yoneda embedding for DG categories, and tensor DG categories.
- **`A∞` homotopies and homotopy inverses** — define homotopies of `A∞` morphisms, and prove that quasi-isomorphisms are invertible up to homotopy over a field.
- **Bar–cobar and twisting (Layer 4)** — the cobar construction and twisting cochains are the next targets, along with twisted operations for Maurer–Cartan elements in the finite and complete-filtration regimes.
