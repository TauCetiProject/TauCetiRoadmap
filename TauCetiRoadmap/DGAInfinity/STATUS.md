## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Kadeishvili's minimal-model theorem for `A∞` algebras over a field is proved, and transfer now gives an `A∞` homotopy equivalence. Right `A∞` modules have arrived with morphisms, homotopies and quasi-isomorphisms, but there are still no `A∞` categories or bimodules. Layers 0–4 are partial and Layers 5–11 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Kadeishvili's theorem** — over a field, the cohomology of an `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) with the induced product and an `A∞` quasi-isomorphism to the algebra that is the identity on cohomology. It is unique up to a non-canonical `A∞` isomorphism ([`isIso_comp_minimalModelInclusion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIso_comp_minimalModelInclusion)).
- **Homological transfer as a homotopy equivalence** — a special contraction transfers the `A∞` structure ([`transfer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer)), with `m₃` in Merkulov's form. Over any commutative ring, the identity is [homotopic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transferHomotopy) to the transferred inclusion composed with the transferred projection. The construction is natural for compatible strict morphisms ([`transferStrictHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Naturality.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transferStrictHom)).
- **`b² = 0` is the Stasheff identities** — the bar coderivation squares to zero exactly when every unsuspended Stasheff identity holds ([`barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff)). The module version gives the [module Stasheff equations](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Components.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.stasheff).
- **Invertibility of `A∞` morphisms** — an `A∞` morphism is invertible exactly when its linear part is bijective ([`isIso_iff_linearPart_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Iso.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isIso_iff_linearPart_bijective)).
- **The DG Yoneda embedding is fully faithful** — for a DG algebra, viewed as a one-object DG category, the Yoneda embedding into right DG modules is an isomorphism on Hom complexes ([`isIso_map_dgYonedaEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.isIso_map_dgYonedaEmbedding)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Right `A∞` modules** ([`AInfinityRightModule`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule)) — square-zero coderivations of the cofree bar comodule `sM ⊗ Tᶜ(sA)`, with [morphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Hom/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModuleHom), homotopies, cohomology and quasi-isomorphisms. This is the input the module half of Kadeishvili and the derived-module layers need.
- **Homotopies of `A∞` morphisms** ([`AInfinityHom.Homotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.Homotopy)) — coderivations along a pair of bar maps, determined by their letter component. Their linear part is a chain homotopy, and homotopic maps agree on cohomology.
- **Koszul-signed substitution** ([`signedCompMultilinearMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/GradedModule/Multilinear/Composition.html#TauCeti.InternalGrading.signedCompMultilinearMap)) — simultaneous substitution of homogeneous operations with dependent input modules, general enough for Hom modules along quiver paths. It is the composition law `A∞` categories will need.

## The frontier

- **`A∞` homotopy inverses** — prove that over a field every `A∞` quasi-isomorphism is invertible up to homotopy. Homotopies, transfer-as-homotopy-equivalence and Kadeishvili's theorem now supply all the ingredients.
- **The `A∞` module Yoneda lemma and the DG category of modules** — the free rank-one module and module homotopies exist. What is missing is the morphism complexes and the Yoneda comparison.
- **`A∞` categories (Layer 2)** — define them on graded linear quivers by applying the new signed substitution to suspended path operations, together with functors and `H⁰`.
- **Kadeishvili beyond algebras** — the module and category versions of the minimal model, then `D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A, m₂, m₃, …)`. These need the items above, and the equivalence also needs Layer 6.
- **Tree formulas and filtered transfer** — identify the transferred operations with planar-tree sums in all arities, and prove the perturbation lemma for complete filtered contractions.
