## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Kadeishvili's minimal-model theorem now holds for `A∞` algebras, categories and right modules over a field, and the homotopy category of twisted objects is triangulated. Layer 0 is done. Layers 1–5 and 8 are partial, and nothing derived (`Perf`, derived modules, Morita theory) has begun. Layers 6, 7 and 9–11 are untouched.

### Named results

- **Kadeishvili's theorem** — over a field, the cohomology of an `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) with the induced product, quasi-isomorphic to the algebra and unique up to `A∞` isomorphism. It also holds for [`A∞` categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory.minimalModel), where the comparison functors are quasi-equivalences for cohomologically unital categories, and for [right modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Minimal/Model.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.minimalModel).
- **Kadeishvili's intrinsic formality criterion** — over a field, a graded algebra `H` with `HH^{n,2-n}(H,H) = 0` for all `n ≥ 3` is [intrinsically formal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/IntrinsicFormality/Criterion.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIntrinsicallyFormal_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt).
- **`m₃` and the triple Massey product** — the triple Massey product is [exactly the coset of the transferred `m₃`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Massey.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.mem_tripleMasseyProduct_iff_sub_minimalModel_m_three_mem) by its indeterminacy. For `Λ(a,b,c)` with `dc = ab` it gives the README's [nonzero `m₃([a],[a],[b]) = [ac]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Heisenberg.html#TauCeti.HeisenbergDGA.minimalModel_m_three).
- **The triangulated pretriangulated envelope** — `H⁰` of twisted objects is [triangulated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject.isTriangulated), with the cone triangles `X ⟶ Y ⟶ Cone(f) ⟶ X[1]` as distinguished triangles. The one-sided twisted complexes make up, up to isomorphism, [the triangulated closure of `C`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/OneSided.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject.isLeast_isoClosure_oneSided).
- **Gerstenhaber obstruction theory for `A∞` structures** — over any commutative ring, deformations are built order by order in brace language, and [vanishing degree-two Hochschild cohomology extends every finite-order deformation to a formal one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Deformation.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.IsDeformationOfOrder.exists_isFormalDeformation).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Homological transfer** ([`transfer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer)) — moves an `A∞` structure along a special contraction by perturbing the bar construction. It is the engine behind every minimal model above, and it works the same way for categories and [modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Transfer.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.transfer).
- **`A∞` functors and quasi-equivalences** ([`AInfinityFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Functor/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityFunctor)) — non-strict functors with higher components. They compose, and a quasi-equivalence [induces an equivalence of homotopy categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Functor/QuasiEquivalence.html#TauCeti.AInfinityFunctor.IsQuasiEquivalence.isEquivalence_mapHomotopyCategory).
- **The Hochschild complex** ([`hochschildDifferential_hochschildDifferential`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.hochschildDifferential_hochschildDifferential)) — braces, the Gerstenhaber bracket and `[m, -]`. It now has a [normalized version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/Normalized.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.PositiveHochschild.normalizedComplex) for strict units and a [version for `A∞` categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Hochschild/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory.PositiveHochschild.complex) on cochains along composable paths. Deformation theory, formality and Massey products all live in this language.

## The frontier

- **`tria`, `Perf` and idempotent completion** — the triangulated closure is in, so what remains is to compare `H⁰` of the DG Karoubi envelope with the idempotent completion and to identify the result with compact objects and finite semi-free modules.
- **`A∞` homotopy inverses** — show that over a field every `A∞` quasi-isomorphism is invertible up to homotopy, now that transformations and homotopies of morphisms share one complex.
- **`A∞` functor categories and the Yoneda functor** — extend transformations from algebra morphisms to `A∞` functors, then build the functor category and the categorical Yoneda functor.
- **Bimodule composition and the derived tensor** — `A∞` bimodules now have morphisms and homotopies but no bar tensor product, and the DG side has no derived tensor. Layers 6 and 9 need both.
- **`D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A, m₂, m₃, …)`** — the algebra and module minimal models are in place. This still needs derived module categories (Layer 5) and the derived invariance of Layer 6.
