## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Kadeishvili's two theorems for `A∞` algebras over a field are proved: the minimal model on cohomology and the intrinsic formality criterion. DG bimodules, `A∞` functors, Hochschild cochains, triple Massey products and twisted objects now exist, so Layers 0–5 and 8 are partial. Layers 6, 7 and 9–11 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Kadeishvili's minimal model theorem** — over a field, the cohomology of an `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) with the induced product and an `A∞` quasi-isomorphism to the algebra, unique up to a non-canonical `A∞` isomorphism ([`isIso_comp_minimalModelInclusion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIso_comp_minimalModelInclusion)).
- **Kadeishvili's intrinsic formality criterion** — over a field, a graded algebra `H` with `HH^{n,2-n}(H,H) = 0` for every `n ≥ 3` is intrinsically formal ([`isIntrinsicallyFormal_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/IntrinsicFormality/Criterion.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIntrinsicallyFormal_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt)), proved by killing obstructions with gauge transformations. It is stated as a sufficient condition only.
- **The transferred `m₃` is a Massey product** — when `xy = 0` and `yz = 0`, the triple Massey product `⟨x, y, z⟩` is exactly the coset of `m₃(x, y, z)` by the indeterminacy ([`mem_tripleMasseyProduct_iff_sub_minimalModel_m_three_mem`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Massey.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.mem_tripleMasseyProduct_iff_sub_minimalModel_m_three_mem)). For the roadmap's example `Λ(a, b, c)`, `dc = ab`, the transferred `m₃` is [nonzero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Heisenberg.html#TauCeti.HeisenbergDGA.minimalModel_m_three_ne_zero).
- **Homological transfer as a homotopy equivalence** — a special contraction [transfers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer) an `A∞` structure, and over any commutative ring the transferred maps are inverse up to an [explicit homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transferHomotopy).
- **The Yoneda lemma for right `A∞` modules** — over a strictly unital `A∞` algebra, evaluation at the unit is a [homotopy equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.yonedaHomotopyEquiv) from the morphism complex out of the free module to the module, provided the unit is a right unit for the module's `m₂`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Braces and the Gerstenhaber bracket** ([`brace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Brace.html#TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.brace)) — the pre-Lie product on Hochschild cochains, with `m{m} = 0` equivalent to `b² = 0` and the bracket descending to positive-arity Hochschild cohomology. This is the language for Layer 8's deformation theory and for factorial-free gauge equivalence.
- **`A∞` functors** ([`AInfinityFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Functor/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityFunctor)) — non-strict functors between `A∞` categories, with composition and the induced functor on `H⁰` for unital ones. They are the prerequisite for transformations, functor categories and the categorical Yoneda functor.
- **Twisted objects of a DG category** ([`DGTwistedObject`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/Basic.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject)) — Maurer–Cartan twists over the shift and additive closures, with the pinned shift signs, twisted differential and cones. They are the first step toward the pretriangulated envelope.

### Roadmap coverage

Left modules and the full Hom-complex Yoneda embedding for a general DG category are not established here. **Layer 2** is partial: functors, `H⁰`, the DG comparison for `mₙ = 0` (`n ≥ 3`) and restriction of modules along `A∞` morphisms landed. ## The frontier

- **`A∞` homotopy inverses** — over a field, every `A∞` quasi-isomorphism should be invertible up to homotopy. Homotopies, transfer and Kadeishvili's theorem supply the ingredients.
- **Pretriangulated envelope** — from twisted objects and cones, prove `H⁰` is triangulated with the cone triangle, then pass to `tria(C)` and `Perf(C)`.
- **`A∞` transformations and the Yoneda functor** — the `A∞` functor category on the new functors, representable modules over an `A∞` category, and `A∞` bimodules.
- **Kadeishvili beyond algebras** — module and category minimal models, then `D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A, m₂, m₃, …)`, which also needs Layer 6.
- **Hochschild deformation theory** — Hochschild chains, cup product, normalized cochains, higher Massey products and invariance under quasi-equivalence remain for Layer 8.
