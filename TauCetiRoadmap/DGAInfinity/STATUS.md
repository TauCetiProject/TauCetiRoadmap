## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Kadeishvili's theorem for `A∞` algebras over a field is proved. Right `A∞` modules now form a DG category and satisfy the Yoneda lemma for the free module, and `A∞` categories exist. ### Named results

- **Kadeishvili's theorem** — over a field, the cohomology of an `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) with the induced product, together with an `A∞` quasi-isomorphism to the algebra that is the identity on cohomology. This structure is unique up to a non-canonical `A∞` isomorphism ([`isIso_comp_minimalModelInclusion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIso_comp_minimalModelInclusion)).
- **Homological transfer as a homotopy equivalence** — a special contraction [transfers](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer) the `A∞` structure. Over any commutative ring, the transferred maps are inverse up to an [explicit homotopy](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transferHomotopy).
- **The Yoneda lemma for right `A∞` modules** — over a strictly unital `A∞` algebra, evaluation at the unit is a [homotopy equivalence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.yonedaHomotopyEquiv) from the morphism complex out of the free rank-one module to the module. The module must have the unit as a right unit for `m₂`.
- **`b² = 0` is the Stasheff identities** — the bar coderivation squares to zero exactly when every unsuspended Stasheff identity holds ([`barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff)).
- **The DG Yoneda embedding is fully faithful** — for a DG algebra, viewed as a one-object DG category, the Yoneda embedding into right DG modules is an isomorphism on Hom complexes ([`isIso_map_dgYonedaEmbedding`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.isIso_map_dgYonedaEmbedding)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The DG category of right `A∞` modules** ([`instDGCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/DGCategory.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModuleCat.instDGCategory)) — morphism complexes of bar-comodule cochains, in which module morphisms are the [closed degree-zero cochains](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Hom/Complex.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModuleHom.equivZeroCocycles). This is the setting for derived `A∞` modules and the module half of Kadeishvili.

The library also has strict units, `H⁰`, opposites, full subcategories, strict functors, and the passage from DG categories.
- **Homotopies of `A∞` morphisms** ([`AInfinityHom.Homotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.Homotopy)) — coderivations along a pair of bar maps. Homotopic maps agree on cohomology, which the homotopy-inverse theorem needs.

## The frontier

- **`A∞` homotopy inverses** — prove that over a field every `A∞` quasi-isomorphism is invertible up to homotopy. Homotopies, transfer and Kadeishvili's theorem supply the ingredients.
- **`A∞` functors and the categorical Yoneda functor** — non-strict functors with higher components, transformations and functor categories on the new `A∞` categories. These lead to representable modules over an `A∞` category and the conversion back from `A∞` categories with `mₙ = 0` for `n ≥ 3` to DG categories.
- **Kadeishvili beyond algebras** — module and category minimal models, and then `D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A, m₂, m₃, …)`. The equivalence also needs Layer 6.
- **Bimodules** — DG and `A∞` `(A, B)`-bimodules with bar tensor composition. These are a prerequisite for the smoothness and Calabi–Yau statements of Layer 9 and the bimodule duals of Layer 11.
- **Tree formulas and filtered transfer** — identify transferred operations with planar-tree sums in all arities, and prove the perturbation lemma for complete filtered contractions.
