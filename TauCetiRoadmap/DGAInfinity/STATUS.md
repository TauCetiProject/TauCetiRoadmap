## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Kadeishvili's minimal-model theorem holds for `A∞` algebras, categories and right modules over a field. The homotopy category of twisted objects is triangulated, and the cobar construction now has its universal property. Layer 0 is done, and Layers 1–5 and 8 are partial. Nothing derived (`Perf`, derived modules, Morita theory) has begun, and Layers 6, 7 and 9–11 are untouched.

### Named results

- **Kadeishvili's theorem** — over a field, the cohomology of an `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) with the induced product, quasi-isomorphic to the algebra and unique up to `A∞` isomorphism. It also holds for [`A∞` categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory.minimalModel) and [right modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Minimal/Model.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.minimalModel).
- **Kadeishvili's intrinsic formality criterion** — over a field, a graded algebra `H` with `HH^{n,2-n}(H,H) = 0` for all `n ≥ 3` is [intrinsically formal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/IntrinsicFormality/Criterion.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIntrinsicallyFormal_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt).
- **`m₃` and the triple Massey product** — the triple Massey product is [exactly the coset of the transferred `m₃`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Massey.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.mem_tripleMasseyProduct_iff_sub_minimalModel_m_three_mem) by its indeterminacy. For `Λ(a,b,c)` with `dc = ab` it gives the README's [nonzero `m₃([a],[a],[b]) = [ac]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Heisenberg.html#TauCeti.HeisenbergDGA.minimalModel_m_three).
- **The triangulated pretriangulated envelope** — `H⁰` of twisted objects is [triangulated](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject.isTriangulated), with cone triangles as distinguished triangles. Up to isomorphism, the one-sided twisted complexes are [the triangulated closure of `C`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/OneSided.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject.isLeast_isoClosure_oneSided).
- **The universal property of the cobar construction** — for a noncounital DG coalgebra `C`, DG algebra maps `ΩC → A` [correspond bijectively to twisting cochains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Coalgebra/Cobar/TwistingCochain.html#TauCeti.IsNonCounitalDGCoalgebra.cobarTwistingCochainEquiv) `C → A`, naturally in `A`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Homological transfer** ([`transfer`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer)) moves an `A∞` structure along a special contraction by perturbing the bar construction. Every minimal model above comes from it, and it works the same way for categories and [modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Transfer.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.transfer).
- **`A∞` functors** ([`AInfinityFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Functor/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityFunctor)) are non-strict functors with higher components. They compose, and a quasi-equivalence [induces an equivalence of homotopy categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Functor/QuasiEquivalence.html#TauCeti.AInfinityFunctor.IsQuasiEquivalence.isEquivalence_mapHomotopyCategory).
- **The Hochschild complex** ([`hochschildDifferential_hochschildDifferential`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.hochschildDifferential_hochschildDifferential)) is built from braces, the Gerstenhaber bracket and `[m, -]`, with a normalized version for strict units and a version for `A∞` categories. Deformation theory, formality and Massey products all live in this language.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 0** is done. **Layer 1** lacks only the derived tensor product. **Layer 2** has algebras, categories, functors, modules, bimodules, morphisms and homotopies. It lacks tensor products, homotopy inverses over a field, transformations of `A∞` functors and functor categories, the Yoneda functor, bimodule composition and unit strictification. **Layer 3** has Kadeishvili's theorem for algebras, categories and modules. The perturbation lemma also works over complete filtrations, where the perturbation operator [raises the filtration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction/Perturbation/Filtered.html#TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.perturbationSeries_apply_mem). Missing are the planar-tree formulas, bimodule minimal models and `D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A)`. **Layer 4** has the bar construction and its completion, arity-nilpotent Maurer–Cartan elements, and now the cobar construction with twisting cochains and its universal property. It lacks the bar side of the adjunction, `U(A)` with its quasi-isomorphism from `A`, and twisted operations in the complete-filtration regime. **Layer 5** has the triangulated pretriangulated envelope, but not derived modules, `Perf` or the comparison with modules. **Layer 8** has Hochschild cochains, [Gerstenhaber obstruction theory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Deformation.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.IsDeformationOfOrder.exists_isFormalDeformation), triple Massey products, the formality criterion, and defining systems for higher Massey products with [their Massey cycle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Massey/Higher.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.MasseyDefiningSystem.cycle_mem_cycles). It lacks Morita invariance and the normalized-inclusion comparison, Deligne groupoids, and the statement that higher `m_n` encode defining systems. **Layers 6, 7 and 9–11** are untouched.

## The frontier

- **The bar side of bar–cobar, and `U(A)`.** The cobar universal property is in, and the bar construction of an `A∞` algebra is a noncounital DG coalgebra. Next comes identifying twisting cochains with coalgebra maps into the bar construction, then the quasi-isomorphism `A → ΩB∞A` over a field.
- **`tria`, `Perf` and idempotent completion.** The triangulated closure is in. Next, compare `H⁰` of the DG Karoubi envelope with the idempotent completion, then identify the result with compact objects and finite semi-free modules.
- **`A∞` homotopy inverses.** Show that over a field every `A∞` quasi-isomorphism is invertible up to homotopy. Transformations and homotopies of morphisms already share one complex.
- **Bimodule composition and the derived tensor product.** `A∞` bimodules have no bar tensor product, and the DG side has no derived tensor. Layers 6 and 9 need both.
- **`D(A) ≃ D_∞(H*A, m₂, m₃, …)`.** The algebra and module minimal models are in place. This still needs derived module categories (Layer 5) and the derived invariance of Layer 6.
