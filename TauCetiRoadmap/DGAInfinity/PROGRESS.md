## DGAInfinity: 2026-08-14 to 2026-09-01 (`87fafed` to `8745177`)

The first stretch of this roadmap is Layer 0, and its two named endpoints on the complex side have landed. Cochain complexes of `R`-modules carry the **Koszul braiding** ([`TauCeti.koszulBraidedCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Monoidal/Braiding.html#TauCeti.koszulBraidedCategory)), symmetric, with both hexagons proved at complex level, and cochain complexes in any `R`-linear preadditive category are **enriched in cochain complexes of `R`-modules** ([`TauCeti.linearHomComplexEnrichedCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/LinearHomComplex/Enrichment.html#TauCeti.linearHomComplexEnrichedCategory)) through the `R`-linear Hom complex: composition is a closed degree-zero map ([`TauCeti.linearHomComplexComp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/LinearHomComplex/Composition.html#TauCeti.linearHomComplexComp)), the underlying additive complex is Mathlib's `HomComplex` ([`TauCeti.forget₂LinearHomComplexIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/LinearHomComplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.forget₂LinearHomComplexIso)), and enriched composition is the braiding followed by Keller composition, so the comparison sign `(-1)^{pq}` is a theorem ([`TauCeti.ι_linearHomComplexEnrichedComp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/LinearHomComplex/Enrichment.html#TauCeti.ι_linearHomComplexEnrichedComp)).

On the coalgebra side, reduced deconcatenation is coassociative ([`TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.deconcatenation_coassoc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Coassoc.html#TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.deconcatenation_coassoc)), the conilpotence filtration and primitives are in place, and the **coderivation/Taylor correspondence** holds in both plain ([`TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.coderivEquivTaylor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Coderivation.html#TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.coderivEquivTaylor)) and Koszul-signed ([`TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.gradedCoderivEquivTaylor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/GradedCoderivation.html#TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.gradedCoderivEquivTaylor)) forms; the coaugmented tensor coalgebra carries a `Coalgebra` instance ([`TauCeti.TensorWords.instCoalgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Coaugmented.html#TauCeti.TensorWords.instCoalgebra)). The **Stasheff identities** exist in both encodings, and the suspended sum without the structural coefficient vanishes exactly when the unsuspended sum with `(-1)^{r+st}` does ([`TauCeti.AInfinity.suspendedStasheffSum_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Stasheff.html#TauCeti.AInfinity.suspendedStasheffSum_eq_zero_iff)). The arity 1–4 equations are evaluated verbatim: arity two is the Leibniz rule with sign `(-1)^{|a|}` ([`TauCeti.AInfinity.stasheffSum_two_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Stasheff.html#TauCeti.AInfinity.stasheffSum_two_eq_zero_iff)), arity three is associativity once `m₃ = 0` ([`TauCeti.AInfinity.stasheffSum_three_eq_zero_iff_of_m_three_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Stasheff.html#TauCeti.AInfinity.stasheffSum_three_eq_zero_iff_of_m_three_eq_zero)).

Supporting work: internal `ℤ`-gradings with shifts, tensor products, opposites, direct sums, and the comparison with Mathlib graded objects; homogeneous linear and multilinear maps, signed one-slot substitution, and the suspension degree bridge. From Layer 3, every contraction of cochain complexes normalizes to a strong deformation retract ([`TauCeti.Contraction.normalize`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction.html#TauCeti.Contraction.normalize)). What is not here: no `A∞` algebra has been defined, so the Stasheff sums are compared with each other, not with the arity components of `b² = 0`; the completed tensor coalgebra, duals, and dependent graded-quiver versions of Layer 0 have not started, nor has anything in Layers 1, 2, or 4 onward.

## DGAInfinity: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-27 (`8745177` to `b1ab119`)

The [square-zero bar-coderivation criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff) now says precisely that an uncurved A∞ algebra's bar differential squares to zero when its unsuspended operations satisfy every Stasheff identity. The first four arities are related explicitly to the coderivation square. [A∞ morphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom) are encoded by maps of reduced bar constructions, and a [quasi-isomorphism between minimal algebras is an isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Minimal.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isQuasiIso_iff_isIso). Strict units, augmentations, cohomology, and the DG algebra special case now have supporting constructions; transfer to minimal models has not landed.

On the DG side, the [differential graded Yoneda lemma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.dgYonedaIso) identifies the Hom complex out of the free rank-one right module with the underlying complex of a module. [Explicit Hom-complex data and enrichment give equivalent DG-category presentations](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/HomComplexData.html#TauCeti.DGCategoryData.equivDGCategory), and a [DG quasi-equivalence induces an equivalence on homotopy categories](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/QuasiEquivalence.html#CategoryTheory.EnrichedFunctor.IsQuasiEquivalence.isEquivalence_mapDGHomotopyCategory). The groundwork also gained [tensor-length completed words](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Completed.html#TauCeti.CompletedTensorWords); derived module equivalences and higher-category analogues remain to be built.
