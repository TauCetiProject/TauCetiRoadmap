## DGAInfinity: 2026-08-14 to 2026-09-01 (`87fafed` to `8745177`)

The first stretch of this roadmap is Layer 0, and its two named endpoints on the complex side have landed. Cochain complexes of `R`-modules carry the **Koszul braiding** ([`TauCeti.koszulBraidedCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Monoidal/Braiding.html#TauCeti.koszulBraidedCategory)), symmetric, with both hexagons proved at complex level, and cochain complexes in any `R`-linear preadditive category are **enriched in cochain complexes of `R`-modules** ([`TauCeti.linearHomComplexEnrichedCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/LinearHomComplex/Enrichment.html#TauCeti.linearHomComplexEnrichedCategory)) through the `R`-linear Hom complex: composition is a closed degree-zero map ([`TauCeti.linearHomComplexComp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/LinearHomComplex/Composition.html#TauCeti.linearHomComplexComp)), the underlying additive complex is Mathlib's `HomComplex` ([`TauCeti.forget₂LinearHomComplexIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/LinearHomComplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.forget₂LinearHomComplexIso)), and enriched composition is the braiding followed by Keller composition, so the comparison sign `(-1)^{pq}` is a theorem ([`TauCeti.ι_linearHomComplexEnrichedComp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/LinearHomComplex/Enrichment.html#TauCeti.ι_linearHomComplexEnrichedComp)).

On the coalgebra side, reduced deconcatenation is coassociative ([`TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.deconcatenation_coassoc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Coassoc.html#TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.deconcatenation_coassoc)), the conilpotence filtration and primitives are in place, and the **coderivation/Taylor correspondence** holds in both plain ([`TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.coderivEquivTaylor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Coderivation.html#TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.coderivEquivTaylor)) and Koszul-signed ([`TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.gradedCoderivEquivTaylor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/GradedCoderivation.html#TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.gradedCoderivEquivTaylor)) forms; the coaugmented tensor coalgebra carries a `Coalgebra` instance ([`TauCeti.TensorWords.instCoalgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Coaugmented.html#TauCeti.TensorWords.instCoalgebra)). The **Stasheff identities** exist in both encodings, and the suspended sum without the structural coefficient vanishes exactly when the unsuspended sum with `(-1)^{r+st}` does ([`TauCeti.AInfinity.suspendedStasheffSum_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Stasheff.html#TauCeti.AInfinity.suspendedStasheffSum_eq_zero_iff)). The arity 1–4 equations are evaluated verbatim: arity two is the Leibniz rule with sign `(-1)^{|a|}` ([`TauCeti.AInfinity.stasheffSum_two_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Stasheff.html#TauCeti.AInfinity.stasheffSum_two_eq_zero_iff)), arity three is associativity once `m₃ = 0` ([`TauCeti.AInfinity.stasheffSum_three_eq_zero_iff_of_m_three_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Stasheff.html#TauCeti.AInfinity.stasheffSum_three_eq_zero_iff_of_m_three_eq_zero)).

Supporting work: internal `ℤ`-gradings with shifts, tensor products, opposites, direct sums, and the comparison with Mathlib graded objects; homogeneous linear and multilinear maps, signed one-slot substitution, and the suspension degree bridge. From Layer 3, every contraction of cochain complexes normalizes to a strong deformation retract ([`TauCeti.Contraction.normalize`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction.html#TauCeti.Contraction.normalize)). What is not here: no `A∞` algebra has been defined, so the Stasheff sums are compared with each other, not with the arity components of `b² = 0`; the completed tensor coalgebra, duals, and dependent graded-quiver versions of Layer 0 have not started, nor has anything in Layers 1, 2, or 4 onward.

## DGAInfinity: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30 (`8745177` to `dec7a58`)

`A∞` algebras now exist. An [`A∞` algebra](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra) is a degree-one coderivation of the reduced bar coalgebra, and the square-zero bar law is equivalent to every unsuspended Stasheff identity ([`barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.barDifferential_sq_iff_stasheff)). This closes Layer 0's `b² = 0 ⇔ (SI_n)` bridge. Morphisms are stored as bar-coalgebra maps with components `fₙ`, and an `A∞` morphism is invertible exactly when its linear part is bijective ([`isIso_iff_linearPart_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Iso.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.isIso_iff_linearPart_bijective)). Strict and cohomological units, augmentations, minimality, formality and arity-nilpotent Maurer–Cartan elements are defined, and DG algebras embed as the `m_{n≥3} = 0` case.

On the transfer side, the [basic perturbation lemma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction/Perturbation.html#TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.perturb) is proved for special contractions under the hypothesis that `1 + δh` is invertible. The [tensor trick](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction/TensorTrick.html#TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.reducedTensorWords) lifts a contraction to the reduced tensor coalgebras. A length-lowering coderivation perturbation keeps the inclusion a coalgebra map ([`isCoalgHom_reducedTensorWords_perturb_incl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Contraction/TensorTrick/Perturbation.html#TauCeti.LinearSpecialContraction.isCoalgHom_reducedTensorWords_perturb_incl)), and invertibility holds for the higher bar differential. These pieces have not yet been combined into a transferred `A∞` structure on cohomology, so Kadeishvili's theorem is still open.

Layer 1 opened across a broad front. It has DG algebras with morphisms, opposites, tensor products and cohomology algebras, and [DG categories](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Basic.html#TauCeti.DGCategory) in both enriched and explicit form ([`equivDGCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/HomComplexData.html#TauCeti.DGCategoryData.equivDGCategory)). It also has `Z⁰`, `H⁰`, DG functors and natural transformations, and full subcategories. There are quasi-equivalences, which [induce equivalences on `H⁰`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/QuasiEquivalence.html#CategoryTheory.EnrichedFunctor.IsQuasiEquivalence.isEquivalence_mapDGHomotopyCategory). Right DG modules form a DG category, and the [DG Yoneda lemma](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.dgYonedaIso) is proved for the free rank-one module. Layer 0 gained the completed tensor coalgebra and graded duals.

## DGAInfinity: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02 (`dec7a58` to `d449639`)

**Kadeishvili's theorem** has landed for `A∞` algebras over a field. The cohomology of any `A∞` algebra carries a [minimal `A∞` model](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel) whose binary operation is the induced cohomology product ([`minimalModel_m_two`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel_m_two)). It comes with an `A∞` quasi-isomorphism to the original algebra that induces the identity on cohomology ([`cohomologyMap_minimalModelInclusion_cohomologyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.cohomologyMap_minimalModelInclusion_cohomologyEquiv)). The uniqueness clause holds as well: a quasi-isomorphism between the algebra and any minimal `A∞` algebra, in either direction, composes with the model's maps to give an `A∞` isomorphism ([`isIso_minimalModelProjection_comp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIso_minimalModelProjection_comp)). Behind this is general [homological transfer](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.transfer) along any special contraction. It works by perturbing the bar construction rather than summing over trees, and it gives quasi-isomorphisms in both directions (TauCeti#10208, TauCeti#10547).

The transfer formulas are identified only through arity three, where the ternary operation has the Kontsevich–Soibelman/Merkulov form ([`minimalModel_m_three`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.minimalModel_m_three)). The planar-tree formula, naturality, and the category, module and bimodule versions are not yet here.

Elsewhere, `A∞` algebras gained [opposites](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Opposite.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.op), and categories enriched in a braided category now have a [tensor product](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Enriched/TensorProduct.html#TauCeti.tensorEnrichedCategory), which gives tensor DG categories with the Koszul composition sign. Cohomology of right DG modules is now a [functor](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Module/Right/Hom/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.DGRightModuleCat.cohomologyFunctor) to modules over the cohomology algebra. Layer 0 gained [operations on composable strings](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Graded/Multilinear.html#TauCeti.GradedLinearQuiver.pathOperationEquiv) in a graded quiver, which `A∞` categories need. Graded coderivations of comodules, the shape of a future `A∞` module structure, also landed.

## DGAInfinity: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-13 (`1d09589` to `c7ae9d7`)

The **Yoneda lemma for right `A∞` modules** has landed ([`yonedaHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.yonedaHomotopyEquiv)). Over an `A∞` algebra with a strict unit `e`, evaluation at `e` is a homotopy equivalence from the morphism complex out of the free rank-one module to the module itself. Its homotopy inverse is given by the Yoneda cochains, so in particular evaluation is a [quasi-isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Yoneda.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.quasiIso_yonedaEval). Two limits apply. The module must have `e` as a right unit for its binary operation, and the statement covers only the free module, not representables over an `A∞` category.

Behind it are the [morphism complexes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Hom/Complex.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModule.homComplex) of right `A∞` modules: cochains are coaction-compatible maps of cofree bar comodules, and the differential is the graded commutator with the bar differentials. The differential squares to zero and satisfies the Leibniz rule. Module morphisms are exactly the [closed degree-zero cochains](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/Hom/Complex.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModuleHom.equivZeroCocycles), and homotopies are the degree `-1` cochains bounding a difference. Right `A∞` modules therefore form a [DG category](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Module/Right/DGCategory.html#TauCeti.AInfinityRightModuleCat.instDGCategory) whose closed degree-zero maps are the module morphisms.

[`A∞` categories](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory) now exist as well. On a graded linear quiver, an `A∞` category is an `A∞` algebra on the total module of morphisms whose operations respect composable strings. It has a [differential that squares to zero](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory.homDifferential_homDifferential), and its composition obeys the [Leibniz rule](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityCategory.homDifferential_comp). Every `A∞` algebra gives a [one-object `A∞` category](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/SingleObj.html#TauCeti.AInfinitySingleObj.aInfinityCategory), built by [transporting the algebra](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Map.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.map) along a linear equivalence. There are no general `A∞` functors yet.

## DGAInfinity: 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-23 (`c7ae9d7` to `bfcff79`)

**Kadeishvili's intrinsic formality criterion** has landed: over a field, a graded algebra `H` is intrinsically formal, so every `A∞` algebra with cohomology algebra `H` is formal, as soon as `HH^{n,2-n}(H,H) = 0` for all `n ≥ 3` ([`isIntrinsicallyFormal_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/IntrinsicFormality/Criterion.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.isIntrinsicallyFormal_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt)). The proof kills higher operations one arity at a time. The first disagreement with `H` is a [Hochschild cocycle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Obstruction.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.hochschildDifferential_obstruction), a [gauge transformation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Gauge.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.gauge) by a coalgebra automorphism removes it, and the infinite composite converges as a [limit of `A∞` morphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hom/Limit.html#TauCeti.AInfinityHom.limit). Over any commutative ring the argument still gives an `A∞` isomorphism between [minimal structures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/IntrinsicFormality/Criterion.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.IsMinimal.exists_isIso_of_forall_hochschildVanishesAt). Underneath sits new Hochschild machinery: braces, the Gerstenhaber bracket with its [pre-Lie identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/TensorCoalgebra/Brace.html#TauCeti.ReducedTensorWords.brace_brace_sub_smul_brace_brace), the brace equation `m{m} = 0`, and the [bracket on Hochschild cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.PositiveHochschild.cohomologyBracket) in positive arity.

Triple Massey products arrived too. The transferred `m₃` of the minimal model lies in `⟨x, y, z⟩`, and the Massey product is [exactly its coset by the indeterminacy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Massey.html#TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.mem_tripleMasseyProduct_iff_sub_minimalModel_m_three_mem). The roadmap's worked example `Λ(a, b, c)` with `dc = ab` is computed: its minimal model has `m₃([a], [a], [b]) = [ac]`, [nonzero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Transfer/Heisenberg.html#TauCeti.HeisenbergDGA.minimalModel_m_three_ne_zero) over any nontrivial ring. Its non-formality is not stated, and higher Massey products are absent.

The categorical side widened. Non-strict [`A∞` functors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/AInfinity/Functor/Basic.html#TauCeti.AInfinityFunctor) compose and induce functors on `H⁰`, and DG categories match `A∞` categories with vanishing higher operations in both directions. DG bimodules have tensor composition with associator, unitors and pentagon, internal Hom with the [tensor-Hom adjunction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/DG/Bimodule/TensorProduct/Adjunction.html#TauCeti.DGBimoduleCat.tensorInternalHomAdjunction), and extension of scalars. Layer 5 has opened with shift and additive closures, [twisted objects](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/DG/Twisted/Basic.html#TauCeti.DGTwistedObject) and cones.
