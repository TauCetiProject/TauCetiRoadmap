## ProfiniteArithmetic: 2026-09-29 to 2026-09-30 (`ae3f7a6` to `0d3161a`)

The closed lower central series of a topological group is now a named object, [`closedLowerCentralSeries`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Closed.html#TauCeti.closedLowerCentralSeries), defined as Tau Ceti's lower `p`-series at `p = 0`. Its headline result says that in a pro-`p` group the terms [intersect trivially](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/LowerCentralSeries.html#TauCeti.IsProP.iInf_closedLowerCentralSeries_eq_bot) (TauCeti#9886). Each term is also shown to be [the closure of the abstract term](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Closed.html#TauCeti.closedLowerCentralSeries_eq_topologicalClosure). On the graded side, degree zero is [identified with the topological abelianization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Graded/Abelianization.html#TauCeti.lcsGradedPieceZeroEquiv). For pro-`p` groups every graded piece gets its [`ℤ_p`-module structure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Graded/PadicModule.html#TauCeti.IsProP.gradedPieceModule), and the bracket is [`ℤ_p`-bilinear](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Graded/PadicModule.html#TauCeti.IsProP.gradedBracket_smul_left). The Heisenberg group over `ℤ_p`, which is meant to detect degree one of a free pro-`p` group, is now topologized and [shown to be pro-`p`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/Heisenberg.html#TauCeti.HeisenbergGroup.isProP_padicInt).

Continuous automorphisms arrived too. The group [`ContinuousAut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContinuousAut) comes with its inner automorphisms, whose kernel is the centre, and the outer group [`ContinuousOut`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Basic.html#TauCeti.ContinuousOut). Alongside them come [topologically characteristic subgroups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Characteristic.html#TauCeti.IsTopCharacteristic), including the pro-`p` kernel and Frattini subgroup, and the fact that [conjugate pairs form a closed set](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.isClosed_isConj_pair) in a compact Hausdorff group. There is no congruence topology yet.

For the profinite integers, `ẑ` is now [the inverse limit of the `ZMod n`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/ZMod.html#TauCeti.zHat.existsUnique_forall_toZMod_eq), with a ring-level universal property, and has [`ℓ`-adic components](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Component.html#TauCeti.zHat.component) that agree with the maximal pro-`ℓ` quotient. The decomposition `ẑ ≃ ∏ ℤ_ℓ`, the idempotents and the units are still missing. On the `ℤ_p`-power, raising to a unit exponent is now shown to be [a homeomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/PadicPow.html#TauCeti.IsProP.padicPowHomeomorph), and the power is shown to pass to quotients.

## ProfiniteArithmetic: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02 (`0d3161a` to `d449639`)

The headline of this window is the [degree-one basis of a free pro-`p` group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/LowerCentralSeries.html#TauCeti.lcsGradedPiece_one_freeProP_bijective): in the closed lower central series of the free pro-`p` group on `r` generators, the brackets `[x̄_i, x̄_j]` with `i < j` form a `ℤ_p`-basis of `gr_1`. Its companion says the classes of the generators form [a `ℤ_p`-basis of `gr_0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/LowerCentralSeries.html#TauCeti.lcsGradedPiece_zero_freeProP_bijective). Injectivity comes from [homomorphisms into the Heisenberg group over `ℤ_p`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/LowerCentralSeries.html#TauCeti.freeProP.exists_heisenberg_detect) that read off one coefficient at a time (TauCeti#10503).

The profinite integers are now [the product of the `ℓ`-adic integers](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.zHat.ringEquivPiPadicInt) as topological rings. They come with the idempotents [`ω_ℓ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.zHat.idem), the [unit group as `∏ ℤ_ℓˣ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Units.html#TauCeti.zHat.unitsEquivPiPadicInt), and the [assembly of compatible characters into `ẑˣ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Units.html#TauCeti.zHat.unitsLift). That finishes Layer 0. On top of it, the [profinite power `x ^ᶻ a`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Pow.html#TauCeti.zpowHat) arrived with its full calculus, including [agreement with the `ℓ`-adic power](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Pow.html#TauCeti.zpowHat_eq_padicPow_component) on a pro-`ℓ` group. Alongside it came closed procyclic subgroups and `ℓ`-parts, which [recover the element in the limit](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Procyclic.html#TauCeti.tendsto_zpowHat_sum_idem).

For automorphisms, the congruence topology is now [compact](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Profinite.html#TauCeti.ContinuousAut.compactSpace) for a topologically finitely generated profinite group, and `ContinuousOut` is profinite as well. Several other results landed too. Automorphisms of a finite `p`-group that are trivial on the Frattini quotient [form a `p`-group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Frattini.html#IsPGroup.isPGroup_ker_mapQuotient_frattini), and every automorphism of the Frattini quotient of a free pro-`p` group [lifts](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.freeProP.mapQuotient_proPFrattini_surjective). Functoriality [along characteristic quotients](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/ClosedQuotient.html#TauCeti.ContinuousAut.mapClosedQuotient) and actions on conjugacy classes are also in. The pro-`p` theorem making `ContinuousAut G` virtually pro-`p` is not yet proved.
