## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0, 1 and 3 are done. Those are the ring `ẑ` with its decomposition and units, the profinite and `ℓ`-adic powers, and the graded `ℤ_p`-Lie algebra of the closed lower central series with its free pro-`p` bases. Layer 2 is done except for one milestone, the outer action of a group extension.

### Named results

- **The pro-`p` automorphism theorem** — for a pro-`p` group `G`, the continuous automorphisms acting trivially on the Frattini quotient [form a pro-`p` group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/ProP.html#TauCeti.IsProP.isProP_ker_mapQuotient_proPFrattini). When `G` is topologically finitely generated this kernel is [open](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/ProP.html#TauCeti.ContinuousAut.isOpen_ker_mapQuotient_proPFrattini), so `ContinuousAut G` is virtually pro-`p`. It rests on the [finite Frattini theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Frattini.html#IsPGroup.isPGroup_ker_mapQuotient_frattini) for finite `p`-groups.
- **The degree-one basis of a free pro-`p` group** — for the free pro-`p` group on finitely many generators, the brackets `[x̄_i, x̄_j]`, `i < j`, form a `ℤ_p`-basis of `gr_1` of the closed lower central series ([`lcsGradedPiece_one_freeProP_bijective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/LowerCentralSeries.html#TauCeti.lcsGradedPiece_one_freeProP_bijective)). The generators likewise give a basis of `gr_0`.
- **The product decomposition of `ẑ`** — the `ℓ`-adic components give an isomorphism of topological rings `ẑ ≃ ∏_ℓ ℤ_ℓ` ([`zHat.ringEquivPiPadicInt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.zHat.ringEquivPiPadicInt)), and on units `ẑˣ ≃ ∏_ℓ ℤ_ℓˣ` ([`zHat.unitsEquivPiPadicInt`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Units.html#TauCeti.zHat.unitsEquivPiPadicInt)).
- **`GL_n(𝔽_p)` as automorphisms of a free pro-`p` group** — the action of continuous automorphisms of the free pro-`p` group of rank `n` on its Frattini quotient is an invertible matrix over `𝔽_p`, and [every invertible matrix occurs](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/Free/GeneralLinear.html#TauCeti.freeProP.continuousAutToGL_surjective).
- **Finite generation of the graded pieces** — each graded piece of the closed lower central series of a topologically finitely generated pro-`p` group is a [finitely generated `ℤ_p`-module](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Graded/FiniteGeneration.html#TauCeti.IsProP.module_finite_lcsGradedPiece).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The profinite power** — [`zpowHat`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Pow.html#TauCeti.zpowHat), `x ^ᶻ a`, is defined for a profinite group in any universe. It is multiplicative against the ring product of `ẑ` and [agrees with the `ℓ`-adic power](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Pow.html#TauCeti.zpowHat_eq_padicPow_component) on a pro-`ℓ` group. Continuous automorphisms [commute with it](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ZHat/Pow.html#TauCeti.smul_zpowHat).
- **The graded Lie algebra** — the direct sum of the graded pieces is a Lie ring, with a [functorial Lie homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Graded/LieRing.html#TauCeti.gradedLieHom) for every continuous homomorphism. For a pro-`ℓ` group it is a [Lie algebra over `ℤ_ℓ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/LowerCentralSeries/Graded/LieRing.html#TauCeti.IsProP.gradedLieAlgebra). This is the object PeripheralActions consumes. It is built for the lower `p`-series in general, and the closed series is the case `p = 0`.
- **The congruence topology on `ContinuousAut`** — for a topologically finitely generated profinite group it is [compact](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Profinite.html#TauCeti.ContinuousAut.compactSpace), Hausdorff and totally disconnected, and `ContinuousOut` is profinite too. `ContinuousOut G` acts on [closed subgroups up to conjugacy](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/ClosedSubgroup.html#TauCeti.ClosedSubgroupConjClasses), the form BelyiMaps needs.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 0** is done: the ring, the inverse-limit property, components, `ẑ ≃ ∏ ℤ_ℓ`, idempotents, units and character assembly. **Layer 1** is done. The last gap, the unit-exponent identity [`closedZpowers (x ^[ℓ] u) = closedZpowers x`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/ProP/PadicPow.html#TauCeti.IsProP.closedZpowers_padicPow), is now present. **Layer 3** is done. The `LieRing` and `LieAlgebra ℤ_[p]` structures, the graded comparison with the lower `p`-series and finite generation of the pieces fill the remaining holes in 3.2 and 3.3. **Layer 2** is partial. 2.1, 2.2 and 2.4 are done, and so are the actions of 2.3 on `G`, on conjugacy classes and on closed subgroups up to conjugacy, as well as functoriality along characteristic quotients. The only thing missing is the outer action of an extension, `E ⧸ N →* ContinuousOut N`, with continuity of `E →* ContinuousAut N`. No declaration for it was found in the source.

## The frontier

- **The outer action of an extension** — for a closed normal subgroup `N` of a topological group `E`, define conjugation `E →* ContinuousAut N` and its descent `E ⧸ N →* ContinuousOut N`, pinned by `outerAction_mk`. When `N` is a topologically finitely generated profinite group, prove continuity for the congruence topology. All the prerequisites in 2.1 and 2.2 are in place, and this is the last milestone of the roadmap.
