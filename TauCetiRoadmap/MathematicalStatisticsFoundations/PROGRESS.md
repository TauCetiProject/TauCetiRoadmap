## MathematicalStatisticsFoundations: 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-19 (`c68ae9d` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first work is in, and its headline is the [Halmos–Savage lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Experiments/Mixture.html#TauCeti.exists_absolutelyContinuous_countableMixture): a nonempty family of measures dominated by an s-finite measure is already dominated by one countable mixture of its own members (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/205). The roadmap asks only for a σ-finite dominating measure, so the result is slightly more general than the target. The mixtures are the fixed-weight sums [`countableMixture`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Experiments/Mixture.html#TauCeti.countableMixture), with weights 2^{-(n+1)} that sum to one. They come with the facts the roadmap lists: a set is null for the mixture exactly when it is null for every component, each component is absolutely continuous with respect to the mixture, and a mixture of laws with densities has the mixed density.

Underneath sits the real-density predicate [`HasDensity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Experiments/Density.html#TauCeti.HasDensity), with its working API. Densities are unique almost everywhere against a σ-finite reference measure, and [integrals transfer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Experiments/Density.html#TauCeti.HasDensity.integral_eq) with no integrability hypothesis, as the roadmap wants. A density that factors through a map is a density of the pushforward. Together this completes the first two subsections of Layer 1; tests, confidence sets, risk and garblings have not started.

## MathematicalStatisticsFoundations: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (`b235000` to `7433fc3`)

The headline of a large window is the [James–Stein theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Estimation/JamesStein.html#TauCeti.james_stein_risk_eq_and_lt): for `X ~ N(θ, I)` in dimension `p ≥ 3`, shrinking `X` by the factor `1 - (p - 2)/‖X‖²` gives risk `p - (p - 2)² E‖X‖⁻²`, strictly below the risk `p` of `X` itself (TauCeti#44179). It rests on [Stein's identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/IntegrationByParts/Multivariate.html#TauCeti.Probability.gaussian_integration_by_parts) under `N(θ, v I)`, proved for `v ≥ 0` where the roadmap asks only `v > 0`. The rest of Layer 4 came with it: the [information inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Estimation/Information.html#TauCeti.sq_deriv_le_variance_mul_fisherInformation) for a regular real-parameter model, the [optimality of the Pitman estimator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Estimation/Pitman.html#TauCeti.pitmanEstimator_risk_zero_le_of_equivariant) among location-equivariant estimators, and UMVU estimators with their [covariance characterization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Estimation/Unbiased.html#TauCeti.isMinimumVarianceUnbiasedEstimator_iff_covariance_eq_zero). Rao–Blackwell and Lehmann–Scheffé are still missing, because sufficiency has not started.

Layer 1 is now complete, with [randomized tests](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Experiments/Tests.html#TauCeti.IsStatisticalTest), their levels and UMP and UMPU tests, the [duality of confidence sets and tests](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Experiments/ConfidenceSets.html#TauCeti.hasConfidenceCoverage_iff_forall_hasTestLevel), and [garblings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Experiments/Risk.html#TauCeti.IsGarbling) that preserve risk. On that base, decision theory got its Bayes, minimax and admissibility criteria, the [minimax theorem for finite statistical games](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Decision/FiniteGame.html#TauCeti.exists_statistical_game_saddle_point) and the [complete class theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Decision/CompleteClass.html#TauCeti.exists_admissible_bayes_risk_le_of_closed_convex). For natural exponential families, the [log partition function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/ExponentialFamily/LogPartition.html#TauCeti.analyticOnNhd_logPartition) is analytic, with the mean as its gradient and the covariance as its Hessian. The mean map of a minimal family is [injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/ExponentialFamily/Mean.html#TauCeti.IsMinimalExponentialFamily.injOn_integral), but it is not yet shown to map onto the interior of the convex hull of the support, and maximum likelihood has not started.

For samples, there are the [order-statistic densities](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Samples/OrderStatistic.html#TauCeti.hasDensity_map_orderStatistics_pi), [uniform ranks](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/OrderStatistic/Ranks.html#TauCeti.map_sort_pi_eq_uniformOn), [Student's law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/GaussianSample/Basic.html#ProbabilityTheory.HasLaw.studentizedMean_gaussianSample) for a Gaussian sample and [Scheffé's simultaneous intervals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Decision/Scheffe.html#TauCeti.measureReal_scheffe_event_eq_one_sub). [Completeness of symmetric statistics](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Completeness/Symmetric.html#TauCeti.ae_eq_zero_pi_of_isCompleteStatistic_of_convex) arrived together with [U-statistics](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Samples/UStatistic.html#TauCeti.uStatistic). The shared vocabulary gained [Metropolis–Hastings reversibility](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Bayes/MetropolisHastings.html#TauCeti.isReversible_metropolisHastingsKernel), a volume-preserving [leapfrog step](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Bayes/Leapfrog.html#TauCeti.measurePreserving_leapfrogStep) and [normal-prior conjugacy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Statistics/Bayes/NormalPosterior.html#TauCeti.normalPosterior_gaussianPDFReal).
