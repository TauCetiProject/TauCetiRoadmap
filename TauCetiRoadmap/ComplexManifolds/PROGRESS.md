## ComplexManifolds: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-24 (`fce49b0` to `fe8ca8b`)

The Riemann sphere now has its Möbius transformations: every `z ↦ (az + b)/(cz + d)` acts holomorphically on `OnePoint ℂ`, so each is a biholomorphism with inverse the transformation of `g⁻¹` ([`OnePoint.instContMDiffConstSMul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/OnePoint/Moebius.html#OnePoint.instContMDiffConstSMul), TauCeti#19887). The same work reads the two installed charts in homogeneous coordinates, so the affine chart sends `[v₀ : v₁]` to `v₀ / v₁` ([`OnePoint.affineChart_equivProjectivization_symm_mk`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/OnePoint/Moebius.html#OnePoint.affineChart_equivProjectivization_symm_mk)), and identifies the inverted chart with the Möbius map `z ↦ 1/z`.

For quotients by free, properly discontinuous actions there is now a descent theory for maps. An invariant map factors through the orbit space ([`orbitRelQuotientLift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/GroupAction/OrbitRelQuotient.html#TauCeti.MulAction.orbitRelQuotientLift)), and the descended map is `C^m` exactly when the original is ([`contMDiff_orbitRelQuotientLift_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/Descent.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_orbitRelQuotientLift_iff)). Over `𝓘(ℂ, E)` this is holomorphic descent. Uniqueness, products and naturality under equivariant maps come with it. Descent of bundle sections is not there yet.

The realification layer gained its map-level half. Holomorphic maps, local diffeomorphisms and biholomorphisms restrict to their real counterparts ([`ContMDiff.restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/RestrictScalars.html#ContMDiff.restrictScalars), [`Diffeomorph.restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Diffeomorph/RestrictScalars.html#Diffeomorph.restrictScalars)), and the real derivative is the complex one with scalars restricted ([`MDifferentiableAt.mfderiv_restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/RestrictScalars.html#MDifferentiableAt.mfderiv_restrictScalars)). Atlases can also be pulled back along a homeomorphism. The resulting comparison diffeomorphism has the homeomorphism as its underlying map ([`Homeomorph.pullbackDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PullbackGroupoid.html#Homeomorph.pullbackDiffeomorph)).
