## ComplexManifolds: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-24 (`fce49b0` to `fe8ca8b`)

The Riemann sphere now has its Möbius transformations: every `z ↦ (az + b)/(cz + d)` acts holomorphically on `OnePoint ℂ`, so each is a biholomorphism with inverse the transformation of `g⁻¹` ([`OnePoint.instContMDiffConstSMul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/OnePoint/Moebius.html#OnePoint.instContMDiffConstSMul), TauCeti#19887). The same work reads the two installed charts in homogeneous coordinates, so the affine chart sends `[v₀ : v₁]` to `v₀ / v₁` ([`OnePoint.affineChart_equivProjectivization_symm_mk`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/OnePoint/Moebius.html#OnePoint.affineChart_equivProjectivization_symm_mk)), and identifies the inverted chart with the Möbius map `z ↦ 1/z`.

For quotients by free, properly discontinuous actions there is now a descent theory for maps. An invariant map factors through the orbit space ([`orbitRelQuotientLift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/GroupAction/OrbitRelQuotient.html#TauCeti.MulAction.orbitRelQuotientLift)), and the descended map is `C^m` exactly when the original is ([`contMDiff_orbitRelQuotientLift_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/Descent.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_orbitRelQuotientLift_iff)). Over `𝓘(ℂ, E)` this is holomorphic descent. Uniqueness, products and naturality under equivariant maps come with it. Descent of bundle sections is not there yet.

The realification layer gained its map-level half. Holomorphic maps, local diffeomorphisms and biholomorphisms restrict to their real counterparts ([`ContMDiff.restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/RestrictScalars.html#ContMDiff.restrictScalars), [`Diffeomorph.restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Diffeomorph/RestrictScalars.html#Diffeomorph.restrictScalars)), and the real derivative is the complex one with scalars restricted ([`MDifferentiableAt.mfderiv_restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/RestrictScalars.html#MDifferentiableAt.mfderiv_restrictScalars)). Atlases can also be pulled back along a homeomorphism. The resulting comparison diffeomorphism has the homeomorphism as its underlying map ([`Homeomorph.pullbackDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PullbackGroupoid.html#Homeomorph.pullbackDiffeomorph)).

## ComplexManifolds: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-08 (`fe8ca8b` to `d2083f0`)

A complex manifold now has its canonical orientation as a real manifold ([`complexManifoldOrientation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#TauCeti.complexManifoldOrientation), TauCeti#20756). The key input is that holomorphic coordinate changes preserve the real orientation, because the real Jacobian is the squared modulus of the complex one ([`orientationPreservingOn_iff_det_fderiv_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#TauCeti.orientationPreservingOn_iff_det_fderiv_ne_zero)). Biholomorphisms preserve it ([`Diffeomorph.isOrientationPreservingAt_complexManifoldOrientation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#Diffeomorph.isOrientationPreservingAt_complexManifoldOrientation)). It restricts to open subsets, it is the ordered product orientation on products, with no sign when the factors are swapped, and it behaves as expected under real and complex recharting.

Compatible open gluing is in place. If the two maps out of each overlap are local diffeomorphisms, the glued space of a `TopCat.GlueData` is a smooth or complex manifold ([`IsCompatible.isManifold`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Gluing/GlueData.html#TopCat.GlueData.IsCompatible.isManifold), TauCeti#19641). Its manifold structure is unique, maps out of it are given by compatible maps on the pieces, and covering subfamilies give the same manifold. The doubled origin is now a worked example: it is a complex manifold whose relation is not closed ([`t2Space_doubleGlueData_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Category/TopCat/GlueData/Double.html#TauCeti.t2Space_doubleGlueData_iff)). Glued spaces also gained a connectedness criterion in terms of the overlap graph, and properness was shown to be local on the target.

Holomorphic bundles have begun. Members of the maximal trivialization atlas are characterized by smooth coordinate changes, and the gauge `exp(Re z)` is shown to be excluded ([`expReTrivialization_notMem_maximalAtlas`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Holomorphic/ExpReGauge.html#TauCeti.expReTrivialization_notMem_maximalAtlas)). A line bundle is trivial exactly when it has a nowhere-zero section, cocycle isomorphisms are bundle equivalences, and finite-rank cores have smooth tensor products and a [determinant line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/ExteriorPower.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.det). Quotients gained descent of invariant sections and bundle maps, compactness from a compact set meeting every orbit, and the `ℂ/ℤ` acceptance example. Realification gained its model-level construction, the tangent-bundle comparison and the standard real coordinates on `ℂⁿ`.
