## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Riemann sphere and the canonical complex orientation are done, and realification, quotient descent and compatible open gluing are nearly done. Finite-gluing topology is mostly in place. Holomorphic vector bundles are partial: trivializations, cocycle isomorphisms, tensor products and determinants have landed, while normal bundles, the canonical-bundle comparison and the deck-character application have not begun.

### Named results

- **The canonical orientation of a complex manifold** — the holomorphic atlas is an oriented atlas of the underlying real manifold, which gives a canonical orientation in the shared orientation carrier with no choices made ([`complexManifoldOrientation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#TauCeti.complexManifoldOrientation)). It works because a holomorphic map's real Jacobian is the squared modulus of its complex Jacobian ([`orientationPreservingOn_iff_det_fderiv_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#TauCeti.orientationPreservingOn_iff_det_fderiv_ne_zero)).
- **Biholomorphisms preserve orientation** — every biholomorphism satisfies the DifferentialGeometry orientation-preserving predicate for the canonical orientations ([`Diffeomorph.isOrientationPreservingAt_complexManifoldOrientation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#Diffeomorph.isOrientationPreservingAt_complexManifoldOrientation)).
- **Compatible open gluing** — if both maps out of each overlap of a `TopCat.GlueData` are local diffeomorphisms, the glued space is a smooth or complex manifold whose canonical inclusions are local diffeomorphisms ([`IsCompatible.isManifold`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Gluing/GlueData.html#TopCat.GlueData.IsCompatible.isManifold)). That manifold structure is unique ([`maximalAtlas_chartedSpace_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Gluing/GlueData.html#TopCat.GlueData.IsCompatible.maximalAtlas_chartedSpace_eq)).
- **The trivial line bundle criterion** — a smooth or holomorphic line bundle is trivial exactly when it has a nowhere-vanishing section ([`exists_trivialization_baseSet_eq_univ_iff_exists_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/FrameTrivialization.html#ContMDiffVectorBundle.exists_trivialization_baseSet_eq_univ_iff_exists_ne_zero)).
- **Smooth and holomorphic descent through quotients** — for a free, properly discontinuous action, an invariant map or section descends to a `C^m` map or section on the orbit space exactly when it is `C^m` upstairs ([`contMDiff_section_orbitRelQuotientRec_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/Section.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_section_orbitRelQuotientRec_iff)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The maximal holomorphic trivialization atlas.** A linear trivialization belongs to it exactly when its coordinate changes with the bundle's trivializations are smooth ([`mem_maximalAtlas_contMDiffFiberwiseLinear_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/MaximalAtlas.html#Bundle.Trivialization.mem_maximalAtlas_contMDiffFiberwiseLinear_iff)). The real-smooth gauge `exp(Re z)` is shown to be excluded, as the acceptance check requires ([`expReTrivialization_notMem_maximalAtlas`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Holomorphic/ExpReGauge.html#TauCeti.expReTrivialization_notMem_maximalAtlas)).
- **The determinant line of a finite-rank core.** [`FiniteRankBundleCore.det`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/ExteriorPower.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.det) is the top exterior power, and its transitions are the determinants of the original ones. Together with smooth tensor products, it is the route to determinant and canonical bundles.
- **Restriction of scalars on models.** [`ModelWithCorners.restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ModelWithCorners/RestrictScalars/Basic.html#ModelWithCorners.restrictScalars) realifies any boundaryless complex model on the same chart carrier. With the [standard real coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Euclidean.html#TauCeti.complexEuclideanRealModel) on `ℂⁿ`, it underlies both the orientation and the gluing model changes.

## The frontier

- **Descent of bundle operations (4.3).** Show that section and bundle-map descent commute with pullback, dual, tensor product and determinant. The descent and finite-rank core APIs are both in place.
- **Finishing the bundle layer (7.2).** Make pullback holomorphic, and compare the finite-rank tensor, dual and determinant cores with the shared SmoothBundlesConnections B2 carriers after realification.
- **The normal bundle of a complex hypersurface (7.4).** Show that `i* TM / TZ` is a holomorphic line bundle and agrees with the transition-cocycle model. It needs the bundle layer above.
- **The canonical bundle (7.5).** A canonical line bundle with Jacobian-determinant transitions already exists on Mathlib's alternating-map carrier. What remains is to relate it to the finite-rank determinant of the holomorphic cotangent bundle under the stated conventions.
- **Remaining gluing laws (5.3, 6.5).** Compatibility of open gluing with products and homeomorphism transport, and invariance of the topological criteria under isomorphisms of gluing data.
