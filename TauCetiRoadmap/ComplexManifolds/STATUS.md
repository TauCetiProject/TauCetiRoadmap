## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Riemann sphere and the canonical complex orientation are done. Realification, quotients and open gluing are each done apart from a few comparison laws. Holomorphic vector bundles are well advanced: trivializations, cocycles, duals, tensors, pullback and deck-character line bundles are in place. The normal bundle of a hypersurface has not begun, and the canonical-bundle comparison and the order bound for deck characters remain open.

### Named results

- **The canonical orientation of a complex manifold** — the holomorphic atlas orients the underlying real manifold in the shared orientation carrier with no choices made, and every biholomorphism preserves this orientation ([`complexManifoldOrientation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#TauCeti.complexManifoldOrientation), [`Diffeomorph.isOrientationPreservingAt_complexManifoldOrientation`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/Orientation.html#Diffeomorph.isOrientationPreservingAt_complexManifoldOrientation)).
- **Compatible open gluing** — if both maps out of each overlap of a `TopCat.GlueData` are local diffeomorphisms, the glued space carries a unique smooth or complex manifold structure in which the canonical inclusions are local diffeomorphisms ([`IsCompatible.isManifold`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Gluing/GlueData.html#TopCat.GlueData.IsCompatible.isManifold)).
- **Holomorphic descent through quotients** — for a free, properly discontinuous action, an invariant map or section descends to a `C^m` map or section of the orbit space exactly when it is `C^m` upstairs ([`contMDiff_section_orbitRelQuotientRec_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/Section.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_section_orbitRelQuotientRec_iff)).
- **Triviality of deck-character line bundles** — over the quotient of a compact connected complex covering manifold, the line bundle of a deck-group character is holomorphically trivial exactly when the character is trivial ([`exists_trivialization_baseSet_eq_univ_characterCore_iff_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/AssociatedBundle.html#IsQuotientCoveringMap.exists_trivialization_baseSet_eq_univ_characterCore_iff_eq_one)).
- **Sections of 𝒪(n) on the Riemann sphere** — the holomorphic sections of 𝒪(n) are the polynomials of degree at most `n`, of dimension `n + 1`, and 𝒪(k) has none for `k < 0` ([`finrank_holomorphicTwistSections`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/OnePoint/LineBundle/Sections.html#OnePoint.finrank_holomorphicTwistSections)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The maximal holomorphic trivialization atlas.** Its members are exactly the linear trivializations with holomorphic coordinate changes, and the real-smooth gauge `exp(Re z)` is shown to be excluded, as the acceptance check requires ([`expReTrivialization_notMem_maximalAtlas`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Holomorphic/ExpReGauge.html#TauCeti.expReTrivialization_notMem_maximalAtlas)).
- **Holomorphic pullback of finite-rank bundles.** Pulling back along a holomorphic map gives a holomorphic core whose total space is biholomorphic to Mathlib's pullback carrier ([`pullbackTotalSpaceDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Pullback.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.pullbackTotalSpaceDiffeomorph)). Pullback commutes strictly with duals and tensor products, which is what bundle descent of these operations will need.
- **The canonical bundle.** [`CanonicalBundle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Cotangent/Canonical.html#TauCeti.CanonicalBundle) is the line of top-degree forms, and its transitions are the Jacobian determinants of the coordinate changes. Alongside the [determinant line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/ExteriorPower.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.det) of a finite-rank core, it is the starting point for 7.5.

## The frontier

- **The order bound for deck characters (7.6).** Show that the order of a character bounds the tensor order of its line bundle. This needs the line bundle of a product of characters identified with the tensor product of their line bundles.
- **Descent of bundle operations (4.3).** Show that section and bundle-map descent commute with pullback, dual, tensor product and determinant. Pullback now commutes strictly with dual and tensor at the core level, so the ingredients are present.
- **The canonical bundle comparison (7.5).** Identify the canonical bundle with the finite-rank determinant of the holomorphic cotangent bundle under the stated dual and top-exterior-power conventions.
- **The normal bundle of a complex hypersurface (7.4).** Show that `i* TM / TZ` is a holomorphic line bundle and agrees with the transition-cocycle model.
- **Remaining comparison laws (1.4, 5.3, 5.4, 6.5).** Realification on open subspaces, transport of open gluing along homeomorphisms, a finite-star gluing example, and invariance of the topological criteria under isomorphisms of gluing data.
