## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Riemann sphere milestone is complete. Realification, quotients and finite-gluing topology are partial: maps, derivatives and the descent of maps are in place, but the commutation laws, bundle descent and the proper-map criteria are not. The canonical complex orientation, the `TopCat.GlueData` atlas adapter and holomorphic vector bundles have not begun.

### Named results

- **Möbius transformations are biholomorphisms of the Riemann sphere.** Every `z ↦ (az + b)/(cz + d)` acts holomorphically on `OnePoint ℂ` with the installed two-chart atlas, and its inverse is the transformation of `g⁻¹` ([`OnePoint.instContMDiffConstSMul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/OnePoint/Moebius.html#OnePoint.instContMDiffConstSMul)).
- **Smooth and holomorphic descent through quotients.** For a free, properly discontinuous action by `C^n` maps, an invariant map descends to a `C^m` map on the orbit space exactly when it is `C^m` upstairs ([`TauCeti.contMDiff_orbitRelQuotientLift_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/Descent.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_orbitRelQuotientLift_iff)). Over `𝓘(ℂ, E)` this is holomorphic descent.
- **Equivariant maps induce maps of quotient manifolds.** A `C^m` map intertwining two such actions descends to a `C^m` map of orbit spaces ([`TauCeti.contMDiff_orbitRelQuotientMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Quotient/Descent.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_orbitRelQuotientMap)).
- **The real derivative of a holomorphic map is its complex derivative with scalars restricted** ([`MDifferentiableAt.mfderiv_restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/RestrictScalars.html#MDifferentiableAt.mfderiv_restrictScalars)). Together with [`ContMDiff.restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/RestrictScalars.html#ContMDiff.restrictScalars), this makes holomorphic maps smooth maps of the underlying real manifolds.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Atlas transport along a homeomorphism.** [`Homeomorph.pullbackChartedSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PullbackGroupoid.html#Homeomorph.pullbackChartedSpace) pulls an atlas back along `e : N ≃ₜ M`, preserving every structure groupoid. It follows the transport design the roadmap names on the Mathlib side. Its comparison diffeomorphism [`Homeomorph.pullbackDiffeomorph`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/PullbackGroupoid.html#Homeomorph.pullbackDiffeomorph) has underlying map `e`, with identity, inverse and composition laws.
- **Restriction of scalars for diffeomorphisms.** [`Diffeomorph.restrictScalars`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Diffeomorph/RestrictScalars.html#Diffeomorph.restrictScalars) turns a biholomorphism into a real diffeomorphism with the same underlying equivalence, compatibly with composition and inverses. Partial and local diffeomorphisms have the same construction.
- **Descent of invariant maps on the standard orbit carrier.** [`TauCeti.MulAction.orbitRelQuotientLift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/GroupAction/OrbitRelQuotient.html#TauCeti.MulAction.orbitRelQuotientLift) comes with uniqueness, composition and products, and continuity descends too. It is what bundle-section descent will build on.

### Roadmap coverage

Milestone 3 (Riemann sphere) is done: the installed analytic atlas is used as is, and the homogeneous-coordinate chart formulas and Möbius biholomorphisms have landed. Milestone 1 is partial. Restriction of scalars covers maps, local and global diffeomorphisms and derivatives, and atlases transport along homeomorphisms. Not established here are transport along a `ContinuousLinearEquiv` of models, commutation of realification with products, open restrictions and recharting, tangent and bundle charts, and the coordinate equivalence `EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin n) ≃ EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (2n))`. Bundle descent (4.3), the compact-fundamental-domain theorem and the commutation with realification are missing. Milestone 6 is partial: glued spaces have a second-countability instance and a Hausdorff criterion through closedness of the chart relation, but connectedness, proper-map locality and the doubled-origin test are absent. Milestones 2, 5 and 7 are untouched. For Milestone 5 the open-cover atlas `chartedSpaceOfIsOpenEmbedding` exists, but the `TopCat.GlueData` compatibility adapter does not.

## The frontier

- **The `TopCat.GlueData` atlas adapter (5.1–5.2).** State overlap compatibility through the two canonical maps out of `D.V (i, j)`. Then feed `D.ι_isOpenEmbedding` and the transition witnesses into the existing open-cover atlas. Every input is installed, and the analytic toric construction already does this by hand for its own data.
- **Finishing realification (1.2–1.5).** Model transport along continuous linear equivalences, the identity-diffeomorphism comparisons with products, open subspaces and recharting, and the standard `ℂⁿ ≃ ℝ²ⁿ` coordinate equivalence.
- **The quotient conveniences (4.1, 4.4).** These are the named complex specialization of local biholomorphy, built from the holomorphy of every action map, the additive versions on `AddAction.orbitRel.Quotient`, and compactness from a compact set meeting every orbit. The `ℂ/ℤ` acceptance check needs them.
- **The canonical complex orientation (Milestone 2).** It waits on the chart-sign orientation carrier from DifferentialGeometry Layer 2, and the first step is positivity of the real determinant of a complex-linear equivalence.
- **Holomorphic vector bundles (7.1–7.3).** First the local characterization over atlas trivializations, excluding real-smooth gauges, then cocycle gluing and the rank-one criterion. The direct-sum, dual, tensor and determinant refinements wait on the shared SmoothBundlesConnections B2 operation carriers. Bundle descent 4.3 waits on this layer.
