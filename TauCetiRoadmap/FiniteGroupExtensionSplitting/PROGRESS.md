## FiniteGroupExtensionSplitting: 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09 (`3789b26` to `7ca783d`)

The headline is the cohomological comparison: for any group, an abelian normal subgroup has a complement exactly when the class of its quotient extension in H²(G ⧸ N, N) vanishes ([`exists_complement_iff_cohomologyClass_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Quotient/Cohomology.html#Subgroup.exists_complement_iff_cohomologyClass_eq_zero)). It is a statement about actual subgroup complements, not just about an obstruction class. It rests on two dictionaries that also landed. Splittings of the quotient extension N → G → G ⧸ N correspond bijectively to complements of N ([`quotientSplittingEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Quotient/Basic.html#Subgroup.quotientSplittingEquiv)), and equivalent extensions have matching splittings ([`splittingEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Splitting.html#GroupExtension.Equiv.splittingEquiv)). The comparison also uses the conjugation action of the quotient on N ([`quotientConjugationAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Quotient/Action.html#Subgroup.quotientConjugationAction)).

The other main piece of work is the [crossed transfer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Transfer/Crossed.html#TauCeti.crossedTransfer), a twisted transfer that moves crossed homomorphisms from a finite-index subgroup H up to G (TauCeti#29092). It sends crossed homomorphisms to crossed homomorphisms, changes by a principal crossed map when the transversal changes, and reduces to the classical transfer when the action is trivial. On a normal subgroup N ≤ H its value is the original map raised to the index of H. Raising it to an inverse exponent gives the coprime-index extension theorem ([`exists_isMulCocycle₁_eq_of_coprime_index`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Transfer/Crossed.html#TauCeti.exists_isMulCocycle₁_eq_of_coprime_index)), which is the analytic core of Gaschütz's splitting theorem. Gaschütz's theorem itself has not been stated yet.

Underneath all of this is a [local complement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/Local.html#Subgroup.IsLocalComplement) API: unique factorisation, the two projections, restriction of a global complement to an intermediate subgroup, and transport along isomorphisms.

## FiniteGroupExtensionSplitting: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

This window proved the roadmap's three main finite-group theorems. [Gaschütz's splitting theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/Gaschutz.html#Subgroup.IsLocalComplement.exists_isComplement'_of_coprime_index) (TauCeti#34706) says that an abelian normal subgroup N with a complement inside some subgroup H, where [G : H] is prime to |N|, has a complement in G. Neither solvability nor coprimality of |N| with [G : N] is assumed. Its Sylow form, [Sylow detection of complements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/Sylow.html#IsPGroup.exists_isComplement'_iff_sylow), says that an abelian normal p-subgroup has a complement in G exactly when it has one in a Sylow p-subgroup. The [invariant-complement theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/Invariant/Basic.html#Subgroup.exists_invariant_complement) then chooses that complement stable under a q-group of automorphisms with q ≠ p, and it also landed in an [internal form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/Invariant/Internal.html#Subgroup.exists_invariant_local_complement) for subgroups normalized inside a larger group.

The construction behind Gaschütz's theorem is now explicit. The [Gaschütz complement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/Gaschutz.html#Subgroup.IsLocalComplement.gaschutzComplement) is the zero locus of a normalized crossed transfer, and changing the transversal [conjugates it by an element of N](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/Gaschutz.html#Subgroup.IsLocalComplement.gaschutzComplement_changeTransversal). To support it, the crossed-projection dictionary was extended to [local complements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/CrossedProjection.html#Subgroup.localCrossedProjectionEquiv) and made compatible with conjugation and restriction. The second, cohomological route also landed. The [restriction formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Quotient/Restriction.html#Subgroup.map_subtype_cohomologyClass_factorSet_quotientExtension) for the class of the quotient extension shows that a local complement in H makes [G : H] [kill that class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Quotient/Restriction.html#Subgroup.IsLocalComplement.index_nsmul_cohomologyClass_eq_zero). The source also has its coprime-index and Sylow vanishing corollaries. Separately, Mathlib's `SemidirectProduct.mulEquivSubgroup` was matched with the complement decomposition.

Worked examples now check the theory against small groups. In S₃, A₃ has [exactly three complements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/FinThree/Subgroup.html#TauCeti.card_isComplement'_alternatingGroup_fin_three), and the Gaschütz complement visibly depends on the transversal. In A₄, the complements of V₄ are [exactly the Sylow 3-subgroups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/Alternating/KleinFour.html#TauCeti.isComplement'_kleinFour_iff_exists_sylow_three), four in all. One small piece is still missing: the principal-crossed-map statement of §1.5 is proved only for complements in the whole group, not for crossed maps on an intermediate subgroup.
