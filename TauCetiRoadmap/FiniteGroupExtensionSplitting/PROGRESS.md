## FiniteGroupExtensionSplitting: 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09 (`3789b26` to `7ca783d`)

The headline is the cohomological comparison: for any group, an abelian normal subgroup has a complement exactly when the class of its quotient extension in H²(G ⧸ N, N) vanishes ([`exists_complement_iff_cohomologyClass_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Quotient/Cohomology.html#Subgroup.exists_complement_iff_cohomologyClass_eq_zero)). It is a statement about actual subgroup complements, not just about an obstruction class. It rests on two dictionaries that also landed. Splittings of the quotient extension N → G → G ⧸ N correspond bijectively to complements of N ([`quotientSplittingEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Quotient/Basic.html#Subgroup.quotientSplittingEquiv)), and equivalent extensions have matching splittings ([`splittingEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Splitting.html#GroupExtension.Equiv.splittingEquiv)). The comparison also uses the conjugation action of the quotient on N ([`quotientConjugationAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/GroupExtension/Quotient/Action.html#Subgroup.quotientConjugationAction)).

The other main piece of work is the [crossed transfer](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Transfer/Crossed.html#TauCeti.crossedTransfer), a twisted transfer that moves crossed homomorphisms from a finite-index subgroup H up to G (TauCeti#29092). It sends crossed homomorphisms to crossed homomorphisms, changes by a principal crossed map when the transversal changes, and reduces to the classical transfer when the action is trivial. On a normal subgroup N ≤ H its value is the original map raised to the index of H. Raising it to an inverse exponent gives the coprime-index extension theorem ([`exists_isMulCocycle₁_eq_of_coprime_index`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Transfer/Crossed.html#TauCeti.exists_isMulCocycle₁_eq_of_coprime_index)), which is the analytic core of Gaschütz's splitting theorem. Gaschütz's theorem itself has not been stated yet.

Underneath all of this is a [local complement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Complement/Local.html#Subgroup.IsLocalComplement) API: unique factorisation, the two projections, restriction of a global complement to an intermediate subgroup, and transport along isomorphisms.
