## ClassifyingSpaces: 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-20 (`67dc743` to `68a457e`)

Every group now has a classifying space, and has it twice. Milnor's model, the orbit space of the countable join of copies of `G`, is proved to be a [`K(G, 1)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/EilenbergMacLane.html#TauCeti.isEilenbergMacLaneSpaceOne_classifyingSpace) for an arbitrary group, with the [existence theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/EilenbergMacLane.html#TauCeti.exists_isEilenbergMacLaneSpaceOne) carrying the standing hypotheses of covering-space theory, the countable join identified with [the universal cover](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/EilenbergMacLane.html#TauCeti.universalGSpaceHomeomorphUniversalCover), and strict functoriality inducing [the given homomorphism on fundamental groups](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Functoriality.html#MonoidHom.classifyingSpaceFundamentalGroupMulEquiv_mapOfEq). The cellular model takes the presentation complex, whose [fundamental group is the presented group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/PresentationComplex.html#TauCeti.PresentationComplex.fundamentalGroupMulEquiv), and [kills its higher homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/KillHomotopyGroups.html#TauCeti.killHomotopyGroups) by a tower of cell attachments; fed the tautological presentation it gives [a cellular `K(G, 1)` for every group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Cellular.html#TauCeti.exists_isEilenbergMacLaneSpaceOne_cellularClassifyingSpace) (TauCeti#18459). That model is so far a relative CW complex over the presentation complex, not yet a classical CW complex, and its local path-connectedness is not proved.

The general CW machinery the roadmap owns arrived alongside: a CW structure [lifts along a covering](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CWComplex/Classical/Covering.html#TauCeti.cwComplexPreimage) and [descends to the orbit space of a free cellular action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CWComplex/Classical/OrbitSpace.html#IsQuotientCoveringMap.cwComplexQuotient), which makes [Milnor's space a CW complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/CWComplex.html#TauCeti.instCWComplexClassifyingSpace) whose cells are orbits of simplices, and the cellular chains of a cover are [free over the group ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Covering.html#TauCeti.cellularChainRepIsoFree). As a by-product, the Euler characteristic is [multiplicative in finite covers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/EulerCharacteristic/Covering.html#TauCeti.eulerChar_eq_mul_of_isCoveringMap).

First checks against algebra: the circle is [homotopy equivalent to the classifying space of ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Circle.html#TauCeti.circleHomotopyEquivClassifyingSpace), inducing the identity on fundamental groups, and the first singular homology of the classifying space is [the first group homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/FirstHomology.html#TauCeti.classifyingSpaceFirstHomologyAddEquivGroupHomology). The general uniqueness theorem, the comparison of the two models, and homology in higher degrees have not started.

## ClassifyingSpaces: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `69cd1ab`)

The classifying space is now unique and computes group (co)homology. Two `K(G, 1)` spaces of CW type are [homotopy equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.IsAspherical.exists_homotopyEquiv), by an equivalence realizing any prescribed isomorphism of fundamental groups up to a change of base point (TauCeti#19023). The cellular model became a genuine CW complex, [one `0`-cell, a `1`-cell per generator, a `2`-cell per relator and higher cells from the tower](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Cellular.html#TauCeti.instCWComplexCellularClassifyingSpace), and with that the two models are [homotopy equivalent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Comparison.html#TauCeti.cellularHomotopyEquivClassifyingSpace), compatibly with the identifications of `π₁` and with the forward map unique up to pointed homotopy. The torus is also shown [homotopy equivalent to Milnor's model of `B(ℤⁿ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Torus.html#TauCeti.torusHomotopyEquivClassifyingSpace).

On the algebraic side, Milnor's countable join is [contractible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Contractible.html#TauCeti.instContractibleSpaceUniversalGSpace), so its singular chains resolve the trivial module, and in every degree [`Hₙ(BG; k)` is group homology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Homology.html#TauCeti.classifyingSpaceSingularHomologyIsoGroupHomology) and [`Hⁿ(BG; M)` is group cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.classifyingSpaceSingularCohomologyIsoGroupCohomology) (TauCeti#20480). These cover trivial coefficients only, and they are not yet stated naturally in the group or compatibly with long exact sequences. The ordered chains of the join form [a free resolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Resolution.html#TauCeti.universalGSpaceResolution), and the explicit map to Mathlib's bar resolution, sending a simplex to its tuple of labels, is [a homotopy equivalence of resolutions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/StandardResolution.html#TauCeti.universalGSpaceStandardHomotopyEquiv), [natural in the group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Naturality.html#MonoidHom.universalGSpaceChainComplexToStandardComplex_naturality). The cellular chains of a contractible free cover are likewise [a projective resolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Resolution.html#TauCeti.cellularResolution).

Supporting work went into CW structures on attaching cells and composing relative complexes, into the result that Hausdorff CW complexes are delta-generated and hence locally path-connected, and into the API for changing base points. Homotopy functoriality, local coefficients, the wedge-of-circles check and `H₂` of a finite cyclic group are still to come.
