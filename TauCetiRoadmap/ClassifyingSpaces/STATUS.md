## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The completion criterion appears to be met. Every group has a `K(G, 1)` in both models, the two are homotopy equivalent, `K(G, 1)` spaces of CW type are unique, the classifying space is a functor to the pointed homotopy category, and its homology and cohomology with local coefficients are Mathlib's group (co)homology, naturally and compatibly with long exact sequences.

### Named results

- **Every group has a classifying space, twice** — Milnor's orbit space of the countable join is [a `K(G, 1)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/EilenbergMacLane.html#TauCeti.exists_isEilenbergMacLaneSpaceOne) for an arbitrary group, and so is [the cellular model built from the tautological presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Comparison.html#TauCeti.exists_isEilenbergMacLaneSpaceOne_cellularClassifyingSpace), each with the standing hypotheses of covering-space theory.
- **Uniqueness of `K(G, 1)` spaces of CW type** — two aspherical spaces of CW type are homotopy equivalent by an equivalence realizing any prescribed isomorphism of fundamental groups, up to the change of base point an unpointed equivalence forces ([`IsAspherical.exists_homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.IsAspherical.exists_homotopyEquiv)).
- **The two models agree** — for a presentation of `G`, the cellular classifying space is homotopy equivalent to Milnor's, inducing the given isomorphism on `π₁` ([`cellularHomotopyEquivClassifyingSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Comparison.html#TauCeti.cellularHomotopyEquivClassifyingSpace)).
- **The classifying-space functor** — `BG` is a faithful functor from groups to the pointed homotopy category whose fundamental group is naturally the identity, refined by the strict functoriality of Milnor's model ([`classifyingSpaceHomotopyFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/HomotopyFunctor.html#TauCeti.classifyingSpaceHomotopyFunctor)).
- **Local-coefficient (co)homology of `BG` is group (co)homology** — for every representation `A` of `G`, `Hₙ(BG; A) ≅ Hₙ(G, A)` ([`classifyingSpaceTwistedHomologyIsoGroupHomology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/LocalCoefficients.html#TauCeti.classifyingSpaceTwistedHomologyIsoGroupHomology)) and `Hⁿ(BG; A) ≅ Hⁿ(G, A)` ([`classifyingSpaceCohomologyIsoGroupCohomology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/LocalCoefficients.html#TauCeti.classifyingSpaceCohomologyIsoGroupCohomology)), natural in the group and the coefficients and matching the connecting maps of coefficient sequences (TauCeti#39024).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **CW structures along coverings.** A CW structure [lifts to a covering space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CWComplex/Classical/Covering.html#TauCeti.cwComplexPreimage) and [descends to the orbit space of a free cellular action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CWComplex/Classical/OrbitSpace.html#IsQuotientCoveringMap.cwComplexQuotient). This makes Milnor's space a CW complex, so Whitehead's theorem can compare the models, and makes the cellular chains of a universal cover [a projective resolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Resolution.html#TauCeti.cellularResolution), for Milnor's join and for [the cellular model of any presented group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/PresentedGroupResolution.html#TauCeti.cellularClassifyingSpaceResolution).
- **The comparison with the bar resolution.** The explicit map sending a simplex of the join to its tuple of labels is [a homotopy equivalence onto Mathlib's standard resolution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/StandardResolution.html#TauCeti.universalGSpaceStandardHomotopyEquiv), natural in the group, and on the join's geometric cellular chains it is [equivariantly homotopic to Mathlib's abstract comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/EquivariantChains.html#TauCeti.universalGSpaceCellularStandardComparisonHomotopy).
- **The local system of a representation.** [Its fibre](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/LocalCoefficients.html#TauCeti.classifyingSpaceLocalSystem) over a point is the coinvariants of `A` tensored with the free module on the fibre of the join, and transport lifts paths to the join. The (co)homology comparisons are proved for the base of any contractible free cover, such as [`IsQuotientCoveringMap.cohomologyIsoGroupCohomology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Twisted/Direct/GroupCohomology.html#IsQuotientCoveringMap.cohomologyIsoGroupCohomology), and specialized to `BG`.

### Roadmap coverage

All four stages are done. Stage 1 (Milnor's model, its covering, its strict functoriality) was complete earlier. Stage 2 is now done: the cellular model is a classical CW complex with semilocal simple connectivity, and its existence theorem has Stage 1's form. Stage 3 is done: maps out of a cellular `K(G, 1)` are classified by `π₁`, uniqueness, CW structures along coverings, the comparison of models, and now homotopy functoriality. Stage 4 is done: cellular resolutions for both models, local-coefficient homology and cohomology natural in both variables with long exact sequences, the bar-resolution comparison, and the checks, including the wedge of circles as a `K(F, 1)` equivalent to both free-group models, and [the vanishing of even-degree integral homology of `BG`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/FiniteCyclic.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_classifyingSpace_singularHomology_int_of_even) for finite cyclic `G`, which answers the `H₂` acceptance check.
