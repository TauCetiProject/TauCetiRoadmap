## PrimitivePrimeDivisors: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`c19a968` to `7ca783d`)

The Bang–Zsigmondy theorem itself is still ahead, but this window closed its two easy ends. Exponents one and two are now settled completely: for coprime `b ≤ a`, [`a² − b²` has a primitive prime divisor exactly when `a + b` is not a power of two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/SmallExponents.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_sq_sub_sq_iff), [equivalently when `a + b` has an odd prime factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/SmallExponents.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_sq_sub_sq_iff_exists_odd_prime_dvd), and [`a − b` has one exactly when `1 < a − b`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/SmallExponents.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_sub_iff). The sporadic exception is checked as well: [`2⁶ − 1 = 63` has no primitive prime divisor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/SmallExponents.html#TauCeti.not_exists_prime_dvd_two_pow_six_sub_one_pow_six), since `3` and `7` already divide `2² − 1` and `2³ − 1` (TauCeti#28985).

On the valuation side, lifting the exponent now starts from an intermediate exponent: if an odd prime `p` divides `xᵈ − yᵈ` and not `d`, then [`v_p(xⁿ − yⁿ) = v_p(xᵈ − yᵈ) + v_p(n)` for every multiple `n` of `d`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiplicity.html#Nat.multiplicity_pow_sub_pow_of_dvd_pow_sub_pow), with the matching formulas at `p = 2` for [odd](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiplicity.html#Nat.multiplicity_pow_two_sub_pow_of_odd) and [even](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiplicity.html#Nat.multiplicity_pow_two_sub_pow_of_even) exponents. These are the exact valuations of `aᵏ − bᵏ` the roadmap asks for; the valuation of the cyclotomic value `Φ_n(a,b)` is not yet derived from them. Separately, the divisibility criteria for sums of powers landed: [if `a/b` has order `2m` modulo `p`, then `p ∣ aᵏ + bᵏ` exactly for the odd multiples `k` of `m`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/ZMod/OrderOf.html#Nat.Prime.dvd_pow_add_pow_iff_of_orderOf_eq_two_mul), resting on the fact that [an element of order `2m` in a domain has `m`-th power `−1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/OrderOfElement/NegOne.html#TauCeti.pow_eq_neg_one_iff_of_orderOf_eq_two_mul).

Finally, an arithmetic estimate aimed at the main existence proof: for `0 < b < a` and `n ≥ 3`, [`aⁿ − bⁿ > n(a + b)` fails only at `(2, 1, 3)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Estimates.html#Nat.mul_add_lt_pow_sub_pow_iff), which is where the exceptional index six comes from. The cyclotomic size bounds it is meant to feed are not yet in place.
