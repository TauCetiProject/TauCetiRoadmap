## PrimitivePrimeDivisors: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`c19a968` to `7ca783d`)

The Bang–Zsigmondy theorem itself is still ahead, but this window closed its two easy ends. Exponents one and two are now settled completely: for coprime `b ≤ a`, [`a² − b²` has a primitive prime divisor exactly when `a + b` is not a power of two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/SmallExponents.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_sq_sub_sq_iff), [equivalently when `a + b` has an odd prime factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/SmallExponents.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_sq_sub_sq_iff_exists_odd_prime_dvd), and [`a − b` has one exactly when `1 < a − b`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/SmallExponents.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_sub_iff). The sporadic exception is checked as well: [`2⁶ − 1 = 63` has no primitive prime divisor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/SmallExponents.html#TauCeti.not_exists_prime_dvd_two_pow_six_sub_one_pow_six), since `3` and `7` already divide `2² − 1` and `2³ − 1` (TauCeti#28985).

On the valuation side, lifting the exponent now starts from an intermediate exponent: if an odd prime `p` divides `xᵈ − yᵈ` and not `d`, then [`v_p(xⁿ − yⁿ) = v_p(xᵈ − yᵈ) + v_p(n)` for every multiple `n` of `d`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiplicity.html#Nat.multiplicity_pow_sub_pow_of_dvd_pow_sub_pow), with the matching formulas at `p = 2` for [odd](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiplicity.html#Nat.multiplicity_pow_two_sub_pow_of_odd) and [even](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiplicity.html#Nat.multiplicity_pow_two_sub_pow_of_even) exponents. These are the exact valuations of `aᵏ − bᵏ` the roadmap asks for; the valuation of the cyclotomic value `Φ_n(a,b)` is not yet derived from them. Separately, the divisibility criteria for sums of powers landed: [if `a/b` has order `2m` modulo `p`, then `p ∣ aᵏ + bᵏ` exactly for the odd multiples `k` of `m`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/ZMod/OrderOf.html#Nat.Prime.dvd_pow_add_pow_iff_of_orderOf_eq_two_mul), resting on the fact that [an element of order `2m` in a domain has `m`-th power `−1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/OrderOfElement/NegOne.html#TauCeti.pow_eq_neg_one_iff_of_orderOf_eq_two_mul).

Finally, an arithmetic estimate aimed at the main existence proof: for `0 < b < a` and `n ≥ 3`, [`aⁿ − bⁿ > n(a + b)` fails only at `(2, 1, 3)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Estimates.html#Nat.mul_add_lt_pow_sub_pow_iff), which is where the exceptional index six comes from. The cyclotomic size bounds it is meant to feed are not yet in place.

## PrimitivePrimeDivisors: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The Bang–Zsigmondy theorem is now proved with its exact exceptions. For coprime `a > b > 0` and `n ≥ 2`, [`aⁿ − bⁿ` has a prime divisor dividing no earlier `aᵏ − bᵏ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Zsigmondy.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_pow_sub_pow), and [it has one exactly when neither `n = 2` with `a + b` a power of two nor `(a, b, n) = (2, 1, 6)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Zsigmondy.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_pow_sub_pow_iff) (TauCeti#39611). The same work gives the corollaries in terms of orders: [a prime modulo which `a/b` has order exactly `n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Zsigmondy.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_orderOf_div_eq), with the base-one forms [for `a ≥ 3`, `n ≥ 3`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Zsigmondy.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_orderOf_eq) and [for `a = 2`, `n ≠ 6`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Zsigmondy.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_orderOf_two_eq). It also gives the sums-of-powers version, [a prime dividing `aⁿ + bⁿ` and no `aᵏ − bᵏ` with `0 < k < 2n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Zsigmondy.html#TauCeti.exists_prime_dvd_pow_add_pow_iff), outside the cases `n = 1` with `a + b` a power of two and `(2, 1, 3)`.

The proof rests on two pieces that also landed here. The first is the description of the nonprimitive prime divisors of the cyclotomic value `Φ_n(a,b)`: for `n ≠ 2`, [such a prime divides `n`, is its largest prime factor, and divides `Φ_n(a,b)` exactly once](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Cyclotomic.html#Nat.Prime.multiplicity_homogeneousCyclotomic_of_not_primitive). So without a primitive prime divisor, [`Φ_n(a,b)` is `1` or that prime](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Cyclotomic.html#Nat.Coprime.homogeneousCyclotomic_eq_one_or_eq_prime_of_not_exists_prime_dvd_pow_sub_pow). The second is the size estimate that rules this out: [`Φ_n(a,b)` exceeds every prime factor of `n` except at `(2, 1, 6)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/PrimitivePrimeDivisors/Zsigmondy.html#Nat.Prime.natCast_lt_homogeneousCyclotomic_iff). Beneath these sit the expansion identities relating `Φ_{np}(a,b)` to `Φ_n(aᵖ, bᵖ)` and the real bounds [`(a − b)^φ(n) < Φ_n(a,b)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Eval.html#TauCeti.sub_pow_totient_lt_homogeneousCyclotomic) and [`Φ_n(a,b) < (a + b)^φ(n)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Eval.html#TauCeti.homogeneousCyclotomic_lt_add_pow_totient), plus some extra divisibility and order lemmas. With these, every milestone the roadmap lists is in place.
