### Named results

- **Naturality of the Spinᶜ first Chern class** — for a continuous map f, the first Chern class of a pulled-back Spinᶜ structure is the pullback of the original class ([`SpinCStructure.firstChernClass_pullback`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/FirstChernClass.html#TauCeti.SpinCStructure.firstChernClass_pullback)); the class is also unchanged by orientation reversal.
- **Spin structures have trivial determinant** — the Spinᶜ structure induced by a Spin structure has first Chern class zero ([`SpinStructure.firstChernClass_toSpinC`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/DeterminantComparison.html#TauCeti.SpinStructure.firstChernClass_toSpinC)).
- **Spinᶜ(n) as a stabilizer** — Spinᶜ(n) is isomorphic, as a topological group, to the subgroup of Spinᶜ(n+1) fixing the last coordinate vector ([`realCliffordSpinCContinuousMulEquivLastStabilizer`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Inclusion.html#CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinCContinuousMulEquivLastStabilizer)).
- **Freeness of the Enriques involution** — the sign change of the first three coordinates of CP⁵ has no fixed point on the common zero locus of the three diagonal quadrics, which is itself smooth ([`enriquesProjectiveInvolution_no_fixed_point`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/EnriquesCover.html#TauCeti.enriquesProjectiveInvolution_no_fixed_point)).
- **Smoothness of the elliptic quartic seed** — the derivative of z₀³z₂ + z₂³z₁ + z₁³z₃ + z₃³z₀ is onto at every nonzero vector of ℂ⁴, whether or not it lies on the surface ([`ellipticQuarticSeedMap_fderiv_surjective`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Surfaces/QuarticSurface/EllipticQuarticSeed.html#TauCeti.ellipticQuarticSeedMap_fderiv_surjective)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The group Spinᶜ** ([`CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup)), Spin × U(1) modulo (−1,−1), with its rotation map to SO, whose kernel is the central circle, and a map from the product of the groups of two orthogonal summands, on which the determinant character is multiplicative.
- **Spinᶜ structures** ([`SpinCStructure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/Basic.html#TauCeti.SpinCStructure)) as principal lifts of the orthonormal frame bundle of a fixed bundle, as the README asks. Pullback, induction from Spin, precomposition with isometries and orientation reversal are built, with reversal involutive up to isomorphism.
- **The canonical Spinᶜ structure of a complex structure** ([`canonicalSpinCStructure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/AlmostComplex.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.canonicalSpinCStructure)) on a rank-four bundle with an orthogonal complex structure. It comes from a Spinᶜ lift of U(2) whose determinant character is the complex determinant.
