## FourManifolds: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-25 (`844fcd5` to `522d48f`)

The first work on this roadmap opens its Spinᶜ layer. A Spinᶜ structure on a real vector bundle now has a [first Chern class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/FirstChernClass.html#TauCeti.SpinCStructure.firstChernClass), the class of its determinant line, and it behaves as it should: it is [natural under pullback](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/FirstChernClass.html#TauCeti.SpinCStructure.firstChernClass_pullback), [unchanged by orientation reversal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/DeterminantComparison.html#TauCeti.SpinCStructure.firstChernClass_reverse), and [zero on the Spinᶜ structure coming from a Spin structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/DeterminantComparison.html#TauCeti.SpinStructure.firstChernClass_toSpinC). The structures being compared also landed: the [Spinᶜ structure of a Spin structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/OfSpin.html#TauCeti.SpinStructure.toSpinC), [orientation reversal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/Reflection/Basic.html#TauCeti.SpinCStructure.reverse), involutive up to isomorphism, and, on a rank-four bundle with an orthogonal complex structure, the [canonical Spinᶜ structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/AlmostComplex.html#TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.canonicalSpinCStructure), built through a [Spinᶜ lift of U(2)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/UnitaryLift.html#TauCeti.unitarySpinCLift) whose determinant character is the complex determinant. The existence criterion W₃ = 0, the H²(X;ℤ)-torsor of lifts, c₁ mod 2 = w₂ and the value of c₁ on the canonical structure are not yet there.

Supporting work went into the group and its bundles: [Spinᶜ(n) as the last-vector stabilizer in Spinᶜ(n+1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Inclusion.html#CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinCContinuousMulEquivLastStabilizer), a [Spinᶜ map for orthogonal sums](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Prod.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroupProd), the circle as kernel of the rotation map, and [local triviality of the Spinᶜ points adapted to a unit section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/UnitSection.html#TauCeti.SpinCStructure.exists_trivialization_sectionPoints), a first step toward restricting Spinᶜ structures to boundaries. Some lemmas on changing a manifold's chart model came with it.

Layer 7 got its explicit polynomial seeds. The quartic z₀³z₂ + z₂³z₁ + z₁³z₃ + z₃³z₀ is [regular away from the origin](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Surfaces/QuarticSurface/EllipticQuarticSeed.html#TauCeti.ellipticQuarticSeedMap_fderiv_surjective), and the Enriques K3 cover, cut out by three diagonal quadrics in CP⁵, is [smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/EnriquesCover.html#TauCeti.enriquesQuadricJacobian_surjective), with a sign-change involution that [acts freely](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Projectivization/EnriquesCover.html#TauCeti.enriquesProjectiveInvolution_no_fixed_point). These are the polynomial facts only. The quotient manifold, the elliptic fibration and the diffeomorphism with E(2) are still to come.
