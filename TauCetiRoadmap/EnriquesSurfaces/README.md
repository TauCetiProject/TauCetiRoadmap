Construct the algebraic geometry of Enriques surfaces, their low-degree linear systems, Reye and double-plane models, and the actions relating these models to integral lattices. Build usable geometric objects and their maps before identifying automorphism groups or counting presentations. The characteristic-dependent theorems, generic loci and equivalences are part of each statement.
