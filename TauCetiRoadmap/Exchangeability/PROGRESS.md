## Exchangeability: 2026-06-25 to 2026-08-01 (`c484d4c` to `6919462`)

De Finetti's theorem landed. `deFinetti` (TauCeti#1444) says that an exchangeable process valued in a nonempty standard Borel space is conditionally i.i.d., and `deFinetti_RyllNardzewski_equivalence` (<https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/Theorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinetti_RyllNardzewski_equivalence>) closes the triangle: contractable iff exchangeable and conditionally i.i.d. The conclusion is the strong one the roadmap asked for, the joint-law disintegration of `(ν, block)` as `∫ δ_{ν ω} ⊗ (ν ω)^{⊗m}`, not merely the mixture identity; the hard direction is `conditionallyIID_of_contractable` (TauCeti#1395), reached by the reverse-martingale route. Uniqueness came with it, both a.e. uniqueness of the directing measure (TauCeti#1430) and uniqueness of the mixing law (TauCeti#1277), as did the unique-mixture form (TauCeti#1462) and the extension to arbitrary countably infinite index types (TauCeti#1451).

Two theorems of independent interest arrived on the way, neither mentioning exchangeability. Lévy's downward theorem, `tendsto_ae_condExp_iInf` (<https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Martingale/Convergence.html#MeasureTheory.tendsto_ae_condExp_iInf>), gives a.s. and L¹ convergence of `μ[f | 𝔽ₙ]` along an arbitrary decreasing filtration (TauCeti#1031), assembled from a finite-horizon time reversal of Mathlib's upcrossing bound (TauCeti#686, TauCeti#734). And the Hewitt–Savage zero-one law (TauCeti#1199), next to a careful separation of the tail, shift-invariant, and exchangeable σ-algebras, with an explicit `Bool`-valued path event witnessing that the tail is not contained in the shift-invariant σ-algebra (TauCeti#1175).

The other two proof routes are unfinished. The Koopman lane is complete as operator theory, up to identifying the mean-ergodic projection with conditional expectation given the invariant σ-algebra (TauCeti#1351, TauCeti#1408), but no `deFinetti_viaKoopman` is stated; the L² lane has its uniform covariance structure and block-average estimates (TauCeti#788, TauCeti#1097) and stops at Cesàro L¹ convergence, short of `deFinetti_viaL2`. Much of the remaining window is product-kernel and cylinder API feeding the common endings.

## Exchangeability: 2026-08-01 to 2026-09-01 (`6919462` to `5b603c6`)

The two outstanding proofs of de Finetti are now complete. [The L²-averaging proof](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/ViaL2/Theorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinetti_viaL2) reaches conditional i.i.d.-ness through disjoint-window product convergence and tail factorization, while [the Koopman proof](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/ViaKoopman/Theorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinetti_viaKoopman) uses the conditional law given the shift-invariant σ-algebra. The sequence theory also gained [the empirical-measure form of de Finetti](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/EmpiricalMeasure.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinetti_empiricalMeasure): on a Polish state space, empirical measures converge weakly almost surely to a directing measure.

At the level of laws, [the de Finetti correspondence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/Correspondence.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinettiEquiv) identifies mixing laws bijectively and affinely with exchangeable path laws. Its product laws are now characterized in three complementary ways: by [triviality of the exchangeable σ-algebra](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/PathSpace/Law/ZeroOne.html#TauCeti.Probability.exchangeableSigma_trivial_iff_iid), [ergodicity of the finitely supported permutation action](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/PathSpace/Exchangeable/Ergodic.html#TauCeti.Probability.exchangeableSigma_trivial_iff_ergodicSMul), and [extremality among exchangeable laws](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/PathSpace/Law/Extreme.html#TauCeti.Probability.exchangeable_extreme_iff_iid). Finite exchangeability now has the promised eventwise de Finetti comparison with the empirical-product mixture, in both directions, with error `choose m 2 / n` ([one direction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/FiniteDeFinetti.html#TauCeti.Probability.ExchangeableAt.prefixLaw_le_sampleWithReplacement_add)).

Layer 8 has also acquired substantial foundations for its two larger representation problems. Recurrent Markov-exchangeable processes now have [conditionally i.i.d. excursions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Recurrence/Excursion.html#TauCeti.Probability.MarkovExchangeable.conditionallyIID_excursionProcess), but the converse representation as a mixture of Markov chains is not yet assembled. For arrays, joint and separate exchangeability, dissociation, block reductions and row-wise de Finetti are in place, and every measurable Aldous–Hoover coding is proved exchangeable ([separate form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/AldousHoover.html#TauCeti.Probability.AldousHoover.separatelyExchangeable_separateArray)); the representation converse has not landed.

## Exchangeability: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-26 (`5b603c6` to `759eb3e`)

The [Diaconis–Freedman theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Recurrence/InitialState.html#TauCeti.Probability.markovExchangeable_iff_mixedMarkovChain) is now proved: a recurrent process is Markov exchangeable exactly when it is a mixture of Markov chains. The converse uses [row exchangeability of the visited successor array](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Recurrence/RowExchangeable.html#TauCeti.Probability.MarkovExchangeable.rowExchangeable_visitedSuccessorProcess); a [spare-state example](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Recurrence/UnvisitedRow.html#TauCeti.Probability.spareStateProcess_not_rowExchangeable_successorProcess) shows why the unmodified successor array does not suffice.

For arrays, [joint dissociation is extremality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/Extreme/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.jointlyDissociated_iff_mem_extremePoints) among jointly exchangeable laws, and exchangeable array laws now decompose into [dissociated components](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/AldousHoover/Decomposition.html#TauCeti.Probability.JointlyExchangeable.exists_dissociated_kernel). A separately exchangeable array also has a [unique column-invariant row-mixing law](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/Representation.html#TauCeti.Probability.SeparatelyExchangeable.existsUnique_rowCodingArrayLaw) giving a canonical row coding. These results stop short of the Aldous–Hoover converse: a coding by global, vertex and cell noise for every exchangeable array is not yet established.

## Exchangeability: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

The Aldous–Hoover representation now holds for separately exchangeable arrays: [a standard-Borel-valued array is separately exchangeable exactly when its law is that of a measurable coding](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/AldousHoover/SeparateRepresentation.html#TauCeti.Probability.separatelyExchangeable_iff_exists_map_separateArray_eq) `X i j = F(U, U_row i, U_col j, U_cell i j)` of independent uniform global, row, column and cell noise (TauCeti#9789). The proof hides infinitely many rows and columns, shows that the crossing strips are [coded by i.i.d. vertex noise](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/Strip/VertexCoding.html#TauCeti.Probability.SeparatelyExchangeable.exists_vertex_strip_coding) given the hidden block, and then generates every visible cell from its context with one common coding function. Along the way it needed a general principle stated with no reference to exchangeability: [conditional independence is a functional representation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Kernel/ConditionalRandomization.html#ProbabilityTheory.CondIndepFun.exists_independent_coding), meaning that conditionally independent variables arise from the conditioning variable by measurable codings of separate uniform noises. Separately exchangeable laws also got their convex theory: [extreme points are exactly the dissociated laws](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/Extreme/Separate.html#TauCeti.Probability.jointlyDissociated_iff_mem_extremePoints_separatelyExchangeable), and every law is a measurable mixture of them.

The jointly exchangeable case is following the same route and is partly there. Its vertex strips are [coded by i.i.d. vertex noise](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/Strip/VertexCoding.html#TauCeti.Probability.JointlyExchangeable.exists_vertex_strip_coding), and [one common coding generates every visible off-diagonal pair](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/Strip/Cell/OffDiagonalCoding.html#TauCeti.Probability.JointlyExchangeable.exists_common_offDiagonalArray_coding), in both orientations, from its square context, which is the crossing strips together with the diagonal. What is still missing is the step that codes the diagonal and puts these pieces together into a single joint Aldous–Hoover coding. The rest of the window was conditional-independence and product-law API for this work.

## Exchangeability: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-05 (`e440f4e` to `1d09589`)

The Aldous–Hoover theorem for jointly exchangeable arrays is proved: [an array with entries in a standard Borel space is jointly exchangeable exactly when its law is that of a measurable coding](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/AldousHoover/Joint/Representation.html#TauCeti.Probability.jointlyExchangeable_iff_exists_map_jointArray_eq) `X i j = F(U, U_i, U_j, U_{ij})` of independent uniform global, vertex and edge noise, where the edge variable is indexed by unordered pairs, so both orientations of a pair share it (TauCeti#10436). The missing steps were to [code the diagonal together with the vertex strips](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/Strip/VertexCoding.html#TauCeti.Probability.JointlyExchangeable.exists_vertex_strip_diagonal_coding) and to [assemble one scalar coding](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/AldousHoover/Joint/Assembly.html#TauCeti.Probability.AldousHoover.assembleJointCoding) from the pair-valued off-diagonal coder, orienting each pair by comparing the two vertices' almost surely distinct continuous marks. With this, Layer 8 and so every target the roadmap lists has landed. On the separate side, [a dissociated separately exchangeable law needs no global variable](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/AldousHoover/Separate/Dissociated.html#TauCeti.Probability.SeparatelyExchangeable.exists_map_separateArray_snd_eq_of_jointlyDissociated).

For sequences, the exchangeable, tail and shift-invariant σ-algebras of a contractable law are now identified up to null sets: [conditioning on exchangeable events is conditioning on tail events](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/ExchangeableSigma/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.ContractableLaw.condExp_exchangeableSigma_ae_eq_pathTail), and likewise for shift-invariant events, with versions for processes on the sample space. Lévy's downward theorem gained its [Lᵖ form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Martingale/Convergence.html#MeasureTheory.MemLp.tendsto_eLpNorm_condExp_iInf), a follow-up the roadmap asked for. Finite de Finetti was rebuilt on a [shared population-sampling construction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/PopulationSampling.html#TauCeti.Probability.samplePopulation) that serves sampling both with and without replacement, as the roadmap specified.
