## Exchangeability: 2026-06-25 to 2026-08-01 (`c484d4c` to `6919462`)

De Finetti's theorem landed. `deFinetti` (TauCeti#1444) says that an exchangeable process valued in a nonempty standard Borel space is conditionally i.i.d., and `deFinetti_RyllNardzewski_equivalence` (<https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/Theorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinetti_RyllNardzewski_equivalence>) closes the triangle: contractable iff exchangeable and conditionally i.i.d. The conclusion is the strong one the roadmap asked for, the joint-law disintegration of `(ν, block)` as `∫ δ_{ν ω} ⊗ (ν ω)^{⊗m}`, not merely the mixture identity; the hard direction is `conditionallyIID_of_contractable` (TauCeti#1395), reached by the reverse-martingale route. Uniqueness came with it, both a.e. uniqueness of the directing measure (TauCeti#1430) and uniqueness of the mixing law (TauCeti#1277), as did the unique-mixture form (TauCeti#1462) and the extension to arbitrary countably infinite index types (TauCeti#1451).

Two theorems of independent interest arrived on the way, neither mentioning exchangeability. Lévy's downward theorem, `tendsto_ae_condExp_iInf` (<https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Martingale/Convergence.html#MeasureTheory.tendsto_ae_condExp_iInf>), gives a.s. and L¹ convergence of `μ[f | 𝔽ₙ]` along an arbitrary decreasing filtration (TauCeti#1031), assembled from a finite-horizon time reversal of Mathlib's upcrossing bound (TauCeti#686, TauCeti#734). And the Hewitt–Savage zero-one law (TauCeti#1199), next to a careful separation of the tail, shift-invariant, and exchangeable σ-algebras, with an explicit `Bool`-valued path event witnessing that the tail is not contained in the shift-invariant σ-algebra (TauCeti#1175).

The other two proof routes are unfinished. The Koopman lane is complete as operator theory, up to identifying the mean-ergodic projection with conditional expectation given the invariant σ-algebra (TauCeti#1351, TauCeti#1408), but no `deFinetti_viaKoopman` is stated; the L² lane has its uniform covariance structure and block-average estimates (TauCeti#788, TauCeti#1097) and stops at Cesàro L¹ convergence, short of `deFinetti_viaL2`. Much of the remaining window is product-kernel and cylinder API feeding the common endings.

## Exchangeability: 2026-08-01 to 2026-09-01 (`6919462` to `5b603c6`)

The two outstanding proofs of de Finetti are now complete. [The L²-averaging proof](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/ViaL2/Theorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinetti_viaL2) reaches conditional i.i.d.-ness through disjoint-window product convergence and tail factorization, while [the Koopman proof](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/ViaKoopman/Theorem.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinetti_viaKoopman) uses the conditional law given the shift-invariant σ-algebra. The sequence theory also gained [the empirical-measure form of de Finetti](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/EmpiricalMeasure.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinetti_empiricalMeasure): on a Polish state space, empirical measures converge weakly almost surely to a directing measure.

At the level of laws, [the de Finetti correspondence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/DeFinetti/Correspondence.html#TauCeti.Probability.deFinettiEquiv) identifies mixing laws bijectively and affinely with exchangeable path laws. Its product laws are now characterized in three complementary ways: by [triviality of the exchangeable σ-algebra](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/PathSpace/Law/ZeroOne.html#TauCeti.Probability.exchangeableSigma_trivial_iff_iid), [ergodicity of the finitely supported permutation action](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/PathSpace/Exchangeable/Ergodic.html#TauCeti.Probability.exchangeableSigma_trivial_iff_ergodicSMul), and [extremality among exchangeable laws](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/PathSpace/Law/Extreme.html#TauCeti.Probability.exchangeable_extreme_iff_iid). Finite exchangeability now has the promised eventwise de Finetti comparison with the empirical-product mixture, in both directions, with error `choose m 2 / n` ([one direction](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/FiniteDeFinetti.html#TauCeti.Probability.ExchangeableAt.prefixLaw_le_sampleWithReplacement_add)).

Layer 8 has also acquired substantial foundations for its two larger representation problems. Recurrent Markov-exchangeable processes now have [conditionally i.i.d. excursions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Recurrence/Excursion.html#TauCeti.Probability.MarkovExchangeable.conditionallyIID_excursionProcess), but the converse representation as a mixture of Markov chains is not yet assembled. For arrays, joint and separate exchangeability, dissociation, block reductions and row-wise de Finetti are in place, and every measurable Aldous–Hoover coding is proved exchangeable ([separate form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Exchangeability/Arrays/AldousHoover.html#TauCeti.Probability.AldousHoover.separatelyExchangeable_separateArray)); the representation converse has not landed.
