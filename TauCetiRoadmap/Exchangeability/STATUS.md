## The frontier

* **`deFinetti_viaL2`.** The nearest substantial target. The L² estimates are in place up to `weighted_sums_converge_L1_of_memLp`; what is needed is `realObservables_determine_directing_measure`, the directing-measure step, and then the calls to the two common endings, which already exist. Nothing here is blocked.
* **`deFinetti_viaKoopman`.** Likewise unblocked. The operator theory and the projection identification are finished, and contractability already gives shift preservation on path space; the remaining work is `conditionallyIID_transfer` and the wrapper.
* **`deFinetti_empiricalMeasure`.** Partly there in a weaker form: `ConditionallyIIDWith.tendsto_integral_empiricalMeasure_apply_sub_sq` gives mean-square convergence of empirical-measure evaluations on a fixed measurable set to the directing measure's. The roadmap target is weak convergence in `ProbabilityMeasure α` against bounded continuous test functions, which needs the weak topology and is not started.
* **`exchangeable_extreme_iff_iid`.** Both stated inputs, Hewitt–Savage and mixing-law uniqueness, have landed, so this corollary looks like a short piece of work that simply has not been written.
* **Layer 8.** Finite de Finetti bounds, ergodic decomposition of exchangeable laws, Markov exchangeability, and Aldous–Hoover for exchangeable arrays are all open. The arrays tower is much larger than the sequence theorem and would want its own sequencing.
* **Mathlib extraction.** The reverse-martingale file (`tendsto_ae_condExp_iInf` and its L¹ form), the Koopman and mean-ergodic material, and the product-kernel measurability lemmas are all stated without reference to exchangeability and are the natural upstreaming candidates. No such extraction is recorded here.
