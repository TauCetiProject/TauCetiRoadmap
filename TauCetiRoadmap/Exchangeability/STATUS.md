## The frontier

* **`deFinetti_viaL2`.** The averaging chain now reaches the canonical directing measure: for every measurable set, the Cesàro averages of its indicators converge in `L¹` to the corresponding evaluation of `directingMeasure` (TauCetiProject/TauCeti#1938, on TauCetiProject/TauCeti#1826 and #1796). This supersedes the roadmap's proposed reconstruction of a directing measure from a determining class: `directingMeasure` is the neutral `condDistrib` construction, so reusing it does not import reverse-martingale convergence. The remaining substantive step is simultaneous disjoint-window product convergence and the resulting finite-block conditional factorization, followed by the existing set-integral common ending and the route-specific wrappers.
* **`deFinetti_viaKoopman`.** The operator theory and projection identification, the invariant conditional law of the initial coordinate given `MeasurableSpace.invariants (shift α)` (TauCetiProject/TauCeti#1846), the path-law transfer `ConditionallyIIDWith.of_pathLaw`, the invariant-event reindexing transport (TauCetiProject/TauCeti#1896), and the set-integral common ending are all available. Nothing yet proves the invariant conditional law directs the process. The remaining mathematics is the invariant-event disjoint-block factorization on path space, and then the endpoint wrappers.
* **`deFinetti_empiricalMeasure`.** Partly there in a weaker form: `ConditionallyIIDWith.tendsto_integral_empiricalMeasure_apply_sub_sq` gives mean-square convergence of empirical-measure evaluations on a fixed measurable set to the directing measure's. The roadmap target is weak convergence in `ProbabilityMeasure α` against bounded continuous test functions, which needs the weak topology and is not started — and, before implementation, a roadmap decision: under `[StandardBorelSpace α]` alone no compatible Polish topology is selected (Mathlib's `upgradeStandardBorel` chooses one), so the target as currently worded is not yet well-posed. The roadmap should either assume `[TopologicalSpace α] [PolishSpace α] [BorelSpace α]`, state convergence against a countable measurable determining class, or explicitly invoke the chosen upgrade.
* **Ergodic and extreme exchangeable laws.** `exchangeable_extreme_iff_iid` has **landed** (TauCetiProject/TauCeti#1785, refactored by TauCetiProject/TauCeti#1819): for a standard Borel state space, a probability law on `ℕ → α` is an extreme point of the exchangeable laws iff it is an infinite product `P^{⊗ℕ}`. The public iff is existential; it does not separately expose an `∃!` API for the product parameter. It reaches the extreme-to-product direction from `deFinetti_mixture` and mixture injectivity rather than through a zero–one characterization. Also landed: `ergodic_shift_infinitePi_const`, that product laws are ergodic for the **one-sided shift**. Two interfaces remain open. `exchangeableSigma_trivial_iff_iid` — an exchangeable law is zero–one on `exchangeableSigma` iff it is a product law — is unproved; the product-to-zero–one direction is Hewitt–Savage, while the converse needs triviality of `exchangeableSigma` to force the directing measure a.s. constant. The `ErgodicSMul` formulation for the **finitely supported permutation action** is also unproved, and the landed shift result does not supply it: shift ergodicity and permutation-action ergodicity are different statements. That interface additionally requires bundling the permutation action and relating exact invariant events to Mathlib's a.e.-invariant formulation.
* **Layer 8.** Finite de Finetti bounds, Markov exchangeability, and Aldous–Hoover for exchangeable arrays remain open; the arrays tower is much larger than the sequence theorem and would want its own sequencing. The unique product-mixture representation and the classification of extreme exchangeable laws as product laws have landed. What remains for ergodic decomposition is the affine decomposition API and, separately, its interpretation through the finitely supported permutation `ErgodicSMul` action.
* **Mathlib extraction.** The reverse-martingale file (`tendsto_ae_condExp_iInf` and its L¹ form), the Koopman and mean-ergodic material, and the product-kernel measurability lemmas are all stated without reference to exchangeability and are the natural upstreaming candidates. No such extraction is recorded here.
