## CountableModelCounting: 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-09 (`7714b4f` to `d92acc5`)

Vaught's conjecture is now stated precisely, in its perfect-set form [`VaughtConjecture`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Vaught.html#TauCeti.VaughtConjecture) and its cardinal form [`CardinalVaughtConjecture`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Vaught.html#TauCeti.CardinalVaughtConjecture), together with the standard reformulations proved from Morley's theorem: a counterexample has [exactly ℵ₁ countable models and no perfect set of them](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Vaught.html#TauCeti.not_hasVaughtDichotomy_iff), CH [proves the cardinal form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Vaught.html#TauCeti.cardinalVaughtConjecture_of_aleph_one_eq_continuum) without touching the perfect-set one, under ℵ₁ < 𝔠 [the two forms coincide](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Vaught.html#TauCeti.cardinalVaughtConjecture_iff_vaughtConjecture), and the conjecture is [equivalent to its statement for countably infinite models](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Vaught.html#TauCeti.vaughtConjecture_iff_nat) (TauCeti#28482).

Underneath this sits most of the counting machinery. The count [`countableModelCount`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Classes.html#TauCeti.countableModelCount) counts models on ℕ and on every finite carrier, and [`classOf`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Classes.html#TauCeti.classOf) identifies its classes with countable models in any universe. Counts and perfect sets are also invariant under language equivalence, so every sentence has a [counterpart in universe 0](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/LanguageEquiv.html#TauCeti.exists_language_zero_countableModelCount). On the stratification side there are [isolating ranks](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Stratification.html#TauCeti.IsIsolatingRank), with the external [Scott height](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Scott/Height.html#TauCeti.scottHeight) as the [canonical instance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Stratification.html#TauCeti.isIsolatingRank_scottHeight). There is also the [stratification bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Stratification.html#TauCeti.mk_isoClasses_le_aleph_one_of_countable_levels): countably many classes at every countable level means at most ℵ₁ isomorphism classes. A level-by-level transfer packaged as [`BFSmall`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/BackAndForth/ExtensionSpectrum.html#TauCeti.BFSmall) gives that bound from local conditions.

Toward the scattered-class lane, [back-and-forth scattered sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/Counting/Scattered/Basic.html#TauCeti.BFScattered) arrived, along with the [Morleyization expansion code](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/Morleyization.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.morleyCode), which [transports thinness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/Morleyization.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.isThinOn_image_morleyCode_iff), and analytic splits of concentrated classes. The first pieces of the Harnik–Makkai reduction also landed: [level classes are sentence cuts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/LevelSentence.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.modelsOf_scottSentenceAtLevel), and [the limit of a back-and-forth chain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/BackAndForth.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.exists_limit_of_bfEquiv_chain) exists. The splitting tree and minimally uncountable sentences have not started.
