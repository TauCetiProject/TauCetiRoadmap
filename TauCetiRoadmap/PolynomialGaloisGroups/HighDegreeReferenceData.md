# Reference generators in degrees six through eleven

This is the fixed mathematical input for PolynomialGaloisGroups Layer1D. It records the standard external `nTj` ordering and zero-based permutation cycles; omitted points are fixed. Each row denotes the subgroup generated by its displayed permutations. None of the transitivity, order, nonconjugacy, completeness or external-label comparisons is proved merely by this list: Layer1D states and proves them.

The source is the [GAP TransGrp library](https://github.com/hulpke/transgrp/tree/165fc21ff497b24b7a5975582b331e6692ba04f1), `lib/trans.grp` for degrees6–7 and `data/trans8.grp` through `data/trans11.grp` for degrees8–11. The source credits Greg Butler and John McKay for the classification and John Conway, John McKay and Alexander Hulpke for names/generators; its source/header/license attribution is retained in the author evidence. The entries here are mathematical permutation data, not copied GAP routines. Compare the [LMFDB group labels](https://www.lmfdb.org/GaloisGroup/) by a proved conjugating permutation, never by abstract group name alone.

The six row counts are16,7,50,34,45,8. External `nTj` is one-based; the native index is `j-1`. The existing fourteen degree-at-most-five references and conservative aliases retain their definitions. The required degree-six acceptance row is `6T6 = <(0,2,4)(1,3,5),(2,5)>`, up to the displayed order of its two generators; it agrees with the [LMFDB6T6 data](https://www.lmfdb.org/GaloisGroup/6T6). The tuple cycles act on `Fin n` and compose as functions.
