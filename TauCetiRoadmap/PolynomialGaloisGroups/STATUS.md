## The frontier

- **Frobenius membership** — add good-prime conditions and connect factor degrees to full cycle types in the Galois image; this consumes the imported Dedekind theorem specified by Number Field Arithmetic and the discriminant results.
- **Resolvent decisions** — complete the quartic discriminant identity and decision table and the factorization-by-orbits theorem; rational roots only give subgroup containment in the reverse direction when the specialized resolvent is separable.
- **Polynomial labels and certificates** — lift the subgroup classification to `HasGaloisLabel` statements and prove the degree-five certificate soundness theorem; the discriminant and sextic alone cannot separate `5T1` from `5T2`.
- **Full symmetric realization** — assemble the existing finite-field patterns and Chinese remainder lift with good-prime Frobenius membership and the group-theoretic recognition steps to obtain the promised polynomial in every positive degree.
