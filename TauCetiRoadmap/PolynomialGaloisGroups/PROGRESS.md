## PolynomialGaloisGroups: 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-01 (`223865e` to `8745177`)

The first work on this roadmap is in its group-theoretic half. Two of Layer 1's recognition theorems are proved: a transitive permutation group of prime degree contains a full cycle ([`TauCeti.exists_isCycle_mem_of_isPretransitive_of_prime_card`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Recognition.html#TauCeti.exists_isCycle_mem_of_isPretransitive_of_prime_card)), and a transitive group containing a cycle that moves all but one point is doubly transitive ([`TauCeti.is_two_pretransitive_of_isCycle_mem_of_card_support_add_one_eq_card`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/MultipleTransitivity.html#TauCeti.is_two_pretransitive_of_isCycle_mem_of_card_support_add_one_eq_card)) and hence primitive ([`TauCeti.isPreprimitive_of_isCycle_mem_of_card_support_add_one_eq_card`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/MultipleTransitivity.html#TauCeti.isPreprimitive_of_isCycle_mem_of_card_support_add_one_eq_card)), with variants phrased through a unique fixed point ([`TauCeti.card_support_add_one_eq_card_iff_existsUnique_fixedPoint`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Basic.html#TauCeti.card_support_add_one_eq_card_iff_existsUnique_fixedPoint)). These are two of the four group-theoretic steps that the realization of Sₙ over ℚ in Layer 9 needs; the two transposition steps are not there yet.

Layer 0's degree bookkeeping landed in TauCeti#5195: a separable polynomial's roots in its splitting field are numbered by `Fin p.natDegree` ([`TauCeti.nonempty_rootSet_splittingField_equiv_fin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Degree.html#TauCeti.nonempty_rootSet_splittingField_equiv_fin)), the image of the root action in any splitting extension has the order of the Galois group ([`TauCeti.natCard_galActionHom_range`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Degree.html#TauCeti.natCard_galActionHom_range)), and that order divides the factorial of the degree ([`TauCeti.natCard_gal_dvd_factorial_natDegree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Degree.html#TauCeti.natCard_gal_dvd_factorial_natDegree)).

The largest pull request, TauCeti#5165, is API for cycle types that record fixed points as parts equal to one, the correction the roadmap calls `fullCycleType`. It is written against the parts of `Equiv.Perm.partition`: away from one they agree with the cycle type ([`Equiv.Perm.count_parts_partition_of_ne_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Partition.html#Equiv.Perm.count_parts_partition_of_ne_one)), the ones count the fixed points ([`Equiv.Perm.count_one_parts_partition`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Partition.html#Equiv.Perm.count_one_parts_partition)), the correction is trivial exactly for fixed-point-free permutations ([`Equiv.Perm.parts_partition_eq_cycleType_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Partition.html#Equiv.Perm.parts_partition_eq_cycleType_iff)), and the parts are constant on conjugacy classes ([`Equiv.Perm.parts_partition_conj`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Partition.html#Equiv.Perm.parts_partition_conj)) and natural in the carrier ([`Equiv.Perm.parts_partition_permCongr`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Partition.html#Equiv.Perm.parts_partition_permCongr)), with sign ([`Equiv.Perm.sign_of_parts_partition`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Partition.html#Equiv.Perm.sign_of_parts_partition)), order, the identity, transpositions, cycles, and the empty type. No declaration named `fullCycleType` appears, and the `Fin 4` examples the roadmap asks for are absent. Nothing else on the roadmap has been touched.

## PolynomialGaloisGroups: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-29 (`8745177` to `163ce80`)

The [classification of transitive subgroups of degree five](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/TransitiveGroupLabel/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_transitiveGroupLabel_five) now assigns exactly one of the five `5Tj` labels to each transitive subgroup of `S₅`; the analogous [degree-four classification](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/TransitiveGroupLabel/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_transitiveGroupLabel_four) also landed. The [quintic resolvent solvability criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Resolvent/Quintic/Solvable.html#TauCeti.isSolvable_gal_iff_exists_isRoot_specialize_quinticF20Spec) relates solvability of an irreducible separable quintic's Galois group to a root of its `F₂₀` resolvent sextic in characteristic other than 2 or 5, provided the sextic is separable. More generally, the [resolvent root criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Resolvent/Root.html#TauCeti.ResolventSpec.exists_isRoot_specialize_iff_exists_le_map_conj) identifies a root with containment in a conjugate of the specification's stabilizer under the same separation condition.

The field and permutation dictionaries also grew: [blocks containing a root correspond to intermediate fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Blocks.html#TauCeti.rootBlockIntermediateFieldOrderIso), and [double transitivity becomes irreducibility over the root field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/MultipleTransitivity.html#TauCeti.is_two_pretransitive_iff_irreducible_divByMonic). The [full cycle type](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Partition.html#Equiv.Perm.fullCycleType), which counts fixed points, and the [factor-degree multiset](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/FactorDegrees.html#Polynomial.factorDegrees) now give the two sides of the planned Frobenius comparison. That comparison, the discriminant test, and the promised realization of `Sₙ` over `ℚ` are not yet established.
