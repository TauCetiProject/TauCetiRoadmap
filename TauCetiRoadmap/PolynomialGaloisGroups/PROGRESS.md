## PolynomialGaloisGroups: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30 (`8745177` to `dec7a58`)

The summit of the roadmap is proved. In every degree n ≥ 1 there is a monic integral polynomial, irreducible over ℚ, whose Galois group is the full symmetric group ([`TauCeti.exists_monic_int_polynomial_hasFullSymmetricGaloisGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Symmetric/Realization.html#TauCeti.exists_monic_int_polynomial_hasFullSymmetricGaloisGroup)). The proof is van der Waerden's. Reductions modulo 2, 3 and 5 with prescribed factorization patterns are glued together by a coefficientwise Chinese remainder theorem. Dedekind's theorem then applies to the result. That theorem comes from Number Field Arithmetic, and here it says that the factor degrees of f mod p form the full cycle type of some element of the Galois image ([`TauCeti.exists_mem_range_galActionHom_fullCycleType_eq_factorDegrees`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Reduction.html#TauCeti.exists_mem_range_galActionHom_fullCycleType_eq_factorDegrees)). The transitive subgroups of S₃, S₄ and S₅ are also classified up to conjugacy ([`TauCeti.existsUnique_transitiveGroupLabel_five`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/TransitiveGroupLabel/Classification.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_transitiveGroupLabel_five)). As a result, every irreducible separable polynomial of degree at most five carries exactly one `nTj` label.

Most of the window is the machinery between these two results. The root-product formula for the discriminant gives the square-discriminant test for containment in Aₙ ([`Polynomial.Monic.isSquare_discr_iff_range_le_alternatingGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Discriminant/Basic.html#Polynomial.Monic.isSquare_discr_iff_range_le_alternatingGroup)). Resolvents are built as universal integral data and then specialized at a polynomial. The quintic case yields the criterion that an irreducible quintic has a solvable Galois group exactly when its separable resolvent sextic has a root ([`TauCeti.isSolvable_gal_iff_exists_isRoot_specialize_quinticF20Spec`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Resolvent/Quintic/Solvable.html#TauCeti.isSolvable_gal_iff_exists_isRoot_specialize_quinticF20Spec)). These results feed the decision tables in degrees three to five. They also feed a quintic certificate with a checker proved sound ([`TauCeti.QuinticCertificate.check_sound`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Certificate/Check.html#TauCeti.QuinticCertificate.check_sound)), which is exercised on one worked quintic for each label. On the group side, general wreath products arrived with the imprimitivity embedding, along with Jordan's theorem for a cycle of prime length ([`TauCeti.alternatingGroup_le_of_isPreprimitive_of_isCycle_mem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Jordan.html#TauCeti.alternatingGroup_le_of_isPreprimitive_of_isCycle_mem)).

Some gaps remain. The quartic table's last row still returns "4T1 or 4T3", because the step that splits that row over the discriminant field is missing. Tschirnhaus transforms and the primitivity of the product wreath action have not been started.

The last row of the quartic table is now split. An irreducible quartic with label 4T1 or 4T3 is cyclic exactly when it factors over its discriminant field ([`TauCeti.hasGaloisLabel_four_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Quartic/Basic.html#TauCeti.hasGaloisLabel_four_zero_iff)), and dihedral exactly when it stays irreducible there ([`TauCeti.hasGaloisLabel_four_two_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Quartic/Basic.html#TauCeti.hasGaloisLabel_four_two_iff)). Both rest on a general criterion: a polynomial stays irreducible over its discriminant field exactly when the even part of its Galois image is still transitive ([`TauCeti.irreducible_map_discrField_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Discriminant/Field.html#TauCeti.irreducible_map_discrField_iff)). The factorization theorem for resolvents also landed. For a separable specialized resolvent, the irreducible factors correspond to the orbits of the Galois image on the cosets of H ([`TauCeti.ResolventSpec.orbitQuotientEquivFactors`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Resolvent/Root.html#TauCeti.ResolventSpec.orbitQuotientEquivFactors)), so the factor degrees are the orbit sizes.

## PolynomialGaloisGroups: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05 (`8a32441` to `1d09589`)

The last open milestone of the roadmap is closed: Tschirnhaus transforms now carry a subgroup bound back to the original polynomial. If `T` is admissible for a monic separable `f` and the resolvent of the transform is separable with a root in the base field, then the Galois image of `f` lies in a conjugate of `H` ([`TauCeti.ResolventSpec.exists_le_map_conj_of_isRoot_specialize_tschirnhausPolynomial`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Resolvent/Tschirnhaus.html#TauCeti.ResolventSpec.exists_le_map_conj_of_isRoot_specialize_tschirnhausPolynomial)). Behind it are the comparison theorems the roadmap asked for: an admissible transform has the same splitting field up to isomorphism ([`Polynomial.TschirnhausAdmissible.nonempty_algEquiv_splittingField`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Tschirnhaus.html#Polynomial.TschirnhausAdmissible.nonempty_algEquiv_splittingField)), and its Galois image is conjugate to that of `f` along `α ↦ T(α)` ([`Polynomial.TschirnhausAdmissible.range_galActionHom_eq_map_rootSetEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Tschirnhaus.html#Polynomial.TschirnhausAdmissible.range_galActionHom_eq_map_rootSetEquiv)). The classical claim that an admissible transform always exists is, as the roadmap specifies, not proved.

Around this, several items were made explicit. Root enumerations force splitting and are injective exactly for separable polynomials ([`Polynomial.IsRootEnumeration.injective_iff_separable`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/RootEnumeration.html#Polynomial.IsRootEnumeration.injective_iff_separable)). Depressing a quartic does not change its label ([`TauCeti.hasGaloisLabel_quartic_iff_depressed`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Depression.html#TauCeti.hasGaloisLabel_quartic_iff_depressed)). The Galois group of a product of two polynomials is the fibre product of their groups over the common subfield, and the full product when the splitting fields are linearly disjoint ([`Polynomial.Gal.mem_range_restrictProd_iff_restrictInfLeft_eq_restrictInfRight`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisGroups/Product.html#Polynomial.Gal.mem_range_restrictProd_iff_restrictInfLeft_eq_restrictInfRight)).

On the group side, the reference subgroups gained their abstract names: `nT1` is cyclic of order n, `4T2` is the Klein four-group, `5T2` is dihedral and `5T3` is `AGL(1, 5)` ([`TauCeti.referenceSubgroupFiveTwoMulEquivAffineGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/TransitiveGroupLabel/Affine.html#TauCeti.referenceSubgroupFiveTwoMulEquivAffineGroup)). The primitivity column of the table is filled in, with only `4T1`, `4T2` and `4T3` imprimitive. Affine groups also show that Jordan's theorem fails when the prime cycle has length n or n − 1 ([`TauCeti.not_forall_alternatingGroup_le_of_isPreprimitive_of_isCycle_mem_of_card_support_add_one_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/Jordan/Counterexamples.html#TauCeti.not_forall_alternatingGroup_le_of_isPreprimitive_of_isCycle_mem_of_card_support_add_one_eq)).
