## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** M0.4, the analytic Black–Scholes layer, is done except for self-financing replication, which still needs a general stochastic integral. M0.1–M0.3 are partial. The Hölder-class pricing equation now has both existence and uniqueness, while completeness and general replication have not begun. Nothing has begun in the MarketImpact sub-roadmap.

### Named results

- **Uniqueness of the Black–Scholes price** — a classical solution of the Black–Scholes equation with linear growth `|C| ≤ A(1 + s)` and the call payoff at maturity is the closed-form call price ([`eq_callValue_of_pde`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.eq_callValue_of_pde); the put is analogous).
- **Well-posedness of the local-volatility pricing equation** — for Hölder local variance bounded below and a Hölder rate, a payoff `h` with `h(eʸ)/(1 + eʸ)` in `C^{2,α}` has exactly one price `C = (1 + s)v(log s, t)` with `v` in `C_b^{2+α,1+α/2}` ([`existsUnique_pde_weightedLogSpotPrice_eq_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/Pricing/Existence.html#TauCeti.Finance.existsUnique_pde_weightedLogSpotPrice_eq_zero)).
- **The discounted Black–Scholes value is a risk-neutral martingale** — under `Q`, the stopped process `e^{−ru}C(S_u, T − u)` is a martingale with finite moments ([`martingale_discounted_callValue_tilted`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/ValueProcess.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.martingale_discounted_callValue_tilted)). Its value is the [discounted conditional payoff](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/ConditionalPrice.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.callValue_eq_condExp_tilted_geometricBrownianMotion).
- **Girsanov's theorem for a constant drift** — tilting by `exp(−κW_T − κ²T/2)` makes `W_t + κ min(t, T)` a Brownian motion ([`sub_mul_min_tilted_mul_eval`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Girsanov.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.sub_mul_min_tilted_mul_eval)), and the augmented natural filtrations of `W` and `W + κt` [agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/NaturalFiltration.html#ProbabilityTheory.IsPreBrownianReal.rightCont_nullAugment_natural_add_drift_tilted).
- **No arbitrage with simple strategies** — in the Black–Scholes market, no self-financing simple stock/bond strategy is an arbitrage ([`not_isArbitrage_geometricBrownianMotion`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/NoArbitrage.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.not_isArbitrage_geometricBrownianMotion)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Simple strategies and their approximation.** [`IsSelfFinancing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/SimpleStrategy/Basic.html#TauCeti.Finance.IsSelfFinancing) matches the elementary wealth identity. Predictable holdings are limits of bounded finite-grid holdings in several senses: weighted square energy, `L¹`, almost-sure pathwise, and [`L¹` in drift and `L²` in diffusion simultaneously](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/SimpleStrategy/MixedApproximation.html#MeasureTheory.Filtration.exists_seq_simpleHolding_tendsto_eLpNorm_mul_one_two).
- **The bond's Stieltjes measure.** With a continuous short rate, the bond price has [a signed measure with density `rβ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/Bond/Trading.html#TauCeti.Finance.vectorMeasureIcc_bondPrice). [Elementary bond gains converge uniformly](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/Bond/Approximation.html#TauCeti.Finance.tendstoUniformlyOn_simpleGains_bondPrice_of_L1) to its integral under `L¹` approximation, so the bond leg `∫b dβ` is settled for general holdings.
- **Admissibility inputs for the delta hedge.** The call and put hedges have [predictable Brownian gains coefficients of finite expected square energy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Energy.html#ProbabilityTheory.IsPreBrownianReal.brownianIntegrable_mul_deriv_callPrice_spot_geometricBrownianMotion) up to maturity. Polynomial-growth prices of geometric Brownian motion [converge in `Lᵖ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/Localization.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.tendsto_eLpNorm_polynomialGrowth_geometricBrownianMotion) along random times tending to maturity.

### Roadmap coverage

The umbrella's milestones are M0.1–M0.4, which are not machine-tracked.

- **M0.4 is done except replication.** It has the formulas, the PDE, parity, the risk-neutral expectation, the Greeks with signs and exact formulas, the bond holdings, the boundary and far-field limits, the extension to maturity, the gamma and vega limits as `τ↓0`, and uniqueness. Self-financing replication of the call and put is missing.
- **M0.1 is partial.** The money-market account and its trading integral, constant-coefficient GBM given by its formula, simple strategies and their approximations are done. The linear SDE with progressive coefficients and the stock integral `∫a dS` are not.
- **M0.2 is partial.** It has the constant-drift density, Girsanov, equality of the filtrations, simple-strategy no-arbitrage, the scaled static-claim arbitrage and predictable versions of bounded progressive coefficients. Martingale representation, completeness, `L²` replication and the rest of the variable-coefficient extension are missing.
- **M0.3 is partial.** The along-path-to-pointwise step and uniqueness and existence in the weighted Hölder class are done. The Feynman–Kac identification of that solution with the conditional price is not established here. The Itô necessity theorem and `Q`-based sufficiency are missing, though the terminal-convergence ingredient of the latter is in place.
- **MarketImpact is untouched.** None of its five layers has any Lean development.

## The frontier

- **The Itô integral as a process.** The library has a terminal `L²` Brownian isometry and a uniqueness theory for Itô integrals, but no existence theorem for general integrands. This is the common blocker for general self-financing (M0.1), replication (M0.2–M0.4) and PDE necessity (M0.3). It belongs to StochasticCalculus S3.
- **Black–Scholes replication.** Predictability and energy of the hedge, drift matching, the martingale value process and terminal convergence are all present. What remains is the Itô step showing that the delta hedge is self-financing with the payoff as its terminal value.
- **Completeness.** For constant coefficients, this means replicating every claim with `e^{−rT}H ∈ L²(Q)`, which needs martingale representation (StochasticCalculus S6.1). The variable-coefficient version also needs a true-martingale density for random `κ`.
- **Global clearing for MarketImpact.** Layer 1's unique positive root of `x(1 − 𝓗(x)) = y` under a derivative margin is one-variable real analysis. It needs no stochastic integral, so it is the nearest MarketImpact target.
- **GBM as an SDE solution.** M0.1 asks for the strong solution of `dS = μS dt + σS dW` with progressive coefficients. That waits on StochasticCalculus S9.4.
