## FinancialMathematics: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`12e85d6` to `273ea28`)

The constant-coefficient Black–Scholes formulas arrived, with both halves of the classical story. On the probabilistic side, the [Black–Scholes call formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Basic.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.riskNeutralPrice_call) and its [put counterpart](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Basic.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.riskNeutralPrice_put) show that the discounted expectation of the payoff under a lognormal terminal spot equals the closed form `sΦ(d₊) − Ke^{−rτ}Φ(d₋)`. The proof changes to the stock numéraire through a [Gaussian exponential-tilting identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Tilted.html#TauCeti.Probability.setIntegral_exp_mul_gaussianReal) (TauCeti#18827). [Put–call parity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Basic.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.callPrice_sub_putPrice) is proved for the closed forms. On the analytic side, the call and put prices satisfy the [Black–Scholes equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Greeks.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.callPrice_pde) for every spot `s > 0` before maturity (TauCeti#19099).

That second result rests on the Greeks. The window gives exact formulas for delta, gamma, vega and the maturity derivative of both options, all hinging on the density identity `sφ(d₊) = Ke^{−rτ}φ(d₋)`. It also proves the sign facts the roadmap asks for: the [call delta lies in (0, 1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Greeks.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.deriv_callPrice_spot_mem_Ioo), and gamma and vega are positive. Supporting this are a few Gaussian CDF facts: symmetry, strict bounds, and upper tails written as standard CDF values.

All of this is a single-variable Gaussian computation. Nothing stochastic is involved. No asset SDE, trading strategy, equivalent measure or replication argument has landed, so "risk-neutral price" here is a defined integral, not a theorem about markets. Also still missing from M0.4: the boundary and far-field limits, the bond holdings, the continuous extension to maturity, the uniqueness class and the behaviour of gamma as τ↓0.

## FinancialMathematics: 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-12 (`273ea28` to `941bb58`)

The closed-form layer of Black–Scholes is now nearly finished, and the stochastic market has started to appear around it. The headline is the [uniqueness of the Black–Scholes call price](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.eq_callValue_of_pde), with its put twin: a classical solution of the Black–Scholes equation with linear growth and the call payoff at maturity is the closed form. The proof does not go through stopped Itô, as the README suggests. It specialises a [comparison principle for the pricing equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/Comparison.html#TauCeti.Finance.le_of_pde_le_of_terminal_le) with bounded variable coefficients, itself built on a whole-line parabolic maximum principle (TauCeti#26332, TauCeti#29938). Around it, the prices were extended continuously to maturity and to zero spot, and the boundary, [far-field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Limits.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.tendsto_callPrice_sub_spot_atTop) and Greek limits arrived, including the [at-the-money gamma blow-up](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Limits.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.tendsto_sqrt_mul_deriv_deriv_callPrice_spot_strike) at rate `1/(Kσ√(2πτ))`. The [hedge bond holdings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/Hedge.html#TauCeti.BlackScholes.hedgeBondHolding_callPrice) arrived too, with the drift-matching identity.

On the probabilistic side, [geometric Brownian motion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Geometric/Basic.html#TauCeti.Probability.geometricBrownianMotion) is defined by its explicit formula, with all real moments and an integrable running maximum. [Girsanov's theorem for a constant drift](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/Girsanov.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.sub_mul_min_tilted_mul_eval) gives the risk-neutral measure, and the [augmented natural filtrations of W and W + κt agree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/BrownianMotion/NaturalFiltration.html#ProbabilityTheory.IsPreBrownianReal.rightCont_nullAugment_natural_add_drift_tilted). Under that measure the [call value is the discounted conditional payoff](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/ConditionalPrice.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.callValue_eq_condExp_tilted_geometricBrownianMotion). Simple strategies got the [simple-strategy formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/SimpleStrategy/Basic.html#TauCeti.Finance.isSelfFinancing_iff_forall_mem_Ioc), and the window proves [no arbitrage with simple strategies](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/BlackScholes/NoArbitrage.html#TauCeti.Probability.IsFilteredPreBrownianReal.not_isArbitrage_geometricBrownianMotion) in the Black–Scholes market, plus the [scaled static-claim arbitrage](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Finance/StaticClaim.html#TauCeti.Finance.exists_staticClaim_profit_gt).

What is still missing is the stochastic integral. Without it there is no general self-financing strategy, no replication of the call or of `L²` claims, and no completeness. GBM is not yet identified as the solution of an SDE. On the PDE side, the weighted log-spot operator has uniqueness in the Hölder class, but the existence theorem has not landed.
