## LocalVariationalFieldTheory: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`2cf86b1` to `7ca783d`)

The finite Dirac model of Layer 3 now has its two acceptance theorems. The real Clifford representation [`diracClifford`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.diracClifford) of Cl(3,1) on ℂ⁴, sending a vector v to −iC(v), [restricts on Spin(3,1) to the Weyl action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.diracClifford_spinGroup) diag(A, (A†)⁻¹) through the native Spin–SL₂ equivalence, and that action [makes Clifford multiplication Spin-covariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.diracVector_smul_mul_diracWeyl) and [preserves the indefinite Hermitian pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.conjTranspose_diracWeyl_mul_mul) H₀ (TauCeti#29825). The same work pins the conventions the roadmap insists on: the time-first to time-last coordinate change, the dual-frame gamma matrices with [the anticommutation relations {Γ^μ, Γ^ν} = 2η^{μν}](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.diracGamma_mul_add_mul_swap), and a chirality matrix Γ⁵ that is diag(1, −1) and anticommutes with every Γ^μ. The Dirac Lagrangian, its currents and the gamma covariance law under Lorentz transformations are not yet here.

Layer 1 got its first two pieces. Real descent is now [an equivalence of categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/StarModuleCat/ComplexDescent.html#TauCeti.complexDescent) between real modules and complex modules with conjugation, built on a new category of star modules; the transport to polynomial field jets is still to come. Separately, [nonlinear first-jet coordinate changes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Jet/Nonlinear.html#TauCeti.firstJetCoordChange) with base-dependent fibre maps satisfy the chain rule, identity and [cocycle laws](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Jet/Nonlinear.html#TauCeti.firstJetCoordChange_comp), and act affinely on derivatives over a fixed field value. These are first-order chart computations only: the global jet bundle J^rE is not yet built.
