## LocalVariationalFieldTheory: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`2cf86b1` to `7ca783d`)

The finite Dirac model of Layer 3 now has its two acceptance theorems. The real Clifford representation [`diracClifford`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.diracClifford) of Cl(3,1) on ℂ⁴, sending a vector v to −iC(v), [restricts on Spin(3,1) to the Weyl action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.diracClifford_spinGroup) diag(A, (A†)⁻¹) through the native Spin–SL₂ equivalence, and that action [makes Clifford multiplication Spin-covariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.diracVector_smul_mul_diracWeyl) and [preserves the indefinite Hermitian pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.conjTranspose_diracWeyl_mul_mul) H₀ (TauCeti#29825). The same work pins the conventions the roadmap insists on: the time-first to time-last coordinate change, the dual-frame gamma matrices with [the anticommutation relations {Γ^μ, Γ^ν} = 2η^{μν}](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/ThreeOne.html#TauCeti.diracGamma_mul_add_mul_swap), and a chirality matrix Γ⁵ that is diag(1, −1) and anticommutes with every Γ^μ. The Dirac Lagrangian, its currents and the gamma covariance law under Lorentz transformations are not yet here.

Layer 1 got its first two pieces. Real descent is now [an equivalence of categories](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/StarModuleCat/ComplexDescent.html#TauCeti.complexDescent) between real modules and complex modules with conjugation, built on a new category of star modules; the transport to polynomial field jets is still to come. Separately, [nonlinear first-jet coordinate changes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Jet/Nonlinear.html#TauCeti.firstJetCoordChange) with base-dependent fibre maps satisfy the chain rule, identity and [cocycle laws](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Jet/Nonlinear.html#TauCeti.firstJetCoordChange_comp), and act affinely on derivatives over a fixed field value. These are first-order chart computations only: the global jet bundle J^rE is not yet built.

## LocalVariationalFieldTheory: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The finite Dirac model now has the [Lorentz covariance of the Dirac matrices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/Covariance.html#TauCeti.sum_smul_diracGamma_mul_diracWeyl), Σ_μ (Λ⁻¹)^ν_μ Γ^μ ρ_W(s) = ρ_W(s)Γ^ν, where Λ is the [Lorentz transformation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/Covariance.html#TauCeti.diracLorentz) that the Spin element s induces on time-first coordinates (TauCeti#36554). The same work builds the conjugate Weyl carrier diag(A, Ā): on SL₂(ℂ), [conjugating Ā by ε gives (A†)⁻¹](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/Covariance.html#TauCeti.diracEpsilon_mul_map_conj_mul_inv_eq_inv_conjTranspose), so the Weyl action is [the conjugate one seen in the frame T = diag(1, ε)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/Covariance.html#TauCeti.diracWeyl_eq_mul_diracConjWeyl_mul). After the Weyl swap U = H₀ and the spatial cycle, the gamma matrices [take the textbook block form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/Covariance.html#TauCeti.fromBlocks_mul_diracGamma_mul) and the chirality matrix [becomes diag(−1, 1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Dirac/Covariance.html#TauCeti.fromBlocks_mul_diracChirality_mul). That finishes the matrix side of §3.10. The Dirac density, its field equations and its currents wait on Layers 2 and 4.

Layer 1 now has jets of every order at a point. [`JetAt`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Jet/Higher.html#TauCeti.JetAt) identifies local sections that have the same derivatives through order r in a bundle chart. Over ℝ or ℂ it is [equivalent to tuples of symmetric multilinear maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Jet/Higher.html#TauCeti.JetAt.coordinatesEquiv). Nonlinear, base-dependent coordinate changes [compose](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Jet/Higher.html#TauCeti.JetAt.map_comp) and commute with truncation (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/279). Every finite symmetric jet is [realised by a smooth function with compact support](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Jet/Realization.html#TauCeti.exists_contDiff_hasCompactSupport_iteratedFDeriv_eq) inside any prescribed neighbourhood. All of this lives in a single chart: the glued bundle JʳE, its affine structure, prolongation and contact forms are not yet here.

Separately, the polynomial model of §1.3 began. [`PolynomialJet`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Variational/PolynomialJet/Basic.html#TauCeti.PolynomialJet) carries [commuting total derivatives](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Variational/PolynomialJet/Basic.html#TauCeti.PolynomialJet.totalDeriv_comm) that [raise jet order by at most one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Variational/PolynomialJet/Basic.html#TauCeti.PolynomialJet.totalDeriv_mem_stage). An [evaluation criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Variational/PolynomialJet/Basic.html#Derivation.aeval_polynomialJet_iff) gives the algebraic chain rule. The differential forms on this model are still missing, and so is the conjugation that §1.2 asks for.
