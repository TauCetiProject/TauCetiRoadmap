## SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups: 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-19 (`030f59c` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first target has landed: [van Dantzig's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/OpenSubgroup/Compact.html#TauCeti.IsTopologicalGroup.locallyCompact_totallyDisconnected_tfae), which says that for a Hausdorff topological group, being locally compact and totally disconnected, being locally compact and nonarchimedean, and having a neighbourhood basis of compact open subgroups at the identity are all equivalent (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/191). It is also available as an [instance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/OpenSubgroup/Compact.html#TauCeti.IsTopologicalGroup.nonarchimedeanGroup_of_weaklyLocallyCompactSpace): a zero-dimensional, weakly locally compact group is nonarchimedean. Both have additive versions.

The proof follows the roadmap's plan. The [left translation stabilizer of a compact open set is open](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/OpenSubgroup/Compact.html#IsCompact.isOpen_stabilizer), so a compact open set containing the identity [contains a compact open subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/OpenSubgroup/Compact.html#IsCompact.exists_openSubgroup_subset). The same work supplies the facts that smoothness will rely on. Compact open subgroups are [directed under intersection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/OpenSubgroup/Compact.html#OpenSubgroup.directedOn_isCompact), and a compact subgroup of a totally disconnected group can be [packaged as a profinite group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Category/ProfiniteGrp/Basic.html#ProfiniteGrp.ofCompactSubgroup). Smooth representations themselves have not appeared yet: neither the smoothness predicate nor the smooth category is in the library.

## SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`b235000` to `d25d526`)

Smooth representations now exist in the library. A representation is [smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/Basic.html#Representation.IsSmooth) when every vector has an open stabiliser, and for a locally profinite group this holds [exactly when each vector is fixed by a compact open subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/Basic.html#Representation.isSmooth_iff_exists_isCompact_openSubgroup) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/1359). On top of this sit the category [`SmoothRep`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/Category.html#TauCeti.SmoothRep), where [taking smooth vectors is right adjoint to the inclusion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/Category.html#TauCeti.SmoothRep.smoothPartAdjunction), so the category has all limits. There are also [admissibility](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/Admissible.html#Representation.IsAdmissible), smooth characters, and a [smooth dual](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/Dual.html#Representation.smoothDual) satisfying the [duality for intertwining maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/Dual.html#Representation.smoothDualHomEquiv) Hom_G(V, W̃) ≃ Hom_G(W, Ṽ). A worked example shows that [V → Ṽ̃ need not be surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/Dual.html#TauCeti.Examples.SmoothDual.not_surjective_smoothDualEval_padic) for ℚ_p acting on ℚ[ℚ_p/ℤ_p]. Finitely generated smooth representations are [exactly the quotients of finite sums of permutation modules A[G/U]](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Smooth/FinitelyGenerated.html#Representation.finite_asModule_iff_exists_surjective_isCompact). What is still missing: the abelian structure and colimits of `SmoothRep`, and the averaging projector that the exactness of invariants depends on.

The groundwork for Hecke algebras also landed. [Invertible pro-order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/UnitProOrder.html#TauCeti.HasUnitProOrder) is in place. The library now has the space of [locally constant compactly supported functions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/LocallyConstant/CompactSupport.html#LocallyConstant.compactlySupported), with its [open–closed exact sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/LocallyConstant/CompactSupport/OpenClosed.html#LocallyConstant.compactlySupported.exact_extendByZero_restrict), and [ring-valued Haar measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/HaarMeasureWithValues/Basic.html#TauCeti.HaarMeasureWithValues), which are [unique up to their volume on one compact open subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/HaarMeasureWithValues/Basic.html#TauCeti.HaarMeasureWithValues.apply_eq_normalized_mul) and have [products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Group/HaarMeasureWithValues/Prod.html#TauCeti.HaarMeasureWithValues.prod). The permutation Hecke algebra of a G-set with finitely many orbits is [identified with the endomorphisms of A[S]](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/OfMulAction/Hecke.html#TauCeti.PermutationHecke.toIntertwiningMapAlgEquiv). Integration and convolution are not there yet.

In the integral layer, [raw Weil–Deligne parameters](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/WeilDeligne/Basic.html#TauCeti.WeilDeligneParameter) arrived with a [Frobenius-semisimplicity predicate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/WeilDeligne/Basic.html#TauCeti.WeilDeligneParameter.IsFrobeniusSemisimple), together with direct sums, [tensor products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/WeilDeligne/Operations.html#TauCeti.WeilDeligneParameter.tensor), duals and twists. Separately, the identity [Symⁿ(exp N) = exp(d Symⁿ(N))](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/SymmetricPower/Derivation.html#SymmetricPower.map_exp) is groundwork for taking symmetric powers of parameters.
