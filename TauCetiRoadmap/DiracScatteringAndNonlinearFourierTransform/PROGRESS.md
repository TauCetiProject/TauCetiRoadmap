## DiracScatteringAndNonlinearFourierTransform: 2026-11-22 to 2026-11-24 (`eecad2d` to `d25d526`)

Layer 1's propagator theory for linear systems with integrable coefficients is close to complete. **Liouville's formula** landed: the determinant of the propagator `N_P(t, s)` is `exp ∫ₛᵗ tr P` ([`det_propagator`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/Propagator/Liouville.html#TauCeti.det_propagator)), proved by Jacobi's formula at an invertible endomorphism (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/3717). Alongside it came the Dyson expansion, a norm-convergent series of iterated integrals with terms bounded by `(∫‖P‖)ⁿ/n!` ([`hasSum_propagatorDysonTerm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/Propagator/Dyson.html#TauCeti.hasSum_propagatorDysonTerm)), and a worked example showing that the propagator is time-ordered and is not `exp ∫ P` ([`propagator_coeff_two_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/ODE/Propagator.html#TauCeti.Examples.Propagator.propagator_coeff_two_zero)). The propagator is holomorphic in a parameter when the coefficients are dominated on compact sets ([`differentiableOn_propagator`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/Propagator/Holomorphic.html#TauCeti.differentiableOn_propagator)), and the coefficient `1/(z − t)` shows that this hypothesis is needed. Variation of constants, `L¹`-continuous dependence on the coefficient ([`norm_propagator_sub_propagator_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/Propagator.html#TauCeti.norm_propagator_sub_propagator_le)) and the nonlinear Carathéodory theorem on an arbitrary interval complete the set.

On the upper half-plane, every nonnegative harmonic function is now known to be `c Im z + P[ν](z)` for some `c ≥ 0` and Poisson-finite `ν` ([`exists_eq_mul_im_add_upperHalfPlanePoissonIntegral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Poisson/PositiveHarmonic.html#TauCeti.exists_eq_mul_im_add_upperHalfPlanePoissonIntegral)), and this pair is unique. Both `c` and `ν(ℝ)` can be read off along the imaginary axis. The proof uses a new general fact: a harmonic function on a simply connected domain is the real part of an analytic function ([`exists_analyticOnNhd_re_eq_of_isSimplyConnected`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Harmonic/Conjugate.html#InnerProductSpace.HarmonicOnNhd.exists_analyticOnNhd_re_eq_of_isSimplyConnected)). The Poisson and Schwarz integrals were extended from measures to `L¹(Π)` densities, with the Cayley comparison to the disc kernel. Nontangential cones also arrived, with the maximal bound `M P[|g|] ≤ max(2, 1 + 2α²) · Mg` ([`nontangentialMaximalFunction_upperHalfPlanePoissonIntegral_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/Nontangential.html#TauCeti.nontangentialMaximalFunction_upperHalfPlanePoissonIntegral_le)).

Still missing are the vague-limit clause of the harmonic representation and the weak- and strong-type maximal bounds. Nothing yet touches Fatou's theorem, Hardy spaces or the Dirac system itself.
