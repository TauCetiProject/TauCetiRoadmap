## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-26 (`5380d8d` to `759eb3e`)

Relative singular homology on topological pairs now has excision and the Mayer–Vietoris sequence, with coefficients in any abelian category with coproducts. The proof follows Hatcher: barycentric subdivision is chain homotopic to the identity ([`TauCeti.singularSubdivisionHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Subdivision/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.singularSubdivisionHomotopy)), and the small-chain theorem ([`TauCeti.smallSingularChainHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Subdivision/Small/Equiv.html#TauCeti.smallSingularChainHomotopyEquiv)) makes the chains subordinate to an open cover a chain deformation retract of all chains. ## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

Two long-awaited results landed. The groupoid van Kampen theorem ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)) says that for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid of the space is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the nonempty finite intersections, with no connectedness assumption on those intersections. It rests on a gluing construction for functors that agree on overlaps ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.glue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Glue.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.glue)). The based and two-open-set corollaries, and the circle and wedge checks, are still to come. The homology of spheres is now computed: the reduced homology of the unit sphere of an `(n+1)`-dimensional real inner product space is one copy of the coefficients in degree `n` ([`TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sphere.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso)) and vanishes elsewhere. As a consequence `Hₙ(Dⁿ, Sⁿ⁻¹) ≅ R` ([`TauCeti.singularHomologyDiskBoundaryPairIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/DiskSphere.html#TauCeti.singularHomologyDiskBoundaryPairIso)). The induction starts from `[−p] − [p]` generating `H̃₀(S⁰)`, but the higher generators are transported from an arbitrarily chosen point, so they carry no orientation convention.

Products have begun. The Alexander–Whitney map on singular chains ([`TopCat.alexanderWhitney`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/AlexanderWhitney.html#TopCat.alexanderWhitney)) is natural in both spaces and both coefficients. It defines the cup product on singular cohomology ([`TopCat.singularCup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCup)), which comes with the Leibniz rule and naturality, and evaluates on a simplex as front face times back face. Associativity, graded commutativity and cap products are not yet there.

On the CW side, the mapping cylinder arrived with its universal property, deformation retraction onto the target, and cofibration of the source ([`TauCeti.MappingCylinder.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/MappingCylinder.html#TauCeti.MappingCylinder.homotopyEquiv)). Cellular maps now induce chain maps of cellular complexes ([`TauCeti.cellularChainComplexMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Map.html#TauCeti.cellularChainComplexMap)), and the degree-zero cellular group is identified with one copy of the coefficients per 0-cell. Smaller additions covered twisted cohomology under homotopic pullbacks and the homology of totally disconnected pairs.

Cellular homology now computes singular homology. Along the way the cellular group `Hₙ(Xⁿ, Xⁿ⁻¹)` is identified with one copy of the coefficients per `n`-cell in every degree ([`TauCeti.cellularChainGroupIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Coproduct.html#TauCeti.cellularChainGroupIso)), and the differential on a cell is described through its attaching map ([`TauCeti.ι_cellularChainGroupIso_inv_comp_cellularDifferential`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Boundary.html#TauCeti.ι_cellularChainGroupIso_inv_comp_cellularDifferential)), though not yet as an integer degree. The first payoff is Euler–Poincaré: for a finite CW complex, the alternating sum of the dimensions of singular homology over a division ring is the alternating cell count times the dimension of the coefficients ([`TauCeti.finsum_finrank_singularHomology_of_finite_cwComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/EulerCharacteristic/Singular.html#TauCeti.finsum_finrank_singularHomology_of_finite_cwComplex)). Van Kampen now has its classical corollaries. It also has a version for families sharing a common pairwise intersection. The fundamental group of a wedge of circles is free on the circles ([`TauCeti.WedgeSum.circleFundamentalGroupMulEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroup/WedgeSum.html#TauCeti.WedgeSum.circleFundamentalGroupMulEquiv)). On the product side, the cup product is now associative and unital ([`TopCat.singularCup_assoc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCup_assoc)). The Eilenberg–Mac Lane shuffle map on singular chains ([`TopCat.shuffle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Shuffle.html#TopCat.shuffle)) also landed, but the homotopies making it inverse to Alexander–Whitney did not. Smaller work covered additivity of cohomology, the two-arc Mayer–Vietoris check on the circle, and mapping tori as a first step toward the Wang sequence.

## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-06 (`41e5e49` to `65aa70a`)

The Eilenberg–Zilber theorem landed for both simplicial sets and singular chains. It says the Alexander–Whitney map `C(X × Y; R ⊗ S) → C(X; R) ⊗ C(Y; S)` is a chain homotopy equivalence, and the shuffle map is its homotopy inverse ([`TopCat.eilenbergZilberHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/EilenbergZilber.html#TopCat.eilenbergZilberHomotopyEquiv), with the simplicial version at [`SSet.eilenbergZilberHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/EilenbergZilber.html#SSet.eilenbergZilberHomotopyEquiv)). Coefficients are allowed in any preadditive monoidal category with coproducts that the tensor product preserves. The singular statement is the simplicial one transported along `Sing (X × Y) ≅ Sing X × Sing Y`. This completes the product maps of Stage 5's first item and gives the cross product what it needs.

The proof uses acyclic models, in two forms. Two natural families of chain maps on the chains of products that agree in degree zero are chain homotopic ([`SSet.prodChainComplexHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/EilenbergZilber.html#SSet.prodChainComplexHomotopy)), and so are two such families on tensor products of chains ([`SSet.tensorChainComplexHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/EilenbergZilber.html#SSet.tensorChainComplexHomotopy)). The second form needs the models `C(Δ[a]) ⊗ C(Δ[b])` to be acyclic. That comes from cone constructions on standard simplices that act as contracting homotopies in positive degrees. Künneth theorems in any coefficient setting, the Tor spectral sequence and graded commutativity of the cup product are still to come.

## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`a5cde7b` to `273ea28`)

The Hurewicz theorem now holds for the classical Hurewicz map `[p] ↦ p_*[Iⁿ]`, not only for its cubical stand-in: if `πₖ(X)` is trivial for every `k < n` and `n ≥ 2`, the map `πₙ(X) → Hₙ(X; ℤ)` is bijective ([`HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Absolute.html#HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz)), packaged as an isomorphism in [`HomotopyGroup.hurewiczAddEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Absolute.html#HomotopyGroup.hurewiczAddEquiv). The bridge is the tautological loop of the sphere, the cube with its boundary collapsed, whose Hurewicz class generates `Hₙ(Sⁿ; R)` ([`TauCeti.isIso_hurewicz_sphereGenLoop`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Sphere.html#TauCeti.isIso_hurewicz_sphereGenLoop)); every Hurewicz class is a push-forward of it. The two Hurewicz maps are shown to agree only up to a universal sign ([`HomotopyGroup.exists_hurewicz_eq_units_smul_cubicalHurewicz`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Absolute.html#HomotopyGroup.exists_hurewicz_eq_units_smul_cubicalHurewicz)), so the sign is not yet pinned (TauCeti#18830).

The vanishing half of relative Hurewicz also landed. If every point of `X` joins `A` by a path and the relative homotopy sets `π_{k+1}(X, A, a)` vanish for `k + 1 < n`, then `Hₘ(X, A; R) = 0` for `m < n` ([`TauCeti.isZero_singularHomology_ofSubset_of_relHomotopyGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Compression/Relative.html#TauCeti.isZero_singularHomology_ofSubset_of_relHomotopyGroup)), and the inclusion of `A` is an isomorphism on homology below `n − 1` and surjective in degree `n − 1` ([`TauCeti.epi_singularHomologyMap_subtypeVal_of_relHomotopyGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Cubical/Compression/Relative.html#TauCeti.epi_singularHomologyMap_subtypeVal_of_relHomotopyGroup)). The proof deforms singular cubes of `X` into `A` and works with relative cubical chains (TauCeti#18810). The degree-`n` isomorphism and homological Whitehead are still missing.

Smaller work split cycles and boundaries off complexes with projective homology, for the Künneth and universal coefficient theorems over arbitrary rings, and added a radial push on the unit ball for deforming neighbourhoods of CW skeleta.

## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

The relative Hurewicz theorem now has its degree-`n` half, though not yet in the generality the roadmap asks for. The relative Hurewicz map `πₙ(X, A) → Hₙ(X, A; ℤ)` is surjective whenever `A` is path connected, every point of `X` joins `A`, and the lower relative homotopy groups vanish ([`TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.surjective_hurewicz`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Relative.html#TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.surjective_hurewicz)). It is bijective when `A` and `X` are both `(n−1)`-connected ([`TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_of_subsingleton_homotopyGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Relative.html#TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_of_subsingleton_homotopyGroup)). The roadmap's version assumes only that `A` and `X` are simply connected, and that injectivity is still open, so homological Whitehead has not landed. The degree-two prerequisite did arrive: `π₂(X, A)` is commutative once `A` is simply connected ([`TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.mul_comm_of_subsingleton`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Relative/Group.html#TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.mul_comm_of_subsingleton)).

The Künneth and universal coefficient theorems were extended from PIDs to hereditary rings, meaning rings over which every module has projective dimension at most one. Over such a ring both sequences are exact and split ([`TopCat.singularHomologyKunnethUnitSplitting`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Kunneth/Sequence.html#TopCat.singularHomologyKunnethUnitSplitting), [`TopCat.singularUniversalCoefficientEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/UniversalCoefficient.html#TopCat.singularUniversalCoefficientEquiv)). The Milnor sequence `0 → lim¹ H → H(lim C) → lim H → 0` now holds for towers of complexes whose shift maps are surjective ([`TauCeti.ComplexTower.exact_limOneToHomology_homologyToLim`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/Milnor.html#TauCeti.ComplexTower.exact_limOneToHomology_homologyToLim)), and lim and lim¹ are unchanged when finitely many initial terms are dropped.

On the product side, the homology of a topological monoid has the Pontryagin product ([`TopCat.pontryaginProduct`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Pontryagin.html#TopCat.pontryaginProduct)). It is associative, natural, and graded commutative in the commutative case. Underneath, the Alexander–Whitney map is coassociative and the homology cross product is associative ([`TopCat.singularHomologyCross_assoc`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/CrossProduct.html#TopCat.singularHomologyCross_assoc)).
