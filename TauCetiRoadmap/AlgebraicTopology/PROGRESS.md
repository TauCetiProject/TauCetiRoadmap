## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-26 (`5380d8d` to `759eb3e`)

Relative singular homology on topological pairs now has excision and the Mayer–Vietoris sequence, with coefficients in any abelian category with coproducts. Excision ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)) removes a set whose closure lies in the interior of the subspace without changing relative homology. The proof follows Hatcher: barycentric subdivision is chain homotopic to the identity ([`TauCeti.singularSubdivisionHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Subdivision/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.singularSubdivisionHomotopy)), and the small-chain theorem ([`TauCeti.smallSingularChainHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Subdivision/Small/Equiv.html#TauCeti.smallSingularChainHomotopyEquiv)) makes the chains subordinate to an open cover a chain deformation retract of all chains. The Mayer–Vietoris sequence of an open cover ([`TopCat.mayerVietorisδ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/MayerVietoris/Basic.html#TopCat.mayerVietorisδ)) is exact, starts with the pinned map `(i_U, −i_V)`, and has a natural connecting morphism. Underneath, relative homology is a homology pretheory with the exactness, homotopy, dimension and (for exact coproducts) additivity axioms ([`TopPair.singularHomologyPretheory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/HomologyPretheory.html#TopPair.singularHomologyPretheory)), alongside the triple sequence, reduced homology and the empty-pair comparison.

The first calculation is the suspension isomorphism `H̃ₖ₊₁(S) ≅ H̃ₖ(equator)` for the unit sphere of a real inner product space ([`TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereSuccIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sphere.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereSuccIso)). The isomorphism `Hₖ₊₁(X, A) ≅ H̃ₖ(A)` for contractible `X` ([`TopPair.isIso_reducedSingularHomologyδ_of_contractibleSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Contractible.html#TopPair.isIso_reducedSingularHomologyδ_of_contractibleSpace)) reduces the disk to its boundary sphere, but the induction down to `S⁰` has not been run, so `Hₙ(Sⁿ) ≅ R` is not yet a theorem. On the CW side, relative CW inclusions are closed cofibrations ([`TauCeti.hasHomotopyExtensionProperty_base`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/Extension/CWComplex.html#TauCeti.hasHomotopyExtensionProperty_base)), a cofibration that is a homotopy equivalence is a strong deformation retract ([`TauCeti.HasHomotopyExtensionProperty.exists_strong_deformation_retraction`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/Extension/DeformationRetract.html#TauCeti.HasHomotopyExtensionProperty.exists_strong_deformation_retraction)), and the cellular chain complex has `d² = 0` ([`TauCeti.cellularChainComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Chains.html#TauCeti.cellularChainComplex)), though its groups are not yet identified with free modules on cells.

Local coefficient systems ([`TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/LocalCoefficient.html#TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem)) came with twisted singular chains and relative twisted homology with its long exact sequence. Singular cohomology, ordinary and twisted, has the long exact sequence of a pair and, untwisted, homotopy invariance ([`TopCat.Homotopy.congr_singularCohomologyMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/HomotopyInvariance.html#TopCat.Homotopy.congr_singularCohomologyMap)). For van Kampen, cover members generate the fundamental groupoid ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.iSup_im_map_subtypeVal_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/CoverGeneration.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.iSup_im_map_subtypeVal_eq_top)) and the Čech cocone exists ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.cechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Diagram.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.cechCocone)), but it is not yet proved to be a colimit.
