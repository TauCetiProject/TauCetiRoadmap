## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-26 (`5380d8d` to `759eb3e`)

Relative singular homology on topological pairs now has excision and the Mayer–Vietoris sequence, with coefficients in any abelian category with coproducts. Excision ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)) removes a set whose closure lies in the interior of the subspace without changing relative homology. The proof follows Hatcher: barycentric subdivision is chain homotopic to the identity ([`TauCeti.singularSubdivisionHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Subdivision/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.singularSubdivisionHomotopy)), and the small-chain theorem ([`TauCeti.smallSingularChainHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Subdivision/Small/Equiv.html#TauCeti.smallSingularChainHomotopyEquiv)) makes the chains subordinate to an open cover a chain deformation retract of all chains. The Mayer–Vietoris sequence of an open cover ([`TopCat.mayerVietorisδ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/MayerVietoris/Basic.html#TopCat.mayerVietorisδ)) is exact, starts with the pinned map `(i_U, −i_V)`, and has a natural connecting morphism. Underneath, relative homology is a homology pretheory with the exactness, homotopy, dimension and (for exact coproducts) additivity axioms ([`TopPair.singularHomologyPretheory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/HomologyPretheory.html#TopPair.singularHomologyPretheory)), alongside the triple sequence, reduced homology and the empty-pair comparison.

The first calculation is the suspension isomorphism `H̃ₖ₊₁(S) ≅ H̃ₖ(equator)` for the unit sphere of a real inner product space ([`TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereSuccIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sphere.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereSuccIso)). The isomorphism `Hₖ₊₁(X, A) ≅ H̃ₖ(A)` for contractible `X` ([`TopPair.isIso_reducedSingularHomologyδ_of_contractibleSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Contractible.html#TopPair.isIso_reducedSingularHomologyδ_of_contractibleSpace)) reduces the disk to its boundary sphere, but the induction down to `S⁰` has not been run, so `Hₙ(Sⁿ) ≅ R` is not yet a theorem. On the CW side, relative CW inclusions are closed cofibrations ([`TauCeti.hasHomotopyExtensionProperty_base`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/Extension/CWComplex.html#TauCeti.hasHomotopyExtensionProperty_base)), a cofibration that is a homotopy equivalence is a strong deformation retract ([`TauCeti.HasHomotopyExtensionProperty.exists_strong_deformation_retraction`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/Extension/DeformationRetract.html#TauCeti.HasHomotopyExtensionProperty.exists_strong_deformation_retraction)), and the cellular chain complex has `d² = 0` ([`TauCeti.cellularChainComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Chains.html#TauCeti.cellularChainComplex)), though its groups are not yet identified with free modules on cells.

Local coefficient systems ([`TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/LocalCoefficient.html#TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem)) came with twisted singular chains and relative twisted homology with its long exact sequence. Singular cohomology, ordinary and twisted, has the long exact sequence of a pair and, untwisted, homotopy invariance ([`TopCat.Homotopy.congr_singularCohomologyMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/HomotopyInvariance.html#TopCat.Homotopy.congr_singularCohomologyMap)). For van Kampen, cover members generate the fundamental groupoid ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.iSup_im_map_subtypeVal_eq_top`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/CoverGeneration.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.iSup_im_map_subtypeVal_eq_top)) and the Čech cocone exists ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.cechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Diagram.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.cechCocone)), but it is not yet proved to be a colimit.

## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

Two long-awaited results landed. The groupoid van Kampen theorem ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)) says that for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid of the space is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the nonempty finite intersections, with no connectedness assumption on those intersections. It rests on a gluing construction for functors that agree on overlaps ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.glue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Glue.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.glue)). The based and two-open-set corollaries, and the circle and wedge checks, are still to come. The homology of spheres is now computed: the reduced homology of the unit sphere of an `(n+1)`-dimensional real inner product space is one copy of the coefficients in degree `n` ([`TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sphere.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso)) and vanishes elsewhere. As a consequence `Hₙ(Dⁿ, Sⁿ⁻¹) ≅ R` ([`TauCeti.singularHomologyDiskBoundaryPairIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/DiskSphere.html#TauCeti.singularHomologyDiskBoundaryPairIso)). The induction starts from `[−p] − [p]` generating `H̃₀(S⁰)`, but the higher generators are transported from an arbitrarily chosen point, so they carry no orientation convention.

Products have begun. The Alexander–Whitney map on singular chains ([`TopCat.alexanderWhitney`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/AlexanderWhitney.html#TopCat.alexanderWhitney)) is natural in both spaces and both coefficients. It defines the cup product on singular cohomology ([`TopCat.singularCup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCup)), which comes with the Leibniz rule and naturality, and evaluates on a simplex as front face times back face. Associativity, graded commutativity and cap products are not yet there.

On the CW side, the mapping cylinder arrived with its universal property, deformation retraction onto the target, and cofibration of the source ([`TauCeti.MappingCylinder.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/MappingCylinder.html#TauCeti.MappingCylinder.homotopyEquiv)). Cellular maps now induce chain maps of cellular complexes ([`TauCeti.cellularChainComplexMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Map.html#TauCeti.cellularChainComplexMap)), and the degree-zero cellular group is identified with one copy of the coefficients per 0-cell. Smaller additions covered twisted cohomology under homotopic pullbacks and the homology of totally disconnected pairs.

## AlgebraicTopology: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-04 (`e440f4e` to `41e5e49`)

Cellular homology now computes singular homology. For a finite-dimensional relative CW complex, the homology of the cellular chain complex is its relative singular homology ([`TauCeti.cellularSingularHomologyIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Comparison.html#TauCeti.cellularSingularHomologyIso)), naturally in cellular maps ([`TauCeti.cellularSingularHomologyIso_hom_naturality`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Naturality.html#TauCeti.cellularSingularHomologyIso_hom_naturality)). Along the way the cellular group `Hₙ(Xⁿ, Xⁿ⁻¹)` is identified with one copy of the coefficients per `n`-cell in every degree ([`TauCeti.cellularChainGroupIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Coproduct.html#TauCeti.cellularChainGroupIso)), and the differential on a cell is described through its attaching map ([`TauCeti.ι_cellularChainGroupIso_inv_comp_cellularDifferential`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Boundary.html#TauCeti.ι_cellularChainGroupIso_inv_comp_cellularDifferential)), though not yet as an integer degree. The first payoff is Euler–Poincaré: for a finite CW complex, the alternating sum of the dimensions of singular homology over a division ring is the alternating cell count times the dimension of the coefficients ([`TauCeti.finsum_finrank_singularHomology_of_finite_cwComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/EulerCharacteristic/Singular.html#TauCeti.finsum_finrank_singularHomology_of_finite_cwComplex)). Infinite-dimensional complexes and cellular approximation are still missing.

Van Kampen now has its classical corollaries. The based Seifert–van Kampen theorem ([`TauCeti.isPushout_fundamentalGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroup/VanKampen.html#TauCeti.isPushout_fundamentalGroup)) makes `π₁(X)` a pushout when `A`, `B` and `A ∩ B` are path connected. It also has a version for families sharing a common pairwise intersection. The fundamental group of a wedge of circles is free on the circles ([`TauCeti.WedgeSum.circleFundamentalGroupMulEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroup/WedgeSum.html#TauCeti.WedgeSum.circleFundamentalGroupMulEquiv)). The circle covered by two arcs with disconnected overlap is handled by the groupoid theorem on two basepoints ([`TauCeti.CircleArcs.isPushout`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Circle.html#TauCeti.CircleArcs.isPushout)), and that recovers `π₁(S¹) ≅ ℤ`.

On the product side, the cup product is now associative and unital ([`TopCat.singularCup_assoc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCup_assoc)). The cap product arrived ([`TopCat.singularCap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cap/Basic.html#TopCat.singularCap)) with its boundary formula, the projection formula and cap–cup associativity. The Eilenberg–Mac Lane shuffle map on singular chains ([`TopCat.shuffle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Shuffle.html#TopCat.shuffle)) also landed, but the homotopies making it inverse to Alexander–Whitney did not. Smaller work covered additivity of cohomology, the two-arc Mayer–Vietoris check on the circle, and mapping tori as a first step toward the Wang sequence.
