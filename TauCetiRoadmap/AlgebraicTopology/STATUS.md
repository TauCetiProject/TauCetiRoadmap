## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Van Kampen (Stage 1) and excision with Mayer–Vietoris (Stage 3) are done. Cellular homology agrees with singular homology for finite-dimensional complexes, and Eilenberg–Zilber now completes the chain-level product maps. Relative homology, CW theory, products, cohomology and Euler characteristic are partial, with Künneth, universal coefficients and duality the main gaps. Hurewicz and Whitehead (Stage 8) have not begun.

### Named results

- **The van Kampen theorem** — for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the finite intersections ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)). The based theorem for path-connected `A`, `B` and `A ∩ B` follows from it ([`TauCeti.isPushout_fundamentalGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroup/VanKampen.html#TauCeti.isPushout_fundamentalGroup)).
- **Excision and Mayer–Vietoris** — relative singular homology does not change when a set whose closure lies in the interior of the subspace is removed ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)). The Mayer–Vietoris sequence follows from it and starts with the pinned map `(i_U, −i_V)` ([`TopCat.mayerVietorisδ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/MayerVietoris/Basic.html#TopCat.mayerVietorisδ)).
- **The Eilenberg–Zilber theorem** — the Alexander–Whitney map `C(X × Y; R ⊗ S) → C(X; R) ⊗ C(Y; S)` is a chain homotopy equivalence with the shuffle map as its inverse ([`TopCat.eilenbergZilberHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/EilenbergZilber.html#TopCat.eilenbergZilberHomotopyEquiv)). The proof is by acyclic models.
- **Cellular equals singular homology** — for a finite-dimensional relative CW complex, naturally in cellular maps ([`TauCeti.cellularSingularHomologyIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Comparison.html#TauCeti.cellularSingularHomologyIso)).
- **Euler–Poincaré** — for a finite CW complex, the alternating sum of the dimensions of singular homology over a division ring equals the alternating cell count times the dimension of the coefficients ([`TauCeti.finsum_finrank_singularHomology_of_finite_cwComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/EulerCharacteristic/Singular.html#TauCeti.finsum_finrank_singularHomology_of_finite_cwComplex)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The homology of spheres** — the unit sphere of an `(n+1)`-dimensional real inner product space has reduced homology one copy of the coefficients in degree `n` and none elsewhere ([`TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sphere.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso)). The generators carry no orientation convention yet.
- **Cup and cap products** — the cup product is associative and unital ([`TopCat.singularCup_assoc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCup_assoc)). The cap product has the boundary and projection formulas ([`TopCat.singularCap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cap/Basic.html#TopCat.singularCap)). Poincaré duality will be stated in terms of these operations.
- **Acyclic models for products** — any two natural families of chain maps on the chains of `K × L` that agree in degree zero are chain homotopic ([`SSet.prodChainComplexHomotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/EilenbergZilber.html#SSet.prodChainComplexHomotopy)). There is a matching statement for tensor products of chains, so later comparisons of product maps reduce to a degree-zero check.

### Roadmap coverage

Stages 1 and 3 are done. Stage 2 has every item but stays partial: its higher sphere generators are transported from an arbitrarily chosen point, so the disk connecting map has no pinned sign. Stage 4 has cellular chains on cells, the comparison and Euler–Poincaré for finite-dimensional complexes, CW cofibrations and mapping cylinders. It lacks integer attaching degrees, the infinite-dimensional and chain-level comparisons, cellular approximation, skeletal induction, and the degree-`m` and projective-space checks. Its other parts are absent: Künneth, Wang and transfer sequences, the Cartan–Leray and Serre spectral sequences, the torus calculation and the Čech double complex. The mapping torus and its monodromy homotopy are in place. Stage 6 has cochains, additivity, cup and cap products and associativity, but no universal coefficients, graded commutativity, fundamental classes or duality. Stage 7 has only the Euler–Poincaré equality. Stage 8 is untouched.

## The frontier

- **Cross product and Künneth** — with Eilenberg–Zilber in hand, the next steps are the cross product, the field Künneth isomorphism and the PID short exact sequence. The general-ring Tor spectral sequence comes after those. Graded commutativity of the cup product also follows from the cross-product comparison.
- **Attaching degrees** — read the cellular differential as an integer matrix of attaching-map degrees, then check that a two-cell complex of degree `m` has differential `m` and compute projective spaces.
- **Pinned sphere generators** — an orientation-normalized generator of `H̃ₙ(Sⁿ)`, which fixes the sign of the disk connecting map and feeds later degree and orientation work.
- **Wang sequence** — the mapping torus and its monodromy homotopy are done. What remains is the mapping-cone long exact sequence with endomorphism `id − f_*`.
- **Cellular approximation and skeletal induction** — CW cofibrations and mapping cylinders are done, so these are the remaining CW inputs to Whitehead's theorem in Stage 8.
