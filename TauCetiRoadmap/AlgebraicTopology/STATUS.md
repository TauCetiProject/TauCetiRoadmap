## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The classical cores of Layers 1–3 are proved: van Kampen, relative singular homology, Brouwer, excision and Mayer–Vietoris. So are the absolute Hurewicz theorem, Whitehead's theorem, Künneth and universal coefficients over hereditary rings, and the homology of tori. The rewritten roadmap asks more of every layer, so none is now complete. Relative Hurewicz holds only in a special case, cohomology has its products but no duality theorem, and every spectral sequence except the Čech one is missing.

### Named results

- **The van Kampen theorem** — for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the finite intersections ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)). The based, free-product and universal-property corollaries follow.
- **Excision** — relative singular homology is unchanged when a set whose closure lies in the interior of the subspace is removed ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)). Mayer–Vietoris follows, and cohomology has the matching excision.
- **The Hurewicz theorem** — for an `(n−1)`-connected space with `n ≥ 2`, the Hurewicz map `πₙ(X) → Hₙ(X; ℤ)` is bijective ([`HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Absolute.html#HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz)). The relative map is bijective only when both spaces of the pair are `(n−1)`-connected ([`TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_of_subsingleton_homotopyGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Relative.html#TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_of_subsingleton_homotopyGroup)).
- **Whitehead's theorem** — a weak homotopy equivalence between spaces of CW type is a homotopy equivalence ([`ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Whitehead.html#ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv)).
- **The homology of a torus is an exterior algebra** — over any commutative ring, the Pontryagin product gives `⋀ⁿ H₁(Tⁱ) ≅ Hₙ(Tⁱ)` ([`TauCeti.torusExteriorPowerEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/Exterior.html#TauCeti.torusExteriorPowerEquiv)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Relative cup products** — `Hᵖ(X, U) × H^q(X, V) → Hⁿ(X, U ∪ V)`, natural and compatible with the absolute product ([`TopCat.singularRelativeCup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup/Relative.html#TopCat.singularRelativeCup)). With the relative cap product and its Kronecker-pairing formula, these are the operations Poincaré–Lefschetz duality is stated with.
- **The integral fundamental class** — a compact oriented manifold has a unique class in `Hₙ(M; ℤ)` restricting to the local orientation at every point (`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.fundamentalClass`). This is the class Poincaré duality caps with.
- **Compactly supported cohomology of directed unions** — `Hⁿ_c(X)` is the colimit of `Hⁿ_c(U)` over a directed open cover ([`TopCat.isColimitCompactSupportCohomologyOpensCocone`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/CompactSupport/DirectedUnion.html#TopCat.isColimitCompactSupportCohomologyOpensCocone)). This lets a duality proof pass from compact pieces to the whole manifold.

## The frontier

- **Twisted excision and Mayer–Vietoris** — the twisted small-chain theorem and the short exact sequence of small twisted chains are proved. What remains is to derive local-coefficient excision and the long exact sequence with first map `(i_U, −i_V)`.
- **Relative Hurewicz and homological Whitehead** — injectivity in degree `n` is still needed when `A` and `X` are only simply connected. Homological Whitehead then follows through the mapping cylinder.
- **Poincaré and Poincaré–Lefschetz duality** — the fundamental class, cap products, compact supports, extension along open embeddings and the directed-union colimit are in place. The duality isomorphism itself remains, then its relative form and the linking pairing.
- **Serre spectral sequence** — a general filtered-space spectral sequence and a bicomplex row filtration exist. The skeletal filtration of the total space and `E² = H_p(B; H_q(F))` with monodromy remain. Euler multiplicativity for bundles waits on it.
- **Proper Whitehead** — this needs the stable fundamental group at an end, and proper cellular approximation and homotopy extension on locally finite CW pairs.
