### Named results

- **Excision** — if the closure of `Z` lies in the interior of `B`, then `(X ∖ Z, B ∖ Z) → (X, B)` induces isomorphisms on relative singular homology, with coefficients in any abelian category with coproducts ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)); it rests on the small-chain theorem, that chains subordinate to an open cover are a chain deformation retract of all chains ([`TauCeti.smallSingularChainHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Subdivision/Small/Equiv.html#TauCeti.smallSingularChainHomotopyEquiv)).
- **The Mayer–Vietoris sequence** — for an open cover `X = U ∪ V`, the sequence `Hₙ(U ∩ V) → Hₙ(U) ⊞ Hₙ(V) → Hₙ(X) → Hₙ₋₁(U ∩ V)` is exact, starts with the pinned `(i_U, −i_V)`, and has a natural connecting morphism ([`TopCat.mayerVietorisδ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/MayerVietoris/Basic.html#TopCat.mayerVietorisδ)).
- **The Eilenberg–Steenrod axioms** — relative singular homology is a homology pretheory satisfying the exactness, homotopy, dimension and, when coproducts are exact in the coefficients, additivity axioms ([`TopPair.singularHomologyPretheory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/HomologyPretheory.html#TopPair.singularHomologyPretheory)); its exactness, dimension and additivity classes are local copies from mathlib4#38369, awaiting a Mathlib pin.
- **The suspension isomorphism for spheres** — for the unit sphere `S` of a real inner product space and `p ∈ S`, `H̃ₖ₊₁(S) ≅ H̃ₖ(S ∩ p⊥)`, through the reduced Mayer–Vietoris map of the cover by the complements of `p` and `−p` ([`TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereSuccIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sphere.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereSuccIso)); without the induction down to `S⁰`, `Hₙ(Sⁿ) ≅ R` is not yet stated.
- **CW pairs are cofibrations** — the base and every skeleton of a relative CW complex have the homotopy extension property, so their inclusions are closed cofibrations ([`TauCeti.hasHomotopyExtensionProperty_base`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/Extension/CWComplex.html#TauCeti.hasHomotopyExtensionProperty_base)); and a cofibration that is a homotopy equivalence is a strong deformation retract ([`TauCeti.HasHomotopyExtensionProperty.exists_strong_deformation_retraction`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/Extension/DeformationRetract.html#TauCeti.HasHomotopyExtensionProperty.exists_strong_deformation_retraction)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Local coefficient systems** — functors from the fundamental groupoid to `R`-modules ([`TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/LocalCoefficient.html#TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem)), with twisted singular chains ([`TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem.twistedChainComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Twisted/Basic.html#TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem.twistedChainComplex)) and relative twisted homology that is functorial in pairs, has a natural long exact sequence, and reduces to ordinary homology for constant systems. Duality with the orientation system will build on it.
- **Singular cohomology** — `Hom(C_*(X, A; R), M)` for pairs, with its long exact sequence and homotopy invariance ([`TopPair.singularCohomology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Relative.html#TopPair.singularCohomology)), and a twisted version with the same long exact sequence ([`TopPair.twistedCohomology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Twisted/Relative.html#TopPair.twistedCohomology)).
- **The cellular chain complex** — `Hₙ(Xⁿ, Xⁿ⁻¹)` in degree `n`, with differential from the triple of consecutive skeleta and `d² = 0` ([`TauCeti.cellularChainComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Chains.html#TauCeti.cellularChainComplex)), not yet identified with free modules on cells.

## The frontier

- **Homology of spheres and disks** — compute `H̃₀(S⁰) ≅ R` and induct with the suspension isomorphism; the contractible-pair isomorphism then gives `Hₙ(Dⁿ, Sⁿ⁻¹) ≅ R`, and pinning its sign closes item 5 of Stages 2 and 3.
- **Cellular homology** — identify `Hₙ(Xⁿ, Xⁿ⁻¹)` with the free module on `n`-cells, which needs the disk calculation, then read the differential as attaching degrees and compare with singular homology.
- **Mapping cylinders, cellular approximation and skeletal induction** — the rest of Stage 4 item 5, built on the cofibration results; Stage 8's Whitehead theorems consume all three.
- **The van Kampen colimit** — derive the Čech relations from the grid subdivision and prove the Čech cocone is a colimit, then the based theorem and the circle and wedge checks. Meanwhile the covered-map naturality lemma assumes the colimit property.
- **Cohomology products and duality** — cup and cap products need the Alexander–Whitney and Eilenberg–Zilber maps of Stage 5, which has not started, and fundamental classes need the Heegaard Floer roadmap's orientation API.
