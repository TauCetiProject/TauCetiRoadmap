## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Van Kampen, relative singular homology, and excision with Mayer–Vietoris (Stages 1–3) are done. The absolute Hurewicz theorem, Whitehead's theorem, Künneth and universal coefficients over hereditary rings, and the homology of projective spaces are proved. Relative Hurewicz is proved only in a special case. Manifold duality, homological Whitehead, and every spectral sequence except the Čech one are still missing.

### Named results

- **The van Kampen theorem** — for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the finite intersections ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)). The based and free-product corollaries follow from it.
- **Excision** — relative singular homology does not change when a set whose closure lies in the interior of the subspace is removed ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)). The Mayer–Vietoris sequence is derived from it.
- **The Hurewicz theorem** — for an `(n−1)`-connected space and `n ≥ 2`, the Hurewicz map `[p] ↦ p_*[Iⁿ]` from `πₙ(X)` to `Hₙ(X; ℤ)` is bijective ([`HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Absolute.html#HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz)). The relative map is surjective in general and bijective when both spaces of the pair are `(n−1)`-connected ([`TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_of_subsingleton_homotopyGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Relative.html#TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_of_subsingleton_homotopyGroup)).
- **Whitehead's theorem** — a weak homotopy equivalence between spaces of CW type is a homotopy equivalence ([`ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Whitehead.html#ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv)).
- **The Künneth theorem over hereditary rings** — over a ring whose modules have projective dimension at most one, such as `ℤ`, the homology of `X × Y` is the tensor sum of the factors' homology plus a `Tor₁` term, via a split short exact sequence ([`TopCat.singularHomologyKunnethUnitSplitting`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Kunneth/Sequence.html#TopCat.singularHomologyKunnethUnitSplitting)). The universal coefficient sequence for cohomology splits under the same hypothesis ([`TopCat.singularUniversalCoefficientEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/UniversalCoefficient.html#TopCat.singularUniversalCoefficientEquiv)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Comparison of Mathlib's CW structures** — a classical relative CW complex in a Hausdorff space becomes a categorical one ([`TauCeti.toRelativeCWComplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CWComplex/Classical/RelativeCWComplex.html#TauCeti.toRelativeCWComplex)), and the converse also holds. This lets a complex built by attaching cells use the cellular theory.
- **Top homology of compact manifolds from local homology** — top homology is the module of parallel families of local classes ([`TauCeti.isLimitLocalHomologySystemCone`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/LocalHomology/TopHomology.html#TauCeti.isLimitLocalHomologySystemCone)). This is the groundwork for fundamental classes.
- **The Milnor sequence** — for a tower of complexes with surjective shift, `lim¹` of homology injects into the homology of the limit with cokernel the limit of homology ([`TauCeti.ComplexTower.exact_limOneToHomology_homologyToLim`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/Milnor.html#TauCeti.ComplexTower.exact_limOneToHomology_homologyToLim)). This is the shared tower tool that the infinite-dimensional skeletal passages need.

### Roadmap coverage

Stages 1–3 are done. Stage 4 has the cellular–singular comparison on homology, incidence numbers as local degrees, projective spaces, cofibrations, cellular approximation and both directions of the CW comparison. It lacks the chain-level comparison through the skeletal filtration. Stage 5 has Eilenberg–Zilber, Künneth over fields and hereditary rings, the Wang sequence, transfer, first homology of tori, the Čech items, and an associative cross product with a Pontryagin product. It lacks the Tor, Cartan–Leray and Serre spectral sequences and the exterior-power torus calculation. Stage 6 has the Milnor sequence, cochains, universal coefficients over hereditary rings, cup, cap and cross products, and local orientations. It lacks the Ext spectral sequence, integral fundamental classes, duality and linking. Stage 7 lacks only fibre-bundle multiplicativity. Stage 8 has relative homotopy groups, the Kan–cubical comparison, absolute Hurewicz, Whitehead, the vanishing half of relative Hurewicz, its surjectivity in degree `n`, and commutativity of `π₂(X, A)` for simply connected `A`. It lacks the degree-`n` relative isomorphism under the roadmap's hypotheses, homological Whitehead and the manifold corollaries.

## The frontier

- **Relative Hurewicz and homological Whitehead** — surjectivity of the relative Hurewicz map is proved ([`TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.surjective_hurewicz`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Relative.html#TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.surjective_hurewicz)). Injectivity is proved only when both spaces are `(n−1)`-connected, and is still needed when `A` and `X` are merely simply connected. Homological Whitehead then follows using the mapping cylinder.
- **Pinning the Hurewicz sign** — the classical and cubical Hurewicz maps are known to agree only up to a universal unit of `ℤ`. The roadmap's orientation conventions ask for that sign to be fixed.
- **Serre spectral sequence** — the spectral sequence of a filtered space exists, and fibre bundles give Serre fibrations. What remains is the skeletal filtration of the total space and `E² = H_p(B; H_q(F))` with monodromy. Euler multiplicativity for bundles waits on it.
- **Fundamental classes and Poincaré duality** — the compact simply connected case gives `Hₙ(M) ≅ R`. The integral fundamental class of an oriented compact manifold, both forms of duality, and the linking pairing remain.
- **Algebraic spectral sequences** — the hereditary-ring Künneth and universal coefficient theorems are done. The general-ring Künneth Tor, universal-coefficient Ext and Cartan–Leray spectral sequences are absent.
