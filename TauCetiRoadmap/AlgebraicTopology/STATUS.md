## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Van Kampen (Stage 1) and excision with Mayer–Vietoris (Stage 3) are done. Cellular homology now agrees with singular homology for finite-dimensional complexes. Relative homology, CW theory, products and cohomology are partial: the main gaps are the Künneth machinery and duality. Hurewicz and Whitehead (Stage 8) have not begun.

### Named results

- **The van Kampen theorem** — for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the finite intersections ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)). The based group theorem for path-connected `A`, `B` and `A ∩ B` follows from it ([`TauCeti.isPushout_fundamentalGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroup/VanKampen.html#TauCeti.isPushout_fundamentalGroup)), and so does freeness of `π₁` of a wedge of circles ([`TauCeti.WedgeSum.circleFundamentalGroupMulEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroup/WedgeSum.html#TauCeti.WedgeSum.circleFundamentalGroupMulEquiv)).
- **Excision and Mayer–Vietoris** — relative singular homology is unchanged when a set with closure inside the interior of the subspace is removed ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)). The Mayer–Vietoris sequence follows, starting with the pinned `(i_U, −i_V)` ([`TopCat.mayerVietorisδ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/MayerVietoris/Basic.html#TopCat.mayerVietorisδ)).
- **The homology of spheres** — the unit sphere of an `(n+1)`-dimensional real inner product space has reduced homology one copy of the coefficients in degree `n` and zero elsewhere ([`TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sphere.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso)).
- **Cellular equals singular homology** — for a finite-dimensional relative CW complex, the comparison is natural in cellular maps ([`TauCeti.cellularSingularHomologyIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Comparison.html#TauCeti.cellularSingularHomologyIso)).
- **Euler–Poincaré** — for a finite CW complex, the alternating sum of the dimensions of singular homology over a division ring is the alternating cell count times the dimension of the coefficients ([`TauCeti.finsum_finrank_singularHomology_of_finite_cwComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/EulerCharacteristic/Singular.html#TauCeti.finsum_finrank_singularHomology_of_finite_cwComplex)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Cup and cap products** — the cup product is associative and unital ([`TopCat.singularCup_assoc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCup_assoc)). The cap product has the boundary formula, the projection formula and cap–cup associativity ([`TopCat.singularCap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cap/Basic.html#TopCat.singularCap)). These are the operations Poincaré duality is stated in.
- **Cellular chains on cells** — the cellular group `Hₙ(Xⁿ, Xⁿ⁻¹)` is one copy of the coefficients per `n`-cell ([`TauCeti.cellularChainGroupIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Coproduct.html#TauCeti.cellularChainGroupIso)). The differential on a cell is described through its attaching map. Together these make cellular calculations possible.
- **The shuffle map** — the Eilenberg–Mac Lane map `C(X) ⊗ C(Y) → C(X × Y)`, natural in spaces and coefficients ([`TopCat.shuffle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Shuffle.html#TopCat.shuffle)). It is the other half of Eilenberg–Zilber.

### Roadmap coverage

Stages 1 and 3 are done. Stage 1 includes the based, two-set and family corollaries, the free product for wedges, and the two-basepoint circle check. Stage 3 now has the two-arc Mayer–Vietoris check on `S¹`. Stage 2 has every item but remains partial: its higher sphere generators are transported from an arbitrarily chosen point, so the disk connecting map has no orientation-pinned sign. Stage 4 has cellular chains on cells, the comparison and Euler–Poincaré for finite-dimensional complexes, CW cofibrations and mapping cylinders. It lacks integer attaching degrees, the infinite-dimensional and chain-level comparisons, cellular approximation, skeletal induction, and the degree-`m` and projective-space checks. Stage 5 has the Alexander–Whitney and shuffle maps and the mapping-torus monodromy homotopy, but no Eilenberg–Zilber homotopies, Künneth, Wang, transfer, spectral sequences or Čech complex. Stage 6 has cochains, additivity, cup and cap products and associativity, but no universal coefficients, fundamental classes or duality. Stage 7 has only the Euler–Poincaré equality, with no Euler-characteristic invariant or its multiplicativity. Stage 8 is untouched.

## The frontier

- **Eilenberg–Zilber** — the chain homotopies making the shuffle and Alexander–Whitney maps mutually inverse. Künneth, cross products and graded commutativity of the cup product all wait on these.
- **Attaching degrees** — read the cellular differential as an integer matrix of attaching-map degrees, then check that a two-cell complex of degree `m` has differential `m` and compute projective spaces.
- **Pinned sphere generators** — an orientation-normalized generator of `H̃ₙ(Sⁿ)`, needed for the disk connecting map's sign and for later degree and orientation work.
- **Wang sequence** — the mapping torus and its monodromy homotopy are in place. What remains is the mapping-cone long exact sequence with endomorphism `id − f_*`.
- **Cellular approximation and skeletal induction** — with CW cofibrations and mapping cylinders done, these are the remaining inputs to Whitehead's theorem in Stage 8.
