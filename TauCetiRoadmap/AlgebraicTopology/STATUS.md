## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Van Kampen, relative singular homology, and excision with Mayer–Vietoris (Stages 1–3) are done, and so are the absolute Hurewicz theorem, Whitehead's theorem, Künneth and universal coefficients over hereditary rings, and the homology of tori. Relative Hurewicz holds only in a special case, cohomology has its products but no duality theorem, and every spectral sequence except the Čech one is missing.

### Named results

- **The van Kampen theorem** — for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the finite intersections ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)). The based and free-product corollaries follow from it.
- **Excision** — relative singular homology does not change when a set whose closure lies in the interior of the subspace is removed ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)). Mayer–Vietoris follows from it, and cohomology now has the matching excision.
- **The Hurewicz theorem** — for an `(n−1)`-connected space and `n ≥ 2`, the Hurewicz map `πₙ(X) → Hₙ(X; ℤ)` is bijective ([`HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Absolute.html#HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz)). The relative map is surjective in general and bijective only when both spaces of the pair are `(n−1)`-connected ([`TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_of_subsingleton_homotopyGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Relative.html#TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_of_subsingleton_homotopyGroup)).
- **Whitehead's theorem** — a weak homotopy equivalence between spaces of CW type is a homotopy equivalence ([`ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Whitehead.html#ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv)).
- **The homology of a torus is an exterior algebra** — over any commutative ring, the Pontryagin product gives `⋀ⁿ H₁(Tⁱ) ≅ Hₙ(Tⁱ)`, naturally in maps of commutative topological monoids ([`TauCeti.torusExteriorPowerEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/Exterior.html#TauCeti.torusExteriorPowerEquiv)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Relative cup products** — `Hᵖ(X, U) × H^q(X, V) → Hⁿ(X, U ∪ V)` for sets open in their union, natural and compatible with the absolute product ([`TopCat.singularRelativeCup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup/Relative.html#TopCat.singularRelativeCup)). Together with the relative cap product and extension of compactly supported classes along open embeddings, these are the operations that Poincaré–Lefschetz duality is stated with.
- **The integral fundamental class** — a compact manifold with an orientation has a unique class in `Hₙ(M; ℤ)` restricting to the local orientation at every point, and it generates when `M` is connected (`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.fundamentalClass`). This is the class that Poincaré duality caps with.
- **Ends and simple connectivity at infinity** — ends are computed as an inverse limit of complementary components ([`TauCeti.EndComponents`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Connected/Ends.html#TauCeti.EndComponents)). Simple connectivity at infinity ([`TauCeti.SimplyConnectedAtInfinity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/SimplyConnectedAtInfinity.html#TauCeti.SimplyConnectedAtInfinity)) is invariant under proper homotopy equivalence and holds for `ℝⁿ`, `n ≥ 3`. These are the inputs to the proper Whitehead theorem.

### Roadmap coverage

Stages 1–3 are done, with Brouwer's fixed-point theorem for compact convex sets now included in Stage 2. Stages 4–8 are all partial. Stage 4 has the cellular–singular comparison on homology, projective spaces, cofibrations, Strøm's NDR characterisation and both directions of the CW comparison, but not the chain-level comparison through the skeletal filtration. Stage 5 has Eilenberg–Zilber, Künneth over hereditary rings, the Wang sequence, transfer, the torus calculation and the Čech items, but lacks the Tor, Cartan–Leray and Serre spectral sequences. Stage 6 has cochains, universal coefficients, Bockstein sequences, cohomological excision, absolute and relative cup, cap and cross products, and the integral fundamental class. It lacks the Ext spectral sequence, duality and linking. Stage 7 lacks only fibre-bundle multiplicativity. Stage 8 has absolute Hurewicz, Whitehead, the vanishing and surjective halves of relative Hurewicz, connectivity of CW pairs from cell dimensions, and the end and simple-connectivity-at-infinity basics. It lacks the relative isomorphism under the roadmap's hypotheses, homological Whitehead, the stable fundamental group at an end, proper Whitehead and the manifold corollaries.

## The frontier

- **Relative Hurewicz and homological Whitehead** — injectivity in degree `n` is needed when `A` and `X` are merely simply connected. When `A` is simply connected, two relative cubes now have the same class exactly when they are homotopic as maps of pairs ([`TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.mk_eq_mk_iff_homotopicWith`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Relative/PairHomotopy.html#TauCeti.RelHomotopyGroup.mk_eq_mk_iff_homotopicWith)). Homological Whitehead would then follow through the mapping cylinder.
- **Poincaré and Poincaré–Lefschetz duality** — the fundamental class, cap products and compactly supported cohomology are in place. What remains is the duality isomorphism itself, then its relative form and the linking pairing.
- **Serre spectral sequence** — the remaining steps are the skeletal filtration of the total space and `E² = H_p(B; H_q(F))` with monodromy. Euler multiplicativity for bundles waits on it.
- **Proper Whitehead** — this needs the stable fundamental group at an end, and proper cellular approximation and homotopy extension on locally finite CW pairs.
- **Algebraic spectral sequences** — the general-ring Künneth Tor, universal-coefficient Ext and Cartan–Leray spectral sequences are absent.
