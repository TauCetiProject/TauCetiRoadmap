### Named results

- **The groupoid van Kampen theorem** — for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid of the space is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the nonempty finite intersections, with no connectedness assumed on intersections ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)).
- **Excision** — if the closure of `Z` lies in the interior of `B`, then `(X ∖ Z, B ∖ Z) → (X, B)` induces isomorphisms on relative singular homology, with coefficients in any abelian category with coproducts ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)). It rests on the small-chain theorem.
- **The Mayer–Vietoris sequence** — for an open cover `X = U ∪ V`, the sequence is exact, starts with the pinned `(i_U, −i_V)`, and has a natural connecting morphism ([`TopCat.mayerVietorisδ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/MayerVietoris/Basic.html#TopCat.mayerVietorisδ)).
- **The homology of spheres** — the unit sphere of an `(n+1)`-dimensional real inner product space has reduced homology one copy of the coefficients in degree `n` and zero elsewhere ([`TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Sphere.html#TauCeti.reducedSingularHomologySphereIso)). As a consequence, `Hₙ(Dⁿ, Sⁿ⁻¹) ≅ R` through the connecting map ([`TauCeti.singularHomologyDiskBoundaryPairIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/DiskSphere.html#TauCeti.singularHomologyDiskBoundaryPairIso)).
- **The Eilenberg–Steenrod axioms** — relative singular homology is a homology pretheory with the exactness, homotopy, dimension and, for exact coproducts, additivity axioms ([`TopPair.singularHomologyPretheory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/HomologyPretheory.html#TopPair.singularHomologyPretheory)). Its classes are local copies from mathlib4#38369.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The cup product** — the Alexander–Whitney diagonal on singular chains defines a cup product on singular cohomology, with the Leibniz rule and naturality in the space ([`TopCat.singularCup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCup)). This opens the product side of Stages 5 and 6.
- **Cofibrations and mapping cylinders** — relative CW inclusions are closed cofibrations ([`TauCeti.hasHomotopyExtensionProperty_base`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/Extension/CWComplex.html#TauCeti.hasHomotopyExtensionProperty_base)). A cofibration that is a homotopy equivalence is a strong deformation retract. The mapping cylinder is homotopy equivalent to its target ([`TauCeti.MappingCylinder.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/MappingCylinder.html#TauCeti.MappingCylinder.homotopyEquiv)). Together these give the replacement of a map by a cofibration that Whitehead's theorems need.
- **Cellular chains and local coefficients** — the cellular chain complex `Hₙ(Xⁿ, Xⁿ⁻¹)` has `d² = 0` and is functorial in cellular maps ([`TauCeti.cellularChainComplexMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/Map.html#TauCeti.cellularChainComplexMap)). Local coefficient systems ([`TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/LocalCoefficient.html#TauCeti.LocalCoefficientSystem)) carry twisted homology and cohomology with long exact sequences.

## The frontier

- **Based van Kampen** — derive the two-open-set pushout and the based theorem for path-connected opens and intersection, then the free-group presentation of a wedge of circles and the multi-basepoint circle check. The colimit theorem is already in place.
- **Cellular homology** — identify `Hₙ(Xⁿ, Xⁿ⁻¹)` with the free module on `n`-cells in positive degree, now that `Hₙ(Dⁿ, Sⁿ⁻¹) ≅ R` is known, then read the differential as attaching degrees and compare with singular homology.
- **Eilenberg–Zilber and the shuffle map** — the inverse of Alexander–Whitney up to homotopy, which Künneth, cross products and cup associativity all need.
- **Cellular approximation and skeletal induction** — the rest of Stage 4 item 5. With mapping cylinders and CW cofibrations in hand, these are the remaining inputs to Stage 8's Whitehead theorems.
- **Pinned sphere generators** — fix an orientation-normalized generator of `H̃ₙ(Sⁿ)` so that the disk connecting map has the sign the acceptance checks require.
