## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Van Kampen, relative singular homology, and excision with Mayer–Vietoris (Stages 1–3) are done. The CW, product, cohomology, Euler-characteristic and homotopy stages are all well under way: Künneth, the universal coefficient theorem, graded commutativity and Whitehead's theorem are proved. Every spectral sequence on the roadmap is still missing, and so are manifold duality and the Hurewicz theorem above degree one.

### Named results

- **The van Kampen theorem** — for any open cover, the fundamental groupoid is the colimit of the fundamental groupoids of the finite intersections ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)). The based pushout theorem follows, and the free product of the fundamental groups of two cover members maps onto `π₁(X)` ([`TauCeti.vanKampenLift_surjective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroup/VanKampen/Basic.html#TauCeti.vanKampenLift_surjective)).
- **Excision and Mayer–Vietoris** — relative singular homology is unchanged by removing a set whose closure lies in the interior of the subspace ([`TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Excision.html#TopPair.isIso_singularHomologyMap_excisionMap_compl)). The Mayer–Vietoris sequence starts with the pinned map `(i_U, −i_V)` ([`TopCat.mayerVietorisδ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/MayerVietoris/Basic.html#TopCat.mayerVietorisδ)).
- **Eilenberg–Zilber and Künneth** — Alexander–Whitney and shuffle maps are inverse chain homotopy equivalences ([`TopCat.eilenbergZilberHomotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/EilenbergZilber.html#TopCat.eilenbergZilberHomotopyEquiv)). As a result the cross product is an isomorphism over a field, and over a principal ideal domain it fits into the short exact sequence with a `Tor₁` term.
- **Euler–Poincaré for spaces of finite CW type** — the Euler characteristic of such a space is the alternating sum of the dimensions of its homology over any division ring ([`TauCeti.eulerChar_eq_finsum_finrank_singularHomology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/EulerCharacteristic/FiniteCWType.html#TauCeti.eulerChar_eq_finsum_finrank_singularHomology)). Hence it is a homotopy invariant, independent of the chosen CW model.
- **Whitehead's theorem** — a weak homotopy equivalence between CW complexes is a homotopy equivalence. The proof goes through compression in the mapping cylinder.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Spaces of finite CW type** ([`TauCeti.FiniteCWType`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/CWComplex/Classical/FiniteCWType.html#TauCeti.FiniteCWType)) — spaces homotopy equivalent to a finite CW complex. Stage 7's Euler-characteristic theorems are stated in this class.
- **Cup and cap products** — the cup product is associative and unital ([`TopCat.singularCup_assoc`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cup.html#TopCat.singularCup_assoc)) and graded commutative. The cap product has the boundary and projection formulas ([`TopCat.singularCap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/Cap/Basic.html#TopCat.singularCap)). Poincaré duality will be stated in these terms.
- **Cellular approximation and skeletal induction** — every map of relative CW complexes is homotopic, relative to the base, to a cellular map. Maps out of a complex can be built one skeleton at a time.

## The frontier

- **Hurewicz in higher degrees** — the Hurewicz maps exist. What remains is the connectivity theorem `πₙ(X) ≅ Hₙ(X; ℤ)` for `n ≥ 2`, then its relative form, and then homological Whitehead for simply connected complexes.
- **Čech–singular comparison** — the double complex exists, and its rows are known to resolve small chains. What remains is the quasi-isomorphism of the total complex with singular chains, followed by the bounded spectral sequence.
- **Attaching degrees** — identify the homological incidence coefficients with geometric degrees of sphere maps. Then check that a degree-`m` two-cell complex has differential `m`, and compute projective spaces.
- **Fundamental classes and Poincaré duality** — local orientations exist, but the orientation local system, fundamental classes and both forms of duality remain. These consume the orientation API owned by differential geometry.
- **Spectral sequences** — the Tor, Ext, Cartan–Leray and Serre spectral sequences are all absent. Multiplicativity of `χ` for fibre bundles waits on the Serre spectral sequence.
