## The frontier

- **Manifold duality** — extend Euclidean bijectivity to arbitrary oriented manifolds using compact-support gluing and cap compatibility, then prove the boundary and twisted Poincaré–Lefschetz forms.
- **Twisted excision and Mayer–Vietoris** — prove general closure-in-interior excision, compatibility with pair connecting maps, and the long exact sequence with first map (i_U, −i_V).
- **Cellular comparison** — construct the skeletal exact couple and chain representatives natural up to specified homotopies, including local coefficients.
- **Relative Hurewicz and homological Whitehead** — prove injectivity for a simply connected subspace and an (n−1)-connected pair, extend the Hurewicz statements to every universe, then apply the mapping cylinder.
- **Serre applications and bundle Euler characteristic** — complete the skeletal-filtration input and obtain DGFloer’s Serre sequence with monodromy and convergence data; bundle multiplicativity still requires this input.
