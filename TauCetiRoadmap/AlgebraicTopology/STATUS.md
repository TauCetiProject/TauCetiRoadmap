## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 1, van Kampen through the fundamental groupoid, is done. The classical homology theorems, absolute Hurewicz for spaces in `Type` and ordinary Whitehead are proved, and Poincaré duality holds on Euclidean space and its convex open subsets. Layers 2–8 remain partial: closed-manifold duality, the general relative Hurewicz theorem and the spectral-sequence applications are still open, and the later representability and obstruction targets have not been assessed.

### Named results

- **The van Kampen theorem**: the fundamental groupoid of an open cover is the colimit of the groupoids of its finite intersections, with no connectedness assumption on the intersections ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)).
- **The Hurewicz theorem**: for an (n−1)-connected space in `Type` with n ≥ 2, the map πₙ(X) → Hₙ(X; ℤ) is bijective ([`HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Absolute.html#HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz)).
- **Whitehead's theorem**: a weak homotopy equivalence between spaces of CW type is a homotopy equivalence ([`ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Whitehead.html#ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv)).
- **Poincaré duality for Euclidean space**: for a homologically oriented finite-dimensional real normed space F, the duality map Hᵖ_c(F; L) → H_q(F; L), p + q = dim F, is bijective ([`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.bijective_dualityMap_self`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/CompactSupport/Euclidean/Duality.html#TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.bijective_dualityMap_self)). The same holds for open subsets with convex image ([`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.bijective_dualityMap_of_convex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/CompactSupport/Euclidean/Duality.html#TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.bijective_dualityMap_of_convex)).
- **The strong Morse inequalities for CW pairs**: over a field, partial alternating sums of relative Betti numbers are bounded by the relative cell counts ([`TauCeti.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_complexBasePair_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cellular/EulerCharacteristic/MorseInequalities.html#TauCeti.sum_range_neg_one_pow_mul_finrank_singularHomology_complexBasePair_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The manifold duality map and its criterion**: D_M: Hᵖ_c(M; L) → H_q(M; L) caps with the fundamental class. It is bijective as soon as capping is bijective along a cofinal family of compact sets, which reduces global duality to compact pieces ([`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.dualityMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/CompactSupport/Duality.html#TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.dualityMap), [`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.bijective_dualityMap_of_cofinal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/CompactSupport/Duality.html#TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.bijective_dualityMap_of_cofinal)).
- **Compact-support Mayer–Vietoris**: for an open cover X = U ∪ V of a Hausdorff space, the sequence through Hⁿ_c(U ∩ V), Hⁿ_c(U) ⊕ Hⁿ_c(V) and Hⁿ_c(X) now has its connecting map and is exact at every term. This is the gluing input for duality ([`TopCat.compactSupportCohomologyMayerVietorisδ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/CompactSupport/MayerVietoris.html#TopCat.compactSupportCohomologyMayerVietorisδ)).
- **Fundamental classes with coefficients**: the local-generator condition that states closed-manifold duality over any ring ([`TauCeti.IsFundamentalClass`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/LocalHomology/IsFundamentalClass.html#TauCeti.IsFundamentalClass)).

Layers 2–8 are partial. Layer 2 lacks §2.5's coordinate reference classes. Layer 3 lacks general closure-in-interior twisted excision and the twisted Mayer–Vietoris long exact sequence. Layer 4 lacks the chain-level and local-coefficient cellular–singular comparison. ## The frontier

- **Closed-manifold Poincaré duality**: run the induction over finite unions of convex charts, using the compact-support Mayer–Vietoris sequence and the cofinal criterion, to reach `poincareDuality_of_isFundamentalClass`. The boundary and twisted Poincaré–Lefschetz forms come after it.
- **Twisted excision and Mayer–Vietoris**: prove general closure-in-interior excision with local coefficients, compatibility with the pair connecting maps, and the long exact sequence whose first map is (i_U, −i_V).
- **Cellular comparison**: build the skeletal exact couple and chain-level representatives that are natural up to specified homotopies, including local coefficients.
- **Relative Hurewicz and homological Whitehead**: prove injectivity for an (n−1)-connected pair with simply connected subspace, extend Hurewicz beyond `Type`, then apply the mapping cylinder.
- **Serre applications and bundle Euler characteristic**: finish the skeletal-filtration input for the Serre spectral sequence with monodromy and convergence. Bundle multiplicativity in Layer 7 waits on it.
