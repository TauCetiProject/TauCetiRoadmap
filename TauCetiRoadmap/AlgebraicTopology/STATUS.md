## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 1, van Kampen through the fundamental groupoid, is done. Absolute Hurewicz in every universe, Whitehead, the Künneth spectral sequence and Poincaré duality on convex Euclidean opens are proved. Layers 2–8 remain partial: closed-manifold duality, relative Hurewicz, the Serre and Cartan–Leray packages and most of §§8.8–8.16 are open.

### Named results

- **The van Kampen theorem**: the fundamental groupoid of an open cover is the colimit of the groupoids of its finite intersections, with no connectedness assumption ([`TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/FundamentalGroupoid/Cech/Colimit.html#TauCeti.FundamentalGroupoid.isColimitCechCocone)).
- **The Hurewicz theorem**: for an (n+1)-connected space in any universe, π_{n+2}(X) → H_{n+2}(X; ℤ) is bijective ([`HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_ulift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Hurewicz/Absolute.html#HomotopyGroup.bijective_hurewicz_ulift)).
- **Whitehead's theorem**: a weak homotopy equivalence between spaces of CW type is a homotopy equivalence ([`ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Homotopy/Whitehead.html#ContinuousMap.IsWeakHomotopyEquivalence.homotopyEquiv)).
- **The Künneth spectral sequence**: over a commutative ring, E²_{p,q} = ⨁_{i+j=q} Tor_p(Hᵢ(X), Hⱼ(Y)) converges to H_{p+q}(X × Y), naturally in both spaces ([`TopCat.singularKunnethSpectralSequence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Kunneth/SpectralSequence.html#TopCat.singularKunnethSpectralSequence)).
- **Poincaré duality for Euclidean space**: for a homologically oriented finite-dimensional real normed space, and for open subsets with convex image, the duality map Hᵖ_c → H_q, p + q = dim, is bijective ([`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.bijective_dualityMap_of_convex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/CompactSupport/Euclidean/Duality.html#TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.bijective_dualityMap_of_convex)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The manifold duality map**: D_M: Hᵖ_c(M; L) → H_q(M; L), capping with the fundamental class. It is bijective once capping is bijective along a cofinal family of compact sets. It is natural for open inclusions, compatible with both Mayer–Vietoris sequences, and passes to directed unions ([`TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.dualityMap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/Cohomology/CompactSupport/Duality.html#TauCeti.HomologicalOrientation.dualityMap)).
- **The Künneth bicomplex**: the tensor product of one complex with a Cartan–Eilenberg resolution of the other. It computes K ⊗ L when K is flat and makes the Künneth spectral sequence natural ([`HomologicalComplex.kunnethBicomplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Monoidal/Homology/Kunneth/CartanEilenberg.html#HomologicalComplex.kunnethBicomplex)).
- **The proper homotopy extension property**: the proper analogue of a cofibration, with Strøm's characterisation and product theorem. It is the input for proper deformation retracts and the proper Whitehead theorem ([`TauCeti.HasProperHomotopyExtensionProperty`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/Extension/Proper/Basic.html#TauCeti.HasProperHomotopyExtensionProperty)).

### Roadmap coverage

Layer 1 is done. Layer 2 has the shuffle comparison of §2.5 but not its coordinate cube, disk and sphere reference classes. Layer 3 has twisted subdivision, small chains, closure-in-interior excision and the twisted Mayer–Vietoris long exact sequence, absolute and for pairs; the earlier report listed these as missing. Not established here: compatibility of twisted excision with connecting maps, and the §3.5 sphere checks. Layer 4 lacks the chain-level and local-coefficient cellular–singular comparison. Layer 5 has Künneth over fields and hereditary rings, the Künneth spectral sequence, transfer, torus homology and Čech descent. It lacks the Künneth edge maps and collapse corollaries, and the Tor-Künneth, Cartan–Leray and Serre packages. Layer 6 has the universal coefficient theorem over a field, cup and cap products, and duality on convex Euclidean opens. It lacks closed-manifold and Poincaré–Lefschetz duality, linking forms and product-ball intersection. Layer 7 has Euler–Poincaré and the strong Morse inequalities and lacks only bundle multiplicativity. Layer 8 has absolute Hurewicz, Whitehead, a highly connected relative Hurewicz, proper cofibrations and the mod p cohomology of BC_p and B(C_p × C_p). The general relative theorem, homological and proper Whitehead remain; §§8.8–8.16 are otherwise unassessed.

## The frontier

- **Closed-manifold Poincaré duality**: prove that duality on U, V and U ∩ V gives duality on U ∪ V by the five lemma; every square now commutes. Then induct over finite unions of convex charts and pass to directed unions to reach `poincareDuality_of_isFundamentalClass`. Poincaré–Lefschetz duality comes after.
- **Künneth edge maps and collapse**: identify the edge map with the cross-product Künneth map, and the E² morphisms with the maps induced on Tor. Then derive the collapse corollaries under flatness or projectivity.
- **Coordinate reference classes (§2.5)**: with the shuffle comparison in place, define the cube class, its sup-ball and disk pushforwards, the disk comparison coefficient and the normalized sphere class.
- **Free actions on spheres (§8.15)**: the cohomology of BC_p and B(C_p × C_p) is now known. What remains is that no continuous action of C_p × C_p on Sⁿ is free, together with the X̃ × Bπ₁X non-equivalence.
- **Relative Hurewicz and homological Whitehead**: prove injectivity for an (n−1)-connected pair with simply connected subspace, then apply it to the mapping cylinder.
