## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** I1 is done: Prékopa–Leindler and Brunn–Minkowski are proved, together with the homothety equality case for convex bodies. The sharp periodic curve inequalities and much of symmetrization are in place. The convex half of I4's ball-rigidity argument is proved, but the sharp perimeter inequality itself, Steiner perimeter comparison, BV Sobolev and the Jordan-curve theorem have not begun.

### Named results

- **The Brunn–Minkowski inequality** — `|A|^(1/n) + |B|^(1/n) ≤ |A + B|^(1/n)` for nonempty Lebesgue-measurable sets, with outer volume on the sum; it rests on the [Prékopa–Leindler inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/PrekopaLeindler.html#TauCeti.hasPrekopaLeindler_volume) in every finite dimension ([`TauCeti.volume_rpow_add_volume_rpow_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Basic.html#TauCeti.volume_rpow_add_volume_rpow_le)).
- **Brunn–Minkowski equality for convex bodies** — for compact convex sets of positive volume and `0 < θ < 1`, equality in the weighted inequality holds exactly when one is a translate and positive dilate of the other ([`Convex.volume_smul_add_smul_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Homothety.html#Convex.volume_smul_add_smul_eq_iff)).
- **The area–energy inequality** — `4π |signedArea γ| ≤ T ∫ ‖γ'‖²` for closed AC plane curves, with equality exactly for constant curves and once-traversed circles. It rests on Wirtinger's inequality with its equality case ([`TauCeti.mul_integral_norm_sq_eq_four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/AreaEnergy.html#TauCeti.mul_integral_norm_sq_eq_four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_iff)).
- **The sharp isodiametric inequality with equality** — a bounded measurable set has at most the volume of a ball of radius `diam / 2`, and attains it exactly when it agrees a.e. with such a ball ([`TauCeti.volume_eq_volume_closedBall_diam_div_two_iff_exists_ae_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Isodiametric.html#TauCeti.volume_eq_volume_closedBall_diam_div_two_iff_exists_ae_eq)).
- **Ball rigidity from the sharp first variation** — a positive-volume convex body whose parallel-volume root `t ↦ |K + t B|^(1/n)` has right derivative `ω_n^(1/n)` at zero is a closed ball. This is the final step of I4's rigidity deduction ([`ConvexBody.hasDerivWithinAt_volume_real_add_smul_closedBall_rpow_iff_exists_eq_closedBall`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Parallel.html#ConvexBody.hasDerivWithinAt_volume_real_add_smul_closedBall_rpow_iff_exists_eq_closedBall)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [**Steiner symmetrization**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Steiner.html#TauCeti.steinerSymmetrization) preserves volume, convexity and Borel measurability, and contracts in `L¹`. It is the object whose perimeter comparison and convergence to a ball are still to come.
- [**Schwarz rearrangement**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/SchwarzRearrangement.html#TauCeti.schwarzRearrangement) is proved through equimeasurability, `Lᵖ` contraction, Hardy–Littlewood and Lipschitz preservation. With [De la Vallée Poussin's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/DeLaValleePoussin.html#MeasureTheory.Integrable.exists_convexOn_superlinear_integrable), this sets up I5's approximation argument.
- **The one-dimensional isoperimetric inequality**: a set of positive finite measure in `ℝ` [has perimeter at least two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Real.html#TauCeti.Real.two_le_perimeter), with equality exactly for a.e. intervals and at least four otherwise. These are the line-by-line facts that Steiner slicing needs.

### Roadmap coverage

I1 is done, including the bridge between positive volume and nonempty interior for convex sets. I2 is partial. Steiner and Schwarz basics are proved, and so is the isodiametric inequality with both equality forms. One direction of the surface-area identification is in: [perimeter is at most the parallel-volume first variation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/MinkowskiContent.html#IsCompact.perimeter_le_of_hasDerivWithinAt). Still missing are Steiner perimeter nonincrease, convergence to the ball, the reverse surface-area identity and parallel derivative, and the convex-body isoperimetric inequality. I3 is partial: Wirtinger and area–energy are done with their equality analysis, but the periodic `W¹,²` form, geometric signed area for BV parameters and the Jordan-area identification are missing. I4 is partial. It has essential convexity, compact convex representatives, the [ball equality formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Ball.html#TauCeti.perimeter_ball_pow) and the convex rigidity step. The sharp inequality, the finite-perimeter slicing comparison and its equality implication, and the frontier corollaries are missing. I5 is partial: it has only Lipschitz rearrangement and De la Vallée Poussin. I6 is untouched.

## The frontier

- **Steiner perimeter nonincrease and slicing equality** — prove `P(S_e E) ≤ P(E)` by finite-perimeter slicing, with the localized comparison showing that equality forces a.e. interval sections. The one-dimensional perimeter bounds are ready; the slicing theorem depends on the GeometricMeasureTheory roadmap.
- **Convex surface area and the parallel derivative** — prove the reverse inequality, identifying the right derivative of `|K + rB|` at zero with `P(K)` and with boundary Hausdorff area. Convex isoperimetry would then follow from Brunn–Minkowski, and the ball-rigidity theorem above would be usable inside I4.
- **The sharp perimeter inequality** — the bounded case and the approximation passage to all finite-volume sets, then rigidity by assembling the pieces already proved.
- **Convergence of Steiner symmetrizations to the ball** — first for bounded finite-perimeter sets, then for all finite-volume sets via the `L¹` contraction.
- **Geometric signed area for rectifiable curves** — move area–energy from AC to continuous BV parameters via arclength, as I6 requires. This depends on GeometricMeasureTheory's arclength parametrization and Jordan index theorem.
