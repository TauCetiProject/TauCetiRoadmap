## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** I1 and I3 are done. The sharp Euclidean isoperimetric inequality holds for every finite-volume measurable set, the planar Jordan-curve theorem is proved with its circle equality case, and Steiner symmetrizations now provably converge to the ball. What remains open is unconditional finite-perimeter rigidity, the convex surface-area identification, the `W¹,¹` Pólya–Szegő theory with the BV Sobolev equality cases, and the planar finite-perimeter compatibility.

### Named results

- **The sharp isoperimetric inequality** — every Lebesgue-measurable set of finite volume in dimension `n ≥ 2` satisfies `P(E)^n ≥ n^n ω_n |E|^(n−1)`, with no boundedness or regularity assumed ([`MeasureTheory.NullMeasurableSet.finrank_pow_mul_volume_ball_mul_volume_pow_le_perimeter_pow`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Isoperimetric.html#MeasureTheory.NullMeasurableSet.finrank_pow_mul_volume_ball_mul_volume_pow_le_perimeter_pow)). Equality forces an a.e. ball only when almost every line section is assumed to be an interval.
- **The isoperimetric theorem for rectifiable Jordan curves** — the inside of a Jordan curve of Hausdorff length `L` has area `A` with `4πA ≤ L²`, with equality exactly for circles, including for parameters that are not absolutely continuous ([`TauCeti.IsJordanCurve.four_pi_mul_volume_real_filledHull_sdiff_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/PlaneSeparation/Isoperimetric.html#TauCeti.IsJordanCurve.four_pi_mul_volume_real_filledHull_sdiff_eq_iff)).
- **Convergence of Steiner symmetrizations to the ball** — for every measurable set of finite volume, some sequence of directions makes the successive symmetrals converge in symmetric-difference volume to the centered ball of the same volume ([`MeasureTheory.NullMeasurableSet.exists_tendsto_volume_iterateSteinerSymmetrization_symmDiff_ball`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Steiner/Convergence.html#MeasureTheory.NullMeasurableSet.exists_tendsto_volume_iterateSteinerSymmetrization_symmDiff_ball)).
- **The Brunn–Minkowski inequality** — `|A|^(1/n) + |B|^(1/n) ≤ |A + B|^(1/n)` for nonempty measurable sets, derived from Prékopa–Leindler, with the homothety equality case for convex bodies ([`TauCeti.volume_rpow_add_volume_rpow_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Basic.html#TauCeti.volume_rpow_add_volume_rpow_le)).
- **The sharp BV Sobolev inequality** — `‖u‖_{n/(n−1)} ≤ (n ω_n^(1/n))⁻¹ |Du|` for `n ≥ 2`, through BV Pólya–Szegő; its equality cases are not yet proved ([`TauCeti.eLpNorm_le_inv_mul_totalVariationOn`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Sobolev/Sharp.html#TauCeti.eLpNorm_le_inv_mul_totalVariationOn)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Steiner symmetrization** [does not increase perimeter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Steiner/Perimeter.html#TauCeti.perimeter_steinerSymmetrization_le) and [fixes, in every direction, exactly the centered balls](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Steiner/Ball.html#TauCeti.forall_steinerSymmetrization_ae_eq_iff_exists_ae_eq_ball). The nonincrease is proved by approximation, so it does not by itself give the slicing equality that rigidity needs.
- **The localized slicing estimate**: [perimeter pays two for each positive-length line section and two more for each noninterval section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Slicing/Excess.html#TauCeti.two_mul_volume_add_two_mul_volume_le_perimeter_of_noninterval_lineSection). This is the lower half of the Steiner comparison that I4's rigidity step needs.
- **Rearrangement inputs for `W¹,¹` Pólya–Szegő**: rearranged Lipschitz functions are Lipschitz, have null critical values, and have [level integrals equal to the derivative of the distribution function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/SchwarzRearrangement/CriticalValues.html#LipschitzWith.ae_setLIntegral_inv_enorm_fderiv_toReal_schwarzRearrangement_eq_ofReal_neg_deriv). In addition, uniformly integrable gradients exclude singular variation in BV limits.

### Roadmap coverage

I1 and I3 are done. I2 is partial: Steiner perimeter nonincrease and convergence to the ball, Schwarz rearrangement, convex isoperimetry, isodiametry with equality and the parallel-volume derivative formulas are in, but the agreement of convex perimeter with Hausdorff boundary area is missing. I4 is partial: the sharp inequality is complete, while rigidity is proved only under the interval-section hypothesis, and the frontier, regular-open and Lipschitz-domain corollaries are missing. I5 is partial: BV Pólya–Szegő and the sharp BV Sobolev bound are in, but not the `W¹,¹` gradient comparison, the radial profile or the equality cases. I6 is partial: the geometric and raw-curve theorems with equality, the trace characterization of rectifiable Jordan curves and the Cantor-parametrized circle are in. The finite-perimeter compatibility via GMT G6 and most remaining acceptance examples (strict ellipse, rectangle and annulus, punctured ball, separated balls) are not in.

## The frontier

- **Steiner equality forces interval sections** — the excess lower bound and the measurability of noninterval sections are in place. What remains is the matching description of `P(S_e E)` through its sections and the resulting equality implication. Combined with the existing conditional rigidity theorem, that completes I4's equality case.
- **Pólya–Szegő in `W¹,¹`** — the Lipschitz convex-integrand estimate `∫ Φ(‖∇h*‖) ≤ ∫ Φ(‖∇h‖)` remains, assembled from the critical-value and distribution-function results already proved, and then the absolute continuity of `D(u*)`. After it come the radial-profile comparison and the BV Sobolev equality cases.
- **Convex surface area equals Hausdorff boundary area** — the last I2 milestone. It also feeds the Lipschitz and `C¹` domain corollaries of I4.
- **Frontier and domain corollaries** — the `Hⁿ⁻¹(frontier E)` form with a.e. rigidity, exact rigidity for bounded regular open sets, and the Lipschitz and `C¹` domain versions. These depend on GMT G5–G6 boundary comparisons.
- **Planar finite-perimeter compatibility and acceptance examples** — identify the Jordan theorem with the finite-perimeter specialization through GMT G6, and prove the strict and rigidity examples listed in the README.
