## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The two convex inequalities underneath everything else are proved: Prékopa–Leindler in every finite dimension, and Brunn–Minkowski for measurable sets. So are the sharp periodic inequalities for absolutely continuous curves, with their equality cases. Symmetrization and convex geometry are partly built. The sharp Euclidean perimeter inequality, BV Sobolev and the Jordan-curve theorem have not begun, apart from the first structural lemmas for rigidity and for rearranging Lipschitz functions.

### Named results

- **The Prékopa–Leindler inequality** — on any finite-dimensional real inner product space, for extended nonnegative measurable functions and every weight `0 ≤ θ ≤ 1`, obtained by tensorizing the one-dimensional case ([`TauCeti.hasPrekopaLeindler_volume`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/PrekopaLeindler.html#TauCeti.hasPrekopaLeindler_volume)).
- **The Brunn–Minkowski inequality** — `|A|^(1/n) + |B|^(1/n) ≤ |A + B|^(1/n)` for nonempty Lebesgue-measurable sets, with outer volume on a possibly non-measurable sum; weighted and real-valued forms are included ([`TauCeti.volume_rpow_add_volume_rpow_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Basic.html#TauCeti.volume_rpow_add_volume_rpow_le)).
- **Wirtinger's inequality with equality** — for absolutely continuous closed functions valued in an inner product space, the mean-zero `L²` norm is at most `(T/2π)` times that of the derivative, with equality exactly for first harmonics ([`TauCeti.integral_norm_sub_average_sq_eq_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Wirtinger.html#TauCeti.integral_norm_sub_average_sq_eq_iff)).
- **The area–energy inequality** — `4π |signedArea γ| ≤ T ∫ ‖γ'‖²` for closed AC plane curves, with equality exactly for constant curves and once-traversed circles in either orientation ([`TauCeti.mul_integral_norm_sq_eq_four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/AreaEnergy.html#TauCeti.mul_integral_norm_sq_eq_four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_iff)). For constant-speed curves it becomes the [isoperimetric inequality in terms of length](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/AreaEnergy.html#HasConstantSpeedOnWith.four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_le_eVariationOn_toReal_sq).
- **Essential convexity** — a measurable set whose almost every line section, in every direction, is an interval up to null sets agrees a.e. with a convex set. This is the first of I4's rigidity targets ([`TauCeti.exists_convex_ae_eq_of_ae_lineSection_ordConnected`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/EssentialConvexity.html#TauCeti.exists_convex_ae_eq_of_ae_lineSection_ordConnected)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [**Steiner symmetrization**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Steiner.html#TauCeti.steinerSymmetrization), built on measure-preserving orthogonal line coordinates. It preserves volume and convexity, contracts in `L¹`, and is equivariant under isometries. These are the inputs to the perimeter comparison and to convergence toward a ball.
- [**Schwarz rearrangement**](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/SchwarzRearrangement.html#TauCeti.schwarzRearrangement), defined from the distribution function. Equimeasurability, layer cake, order preservation, `Lᵖ` contraction and the Hardy–Littlewood inequality are all proved. It also [preserves Lipschitz bounds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/SchwarzRearrangement/Lipschitz.html#TauCeti.lipschitzWith_toReal_schwarzRearrangement), which is the first step toward Pólya–Szegő.
- **Parallel-volume concavity for convex bodies**: `t ↦ |K + tL|^(1/n)` is concave, and its right derivative at zero meets the Brunn–Minkowski bound [exactly when the function is affine](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Parallel.html#ConvexBody.hasDerivWithinAt_volume_real_add_smul_rpow_iff). This is the tangent-line step of I4's ball-rigidity argument.

## The frontier

- **Brunn–Minkowski equality for convex bodies** — prove that equality in the weighted inequality for positive-volume compact convex sets forces a homothety, together with the bridge between positive volume and nonempty interior. This closes I1 and is consumed directly by I4's rigidity proof.
- **Steiner perimeter nonincrease** — prove `P(S_e E) ≤ P(E)` for finite-perimeter sets using the GeometricMeasureTheory roadmap's slicing and approximation. The localized comparison and its interval-section equality implication are what I4 needs.
- **Convergence of Steiner symmetrizations to the ball** — find directions whose iterated symmetrals converge in `L¹` to the volume-matched ball, first for bounded finite-perimeter sets and then for all finite-volume sets via the `L¹` contraction already proved.
- **Convex surface area and the outer-parallel derivative** — identify the perimeter of a convex body with its boundary Hausdorff area and with the right derivative of `|K + r B|`. Then derive the convex-body isoperimetric inequality from Brunn–Minkowski.
- **Geometric signed area for rectifiable curves** — move the area–energy results from AC parameters to arbitrary continuous BV parameters via arclength, as I6 requires. This depends on the GeometricMeasureTheory roadmap's arclength parametrization and Jordan index theorem.
