## Isoperimetry: 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-20 (`fa2f76c` to `68a457e`)

The first window of this roadmap brought both of its classical convex inequalities. The [Prékopa–Leindler inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/PrekopaLeindler.html#TauCeti.hasPrekopaLeindler_volume) now holds on every finite-dimensional real inner product space, proved in one dimension and then tensorized over products (TauCeti#17071). From it comes the [Brunn–Minkowski inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Basic.html#TauCeti.volume_rpow_add_volume_rpow_le) `|A|^(1/n) + |B|^(1/n) ≤ |A + B|^(1/n)` for nonempty Lebesgue-measurable sets, with outer volume on the sum, plus its weighted and real-valued forms. The equality case for compact convex bodies has not landed yet. On the curve side, [Wirtinger's inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Wirtinger.html#TauCeti.integral_norm_sub_average_sq_le) for absolutely continuous functions valued in an inner product space came with [its equality case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Wirtinger.html#TauCeti.integral_norm_sub_average_sq_eq_iff), the first harmonics. The [area–energy inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/AreaEnergy.html#TauCeti.four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_le) `4π |signedArea γ| ≤ T ∫ ‖γ'‖²` is proved with [equality exactly for constant curves and once-traversed circles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/AreaEnergy.html#TauCeti.mul_integral_norm_sq_eq_four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_iff), and that gives the [isoperimetric inequality for constant-speed closed curves](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/AreaEnergy.html#HasConstantSpeedOnWith.four_pi_mul_abs_signedArea_le_eVariationOn_toReal_sq).

The symmetrization layer also grew quickly. [Steiner symmetrization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Steiner.html#TauCeti.steinerSymmetrization) has its measure-theoretic basics: it preserves volume and convexity, contracts in `L¹`, and behaves correctly under translations, dilations and isometries. Perimeter nonincrease and convergence to a ball are still missing. [Schwarz rearrangement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/SchwarzRearrangement.html#TauCeti.schwarzRearrangement) arrived with equimeasurability, `Lᵖ` contraction, the [Hardy–Littlewood inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/SchwarzRearrangement.html#TauCeti.lintegral_mul_le_lintegral_schwarzRearrangement_mul) and preservation of Lipschitz bounds. Alongside these came the [isodiametric inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Isodiametric.html#TauCeti.volume_le_volume_closedBall_diam_div_two), without its equality case.

The first structural step of the ball-rigidity argument also landed: [essential convexity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/EssentialConvexity.html#TauCeti.exists_convex_ae_eq_of_ae_lineSection_ordConnected). It says that a measurable set whose almost every line section, in every direction, is an interval agrees a.e. with a convex set. The sharp perimeter inequality itself, sharp BV Sobolev and the Jordan-curve theorem have not started.

## Isoperimetry: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `e8ea0d1`)

The convex layer is now complete with [the equality case of Brunn–Minkowski for convex bodies](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Homothety.html#Convex.volume_smul_add_smul_eq_iff): for compact convex sets of positive volume and `0 < θ < 1`, equality holds exactly when one body is a translate and positive dilate of the other. There is also [an additive form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Homothety.html#Convex.volume_real_smul_add_smul_rpow_eq_iff) with arbitrary positive coefficients (TauCeti#20109). The proof compares height quantiles along every direction, using a Brunn–Minkowski inequality for hyperplane sections and the fact that the mean quantile is the centroid's height. This feeds straight into the ball-rigidity argument: [a convex body whose parallel-volume root has the sharp right derivative at zero is a closed ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Parallel.html#ConvexBody.hasDerivWithinAt_volume_real_add_smul_closedBall_rpow_iff_exists_eq_closedBall).

The [isodiametric inequality now has its equality case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Isodiametric.html#TauCeti.volume_eq_volume_closedBall_diam_div_two_iff_exists_ae_eq): a bounded measurable set with the volume of a ball of radius `diam / 2` agrees a.e. with such a ball, and a closed set is literally one. On the perimeter side, the [isoperimetric inequality on the line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Real.html#TauCeti.Real.two_le_perimeter) landed with [its equality case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Real.html#TauCeti.Real.perimeter_eq_two_iff) and the bound of four for sets that are not a.e. intervals. These are the one-dimensional facts that Steiner slicing will apply to each line. Balls were shown to [attain equality in the isoperimetric inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Ball.html#TauCeti.perimeter_ball_pow), and [the perimeter of a compact set is at most the first variation of its parallel volume](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/MinkowskiContent.html#IsCompact.perimeter_le_of_hasDerivWithinAt). That is one direction of the convex surface-area identification.

Smaller pieces include [De la Vallée Poussin's lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/DeLaValleePoussin.html#MeasureTheory.Integrable.exists_convexOn_superlinear_integrable), which I5's approximation argument needs, and compact convex representatives for sets that are a.e. convex. The area–energy equality case was also restated as an a.e. angular-velocity equation. The sharp perimeter inequality itself, Steiner perimeter nonincrease and the Jordan-curve theorem have not started.

## Isoperimetry: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-08 (`e8ea0d1` to `d2083f0`)

The headline inequality has landed: [the sharp isoperimetric inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Isoperimetric.html#MeasureTheory.NullMeasurableSet.finrank_pow_mul_volume_ball_mul_volume_pow_le_perimeter_pow) `P(E)^n ≥ n^n ω_n |E|^(n−1)` holds for every Lebesgue-measurable set of finite positive volume, with no boundedness or regularity assumed. It is proved first for regular superlevel sets of smooth functions, using Brunn–Minkowski on thin shells and a calibration estimate, and then passed to all sets by approximation (TauCeti#25271). Rigidity is only conditional so far: equality forces an a.e. ball [when almost every line section is an interval](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Isoperimetric.html#MeasureTheory.NullMeasurableSet.perimeter_pow_eq_iff_exists_ae_eq_ball_of_ae_lineSection_ordConnected), and the step showing that equality yields such sections is still missing. Along the way, [Steiner symmetrization does not increase perimeter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/Steiner/Perimeter.html#TauCeti.perimeter_steinerSymmetrization_le), the [first variation of a convex body's parallel volume is its perimeter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/ParallelVolume.html#Convex.hasDerivWithinAt_measureReal_add_smul_closedBall), and [convex isoperimetry holds with equality exactly for balls](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/BrunnMinkowski/Isoperimetric.html#ConvexBody.perimeter_pow_eq_iff_exists_eq_closedBall).

The plane got its classical theorem. [Every rectifiable Jordan curve satisfies `4πA ≤ L²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/PlaneSeparation/Isoperimetric.html#TauCeti.IsJordanCurve.four_pi_mul_volume_real_filledHull_sdiff_le), with Hausdorff length and the Lebesgue area of the inside, and [equality exactly for circles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/PlaneSeparation/Isoperimetric.html#TauCeti.IsJordanCurve.four_pi_mul_volume_real_filledHull_sdiff_eq_iff). The same holds for continuous bounded-variation parameters that need not be absolutely continuous. Behind this are a [geometric signed area](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Examples/CurveArea/RectifiableSignedArea.html#TauCeti.rectifiableSignedArea) for rectifiable curves, computed through arclength, and the identification of signed area with the orientation sign times the enclosed area. The periodic Wirtinger inequality also reached the [canonical `W¹,²` space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Poincare/Wirtinger/Periodic.html#TauCeti.W1p.integral_norm_sub_average_sq_le).

On the functional side, [BV Pólya–Szegő](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/SchwarzRearrangement.html#TauCeti.totalVariationOn_toReal_schwarzRearrangement_le) and the [sharp BV Sobolev bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/BoundedVariation/Sobolev/Sharp.html#TauCeti.eLpNorm_le_inv_mul_totalVariationOn) with its constant `(n ω_n^(1/n))⁻¹` landed, though its equality cases did not. Supporting work prepared the remaining steps: the perimeter slicing inequality, the fact that sets fixed by every Steiner symmetrization are balls, and Jensen bounds for mollified gradients.
