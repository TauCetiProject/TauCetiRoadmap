## Subfactors: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`657f399` to `7ca783d`)

The first ingredient of Jones's positivity theorem landed: for a self-adjoint loop value, the Temperley–Lieb algebra now carries its [analytic adjoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.instStarRing), the conjugate-linear, product-reversing involution fixing every generator (TauCeti#31519). It is the [unique such map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.star_unique), [commutes with adding a strand](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.strandIncl_star), and [cannot exist](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.isSelfAdjoint_of_star_e) unless the loop value is self-adjoint; [algebra maps preserve it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.map_star_iff) exactly when they send generators to self-adjoint elements. This settles section 3.1 of the roadmap.

Alongside it came the trigonometry behind the necessity half of the positivity theorem (TauCeti#29609): at δ = 2cos θ the quantum integers are [ratios of sines](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Real.html#TauCeti.qInt_two_mul_cos), [positive up to ⌊π/θ⌋](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Real.html#TauCeti.qInt_two_mul_cos_pos_of_le_floor) and [negative just after](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Real.html#TauCeti.qInt_two_mul_cos_floor_add_one_neg) when π/θ is not an integer, while at δ = 2cos(π/m) the weights before [m] are positive and [m] itself [vanishes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Real.html#TauCeti.qInt_two_mul_cos_pi_div_eq_zero). For δ ≥ 2 every [positive-index quantum integer is positive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Order.html#TauCeti.qInt_pos_of_two_le). Jones's positivity theorem itself is not yet proved, and nothing on the operator-algebra side (correspondences, the basic construction, the Jones tower) has begun.
