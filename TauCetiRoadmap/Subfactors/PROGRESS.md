## Subfactors: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`657f399` to `7ca783d`)

The first ingredient of Jones's positivity theorem landed: for a self-adjoint loop value, the Temperley–Lieb algebra now carries its [analytic adjoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.instStarRing), the conjugate-linear, product-reversing involution fixing every generator (TauCeti#31519). It is the [unique such map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.star_unique), [commutes with adding a strand](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.strandIncl_star), and [cannot exist](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.isSelfAdjoint_of_star_e) unless the loop value is self-adjoint; [algebra maps preserve it](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Star.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.map_star_iff) exactly when they send generators to self-adjoint elements. This settles section 3.1 of the roadmap.

Alongside it came the trigonometry behind the necessity half of the positivity theorem (TauCeti#29609): at δ = 2cos θ the quantum integers are [ratios of sines](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Real.html#TauCeti.qInt_two_mul_cos), [positive up to ⌊π/θ⌋](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Real.html#TauCeti.qInt_two_mul_cos_pos_of_le_floor) and [negative just after](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Real.html#TauCeti.qInt_two_mul_cos_floor_add_one_neg) when π/θ is not an integer, while at δ = 2cos(π/m) the weights before [m] are positive and [m] itself [vanishes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Real.html#TauCeti.qInt_two_mul_cos_pi_div_eq_zero). For δ ≥ 2 every [positive-index quantum integer is positive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Order.html#TauCeti.qInt_pos_of_two_le). Jones's positivity theorem itself is not yet proved, and nothing on the operator-algebra side (correspondences, the basic construction, the Jones tower) has begun.

## Subfactors: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

Jones's positivity theorem is proved. For δ > 0, the Hermitian forms tr_n(x†y) of the Temperley–Lieb Markov trace are positive semidefinite on every number of strands exactly when δ = 2cos(π/m) for some m ≥ 3 or δ ≥ 2 ([`forall_map_star_mul_self_nonneg_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Positivity.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.forall_map_star_mul_self_nonneg_iff), stated for the spin-model trace as [`markovTrace_nonneg_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Positivity.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.markovTrace_nonneg_iff); TauCeti#38770). Its refinement also landed: at an inadmissible δ > 0, the form on n strands is positive exactly when n < ⌊π/arccos(δ/2)⌋ ([`forall_map_star_mul_self_nonneg_iff_lt_floor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Positivity.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.forall_map_star_mul_self_nonneg_iff_lt_floor); TauCeti#40289). Necessity follows the roadmap's route: the Jones–Wenzl projections are self-adjoint, and the one at the first bad level has negative trace [k+1] ([`exists_map_star_mul_self_neg`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Positivity.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.exists_map_star_mul_self_neg)). For sufficiency, a positive spin model handles δ ≥ 2. For δ = 2cos(π/m), a [path representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/PathModel.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.pathRep) on walks in any finite graph whose squared vertex weights form an adjacency eigenvector gives a [positive Markov trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/PathModel.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.map_star_mul_self_nonneg_of_graph), applied to the Dynkin graph A_(m−1). Below the bad level, truncated path models and a [Markov uniqueness up to N strands](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Trace/Basic.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTraceUpTo.unique) close the gap.

The consequences that 3.5 will use came with it: [Cauchy–Schwarz](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Positivity.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.norm_map_star_mul_sq_le), the [trace radical as the quadratic null space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Positivity.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.mem_traceRadical_iff_map_star_mul_self_eq_zero), and the [pullback criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Positivity.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.mem_jonesLoopParameters_of_pullback), which forces δ to be admissible whenever star-maps into algebras with positive functionals pull back to positive multiples of the trace. The Jones quotients now carry a [faithful positive trace](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TemperleyLieb/Positivity.html#TauCeti.TemperleyLieb.IsMarkovTrace.quotientStarPositiveFunctional). Separately, the reusable lemma of 3.3, that a finite-dimensional star algebra with a faithful positive functional is a [C*-algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/StarAlgebra/GNS/FiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.StarPositiveFunctional.cstarAlgebra) under the operator norm of left multiplication, gained its norm characterisation. Supporting work covered star-stable ideals and their quotients, and walks in simple graphs.

Still missing: the path representation's kernel has not been identified with the trace radical, and the C*-tower of the Jones quotients has not been built. The index restriction still waits on Layer 2's Jones tower.
