## PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-08 (`9c2503e` to `d2083f0`)

The roadmap's first window built most of the planar Cauchy–Riemann toolkit (Layer 3). The [Calderón–Zygmund inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/CalderonZygmund.html#TauCeti.exists_eLpNorm_fderiv_le_eLpNorm_conjWirtingerDeriv) bounds the full derivative of a map by its `∂̄` in every `Lᵖ` with `1 < p < ∞`. The [Beurling operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/Beurling.html#TauCeti.beurlingLp) is now a bounded operator on all of `Lᵖ`, obtained from its Fourier multiplier on `L²` (TauCeti#28836). The [Cauchy transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Pompeiu/Basic.html#TauCeti.cauchyTransform) inverts `∂̄` on compactly supported `C¹` data, is bounded on `Lᵖ` between discs, and [gains one Hölder derivative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Pompeiu/Holder/Gain.html#TauCeti.exists_contDiffOn_holderOnWith_fderiv_pi_inv_smul_integral_sub_inv_smul), so `C^{0,α}` data give `C^{1,α}` solutions. [Weyl's lemma for `∂̄`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/Weyl.html#TauCeti.HasWeakConjWirtingerDerivOn.exists_differentiableOn_ae_eq) says a distributional solution of `∂̄u = 0` agrees almost everywhere with a holomorphic function. It rests on a vector-valued [Weyl lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Harmonic/Weyl.html#TauCeti.WeaklyHarmonicOn.exists_harmonicOnNhd_ae_eq) for harmonic functions. Two things are still missing. The Hölder gain is proved only at order zero, and no estimate yet allows Sobolev coefficients.

Layer 2's definitions are in place. [Finite-regularity almost complex structures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/ContMDiff.html#TauCeti.ContMDiffAlmostComplexStructure) come with pullbacks, products, chart expressions and [domain-dependent families](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Family.html#TauCeti.ContMDiffAlmostComplexStructureFamily), and the [Floer forcing term](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Inhomogeneous/Floer.html#TauCeti.InhomogeneousTerm.floer) is an instance of the inhomogeneous terms. A radial-rotation example [shows the regularity index is not redundant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/RadialRotation.html#TauCeti.ContMDiffAlmostComplexStructure.not_exists_radialRotation_successor). [Totally real embeddings](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/TotallyReal/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsTotallyRealEmbedding) are defined, and [Lagrangian embeddings are totally real](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/TotallyReal/Basic.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.IsLagrangianEmbedding.isTotallyRealEmbedding) for tame structures. Isothermal coordinates and the adapted boundary chart are not done. So far there is only the [closed half-plane model](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/ClosedUpperHalfPlane.html#TauCeti.closedUpperHalfPlaneModel), constant-coefficient Beltrami maps, and a topological doubling.

Layer 1 now has the vector-valued [local Sobolev class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Local/Basic.html#TauCeti.MemWkpLoc) with its basic API, the [higher-order reflection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Trace/Reflection/Higher.html#TauCeti.higherReflection) along a normal line, and a scalar local Rellich theorem. Groundwork for the splitting step of Riemann–Roch also landed. This includes [nowhere-vanishing sections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/Generic.html#TauCeti.exists_contMDiffSection_forall_ne_zero_of_finrank_lt) of bundles whose rank exceeds the dimension of the base, and Hermitian metrics on complex bundles.

## PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-13 (`d2083f0` to `aa6e43e`)

The constant-coefficient core of Layer 3 is now complete. The Cauchy transform [gains a full Hölder derivative at every order](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Pompeiu/Holder/Higher.html#TauCeti.exists_contDiffOn_holderOnWith_iteratedFDeriv_cauchyTransform), so it is a [bounded operator `C^{k,α}(D_r) → C^{k+1,α}(D_R)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Pompeiu/Holder/Higher.html#TauCeti.cauchyTransformCkCLM). The [Beurling operator](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Pompeiu/Holder/Beurling.html#TauCeti.beurlingCkCLM) is bounded on the same Hölder scale, and [`T` maps `Lᵖ(D_r)` boundedly into `W^{1,p}(D_R)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/Beurling/Lp.html#TauCeti.eLpNorm_cauchyTransform_add_eLpNorm_wirtingerPartsCLM_beurlingLp_le). Built on these are the [interior `W^{k+1,p}` estimate for `∂̄`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/Regularity.html#TauCeti.exists_eLpNormWkp_le_of_hasWeakConjWirtingerDerivOn), the matching [weak regularity theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/Regularity.html#TauCeti.HasWeakConjWirtingerDerivOn.memWkpLoc_succ), and [regularity for the divergence-form Laplace equation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/DivergenceLaplace.html#TauCeti.memWkpLoc_one_of_forall_integral_laplacian_smul_eq), which the Sobolev-coefficient estimates will use.

Layer 1 gained most of its embedding and product theory. This window added [Morrey's embedding `W^{k+1,p}_loc ⊆ Cᵏ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Local/Morrey/Higher.html#TauCeti.MemWkpLoc.exists_contDiffOn_ae_eq) for `p > 2`, plus [mixed-exponent products](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Local/Multiplication/Higher.html#TauCeti.MemWkpLoc.bilin_succ) and [composition with smooth maps](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Local/ChainRule/Higher.html#TauCeti.MemWkpLoc.contDiffOn_comp_succ) at every order. The classes are [invariant under `Cᵏ` diffeomorphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Local/ChangeOfVariables/Higher.html#TauCeti.memWkpLoc_comp_openPartialHomeomorph_iff), including on half-discs, and there is a [vector-valued local Rellich theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Local/Compactness.html#Module.Basis.exists_subseq_memWkpLoc_tendsto_wkp). In Layer 2, [adapted charts](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/AdaptedChart.html#TauCeti.exists_adaptedChart) along a totally real submanifold now exist and [converge with their data](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/AdaptedChart/Family.html#TauCeti.exists_adaptedChart_family). The [planar Beltrami chart](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Beltrami/Chart.html#TauCeti.Beltrami.exists_isComplexLinear_chart) handles small Hölder coefficients, and [transitions between isothermal charts are holomorphic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Beltrami/Transition.html#TauCeti.Beltrami.differentiableOn_symm_trans_of_fderiv_comp).

Later layers got groundwork but not their main theorems. [Weak solutions with Sobolev coefficients](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CauchyRiemann/RoughCoefficients/Weak.html#TauCeti.IsWeakCRSolution) are defined, but no estimate for them is proved yet. [Unique continuation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CauchyRiemann/UniqueContinuation.html#TauCeti.eqOn_zero_of_hasSimilarityFactorizationAt_of_eqOn_zero) holds for solutions that admit a similarity factorization. That factorization is proved only in the scalar case, so the vector similarity principle is still missing. The [Riemann–Hilbert problem on the disc](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UnitDisc/RiemannHilbert.html#TauCeti.exists_diffContOnCl_sub_zpow_mul_conj_eq_iff) is solved, and the index of `∂̄` is computed on the [annulus](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Annulus/RealBoundaryKernel.html#TauCeti.Complex.annulusRealBoundary_index_eq_zero) and the [torus](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Torus/CauchyRiemannOperator.html#TauCeti.UnitAddTorus.finrank_ker_sub_finrank_cokernel_cauchyRiemannOperatorₗ). These are test cases for Riemann–Roch. This summary does not cover the window's manifold-level modules.
