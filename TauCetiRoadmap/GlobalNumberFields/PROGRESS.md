## GlobalNumberFields: 2026-08-30 to 2026-09-01 (`12f181a` to `8745177`)

The first result to land in this roadmap is its most heavily consumed one: **Artin–Whaples weak approximation at mixed places** ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.weakApproximation_denseRange`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Approximation/Weak.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.weakApproximation_denseRange)). For finite sets of finite places and of infinite places, the diagonal map from a number field into the product of the corresponding adic and archimedean completions has dense range. This is the exact statement the roadmap promises to Global Quadratic Forms, in the mixed form that a finite-place congruence corollary alone would not supply, and it completes the headline target of Layer 1 (TauCeti#5200). The rest of Layer 1 has not been touched: the derived simultaneous-target and real-sign statements for an element of `Kˣ`, the total sign homomorphism to the product of `ℤˣ` over real places, and its surjectivity on `Kˣ` all remain to be done.

The only other work so far is Layer 0 infrastructure at the infinite places (TauCeti#5386). The **normalized absolute value on an infinite completion** ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.infiniteCompletionNormalizedAbsValue`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Places/Completion.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.infiniteCompletionNormalizedAbsValue)) is a monoid-with-zero homomorphism on `w.Completion` sending `x` to `‖x‖ ^ w.mult`, so it is the ordinary absolute value at a real place and the squared absolute value at a complex place, as the roadmap's conventions require. It is continuous, vanishes exactly at zero, and on the dense copy of `K` agrees with the normalized infinite-place value ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.infiniteCompletionNormalizedAbsValue_algebraMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Places/Completion.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.infiniteCompletionNormalizedAbsValue_algebraMap)), with separate real and complex evaluation lemmas.

Nothing has landed on the finite side of Layer 0: no normalized finite-place absolute value, no finiteness of the places where `|x|_v ≠ 1`, no transport of the product formula, and no functoriality under extensions. Layers 2 through 11 have no declarations yet.

## GlobalNumberFields: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`9f176e4` to `0d3161a`)

The window's headline is the idelic description of finite-order characters. [Finite-order Hecke characters are exactly the ray class characters](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/FiniteOrder.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.isFiniteOrder_iff_exists_rayClassCharacter): a Hecke character has finite order exactly when it is pulled back from some ray class group, and exactly when [its kernel is open](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/FiniteOrder.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.isFiniteOrder_iff_isOpen_ker). Underneath it is the [cofinality of ray subgroups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Ideles/Ray/OpenSubgroup.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.exists_raySubgroup_le_of_isOpen): every open subgroup of the idele class group contains one (TauCeti#9083). The ray-class count also gained its [trivial-modulus agreement](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/RayClass/Count/TrivialModulus.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.tendsto_rayClassIdealCountingFunction_one) with the total ideal count, which was the last missing piece of the counting layer.

Infinity types, which had not been started, now have a real foundation. The continuous characters of [ℝˣ](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Complex/ArchimedeanCharacter.html#TauCeti.realUnitsCharacterEquiv) and [ℂˣ](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Complex/ArchimedeanCharacter.html#TauCeti.complexUnitsCharacterEquiv) are classified by a complex exponent together with a sign or an integer frequency. On top of this sit the continuous, algebraic, and finite-order carriers and the [infinity type](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/InfinityType.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.infinityType) of a Hecke character. Algebraicity on the identity component is [characterized](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/InfinityType/IdentityComponent.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ContinuousInfinityType.isAlgebraicOnIdentityComponent_iff) as an algebraic type up to a real sign twist. So far this is a predicate on infinity types only: there is no `HeckeCharacter.IsAlgebraic` yet, and no cyclotomic arithmetic.

Orders moved furthest: they now have a [conductor](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/Conductor.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.conductor), proper fractional ideals, the wide [Picard group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/Picard.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.Pic), and the [narrow Picard group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/NarrowPic.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NarrowPic). They also have a surjection from the narrow group to the wide group whose kernel is the narrow classes of principal ideals, and [morphisms of orders](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/Hom.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.Hom) that preserve total positivity. The maps these morphisms should induce on Picard groups are not built yet, and neither is the ideal class monoid.

## GlobalNumberFields: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-04 (`0d3161a` to `41e5e49`)

Hecke characters now have conductors and an algebraicity theory. Over `ℚ`, [ray class characters of `(n)·∞` are Dirichlet characters modulo `n`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/RayClass/Character/Dirichlet.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratDirichletEquiv). The match respects conductors, and the real place lies in the conductor exactly when the Dirichlet character is odd (TauCeti#10508). In general, local components at finite places give a [finite conductor](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/FiniteComponent.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.finiteConductor), and a finite-order character has a [conductor](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/Conductor.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.conductor), the least modulus it comes from. [Algebraic characters](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/Algebraic.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.IsAlgebraic) of Weil type `A₀` are defined, and [every finite-order character is algebraic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/Algebraic.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.isAlgebraic_of_isFiniteOrder). A [nontrivial unitary norm character is not algebraic](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/Algebraic.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.not_isAlgebraic_normCharacter), and it does not have finite order either. Purity, norm twists, unit compatibility and the ideal-side character followed.

Cyclotomic arithmetic began. The [ray class group of `(n)·∞` is the Galois group of `ℚ(ζ_n)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Cyclotomic/RayClass.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratModulusEquivGal), and under this identification the ray class of a prime is its Frobenius. The ramified primes are worked out, including the [exception `ℚ(ζ₆)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/RamifiedPrimes.html#IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.ramifiedPrimes_eq_singleton_three), which is unramified at `2`. On the adelic side, the [identity component of the idele class group is the intersection of the ray subgroups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Ideles/IdentityComponent.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.iInf_raySubgroup), with a compact, totally disconnected quotient. `ℚ` gained its [standard adele fundamental domain](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Adeles/RatFundamentalDomain.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratFundamentalDomain). For extensions there are now [idele class norm maps](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Ideles/Norm/Relative.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ideleClassNormMap), compatible with towers, and a Galois action on adeles. Topological base change is so far proved only for [infinite adeles](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Adeles/InfiniteBaseChange.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.infiniteAdeleBaseChangeEquiv).

Orders gained [Picard functoriality](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/Functoriality.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.Hom.mapPic), including the naturality square. They also gained an [ideal class monoid](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/IdealClassMonoid.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.IdealClassMonoid) whose units are the Picard group, the [index formula for discriminants](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/Discriminant.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.discr_eq_index_sq_mul), and extension and contraction of ideals away from the conductor. The Gorenstein criterion and the cubic proper-but-noninvertible example are still missing.
