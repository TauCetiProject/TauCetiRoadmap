### Named results

- **The Dirichlet dictionary over ℚ** — ray class characters of `(n)·∞` are Dirichlet characters modulo `n`. The finite conductor matches the Dirichlet conductor, and the real place drops out of the conductor exactly for even characters ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratDirichletEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/RayClass/Character/Dirichlet.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratDirichletEquiv)).
- **The cyclotomic ray class group is the Galois group** — the ray class group of `ℚ` modulo `(n)·∞` is `Gal(ℚ(ζ_n)/ℚ)`, and the ray class of a prime not dividing `n` is its arithmetic Frobenius ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratModulusEquivGal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Cyclotomic/RayClass.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratModulusEquivGal)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The conductor of a finite-order Hecke character** is the least modulus from which the character comes. Its finite part is the product of the local conductor exponents, and its ray class representative is primitive ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.conductor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/Conductor.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.conductor)).
- **The norm map of idele classes** `C_L → C_K` is defined for an extension of number fields. Its components are products of local norms, and it composes in towers. It is the carrier any later norm-index argument will use ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ideleClassNormMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Ideles/Norm/Relative.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ideleClassNormMap)).
