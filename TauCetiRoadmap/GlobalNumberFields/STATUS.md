## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Nine of the twelve layers are done. They cover places and the product formula, weak approximation, ray-class carriers and ideal counting, the additive adele quotient, the ray-class dictionary, extensions of adeles and ideles, Hecke characters, and algebraic characters with cyclotomic arithmetic. Finite adeles, the idelic agreement corollaries, and orders are still partial, and each has a named gap.

### Named results

- **The ray class ideal count** — every ray class has the same main coefficient, including the Euler factors at primes dividing the modulus, and a uniform power-saving error ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.rayClassIdealCount`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/RayClass/Count/Asymptotic.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.rayClassIdealCount)).
- **Finite-order Hecke characters are ray class characters** — a Hecke character has finite order exactly when it factors through some ray class group ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.isFiniteOrder_iff_exists_rayClassCharacter`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/FiniteOrder.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.isFiniteOrder_iff_exists_rayClassCharacter)).
- **The cyclotomic ray class group is the Galois group** — the ray class group of `ℚ` modulo `(n)·∞` is `Gal(ℚ(ζ_n)/ℚ)`, and the ray class of a prime not dividing `n` is its arithmetic Frobenius ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratModulusEquivGal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Cyclotomic/RayClass.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.ratModulusEquivGal)).
- **The splitting law in cyclotomic fields** — if `n = p^k m` with `p ∤ m`, then `p` factors in `ℚ(ζ_n)` as a product of distinct primes to the power `φ(p^k)`, and each has residue degree equal to the order of `p` mod `m` ([`TauCeti.NumberField.span_natCast_eq_prod_primesOverFinset_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Splitting.html#TauCeti.NumberField.span_natCast_eq_prod_primesOverFinset_pow)).
- **The Picard group of an order is a ray class quotient** — `Pic O` is the image of the ray class group of the conductor. The kernel consists of the classes of ideals generated by an element of the order, so `Pic O` is finite ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.mem_ker_rayClassToPic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/RayClass.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.mem_ker_rayClassToPic)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Base change of Hecke characters** is pullback along the idele class norm. It preserves algebraicity, and it acts on infinity types as the local norms dictate. This makes characters of a base field available over its extensions ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.baseChange`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/HeckeCharacter/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.HeckeCharacter.baseChange)).
- **Topological adele base change** `𝔸_K ⊗_K L ≃ 𝔸_L` is a continuous algebra equivalence. It is the product of its finite and infinite halves and composes in towers, so later idelic arguments can move open sets between `K` and `L` ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.adeleBaseChangeEquiv_tower`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Adeles/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.adeleBaseChangeEquiv_tower)).
- **Gorenstein orders** are those whose trace dual is invertible. Maximal and quadratic orders are Gorenstein, which sets up the statement that proper ideals are invertible ([`TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.IsGorenstein`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Orders/Gorenstein.html#TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.IsGorenstein)).

## The frontier

- **Proper ideals of Gorenstein orders are invertible** — the general criterion, of which the quadratic case is already proved. The trace dual and its basic properties are in place.
- **Agreement corollaries from idelic compactness** — recover finiteness of the class group and Dirichlet's unit theorem from compactness of the norm-one idele class group. Every ideal class is already the class of a norm-one idele.
- **The placewise finite-adele API** — Layer 4 asks for projections, integral subrings, single-place elements and the unit group. The earlier reports record this API as incomplete, and this window added nothing to it.
