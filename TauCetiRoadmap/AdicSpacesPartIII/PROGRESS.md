## AdicSpacesPartIII: 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-13 (`6203ed6` to `aa6e43e`)

The formal spectrum now exists. [`Spf A`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/Spf/Basic.html#TauCeti.FormalScheme.Spf) of an adic ring of finite ideal type is built as in EGA I §10.1, as a locally ringed space whose structure sheaf is the limit of the structure sheaves of the thickenings `Spec(A/I^{n+1})`. Its global sections are `A` ([`globalSectionsIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/Spf/StructureSheaf.html#TauCeti.FormalScheme.Spf.globalSectionsIso)), the basic open `D(f)` is the formal spectrum of the completed localisation ([`restrictBasicOpenIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/Spf/Restrict.html#TauCeti.FormalScheme.Spf.restrictBasicOpenIso)), the stalks are local with residue field `Frac(A/p)` ([`stalkResidueFieldEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/Spf/Basic.html#TauCeti.FormalScheme.Spf.stalkResidueFieldEquiv)), and nothing depends on the choice of ideal of definition ([`structureSheafIso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/Spf/IdealIndependence.html#TauCeti.FormalScheme.Spf.structureSheafIso)). Continuous ring maps induce morphisms ([`Spf.map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/Spf/Morphism.html#TauCeti.FormalScheme.Spf.map)), and distinct maps give distinct morphisms; the converse, that every morphism of formal spectra comes from a continuous map, is not yet proved. On top of this sits a first definition of [formal schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/Basic.html#TauCeti.FormalScheme), into which ordinary schemes embed fully faithfully (TauCeti#35574).

Underneath is the commutative algebra of EGA I chapter 0. The [completed localisation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicRing/CompletedAway.html#TauCeti.AdicRing.CompletedAway) has its universal property and flatness, and agrees with Huber's rational localisation `A⟨1/f⟩` ([`completionLocEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicRing/CompletedAway/RationalLocalization.html#TauCeti.AdicRing.CompletedAway.completionLocEquiv)). The [completed tensor product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicRing/CompletedTensorProduct.html#TauCeti.AdicRing.CompletedTensorProduct) has its universal property and is [noetherian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/AdicRing/CompletedTensorProduct/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.AdicRing.CompletedTensorProduct.isNoetherianRing) under the usual finiteness hypotheses. Over a noetherian complete adic ring, [finite modules are the same as `I`-adic systems](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/Reduction.html#TauCeti.ModuleTower.finiteReduction_isEquivalence) with finite bottom term.

Layers R2 and R3 also have their first results. R2.1 has [algebras topologically of finite presentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TopologicallyFiniteType/FinitePresentation.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsTopologicallyFinitePresentation) over a complete rank-one valuation ring, with [admissible algebras characterised as those that are also flat](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/TopologicallyFiniteType/FinitePresentation.html#TauCeti.Huber.isAdmissibleAlgebra_iff_flat), and a theory of [coherent modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Coherent/Basic.html#Module.IsCoherent) prepares for the coherence of such algebras. R2.10's [fractional valuation bounds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/ValuationSpectrum/FractionalBound.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.fracPowLE) `|f| ≤ |g|^q` landed, and R3.8 has the [trace on finite projective modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Trace/Projective.html#LinearMap.projectiveTrace), with cyclicity and base change.
