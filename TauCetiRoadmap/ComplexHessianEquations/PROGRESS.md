## ComplexHessianEquations: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`ae0d6a0` to `0253c4f`)

The finite-dimensional spectral calculus arrived nearly whole. Lewis's theorem is proved: a spectral function `A ↦ f(λ(A))` on Hermitian matrices is differentiable exactly when the symmetric `f` is differentiable at the eigenvalue vector ([`differentiableAt_spectralFunction_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Eigenvalues/SpectralFunction.html#Matrix.differentiableAt_spectralFunction_iff)), with its derivative read off an eigenbasis ([`hasFDerivAt_spectralFunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Eigenvalues/SpectralFunction.html#Matrix.hasFDerivAt_spectralFunction)). The route went through the differentiability of Ky Fan sums at a spectral gap ([`hasFDerivAt_prefixSum_eigenvalues₀`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Eigenvalues/KyFan.html#Matrix.hasFDerivAt_prefixSum_eigenvalues₀)). Alongside it came the concavity of spectral functions of concave symmetric `f`, by majorization ([`concaveOn_spectralFunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SpectralFunction.html#Matrix.concaveOn_spectralFunction)), its tangent-plane form ([`sub_le_fderiv_spectralFunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/SpectralFunction/Tangent.html#Matrix.sub_le_fderiv_spectralFunction)), monotonicity in the Loewner order, and the gradient's determinant, Euler identity and positive definiteness. The `C¹` clause of Lewis's theorem is still missing.

The Gårding-cone layer has begun with the algebra of elementary symmetric functions: the recurrence, derivative and shift identities, Newton's inequalities ([`esymm_div_choose_mul_esymm_div_choose_le_sq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/Symmetric/NewtonMaclaurin.html#Multiset.esymm_div_choose_mul_esymm_div_choose_le_sq)) through polynomial Rolle, and Maclaurin's inequalities. Hyperbolic polynomials and Gårding's theorem have not started.

On the manifold side, eigenvalues relative to a Hermitian form now exist ([`relEigenvalues`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/RelativeEigenvalues/Basic.html#LinearMap.IsSymm.relEigenvalues)), as does the real (1,1)-form `i∂∂̄u` built from the Levi form ([`ddbarForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/DdbarForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.ddbarForm)). It is continuous for `C²` functions and agrees with `(1/2) d dᶜ`. With it comes the Hermitian form of `χ + i∂∂̄φ`, which has a sorted eigenvalue vector relative to any Hermitian metric ([`exists_isRelEigenvalueVector_ddbarHermitianForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/DdbarHermitianForm.html#TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.Compatible.exists_isRelEigenvalueVector_ddbarHermitianForm)).
