## OperatorTheory: 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-19 (`80e37ff` to `a5cde7b`)

This first report covers the whole build-out of the operator-theory family, and its headline is the [polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Polar/Bounded.html#ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus) `T = U|T|` for bounded operators between Hilbert spaces, with no dimension restriction (TauCeti#16936). The polar factor comes with its kernel, its range, its initial and final projections and a uniqueness statement, and it sits on top of the [operator modulus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Modulus.html#ContinuousLinearMap.modulus). The finite-dimensional groundwork landed alongside it: the [Courant–Fischer min–max theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/CourantFischer.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.eigenvalues_eq_iSup_iInf_re_inner), Weyl's inequality, singular systems and the Moore–Penrose inverse.

The spectral inequalities followed in finite dimension. They include the [Ky Fan triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/KyFan.html#LinearMap.kyFanSum_add_le), and [Fan dominance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/FanDominance.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.map_le_of_kyFanSum_le), which says that Ky Fan sums control every unitarily invariant norm, for rectangular maps as well as square ones. Also here are the forward Schur–Horn inequality, the [Hoffman–Wielandt inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HoffmanWielandt.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.sum_sq_eigenvalues_sub_le_sum_sq_norm_apply) and Davis's eigenvalue-change bound. These feed the [Davis–Kahan `sin Θ` theorem in unitarily invariant norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/Basic.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_map_sub) under an interval–exterior gap, and a [dimension-free operator-norm version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/SinTheta.html#Submodule.mul_projectionGap_le_norm_sub) for the projector gap. The [Haagerup–Zsidó kernel has total mass π/2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/HaagerupZsido/Mass.html#TauCeti.HaagerupZsido.integral_norm_reciprocalKernel), but the pairwise `π/2` Sylvester bound that mass is for, and Rosenblum's theorem, have not landed.

In infinite dimension, [approximation numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.singularValue) now characterise compactness, satisfy a [Ky Fan triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/KyFan.html#ContinuousLinearMap.kyFanGauge_add_le) on complete sources, and give Hilbert–Schmidt operators their model as `ℓ²` of columns. For unbounded operators, the [Kato–Rellich theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/KatoRellich.html#IsSelfAdjoint.add_of_isRelativelyBounded) and a [unitary Cayley transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Cayley.html#IsSelfAdjoint.cayley_mem_unitary) are in. Projection-valued measures are not, so there is no Borel calculus yet and no spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator.

## OperatorTheory: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-24 (`a5cde7b` to `271e1af`)

The headline is the sharp [`π/2` Sylvester bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/Sylvester/Pairwise.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sylvester_apply) of Bhatia, Davis and McIntosh. If two symmetric operators on finite-dimensional spaces have spectra at least `δ` apart, every solution of `AX − XB = C` satisfies `δ·N(X) ≤ (π/2)·N(C)` in every unitarily invariant norm (TauCeti#18808). This is the theorem the Haagerup–Zsidó kernel was built for. It is not yet applied to give the pairwise `π/2` `sin Θ` theorem, and Rosenblum's theorem is still open. The Davis–Kahan family grew around it: an [equal-rank spectral-projector form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/SpectralPerturbation.html#Module.End.mul_norm_sub_starProjection_pointSpectralSubspace_le), `sin Θ` bounds for [singular subspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SingularSubspace/Perturbation.html#LinearMap.mul_norm_sinThetaMap_rightSingularSubspace_le), and the first statistical pieces, with corresponding eigenblocks, Hermitian dilations and a [Yu–Wang–Samworth bound for right singular vectors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/YuWangSamworth/SingularVectors.html#LinearMap.sq_mul_sum_sum_norm_inner_rightSingularBasis_sq_le_four_mul_card_mul_sq).

[Projection-valued measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure) have arrived. Bounded Borel functions now form a commutative C⋆-algebra, and [integrating them against a PVM](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Integral.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.integral) is a unital ⋆-homomorphism, with a [dominated convergence theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/DominatedConvergence.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.tendsto_integral_apply_filter_of_dominated_convergence), null sets, pushforward and polarization. No operator has a PVM attached to it yet. On the unbounded side there are now direct sums of closed operators, a domain-aware Sylvester equation and Borel measurability of the real spectrum.

Operator ideals gained their concrete families. The [trace class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/TraceClass.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.traceClass) and [Hilbert–Schmidt](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/HilbertSchmidt.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.hilbertSchmidt) families are the Schatten classes at `p = 1` and `p = 2`, and the operator norm is the endpoint at `∞`. The Schatten classes [increase with `p`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/Schatten.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.finiteSubmodule_schatten_le_of_le), every symmetric-gauge family is Ky Fan dominant, and [approximation numbers are invariant under the modulus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/Modulus.html#ContinuousLinearMap.singularValue_modulus). Toeplitz operators began too. Their [norm is the `L∞` norm of the symbol](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Toeplitz.html#TauCeti.norm_toeplitz), Hankel operators with continuous symbols are compact, and so are the [Toeplitz semicommutators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Compact.html#TauCeti.isCompactOperator_toeplitz_comp_sub_toeplitz_toLp) `T_a T_b − T_{ab}` when `b` is continuous.

## OperatorTheory: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-09 (`271e1af` to `7ca783d`)

The headline is the spectral theorem for bounded normal operators. A normal operator `a` now has a spectral measure, a projection-valued measure on its spectrum whose integral is a Borel functional calculus, and [integrating the coordinate function recovers `a`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/BorelCalculus/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.borelCalculus_restrict_id), so `a = ∫ z dP(z)` (TauCeti#28859). The measure is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/BorelCalculus/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.eq_spectralPVM_iff) and has full support on the spectrum, and an operator [commutes with every Borel function of `a`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/BorelCalculus/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.forall_commute_borelCalculus_iff) exactly when it commutes with `a`. It comes out of a general construction: every unital ⋆-representation of the continuous functions on a compact space gets a projection-valued measure, [unique among those with regular diagonal measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Representation/OfRepresentation.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.eq_ofRepresentation_iff), resting on new work on integration, regularity and uniqueness for scalar measures. The spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator is still missing, but its Yosida approximants are in: they are self-adjoint, commute, and [converge to the operator on its domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Yosida.html#IsSelfAdjoint.tendsto_symmetricYosidaApproximation_apply).

In perturbation theory, the sharp Sylvester bound from the last window now gives the [pairwise `π/2` Davis–Kahan `sin Θ` theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/Pairwise.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sub), and the [Davis–Kahan `sin 2Θ` theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/DoubleAngle.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_sinTwoAngleOperator_le_two_mul_map_sub) arrived with it, both in finite dimension. The Yu–Wang–Samworth results gained their [Frobenius `sin Θ` form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/YuWangSamworth/SinTheta.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.sinThetaFrobenius_eigenvectorSpan_le) and the left-singular-vector bound. Rosenblum's theorem is still open. Separately, the [spectral theorem for compact normal operators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Spectral/CompactNormal.html#IsStarNormal.orthogonalComplement_iSup_eigenspaces_eq_bot) landed: their eigenspaces span a dense subspace.

Toeplitz operators gained the [Brown–Halmos theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/BrownHalmos.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_toeplitz_eq_iff_adjoint_comp_comp_eq), which says `T` is a Toeplitz operator exactly when `S†TS = T` for the shift `S`, and the [Fredholm index `−k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Index.html#TauCeti.index_toeplitz_fourierLp) of the Toeplitz operator of `e_k`. Alongside these came the Szegő kernel, Hankel factorizations of `T_aT_b − T_{ab}`, and the finite-section method. That method [converges when the sections are stable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HilbertBasis/FiniteSection/Stability.html#ContinuousLinearMap.tendsto_finiteSection_solution), and a unitary example shows that an invertible operator need not have stable sections.

## OperatorTheory: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`7ca783d` to `a6027b1`)

The headline is the commutant of the shift on Hardy space. The boundary symbols with no negative Fourier coefficients form [boundary H∞](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.hardyMultiplierAlgebra), a closed subalgebra of `L∞` that [acts isometrically on Hardy space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.isometry_hardyMultiplierHom) by multiplication, and [every bounded operator commuting with the forward shift is multiplication by a unique such symbol](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_toeplitz_eq_iff_commute) (TauCeti#30505). A Toeplitz operator therefore [commutes with the shift exactly when its symbol is analytic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.commute_toeplitz_fourierLp_one_iff). This is the H∞ multiplier comparison the Toeplitz roadmap asks for alongside its Hankel product identities. It is stated for boundary symbols only and does not identify them with bounded holomorphic functions on the disk.

The rest of the window was supporting work. The finite Ritz-residual `tan Θ` proof now rests on [Galerkin orthogonality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/RayleighRitz.html#LinearMap.inner_map_ritzResidual_eq_zero) and on reading [principal tangents as sine over cosine](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/Acute.html#Submodule.principalTangents_range_apply_eq_div_singularValues). Unitary conjugation of a densely defined operator [keeps its domain dense](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Constructions.html#LinearPMap.dense_unitaryConj_domain). Most other pull requests revisited results that were already in place, among them reduced parts of self-adjoint operators, domain-aware intertwining, Ky Fan's trace inequality, partial isometries and a scalar Radon–Nikodym theorem. The spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator and Rosenblum's theorem are still open.

## OperatorTheory: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`a6027b1` to `aa6e43e`)

The headline is the Gohberg–Krein theorem: the Toeplitz operator of a continuous symbol on the circle is [Fredholm exactly when the symbol never vanishes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Fredholm.html#TauCeti.isFredholm_toeplitz_toLp_iff), and its [index is minus the winding number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Fredholm.html#TauCeti.index_toeplitz_toLp) of the symbol about `0` (TauCeti#28098). Both statements survive compact perturbations. The [winding number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Winding.html#ContinuousMap.windingNumber) is the circle degree of the normalized symbol. It [agrees with the contour winding number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Winding.html#ContinuousMap.coe_windingNumber_eq_contour_windingNumber_unitAddCircle) for piecewise `C¹` symbols, and it [classifies nowhere-zero symbols up to homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Winding.html#ContinuousMap.windingNumber_eq_iff_exists_homotopy_fourier). It is also stable under small perturbations, in Rouché form. The invertibility criterion for continuous symbols (nowhere zero with winding number zero) is not among the declarations visible here, though that pull request's declaration list was truncated. Matrix symbols have not started.

Rosenblum's theorem also landed: a bounded operator that [intertwines two self-adjoint operators with disjoint spectra is zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Rosenblum.html#LinearPMap.Intertwines.eq_zero_of_disjoint_spectrum), stated for unbounded operators and [specialized to bounded ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Rosenblum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.eq_zero_of_comp_eq_comp_of_disjoint_spectrum). So the domain-aware Sylvester equation has at most one bounded solution. The [dilation pairwise-gap bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HermitianDilation/SinTheta.html#LinearMap.mul_norm_sinThetaMap_pointSpectralSubspace_hermitianDilation_le_pi_div_two_mul_norm_sub) applies the pairwise `π/2` `sin Θ` theorem to Hermitian dilations, which completes the statistical part of spectral-subspace perturbation. What is still missing there is the domain-aware `π/2` Sylvester bound.

The rest was supporting work. It includes [bijectivity of the finite Sylvester operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Sylvester.html#ContinuousLinearMap.sylvester_bijective), [spectral mapping for the Cayley transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/LinearPMap/Cayley.html#LinearPMap.mem_spectrum_cayley_iff) of a partial operator, and Hilbert–Schmidt results that now allow targets that are not complete. Other pull requests revisited relative boundedness, graph cores and the real-scalar functional calculus. The spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator is still open.

## OperatorTheory: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`aa6e43e` to `b235000`)

The headline is Toeplitz operators with matrix symbols. The block Hardy space is `r` copies of H², and a symbol is now an essentially bounded function with values in `r × r` matrices. Its [block Toeplitz operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Block/Toeplitz.html#TauCeti.blockToeplitz) has [norm equal to the symbol's `L∞` norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Block/Toeplitz.html#TauCeti.norm_blockToeplitz), and the [block Brown–Halmos theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Block/Toeplitz.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_blockToeplitz_eq_iff_adjoint_comp_comp_eq) says an operator is block Toeplitz exactly when `S†TS = T` for the block shift (TauCeti#27851). Block Hankel operators came with them. For continuous symbols they are [compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Block/Hankel.html#TauCeti.isCompactOperator_blockHankel_toLp), and so are the [semicommutators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Block/Hankel.html#TauCeti.isCompactOperator_blockToeplitz_comp_sub_blockToeplitz_toLp) `T_A T_B − T_{AB}`. The Fredholm theory of matrix symbols, with its index as the winding of the determinant, has not started. The [Fejér kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Fejer.html#TauCeti.fejerKernel) also arrived, but Fejér means have not.

Some earlier work is recorded here late. It includes [Coburn's alternative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Coburn.html#TauCeti.ker_toeplitz_eq_bot_or_ker_adjoint_toeplitz_eq_bot): for a nonzero `L∞` symbol, either `T_a` or its adjoint is injective, so a Toeplitz operator that is [Fredholm of index zero is invertible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Coburn.html#TauCeti.isInvertible_toeplitz_of_isFredholm_of_index_eq_zero) (TauCeti#26328). The finite-section groundwork arrived with it: compressions, extensions by zero, and [reflected sections converging strongly](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/FiniteSection.html#TauCeti.tendsto_finiteReflection_zeroExtendedFiniteSection_toeplitz) to the Toeplitz operator of the reflected symbol. Also among the late work is a [Ritz-residual `tan Θ` bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/RitzTangent.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_tanThetaMap_le_map_ritzResidual) in every unitarily invariant seminorm.

Elsewhere, the [closed-unit-ball form of the min–max formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/MinMax.html#ContinuousLinearMap.singularValue_eq_iInf_sSup_image_orthogonal_inter_unitClosedBall) for approximation numbers completes the first part of operator ideals. Von Neumann's theorem that the adjoint of a closable operator is [densely defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/SubmoduleAdjoint.html#LinearPMap.dense_adjoint_domain) also landed, along with [spectral permanence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ContinuousFunctionalCalculus/Subalgebra.html#StarAlgHom.spectrum_apply_eq_of_injective_of_isClosed_range) along injective ⋆-homomorphisms with closed range. The spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator is still open.

## OperatorTheory: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`b235000` to `afdb531`)

The [spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#LinearPMap.spectralPVM) has landed (TauCeti#41596). It is built by moving the Cayley transform's spectral measure to the real line, once the point `1` is shown to [carry no mass](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#LinearPMap.cayley_spectralPVM_proj_singleton_one). It satisfies the [resolvent formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#IsSelfAdjoint.inner_resolvent_apply_eq_integral) `⟪ξ, R(z)ξ⟫ = ∫ (z − s)⁻¹ dμ_ξ(s)`, and it is [the only projection-valued measure that does](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#IsSelfAdjoint.eq_spectralPVM_iff). For a bounded operator it agrees with the bounded spectral measure. Spectral subspaces reduce the operator, and its [restriction to each is self-adjoint](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#IsSelfAdjoint.isSelfAdjoint_restrict_range_spectralPVM_proj). Further results give gap resolvents, form bounds from spectral support, and bounded [spectral-gap inverses](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Bounded.html#LinearPMap.spectralGapInverse).

The [unbounded spectral integral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/SpectralIntegral.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.spectralIntegral) also arrived. For any measurable symbol it gives a closed, densely defined operator, [determined by its domain and matrix elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/SpectralIntegral.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.existsUnique_spectralIntegral), and that operator is self-adjoint exactly when the symbol is almost everywhere real. [Maximal multiplication operators on `L²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/MaximalMultiplication/Basic.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.existsUnique_maximal_multiplier) arrived with their adjoints and resolvent sets. On symmetric operators, the resolvent set is shown to take [one of four forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/SymmetricSpectrum.html#LinearPMap.IsFormalAdjoint.resolventSet_alternatives), and such an operator is [self-adjoint exactly when its spectrum is real](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/SymmetricSpectrum.html#LinearPMap.IsFormalAdjoint.isSelfAdjoint_iff_spectrum_subset). Smaller additions include [unitary conjugation of partial maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/LinearPMap/Conj.html#LinearPMap.conj), [domain-aware Sylvester equations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Module/LinearPMap/Sylvester.html#LinearPMap.SylvesterEquation), square-root estimates, and the fact that complexification preserves the projection gap.

Two things are still missing. The unitary group generated by a self-adjoint operator, and with it Stone uniqueness, has not landed; only the Yosida approximants are in place. The spectral integral has also not yet been tied back to the spectral measure, so `A = ∫ s dP_A(s)` is not proved.
