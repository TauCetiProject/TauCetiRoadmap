## OperatorTheory: 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-19 (`80e37ff` to `a5cde7b`)

This first report covers the whole build-out of the operator-theory family, and its headline is the [polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Polar/Bounded.html#ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus) `T = U|T|` for bounded operators between Hilbert spaces, with no dimension restriction (TauCeti#16936). The polar factor comes with its kernel, its range, its initial and final projections and a uniqueness statement, and it sits on top of the [operator modulus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Modulus.html#ContinuousLinearMap.modulus). The finite-dimensional groundwork landed alongside it: the [Courant–Fischer min–max theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/CourantFischer.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.eigenvalues_eq_iSup_iInf_re_inner), Weyl's inequality, singular systems and the Moore–Penrose inverse.

The spectral inequalities followed in finite dimension. They include the [Ky Fan triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/KyFan.html#LinearMap.kyFanSum_add_le), and [Fan dominance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/FanDominance.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.map_le_of_kyFanSum_le), which says that Ky Fan sums control every unitarily invariant norm, for rectangular maps as well as square ones. Also here are the forward Schur–Horn inequality, the [Hoffman–Wielandt inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HoffmanWielandt.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.sum_sq_eigenvalues_sub_le_sum_sq_norm_apply) and Davis's eigenvalue-change bound. These feed the [Davis–Kahan `sin Θ` theorem in unitarily invariant norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/Basic.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_map_sub) under an interval–exterior gap, and a [dimension-free operator-norm version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/SinTheta.html#Submodule.mul_projectionGap_le_norm_sub) for the projector gap. The [Haagerup–Zsidó kernel has total mass π/2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/HaagerupZsido/Mass.html#TauCeti.HaagerupZsido.integral_norm_reciprocalKernel), but the pairwise `π/2` Sylvester bound that mass is for, and Rosenblum's theorem, have not landed.

In infinite dimension, [approximation numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.singularValue) now characterise compactness, satisfy a [Ky Fan triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/KyFan.html#ContinuousLinearMap.kyFanGauge_add_le) on complete sources, and give Hilbert–Schmidt operators their model as `ℓ²` of columns. For unbounded operators, the [Kato–Rellich theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/KatoRellich.html#IsSelfAdjoint.add_of_isRelativelyBounded) and a [unitary Cayley transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Cayley.html#IsSelfAdjoint.cayley_mem_unitary) are in. Projection-valued measures are not, so there is no Borel calculus yet and no spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator.

## OperatorTheory: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-24 (`a5cde7b` to `271e1af`)

The headline is the sharp [`π/2` Sylvester bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/Sylvester/Pairwise.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sylvester_apply) of Bhatia, Davis and McIntosh. If two symmetric operators on finite-dimensional spaces have spectra at least `δ` apart, every solution of `AX − XB = C` satisfies `δ·N(X) ≤ (π/2)·N(C)` in every unitarily invariant norm (TauCeti#18808). This is the theorem the Haagerup–Zsidó kernel was built for. It is not yet applied to give the pairwise `π/2` `sin Θ` theorem, and Rosenblum's theorem is still open. The Davis–Kahan family grew around it: an [equal-rank spectral-projector form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/SpectralPerturbation.html#Module.End.mul_norm_sub_starProjection_pointSpectralSubspace_le), `sin Θ` bounds for [singular subspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SingularSubspace/Perturbation.html#LinearMap.mul_norm_sinThetaMap_rightSingularSubspace_le), and the first statistical pieces, with corresponding eigenblocks, Hermitian dilations and a [Yu–Wang–Samworth bound for right singular vectors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/YuWangSamworth/SingularVectors.html#LinearMap.sq_mul_sum_sum_norm_inner_rightSingularBasis_sq_le_four_mul_card_mul_sq).

[Projection-valued measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure) have arrived. Bounded Borel functions now form a commutative C⋆-algebra, and [integrating them against a PVM](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Integral.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.integral) is a unital ⋆-homomorphism, with a [dominated convergence theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/DominatedConvergence.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.tendsto_integral_apply_filter_of_dominated_convergence), null sets, pushforward and polarization. No operator has a PVM attached to it yet. On the unbounded side there are now direct sums of closed operators, a domain-aware Sylvester equation and Borel measurability of the real spectrum.

Operator ideals gained their concrete families. The [trace class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/TraceClass.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.traceClass) and [Hilbert–Schmidt](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/HilbertSchmidt.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.hilbertSchmidt) families are the Schatten classes at `p = 1` and `p = 2`, and the operator norm is the endpoint at `∞`. The Schatten classes [increase with `p`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/Schatten.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.finiteSubmodule_schatten_le_of_le), every symmetric-gauge family is Ky Fan dominant, and [approximation numbers are invariant under the modulus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/Modulus.html#ContinuousLinearMap.singularValue_modulus). Toeplitz operators began too. Their [norm is the `L∞` norm of the symbol](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Toeplitz.html#TauCeti.norm_toeplitz), Hankel operators with continuous symbols are compact, and so are the [Toeplitz semicommutators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Compact.html#TauCeti.isCompactOperator_toeplitz_comp_sub_toeplitz_toLp) `T_a T_b − T_{ab}` when `b` is continuous.
