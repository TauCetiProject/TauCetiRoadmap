## OperatorTheory: 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-19 (`80e37ff` to `a5cde7b`)

This first report covers the whole build-out of the operator-theory family, and its headline is the [polar decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Polar/Bounded.html#ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus) `T = U|T|` for bounded operators between Hilbert spaces, with no dimension restriction (TauCeti#16936). The polar factor comes with its kernel, its range, its initial and final projections and a uniqueness statement, and it sits on top of the [operator modulus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Modulus.html#ContinuousLinearMap.modulus). The finite-dimensional groundwork landed alongside it: the [Courant–Fischer min–max theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/CourantFischer.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.eigenvalues_eq_iSup_iInf_re_inner), Weyl's inequality, singular systems and the Moore–Penrose inverse.

The spectral inequalities followed in finite dimension. They include the [Ky Fan triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/KyFan.html#LinearMap.kyFanSum_add_le), and [Fan dominance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/FanDominance.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.map_le_of_kyFanSum_le), which says that Ky Fan sums control every unitarily invariant norm, for rectangular maps as well as square ones. Also here are the forward Schur–Horn inequality, the [Hoffman–Wielandt inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HoffmanWielandt.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.sum_sq_eigenvalues_sub_le_sum_sq_norm_apply) and Davis's eigenvalue-change bound. These feed the [Davis–Kahan `sin Θ` theorem in unitarily invariant norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/Basic.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_map_sub) under an interval–exterior gap, and a [dimension-free operator-norm version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/SinTheta.html#Submodule.mul_projectionGap_le_norm_sub) for the projector gap. The [Haagerup–Zsidó kernel has total mass π/2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/HaagerupZsido/Mass.html#TauCeti.HaagerupZsido.integral_norm_reciprocalKernel), but the pairwise `π/2` Sylvester bound that mass is for, and Rosenblum's theorem, have not landed.

In infinite dimension, [approximation numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.singularValue) now characterise compactness, satisfy a [Ky Fan triangle inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/ApproximationNumber/KyFan.html#ContinuousLinearMap.kyFanGauge_add_le) on complete sources, and give Hilbert–Schmidt operators their model as `ℓ²` of columns. For unbounded operators, the [Kato–Rellich theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/KatoRellich.html#IsSelfAdjoint.add_of_isRelativelyBounded) and a [unitary Cayley transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Cayley.html#IsSelfAdjoint.cayley_mem_unitary) are in. Projection-valued measures are not, so there is no Borel calculus yet and no spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator.

## OperatorTheory: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-23 (`a5cde7b` to `bfcff79`)

The headline is the [pairwise `π/2` Sylvester bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/Sylvester/Pairwise.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sylvester_apply) (TauCeti#18808). Take symmetric operators `A` and `B` in finite dimension whose eigenvalues are pairwise at least `δ` apart. Then every solution of `AX − XB = C` satisfies `δ N(X) ≤ (π/2) N(C)` in every unitarily invariant norm `N`. This is what the Haagerup–Zsidó kernel was built for. The bound also comes in a [simultaneous Ky Fan form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/Sylvester/Pairwise.html#ContinuousLinearMap.mul_kyFanSum_le_pi_div_two_mul_kyFanSum_sylvester_apply) and a [unitary-orbit form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/Sylvester/Pairwise.html#ContinuousLinearMap.smul_mem_twoSidedUnitaryOrbitHull_sylvester_apply). The Davis–Kahan family grew in three directions: a [canonical spectral-subspace residual bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/Spectral.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_orthogonal_pointSpectralSubspace_comp_le_map_sylvester_apply), an [equal-rank spectral-projector bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/SpectralPerturbation.html#Module.End.mul_norm_sub_starProjection_pointSpectralSubspace_le), and [`sin Θ` bounds for singular subspaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SingularSubspace/Perturbation.html#LinearMap.mul_norm_sinThetaMap_rightSingularSubspace_le). The pairwise `π/2` `sin Θ` theorem that the new bound feeds is not stated yet. Work on the Yu–Wang–Samworth variant began with three definitions: [corresponding eigenblocks](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Spectral/Eigenframe.html#LinearMap.IsSymmetric.CorrespondingEigenblocks), the [Frobenius sine](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/FrobeniusSine.html#Submodule.sinThetaFrobenius) and the [Hermitian dilation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HermitianDilation/Basic.html#LinearMap.hermitianDilation).

Operator ideals now have the standard families. The [trace-class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/TraceClass.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.traceClass) and [Hilbert–Schmidt](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/HilbertSchmidt.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.hilbertSchmidt) ideals are the Schatten classes at `p = 1` and `p = 2`, and the operator norm is the class at `p = ∞`. Also in are the [inclusions `S_p ⊆ S_q`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/Schatten.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.finiteSubmodule_schatten_le_of_le) for `p ≤ q`, [Ky Fan dominance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/KyFan/Dominance.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.IsKyFanDominant) for every symmetric-gauge ideal, and a [block-sum formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/BlockSum.html#TauCeti.SymmetricGauge.opGauge_withLpProdMap) for gauges.

Self-adjoint spectral theory got [projection-valued measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure) (TauCeti#17527), with pushforwards, null sets and polarized complex measures. Bounded Borel functions now form a [commutative C*-algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/BoundedBorel.html#TauCeti.BoundedBorelFunction.instCommCStarAlgebra) in which [simple functions are dense](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/BoundedBorel.html#TauCeti.BoundedBorelFunction.denseRange_ofSimpleFunc). There is no integral against a PVM yet, and so no Borel functional calculus. For unbounded operators, the [real spectrum is Borel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Resolvent/RealSpectrum.html#LinearPMap.measurableSet_preimage_ofReal_spectrum). The [domain-aware Sylvester equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Sylvester.html#LinearPMap.SylvesterEquation) and [spectral separation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/SpectralSeparation.html#LinearPMap.SpectralSetsSeparated) that Rosenblum's theorem needs are now defined. On the Hardy space, [Hankel operators with continuous symbol](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Compact.html#TauCeti.isCompactOperator_hankel_toLp) and [Toeplitz semicommutators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Compact.html#TauCeti.isCompactOperator_toeplitz_comp_sub_toeplitz_toLp) are now proved compact.
