### Named results

- **The spectral theorem for bounded normal operators**: a normal operator is the integral `a = ∫ z dP(z)` of the coordinate function against a unique projection-valued measure on its spectrum. See [`ContinuousLinearMap.borelCalculus_restrict_id`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/BorelCalculus/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.borelCalculus_restrict_id).
- **Polar decomposition for bounded operators**: every bounded operator between complete Hilbert spaces factors as `T = U|T|`, with `U` the unique partial isometry vanishing on `ker T`, in any dimension. See [`ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Polar/Bounded.html#ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus).
- **The pairwise `π/2` Davis–Kahan `sin Θ` theorem**: if selected spectra of symmetric `A` and `B` are `δ` apart, then `δ·N(sin Θ) ≤ (π/2)·N(B − A)` in every unitarily invariant norm, in finite dimension. See [`TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/Pairwise.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sub).
- **The Brown–Halmos theorem**: a bounded operator `T` on Hardy space is a Toeplitz operator, with a unique bounded symbol, exactly when `S†TS = T` for the forward shift `S`. See [`TauCeti.existsUnique_toeplitz_eq_iff_adjoint_comp_comp_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/BrownHalmos.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_toeplitz_eq_iff_adjoint_comp_comp_eq).
- **The commutant of the shift**: every bounded operator on Hardy space commuting with the forward shift is multiplication by a unique boundary H∞ symbol, and that representation is isometric. See [`TauCeti.existsUnique_toeplitz_eq_iff_commute`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Multiplier.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_toeplitz_eq_iff_commute).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Projection-valued measures of representations**: every unital ⋆-representation of the continuous functions on a compact space has a projection-valued measure, [unique among those with regular diagonal measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Representation/OfRepresentation.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.eq_ofRepresentation_iff). The bounded spectral theorem is its special case, and it is the intended route to the unbounded one.
- **Yosida approximants of a self-adjoint operator**: bounded, self-adjoint and commuting, they [converge to the operator on its domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Yosida.html#IsSelfAdjoint.tendsto_symmetricYosidaApproximation_apply) and are the route to the generated unitary group.
- **Schatten classes as operator-ideal families**: the [trace class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/TraceClass.html#TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.traceClass) and Hilbert–Schmidt families sit at `p = 1, 2` on top of approximation numbers, and a symmetric gauge is recovered from the family it induces.

## The frontier

- **The Fredholm theory of continuous Toeplitz symbols**: the criterion that `T_a` is Fredholm exactly when `a` has no zeros, and the index as minus the winding number. The monomial index, compact semicommutators and circle degree are in place, and invertibility then follows from Coburn's alternative.
- **The dilation pairwise-gap bound**: apply the pairwise `π/2` `sin Θ` theorem to Hermitian dilations. Both ingredients exist.
- **The spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator**: transport the Cayley transform's spectral measure to `ℝ`, which needs the Cayley singular point to carry no mass, then derive the resolvent formula. Self-adjoint Part E and most of the unbounded-operators roadmap wait on this.
- **Rosenblum's theorem**: the domain-aware Sylvester equation, coercive invertibility and domain-aware intertwiners exist. What remains is the domain-aware `π/2` bound and the uniqueness argument for intertwiners.
- **Unbounded operators without the spectral measure**: the resolvent-set alternatives for closed symmetric operators have every cited ingredient in place, and the maximal multiplication operator needs only `Lp` theory.
