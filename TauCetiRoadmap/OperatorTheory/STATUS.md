### Named results

- **The spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator** — a projection-valued measure on `ℝ`, obtained from the Cayley transform, that satisfies the resolvent formula. It is the only one that does, and it agrees with the bounded spectral measure for bounded operators. See [`LinearPMap.spectralPVM`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#LinearPMap.spectralPVM) and [`IsSelfAdjoint.inner_resolvent_apply_eq_integral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Basic.html#IsSelfAdjoint.inner_resolvent_apply_eq_integral).
- **The spectral theorem for bounded normal operators** — a normal operator is `a = ∫ z dP(z)` for a unique projection-valued measure on its spectrum. See [`ContinuousLinearMap.borelCalculus_restrict_id`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/BorelCalculus/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.borelCalculus_restrict_id).
- **Polar decomposition for bounded operators** — every bounded operator between complete Hilbert spaces factors as `T = U|T|`, where `U` is the unique partial isometry that vanishes on `ker T`. See [`ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Polar/Bounded.html#ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus).
- **The Gohberg–Krein theory of continuous Toeplitz operators** — `T_a` is Fredholm exactly when the symbol `a` never vanishes, and its index is minus the winding number. See [`TauCeti.index_toeplitz_toLp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Fredholm.html#TauCeti.index_toeplitz_toLp).
- **The pairwise `π/2` Davis–Kahan `sin Θ` theorem** — in finite dimension, if selected spectra are `δ` apart, then `δ·N(sin Θ) ≤ (π/2)·N(B − A)` in every unitarily invariant norm. See [`TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/Pairwise.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sub).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The unbounded spectral integral** — [`∫ f dP` for any measurable symbol](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/SpectralIntegral.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.spectralIntegral). It is closed and densely defined, its adjoint is the integral of the conjugate symbol, and it is self-adjoint exactly when the symbol is almost everywhere real.
- **Maximal multiplication operators** — [multiplication by a measurable function on `L²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/MaximalMultiplication/Basic.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.maximalMultiplication), on its largest domain. The resolvent set is computed, and the operator is self-adjoint for real symbols.
- **Block Toeplitz operators** — [compressions of matrix-valued `L∞` multipliers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Block/Toeplitz.html#TauCeti.blockToeplitz) to copies of H².
