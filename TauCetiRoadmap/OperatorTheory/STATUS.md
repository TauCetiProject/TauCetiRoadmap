### Named results

- **The spectral theorem for bounded normal operators**: a normal operator is the integral `a = ∫ z dP(z)` of the coordinate function against a unique projection-valued measure on its spectrum. See [`ContinuousLinearMap.borelCalculus_restrict_id`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/BorelCalculus/Basic.html#ContinuousLinearMap.borelCalculus_restrict_id).
- **Polar decomposition for bounded operators**: every bounded operator between complete Hilbert spaces factors as `T = U|T|`, with `U` the unique partial isometry vanishing on `ker T`, in any dimension. See [`ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Polar/Bounded.html#ContinuousLinearMap.polarFactor_comp_modulus).
- **The Gohberg–Krein theorem**: the Toeplitz operator of a continuous symbol on the circle is Fredholm exactly when the symbol never vanishes, and then its index is minus the symbol's winding number. See [`TauCeti.isFredholm_toeplitz_toLp_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Fredholm.html#TauCeti.isFredholm_toeplitz_toLp_iff) and [`TauCeti.index_toeplitz_toLp`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Hardy/Fredholm.html#TauCeti.index_toeplitz_toLp).
- **Rosenblum's theorem**: a bounded operator intertwining two self-adjoint operators, bounded or not, with disjoint spectra is zero, so the Sylvester equation has at most one bounded solution. See [`LinearPMap.Intertwines.eq_zero_of_disjoint_spectrum`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Rosenblum.html#LinearPMap.Intertwines.eq_zero_of_disjoint_spectrum).
- **The pairwise `π/2` Davis–Kahan `sin Θ` theorem**: if selected spectra of symmetric `A` and `B` are `δ` apart, then `δ·N(sin Θ) ≤ (π/2)·N(B − A)` in every unitarily invariant norm, in finite dimension. See [`TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sub`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/SinTheta/Pairwise.html#TauCeti.UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.mul_map_starProjection_comp_starProjection_le_pi_div_two_mul_map_sub).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The winding number of a nowhere-zero circle function**: [defined](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Winding.html#ContinuousMap.windingNumber) as the circle degree of the normalized symbol, it agrees with the contour winding number for piecewise `C¹` symbols and classifies symbols up to homotopy. The Toeplitz index is measured against it.
- **Projection-valued measures of representations**: every unital ⋆-representation of the continuous functions on a compact space has a projection-valued measure, [unique among those with regular diagonal measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Representation/OfRepresentation.html#TauCeti.ProjValMeasure.eq_ofRepresentation_iff). The bounded spectral theorem is its special case.
- **Yosida approximants of a self-adjoint operator**: bounded, self-adjoint and commuting, they [converge to the operator on its domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/Yosida.html#IsSelfAdjoint.tendsto_symmetricYosidaApproximation_apply) and are the route to the generated unitary group.

## The frontier

- **Invertibility of continuous Toeplitz operators**: `T_a` is invertible exactly when `a` is nowhere zero with winding number zero. Coburn's alternative and the Gohberg–Krein index are both in place.
- **The domain-aware `π/2` Sylvester bound**: the last item of spectral-subspace perturbation. The finite pairwise bound, Rosenblum uniqueness and the domain-aware Sylvester equation all exist.
- **The spectral measure of an unbounded self-adjoint operator**: transport the Cayley transform's spectral measure to `ℝ`. [Spectral mapping for the Cayley transform](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/LinearPMap/Cayley.html#LinearPMap.mem_spectrum_cayley_iff) is now available; what remains is showing the singular point `1` carries no mass, then the resolvent formula. Self-adjoint Part E and most of the unbounded-operators roadmap wait on this.
- **Matrix Toeplitz symbols**: identify the block shift algebra with matrices over the scalar one, and prove the Fredholm criterion and the index as the winding of the determinant.
- **Unbounded operators without the spectral measure**: the resolvent-set alternatives for closed symmetric operators have every cited ingredient in place, and the maximal multiplication operator needs only `Lp` theory.
