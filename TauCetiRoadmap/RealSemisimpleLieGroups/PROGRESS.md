## RealSemisimpleLieGroups: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-25 (`2e2da0e` to `522d48f`)

The roadmap's Lie-algebra floor is now in place. A [Cartan involution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/Basic.html#LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution) is an involutive automorphism θ with `−B(X, θY)` positive definite. The library proves the Cartan decomposition 𝔤₀ = 𝔨 ⊕ 𝔭 with its bracket and Killing-form relations. It also proves that such a θ [forces semisimplicity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/Basic.html#LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.isSemisimple), that it is [determined by 𝔨](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/Basic.html#LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.eq_of_fixedSubalgebra_eq), and that 𝔨 is its centre plus a semisimple derived algebra. Knapp's criterion that `X ↦ −X*` is a Cartan involution of a conjugate-transpose-closed matrix algebra [exactly when its centre vanishes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/Matrix.html#LieSubalgebra.isCartanInvolution_restrictNegConjTranspose_iff) is applied to 𝔰𝔬(p, q), [𝔰𝔲(p, q)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/IndefiniteUnitary/Cartan.html#TauCeti.isCartanInvolution_restrictNegConjTranspose_specialIndefiniteUnitaryLieAlgebra), 𝔰𝔭(p, q) and the complex classical algebras viewed as real (TauCeti#45373). The Killing forms of 𝔰𝔩ₙ, 𝔰𝔬(p, q) and [𝔰𝔲(p, q)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/IndefiniteUnitary.html#TauCeti.killingForm_specialIndefiniteUnitaryLieAlgebra_apply) are computed along the way.

On the restricted-root side, maximal abelian subspaces of 𝔭 exist, and every element of 𝔭 lies in one. For any abelian 𝔞 ⊆ 𝔭, 𝔤₀ is the [direct sum of the restricted root spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/RestrictedRoot/Decomposition.html#LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.isInternal_restrictedRootSpace). This is proved directly, by simultaneously diagonalizing the self-adjoint operators ad H, with no Lie group involved. Each root λ has a [restricted coroot](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/RestrictedRoot/Sl2.html#LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.restrictedCoroot) and an attached 𝔰𝔩₂ triple. From these come [integrality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/RestrictedRoot/Sl2.html#LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.exists_int_apply_restrictedCoroot), meaning ν(H'_λ) ∈ ℤ, and the rule that [only ±½λ, ±λ and ±2λ can be roots](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/RestrictedRoot/Sl2.html#LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.mem_of_smul_mem_restrictedRoots). This is the input for the restricted root system, but those facts are not yet packaged as a `RootPairing`.

Still missing are the conjugacy of Cartan involutions, real rank, and everything at the group level.
