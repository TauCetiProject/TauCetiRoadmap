## HopfRinow: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30 (`8745177` to `e440f4e`)

**The Hopf–Rinow theorem** is proved ([`TauCeti.Manifold.tfae_expDomain_eq_univ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/HopfRinow.html#TauCeti.Manifold.tfae_expDomain_eq_univ)). On a finite-dimensional boundaryless Riemannian manifold whose metric is the Riemannian distance, five conditions are equivalent: `exp_p` is defined on all of `T_p M`, the manifold is proper, it is complete, every geodesic runs for all time, and it has do Carmo's compact exhaustion. Completeness at one point propagates to every point ([`TauCeti.Manifold.isGeodesicallyCompleteAt_iff_forall`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/HopfRinow.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isGeodesicallyCompleteAt_iff_forall)), and an everywhere-defined `exp_p` joins `p` to every point by a minimizing geodesic ([`TauCeti.Manifold.exists_isGeodesicCurveOn_Icc_pathELength_eq_edist`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Minimizing.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_isGeodesicCurveOn_Icc_pathELength_eq_edist)). Do Carmo's Corollary 2.9 follows: **a compact Riemannian manifold is geodesically complete** ([`TauCeti.Manifold.isGeodesicallyCompleteAt_of_compactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Compact.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isGeodesicallyCompleteAt_of_compactSpace)). Because the proof goes through the extended metric, it needs no connectedness.

The window built everything underneath. The geodesic spray is chart-independent and smooth. A smooth-dependence theorem for ODEs gives the smooth local geodesic flow ([`TauCeti.Manifold.exists_contMDiffAt_localGeodesicFlow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Flow.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_contMDiffAt_localGeodesicFlow)) and maximal geodesics. The exponential map is smooth on its open, star-shaped domain and is a local diffeomorphism at the origin. On normal domains the Riemannian logarithm inverts it. Next come **the Gauss lemma** ([`TauCeti.Manifold.inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_radial`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_radial)), radial minimization and the escape estimate. The last of these is rigidity: a minimizer inside a normal neighbourhood is the radial geodesic up to monotone reparametrization ([`TauCeti.Manifold.IsNormalDomain.exists_monotoneOn_eq_riemannianExp_smul_of_pathELength_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Rigidity.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsNormalDomain.exists_monotoneOn_eq_riemannianExp_smul_of_pathELength_eq)). Arc-length reparametrization and lower semicontinuity of length closed out the distance layer.

The first variation of energy landed ([`TauCeti.Manifold.hasDerivAt_energy_of_fixed_endpoints`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/FirstVariation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.hasDerivAt_energy_of_fixed_endpoints)), but only in one direction: geodesics are critical, while critical curves are not yet shown to be geodesics. Both worked examples are in: Euclidean space and the open unit ball, which is incomplete. Smooth Riemannian isometries ([`TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Basic.html#TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry)) preserve distance, the Levi-Civita connection and completeness. They are not yet shown to carry geodesics or exponential maps.

## HopfRinow: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02 (`e440f4e` to `d449639`)

The roadmap's two open items are closed. **The variational characterization of geodesics** ([`TauCeti.Manifold.isEnergyCritical_iff_isGeodesicCurveOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/FirstVariation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isEnergyCritical_iff_isGeodesicCurveOn)) says that on a boundaryless manifold, a `C²` curve is a critical point of energy among fixed-endpoint variations exactly when it is a geodesic on the open interval. It rests on a new definition of criticality ([`TauCeti.Manifold.IsEnergyCritical`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/FirstVariation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsEnergyCritical)) and on the missing converse, that critical curves are geodesics ([`TauCeti.Manifold.IsEnergyCritical.isGeodesicCurveOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/FirstVariation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsEnergyCritical.isGeodesicCurveOn)). Smooth Riemannian isometries now carry geodesics ([`TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.isGeodesicCurveOn_comp_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Geodesic.html#TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.isGeodesicCurveOn_comp_iff)), preserve maximal intervals, and intertwine exponential maps, `Φ ∘ exp_p = exp_{Φ p} ∘ dΦ_p` ([`TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.riemannianExp_mfderiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Exponential.html#TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.riemannianExp_mfderiv)). The proof runs through naturality of the covariant derivative along a curve. This is the input the constant-curvature model spaces were waiting for.

The rest of the window strengthened existing theory. The maximal geodesic flow is now smooth jointly in initial data and time on its open domain ([`TauCeti.Manifold.contMDiffOn_maximalGeodesic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Flow.html#TauCeti.Manifold.contMDiffOn_maximalGeodesic)), and so is `exp` as a map on the tangent bundle. Inside a normal neighbourhood, a geodesic segment no longer than the radial one is that segment ([`TauCeti.Manifold.IsNormalDomain.eqOn_riemannianExp_smul_of_geodesic_pathELength_le`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Manifold.IsNormalDomain.eqOn_riemannianExp_smul_of_geodesic_pathELength_le)), and the rigidity theorems were extended to piecewise-`C¹` competitors. Smaller additions: escape lemmas for maximal geodesics, constant speed on each component of a disconnected parameter set, and sharper worked examples, with Euclidean geodesics on any interval and the exact maximal intervals of the open unit ball.
