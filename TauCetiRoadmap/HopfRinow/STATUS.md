## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The Hopf–Rinow theorem, do Carmo's compactness corollary and the whole Riemannian theory beneath them are proved, so Layers 0, 2, 3 and 4 are done. Layer 1 is partial again, because the roadmap now also asks for a Levi-Civita connection for nondegenerate, possibly indefinite, metrics and for smooth families of them. Their uniqueness and coordinate formulas are in; existence, families and the variation formula are not.

### Named results

- **The Hopf–Rinow theorem** — fix a point `p` of a Riemannian manifold whose metric is the Riemannian distance. Then five conditions are equivalent: `exp_p` is defined on all of `T_p M`, the manifold is proper, it is complete, every geodesic runs for all time, and it has a compact exhaustion along which distance from `p` diverges ([`TauCeti.Manifold.tfae_expDomain_eq_univ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/HopfRinow.html#TauCeti.Manifold.tfae_expDomain_eq_univ)). An everywhere-defined `exp_p` also joins `p` to each point by a minimizing geodesic.
- **Compact manifolds are geodesically complete** — do Carmo's Corollary 2.9, proved through the extended metric, so with no connectedness hypothesis ([`TauCeti.Manifold.isGeodesicallyCompleteAt_of_compactSpace`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Compact.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isGeodesicallyCompleteAt_of_compactSpace)).
- **The variational characterization of geodesics** — on a boundaryless `C^n` manifold with `2 ≤ n ≤ ∞`, a `C^n` curve is critical for energy among `C^n` fixed-endpoint variations exactly when it is a geodesic on the open interval ([`TauCeti.Manifold.isEnergyCritical_iff_isGeodesicCurveOn`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/FirstVariation.html#TauCeti.Manifold.isEnergyCritical_iff_isGeodesicCurveOn)).
- **The Gauss lemma** — the differential of `exp_p` preserves inner products with the radial direction everywhere on its domain ([`TauCeti.Manifold.inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_radial`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Gauss/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.inner_mfderiv_riemannianExp_radial)). From it, a minimizer inside a normal neighbourhood is the radial geodesic up to monotone reparametrization.
- **The Christoffel index formula for nondegenerate metrics** — for any symmetric nondegenerate metric read in coordinates, `Γ^k_ij = ½ Σ_l g^(kl)(∂_i g_jl + ∂_j g_il − ∂_l g_ij)`, and the symbols are one derivative less smooth than the metric ([`TauCeti.Manifold.repr_christoffelSecondKind`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/LeviCivita/Christoffel.html#TauCeti.Manifold.repr_christoffelSecondKind)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The exponential map ([`TauCeti.Manifold.riemannianExp`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/Exponential.html#TauCeti.Manifold.riemannianExp)) is defined through maximal geodesics of the smooth geodesic spray. It is smooth on its open, star-shaped domain, and its local inverse supplies normal coordinates, whose Christoffel symbols vanish at the centre.
- Smooth nondegenerate metrics ([`TauCeti.Manifold.ContMDiffNondegenerateMetric`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/NondegenerateMetric/Basic.html#TauCeti.Manifold.ContMDiffNondegenerateMetric)) carry smooth musical isomorphisms and a signature that is constant on components. A connection is Levi-Civita for one exactly when it satisfies the Koszul formula ([`TauCeti.Manifold.ContMDiffNondegenerateMetric.isLeviCivitaConnection_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/LeviCivita/Nondegenerate.html#TauCeti.Manifold.ContMDiffNondegenerateMetric.isLeviCivitaConnection_iff)), so it is unique, and for a Riemannian metric this agrees with Mathlib's notion.
- Smooth Riemannian isometries ([`TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/Basic.html#TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry)) intertwine exponential maps and carry geodesics, maximal intervals, distance and completeness, which is the interface the constant-curvature model spaces need.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layers 0, 2, 3 and 4 are done, and so are both worked examples.** Layer 0 settles the distance convention by corner smoothing. Layer 2 has normal neighbourhoods and coordinates, the Gauss lemma, rigidity of minimizers and the variational characterization. Layer 3 has the full equivalence with `(a_p) ⇒ (f_p)` and base-point propagation. Layer 4 has the compactness corollary, the length-space and geodesic-space API, and isometry transport. **Layer 1 is done for Riemannian metrics but partial for the new nondegenerate supplier.** The geodesic spray, smooth flow, exponential map and `(a_p) ↔ (d_p)` are all proved. For nondegenerate metrics, the carrier, smooth musical maps, locally constant signature, compatibility predicate, Koszul characterization, uniqueness, positive-definite comparison and coordinate Christoffel formula are in. Missing are existence of the connection on a manifold, its restriction, naturality and along-curve API in this generality, the smooth parameter families over `P × M`, and the variation formula.

## The frontier

- **Existence of the nondegenerate Levi-Civita connection** — glue the coordinate Christoffel construction into a covariant derivative on the whole manifold, show local constructions agree on overlaps by nondegeneracy, and prove it is smooth.
- **Restriction, naturality and the along-curve API for nondegenerate metrics** — restate restriction to open sets, naturality under metric-preserving diffeomorphisms, and covariant differentiation along curves for the general connection. This waits on existence.
- **Smooth metric families** — realize a smooth family of nondegenerate metrics over a parameter manifold `P` as a section over `P × M`, and prove the connection coefficients are jointly smooth in the parameter and the point, with fixed signature on each component.
- **The variation formula for the connection** — for `h = ∂_t g_t`, prove `2g((∂_t∇)_X Y, Z) = (∇_X h)(Y, Z) + (∇_Y h)(X, Z) − (∇_Z h)(X, Y)`. The local variational field theory roadmap consumes this for Lorentzian metric variation.
- **Metric isometries** — the README deliberately leaves out the Myers–Steenrod theorem, that a distance-preserving bijection is a smooth Riemannian isometry. Transport for a bare metric isometry would need it as a separate milestone.
