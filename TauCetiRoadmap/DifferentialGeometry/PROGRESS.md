## DifferentialGeometry: 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-19 (`a3b8171` to `a5cde7b`)

Stokes' theorem is proved for manifolds with boundary: [`integralTopForm_mextDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Stokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv) says that `∫_M dω = ∫_{∂M} ω` for a compactly supported `C¹` form on an oriented smooth manifold modelled on the half-space (TauCeti#17353), with the [boundaryless case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/BoundarylessStokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv_eq_zero) and the interval case as corollaries. Under it sit the oriented integral of top forms, built from signed chart integrals, and the [outward-first boundary orientation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/InducedOrientation.html#TauCeti.boundaryOrientation_spec). Corners, densities and the homotopy operator are still to come.

Green's theorem landed in the strong form that HOL Light proves. [`green_ac_winding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Winding.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding) handles any closed absolutely continuous curve and a field that is `C¹` near it: the circulation equals the curl integrated against the winding number, and both integrability conditions are conclusions, not hypotheses. Its [index-one specialization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/IndexOne.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_index_one) gives the textbook statement over the bounded complementary region (TauCeti#17258). The proof goes through a [winding-weighted polygonal identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Polygonal/Green.html#TauCeti.Green.green_polygonal) and polygonal approximation. Alongside it is the Isabelle-style theorem for [finite type-I/type-II divisions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Division/Green.html#TauCeti.Green.Division.integrable_and_integral_add_integral_eq_integral_sub), with Henstock–Kurzweil boundary integrals, and worked examples such as the [ellipse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Examples/CurveArea/Green.html#TauCeti.Green.Examples.green_ellipse_area) and the [shoelace formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Examples/Shoelace.html#TauCeti.Green.Examples.integral_windingWeight_shoelace).

[De Rham cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Basic.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology) now exists. It is functorial, has a [wedge product on classes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Wedge.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.wedge_mk), is [one-dimensional in degree zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/DegreeZero.html#TauCeti.finrank_deRhamCohomology_zero) on a connected manifold, and [vanishes in positive degrees on a normed space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/NormedSpace.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_deRhamCohomology_normedSpace). A [compactly supported version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/CompactSupport.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology) is functorial for proper maps. Much of the rest of the window was groundwork: the wedge algebra, the manifold exterior derivative and its naturality, and Cartan's formula.

## DifferentialGeometry: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-24 (`a5cde7b` to `271e1af`)

Mayer–Vietoris has arrived for de Rham cohomology. For two open subsets of a Hausdorff σ-compact finite-dimensional manifold, [the Mayer–Vietoris sequence of de Rham complexes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact) is short exact (TauCeti#19561). [Its connecting map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.mayerVietorisδ) is [computed through a cut-off function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.mayerVietorisδ_mk_eq_mk_extendByZero), as in Lee's Corollary 17.42. The [compactly supported sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/CompactlySupportedMayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedMayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact), with its arrows reversed by [extension by zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/ExtendByZero.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology.extendByZero), is short exact as well. Still missing are the naturality of the connecting map and the acceptance computation of the cohomology of spheres.

[Homotopy invariance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/HomotopyInvariance.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.map_eq_of_homotopy) is proved: smoothly homotopic maps induce the same map on de Rham cohomology, with boundary and corners allowed. It rests on a [homotopy operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/HomotopyOperator.html#TauCeti.homotopyOperator) on `M × [0, 1]` and its [cochain-homotopy identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/HomotopyOperator.html#TauCeti.mextDeriv_homotopyOperator_add_homotopyOperator_mextDeriv), which is proved directly rather than through Stokes. [Line integrals of forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Path.html#TauCeti.pathIntegral) arrived along with their [fundamental theorem of calculus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Path.html#TauCeti.pathIntegral_mextDeriv), and they give the first nonzero class: the angular form has period `2π`, so it is [not exact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Circle.html#TauCeti.circleAngularClosedForm_notMem_exactForms) and [`H¹_dR(S¹)` is nontrivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Circle.html#TauCeti.nontrivial_deRhamCohomology_circle_one). The converse exactness criterion and `H¹ = 0` for simply connected manifolds are not yet here.

The [Poincaré pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/PoincarePairing.html#TauCeti.poincarePairing) `([φ], [ψ]) ↦ ∫_M φ ∧ ψ` is now defined on classes, and duality itself has not started. The rest of the window was supporting work. It typed curvature as an [endomorphism-valued two-form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Curvature.html#TauCeti.CurvatureForm), added associativity of the wedge, [pullback of orientations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Orientation/Pullback.html#TauCeti.Manifold.Orientation.pullback), and the [symmetry of the Hessian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Laplacian.html#TauCeti.Manifold.hessian_symm), and laid analytic groundwork for Green's theorem through arclength-parametrized curve integrals.

## DifferentialGeometry: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-08 (`271e1af` to `a4fcb57`)

The de Rham cohomology of spheres is computed: for `n ≥ 1`, [`H^k(Sⁿ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Sphere.html#TauCeti.finrank_deRhamCohomology_sphere) is a line in degrees `0` and `n` and vanishes in every other degree (TauCeti#24268). The computation runs Mayer–Vietoris over the two stereographic charts. The [naturality of the connecting map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.mayerVietorisδ_naturality) is also proved, which completes the Mayer–Vietoris layer. The rest of the de Rham layer is complete too. A closed 1-form on a connected manifold is [exact exactly when all its periods vanish](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/PathIntegral.html#TauCeti.mem_exactForms_one_iff_forall_pathIntegral_eq_zero), so [`H¹` vanishes on simply connected manifolds](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/SimplyConnected.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_deRhamCohomology_one_of_simplyConnectedSpace). On `ℝⁿ`, compactly supported cohomology is [ℝ in the top degree, by integration](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Euclidean.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomologyNormedSpaceEquiv), and [zero in every other degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Euclidean.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology_normedSpace). Cohomology is now also a [graded-commutative ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Algebra.html#TauCeti.deRhamAlgebra.gRing).

The de Rham theorem and Poincaré duality now have their first pieces. The [Mayer–Vietoris induction principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietoris_induction) is proved. So are [smooth singular simplices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/Basic.html#TauCeti.smoothSingularSubcomplex), [integration of forms over them](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/Integral.html#TauCeti.SmoothSimplex.integral), and [Stokes' theorem on the flat standard simplex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/DifferentialForm/Simplex.html#ContDiffOn.integral_extDerivWithin_fullSimplex). Integration on top compactly supported cohomology is [onto](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Integration/Surjective.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology.surjective_integral) for every nonempty oriented boundaryless manifold, though it is not yet shown to be injective on connected ones. The smoothing theorem, the de Rham map and duality itself are still to come.

The [mapping degree at a regular value](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Degree/Basic.html#TauCeti.degreeAtRegularValue) is defined, and [its integral formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Degree/Integral.html#TauCeti.exists_mem_nhds_integralTopForm_mpullback_eq_degreeAtRegularValue_mul) holds for forms supported near that value. Flows now [depend smoothly on initial conditions over Banach spaces](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/ODE/LocalFlow.html#ODE.exists_contDiffAt_localFlow), and the [maximal flow is jointly smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/IntegralCurve/Flow.html#TauCeti.contMDiffOn_maximalIntegralCurve) on complete models. The orientation double cover became a [smooth manifold](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Orientation/Cover/Smooth.html#TauCeti.Manifold.OrientationCover.instIsManifold) with [descent of forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Orientation/Cover/Descent.html#TauCeti.Manifold.OrientationCover.exists_mpullback_proj_eq_iff), and [manifolds with no index-two subgroup of `π₁` are orientable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Orientation/Cover/FundamentalGroup.html#TauCeti.Manifold.orientable_of_forall_index_ne_two). The remaining work was supporting: pointwise [densities](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Density/Basic.html#TauCeti.Density), the [Riemannian volume form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeForm.html#TauCeti.riemannianVolumeForm), and plaque charts for integral manifolds.

## DifferentialGeometry: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-11 (`a4fcb57` to `d9fbe7b`)

The top compactly supported cohomology of a manifold is computed in the orientable case: on a connected, oriented, boundaryless σ-compact manifold of dimension `n`, [integration is an isomorphism `H^n_c(M) ≅ ℝ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Integration/Bijective.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomologyIntegralEquiv) (TauCeti#29135). The proof writes every class as a sum of extensions by zero from Euclidean neighbourhoods and joins those neighbourhoods by chains. The nonorientable half, `H^n_c(M) = 0`, is not yet proved, but its inputs arrived. On the orientation double cover, [every class pulled back from `M` integrates to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/OrientationCover.html#TauCeti.Manifold.OrientationCover.integral_map_proj_eq_zero), and [primitives of pulled-back forms descend to `M`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Orientation/Cover/Descent.html#TauCeti.Manifold.OrientationCover.exists_mextDerivₗ_eq_of_mextDerivₗ_eq_mpullback_proj). The orientation layer also got its negative acceptance test: [the open Möbius band is not orientable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/MoebiusBand.html#TauCeti.MoebiusBand.not_orientable). This follows from a general criterion: [a mapping torus is orientable exactly when its diffeomorphism preserves orientation everywhere](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Instances/MappingTorus/Orientation.html#TauCeti.SmoothMappingTorus.orientable_iff_forall_isOrientationPreservingAt).

The Frobenius layer's integral manifolds and involutivity are now complete. A smooth distribution is involutive [exactly when `dα` vanishes on it for every annihilating one-form `α`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/Annihilator.html#TauCeti.isInvolutiveDistribution_iff_forall_mextDeriv), and [a distribution with integral manifolds through every point is involutive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/IntegralManifold/Involutive.html#TauCeti.IsContMDiffDistribution.isInvolutiveDistribution_of_forall_exists_integralManifold), which is the easy converse of Frobenius. In the other direction, [the local integral manifold of an involutive distribution](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/IntegralManifold/Existence.html#TauCeti.IsInvolutiveDistribution.exists_isIntegralManifold) now comes embedded and in slice position in a flat chart. Global leaves are still missing.

Elsewhere, Green's theorem now covers [finite integer-weighted families of closed absolutely continuous curves](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/FiniteSum.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding_sum). [The divergence theorem on the coordinate quadrant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/DivergenceTheorem/Quadrant.html#ContDiffOn.integral_divergence_coordinate_quadrant) is a first flat step towards Stokes with corners. Integral curves on manifolds [depend smoothly on their initial point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/IntegralCurve/SmoothFlow.html#TauCeti.Manifold.exists_contMDiffAt_localFlow). The rest of the window was chart-level regularity for forms and groundwork for the Morse–Sard theorem.
