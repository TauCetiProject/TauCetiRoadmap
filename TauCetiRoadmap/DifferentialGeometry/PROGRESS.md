## DifferentialGeometry: 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-19 (`a3b8171` to `a5cde7b`)

Stokes' theorem is proved for manifolds with boundary: [`integralTopForm_mextDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Stokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv) says that `∫_M dω = ∫_{∂M} ω` for a compactly supported `C¹` form on an oriented smooth manifold modelled on the half-space (TauCeti#17353), with the [boundaryless case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/BoundarylessStokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv_eq_zero) and the interval case as corollaries. Under it sit the oriented integral of top forms, built from signed chart integrals, and the [outward-first boundary orientation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/InducedOrientation.html#TauCeti.boundaryOrientation_spec). Corners, densities and the homotopy operator are still to come.

Green's theorem landed in the strong form that HOL Light proves. [`green_ac_winding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Winding.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding) handles any closed absolutely continuous curve and a field that is `C¹` near it: the circulation equals the curl integrated against the winding number, and both integrability conditions are conclusions, not hypotheses. Its [index-one specialization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/IndexOne.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_index_one) gives the textbook statement over the bounded complementary region (TauCeti#17258). The proof goes through a [winding-weighted polygonal identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Polygonal/Green.html#TauCeti.Green.green_polygonal) and polygonal approximation. Alongside it is the Isabelle-style theorem for [finite type-I/type-II divisions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Division/Green.html#TauCeti.Green.Division.integrable_and_integral_add_integral_eq_integral_sub), with Henstock–Kurzweil boundary integrals, and worked examples such as the [ellipse](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Examples/CurveArea/Green.html#TauCeti.Green.Examples.green_ellipse_area) and the [shoelace formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Examples/Shoelace.html#TauCeti.Green.Examples.integral_windingWeight_shoelace).

[De Rham cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Basic.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology) now exists. It is functorial, has a [wedge product on classes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Wedge.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.wedge_mk), is [one-dimensional in degree zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/DegreeZero.html#TauCeti.finrank_deRhamCohomology_zero) on a connected manifold, and [vanishes in positive degrees on a normed space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/NormedSpace.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_deRhamCohomology_normedSpace). A [compactly supported version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/CompactSupport.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology) is functorial for proper maps. Much of the rest of the window was groundwork: the wedge algebra, the manifold exterior derivative and its naturality, and Cartan's formula.

## DifferentialGeometry: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-24 (`a5cde7b` to `271e1af`)

Mayer–Vietoris has arrived for de Rham cohomology. For two open subsets of a Hausdorff σ-compact finite-dimensional manifold, [the Mayer–Vietoris sequence of de Rham complexes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact) is short exact (TauCeti#19561). [Its connecting map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.mayerVietorisδ) is [computed through a cut-off function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.mayerVietorisδ_mk_eq_mk_extendByZero), as in Lee's Corollary 17.42. The [compactly supported sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/CompactlySupportedMayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedMayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact), with its arrows reversed by [extension by zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/ExtendByZero.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology.extendByZero), is short exact as well. Still missing are the naturality of the connecting map and the acceptance computation of the cohomology of spheres.

[Homotopy invariance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/HomotopyInvariance.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.map_eq_of_homotopy) is proved: smoothly homotopic maps induce the same map on de Rham cohomology, with boundary and corners allowed. It rests on a [homotopy operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/HomotopyOperator.html#TauCeti.homotopyOperator) on `M × [0, 1]` and its [cochain-homotopy identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/HomotopyOperator.html#TauCeti.mextDeriv_homotopyOperator_add_homotopyOperator_mextDeriv), which is proved directly rather than through Stokes. [Line integrals of forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Path.html#TauCeti.pathIntegral) arrived along with their [fundamental theorem of calculus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Path.html#TauCeti.pathIntegral_mextDeriv), and they give the first nonzero class: the angular form has period `2π`, so it is [not exact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Circle.html#TauCeti.circleAngularClosedForm_notMem_exactForms) and [`H¹_dR(S¹)` is nontrivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Circle.html#TauCeti.nontrivial_deRhamCohomology_circle_one). The converse exactness criterion and `H¹ = 0` for simply connected manifolds are not yet here.

The [Poincaré pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/PoincarePairing.html#TauCeti.poincarePairing) `([φ], [ψ]) ↦ ∫_M φ ∧ ψ` is now defined on classes, and duality itself has not started. The rest of the window was supporting work. It typed curvature as an [endomorphism-valued two-form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Curvature.html#TauCeti.CurvatureForm), added associativity of the wedge, [pullback of orientations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Orientation/Pullback.html#TauCeti.Manifold.Orientation.pullback), and the [symmetry of the Hessian](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Laplacian.html#TauCeti.Manifold.hessian_symm), and laid analytic groundwork for Green's theorem through arclength-parametrized curve integrals.
