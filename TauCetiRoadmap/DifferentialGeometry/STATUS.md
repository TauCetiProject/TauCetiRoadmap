## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The exterior calculus is done. Stokes' theorem holds for manifolds with boundary, Green's theorem is proved in both strong forms, and de Rham cohomology now has the homotopy operator and the Mayer–Vietoris sequence. Homotopy invariance, the sphere computations, flows, Frobenius, orientations and the Riemannian integral theorems are partial. The de Rham theorem, Poincaré duality, the degree and the hairy ball theorem have not begun.

### Named results

- **Stokes' theorem** — `∫_M dω = ∫_{∂M} ω` for a compactly supported `C¹` form on an oriented smooth manifold over the half-space, with the outward-first boundary orientation ([`integralTopForm_mextDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Stokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv)).
- **Green's theorem for absolutely continuous curves** — the circulation of a field that is `C¹` near a closed absolutely continuous curve equals its curl weighted by the winding number, with no simplicity assumption ([`green_ac_winding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Winding.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding)).
- **The Mayer–Vietoris sequence** — for open `U` and `V` in a Hausdorff σ-compact finite-dimensional manifold, `0 → Ω^•(U ⊔ V) → Ω^•(U) ⊞ Ω^•(V) → Ω^•(U ⊓ V) → 0` is short exact ([`mayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact)). The connecting map has its [partition-of-unity formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.mayerVietorisδ_mk_eq_mk_extendByZero), and the [compactly supported version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/CompactlySupportedMayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedMayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact) is exact as well.
- **The cylinder homotopy formula** — `d(hφ) + h(dφ) = ι₁^*φ − ι₀^*φ` for `C¹` Banach-valued forms on `M × [0, 1]`, proved directly so that `M` may have boundary or corners ([`mextDeriv_homotopyOperator_add_homotopyOperator_mextDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/HomotopyOperator.html#TauCeti.mextDeriv_homotopyOperator_add_homotopyOperator_mextDeriv)).
- **The angular class on the circle** — `dθ` has period `2π`, so its class is nonzero in `H¹_dR(S¹)` ([`mk_circleAngularClosedForm_ne_zero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Circle.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.mk_circleAngularClosedForm_ne_zero)).

## The frontier

- **Homotopy invariance (6.3).** The operator and its identity exist. What remains is to apply them to a smooth homotopy to get `deRhamCohomology.map_eq_of_homotopy`, then the exactness criterion through line integrals and `H¹_dR = 0` for simply connected `M`.
- **Spheres from Mayer–Vietoris (7.2–7.3).** Still needed: naturality of the connecting map and the acceptance computation of `H^k_dR(Sⁿ)` from the two-cap cover. The overlap is a cylinder over `Sⁿ⁻¹`, so this probably waits on homotopy invariance.
- **Densities and Stokes with corners (5.5).** Densities and their integral, the abstract boundary, and the `|∫_{S¹} dθ| = 2π` acceptance test as a top-form integral have not started. Stokes on `M × [0, 1]`, which 10.5 needs, is also open.
- **Banach flows (3.1–3.2).** The gating analysis is smooth dependence on initial conditions for Banach-space fields. Straightening, commuting flows and time-dependent flows come after it.
- **Frobenius (4.2–4.4).** Missing are the two involutivity criteria, flat charts from the existing graph-coordinate theorem, and maximal leaves of an involutive distribution.
