## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Forms and the exterior calculus, Stokes' theorem for manifolds with boundary, Green's theorem, de Rham cohomology and Mayer–Vietoris are complete, and top cohomology is now computed in both the orientable and the nonorientable case. The global degree exists with its integral formula and homotopy invariance. Orientations, flows, Frobenius, corners Stokes, the de Rham theorem, Poincaré duality, the degree calculus and the Riemannian layer are partial, and the hairy ball theorem has not begun.

### Named results

- **Stokes' theorem** — `∫_M dω = ∫_{∂M} ω` for a compactly supported `C¹` form on an oriented manifold over the half-space, with the outward-first boundary orientation ([`integralTopForm_mextDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Stokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv)).
- **Green's theorem for absolutely continuous curves** — the circulation of a field that is `C¹` near a closed absolutely continuous curve equals its curl weighted by the winding number, with no simplicity assumption ([`green_ac_winding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Winding.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding)).
- **The de Rham cohomology of spheres** — for `n ≥ 1`, `H^k_dR(Sⁿ)` is one-dimensional in degrees `0` and `n` and zero otherwise ([`finrank_deRhamCohomology_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Sphere.html#TauCeti.finrank_deRhamCohomology_sphere)).
- **Top compactly supported cohomology** — on a connected boundaryless σ-compact `M`, integration gives [`H^n_c(M) ≅ ℝ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Integration/Bijective.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomologyIntegralEquiv) when `M` is oriented, and [`H^n_c(M) = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/OrientationCover.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology_of_not_orientable) when it is not orientable, so [`H^n_c(M)` detects orientability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/OrientationCover.html#TauCeti.nontrivial_compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology_iff_orientable).
- **The degree formula** — for a smooth map between compact connected oriented boundaryless manifolds of equal dimension, `∫_M f^*ω = deg(f) ∫_N ω` ([`integralTopForm_mpullback_eq_degree_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Degree/Global.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mpullback_eq_degree_mul)), where the [degree](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Degree/Basic.html#TauCeti.degree) is the signed fibre count, [independent of the regular value](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Degree/Global.html#TauCeti.degreeAtRegularValue_eq_of_isRegularValue) and [invariant under smooth homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Degree/Global.html#TauCeti.degree_eq_of_homotopy).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[The de Rham integration homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/DeRham.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.integration)** sends de Rham cohomology to smooth singular cohomology, naturally in smooth maps. It is built from a [cochain map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/DeRham.html#TauCeti.deRhamIntegration) that integrates forms over [smooth singular simplices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/Basic.html#TauCeti.smoothSingularSubcomplex), and is the chain-level half of the de Rham map.
- **The [Mayer–Vietoris induction principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietoris_induction)** is what both the de Rham theorem and Poincaré duality are meant to run on.
- **[Proper maps cover their regular values](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Covering.html#TauCeti.isCoveringMapOn_setOf_isRegularValue)** between manifolds of equal dimension. They supply the evenly covered neighbourhoods on which the roadmap's route computes the degree formula.

## The frontier

- **The antipodal degree (10.5)**: `deg(antipodal) = (−1)^(n+1)` on `Sⁿ`, with `z ↦ zⁿ` of degree `n` on `S¹`. It needs the outward orientation of `Sⁿ` and its antipodal sign from Layer 2, which are missing.
- **The hairy ball theorem (11)**: the great-circle homotopy argument now has homotopy invariance of the degree; it still needs the antipodal degree and a smooth approximation of a continuous tangent field.
- **Poincaré duality (9.3)**: both halves of top cohomology are in place, so what remains is to run the induction principle from the `ℝⁿ` base case through the five lemma for the two Mayer–Vietoris sequences.
- **The de Rham theorem (8.3, 8.5)**: the smoothing theorem, by relative Whitney approximation for boundaryless `M` or the quasi-isomorphism route with corners, then the induction to show the de Rham homomorphism is bijective.
- **Densities and corners Stokes (5.5)**: densities on manifolds and their integral, extending the flat quadrant case. The Riemannian density (12.4) and the comparisons left in 5.6 depend on them.
