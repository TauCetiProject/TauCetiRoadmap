## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Forms and the exterior calculus, Stokes' theorem for manifolds with boundary, Green's theorem, and the de Rham and Mayer–Vietoris layers are complete, ending with the cohomology of spheres. Orientations, flows, Frobenius, integration with corners, the de Rham theorem, Poincaré duality, the degree and the Riemannian layer are partial. Each has its foundations, and the de Rham theorem, duality and the global degree are still missing. The hairy ball theorem has not begun.

### Named results

- **Stokes' theorem** — `∫_M dω = ∫_{∂M} ω` for a compactly supported `C¹` form on an oriented manifold over the half-space, with the outward-first boundary orientation ([`integralTopForm_mextDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Stokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv)).
- **Green's theorem for absolutely continuous curves** — the circulation of a field that is `C¹` near a closed absolutely continuous curve equals its curl weighted by the winding number, with no simplicity assumption ([`green_ac_winding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Winding.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding)), and it extends to [finite integer-weighted families of curves](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/FiniteSum.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding_sum).
- **The de Rham cohomology of spheres** — for `n ≥ 1`, `H^k_dR(Sⁿ)` is one-dimensional in degrees `0` and `n` and zero otherwise ([`finrank_deRhamCohomology_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Sphere.html#TauCeti.finrank_deRhamCohomology_sphere)), computed by [Mayer–Vietoris](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact) and [homotopy invariance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/HomotopyInvariance.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.map_eq_of_homotopy).
- **Top compactly supported cohomology of an oriented manifold** — on a connected oriented boundaryless σ-compact `M`, integration is an isomorphism `H^n_c(M) ≅ ℝ` ([`compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomologyIntegralEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Integration/Bijective.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomologyIntegralEquiv)). It generalises the earlier computation of `H^n_c(ℝⁿ)`.
- **The local Frobenius theorem** — an involutive distribution of constant rank is constant in a suitable chart, so through every point passes [an embedded integral manifold in slice position](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/IntegralManifold/Existence.html#TauCeti.IsInvolutiveDistribution.exists_isIntegralManifold). Involutivity is characterised [by annihilating one-forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Distribution/Annihilator.html#TauCeti.isInvolutiveDistribution_iff_forall_mextDeriv).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[Smooth singular simplices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/Basic.html#TauCeti.smoothSingularSubcomplex)**, with [integration of forms over them](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/Integral.html#TauCeti.SmoothSimplex.integral), form the chain-level side of the de Rham map.
- **The [Mayer–Vietoris induction principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietoris_induction)** is what both the de Rham theorem and Poincaré duality are meant to run on.
- **The smooth orientation double cover** now carries integration and descent. Classes pulled back to it [integrate to zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/OrientationCover.html#TauCeti.Manifold.OrientationCover.integral_map_proj_eq_zero), and [primitives descend](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Orientation/Cover/Descent.html#TauCeti.Manifold.OrientationCover.exists_mextDerivₗ_eq_of_mextDerivₗ_eq_mpullback_proj). This is what the nonorientable top-cohomology computation needs.

## The frontier

- **Nonorientable top cohomology (9.2)**: `H^n_c(M) = 0` for connected nonorientable `M`. The orientable isomorphism, vanishing integrals on the cover and descent of primitives are all in place, so what remains is to assemble them.
- **Poincaré duality (9.3)**: run the induction principle from the `ℝⁿ` base case through the five lemma for the two Mayer–Vietoris sequences.
- **The de Rham map (8.3–8.5)**: the smoothing theorem by relative Whitney approximation, Stokes for smooth chains in a manifold, and the de Rham homomorphism, before the induction can give the isomorphism.
- **The global degree (10.3–10.5)**: independence of the regular value needs 3.3's homogeneity, which is missing. The degree calculus and the antipodal degree that the hairy ball theorem waits on come after it.
- **Densities and corners Stokes (5.5)**: densities on manifolds and their integral, extending the flat quadrant case. The Riemannian density (12.4) and the comparisons left in 5.6 depend on them.
