## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The exterior calculus on manifolds is done. Stokes' theorem holds for manifolds with boundary, and Green's theorem is proved in the roadmap's strong forms. De Rham cohomology now has homotopy invariance, both Mayer–Vietoris short exact sequences with their connecting map, and a nonzero class on the circle. Flows, Frobenius, the degree and the Riemannian layer are partial at the definitions. The de Rham theorem, Poincaré duality proper and the hairy ball theorem have not begun.

### Named results

- **Stokes' theorem**: `∫_M dω = ∫_{∂M} ω` for a compactly supported `C¹` form on an oriented manifold over the half-space, with the outward-first boundary orientation ([`integralTopForm_mextDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Stokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv)).
- **Green's theorem for absolutely continuous curves**: the circulation of a field that is `C¹` near a closed absolutely continuous curve equals its curl weighted by the winding number, with no simplicity assumption ([`green_ac_winding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Winding.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding)).
- **The Mayer–Vietoris sequence**: for two open subsets of a Hausdorff σ-compact finite-dimensional manifold, the restriction sequence of de Rham complexes is short exact ([`mayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact)). Its connecting map is [described through a cut-off function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.mayerVietorisδ_mk_eq_mk_extendByZero), and the [compactly supported sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/CompactlySupportedMayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedMayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact) is exact too.
- **Homotopy invariance of de Rham cohomology**: smoothly homotopic maps induce the same map on cohomology, with boundary and corners allowed ([`deRhamCohomology.map_eq_of_homotopy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/HomotopyInvariance.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.map_eq_of_homotopy)).
- **The Poincaré lemma and its first counterexample**: cohomology [vanishes in positive degrees on a normed space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/NormedSpace.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_deRhamCohomology_normedSpace). On the circle, the angular form has period `2π`, so [`H¹_dR(S¹)` is nontrivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Circle.html#TauCeti.nontrivial_deRhamCohomology_circle_one).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The [homotopy operator](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/HomotopyOperator.html#TauCeti.homotopyOperator) on `M × [0, 1]`**. Its [cochain-homotopy identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/HomotopyOperator.html#TauCeti.mextDeriv_homotopyOperator_add_homotopyOperator_mextDeriv) is proved directly rather than through Stokes, which is why homotopy invariance holds on manifolds with boundary.
- **[Compactly supported cohomology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/CompactSupport.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology)** comes with [extension by zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/ExtendByZero.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology.extendByZero) along open inclusions, a module structure over `H^•_dR` through the wedge, and [integration of top classes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/PoincarePairing.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology.integral). Together these define the [Poincaré pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/PoincarePairing.html#TauCeti.poincarePairing) `∫_M φ ∧ ψ`, which is what layer 9 will build on.
- **[Line integrals of forms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Path.html#TauCeti.pathIntegral)** come with their [fundamental theorem of calculus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Path.html#TauCeti.pathIntegral_mextDeriv) and [agree with the oriented integral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Path.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mpullback_Icc_eq_pathIntegral) over an interval. They are the periods that detect nonzero classes.

## The frontier

- **Spheres and naturality (layer 7)**: compute `H^k_dR(Sⁿ)` from the two-cap cover using Mayer–Vietoris, homotopy invariance and the Poincaré lemma, which are all in place. The connecting map's naturality is the other missing piece.
- **Periods and simply connected manifolds (6.3)**: a closed 1-form whose periods all vanish is exact, so `H¹_dR = 0` for simply connected `M`. Path integrals already exist.
- **`H^•_c(ℝⁿ)` (6.4) and top cohomology (9.2)**: these are the base cases for Poincaré duality. Integration on `H^n_c` is defined, but the isomorphism with `ℝ` for connected oriented `M` is not proved.
- **Densities and Stokes with corners (5.5)**: densities and their integral, the abstract boundary, and the `|∫_{S¹} dθ| = 2π` acceptance test. The comparisons left in 5.6 are waiting on this.
- **Smooth chains (8.3–8.4)**: the de Rham theorem needs smooth simplices, Stokes for chains and the de Rham map. Nothing has been started, though layer 7's sequence is now available for the induction.
