### Named results

- **Stokes' theorem**: `∫_M dω = ∫_{∂M} ω` for a compactly supported `C¹` form on an oriented manifold over the half-space, with the outward-first boundary orientation ([`integralTopForm_mextDeriv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm/Integration/Stokes.html#TauCeti.integralTopForm_mextDeriv)).
- **Green's theorem for absolutely continuous curves**: the circulation of a field that is `C¹` near a closed absolutely continuous curve equals its curl weighted by the winding number, with no simplicity assumption ([`green_ac_winding`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Green/Winding.html#TauCeti.Green.green_ac_winding)).
- **The de Rham cohomology of spheres**: for `n ≥ 1`, `H^k_dR(Sⁿ)` is one-dimensional in degrees `0` and `n` and zero otherwise ([`finrank_deRhamCohomology_sphere`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Sphere.html#TauCeti.finrank_deRhamCohomology_sphere)), computed from the two-cap cover with the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietorisShortComplex_shortExact) and [homotopy invariance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/HomotopyInvariance.html#TauCeti.deRhamCohomology.map_eq_of_homotopy).
- **Simply connected manifolds have no first cohomology**: a closed 1-form is [exact exactly when its periods vanish](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/PathIntegral.html#TauCeti.mem_exactForms_one_iff_forall_pathIntegral_eq_zero), so [`H¹_dR(M) = 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/SimplyConnected.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_deRhamCohomology_one_of_simplyConnectedSpace) when `M` is simply connected.
- **Compactly supported cohomology of `ℝⁿ`**: integration is an [isomorphism `H^n_c(ℝⁿ) ≅ ℝ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Euclidean.html#TauCeti.compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomologyNormedSpaceEquiv), and the [other degrees vanish](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/DeRham/Euclidean.html#TauCeti.subsingleton_compactlySupportedDeRhamCohomology_normedSpace). This is the base case for Poincaré duality.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[Smooth singular simplices](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/Basic.html#TauCeti.smoothSingularSubcomplex)** form a sub-simplicial set of the singular simplices. [Integration of forms over them](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/SmoothSingular/Integral.html#TauCeti.SmoothSimplex.integral) is natural, and [Stokes' theorem on the standard simplex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/DifferentialForm/Simplex.html#ContDiffOn.integral_extDerivWithin_fullSimplex) is proved. Together these are the chain-level side of the de Rham map.
- **The [Mayer–Vietoris induction principle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/MayerVietoris.html#TauCeti.mayerVietoris_induction)**, with countable disjoint unions among its hypotheses, is what both the de Rham theorem and Poincaré duality are meant to run on.
- **The [degree at a regular value](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Degree/Basic.html#TauCeti.degreeAtRegularValue)** is the signed count of preimages. It satisfies the [integral formula for forms supported near the value](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Degree/Integral.html#TauCeti.exists_mem_nhds_integralTopForm_mpullback_eq_degreeAtRegularValue_mul).

## The frontier

- **Top cohomology (9.2)**: integration `H^n_c(M) → ℝ` is onto, and bijective on manifolds diffeomorphic to `ℝⁿ`. Injectivity on connected oriented `M` remains, along with vanishing for nonorientable `M` by descent along the orientation cover, which now exists.
- **Poincaré duality (9.3)**: run the induction principle from the `ℝⁿ` base case, now that `H^•_c(ℝⁿ)` and the compactly supported Mayer–Vietoris sequence are both available.
- **The de Rham map (8.3–8.5)**: the smoothing theorem by relative Whitney approximation, Stokes for smooth simplices and chains in a manifold, and `deRhamHom`, before the induction can give `deRhamEquiv`.
- **The global degree (10.3–10.5)**: show that the degree is independent of the regular value. That needs 3.3's homogeneity, which is missing. Then come the degree calculus and the `deg antipodal` gate that the hairy ball theorem (layer 11) waits on.
- **Densities and corners Stokes (5.5)**: densities on manifolds, `integralDensity`, and the abstract boundary. The Riemannian density (12.4) and the comparisons left in 5.6 depend on these.
