## AnalyticNumberTheoryPartII: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`852ff49` to `d25d526`)

The [Barnes G-function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/BarnesG/Basic.html#TauCeti.barnesG) now exists, defined by its canonical Weierstrass product: it is entire, satisfies the [Gamma recurrence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/BarnesG/Basic.html#TauCeti.barnesG_add_one) G(z+1)=Γ(z)G(z), takes superfactorial values at the integers, and [vanishes to order n+1 at −n](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/BarnesG/Basic.html#TauCeti.analyticOrderAt_barnesG_neg_natCast), with its logarithmic derivative and positivity on the real axis. A second, independent construction, [`Complex.barnesG`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/BarnesG.html#Complex.barnesG), landed alongside it and duplicates most of this. The asymptotic expansion on the slit plane is not yet there. Barnes G feeds the constants of the zeta moment conjecture: the [arithmetic factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta/MomentConstants.html#TauCeti.zetaMomentArithmeticFactor) a(k) and the [Barnes factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta/MomentConstants.html#TauCeti.zetaMomentBarnesFactor) g(k) are defined and positive, with the check [a(2)g(2)=1/(2π²)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta/MomentConstants.html#TauCeti.zetaMomentArithmeticFactor_two_mul_zetaMomentBarnesFactor_two). The [Hurwitz series with a complex parameter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/ComplexHurwitzSeries.html#TauCeti.complexHurwitzSeries) also landed, with its shift relation and [Gamma-integral representation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/ComplexHurwitzSeries.html#TauCeti.complexHurwitzSeries_eq_integral).

On the Bost–Connes side, the [first trigonometric Eisenstein function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BostConnes/TrigonometricEisenstein.html#TauCeti.BostConnes.firstEisenstein) in ℚ[ℚ/ℤ] and its [higher analogues](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BostConnes/TrigonometricEisenstein.html#TauCeti.BostConnes.eisenstein) are built, with their [cotangent values](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BostConnes/TrigonometricEisenstein.html#TauCeti.BostConnes.lift_eisenstein_eq_cot) at characters. Through a Cayley relation and a doubling relation, the first functions [generate ℚ[ℚ/ℤ] as a ℚ-algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BostConnes/TrigonometricEisenstein.html#TauCeti.BostConnes.adjoin_range_firstEisenstein). The Bost–Connes algebra itself is not yet defined.

For Layer 5, binary cubic forms carry the twisted GL₂ action, with [discriminant covariance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Cubic/BinaryForm.html#Cubic.discr_smul) disc(g·f)=det(g)²disc(f) (TauCeti#42154). The [odd quadratic twist](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/Quadratic/OddTwist.html#DirichletCharacter.oddQuadraticTwist) of a character modulo 8 is defined, and its [L-function matches the odd Jacobi series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/Quadratic/OddTwist.html#DirichletCharacter.LFunction_oddQuadraticTwist_eq_LSeries). Restricted products gained open level subgroups on which Haar measure becomes a product measure.
