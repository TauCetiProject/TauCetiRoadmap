### Named results

- **The Barnes G-function** — defined by its canonical Weierstrass product, [`barnesG`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/BarnesG/Basic.html#TauCeti.barnesG) is entire, satisfies [G(z+1)=Γ(z)G(z)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/BarnesG/Basic.html#TauCeti.barnesG_add_one) away from z=0, and has a zero of [exact order n+1 at −n](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/BarnesG/Basic.html#TauCeti.analyticOrderAt_barnesG_neg_natCast). Its [logarithmic derivative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/BarnesG/Basic.html#TauCeti.logDeriv_barnesG_add_one) is also proved. An independent copy, [`Complex.barnesG`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/BarnesG.html#Complex.barnesG), proves much of the same and is a candidate for consolidation.
- **The zeta-moment constants** — the Keating–Snaith arithmetic factor [a(k)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta/MomentConstants.html#TauCeti.zetaMomentArithmeticFactor) and Barnes factor [g(k)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta/MomentConstants.html#TauCeti.zetaMomentBarnesFactor) are positive, and [a(2)g(2)=1/(2π²)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta/MomentConstants.html#TauCeti.zetaMomentArithmeticFactor_two_mul_zetaMomentBarnesFactor_two) is the predicted fourth-moment coefficient. The general-k moment asymptotic remains a conjecture.
- **Eisenstein generation of ℚ[ℚ/ℤ]** — the first trigonometric Eisenstein functions [generate the rational group algebra of ℚ/ℤ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BostConnes/TrigonometricEisenstein.html#TauCeti.BostConnes.adjoin_range_firstEisenstein), and the higher ones take the [cotangent-polynomial values](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/BostConnes/TrigonometricEisenstein.html#TauCeti.BostConnes.lift_eisenstein_eq_cot) P_k(cot(πt)/2i) at characters.
- **Discriminant covariance for binary cubic forms** — under the twisted GL₂ action, [disc(g·f)=det(g)²disc(f)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Cubic/BinaryForm.html#Cubic.discr_smul) over every commutative ring.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The [complex-parameter Hurwitz series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/ComplexHurwitzSeries.html#TauCeti.complexHurwitzSeries), with its shift relation, its comparison with ζ and its [Gamma-integral form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/ComplexHurwitzSeries.html#TauCeti.complexHurwitzSeries_eq_integral), is the starting point for the complex Hurwitz continuation of §4.12.
- The [odd quadratic twist](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/Quadratic/OddTwist.html#DirichletCharacter.oddQuadraticTwist) of a character modulo 8 by a Jacobi symbol, whose [L-function is the odd Jacobi series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/Quadratic/OddTwist.html#DirichletCharacter.LFunction_oddQuadraticTwist_eq_LSeries), supplies the coefficients of the quadratic double Dirichlet series.
- [Haar measure on restricted products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Haar/RestrictedProduct.html#TauCeti.exists_unique_restrictedHaar) is unique under the usual normalization, and it [restricts to a product measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Haar/RestrictedProduct.html#TauCeti.measurePreserving_restrictedHaar_levelOpenSubgroupEquiv) on each open level subgroup. This is the start of the adelic harmonic analysis behind the cubic zeta integral.

## The frontier

- **Barnes G asymptotics (§4.3, §4.3a)** — the expansion of Ln G(z+1) on the slit plane, with the periodic-Bernoulli remainder and its sharp sector bound. This also needs a normalized analytic logarithm of G and of Γ(z+1). Merging the two Barnes G constructions first would avoid doing this twice.
- **Lerch transcendent (§4.1)** — the series Φ(z,s,c) on the disc and its joint holomorphy, the gateway to the covering-space continuation of §4.5 (which also needs UniversalCovers).
- **Complex Hurwitz continuation (§4.12)** — continue the complex-parameter series past Re s=1, keeping the pole at s=1.
- **The rational Bost–Connes algebra (§§6.18, 6.21, 6.37)** — define the Hecke algebra and its presentation so the Eisenstein generation result can be upgraded to the equality of the arithmetic algebra with the Bost–Connes rational form.
- **Quadratic double Dirichlet series (§§5.1, 5.14–5.16)** — with the odd twist in place, the primitive twist data and the sixteen series Z(s,w) are the next steps toward their continuation.
