## ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`0874260` to `58174b5`)

The roadmap's first results are Arakelov divisors on `Spec O_K` and the compactified Picard group. An [Arakelov divisor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArakelovDivisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.ArakelovDivisor) is a fractional ideal together with a real weight at each infinite place. [Principal divisors have degree zero](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArakelovDivisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.ArakelovDivisor.degree_principal), which is the product formula. The divisor class group sits in the exact sequence `0 → ℝ^{r₁+r₂}/log|O_K^×| → Pic^(O_K) → Cl(K) → 0`: the left map is [injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArakelovDivisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.ArakelovPicardGroup.ofInfinitePartQuotient_injective), the sequence is [exact in the middle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArakelovDivisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.ArakelovPicardGroup.exact_ofInfinitePartQuotient_toClassGroup), and the right map is [surjective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArakelovDivisor/Basic.html#TauCeti.ArakelovPicardGroup.toClassGroup_surjective) (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/1706). Each divisor `D` also gives a hermitian line `O(D)` [whose Arakelov degree is the degree of `D`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArakelovDivisor/HermitianLine.html#TauCeti.ArakelovDivisor.sectionDegree_hermitianMetric), and [adding a principal divisor gives an isometric line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/ArakelovDivisor/HermitianLine.html#TauCeti.ArakelovDivisor.isometryEquivAddPrincipal). The divisor classes have not yet been identified with isometry classes of hermitian lines, because that group is not defined.

Under this sits a theory of [hermitian vector bundles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/HermitianBundle/Basic.html#TauCeti.HermitianBundle) over `O_K`. It has orthogonal sums, tensor products, [duals](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/HermitianBundle/Dual.html#TauCeti.HermitianBundle.dual) with an isometric double dual, sub-bundles with [quotient metrics](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/HermitianBundle/Submodule.html#TauCeti.HermitianBundle.quotient), and base change that behaves well in towers. The degree is computed from a chosen section, and on a line it is [independent of the section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/HermitianBundle/Degree/Basic.html#TauCeti.HermitianMetric.sectionDegree_eq_sectionDegree). The degree's behaviour under duality and under base change is proved only when the section generates the module, that is, for free lines. Determinants are missing, so there is no higher-rank degree yet. An earlier, separate `HermitianVectorBundle` carrier also remains.

There is also an early step toward Layer 2: the lattice `ℤ^g + τℤ^g` in `ℂ^g` [has covolume `|det Im τ|`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/PeriodMatrix.html#ZLattice.covolume_eq_abs_det_im). This is the lattice half of the statement that the Faltings norm is a covolume.
