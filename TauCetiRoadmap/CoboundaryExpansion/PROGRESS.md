## CoboundaryExpansion: 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09 (`419b078` to `7ca783d`)

The coefficient layer of the roadmap is now complete: every group norm the later layers need is in place, though no cochain has been defined yet. The centrepiece is the [moved-point norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/MovedNorm.html#Equiv.Perm.movedNorm) on a finite permutation group, which counts the points a permutation moves. It is [conjugation invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/MovedNorm.html#Equiv.Perm.isConjInvariant_movedNorm), and the [Hamming distance between two permutations is the moved-point norm of their quotient](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/MovedNorm.html#Equiv.Perm.hammingDist_eq_movedNorm). Alongside it comes the [parity retraction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/ParityRetract.html#Equiv.Perm.parityRetract), the homomorphism sending even permutations to the identity and odd ones to a fixed transposition, which [does not increase the moved-point norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/ParityRetract.html#Equiv.Perm.movedNorm_parityRetract_le). This is the lemma the headline will use to show that `Sym(3)` gauges do no better than `{e, t}` gauges on Kozlov's binary witnesses.

Underneath sits a general theory of [conjugation-invariant seminorms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Group/ConjInvariant.html#GroupSeminorm.IsConjInvariant). Conjugation invariance is exactly [right invariance of the associated metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Group/ConjInvariant.html#GroupNorm.isConjInvariant_iff_dist_mul_right). Conversely, every bi-invariant metric comes from such a norm, and the two constructions are mutually inverse. The property survives cyclic rotation of a product, pullback along injective homomorphisms (which gives restriction to subgroups), positive scaling, sums and maxima (TauCeti#27680, TauCeti#29436).

Looking ahead to the cross-degree convention, Chapman and Lubotzky's [distance with errors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/DistErr.html#Equiv.Perm.distErr) between permutations of different degrees is now defined. It is symmetric, it reduces to the normalized Hamming distance in equal degree, and it is bounded below by the share of points outside the smaller degree. The cross-degree cost and constant built from it are still to come.

## CoboundaryExpansion: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-19 (`7ca783d` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first main theorem has landed: Beasley's [star bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/StarBound.html#TauCeti.card_mul_gaugeDist_le), which says that under a conjugation-invariant norm every cochain on the complete complex `K_k` satisfies `k·D(α) ≤ 3·N(α)`. Hence [`k/3` bounds the Cheeger constant from below](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/StarBound.html#TauCeti.card_div_three_le_h1) whenever some cochain has positive gauge distance, and [the bound is attained exactly when every star gauge is optimal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/StarBound.html#TauCeti.Cochain.defect_div_gaugeDist_eq_card_div_three_iff). This arrived with the whole cochain theory beneath it (TauCeti#40573). [Group-valued cochains](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/Cochain/Basic.html#TauCeti.Cochain) now have holonomy, defect, gauge action and gauge distance. Each is invariant under reordering, relabelling and gauges, and monotone under norm-decreasing changes of coefficients. On top of them sit the counting, normalized and same-degree permutation constants, the last [bounded above by the `𝔽₂` constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/CheegerConstant.html#TauCeti.h1Sym_le_h1_movedNorm_fin_two).

Balanced replication is also done. Replacing each vertex by a cluster [multiplies the defect by `q³`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/Replication.html#TauCeti.Cochain.defect_replicate) and [the gauge distance by exactly `q²`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/Replication.html#TauCeti.Cochain.gaugeDist_replicate). Both hold for every group norm, and the second holds even when no optimal gauge exists. The proof rests on a section-averaging double count over lexicographic products, which was built as a standalone optimisation lemma (TauCeti#36481). On the `𝔽₂` side, [Kozlov's staircase graphs](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Staircase.html#SimpleGraph.staircase) are [cut-minimal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Staircase.html#SimpleGraph.isCutMinimal_staircase) and satisfy his [odd-triangle formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Staircase.html#SimpleGraph.card_oddTriangleFinset_staircase). These are still pure graph theory: no bridge to `𝔽₂`-valued cochains exists yet, so Kozlov's theorem is not stated.

For the cross-degree convention, the distance with errors gained an agreement-count API, invariance under multiplication and inversion, and the baseline bounds. The seed's [cross-degree gauge infimum over all degrees is `2`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/CrossDegree.html#TauCeti.ciInf₂_sum_distErr_fin_four_of_eq_setOf_isSwap), attained in degree three, and the same holds for its replications. These are statements about raw edge assignments, because the cross-degree cost and constant are not yet defined. No exact value of any constant, `Sym(3)` or otherwise, has been proved.
