### Named results

- **The star bound** — under a conjugation-invariant norm, every cochain on the complete complex on `k` vertices satisfies `k·D(α) ≤ 3·N(α)` ([`TauCeti.card_mul_gaugeDist_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/StarBound.html#TauCeti.card_mul_gaugeDist_le)). Hence `k/3 ≤ h₁(K_k, ℓ)` whenever some cochain has positive gauge distance ([`TauCeti.card_div_three_le_h1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/StarBound.html#TauCeti.card_div_three_le_h1)). This covers every nontrivial coefficient group once `k ≥ 3`.
- **The equality case of the star bound** — a cochain attains the ratio `k/3` exactly when every star gauge is an optimal gauge for it ([`TauCeti.Cochain.defect_div_gaugeDist_eq_card_div_three_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/StarBound.html#TauCeti.Cochain.defect_div_gaugeDist_eq_card_div_three_iff)).
- **Balanced replication** — replacing each vertex by a cluster of `q` multiplies the defect by `q³` and the gauge distance by exactly `q²`, for every group norm, even when no optimal gauge exists ([`TauCeti.Cochain.gaugeDist_replicate`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/Replication.html#TauCeti.Cochain.gaugeDist_replicate)).
- **Kozlov's staircase theorem** — the staircase graph is cut-minimal ([`SimpleGraph.isCutMinimal_staircase`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Staircase.html#SimpleGraph.isCutMinimal_staircase)). On `n = c(2t+1)` vertices, its odd triangles number `n/3` times its edges ([`SimpleGraph.card_oddTriangleFinset_staircase`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Staircase.html#SimpleGraph.card_oddTriangleFinset_staircase)).
- **The parity retraction is norm-decreasing** — sending odd permutations to a fixed transposition never increases the number of moved points ([`Equiv.Perm.movedNorm_parityRetract_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/ParityRetract.html#Equiv.Perm.movedNorm_parityRetract_le)). This is what will carry Kozlov's binary witnesses into `Sym(3)`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Group-valued cochains** ([`TauCeti.Cochain`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/Cochain/Basic.html#TauCeti.Cochain)) come with holonomy, defect, gauge action and [gauge distance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/Cochain/Basic.html#TauCeti.Cochain.gaugeDist). All are invariant under reordering, relabelling and gauges, attained for finite groups, and monotone under norm-decreasing changes of coefficients ([`TauCeti.Cochain.gaugeDist_map_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/Cochain/Invariance.html#TauCeti.Cochain.gaugeDist_map_le)). That monotonicity is the half of the `Sym(3)` reduction that does not depend on the parity retraction.
- **The Cheeger constants** [`TauCeti.h1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/CheegerConstant.html#TauCeti.h1), its normalized form and the same-degree permutation constant [`TauCeti.h1Sym`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/CheegerConstant.html#TauCeti.h1Sym) have characterisations for upper and lower bounds and depend only on the vertex count. They also satisfy the comparison `h1Sym ≤ h₁(·, 𝔽₂)`, so each value theorem reduces to exhibiting one witness.
- **The distance with errors** ([`Equiv.Perm.distErr`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/GroupTheory/Perm/DistErr/Basic.html#Equiv.Perm.distErr)) has its baseline bounds across degrees. For the seed, the cross-degree gauge infimum over every degree at least two is computed as `2` ([`TauCeti.ciInf₂_sum_distErr_fin_four_of_eq_setOf_isSwap`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/CoboundaryExpansion/CrossDegree.html#TauCeti.ciInf₂_sum_distErr_fin_four_of_eq_setOf_isSwap)), and the same holds for its replications.
