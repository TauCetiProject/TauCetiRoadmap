# K3 surfaces: cohomology, periods, cones and Torelli

Construct the geometry of K3 surfaces and its integral cohomology, deformation, period and automorphism APIs. The outputs include actual marked families and surface isomorphisms, not an assignment of a lattice or a period to a name. The integral gluing and Enriques-involution applications use the existing Integral Lattices theory. The construction is over actual schemes and complex manifolds; a collection of arbitrary propositions asserting the conclusions is not a surface interface.

## Conventions and existing suppliers

An algebraic K3 surface over an algebraically closed field k is a smooth projective geometrically integral surface X/k with an invertible-sheaf isomorphism ω_X≃O_X and H¹(X,O_X)=0. Keep a choice of this isomorphism, or equivalently a nonzero two-form σ, as input to constructions that use it. Changing σ by a unit must produce the stated comparison maps. The initial algebraic lane includes characteristic p>2. Characteristic two requires separate inseparability hypotheses and is not an implicit specialization of that lane.

The complex analytic lane includes compact connected complex surfaces with trivial canonical bundle and H¹(O)=0. K1 constructs their Kähler structure theorem, rather than building Kählerness into an unexplained comparison with the algebraic definition. Analytic surfaces need not be projective. Algebraic surfaces over ℂ and analytic surfaces admitting a positive integral (1,1)-class are related by the actual analytification/algebraization equivalence below. Every H² integral object is the actual singular cohomology of the underlying space, with its cup pairing and complex scalar-extension maps.

Use `Over (Scheme.Spec.obj (Opposite.op (CommRingCat.of k)))`, native scheme morphisms and their smooth/projective/finite/étale properties. Use `Scheme.Modules`, native invertible sheaves, pullback, tensor, dual, Hom and global sections; do not give a second Picard group or vector-bundle category. Analytification uses actual charted spaces and the coherent analytic sheaf and bundle constructions of Complex Kähler Geometry (CKG), with GAGA maps on morphisms and coherent cohomology. A cohomological marking is an integral isometry, not a rational matrix with an asserted determinant.

## K0. Algebraic surfaces and the early differential theorem

Construct the actual surface category, pullback of ω, the chosen two-form, tangent/cotangent sheaves and their global-section maps. Contraction v↦ι_vσ is an isomorphism T_X≃Ω¹_X, with inverse in local étale coordinates and compatibility with pullback and rescaling σ. Prove ω trivial gives h⁰(ω)=1 and χ(O)=2. Derive c₂(X)=24 from the algebraic Noether/Riemann–Roch formula and the actual degree of the second Chern class; this computation uses neither an integral numerical-lattice classification nor characteristic-zero topology.

### K0.A. Algebraic absence of vector fields in characteristic p>2

For every algebraically closed k of characteristic p>2 and every algebraic K3 X/k, prove H⁰(X,T_X)=0 and, by the contraction isomorphism, H⁰(X,Ω¹_X)=0. This is a required early algebraic theorem. It must not consume unobstructed K3 moduli, a Witt lift, Hodge symmetry in positive characteristic, an Enriques numerical lattice or K6 Torelli. Own the following proof construction at this phase.

1. First build the surface-resolution adapter needed for these quotients: take a separating projective pencil avoiding the finite singular set of the normal excellent projective quotient, resolve its smooth base points, and use Stable Reduction4's normalized-blowup resolution of models with smooth generic curve over each relevant local base DVR. Prove the covers glue to a projective resolution and retain canonical discrepancies. Curve normalization in Scheme and Stack Foundations4.13 is not this surface theorem, and a characteristic-zero Hironaka input is not permitted. Then a nonzero derivation D of O_X defines a rank-one saturated foliation on the smooth locus. Construct its height-one purely inseparable quotient from the invariant subalgebras, the normalization, local zero/divisorial data, the canonical-divisor formula and its minimal-resolution comparison. Carry additive and multiplicative p-closed cases separately, with the actual restricted p-power of derivations. Produce from the vector field a primitive nef divisor D₀ with D₀²=0 and the associated relatively minimal genus-one pencil; prove the generic fibre is smooth, that there are no multiple fibres, and that there are at least two singular fibres. In characteristic three, establish the smooth-generic-fibre pencil construction rather than silently substituting a quasi-elliptic fibration. This vector-field-to-pencil lemma is part of K0.A, with its invariant-function, divisor, Riemann–Roch and resolution proof, not an assumption that every K3 is elliptic. In particular it cannot assume Picard rank at least five or the desired differential vanishing.
2. For that actual pencil f:X→ℙ¹, construct the map of connected automorphism group schemes to Aut(ℙ¹), its vertical kernel and horizontal image, and their tangent maps. Use the Jacobian/torsor model to determine the vertical component when there are no multiple fibres. Determine which α_p or μ_p horizontal actions extend across every minimal regular fibre by local Weierstrass coordinates, including wild conductor contributions. The corrected fibre analysis, not an unrestricted linearization assertion about a non-linearly-reductive group, is the producer.
3. Prove the resulting exhaustive alternatives with nonzero vector field: c₂=0; a rational Jacobian isotrivial surface with two singular fibres; the characteristic-three exceptional two-fibre configurations; or a single-singular-fibre isotrivial case in the small characteristics. Compute the local discriminant/conductor and component contributions using the minimal equations and Ogg's formula. Show none of the alternatives with at least two singular fibres has c₂=24. The characteristic-three wild alternatives must be explicitly eliminated, not discarded with the characteristic-two branch.
4. Conclude that D could not exist, transport through contraction, and export zero-section and functorial global-section statements. Supply the algebraic Serre-dual consequence H²(T_X)=0 separately, with its actual duality map; this consequence is not an input to steps 1–3.

This selects the corrected algebraic vector-field proof route. The historical Rudakov–Shafarevich intermediate elliptic statements require corrections; Martin's *Infinitesimal automorphisms of algebraic varieties and vector fields on elliptic surfaces*, Theorem D and Corollary 1.1, identifies the corrected route. The needed pencil and local-action statements above are explicit K0.A theorem targets with their own construction. A reference to that paper does not install a proof or a new assumed vanishing axiom.

An Enriques consumer may pull back Ω¹_S⊗K_S along its finite étale canonical cover X→S, trivialize the pulled-back K_S by the actual cover algebra, use faithful flatness to obtain an injection on H⁰ and then use K0.A. Algebraic Serre duality identifies its vanishing with H²(S,T_S)=0. This consumer requires no conclusion about Num(S), no Picard-preserving lift and no complex period. Characteristic zero has the independent algebraic-to-complex/spreading or complex Hodge proof with its exact field-of-definition descent; characteristic two is stated separately.

## K2. Ricci-flat metrics, topology and twistor ingredients

For every Kähler class a on a complex K3, construct the unique Ricci-flat Kähler form in a. With a reference form ω and a normalized positive volume form V from σ∧σ̄, solve

    (ω+i∂∂̄φ)² = c V,    ω+i∂∂̄φ>0,    ∫_X φ ω²=0,

where c is fixed by equality of total volumes. Develop the continuity path and its normalization, scalar linearization, openness by the elliptic inverse on mean-zero functions, and closedness using the C⁰, second-order and higher elliptic estimates and positive lower bounds. Keep the estimates' background metric, volume, constants and parameter compactness explicit. These nonlinear estimates and existence are owned here; CKG's linear Laplace and Sobolev theorems alone do not prove them. Prove uniqueness by integration, the Ricci/Chern-curvature calculation, smooth dependence on smooth compact parameter families, and naturality under biholomorphisms.

Construct the parallel holomorphic two-form and the quaternionic parallel complex structures I,J,K from this metric. Prove their algebra, integrability, closed Kähler forms and normalized three-plane orientation. At this stage prove the needed compact Ricci-flat surface splitting statement: the universal cover is a compact simply connected factor times an Euclidean factor; in real dimension four the Euclidean case would give a finite torus quotient. Exclude that case using c₂=24, and exclude a nontrivial finite symplectic deck action by holomorphic Lefschetz/χ(O) multiplicativity. This proves simple connectivity of the K3 surface and torsion-freeness of integral H². The splitting and deck arguments are proved here from their geometric hypotheses, not taken from an unbuilt general hyperkähler classification.

Use actual singular Poincaré duality, Wu's parity formula with c₁=0, Chern–Gauss–Bonnet and the signature formula to obtain the even unimodular H² cup lattice of signature (3,19). Consume Integral Lattices6A/0G for an integral marking H²(X,ℤ)≃Λ, Λ=U³⊕E₈(−1)². Provide pullback isometries, orientation and composition laws. No rational isometry or example-specific diffeomorphism substitutes for this marking. ## K4. Periods and local Torelli

Let B be the cup form on Λ. In the actual native projectivization of Λ⊗ℂ define

    Ω_Λ = { [z] : B(z,z)=0, B(z,z̄)>0 }.

Prove the second expression is real, the conditions are independent of nonzero complex rescaling, the domain is a smooth complex20-fold, and identify it with oriented positive real two-planes. For h fixed, define Ω_h=Ω_Λ∩ℙ(h⊥⊗ℂ), with its two orientation components, the primitive embedding and the actual marking-change action. For signature (3,19), Ω_Λ itself is connected; do not transfer the two-component statement of Ω_h or the marked-moduli space to it.

The period of a marked family is the image of its holomorphic two-form line under the integral local-system marking and scalar extension. Construct this map and prove it is holomorphic, compatible with base change, pullback and marking changes. Identify its derivative with the Kodaira–Spencer/contraction map H¹(T_X)→Hom(H²,⁰,H¹,¹); prove it is an isomorphism. Define rational Hodge loci and their equations B(v,z)=0 for each nonzero rational v, prove properness unless the equation is forced by the chosen lattice polarization, and prove the countability/Baire statements on a nonempty period ball. Countable avoidance gives a very-general set; it does not produce a Zariski-open algebraic locus.
