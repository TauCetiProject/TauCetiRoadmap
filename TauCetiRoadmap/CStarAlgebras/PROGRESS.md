## CStarAlgebras: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`2e14a4d` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap's first target, the matrix-order examples and the compression criterion of C1.1, is complete. The general statement is [the compression criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Compression.html#IsStarProjection.conjugate_le_iff_commute): for a positive element `T` and a star projection `p`, the compression `pTp` lies below `T` exactly when `p` commutes with `T`. It is proved in any C⋆-algebra, without the unit the roadmap asks for, and the forward direction does not need positivity ([one direction alone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Compression.html#IsStarProjection.commute_of_conjugate_le)). The key step is [a zero-corner lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Compression.html#TauCeti.star_mul_mul_eq_zero_iff_of_nonneg): for positive `a`, `b*ab = 0` exactly when `ab = 0` (TauCeti#32971).

The rest of the target is a set of 2×2 matrix computations (TauCeti#34821). Positivity in the C⋆-algebra of square matrices [agrees with positive semidefiniteness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#CStarMatrix.nonneg_iff_posSemidef), and a Hermitian 2×2 matrix is positive semidefinite [exactly when its trace and determinant are nonnegative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/PosSemidef/FinTwo.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.posSemidef_iff_trace_nonneg_and_det_nonneg). On top of these, the examples file shows that the flip matrix has [the averaging projection as its positive part](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#TauCeti.MatrixOrderExample.posPart_flip), not the identity. It computes exactly which diagonal matrices dominate the flip and which dominate its positive part, and reads off three facts from those regions: [taking positive parts is not monotone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#TauCeti.MatrixOrderExample.flip_le_diagonal_not_posPart), [neither is squaring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#TauCeti.MatrixOrderExample.averagingProjection_le_diagonal_not_sq), and [compression by a coordinate projection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#TauCeti.MatrixOrderExample.not_compression_averagingProjection_le) need not decrease a positive matrix.
