## CStarAlgebras: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`2e14a4d` to `1c6a1a4`)

The roadmap's first target, the matrix-order examples and the compression criterion of C1.1, is complete. The general statement is [the compression criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Compression.html#IsStarProjection.conjugate_le_iff_commute): for a positive element `T` and a star projection `p`, the compression `pTp` lies below `T` exactly when `p` commutes with `T`. It is proved in any C⋆-algebra, without the unit the roadmap asks for, and the forward direction does not need positivity ([one direction alone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Compression.html#IsStarProjection.commute_of_conjugate_le)). The key step is [a zero-corner lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Compression.html#TauCeti.star_mul_mul_eq_zero_iff_of_nonneg): for positive `a`, `b*ab = 0` exactly when `ab = 0` (TauCeti#32971).

The rest of the target is a set of 2×2 matrix computations (TauCeti#34821). Positivity in the C⋆-algebra of square matrices [agrees with positive semidefiniteness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#CStarMatrix.nonneg_iff_posSemidef), and a Hermitian 2×2 matrix is positive semidefinite [exactly when its trace and determinant are nonnegative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/PosSemidef/FinTwo.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.posSemidef_iff_trace_nonneg_and_det_nonneg). On top of these, the examples file shows that the flip matrix has [the averaging projection as its positive part](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#TauCeti.MatrixOrderExample.posPart_flip), not the identity. It computes exactly which diagonal matrices dominate the flip and which dominate its positive part, and reads off three facts from those regions: [taking positive parts is not monotone](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#TauCeti.MatrixOrderExample.flip_le_diagonal_not_posPart), [neither is squaring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#TauCeti.MatrixOrderExample.averagingProjection_le_diagonal_not_sq), and [compression by a coordinate projection](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/MatrixOrder.html#TauCeti.MatrixOrderExample.not_compression_averagingProjection_le) need not decrease a positive matrix.

## CStarAlgebras: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

Completely positive and completely bounded maps arrived this window, and with them the first named theorems past Layer 1. The headline is [the Kadison–Schwarz inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap.html#TauCeti.IsNPositive.star_map_mul_map_le_of_map_one): a unital 2-positive map satisfies `φ(a)*φ(a) ≤ φ(a*a)`, with [a version for non-unital maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap.html#TauCeti.IsNPositive.star_map_mul_map_le) scaled by `‖φ(1)‖`. It rests on a new notion of [`n`-positivity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap.html#TauCeti.IsNPositive), and the same work shows that a completely positive map on a unital algebra has [operator norm `‖φ(1)‖`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap.html#CompletelyPositiveMapClass.opNorm_ofClass_eq_norm_map_one). The roadmap's warning example is also in: the transpose on `M₂(ℂ)` is positive but [not 2-positive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap.html#TauCeti.CompletelyPositiveMapExample.not_isNPositive_two_transpose) (TauCeti#38424). Alongside it come [the cb norm](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyBounded.html#LinearMap.cbNorm) for linear maps between C⋆-algebras and its basic normed-space behaviour, with the transpose on `M_n(ℂ)` [having cb norm `n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyBounded.html#CStarMatrix.cbNorm_transposeₗ). This is complete boundedness between C⋆-algebras only: abstract operator spaces have not been started, and neither has the identity `‖φ‖_cb = ‖φ(1)‖`.

Layer 2 opened with [extension of states](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/State/Extension.html#StarSubalgebra.exists_mem_stateSpace_forall_apply_coe_eq): every state of a unital closed star subalgebra extends to a state of the whole algebra. The pure-state extension and the nonunital case are still to come. In Layer 1, half of C1.2 is now done. For a complete star algebra without an identity, the left action [embeds the unitization isometrically](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Algebra/Unitization/LeftAction.html#Unitization.isometry_lift_Lmul) into the bounded operators, and its image [is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Algebra/Unitization/LeftAction.html#Unitization.isClosed_range_lift_Lmul). The `C[0,1]` maximal-ideal example has not landed.

The constructions of C5.4 have begun. The bounded product of unital C⋆-algebras [is a C⋆-algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/BoundedProduct.html#lp.inftyCStarAlgebra), its [`c₀` ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Lp/C0Ideal.html#lp.c0Ideal) is closed, and the quotient norm is [the limit superior of the coordinate norms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Lp/C0Ideal.html#lp.norm_mk_c0Ideal). The rest of the window was supporting work: positivity lemmas for C⋆-matrices, a C⋆-algebraic Cauchy–Schwarz bound for finite sums, and functoriality for maps between quotients.
