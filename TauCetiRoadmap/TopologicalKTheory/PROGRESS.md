## TopologicalKTheory: 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-08 (`ac6d465` to `d2083f0`)

This window built most of the first layer, the category of finite-rank bundles. Over a compact Hausdorff base every real or complex bundle is now a [direct summand of a trivial bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Complement.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.exists_biprod_iso_trivial), with the complement built as an orthogonal complement. That rests on the [existence of continuous Riemannian metrics](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Riemannian/Existence.html#TauCeti.nonempty_continuousRiemannianMetric) over paracompact bases, and on the fact that [the ranges of a continuous family of projections form a vector bundle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/IdempotentRange.html#TauCeti.rangeVectorBundle). Along the way came a [Tietze extension for sections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Extension.html#TauCeti.exists_continuous_section_extension), [extension of bundle isomorphisms from closed sets](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/Extension.html#TauCeti.exists_continuous_clm_bundle_extension_isInvertible), and the theorem that [kernels of constant-rank bundle maps are subbundles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/ConstantRank.html#TauCeti.hasLocalSubbundleFrames_ker).

The coordinate model of bundles, [finite-rank cores](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Basic.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore), became a working category. It has Whitney sums as [biproducts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Biproduct.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.instHasBinaryBiproducts), a [pullback functor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Pullback.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.pullbackFunctor), [duality as an anti-equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Dual.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.instIsEquivalenceOppositeDualFunctor), a [symmetric monoidal tensor product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Monoidal.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.instSymmetricCategory), and realification and complexification, with [realified complexification equal to E ⊕ E](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Realification.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankBundleCore.realifyComplexifyIso). Those cores have constant rank. Their variable-rank counterpart, the [atlas category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/VectorBundle/FiniteRank/Atlas/Category.html#TauCeti.FiniteRankAtlas.instCategory), has only just been started.

The finite Grassmannian, [defined as a quotient of frames](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Grassmannian/Basic.html#TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian), is now [compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Grassmannian/Orthonormal.html#TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian.instCompactSpace) and Hausdorff, with [graph charts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Grassmannian/Graph.html#TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian.isOpenEmbedding_graph), a tautological bundle with [γ ⊕ γ⊥ trivial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Grassmannian/Tautological.html#TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian.tautologicalProdOrthogonalHomeomorph), and stabilization maps. Their [colimit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Grassmannian/Infinite.html#TauCeti.infiniteGrassmannian.isColimit) is the infinite Grassmannian. Groundwork also landed for two later steps: cylinder subdivisions for homotopy invariance, and the [companion-pencil factorization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/CompanionPencil.html#TauCeti.companionPencil_eq_mul) used in the Bott periodicity proof. Neither theorem is proved yet.
