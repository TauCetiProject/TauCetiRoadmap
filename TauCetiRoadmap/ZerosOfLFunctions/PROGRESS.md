## ZerosOfLFunctions: 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-09 (`12e0b2f` to `7ca783d`)

The roadmap's foundation went in. The centrepiece is the [entire completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData.entireCompletion) of a meromorphically continued L-function record. It is the analytic extension of the pole-cleared completed function, not the raw product with its fake zeros at the poles, and it comes with the [divisor formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData.HasMeromorphicContinuation.divisor_entireCompletion) adding back the polar orders (TauCeti#28747). For ζ it is [twice Riemann's ξ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/RiemannZeta.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.zetaEntireCompletion), equal to 1 at 0 and 1 and [symmetric under s ↦ 1 − s](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/RiemannZeta.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.zetaEntireCompletion_one_sub). For a primitive nonprincipal Dirichlet character it [has the same zeros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Dirichlet.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.dirichletData_entireCompletion_eq_zero_iff) as the L-function on the right half-plane.

Around it sit the growth predicates: the [order bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/GrowthOrder.html#TauCeti.OrderLE) with its closure laws, [Liouville for negative order](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/GrowthOrder.html#TauCeti.OrderLE.isConst), and [vertical-strip growth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/VerticalStripGrowth.html#TauCeti.HasVerticalStripGrowth). On the gamma side there is a [holomorphic log Γ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/Log.html#TauCeti.logGamma) on the slit plane, exact logarithmic derivatives of both gamma factors, and their pole orders. Stirling's formula has not been started. The first pieces of counting and factorization also arrived. These are the [zero count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Counting/Basic.html#TauCeti.zeroCount) and [signed divisor count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Counting/Basic.html#TauCeti.divisorCount), [additive across half-open rectangles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Counting/Rectangle.html#TauCeti.zeroCount_reProdIm_Ioc_eq_add), together with the [genus-one Weierstrass factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CanonicalProduct/ElementaryFactor.html#Complex.weierstrassFactorOne). A further result shows that [two holomorphic functions with equal divisors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/DivisorFactorization.html#TauCeti.exists_differentiableOn_eqOn_mul_exp_of_divisor_eq) on a simply connected domain differ by an exponential factor.

One result points toward zero-free regions: [a common real-zero gap at 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/LFunction/FiniteFamily.html#TauCeti.DirichletCharacter.exists_pos_real_zero_gap) for the nonprincipal Dirichlet characters of bounded level. The gap depends on the level bound, so it says nothing about exceptional zeros in an unbounded family.

## ZerosOfLFunctions: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`7ca783d` to `e06d672`)

Zero counting got its two classical tools. [Jensen's bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Counting/Jensen.html#AnalyticOnNhd.zeroCount_le) caps the zeros in a smaller disc by `log (M/‖f c‖)/log (R/r)`, with a [trailing-coefficient form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Counting/Jensen.html#AnalyticOnNhd.zeroCount_le_analyticOrderNatAt_add) for a zero at the centre. From it, an entire function of order at most `A` has [`O(R^B)` zeros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/GrowthOrder/Zero/Count.html#TauCeti.OrderLE.isBigO_sum_divisor) in the disc of radius `R`, and [summable reciprocal `p`th powers of its zeros](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/GrowthOrder/Zero/Summability.html#TauCeti.OrderLE.summable_divisor_mul_norm_rpow), for every `B, p > A`. That is the input the canonical product needs. The [argument principle on a rectangle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Argument/Rectangle.html#TauCeti.Contour.argumentPrinciple_rectBoundary) also landed, built on a counterclockwise [rectangle boundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Curve/Rectangle.html#Complex.rectBoundary) with winding number one inside (TauCeti#32879). Two pieces of Hadamard's proof are in: [a zero-free entire function of order at most one is the exponential of an affine function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/GrowthOrder/Exponential.html#TauCeti.OrderLE.exists_eq_cexp_affine_of_forall_ne_zero), and a [Phragmén–Lindelöf principle for strips](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/PhragmenLindelof/VerticalStrip.html#TauCeti.PhragmenLindelof.vertical_strip_of_isFiniteOrder) that takes finite order as its growth hypothesis.

On the L-function side, every record now has an [entire reciprocal gamma factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/GammaFactor/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData.invGammaFactor), whose zeros are the shifted gamma poles counted with multiplicity. The functional equation now moves divisors of the entire completion [under `s ↦ 1 − conj s`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData.HasFunctionalEquation.divisor_entireCompletion_one_sub_conj). For a primitive nonprincipal Dirichlet character this puts [every zero of the completion in the open critical strip](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Dirichlet/Zeros.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.re_mem_Ioo_of_dirichletData_entireCompletion_eq_zero). Its [logarithmic derivative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Dirichlet/LogDeriv.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.logDeriv_dirichletData_entireCompletion) splits as `½ log N` plus a digamma term plus `L'/L`, and one [absolute constant bounds the gamma term](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Dirichlet/LogDeriv.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.exists_abs_re_logDeriv_dirichletData_entireCompletion_sub_le) across all moduli. These are inputs to the Dirichlet zero-free region.

Several things are still missing. Phragmén–Lindelöf takes constant edge bounds only, not polynomial ones. The rectangle argument principle has no divisor-count form yet. No order bound for the reciprocal gamma factor, no continued L-function and no Stirling estimate has been proved.
