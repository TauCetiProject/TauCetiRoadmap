### Named results

- **Stirling's formula** — on a sector `|arg s| ≤ π − δ`, [log Γ(s) is `(s − 1/2) log s − s + log(2π)/2`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/Stirling/Basic.html#TauCeti.norm_logGamma_sub_stirling_le) up to an error of at most `2/((1 − cos δ)³ ‖s‖)`. On vertical strips, [`log ‖Γ(σ + it)‖ = (σ − 1/2) log |t| − π|t|/2 + log(2π)/2 + O(1/|t|)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Gamma/Stirling/Vertical.html#TauCeti.isBigO_log_norm_Gamma_vertical_sub) uniformly for σ in a compact interval.
- **The canonical product** — for an entire function nonzero at 0 whose zeros have summable inverse squares, the [genus-one product over its divisor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CanonicalProduct/Divisor.html#TauCeti.divisor_divisorProductOne) is entire with exactly the same divisor. Its [logarithmic derivative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/CanonicalProduct/Divisor.html#TauCeti.logDeriv_divisorProductOne) is the paired series `∑ m_ρ (1/(s − ρ) + 1/ρ)`.
- **Order one for Dirichlet completions** — for a primitive nonprincipal character, the completed L-function [is entire of order at most one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Dirichlet/Growth.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.dirichletData_completed_orderLE_one), with an explicit bound `exp(C(1 + ‖s‖) log(2 + ‖s‖))`. Every zero of this completion [has real part strictly between 0 and 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Dirichlet/Zeros.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.re_mem_Ioo_of_dirichletData_entireCompletion_eq_zero).
- **The argument principle on a rectangle, in divisor form** — when no zero or pole lies on the boundary, [the integral of `f'/f` around a rectangle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Contour/Argument/Rectangle/Divisor.html#TauCeti.Contour.argumentPrinciple_rectBoundary_divisor) is `2πi` times the signed divisor count inside it.
- **The Riemann hypothesis as a statement about the completion** — RH is [equivalent to every zero of `completedRiemannZeta` lying on the critical line](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/CriticalLine.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.allOnCriticalLine_completedRiemannZeta_iff), with trivial zeros and poles correctly excluded. A [symmetry criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/CriticalLine.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.allOnCriticalLine_of_zeroCount_eq_one) puts a lone zero of a reflection-invariant region on the line.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [The entire completion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Zeros/Completion/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData.entireCompletion) of a meromorphically continued record is what every count, factorization and order statement is about. Its divisor is known exactly.
- [The zero count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Counting/Basic.html#TauCeti.zeroCount) counts zeros with multiplicity in a region. Jensen's bound and the finite-order zero counts are stated in it.
- [A zero certificate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/Counting/Certificate.html#TauCeti.HasZerosInRects) is a list of rectangles with multiplicities that accounts for every zero in a larger rectangle. Numerical zero lists can now be stated against it.
