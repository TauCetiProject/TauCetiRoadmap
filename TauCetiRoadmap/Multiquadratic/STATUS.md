## Where this roadmap stands

The Galois half is complete in both stages: [`isGalois`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Galois/Basic.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.isGalois) and `aut_exponent_eq_two` for the easy facts, and the explicit `galoisGroupEquiv` sending an automorphism to its sign pattern, with `card_aut_adjoin_range = 2^|ι|`. The subspace dictionary is in place: [`finrank_top_over_intermediateField`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/RelativeDegree.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.finrank_top_over_intermediateField) gives `[M : F] = 2^(dim U)` and `galoisGroupEquivIntermediateFieldSubmodule` identifies the relative Galois group with that subspace. The packaged order-reversing bijection between intermediate fields and `𝔽₂`-subspaces exists so far only for prime radicands (`intermediateFieldEquivSubmoduleSqrtPrimes`, with the subfield count); the general-radicand version is assembled from ingredients rather than stated.

## The frontier

The nearest target is the identification of the candidate genus field. Two pieces are needed and neither is started: an intrinsic definition of the genus field of `ℚ(√d)` (unramified at all places including the infinite ones, abelian over `ℚ`), and a proof that `candidateGenusField` meets it. That proof needs ramification data for `ℚ(√p*)` at `2` and at `∞` — the same gap that blocks the rest of Layer 1, so the ramified-prime splitting behaviour is worth doing first and serves both.

After that, `Gal(K_gen/K) ≅ Cl(K)/Cl(K)²`. The inversion input is done (`mulEquiv_ringOfIntegersQuadraticConj_apply_eq_inv`, and `elementaryTwoQuotientCongr_ringOfIntegersQuadraticConj_apply_eq_self`); what is absent is any reciprocity or Artin-map machinery to connect ideal classes to automorphisms, and the ambiguous-class-number formula of Layer 2, which is the classical alternative route. Once that isomorphism exists, `2-rank = t - 1` for imaginary `K` follows from `finrank_candidateGenusField_over_candidateGenusFieldBase`, which already supplies the `2^(t-1)` on the field side. The real case additionally needs the narrow class group and the narrow genus field, neither defined here.

Smaller, unblocked work: the general-radicand subfield lattice as a packaged order-reversing bijection (only the prime-radicand form exists); the splitting law restated in terms of prime discriminants rather than squarefree radicands; and the class-number computations for `ℚ(√-5)` and `ℚ(√-21)` that the roadmap's acceptance criteria ask for. The status of the erdos CM field `ℚ(i, √q₀, …, √q_{g-1})` as a multiquadratic field of degree `2^(g+1)` cannot be read off the declarations recorded here; the file is present but contributes none.
