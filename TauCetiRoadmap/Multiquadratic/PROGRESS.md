## Multiquadratic: 2026-06-13 to 2026-08-01 (`1d6c6f4` to `6919462`)

The degree theorem is in place: under square-class independence of the radicands (no nonempty subset product a square in `K`), [`[K(√d₁,…,√dₙ) : K] = 2ⁿ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Degree.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.finrank_adjoin_range) (TauCeti#146, TauCeti#147), together with the square-class descent engine it rests on. The Galois side followed: the extension is Galois, an automorphism is determined by its sign pattern on the generators, and the group has exponent exactly two and order `2ⁿ` (TauCeti#150, TauCeti#277, TauCeti#997). Relative degrees and relative Galois groups over an intermediate field are read off the attached `𝔽₂`-subspace (TauCeti#965, TauCeti#1434), and the subfield lattice is counted for prime radicands (TauCeti#424).

The splitting law arrived through Frobenius elements: an arithmetic Frobenius at an odd prime `p` acts on a square root of `d` by `legendreSym p d`, so the Frobenius of a multiquadratic field is the vector of Legendre symbols and is [trivial exactly when every radicand is a residue mod `p`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.NumberField.isArithFrobAt_multiquadratic_eq_one_iff) (TauCeti#1056, TauCeti#173) — stated away from the ramified primes, as the roadmap asks. Alongside it, `Cl/Cl²` was built as the maximal elementary-2 quotient, kept distinct from `Cl[2]` while proved equinumerous with it (TauCeti#294, TauCeti#555), with the exact unit-square index `2^(rank+1)` (TauCeti#1038) and quadratic conjugation shown to invert ideal classes, hence to act trivially on `Cl/Cl²` (TauCeti#1631).

Toward the summit, the [compositum of the prime-discriminant quadratic fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/CandidateGenusField/Basic.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.candidateGenusField) dividing the discriminant of `ℚ(√d)` now exists, with degree `2ᵗ` over `ℚ`, containing `√d`, and of degree `2^(t-1)` over that base with even-parity sign group (TauCeti#1366, TauCeti#1389, TauCeti#1639). It is named the *candidate* genus field, and honestly so: nothing yet identifies it with the maximal unramified extension abelian over `ℚ`, and neither `Gal(K_gen/K) ≅ Cl/Cl²` nor the 2-rank formula is touched. Much of the rest was fundamental-discriminant arithmetic (TauCeti#1045, TauCeti#1095) and file reorganisation.
