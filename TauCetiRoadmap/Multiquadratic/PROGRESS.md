## Multiquadratic: 2026-06-13 to 2026-08-01 (`1d6c6f4` to `6919462`)

The degree theorem is in place: under square-class independence of the radicands (no nonempty subset product a square in `K`), [`[K(√d₁,…,√dₙ) : K] = 2ⁿ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Degree.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.finrank_adjoin_range) (TauCeti#146, TauCeti#147), together with the square-class descent engine it rests on. The Galois side followed: the extension is Galois, an automorphism is determined by its sign pattern on the generators, and the group has exponent exactly two and order `2ⁿ` (TauCeti#150, TauCeti#277, TauCeti#997). Relative degrees and relative Galois groups over an intermediate field are read off the attached `𝔽₂`-subspace (TauCeti#965, TauCeti#1434), and the subfield lattice is counted for prime radicands (TauCeti#424).

The splitting law arrived through Frobenius elements: an arithmetic Frobenius at an odd prime `p` acts on a square root of `d` by `legendreSym p d`, so the Frobenius of a multiquadratic field is the vector of Legendre symbols and is [trivial exactly when every radicand is a residue mod `p`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.NumberField.isArithFrobAt_multiquadratic_eq_one_iff) (TauCeti#1056, TauCeti#173) — stated away from the ramified primes, as the roadmap asks. Alongside it, `Cl/Cl²` was built as the maximal elementary-2 quotient, kept distinct from `Cl[2]` while proved equinumerous with it (TauCeti#294, TauCeti#555), with the exact unit-square index `2^(rank+1)` (TauCeti#1038) and quadratic conjugation shown to invert ideal classes, hence to act trivially on `Cl/Cl²` (TauCeti#1631).

Toward the summit, the [compositum of the prime-discriminant quadratic fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/CandidateGenusField/Basic.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.candidateGenusField) dividing the discriminant of `ℚ(√d)` now exists, with degree `2ᵗ` over `ℚ`, containing `√d`, and of degree `2^(t-1)` over that base with even-parity sign group (TauCeti#1366, TauCeti#1389, TauCeti#1639). It is named the *candidate* genus field, and honestly so: nothing yet identifies it with the maximal unramified extension abelian over `ℚ`, and neither `Gal(K_gen/K) ≅ Cl/Cl²` nor the 2-rank formula is touched. Much of the rest was fundamental-discriminant arithmetic (TauCeti#1045, TauCeti#1095) and file reorganisation.

## Multiquadratic: 2026-08-01 to 2026-09-03 (`6919462` to `0ee6c02`)

The prime-discriminant compositum is now genuinely a genus field, not merely a candidate. For negative squarefree `d`, it is [the genus field of `ℚ(√d)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/GenusField.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.isGenusField_candidateGenusField): it is abelian over `ℚ`, unramified over the quadratic subfield at every place, and maximal with these properties. For any squarefree nonsquare `d`, the same compositum is [the narrow genus field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Unramified/NarrowGenusField.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.isNarrowGenusField_candidateGenusField), with unramifiedness required at finite places. The ramification analysis now includes the delicate prime `2`, which [ramifies exactly when `d` is not `1` modulo `4`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Quadratic/Ramification.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.isUnramifiedIn_two_iff_mod_four_eq_one).

On the class-group side, [the imaginary quadratic 2-rank formula](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Quadratic/TwoRank.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.twoRank_eq_ncard_ramifiedPrimes_sub_one) is proved: `rank₂ Cl(ℚ(√d)) = t - 1`, where `t` is the number of ramified rational primes. The worked examples are complete at this level: [`ℚ(√-5)` has class number `2`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/MinusFive/ClassNumber.html#TauCeti.NumberField.classNumber_eq_two_of_minpoly_eq_X_sq_add_five), while [`ℚ(√-21)` has class number `4`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/MinusTwentyOne/ClassNumber.html#TauCeti.NumberField.classNumber_eq_four_of_minpoly_eq_X_sq_add_twenty_one), with 2-ranks `1` and `2` respectively.

The real quadratic case is not yet complete. The [narrow class group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/NarrowClassGroup/Basic.html#NumberField.NarrowClassGroup) and its elementary-2 quotient now exist, and [genus characters](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Quadratic/GenusCharacter/Basic.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.genusCharFun) have reached coprime ideals and narrow-principal invariance, but only the upper bound `rank₂ Cl⁺ ≤ t - 1` has landed. Likewise, the relative genus-field Galois group and `Cl/Cl²` are known to have [equal order](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/CandidateGenusField/Relative/ClassGroup.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.card_aut_candidateGenusField_over_base_eq_card_elementaryTwoQuotient), not yet a stated isomorphism.

## Multiquadratic: 2026-09-20 to 2026-09-26 (`fb8b54e` to `759eb3e`)

For squarefree radicands, the multiquadratic decomposition law is now complete at every unramified prime. At an odd prime p dividing no radicand, every prime above p is unramified, with [residue degree one exactly when every radicand is a square mod p](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/ResidueDegree.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.inertiaDeg_eq_one_iff_forall_legendreSym_eq_one) and two otherwise; when every radicand is 1 mod 4, 2 is unramified too and [splits completely exactly when every radicand is 1 mod 8](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/ResidueDegree.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.ncard_primesOver_two_eq_finrank_iff). The conjugate-ideal transversal count, migrated from the Erdős unit-distance formalization as a lower bound, is now exact: in a Dedekind domain, if σ pairs off a finite set S of primes without fixed points, [exactly 2^(#S/2) ideals A satisfy A · σA = ∏ S](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/ConjugateFactorization.html#TauCeti.ncard_setOf_mul_map_eq_prod). ## Multiquadratic: 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-01 (`759eb3e` to `8a32441`)

The decomposition law now reaches the odd ramified primes. For squarefree radicands and an odd prime p dividing some radicand, every prime above p has [ramification index 2](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/RamificationIndex.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.ramificationIdx_eq_two) and an [inertia group of order 2](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/RamificationIndex.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.card_inertia_eq_two), whose nontrivial element negates exactly the square roots of the radicands divisible by p. The [residue degree is 1](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/RamificationIndex.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.inertiaDeg_eq_one_iff_of_squarefree) exactly when every radicand prime to p is a square mod p and all the radicands divisible by p have p-free parts with the same Legendre symbol. Otherwise it is 2. Under square-class independence of n radicands, this means p has [2ⁿ⁻¹ primes above it](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/RamificationIndex.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.ncard_primesOver_eq_two_pow_sub_one_of_dvd) or [2ⁿ⁻²](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/RamificationIndex.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.ncard_primesOver_eq_two_pow_sub_two). The proof goes through two general facts about Frobenius elements at possibly ramified primes: the [residue degree divides a Frobenius's order](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius/DecompositionGroup.html#Ideal.inertiaDeg_dvd_orderOf_of_isArithFrobAt), and it [equals 1 exactly when a Frobenius lies in inertia](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Frobenius/DecompositionGroup.html#Ideal.inertiaDeg_eq_one_iff_mem_inertia_of_isArithFrobAt). The ramified prime 2 is still not covered.

The genus field is now identified with its character subfield, which the last report listed as the nearest open target: inside a cyclotomic field of level |D|, the genus field of ℚ(√d) is [the subfield cut out by the genus character group](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Cyclotomic/CandidateGenusField.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.candidateGenusField_eq_map_characterSubfield_genusCharGroup). The genus field is also unique up to isomorphism. An abelian field that is unramified at the finite primes over ℚ(√d) and has the maximal degree 2ᵗ is [isomorphic to the prime-discriminant compositum](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Unramified/Maximality.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.nonempty_algEquiv_candidateGenusField). Any two [genus fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/GenusField.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.IsGenusField.exists_algEquiv_apply_eq) or [narrow genus fields](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Multiquadratic/Unramified/NarrowGenusField.html#TauCeti.Multiquadratic.IsNarrowGenusField.exists_algEquiv_apply_eq) for the same radicand are isomorphic by a map that matches their chosen square roots.
