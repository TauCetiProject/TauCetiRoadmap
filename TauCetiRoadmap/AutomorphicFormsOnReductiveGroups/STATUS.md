### Named results

- **Flath's finite-dimensional factorization** — over an algebraically closed field, a finite-dimensional simple module over `A ⊗ B` is `V ⊗ W` with `V` and `W` simple ([`exists_tensorProduct_equiv_of_isSimpleModule`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TensorProduct/SimpleModule.html#TauCeti.exists_tensorProduct_equiv_of_isSimpleModule)), and the factors are unique up to isomorphism ([`nonempty_linearEquiv_of_tensorPairAction_equiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TensorProduct/SimpleModule.html#TauCeti.nonempty_linearEquiv_of_tensorPairAction_equiv)). This is the corner step of §3.4, proved under other names than the README's `finiteCornerFactorization`.
- **Simple modules and their corners** — for an idempotent `e`, isomorphism classes of simple `A`-modules with nonzero corner correspond bijectively to simple `eAe`-modules ([`simpleCornerSkeletonEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Morita/Corner/Simple.html#IsIdempotentElem.simpleCornerSkeletonEquiv)); this is what lets Flath's argument pass through finite level/type corners.
- **`G`-finite vectors are smooth** — in a strongly continuous representation of a finite-dimensional real Lie group, every vector with finite-dimensional orbit span is a smooth vector ([`IsGFinite.isLieSmoothVector`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/GFiniteLieSmooth.html#Representation.IsGFinite.isLieSmoothVector)).
- **The relative Maschke theorem** — if a subgroup of finite index invertible in `k` acts semisimply, so does the whole group ([`isSemisimpleRepresentation_of_comp_subtype`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Subrepresentation/RelativeMaschke.html#Representation.isSemisimpleRepresentation_of_comp_subtype)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Smooth vectors and the derived representation.** Smooth vectors of a Banach representation of a Lie group ([`IsLieSmoothVector`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/LieSmoothVector.html#ContRepresentation.IsLieSmoothVector)) carry a derived Lie algebra action that is `Ad`-covariant and extends, also after complexification, to a `U(𝔤)`-module structure ([`lieSmoothVectorsEnvelopingAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/LieSmoothVector/Enveloping.html#ContRepresentation.lieSmoothVectorsEnvelopingAction)). For `ℝ` they agree with the smooth vectors of the associated semigroup. This is the carrier on which Harish-Chandra modules will be cut out.
- **Test functions on a Lie group.** Compactly supported smooth functions with translations, inversion and involution, fixed-support seminorms, the LF topology with its continuity criterion, and smooth approximate identities ([`LieTestFunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/TestFunction/Basic.html#TauCeti.LieTestFunction)). The integrated operators of these approximate identities tend strongly to the identity ([`tendsto_lieIntegratedOperator_smoothApproximateIdentity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/Integrated/Lie.html#ContRepresentation.tendsto_lieIntegratedOperator_smoothApproximateIdentity)).
- **Integration and projectors beyond Banach spaces.** A Haar integral of continuous maps into complete locally convex spaces, with uniqueness, the seminorm bound and agreement with the Bochner integral ([`compactIntegral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/LocallyConvex.html#ContinuousMap.compactIntegral)), and the isotypic projectors `e_σ` on Banach representations of compact groups ([`banachIsotypicProjector`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Compact/Character/BanachIsotypicProjection.html#ContRepresentation.banachIsotypicProjector)), which are idempotent and mutually orthogonal.

## The frontier

- **Density of smooth vectors (§2.7).** Prove that `π(a)v` is smooth with `dπ(X)π(a)v = π(−L_X a)v`; together with the approximate-identity limit already proved this gives `lieSmoothVectors_dense`. The word-seminorm Fréchet topology comes next.
- **`compactFinite_dense_locallyConvex` (§2.1).** The integral exists; what remains is the locally convex projectors `e_σ` with their Banach comparison and Peter–Weyl approximation in every continuous seminorm, plus differentiation under the integral.
- **Convolution of Lie test functions (§0.1a).** Convolution with its support bound, associativity and the involution, the Fréchet property of fixed-support spaces and `u_U * a → a`; these precede §0.2's adelic test functions.
- **Admissible and Harish-Chandra modules (§§2.1–2.2).** `IsAdmissible`, `IsHarishChandraModule` and the finite-length and central-finiteness theorems, now that generalized infinitesimal characters exist.
- **Restricted tensor products and Flath (§§3.4–3.5).** The corner factorization is ready; the restricted tensor product, gluing across larger corners and the spherical-line bound remain, and the global theorem also waits on §3.2's automorphic carrier.
