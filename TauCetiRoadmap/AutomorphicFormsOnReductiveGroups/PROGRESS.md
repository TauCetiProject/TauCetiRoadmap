## AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-24 (`f75648f` to `afdb531`)

The finite-dimensional heart of Flath's theorem is in: over an algebraically closed field, a finite-dimensional simple module over `A ⊗ B` is an exterior tensor product of simple modules ([`exists_tensorProduct_equiv_of_isSimpleModule`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TensorProduct/SimpleModule.html#TauCeti.exists_tensorProduct_equiv_of_isSimpleModule)), and its factors are unique up to isomorphism ([`nonempty_linearEquiv_of_tensorPairAction_equiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/TensorProduct/SimpleModule.html#TauCeti.nonempty_linearEquiv_of_tensorPairAction_equiv)). The corner arguments it feeds also landed: a nonzero corner `eV` of a simple module is simple over `eAe` and determines `V` ([`nonempty_linearEquiv_of_corner`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Idempotents/Corner/Simple.html#IsIdempotentElem.nonempty_linearEquiv_of_corner)), and simple `A`-modules with nonzero corner correspond to simple `eAe`-modules ([`simpleCornerSkeletonEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Morita/Corner/Simple.html#IsIdempotentElem.simpleCornerSkeletonEquiv)). Restricted tensor products and the global factorization have not started.

The other main thread is smooth vectors of Banach representations of Lie groups. They are defined ([`IsLieSmoothVector`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/LieSmoothVector.html#ContRepresentation.IsLieSmoothVector)), the derived action is a Lie algebra homomorphism that is covariant under `Ad` and extends to a `U(𝔤)`-module structure ([`lieSmoothVectorsEnvelopingAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/LieSmoothVector/Enveloping.html#ContRepresentation.lieSmoothVectorsEnvelopingAction)), and `G`-finite vectors are smooth ([`IsGFinite.isLieSmoothVector`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/GFiniteLieSmooth.html#Representation.IsGFinite.isLieSmoothVector)). Test functions on a Lie group, with their LF topology and smooth approximate identities, now exist ([`LieTestFunction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/TestFunction/Basic.html#TauCeti.LieTestFunction)), and the integrated operators `π(u_U)` tend strongly to the identity ([`tendsto_lieIntegratedOperator_smoothApproximateIdentity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Continuous/Integrated/Lie.html#ContRepresentation.tendsto_lieIntegratedOperator_smoothApproximateIdentity)). Smoothness of `π(a)v`, and hence density of smooth vectors, is still missing.

Smaller pieces: a Haar integral for continuous maps into complete locally convex spaces ([`compactIntegral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Integral/LocallyConvex.html#ContinuousMap.compactIntegral)), idempotent and mutually orthogonal isotypic projectors on Banach representations of compact groups, `K`-finite vectors, generalized infinitesimal characters with their duals, and the relative Maschke theorem ([`isSemisimpleRepresentation_of_comp_subtype`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Subrepresentation/RelativeMaschke.html#Representation.isSemisimpleRepresentation_of_comp_subtype)).
