## OrthogonalSpinGroups: 2026-09-24 to 2026-09-26 (`2379e11` to `759eb3e`)

The Eichler transvections arrived with their canonical Spin lifts: for an isotropic `u`, the elements `1 + ι w · ι u` form an injective homomorphism from `u^⊥ / K·u` into Spin(Q) whose projection to SO(Q) is `w ↦ E_{u,w}` ([`spinToSpecialOrthogonal_comp_spinTransvectionHom`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Transvection.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinToSpecialOrthogonal_comp_spinTransvectionHom)), so transvections have trivial spinor norm. The local spinor-norm table gained its real rows (for example [`spinorNorm_surjective_of_indefinite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Real/SpinorNorm.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinorNorm_surjective_of_indefinite)), its dimension-one row, and surjectivity on SO(Q) for every nondegenerate isotropic space ([`spinorNorm_surjective_of_not_anisotropic`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/Isotropic.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinorNorm_surjective_of_not_anisotropic)), which settles the isotropic p-adic rows; the image on O(Q) is the image on SO(Q) joined with the class of one anisotropic value ([`spinorNorm_range_orthogonal_eq_sup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/Range.html#CliffordAlgebra.spinorNorm_range_orthogonal_eq_sup)). The anisotropic p-adic rows remain open.

Mathlib's closure-defined Lipschitz group was shown to be exactly the products of anisotropic vectors ([`mem_lipschitzGroup_iff_exists_list`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/Generators.html#CliffordAlgebra.mem_lipschitzGroup_iff_exists_list)), and its elements acting trivially on V are exactly the nonzero scalars ([`ker_lipschitzToOrthogonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/Kernel.html#CliffordAlgebra.ker_lipschitzToOrthogonal)). The reverse Clifford norm [`cliffordNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/ReverseNorm.html#CliffordAlgebra.cliffordNorm) sends a vector to `Q v` with no sign, and Mathlib's star-unitary Spin group is exactly the even Lipschitz elements of reverse norm one ([`mem_spinGroup_iff_mem_even_and_cliffordNorm_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/ReverseNorm.html#CliffordAlgebra.mem_spinGroup_iff_mem_even_and_cliffordNorm_eq_one)). The existing spinor norm was rebuilt on this norm, so a reflection has spinor norm `[Q v]` rather than `[-Q v]`, and the theorem that the image of Spin in SO(Q) is the spinor kernel ([`range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm)) was re-proved on that footing; the spinor norm is now also natural under isometries, field extensions and orthogonal sums. The even unitary carrier [`evenUnitaryGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/EvenUnitary.html#CliffordAlgebra.evenUnitaryGroup) for the low-rank identifications landed, identified so far only in the positive definite real three-dimensional case, as the unit quaternions.

Layer 0 gained the determinant of O(Q), with values in `μ₂`, and the theorem that SO(Q) has index two for nondegenerate Q in positive dimension ([`index_specialOrthogonalWithin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.index_specialOrthogonalWithin)); injective base change of orthogonal groups, compatible with towers; the orthogonal-sum embedding, whose image is exactly the isometries preserving both summands ([`mem_range_orthogonalGroupProd_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Prod.html#QuadraticMap.mem_range_orthogonalGroupProd_iff)); and the conjugation law and two coefficient spellings for reflections.

## OrthogonalSpinGroups: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `e440f4e`)

The low-rank identification of Spin landed in both directions: for a nondegenerate form of dimension one to five, every even Clifford unit with `reverse x * x = 1` already lies in the Lipschitz group, so Spin(Q) is exactly the even unitary group U(C₀, σ) ([`evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Five.html#CliffordAlgebra.evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup)); and over any infinite field of characteristic not two, from dimension six on Spin is a proper subgroup ([`range_spinGroup_toUnits_ne_evenUnitaryGroup_of_six_le_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Six.html#CliffordAlgebra.range_spinGroup_toUnits_ne_evenUnitaryGroup_of_six_le_finrank)), with the split rational witness the roadmap asked for. Along the way Mathlib's closure-defined Lipschitz group was shown to coincide with the classical Clifford group of units whose twisted conjugation preserves the vectors ([`mem_lipschitzGroup_iff_involute_act_ι_mem_range_ι`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/CliffordGroup.html#CliffordAlgebra.mem_lipschitzGroup_iff_involute_act_ι_mem_range_ι)), for a nondegenerate form representing a unit. The dimension-by-dimension readings (quaternion algebras, the split and nonsplit dimension-four cases, the symplectic group) are not yet stated.

Layer 1's loose ends were largely tied: the rescaling criterion, that a Lipschitz element scales into Spin exactly when it is even with square Clifford norm, is now its own theorem ([`exists_scalarUnits_mul_mem_spinGroup_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/ReverseNorm.html#CliffordAlgebra.exists_scalarUnits_mul_mem_spinGroup_iff)); Spin → SO is shown not onto for the rational hyperbolic plane; and the Lipschitz and Spin groups base change along field extensions, carrying transvection lifts to transvection lifts. Layer 0 gained SO(Q) ≃* SO(B) for the polar form ([`specialOrthogonalGroupEquivSpecialIsometryGroupPolarBilin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.specialOrthogonalGroupEquivSpecialIsometryGroupPolarBilin)), completing the bilinear dictionary.

Local topology began: `V ≃ₗ[K] V` is a topological group in the forward-and-inverse topology ([`instIsTopologicalGroupLinearEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Module/GeneralLinearGroup.html#TauCeti.instIsTopologicalGroupLinearEquiv)), the module topology is Hausdorff and locally compact over a finite basis, the orthogonal group is closed in the endomorphisms ([`isClosed_range_orthogonalGroup_toLinearMap`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Closed.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.isClosed_range_orthogonalGroup_toLinearMap)), and the Clifford norm and the Spin transvection lifts are continuous.
