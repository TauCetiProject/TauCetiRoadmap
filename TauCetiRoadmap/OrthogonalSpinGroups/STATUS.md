## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0 and 2 are done. Layer 1 lacks only the symplectic reading of dimension five. Layer 3 has its compact-open data, the finite and full adelic point groups with their rational diagonals, and the adelic spinor norm. It lacks the points away from a finite set of places, discreteness of the rational points in the full adeles, and the double cosets.

### Named results

- **The image of Spin is the spinor kernel** — for a nondegenerate form over a field of characteristic not two, the image of `Spin(Q) → SO(Q)` is exactly the kernel of the spinor norm ([`range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm)).
- **Spin is the even unitary group up to dimension five, and not beyond** — for `1 ≤ dim V ≤ 5`, Spin(Q) = U(C₀, σ) ([`evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Five.html#CliffordAlgebra.evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup)). Over an infinite field the inclusion is strict from dimension six on ([`range_spinGroup_toUnits_ne_evenUnitaryGroup_of_six_le_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Six.html#CliffordAlgebra.range_spinGroup_toUnits_ne_evenUnitaryGroup_of_six_le_finrank)).
- **Nonsplit quaternary Spin is a quaternion norm-one group** — in dimension four, when the discriminant algebra is a field `E`, Spin(Q) is isomorphic to the norm-one group of a quaternion algebra over `E` ([`exists_spinGroupEquivQuaternionUnitaryOverCenter_of_finrank_eq_four`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/NonsplitCenter.html#CliffordAlgebra.exists_spinGroupEquivQuaternionUnitaryOverCenter_of_finrank_eq_four)).
- **Compactness criterion** — over a locally compact field such as ℝ or `ℚ_p`, O(Q) is compact exactly when Q is anisotropic ([`isCompact_orthogonalGroup_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Compact.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.isCompact_orthogonalGroup_iff)). The same holds for Spin, because `Spin → SO` is a proper map ([`isProperMap_spinToSpecialOrthogonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Proper.html#CliffordAlgebra.isProperMap_spinToSpecialOrthogonal)).
- **Local spinor kernel indices** — over `ℚ_p` in dimension at least three the spinor norm is onto, and the image of Spin has index four for odd p ([`index_range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_four_of_three_le_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/SpinorNorm/HigherRank.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.index_range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_four_of_three_le_finrank)) and index eight at p = 2.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`cliffordNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/ReverseNorm.html#CliffordAlgebra.cliffordNorm), the reverse norm on the Lipschitz group. The spinor norm descends from it, and its continuity makes the local spinor norm continuous and the spinor kernel open.
- [`fullAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/FullAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.fullAdelicSpin) and its finite counterpart [`finiteAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/FiniteAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.finiteAdelicSpin), with their O and SO companions, are restricted products over a compatible compact-open tuple. Injective rational diagonals such as [`fullAdelicSpinDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Diagonal/Full.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.fullAdelicSpinDiagonal) map into them and commute with Spin → SO → O. These are the objects any discreteness or approximation statement will be about.
- [`adelicSpinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/AdelicSpinorNorm.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.adelicSpinorNorm), the continuous spinor norm on finite adelic SO, whose p-component is the local spinor norm. Its kernel contains the image of adelic Spin.

## The frontier

- **Points away from S (3D)** — `G(𝔸^S)` for O, SO and Spin, with the restrictions from `G(𝔸_f)`, the componentwise maps, and the diagonals in that flavour. The generic away-from-S restricted products exist in the library; the orthogonal instantiation does not.
- **Discreteness in the full adeles (3E)** — `G(ℚ)` discrete in `G(𝔸)`. The full adelic diagonals it is a statement about are now in place.
- **The finite-adelic counterexample (3E)** — for a split rational space of dimension at least three, highly divisible integer transvection parameters accumulate at the identity in `SO(V)(𝔸_f)`, so the diagonal image is not discrete there.
- **Double cosets (3G)** — `G(ℚ) \ G(𝔸_f) / U` with its three comparison maps: for an inclusion of compact opens, for conjugate compact opens, and for a componentwise equivalence of tuples. Nothing is stated yet.
- **Dimension five (1F)** — U(C₀, σ) as the symplectic group `Sp(C₀, σ)` of a degree-four central simple algebra with symplectic involution, and `Spin₅ ≅ Sp₄` in the split case. Only the degree count is in place.
