## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** All four layers are done. The roadmap now goes from the orthogonal group and its spinor norm, through the local topology and the p-adic spinor-norm table, to adelic O, SO and Spin in all three flavours. At that end sit their rational diagonals, the adelic spinor norm, discreteness in the full adeles, and the class sets. What remains is owned by successor roadmaps.

### Named results

- **The image of Spin is the spinor kernel** — for a nondegenerate form over a field of characteristic not two, the image of `Spin(Q) → SO(Q)` is exactly the kernel of the spinor norm ([`range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm)).
- **Spin is the even unitary group up to dimension five, and not beyond** — for `1 ≤ dim V ≤ 5`, Spin(Q) = U(C₀, σ) ([`evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Five.html#CliffordAlgebra.evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup)). From dimension six the inclusion is strict, and for three hyperbolic planes Spin is SL₄(K) inside U(C₀, σ) ≅ GL₄(K) ([`hyperbolicSixSpinEquivSpecialLinear`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Six/SpecialLinear.html#TauCeti.hyperbolicSixSpinEquivSpecialLinear)).
- **Spin₅ is a twisted form of Sp₄** — over any separably closed extension, the even Clifford algebra of a five-dimensional form becomes M₄ with reversal as the symplectic adjoint, and Spin(Q) embeds in Sp₄ ([`exists_injective_spinGroup_symplecticGroup_of_finrank_eq_five`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Symplectic.html#CliffordAlgebra.exists_injective_spinGroup_symplecticGroup_of_finrank_eq_five)). When that algebra already splits over K, Spin(Q) ≅ Sp₄(K) ([`exists_spinGroupEquivSymplecticGroup_of_finrank_eq_five`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Symplectic.html#CliffordAlgebra.exists_spinGroupEquivSymplecticGroup_of_finrank_eq_five)).
- **Compactness criterion** — over a locally compact field such as ℝ or `ℚ_p`, O(Q) is compact exactly when Q is anisotropic ([`isCompact_orthogonalGroup_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Compact.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.isCompact_orthogonalGroup_iff)). The same holds for SO, and for Spin, because `Spin → SO` is proper.
- **Rational points are discrete in the full adeles, not the finite ones** — for every compatible compact-open tuple, rational O, SO and Spin are discrete in `G(𝔸)` ([`discreteTopology_range_fullAdelicSpinDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Diagonal/Discrete.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.discreteTopology_range_fullAdelicSpinDiagonal)). For an isotropic rational space of dimension at least three they are not discrete in `G(𝔸_f)`, because rational transvections accumulate at the identity ([`not_discreteTopology_range_finiteAdelicSpinDiagonal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Diagonal/NonDiscrete/Spin.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.not_discreteTopology_range_finiteAdelicSpinDiagonal)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The adelic point groups in all three flavours: finite ([`finiteAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/FiniteAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.finiteAdelicSpin)), away from S ([`awayAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/AwayAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.awayAdelicSpin)) and full ([`fullAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/FullAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.fullAdelicSpin)), each also for O and SO. All of them come with the injective rational diagonals and the componentwise maps Spin → SO → O. These are what a strong-approximation or Tamagawa statement for orthogonal groups will be about.
- [`adelicSpinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/AdelicSpinorNorm.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.adelicSpinorNorm), the continuous spinor norm on finite adelic SO, whose p-component is the local spinor norm. The successor needs its kernel to describe the closure of the rational Spin image.
- The class sets `G(ℚ) \ G(𝔸_f) / U`, such as [`finiteAdelicSpinDoubleCoset`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/DoubleCoset/Basic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.finiteAdelicSpinDoubleCoset). They have separate comparisons for an inclusion of compact opens (a surjection), for conjugate compact opens (a bijection), and for a componentwise equivalence of tuples. These are where genera and spinor genera will be counted.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layers 0 and 2** were already done. Layer 2 has since gained the compactness criterion for SO and the spinor norm of an anisotropic binary form on O. **Layer 1** is now done. 1F covers every dimension from zero to six: Spin = U(C₀, σ) for dimensions one to five, and dimension zero is shown to be the exception. Dimension three is the quaternion reading, including the rational sum of three squares. Dimension four has both the split and the nonsplit cases, with the rational models `H ⟂ H` and x² + y² + z² + 2w². Dimension five is symplectic. Dimension six has the strictness and the split SL₄ ⊂ GL₄ witness. The symplectic group appears through a splitting extension, not as an intrinsic carrier `Sp(C₀, σ)`, which the roadmap assigns to `AlgebrasWithInvolution`. **Layer 3** is now done. Every milestone from 3C to 3G is in place, including the points away from S, discreteness in the full adeles, the finite-adelic counterexample (proved for isotropic spaces, not just split ones), and the double cosets with all three comparisons. As the roadmap specifies, the double cosets are not claimed to be finite.
