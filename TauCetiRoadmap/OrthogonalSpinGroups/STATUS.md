### Named results

- **The image of Spin is the spinor kernel**: for a nondegenerate form over a field of characteristic not two, the image of `Spin(Q) → SO(Q)` is exactly the kernel of the spinor norm ([`range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/Basic.html#CliffordAlgebra.range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_ker_spinorNorm)).
- **Spin is the even unitary group up to dimension five, and not beyond**: for `1 ≤ dim V ≤ 5`, Spin(Q) = U(C₀, σ) ([`evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Five.html#CliffordAlgebra.evenUnitaryGroup_le_lipschitzGroup)). Over an infinite field the inclusion is strict from dimension six on ([`range_spinGroup_toUnits_ne_evenUnitaryGroup_of_six_le_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/LowRank/Six.html#CliffordAlgebra.range_spinGroup_toUnits_ne_evenUnitaryGroup_of_six_le_finrank)).
- **Compactness criterion**: over a locally compact field such as ℝ or `ℚ_p`, O(Q) is compact exactly when Q is anisotropic ([`isCompact_orthogonalGroup_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Compact.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.isCompact_orthogonalGroup_iff)). The same holds for Spin, because `Spin → SO` is a proper map ([`isProperMap_spinToSpecialOrthogonal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Proper.html#CliffordAlgebra.isProperMap_spinToSpecialOrthogonal)).
- **Local spinor kernel indices**: over `ℚ_p` in dimension at least three the spinor norm is onto, and the image of Spin has index four for odd p ([`index_range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_four_of_three_le_finrank`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/QuadraticForm/SpinorNorm/HigherRank.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.index_range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_four_of_three_le_finrank)) and index eight at p = 2.
- **Reference images at almost every prime**: for the integral compact opens of a rational basis in dimension at least two, the local spinor-norm image of `U_p^{SO}` is the group of unit square classes at all but finitely many primes ([`eventually_localSpinorNormImage_integral_eq_unitSquareClasses`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/Integral/CompactOpen.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.eventually_localSpinorNormImage_integral_eq_unitSquareClasses)). In dimension one this fails for every tuple, and the library records that.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [`cliffordNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/ReverseNorm.html#CliffordAlgebra.cliffordNorm), the reverse norm on the Lipschitz group. The spinor norm descends from it, and its continuity makes the local spinor norm continuous and the spinor kernel open.
- [`finiteAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/FiniteAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.finiteAdelicSpin) and [`fullAdelicSpin`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/FullAdelic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.fullAdelicSpin), with their O and SO companions. These are the restricted products over a compatible compact-open tuple, and the full versions add a real factor. Continuous maps Spin → SO → O connect them.
- [`adelicSpinorNorm`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/AdelicSpinorNorm.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.adelicSpinorNorm), the continuous spinor norm on finite adelic SO. Its target is the restricted product of square classes relative to the reference images, and its p-component is the local spinor norm. Its kernel, the [adelic spinor kernel](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticForm/OrthogonalGroup/AdelicSpinorNorm.html#TauCeti.QuadraticMap.OrthogonalCompactOpens.adelicSpinorKernel), contains the image of adelic Spin.

### Roadmap coverage

**Layer 0** is done, worked examples included. **Layer 2** is now done as well. The library has SO(Q) closed and open in O(Q), locally compact, with a continuous determinant, which completes 2B. **Layer 1** is partial. 1A to 1E are done. 1F has Spin = U(C₀, σ) through dimension five and strictness in dimension six. It also has the three-dimensional quaternion reading and the split dimension-four case over a general field, where Spin is a product of two norm-one quaternion groups. The nonsplit dimension-four case has only the count that C₀ has dimension four over its centre. Dimension five has only the degree count, not the symplectic group. **Layer 3** is partial. 3C is done: openness of the reference images, their identification at almost every prime, and the dimension-one rejection test. 3F is done, evaluation rule included. 3D has finite and full adelic points but not the points away from S. 3E has the integral carriers and the finite adelic diagonals for all three groups, with injectivity and the commuting Spin/SO square. It lacks the full adelic diagonals, discreteness in `G(𝔸)` and the finite-adelic counterexample. 3G is untouched.

## The frontier

- **Points away from S (3D)**: `G(𝔸^S)` for O, SO and Spin, with the restrictions from `G(𝔸_f)` and the componentwise maps in that flavour.
- **Discreteness and its counterexample (3E)**: the full adelic diagonals and `G(ℚ)` discrete in `G(𝔸)`. Also the transvection-family counterexample showing it is not discrete in `G(𝔸_f)`. The full adelic groups and finite diagonals are in place.
- **Double cosets (3G)**: `G(ℚ) \ G(𝔸_f) / U` with its three comparison maps, for an inclusion of compact opens, for conjugate compact opens, and for a componentwise equivalence of tuples. Nothing is stated yet.
- **Nonsplit dimension four (1F)**: C₀ as a quaternion algebra over the discriminant field, with Spin its norm-one group. The dimension count over the centre ([`finrank_even_over_center_of_finrank_eq_four`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Even/Center.html#CliffordAlgebra.finrank_even_over_center_of_finrank_eq_four)) is the first step.
- **Dimension five (1F)**: U(C₀, σ) as the symplectic group `Sp(C₀, σ)` of a degree-four central simple algebra.
