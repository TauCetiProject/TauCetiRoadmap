## PositiveMass: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-25 (`e827b40` to `522d48f`)

The roadmap's first window laid down the objects the positive mass theorem is about. An [asymptotically Euclidean end](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/AsymptoticallyEuclidean/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsAsymptoticallyEuclideanEnd) is now a parametrization of the exterior of a ball whose metric coefficients decay to `δ_ij` with two derivatives, and [finite-ended structures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/AsymptoticallyEuclidean/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsFiniteEndedAsymptoticallyEuclidean) collect finitely many disjoint ones. The ADM mass enters as the limit of the [normalized coordinate flux](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/AsymptoticallyEuclidean/Flux.html#TauCeti.normalizedCoordinateFlux), and the roadmap's normalization checks are proved: the metric `(1 + c r^(2−n))^(4/(n−2)) δ` [has mass 2c](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/AsymptoticallyEuclidean/Flux.html#TauCeti.tendsto_normalizedCoordinateFlux_conformal_one_add_mul_rpow), so spatial Schwarzschild has mass m. The headline is the [conformal change of mass](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/AsymptoticallyEuclidean/Conformal.html#TauCeti.IsAsymptoticallyEuclideanEnd.tendsto_normalizedCoordinateFlux_rpow_mul): if an end has mass m and `u = 1 + c r^(2−n)` to first order, then `u^(4/(n−2)) g` has mass m + 2c (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/2449). Here the mass is assumed to exist; the theorem that the flux converges under integrable scalar curvature has not landed.

Around this came the geometry of an end: its decay classes (the inverse metric and the [volume density](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/AsymptoticallyEuclidean/Decay.html#TauCeti.IsAsymptoticallyEuclideanEnd.isSymbolAtInfinity_sqrt_det_pullbackMetricCoeff_sub_one) differ from the Euclidean ones by `O₂(|x|^(−t))`), the [distance asymptotics](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/AsymptoticallyEuclidean/Distance.html#TauCeti.IsAsymptoticallyEuclideanEnd.isLittleO_dist_sub_norm) `d(p, φ(x)) = |x| + o(|x|)`, and invariance of the flux under isometries together with [lifting finite-ended structures to finite Riemannian covers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/AsymptoticallyEuclidean/Covering.html#TauCeti.IsFiniteEndedAsymptoticallyEuclidean.exists_lift), each lifted end keeping the mass of the end below. Bartnik's [weighted Lᵖ_δ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Sobolev/Weighted.html#TauCeti.MemWeightedLp) and Sobolev classes were set up on Euclidean exteriors, with the sharp statement that `O(r^(−t))` decay gives membership exactly for δ > −t. A general calculus of symbols at infinity supports all of this.

One piece of the nonspin route also arrived: Lohkamp's smooth [concave truncation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/ConcaveTruncation.html#TauCeti.concaveTruncation), which turns a harmonic function below 1 into a superharmonic one that is constant far out, with the [choice of levels](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Harmonic/ConcaveTruncation.html#TauCeti.exists_concaveTruncation_comp_of_tendsto_one) needed to make a negative-mass end exactly flat (TauCeti#42029).
