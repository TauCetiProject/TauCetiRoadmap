## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The divided-power augmentation section (§0.DP.3) is done, and its two predecessors are partly done: homogeneous polynomial laws and the grading of the divided power algebra are in place, but multiplicative laws and Roby representability are not. Everything from the Koszul complex onward, including the cotangent complex, derived limits, completion, effectivity and Matlis duality, has not begun.

### Named results

**The grading of the divided power algebra** — over any commutative ring, `Γ_R(M)` is the [internal direct sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Grading.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.isInternal_homogeneousSubmodule) of its degree-`d` pieces, and so an [ℕ-graded algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Grading.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.instGradedAlgebra), with directness proved without dividing by factorials.

**Degree-one polynomial laws are linear maps** — a polynomial law is [homogeneous of degree one exactly when it is the base change of a linear map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PolynomialLaw/Homogeneous.html#PolynomialLaw.isHomogeneousOfDegree_one_iff), namely of its ground map; this is the degree-one case of Roby's correspondence.

**Generators of the augmentation ideal** — the kernel of the augmentation is [generated by the positive divided powers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.augmentationIdeal_eq_span), with no freeness hypothesis on `M`.

**Degree one does not suffice** — in `Γ_{𝔽₂}(𝔽₂)`, `γ₂(1)` [is not in the ideal generated by the degree-one elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.dp_two_not_mem_span_dp_one), detected by an ordinary algebra map to the dual numbers.

**Base ideal meets augmentation ideal in their product** — for an ideal `I` of `R`, the extended ideal `IΓ` [meets the augmentation ideal exactly in `IΓ · Γ_R(M)₊`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.map_algebraMap_inf_augmentationIdeal_eq_mul), with no flatness assumption.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- [Homogeneous polynomial laws](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PolynomialLaw/Homogeneous.html#PolynomialLaw.IsHomogeneousOfDegree), tested after every scalar extension rather than on ground points. They form a submodule for each degree, and composition multiplies degrees. This is the target side of Roby representability.
- The [augmentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.augmentation) `Γ_R(M) → R`, natural in `M` and along base change. It comes with the [splitting of `Γ_R(M)` into scalars plus augmentation ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.augmentationSplitting).
- The [splitting of any augmented algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Ideal/IsAugmentation.html#AlgHom.equivProdKer) into scalars and the kernel, proved once for an arbitrary algebra map to `R` and then specialised to divided powers.

## The frontier

- **Multiplicative polynomial laws** (§0.DP.1) — define `PolynomialLaw.IsMultiplicative`, then prove that it is closed under composition and that degree-one multiplicative laws to `A` are algebra maps. This is self-contained, and the homogeneous half it mirrors is in place.
- **The universal homogeneous law `γ^univ_d`** (§0.DP.2) — construct the degree-`d` law `M → Γ^d_A(M)` with its coefficient formula. The grading it needs has landed, and every later Roby statement depends on it.
- **Roby representability and base change** (§0.DP.2) — show that `Hom_A(Γ^d_A(M), N)` is the module of degree-`d` homogeneous laws with no flatness assumption, and deduce `B ⊗ Γ^d_A(M) ≅ Γ^d_B(B ⊗ M)`. The prerequisite is `γ^univ_d`.
- **The Koszul complex of a finite sequence** (§0.K.1) — this has no dependence on the divided-power lane and can start now from Mathlib's exterior powers and regular sequences.
- **Mittag-Leffler and the six-term sequence for towers** (§1.T.1) — extend the existing `lim`/`lim¹` file with the Mittag-Leffler vanishing criterion and the long exact sequence of a short exact sequence of towers.
