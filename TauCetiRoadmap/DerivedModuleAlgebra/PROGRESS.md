## DerivedModuleAlgebra: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`4b18c0c` to `273ea28`)

The first window of this roadmap built its integral-power inputs. Divided power algebras are now graded: the degree-`d` pieces form an [internal direct sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Grading.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.isInternal_homogeneousSubmodule) making `Γ_R(M)` an [ℕ-graded algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Grading.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.instGradedAlgebra) over any commutative ring, with no division by factorials (TauCeti#18740). The [augmentation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.augmentation) `Γ_R(M) → R` came with a full account of its kernel: the augmentation ideal is [generated by the positive divided powers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.augmentationIdeal_eq_span), but [not by the degree-one ones](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.dp_two_not_mem_span_dp_one), since `γ₂(1)` escapes them over `𝔽₂`. An extended base ideal [meets the augmentation ideal exactly in their product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Augmentation.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.map_algebraMap_inf_augmentationIdeal_eq_mul), proved for any augmented algebra and then specialised.

On the polynomial-law side, [homogeneity of degree `n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PolynomialLaw/Homogeneous.html#PolynomialLaw.IsHomogeneousOfDegree) is defined after every scalar extension, and [the degree-one laws are exactly the base changes of linear maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PolynomialLaw/Homogeneous.html#PolynomialLaw.isHomogeneousOfDegree_one_iff) (TauCeti#18403). Multiplicative laws have not been started, and neither has the universal law `γ_d`, so Roby representability is still ahead. Nothing yet touches the Koszul, cotangent, completion or duality parts of the roadmap.

## DerivedModuleAlgebra: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

Roby's representability theorem landed: composition with the universal law `γ_d` [identifies linear maps `Γ^d_R(M) → N` with homogeneous degree-`d` polynomial laws `M → N`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/Representability.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.homogeneousLawEquiv), with no flatness hypothesis on `M` (TauCeti#20454). The [universal law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/UniversalLaw.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.gamma) came first, and the surjectivity half runs through new [coefficients of polynomial laws](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PolynomialLaw/Coeff.html#PolynomialLaw.coeff) at the generic combination `∑ Xⱼ ⊗ mⱼ`. Alongside it, [base change of divided power algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/BaseChange.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.baseChangeEquiv) gives `S ⊗_R Γ_R(M) ≅ Γ_S(S ⊗_R M)` as `S`-algebras for any commutative `S`, flat or not, together with its [degree-`d` restriction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DividedPowerAlgebra/BaseChange.html#DividedPowerAlgebra.baseChangeHomogeneousEquiv), built using a divided-power exponential. Multiplicative polynomial laws are now defined, and in degree one they are [exactly the algebra maps](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PolynomialLaw/Multiplicative.html#PolynomialLaw.algHomEquiv). This part still lacks the tensor map on `Γ^d`, the Roby algebra of multiplicative laws and the comparison with symmetric tensors.

The [Koszul complex](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/KoszulComplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.koszulComplex) of a linear functional arrived with [zeroth homology `R/(xᵢ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/KoszulComplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.koszulComplex.homologyZeroEquivSpan), the [wedge homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/KoszulComplex/Basic.html#TauCeti.koszulComplex.smulHomotopy) showing that every value of `φ` kills homology, and [acyclicity in positive degrees for a weakly regular sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/KoszulComplex/Regular.html#TauCeti.koszulComplex.exactAt_of_isWeaklyRegular). The identification `K(x, y) ≅ K(x) ⊗ K(y)` is not there yet.

For towers of modules, [Mittag-Leffler towers have vanishing `lim¹`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/MittagLeffler.html#TauCeti.ModuleTower.subsingleton_limOne_of_isMittagLeffler), and a levelwise short exact sequence of towers now has its [connecting map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/Exact.html#TauCeti.ModuleTower.connecting) and an exact six-term sequence. The multiplication-by-`p` tower is the worked example: its [`lim¹` is `ℤ_p/ℤ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Tower/Padic.html#TauCeti.ModuleTower.limOneMultiplicationTowerEquiv) by `p`-adic summation, so it is not Mittag-Leffler. Separately, [every module has an injective envelope](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Injective/Envelope/Existence.html#TauCeti.exists_isInjectiveEnvelope), which Matlis duality will need.
