## QuadraticCharactersAndReduction: 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-11 (`21fe702` to `1c6a1a4`)

The first work on this roadmap is the trichotomy for quadratic algebras, the opening step of Layer 3 (TauCeti#35765). [`QuadraticAlgebra.trichotomy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/QuadraticAlgebra/Trichotomy.html#QuadraticAlgebra.trichotomy) says that over any field with `2 ≠ 0`, the algebra `K[X]/(X² − r)` is either `K × K`, a field, or the ring of dual numbers. Each shape is pinned down by a condition on `r`: it splits exactly when `r` is a nonzero square ([`nonempty_algEquiv_prod_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/QuadraticAlgebra/Trichotomy.html#QuadraticAlgebra.nonempty_algEquiv_prod_iff)), is a field exactly when `r` is not a square, in any characteristic ([`isField_iff_not_isSquare`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/QuadraticAlgebra/Trichotomy.html#QuadraticAlgebra.isField_iff_not_isSquare)), and is the dual numbers exactly when `r = 0`. The roadmap's finite-field statement is [`FiniteField.quadraticAlgebra_trichotomy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/QuadraticAlgebra/Trichotomy.html#FiniteField.quadraticAlgebra_trichotomy), for fields of odd cardinality. In even cardinality the algebra is always the dual numbers and never `F × F`, so the oddness hypothesis is needed.

The repeated-root case comes with an explicit isomorphism [`algEquivDualNumberOfDoubleRoot`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/QuadraticAlgebra/DualNumber.html#QuadraticAlgebra.algEquivDualNumberOfDoubleRoot) for the polynomial `(X − r)²`. It also has its norm `(x + ry)²` and its nilpotents, and the dual numbers are shown to be neither a field nor reduced. The split case gained its norm, trace and conjugation in coordinates. The Frobenius identities and the integral Frobenius congruence, which complete Layer 3, have not been started.

## QuadraticCharactersAndReduction: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

Quadratic Dirichlet characters are now classified at every prime-power level. At an odd prime power a character is quadratic exactly when it is principal or the lifted Legendre character ([the odd prime-power classification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/OddPrimePower/Quadratic.html#DirichletCharacter.isQuadratic_iff_eq_one_or_eq_changeLevel_quadraticChar)), and at level `2^k` the quadratic characters are the principal one and the lifts of `χ₄`, `χ₈` and `χ₈′` ([the dyadic classification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/Dyadic/Classification.html#DirichletCharacter.isQuadratic_iff_of_level_two_pow)). A new [local factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/LocalFactor.html#DirichletCharacter.localFactor) splits any character into prime-power pieces, and [the conductor is the product of the local conductors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/LocalFactor.html#DirichletCharacter.conductor_eq_prod_conductor_localFactor); for a quadratic character these are `1` or `p` at odd `p` and `1`, `4` or `8` at `2`. A complex character is also shown to be quadratic exactly when its values are real. Still missing from Layer 1 is the discriminant theorem for an arbitrary, possibly imprimitive, quadratic character.

The integral Frobenius congruence landed: if `r` is not a square modulo the prime `p`, then `(u + v√r)^p ≡ u − v√r (mod p)` in `ℤ[√r]` ([`frobenius_congruence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticOrder/Frobenius.html#QuadraticAlgebra.frobenius_congruence)), and the hypothesis is shown to be sharp, including at `2` (TauCeti#37843). It rests on Frobenius over a finite field, where `z^Q` is the conjugate of `z` exactly in the inert case ([`pow_card_eq_star`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/QuadraticAlgebra/FiniteField.html#QuadraticAlgebra.pow_card_eq_star)), and on reduction modulo `m`, now complete: `ℤ[ω]/(m)` is the quadratic algebra over `ℤ/m` for every `m`, including `0` ([`quotientSpanNatCastEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticOrder/Reduction.html#QuadraticAlgebra.quotientSpanNatCastEquiv)), with `m²` elements. The work also covers injective reduction on coordinate rectangles and the comparison of `ℤ[√d]` with `ℤ[(1 + √d)/2]`, which is an isomorphism modulo every odd `m` and fails modulo `2` ([`quotientRingEquivOfDiscrZero`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/QuadraticOrder/IndexTwo.html#QuadraticAlgebra.quotientRingEquivOfDiscrZero)).

Finally, the two complex embeddings of `ℚ[√r]` exist for every rational `r`, every `ℚ`-algebra map to `ℂ` is one of them, and each satisfies the coordinate bound `‖σ(u + vω)‖ ≤ |u| + |v|√|r|` ([`norm_embedding_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Quadratic/EmbeddingBound.html#QuadraticAlgebra.norm_embedding_le)).
