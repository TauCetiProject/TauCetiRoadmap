## ConvexLifting: 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-19 (`888730a` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first layer, the exact lift of a monotone composition, landed in one piece (https://git.taucetiproject.org/taucetiproject/tauceti/pulls/295). A constraint `h (g x) ≤ b` on a domain `D` cuts out the [original set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Order/CompositionLift.html#TauCeti.CompositionLift.originalSet). Adding an auxiliary height `y` with `g x ≤ y` and `h y ≤ b` gives the [lifted set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Order/CompositionLift.html#TauCeti.CompositionLift.liftedSet). The [exact projection theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Order/CompositionLift.html#TauCeti.CompositionLift.fst_image_liftedSet) says that the lift projects onto the original set whenever `g` maps `D` into the outer domain and `h` is monotone there. No convexity or differentiability is needed, and the outer function may take values in any preorder, not just the reals. The same layer gives the [epigraph criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Order/CompositionLift.html#TauCeti.CompositionLift.epigraph_comp_eq) in the form Mathlib's convex-epigraph API expects. It also shows that an objective in the original variables has the [same minimisers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Order/CompositionLift.html#TauCeti.CompositionLift.isMinOn_comp_fst_liftedSet_iff) on both sets and [attains its minimum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Order/CompositionLift.html#TauCeti.CompositionLift.exists_isMinOn_liftedSet_iff) on one exactly when it does on the other.

A companion examples file works through every check the roadmap asks for. These include a [decreasing outer function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Order/CompositionLift.html#TauCeti.CompositionLift.fst_image_liftedSet_sq_one_sub_ne) whose lift projects onto strictly more than the original set, so the monotonicity hypothesis cannot be dropped. Another is an open interval on which [neither problem attains its infimum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/Order/CompositionLift.html#TauCeti.CompositionLift.not_exists_isMinOn_liftedSet_Ioo). The finite mixed-integer models, the derivative-matched outer approximations and the finite support elimination haven't been started yet.
