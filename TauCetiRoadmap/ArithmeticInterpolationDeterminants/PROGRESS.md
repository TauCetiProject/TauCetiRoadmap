## ArithmeticInterpolationDeterminants: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13 (`80d8d86` to `aa6e43e`)

The headline of this first window is [the confluent Vandermonde determinant](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/ConfluentVandermonde.html#Matrix.det_confluentVandermonde): over every commutative ring, the matrix of Hasse jets of the monomials at nodes `a i` with multiplicities `m i` has determinant `∏_{i<j} (a j − a i)^(m i · m j)`, with the sign fixed by the lexicographic order of the jet rows (TauCeti#37106). It comes with its behaviour under reordering, translating and scaling the nodes, the nonvanishing criterion over a domain, and the reduction to the classical Vandermonde determinant at simple nodes.

Most of the window went into the general determinant toolkit that an interpolation determinant is estimated with. On the algebraic side there are [determinants lying in the product of the row ideals](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Determinant/IdealDivisibility.html#Matrix.det_mem_prod_row_ideals), invariance of minors under unitriangular row changes, [the Gram determinant under a change of family](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Gram/ChangeOfFamily.html#Matrix.det_gram_matrix_smul), and [Hasse derivatives of a product landing in an ideal power](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/DerivativeIdealPow.html#Polynomial.hasseDeriv_prod_mem_ideal_pow), the lemma of Iwaniec–Kowalski §11.4. Minors and row independence now [lift along ring maps that need not be injective](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Rank.html#Matrix.linearIndependent_row_of_map), such as reduction mod a prime, and a basis reduces modulo an arbitrary extended ideal. For size, there is [the row-dependent factorial bound](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/AbsoluteValue.html#Matrix.abv_det_le_factorial_prod) `abv (det A) ≤ n! ∏ B_i` for any absolute value, Hadamard's inequality for square matrices, and [the sharp bound on evaluated Hasse derivatives](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Polynomial/HasseDerivBound.html#Polynomial.norm_eval_hasseDeriv_le) packaged as row bounds for jet matrices. Finally, the integral-norm side closes with [the prime-sum lower bound](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/RingOfIntegers/Norm.html#NumberField.RingOfIntegers.sum_mul_log_le_finrank_mul_log) `∑ e_p log p ≤ n log C` for a nonzero algebraic integer.

The Frobenius substitution is only begun: [the power substitution](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MvPolynomial/PowerSubstitution.html#MvPolynomial.powerSubstitution) `F ↦ F(X, X^B)` with its injectivity below `Z`-degree `B`, and [the linearity of Hasse derivatives of order below `p^e`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Polynomial/Expand.html#Polynomial.hasseDeriv_expand_mul) over polynomials in `X^(p^e)`. The derivative identities through the substitution, the Frobenius-graph nonvanishing test, the jet evaluation map, and Hermite interpolation itself have not landed.
