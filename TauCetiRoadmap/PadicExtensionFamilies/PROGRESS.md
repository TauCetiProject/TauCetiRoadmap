## PadicExtensionFamilies: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-19 (`31a5ca7` to `b235000`)

The roadmap's first window fills in the arithmetic of the page header in Layer 0. The headline is the [height](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Discriminant/Height.html#TauCeti.height) `H = c/f + 1 − e` of a finite separable extension of nonarchimedean local fields, with its [mean](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Discriminant/Height.html#TauCeti.mean) `m = H/e` (TauCeti#41400). On totally ramified subextensions [the height is Serre's wild exponent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/MassFormula/Basic.html#IntermediateField.wildExponent_eq_height) from the mass formula, so it is the right extension of that invariant. It [recovers the discriminant exponent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Discriminant/Height.html#TauCeti.discriminantExponent_eq_inertiaDegree_mul_add_height) as `c = f(e − 1) + fH`, [vanishes exactly in the tame case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Discriminant/Height.html#TauCeti.height_eq_zero_iff), satisfies the wild bound [`H ≤ v_L(e)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/Discriminant/Height.html#TauCeti.height_le_natCastValuation), and does not change when one passes to the totally ramified step of the canonical tower. The worked example `ℚ₂(i)` gets height one and mean one half.

Alongside this, the ramification index now splits as `e = ε p^w` into a [tame index](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/RamificationIndex.html#TauCeti.tameRamificationIndex) and a [wild exponent](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/RamificationIndex.html#TauCeti.wildRamificationExponent) (TauCeti#39020). Tame indices multiply in towers and wild exponents add. For Galois extensions, `ε` [divides `q^f − 1`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/TamelyRamified/Basic.html#TauCeti.tameRamificationIndex_dvd_natCard_pow_inertiaDegree_sub_one) and is the order of inertia modulo wild inertia. Existing results on the maximal tame subextension were restated in the new vocabulary. Nothing has landed yet for the discriminant square class, the automorphisms and subfields of §0.5, or any layer from the ramification polygon onward.
