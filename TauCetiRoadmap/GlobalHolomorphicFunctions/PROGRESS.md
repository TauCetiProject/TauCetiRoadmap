## GlobalHolomorphicFunctions: 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-19 (`d05364b` to `b235000`)

The Mittag-Leffler theorem is proved in both of the roadmap's forms. The [isolated-singularities theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/MittagLeffler.html#TauCeti.exists_holomorphic_prescribed_isolated_singularities) takes, at each point of a relatively closed discrete set E in Ω, a germ holomorphic on a punctured neighbourhood (essential singularities allowed) and produces one function holomorphic on Ω \ E with exactly those singular germs. The [classical finite-principal-part version](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/MittagLeffler.html#TauCeti.exists_meromorphic_prescribed_principal_parts) is the special case where every germ is meromorphic, and a [normal-form variant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/MittagLeffler.html#TauCeti.exists_meromorphicNFOn_prescribed_principal_parts) also fixes the values at the poles. Both hold for any Ω ⊆ ℂ, open or not (TauCeti#41525). The proof follows the roadmap: each singularity is replaced by the negative part of its Laurent series, which is [holomorphic on the whole punctured plane](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Laurent/Expansion.html#TauCeti.exists_analyticOnNhd_compl_singleton_tendsto_cocompact_sub_analyticAt). Batches of these are pushed off successive compacts by [Runge approximation of negative Laurent parts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Runge/Laurent.html#TauCeti.IsFilledIn.exists_differentiableOn_norm_laurentNegPart_sub_lt).

Around the existence theorems sit the uniqueness and functoriality results the roadmap asks for. Two solutions [differ by a function holomorphic on Ω](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/MittagLeffler.html#TauCeti.HasPrescribedSingularities.exists_differentiableOn_eqOn_sub). Solutions restrict, translate and scale, and they [pull back along analytic maps that are nowhere locally constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/MittagLeffler.html#TauCeti.HasPrescribedSingularities.comp_of_analyticOnNhd). Two meromorphic germs [have equal principal parts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Meromorphic/PrincipalPart.html#TauCeti.meromorphicGerms.principalPart_eq_principalPart_iff) exactly when they differ by an analytic germ (TauCeti#41587). The supporting work is mostly point-set topology: lemmas on the codiscrete filter, and [removable-singularity patching across a discrete set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/RemovableSingularity.html#TauCeti.exists_differentiableOn_eqOn_of_analyticAt_nhdsNE). One Layer 4 target is still missing: writing a meromorphic function as a quotient of holomorphic ones. It waits on the divisor realization of Layer 3.
