## QuantumGroups: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-20 (`be5cab3` to `68a457e`)

The algebraic rank-one lane opened with its first classification theorem. At a generic parameter (q nonzero and not a root of unity), [every finite-dimensional simple U_q(sl₂)-module is isomorphic to exactly one signed ladder module L_m^ε](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/SimpleClassification.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.existsUnique_linearEquiv_ladderModule) (TauCeti#18061). This is the (m+1)-dimensional module on which K acts with eigenvalues εq^(m−2j). The proof follows the roadmap's route. The ladder modules are [simple](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.isSimpleModule), have scalar endomorphisms and are [pairwise non-isomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.nonempty_linearEquiv_iff). A [highest-weight vector](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Weights.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.exists_highest_weight_vector) is found from the finiteness of K's spectrum, without first assuming K diagonalizable. The [commutator formula for E and F^r](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Commutator.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.e_mul_f_pow) then forces that highest weight to be ±q^m.

Beneath this sits the algebra itself. [U_q(sl₂)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Basic.html#TauCeti.UqSl2) is presented as a quotient of a free algebra, with its universal property and a native [Hopf algebra structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Hopf.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.instHopfAlgebra). Its antipode is [bijective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Antipode.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.antipode_bijective), with square equal to conjugation by K⁻¹. A general [Ore extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/OreExtension/Basic.html#TauCeti.OreExtension) R[X; σ, δ] was built with its universal property, degree theory and the domain theorem, and is identified with Mathlib's skew polynomials when δ = 0. Writing U_q(sl₂) as an iterated Ore tower yields the [PBW basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/PBW.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.pbwBasis) F^a K^b E^c and shows that [U_q(sl₂) is a domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/OreTower.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.instIsDomain). Other pieces that landed are [Verma modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Verma.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.VermaModule) with their singular-vector criterion, generalized weight spaces, and the [central](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Casimir.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.casimir_mem_center) quantum Casimir element, whose scalars on the L_m^ε are [pairwise distinct](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Casimir.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.casimirScalar_units_mul_pow_inj).

Several things have not landed yet. Complete reducibility of finite-dimensional modules is still missing, and so are the description of the center as ℂ[Ω] and the simple quotients of Verma modules. Tensor products and Clebsch–Gordan theory have not started. Nothing on the compact side of the roadmap (C*-bialgebras, Haar states, SUq(2), reconstruction) has begun.

## QuantumGroups: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `e8ea0d1`)

The rank-one lane reached complete reducibility. At a generic parameter, [every finite-dimensional U_q(sl₂)-module is semisimple](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/CompleteReducibility.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.isSemisimpleModule_of_finiteDimensional) (TauCeti#19019), with no assumption that K acts diagonalizably. The proof splits a module into Casimir blocks and shows that K acts by the scalar εq^m on the top generalized weight space of each block. A Zorn argument extends this to arbitrary modules: [a module is locally finite exactly when it is a direct sum of signed ladder modules L_m^ε](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LocallyFinite.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.isLocallyFiniteModule_iff_exists_linearEquiv_dfinsupp_ladderModule), and the multiplicities are packaged by a natural [evaluation isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LocallyFinite.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.ladderModuleTensorHomEquiv) V ≃ ⊕ L_m^ε ⊗ Hom(L_m^ε, V). The type-1 distinction is settled too. [Type-1 locally finite modules are exactly the direct sums of the positive L_m^+](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/TypeOne.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.isTypeOne_and_isLocallyFiniteModule_iff_exists_linearEquiv_dfinsupp_ladderModule), while the [negative ladder modules are not of type 1](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/TypeOne.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.not_isTypeOne).

The algebra itself is now fully described as well. [The center of U_q(sl₂) is the polynomial algebra on the Casimir element](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Center.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.polynomialAlgEquivCenter), proved by leading-coefficient reduction in the PBW basis. Other results that landed: the [triangular decomposition](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Triangular.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.triangularEquiv) U⁻ ⊗ U⁰ ⊗ U⁺ ≃ U_q(sl₂); the [coproduct transported through q ↦ q⁻¹](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/InverseParameter/Coproduct.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.inverseParameterComul), shown to differ from the standard one; and the [submodules of Verma modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Verma/Submodule.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.VermaModule.eq_bot_or_eq_span_singleton_vec_succ_or_eq_top) together with their [simple quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Verma/Submodule.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.VermaModule.ladderModuleEquivQuotient).

The first steps were also taken toward the later items. [Quantum binomial coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/QuantumInteger/Binomial.html#TauCeti.quantumBinomial) give the [coproduct of E^r](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/ComulPow.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.comul_e_pow). The [compact real form's star structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Star.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.instStarRing) is now in place, though the invariant inner products and the comparison with SUq(2) are not. At v = 1 the positive ladder modules become the [standard sl₂-module V(m)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/ClassicalSpecialization.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.ClassicalLadder.equivSl2Std). The tensor category and Clebsch–Gordan theory have not started, and neither has the compact lane.

## QuantumGroups: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-11 (`e8ea0d1` to `d9fbe7b`)

The tensor theory of U_q(sl₂) arrived in this window. At a generic parameter, the quantum Clebsch–Gordan decomposition [L_m^ε ⊗ L_n^δ ≃ ⊕ L_(m+n−2j)^(εδ)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/ClebschGordan/Basic.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.clebschGordanEquiv) is now an explicit isomorphism, built from highest vectors pinned by the roadmap's recurrence (TauCeti#23212). Its channel projections turn out to be Casimir spectral projections, natural in both factors. On top of it come the [6j symbols](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/ClebschGordan/SixJ.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.sixJ), which are the change-of-decomposition matrices on triple products under the actual associator, and the [Biedenharn–Elliott pentagon identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/ClebschGordan/Pentagon.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.sixJ_pentagon) they satisfy on fourfold products (TauCeti#27940).

The categorical setting landed as well. Finite-dimensional modules over any Hopf algebra now form a [monoidal category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/Basic.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat). It is [rigid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/Rigid.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat.rigidCategory) when the antipode is bijective, and it is [pivotal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/Pivotal.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat.pivotalCategory) when a group-like element implements S². For a group algebra it is [monoidally equivalent to FDRep](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/FDRep.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat.equivalenceFDRep). At U_q with pivot K⁻¹ the category is [spherical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Pivotal.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.sphericalCategory), and L_m^ε has [quantum dimension ε[m+1]_q](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Pivotal.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.quantumDim_ladderModule). Formal characters are multiplicative and [determine a finite-dimensional module up to isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Character.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.nonempty_linearEquiv_iff_formalCharacter_eq). For the compact real form, each L_m^+ carries a [positive invariant inner product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule/InnerProduct.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.invariantForm), and that form is [unique up to scalar](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule/InnerProduct.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.eq_smul_invariantForm).

Layer 4 got its first algebraic pieces: invariant integrals on Hopf algebras, their [strict positivity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral/Positive.html#LinearMap.IsLeftInvariantIntegral.map_star_mul_self_pos) when they are positive and nonzero, and [algebraic unitarization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Unitarization.html#TauCeti.Comodule.exists_basis_coefficientMatrix_mem_unitary) of finite comodules by Haar averaging. Several things are still missing: the compatibility of the Clebsch–Gordan maps with duals, the comparison with SUq(2), and everything analytic in Layers 0–7.

## QuantumGroups: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-11 (`d9fbe7b` to `1b19ebb`)

The four pull requests in this section landed earlier and are recorded here late. The main result is the [representation ring](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.repRing) of U_q(sl₂): the split Grothendieck ring of finite-dimensional modules, with its product coming from the tensor product (TauCeti#26636). At a generic parameter the classes [L_m^ε] of the signed ladder modules [form a ℤ-basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.repRingBasis), and the [coordinates of a module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.repRingBasis_repr_of) in that basis are its multiplicities dim Hom_U(L_m^ε, M). The formal character gives an [injective ring homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.repRingCharacter_injective) into ℤ[ℂ], so the ring is [commutative](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.repRing_mul_comm) even though no braiding is available. Clebsch–Gordan becomes the [multiplication rule](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.of_ladderModule_mul_of_ladderModule) [L_m^ε]·[L_n^δ] = Σ_(j ≤ min(m,n)) [L_(m+n−2j)^(εδ)]. Underneath it, the finite Hopf-module category gained a zero object and [binary biproducts](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/Biproducts.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat.prodBiconeIsBilimit), and every finite-dimensional module was [written as a finite product of signed ladder modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LocallyFinite.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.exists_linearEquiv_pi_ladderModule).

On the compact-real-form side, each ladder module now has a [normalized basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule/NormalizedBasis.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.normalizedBasis), the ladder basis divided by its lengths, which is [orthonormal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule/NormalizedBasis.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.invariantForm_normalizedBasis) for the invariant form. In this basis E and F are off-diagonal and K is diagonal. On the fundamental module E has [entry q^(−1/2)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule/NormalizedBasis.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.toMatrix_rep_e_normalizedBasis_one) and F has entry q^(1/2), which are exactly the values §8.6 fixes for the SUq(2) coefficient pairing. A small matrix lemma, that [conjugating by a matrix whose conjugate transpose is its inverse preserves unitarity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/UnitaryGroup/Basic.html#Matrix.conjTranspose_mul_mul_mem_unitary), supports the same comparison.

The pairing with SUq(2) and the monoidal equivalence it should induce are still missing, and SUq(2) itself is not built. The Hom and End formulas of §8.3 are also absent, as is the compatibility of the Clebsch–Gordan maps with duals. The README asks for the image of the character map to be identified with the span of the ladder characters. That follows from the basis and injectivity, but it is not stated as a theorem.
