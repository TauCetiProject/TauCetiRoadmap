## QuantumGroups: 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-20 (`be5cab3` to `68a457e`)

The algebraic rank-one lane opened with its first classification theorem. At a generic parameter (q nonzero and not a root of unity), [every finite-dimensional simple U_q(sl₂)-module is isomorphic to exactly one signed ladder module L_m^ε](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/SimpleClassification.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.existsUnique_linearEquiv_ladderModule) (TauCeti#18061). This is the (m+1)-dimensional module on which K acts with eigenvalues εq^(m−2j). The proof follows the roadmap's route. The ladder modules are [simple](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.isSimpleModule), have scalar endomorphisms and are [pairwise non-isomorphic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.nonempty_linearEquiv_iff). A [highest-weight vector](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Weights.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.exists_highest_weight_vector) is found from the finiteness of K's spectrum, without first assuming K diagonalizable. The [commutator formula for E and F^r](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Commutator.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.e_mul_f_pow) then forces that highest weight to be ±q^m.

Beneath this sits the algebra itself. [U_q(sl₂)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Basic.html#TauCeti.UqSl2) is presented as a quotient of a free algebra, with its universal property and a native [Hopf algebra structure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Hopf.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.instHopfAlgebra). Its antipode is [bijective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Antipode.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.antipode_bijective), with square equal to conjugation by K⁻¹. A general [Ore extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/OreExtension/Basic.html#TauCeti.OreExtension) R[X; σ, δ] was built with its universal property, degree theory and the domain theorem, and is identified with Mathlib's skew polynomials when δ = 0. Writing U_q(sl₂) as an iterated Ore tower yields the [PBW basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/PBW.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.pbwBasis) F^a K^b E^c and shows that [U_q(sl₂) is a domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/OreTower.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.instIsDomain). Other pieces that landed are [Verma modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Verma.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.VermaModule) with their singular-vector criterion, generalized weight spaces, and the [central](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Casimir.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.casimir_mem_center) quantum Casimir element, whose scalars on the L_m^ε are [pairwise distinct](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Casimir.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.casimirScalar_units_mul_pow_inj).

Several things have not landed yet. Complete reducibility of finite-dimensional modules is still missing, and so are the description of the center as ℂ[Ω] and the simple quotients of Verma modules. Tensor products and Clebsch–Gordan theory have not started. Nothing on the compact side of the roadmap (C*-bialgebras, Haar states, SUq(2), reconstruction) has begun.
