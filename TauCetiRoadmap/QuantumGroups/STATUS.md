### Named results

- **Classification and complete reducibility** — at a generic parameter, every finite-dimensional simple U_q(sl₂)-module is [exactly one signed ladder module L_m^ε](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/SimpleClassification.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.existsUnique_linearEquiv_ladderModule), and every finite-dimensional module is [semisimple](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/CompleteReducibility.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.isSemisimpleModule_of_finiteDimensional), even when K is not assumed diagonalizable.
- **The Hom and End formulas** — dim Hom_U(V,W) is the [sum over (m,ε) of the products of the multiplicities](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Multiplicity.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.finrank_linearMap_eq_finsum_ladderModule) of L_m^ε in V and W, and End_U(V) is a [product of full matrix algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Multiplicity.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.nonempty_end_algEquiv_pi_matrix_ladderModule) on the multiplicity spaces. Both hold for locally finite modules with finite multiplicities.
- **The quantum Clebsch–Gordan decomposition** — L_m^ε ⊗ L_n^δ ≃ ⊕_(j ≤ min(m,n)) L_(m+n−2j)^(εδ), as an explicit isomorphism built from normalized highest vectors ([`clebschGordanEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/ClebschGordan/Basic.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.clebschGordanEquiv)).
- **The Biedenharn–Elliott pentagon identity** — the 6j change-of-decomposition entries, taken under the actual tensor associator, satisfy the pentagon relation on fourfold products ([`sixJ_pentagon`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/ClebschGordan/Pentagon.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.sixJ_pentagon)). In coupled ladder bases the associator's matrix is [block-diagonal with 6j entries](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/ClebschGordan/SixJ.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.toMatrix_assoc_clebschGordanBasis).
- **The representation ring of U_q(sl₂)** — the ladder classes form a [ℤ-basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.repRingBasis), and the character map into ℤ[ℂ] is [injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.repRingCharacter_injective). The ring is therefore commutative, and Clebsch–Gordan is its [multiplication rule](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/RepresentationRing.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.of_ladderModule_mul_of_ladderModule).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The finite Hopf-module category** ([`FiniteHopfModuleCat`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/Basic.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat)): finite-dimensional modules over any Hopf algebra, monoidal with biproducts. It is [rigid](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/Rigid.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat.rigidCategory) when the antipode is bijective and [pivotal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/Pivotal.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat.pivotalCategory) for a group-like pivot. At U_q with pivot K⁻¹ it is [spherical](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/Pivotal.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.sphericalCategory). For a group algebra, its comparison with FDRep [respects duals and pivots](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FiniteHopfModuleCat/FDRep/Pivotal.html#TauCeti.FiniteHopfModuleCat.isPivotal_ofFDRep).
- **Signed ladder modules and their unitary bases**: L_m^ε are the explicit simples in which every Layer 8 theorem is stated. The positive ones carry a [unique positive invariant inner product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule/InnerProduct.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.eq_smul_invariantForm) and an [orthonormal normalized basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/QuantumGroups/UqSl2/LadderModule/NormalizedBasis.html#TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule.normalizedBasis). In that basis, the fundamental matrices have the entries §8.6 fixes for the SUq(2) pairing.
- **Algebraic invariant integrals** ([`IsLeftInvariantIntegral`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Integral.html#LinearMap.IsLeftInvariantIntegral)): a nonzero positive integral is strictly positive and [unitarizes every finite comodule](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Unitarization.html#TauCeti.Comodule.exists_basis_coefficientMatrix_mem_unitary), so the Hopf star algebra is [linearly reductive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/LinearlyReductive/PositiveIntegral.html#LinearMap.IsRightInvariantIntegral.isLinearlyReductive). This is the positive-integral side of `compactType_iff`.
