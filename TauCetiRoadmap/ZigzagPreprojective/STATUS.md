## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0–2 are done. On the preprojective side, the finite ADE theory is complete: finite-dimensionality and self-injectivity hold for every orientation. The non-Dynkin theory, meaning Koszul resolutions and the Hilbert series, is proved for every connected graph containing a cycle, but not for the non-Dynkin trees. The braid complexes satisfy their inverse, commutation and braid relations. The Calabi–Yau completion identifications, derived Koszul duality and Liu–Wang formality have not begun.

### Named results

- **The finite/infinite dichotomy for preprojective algebras** — for every orientation, `Π(Q)` is finite-dimensional over every field when the graph is simply-laced Dynkin ([`TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_isSimplyLaced`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/FiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_isSimplyLaced)) and infinite-dimensional when it is connected and non-Dynkin ([`TauCeti.not_module_finite_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_of_not_exists_dynkinType_iso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/InfiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.not_module_finite_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_of_not_exists_dynkinType_iso)). In the finite case it is also left and right self-injective, by a general theorem for finite-dimensional preprojective algebras (`TauCeti.moduleInjective_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_isSimplyLaced`).
- **Koszul resolutions for preprojective algebras off trees** — if every vertex of `Q` is the tail of an arrow, then the Koszul complex `0 → e_vΠ → ⨁_b e_iΠ → e_vΠ → S_v → 0` is exact over any commutative ring ([`TauCeti.forall_preprojectiveMk_ofArrow_reverse_mul_eq_zero_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/Sinkless.html#TauCeti.forall_preprojectiveMk_ofArrow_reverse_mul_eq_zero_iff)). By orientation independence the same holds for every orientation of a connected graph containing a cycle, where the resolution is linear and graded and the Hilbert series is `((1 + t²) I − t A)⁻¹` (`TauCeti.preprojectiveHilbertSeries_eq_inv_of_not_isAcyclic`).
- **Koszulity of zigzag algebras of graphs with a cycle** — every graded vertex simple of such a zigzag algebra has a linear projective resolution, with the signless preprojective algebra as Koszul dual (`TauCeti.exists_isLinear_gradedProjectiveResolution_zigzagGradedSimple_of_not_isAcyclic`). For a Dynkin diagram with at least two vertices, some simple has no linear resolution (`TauCeti.exists_not_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_of_isSimplyLaced`).
- **The Khovanov–Seidel braid relations** — `B_i B_j ≃ B_j B_i` for nonadjacent vertices and `B_i B_j B_i ≃ B_j B_i B_j` for adjacent ones, as homotopy equivalences of complexes of graded bimodules (`TauCeti.zigzagBraidComplexAdjacentHomotopyEquiv`). Each `B_i` is also inverse to its coevaluation complex.
- **Couture's classification of skew-zigzag algebras** — two skew parameters have the same class in `H¹(G, kˣ)` exactly when their algebras are isomorphic fixing the vertex idempotents ([`TauCeti.SkewZigzagParameter.cohomologyClass_eq_iff_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.SkewZigzagParameter.cohomologyClass_eq_iff_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The graded Cartan matrix `(1 + q²) I + q A_G` of the zigzag algebra ([`TauCeti.zigzagGradedCartanMatrix_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/CartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.zigzagGradedCartanMatrix_eq)). At `q = −t` its inverse is the preprojective Hilbert series, and the multiplicities in the zigzag linear resolutions are its inverse's coefficients.
- The quadratic dual of the zigzag algebra, identified with the signless preprojective algebra. This is the ordinary preprojective algebra for bipartite graphs, and provably not gauge-equivalent to it for non-bipartite graphs with `2 ≠ 0` ([`TauCeti.quadraticDualQuadraticZigzagEquivSignless`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Quadratic/Dual.html#TauCeti.quadraticDualQuadraticZigzagEquivSignless)).
- Quartic loop deformations, which give minimal, strictly unital `A∞` structures on the zigzag algebra, with Liu–Wang's affine `D₄` example as one instance ([`TauCeti.zigzagLoopAInfinityAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/Loop.html#TauCeti.zigzagLoopAInfinityAlgebra)).

### Roadmap coverage

For those trees, the preprojective Koszul theorem and Hilbert series, the inverse-Cartan comparison and zigzag Koszulity are all open. ## The frontier

- **Non-Dynkin trees.** Prove the left-end exactness of the preprojective Koszul complex for affine `Dₙ`, `E₆`, `E₇`, `E₈` and the other non-Dynkin trees, then deduce the Hilbert series and zigzag Koszulity. These trees have no orientation without sinks, so the current argument does not reach them.
- **The braid group action and `K₀`.** Assemble the proved relations into an action of the Artin braid group on the homotopy category of graded modules, and compare its action on graded `K₀` with the reflection representation.
- **Spherical twists and transfer.** Prove the Seidel–Thomas type-`A_m` relations and the transfer of the action through a supplied derived equivalence.
- **Liu–Wang formality.** Prove that the affine `D₄` deformation is nontrivial up to `A∞` isomorphism, then prove the Hochschild comparison and intrinsic formality for trees. This needs derived Koszul duality for `Π₂(Q)`, which has not begun.
- **Calabi–Yau completions.** Identify `Π₂(Q)` and `Γ₃(Q, W)` with the sibling roadmap's derived and deformed completions. This waits on that roadmap's completion layer.
