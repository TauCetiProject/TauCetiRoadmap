### Named results

- **The Koszulity dichotomy for zigzag algebras** — for a connected graph on at least two vertices, every graded vertex simple has a linear projective resolution exactly when the graph is not Dynkin ([`TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Projective/Koszul/NonDynkin.html#TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff)). In the Koszul case the dual is the signless preprojective algebra.
- **The ADE dichotomy for preprojective algebras** — for a connected graph and any orientation, `Π(Q)` is finite-dimensional exactly when the graph is Dynkin ([`TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/FiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff)), and Koszul exactly when it is not ([`TauCeti.forall_preprojectiveKoszulLeft_injective_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/Koszul/NonDynkin.html#TauCeti.forall_preprojectiveKoszulLeft_injective_iff)). In the infinite case its Hilbert series is the inverse zigzag Cartan matrix at `q = −t`.
- **The zigzag braid group action** — the Artin group of any finite graph acts on the homotopy category of graded zigzag modules through the Khovanov–Seidel complexes ([`TauCeti.zigzagBraidArtinAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Braid/Action.html#TauCeti.zigzagBraidArtinAction)). On graded `K₀` it acts by deformed reflections, which are the standard simple reflections at `q = −1` ([`TauCeti.zigzagBraidK0Action`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Braid/K0.html#TauCeti.zigzagBraidK0Action)).
- **Formality of Liu–Wang's affine `D₄` example** — with the displayed `m₄`, the deformation is `A∞` isomorphic to the strict zigzag algebra, since `m₄` is an explicit coboundary ([`TauCeti.isFormal_liuWangAffineD4AInfinityAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/AffineD4.html#TauCeti.isFormal_liuWangAffineD4AInfinityAlgebra)). A single rotation of the walk is not, so `HH^{4,−2}` is nonzero at any vertex with four neighbours ([`TauCeti.not_hochschildVanishesAt_four_zigzagStrictAInfinityAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/HochschildClass.html#TauCeti.not_hochschildVanishesAt_four_zigzagStrictAInfinityAlgebra)).
- **Couture's classification of skew-zigzag algebras** — two skew parameters have the same class in `H¹(G, kˣ)` exactly when their algebras are isomorphic by a map fixing the vertex idempotents ([`TauCeti.SkewZigzagParameter.cohomologyClass_eq_iff_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.SkewZigzagParameter.cohomologyClass_eq_iff_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

Together with the zero-differential DG model of the projectives, it is groundwork for the spherical-twist comparison.

## The frontier

- **The derived braid action.** Each braid functor now descends to derived categories. What remains is to transport the inverse and braid relations to obtain the Artin action there, as the roadmap asks.
- **Spherical twists.** Construct the Seidel–Thomas twists for an `A_m`-configuration of spherical objects, prove their braid relations, and compare them with the zigzag complexes. The new trace pairing on projective Homs supplies one ingredient, but no Serre functor or sphericality statement exists yet.
- **Liu–Wang's example, as stated.** In Tau Ceti's sign conventions the displayed `m₄` is formal, so that target needs restating. A single-rotation loop operation is the natural replacement, but no result yet shows the resulting deformation is not formal.
- **The Hochschild comparison and intrinsic formality.** Prove `HH^{2,q}(Π₂(Q)) ≅ (Π/[Π, Π])_{q+2}` and transport it to the zigzag algebra, then Liu–Wang's theorem that trees are intrinsically formal exactly in the ADE case outside bad characteristic. This needs the small bimodule resolution and derived Koszul duality, which have not begun.
- **Calabi–Yau completions.** Identify `Π₂(Q)` and `Γ₃(Q, W)` with the sibling roadmap's derived and deformed completions. This waits on that roadmap's completion layer.
