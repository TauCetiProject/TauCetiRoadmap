### Named results

- **The Koszulity dichotomy for zigzag algebras.** For a connected graph on at least two vertices, every graded vertex simple has a linear projective resolution exactly when the graph is not Dynkin ([`TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Projective/Koszul/NonDynkin.html#TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff)).
- **The ADE dichotomy for preprojective algebras.** For a connected graph and any orientation, `Π(Q)` is finite-dimensional exactly when the graph is Dynkin ([`TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/FiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff)), and Koszul exactly when it is not. In the Dynkin case it is self-injective over every field ([`TauCeti.moduleInjective_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_iso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/SelfInjective.html#TauCeti.moduleInjective_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_iso)).
- **Failure of intrinsic formality away from ADE.** When `2` is invertible, the zigzag algebra of a graph with a vertex of valency at least four, or with two branch vertices in one component, carries a non-formal minimal `A∞` deformation ([`TauCeti.not_isIntrinsicallyFormal_zigzagStrictAInfinityAlgebra_of_three_le_degree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/NonFormal.html#TauCeti.not_isIntrinsicallyFormal_zigzagStrictAInfinityAlgebra_of_three_le_degree)). The affine `D₄` witness is Liu–Wang's walk taken alone; their displayed `m₄` is formal.
- **The cyclic quotient in good characteristic.** For `E₈` with `2`, `3` and `5` invertible, every element of positive degree in `Π` is a sum of commutators ([`TauCeti.preprojectiveGrade_E8_le_commutatorSubmodule`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/TypeE/CyclicQuotient.html#TauCeti.preprojectiveGrade_E8_le_commutatorSubmodule)). The analogous statements hold for `A`, `D`, `E₆` and `E₇`, and for `Dₙ` degree four vanishes exactly outside characteristic two.
- **The zigzag braid action on the derived category.** The Artin group of any finite graph acts through the Khovanov–Seidel complexes, after descending from the homotopy category ([`TauCeti.zigzagBraidDerivedArtinAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Braid/Module/Derived/Action.html#TauCeti.zigzagBraidDerivedArtinAction)). On graded `K₀` it acts by deformed simple reflections.

These are the finiteness and duality inputs that Seidel–Thomas sphericality asks for.

## The frontier

- **The positive-degree cyclic quotient theorem.** Define `IsBadPrime` and assemble the per-type results into the single criterion. What is missing is the characteristic-three classes for `E₇` and `E₈`, and the non-Dynkin half for graphs that are not trees.
- **The non-ADE half of Liu–Wang's theorem.** Trees with a single trivalent vertex, the `Ẽ₆`, `Ẽ₇`, `Ẽ₈` shapes, carry nonzero classes in `HH^{n,2−n}` for some `n ∈ {6, 8, 12}`, but no non-formal deformation has been built from them. Characteristic two is also open, since every current non-formality result assumes `2` is invertible.
- **The ADE half and the Hochschild comparison.** Proving `HH^{2,q}(Π₂(Q)) ≅ (Π/[Π, Π])_{q+2}` needs the small bimodule resolution and derived Koszul duality. Neither has begun.
- **Spherical twists.** Build the Seidel–Thomas twists for an `A_m`-configuration, prove their braid relations, and compare them with the zigzag complexes. The twisted closure and supertrace supply finiteness and a trace, but no sphericality statement exists yet.
- **Affine recognition.** Prove that a connected graph carries a nonzero stuck position without precarious vertices exactly when it is an affine diagram of a unique type, as §0.1 now asks.
