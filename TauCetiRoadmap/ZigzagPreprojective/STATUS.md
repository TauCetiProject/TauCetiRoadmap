### Named results

- **Affine recognition.** A connected finite simple graph carries a nonzero weight `a : V → ℕ` with `∑_{j∼i} a_j = 2a_i` at every vertex exactly when it is the graph of a unique valid affine simply-laced diagram ([`SimpleGraph.Connected.exists_sum_eq_two_mul_iff_existsUnique_affineDynkinType_iso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/AffineDynkinType/Recognition.html#SimpleGraph.Connected.exists_sum_eq_two_mul_iff_existsUnique_affineDynkinType_iso)).
- **The Koszulity dichotomy for zigzag algebras.** For a connected graph on at least two vertices, every graded vertex simple has a linear projective resolution exactly when the graph is not Dynkin ([`TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Projective/Koszul/NonDynkin.html#TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff)).
- **The ADE dichotomy for preprojective algebras.** For a connected graph and any orientation, `Π(Q)` is finite-dimensional exactly when the graph is Dynkin ([`TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/FiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff)), and Koszul exactly when it is not. In the Dynkin case it is self-injective over every field ([`TauCeti.moduleInjective_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_iso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/SelfInjective.html#TauCeti.moduleInjective_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_iso)).
- **Failure of intrinsic formality away from ADE.** When `2` is invertible, the zigzag algebra of a graph with a vertex of valency at least four, or with two branch vertices in one component, carries a non-formal minimal `A∞` deformation ([`TauCeti.not_isIntrinsicallyFormal_zigzagStrictAInfinityAlgebra_of_three_le_degree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/NonFormal.html#TauCeti.not_isIntrinsicallyFormal_zigzagStrictAInfinityAlgebra_of_three_le_degree)). Liu–Wang's displayed affine `D₄` deformation is itself formal; the witness is their walk taken alone.
- **The zigzag braid action on the derived category.** The Artin group of any finite graph acts through the Khovanov–Seidel complexes ([`TauCeti.zigzagBraidDerivedArtinAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Braid/Module/Derived/Action.html#TauCeti.zigzagBraidDerivedArtinAction)), and on graded `K₀` by deformed simple reflections.

## The frontier

- **The positive-degree cyclic quotient theorem.** Define `IsBadPrime` and assemble the per-type vanishing results into one criterion. Still missing are the characteristic-three classes for `E₇` and `E₈`, and the non-Dynkin half for graphs that are not trees.
- **The non-ADE half of Liu–Wang's theorem.** Trees with a single trivalent vertex, the `Ẽ₆`, `Ẽ₇`, `Ẽ₈` shapes, carry nonzero classes in `HH^{n,2−n}`, but no non-formal deformation has been built from them. Characteristic two is open, since every non-formality result assumes `2` is invertible.
- **The Hochschild comparison.** Proving `HH^{2,q}(Π₂(Q)) ≅ (Π/[Π, Π])_{q+2}` needs a small cofibrant bimodule resolution of `Π₂(Q)` and derived Koszul duality. The standard resolution of a DG path algebra now exists, but neither the comparison nor the duality has begun.
- **Spherical twists.** Build the Seidel–Thomas twists for an `A_m`-configuration, prove their braid relations, and compare them with the zigzag complexes. The twisted closure supplies finiteness and a trace, but no sphericality statement exists yet.
- **Calabi–Yau completions.** Identify `Π₂(Q)` and `Γ₃(Q, W)` with the 2-Calabi–Yau and deformed 3-Calabi–Yau completions. These consume the completions from the DGAInfinity roadmap rather than building local copies.
