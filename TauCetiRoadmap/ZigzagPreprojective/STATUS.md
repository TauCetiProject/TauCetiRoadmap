### Named results

- **The cyclic quotient criterion** — over a field, for a connected graph with any orientation, the preprojective algebra's cyclic quotient vanishes in positive degrees exactly when the graph is simply-laced Dynkin and the characteristic is not a bad prime of its type ([`TauCeti.forall_pos_preprojectiveGrade_le_commutatorSubmodule_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/CyclicQuotient.html#TauCeti.forall_pos_preprojectiveGrade_le_commutatorSubmodule_iff)).
- **Non-formality for non-Dynkin trees** — over any nontrivial `ℚ`-algebra, the zigzag algebra of a finite non-Dynkin tree carries a non-formal minimal strictly unital `A∞` deformation, built from an explicit loop operation ([`SimpleGraph.IsTree.not_isZigzagAlgebraIntrinsicallyFormal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/Tree.html#SimpleGraph.IsTree.not_isZigzagAlgebraIntrinsicallyFormal)).
- **The ADE dichotomy for preprojective algebras** — for a connected graph and any orientation, `Π(Q)` is finite-dimensional exactly when the graph is Dynkin ([`TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/FiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff)), and in that case it is self-injective over every field.
- **The Koszulity dichotomy for zigzag algebras** — for a connected graph on at least two vertices, every graded vertex simple has a linear resolution exactly when the graph is not Dynkin ([`TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Projective/Koszul/NonDynkin.html#TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff)).
- **Affine recognition** — a connected finite simple graph carries a nonzero natural weight with neighbour sums twice each value exactly when it is a unique valid affine simply-laced diagram ([`SimpleGraph.Connected.exists_sum_eq_two_mul_iff_existsUnique_affineDynkinType_iso`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/AffineDynkinType/Recognition.html#SimpleGraph.Connected.exists_sum_eq_two_mul_iff_existsUnique_affineDynkinType_iso)).

## The frontier

- **The ADE half of Liu–Wang's theorem.** Kadeishvili's criterion and the cyclic quotient theorem are both in place. What connects them is the comparison `HH^{2,q}(Π₂(Q)) ≅ (Π/[Π, Π])_{q+2}` transported across derived Koszul duality. Neither the small cofibrant bimodule resolution nor the duality has been built.
- **Non-formality in positive characteristic.** For graphs with a vertex of valency at least four, or with two branch vertices, non-formality needs only `2` invertible. For single-branch trees it is proved only over `ℚ`-algebras, and characteristic two is open throughout. The exact theorem also asks for non-formality at bad primes for Dynkin trees.
- **Spherical twists.** Build the Seidel–Thomas twists for an `A_m`-configuration, prove their braid relations, and compare them with the zigzag complexes.
- **Calabi–Yau completions.** Identify `Π₂(Q)` and `Γ₃(Q, W)` with the 2-Calabi–Yau and deformed 3-Calabi–Yau completions, using the completions from the DGAInfinity roadmap.
- **Layer 0's converse.** Show that a singular positive semidefinite Cartan matrix yields a positive integral null vector.
