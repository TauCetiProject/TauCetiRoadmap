## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layers 0–5 are done: the strict and skew zigzag theory, the finite/infinite and Koszul dichotomies for preprojective algebras, and the classical Koszulity of zigzag algebras now cover every connected graph, non-Dynkin trees included. ### Named results

- **The Koszulity dichotomy for zigzag algebras** — for a connected graph on at least two vertices, every graded vertex simple has a linear projective resolution exactly when the graph is not Dynkin ([`TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Projective/Koszul/NonDynkin.html#TauCeti.forall_exists_isLinear_zigzagGradedSimple_iff)). In the Koszul case the dual is the signless preprojective algebra.
- **The ADE dichotomy for preprojective algebras** — for a connected graph and any orientation, `Π(Q)` is finite-dimensional exactly when the graph is Dynkin ([`TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/FiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_iff)), and Koszul exactly when it is not ([`TauCeti.forall_preprojectiveKoszulLeft_injective_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/Koszul/NonDynkin.html#TauCeti.forall_preprojectiveKoszulLeft_injective_iff)). It is self-injective in the finite case. In the infinite case its Hilbert series is `((1 + t²) I − t A)⁻¹`, the inverse zigzag Cartan matrix at `q = −t`.
- **The zigzag braid group action** — the Artin group of any finite graph acts on the homotopy category of graded zigzag modules by tensoring with the Khovanov–Seidel complexes ([`TauCeti.zigzagBraidArtinAction`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Braid/Action.html#TauCeti.zigzagBraidArtinAction)). On graded `K₀` it acts by deformed reflections, which are the standard simple reflections at `q = −1` ([`TauCeti.zigzagBraidK0Action`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Braid/K0.html#TauCeti.zigzagBraidK0Action)).
- **Formality of Liu–Wang's affine `D₄` example** — with the displayed values of `m₄`, the deformation is `A∞` isomorphic to the strict zigzag algebra, because `m₄` is the coboundary of an explicit backtrack cochain ([`TauCeti.isFormal_liuWangAffineD4AInfinityAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/AffineD4.html#TauCeti.isFormal_liuWangAffineD4AInfinityAlgebra)). A single rotation of the walk is not a coboundary, so `HH^{4,−2}` is nonzero at any vertex with four neighbours ([`TauCeti.not_hochschildVanishesAt_four_zigzagStrictAInfinityAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/HochschildClass.html#TauCeti.not_hochschildVanishesAt_four_zigzagStrictAInfinityAlgebra)).
- **Couture's classification of skew-zigzag algebras** — two skew parameters have the same class in `H¹(G, kˣ)` exactly when their algebras are isomorphic by a map fixing the vertex idempotents ([`TauCeti.SkewZigzagParameter.cohomologyClass_eq_iff_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.SkewZigzagParameter.cohomologyClass_eq_iff_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv)).

## The frontier

- **Liu–Wang's example, as stated.** The roadmap uses Example 4.7 as a nontrivial non-ADE test. In Tau Ceti's sign conventions the displayed `m₄` is formal, so that target needs restating. A single-rotation loop operation is the natural replacement, since it is not a coboundary, but no result yet shows the resulting deformation is not formal.
- **The Hochschild comparison.** Prove `HH^{2,q}(Π₂(Q)) ≅ (Π/[Π, Π])_{q+2}` and transport it to the zigzag algebra. The cyclic side is already nonzero for graphs containing affine `Dₙ`, `E₆`, `E₇` or `E₈`, in degrees `4`, `6`, `8` and `12`. This needs the small bimodule resolution and derived Koszul duality, which have not begun.
- **Intrinsic formality for trees.** Liu–Wang's theorem says formality holds exactly for ADE trees outside bad characteristic. It waits on the Hochschild comparison above.
- **Spherical twists.** Construct the Seidel–Thomas twists for an `A_m`-configuration of spherical objects, prove their braid relations, and compare them with the zigzag complexes.
- **Calabi–Yau completions.** Identify `Π₂(Q)` and `Γ₃(Q, W)` with the sibling roadmap's derived and deformed completions. This waits on that roadmap's completion layer.
