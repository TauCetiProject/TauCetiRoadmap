## ZigzagPreprojective: 2026-08-17 to 2026-09-01 (`9c5e581` to `8745177`)

The strict zigzag algebra of a finite simple graph now exists with its basic invariants, and the headline is the **graded Cartan formula** ([`TauCeti.zigzagGradedCartanMatrix_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/CartanMatrix.html#TauCeti.zigzagGradedCartanMatrix_eq)): for a graph with no isolated vertex the graded Cartan matrix is `(1 + q²) I + q A_G`, proved entrywise, specialising to `2I + A_G` at `q = 1` and `2I − A_G` at `q = −1`, and identified with the graded dimensions of the Hom spaces between vertex projectives ([`TauCeti.zigzagGradedCartanMatrix_eq_sum_finrank_zigzagProjectiveHomOfDegree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Hom.html#TauCeti.zigzagGradedCartanMatrix_eq_sum_finrank_zigzagProjectiveHomOfDegree)). Beneath it: the **vertex–arrow–volume basis** ([`TauCeti.zigzagBasis`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Basis.html#TauCeti.zigzagBasis)) giving `dim Z(G) = 2|V| + 2|E|`, the **symmetric perfect Frobenius trace pairing** ([`TauCeti.zigzagTracePairing_isPerfPair`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Trace.html#TauCeti.zigzagTracePairing_isPerfPair)), the **centre of dimension `|V| + 1`** for a connected graph ([`TauCeti.finrank_center_nonisolatedZigzagQuotient`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Center.html#TauCeti.finrank_center_nonisolatedZigzagQuotient)), the radical filtration with socle equal to `rad²` ([`TauCeti.socle_nonisolatedZigzagQuotient_eq_jacobson_sq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Socle.html#TauCeti.socle_nonisolatedZigzagQuotient_eq_jacobson_sq)), and the **quadratic presentation** for connected graphs with at least three vertices ([`TauCeti.zigzagIdeal_eq_quadraticZigzagIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Relations.html#TauCeti.zigzagIdeal_eq_quadraticZigzagIdeal)).

On the preprojective side, **orientation independence** ([`TauCeti.reorientPreprojectiveAlgebraEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/Orientation.html#TauCeti.reorientPreprojectiveAlgebraEquiv)) is proved by the explicit sign-rescaling isomorphism for any set of reversed arrows over any commutative ring, and extended to arbitrary unit-valued gauges ([`TauCeti.preprojectiveAlgebraEquivGauged`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/Gauge.html#TauCeti.preprojectiveAlgebraEquivGauged)). Layer 0's affine simply-laced diagrams landed with connectedness, the marks as null vector, **positive semidefiniteness with one-dimensional radical** ([`TauCeti.AffineDynkinType.posSemidef_realCartanMatrix`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/AffineDynkinType/PositiveSemidefinite.html#TauCeti.AffineDynkinType.posSemidef_realCartanMatrix)), and Bourbaki-numbered deletion of the affine node ([`TauCeti.AffineDynkinType.comap_graph_bourbakiNode`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/AffineDynkinType/Deletion.html#TauCeti.AffineDynkinType.comap_graph_bourbakiNode)).

Supporting work: path-length gradings and their descent to quotients by homogeneous two-sided ideals (TauCeti#4201, TauCeti#4421), path algebras functorial in the quiver, corner submodules with the `Hom(Ae, Af) ≅ eAf` dictionary, and a general socle. ## ZigzagPreprojective: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30 (`8745177` to `dec7a58`)

The headline is **Couture's classification of skew-zigzag algebras**. Two skew parameters on a finite graph have the same class in `H¹(G, kˣ)` exactly when their algebras are isomorphic by an isomorphism fixing the vertex idempotents ([`cohomologyClass_eq_iff_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Cohomology.html#TauCeti.SkewZigzagParameter.cohomologyClass_eq_iff_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv)). A graded form and a form up to graph automorphisms, for isomorphisms that permute the idempotents, also landed ([`exists_firstCohomologyRelabel_eq_iff_exists_vertexPermuting_algEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Skew/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.SkewZigzagParameter.exists_firstCohomologyRelabel_eq_iff_exists_vertexPermuting_algEquiv)). The consequences are proved as well. On a nontrivial tree every skew algebra is the ordinary one ([`nonempty_algEquiv_zigzagAlgebra_of_isTree`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Tree.html#TauCeti.nonempty_algEquiv_zigzagAlgebra_of_isTree)). On an odd cycle with `2 ≠ 0`, the exterior parameter gives a genuinely different algebra ([`not_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv_skewZigzagQuotient_exteriorCycle`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Cycle.html#TauCeti.not_exists_vertexFixing_algEquiv_skewZigzagQuotient_exteriorCycle)). Skew algebras also received a basis, a centre, a grading and a perfect trace pairing.

On the preprojective side, the **preprojective algebra of a connected non-Dynkin graph is infinite-dimensional** ([`not_module_finite_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_of_not_exists_dynkinType_iso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/InfiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.not_module_finite_preprojectiveAlgebra_orientedQuiver_of_not_exists_dynkinType_iso)). The proof uses a Golod–Shafarevich-type growth bound. The finite side reaches only `A₁` and `A₂`. The **quadratic dual of the zigzag algebra is the signless preprojective algebra** ([`quadraticDualQuadraticZigzagEquivSignless`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Quadratic/Dual.html#TauCeti.quadraticDualQuadraticZigzagEquivSignless)). For a bipartite graph this is the ordinary preprojective algebra. For a non-bipartite graph with `2 ≠ 0`, no gauge makes it so. Layer 6 has begun: the two-dimensional Ginzburg DG algebra `Π₂(Q)` exists, with `H⁰(Π₂(Q)) ≅ Π(Q)` ([`preprojectiveEquivGinzburgTwoCohomologyZero`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Ginzburg/ZerothCohomology.html#TauCeti.preprojectiveEquivGinzburgTwoCohomologyZero)), and so does the three-dimensional `Γ₃(Q, W)`.

The public componentwise zigzag algebra now has, for every finite graph, dimension `2|V| + 2|E|` and a centre of dimension `|V|` plus the number of components. It is also proved self-injective ([`moduleInjective_zigzagAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/SelfInjective.html#TauCeti.moduleInjective_zigzagAlgebra)). The named `A₂`, `D₄`, `E₈` and affine `E₈` computations landed too. Still missing: Koszulity, finite-dimensionality of the ADE preprojective algebras beyond `A₂`, the Calabi–Yau completion comparisons, and Layers 7–8.

## ZigzagPreprojective: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-04 (`dec7a58` to `41e5e49`)

The headline is that **preprojective algebras of finite ADE type are finite-dimensional**: for every orientation of a simply-laced Dynkin diagram, `Π(Q)` is finite-dimensional over every field, characteristic two included, and so is its signless counterpart ([`finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_isSimplyLaced`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/FiniteDimensional.html#TauCeti.finiteDimensional_preprojectiveAlgebra_of_isSimplyLaced)). The proof bounds path lengths family by family: every path of length at least `n` vanishes for `Aₙ`, `4n` for `Dₙ`, `24` for `E₆` and `46` for `E₈` ([`isAdmissibleIdeal_preprojectiveIdeal_E8`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Preprojective/ADE/TypeE/Basic.html#TauCeti.isAdmissibleIdeal_preprojectiveIdeal_E8)). These bounds come from small lemmas about products of square-zero and cube-zero elements (TauCeti#10854). Together with last window's non-Dynkin theorem, this gives the full finite/infinite dichotomy. Self-injectivity is still proved only for `A₁` and `A₂`.

Layers 7 and 8 have begun. Liu–Wang's quartic `A∞` deformation of the affine `D₄` zigzag algebra now exists, with the four displayed values of `m₄`, the Stasheff identities, strict unitality and the bidegree ([`liuWangAffineD4AInfinityAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/AffineD4.html#TauCeti.liuWangAffineD4AInfinityAlgebra)). It is one instance of a general deformation attached to closed walks of length four ([`zigzagLoopAInfinityAlgebra`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/AInfinity/Loop.html#TauCeti.zigzagLoopAInfinityAlgebra)). That the deformation is nontrivial up to `A∞` isomorphism is not proved. The Khovanov–Seidel braid complexes `B_i = [P_i ⊗ e_i Z ⟶ Z]` and their inverses are now constructed as complexes of graded bimodules ([`zigzagBraidComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Braid/Basic.html#TauCeti.zigzagBraidComplex)), but the braid relations are not. The completed Ginzburg algebra `Π̂₂(Q)` also landed, with `Π₂(Q)` as its finite-support part ([`completedGinzburgTwo`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Ginzburg/Completion.html#TauCeti.completedGinzburgTwo)).

Work toward Koszulity has started. The first two stages of the Koszul complex of a zigzag simple are exact away from `A₂` components ([`range_zigzagProjectiveQuadraticDifferential_eq_ker`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Quiver/Zigzag/Projective/QuadraticPresentation.html#TauCeti.range_zigzagProjectiveQuadraticDifferential_eq_ker)), and `A₁` and `A₂` have explicit periodic resolutions. Elsewhere, vertex projectives gained a bound-quiver presentation, Loewy layers and graded Ext-Euler values. Couture's classification gained its graded form modulo `Aut(G)`, and cyclic derivatives arrived for the three-dimensional side.
