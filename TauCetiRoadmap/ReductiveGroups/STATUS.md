## The frontier

- **Maximal-torus conjugacy.** Remove the assumption that diagonalizable subgroups conjugate into a reference torus, and establish common maximal tori for pairs of Borels.
- **Parabolics as cocharacter subgroups.** Identify every parabolic `k`-subgroup with `P(λ)` and prove transitivity of `U(k)` on its Levi complements; this is needed for Layer 7b.
- **Rank-one root maps.** Parameterize the contracted centralizer subgroups by the additive group, construct rank-one central isogenies, then prove the commutator and height-filtration laws needed for Bruhat coordinates.
- **Characteristic-zero Levi complements.** Construct intrinsic vector layers, regular sections and the cohomological splitting argument, then prove existence and unipotent conjugacy of complements over the ground field.
- **Signed ADE folding.** Build the pinned action, integral fixed basis and bracket, and folded presentation; retain the distinction between integral fixed lattices and their scalar extensions.
