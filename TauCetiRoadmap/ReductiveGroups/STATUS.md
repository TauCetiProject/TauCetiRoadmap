## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The foundations are done: the group-scheme dictionary, comodules, the Lie algebra, tori, the unipotent radical and Matsushima's affine quotient. Borel conjugacy, closed orbits and a finite Weyl group of a torus are proved, but root data, torus conjugacy and parabolics as `P(λ)` are still missing, so the structure theory is partial. Levi complements in characteristic zero, most of the finite-field lane (Layers 7b–7e and 7g), classification and the spectral Weyl lane have not begun.

### Named results

- **Conjugacy of Borel subgroups** — over an algebraically closed field, [any two Borel subgroups of a finite-type affine group are conjugate by a rational point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Conjugacy/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq), with no smoothness, reducedness or connectedness assumed.
- **The closed-orbit lemma** — for a finite-type group scheme acting on a nonempty finite-type scheme over an algebraically closed field, orbits are locally closed with lower-dimensional boundary, so [some orbit is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupAction/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SchemeAction.exists_isClosed_orbit).
- **Matsushima's criterion** — in characteristic zero, for a faithful reductive `G`, [the fppf quotient `GLₙ/G` is `Spec O(GLₙ)^G`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/GeneralLinear.html#TauCeti.Comodule.isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_ker_coordinateBialgHom), and the affine quotient [commutes with extension of the base field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.fppfHomogeneousQuotientBaseChangeIso).
- **The Weyl group of a diagonalizable subgroup** — it [is finite](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/WeylGroup.html#BialgHom.finite_weylGroup), equals the rational normalizer modulo the rational centralizer, permutes the roots preserving multiplicity, and for the diagonal torus of `GL_N` [is the symmetric group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/DiagonalTorus/WeylGroup.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.permMulEquivDiagonalTorusWeylGroup).
- **Simplicity of binary forms in characteristic `p`** — the degree-`d` forms under `SL₂` [are simple exactly when `d + 1 = (a + 1)pᵐ` with `a < p`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialLinear/BinaryForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.SpecialLinear.isAtom_binaryForms_iff_of_charP), and their simple socle has dimension the product of `dⱼ + 1` over the base-`p` digits of `d`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Parabolic subgroups, algebraic and dynamic.** A [parabolic subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic) contains a Borel after base change to an algebraic closure. For a cocharacter `λ`, explicit Hopf ideals cut out `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)` with [`P(λ)/U(λ) ≅ Z(λ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Quotient.html#BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso), and `U(λ)` is geometrically connected with no smoothness assumption.
- **Induced comodules.** [Induction along a coalgebra map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Induced.html#TauCeti.Comodule.induced) is the cotensor equalizer, with Frobenius reciprocity. This is what the modules `∇(λ)` will be built from.
- **Frobenius-fixed points of arbitrary forms.** For any `F_q`-form `G₀`, [`G₀(F_q)` is the group fixed by the geometric Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Frobenius/FiniteField.html#TauCeti.FiniteField.rationalPointsMulEquivFixedSubgroupGeometricFrobenius), finite and natural in `G₀`.

## The frontier

- **Root datum of a split reductive group.** The subgroups [`U_G(λ) ∩ Z_G(ker α)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Unipotent/KernelCentralizer.html#TauCeti.SplitMaximalTorus.unipotentCentralizerKerHopfIdeal) are smooth and connected, and their Lie algebra is the sum of root spaces on positive multiples of `α`. It remains to parametrize them by `𝔾ₐ`, build the rank-one map and coroot, and show the Weyl group is generated by reflections.
- **Conjugacy of maximal tori.** The general theorem still assumes that diagonalizable subgroups conjugate into a reference torus. The vector-group step is in place: [natural splittings of `V_a ⋊ D` are conjugate to the standard one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/SemidirectProduct.html#TauCeti.Comodule.exists_splitting_eq_conj_of_naturality).
- **Parabolics as `P(λ)`.** Each parabolic `k`-subgroup must be identified with some `P(λ)`, and `U(k)` shown to act simply transitively on its Levi subgroups. Layer 7b waits on this.
- **Induced and simple modules.** Induction now allows `∇(λ) = Ind_(B⁻)^G k_λ`; finite dimensionality, the highest line, the simple socle `L(λ)` and classification by dominant weights remain.
- **Projective closed orbits and Lang's theorem.** Layer 3A next needs properness of the orbit model and the restriction `V → H⁰(X, O(1))`. Nothing here yet applies Lang's theorem to `F`-stable parabolics, unipotent radicals or centralizers, so Layers 7b–7e cannot start.
