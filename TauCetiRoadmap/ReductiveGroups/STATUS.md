### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Quotients and extensions of affine groups.** Over a field, the functions invariant under a normal subgroup `N` form the coordinate ring of [`G ⧸ N`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Coinvariants/Quotient.html#TauCeti.CommHopfAlgCat.exists_comp_coinvariantsι_iff), which has the universal property of the quotient. Together with [`IsShortExact`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Quotient/Kernel/Exact.html#TauCeti.CommHopfAlgCat.IsShortExact) and the fact that every faithfully flat morphism is the quotient by its kernel, this lets extensions be built as well as recognised.
- **Subgroups as stabilizers.** A closed subgroup with finitely generated ideal is [the stabilizer of a subspace in a finite-dimensional piece of the regular representation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/SubspaceStabilizer.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.exists_finite_subcomodule_stabilizer). Exterior powers of a comodule are now [subcomodules](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/ExteriorAlgebra.html#TauCeti.Comodule.exteriorPowerSubcomodule), which is needed to turn the subspace into a line.
- **Explicit Chevalley carriers.** These are toral closures of root subgroups inside general linear groups over `ℤ`, such as the [`E₆` minuscule carrier](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/E6/Minuscule/GroupScheme.html#TauCeti.E6Minuscule.groupScheme). Most, including the standard type-`A` carrier, are generated by their simple root subgroups, so morphisms out of them are determined there. Their diagram symmetries are the [unique automorphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/D4/Tripled/Triality.html#TauCeti.D4Tripled.eq_trialityAutomorphism_of_rootSubgroup) realizing the symmetry on those subgroups. The CFSG consumer builds its finite groups of Lie type on them.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 0–2 and 4 are done. Layer 3 has Hopf ideals, kernels, component groups, fppf quotients with kernel–cokernel exactness, short exact sequences, subgroups as stabilizers, and now the quotient by a normal subgroup as an affine group. It lacks the proof that this quotient map is faithfully flat with kernel `N`, and representability of `G/H` for a general subgroup. Layer 5 can recognise the unipotent radical but has no general maximality theorem. Layer 6 has reductivity of the classical groups, radicals, central isogenies, and linear reductivity characterised by splitting. It lacks the characteristic-zero equivalence with linear reductivity, and simply connected covers. Layer 7 has Borel subgroups and maximal tori on the geometric fibre, dynamic parabolics, Tits systems for `GLₙ` and `SLₙ`, and root data for three split classical families. Containment of connected solvable subgroups in a conjugate of the upper-triangular group is proved for `GLₙ` and `SLₙ` only. Layer 8 is untouched. Layer 9 has explicit carriers for many types, with triality, special isogenies and uniqueness of graph automorphisms, and the `E₆` carrier has trivial unipotent radical when reduced. It lacks a construction from an arbitrary root datum and the pinned isomorphism theorem.

## The frontier

- **Takeuchi's theorem for `G ⧸ N`.** Over a field, the projection onto the coinvariants must be shown faithfully flat with kernel exactly `N`. Then `G ⧸ N` represents the fppf quotient sheaf, and every normal subgroup sits in a short exact sequence.
- **Chevalley's theorem, line form, and representable quotients.** The subspace stabilizer has to become the stabilizer of a line in an exterior power. Exterior powers are now subcomodules, so what remains is the top-degree argument, then representability of `G/H` and affineness when `H` is reductive.
- **Conjugacy of Borels and root data in general.** Geometric conjugacy is needed for an arbitrary smooth connected group; so far it is known, as containment in a conjugate of the upper-triangular group, only for `GLₙ` and `SLₙ`. It blocks extracting the root datum of an arbitrary reductive group and identifying `N(T)/T` with its Weyl group.
- **The unipotent radical.** It remains to prove that the connected normal smooth unipotent subgroup is maximal in general. The present criteria only recognise it.
- **The pinned isomorphism theorem.** Uniqueness of automorphisms with given action on root subgroups is proved carrier by carrier. Existence for an arbitrary isomorphism of root data is still missing, as is the identification of each carrier with the pinned simply connected group of its type.
