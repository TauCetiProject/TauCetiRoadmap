## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The foundations are done: the group-scheme dictionary, representations as comodules, the Lie algebra, tori, solvable and unipotent groups, and now Matsushima's affine quotient with its base change. Borel conjugacy, closed orbits and a Weyl group for any torus are proved; root data, Bruhat and the full parabolic theory are not. Layer 8 has begun, and Layer 9's explicit carriers now have Weyl and commutator relations. Most of the finite-field lane (Layers 7b–7e and 7g), Levi complements in characteristic zero, the classification theorems and the spectral Weyl lane have not begun.

### Named results

- **Conjugacy of Borel subgroups** — over an algebraically closed field, [any two Borel subgroups of a finite-type affine group are conjugate by a rational point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Conjugacy/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq), with no smoothness, reducedness or connectedness assumed.
- **The closed-orbit lemma** — when a finite-type group scheme acts on a nonempty finite-type scheme over an algebraically closed field, orbits are locally closed with lower-dimensional boundary, so [some orbit is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupAction/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SchemeAction.exists_isClosed_orbit).
- **Matsushima's criterion** — in characteristic zero, for a faithful reductive `G`, [`GLₙ/G` is the affine scheme `Spec O(GLₙ)^G`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/GeneralLinear.html#TauCeti.Comodule.isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_ker_coordinateBialgHom), and the affine quotient [commutes with extension of the base field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.fppfHomogeneousQuotientBaseChangeIso).
- **Structure of connected solvable groups** — over an algebraically closed field in every characteristic, [the unipotent radical of a reduced connected solvable group is the common kernel of its characters](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Solvable/UnipotentRadical.html#TauCeti.FiniteTypeCommHopfAlgCat.unipotentRadicalDefiningIdeal_eq_characterKernelIdeal), and [the quotient by it is a torus](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Solvable/UnipotentRadical.html#TauCeti.FiniteTypeCommHopfAlgCat.torus_coinvariants_unipotentRadical).
- **Simplicity of binary forms** — the degree-`d` binary forms under `SL₂` [are simple exactly when no binomial coefficient `d choose n` vanishes in `k`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialLinear/BinaryForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.SpecialLinear.isAtom_binaryForms_iff); in characteristic `p` that is [`d + 1 = (a + 1)pᵐ` with `a < p`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialLinear/BinaryForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.SpecialLinear.isAtom_binaryForms_iff_of_charP).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The Weyl group of a torus.** For a closed diagonalizable subgroup, [the Weyl group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/WeylGroup.html#BialgHom.weylGroup) is the finite group of character-lattice automorphisms induced by the normalizer, [isomorphic to normalizer modulo centralizer on rational points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/WeylGroup.html#BialgHom.normalizerQuotientCentralizerEquiv). For a split maximal torus of a reductive group it permutes the roots with their multiplicities.
- **Parabolic subgroups, algebraic and dynamic.** A [parabolic subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic) is a smooth closed subgroup that contains a Borel after base change to an algebraic closure. For a cocharacter `λ`, Hopf ideals cut out `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)`, with [`P(λ)/U(λ) ≅ Z(λ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Quotient.html#BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso), and `U(λ)` is geometrically connected without smoothness assumptions.
- **Induced comodules.** [Induction along a coalgebra morphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Induced.html#TauCeti.Comodule.induced) is the cotensor product, with [Frobenius reciprocity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Induced.html#TauCeti.Comodule.inducedEquiv). It is the construction behind the costandard modules `∇(λ)` of Layer 10.

## The frontier

- **Root datum of a split reductive group.** The Weyl group already permutes the roots. What remains is attaching coroots, showing each reflection `s_α` lies in the Weyl group, and identifying that group with the reflection group of the root system.
- **Rank-one root maps.** [`U(λ) ∩ Z(ker α)` has Lie algebra a sum of root spaces `𝔤_{nα}`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Unipotent/KernelCentralizer.html#TauCeti.SplitMaximalTorus.lieSubalgebra_unipotentCentralizerKerHopfIdeal_toSubmodule) and is smooth and connected. Its identification with `𝔾ₐ`, the rank-one chart and central isogeny, and the commutator relations remain.
- **Conjugacy of maximal tori.** The general theorem still assumes that diagonalizable subgroups conjugate into a reference torus. For vector groups, [natural splittings of `V ⋊ D` are conjugate to the standard one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/SemidirectProduct.html#TauCeti.Comodule.exists_splitting_eq_conj_of_naturality).
- **Parabolics as `P(λ)`.** Each parabolic `k`-subgroup must be identified with some `P(λ)`, with `U(k)` acting transitively on Levi subgroups. Layer 7b waits on this, and on Lang-theorem adapters for `F`-stable parabolics.
- **Projective closed orbits.** Closed orbit schemes in proper schemes are proper. Layer 3A next needs their complex comparison and the equivariant restriction `V → H⁰(X, O(1))`.
- **Carriers as pinned groups.** Work on the exceptional, tripled `D₄` and spin carriers has produced Weyl and Chevalley commutator relations, rank-one root groups and torus centralizers. Identifying these carriers with pinned groups remains.
