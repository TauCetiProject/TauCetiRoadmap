- **Simplicity of binary forms** — in characteristic p, the degree-d rational `SL₂` module is simple exactly when `d + 1 = (a + 1)pᵐ` for some `a < p` ([`isAtom_binaryForms_iff_of_charP`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialLinear/BinaryForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.SpecialLinear.isAtom_binaryForms_iff_of_charP)).

## The frontier

- **Root datum of a reductive group.** Remove the extra conjugacy hypothesis for maximal tori, construct rank-one root maps and coroots, and identify the normalizer action with the root-system Weyl group.
