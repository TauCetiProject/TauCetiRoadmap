## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The foundations are done: the group-scheme dictionary, representations as comodules, the Lie algebra, tori and the unipotent radical. Borel conjugacy, closed orbits and Matsushima's affine quotient are proved in general. The structure theory beyond Borel conjugacy is partial. Most of the finite-field lane (Layers 7b–7e and 7g) has not begun, and neither have Levi complements in characteristic zero, classification or the spectral Weyl lane.

### Named results

- **Conjugacy of Borel subgroups** — over an algebraically closed field, [any two Borel subgroups of a finite-type affine group are conjugate by a rational point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Conjugacy/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq). The ambient group need not be smooth, reduced or connected.
- **The closed-orbit lemma** — when a finite-type group scheme acts on a nonempty finite-type scheme over an algebraically closed field, orbits are locally closed with lower-dimensional boundary. So [some orbit is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupAction/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SchemeAction.exists_isClosed_orbit), and every orbit closure contains a closed orbit.
- **Matsushima's criterion for `GLₙ/G`** — in characteristic zero, for a faithful reductive `G`, [the fppf quotient `GLₙ/G` is the affine scheme `Spec O(GLₙ)^G`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/GeneralLinear.html#TauCeti.Comodule.isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_ker_coordinateBialgHom).
- **Cartier's theorem** — [an affine group scheme of finite type over a field of characteristic zero is smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Group/Cartier.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.smooth_of_grpObj_of_charZero).
- **Reductive equals linearly reductive in characteristic zero** — a geometrically connected affine group of finite type is reductive exactly when all its representations are completely reducible (`reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_iff_linearlyReductive`).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Parabolic subgroups, algebraic and dynamic.** A [parabolic subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic) is a smooth subgroup that contains a Borel after base change to an algebraic closure. For a cocharacter `λ`, explicit Hopf ideals cut out the subgroups `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)`, with [`P(λ)/U(λ) ≅ Z(λ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Quotient.html#BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso). All three are smooth when `G` is.
- **Normalizers and centralizers of diagonalizable subgroups.** Both are closed subgroup schemes, [their quotient is finite étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/Quotient.html#BialgHom.algebraEtale_coinvariants_centralizer_normalizer), and [the centralizer is smooth when `G` is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Centralizer/Smooth.html#BialgHom.smoothCommHopfAlgProperty_quotient_centralizerHopfIdeal). A Weyl group `N(T)/Z(T)` needs this.
- **Frobenius-fixed points of arbitrary forms.** For any `F_q`-form `G₀`, [`G₀(F_q)` is the group fixed by the geometric Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Frobenius/FiniteField.html#TauCeti.FiniteField.rationalPointsMulEquivFixedSubgroupGeometricFrobenius). It is finite and natural in `G₀`.

Layer 10 has weights and formal characters, but not induced or simple modules in general. Layers 6A, 7b–7e, 7g, 8 and 11 are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Conjugacy of maximal tori.** The general theorem still assumes that diagonalizable subgroups conjugate into a reference torus. The vector-group step for solvable groups is in place: [two splittings of a semidirect product by a diagonalizable group are conjugate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/SemidirectProduct.html#TauCeti.Comodule.IsRegularCocycle₁.isConj_pointSplitting).
- **Parabolics as `P(λ)`.** It remains to identify each parabolic `k`-subgroup with some `P(λ)` and to prove that `U(k)` acts transitively on Levi subgroups. [Distinct points of `U(λ)` already give distinct Levi factors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Conjugation.html#BialgHom.injOn_conjugate_leviDefiningHopfIdeal). Layer 7b waits on this.
- **Root datum and Weyl group of a reductive group.** Centralizers of character kernels start the rank-one root maps. Extracting roots and coroots and identifying `N(T)/T` with the Weyl group remain.
- **Signed ADE folding.** Signed cycles contribute `A` or `A[2]`, and [orbit column sums of a forest's Cartan matrix are one or two](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/OrbitCartan.html#SimpleGraph.IsAcyclic.exists_graphCartanMatrix_sum_orbit). What remains is the pinned action on the Lie carrier, the integral fixed basis and bracket, and the folded presentation.
- **Projective closed orbits and Lang's theorem.** Layer 3A next needs properness of the orbit model and the equivariant restriction `V → H⁰(X, O(1))`. ReductiveGroupsPartII owns Lang's theorem, and nothing here yet applies it to `F`-stable parabolics, unipotent radicals or centralizers, so Layers 7b–7e cannot start.
