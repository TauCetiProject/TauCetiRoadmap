## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The foundations are done: the group-scheme dictionary, representations as comodules, the Lie algebra, tori and the unipotent radical. Borel conjugacy, closed orbits and Matsushima's affine quotient are proved in general; base change of the quotient completes Layer 6B. Structure theory beyond Borel conjugacy is partial, and so is the Layer 9 folding programme. Most of the finite-field lane (Layers 7b–7e and 7g) has not begun. Neither have Levi complements in characteristic zero, classification or the spectral Weyl lane.

### Named results

- **Conjugacy of Borel subgroups** — over an algebraically closed field, [any two Borel subgroups of a finite-type affine group are conjugate by a rational point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Conjugacy/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq). The ambient group need not be smooth, reduced or connected.
- **The closed-orbit lemma** — when a finite-type group scheme acts on a nonempty finite-type scheme over an algebraically closed field, orbits are locally closed with lower-dimensional boundaries, so [some orbit is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupAction/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SchemeAction.exists_isClosed_orbit).
- **Matsushima's criterion for `GLₙ/G`** — in characteristic zero, for a faithful reductive `G`, [the fppf quotient `GLₙ/G` is the affine scheme `Spec O(GLₙ)^G`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/GeneralLinear.html#TauCeti.Comodule.isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_ker_coordinateBialgHom). For a linearly reductive subgroup of a geometrically reduced group, [the affine quotient commutes with every extension of the base field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.fppfHomogeneousQuotientBaseChangeIso).
- **Cartier's theorem** — [an affine group scheme of finite type over a field of characteristic zero is smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Group/Cartier.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.smooth_of_grpObj_of_charZero).
- **Reductive equals linearly reductive in characteristic zero** — a geometrically connected affine group of finite type is reductive exactly when all its representations are completely reducible (`reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_iff_linearlyReductive`).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Parabolic subgroups, algebraic and dynamic.** A [parabolic subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic) is a smooth subgroup that contains a Borel after base change to an algebraic closure. For a cocharacter `λ`, explicit Hopf ideals cut out `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)`, with [`P(λ)/U(λ) ≅ Z(λ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Quotient.html#BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso).
- **Normalizers and centralizers of diagonalizable subgroups.** Both are closed subgroup schemes, and [their quotient is finite étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/Quotient.html#BialgHom.algebraEtale_coinvariants_centralizer_normalizer). This is what a Weyl group `N(T)/Z(T)` needs. Inside the centralizer of a character kernel, [the part contracted by `λ` has Lie algebra the weight spaces `𝔤_{nα}` with `n⟨α,λ⟩ > 0`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Unipotent/KernelCentralizer.html#TauCeti.SplitMaximalTorus.lieSubalgebra_unipotentCentralizerKerHopfIdeal_toSubmodule).
- **Signed-cycle fixed modules.** For a family of signed cycles, [the fixed module has one copy of the coefficients per positive cycle and 2-torsion per negative one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/WeightedCycle/Family/Signed.html#Module.Basis.fixedEquivSignedFamily), and [that 2-torsion survives base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/WeightedCycle/Family/BaseChange.html#Module.Basis.fixedSubmoduleBaseChange_surjective_iff). It underlies the integral fixed lattice of a folded Lie algebra.

## The frontier

- **Conjugacy of maximal tori.** The general theorem still assumes that diagonalizable subgroups conjugate into a reference torus. For solvable groups, [two splittings of a semidirect product by a diagonalizable group are conjugate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/SemidirectProduct.html#TauCeti.Comodule.IsRegularCocycle₁.isConj_pointSplitting).
- **Rank-one root groups.** Layer 7C next needs the smooth root subgroups parametrised by `𝔾ₐ`, the rank-one chart and central isogeny, then the commutator relations and height filtration.
- **Signed ADE folding.** The sign law, relabellings and fixed lattices are in place. What remains is the fixed Lie algebra of a diagram automorphism with its integral basis and bracket, its root system, and the folded presentation.
- **Parabolics as `P(λ)`.** Each parabolic `k`-subgroup must be identified with some `P(λ)`, with `U(k)` acting transitively on its Levi subgroups. Layer 7b waits on this.
- **Projective closed orbits and Lang's theorem.** Layer 3A next needs properness of the orbit model and the equivariant restriction `V → H⁰(X, O(1))`. Lang's theorem, owned by ReductiveGroupsPartII, is not yet applied to `F`-stable parabolics, unipotent radicals or centralizers, so Layers 7b–7e cannot start.
