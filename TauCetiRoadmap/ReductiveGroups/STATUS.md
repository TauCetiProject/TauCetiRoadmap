### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Short exact sequences of affine groups.** [`IsShortExact`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Quotient/Kernel/Exact.html#TauCeti.CommHopfAlgCat.IsShortExact) asks for a faithfully flat quotient map whose scheme-theoretic kernel is the subgroup. Such sequences are exact on points over every algebra, and every faithfully flat morphism is [the quotient by its kernel](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Quotient/Kernel/Coinvariants.html#TauCeti.CommHopfAlgCat.coinvariants_kernelHopfIdeal_eq_range), so extensions can be stated and computed with.
- **Subgroups as stabilizers.** A closed subgroup with finitely generated ideal is [the stabilizer of a subspace in a finite-dimensional piece of the regular representation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/SubspaceStabilizer.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.exists_finite_subcomodule_stabilizer). This is the first half of Chevalley's theorem and the usual starting point for constructing `G/H`.
- **Explicit Chevalley carriers.** These are toral closures of root subgroups inside general linear groups over `ℤ`, such as the [`E₆` minuscule carrier](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/E6/Minuscule/GroupScheme.html#TauCeti.E6Minuscule.groupScheme). Most are now generated by their simple root subgroups, so morphisms out of them are determined there. Their diagram symmetries are the [unique automorphisms](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/D4/Tripled/Triality.html#TauCeti.D4Tripled.eq_trialityAutomorphism_of_rootSubgroup) realizing the symmetry on those subgroups. The CFSG consumer builds its finite groups of Lie type on these carriers.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 0–2 and 4 are done. Layer 3 has Hopf ideals, kernels, component groups, fppf quotients with kernel–cokernel exactness, short exact sequences, and an isogeny criterion. It now also has subgroups as subspace stabilizers. It lacks representability of the quotient by a general subgroup. Layer 5 can recognise the unipotent radical but has no general maximality theorem. Layer 6 has reductivity of the classical groups, radicals, central isogenies, and linear reductivity characterised by splitting. It lacks the characteristic-zero equivalence with linear reductivity and simply connected covers. Layer 7 has Borel subgroups and maximal tori on the geometric fibre, dynamic parabolics, Tits systems for `GLₙ` and `SLₙ`, and root data for three split classical families. Borel conjugacy is proved only for `GLₙ` and `SL₂`. Layer 8 is untouched. Layer 9 has explicit carriers for many types, with triality, special isogenies and uniqueness of graph automorphisms. It lacks a construction from an arbitrary root datum and the pinned isomorphism theorem. The worked examples now include `PGLₙ` as the automorphism group scheme of the matrix algebra.

## The frontier

- **Chevalley's theorem, line form, and representable quotients.** The subspace stabilizer has to be converted into the stabilizer of a line in an exterior power; the exterior-algebra lemmas for this are in place. That is the input for showing that `G/H` is a scheme, and that it is affine when `H` is reductive.
- **Conjugacy of Borels and maximal tori.** Geometric conjugacy is needed for an arbitrary smooth connected group. It is known only for `GLₙ` and `SL₂`, and the general case is what blocks root data.
- **Root datum of an arbitrary reductive group.** The roots have to be extracted from a maximal torus, and `N(T)/T` identified with the Weyl group. That identification exists for `Sp₂ₘ` on rational points; the general case needs conjugacy first.
- **The unipotent radical.** It remains to prove that the connected normal smooth unipotent subgroup is maximal in general. The present criteria only recognise it.
- **The pinned isomorphism theorem.** Uniqueness of automorphisms with given action on root subgroups is proved carrier by carrier. Existence for an arbitrary isomorphism of root data is still missing, as is the identification of each carrier with the pinned simply connected group of its type.
