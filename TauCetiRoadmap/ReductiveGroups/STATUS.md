## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The foundations are done: the group-scheme dictionary, representations as comodules, the Lie algebra, tori and the unipotent radical. Borel conjugacy, closed orbits and Matsushima's affine quotient are now proved in general. The structure theory beyond Borel conjugacy is partial. Most of the finite-field lane (Layers 7b–7e and 7g), Levi complements in characteristic zero, classification and the spectral Weyl lane have not begun.

### Named results

- **Conjugacy of Borel subgroups** — over an algebraically closed field, [any two Borel subgroups of a finite-type affine group are conjugate by a rational point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Conjugacy/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq). The ambient group need not be smooth, reduced or connected.
- **The closed-orbit lemma** — for a finite-type group scheme acting on a nonempty finite-type scheme over an algebraically closed field, orbits are locally closed with lower-dimensional boundary, so [some orbit is closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupAction/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SchemeAction.exists_isClosed_orbit), and every orbit closure contains a closed orbit.
- **Matsushima's criterion for `GLₙ/G`** — in characteristic zero, for a faithful reductive `G`, [the fppf quotient `GLₙ/G` is the affine scheme `Spec O(GLₙ)^G`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/GeneralLinear.html#TauCeti.Comodule.isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_ker_coordinateBialgHom).
- **Cartier's theorem** — [an affine group scheme of finite type over a field of characteristic zero is smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Group/Cartier.html#TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.smooth_of_grpObj_of_charZero).
- **Reductive equals linearly reductive in characteristic zero** — a geometrically connected affine group of finite type is reductive exactly when all its representations are completely reducible (`reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_iff_linearlyReductive`).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Parabolic subgroups, algebraic and dynamic.** A [parabolic subgroup](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic) is a smooth subgroup containing a Borel after base change to an algebraic closure. For a cocharacter `λ`, the subgroups `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)` are cut out by explicit Hopf ideals, with [`P(λ)/U(λ) ≅ Z(λ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Quotient.html#BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso), and all three are smooth when `G` is.
- **Normalizers and centralizers of diagonalizable subgroups.** Both are closed subgroup schemes, [their quotient is finite étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/Quotient.html#BialgHom.algebraEtale_coinvariants_centralizer_normalizer), and [the centralizer is smooth when `G` is](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Centralizer/Smooth.html#BialgHom.smoothCommHopfAlgProperty_quotient_centralizerHopfIdeal). A Weyl group `N(T)/Z(T)` needs this.
- **Frobenius-fixed points of arbitrary forms.** For any `F_q`-form `G₀`, [`G₀(F_q)` is the group fixed by the geometric Frobenius](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Frobenius/FiniteField.html#TauCeti.FiniteField.rationalPointsMulEquivFixedSubgroupGeometricFrobenius). It is natural in `G₀` and finite.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 0, 1, 2, 4 and 5 are done. Layer 3 lacks only representability of `G/H` beyond reduced groups over algebraically closed fields; `SL₂/N⁺` is now the punctured plane over every ring. Layer 3A has orbit maps, stabilizers, local closedness, the dimension bound and closed orbits. It lacks properness, the complex comparison, restriction to sections of `O(1)` and the worked tests. Layer 5P has the finite exp/log, and in `GLₙ` their restriction to closed subgroups and Lie algebras. It lacks independence of the realization and the comparison with the complex Lie exponential. Layer 6 lacks simply connected covers and, in characteristic `p`, linear reductivity forcing a torus outside the solvable case. Layer 6B lacks base change of the Matsushima quotient. Layer 7 has Borel conjugacy and parabolics. It lacks unconditional maximal-torus conjugacy, root data, `N(T)/T` as the Weyl group, and Levi decompositions of parabolic `k`-subgroups. Layer 7C has centralizers of character kernels with their Lie algebras, and longest-element inversion sets, but no rank-one root maps. Layer 7a lacks the Lang application adapters. Layer 7f has the GL₃ example but not the Harish-Chandra adapter. Layer 9 has carriers and the simply laced shell Lie algebra with a Serre system, but no uniform construction. Layer 10 has weights, formal characters and binary forms, but not induced or simple modules in general. Layers 6A, 7b–7e, 7g, 8 and 11 are untouched.

## The frontier

- **Conjugacy of maximal tori.** The general theorem still assumes that diagonalizable subgroups conjugate into a reference torus. The vector-group step for solvable groups, where [two splittings of a semidirect product by a diagonalizable group are conjugate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/SemidirectProduct.html#TauCeti.Comodule.IsRegularCocycle₁.isConj_pointSplitting), is now in place.
- **Parabolics as `P(λ)`.** It remains to identify each parabolic `k`-subgroup with some `P(λ)` and to prove that `U(k)` acts transitively on Levi subgroups. [Distinct points of `U(λ)` already give distinct Levi factors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Conjugation.html#BialgHom.injOn_conjugate_leviDefiningHopfIdeal). Layer 7b waits on this.
- **Root datum and Weyl group of a reductive group.** Centralizers of character kernels, with Lie algebra the sum of the root spaces on `ℤα`, are the start of the rank-one root maps. Extracting roots and coroots and identifying `N(T)/T` with the Weyl group remain.
- **Projective closed orbits.** With closed orbits in hand, Layer 3A next needs properness of the orbit model and the equivariant restriction `V → H⁰(X, O(1))`.
- **Lang's theorem.** ReductiveGroupsPartII owns it. Nothing here yet applies it to `F`-stable parabolics, unipotent radicals or centralizers, so Layers 7b–7e cannot start.
