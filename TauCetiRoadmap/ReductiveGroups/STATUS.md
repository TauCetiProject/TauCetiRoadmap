## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The foundations are done: the group-scheme dictionary, representations as comodules, the Lie algebra, tori and the unipotent radical. Borel subgroups, quotients, reductivity and now closed orbits and parabolic subgroups are largely in place. The structure theory beyond Borel conjugacy is partial. The finite-field lanes 7b–7e and 7g, Levi complements in characteristic zero, classification and the spectral Weyl lane have not begun.

### Named results

- **Conjugacy of Borel subgroups** — over an algebraically closed field, [any two Borel subgroups of a finite-type affine group are conjugate by a rational point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Conjugacy/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq). The ambient group need not be smooth, reduced or connected.
- **Existence of closed orbits** — over an algebraically closed field, [every nonempty finite-type scheme with an action of a finite-type group scheme contains a closed orbit](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupAction/ClosedOrbit.html#TauCeti.SchemeAction.exists_isClosed_orbit).
- **Matsushima's criterion, affine direction** — in characteristic zero, for a reductive closed subgroup `N`, [the fppf quotient `G/N` is `Spec O(G)^N`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/Coinvariants.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_of_reductive). The underlying statement for linearly reductive `N` holds in every characteristic.
- **Reductive equals linearly reductive in characteristic zero** — a geometrically connected affine group of finite type is reductive exactly when all its representations are completely reducible (`reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_iff_linearlyReductive`).
- **The embedding theorem** — by [`exists_isClosedImmersion_generalLinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Embedding.html#TauCeti.AffineGroupSchemeCat.exists_isClosedImmersion_generalLinear), every affine group scheme of finite type over a field is a closed subgroup of some `GLₙ`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dynamic parabolics as smooth subgroup schemes.** For a cocharacter `λ`, the subgroups `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)` are cut out by explicit Hopf ideals. The Levi subgroup is [the centralizer of `λ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Centralizer.html#BialgHom.leviDefiningHopfIdeal_eq_centralizerHopfIdeal), and [`P(λ)/U(λ) ≅ Z(λ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Quotient.html#BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso). All three [are smooth](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Smooth.html#BialgHom.smoothCommHopfAlgProperty_quotient_parabolicDefiningHopfIdeal) when `G` is smooth of finite type over a Noetherian ring.
- **Parabolic subgroups over a field.** A subgroup is [parabolic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic) when it is smooth and its geometric fibre contains a Borel subgroup. Layer 7b builds on this notion.
- **Normalizers and centralizers of diagonalizable subgroups.** Both are closed subgroup schemes, and [their quotient is finite étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/Quotient.html#BialgHom.algebraEtale_coinvariants_centralizer_normalizer). This is the scheme-level finiteness a Weyl group `N(T)/Z(T)` needs.

## The frontier

- **Parabolics as dynamic parabolics.** Both notions exist and `P(λ)` is smooth. What remains is to prove that, in a connected reductive group, `P(λ)` is parabolic and every parabolic arises this way, then to get `P = L ⋉ U` over `k` and simple transitivity of `U(k)` on Levi subgroups.
- **Conjugacy of maximal tori.** The general theorem is conditional on conjugating diagonalizable subgroups into a reference torus. That hypothesis is discharged only for `GLₙ`, `SLₙ` and `Sp`. Borel conjugacy, together with the maximal torus inside each Borel, is the natural route.
- **Root datum and Weyl group of a reductive group.** The finite étale quotient `N/Z` and the adjoint weight spaces are in place. Roots and coroots still need to be extracted for a general maximal torus, and `N(T)/T` identified with the Weyl group.
- **Simply connected covers.** Only the predicate exists. Constructing covers, and central isogenies beyond `SLₙ → PGLₙ`, comes before classification.
- **Opening the finite-field lanes.** Layer 7a's point comparison and finiteness are done. Layer 7b needs the parabolic comparison above, plus Lang-theorem application adapters for `F`-stable parabolics and unipotent radicals. Lang's theorem itself is owned by ReductiveGroupsPartII.
