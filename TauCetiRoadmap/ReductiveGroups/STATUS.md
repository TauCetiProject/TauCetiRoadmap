## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The three-way group-scheme dictionary, representation theory, the Lie algebra, and the layer on tori and Jordan decomposition are done. Subgroups and quotients, radicals, reductivity, structure theory and the explicit pinned carriers are partial, and classification has not begun.

### Named results

- **The Hopf algebra–affine group scheme anti-equivalence**: [`commHopfAlgCatOpEquivAffineGroupSchemeCat`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.commHopfAlgCatOpEquivAffineGroupSchemeCat) identifies commutative Hopf algebras, contravariantly, with affine group schemes.
- **The embedding theorem**: [`exists_isClosedImmersion_generalLinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Embedding.html#TauCeti.AffineGroupSchemeCat.exists_isClosedImmersion_generalLinear) embeds every finite-type affine group scheme over a field as a closed subgroup of some general linear group.
- **Tannakian reconstruction**: [`fgPointTensorIsoEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Tannaka/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.Tannaka.fgPointTensorIsoEquiv) recovers the points of a commutative Hopf algebra as the tensor automorphisms of scalar extension on its finite-dimensional comodules.
- **Reductivity of the classical groups**: [`GLₙ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/Reductive.html#TauCeti.GeneralLinear.reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_finiteTypeCoordinateHopfAlgebra), [`SLₙ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialLinear/Reductive.html#TauCeti.SpecialLinear.reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_finiteTypeCoordinateHopfAlgebra) and [`Sp₂ₘ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Symplectic/Reductive.html#TauCeti.Symplectic.reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_finiteTypeCoordinateHopfAlgebra) are reductive over every field, and [`SOₙ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialOrthogonal/Reductive.html#TauCeti.SpecialOrthogonal.reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_finiteTypeCoordinateHopfAlgebra) is reductive in every dimension away from characteristic two.
- **Borel subgroups**: every finite-type affine group over a field [has a Borel subgroup on its geometric fibre](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.exists_geometricBorel). Conjugacy of Borels is proved only for particular groups: `GLₙ` and, over an algebraically closed field, [`SL₂`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialLinear/Borel/Conjugacy.html#TauCeti.SpecialLinear.Borel.exists_conjugate_eq_of_isBorelOverAlgClosed).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Split classical root data.** The diagonal-torus root data of [`SL_{r+1}`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialLinear/DiagonalTorus/RootDatum.html#TauCeti.SpecialLinear.diagonalRootDatum) and [`Sp₂ₘ`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Symplectic/DiagonalTorus/RootDatum.html#TauCeti.Symplectic.diagonalRootDatum) join the one for `GLₙ`. Each ties explicit root subgroups to the character and cocharacter lattices of the torus.
- **Tits systems of the linear groups.** Tits systems have an abstract theory of Bruhat cells. The [standard Tits system of `SL_{n+1}(k)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/SpecialLinearGroup/Diagonal/TitsSystem.html#TauCeti.slTitsSystem) is pulled back from the one for `GLₙ₊₁`, which gives the BN-pair interface to the downstream consumers who need only that.
- **Fppf quotient sheaf.** [`fppfQuotientSheaf`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Basic.html#TauCeti.CommHopfAlgCat.fppfQuotientSheaf) gives the quotient by a normal Hopf ideal as a group object in sheaves. General representability by a scheme remains open.

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 0–2 and 4 are done; tori are now also [classified by their cocharacter lattices](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Torus/Cocharacter/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.TorusCommHopfAlgCat.cocharacterLatticeFunctor_isEquivalence) over a perfect field. Layer 3 has Hopf ideals, kernels, component groups, fppf quotients and the invariants of a normal subgroup. It lacks general representability of quotients and short exact sequences. Layer 5 has geometric unipotence and criteria for recognising the unipotent radical, but no general maximality theorem. Layer 6 has reductivity of the classical groups, radicals, central isogenies, and linear reductivity characterised by splitting. It lacks the characteristic-zero equivalence between reductive and linearly reductive, and simply connected covers. Layer 7 has existence of Borels and maximal tori, dynamic parabolics and Levis, Tits systems for `GLₙ` and `SLₙ`, and root data for three split classical families. Conjugacy is known only group by group, and nothing extracts a root datum from an arbitrary reductive group. Layer 8 is untouched. Layer 9 has explicit carriers for many types and the `Sp₄` special isogeny. Neither a construction from an arbitrary root datum nor the pinned isomorphism theorem exists yet.

## The frontier

- **Conjugacy of Borels and maximal tori.** Prove geometric conjugacy for an arbitrary smooth connected group. It is known for `GLₙ` and `SL₂`, and existence and invariance under conjugation are general, so the general case is what blocks root data.
- **Root datum of an arbitrary reductive group.** Extract the roots from a maximal torus and identify the quotient of its normalizer as the Weyl group. This needs conjugacy first.
- **The unipotent radical.** Prove that the connected normal smooth unipotent subgroup is maximal in general. The present criteria only recognise it.
- **Uniform pinned Chevalley–Demazure groups.** Construct the split group scheme over `ℤ` from an arbitrary root datum and prove the pinned isomorphism theorem. The Geck carrier now has a closed-immersion root subgroup at every root, and for `G₂`, `F₄` and `E₈` it is generated by its simple root subgroups alone.
- **Remaining numbered symmetries and special isogenies.** Record triality on the tripled `D₄` carrier, construct the short-root `G₂` carrier, and prove the `F₄` and `G₂` special isogenies together with their square and root-subgroup relations.
