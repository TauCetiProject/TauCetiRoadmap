## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The foundations are done: the group-scheme dictionary, representations as comodules, the Lie algebra, tori and the unipotent radical. The general theory of Borel subgroups, quotients and reductivity is now largely in place. The structure theory beyond Borel conjugacy is partial. The finite-field lanes (Layers 7b–7g), Levi complements in characteristic zero, classification and the spectral Weyl lane have not begun.

### Named results

- **Conjugacy of Borel subgroups** — over an algebraically closed field, [any two Borel subgroups of a finite-type affine group are conjugate by a rational point](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Conjugacy/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq). The ambient group need not be smooth, reduced or connected.
- **Matsushima's criterion, affine direction** — in characteristic zero, for a reductive closed subgroup `N`, [the fppf quotient `G/N` is `Spec O(G)^N`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/Coinvariants.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_of_reductive). The underlying statement, for linearly reductive `N`, holds in any characteristic.
- **Representability of homogeneous quotients** — for a reduced affine group of finite type over an algebraically closed field, the fppf quotient by any closed subgroup is represented by the orbit of a line in projective space (`HopfIdeal.exists_fppfHomogeneousQuotient_iso_projectiveOrbitPoints`).
- **Reductive equals linearly reductive in characteristic zero** — a geometrically connected affine group of finite type is reductive exactly when all its representations are completely reducible (`reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_iff_linearlyReductive`).
- **The embedding theorem** — by [`exists_isClosedImmersion_generalLinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Embedding.html#TauCeti.AffineGroupSchemeCat.exists_isClosedImmersion_generalLinear), every affine group scheme of finite type over a field is a closed subgroup of some `GLₙ`.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dynamic parabolics as subgroup schemes.** For a cocharacter `λ`, the subgroups `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)` are represented by explicit Hopf ideals. The Levi is [the scheme-theoretic centralizer of `λ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Centralizer.html#BialgHom.leviDefiningHopfIdeal_eq_centralizerHopfIdeal), and [`P(λ)/U(λ) ≅ Z(λ)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Levi/Quotient.html#BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso) as an fppf quotient. This is the cocharacter description that Layer 7 asks parabolics to be compared with.
- **Normalizers and centralizers of diagonalizable subgroups.** Both are closed subgroup schemes, and [their quotient is finite étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/Quotient.html#BialgHom.algebraEtale_coinvariants_centralizer_normalizer). This is the scheme-level finiteness a Weyl group `N(T)/Z(T)` needs.
- **Pinnings and explicit Chevalley carriers.** A [pinning](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Pinning/Basic.html#TauCeti.Pinning) adds a split maximal torus and trivialized simple root spaces. The carriers are toral closures of root subgroups inside general linear groups over `ℤ`, for many types.

## The frontier

- **Conjugacy of maximal tori.** The general theorem exists conditionally: it assumes that diagonalizable subgroups can be conjugated into a reference torus. That hypothesis is discharged only for `GLₙ`, `SLₙ` and `Sp`. Borel conjugacy, together with the maximal torus inside each Borel, is the natural route to removing it.
- **Root datum and Weyl group of a reductive group.** The finite étale quotient `N/Z` and the adjoint weight spaces are now in place. What remains is to extract roots and coroots for a general maximal torus and to identify `N(T)/T` with the Weyl group.
- **Parabolic subgroups over a field.** `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)` are subgroup schemes with a Levi decomposition. The roadmap's parabolic `k`-subgroups still need defining and identifying with `P(λ)`, and simple transitivity of `U(k)` on Levi subgroups needs proving. Layer 7b depends on this.
- **Simply connected covers.** Only the predicate exists. Constructing covers, and central isogenies beyond `SLₙ → PGLₙ`, comes before classification.
- **Lang's theorem.** Nothing in the finite-field lane goes beyond Frobenius fixed points of split models. Without Lang–Steinberg, Layers 7b–7g cannot start.
