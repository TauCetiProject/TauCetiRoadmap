## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Four layers are done: the three-way group-scheme dictionary, representations as comodules, the Lie algebra, and tori with Jordan decomposition. Subgroups and quotients, the unipotent radical, reductivity, structure theory and the pinned Chevalley lane are partial; structure theory now has Borel conjugacy in full. Classification has not begun.

### Named results

- **The Hopf algebra–affine group scheme anti-equivalence** — [`commHopfAlgCatOpEquivAffineGroupSchemeCat`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.commHopfAlgCatOpEquivAffineGroupSchemeCat) identifies commutative Hopf algebras with affine group schemes, contravariantly.
- **The embedding theorem** — every affine group scheme of finite type over a field is a closed subgroup of some general linear group ([`exists_isClosedImmersion_generalLinear`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Embedding.html#TauCeti.AffineGroupSchemeCat.exists_isClosedImmersion_generalLinear)).
- **Tannakian reconstruction** — the points of a commutative Hopf algebra over a field are recovered from its finite-dimensional comodules ([`fgPointTensorIsoEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Representation/Tannaka/Equivalence.html#TauCeti.Tannaka.fgPointTensorIsoEquiv)).
- **Conjugacy of Borel subgroups** — any two Borel subgroups of a finite-type affine group over an algebraically closed field are conjugate by a rational point ([`IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Borel/Conjugacy/Existence.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsBorelOverAlgClosed.exists_conjugate_eq)).
- **Matsushima's criterion, affine direction** — in characteristic zero, the quotient of a group of finite type by a reductive closed subgroup `N` is affine, represented by the `N`-invariant functions ([`isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_of_reductive`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/Quotient/Homogeneous/Coinvariants.html#TauCeti.HopfIdeal.isIso_fppfHomogeneousQuotientToCoinvariantsPoints_of_reductive)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Dynamic parabolics as subgroup schemes.** The [parabolic of a cocharacter](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Representability.html#TauCeti.Cocharacter.parabolicDefiningHopfIdeal) and its Levi and unipotent parts are closed subgroup schemes. The Levi is the centralizer of the cocharacter, and `P = U ⋊ Z` holds as schemes and on Lie algebras. This is the root-data-free route the roadmap keeps open for consumers.
- **Centralizers and normalizers of diagonalizable subgroups.** These are [closed subgroup schemes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Centralizer/Basic.html#BialgHom.centralizerHopfIdeal), and the normalizer modulo the centralizer is [finite étale](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DiagonalizableGroup/Normalizer/Quotient.html#BialgHom.algebraEtale_coinvariants_centralizer_normalizer). This is the rigidity input for a finite Weyl group `N(T)/T`.
- **Explicit Chevalley carriers.** These are toral closures of root subgroups inside general linear groups over `ℤ`, such as the [`E₆` minuscule carrier](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/E6/Minuscule/GroupScheme.html#TauCeti.E6Minuscule.groupScheme).

### Roadmap coverage

Layers 0–2 and 4 are done. **Layer 3** has Hopf ideals, kernels, component groups with their universal property for maps to étale groups, and Chevalley's theorem on subgroups. It has quotients by normal subgroups of a geometrically reduced group, represented by coinvariants. It also has affine quotients by linearly reductive subgroups, and by reductive subgroups in characteristic zero. Representability of `G/H` for a general closed `H` is missing. **Layer 5** can recognise the unipotent radical but has no general maximality theorem. **Layer 6** proves `GLₙ`, `SLₙ`, `Sp₂ₘ` and `SOₙ` reductive, the last away from characteristic two. It has radicals, central isogenies and derived subgroups: the derived subgroup of `GLₙ` is `SLₙ`, and the derived subgroup of a reductive group is reductive. `GLₙ` is linearly reductive in characteristic zero. It lacks the general characteristic-zero equivalence with linear reductivity and simply connected covers. `SLₙ → PGLₙ` was last known to be short of a central isogeny. **Layer 7** has Borel conjugacy in general, a maximal torus in every Borel, dynamic parabolics with Levi decomposition, Tits systems for `GLₙ` and `SLₙ`, and root data for three split classical families. It lacks conjugacy of maximal tori, the root datum of an arbitrary reductive group, and `N(T)/T` as the Weyl group. **Layer 8** is untouched. **Layer 9** stands as last reported: pinnings, explicit carriers and special isogenies, but no construction from an arbitrary root datum and no pinned isomorphism theorem. The latest survey did not reach this lane.

## The frontier

- **Conjugacy of maximal tori.** With Borel conjugacy and maximal tori in Borels in place, what remains is conjugacy of maximal tori within a connected solvable group, which would give conjugacy in general.
- **The Weyl group and the root datum of a reductive group.** Normalizer modulo centralizer is finite étale for any diagonalizable subgroup. Still missing: identifying the centralizer of a maximal torus in a reductive group with the torus, reading `N(T)/T` as the Weyl group, and extracting the root datum beyond the three classical families.
- **Representable quotients `G/H`.** The affine case for reductive `H` is done in characteristic zero. For general `H`, the projective-orbit ingredients still have to be combined into global flatness of the orbit map, and the orbit identified with `G/H`. The converse direction of Matsushima's criterion is also open.
- **Reductive versus linearly reductive in characteristic zero.** `GLₙ` is done, and exact invariants exist. The general equivalence is missing.
- **Carriers as pinned groups.** The spin, tripled `D₄` and `E₆` carriers still have to be identified with the smooth connected subgroups their root subgroups generate, and then pinned. The existence half of the pinned isomorphism theorem is also missing.
