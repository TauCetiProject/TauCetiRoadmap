### Named results

- **The Hille–Yosida characterization** — a densely defined operator on a real Banach space generates a C₀ semigroup with `‖S(t)‖ ≤ M e^{ωt}` exactly when `M ≥ 1`, `(ω, ∞)` lies in its resolvent set, and `‖R(λ,A)ⁿ‖ ≤ M/(λ−ω)ⁿ` for all `n ≥ 1` ([`hilleYosida_generation_iff`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/HilleYosida/Generation.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.hilleYosida_generation_iff)).
- **Stone's theorem** — an operator `A` on a complex Hilbert space is self-adjoint exactly when `iA` generates a unitary C₀-group, which is then unique ([`isSelfAdjoint_iff_exists_isUnitary_complexGenerator_eq_I_smul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Stone/Unbounded.html#LinearPMap.isSelfAdjoint_iff_exists_isUnitary_complexGenerator_eq_I_smul)).
- **The Hausdorff–Bernstein–Widder theorem** — a function is continuous on `[0, ∞)` and completely monotone on `(0, ∞)` exactly when it is the Laplace transform of a finite positive measure on `ℝ≥0`, and that measure is unique ([`hausdorff_bernstein_widder`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/HausdorffBernsteinWidder.html#TauCeti.hausdorff_bernstein_widder)).
- **Bochner's theorem** — on a finite-dimensional real inner-product space the continuous positive-definite functions are exactly the Fourier transforms of finite Borel measures, and the measure is unique; the characteristic-function form and the classical statement on `ℝ` follow ([`bochner`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/BochnerTheorem.html#TauCeti.bochner)).
- **The Berg–Christensen–Ressel representation** — a function on `ℝ≥0 × V` is bounded, continuous and positive definite exactly when it is the Laplace–Fourier transform of a finite measure, which is unique ([`bcr_semigroup_bochner`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/SemigroupGroup/FourierLaplace/Existence.html#TauCeti.bcr_semigroup_bochner)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- The normed [`Complexification`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Complexification.html#TauCeti.Complexification) of a real Banach space carries the Taylor norm, so complexifying operators and C₀-semigroups loses no constants. This is how the real-Banach-first theory reaches a complex resolvent set with the Hille–Yosida bounds and a [holomorphic resolvent](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Complex.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.analyticOnNhd_resolvent_complexGenerator).
- [`IsCompleteBernsteinFunction`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/CompleteBernstein.html#TauCeti.IsCompleteBernsteinFunction), defined by the representation `a + bt + ∫ t/(t+x) dμ(x)`, is the common target of the Stieltjes correspondences and of the [Pick characterization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/Pick.html#TauCeti.isCompleteBernsteinFunction_iff_continuousWithinAt_nonneg_exists_analyticOnNhd), which recovers representing data from a holomorphic extension to the slit plane.
- The [`pontryaginMeasureTransform`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Pontryagin/Measure.html#MeasureTheory.FiniteMeasure.pontryaginMeasureTransform), the Fourier–Stieltjes transform of a finite measure on a Pontryagin dual, is the map an LCA Bochner theorem must invert. It is already proved positive definite, bounded by the total mass and, for tight measures, continuous.

### Roadmap coverage

In Part A every milestone and every item of the API list is proved, including uniqueness for the Cauchy problem, the duality-map form of dissipativity and a resolvent made holomorphic through complexification; the C₀-group stretch is complete with Stone's theorem. Part C has Bochner on `V` (with `ℝ` and `ℝᵈ` forms), BCR with uniqueness, and all three acceptance examples (`V = ℝ`, the Gaussian measure, `V = 0` recovering Hausdorff–Bernstein–Widder); the README's note that the BCR existence half remains is out of date, since `bcr_semigroup_bochner` proves both halves. Of Part C's LCA stretch only the measure-to-function direction exists. ## The frontier

- **The reciprocal Stieltjes–complete Bernstein correspondence** — prove that a nonzero `f` is complete Bernstein exactly when `1/f` is Stieltjes (by the existing `t f(t)` correspondence, equivalently that `t/f(t)` is again complete Bernstein). This is the one item missing from Part B; the Pick characterization, its natural input, is in place.
- **Bochner's theorem on LCA groups** — show that every continuous positive-definite function on a locally compact abelian group is the transform of a unique finite regular measure on the dual. Only the easy direction, from measures to functions, is established; the README ties this stretch to upstream Pontryagin-duality support.
