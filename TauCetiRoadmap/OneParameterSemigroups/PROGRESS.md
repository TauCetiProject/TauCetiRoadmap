## OneParameterSemigroups: 2026-06-21 to 2026-08-03 (`8c583a9` to `11ef09d`)

Bernstein's theorem landed: a completely monotone function on `[0,∞)` is the Laplace transform of a unique finite positive measure on `ℝ≥0` ([`existsUnique_isFiniteMeasure_integral_exp_neg_mul_eq_of_isCompletelyMonotone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/Unique.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isFiniteMeasure_integral_exp_neg_mul_eq_of_isCompletelyMonotone), TauCeti#1482 and TauCeti#1635). Existence runs through the rescaled Chafaï approximating measures, whose tightness gives a weak cluster point; uniqueness rests on a sharp Laplace-determinacy lemma, that a finite measure on `ℝ≥0` is already pinned down by its Laplace transform at the natural numbers. The converse direction, that such a transform is completely monotone, is not yet stated, so this is not an iff.

On the semigroup side the generator now determines the semigroup: two C₀ semigroups on a real Banach space with the same infinitesimal generator are equal ([`eq_of_generator_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.eq_of_generator_eq), TauCeti#1757), with the identity semigroup and the operator exponential `exp (t • A)` as the corollaries. Supporting work filled in the generator itself: dense domain, closedness, invariance of `D(A)` under the semigroup, and the abstract Cauchy problem, where orbits of domain vectors are [classical solutions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/CauchyProblem.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.isClassicalSolution_realOperator) and all orbits are mild ones. Dissipativity arrived with the [converse of Lumer-Phillips](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/Basic.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.ContractionSemigroup.isMDissipative_generator) — the generator of a contraction semigroup is m-dissipative — and the Hilbert characterization `⟪Ax, x⟫ ≤ 0`. The resolvent grew the API the generation theorems will need: the resolvent identity, smoothness in `λ`, the iterated derivative formula `dⁿR/dλⁿ = (-1)ⁿ n! R(λ)ⁿ⁺¹`, and `‖R(λ)ⁿ‖ ≤ λ⁻ⁿ` in the contraction case.

Positive-definite functions gained the [Kolmogorov decomposition](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/Kernel/Kolmogorov.html#TauCeti.IsPositiveDefiniteKernel.kolmogorovIsometry) of a positive-definite kernel, together with closure, continuity, normalization and pullback lemmas, a conversion between Mathlib's `e^{-2πi⟨·,·⟩}` convention and characteristic functions, and the Gaussian example.

## OneParameterSemigroups: 2026-08-03 to 2026-09-03 (`11ef09d` to `34ee8bb`)

The [Hille–Yosida characterization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/HilleYosida/Generation.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.hilleYosida_generation_iff) and the [Lumer–Phillips characterization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/LumerPhillips.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.exists_contractionSemigroup_generator_eq_iff) landed, giving the two generation criteria at their intended generality on real Banach spaces. Their common Yosida construction now includes compact-time convergence of the approximating exponentials, identification of the limiting generator, and the sharp general `(M, ω)` resolvent-power estimates. The semigroup theory also gained the [bounded perturbation theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/BoundedPerturbation.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.exists_generator_eq_vadd), dense iterated generator domains, and the multiplication-semigroup example.

[Bochner’s theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/BochnerTheorem.html#TauCeti.bochner) is now proved on an arbitrary finite-dimensional real inner-product space, with a unique finite Borel representing measure. That result feeds the [Berg–Christensen–Ressel representation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/SemigroupGroup/FourierLaplace/Existence.html#TauCeti.bcr_semigroup_bochner) for bounded continuous positive-definite functions on `ℝ≥0 × V`; the proof develops spatial Bochner slices, their decreasing Radon–Nikodym densities, and the fibrewise Bernstein kernels needed to assemble the Laplace–Fourier measure.

The completely monotone lane now has the full finite-measure [Hausdorff–Bernstein–Widder equivalence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/HausdorffBernsteinWidder.html#TauCeti.hausdorff_bernstein_widder), together with its open-half-line, possibly infinite-measure form, composition with Bernstein functions, and the [Lévy–Khintchine representation with a unique triplet](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/LevyKhintchine/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.IsBernsteinFunction.existsUnique_eqOn_bernsteinLevyKhintchineExponent). C₀ groups and unitary groups have also begun as a stretch layer, including skew-adjointness of a unitary group’s generator and the bounded self-adjoint exponential example; the full unbounded Stone theorem is not established here.

## OneParameterSemigroups: 2026-09-03 to 2026-09-26 (`34ee8bb` to `759eb3e`)

Stone's theorem is proved for unbounded operators: an operator `A` on a complex Hilbert space is self-adjoint exactly when `iA` generates a unitary C₀-group, and that group is unique ([`isSelfAdjoint_iff_exists_isUnitary_complexGenerator_eq_I_smul`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Stone/Unbounded.html#LinearPMap.isSelfAdjoint_iff_exists_isUnitary_complexGenerator_eq_I_smul)). It comes out of Lumer–Phillips: `±iA` are m-dissipative, and the contraction semigroups they generate glue into a group. The resolvent went complex: a [complexification](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Complexification.html#TauCeti.Complexification) of real Banach spaces whose Taylor norm keeps operator norms and growth bounds exact turns each real C₀-semigroup into a complex-linear one, with a [holomorphic resolvent](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Resolvent/Complex.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.analyticOnNhd_resolvent_complexGenerator) on `Re λ > ω` obeying the Hille–Yosida bounds. Also new: [uniqueness for the abstract Cauchy problem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/CauchyProblem/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.IsClassicalSolution.unique) and duality-map forms of [dissipativity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/Duality.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.isDissipative_iff_exists_mem_dualitySet_apply_nonpos) and [Lumer–Phillips](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/LumerPhillips.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.exists_contractionSemigroup_generator_eq_iff_exists_mem_dualitySet_apply_nonpos).

In Part B the [Pick characterization of complete Bernstein functions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/Pick.html#TauCeti.isCompleteBernsteinFunction_iff_continuousWithinAt_nonneg_exists_analyticOnNhd) landed (Schilling–Song–Vondraček, Theorem 6.2): a function is complete Bernstein exactly when it is right-continuous at `0`, nonnegative on `(0, ∞)`, and extends holomorphically to the slit plane, mapping the upper half-plane into its closure. The route runs through the [Herglotz representation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Herglotz.html#TauCeti.exists_isFiniteMeasure_eq_herglotzTransform_add) on the disc, a Nevanlinna representation on the line, and a Stieltjes–Perron argument clearing the measure off the positive half-axis. Around it, Stieltjes functions were characterized as [nonnegative constants plus Laplace transforms of completely monotone functions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/Laplace.html#TauCeti.isStieltjesFunction_iff_exists_isCompletelyMonotoneOnIoi) and matched with complete Bernstein functions through [`t f(t)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/CompleteBernstein.html#TauCeti.isStieltjesFunction_iff_exists_isCompleteBernsteinFunction_eqOn_mul) and [`f(1/t)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/Inversion.html#TauCeti.isStieltjesFunction_iff_exists_isCompleteBernsteinFunction_eqOn_comp_inv); both classes have [unique representing data](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.IsStieltjesFunction.existsUnique_representsStieltjes), and Bernstein functions were characterized by their [completely monotone exponentials](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/Exponential/Basic.html#TauCeti.isBernsteinFunction_iff_nonneg_and_forall_isContinuousCompletelyMonotoneOnIoi_exp_neg_mul). The reciprocal correspondence (`f` complete Bernstein iff `1/f` Stieltjes) is not yet proved.

Part C gained [Bochner's theorem in characteristic-function form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/CharFun/Bochner.html#TauCeti.bochner_charFun), with its probability and real-line versions, and closed its acceptance examples: the [Gaussian's representing measure](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/Gaussian/Measure.html#TauCeti.map_smul_stdGaussian_eq_bochnerMeasure_cexp_neg_sq_norm) is identified, and BCR with no spatial variable is [exactly Hausdorff–Bernstein–Widder](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/SemigroupGroup/FourierLaplace/ZeroSpatial.html#TauCeti.bcr_zero_spatial_iff_hausdorff_bernstein_widder). Part B's last missing piece landed: [complete Bernstein functions are exactly the reciprocals of Stieltjes functions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/Reciprocal.html#TauCeti.isCompleteBernsteinFunction_iff_continuousWithinAt_isStieltjesFunction_inv) (Schilling–Song–Vondraček, Chapter 7). A function is complete Bernstein when it is right-continuous at `0` and `1/f` is Stieltjes, with no nonvanishing hypothesis needed (TauCeti#9798). The proof goes through the Pick characterization and the [conjugate](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Stieltjes/Reciprocal.html#TauCeti.IsCompleteBernsteinFunction.exists_isCompleteBernsteinFunction_eqOn_div) `t/f(t)` of a complete Bernstein function. Alongside it, the [extreme rays of the completely monotone cone](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/Extremal.html#TauCeti.IsContinuousCompletelyMonotoneOnIoi.exists_eq_mul_exp_neg_mul_of_extreme_ray) were shown to be exponentials `f(0)e^{-pt}`. That rests on a measure-theoretic lemma: a finite measure on a standard Borel space that is proportional to each of its restrictions [is a point mass](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Atom.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.exists_eq_smul_dirac_of_forall_restrict_eq_smul).

In Part A, the [smooth vectors](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/SmoothVectors.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.smoothVectors) of a C₀ semigroup, the vectors in every `D(Aⁿ)`, are [dense](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/SmoothDensity.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.dense_smoothVectors). They are characterized by their orbits: `x ∈ D(Aⁿ)` exactly when `t ↦ S(t)x` is [`Cⁿ` on `[0, ∞)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/SmoothVectors.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.mem_domainPow_iff_contDiffOn_realOperator).

Part C's LCA stretch gained groundwork but no representation theorem. A positive-definite function on an abelian group is now a [matrix coefficient of its GNS unitary representation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/Function/GNS.html#TauCeti.IsPositiveDefiniteSub.eq_inner_gnsRepresentation), and that representation is [strongly continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/Function/GNS.html#TauCeti.IsPositiveDefiniteSub.continuous_gnsRepresentation_apply) when the function is continuous at `0`. On the measure side, the Pontryagin Fourier–Stieltjes transform received small additivity and point-mass lemmas. The converse Bochner direction on LCA groups is still open. It would need spectral measures on top of the GNS representation.

## OneParameterSemigroups: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-05 (`835fbbd` to `1d09589`)

Bochner's theorem now holds on every locally compact abelian group, which completes Part C's stretch goal: a function is continuous and positive definite exactly when it is the Fourier–Stieltjes transform of a unique finite inner regular measure on the Pontryagin dual ([`continuous_and_isPositiveDefiniteSub_iff_existsUnique_innerRegular_pontryaginMeasureTransform_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/LocallyCompactGroup.html#TauCeti.continuous_and_isPositiveDefiniteSub_iff_existsUnique_innerRegular_pontryaginMeasureTransform_eq), TauCeti#11222). Existence starts from last window's GNS representation. That representation is integrated against `L¹` weights, giving a commutative C⋆-algebra of operators each of whose nonzero characters determines a unique continuous character of the group. Positive functionals on that algebra become measures on its character space, which yields a [spectral theorem for strongly continuous unitary representations](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Pontryagin/StronglyContinuous.html#ContRepresentation.exists_pontryaginMeasureTransform_eq_inner) in cyclic form. Uniqueness comes from the fact that the characters separate points, so the [transform is injective on inner regular measures](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Fourier/Pontryagin/Uniqueness.html#MeasureTheory.FiniteMeasure.pontryaginMeasureTransform_injOn_innerRegular). When the group is second countable its dual is Polish, and the [Borel form](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/LocallyCompactGroup.html#TauCeti.continuous_and_isPositiveDefiniteSub_iff_existsUnique_pontryaginMeasureTransform_eq) needs no regularity hypothesis. Two classical special cases came first: [Bochner on discrete abelian groups](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/DiscreteGroup.html#TauCeti.isPositiveDefiniteSub_iff_exists_pontryaginMeasureTransform_eq) and [Herglotz's theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/Herglotz.html#TauCeti.isPositiveDefiniteSub_iff_exists_isFiniteMeasure_integral_zpow_eq), which says that the positive-definite sequences on `ℤ` are exactly the moment sequences of finite measures on the circle.

Part A gained [multiplication semigroups on ℓᵖ](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Multiplication/Lp/Basic.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.ContractionSemigroup.ofLpMultiplication), with explicit generator domain and resolvent. They supply the standard example of a strongly continuous semigroup that is [not norm-continuous](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Multiplication/Lp/Basic.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.ContractionSemigroup.ofLpMultiplication_not_continuousAt_zero) when the multiplier is unbounded. In Part B, the [tail integral of an integrable completely monotone function](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Integral/Tail.html#TauCeti.IsCompletelyMonotoneOnIoi.isContinuousCompletelyMonotoneOnIoi_integral_Ioi) is again completely monotone, which fills in the integral-closure item.
