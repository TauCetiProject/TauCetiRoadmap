## OneParameterSemigroups: 2026-06-21 to 2026-08-03 (`8c583a9` to `11ef09d`)

Bernstein's theorem landed: a completely monotone function on `[0,∞)` is the Laplace transform of a unique finite positive measure on `ℝ≥0` ([`existsUnique_isFiniteMeasure_integral_exp_neg_mul_eq_of_isCompletelyMonotone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/Unique.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isFiniteMeasure_integral_exp_neg_mul_eq_of_isCompletelyMonotone), TauCeti#1482 and TauCeti#1635). Existence runs through the rescaled Chafaï approximating measures, whose tightness gives a weak cluster point; uniqueness rests on a sharp Laplace-determinacy lemma, that a finite measure on `ℝ≥0` is already pinned down by its Laplace transform at the natural numbers. The converse direction, that such a transform is completely monotone, is not yet stated, so this is not an iff.

On the semigroup side the generator now determines the semigroup: two C₀ semigroups on a real Banach space with the same infinitesimal generator are equal ([`eq_of_generator_eq`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generator/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.eq_of_generator_eq), TauCeti#1757), with the identity semigroup and the operator exponential `exp (t • A)` as the corollaries. Supporting work filled in the generator itself: dense domain, closedness, invariance of `D(A)` under the semigroup, and the abstract Cauchy problem, where orbits of domain vectors are [classical solutions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/CauchyProblem.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.isClassicalSolution_realOperator) and all orbits are mild ones. Dissipativity arrived with the [converse of Lumer-Phillips](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Dissipative/Basic.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.ContractionSemigroup.isMDissipative_generator) — the generator of a contraction semigroup is m-dissipative — and the Hilbert characterization `⟪Ax, x⟫ ≤ 0`. The resolvent grew the API the generation theorems will need: the resolvent identity, smoothness in `λ`, the iterated derivative formula `dⁿR/dλⁿ = (-1)ⁿ n! R(λ)ⁿ⁺¹`, and `‖R(λ)ⁿ‖ ≤ λ⁻ⁿ` in the contraction case.

Positive-definite functions gained the [Kolmogorov decomposition](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/Kernel/Kolmogorov.html#TauCeti.IsPositiveDefiniteKernel.kolmogorovIsometry) of a positive-definite kernel, together with closure, continuity, normalization and pullback lemmas, a conversion between Mathlib's `e^{-2πi⟨·,·⟩}` convention and characteristic functions, and the Gaussian example.

## OneParameterSemigroups: 2026-08-03 to 2026-09-03 (`11ef09d` to `34ee8bb`)

The [Hille–Yosida characterization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/HilleYosida/Generation.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.hilleYosida_generation_iff) and the [Lumer–Phillips characterization](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/LumerPhillips.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.exists_contractionSemigroup_generator_eq_iff) landed, giving the two generation criteria at their intended generality on real Banach spaces. Their common Yosida construction now includes compact-time convergence of the approximating exponentials, identification of the limiting generator, and the sharp general `(M, ω)` resolvent-power estimates. The semigroup theory also gained the [bounded perturbation theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Semigroups/Generation/BoundedPerturbation.html#TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousSemigroup.exists_generator_eq_vadd), dense iterated generator domains, and the multiplication-semigroup example.

[Bochner’s theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Bochner/BochnerTheorem.html#TauCeti.bochner) is now proved on an arbitrary finite-dimensional real inner-product space, with a unique finite Borel representing measure. That result feeds the [Berg–Christensen–Ressel representation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/PositiveDefinite/SemigroupGroup/FourierLaplace/Existence.html#TauCeti.bcr_semigroup_bochner) for bounded continuous positive-definite functions on `ℝ≥0 × V`; the proof develops spatial Bochner slices, their decreasing Radon–Nikodym densities, and the fibrewise Bernstein kernels needed to assemble the Laplace–Fourier measure.

The completely monotone lane now has the full finite-measure [Hausdorff–Bernstein–Widder equivalence](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/HausdorffBernsteinWidder.html#TauCeti.hausdorff_bernstein_widder), together with its open-half-line, possibly infinite-measure form, composition with Bernstein functions, and the [Lévy–Khintchine representation with a unique triplet](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/CompletelyMonotone/Bernstein/LevyKhintchine/Uniqueness.html#TauCeti.IsBernsteinFunction.existsUnique_eqOn_bernsteinLevyKhintchineExponent). C₀ groups and unitary groups have also begun as a stretch layer, including skew-adjointness of a unitary group’s generator and the bounded self-adjoint exponential example; the full unbounded Stone theorem is not established here.
