## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** The graph-regularity tower (Layers 0–4) and the arity-3 vocabulary through top regularity (Layers 5–7) are done, and so is the arity-3 induced counting estimate over a regular complex. The strong arity-3 regular approximation of Layer 8 is proved as two separate halves, one for pair colors and one for triples, but the halves have not been chained and the theorem has not been stated. The composed counting endpoint waits on it.

### Named results

- **The arity-3 induced counting lemma** — suppose a coloring of the triples is close in edit distance to one that is regular over a triadic complex. Then its labelled induced copies of any fixed colored 3-pattern on `k` vertices are within `ε|V|^k` of the prediction from cell sizes, pair-color densities and relative top densities ([`abs_inducedCopyCount_sub_expectedInducedCount_le_of_topRegular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/InducedCounting.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.abs_inducedCopyCount_sub_expectedInducedCount_le_of_topRegular)). Rank-one top regularity suffices.
- **Scheduled strong top-regular approximation** — every lower skeleton has a refinement of its pair palette, with the same cells and a palette bound that does not depend on the host, together with an `ε`-close recoloring of the triples. That recoloring is top-regular at scheduled tolerance and rank, both evaluated at the returned complexity, over all but a scheduled proportion of polyads ([`exists_approximates3_topRegularOverMostPolyads`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/Recoloring.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.exists_approximates3_topRegularOverMostPolyads)). This is the triple half of Layer 8.
- **Strong regular approximation of pair-color systems** — on a large enough host, every equipartition has an equitable refinement of bounded size and a recoloring of at most `α|V|²` ordered pairs that makes the lower skeleton regular at the schedule's value at its own lower complexity, a cell paired with itself included ([`exists_refining_equipartition_lowerSkeletonRegular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/PairColor/Approximation.html#TauCeti.PairColorSystem.exists_refining_equipartition_lowerSkeletonRegular)). This is the pair half of Layer 8.
- **Strong graph regularity with induced counting** — from any equipartition there are nested equipartitions with a uniform coarse level, a fine level uniform at a scheduled scale, and a small energy gap ([`exists_strong_regular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/StrongRegularity.html#SimpleGraph.exists_strong_regular)). Their coarse densities predict the induced copies of every three-vertex graph to within `(15τ + 3√ε)|V|³ + 6m|V|²` ([`inducedGraphCount3_estimate`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/StrongRegularity.html#SimpleGraph.StrongRegular.inducedGraphCount3_estimate)).
- **Finite Frieze–Kannan weak regularity** — every finite graph has a partition into at most `4^⌈1/ε²⌉` parts whose stepped prediction matches the edge count on every rectangle to within `ε|V|²` ([`frieze_kannan`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/FriezeKannan.html#SimpleGraph.frieze_kannan)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The induced-count prediction** — [`expectedInducedCount`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/Placement.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.expectedInducedCount) sums, over placements of the pattern into cells and routes of pair colors, the falling-factorial injection count times the pair and relative top densities. The counting lemma compares against it.
- **Polyad energy** — [`polyadEnergy`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Polyad/Energy/Basic.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.polyadEnergy) is monotone under refinement of the lower skeleton. A small gap in it is what the coupled recoloring turns into coarse top regularity.
- **Triadic complexes** — [`TriadicComplex3`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/Basic.html#TauCeti.TriadicComplex3) bundles vertex cells, a pair palette and selected polyads, with its computed complexity, polyad decompositions, controlled vertex cells and exceptional polyad mass. Both halves of Layer 8 and the counting lemma are stated over it.

## The frontier

- **The strong regular approximation predicate** — bundle the edit bound, the polyad decomposition, lower-skeleton regularity, top regularity over most polyads and the complexity bound as `IsStrongRegularApproximation3`, then prove its monotonicity and edit transfer. Each condition already exists on its own, so nothing blocks this.
- **The arity-3 regularity theorem** — `exists_strong_regular_approximation3`. The remaining step is to chain the pair-color approximation with the top-regular approximation. The second refines the pair palette, and its output is not asserted to keep the lower skeleton regular at the schedule's value at the new complexity. The README's route repairs this by recoloring the irregular cell pairs and charging their polyads to the exceptional mass. A host bound and equitable control at `t₀` cells must also be carried through.
- **The composed counting endpoint** — `exists_strong_regular_approximation3_counting`. `Suggested.lean` derives it from the statement of §8.2 and the counting estimate, so it waits only on the theorem above.
