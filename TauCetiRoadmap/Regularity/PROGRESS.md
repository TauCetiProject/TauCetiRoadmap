## Regularity: 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-20 (`5bc4ac5` to `68a457e`)

The headline is a seeded Szemerédi regularity lemma: [`exists_regular_equipartition_almost_refining`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/SeededRegularity.html#SimpleGraph.exists_regular_equipartition_almost_refining) takes any equipartition of a finite graph and returns an ε-uniform equipartition that almost refines it, cell by cell, with an explicit bound on the number of parts. Mathlib's lemma takes no seed, and its output cannot be post-refined, so this needed its own run of the energy induction (TauCeti#17390). A companion, [`exists_regular_exact_refining_equipartition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/SeededRegularity.html#SimpleGraph.exists_regular_exact_refining_equipartition), refines the seed exactly, which is the first ingredient of strong regularity. Both handle small hosts with the singleton partition, so neither needs a large-host hypothesis. Underneath them sits the size-weighted energy: it is [monotone under refinement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Partition/Energy.html#SimpleGraph.weightedEnergy_mono_of_refines), and a small energy gap [bounds the weighted mean deviation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Partition/Energy.html#SimpleGraph.weightedDensityDeviation_abs_le) of fine densities from coarse ones by √ε.

The arity-3 side gained its vocabulary up through top regularity. There are now uniform hypergraphs, total triple colorings with labelled induced copy counts, and graded hypergraph complexes with round trips to Mathlib's simplicial complexes. On the pair level, coherent [pair-color systems](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/PairColor/Basic.html#TauCeti.PairColorSystem) feed a lower skeleton whose pair regularity reduces to Mathlib's uniformity for graphs. Above that come [polyads](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Polyad/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polyad3), subpolyads, and relative triple densities. The rank-r condition [`IsTopRegularOverPolyad`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Polyad/TopRegular.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.IsTopRegularOverPolyad) is defined and shown to imply the weaker vertex-box condition. These are definitions with basic laws and examples. No hypergraph regularity theorem has been proved yet.

Strong graph regularity has its counting bookkeeping, a [repeated-cell bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/InducedCount.html#SimpleGraph.inducedGraphCount3_le_sum_card_induced_add) for induced three-vertex counts, but not yet the strong-regularity structure or its counting estimate. Finite Frieze–Kannan regularity has not started.
