## Regularity: 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-20 (`5bc4ac5` to `68a457e`)

The headline is a seeded Szemerédi regularity lemma: [`exists_regular_equipartition_almost_refining`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/SeededRegularity.html#SimpleGraph.exists_regular_equipartition_almost_refining) takes any equipartition of a finite graph and returns an ε-uniform equipartition that almost refines it, cell by cell, with an explicit bound on the number of parts. Mathlib's lemma takes no seed, and its output cannot be post-refined, so this needed its own run of the energy induction (TauCeti#17390). A companion, [`exists_regular_exact_refining_equipartition`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/SeededRegularity.html#SimpleGraph.exists_regular_exact_refining_equipartition), refines the seed exactly, which is the first ingredient of strong regularity. Both handle small hosts with the singleton partition, so neither needs a large-host hypothesis. Underneath them sits the size-weighted energy: it is [monotone under refinement](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Partition/Energy.html#SimpleGraph.weightedEnergy_mono_of_refines), and a small energy gap [bounds the weighted mean deviation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Partition/Energy.html#SimpleGraph.weightedDensityDeviation_abs_le) of fine densities from coarse ones by √ε.

The arity-3 side gained its vocabulary up through top regularity. There are now uniform hypergraphs, total triple colorings with labelled induced copy counts, and graded hypergraph complexes with round trips to Mathlib's simplicial complexes. On the pair level, coherent [pair-color systems](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/PairColor/Basic.html#TauCeti.PairColorSystem) feed a lower skeleton whose pair regularity reduces to Mathlib's uniformity for graphs. Above that come [polyads](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Polyad/Basic.html#TauCeti.Polyad3), subpolyads, and relative triple densities. The rank-r condition [`IsTopRegularOverPolyad`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Polyad/TopRegular.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.IsTopRegularOverPolyad) is defined and shown to imply the weaker vertex-box condition. These are definitions with basic laws and examples. No hypergraph regularity theorem has been proved yet.

Strong graph regularity has its counting bookkeeping, a [repeated-cell bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/InducedCount.html#SimpleGraph.inducedGraphCount3_le_sum_card_induced_add) for induced three-vertex counts, but not yet the strong-regularity structure or its counting estimate. Finite Frieze–Kannan regularity has not started.

## Regularity: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `e8ea0d1`)

Two graph-regularity theorems landed. The first is finite Frieze–Kannan weak regularity, [`frieze_kannan`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/FriezeKannan.html#SimpleGraph.frieze_kannan): every finite graph has a vertex partition into at most `4^⌈1/ε²⌉` parts whose stepped prediction gets the edge count between every pair of vertex sets right to within `ε|V|²`. That bound is slightly better than the roadmap asks for. It comes with a [refining form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/FriezeKannan.html#SimpleGraph.frieze_kannan_refining) and the count-scaled [cut discrepancy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/CutDiscrepancy.html#SimpleGraph.cutDiscrepancy) it is stated with. The second is strong graph regularity, [`exists_strong_regular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/StrongRegularity.html#SimpleGraph.exists_strong_regular). From any equipartition it produces nested equipartitions: a coarse one that is ε-uniform and almost refines the seed, and a fine one that is uniform at the scale the schedule `F` assigns to the coarse size, with energy gap at most ε and complexity bounded independently of the graph.

The induced three-vertex counting estimate that should follow has not been assembled, but all its pieces are now in place. There is an [induced counting lemma for three uniform cells](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/Counting.html#SimpleGraph.abs_card_induced_fin_three_sub_le), built on rectangle and test-function discrepancy bounds for uniform pairs. There is an [energy-transfer bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/SteppedInducedCount.html#SimpleGraph.abs_steppedInducedGraphCount3_sub_le) of `3√ε|V|³` between stepped predictions. And there is the repeated-cell bookkeeping. Separately, the first dense-graph-limits comparison is proved: [the size-weighted energy of a partition equals the partition energy of the graph's graphon](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graphon/Energy.html#SimpleGraph.graphonPartitionEnergy_finiteGraphGraphon).

On the arity-3 side, top regularity over a polyad gained its [slicing lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Polyad/TopRegular.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.IsTopRegularOverPolyad.of_pairSupport_subset) and density inheritance. [Triadic complexes](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/Basic.html#TauCeti.TriadicComplex3) now exist, with their computed complexity, polyad decompositions, exceptional polyad mass and "top-regular over most polyads". These are the definitions the strong arity-3 approximation will be stated with. That theorem itself has not started.

## Regularity: 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-11 (`e8ea0d1` to `3895f92`)

The arity-3 induced counting lemma is proved. [`abs_inducedCopyCount_sub_expectedInducedCount_le_of_topRegular`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/InducedCounting.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.abs_inducedCopyCount_sub_expectedInducedCount_le_of_topRegular) says that if a coloring `H` of the triples is close in edit distance to one that is regular over a triadic complex (regular lower skeleton, top-regular over all but a small mass of polyads, enough equitable vertex cells), then the labelled induced copies in `H` of any fixed colored 3-pattern on `k` vertices are within `ε|V|^k` of the [prediction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/Placement.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.expectedInducedCount) built from cell sizes, pair-color densities and relative top densities. Top regularity at rank one turns out to suffice. Underneath sit pattern placements with their [falling-factorial injection count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/Placement.html#TauCeti.placementInjectionCount_eq_prod_descFactorial), local counting along regular routes, and separate bounds for exceptional routes, repeated cells and the edit. What is missing is the input: no theorem yet produces such a regular approximation, and no fixed pattern has been worked through. On the graph side, the [strong-regularity counting estimate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Regularity/Graph/StrongRegularity.html#SimpleGraph.StrongRegular.inducedGraphCount3_estimate) for induced three-vertex patterns closes Layer 4, with constant 15 where the roadmap asked for 16.

Much of the rest went into that missing approximation theorem. [Top regularization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/TopRegularization.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.exists_topRegularOverMostPolyads) refines the pair palette until a coloring is top-regular over most polyads, driven by a new polyad energy increment, and its [strong form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Triadic/StrongTopRegularization.html#TauCeti.Colored3Graph.exists_strong_topRegularOverMostPolyads) iterates this at scheduled tolerances and ranks until the energy gap is small. The lower skeleton gained a [regularity lemma for pair-color systems](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/PairColor/Regularization.html#TauCeti.PairColorSystem.exists_refinement_sum_irregularPairs_le), regular recoloring of irregular pairs, quasirandom splitting and divisible padding, and top regularity was shown to survive moving a few vertices or recoloring a few pairs. The approximation theorem itself has not been stated.

Smaller gaps closed too: [homomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/HomDensity.html#TauCeti.UniformHypergraph.homDensity) and [injective homomorphism densities](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/HomDensity.html#TauCeti.UniformHypergraph.injHomDensity) of uniform hypergraphs, the [size-weighted hypergraph energy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Energy.html#TauCeti.UniformHypergraph.weightedEnergy), [labelled copies](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/Complex/Counting.html#TauCeti.HypergraphComplex.labelledCopyCount) of a hypergraph complex, and the [comparison of vertex-box with pair regularity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Hypergraph/PairColor/VertexBox.html#TauCeti.PairColorSystem.IsVertexBoxRegularPair.isRegularPair_of_disjoint) at arity 2.
