## CountableStructureSpacesPartII: 2026-11-22 to 2026-11-25 (`b2edd86` to `522d48f`)

The first theorem of Friedman and Stanley has landed for graphs: [countable graphs are Borel complete](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/GraphClass.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.borelComplete_graphClass), so isomorphism on any invariant Borel class of countable structures, in any countable relational language, Borel reduces to isomorphism of simple graphs on ℕ. The reduction goes through the roadmap's two gadgets: the [incidence graph](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/IncidenceGraph.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.isoSetoid_iff_exists_iso_incidenceGraph) of a structure, which records isomorphism as colour-preserving graph isomorphism, and [clique tags](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Clique/Tag.html#SimpleGraph.nonempty_iso_cliqueTag_iff), which erase the colours. Underneath sit the foundations: [isomorphism restricted to a class](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/ClassIso.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.classIso), [set reducibility](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/BorelReducibility.html#Set.BorelReducible) with E₀ showing it is [strictly weaker](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/SetTheory/Descriptive/EZero.html#TauCeti.not_forall_borelReducible_of_borelReducible_setOf) than Borel reducibility, relabelling along bijections of carriers, and the definition of [Borel completeness](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/BorelComplete.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.BorelComplete) with its transfer, universe and renaming laws.

For languages with function symbols there is now a Polish [functional coding space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/Functional/Coding.html#TauCeti.FunctionalConfiguration), and isomorphism on any class of it is [Borel bireducible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/Functional/ClassIso.html#TauCeti.FunctionalConfiguration.borelBireducible_classIso_image_graphCode) with isomorphism on its graph codes; the final step, that Borel complete classes absorb these classes too, is not yet stated.

Linear orders and trees are half-built. The coloured Cantor theorem for ℚ, coloured by the 2-adic valuation of the denominator ([Rat.exists_orderIso_multiplicity_den_eq](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/DenominatorColouring.html#Rat.exists_orderIso_multiplicity_den_eq)), and the [unravelling lemma](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/ModelTheory/RelationalConfiguration/Unravelling.html#TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration.isoSetoid_iff_exists_orderIso_unravelColor) are in, as are the coded linear orders and trees and the two presentations of trees. The Borel completeness of linear orders and trees, the universal embedding, the characterisations and all of Layer 6 have not started; the permutation group's Borel σ-algebra is in place for the embedding.
