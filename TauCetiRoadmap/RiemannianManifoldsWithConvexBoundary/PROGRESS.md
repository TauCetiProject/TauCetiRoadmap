## RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary: 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-25 (`9604cd6` to `522d48f`)

The roadmap's first layer is now nearly complete: the boundary of a Riemannian manifold with boundary has a [second fundamental form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.boundarySecondFundamentalForm) `II`, defined as minus the Hessian of a chart's normal coordinate over the length of its differential, and proved equal to each of its usual descriptions: through [any boundary defining function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_apply), through the covariant derivative of [an extension of the outward normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_apply_eq_inner_of_eq_outwardUnitNormal), and through [tangent vector fields](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_apply_eq_neg_inner_outwardUnitNormal). It is symmetric, a [smooth section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Smooth.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_boundarySecondFundamentalForm_apply) and [natural under isometries](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Isometry.html#TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_map). On top of it come the [Weingarten map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/Weingarten.html#TauCeti.boundaryWeingarten), [principal curvatures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/Weingarten.html#TauCeti.boundaryPrincipalCurvatures), the [Weingarten equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/Weingarten.html#TauCeti.leviCivitaConnection_eq_mfderiv_boundaryWeingarten) and [`tr_h II = n H`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/Weingarten.html#TauCeti.sum_boundarySecondFundamentalForm_eq_mul_boundaryMeanCurvature), and the [conformal change law](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/Conformal.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_rescale) `II^ĝ = e^φ (II − dφ(ν_in) h)` for `ĝ = e^{2φ} g`. Still missing from that layer: the conformal laws for principal and mean curvatures, and smoothness and isometry-invariance of the Weingarten map and mean curvature.

Underneath sits the hypersurface groundwork. The [boundary tangent space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/TangentSpace.html#TauCeti.boundaryTangentSpace) is a hyperplane splitting tangent vectors into [outward, tangent and inward](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/TangentSpace.html#TauCeti.isOutwardPointing_or_mem_boundaryTangentSpace_or_isOutwardPointing_neg) ones. The [inward unit normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/InwardUnitNormal.html#TauCeti.inwardUnitNormal) gets its sign test and chart formula, with no Hausdorff hypothesis. [Boundary defining functions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/DefiningFunction/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn) exist [globally](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/DefiningFunction/Basic.html#TauCeti.exists_isBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn_univ) and any two differ by a [positive smooth factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/InwardUnitNormal.html#TauCeti.IsBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn.exists_eq_mul_norm_div_norm), proved by Hadamard division on a half-space. The closed unit ball is set up as a test case, with its [Euclidean metric](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/ClosedBall/Basic.html#TauCeti.closedBallEuclideanMetric) and radial [outward normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/ClosedBall/OutwardUnitNormal.html#TauCeti.outwardUnitNormal_closedBall). Convexity itself (Layer 2) and everything after it has not started.
