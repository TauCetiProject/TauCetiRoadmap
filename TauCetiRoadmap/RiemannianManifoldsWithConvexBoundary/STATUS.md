### Named results

- **The second fundamental form and its descriptions** — [`boundarySecondFundamentalForm`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.boundarySecondFundamentalForm) is defined from a chart's normal coordinate. It equals `−Hess ρ/|dρ|` for [every boundary defining function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.IsBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_apply) `ρ` and `⟨∇_X N, Y⟩` for [every extension `N` of the outward normal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Basic.html#TauCeti.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_apply_eq_inner_of_eq_outwardUnitNormal). The first of these is what the Hessian characterisation of convexity will rest on.
- **Smoothness and naturality of `II`** — the second fundamental form is a [smooth section](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Smooth.html#TauCeti.contMDiff_boundarySecondFundamentalForm_apply) of the bundle of bilinear forms on `T∂M`, and isometries of manifolds with boundary [pull it back to itself](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/SecondFundamentalForm/Isometry.html#TauCeti.RiemannianIsometry.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_map).
- **The Weingarten equation** — the tangential part of `∇_u N`, for any extension `N` of the outward normal, is the [Weingarten map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/Weingarten.html#TauCeti.leviCivitaConnection_eq_mfderiv_boundaryWeingarten) applied to `u`. The map is self-adjoint, its eigenvalues are the principal curvatures, and `tr_h II = n H` ([`sum_boundarySecondFundamentalForm_eq_mul_boundaryMeanCurvature`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/Weingarten.html#TauCeti.sum_boundarySecondFundamentalForm_eq_mul_boundaryMeanCurvature)).
- **The conformal change law** — for `ĝ = f g`, the [rescaled second fundamental form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary/Conformal.html#Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.boundarySecondFundamentalForm_rescale) is `√f (II − (2f)⁻¹ df(ν_in) h)`, which is `e^φ (II − dφ(ν_in) h)` for `f = e^{2φ}`.
- **Comparison of boundary defining functions** — two defining functions on the same open set [differ by a smooth positive factor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/InwardUnitNormal.html#TauCeti.IsBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn.exists_eq_mul_norm_div_norm), equal to `|dρ'|/|dρ|` on the boundary. The proof uses [Hadamard division on a half-space](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Calculus/Hadamard/HalfSpace.html#TauCeti.exists_contDiffOn_eq_smul_of_eq_zero).
