## AdicSpaces: 2026-08-08 to 2026-08-12 (`76db282` to `c9165b9`)

The valuation spectrum is now [spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PatchPresentation.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.instSpectralSpace): the proof constructs its compact patch presentation and applies the spectrality criterion. The ideal-relative space [`Spv(A,I)` is spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/SpvOfIdeal/Spectral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spectralSpace_spvOfIdeal) as well, with the [restriction map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/RestrictToIdeal.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.restrictToIdealCodRestrict) constructed as a continuous retraction. The supporting topology includes the general theorem that [pro-constructible subspaces of spectral spaces are spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/ProConstructible.html#TauCeti.IsProConstructible.spectralSpace).

The topological-algebra foundation now contains bounded and power-bounded elements, Huber and Tate rings, their completion structures, and [weighted restricted power series](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/WeightedRestrictedSeries.html#TauCeti.Huber.weightedRestrictedSubring), including dense polynomials and continuous evaluation. [Henkel's open mapping theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/OpenMapping/Henkel.html#TauCeti.HasZeroSequenceOfUnits.isOpenMap) supplies the later strictness machinery. For continuous valuations, only the inclusion [`Cont A ⊆ Spv(A,IA)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Cont/OfIdeal.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.cont_subset_spvOfIdeal_extendedIdealOfDefinition) has landed; the reverse characterization, closedness and spectrality remain, and no `Spa`, rational-subset, presheaf or adic-space construction is present yet.
