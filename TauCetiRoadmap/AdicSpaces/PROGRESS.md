The supporting topology includes the general theorem that [pro-constructible subspaces of spectral spaces are spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/ProConstructible.html#TauCeti.IsProConstructible.spectralSpace).

## AdicSpaces: 2026-08-12 to 2026-08-21 (`c9165b9` to `01f38d7`)

[Wedhorn’s Theorem 7.10](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Cont/OfIdeal.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.cont_eq_spvOfIdeal_inter_setOfPred_forall_vlt_one) now characterises the continuous valuations as the points of `Spv(A,IA)` that are strictly sub-unit on an ideal of definition. The resulting locus is [closed and spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Cont/Spectral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spectralSpace_cont_of_pairOfDefinition), and the [adic spectrum is spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Spectral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spectralSpace_spa_of_pairOfDefinition) as well. Its rational subsets now [form a basis](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/RationalSubset/Basis.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.isTopologicalBasis_spaRationalFamily), are [quasi-compact](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/RationalSubset/Basis.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.isCompact_of_mem_spaRationalFamily) and [closed under finite intersections](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/RationalSubset/Basic.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.rationalSubset_inter), and [every open cover of one has a finite rational refinement](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/RationalSubset/Basis.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.exists_finite_spaRationalFamily_refinement).

On the coordinate side, the completed rational localisation `A⟨T/s⟩` has its [universal property for complete Hausdorff targets](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.existsUnique_continuous_ringHom_completion_locTopology), its [integrally closed plus ring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/Plus.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.completedPlusSubring), and [comparison](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/Presentation.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.presentationRingEquiv) and [restriction maps](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/CompleteSeparated/RefinementCategory.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.Presentation.restrictionHom) between presentations. [Complete separated topological commutative rings have all small limits](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Category/TopCommRingCat/CompleteSeparated/Limits.html#TauCeti.CompleteSeparatedTopCommRingCat.instHasLimits), supplying the target category for the eventual structure presheaf. The [canonical map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spaLocToRationalSubset) `Spa(A_U,A_U⁺) → R(T/s)` is so far proved only to be continuous and to land in the rational subset, not to be a homeomorphism; the structure presheaf has therefore not yet landed.

The underlying algebra also gained [Wedhorn’s criterion for rings of definition](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/RingOfDefinition.html#TauCeti.Huber.exists_pairOfDefinition_ringOfDefinition_eq_iff) and an [Artin–Rees controlled lifting theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Ideal/ArtinRees.html#TauCeti.ArtinRees.exists_controlled_lift), alongside restricted-series base-change machinery and the definition of strong noetherianness.
