## AdicSpaces: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-09 (`f88533f` to `2f71ccd`)

The rational localisation now has its geometry: [the adic spectrum of the topological localisation `A(T/s)`, with plus ring the integral closure of `A⁺[T/s]`, is homeomorphic to the rational subset `R(T/s)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/Homeomorph.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spaLocalizationHomeomorph) (TauCeti#5629), supplying the injectivity and openness the earlier surjectivity statement lacked. The identification is for the uncompleted topological localisation, not the completed coordinate ring `A⟨T/s⟩` the roadmap asks for. Through it, [every rational subset of the localisation comes from a rational open upstairs](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/RationalSubset.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.exists_spaLocalizationHomeomorph_preimage_rationalSubset_eq), Wedhorn's Proposition 8.2(2). Two other gaps in Layer 2 closed: [the adic spectrum is empty exactly when `1` lies in the closure of `0`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Emptiness.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spa_eq_empty_iff_one_mem_closure_zero), with no extra hypothesis, and [rational subsets are unchanged by small perturbations of their defining data](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/RationalSubset/Perturbation.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.exists_mem_nhds_forall_rationalSubset_eq_of_sub_mem), over a Huber ring where Wedhorn assumes a complete Hausdorff affinoid ring.

Wedhorn's Remark 7.55 is now an isomorphism of topological rings: adjoining one numerator presents [`A⟨T'/s⟩` as the Laurent quotient `A⟨T/s⟩⟨X⟩ / (t/s − X)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/Laurent/Identification.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.laurentQuotientRingEquiv) (TauCeti#5755). That presentation drives what follows: [restriction maps of numerator enlargements are flat](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/Laurent/Flat.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.flat_restrictionRingHomOfSubset_of_isStronglyNoetherian_base), which is Proposition 8.30, and the same induction shows [strong noetherianness survives a numerator enlargement](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/Laurent/StronglyNoetherian.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isStronglyNoetherian_completion_of_subset). Both assume strong noetherianness of the base presentation rather than deriving it.

Supporting work added residue-field valuations at points of the valuation spectrum, the separatedness and strictness halves of Wedhorn Proposition 6.18 for finite modules over complete noetherian Tate rings, the iteration isomorphism `A⟨X₁,…,X_{k+m}⟩ ≅ A⟨X₁,…,Xₖ⟩⟨Y₁,…,Y_m⟩` as topological rings, and an evaluation map `A⟨X₁,…,Xₖ⟩ → A⟨T/s⟩` with [dense range](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/Evaluation.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.denseRange_rationalEvalHom) when the numerators and `s` generate the unit ideal. Density only; closed range, and with it a strict presentation of `A⟨T/s⟩`, is not proved here.
