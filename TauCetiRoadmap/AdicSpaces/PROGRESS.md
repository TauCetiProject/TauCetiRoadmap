## AdicSpaces: 2026-08-08 to 2026-08-12 (`76db282` to `c9165b9`)

The valuation spectrum is now [spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PatchPresentation.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.instSpectralSpace): the proof constructs its compact patch presentation and applies the spectrality criterion. The ideal-relative space [`Spv(A,I)` is spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/SpvOfIdeal/Spectral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spectralSpace_spvOfIdeal) as well, with the [restriction map](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/RestrictToIdeal.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.restrictToIdealCodRestrict) constructed as a continuous retraction. The supporting topology includes the general theorem that [pro-constructible subspaces of spectral spaces are spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Spectral/ProConstructible.html#TauCeti.IsProConstructible.spectralSpace).

The topological-algebra foundation now contains bounded and power-bounded elements, Huber and Tate rings, their completion structures, and [weighted restricted power series](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/WeightedRestrictedSeries.html#TauCeti.Huber.weightedRestrictedSubring), including dense polynomials and continuous evaluation. [Henkel's open mapping theorem](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/OpenMapping/Henkel.html#TauCeti.HasZeroSequenceOfUnits.isOpenMap) supplies the later strictness machinery. For continuous valuations, only the inclusion [`Cont A ⊆ Spv(A,IA)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Cont/OfIdeal.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.cont_subset_spvOfIdeal_extendedIdealOfDefinition) has landed; the reverse characterization, closedness and spectrality remain, and no `Spa`, rational-subset, presheaf or adic-space construction is present yet.

## AdicSpaces: 2026-08-12 to 2026-09-01 (`c9165b9` to `f88533f`)

[Wedhorn’s characterization of continuous valuations](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Cont/OfIdeal.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.cont_eq_spvOfIdeal_inter_setOfPred_forall_vlt_one) is now complete: `Cont A` is exactly the part of `Spv(A,IA)` strictly sub-unit on an ideal of definition. Consequently [the continuous locus is spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Cont/Spectral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.instSpectralSpaceElemContOfIsHuberRing), and [the adic spectrum `Spa(A,A⁺)` is spectral](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Spectral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.instSpectralSpaceElemSpaOfIsHuberRing). Rational subsets now form a [quasi-compact basis](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/RationalSubset/Basis.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.isTopologicalBasis_spaRationalFamily), and the plus ring can be [recovered from its sub-unit locus on `Spa`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Integral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.coe_eq_setOf_forall_vle_one).

The algebra beneath rational domains also advanced substantially. The completed localization `A⟨T/s⟩` has its [universal property for complete Hausdorff targets](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/LocalizationTopology/Completion.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.existsUnique_continuous_ringHom_completion_locTopology), plus ring and presentation-change maps; its induced map on adic spectra has [image exactly `R(T/s)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/Surjective.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.image_comap_algebraMap_spa_eq_rationalSubset), though the promised homeomorphism is not yet established. Complete separated topological rings now have [all small limits](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Category/TopCommRingCat/CompleteSeparated/Limits.html#TauCeti.CompleteSeparatedTopCommRingCat.instHasLimits), supporting a [presentation-indexed candidate structure presheaf](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Basic.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.presentationLimitPresheaf). On the acyclicity route, restricted power-series algebras are now [faithfully flat over complete noetherian Tate rings](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/Flat.html#TauCeti.Huber.faithfullyFlat_restrictedMvPowerSeriesSubring), but rational-localization flatness, Čech exactness and sheafiness remain.
