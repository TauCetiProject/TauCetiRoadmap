### Named results

- **Spectrality of the adic spectrum** — for every Huber ring, `Spa(A,A⁺)` is [a spectral space](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Spectral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.instSpectralSpaceElemSpaOfIsHuberRing) (Wedhorn's Theorem 7.35).

- **Wedhorn's Proposition 8.2(2)** — pullback along `A → A⟨T/s⟩` is [a homeomorphism from `Spa(A⟨T/s⟩, A_U⁺)` onto `R(T/s)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/CompletedHomeomorph.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spaCompletedLocalizationHomeomorph) under which [rational subsets correspond](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/CompletedRationalSubset.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.bijOn_preimage_spaCompletedLocalizationHomeomorph_spaRationalFamily).

- **Faithful flatness along a rational cover** — for a finite rational cover of `Spa(A,A⁺)` over a complete Hausdorff strongly noetherian Tate ring, the map from `A` into the product of the coordinate rings is [faithfully flat](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/FaithfullyFlat.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.faithfullyFlat_pi_toCompletionLoc), hence injective: Wedhorn's Corollary 8.32.

- **Lemma 8.33 on rational subsets** — over a strongly noetherian Tate ring, the two-piece Laurent cover of any rational subset has sections that [glue](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/LaurentCover/Restrict.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.exists_presentationLimitMap_eq_of_inf_laurentCoverOpen), and every section on the overlap is [a difference of restrictions](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/LaurentCover/Restrict.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.surjective_presentationLimitMap_sub_inf_laurentCoverOpen): exactly the form Lemma 8.34's induction consumes.

- **The one-variable Tate algebra is a principal ideal ring** — over a complete nonarchimedean field, [every ideal of the restricted power series is principal](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Ideal.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.isPrincipalIdealRing_isRestricted_subring), by Weierstrass division and preparation, so `K⟨T⟩` is [noetherian](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Restricted/Noetherian.html#TauCeti.Huber.isNoetherianRing_restrictedMvPowerSeriesCompletion_one).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The structure presheaf** — built as a limit over admissible presentations and [identified with Wedhorn's limit over rational subsets](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/SubsetLimit.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.presentationLimitIsoRationalSubsetLimit), it is `A⟨T/s⟩` on a rational open, `A` on the whole spectrum, has [local stalks](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Stalk/LocalRing.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.isLocalRing_stalk_presentationLimitPresheafInCommRingCat), and is [a sheaf once it is one on rational opens](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/KanExtension.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.isSheaf_presentationLimitPresheaf_of_isSheaf_rational).

- **The category of pre-adic spaces** — Wedhorn's [`𝒱^pre`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PreAdicSpace/Hom.html#TauCeti.PreAdicSpace.instCategory), with [affinoid objects](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PreAdicSpace/Affinoid.html#TauCeti.AffinoidPreAdicSpace) and the [sheafy subcategory `𝒱`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/PreAdicSpace/Sheaf.html#TauCeti.SheafyPreAdicSpace). It is where affinoid adic spaces will live, so Layer 5 can begin as soon as sheafiness is available.

- **Sheafy and stably sheafy rings** — Wedhorn's Definition 8.26 as [`IsSheafyRing`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/SheafyRing.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsSheafyRing), [invariant under completion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/SheafyRing.html#TauCeti.Huber.isSheafyRing_completion_iff) and [implied by stable sheafiness](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/SheafyRing.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsStablySheafyRing.isSheafyRing). These are the targets Layer 4's two theorems must hit.

## The frontier

- **Tate acyclicity for finite rational covers** — Lemma 8.33 now holds on every rational subset and [units on a rational subset are characterised by support](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/Units.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.isUnit_toCompletionLoc_iff_forall_notMem_supp), so what remains is the induction of Lemma 8.34 from Laurent to standard to arbitrary finite covers, then sheafiness through the rational-open criterion and all-degree Čech exactness.

- **Pre-adic and adic spaces** — define pre-adic spaces as objects of `𝒱^pre` locally isomorphic to affinoid ones; with sheafiness this opens Layer 5's adic spaces, open subspaces and gluing.

- **Noetherianity of `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩`** — the one-variable case is done; the classical induction needs Weierstrass division over `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ₋₁⟩`, not only over the field. Until then Layer 4's strongly noetherian Tate rings have no nonzero example here.

- **Stably uniform affinoids are sheafy** — [stable uniformity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/StableUniform.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsStablyUniform) is defined and [preserved by rational localisation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/StableUniform.html#TauCeti.Huber.PairOfDefinition.isStablyUniform_completion_locTopology); the Buzzard-Verberkmoes theorem itself remains, and it does not wait on acyclicity.

- **Example 7.57** — classify the points of the closed unit disc and describe its rational subdomains; the Gauss points about the origin are the only non-classical points so far.
