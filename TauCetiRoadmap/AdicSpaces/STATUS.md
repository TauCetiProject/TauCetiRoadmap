### Named results

- **Spectrality of the adic spectrum** — for every Huber ring, `Spa(A,A⁺)` is [a spectral space](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Spectral.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.instSpectralSpaceElemSpaOfIsHuberRing) (Wedhorn's Theorem 7.35).

- **Wedhorn's Proposition 8.2(2)** — pullback along `A → A⟨T/s⟩` is [a homeomorphism from `Spa(A⟨T/s⟩, A_U⁺)` onto `R(T/s)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/CompletedHomeomorph.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spaCompletedLocalizationHomeomorph) under which [rational subsets correspond](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/CompletedRationalSubset.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.bijOn_preimage_spaCompletedLocalizationHomeomorph_spaRationalFamily).

- **Local stalks** — every stalk of the structure presheaf is [a local ring](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Stalk/LocalRing.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.isLocalRing_stalk_presentationLimitPresheafInCommRingCat) whose maximal ideal is [the support of the point valuation](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Stalk/LocalRing.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.supp_presentationLimitStalkValuation), which therefore [factors through the residue field](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Stalk/LocalRing.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.presentationLimitStalkResidueValuation) (for a pair of definition whose ring lies in `A⁺`).

- **Faithful flatness along a rational cover** — for a finite rational cover of `Spa(A,A⁺)` over a complete Hausdorff strongly noetherian Tate ring, the map from `A` into the product of the coordinate rings is [faithfully flat](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/FaithfullyFlat.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.faithfullyFlat_pi_toCompletionLoc), hence injective: Wedhorn's Corollary 8.32.

- **Lemma 8.33 for the structure presheaf** — over the same rings, sections over `{|f| ≤ 1}` and `{|f| ≥ 1}` that agree on the overlap [come from a global section](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/LaurentCover.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.exists_presentationLimitMap_eq_of_laurentCoverOpen), [uniquely](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/LaurentCover.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.injective_presentationLimitMap_laurentCoverOpen). Degree zero only; [surjectivity onto the overlap](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/Localization/LaurentCover.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.laurentCover_surjective) is proved for the coordinate rings.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The structure presheaf** — built as a limit over admissible presentations and [identified with Wedhorn's limit over rational subsets](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/SubsetLimit.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.presentationLimitIsoRationalSubsetLimit), it is [`A⟨T/s⟩` on a rational open](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Rational.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.presentationLimitRationalIso) and [`A` on the whole spectrum](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/GlobalSections.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.presentationLimitTopIso) of a complete Hausdorff pair.

- **The plus presheaf `𝒪_X⁺`** — [the sections whose germs are sub-unit at every point](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Plus.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.presentationLimitPlusPresheaf), equal to [`A_U⁺` on a rational open](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Plus.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.map_completedPlusSubring_eq_presentationLimitPlusSubring) (Wedhorn's Proposition 8.16). With the local stalks, it completes §3.3.

- **Restriction to a rational subset** — [Wedhorn's Remark 8.4](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Spa/StructurePresheaf/Localization.html#TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.presentationLimitLocIso): over rational opens inside `R(T/s)`, the structure presheaf of `Spa(A,A⁺)` is naturally that of `Spa(A⟨T/s⟩, A_U⁺)`. It lets Lemma 8.33 be applied on rational subsets, as Lemma 8.34's induction needs.

## The frontier

- **Tate acyclicity for finite rational covers** — run Lemma 8.34: apply Lemma 8.33 on rational subsets through Remark 8.4, pass from Laurent to standard covers, and reach arbitrary finite covers by Lemma 7.54. Part (ii)'s units are so far only elements vanishing nowhere on a piece, not yet shown invertible in the sections. [A criterion](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Category/TopCommRingCat/Sheaf.html#TauCeti.TopCommRingCat.isSheaf_of_isSheaf_forget) reduces the topological half of sheafiness to inducing maps into products.

- **Pre-adic spaces** — define `𝒱^pre`, affinoid pre-adic spaces and the three sheafiness predicates with their implications. Their stalk inputs now exist; this is the entry to Layers 5 and 6.

- **Strong noetherianness of complete rank-one fields** — noetherianity of `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ⟩` (BGR 5.2.6) remains, and the classical proof needs Weierstrass division over `K⟨X₁,…,Xₙ₋₁⟩`, not only over the field. Until then Layer 4's strongly noetherian Tate rings have no nonzero example here.

- **Stably uniform affinoids are sheafy** — [uniformity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Huber/Uniform.html#TauCeti.Huber.IsUniform) is defined; stable uniformity and the Buzzard-Verberkmoes theorem remain. It needs no noetherian hypothesis, so it does not wait on acyclicity.

- **The rest of Layer 2** — the hypotheses under which a morphism of Huber pairs induces a spectral map, and Example 7.57's description of the closed unit disc.
