## AdelicAlgebraicGroups: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-24 (`b662f03` to `d25d526`)

The roadmap's first results set up adelic points and their basic topology. For a finite-type commutative Hopf algebra over a number field `K`, the [adelic points](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/AdelicPoints/Basic.html#TauCeti.AdelicPoints) `G(𝔸_K)` are defined with the point topology, along with their finite, infinite and local parts. The rational points are [discrete](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/AdelicPoints/Discrete.html#TauCeti.AdelicPoints.isDiscrete_range_diagonal) and [closed](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/AdelicPoints/Discrete.html#TauCeti.AdelicPoints.isClosed_range_diagonal) in them. In the finite adeles alone, `G(K)` is discrete [exactly when it meets a compact open subgroup in a finite set](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/AdelicPoints/Discrete.html#TauCeti.AdelicPoints.discreteTopology_range_finiteDiagonal_iff), and as a worked example, [`K` itself is not discrete in its finite adeles](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Examples/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/AdelicPoints/Discrete.html#TauCeti.FiniteAdelicDiscreteness.not_discreteTopology_range_algebraMap). The adelic points also [split off any finite set of places](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/AdelicPoints/Away.html#TauCeti.AdelicPoints.awayContinuousMulEquiv).

On the measure side, the Haar character of a nonarchimedean local field [is `q^(-v(x))`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/HaarCharacter.html#MeasureTheory.distribHaarChar_eq_zpow), and the Haar measure giving the integers mass one is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/HaarCharacter.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_isAddHaarMeasure_measure_integer_eq_one). The local form measures `|dx/x|` [cannot be multiplied without convergence factors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LocalField/ConvergenceFactor.html#TauCeti.not_eventually_smul_withDensity_setOf_valuation_eq_one). For restricted products, rescaling the local measures [rescales the product by the finite product of the scalars](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Haar/RestrictedProduct.html#TauCeti.restrictedHaar_eq_finprod_smul), and a restricted product of unimodular groups [is unimodular](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Measure/Haar/RestrictedProduct.html#RestrictedProduct.modularCharacter_eq_one). The modular character is shown to be trivial on compact subgroups and on the centre.

For `GL₂`, the congruence levels `K₀(N)`, `K₁(N)` and `K(N)` in `GL₂(𝔸_f)` over a Dedekind domain are [compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/FiniteAdeleRing/CongruenceLevel.html#IsDedekindDomain.FiniteAdeleRing.isCompact_principalLevel) and open. For compact `U`, the component group satisfies [`GL₂(ℚ)⁺ ∩ gUg⁻¹ = SL₂(ℚ) ∩ gUg⁻¹`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/AdelicComponentGroup.html#TauCeti.AdelicExamples.GL2.componentGroup_eq_ker_det_inf), and `GL₂(ℝ)/ℝ^× SO(2)` is [homeomorphic to `ℂ ∖ ℝ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/UpperLowerHalfPlane.html#TauCeti.UpperLowerHalfPlane.quotientStabilizerHomeomorph). The decomposition of the level quotient into copies of `Γ\ℍ` that these pieces lead to has not been proved.
