## ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII: 2026-11-22 to 2026-11-25 (`5db379b` to `7433fc3`)

The coprime-action strand opened with its first main construction, Glauberman's canonical extension. When a finite group `S` acts on `Γ` with coprime orders, every `S`-invariant irreducible character of `Γ` has exactly one irreducible extension to `Γ ⋊ S` afforded by a representation of determinant `1` on `S` ([existsUnique_canonicalExtension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Glauberman/CanonicalExtension/Basic.html#TauCeti.existsUnique_canonicalExtension)). Its field of values is that of the original character and its values on `S` are rational integers ([canonicalExtension_values](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Glauberman/CanonicalExtension/Values.html#TauCeti.canonicalExtension_values)). Underneath sit Gallagher's normalised extension of a representation with scalar endomorphisms, proved over any field ([existsUnique_semidirectProduct_extension_det_eq_one](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Glauberman/NormalizedExtension.html#Representation.existsUnique_semidirectProduct_extension_det_eq_one)), and the fact that cyclotomic fields of coprime orders meet in `ℚ` ([inf_eq_bot_of_coprime](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/CoprimeCompositum.html#IsCyclotomicExtension.inf_eq_bot_of_coprime)). The extension is also shown to restrict to subgroups of `S` and to commute with isomorphisms and Galois conjugation.

Around it came three classical results. Gallagher's correspondence ([gallagher_bijOn](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Gallagher.html#TauCeti.gallagher_bijOn)): if a character of `G` extends `θ` from a normal subgroup `N`, multiplying by the irreducible characters of `G/N` gives exactly the irreducible characters over `θ`. Brauer's permutation lemma ([natCard_fixedBy_irreducibleCharacters_eq_natCard_fixedBy_conjClasses](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Automorphism.html#TauCeti.natCard_fixedBy_irreducibleCharacters_eq_natCard_fixedBy_conjClasses)): an automorphism fixes as many irreducible characters as conjugacy classes. And the degree bounds, ending in the divisibility of the index `|G : N|` by `χ(1)/θ(1)` ([exists_finrank_eq_mul_dvd_index_of_liesOver](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Induction/Clifford/DegreeRatio.html#FDRep.exists_finrank_eq_mul_dvd_index_of_liesOver)), with Schur's theorem on projective representations along the way. A certified character table of `SL(2,3)`, the roadmap's running example, was also computed.

Still missing: the fixed-class lemma for coprime actions, and with it the whole cyclic case and the Glauberman correspondence itself. The block-theory half, from Brauer's theorem on heights to Reynolds' theorem, has not started.
