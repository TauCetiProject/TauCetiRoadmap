### Named results

- **Circles are traversal orbits modulo reversal.** Each crossing-bearing circle corresponds to exactly one pair of mutually reverse directed traversal orbits of the smoothing ([`crossingCircleEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ResolvedCircle.html#TauCeti.PDCode.crossingCircleEquiv), [`two_mul_card_crossingCircle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ResolvedCircle.html#TauCeti.PDCode.two_mul_card_crossingCircle)).

- **The Khovanov Frobenius equation.** The dual numbers with `Δ1 = 1⊗ε + ε⊗1` and `Δε = ε⊗ε` satisfy the Frobenius equation, identifying `Δ∘m` with both mixed composites ([`isFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.isFrobenius)). ### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The signed cube totalization** ([`cubeComplex`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Cube/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.cubeComplex)). It turns any commutative cube of subsets of a finite ordered set, in a preadditive category with finite biproducts, into a cochain complex using the roadmap's sign `(−1)^#{b∈S : b<a}`. It is functorial and additive in the cube ([`cubeComplexFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Cube/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.cubeComplexFunctor)), and is the frame into which the resolution cube will go.
- **The Khovanov coalgebra on `R[ε]`** ([`Khovanov.coalgebra`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.coalgebra)). It is a scoped choice of structure that commutes with base change as a coalgebra ([`baseChangeCoalgEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.baseChangeCoalgEquiv)), which is what the coefficient story in K5 will need.
- **Resolved circles** ([`ResolvedCircle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ResolvedCircle.html#TauCeti.PDCode.ResolvedCircle)). These are the actual finite, decidable circle sets of a smoothing, in place of a bare count, so that merge and split maps can later act on named factors.
