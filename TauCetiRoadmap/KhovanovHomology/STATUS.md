## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Khovanov homology of planar oriented diagrams is defined and finite. It computes the Jones polynomial, it is never zero, and it has a normalized skein long exact sequence. It is not yet a link invariant. Layers 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 are partial, and Layers 4, 7 and 8 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Khovanov's theorem** — for a nonempty planar oriented diagram, the graded Euler characteristic of `Khⁱʲ(D;R)` is `(q + q⁻¹)` times the Jones polynomial at `t^(1/2) = −q`. This holds over any Noetherian ring with the rank-nullity property, such as `ℤ` or a field ([`khovanovEulerChar_eq_mul_eval₂_jonesPolynomial`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/EulerCharacteristic.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.khovanovEulerChar_eq_mul_eval₂_jonesPolynomial)).
- **Nonvanishing** — at `q = 1` the Euler characteristic of a `c`-component diagram is `2^c`, so `Kh(D; ℤ)` is nonzero for every planar diagram, the empty one included. The proof is combinatorial, through the state sum at loop value `−2` ([`exists_not_isZero_khovanovHomology_int`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Nonvanishing.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.exists_not_isZero_khovanovHomology_int)).
- **The skein exact sequence** — at any crossing, the homologies of `D` and of its two resolutions form a long exact sequence for every choice of orientations on the resolutions, with the normalizing shifts explicit. Its connecting map is the saddle map ([`khovanovHomologySkeinδ_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/SkeinSequence.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.khovanovHomologySkeinδ_eq)).
- **The planarity criterion** — a PD code with connected graph is planar exactly when the chord diagram left by contracting a spanning tree has no interleaving bands ([`isPlanar_iff_isNoncrossingMatching`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ChordDiagram.html#TauCeti.PDCode.isPlanar_iff_isNoncrossingMatching)).
- **Planar cube edges are merges or splits** — two states of a planar code that differ at one crossing either merge two circles or split one, so the resolution cube is well defined ([`IsPlanar.card_circlesAt_eq_two_or_card_circlesAt_eq_two`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ResolvedCircle/Planar.html#TauCeti.PDCode.IsPlanar.card_circlesAt_eq_two_or_card_circlesAt_eq_two)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

These give the skein sequence and the [mirror comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Mirror.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.khovanovComplexMirrorIso) of complexes.

These supply the oriented inputs of skein arguments.

## The frontier

- **Reidemeister invariance (Layer 4).** Chain homotopy equivalences for the three moves and their variants, then invariance along move words. The skein sequence and reordering isomorphisms are available. The README wants these maps as formal local maps, which waits on the Layer 2 cobordism category.
- **Coefficients and Künneth (Layer 5).** Reindex bounded cochain complexes so that Tau Ceti's chain-level universal-coefficient and Künneth sequences apply. Then export them for `Kh`, and turn the mirror-dual complex into the Hom/Ext statement on homology.
- **Certified computations (Layer 6).** Integer matrices and Smith normal forms on the enhanced-state model, then the Hopf links and both trefoils, including the `ℤ/2` at `(3,7)`. The skein sequence now supplies the recurrence that `T(2, m)` needs, though §6.4 also asks for Layer 4's cancellation.
- **The formal surface category (Layer 2).** Surface presentations, the cell-count grading, the `S`, `T`, `4Tu` quotient and `Mat_`. These sit on the local planar calculus of Layer 1 and are needed for Layer 3's open-diagram complexes and Layer 4.
- **Plane geometry (Layer 8).** Plane realizations of PD-codes and the relative Reidemeister theorem, needed for invariance of smooth links.
