## KhovanovHomology: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (`f795de6` to `273ea28`)

The first pieces of ordinary Khovanov homology are in, one from each of the three opening layers. The algebra placed on each circle now exists: the dual numbers `R[ε]` carry the [Khovanov coalgebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.coalgebra), with `Δ1 = 1⊗ε + ε⊗1`, `Δε = ε⊗ε` and a counit that reads off the coefficient of `ε`. It satisfies the [Frobenius equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.isFrobenius), so `Δ∘m` equals both mixed composites. Its Frobenius form is the [perfect trace pairing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.mul_compr₂_counit), which gives the neck-cutting expansion `a = ε(εa)·1 + ε(a)·ε`. The structure is also [compatible with base change](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.baseChangeCoalgEquiv) as a coalgebra. Because this coalgebra is a choice rather than a canonical one, it is installed only as scoped instances.

On the diagram side, the circles of a smoothed PD code are now genuine finite objects. [`ResolvedCircle`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ResolvedCircle.html#TauCeti.PDCode.ResolvedCircle) is the quotient of half-edges by the arc matching and the chosen smoothing, plus one label for each crossing-free component, and the [circle count](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ResolvedCircle.html#TauCeti.PDCode.card_resolvedCircle) shows there are exactly the native loop count of them. The proof identifies circles with [directed traversal orbits modulo reversal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ResolvedCircle.html#TauCeti.PDCode.crossingCircleEquiv). It rests on a general fact: each class generated by two perfect matchings contains [exactly two cycles of their product](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/Enumerative/PerfectMatching.html#TauCeti.IsPerfectMatching.two_mul_card_quotient_eqvGen).

The third piece is the [signed cube totalization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Cube/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.Functor.cubeComplex). It turns a commutative cube `Finset ι ⥤ C`, with `C` preadditive with finite biproducts, into a cochain complex with the edge `S → S∪{a}` signed by `(−1)^#{b∈S : b<a}`. The construction is [additive and functorial in the cube](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Cube/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.cubeComplexFunctor). Still missing: the crossing-reordering isomorphism, the merge/split classification for planar codes, and the graded circle tensors. So no Khovanov complex of a diagram exists yet.

## KhovanovHomology: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-25 (`273ea28` to `e8ea0d1`)

The state-sum half of the Jones comparison has landed: the [enhanced state sum](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/EnhancedState.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.enhancedStateSum) of a nonempty oriented diagram, the signed count of enhanced states by cochain and quantum degree, [equals `(q + q⁻¹)` times the Jones polynomial at `t^(1/2) = −q`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/EnhancedState.html#TauCeti.OrientedPDCode.enhancedStateSum_eq_mul_eval₂_jonesPolynomial) (TauCeti#19247). The empty diagram gives `1`, and the [right-handed trefoil](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/EnhancedState.html#TauCeti.enhancedStateSum_rightHandedTrefoilPDCode) is computed as `q + q³ + q⁵ − q⁹`. No complex exists yet, so this is not yet a statement about `χ_q Kh`: Euler–Poincaré still has to be applied to an actual complex.

Most of the window built the pieces of that complex. `R[ε]` now has its [quantum grading](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/QuantumGrading.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.quantumGrading), with `1` in degree `1`, `X` in degree `−1`, and multiplication and comultiplication of degree `−1`. [Circle tensors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/CircleTensor.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.CircleTensor) over finite circle sets come with the enhanced-state basis, a merge along any map of circle sets, and a [split](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Split.html#TauCeti.Khovanov.fiberwiseSplit) defined as its adjoint for the Frobenius pairing. On the diagram side, [two adjacent states never both have two circles through the changed crossing](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/PDCode/ResolvedCircle/Adjacent.html#TauCeti.PDCode.card_circlesAt_add_card_circlesAt_le_three), so each cube edge is a merge, a split or a one-to-one change. This yields the saddle correspondences and the actual [resolution merge](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/Merge.html#TauCeti.PDCode.resolutionMergeMap) and [split](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/KnotTheory/Khovanov/ResolutionSplit.html#TauCeti.PDCode.resolutionSplitMap) maps, both of degree `−1`. Circles are also transported along relabelling, mirroring and disjoint union.

Towards ruling out the one-to-one case on planar codes, the [boundary-count criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Combinatorics/ChordDiagram.html#Function.Involutive.orbitCount_finRotate_mul_add_orbitCount_eq_iff) for chord diagrams is in: `m` bands have at most `m + 1` boundary cycles, with equality exactly when no two interleave. This is the one-vertex step of the roadmap's disc/band argument. The spanning-tree contraction that connects it to `IsPlanar` has not landed yet.
