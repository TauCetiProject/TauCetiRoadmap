### Named results

- **The real sign splitting in degree two** — for an ordered field, every class of the Matsumoto group is uniquely `ε{−1, −1} + d` with `d` in the span of symbols having a positive entry. `{−1, −1}` has order exactly two, and the span is divisible over a real closed field ([`positiveEntryEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/MatsumotoGroup/OrderedField.html#TauCeti.MatsumotoGroup.positiveEntryEquiv)).
- **Steinberg's vanishing for finite fields** — every symbol of the Matsumoto group of a finite field is zero, so its degree-two symbol group is trivial ([`symbol_eq_zero_of_finite`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/MatsumotoGroup/Basic.html#TauCeti.MatsumotoGroup.symbol_eq_zero_of_finite)).
- **Finite-rank Steinberg groups are centrally closed** — for an index set with at least five elements, every central extension of `St_n(R)` splits uniquely. This is the splitting half of §3.2 ([`isUniversalCentralExtension_id_of_five_le_card`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Steinberg/UniversalCentralExtension.html#TauCeti.IndexedSteinbergGroup.isUniversalCentralExtension_id_of_five_le_card)).
- **The Steinberg relation for the tame symbol** — the tame symbol of a discrete valuation is trivial on `(r, 1 − r)`. It is also bilinear and inverted by swapping its entries ([`tameSymbol_of_add_eq_one`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Valuation/Discrete/TameSymbol.html#Valuation.tameSymbol_of_add_eq_one)).
- **The Dennis–Stein lift-change sequence** — for a surjection `B → B′` of commutative rings whose kernel `L` lies in the Jacobson radical, `D(B, L) → D(B) → D(B′) → 0` is exact ([`exact_toDennisSteinGroup_map`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DennisStein/LiftChange.html#TauCeti.DennisSteinGroup.exact_toDennisSteinGroup_map)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Milnor K-theory** — the graded ring of a commutative ring, with symbols, degree pieces, a universal property for maps into rings and functoriality. Degree zero is ℤ and degree one is the units. ([`MilnorK`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MilnorK/Basic.html#TauCeti.MilnorK)).
- **The tame symbol** — defined with the roadmap's normalisation, which is the inverse of the K-book's. ([`tameSymbol`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Valuation/Discrete/TameSymbol.html#Valuation.tameSymbol)).
- **Dennis–Stein presentation groups** — the absolute group `D(A)` and the relative group `D(R, I)`. These are the sources of the generator maps to `K₂` that Layer 13 still has to construct ([`DennisSteinGroup`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DennisStein/Basic.html#TauCeti.DennisSteinGroup)).
