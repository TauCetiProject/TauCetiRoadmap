## K2SymbolsBrauer: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-24 (`4f0383e` to `d25d526`)

Milnor K-theory now exists in the library. [`MilnorK`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MilnorK/Basic.html#TauCeti.MilnorK) is the tensor algebra on the units modulo the two-sided ideal generated by `a ⊗ b` with `a + b = 1`. It comes with symbols, grading, universal property and functoriality, degree zero identified with ℤ and degree one with the units, and over a field the sign rules `{a, b} = −{b, a}` and `{a, a} = {a, −1}`. In degree two the real calculation now holds for every ordered field: the [splitting of the Matsumoto group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/MatsumotoGroup/OrderedField.html#TauCeti.MatsumotoGroup.positiveEntryEquiv) writes each class uniquely as `ε{−1, −1} + d`, with `d` in the span of symbols that have a positive entry, and over a real closed field that span is [divisible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/MatsumotoGroup/OrderedField.html#TauCeti.MatsumotoGroup.positiveEntrySpan_nsmul_right_surjective). Higher degrees over ℝ are still open, as is unique divisibility over algebraically closed fields, though its inputs landed: a [root factorization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/MilnorK/Divisible.html#TauCeti.exists_ofMul_tmul_ofMul_eq_nsmul_sum) of Steinberg tensors and divisibility of tensor products.

The [tame symbol](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Valuation/Discrete/TameSymbol.html#Valuation.tameSymbol) of a discrete valuation arrived with the roadmap's sign convention, computed through unit parts relative to an order-one parameter. It is bilinear, is inverted by swapping its entries and [satisfies the Steinberg relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Valuation/Discrete/TameSymbol.html#Valuation.tameSymbol_of_add_eq_one), but it is not yet a homomorphism out of `K₂`, and work on the higher residues has not started. On the Steinberg side, the row and column root subgroups are injective and [stay faithful in the stable group](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Steinberg/RowColumn.html#TauCeti.SteinbergGroup.ofFin_comp_colHom_injective).

The Dennis–Stein layer has its algebraic scaffolding. This includes the presentation groups [`D(A)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DennisStein/Basic.html#TauCeti.DennisSteinGroup) and [`D(R, I)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DennisStein/Relative.html#TauCeti.RelativeDennisSteinGroup), the [lift-change sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DennisStein/LiftChange.html#TauCeti.DennisSteinGroup.exact_toDennisSteinGroup_map) for surjections whose kernel lies in the Jacobson radical, and a [detector](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/DennisStein/ZModFour.html#TauCeti.DennisSteinGroup.zmodFourDetector) on `D(ℤ/4)`. Threefold stability is characterized: [a field is threefold stable exactly when it has at least four elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/ThreefoldStable.html#TauCeti.isThreefoldStable_iff_four_le_card). Van der Kallen's module is [the Kähler differentials when 2 is invertible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Kaehler/VanDerKallen.html#TauCeti.VanDerKallenModule.kaehlerEquiv). None of this is yet compared with `K₂` itself.
