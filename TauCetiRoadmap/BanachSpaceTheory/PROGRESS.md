## BanachSpaceTheory: 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-19 (`ed918bd` to `9fc5d5a`)

The roadmap opened with a strong first round of classical duality theory. Among the named theorems that landed are the [closed-range theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/Adjoint.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isClosed_range_precomp_iff) (an operator between Banach spaces has closed range exactly when its adjoint does), with the `c₀₀ ⊂ ℓ²` example showing that completeness of the domain is needed, and [Goldstine's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/Goldstine.html#NormedSpace.closure_image_inclusionInDoubleDualWeak_closedBall). The [bipolar theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/LocallyConvex/Bipolar.html#AbsConvex.bipolar_eq_closure), the [Bishop–Phelps theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/BishopPhelps.html#TauCeti.dense_setOf_exists_norm_apply_eq_norm) in its functional, support-point and support-functional forms, and the [Milman–Pettis theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Convex/Uniform.html#TauCeti.surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual_of_uniformConvexSpace) also landed. Reflexivity now has its basic theory: it holds exactly when the [closed unit ball is weakly compact](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/Reflexive/WeakCompact.html#TauCeti.surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual_iff_isCompact_toWeakSpace_closedBall), and it passes to subspaces, quotients and duals. Weakly compact operators also arrived, with [Gantmacher's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/WeaklyCompact.html#ContinuousLinearMap.isWeaklyCompact_precomp_iff). Eberlein–Šmulian, James's theorem and Krein–Šmulian are still to come.

On the basis side, the main result is [James's basis criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Reflexive.html#SchauderBasis.surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual_iff): a Banach space with a Schauder basis is reflexive exactly when the basis is shrinking and boundedly complete. Supporting it are monotone renorming, subsequences, dual bases and the small-perturbation theorem. The canonical unconditional bases of `c₀` and `ℓᵖ` arrived along with the dualities [c₀* = ℓ¹](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/ZeroAtInfty/Dual.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.dualEquiv) and [(ℓ¹)* = ℓ∞](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Lp/Dual.html#lp.dualOneEquiv). These feed the [approximation property](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/ApproximationProperty/Basic.html#TauCeti.HasApproximationProperty) and its bounded and metric variants: a basis gives the BAP, `c₀` and `ℓᵖ` have the MAP, and all three properties transport along equivalences and projections.

Two later layers have their opening definitions: the [absolutely p-summing operators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming.html#TauCeti.absolutelySummingOperators), with the `πₚ` gauge and the inclusion for `p ≤ q`, and the [Banach–Mazur distance](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/BanachMazur.html#TauCeti.banachMazurDist), with its multiplicative triangle inequality. Tensor norms, the function-space duality of B5, and all of the depth milestones beyond these first layers have not started.

## BanachSpaceTheory: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25 (`9fc5d5a` to `69cd1ab`)

The first depth milestone of the roadmap has landed: [Pietsch domination](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming/Pietsch.html#ContinuousLinearMap.summingENorm_le_ofReal_iff_exists_isProbabilityMeasure). An operator has `p`-summing norm at most `C` exactly when some regular Borel probability measure on the weak-star compact dual ball dominates it, in the sense that `‖Tx‖ ≤ C (∫ |f(x)|ᵖ dν)^(1/p)` (TauCeti#19739). The [Pietsch factorization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming/Factorization.html#ContinuousLinearMap.summingENorm_le_ofReal_iff_exists_eq_comp_codRestrict_topologicalClosure) through the closure of the canonical image of `E` in `Lᵖ(ν)` follows, along with a version on the image itself for incomplete targets. Underneath, the summing operators are now the Banach ideal [`Πₚ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming/Basic.html#TauCeti.AbsolutelySumming), with the rank-one norm, the ideal inequalities and the `p = 1` [characterisation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming/Basic.html#TauCeti.mem_absolutelySummingOperators_one_iff) by unconditionally and absolutely convergent series.

On weak compactness, [Šmulian's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/WeakSpace/SeqCompact.html#TauCeti.isSeqCompact_toWeakSpace_of_isCompact) gives one half of Eberlein–Šmulian: weakly compact sets are weakly sequentially compact, with no separability assumption. The converse is still open. [Clarkson's inequality](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/UniformConvex.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.norm_add_rpow_add_norm_sub_rpow_le) makes `Lᵖ` uniformly convex, and by Milman–Pettis [reflexive](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/UniformConvex.html#MeasureTheory.Lp.surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual), but only for `2 ≤ p < ∞`. The classical sequence-space examples are now in place: [Schur's theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Lp/Schur.html#lp.tendsto_of_tendsto_toWeakSpace_one) for `ℓ¹` and the non-reflexivity of [`c₀`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/ZeroAtInfty/Reflexive.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.not_surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual) and [`ℓ¹`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Lp/Reflexive.html#lp.not_surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual_one). Their coordinate bases show that shrinking and bounded completeness are independent. Unconditional bases gained coordinate multipliers, rearrangement, and the [equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Bases/Unconditional.html#GeneralSchauderBasis.exists_forall_norm_proj_le_iff) between bounded finite projections and bounded signed multipliers.

Two later layers took their first real steps. The algebraic [projective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/TensorProduct/ProjectiveSeminorm.html#TauCeti.ProjectiveTensor) and [injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/TensorProduct/InjectiveSeminorm.html#TauCeti.InjectiveTensor) tensor norms arrived as definite cross norms with `ε ≤ π`. Their completions are not yet built. For the local theory there is now [finite representability](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/FinitelyRepresentable.html#TauCeti.FinitelyRepresentable), which is transitive and passes to subspaces, and [`η`-nets of the sphere](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/CoveringNumber.html#TauCeti.exists_finset_isCover_sphere) of size `(1+2/η)ᵏ`, one of the ingredients Dvoretzky's theorem needs.
