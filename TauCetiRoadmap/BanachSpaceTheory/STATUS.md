## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Three of the five depth milestones are proved: Pietsch domination, Grothendieck's inequality and local reflexivity. B1 is essentially done, and B2 and B3 are each one milestone short (James's theorem and the Haar basis). B4's tensor and nuclear-operator core is largely in place; B5, B6 and B7 are partial. ### Named results

- **Grothendieck's inequality** — for a finite scalar matrix, the supremum of `|∑ aᵢⱼ ⟨uᵢ, vⱼ⟩|` over Hilbert unit vectors is at most `κ_ℝ = 8` times the supremum over real unit scalars ([`abs_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_eight_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Grothendieck.html#Matrix.abs_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_eight_mul)), with `κ_ℂ = 16` in the [complex case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Grothendieck.html#Matrix.norm_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_sixteen_mul) and a [version for bilinear forms on `C(K)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/Grothendieck.html#LinearMap.abs_sum_apply_apply_le_eight_mul).
- **The principle of local reflexivity** — a finite-dimensional piece of the bidual embeds into the space with distortion `1 + ε`, preserving the pairing with a finite-dimensional subspace of the dual ([`exists_continuousLinearMap_of_doubleDual`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/LocalReflexivity.html#LinearIsometry.exists_continuousLinearMap_of_doubleDual)). Consequently the bidual is [finitely representable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/LocalReflexivity.html#TauCeti.finitelyRepresentable_doubleDual) in the space.
- **The Pietsch domination theorem** — `πₚ(T) ≤ C` exactly when a regular Borel probability measure on the weak-star compact dual ball dominates `‖Tx‖` by `C (∫ |f(x)|ᵖ dν)^(1/p)` ([`exists_isProbabilityMeasure_of_summingENorm_le_ofReal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming/Pietsch.html#ContinuousLinearMap.exists_isProbabilityMeasure_of_summingENorm_le_ofReal)), with factorization through `Lᵖ(ν)`.
- **The Eberlein–Šmulian theorem** — a set in a normed space is weakly compact exactly when it is weakly sequentially compact, with no completeness or separability assumption ([`isCompact_toWeakSpace_iff_isSeqCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/WeakSpace/EberleinSmulian.html#TauCeti.isCompact_toWeakSpace_iff_isSeqCompact)). A [relative form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/WeakSpace/EberleinSmulian.html#TauCeti.isCompact_closure_toWeakSpace_iff) is proved too.
- **The Krein–Šmulian theorem** — a convex set in the dual of a Banach space is weak-star closed exactly when its intersections with all closed balls about zero are ([`isClosed_iff_forall_isClosed_inter_closedBall`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/KreinSmulian.html#Convex.isClosed_iff_forall_isClosed_inter_closedBall)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Completed projective and injective tensor products** ([`CompletedProjectiveTensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/TensorProduct/Completion.html#TauCeti.CompletedProjectiveTensor), [`CompletedInjectiveTensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/TensorProduct/Completion.html#TauCeti.CompletedInjectiveTensor)). They come with the universal property, series representations and the identification of `E* ⊗̂ε F` with the [approximable operators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/TensorProduct/Approximable.html#TauCeti.CompletedInjectiveTensor.range_toOperator), so the comparisons between the approximation property and tensor products can now be stated.
- **Nuclear operators** ([`NuclearOperator`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nuclear.html#TauCeti.NuclearOperator)). They form a Banach space as a quotient of `E* ⊗̂π F`. Under the approximation property the representation is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/ApproximationProperty/TensorProduct.html#TauCeti.CompletedProjectiveTensor.injective_toContinuousLinearMap_of_hasApproximationProperty).
- **The Dunford–Pettis property** ([`HasDunfordPettisProperty`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/DunfordPettis/Basic.html#TauCeti.HasDunfordPettisProperty)). It is proved for `ℓ¹` and [`c₀`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/DunfordPettis/Dual.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.hasDunfordPettisProperty), and shown to fail for infinite-dimensional reflexive spaces. This is the first B5 material.

### Roadmap coverage

**B1** is essentially done. **B2** is done except James's theorem; uniform convexity of `Lᵖ` now covers all `1 < p < ∞`. **B3** is done except the Haar basis. It now has [`(ℓᑫ)* = ℓᵖ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Lp/Dual.html#lp.dualEquiv) and the reflexivity of `ℓᵖ`. **B4** is partial. Done: completed tensors and their universal property, nuclear operators, [Grothendieck's AP equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/ApproximationProperty/CompactOperator.html#TauCeti.hasApproximationProperty_iff_forall_isCompactOperator), and integral bilinear forms as the dual of `E ⊗ε F`. Missing: the converse of "AP implies `F ⊗̂π E → F ⊗̂ε E` is injective", the MAP for `Lᵖ[0,1]`, and the isometric nuclear/trace-class identification on Hilbert spaces, where only the Schmidt decomposition and one inequality are in. B4.C, B4.0 and B4.1 have no reported work. **B5** is partial and early. It has `(L¹)* = L∞` for finite measures (the README asks for σ-finite), the `Lᵖ` pairing as an isometric embedding into the dual, regular scalar measures embedded injectively in `C(K)*`, and the Dunford–Pettis property. The Hilbert classification, Banach–Stone and `L∞ ≃ C(K_μ)` are missing. **B6** has Pietsch and Grothendieck's inequality, but not B6.Cotype2 or the `π₂`/`π₁` operator corollaries. **B7** has finite representability, local reflexivity and some Dvoretzky ingredients, but lacks the trace-duality corollary of local reflexivity, John's ellipsoid, the projection theorems and Dvoretzky.

## The frontier

- **James's theorem** — the separable reduction and the theorem itself; the finite and countable norming sets are in place.
- **The Haar basis of `Lᵖ[0,1]`** — construct the basis, then integrate the midpoint estimate over dyadic atoms to get the sign-transform bound `max(p, p/(p−1)) − 1`. The scalar Bellman inequalities and dyadic `Lᵖ` convergence are done.
- **Grothendieck's operator corollaries** — `π₁(T) ≤ κ_𝕜 ‖T‖` for `T : L¹(μ) → H` needs only the finite-atom argument and density. `π₂(T) ≤ κ_𝕜 ‖T‖` on `C(K)` is blocked on B5's `L∞ ≃ C(K_μ)`.
- **Dvoretzky's theorem** — Gaussian concentration (Maurey–Pisier), the expected-maximum bound for Gaussians and Rademacher convexity are in. Still needed: spherical concentration, the random-rotation net lemma and the Dvoretzky–Rogers step, which needs John's ellipsoid.
- **The remaining AP–tensor comparisons** — the converse injectivity criterion and B4.0's reflexive MAP comparison. B7's trace-duality corollary, which B4.0's metric criterion needs for general spaces, can now be derived from local reflexivity.
