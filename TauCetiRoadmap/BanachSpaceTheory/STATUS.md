## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Three of the five depth milestones are proved: Pietsch domination, Grothendieck's inequality and local reflexivity. B1 is essentially done. B2 lacks only James's theorem, whose separable reduction is in place, and B3 only the unconditionality of the Haar basis. ### Named results

- **Grothendieck's inequality** — for a finite scalar matrix, the supremum of `|∑ aᵢⱼ ⟨uᵢ, vⱼ⟩|` over Hilbert unit vectors is at most `κ_ℝ = 8` times the supremum over real unit scalars ([`abs_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_eight_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Grothendieck.html#Matrix.abs_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_eight_mul)), with `κ_ℂ = 16` in the [complex case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Grothendieck.html#Matrix.norm_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_sixteen_mul) and a [version for bilinear forms on `C(K)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/Grothendieck.html#LinearMap.abs_sum_apply_apply_le_eight_mul).
- **The principle of local reflexivity** — a finite-dimensional piece of the bidual embeds into the space with distortion `1 + ε`, preserving the pairing with a finite-dimensional subspace of the dual ([`exists_continuousLinearMap_of_doubleDual`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/LocalReflexivity.html#LinearIsometry.exists_continuousLinearMap_of_doubleDual)). Consequently the bidual is [finitely representable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/LocalReflexivity.html#TauCeti.finitelyRepresentable_doubleDual) in the space.
- **The Pietsch domination theorem** — `πₚ(T) ≤ C` exactly when a regular Borel probability measure on the weak-star compact dual ball dominates `‖Tx‖` by `C (∫ |f(x)|ᵖ dν)^(1/p)` ([`summingENorm_le_ofReal_iff_exists_isProbabilityMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming/Pietsch.html#ContinuousLinearMap.summingENorm_le_ofReal_iff_exists_isProbabilityMeasure)), with factorization through `Lᵖ(ν)`.
- **The Eberlein–Šmulian theorem** — a set in a normed space is weakly compact exactly when it is weakly sequentially compact, with no completeness or separability assumption ([`isCompact_toWeakSpace_iff_isSeqCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/WeakSpace/EberleinSmulian.html#TauCeti.isCompact_toWeakSpace_iff_isSeqCompact)). A [relative form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/WeakSpace/EberleinSmulian.html#TauCeti.isCompact_closure_toWeakSpace_iff) is proved too.
- **The Krein–Šmulian theorem** — a convex set in the dual of a Banach space is weak-star closed exactly when its intersections with all closed balls about zero are ([`isClosed_iff_forall_isClosed_inter_closedBall`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/KreinSmulian.html#Convex.isClosed_iff_forall_isClosed_inter_closedBall)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Completed projective and injective tensor products** ([`CompletedProjectiveTensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/TensorProduct/Completion.html#TauCeti.CompletedProjectiveTensor), [`CompletedInjectiveTensor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/TensorProduct/Completion.html#TauCeti.CompletedInjectiveTensor)). They come with the universal property, series representations and the identification of `E* ⊗̂ε F` with the [approximable operators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/TensorProduct/Approximable.html#TauCeti.CompletedInjectiveTensor.range_toOperator), so the comparisons between the approximation property and tensor products can now be stated.
- **Nuclear operators** ([`NuclearOperator`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nuclear.html#TauCeti.NuclearOperator)). They form a Banach space as a quotient of `E* ⊗̂π F`. Under the approximation property the representation is [unique](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/ApproximationProperty/TensorProduct.html#TauCeti.CompletedProjectiveTensor.injective_toContinuousLinearMap_of_hasApproximationProperty).
- **The Dunford–Pettis property** ([`HasDunfordPettisProperty`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/DunfordPettis/Basic.html#TauCeti.HasDunfordPettisProperty)). It is proved for `ℓ¹` and [`c₀`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/DunfordPettis/Dual.html#ZeroAtInftyContinuousMap.hasDunfordPettisProperty), and shown to fail for infinite-dimensional reflexive spaces. This is the first B5 material.

### Roadmap coverage

**B1** is essentially done. **B2** is done except James's theorem, whose [separable reduction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/Reflexive/James.html#TauCeti.exists_isClosed_isSeparable_not_surjective_inclusionInDoubleDual) and triangular criterion for nonreflexivity are proved; uniform convexity of `Lᵖ` now covers all `1 < p < ∞`. **B3** is done except the unconditionality of the Haar basis. It now has [`(ℓᑫ)* = ℓᵖ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Lp/Dual.html#lp.dualEquiv), the reflexivity of `ℓᵖ`, and the [Haar basis](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/HaarBasis.html#TauCeti.unitInterval.haarBasis) of `Lᵖ[0,1]` for `1 ≤ p < ∞`, whose projections are the dyadic averages; the sign-transform bound is missing. **B4** is partial. Done: completed tensors and their universal property, nuclear operators, [Grothendieck's AP equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/ApproximationProperty/CompactOperator.html#TauCeti.hasApproximationProperty_iff_forall_isCompactOperator), integral bilinear forms as the dual of `E ⊗ε F`, and the [MAP of `Lᵖ[0,1]`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/ApproximationProperty/UnitInterval.html#TauCeti.unitInterval.hasMetricApproximationProperty_Lp) for `1 ≤ p < ∞`. Missing: the converse of "AP implies `F ⊗̂π E → F ⊗̂ε E` is injective", and the isometric nuclear/trace-class identification on Hilbert spaces, where only the Schmidt decomposition and one inequality are in. B4.C, B4.0 and B4.1 have no reported work. **B5** is partial and early. It has `(L¹)* = L∞` for finite measures (the README asks for σ-finite), the `Lᵖ` pairing as an isometric embedding into the dual, regular scalar measures embedded injectively in `C(K)*`, and the Dunford–Pettis property. `L∞` is now a commutative C⋆-algebra, so Mathlib's Gelfand transform gives the complex `L∞ ≃ C(K_μ)`. Its real form, the Hilbert classification and Banach–Stone are missing. **B6** has Pietsch, Grothendieck's inequality and its corollary [`π₁(T) ≤ 16‖T‖`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming/Grothendieck.html#ContinuousLinearMap.summingENorm_one_le_ofReal_sixteen_mul_norm) for `T : L¹(μ) → H` (`8‖T‖` over `ℝ`), but not B6.Cotype2 or `π₂` on `C(K)`. **B7** has finite representability, local reflexivity, [maximal-volume ellipsoids](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/JohnEllipsoid.html#TauCeti.exists_isMaxVolumeEllipsoid) with a contact point, [John's position](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/JohnPosition.html#IsCompact.exists_isJohnPosition_preimage) for compact neighbourhoods of zero, and some Dvoretzky ingredients. It lacks the trace-duality corollary of local reflexivity, John's decomposition `∑ cᵢ uᵢ ⊗ uᵢ = I` with `∑ cᵢ = n` and its corollaries, the projection theorems and Dvoretzky.

## The frontier

- **James's theorem** — the theorem itself; the separable reduction, the triangular criterion and the finite and countable norming sets are in place.
- **Unconditionality of the Haar basis** — integrate the midpoint estimate over dyadic atoms to get the sign-transform bound `max(p, p/(p−1)) − 1`. The basis, the scalar Bellman inequalities and dyadic `Lᵖ` convergence are done.
- **Grothendieck's `π₂` corollary** — `π₂(T) ≤ κ_𝕜 ‖T‖` for `T : C(K) → L¹(μ)` needs the real form of B5's `L∞ ≃ C(K_μ)` and its square-function comparison. The `π₁` corollary is done.
- **Dvoretzky's theorem** — Gaussian concentration (Maurey–Pisier), the expected-maximum bound for Gaussians and Rademacher convexity are in. Still needed: spherical concentration, the random-rotation net lemma and the Dvoretzky–Rogers step, which needs John's contact-point decomposition.
- **The remaining AP–tensor comparisons** — the converse injectivity criterion and B4.0's reflexive MAP comparison. B7's trace-duality corollary, which B4.0's metric criterion needs for general spaces, can now be derived from local reflexivity.
