### Named results

- **Grothendieck's inequality** — for a finite scalar matrix, the supremum of `|∑ aᵢⱼ ⟨uᵢ, vⱼ⟩|` over Hilbert unit vectors is at most `κ_ℝ = 8` times the supremum over real unit scalars ([`abs_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_eight_mul`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Grothendieck.html#Matrix.abs_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_eight_mul)), with `κ_ℂ = 16` in the [complex case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Matrix/Grothendieck.html#Matrix.norm_sum_sum_mul_inner_le_sixteen_mul) and versions for bilinear forms on `C(K)` over [`ℝ`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/Grothendieck.html#LinearMap.abs_sum_apply_apply_le_eight_mul) and [`𝕜`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/ContinuousMap/Grothendieck.html#LinearMap.norm_sum_apply_apply_le_sixteen_mul).
- **The Pietsch domination theorem** — `πₚ(T) ≤ C` exactly when a regular Borel probability measure on the weak-star compact dual ball dominates `‖Tx‖` by `C (∫ |f(x)|ᵖ dν)^(1/p)` ([`summingENorm_le_ofReal_iff_exists_isProbabilityMeasure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/AbsolutelySumming/Pietsch.html#ContinuousLinearMap.summingENorm_le_ofReal_iff_exists_isProbabilityMeasure)), with factorization through `Lᵖ(ν)`.
- **The principle of local reflexivity** — a finite-dimensional piece of the bidual embeds into the space with distortion `1 + ε`, preserving the pairing with a finite-dimensional subspace of the dual ([`exists_continuousLinearMap_of_doubleDual`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/LocalReflexivity.html#LinearIsometry.exists_continuousLinearMap_of_doubleDual)), so the bidual is [finitely representable](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/LocalReflexivity.html#TauCeti.finitelyRepresentable_doubleDual) in the space.
- **John's theorem** — a maximal-volume ellipsoid decomposes the identity over its contact points as `∑ cᵢ ⟪uᵢ, x⟫ uᵢ = x`, with positive weights summing to the dimension ([`exists_sum_rankOne_eq_id`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/JohnEllipsoid.html#ContinuousLinearEquiv.IsMaxVolumeEllipsoid.exists_sum_rankOne_eq_id)); hence every `n`-dimensional space is within [Banach–Mazur distance `√n`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/JohnEllipsoid.html#TauCeti.banachMazurDist_le_sqrt_finrank) of Euclidean space.
- **The Eberlein–Šmulian theorem** — a set in a normed space is weakly compact exactly when it is weakly sequentially compact, with no completeness or separability assumption ([`isCompact_toWeakSpace_iff_isSeqCompact`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/WeakSpace/EberleinSmulian.html#TauCeti.isCompact_toWeakSpace_iff_isSeqCompact)).

### Roadmap coverage

**B1** is essentially done; it now also has the [spectrum of the Banach transpose](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/Dual/Spectrum.html#ContinuousLinearMap.spectrum_precomp) and the dual of a subspace as a quotient by its annihilator. **B2** is done except James's theorem, whose separable reduction and triangular criterion are proved; Krein–Šmulian and Eberlein–Šmulian are in. **B3** is done except the unconditionality of the Haar basis. **B4** is partial: completed tensors, nuclear operators, Grothendieck's AP equivalence and the MAP of `Lᵖ[0,1]` are done, while the converse injectivity criterion and the isometric nuclear/trace-class identification on Hilbert spaces are missing. B4.C is largely done (AP, BAP, MAP and reflexivity all transfer along complexification), though compactness under complexification is not established here; B4.0 and B4.1 are untouched. **B5** is partial: `(L¹)* = L∞` for finite measures only, the Dunford–Pettis property, and the Dunford–Pettis weak-compactness criterion in `L¹` are in; weak sequential completeness of `L¹` is unclear, and the real `L∞ ≃ C(K_μ)`, the Hilbert classification and Banach–Stone are missing. **B6** has Pietsch, Grothendieck's inequality and the `π₁` corollary; B6.Cotype2 has its definitions and Gaussian weak-2 domination but not Kahane's inequality or the cotype-2 summing bound, and the `π₂` corollary is missing. **B7** has finite representability, local reflexivity, John's theorem, the [Kadec–Snobar](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/KadecSnobar.html#Submodule.exists_projection_norm_le_sqrt_finrank) and [Garling–Gordon](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Module/KadecSnobar.html#Submodule.exists_isIdempotentElem_ker_eq_norm_le_sqrt_finrank_add) projection theorems, and several Dvoretzky ingredients; it lacks the trace-duality corollary of local reflexivity and Dvoretzky's theorem.

## The frontier

- **Dvoretzky's theorem**: Gaussian concentration, Rademacher convexity and the [spherical mean bound](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Probability/Distributions/Uniform/Sphere/Norm.html#TauCeti.Probability.mul_sqrt_log_card_div_le_integral_norm_sum_smul_uniformSphereMeasure) `c √(log m / m) / 32` are in. Still needed: concentration on the sphere, Haar rotations, the Dvoretzky–Rogers basis (John's position is now available) and the random-rotation net lemma.
- **The cotype-2 summing bound**: Kahane's inequality, the Gaussian truncation and absorption argument giving `π₂(U) ≤ 2C t(C) R₂(Ue_i)`, and its complex version. The Gaussian weak-2 comparison it starts from is done.
- **James's theorem**: the theorem itself; the separable reduction, the triangular criterion and the norming sets are in place.
- **Unconditionality of the Haar basis**: integrate the midpoint estimate over dyadic atoms to get the sign-transform bound `max(p, p/(p−1)) − 1`. The basis and the scalar Bellman inequalities are done.
- **Grothendieck's `π₂` corollary**: `π₂(T) ≤ κ_𝕜 ‖T‖` for `T : C(K) → L¹(μ)` needs the real form of B5's `L∞ ≃ C(K_μ)` and its square-function comparison.
