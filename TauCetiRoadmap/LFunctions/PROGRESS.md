## LFunctions: 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-08 (`fbd9013` to `d2083f0`)

Mertens' first and second theorems now hold over any number field. The first says [the sum of log N𝔭 / N𝔭 over primes of norm up to x is log x + O(1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Mertens.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.isBigO_primeSummatory_log_absNorm_div_sub_log). The second says [∑ 1/N𝔭 − log log x converges](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Mertens.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.tendsto_primeSummatory_inv_absNorm_sub_log_log) to a [Mertens constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Mertens.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.mertensConstant) B_K. Mertens' product, the third theorem with its e^γ and the residue of ζ_K, has not landed. The general [completed L-function record](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Data/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData) also arrived, with duals, the [card of a Dirichlet series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Data/Series.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData.ofSeries) and the arithmetic-to-analytic translation of functional equations. Primitive Dirichlet characters already have cards in it, including [their functional equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Dirichlet/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.dirichletData_hasFunctionalEquation) with the Gauss-sum root number.

Most of the window went into Hecke's method, following Neukirch VII §§3–5. The pieces are [Poisson summation over an ideal lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/CanonicalEmbedding/PoissonSummation.html#TauCeti.NumberField.mixedEmbedding.tsum_idealLattice_eq_tsum_mixedDual_mixedFourier) in the mixed space and [the theta transformation with one parameter per infinite place](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/Inversion.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.latticeTheta_inv). They also include [the volume of a unit fundamental domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/Surface/Volume.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.surfaceHaar_of_isUnitFundamentalDomain), Neukirch's (5.6), and [the transformation law of the Mellin kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/MellinKernel.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.mellinKernel_inv). Mathlib's functional-equation pairs were [extended to a level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/FEPairWithLevel.html#TauCeti.FEPairWithLevel.Λ_eq). Nothing yet connects the kernel to the partial zeta functions, so ζ_K is still not continued or given its functional equation.

On the arithmetic side, partial zeta functions of ray classes [sum to ζ_K times the deleted Euler factors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/DedekindZeta/PartialZeta.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.sum_LSeries_rayClassCoeff), share one residue, and continue past Re s = 1. [Hecke L-functions of ray class characters](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Hecke/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.heckeLFunctionC) are defined as canonical meromorphic continuations, with primitive characters bundled with their conductor and orthogonality between the two families. A generic [3-4-1 nonvanishing criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Nonvanishing.html#TauCeti.LSeries.ne_zero_of_threeFourOne) is in place but has not been applied to Hecke L-functions.

## LFunctions: 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-19 (`d2083f0` to `b235000`)

[Mertens' product now holds over any number field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Mertens.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.tendsto_prod_one_sub_inv_absNorm_div_log): ∏_{N𝔭 ≤ x} (1 − N𝔭⁻¹)⁻¹ ~ e^γ κ_K log x, with κ_K the residue of ζ_K at s = 1 (TauCeti#29835). It comes with [the closed form of the Mertens constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Mertens.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.mertensConstant_eq), B_K = γ + log κ_K + ∑_𝔭 (log(1 − N𝔭⁻¹) + N𝔭⁻¹). The residue is Mathlib's constant, read off the half-plane; the continued ζ_K was not needed.

Hecke's method for ζ_K now has almost all of its pieces. [Hecke's unfolding of the theta integral](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/DedekindZeta/Unfolding.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.integral_unitCone_archNorm_cpow_mul_latticeTheta_sub_one) over the cone above a unit fundamental domain gives [the Mellin representation of a partial zeta function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/DedekindZeta/Unfolding.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.hasMellin_mellinKernel_idealLattice_sub_mellinConstant), Neukirch's (5.5). The kernel [tends exponentially fast to its constant term](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/Mellin/Kernel.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.exists_norm_mellinKernel_sub_le), forms [a functional-equation pair](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/Mellin/Pair.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.mellinKernelFEPair) with its dual, and on the dual side [is the kernel of the trace-dual ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/Mellin/Kernel.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.mellinKernel_mixedDual_idealLattice). The [Epstein regression](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/Epstein.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.radialMellin_eq_epsteinZeta) also landed, along with change of modulus for the partial zeta series. These steps are not yet chained: there is no completed partial zeta function, so ζ_K is still neither continued nor given its functional equation. Three earlier pull requests are recorded here late. They added the [continued Dedekind zeta function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/DedekindZeta/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.dedekindZetaC) with [its quadratic factorization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/DedekindZeta/Quadratic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.dedekindZetaC_eq_riemannZeta_mul_LFunction), the multi-parameter Gaussian, and an [average-coefficient-growth predicate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Data/Growth.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData.HasAverageCoefficientGrowth) on cards.

Grossencharacters have begun. A [presentation of an algebraic Hecke character](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Grossencharacter/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.Grossencharacter) is determined by its idele class character, and it has inverses and a [presented L-function](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Grossencharacter/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.Grossencharacter.lFunctionC), though nothing yet continues that function. [Hecke's unit relation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Grossencharacter/Compatibility.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.Grossencharacter.compatibility) holds with the inverse sign, and [Hecke's finite character](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Grossencharacter/FiniteCharacter.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.Grossencharacter.finiteCharacter) factors through [a unique residue character](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Grossencharacter/FiniteCharacter.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.Grossencharacter.existsUnique_residueCharacter).
