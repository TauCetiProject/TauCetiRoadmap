## LFunctions: 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-08 (`fbd9013` to `d2083f0`)

Mertens' first and second theorems now hold over any number field. The first says [the sum of log N𝔭 / N𝔭 over primes of norm up to x is log x + O(1)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Mertens.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.isBigO_primeSummatory_log_absNorm_div_sub_log). The second says [∑ 1/N𝔭 − log log x converges](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Mertens.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.tendsto_primeSummatory_inv_absNorm_sub_log_log) to a [Mertens constant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Mertens.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.mertensConstant) B_K. Mertens' product, the third theorem with its e^γ and the residue of ζ_K, has not landed. The general [completed L-function record](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Data/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData) also arrived, with duals, the [card of a Dirichlet series](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Data/Series.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.AnalyticLFunctionData.ofSeries) and the arithmetic-to-analytic translation of functional equations. Primitive Dirichlet characters already have cards in it, including [their functional equation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Dirichlet/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.dirichletData_hasFunctionalEquation) with the Gauss-sum root number.

Most of the window went into Hecke's method, following Neukirch VII §§3–5. The pieces are [Poisson summation over an ideal lattice](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/CanonicalEmbedding/PoissonSummation.html#TauCeti.NumberField.mixedEmbedding.tsum_idealLattice_eq_tsum_mixedDual_mixedFourier) in the mixed space and [the theta transformation with one parameter per infinite place](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/Inversion.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.latticeTheta_inv). They also include [the volume of a unit fundamental domain](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/Surface/Volume.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.surfaceHaar_of_isUnitFundamentalDomain), Neukirch's (5.6), and [the transformation law of the Mellin kernel](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Theta/MellinKernel.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.mellinKernel_inv). Mathlib's functional-equation pairs were [extended to a level](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/FEPairWithLevel.html#TauCeti.FEPairWithLevel.Λ_eq). Nothing yet connects the kernel to the partial zeta functions, so ζ_K is still not continued or given its functional equation.

On the arithmetic side, partial zeta functions of ray classes [sum to ζ_K times the deleted Euler factors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/DedekindZeta/PartialZeta.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.sum_LSeries_rayClassCoeff), share one residue, and continue past Re s = 1. [Hecke L-functions of ray class characters](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LFunctions/Hecke/Basic.html#TauCeti.LFunctions.heckeLFunctionC) are defined as canonical meromorphic continuations, with primitive characters bundled with their conductor and orthogonality between the two families. A generic [3-4-1 nonvanishing criterion](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/LSeries/Nonvanishing.html#TauCeti.LSeries.ne_zero_of_threeFourOne) is in place but has not been applied to Hecke L-functions.
