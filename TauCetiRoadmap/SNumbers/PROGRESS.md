## SNumbers: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11 (`8c42da5` to `1c6a1a4`)

The s-numbers roadmap has made its first move. Pietsch's axioms are now in place as [`IsSNumberSequence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Basic.html#TauCeti.SNumbers.IsSNumberSequence): an s-number sequence starts at the operator norm and decreases, is stable under perturbation and two-sided composition, vanishes at index `n` on operators of rank at most `n`, and is normalized on the Euclidean identities. Beside it sits the strict variant [`IsStrictSNumberSequence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Basic.html#TauCeti.SNumbers.IsStrictSNumberSequence), which normalizes the identity of every normed space below its dimension, infinite dimension included. The elementary consequences every sequence shares follow from the axioms: the norm bound, Lipschitz continuity in the operator, and absolute homogeneity (TauCeti#33152).

The approximation numbers, taken from the operator-ideals library, [form a strict s-number sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Approximation.html#TauCeti.SNumbers.isStrictSNumberSequence_approximation) over every nontrivially normed field. The roadmap expected this to need a complete scalar field for Riesz's lemma, but the proof here gets by on a rank–nullity argument and needs neither a complete field nor a complete space (TauCeti#33658). The [upper half of the extremal sandwich](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Approximation.html#TauCeti.SNumbers.IsSNumberSequence.le_singularValue) also landed: every s-number sequence lies below the approximation numbers. The other four classical sequences (Gelfand, Kolmogorov, Bernstein and Hilbert numbers) and the entropy numbers have not been started.

## SNumbers: 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-19 (`1c6a1a4` to `b235000`)

All four of the remaining classical s-number sequences now exist, and each satisfies Pietsch's axioms. The [Gelfand numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Gelfand.html#ContinuousLinearMap.gelfandNumber) measure `T` on closed subspaces of codimension at most `n`, and the [Bernstein numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Bernstein.html#ContinuousLinearMap.bernsteinNumber) give the best lower bound of `T` on an `(n+1)`-dimensional subspace. Both [are](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Gelfand.html#TauCeti.SNumbers.isStrictSNumberSequence_gelfand) [strict](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Bernstein.html#TauCeti.SNumbers.isStrictSNumberSequence_bernstein) s-number sequences over every nontrivially normed field. The [Kolmogorov numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Kolmogorov.html#ContinuousLinearMap.kolmogorovNumber), defined through quotient norms on the target, are [strict over a complete scalar field](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Kolmogorov.html#TauCeti.SNumbers.isStrictSNumberSequence_kolmogorov) (TauCeti#35196). The roadmap expects the weaker, non-strict statement over any nontrivially normed field. Here it is only derived from the strict one, so it also assumes completeness. The [Hilbert numbers](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Hilbert.html#ContinuousLinearMap.hilbertNumber), the supremum of the approximation numbers of the compressions `B T A` through `ℓ₂` by contractions, [form an s-number sequence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Hilbert.html#TauCeti.SNumbers.isSNumberSequence_hilbert) over ℝ or ℂ, as expected, since they are not strict.

Each sequence comes with the calculus the roadmap asked for: its value at index zero is the norm, it vanishes from the rank onwards, it obeys the ideal inequality, and its infimum or supremum is attained up to ε. Two alternative formulas also landed: the [unit-ball form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Kolmogorov.html#ContinuousLinearMap.kolmogorovNumber_eq_iInf_sSup_infNndist) of the Kolmogorov numbers over a densely normed field, and the [ratio form](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/SNumbers/Hilbert.html#ContinuousLinearMap.hilbertNumber_eq_iSup_div) of the Hilbert numbers. Smaller supporting lemmas cover norms of quotient maps and of restrictions, and the fact that compression by contractions does not increase approximation numbers.

Still missing are Pietsch's lifting identity for the Kolmogorov numbers and the whole of Milestone A4: metric injections and surjections, injectivity of `cₙ` and surjectivity of `dₙ`.
