## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done. Layers 1 through 4 each have a recognised theorem in place: cubic reciprocity, Christol's theorem, the Hermite normal form with Capelli's and Dumas's theorems, and Markoff's theorem. Most of their targets remain open, and Layers 5 to 7 have only first definitions and isolated results. None of the later summits (quartic, Eisenstein and general power reciprocity, Cobham, the Smyth–Dobrowolski–Dimitrov bounds, Fröhlich–Taylor) has been reached, though steps toward quartic reciprocity and Cobham's theorems are in.

### Named results

- **The law of cubic reciprocity** — for primary primes π₁, π₂ of ℤ[ω] with different norms, (π₂/π₁)₃ = (π₁/π₂)₃, with a companion form against rational integers prime to 3 ([`IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Three/CubicReciprocity.html#TauCeti.IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity)).
- **Christol's theorem** — over a finite field with q elements, a power series is algebraic over K[X] exactly when its coefficients are q-automatic. The two directions are separate theorems ([`PowerSeries.isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Automatic.html#PowerSeries.isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic), [`PowerSeries.isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Christol.html#PowerSeries.isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff)).
- **Markoff's theorem** — every positive solution of x² + y² + z² = 3xyz is obtained from (1, 1, 1) by a word in the Vieta moves ([`exists_foldr_markoffVieta_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Markoff/Descent.html#TauCeti.exists_foldr_markoffVieta_eq)).
- **Existence and uniqueness of the Hermite normal form** — every sublattice of ℤᵐ has exactly one basis in Hermite normal form ([`Matrix.existsUnique_isHermiteNormalForm_range_mulVecLin_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/HermiteNormalForm/Existence.html#Matrix.existsUnique_isHermiteNormalForm_range_mulVecLin_eq)).
- **Capelli's theorem** — over any field, Xⁿ − a is irreducible exactly when a is not a p-th power for any prime p dividing n and, when 4 divides n, a is not of the form −4b⁴ ([`X_pow_sub_C_irreducible_iff_forall_prime`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Kummer/Capelli.html#TauCeti.X_pow_sub_C_irreducible_iff_forall_prime)).

## The frontier

- **Quartic reciprocity.** The Jacobi sum of the quartic character, the fourth power of the quartic Gauss sum, the supplementary laws for i and −1 and triviality on rational integers are proved. What remains is the reciprocity law itself and the supplement for 1 + i, whose [rational-denominator case](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Four/OneAddRoot.html#IsPrimitiveRoot.powerResidueSymbolIdeal_span_intCast_mk_one_add_toInteger) is proved.
- **The Markoff tree.** The descent is in place; still missing are the statement that the positive Markoff graph is a tree in which every vertex has degree three, and pairwise coprimality of the coordinates.
- **Cobham's theorems.** Characterisation by uniform morphisms and base independence for multiplicatively independent bases. The automata and kernel material is ready, as are several steps of the base-independence proof: the close-powers lemma for two bases, uniform repeated representatives, gluing local periods into a periodic tail, and automata reading bounded digits.
- **Hermite matrices of ideals and class-group presentations.** With the Hermite normal form available, the ideal-matrix description, norms as products of pivots and relation-lattice presentations of Layer 5 can begin.
- **Maximal orders over a given order.** The ascending chain condition on overorders is proved, but the statement that every order with nondegenerate trace lies in a maximal order is not. That result is the next step toward normal integral bases. Fröhlich–Taylor remains far off.
