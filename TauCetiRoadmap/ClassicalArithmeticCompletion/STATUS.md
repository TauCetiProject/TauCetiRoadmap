## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done. Layers 1 through 7 are all partial. The high points are cubic reciprocity, both directions of Christol's theorem and Markoff's theorem. Several principal milestones have not begun: quartic and Eisenstein reciprocity, Cobham's theorem, the Smith normal form, the class-number and regulator comparisons, the Smyth, Dobrowolski and Dimitrov bounds, and the Fröhlich–Taylor theorem.

### Named results

- **The law of cubic reciprocity** — for primary primes π₁, π₂ of ℤ[ω] with distinct norms other than 3, (π₂/π₁)₃ = (π₁/π₂)₃ ([`IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Three/CubicReciprocity.html#TauCeti.IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity)), with a companion form pairing a primary prime with a rational integer ([`cubic_reciprocity_intCast`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Three/CubicReciprocity.html#TauCeti.IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity_intCast)).
- **Stickelberger's relation** — in the m-th cyclotomic field, the Gauss sum power Φ(A) generates the ideal A^γ, γ being the Stickelberger element, for every ideal A prime to m ([`Ideal.span_stickelbergerPhi`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/PowerResidueSymbol/StickelbergerPhi/Relation.html#Ideal.span_stickelbergerPhi)).
- **Christol's theorem, both directions** — over a finite field with q elements, a power series is algebraic over K[X] exactly when its coefficient sequence is q-automatic ([`isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Christol.html#PowerSeries.isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff), [`isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Automatic.html#PowerSeries.isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic)). The proof uses Eilenberg's kernel characterisation of automaticity ([`isAutomatic_iff_finite_kernel`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Computability/AutomaticSequence/Kernel.html#TauCeti.isAutomatic_iff_finite_kernel)).
- **Markoff's theorem** — every positive integer solution of x² + y² + z² = 3xyz is obtained from (1, 1, 1) by a word in the three Vieta moves ([`exists_foldr_markoffVieta_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Markoff/Descent.html#TauCeti.exists_foldr_markoffVieta_eq)).
- **Capelli's theorem** — over any field, Xⁿ − a is irreducible exactly when a is not a p-th power for any prime p dividing n and, when 4 divides n, a is not of the form −4b⁴ ([`X_pow_sub_C_irreducible_iff_forall_prime`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Kummer/Capelli.html#TauCeti.X_pow_sub_C_irreducible_iff_forall_prime)).

### Roadmap coverage

Layer 0, the Chinese remainder theorem at arbitrary integer moduli, is done. Layer 1 is partial. Unit groups modulo prime powers, primitive conductors, rational squarefree parts, cubic reciprocity, Stickelberger's relation and the quartic supplementary laws for i and −1 are proved. The norm-residue and Hilbert symbols, the completed quadratic symbol, quartic reciprocity and Eisenstein reciprocity are not. Layer 2 is partial: automata, kernels, Eilenberg's theorem and both halves of Christol are in. Cobham, Farey sequences, Eulerian numbers and Lucas sequences are not. Layer 3 is partial, with the Hermite normal form, Capelli and the one-outer-root irreducibility criterion; Smith normal form, Perron, Newton polygons and integer-valued polynomials are untouched. Layer 4 is partial: Markoff's theorem is done, but not the Markoff tree, the Ghosh–Sarnak levels or the Pell and three-square material. Layer 5 is partial (the Hermite matrix of an ideal; negative Pell for every squarefree radicand). Layer 6 has only Pisot numbers, with the golden ratio as first example. Layer 7 has its order-theoretic foundations and Stickelberger's Gauss-sum valuation, but no normal-integral-basis theorems.

## The frontier

- **Quartic reciprocity.** The supplementary laws for i and −1, primary Gaussian integers and the quartic Gauss and Jacobi sums are proved. What remains is the supplement for 1 + i, the mixed law against a rational integer, and the reciprocity law itself.
- **Christol as one statement, then Cobham.** The two directions should be combined into the roadmap's equivalence, including the base-p form; the base-power invariance [`isAutomatic_pow_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Computability/AutomaticSequence/PowerBase.html#TauCeti.isAutomatic_pow_iff) already supplies the translation. Cobham's characterisation by uniform morphisms and his base-independence theorem have not started.
- **Eisenstein reciprocity.** Stickelberger's relation and the residue symbol of Φ(A) at a second prime are in place. Next is Φ(α) = ±α^γ for primary α in ℤ[ζ_l], then the reciprocity law.
- **Smith normal form and class-group presentations.** With Hermite forms and ideal Hermite matrices available, the next targets are the norm of an ideal as the product of its pivots, the Smith normal form of a rectangular integer matrix, and the class group read off a relation matrix.
- **Normal integral bases.** Layer 7 has orders, maximal orders and locally free lattices. Tame ramification, Noether's theorem and the locally free class group come before anything on the Fröhlich–Taylor obstruction.
