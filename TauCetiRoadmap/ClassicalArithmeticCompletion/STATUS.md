### Named results

- **The law of cubic reciprocity** — for primary primes π₁, π₂ of ℤ[ω] with different norms, (π₂/π₁)₃ = (π₁/π₂)₃ ([`IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Three/CubicReciprocity.html#TauCeti.IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity)).
- **Stickelberger's relation** — in the m-th cyclotomic field, the ideal generated by the Gauss-sum power Φ(A) of an ideal A of norm prime to m is A raised to the Stickelberger element ([`Ideal.span_stickelbergerPhi`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/PowerResidueSymbol/StickelbergerPhi/Relation.html#Ideal.span_stickelbergerPhi)).
- **Christol's theorem** — over a finite field with q elements, a power series is algebraic over K[X] exactly when its coefficients are q-automatic ([`PowerSeries.isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Automatic.html#PowerSeries.isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic), [`PowerSeries.isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Christol.html#PowerSeries.isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff)).
- **Cobham's theorem** — for multiplicatively independent k, l ≥ 2, a sequence that is both k-automatic and l-automatic is eventually periodic ([`isAutomatic_and_isAutomatic_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Computability/AutomaticSequence/Cobham.html#TauCeti.isAutomatic_and_isAutomatic_iff)).
- **The Markoff tree** — the positive solutions of x² + y² + z² = 3xyz, joined by Vieta moves, form a tree in which every vertex has degree three, so every triple is reached from (1, 1, 1) along a unique path ([`markoffGraph_isTree`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Markoff/Tree.html#TauCeti.markoffGraph_isTree)).

## The frontier

- **Quartic reciprocity.** Quartic Gauss and Jacobi sums and the supplements for i and −1 are in. The law itself and the general 1 + i supplement remain.
- **Eisenstein reciprocity.** Its main input, Stickelberger's relation with the primary ±α^γ form, is proved. The residue symbol of Φ(P) at a second prime is one missing step.
- **Explicit class groups.** With Minkowski's bound and relation lattices in place, norms as Hermite pivots and the class groups of ℚ(√−14) and ℚ(√−30) are next.
- **Locally free classes through ideles.** The locally free class group and ideles of an order exist separately. Rank-one lattices by idele double cosets, and idele representatives covering the class group, remain; Fröhlich–Taylor is still far off.
- **Markoff coprimality and Cobham's uniform morphisms.** Two short follow-ups: Markoff coordinates are pairwise coprime, and automatic sequences are images of fixed points of uniform morphisms.
