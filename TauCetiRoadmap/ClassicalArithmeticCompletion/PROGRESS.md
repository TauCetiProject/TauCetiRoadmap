## ClassicalArithmeticCompletion: 2026-10-18 to 2026-11-06 (`8e902e1` to `385ec2d`)

The law of cubic reciprocity is proved: for primary primes π₁, π₂ of ℤ[ω] with different norms, (π₂/π₁)₃ = (π₁/π₂)₃ ([`IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Three/CubicReciprocity.html#TauCeti.IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity)), along with the version against a rational integer prime to 3 ([`IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity_intCast`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Three/CubicReciprocity.html#TauCeti.IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity_intCast)). Underneath it sits a power residue symbol at any ideal prime to n, primary normalisations in ℤ[ω] and ℤ[i], and the Gauss-sum power Φ(P) with its Jacobi-sum evaluations (TauCeti#24325). On the quartic side the supplementary laws for i and −1 and the fourth power of the quartic Gauss sum are in, but quartic reciprocity itself is not. Stickelberger's congruence ([`stickelberger_congruence`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/GaussSum/Stickelberger.html#TauCeti.stickelberger_congruence)) also landed, along with the digit-sum valuation of Gauss sums that follows from it.

Christol's theorem is now proved in both directions: over a finite field with q elements, a power series is algebraic over K[X] exactly when its coefficients are q-automatic ([`PowerSeries.isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Automatic.html#PowerSeries.isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic), [`PowerSeries.isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Christol.html#PowerSeries.isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff)). Both directions rest on automata with output, Cartier operators, Ore relations and Eilenberg's theorem that a sequence is automatic exactly when its k-kernel is finite ([`isAutomatic_iff_finite_kernel`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Computability/AutomaticSequence/Kernel.html#TauCeti.isAutomatic_iff_finite_kernel)). The two directions are separate statements, and nothing has started on Cobham's theorems.

Elsewhere, Markoff's theorem landed: every positive Markoff triple is reached from (1, 1, 1) by Vieta moves ([`exists_foldr_markoffVieta_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Markoff/Descent.html#TauCeti.exists_foldr_markoffVieta_eq)). The graph is not yet shown to be a tree. The Hermite normal form now exists and is unique, so every sublattice of ℤᵐ has exactly one Hermite basis ([`Matrix.existsUnique_isHermiteNormalForm_range_mulVecLin_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/HermiteNormalForm/Existence.html#Matrix.existsUnique_isHermiteNormalForm_range_mulVecLin_eq)). Capelli's theorem settles when Xⁿ − a is irreducible ([`X_pow_sub_C_irreducible_iff_forall_prime`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Kummer/Capelli.html#TauCeti.X_pow_sub_C_irreducible_iff_forall_prime)). The negative Pell equation is soluble exactly when 𝓞_K has a unit of norm −1. Arithmetic orders arrived too, with maximal orders and the ascending chain condition on overorders. Pisot numbers were defined, with the golden ratio as the first example.
