## ClassicalArithmeticCompletion: 2026-10-18 to 2026-11-06 (`8e902e1` to `a67a650`)

The law of cubic reciprocity is now proved. For primary primes π₁, π₂ of ℤ[ω] with distinct norms other than 3, it says (π₂/π₁)₃ = (π₁/π₂)₃ ([`IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Three/CubicReciprocity.html#TauCeti.IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity)), and a companion version pairs a primary prime with a rational integer ([`cubic_reciprocity_intCast`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/Cyclotomic/Three/CubicReciprocity.html#TauCeti.IsPrimaryEisenstein.cubic_reciprocity_intCast)). Underneath it sit an n-th power residue symbol at primes and ideals of a number field ([`Ideal.powerResidueSymbolIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/PowerResidueSymbol/Ideal.html#Ideal.powerResidueSymbolIdeal)), and Stickelberger's relation, which factors the Gauss sum power Φ(P) = g(P)^m as P^γ in the m-th cyclotomic field ([`Ideal.span_stickelbergerPhi`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/NumberField/PowerResidueSymbol/StickelbergerPhi/Relation.html#Ideal.span_stickelbergerPhi)). On the quartic side, primary Gaussian integers, the supplementary laws for i and −1 and the quartic Gauss and Jacobi sums are all in place, but quartic reciprocity itself is not.

Both directions of Christol's theorem landed. Over a finite field with q elements, a power series is algebraic over K[X] if ([`isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Christol.html#PowerSeries.isAlgebraic_of_isAutomatic_coeff)) and only if ([`isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Automatic.html#PowerSeries.isAutomatic_coeff_of_isAlgebraic)) its coefficients are q-automatic. They rest on a new theory of automata with output, including Eilenberg's theorem that a sequence is automatic exactly when its k-kernel is finite ([`isAutomatic_iff_finite_kernel`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Computability/AutomaticSequence/Kernel.html#TauCeti.isAutomatic_iff_finite_kernel)), and on Cartier operators. The two directions have not yet been combined into one statement, the base-p form is not stated, and Cobham's theorem has not started.

Markoff's theorem also landed: every positive solution of x² + y² + z² = 3xyz comes from (1, 1, 1) by Vieta moves ([`exists_foldr_markoffVieta_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Markoff/Descent.html#TauCeti.exists_foldr_markoffVieta_eq)). So did Capelli's criterion for the irreducibility of Xⁿ − a ([`X_pow_sub_C_irreducible_iff_forall_prime`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/FieldTheory/Kummer/Capelli.html#TauCeti.X_pow_sub_C_irreducible_iff_forall_prime)) and the existence and uniqueness of the Hermite normal form ([`existsUnique_isHermiteNormalForm_range_mulVecLin_eq`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/HermiteNormalForm/Existence.html#Matrix.existsUnique_isHermiteNormalForm_range_mulVecLin_eq)). Integral Galois modules got their first foundations: orders, the theorem that every order with nondegenerate regular trace lies in a maximal order ([`IsOrder.exists_le_isMaximalOrder`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/IntegralOrder/Maximal.html#TauCeti.IntegralOrder.IsOrder.exists_le_isMaximalOrder)), locally free lattices and descent of projectivity. Smaller pieces include the Chinese remainder theorem for non-coprime moduli, the dyadic shape of primitive quadratic conductors and Pisot numbers.
