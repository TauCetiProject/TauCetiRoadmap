## ClassicalArithmeticCompletion: 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-21 (`8e902e1` to `f91b639`)

The roadmap's first window closes its only Layer 0 milestone, the [Chinese remainder theorem at non-coprime moduli](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/Int/ChineseRemainder.html#Int.exists_modEq_and_modEq_iff_modEq_gcd): x ≡ a (mod m) and x ≡ b (mod n) have a common solution exactly when a ≡ b modulo gcd(m, n), for any integer moduli, with a zero modulus read as equality. Layer 1 gets its elementary groundwork. The units modulo 2^(e+2) are [identified with ℤ/2 × ℤ/2^e](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/ZMod/UnitsTwoPow.html#TauCeti.ZMod.unitsTwoPowEquiv), generated by −1 and 5. Over an odd prime p, [units congruent to 1 modulo p are squares](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/ZMod/PrincipalUnits.html#ZMod.isSquare_of_unitsMap_eq_one) modulo every power of p. Two Dirichlet characters that agree at all but finitely many primes [share their conductor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/PrimeValues.html#DirichletCharacter.conductor_eq_of_eventually_apply_prime_eq) and primitive character, and [are equal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/DirichletCharacter/PrimeValues.html#DirichletCharacter.eq_of_eventually_apply_prime_eq) at a common level. The [squarefree part of a rational number](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Data/Rat/SquarefreePart.html#Rat.squarefreePart) arrived with its uniqueness, its invariance on square classes and the criterion that a prime divides it exactly when its valuation is odd.

In Layer 2, deterministic automata with output and [k-automatic sequences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Computability/AutomaticSequence.html#TauCeti.IsAutomatic) are now defined, with closure under maps and pairing and the fact that [eventually periodic sequences are automatic](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Computability/AutomaticSequence.html#TauCeti.isAutomatic_of_eventually_periodic) (TauCeti#18960). The Cartier operators on Laurent series over a field with q elements arrived as residue-class components of the exponents, with the decomposition [f = Σ X^r (Λ_r f)^q](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/LaurentSeries/Cartier.html#LaurentSeries.sum_single_mul_contract_pow_card) and the rule that multiplication by a q-th power passes through them. A small cyclotomic lemma shows that, for odd n, the n-th and 2n-th cyclotomic levels give the same extension whenever 2 ≠ 0.

These are the definitions that Christol's theorem will need, but neither Christol nor Cobham has started. Higher reciprocity, the summit of Layer 1, has not started either, and nothing has landed yet in Layers 3–7.
