## StableHomotopyKTheory: 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-25 (`c8991e8` to `7433fc3`)

The first pieces of Layers 1 and 2 landed. The classifying space of a small category is now the realised nerve ([`classifyingSpace`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Category.html#TauCeti.CategoryTheory.classifyingSpace)), with vertices, edge paths that compose up to homotopy rel endpoints ([`edge_trans_homotopic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Category.html#TauCeti.CategoryTheory.classifyingSpace.edge_trans_homotopic)) and the resulting functor into the fundamental groupoid ([`toFundamentalGroupoid`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/ClassifyingSpace/Category.html#TauCeti.CategoryTheory.classifyingSpace.toFundamentalGroupoid)). On the simplicial side, a simplicial set is a nerve exactly when it is strict Segal ([`mem_essImage_nerveFunctor_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Nerve/EssImage.html#SSet.mem_essImage_nerveFunctor_iff)), and the nerve preserves filtered colimits ([`preservesFilteredColimitsOfSize`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Nerve/FilteredColimit.html#CategoryTheory.nerveFunctor.preservesFilteredColimitsOfSize)).

Homology of a category with coefficients in a functor ([`categoryHomology`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Nerve/Homology/Basic.html#TauCeti.categoryHomology)) arrived with the interface the roadmap asks for (TauCeti#41437): functoriality, the long exact sequence, normalised chains, H₀ as the colimit ([`categoryHomology₀NatIsoColim`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Nerve/Homology/Zero.html#TauCeti.categoryHomology₀NatIsoColim)) and vanishing above degree zero when there is a terminal object. The roadmap's warning example is checked too: for ℤ/2 with integer coefficients H₂ vanishes ([`categoryHomologyTwoSingleObjZModTwoIsoKernel`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialSet/Nerve/Homology/CyclicTwo.html#TauCeti.categoryHomologyTwoSingleObjZModTwoIsoKernel)) although H²(ℤ/2; ℤ) does not. Free corepresentable coefficients are acyclic and functor categories have enough projectives, the first steps toward identifying category homology with the derived functors of colim; that comparison is not yet stated.

In Layer 2, homotopy pullbacks ([`HomotopyPullback`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/HomotopyPullback/Basic.html#TauCeti.HomotopyPullback)) and homotopy-cartesian squares landed with symmetry, the loop-space check, and the theorem that a strict pullback along a Serre fibration is homotopy-cartesian ([`isHomotopyCartesian_of_right`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Homotopy/HomotopyPullback/Cartesian.html#CategoryTheory.IsPullback.isHomotopyCartesian_of_right)), built on the comparison of strict and homotopy fibres of a Serre fibration (TauCeti#41633). Separately, the core of a symmetric monoidal category is now symmetric monoidal ([`instSymmetricCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Core.html#CategoryTheory.Core.instSymmetricCategory)), opening Layer 4. Quillen's Theorems A and B are still ahead.
