## PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras: 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-20 (`dc2ed81` to `68a457e`)

This first window reaches the roadmap's first end theorem. Integral measures on a profinite group are now [identified with its completed group algebra](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/CompletedGroupAlgebra.html#AbstractMeasure.completedGroupAlgebraEquiv) as ℤ_p-algebras: the comparison map is [continuous from the weak topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/CompletedGroupAlgebra.html#AbstractMeasure.continuous_toCompletedGroupAlgebra), and surjectivity is TauCeti#18107. The homeomorphism half of the target and finite free base change of coefficients are not there yet. Its groundwork is the finite coordinates of a measure: a measure is [determined by its binary projections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/FiniteProjection.html#AbstractMeasure.finiteProjection_ext), finite projections are compatible with convolution, and locally constant functions descend through a [tower of finite quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/QuotientTower.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuotientTower). Underneath that, the clopen restriction, decomposition and topology theory of Layer 0 is complete, including the [non-compactness of the measure unit ball](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Dirac.html#TauCeti.AbstractMeasure.not_isCompact_measure_unitBall).

On ℤ_p, the Mahler–Amice calculus has begun. Multiplying a measure by x acts on its Amice transform as the [Mahler derivation](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Moments.html#AbstractMeasure.amiceTransform_weight_id) (1+T)d/dT, which gives the [moments as factorial coefficients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Moments.html#AbstractMeasure.ordinaryMoment_eq_factorial_coeff) of A_μ(e^T − 1). A measure supported on the units is [uniquely divisible by x](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/InverseWeight.html#AbstractMeasure.existsUnique_unitSupported_div_id), and the [Cartier operators](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Cartier.html#PowerSeries.cartier) on power series have arrived. There are no φ/ψ operators, root-of-unity averaging or bounded Amice norm yet.

On the algebraic side, [pseudo-measures](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/Pseudomeasure.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.pseudomeasures) now have an [evaluation that is unique and independent of the clearing element](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/Pseudomeasure.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.existsUnique_evalAt), though evaluation at characters of ℤ_pˣ is still missing. [Pseudo-null modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/PseudoNull.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.IsPseudoNull) are defined, with the criterion that over a two-dimensional noetherian local ring with finite residue field, such as O⟦T⟧, they are [exactly the finite modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/PseudoNull.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.isPseudoNull_iff_finite). Power series over a regular local ring are [regular local](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/PowerSeries.html#TauCeti.PowerSeries.isRegularLocalRing).

## PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-25 (`68a457e` to `e8ea0d1`)

The Weierstrass theory of Λ = O⟦T⟧ took a large step this window. Over a complete discrete valuation ring with uniformiser ϖ, the [height-one primes of O⟦T⟧](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/HeightOne.html#PowerSeries.height_eq_one_iff_of_isDiscreteValuationRing) are now classified: they are (ϖ) and the ideals (F) for F an irreducible distinguished polynomial. The [prime elements](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/HeightOne.html#PowerSeries.prime_iff_of_isDiscreteValuationRing) are classified the same way. On the quotient O⟦T⟧/(g), multiplication by T has [characteristic polynomial the distinguished factor of g](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/PowerSeries/Weierstrass/Charpoly.html#PowerSeries.charpoly_lmul_mk_X_eq_weierstrassDistinguished), and the quotient is free of rank its degree. On the cyclotomic side, ω_n = (1+T)^{pⁿ} − 1 [factors as ξ_0 ξ_1 ⋯ ξ_n](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/PrimePow.html#Polynomial.prod_range_cyclotomic_prime_pow_comp_X_add_one), and each ξ_m = Φ_{p^m}(1+T) is distinguished and [irreducible](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/PrimePow.html#Polynomial.irreducible_cyclotomic_prime_pow_comp_X_add_one) over ℤ_p.

The Iwasawa coordinate arrived as well (TauCeti#19183). The [level map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/LevelCoordinate.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.levelCoordinate) O⟦T⟧ → O[ℤ/pⁿ], sending T to [1] − 1, is surjective with [kernel (ω_n)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/LevelCoordinate.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.ker_levelCoordinate). It is unique even without a continuity assumption, and the level maps are compatible and [jointly injective](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/LevelCoordinate.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.iwasawaCoordinate_injective). Their continuity and the identification of the image with the compatible families, which would give O⟦T⟧ ≅ O⟦ℤ_p⟧, are still to come. The initial Fitting ideal now [annihilates its module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FittingIdeal/Determinant.html#TauCeti.fittingIdeal_zero_le_annihilator) and is [multiplicative on products](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FittingIdeal/Prod.html#TauCeti.fittingIdeal_prod_zero). Both rest on new determinant lemmas, among them Cramer's rule for endomorphisms.

In Layer 1, integral measures on a group with a tower of finite quotients are now [exactly the compatible families of their finite projections](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/TowerCoordinates.html#AbstractMeasure.towerCoordinatesEquiv). For ℤ_pˣ, this gives [measures from unit coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/TowerCoordinates.html#AbstractMeasure.existsUnique_finiteProjection_unitsToZModPow_eq). The weak-topology half of the unit coordinates (closed embedding, compactness) is not there yet.
