### Named results

- **The structure theorem for Iwasawa modules.** Over a complete discrete valuation ring O, every finitely generated O⟦T⟧-module is [pseudo-isomorphic to an elementary module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/Elementary.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.exists_isPseudoIsomorphism_elementary) Λʳ ⊕ ⨁ Λ/(ϖ^{m_i}) ⊕ ⨁ Λ/(F_j^{n_j}), with each F_j irreducible and distinguished.
- **The Iwasawa algebra is the completed group algebra of ℤ_p.** For a noetherian complete local O, the map T ↦ [1] − 1 gives an [isomorphism O⟦T⟧ ≅ O[[ℤ_p]]](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/CompletedCoordinate.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.completedCoordinateEquiv), which over ℤ_p is [the existing power-series coordinate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/CompletedCoordinate.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.completedCoordinateEquiv_eq_powerSeriesCoordinate).
- **Measures are the completed group algebra, topologically.** For a profinite group Γ, D(Γ, ℤ_p) and ℤ_p[[Γ]] are [isomorphic ℤ_p-algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/CompletedGroupAlgebra.html#AbstractMeasure.completedGroupAlgebraEquiv), and the comparison is [a homeomorphism from the weak topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/CompletedGroupAlgebra.html#AbstractMeasure.isHomeomorph_toCompletedGroupAlgebra_weak), so integral measures are weakly compact.
- **Unit measures are the kernel of ψ.** Integral measures on ℤ_pˣ are, through the Amice transform, [exactly the integral power series killed by ψ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Units/Basic.html#AbstractMeasure.unitsMeasureAmiceEquiv), [homeomorphically](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Units/Amice.html#AbstractMeasure.isHomeomorph_unitsMeasureAmiceEquiv_weak) for the weak and coefficientwise topologies.
- **Evaluating pseudo-measures at characters.** A [pseudo-measure on ℤ_pˣ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Units/Pseudomeasure.html#AbstractMeasure.unitsPseudomeasures) has a unique [value at every nontrivial continuous character](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Units/Pseudomeasure.html#ContinuousMonoidHom.unitCharacterEval), continuous in the character. These values [determine the pseudo-measure](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Units/Pseudomeasure.html#AbstractMeasure.unitCharacterEval_ext).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[The characteristic ideal](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/Char/Ideal.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.charIdeal)**, built from the [characteristic divisor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/Char/Divisor.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.charDivisor) of local lengths at height-one primes. It is [multiplicative in exact sequences](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/Char/Ideal.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.charIdeal_eq_mul_of_exact) and [invariant under pseudo-isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/Char/Ideal.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.IsPseudoIsomorphism.charIdeal_eq), which is what the structure theorem needs to read off invariants.
- **[Towers of finite quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/Group/Profinite/QuotientTower.html#TauCeti.FiniteQuotientTower).** Measures along a tower form a [closed embedding into the product of their coordinates](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Topology/TowerCoordinates.html#AbstractMeasure.isClosedEmbedding_towerCoordinates_weak). The same framework serves the measure comparison, the unit coordinates of ℤ_pˣ and the completed Iwasawa coordinate.
- **[Euler quotients](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Torsion/EulerQuotient.html#TauCeti.natCard_quotSMulTop_div_natCard_torsionBy_of_exact)** #(M/rM)/#M[r] are multiplicative in exact sequences and [invariant under pseudo-isomorphism](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/PseudoIsomorphism.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.IsPseudoIsomorphism.natCard_quotSMulTop_div_natCard_torsionBy_eq). This reduces the growth formula to elementary modules.
