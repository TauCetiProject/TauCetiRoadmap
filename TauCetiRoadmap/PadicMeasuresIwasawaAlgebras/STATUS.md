### Named results

- **The structure theorem for Iwasawa modules** — over a complete discrete valuation ring O, every finitely generated O⟦T⟧-module is [pseudo-isomorphic to an elementary module](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/Elementary.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.exists_isPseudoIsomorphism_elementary) Λʳ ⊕ ⨁ Λ/(ϖ^{m_i}) ⊕ ⨁ Λ/(F_j^{n_j}), with each F_j irreducible and distinguished.
- **The characteristic ideal from μ and F_M** — for a torsion module, the [characteristic ideal is (ϖ^μ F_M)](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/LambdaInvariant.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.charIdeal_eq_span_C_pow_mul_charPoly), where F_M is the product of the distinguished generators of the horizontal height-one primes, with multiplicity.
- **The Euler quotient at finite level** — when M/ω_nM is finite, [#(M/ω_nM)/#M[ω_n] = q^{μpⁿ}·#(O/Res(F_M, ω_n))](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/EulerQuotient.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.natCard_quotSMulTop_one_add_X_pow_sub_one_div_natCard_torsionBy), for any complete discrete valuation ring with finite residue field. This is an exact identity at each level, the finite-level shadow of the growth formula.
- **Measures are the completed group algebra** — for a profinite group Γ, D(Γ, ℤ_p) and ℤ_p[[Γ]] are [isomorphic ℤ_p-algebras](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/CompletedGroupAlgebra.html#AbstractMeasure.completedGroupAlgebraEquiv), by [a homeomorphism from the weak topology](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/CompletedGroupAlgebra.html#AbstractMeasure.isHomeomorph_toCompletedGroupAlgebra_weak).
- **Unit measures are the kernel of ψ** — through the Amice transform, integral measures on ℤ_pˣ are [exactly the integral power series killed by ψ](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/NumberTheory/Padics/Measure/Units/Basic.html#AbstractMeasure.unitsMeasureAmiceEquiv), homeomorphically.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **[The λ-invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/LambdaInvariant.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.lambdaInvariant) and [characteristic polynomial](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/LambdaInvariant.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.charPoly)**, beside the [μ-invariant](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/MuInvariant.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.muInvariant). All three are defined intrinsically from the characteristic divisor, are additive in exact sequences and are read off any elementary module, so the growth formula can be stated and proved block by block.
- **[The Iwasawa coordinate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Iwasawa/CompletedCoordinate.html#TauCeti.Iwasawa.completedCoordinateEquiv) O⟦T⟧ ≅ O[[ℤ_p]]**, sending T to [1] − 1. It connects the module theory of Layer 4 with the measures of Layers 1–3.
- **[Quadratic presentations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Module/Presentation/Quadratic.html#TauCeti.QuadraticPresentation)**, now closed under extensions, with Fitting ideals [multiplicative in extensions](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/FittingIdeal/Extension.html#TauCeti.fittingIdeal_eq_mul_of_exact) by Dasgupta–Kakde's Lemmas 2.6 and 2.7. They are the working tool for Layer 6.
