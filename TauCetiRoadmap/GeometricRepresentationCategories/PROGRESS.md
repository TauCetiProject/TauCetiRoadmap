## GeometricRepresentationCategories: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`3bc1373` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's first layer, R1 on categorical group actions, landed in a single sweep. The central construction turns a monoid action on a category into a [pseudofunctor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Pseudofunctor.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.pseudofunctor) out of the one-object bicategory. The [round-trip theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Pseudofunctor.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.monoidalLeftAction_pseudofunctor) shows that going back recovers the original action, associator and unitor included, and not just its action functors. This is the comparison that Mathlib's action file lists as a TODO (TauCeti#18648). On top of it, [equivariant functors are exactly the strong transformations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/StrongTrans.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.strongTransEquiv) between the associated pseudofunctors, and [equivariant natural transformations are exactly the modifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Modification.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.modificationEquiv) between those, compatibly with identities and composites.

The rest of the window filled in what R1 asks of these interfaces. An action [restricts to the full subcategory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/FullSubcategory.html#CategoryTheory.ObjectProperty.fullSubcategoryMonoidalLeftAction) of any stable object property, and restricting in two steps agrees with restricting once. Linearity and additivity carry over, and exact actions stay exact on full subcategories that contain zero and are closed under finite products and kernels or cokernels. Equivariant functors compose, and the inverse of an equivariant equivalence is equivariant again, by way of [right adjoints of oplax left-linear functors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/LinearFunctor.html#CategoryTheory.Adjunction.rightAdjointLaxLeftLinear). The source's [objectwise congruence-invariance predicate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Congruence.html#TauCeti.IsObjectwiseCongruenceInvariant) is defined, and it is [invariant under equivariant equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Congruence.html#TauCeti.isObjectwiseCongruenceInvariant_iff_of_equivalence) and under restriction.

All of this is general category theory. Nothing yet touches the affine Lie-algebra module categories, critical characters or groupoid transport of R2–R5.

## GeometricRepresentationCategories: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-26 (`c1c6773` to `93d1fe5`)

This window carried the roadmap past its first layer. R1 closed its open gap: left actions of a discrete monoid on categories and pseudofunctors out of the one-object bicategory now [correspond bijectively](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Pseudofunctor.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.pseudofunctorEquiv), and the [reverse round trip](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Pseudofunctor.html#CategoryTheory.Pseudofunctor.pseudofunctor_monoidalLeftAction) recovers a pseudofunctor's unit and composition comparisons, not just its functors. For R2, [twisting modules by ring automorphisms](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Twist.html#MonoidHom.restrictScalarsMonoidalLeftAction) becomes a coherent action on `ModuleCat A` whose unit and [product comparisons](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Twist.html#MonoidHom.actionLeftTensorIso_restrictScalarsMonoidalLeftAction) are exactly Mathlib's restriction-of-scalars isomorphisms, as the README pins. It restricts to discrete modules and to [smooth modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Twist.html#MonoidHom.isStableUnderLeftAction_smoothModules) for a cofinal family of left ideals. The supplied central-character fibers and rank-one actions are not attached yet.

For R4, the README's example of why coherence has content landed. With identity transport functors and central compositors, [associativity is exactly the 2-cocycle identity](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Bicategory/Functor/CentralCocycle.html#TauCeti.centralUnitIso_associativity_iff), and changing the trivialisations [multiplies the cocycle by a coboundary](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Bicategory/Functor/CentralCocycle.html#TauCeti.centralUnitIso_naturality_comp_iff). The coherent equivariant lift type itself also appeared, together with its pullback along a functor of parameter categories. It comes with transport functors on the prescribed fibers, their mixed coherence with the `H`-actions, and the isotropy action of each endomorphism monoid. Its declarations are not in this window's extracted list, so they go unlinked here.

On the infinitesimal side (R5.2), the [self-extension](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/DerivationExtension.html#TauCeti.DerivationExtension) `E_δ(M) = M ⊕ M` of a module along a derivation is built. It is [short exact among discrete modules](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/DerivationExtension.html#TauCeti.DerivationExtension.shortExact_discreteShortComplex) when δ is continuous, and changing δ by an inner derivation `ad b` gives the [natural comparison](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/DerivationExtension.html#TauCeti.DerivationExtension.innerEquiv) `(u, v) ↦ (u + b·v, v)` (TauCeti#20289). Its Ext class and the outer-derivation map θ are still to come.
