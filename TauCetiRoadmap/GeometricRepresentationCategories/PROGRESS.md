## GeometricRepresentationCategories: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (`3bc1373` to `c1c6773`)

The roadmap's first layer, R1 on categorical group actions, landed in a single sweep. The central construction turns a monoid action on a category into a [pseudofunctor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Pseudofunctor.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.pseudofunctor) out of the one-object bicategory. The [round-trip theorem](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Pseudofunctor.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.monoidalLeftAction_pseudofunctor) shows that going back recovers the original action, associator and unitor included, and not just its action functors. This is the comparison that Mathlib's action file lists as a TODO (TauCeti#18648). On top of it, [equivariant functors are exactly the strong transformations](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/StrongTrans.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.strongTransEquiv) between the associated pseudofunctors, and [equivariant natural transformations are exactly the modifications](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Modification.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.modificationEquiv) between those, compatibly with identities and composites.

The rest of the window filled in what R1 asks of these interfaces. An action [restricts to the full subcategory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/FullSubcategory.html#CategoryTheory.ObjectProperty.fullSubcategoryMonoidalLeftAction) of any stable object property, and restricting in two steps agrees with restricting once. Linearity and additivity carry over, and exact actions stay exact on full subcategories that contain zero and are closed under finite products and kernels or cokernels. Equivariant functors compose, and the inverse of an equivariant equivalence is equivariant again, by way of [right adjoints of oplax left-linear functors](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/LinearFunctor.html#CategoryTheory.Adjunction.rightAdjointLaxLeftLinear). The source's [objectwise congruence-invariance predicate](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Congruence.html#TauCeti.IsObjectwiseCongruenceInvariant) is defined, and it is [invariant under equivariant equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Congruence.html#TauCeti.isObjectwiseCongruenceInvariant_iff_of_equivalence) and under restriction.

All of this is general category theory. Nothing yet touches the affine Lie-algebra module categories, critical characters or groupoid transport of R2–R5.
