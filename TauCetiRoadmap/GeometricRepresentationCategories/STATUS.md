## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** R1, the generic layer of categorical actions, is done. R2, R4 and R5 have most of their generic content: twist actions on character fibers, the bicategory of lifts with normalization and reconstruction, and the map from continuous outer derivations to Ext¹. Everything that depends on the actual critical family is missing: R3 has not begun in substance, and with it the critical conjecture, θ, descent to local systems and the relative families.

### Named results

- **Outer derivations give self-extension classes** — sending a continuous derivation δ to the class of `E_δ(M)` defines a [center-linear map](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/DerivationExtension/Outer.html#TauCeti.DerivationExtension.outerExtClass) `OutDer_cont(A) → Ext¹(M, M)` in discrete modules, [natural in M](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/DerivationExtension/Outer.html#TauCeti.DerivationExtension.outerExtClass_naturality) in the README's pushforward-equals-pullback form.
- **The Baer sum of self-extensions** — [the Baer sum of `E_δ(M)` and `E_η(M)` is `E_(δ+η)(M)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/DerivationExtension/BaerSum.html#TauCeti.DerivationExtension.extClass_baerSum), resting on a [general identification](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Ext/BaerSum.html#CategoryTheory.ShortComplex.ShortExact.extClass_baerSum) of pullback/pushout Baer sum with addition of Ext classes in any abelian category with `HasExt`.
- **Actions are pseudofunctors** — left actions of a discrete monoid on categories [correspond bijectively](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Pseudofunctor.html#CategoryTheory.MonoidalCategory.MonoidalLeftAction.pseudofunctorEquiv) to pseudofunctors `LocallyDiscrete (SingleObj M) ⥤ᵖ Cat`, with both round trips recovering the coherence cells.
- **Coherence is a 2-cocycle condition** — for identity transport functors with central compositors, [associativity holds exactly when the compositors form a 2-cocycle](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Bicategory/Functor/CentralCocycle.html#TauCeti.centralUnitIso_associativity_iff), and changing trivialisations acts by a coboundary.
- **Reindexing along an equivalence is a biequivalence** — an equivalence `Γ ≌ Λ` [induces a biequivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Bicategory/FunctorBicategory/Biequivalence.html#CategoryTheory.Pseudofunctor.precompBiequivalence) between pseudofunctor families indexed by `Γ` and by `Λ`, the generic core of R5.1's descent.

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The bicategory of lifts.** Coherent equivariant lifts form a [bicategory](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Bicategory/Functor/EquivariantGroupoidLift/Bicategory.html#TauCeti.EquivariantGroupoidLift.instBicategory) whose hom categories are groupoids. [Normalization](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Bicategory/Functor/EquivariantGroupoidLift/NormalizedTransport/Functor.html#TauCeti.EquivariantGroupoidLift.normalizationPseudofunctor) and [reconstruction](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Bicategory/Functor/EquivariantGroupoidLift/NormalizedTransport/Reconstruction.html#TauCeti.EquivariantGroupoidLift.reconstructionPseudofunctor) pseudofunctors connect it to transport data on the actual fibers. This is the type in which R4's conjecture will be stated.
- **The character-fiber family.** Twists between strict discrete character fibers form a [pseudofunctor over the action category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/CentralCharacter/ActionCategory.html#TauCeti.discreteCentralCharacterFamily), with cells pinned to Mathlib's restriction-of-scalars isomorphisms. At a fixed point it [restricts to the coherent fiber action](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/CentralCharacter/ActionCategory.html#TauCeti.monoidalLeftAction_comp_discreteCentralCharacterFamily). This is the shape R3's `H⋊S` family needs.
- **Continuous outer derivations.** [`OutDer_cont(A)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Derivation/Continuous.html#TauCeti.ContinuousOuterDerivation) is the quotient by the actual inner image (not its closure), with quotient topology, a center-module structure, the Lie–Rinehart identity and a continuous anchor to derivations of the center. It is the target of θ.

### Roadmap coverage

**R1** is done; transported actions are now [linear](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Action/Transport.html#CategoryTheory.Equivalence.linear_actionLeft_transport), closing the last gap. **R2** is done at the generic level: fiber actions, twist functors between fibers, equivariant inclusions, iterated restrictions and central-quotient presentations. Not established here: the rank-one fiber actions with their pinned `rankOneTwistUnit` and `rankOneTwistComp` cells, and consumption of the exact A5/A8 carriers. **R3** is untouched apart from generic semidirect-product lemmas for action categories. **R4** is partial: the lift bicategory, normalization, reconstruction, the counit equivalence, the trivial-action comparison, the linear refinement and the cocycle obstruction are in place; the unit equivalence and assembled biequivalence were not seen in the extracted declarations (which were truncated for a few pull requests), and the critical conjecture awaits R3. **R5.1** has the generic reindexing biequivalence only. **R5.2** has `OutDer_cont`, self-extensions, their Ext classes, Baer sums and the dual-number character, but not θ, `D_ξ` or the lift `s̃`. **R5.3** has generic coefficient base change, scalar extension of derivations, first-order automorphisms and their [commutator formula](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Lie/Derivation/FirstOrder.html#TauCeti.derivationLieAlgebra.commutatorElement_firstOrderAlgEquiv), but no `Art_ℂ`, `H_R`, `C_R(p)` or relative lift type.

## The frontier

- **The lift biequivalence (R4)** — supply the unit equivalence, normalization followed by reconstruction back to the identity, and assemble the `TauCeti.Biequivalence` with the existing counit.
- **The map θ (R5.2)** — construct `θ : Ω¹_cont(Z) → OutDer_cont(A)` through the representatives `D_ξ` and the ordered lift `s̃`, then compose with the existing Ext-class map. This needs AffineLieAlgebras A6's deformation and sections, FormalConnectionsOpers F5's chart and A8's central generators.
- **Descent for lift packages (R5.1)** — extend the reindexing biequivalence to products with `SingleObj H` and prescribed fiber actions, then apply it to `IsomOp → Loc`, whose essential surjectivity is F6's oper-lift theorem.
- **The critical family (R3)** — build `Ccrit(p)` from A8's central characters and F5's evaluation, its `H` action, and the `H⋊S` pseudofunctor on the action category; R2's rank-one fiber actions belong here too.
- **The relative family (R5.3)** — finite-Artinian coefficient rings, `H_R`, the fibers `C_R(p)` with their base-change functors, and the relative lift type, building on the base-change pseudofunctor already in place.
