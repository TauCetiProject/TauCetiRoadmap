## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done. Happel's theorem is proved, and three Frobenius models are triangulated through it: `HMF(S,w)`, curved duplexes, and complexes with split conflations. Layers 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 are partial, and the flagship `k[x]/(x^n)` example is mostly done. Singularity categories, a triangulation on `K_n`, `D_n`, and triangulated curved modules are missing. Layer 4 has not begun.

### Named results

- **Happel's theorem** — the stable category of a Frobenius exact category is triangulated, with the shift generated by stable suspension and the triangles coming from conflations ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated)).
- **`HMF(S,w)` is triangulated** — finite-projective matrix factorizations with componentwise split conflations form a Frobenius category whose stable category is `HMF(S,w)`, and its shift by one is the parity shift ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated)).
- **The homotopy category of curved duplexes is triangulated** — this holds for any curvature, by the same Frobenius route ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated)).
- **The indecomposables over `k[x]/(x^n)`** — over any field, the finitely generated indecomposable right modules over `A = k[x]/(x^n)` are exactly the cyclic modules `M_i = A/(x^i)` for `1 ≤ i ≤ n`, up to isomorphism ([`TauCeti.isIndecomposableModule_iff_exists_linearEquiv_quotient_span_op_root_X_pow_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Truncated/Module.html#TauCeti.isIndecomposableModule_iff_exists_linearEquiv_quotient_span_op_root_X_pow_pow)).
- **Syzygies and stable Homs over `k[x]/(x^n)`** — `ΩM_i ≅ M_(n-i)`, so every `M_i` is stably two-periodic ([`TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleLoopCyclicModuleRootPowIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable/Syzygy.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleLoopCyclicModuleRootPowIso)), and the stable Hom space `M_i → M_j` has dimension `min(i, j, n-i, n-j)` ([`TauCeti.FGModuleCat.finrank_stableModuleHom_cyclicModule_root_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable/CyclicHom.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.finrank_stableModuleHom_cyclicModule_root_pow)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Morphism-ideal quotients** — one two-sided ideal construction underlies the stable category, the duplex homotopy category and `HMF(S,w)` ([`TauCeti.MorphismIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/MorphismIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.MorphismIdeal)).
- **Complexes as a Frobenius category** — for any shape in which every index has a successor and a predecessor, including `ZMod n`-periodic shapes, the contractible complexes are the projective-injectives and the stable category is the homotopy category ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.homologicalComplex_split_isFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/HomologicalComplex/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.homologicalComplex_split_isFrobenius)). For periodic complexes, stable suspension is the signed cyclic shift and the usual mapping-cone triangle is distinguished in the stable category ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.mappingCone_distinguished`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Connecting.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.mappingCone_distinguished)).
- **The DG category of curved modules** — right curved modules form a DG category, and its homotopy category identifies maps that differ by the boundary of an odd homotopy ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.instDGCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/DGCategory.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.instDGCategory)).

## The frontier

- **Install the triangulation on `K_n`** — the mapping-cone triangles are now known to be distinguished in the stable category. What remains is to transport Happel's triangulation to Mathlib's periodic homotopy category as instances, and then repeat for differential modules. `D_n` and all of Layer 4 wait on this.
- **Finish `k[x]/(x^n)`** — the indecomposables, syzygies and stable Hom dimensions are in. Still missing: the alternating minimal free resolution, and the link between `S --x^i--> S --x^(n-i)--> S` in `HMF` and `M_i`.
- **Shifts and cones of curved modules** — the curved homotopy category still has no triangulation. Layer 6's acyclic classes and derived categories of the second kind depend on it.
- **Triangle functors in Layer 8** — now that `HMF(S,w)` and duplexes are both triangulated, the inclusion of `HMF` into duplexes and base change to `S/(w)` can be shown to be triangle functors.
- **Gorenstein-projectives as a Frobenius category** — prove that the definition does not depend on the complete resolution, state it for right modules, and build `Gproj-R`. `D_sg` and Buchweitz's theorem come after.
