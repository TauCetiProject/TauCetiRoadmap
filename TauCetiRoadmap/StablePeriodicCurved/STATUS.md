## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done. Every Frobenius model the roadmap names now has a triangulated homotopy category: periodic complexes, curved duplexes, `HMF(S,w)` and curved modules. `D_n` exists. Layers 1–8 are all partial. Layer 4 has started, and the flagship `k[x]/(x^n)` calculation is done up to the hypersurface comparisons. Missing: singularity categories, the derived categories of the second kind, and every named comparison theorem (Buchweitz, Keller–Vossieck, Stai, Eisenbud, Orlov).

### Named results

- **Happel's theorem** — the stable category of a Frobenius exact category is triangulated, with the shift generated by stable suspension and the triangles coming from conflations ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated)).
- **The periodic homotopy category is triangulated by cones** — `K_n` is triangulated with the signed cyclic shift, and a triangle is distinguished exactly when it is isomorphic to a mapping-cone triangle ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.mem_distTriang_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.mem_distTriang_iff)).
- **`HMF(S,w)` is triangulated** — finite-projective matrix factorizations form a Frobenius category whose stable category is `HMF(S,w)`, and the shift by one is the parity shift ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated)).
- **The curved homotopy category is triangulated** — curved right modules have no homology, but their homotopy category is triangulated ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.homotopyIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.homotopyIsTriangulated)).
- **The indecomposables over `k[x]/(x^n)`** — over any field, the finitely generated indecomposables are exactly the `M_i = A/(x^i)` ([`TauCeti.isIndecomposableModule_iff_exists_linearEquiv_quotient_span_op_root_X_pow_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Truncated/Module.html#TauCeti.isIndecomposableModule_iff_exists_linearEquiv_quotient_span_op_root_X_pow_pow)). They are stably two-periodic, with stable Hom dimension `min(i, j, n-i, n-j)`, and each has a [free resolution alternating `x^i` and `x^(n-i)`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Projective/CyclicResolution.html#TauCeti.cyclicModuleRootPowProjectiveResolution).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The orbit category `D/[n]`** — built from the direct-sum Hom groups, with Keller's descent and uniqueness ([`TauCeti.OrbitCategory.lift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Shift/OrbitCategory.html#TauCeti.OrbitCategory.lift)).
- **Compression `Δ_n`** — it folds bounded complexes into `n`-periodic ones and is a triangle functor `D^b → D_n` ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.instIsTriangulatedBoundedDerivedCategoryDerivedCompressionFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Compression/Derived.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.instIsTriangulatedBoundedDerivedCategoryDerivedCompressionFunctor)).
- **Acyclicity of the second kind** — absolutely acyclic, coacyclic and contraacyclic curved modules are defined as closures of totalizations inside the triangulated curved homotopy category ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.absolutelyAcyclic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/Acyclic.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.absolutelyAcyclic)).

## The frontier

- **Stai's orbit embedding** — the orbit functor `D^b(mod-Λ)/[n] → D_n(mod-Λ)` exists. What remains is full faithfulness, the essential image and Theorem 4.3. These need finite global dimension and `D_n ≃ K_n(proj-Λ)` through Proposition 3.10.
- **Derived categories of the second kind** — prove that absolutely acyclic objects are contraacyclic (the countable-product thickness lemma is in), then form `D_abs`, `D_co` and `D_ctr` with their comparison functors.
- **Gorenstein-projectives and singularities** — show that the stable-cycles functor is an equivalence, using the complete-resolution quasi-inverse. Then define `D_sg` and prove Buchweitz's theorem.
- **The cokernel comparison** — upgrade the stable cokernel functor `HMF(S,w) → stmod(S/(w))` to a triangle functor and land it in stable MCM modules. Eisenbud's theorem and the end of the flagship example wait on this.
- **Idempotent completion and `K₁`** — the remaining Layer 1 compatibility, and the triangulated identification of the differential-module stable category with `K₁`.
