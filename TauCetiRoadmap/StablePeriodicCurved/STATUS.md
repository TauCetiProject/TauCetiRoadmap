## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done. Happel's theorem is proved, and three Frobenius models are now triangulated through it: `HMF(S,w)`, curved duplexes, and complexes with split conflations. Layers 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 are partial. Singularity categories, `D_n`, and anything triangulated for curved modules are still missing. Layer 4 has not begun.

### Named results

- **Happel's theorem** — the stable category of a Frobenius exact category, with the shift generated by stable suspension and the triangles coming from conflations, is triangulated ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated)). Exact functors that preserve projective-injectives induce triangle functors ([`TauCeti.StableConflationExact.stableFunctorIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Functor/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.StableConflationExact.stableFunctorIsTriangulated)).
- **`HMF(S,w)` is triangulated** — finite-projective matrix factorizations with componentwise split conflations form a Frobenius category whose stable category is `HMF(S,w)`. Its shift by one is the parity shift ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated)).
- **The homotopy category of curved duplexes is triangulated** — this is proved for any curvature, by the same Frobenius route ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated)).
- **Syzygies over `k[x]/(x^n)`** — for `M_i = A/(x^i)`, `ΩM_i ≅ M_(n-i)`, so every `M_i` is stably two-periodic ([`TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleLoopCyclicModuleRootPowIso`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable/Syzygy.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleLoopCyclicModuleRootPowIso)). Over any field, the stable Hom space `M_i → M_j` has dimension `min(i, j, n-i, n-j)` ([`TauCeti.FGModuleCat.finrank_stableModuleHom_cyclicModule_root_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable/CyclicHom.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.finrank_stableModuleHom_cyclicModule_root_pow)).
- **The curved opposite** — the Koszul-signed graded opposite of a curved DG algebra has curvature `-op(w)`. Left curved modules are exactly right curved modules over it ([`TauCeti.isCurvedDGLeftModule_iff_gradedOppositeRight`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Opposite.html#TauCeti.isCurvedDGLeftModule_iff_gradedOppositeRight)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Morphism-ideal quotients** — one two-sided ideal construction underlies the stable category, the duplex homotopy category and `HMF(S,w)` ([`TauCeti.MorphismIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/MorphismIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.MorphismIdeal)). Identifying a stable ideal with null-homotopy is what turns each Frobenius statement into a triangulation.
- **Complexes as a Frobenius category** — for any shape in which every index has a successor and a predecessor, including `ZMod n`-periodic shapes, the contractible complexes are the projective-injectives ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.homologicalComplex_split_isFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/HomologicalComplex/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.homologicalComplex_split_isFrobenius)). The stable category is the homotopy category, which gives the route to triangulating `K_n`.
- **The DG category of curved modules** — right curved modules form a DG category, and its homotopy category identifies maps that differ by the boundary of an odd homotopy ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.instDGCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/DGCategory.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.instDGCategory)).

## The frontier

- **Finish `k[x]/(x^n)`** — prove that `M_1, …, M_n` are the indecomposables, and give the alternating minimal free resolution. Then connect `S --x^i--> S --x^(n-i)--> S` in `HMF` to `M_i`.
- **Triangulate `K_n` and differential modules** — transport Happel's triangulation along the stable ≃ homotopy equivalence to Mathlib's periodic homotopy category, check that it agrees with the cone triangles, and repeat for differential modules. `D_n` and all of Layer 4 wait on this.
- **Shifts and cones of curved modules** — the curved homotopy category still has no triangulation. Layer 6's acyclic classes and derived categories of the second kind depend on it.
- **Triangle functors in Layer 8** — with `HMF(S,w)` and duplexes both triangulated, the inclusion of `HMF` into duplexes and base change to `S/(w)` can now be shown to be triangle functors.
- **Gorenstein-projectives as a Frobenius category** — prove independence of the complete resolution, for right modules, and build `Gproj-R` ahead of `D_sg` and Buchweitz's theorem.
