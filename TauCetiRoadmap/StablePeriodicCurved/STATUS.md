### Named results

- **Buchweitz's theorem** — over an Iwanaga–Gorenstein ring, the stable category of finitely generated Gorenstein-projective modules is equivalent to the singularity category, via Chen's functor, which is a triangle functor ([`TauCeti.IsIwanagaGorenstein.isEquivalence_toSingularityCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/GorensteinProjective/Buchweitz.html#TauCeti.IsIwanagaGorenstein.isEquivalence_toSingularityCategory)). For a self-injective algebra it gives `stmod-A ≃ D_sg(A)`.
- **The Keller–Vossieck theorem** — for any Frobenius exact category `E`, placing objects in degree zero gives an equivalence from `stable(E)` to `D^b(E)/K^b(proj E)`, with no idempotent completeness assumed ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.isEquivalence_stableToSingularity`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/DerivedCategory/Singularity/KellerVossieck.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.isEquivalence_stableToSingularity)).
- **Orlov's affine Landau–Ginzburg theorem** — for a regular finite-type algebra `S` over a field and a flat potential `w`, the cokernel functor `HMF(S,w) → D_sg(S/(w))` is an equivalence ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.quotientSingularityCokernel_isEquivalence_of_flat_aeval`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Cokernel/Orlov.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.quotientSingularityCokernel_isEquivalence_of_flat_aeval)). It is a special case of a triangulated equivalence valid whenever `S` has finite global dimension, which also covers Eisenbud's regular local setting.
- **Positselski's Theorem 7.8(a)** — under a uniform bound on graded projective dimension, the absolutely acyclic, coacyclic and contraacyclic curved modules coincide, so the three derived categories of the second kind agree ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.AbsoluteDerivedCategory.isEquivalence_toCoderived_of_hasProjectiveDimensionLT`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/GlobalDimension.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.AbsoluteDerivedCategory.isEquivalence_toCoderived_of_hasProjectiveDimensionLT)).
- **Stai's triangulated hull** — in finite global dimension, the image of the orbit functor `D^b/[n] → D_n` generates `D_n` under shifts and cones alone, and the orbit functor is fully faithful ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.eq_top_of_essImage_orbitDerivedCompressionFunctor_le`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Compression/Generation.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.eq_top_of_essImage_orbitDerivedCompressionFunctor_le)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The singularity category of an exact category** — `D^b(E)/K^b(proj E)`, built on bounded and bounded-below derived categories of exact categories with `K⁺(inj E) ≃ D⁺(E)`; this is what lets Keller–Vossieck be stated without an abelian ambient ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.SingularityCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/DerivedCategory/Singularity/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.SingularityCategory)).
- **Derived categories of the second kind** — the absolute, co- and contraderived Verdier quotients of the curved homotopy category, with comparison triangle functors ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.AbsoluteDerivedCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/SecondKind.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.AbsoluteDerivedCategory)).
- **The free curved module functor** — left adjoint to forgetting the differential, it supplies contractible graded-projective covers, the key input for Theorem 7.8(a) and for the graded-model theorems still ahead ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.freeFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/Graded/Free.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.freeFunctor)).

## The frontier

- **Keller–Vossieck as a triangle equivalence** — give the functor `stable(E) → D_sg(E)` its shift compatibility and prove it triangulated, then add the idempotent-completion compatibility theorem the roadmap asks for.
- **Positselski's graded models** — upgrade the bijectivity of localization on graded-injective and graded-projective modules to the Theorem 7.9 equivalences under conditions `(*)` and `(**)`, and prove the DG-case coincidences of Theorem 7.8(b).
- **Eisenbud's MCM statements** — identify finitely generated Gorenstein-projectives over the hypersurface with MCM modules, and prove eventual two-periodicity of minimal resolutions as in Theorem 6.1. Periodic resolutions and the two-periodic resolution of a factorization's cokernel are already available.
- **Stai's essential image and Proposition 3.10** — identify the image of the orbit functor with the gradable objects, and relatively projective differential modules with homotopically projective ones.
- **Reconciliation** — totalizations of periodic complexes, the right-to-left comparison over the opposite algebra, and the commuting comparison diagrams of Layer 8.
