## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done, and Layer 1's headline, Happel's theorem that the stable category of a Frobenius exact category is triangulated, is proved. The rest of Layer 1 and Layers 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8 are partial: matrix factorizations have most of their additive structure but no triangulation. Layers 4 and 6 have not begun.

### Named results

- **Happel's theorem** — the stable category of a Frobenius exact category, with the shift generated by stable suspension and the triangles generated by conflations, is triangulated ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated)). The pretriangulated structure is a definition to install locally, not a global instance.
- **The module-category Frobenius theorem** — for a finite-dimensional algebra over a field that is self-injective on both sides, the finite-dimensional modules form a Frobenius exact category ([`FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Frobenius.html#FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius)).
- **Frobenius algebras are self-injective** — a finite-dimensional algebra with a Frobenius functional is injective over itself on the left ([`LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_self)) and on the right.
- **Duality of matrix factorizations** — transposition, with the roadmap's pinned sign, is a contravariant additive functor from factorizations of `w` to factorizations of `-w` ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.dualFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Dual.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.dualFunctor)), and each factorization is isomorphic to its double dual.
- **Two-periodic complexes are curved duplexes of curvature zero** — this equivalence also holds after passing to homotopy categories ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Duplex.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Morphism-ideal quotients** — the stable category, the duplex homotopy category and `HMF(S,w)` are all quotients by a two-sided ideal ([`TauCeti.MorphismIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/MorphismIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.MorphismIdeal)). Each one therefore inherits its linear structure, universal property and descent of functors from a single construction.
- **Disk factorization of null-homotopies** — a map of matrix factorizations is null-homotopic exactly when it factors through the disk sum on its source ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.mem_nullHomotopic_iff_factors_diskSum`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/DiskFactorization.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.mem_nullHomotopic_iff_factors_diskSum)), and contractible factorizations are retracts of disk sums. This is the input for identifying projective-injectives with contractibles in the Frobenius structure.
- **Matrix factorizations inside curved duplexes** — the homotopy category `HMF(S,w)` embeds fully faithfully in the homotopy category of all curved duplexes of finitely generated modules ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.homotopyInclusion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/HomotopyInclusion.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.homotopyInclusion)).

## The frontier

- **Triangulated `HMF(S,w)` and duplex homotopy categories** — cones, parity-shift compatibility and finite biproducts exist ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.cone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Cone.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.cone)). What remains is a ℤ-shift on the homotopy category, distinguished triangles and the axioms. Alternatively, the componentwise split Frobenius structure plus Happel's theorem would give the triangulation directly.
- **Happel's triangle functors and closure properties** — show that the descended exact functors are triangle functors, and treat sums, opposites, idempotent completion and extension-closed subcategories.
- **The `k[x]/(x^n)` calculation** — triangulated `stmod-A` is now within reach. The indecomposables `M_i`, the syzygies `ΩM_i ≅ M_(n-i)`, the stable Hom dimensions and the identification of the base-changed factorization's cokernel with `M_i` remain.
- **Differential modules and `K_n(A)`** — no differential-module type exists yet. Layer 3 needs one, along with periodic cones and the split Frobenius structure, before periodic homology, `D_n(A)` and Layer 4's compression.
- **Curved DG algebras and modules** — the right-convention algebra with non-central curvature, the opposite with curvature `-op(w)`, and curved modules with their homotopy category. Layer 6 cannot start before them.
