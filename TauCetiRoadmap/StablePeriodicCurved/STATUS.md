## The frontier

- **Buchweitz's theorem** — show that Chen's functor is essentially surjective over an Iwanaga–Gorenstein ring. The pieces are in place: every object of `D_sg` is a shifted module with vanishing `Ext` against modules of bounded injective dimension, and over such a ring those modules are exactly the Gorenstein-projectives. The self-injective case `stmod-A ≃ D_sg(A)` follows.
- **Eisenbud's comparison** — over a regular local ring, finitely generated Gorenstein-projectives on the hypersurface have projective dimension at most one over `S` ([`hasProjectiveDimensionLE_restrictScalars_quotient_of_isRegularLocalRing`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/GorensteinProjective/Hypersurface.html#TauCeti.IsGorensteinProjective.hasProjectiveDimensionLE_restrictScalars_quotient_of_isRegularLocalRing)). With the essential-image criterion, this should make the cokernel embedding an equivalence. What remains after that is to identify these modules with MCM modules and to prove eventual two-periodicity.
- **Positselski's models** — upgrade the graded-injective and graded-projective bijectivity results to equivalences under conditions `(*)` and `(**)`, and prove the Theorem 7.8 coincidences under finite graded global dimension.
- **Stai's essential image and Proposition 3.10** — identify the image of the orbit functor with the gradable objects, and relatively projective differential modules with homotopically projective ones.
- **Keller–Vossieck and Orlov** — neither has started.
