## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done: morphism-ideal quotients, Frobenius exact categories and their stable categories, Frobenius functionals and the module-category Frobenius theorem. Layer 1 has the suspension–loop autoequivalence, the shift and Happel's standard triangles but not the triangulated axioms; Layers 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8 have first pieces, and Layers 4 and 6 have not begun.

### Named results

- **The module-category Frobenius theorem** — for a finite-dimensional algebra over a field that is self-injective on both sides, the finite-dimensional modules form a Frobenius exact category ([`FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Frobenius.html#FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius)), so projective and injective modules coincide.
- **Happel's suspension–loop equivalence** — in the stable category of a Frobenius exact category, stable suspension and stable loops are quasi-inverse additive autoequivalences ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableSuspensionEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Autoequivalence.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableSuspensionEquivalence)).
- **Frobenius algebras are self-injective** — a finite-dimensional algebra with a Frobenius functional is injective over itself on the left ([`LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_self)) and on the right ([`LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_op_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_op_self)).
- **Truncated polynomial algebras are symmetric Frobenius** — for `n > 0` the coefficient of `x^(n-1)` is a symmetric Frobenius functional on `R[X]/(X^n)` over any commutative ring `R` ([`AdjoinRoot.isSymmetricFrobeniusFunctional_lastCoeff_X_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/AdjoinRoot.html#AdjoinRoot.isSymmetricFrobeniusFunctional_lastCoeff_X_pow)).
- **Two-periodic complexes are curved duplexes of curvature zero** — duplexes with `w = 0` are equivalent to Mathlib's `ZMod 2`-graded complexes, also after passing to homotopy categories ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Duplex.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Morphism-ideal quotients** — the stable category, the duplex homotopy category and `HMF(S,w)` are all quotients by a two-sided ideal ([`TauCeti.MorphismIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/MorphismIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.MorphismIdeal)), so each inherits its linear structure, universal property and the descent of functors and equivalences from one construction.
- **The shift generated by an autoequivalence** — [`CategoryTheory.Equivalence.hasShift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Shift/Autoequivalence.html#CategoryTheory.Equivalence.hasShift) turns any autoequivalence into a coherent ℤ-shift; applied to stable suspension it gives the shift ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableHasShift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Shift.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableHasShift)) against which Happel's triangles are stated.
- **Finite-projective matrix factorizations** — [`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Basic.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization) is the full subcategory of curved duplexes of finitely generated modules with projective components, so its null-homotopies, parity shift and homotopy category `HMF(S,w)` come from duplexes.

### Roadmap coverage

- **Layer 0** — done: ideal quotients, the factor-through ideal, Frobenius exact categories and `Stable E`, Frobenius functionals with the Nakayama automorphism, and the module-category theorem.
- **Layer 1** — partial: the autoequivalence, independence of presentations, the shift, standard and cone triangles, and descent of exact functors with a suspension comparison are proved; the triangulated axioms, triangle functors, sums, opposites, idempotent completion and extension-closed subcategories are not.
- **Layers 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8** — partial. Layer 2 has the Frobenius theorem and `stmod-A` as an additive quotient, but no triangulation, Gorenstein-projectives or singularity categories. Layer 3 has the periodic ℤ-shift and the curvature-zero duplex equivalence, but no differential modules, split Frobenius structure, triangulated `K_n` or `D_n`. Layer 5 has only the curved-duplex specialization, with its homotopy category, parity shift and cones. Layer 7 has matrix factorizations and their homotopy category, not yet triangulated. Layer 8 has only the inclusion of matrix factorizations into curved duplexes.
- **Layers 4 and 6** — untouched.
- **Worked examples** — only the Frobenius functional on `k[x]/(x^n)` and the factorization `x^i, x^(n-i)` of `x^n` exist.

## The frontier

- **Happel's triangulation** — prove rotation, completion of morphisms of triangles and the octahedral axiom for the distinguished triangles ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableDistinguishedTriangles`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulation.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableDistinguishedTriangles)) and install `Pretriangulated` and `IsTriangulated`; the shift and a cone triangle on every morphism exist. Triangle functors and a triangulated `stmod-A` wait on this.
- **Triangulated homotopy categories of duplexes and matrix factorizations** — the duplex cone and its parity-shift compatibility exist ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.cone`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Cone.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.cone)); a ℤ-shift on the homotopy category, distinguished triangles and the axioms do not, and `HMF(S,w)` also needs closure under cones and direct sums.
- **The `k[x]/(x^n)` calculation** — `stmod-A` and the stable loop functor are available; the indecomposables `M_i = A/(x^i)`, the syzygies `ΩM_i ≅ M_(n-i)`, the stable Hom dimensions and the base change of `x^i, x^(n-i)` to `A` remain.
- **Differential modules and `K_n(A)`** — no differential-module type exists; Layer 3 needs it with its bridges, periodic cones, and the componentwise split Frobenius structure whose stable category is `K_n(A)`, before periodic homology, `D_n(A)` and Layer 4's compression.
- **Curved DG algebras and modules** — the right-convention curved DG algebra with non-central curvature, its graded opposite with curvature `-op(w)`, curved modules and their homotopy category; Layer 6's derived categories of the second kind cannot start before them.
