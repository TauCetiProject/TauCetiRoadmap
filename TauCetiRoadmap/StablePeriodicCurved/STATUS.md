## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done. Happel's theorem and its functoriality are proved, so the core of Layer 1 is in place. Layers 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 all have their basic objects, curved DG algebras and modules included, but none of their triangulations or comparison theorems yet. Layer 4 has not begun.

### Named results

- **Happel's theorem** — the stable category of a Frobenius exact category, with the shift generated by stable suspension and the triangles generated by conflations, is triangulated ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated)).
- **Exact functors induce triangle functors** — an exact functor between Frobenius exact categories that preserves projective-injectives descends to a triangle functor of stable categories, with its shift comparison built from injective presentations ([`TauCeti.StableConflationExact.stableFunctorIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Functor/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.StableConflationExact.stableFunctorIsTriangulated)).
- **The module-category Frobenius theorem** — if a finite-dimensional algebra over a field is self-injective on both sides, its finite-dimensional modules form a Frobenius exact category ([`FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Frobenius.html#FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius)).
- **The curved opposite** — the Koszul-signed graded opposite of a curved DG algebra is curved with curvature `-op(w)`, not `op(w)` ([`TauCeti.IsCurvedDGAlgebra.gradedOpposite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Algebra/Opposite.html#TauCeti.IsCurvedDGAlgebra.gradedOpposite)). Left curved modules are exactly right curved modules over it ([`TauCeti.isCurvedDGLeftModule_iff_gradedOppositeRight`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Opposite.html#TauCeti.isCurvedDGLeftModule_iff_gradedOppositeRight)).
- **Two-periodic complexes are curved duplexes of curvature zero** — this equivalence also holds on homotopy categories ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Duplex.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **Morphism-ideal quotients** — the stable category, the duplex homotopy category and `HMF(S,w)` are all quotients by one two-sided ideal construction ([`TauCeti.MorphismIdeal`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Preadditive/MorphismIdeal/Basic.html#TauCeti.MorphismIdeal)).
- **The DG category of curved modules** — the curved Hom differential squares to zero, so right curved modules form a DG category ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.instDGCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/DGCategory.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.instDGCategory)). In its homotopy category, two maps agree exactly when they differ by the boundary of an odd homotopy.
- **Gorenstein-projective modules** — degree-zero cycles of totally acyclic complexes ([`TauCeti.IsGorensteinProjective`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/TotallyAcyclic.html#TauCeti.IsGorensteinProjective)). They are stated for left modules, where the roadmap uses right modules.

None of these is yet a functor between homotopy categories. Compression, localization, the Gorenstein, cokernel and DG-to-curved functors, and the diagrams are missing.

## The frontier

- **Shifts and cones of curved modules** — the curved homotopy category exists but has no shift, cone or triangulation. Layer 6's acyclic classes wait on this.
- **Triangulated `HMF(S,w)` and duplex homotopy categories** — cones, parity shift and biproducts exist. What remains is a ℤ-shift, distinguished triangles and the axioms, or else the componentwise split Frobenius structure, after which Happel's theorem applies.
- **Gorenstein-projectives as a Frobenius category** — prove independence of the complete resolution, for right modules as the roadmap specifies, then build `Gproj-R`, its stable category and the comparison with totally acyclic complexes ahead of `D_sg`.
- **The split Frobenius structure on differential modules and periodic complexes** — identify the projective-injectives with contractible objects, so that `K₁` and `K_n` become triangulated. `D_n` and all of Layer 4 depend on this.
- **The `k[x]/(x^n)` calculation** — Happel's theorem now applies to `stmod-A`. The indecomposables `M_i`, the syzygies `ΩM_i ≅ M_(n-i)` and the stable Hom dimensions remain.
