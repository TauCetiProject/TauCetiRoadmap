## Where this roadmap stands

**At a glance.** Layer 0 is done, Layer 1 nearly so, and every homotopy category the roadmap triangulates (Frobenius stable categories, `K_n`, duplexes, curved modules, `HMF(S,w)`) now is. `D_n` exists and Layer 4 has started. Singularity categories, derived categories of the second kind, Stai's hull theorem, tensor products and the hypersurface comparisons have not begun.

### Named results

- **Happel's theorem** — the stable category of a Frobenius exact category is triangulated, with shift generated by stable suspension and triangles coming from conflations ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated)).
- **The periodic homotopy category is triangulated** — `K_n` carries Happel's triangulation transported from the split Frobenius structure, and its distinguished triangles are exactly the mapping-cone triangles ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.mem_distTriang_iff`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.mem_distTriang_iff)).
- **Curved modules have a triangulated homotopy category** — although curved modules have no homology, the graded-split structure is Frobenius, and the curved homotopy category is triangulated with the cohomological shift ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.homotopyIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.homotopyIsTriangulated)).
- **Compression is a triangle functor** — summing the terms of a bounded complex by residue mod `n` gives a triangle functor `D^b → D_n` ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.instIsTriangulatedBoundedDerivedCategoryDerivedCompressionFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Compression/Derived.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.instIsTriangulatedBoundedDerivedCategoryDerivedCompressionFunctor)), induced from the one on `K^b → K_n`.
- **The stable module theory of `k[x]/(x^n)`** — over any field, the `M_i = A/(x^i)` are the indecomposables ([`TauCeti.isIndecomposableModule_iff_exists_linearEquiv_quotient_span_op_root_X_pow_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/RingTheory/Polynomial/Truncated/Module.html#TauCeti.isIndecomposableModule_iff_exists_linearEquiv_quotient_span_op_root_X_pow_pow)), each has a free resolution alternating `x^i` and `x^(n-i)` ([`TauCeti.cyclicModuleRootPowProjectiveResolution`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Projective/CyclicResolution.html#TauCeti.cyclicModuleRootPowProjectiveResolution)), and stable Homs have dimension `min(i, j, n-i, n-j)` ([`TauCeti.FGModuleCat.finrank_stableModuleHom_cyclicModule_root_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable/CyclicHom.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.finrank_stableModuleHom_cyclicModule_root_pow)).

### Notable definitions and infrastructure

- **The periodic derived category** — `D_n` is the Verdier quotient of `K_n` by the acyclic complexes ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.DerivedCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Derived.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.DerivedCategory)), and also the localization at quasi-isomorphisms; every Stai statement targets it.
- **The orbit category** — `D/[n]` is built from its hom groups `⨁ Hom(X, Y[nj])` ([`TauCeti.OrbitCategory`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Shift/OrbitCategory.html#TauCeti.OrbitCategory)), and it has Keller's universal property, both existence and uniqueness of the descent ([`TauCeti.OrbitCategory.isoLift`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Shift/OrbitCategory.html#TauCeti.OrbitCategory.isoLift)).
- **Second-kind acyclic classes** — inside the curved homotopy category, totalizations of short exact sequences generate the absolutely acyclic, coacyclic ([`TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.coacyclic`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/Acyclic.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.coacyclic)) and contraacyclic classes, the kernels for `D_abs`, `D_co` and `D_ctr`.

## The frontier

- **Derived categories of the second kind** — the three acyclic classes exist. What remains is to form `D_abs`, `D_co` and `D_ctr` as Verdier quotients, prove `Ac_abs ⊆ Ac_co` and `Ac_abs ⊆ Ac_ctr`, and build the comparison functors. The generic inclusion of the thick closure into the localizing closure is already proved ([`triangEnvelope_le_localizingClosure`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Triangulated/LocalizingClosure.html#CategoryTheory.ObjectProperty.triangEnvelope_le_localizingClosure)).
- **Stai's orbit and hull theorems** — descend compression from `D^b(mod-Λ)` to its orbit category, then prove full faithfulness, the essential image, and generation of `D_n` under finite global dimension.
- **Buchweitz's theorem** — prove that stable cycles give an equivalence between the totally acyclic homotopy category and stable Gorenstein-projectives. Then define `D_sg`; the bounded derived category is now available as a localization of `K^b`.
- **The flagship matrix factorization** — its reduction has cokernel `M_i` and `k[x]` is flat over `k[x^n]`; matching `HMF`, stable MCM and `D_sg` waits on the three hypersurface comparisons.
- **Tensor products** — neither curved modules nor matrix factorizations have them yet, and Layer 7's summed-potential API depends on them.
