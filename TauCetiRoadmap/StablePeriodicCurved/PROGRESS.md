## StablePeriodicCurved: 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-26 (`97e4837` to `759eb3e`)

This first stretch takes the stable half of the roadmap to the threshold of Happel's theorem. The **module-category Frobenius theorem** ([`FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Frobenius.html#FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius)) makes the finite-dimensional modules over a finite-dimensional algebra that is self-injective on both sides a Frobenius exact category, whose stable category is `stmod-A` ([`TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleCategory)). In the stable category of any Frobenius exact category, suspension and loops are quasi-inverse additive autoequivalences ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableSuspensionEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Autoequivalence.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableSuspensionEquivalence)), independent of the chosen presentations up to canonical isomorphism. Suspension generates a ℤ-shift ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableHasShift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Shift.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableHasShift)), conflations give Happel's standard triangles ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConflationTriangle_mem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulation.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConflationTriangle_mem)), and every stable morphism lies in a distinguished cone triangle ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConeTriangleOf_mem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulation.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConeTriangleOf_mem)). Rotation, morphisms of triangles and the octahedral axiom are not yet proved, so the stable category has no `Pretriangulated` instance.

Beneath this sit morphism-ideal quotients with their universal property, induced functors, equivalences and opposites (TauCeti#6816, TauCeti#7212), the factor-through ideal, and relative injectives completing Frobenius exact structures. On the algebra side, Frobenius functionals on a finite-dimensional algebra correspond exactly to right-module isomorphisms `A ≅ A⁺` ([`TauCeti.frobeniusFunctionalEquivDualEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#TauCeti.frobeniusFunctionalEquivDualEquiv)), and they make it self-injective on both sides ([`LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_self), [`LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_op_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_op_self)). For `n > 0` the top coefficient is a symmetric Frobenius functional on `R[X]/(X^n)` over any commutative ring ([`AdjoinRoot.isSymmetricFrobeniusFunctional_lastCoeff_X_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/AdjoinRoot.html#AdjoinRoot.isSymmetricFrobeniusFunctional_lastCoeff_X_pow)), the first step of the flagship `k[x]/(x^n)` example.

On the periodic and curved side, curved duplexes ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Duplex.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex)), pairs `X₀ ⇄ X₁` whose two composites are `w • 𝟙`, have a homotopy category, a parity shift, contractible disks and mapping cones. At `w = 0` they are Mathlib's two-periodic complexes, and the equivalence passes to homotopy categories ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Duplex.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence)); periodic complexes of every period carry a ℤ-shift ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.instHasShift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Shift.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.instHasShift)). Finite-projective matrix factorizations ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Basic.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization)) are the duplexes of finitely generated modules with projective components; they have a homotopy category and include `S[X] --X^i--> S[X] --X^(n-i)--> S[X]`. Gorenstein-projectives, differential modules, curved DG algebras and modules, compression and derived categories of the second kind have not begun.

Matrix factorizations gained a [tensor bifunctor](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Tensor/Homotopy.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.homotopyTensor) on `HMF`. They also gained a stable cokernel functor to `S/(w)`-modules, which [carries the shift to `Ω`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Cokernel/Syzygy.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.shiftFunctorOneCompQuotientStableCokernelIso). The flagship example now has the [alternating free resolution of `M_i`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Projective/CyclicResolution.html#TauCeti.cyclicModuleRootPowProjectiveResolution), and `S --x^i--> S --x^(n-i)--> S` has [cokernel `M_i`](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Polynomial/Cokernel.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.powerXOfLECokernelQuotientIso).

Finitely generated Gorenstein-projectives [form a Frobenius category](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/GorensteinProjective/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.GorensteinProjectiveModuleCat.exactStructure_isFrobenius), and complete resolutions are [unique up to homotopy](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/GorensteinProjective/CompleteResolution.html#CochainComplex.IsTotallyAcyclic.homotopyEquiv). The stable equivalence with totally acyclic complexes, `D_sg` and Buchweitz's theorem are still missing. In Layer 1, the opposite-category comparison is now a [triangle equivalence](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Opposite/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.projectiveStableOpEquivalenceIsTriangulated).
