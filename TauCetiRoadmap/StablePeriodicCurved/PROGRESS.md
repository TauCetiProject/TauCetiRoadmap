## StablePeriodicCurved: 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-26 (`97e4837` to `759eb3e`)

This first stretch takes the stable half of the roadmap to the threshold of Happel's theorem. The **module-category Frobenius theorem** ([`FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Frobenius.html#FGModuleCat.abelian_isFrobenius)) makes the finite-dimensional modules over a finite-dimensional algebra that is self-injective on both sides a Frobenius exact category, whose stable category is `stmod-A` ([`TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleCategory`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleCategory)). In the stable category of any Frobenius exact category, suspension and loops are quasi-inverse additive autoequivalences ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableSuspensionEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Autoequivalence.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableSuspensionEquivalence)), independent of the chosen presentations up to canonical isomorphism. Suspension generates a ℤ-shift ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableHasShift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Shift.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableHasShift)), conflations give Happel's standard triangles ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConflationTriangle_mem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulation.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConflationTriangle_mem)), and every stable morphism lies in a distinguished cone triangle ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConeTriangleOf_mem`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulation.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConeTriangleOf_mem)). Rotation, morphisms of triangles and the octahedral axiom are not yet proved, so the stable category has no `Pretriangulated` instance.

Beneath this sit morphism-ideal quotients with their universal property, induced functors, equivalences and opposites (TauCeti#6816, TauCeti#7212), the factor-through ideal, and relative injectives completing Frobenius exact structures. On the algebra side, Frobenius functionals on a finite-dimensional algebra correspond exactly to right-module isomorphisms `A ≅ A⁺` ([`TauCeti.frobeniusFunctionalEquivDualEquiv`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#TauCeti.frobeniusFunctionalEquivDualEquiv)), and they make it self-injective on both sides ([`LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_self), [`LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_op_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/Basic.html#LinearMap.IsFrobeniusFunctional.moduleInjective_op_self)). For `n > 0` the top coefficient is a symmetric Frobenius functional on `R[X]/(X^n)` over any commutative ring ([`AdjoinRoot.isSymmetricFrobeniusFunctional_lastCoeff_X_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Algebra/Frobenius/AdjoinRoot.html#AdjoinRoot.isSymmetricFrobeniusFunctional_lastCoeff_X_pow)), the first step of the flagship `k[x]/(x^n)` example.

On the periodic and curved side, curved duplexes ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Duplex.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex)), pairs `X₀ ⇄ X₁` whose two composites are `w • 𝟙`, have a homotopy category, a parity shift, contractible disks and mapping cones. At `w = 0` they are Mathlib's two-periodic complexes, and the equivalence passes to homotopy categories ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Duplex.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.periodicComplexEquivalence)); periodic complexes of every period carry a ℤ-shift ([`TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.instHasShift`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/Shift.html#TauCeti.PeriodicComplex.instHasShift)). Finite-projective matrix factorizations ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Basic.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization)) are the duplexes of finitely generated modules with projective components; they have a homotopy category and include `S[X] --X^i--> S[X] --X^(n-i)--> S[X]`. Gorenstein-projectives, differential modules, curved DG algebras and modules, compression and derived categories of the second kind have not begun.

## StablePeriodicCurved: 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-30 (`759eb3e` to `0d3161a`)

**Happel's theorem** is now proved: the stable category of a Frobenius exact category, with the shift generated by stable suspension and the triangles coming from conflations, is triangulated ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableIsTriangulated)). The missing axioms went in one after another: rotation of distinguished triangles, completion of a commutative square to a morphism of triangles ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.complete_stable_distinguished_triangle_morphism`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/TriangleMorphism.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.complete_stable_distinguished_triangle_morphism)), the pretriangulated structure ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stablePretriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Pretriangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stablePretriangulated)), and finally an octahedron built from a Noether conflation ([`TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConflationOctahedron`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobenius.stableConflationOctahedron)). Both are stated as definitions to install locally rather than as global instances. Triangle functors, and the compatibility with sums, opposites and idempotent completion, are still to come.

Matrix factorizations also picked up much of the structure that Layer 7 asks for. There are finite biproducts, mapping cones compatible with parity shift, and duality as a contravariant additive functor from potential `w` to `-w` with the roadmap's pinned sign ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.dualFunctor`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Dual.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.dualFunctor)), together with the double-dual isomorphism. A map is null-homotopic exactly when it factors through the disk sum on its source ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.mem_nullHomotopic_iff_factors_diskSum`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/DiskFactorization.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.mem_nullHomotopic_iff_factors_diskSum)), which is groundwork for the Frobenius structure. Two Layer 8 functors now exist: base change to a two-periodic complex over `S/(w)` ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.quotientPeriodicComplex`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/BaseChange.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.quotientPeriodicComplex)), and a fully faithful embedding of `HMF(S,w)` into the curved-duplex homotopy category ([`TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.homotopyInclusion`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/HomotopyInclusion.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.homotopyInclusion)). `HMF(S,w)` is not yet triangulated.

Curved duplexes gained a two-periodic Hom complex and the square-zero duplex from the worked examples, which is contractible and has zero periodic homology ([`TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.isZero_squareZero_periodicHomology`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/SquareZeroDuplex.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.isZero_squareZero_periodicHomology)).

## StablePeriodicCurved: 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02 (`0d3161a` to `d449639`)

The curved half of the roadmap now has its basic objects. A [curved DG algebra](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Algebra/Defs.html#TauCeti.IsCurvedDGAlgebra) has a differential whose square is the commutator with a curvature `w` that need not be central. The graded opposite is again curved with curvature `-op(w)`, the roadmap's pinned sign ([`IsCurvedDGAlgebra.gradedOpposite`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Algebra/Opposite.html#TauCeti.IsCurvedDGAlgebra.gradedOpposite)), and a change of connection shifts the curvature to `w - da - a²`. Right curved modules followed, with left modules shown to be exactly right modules over the opposite ([`isCurvedDGLeftModule_iff_gradedOppositeRight`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Opposite.html#TauCeti.isCurvedDGLeftModule_iff_gradedOppositeRight)). The curved Hom differential squares to zero ([`curvedDifferential_comp_self`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/HomComplex.html#TauCeti.dgRightModuleCochains.curvedDifferential_comp_self)), so curved modules form a DG category. Its homotopy category identifies two maps exactly when they differ by the boundary of an odd homotopy ([`homOf_eq_iff_exists_homotopy`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Module/Right/DGCategory.html#TauCeti.CurvedDGRightModuleCat.homOf_eq_iff_exists_homotopy)). Shifts, cones and a triangulation are still to come.

On the stable side, Happel's construction is now functorial: an exact functor preserving projective-injectives induces a triangle functor between stable categories ([`stableFunctorIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Functor/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.StableConflationExact.stableFunctorIsTriangulated)). Most of the work went into general machinery that transports shift compatibility from a single intertwining isomorphism. Layer 2 got [totally acyclic complexes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/TotallyAcyclic.html#CochainComplex.IsTotallyAcyclic) and [Gorenstein-projective modules](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/TotallyAcyclic.html#TauCeti.IsGorensteinProjective), stated for left modules. Projectives qualify, and so does the ideal `Ab` for an exact pair of zero-divisors `a, b` ([`isGorensteinProjective_span_singleton`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/TotallyAcyclic.html#TauCeti.isGorensteinProjective_span_singleton)). Independence of the complete resolution has not been proved.

Layer 3 now has [differential modules](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/DifferentialModule.html#TauCeti.DifferentialModule). They are equivalent to one-periodic complexes and to Mathlib's differential objects, and two-periodic complexes map to them by [forgetting parity](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Periodic/DifferentialModule.html#TauCeti.DifferentialModule.forgetParity). Duality also now covers curved duplexes with projective components, including the double-dual isomorphism.

## StablePeriodicCurved: 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-05 (`d449639` to `1d09589`)

**`HMF(S,w)` is now triangulated** ([`MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated)), and so is the homotopy category of curved duplexes ([`CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Triangulated.html#TauCeti.CurvedDuplex.HomotopyCategory.instIsTriangulated)). In both cases the shift by one is the parity shift. Both come from the route the roadmap prescribes. With componentwise split conflations, finite-projective matrix factorizations form a [Frobenius exact category](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.splitExact_isFrobenius) whose projective-injectives are the contractible factorizations, and the same holds for [curved duplexes](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Curved/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.curvedDuplex_split_isFrobenius). The stable category is then [equivalent to the homotopy category](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CommutativeAlgebra/MatrixFactorization/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.MatrixFactorization.stableHomotopyEquivalence), stable suspension becomes parity shift, and Happel's triangles carry over. The same argument shows that [complexes of any shape](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/HomologicalComplex/Frobenius.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.homologicalComplex_split_isFrobenius) in which every index has a successor and a predecessor form a Frobenius category, and its stable category is [their homotopy category](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/HomologicalComplex/HomotopyCategory.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.homologicalComplexSplitStableHomotopyEquivalence). This covers `ZMod n`-periodic complexes, but no triangulation has yet been installed on `K_n` itself, and differential modules are not covered.

The flagship `k[x]/(x^n)` calculation is mostly done. For `M_i = A/(x^i)` the stable syzygy is [`ΩM_i ≅ M_(n-i)`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable/Syzygy.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleLoopCyclicModuleRootPowIso), so [`Ω²M_i ≅ M_i`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable/Syzygy.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.stableModuleLoopLoopCyclicModuleRootPowIso). Over any field, the stable Hom space `M_i → M_j` has dimension `min(i, j, n-i, n-j)` ([`finrank_stableModuleHom_cyclicModule_root_pow`](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/Algebra/Category/FGModuleCat/Stable/CyclicHom.html#TauCeti.FGModuleCat.finrank_stableModuleHom_cyclicModule_root_pow)). It has not yet been proved that the `M_i` are all the indecomposables.

Layer 1 also gained much of its missing API. Descended natural transformations [commute with shifts](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Functor/NatTrans.html#TauCeti.StableConflationExact.stableNatTrans_commShift). Extension-closed [Frobenius subcategories](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/FullSubcategory.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.IsFrobeniusSubcategory) embed fully faithfully on stable categories by a triangle functor. Stable categories also commute with [products](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Product.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.projectiveStableProdEquivalence) and [opposites](https://taucetiproject.github.io/TauCeti/docs/TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Exact/Stable/Opposite/Basic.html#TauCeti.ExactStructure.projectiveStableOpEquivalence), with suspension in the opposite matching loops. Idempotent completion is still missing.
